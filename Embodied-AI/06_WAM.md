# World Action Models — Deep Dive

> [!abstract] Overview
> World Action Models (WAMs) learn to predict future states of the environment, giving robots the ability to "imagine" consequences before acting. Unlike VLAs that map observations directly to actions, WAMs explicitly model dynamics — enabling planning, robustness to perturbation, and sample-efficient learning. This note maps the full WAM landscape across five paradigms: VideoGen, latent prediction (JEPA family), model-based RL ([[1912.01603|Dreamer]] lineage), VLM-integrated, and efficient/action-centered designs.

## Part A — Design Space

*The dimensions every WAM choice sits on: where to predict, when to predict, how to condition.*

### 1. The Design Space

> [!info] WAM Definition & Cascaded vs Joint Taxonomy
> [[2605.12090|WAM-Survey]] is the first paper to formally define WAMs and disambiguate them from VLAs (no dynamics model) and pure World Models (no action generation). It splits the architectural landscape along a single axis — ==Cascaded== (sequential: predict next state, then derive action) vs ==Joint== (unified state-action prediction) — and surveys four data-ecosystem axes (robot, human, simulation, internet-scale video) plus emerging evaluation protocols. The Cascaded/Joint distinction is the field-defining taxonomy that subsequent sections of this note implicitly use.

Three axes define where a WAM sits in the design landscape:

| Axis | Options | Trade-off |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Where to predict** | Pixel space ([[2602.15922\|DreamZero]]), Latent space (JEPA, [[2504.02792\|UWM]]), Action space ([[2205.09991\|Diffuser]]) | Pixel = rich but slow; Latent = fast but abstract; Action = efficient but no visual feedback |
| **When to predict** | Training-time only ([[2603.16666\|Fast-WAM]]), Test-time imagination ([[2602.15922\|DreamZero]]) | Training-time = fast inference; Test-time = more robust but 4.8x slower |
| **What to predict** | Full video (Cosmos), Optical flow ([[2508.18269\|FlowVLA]]), Compressed latent ([[2602.22010\|WoG]]), Future embeddings (JEPA) | Full video = interpretable but expensive; Latent = efficient but opaque |

**Where to predict** determines computational cost and expressiveness. Pixel-space prediction ([[2602.15922|DreamZero]]'s 14B DiT) generates full video frames — maximally expressive but requires iterative denoising (~150ms per frame). Latent-space prediction ([[2602.10098|VLA-JEPA]]'s V-JEPA2 predictor) operates on compressed embeddings — a single forward pass (~10ms) but loses fine visual detail. Action-space prediction ([[2205.09991|Diffuser]]) skips visual prediction entirely — fastest but provides no visual feedback for planning or debugging.

**When to predict** is the 2026 insight from [[2603.16666|Fast-WAM]]: you need video generation at training time (to learn spatiotemporal priors from internet video) but NOT at test time (where it adds 4.8x latency). The world model's value is in the representations it creates during training, not in the predictions it makes during deployment.

**What to predict** trades off between interpretability and efficiency. Full video (Cosmos) is human-readable but expensive. Optical flow ([[2508.18269|FlowVLA]]) captures motion efficiently. Future embeddings (JEPA) are opaque but compact. The choice depends on whether a human needs to inspect the predictions (development/debugging) or only the policy needs them (deployment).

#### 1.1 Surveys, Definitions & Position Papers

Conceptual framing of the WAM landscape: formal definitions disambiguating WAMs from VLAs and pure World Models, taxonomies of how video generation becomes world modeling, consistency/causality principles, and position papers on what the field still lacks.

- **[[2607.00836|World Action Models Tutorial]]** — A tutorial formalizing ==world model== vs ==world action model== definitions via an observation-space/state-space prediction taxonomy, then classifying WAMs into four paradigms: ==imagine-then-execute==, ==video-feature-conditioned action==, ==joint video-action modeling==, ==auxiliary video prediction==.
- **[[2606.20781|WAM-Survey-2026]]** — A survey establishing a precise ==World Action Model== definition (vs World Models / VLAs) plus a two-tier taxonomy: three design philosophies (==Render-and-Decode==, ==Latent-Only==, ==Video-Generation-Free==) and a four-axis anatomy (Predictive Substrate, Action Coupling, Backbone, Deployment Regime); maps the richness-vs-compute trade-off.
- **[[2606.06556|Robots-Need-More]]** — A position paper arguing generalist robots must overcome the ==grounding bottleneck== (turning unstructured physical data into robot-usable supervision) via four pillars: ==Physical Data Engines==, Task-preserving Retargeting, Physics-Grounded World Models, Self-Improving Deployment Loops; reframes the "next foundation model" as a compounding system.
- **[[2606.05960|a-Data-Flywheel]]** — A ==logistics data flywheel== position paper (industrial-data conversion, WM-DAgger recovery synthesis, deployment-feedback loop) from the same team as [[2604.11351|WM-DAgger]]; validates the recipe at **93.3%** soft-bag-pushing with only **5** demos and **84.4%/70.0%** seen/unseen pick-and-place.
- **[[2604.28185|Visual-Generation-Survey]]** — A five-level taxonomy tracing visual generation's evolution from atomic pixel mapping to ==agentic world modeling==, with in-the-wild stress tests diagnosing frontier models; finds leading closed systems already run an **L4 agentic generation** stack (VLM + external control loop) while exposing gaps in spatial and causal reasoning.
- **[[2604.17651|I-WM]]** — A vision paper for ==Infrastructure-centric World Models== using fixed roadside sensors to add "temporal depth" to ego-vehicle "spatial breadth," via a ==dual-layer architecture== (annotation-free perception + self-supervised generative modeling) + a three-tier data strategy (proprietary recordings, V2X datasets, simulation), with an Infrastructure-VLA roadmap.
- **[[2604.04707|OpenWorldLib]]** — A standardized ==world-model definition== (internal representations + action-conditioned simulation + long-term memory) plus a modular five-component inference framework (==Operator/Synthesis/Reasoning/Representation/Memory==); benchmarks interactive video generators (Hunyuan-WorldPlay, Cosmos, WoW) + 3D generators under one codebase.
- **[[2605.00080|WM-for-Robot-Learning-Survey]]** — A robotics-centric survey categorizing world models by ==architectural coupling== (decoupled predict-then-act → unified MoE/MoT/VLA backbones → latent-space modeling) and functional role (policy substrate, RL environment, real-time evaluator); finds long-horizon tasks remain the persisting bottleneck across integration styles.
- **[[2602.23152|Trinity-of-Consistency]]** — A theoretical framework positing ==Modal + Spatial + Temporal consistency== as the defining principle of General World Models, plus ==CoW-Bench== (18 sub-tasks, 16 atomic failure checks); current models excel at local plausibility but collapse on global world-state maintenance and entangled spatio-temporal-modal constraints.
- **[[2601.17067|Video-Generation-as-World-Models-Survey]]** — A mechanistic survey taxonomizing video generators by ==State Construction== (implicit vs explicit) and ==Dynamics Modeling== (causal architecture reformulation vs external causal integration); finds emergent physical coherence but ~**24%** causal accuracy on Physics-IQ — surface correlations, not robust simulators.
- **[[2511.08585|Visual-World-Roadmap]]** — A conceptual roadmap with a ==four-generation taxonomy== (Faithfulness → Interactiveness → Planning → Stochasticity) defining a ==physical world model== as a next-scene video prediction engine; places Sora/Kling in gens 1–2, NWM/Genie 3 in gen 3, and frames stochasticity (low-probability events) as the underexplored frontier.
- **[[2506.22355|Embodied-AI-World-Modeling]]** — A Meta AI position paper arguing embodied agents need a ==world-modeling approach== with distinct ==JEPA-based physical world models== + ==mental world models== for human intent, plus rigorous physical/causal-reasoning benchmarks; SOTA video-LMs hit only **40.2%** on Minimal Video Pairs (vs **92.9%** human), and VLWM lifts planning SR **+20%**.
- **[[2506.01622|General-Agents-World-Models]]** — A formal ==proof by reduction== that any ==bounded goal-conditioned agent== solving multi-step (n>1) ==LTL== goals must contain an extractable predictive world model, recoverable with error **O(δ/√n)+O(1/n)**; myopic (n=1) agents need none — no model-free shortcut to general goal-directed competence.
- **[[2505.09305|Embodied-intelligent-industrial-robotics]]** — A survey + knowledge-driven framework for industrial embodied AI built on five modules centered on a ==World Model== integrating industrial knowledge graphs + semantic maps, with ==LLM task planning== + a ==skill-language== controller and a bidirectional digital-twin loop; validated on a flexible valve-assembly case study.
- **[[2503.04641|Multimodal-Generative-Models-Survey]]** — A unified survey organizing generative models by ==dimensionality progression== (2D → Video → 3D → 4D appearance/dynamics/geometry), tracing cross-dimensional priors (2D SDS supervision lifts 3D/4D; higher-dim structure regularizes lower-dim generation) toward unified spatiotemporal world-model backbones.
- **[[2411.14499|Understanding-World-or-Predicting-Future-Survey]]** — Categorizes world models by two functions — ==implicit representation== vs ==future-state prediction== — tracing their lineage from cognitive theories to deep learning across game, embodied, urban, and societal intelligence; names physical-rule adherence and sim-to-real as persisting open problems.
- **[[2407.06886|Aligning-Cyber-Space-Embodied-AI-Survey]]** — A structured review spanning embodied robots, simulators, perception, interaction, agent architectures, and sim-to-real adaptation, integrating MLMs and World Models into one taxonomy; proposes the ==ARIO (All Robots In One)== dataset standard and a **3M-episode / 321,064-task** unified dataset to fix cross-platform data heterogeneity.
- **[[2603.28489|Video-Generation-Efficiency-Survey]]** — A three-dimensional taxonomy (==efficient modeling paradigms==, ==network architectures==, ==inference algorithms== — diffusion distillation, sparse attention, quantization) organizing the efficiency techniques that make video-generation world simulators practically deployable for autonomous driving, embodied AI, and interactive simulation.
- **[[2607.11689|WAM-to-Embodied-Brains-Roadmap]]** — Functionally decomposes physical agents into an **Embodied Brain** (general reasoning) and a **Physical Harness** (execution) mediated by explicit contracts — a **WAM Prediction Contract** exposing decision-relevant consequences plus Embodiment/Task/Trace Cards for standardization; names three coupled gaps as the field's progress barrier.

#### 1.2 Benchmarks & Evaluation

Benchmarks that probe the *capabilities* a WAM must have — memory consistency across revisits, fine-grained action control, and forward/inverse world-modeling competence — rather than raw visual fidelity. The axis: what aspect of world understanding the benchmark isolates and how it scores it.

- **[[2607.02642|GigaWorld-1]]** — A roadmap pairing GigaWorld-1-Plus (**5B**) with ==WMBench==, an outcome-centric VLM evaluator for scoring WMs as robot-policy proxies; **0.6834** avg score beating Cosmos-Predict2.5 (**0.6123**), **87.80%** exact agreement with human ratings, stable **40s** autoregressive rollouts.
- **[[2606.09803|Echo-Memory]]** — A controlled study of ==memory in action world models== that fixes the video DiT backbone and sweeps four memory families (Context, Compression, Spatial, State-Space) under a three-branch replay/in-domain/open-domain protocol; block-wise State-Space recurrence tops open-domain VLM score at **69.00** vs raw Context **58.63** and Spatial max **17.12**.
- **[[2606.04811|Dream.exe]]** — A ==closed-loop benchmark== converting generated manipulation videos into ==executable robot trajectories== scored in a ==MuJoCo== simulator over 101 RoboCasa365 episodes at three complexity levels; general video generators reach **21.4%** binary SR on multi-object but mostly **zero** on multi-stage, while Cosmos-Policy hits **E-SR 0.75** executability.
- **[[2605.29360|MiraBench]]** — A hierarchical benchmark probing ==action-conditioned reliability== of robotic world models across Physics Adherence, Action-Following, and ==Optimism-Bias Detection== via ==failure-inducing counterfactual== actions scored by VLM judges; success-only post-training + scaling (DreamDojo 2B→14B) raise completion but *collapse* failure preservation (**48.7%→12.8%**).
- **[[2605.27589|What-If-World]]** — A causal benchmark for general world models: a ==taxonomy of six physical primitives== over **319 contrastive prompt pairs** (each differing in one semantic variable) scored by the ==APEO VLM-judge== on paired causal correctness; no model exceeds **52%** paired APEO, single-video eval over-estimates by up to **52.2 pp** — fidelity ≠ causality.
- **[[2602.08025|MIND-Bench]]** — The first open-domain closed-loop ==revisited benchmark== isolating ==memory consistency== (temporal stability + contextual coherence) and ==action control== (accuracy + motion-scale generalization), built from **250** UE5 1080p videos over 8 scenes + an autoregressive MIND-World baseline; action accuracy deteriorates even with memory and in-domain actions.
- **[[2512.10958|WorldLens]]** — A full-spectrum driving-WM benchmark spanning 24 metrics across Generation/Reconstruction/Action-Following/Downstream-Task/Human-Preference, with the WorldLens-26K human-annotated dataset + a fine-tuned ==WorldLens-Agent== VLM auto-evaluator; no model excels universally, human realism scores average **2-3/10**.
- **[[2511.20937|ENACT]]** — A benchmark formalizing embodied cognition as ==forward + inverse world modeling== via two VQA sequence-reordering tasks over BEHAVIOR-simulated long-horizon egocentric interactions; top VLMs lag humans (**>90%** PA) beyond the shortest horizon, **81–84%** of errors from omission + hallucination — exposing weak interactive memory + a forward-worse-than-inverse gap.
- **[[2511.19584|MMBench-World-Models]]** — A benchmark + model pair: the **200**-task MMBench + Newt, a language-conditioned ==multitask world model== on ==TD-MPC2== with a four-pronged demo-integration strategy; **0.438** normalized score at 100M steps beating PPO/FastTD3, **16×**-horizon open-loop control (48 steps), **0.868** on 20 held-out tasks after 100k finetune steps.
- **[[2510.18135|World-in-World]]** — First closed-loop benchmark testing WMs' ==embodied utility== across four tasks (Active Recognition, ImageNav, A-EQA, Robotic Manipulation) via a ==Unified Action API== + counterfactual rollout planning; controllability + post-training data scaling beat visual quality (**58.26%→62.61%** AR).
- **[[2510.08398|VideoVerse]]** — A ten-dimension T2V ==world-model-capability== benchmark using ==hidden-semantics== prompts (implicit physics/causality, not explicit description) + Gemini-judged QA across 300 prompts; Sora-2/Veo-3 lead but even Veo-3 drops **80.3%→72.4%** on world-model vs basic dimensions.
- **[[2411.02385|PhyWorld]]** — A systematic ==Box2D/PHYRE simulation testbed== isolating ==in-distribution/OOD/combinatorial== generalization for scaled ==DiT video diffusion== (up to **456M** params, **6M** videos); OOD velocity error stays **an order of magnitude** higher than ID regardless of scale — scaling alone doesn't teach physical laws, only "case-based" pattern-matching.

## Part B — WAM Paradigms

*Five architectural families: VideoGen, latent prediction, Dreamer/RSSM, VLM-integrated, efficient/action-centered.*

### 2. VideoGen WAMs

Video diffusion models repurposed as world simulators. The richest source of physics priors — trained on internet-scale video data.

#### 2.1 Planning as Video Generation

The foundational insight: generating a video of the future IS a plan. Condition the diffusion model on the current observation and a language instruction, then denoise to produce a future video; an inverse dynamics model extracts the motor commands. This axis defines *what the WAM outputs* — a goal video, not actions directly.

### 3. Latent Prediction WAMs

Predict in representation space rather than pixel space — faster, more abstract, and avoids wasting capacity on irrelevant visual details. See [[07_Latent-World-Models#2. JEPA Evolution: Visual-Only → Dense → Vision-Language → Vision-Language-Action]] for the detailed JEPA evolution.

#### 3.1 JEPA Family

Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture: predict future embeddings from current embeddings rather than reconstructing pixels. The compressed-prediction axis is what makes latent WAMs ~10ms/step vs ~150ms for VideoGen.

- **[[2608.05720|PhyLatent]]** — Extends the ==LeWM== backbone with three training-only pathways (==Static Visual Invariance Constraint==, ==Physical State Grounding==, ==Counterfactual Action Separation Constraint==) fixing local latent pathologies discarded at inference; cuts Physical Identifiability Collapse **85.8%**, lifts MPC success **70%→78.1%** on OGBench-Cube and **81%→98%** on TwoRooms.
- **[[2608.01926|ProWorld]]** — Embeds visual observations into a ==hyperbolic latent space== to encode ==goal-conditioned progress order== via a ==progress-induced entailment cone== + future-discrimination loss, planned with ==CEM== over a progress-aware cost; **+9.67pp** avg SR over LeWM across four goal-reaching tasks, **+37.16%** relative on long-horizon AntMaze-L.
- **[[2607.25337|TD-JEPA]]** — Extends the ==LeWM== backbone with a directed ==Metric-Residual Network head== parameterizing a ==temporal cost function==, calibrated by ==reward-free temporal labels== plus a ==cross-trajectory hinge loss== against cost collapse; **100.0%** Two-Room, **82.2%** OGBench-Cube — directed cost wins on topological tasks, latent L2 on contact-rich ones.
- **[[2606.23444|SkyJEPA]]** — A long-horizon ==JEPA-style latent dynamics== model for zero-shot sim-to-real quadrotor control, with a ==physics-inspired prober== mapping latent predictions to physical state via residual corrections to a differentiable kinematic model; **3.9×** lower open-loop position RMSE, 26–38% lower real closed-loop position RMSE, robust to propeller/payload changes.
- **[[2603.22281|ThinkJEPA]]** — A JEPA WM with a ==VLM "thinker" branch== + ==dual-temporal perception== (dense for JEPA + sparse for VLM) + ==hierarchical pyramid feature extraction== injected via ==FiLM==; **ADE 0.061 / FDE 0.056** on EgoDex (vs **6** trajectory baselines), maintains best stability at H=**32** recursive rollout.
- **[[2603.19312|LeWM]]** — A ==Two-term objective== (MSE + ==Sketched-Isotropic-Gaussian Regularizer==) trained end-to-end on ViT-Tiny + Transformer with AdaLN and no stop-grad/EMA; it lifts Push-T SR **+18%** over PLDM and plans **48× faster** (<1 s/cycle), with temporal-latent paths straightening as an emergent effect.

#### 3.3 Self-Supervised Latent Models

Learn world representations with video-first self-supervised predictive objectives: the visual prediction signal over raw video / image streams does the heavy lifting, while several entries layer weak action or language signals on top (action-grounded alignment, latent-action targets, language-conditioned slots).

- **[[2608.05903|Robust-WAM]]** — A post-training method injecting learnable ==semantic foresight query tokens== into the action stream, aligned with appearance-invariant ==DINOv3 CLS embeddings== of future frames via shared positional encodings; lifts FastWAM's LIBERO-Plus OOD **49.7%→58.9%**, cuts a real-robot InD-to-OOD gap from **22.7 to 2.7** points under colored illumination.

#### 3.4 Latent-Action Models from Unlabeled Video

The data axis that makes latent WAMs scalable: when video carries no action labels, jointly learn an ==inverse dynamics model== to infer a *latent action* between frames and a ==forward dynamics model== to predict the next frame from it. The latent action becomes a pseudo-label that lets internet-scale and human video train policies — the axis here is how the latent-action space is *regularized* so it captures dynamics without leaking future appearance.

- **[[2607.09185|CD-LAM]]** — ==Causally debiased fine-tuning== for latent-action models via an ==embodiment-centric reconstruction loss==, an ==action-centric contrastive loss==, and ==latent-space calibration==, bridged to control by an ==MLP bridge==; zero-transition response **0.527 → 0.043**, camera-shift **3.6–8.3×** lower, FDCE **−42%** at 2B, matching baselines with **12×** fewer updates.
- **[[2606.16917|UMA]]** — A Unified Motion-Action model using ==3D object motion trajectories== as a shared interface bridging control + dynamics in one masked ==diffusion transformer== under ==flow matching==, with a contrastive task latent; **+20–25pp** zero-shot real control over UVA/COIL, sharper dynamics (MSE **0.054→0.042** vs PointWorld), strong few-shot via soft-prompt tuning on video.
- **[[2606.10363|HiMem-WAM]]** — A Hierarchical Memory-Gated World-Action Model combining ==hierarchical latent action== learning with a boundary-aware memory-gated module that writes compact task states at skill transitions, no test-time video gen; **97.7%** LIBERO, **76.0%** LIBERO-Plus, **26.3%** RMBench memory tasks (vs ACT **10.8%**), +**25.0%** real Hard tasks.
- **[[2605.15725|DiLA]]** — A ==Disentangled Latent Action== WM splitting video into dynamics-relevant ==structure== and dynamics-irrelevant ==content== pathways under a strict ==information bottleneck== (IDM infers latent actions, FDM predicts future structure; ==Mamba== content pathway); robust action transfer human→robot + virtual→real, surpassing SOTA SSIM/LPIPS on SSv2/RT-1.
- **[[2604.16391|DeFI]]** — Decouples forward/inverse dynamics pretraining: ==Stable-Video-Diffusion== forward model on mixed human+robot video, ==VQ-VAE Transformer== inverse model self-supervised on action-free video, then coupled fine-tuning; **4.51** CALVIN ABC-D length (vs OpenVLA **3.27**), **81.3%** real Franka SR at **60%** of the data.
- **[[2604.03340|AC-LAM]]** — Imposes a scene-wise ==additive composition prior== (z_ik ≈ z_ij + z_jk) on continuous latent actions via an ==L_AC-FDM forward-dynamics loss==, inducing identity/inverse algebra + suppressing scene/future leakage; residual **0.086** vs baselines' **0.960**, Pearson **r=0.489** vs **0.289**, **55%** vs **42%** sim SR, **60%** vs **40%** real SR.
- **[[2603.05815|HiLAM]]** — A ==hierarchical latent actions== model: an IDM infers low-level latent actions, an ==H-Net dynamic-chunking== module discovers variable-length high-level skills, both used as ==pseudo-labels== to pretrain a two-level policy on actionless video; **94%** LIBERO-Long, **84%** at half the expert demos — first LAM to capture long-horizon skill structure.
- **[[2602.12215|LDA-1B]]** — A **1.6B** ==latent dynamics action model== predicting ==structured DINO latent== (not pixels) in an MM-DiT, with ==universal data ingestion== assigning distinct roles to high-/low-quality/actionless data across **30K+ hours** (EI-30K); **55.4%** RoboCasa-GR1 (vs GR00T-N1.6 **47.6%**), monotonic scaling to 1.6B params.
- **[[2602.02762|LAPO+]]** — A theory + algorithm unifying ==VM-IDM== and ==IDM labeling== into one consistent IDM policy, showing IDM sample-efficiency comes from reduced complexity + stochasticity, then adding IDM-based decoding to ==latent action policy learning==; IDM labeling beats BC in **9/16** ProcGen with low-data gains, matching/exceeding policy sampling within UVA on Push-T + Libero10.
- **[[2601.05230|Latent-Action-World-Models]]** — A framework that learns a LAM + WM ==in the wild== on unlabeled video over a frozen ==V-JEPA 2-L== encoder, studying ==sparsity / noise / discretization== regularizers via ==future-leakage== + ==transferability== metrics; noise-added continuous latent actions plan competitively with action-labeled baselines on DROID + RECON.
- **[[2510.26433|CoLA-World]]** — A framework that co-evolves a fresh LAM with a pretrained ==OpenSora== world model in ==one stage==; a ==warm-up phase== (frozen WM as tutor) prevents the ==representational collapse== that dooms naive joint training, then end-to-end ==synergistic co-evolution== keeps a diverse latent-action codebook, matching/beating two-stage FVD with better OOD adaptation.

### 4. Dreamer Lineage

Model-based RL from scratch: learn a latent dynamics model (RSSM) and plan via imagination in latent space. The oldest WAM paradigm, still evolving — and the only one that works without internet-scale video or a pretrained VLM.

#### 4.1 RSSM & Latent Imagination

The foundational architecture: a recurrent latent state-space model (RSSM) that supports planning by rolling forward in imagination. The agent "imagines" thousands of action sequences in latent space and selects the best via a learned value function — no physical actions required during planning.

- **[[1803.10122|World-Models]]** — The foundational ==V-M-C== decomposition: a ==VAE== compresses vision, an ==MDN-RNN== predicts dynamics, a tiny ==CMA-ES== controller learns the policy — trainable *entirely inside the learned "dream"*; **906** CarRacing-v0 (SOTA at the time) and a VizDoom dream-trained policy transfers to the real env — the original imagination-as-training bet.
- **[[1811.04551|PlaNet]]** — A purely ==model-based== agent learning a ==Recurrent State-Space Model== with combined deterministic + stochastic latent paths and planning via ==CEM-MPC== in latent space; matches model-free D4PG/A3C on DeepMind Control while being ~**200x** more data-efficient, with pixel-accurate 50-step latent rollouts — the RSSM foundation.
- **[[1912.01603|Dreamer]]** — A latent-imagination agent via ==RSSM==: an ==actor-critic== trained purely in imagination by propagating analytic gradients of multi-step ==lambda-returns== through the learned dynamics; **823** DMC-Suite avg at **5M** steps (beating D4PG's **786** at **100M**) with **20× greater data-efficiency** than model-free agents.
- **[[2010.02193|DreamerV2]]** — A model-based RL agent learning a ==Recurrent State-Space Model== with ==discrete categorical latents== (straight-through gradients) + ==KL balancing==, training actor-critic purely in latent imagination; first to reach human-level on the full **55**-game Atari benchmark inside a separately trained world model, beating top single-GPU model-free agents.
- **[[2206.14244|MWM-Masked-WM]]** — A decoupled visual model-based RL framework with an ==autoencoder using convolutional feature masking== + ==auxiliary reward prediction==, then an ==RSSM== on frozen latents + actor-critic trained in imagination; **81.7%** aggregate success on 50 Meta-world tasks (vs DreamerV2 **67.9%**) — decoupling representation from dynamics captures fine-grained detail.
- **[[2301.04104|DreamerV3]]** — A universal model-based RL agent with fixed hyperparameters across **150+** diverse tasks; ==symlog normalization== and ==KL balancing== stabilize training across Atari, control, and locomotion without per-task tuning. The modern domain-agnostic substrate.
- **[[2308.01399|Dynalang]]** — Extends [[2301.04104|DreamerV3]]'s RSSM to a ==multimodal world model== jointly predicting future vision AND language tokens, grounding diverse language (descriptions, rules, corrections) as a ==future-prediction signal== rather than direct policy conditioning; outperforms IMPALA/R2D2 on HomeGrid/Messenger/VLN-CE and enables ==text-only pretraining==.
- **[[2604.24661|ACO-MoE]]** — A frozen ==Mixture-of-Experts== preprocessor: a ==perturbation router== + dual-stream RGB-restoration/foreground-mask experts strip DreamerV3-style WMs' ==representation contamination==, evaluated on the new ==Visual Degraded Control Suite== (Markov-switching degradations); **95.3%** clean-performance recovery, **+78.5%** over best baseline.
- **[[2603.18202|R2-Dreamer]]** — A redundancy-reduced ==DreamerV3== variant that ==removes the image decoder== and drops external augmentation, replacing them with a Barlow-Twins-style self-supervised redundancy-reduction objective for compact latent states; competitive on 20 DMC and Meta-World MT1 tasks, a clear edge on DMC-Subtle, and **1.59x** faster training than DreamerV3.
- **[[2403.04253|R2I]]** — A ==Recall to Imagine== agent swapping the RSSM recurrence for ==State Space Models== (an S4 variant, S3M) to fix vanishing-gradient long-term memory; superhuman on **9×9–13×13** Memory Maze, new SOTA on POPGym/BSuite, and up to **9×** faster training than DreamerV3 — SSM hidden states carry the long-range memory the RNN loses.
- **[[2509.24527|Dreamer-4]]** — A three-phase scalable world model (==causal tokenizer== + ==efficient transformer== + ==shortcut forcing with x-prediction==, K=4 sampling steps); first agent to obtain Minecraft diamonds offline (**0.7%** SR), real-time **21 FPS** on a single H100, action conditioning from only **100 hr** of labeled data out of **2541 hr** total.
- **[[2601.19336|EAWM]]** — An ==Event-Aware World Model==: an automatic multi-modal event generator + event predictor + ==Generic Event Segmentor== focus prediction loss on sparse information-rich "events"; **+45%** over DreamerV3 on DMC-GB2 generalization, **1.818** Atari-100k HNS, **723.8** DMC-500k — event segmentation beats holistic pixel prediction.
- **[[2509.24804|DyMoDreamer]]** — A model that extends the ==RSSM== with a ==dynamic modulation== mechanism: lightweight ==inter-frame differencing masks== extract dynamic features that enrich recurrent state, reconstruction, and reward prediction; **156.6%** mean HNS on Atari-100k, **832** DMC-Vision (**+5.5%** over DreamerV3), **+9.5%** Crafter — separating static vs agent-controllable latents.
- **[[2508.20294|DALI]]** — A model that extends ==DreamerV3== with a self-supervised context encoder inferring ==dynamics-aligned latent context== from short histories (forward-dynamics + cross-modal alignment loss) for zero-shot generalization; up to **+96.4%** over context-unaware Dreamer-DR on Ball-in-Cup extrapolation, beating even ground-truth-context baselines by **33.8–63.9%**.
- **[[2502.11377|PrivilegedDreamer]]** — A **DreamerV2**-like agent with a ==dual recurrent architecture== explicitly estimating unobservable ==hidden parameters== via an LSTM module + a prediction head, then conditioning world model, actor, and critic on them for rapid HIP-MDP adaptation; **+41%** average reward over the next-best model across five tasks, converging within a few steps.
- **[[2502.20168|Model-Based]]** — Swaps DreamerV3's RSSM for a parallelizable ==S5 state-space== dynamics backbone (with an episode-reset-aware associative scan) plus a ==privileged world model== reconstructing ground-truth state instead of images; **10×** faster world-model training, **4×** faster overall MBRL, real racing-drone Split-S/Figure-8 flight at matched sample efficiency.

#### 4.2 Exploration & Intrinsic Motivation

Variants that bolt explicit exploration signals onto the RSSM to push the agent into rarely-visited states. The axis: *how to construct an intrinsic reward when the extrinsic one is sparse*.

- **[[2506.00138|Virtual-Zebrafish-RL]]** — A model-based intrinsic-motivation algorithm, ==3M-Progress==, rewarding ==KL divergence between an online-learned WM and a fixed ethological-prior WM== to drive stable exploration in a MuJoCo zebrafish; highest behavioral alignment with larval zebrafish and captures nearly all explainable neural-glial whole-brain variance.
- **[[2005.05960|Plan2Explore]]** — A two-phase self-supervised exploration method on a ==PlaNet/Dreamer latent dynamics model==: an intrinsic ==latent-disagreement== reward (==ensemble of predictors==) plans *in imagination* for *expected future* novelty; zero-shot matches/surpasses fully-supervised Dreamer and few-shot adapts in **100–150 episodes** after **1M** exploration steps.
- **[[2007.07853|γ-Progress]]** — A ==prediction-gain curiosity== signal comparing the current model to an ==exponentially-decaying mixture== of past models (constant memory) + ==disentangled per-agent dynamics== with DQN controller; rewards *progress* rather than uncertainty, defeats the "white noise" problem and induces emergent animate-attention matching human gaze patterns.
- **[[2503.21047|CBET-DreamerV3]]** — A ==Change-Based Exploration Transfer== adapted for [[2301.04104|DreamerV3]] via two world-model + policy instances; rewards latent-state transitions, modest gains in Crafter but *negative* in Minigrid — proving intrinsic motivation is context-dependent even with modern model-based RL. **Doubles VRAM** as a cost.
- **[[1605.09674|VIME]]** — Introduces intrinsic-motivation exploration via ==information gain== on a ==Bayesian neural network== dynamics model approximated with ==variational inference==; solves sparse-reward MountainCar/SwimmerGather where heuristic exploration fails — predates [[2005.05960|Plan2Explore]] and [[2007.07853|γ-Progress]].
- **[[2605.12084|QOED]]** — ==Quasi-Optimal Experimental Design== adaptively identifies critical parameters via ==Fisher Information Matrix== eigen-analysis, then a ==Schur-complement== objective maximizes info gain while suppressing nuisance params, tractable via a differentiable ==Transformer shortcut== dynamics model; **35.23%** better dynamics RMSE than BOED, **89%** rod-balance success.

#### 4.3 Physical-Robot & Continual

Adaptations to embodied / continual deployment settings — where the agent runs on real hardware or must learn across a task stream without forgetting.

- **[[2606.27353|VND]]** — A continual robot-policy framework alternating real-world data collection, ==Variational Neural Dynamics== learning, and policy refinement in differentiable sim: a recurrent encoder infers a latent condition driving a ==neural residual== over a physics prior; **~5×** faster recovery to recurring wind, **−53.3%** large-disturbance tracking error vs LOTF.
- **[[2606.05015|Quadrotor-World-Model-Study]]** — A study of ==DreamerV3-based world models== for quadrotor navigation across four ==randomness levels (L1–L4)==, with actor policies fine-tuned ==inside latent imagination==; Sobol-sampled WM3 holds win rates **>72.0%** on OOD layouts and flies an unseen corridor (gaps as narrow as **0.67 m**) plus **12 m** open-loop traverses on imagination alone.
- **[[2604.08780|Hardware-Agnostic-Quadruped-WM]]** — A ==DreamerV3 RSSM== world model conditioned on robot morphology via a ==Physical Morphology Encoder== (normalized USD specs) + ==Adaptive Reward Normalization==, disentangling universal locomotion physics from hardware; zero-shot locomotion on unseen real Unitree Go1 + ANYmal-D with **zero falls**, tracking near specialist controllers.
- **[[2508.15755|NeRD]]** — A ==hybrid prediction framework== replacing only a classical simulator's forward-dynamics + contact solver with a ==causal Transformer== over a ==robot-centric, spatially-invariant state==, predicting relative state deltas; PPO policies trained inside NeRD transfer zero-shot at **<5%** reward error, real Franka end-effector error **1.927 mm**.
- **[[2206.14176|DayDreamer]]** — An adaptation of [[1912.01603|Dreamer]]'s ==world model== to physical robots via an ==asynchronous actor-learner architecture== for continuous online learning; **1 hour** of physical learning for quadruped locomotion (vs days for model-free RL). The sim-to-real-without-sim baseline.
- **[[2211.15944|Continual-Dreamer]]** — An extension of ==DreamerV2== with ==persistent FIFO replay across tasks== + ==Plan2Explore intrinsic exploration== + ==Reservoir Sampling==; robustly mitigates catastrophic forgetting on Minihack and beats model-free baselines in average return on Minigrid — first systematic study of world models for task-agnostic Continual RL.
- **[[2604.02911|DreamTIP]]** — An extension of [[1912.01603|Dreamer]] with a ==Task-Invariant Properties predictor==, LLM-extracted TIPs + ==mixed-replay real-world adaptation== + ==cosine-similarity regularization==; **+28.1%** avg over SOTA across 8 sim transfer tasks, **100%** real-world Unitree Go2 on 52 cm Climb (vs WMP **10%**) from as few as **5** real trajectories.
- **[[2505.15589|Reflexive-World-Models]]** — A framework separating a frozen RL policy + WM from a lightweight ==online adaptive control== module treating the WM's predicted latent states as ==implicit reference trajectories==, correcting actions not weights; **0.6003** median reward under perturbation vs **0.3600** domain-randomized TD-MPC2, adapting in **1.8 h** vs **16.5 h**.
- **[[2501.10100|RWM]]** — A ==dual-autoregressive== GRU world model predicting observations + privileged contacts, paired with ==MBPO-PPO== imagination rollouts; stable hundreds-of-steps prediction on ANYmal-D, zero-shot transfer to physical ANYmal-D and Unitree-G1.
- **[[2407.04942|FOSP]]** — A ==DreamerV3==-based safe model-based RL framework with a ==cost decoder==, in-sample conservative value estimation, a ==Reachability Estimation Function==, and safe policy expansion for offline-to-online transfer; near-zero violations on Safety-Gymnasium and few-shot (**40-step**) adaptation to unseen safety regions on a real Franka SafeReach task.
- **[[1803.11347|GrBAL]]** — Meta-learns a dynamics model for rapid ==online adaptation== via ==MAML==-style gradient updates (GrBAL) or ==LSTM== recurrence (ReBAL), fed into ==MPC==; adapts a real 6-legged millirobot to a missing leg, novel terrain, and payloads with **1000×** less data than model-free meta-RL.

#### 4.5 Planning over Learned World Models

Decision-time planning over a learned dynamics model: MPC, CEM/MPPI sampling, MCTS, or gradient-based trajectory optimization rolls the model forward at every control step. Not strict [[1912.01603|Dreamer]]/RSSM derivatives, but the same imagination-as-planning bet executed by an online planner instead of an amortized policy.

- **[[2607.25236|VisualPatchWorld]]** — Induces *executable code* world models: ==visual abstraction== to object-centric scene graphs feeds ==two-level program induction== that probes for the right ==dynamical sketch== then fits parameters by ==multi-step rollout error==, planned with ==CEM-MPC==; **69.0%** mean success (**+23.5pp** over POMDP-Coder), PushT **22→88–96%** with hybrid scoring.
- **[[2607.23602|ASAR]]** — Diagnoses ==proposal overgeneration== — more candidates means more infeasible sequences with misleadingly low predicted cost — then rebuilds an action from an ==adjacent set== of low-cost prefixes weighted by a ==local prefix isolation score==; **+28.0pp** event completion on Carry-and-Release (0.067→**0.347**), synthesis beating selection by **13.7pp**.
- **[[2607.12547|Hi-LeWM]]** — A hierarchical extension of frozen [[2603.19312|LeWM]] adding ==Latent Macro-Action Encoder== + ==High-Level Predictor==, fixing ==planner-induced support mismatch== via ==Empirical-Macro CEM== + ==Staged Execution==; lifts PushT d=75 **15.3%→32.7%** (+14.7pp) and d=50 **64.0%** (+11.3pp) — unconstrained hierarchy hurts long-horizon planning.
- **[[2607.02403|ACID (Inverse Dynamics Consistency)]]** — Augments MPC/CEM planning cost with an IDM-based ==cycle action consistency== residual verifying step-wise realizability of predicted transitions, via a ==scale-invariant adaptive weight==; **+14.0%** Reacher SR (PLDM), **30** CEM samples matching baseline's **300**.
- **[[2606.23079|AdaReP]]** — A training-free, model-agnostic ==adaptive re-planning== wrapper for neural world-model MPC adjusting ==replanning frequency== from two online signals (representation deviation + a dynamics-sensitivity estimate) via an exponential-decay reuse tolerance; **34.9–59.2%** fewer function evaluations in sim, **>80%** fewer world-model queries on a real Franka.
- **[[2606.16480|HOLO-MPPI]]** — A ==hierarchical motion planner== learning a high-level ==DQN== policy over abstract actions plus a ==learned world model== whose rollouts seed a low-level ==MPPI== sampling prior, refined online by a task cost; **68.0%** multi-scenario highway SR (vs **57.8%** vanilla MPPI / **56.2%** end-to-end RL) at **169 ms** planning for 5 Hz control.
- **[[2606.14418|COMET]]** — An object-centric planner running ==MCTS in slot-structured latent space== over a frozen object encoder + a transformer world model with an ==action-object binding== mechanism + ==object-causal attention== in policy/value nets; higher early-training score + faster convergence across 8 object-oriented visual-control tasks, relevance maps concentrating on task objects.
- **[[2606.08775|WorldDP]]** — A hierarchical framework pairing an upper-tier ==object-centric== world model (SAM2-guided Object-Centric Encoder + Particle-Filter MPC with a contact predictor) with a lower-tier goal-conditioned diffusion policy for multi-stage robot tasks; **30%** Cube-Triple and **20%** Scene-Single-Composite full completion, **72–74%** on simpler subtasks, beating all baselines.
- **[[2605.09196|RigidFormer]]** — An ==object-centric Transformer== for multi-object ==rigid-body dynamics== from point clouds via ==Anchor-Vertex Pooling== + ==differentiable Kabsch rigid projection==; **0.161 m / 15.33°** translation/orientation error on MOVi-B (vs HopNet **0.176 m / 17.91°**) at **23.9 FPS** (**8×** faster than FIGNet, **101×** than HopNet), scaling to **217** objects.
- **[[2605.04709|ELVIS]]** — An ==Ensemble-Calibrated Latent Imagination== method coupling an ==RSSM== belief state with ==GMM-MPPI== + an ==ensemble-UCB-gated λ-return==; SOTA across **14** DMC visual tasks beating TD-MPC2/DreamerV3, zero-shot sim-to-real sand-spraying at **2.2 mm** Rrms under occlusion.
- **[[2603.14392|WestWorld]]** — A knowledge-encoded scalable trajectory world model for diverse robots: a ==Knowledge-Encoded Embedding== converts kinematic trees into traversal indices added to trajectory embeddings + a ==System-Aware Mixture-of-Experts== learns distinct system dynamics; lower zero-shot MAE on unseen systems (**16.350** Walker2D vs TDM 20.122), real Unitree Go1 MPPI deployment.
- **[[2602.00475|GRASP]]** — A ==parallel gradient-based planning== method via "virtual states" + ==Langevin-style noise injection== + ==grad-cut dynamics loss== + periodic full-rollout synchronization; **43.4%** Push-T success at 50-step horizon vs CEM **30.2%** / vanilla GD **37.6%** / Latent Collocation **4.2%** — the canonical lifted-state planner.
- **[[2512.09929|OWM]]** — A method that closes the world-model ==train-test gap== for gradient-based planning with two fixes over a frozen ==DINOv2== + ViT latent WM: ==Online WM== expands training data with environment-corrected GBP rollouts, ==Adversarial WM== finetunes on perturbed latents to smooth the loss landscape; **+30%** open-loop SR, matching CEM at **10×** less compute.
- **[[2511.01177|XEmbody-Particle-WM]]** — A cross-embodiment ==particle-based GNN world model== (DPI-Net) representing hands + objects as ==displacement fields==, trained on simulated-robot + real-human data and planned with ==CEM-MPC==; zero-shot to unseen 6-/12-DoF hands matching target-specific models, a **1:1** sim:human ratio optimal, enabling real deformable reshaping without fine-tuning.
- **[[2509.13095|SeqWM]]** — A multi-robot MBRL method recasting joint dynamics as ==autoregressive sequence modeling==: each agent's WM predicts its trajectory conditioned on preceding agents' predictions (explicit ==intention sharing==) + an ==MPPI== sequential planner; near-optimal in 2–4M steps, real Unitree Go2-W cooperation at **12.8 ms/step**.
- **[[2506.23126|ParticleFormer]]** — A 3D point-cloud world model replacing predefined interaction graphs with ==transformer self-attention== dynamics + a ==Chamfer + Hausdorff== loss on raw point clouds + per-particle ==material encodings== (rigid/granular/rope); beats GNN + 2D baselines on dynamics, more hyperparameter-robust, lowers final-state error under MPC on multi-material manipulation.
- **[[2505.16249|3D-Occ-MPC]]** — A learning-based predictive controller for elasto-plastic manipulation using a ==3D occupancy== state from multi-view RGB + a ==3D-CNN + GNN== dynamics model + ==shape-based action initialization==; lower state-inference error than patch/crop baselines (EMD **28.6e-3**), beats RoboCraft/RoboCook on deformation, shapes deformables to complex 3D goals in sim + real.
- **[[2502.14819|PLDM]]** — A planner using a ==reconstruction-free JEPA== latent dynamics model learned from ==reward-free offline data==, then ==MPPI-MPC== at test time; beats model-free RL on unseen maze layouts, zero-shot inverts the objective for state-avoidance, ~**80%** SR from a few thousand transitions — outperforms reconstruction-based DreamerV3/TD-MPC2 offline.
- **[[2409.08750|DexSim2Real2]]** — Actively interacts to build an ==explicit digital-twin world model== (3D AIGC mesh + movable-part segmentation) of unseen articulated objects, then plans via ==iCEM sampling-based MPC== with ==eigengrasp== for dexterous hands; **7.20°** vs UMPNet's **16.00°** laptop error, **18/20** cabinet manipulations within 30% error, joint-axis error **<9°**.
- **[[2310.18534|Multi-Time-Scale-WM]]** — A hierarchical probabilistic WM (==MTS3==) coupling ==fast + slow time-scale state-space models== with locally-linear Gaussian dynamics + ==Bayesian aggregation== of abstract actions for closed-form inference; accurate long-horizon action-conditional prediction + calibrated uncertainty on 6 datasets, beating deep-Kalman/RNN baselines.
- **[[2103.14256|SLDS-Differentiable-Control]]** — A contact-rich controller modeling tasks as ==Switching Linear Dynamical Systems== with neural mode classification + explicit ==impact dynamics==, learning ==differentiable LQR== policies under a ==hybrid predictive-reactive MPC==; **100%** Box2D sim with lower LQR cost, **80%** real Franka dynamic pickup vs **60%** for a reactive baseline.
- **[[2011.01968|DSR-Net]]** — An end-to-end ==3D recurrent== world model learning a ==Dynamic Scene Representation== with object permanence + amodal completeness, jointly tracking, reconstructing, and predicting object dynamics from depth + actions, paired with ==MPC==; **5.54 cm** visible-surface flow MSE, **0.772** amodal IoU, **0.72** voxel IoU on planar pushing vs **0.31** SE3-Net.
- **[[1911.10601|Scaling-Active-Inference]]** — A method that scales ==active inference== to high-dim continuous control via ==amortized recognition networks== + ==CEM== over ==expected free energy== + ==particle-based trajectory sampling==; reaches optimal Pendulum in **<100 epochs** (vs **>1000** for DDPG) and converges with an order of magnitude fewer samples.
- **[[1812.03399|Latent-Variable-MBRL]]** — A ==hierarchical probabilistic model-based control== framework adding ==auxiliary latent variables== over a ==probabilistic NN ensemble== with ==online Bayesian inference== updating the latent posterior from the latest transition for real-time adaptation; positive transfer in training, mid-episode adaptation to gravity changes on HalfCheetah.
- **[[1805.12114|PETS]]** — ==Probabilistic ensembles== of neural dynamics models capture both ==aleatoric== and ==epistemic uncertainty==, planned via ==trajectory sampling== + ==CEM== inside ==MPC==; matches SAC/PPO asymptotic performance on Half-cheetah with **8×**/**125×** fewer samples — the foundational uncertainty-aware MBRL recipe MBPO and later ensemble methods build on.

#### 4.6 TD-MPC & Diffusion-Planner Lineages

The two named planning dynasties: the [[2203.04955|TD-MPC]] family (reward-driven TOLD latents + terminal value functions under MPPI) and the [[2205.09991|Diffuser]] family (trajectory-level denoising as planning), plus the methods that extend or plug into them.

- **[[2606.16286|FlowMPC]]** — A method refining an imitation-learned ==flow-matching== policy at inference by scoring its candidate trajectories with a learned ==TD-MPC2== world model under ==MPPI== planning, reusing the policy's visual latent; end SR **93.1%→97.4%** PickCube and **56.8%→66.4%** PickSingleYCB, lifting both anytime and end success on ManiSkill manipulation.
- **[[2605.04568|Dream-MPC]]** — A ==gradient-based MPC== warm-started by rolling a ==stochastic policy network== in latent space + an ==uncertainty-regularization== term; plugged into TD-MPC2/BMPC/Dreamer, lifts BMPC IQM **+26.7%** and mean score **+20.5%** with only **75** WM evaluations/step (vs thousands) across **24** continuous-control tasks.
- **[[2503.00653|DC-MPC]]** — An MBRL algorithm on a ==Discrete Codebook World Model== using ==Finite Scalar Quantization== for stochastic discrete latents, modeling transitions as a ==cross-entropy classifier== with Straight-Through Gumbel-softmax and no observation reconstruction; matches/beats TD-MPC2 and substantially outperforms DreamerV3 across 30 DMControl, 45 Meta-World, 5 MyoSuite tasks.
- **[[2405.18418|Puppeteer]]** — A ==hierarchical world model== on two ==TD-MPC2== instances for **56-DoF** visual whole-body control: a low-level ==tracking agent== pretrained on ==MoCap==, a high-level ==puppeteering agent== plans from vision; matches direct TD-MPC2 on 8 tasks with **97.8%** human preference for naturalness, zero-shot to **3×** larger gaps.
- **[[2310.16828|TD-MPC2]]** — Scales [[2203.04955|TD-MPC]] into one robust recipe via ==discrete regression==, ==SimNorm== latent normalization, and ==Q-function ensembles==; a single hyperparameter set solves **104** continuous-control tasks and a **317M**-param model spans **80** tasks — the canonical MPC+TD baseline this section's Dream-MPC/DC-MPC/Puppeteer entries compare against.
- **[[2302.01877|AdaptDiffuser]]** — A ==self-evolving diffusion planner== that generates ==reward-guided synthetic trajectories==, filtered by an ==inverse-dynamics discriminator== for dynamics consistency + reward; **+20.8%** over [[2205.09991|Diffuser]] on Maze2D, **+27.9%** zero-shot on unseen KUKA pick-and-place — proves diffusion planners can refine themselves without new expert data.
- **[[2210.15629|LCD]]** — A hierarchical trajectory-diffusion planner in the [[2205.09991|Diffuser]] lineage: a ==high-level diffusion planner== generates temporally-abstracted latent goals (32-dim, from a frozen goal-conditioned RL encoder) executed by a low-level policy; **88.7%** CALVIN One-Horizon and **61.00%** zero-shot SR (vs Diffuser-1D's **12.67%**) at **3.3-15x** faster inference.
- **[[2205.09991|Diffuser]]** — A ==denoising diffusion== planner over entire ==state-action trajectories== with non-autoregressive ==U-Net== + ==classifier-guided sampling== for rewards; **>100** Maze2D scores beating MPPI/CQL/IQL on long-horizon sparse-reward tasks, adapts to new objectives by swapping only the guidance — the canonical diffusion-planning baseline.
- **[[2203.04955|TD-MPC]]** — Learns a ==Task-Oriented Latent Dynamics (TOLD)== model purely from reward + ==latent-consistency losses== (no pixel reconstruction), planned via ==MPPI== with a learned terminal value function; solves DMControl **Dog** in **1M steps**, unsolved by prior methods — the TD-learning-meets-MPC lineage [[2310.16828|TD-MPC2]] later scales to 104 tasks.

#### 4.7 Offline, Robust & Sample-Efficient Model-Based RL

Model-based RL as a policy-training algorithm rather than a decision-time planner: offline RL over learned models, robustness to model error and adversarial dynamics, sample-efficient imagination training, and the theory of when to trust the model.

- **[[2606.21173|P-learning]]** — Inverts the ==Bellman equation== to extract an explicit world model from a goal-conditioned agent's fixed Q-values/policy/reward, resolving the ==value-equivalence problem==; **NMSE 1.2e-4** on Reacher, extracted models plan quasi-optimal OOD goals (e.g. velocity) never directly rewarded.
- **[[2604.08958|WOMBET]]** — An ==uncertainty-aware world-model planning== method with an ==uncertainty penalty== generating offline trajectories from a source task + ==dual-criterion filtering== (high return + low uncertainty) + ==adaptive sampling== mixing offline/online data; achieves comparable or higher asymptotic returns than SAC/PPO/TD3 on MuJoCo using **<half** the interaction budget.
- **[[2603.22430|MPCwDWM]]** — An ==Inference-Time Policy Optimization== framework for offline RL with a ==differentiable diffusion world model==: offline trains a diffusion transition sampler + reward + ReBRAC policy, then at inference updates policy params by gradient ascent on an MPC objective over imagined rollouts; **98.5** avg D4RL Gym, **85.07** AntMaze, FlowMPC at **8×** lower latency.
- **[[2603.08118|RVL]]** — A model-based offline-RL method (ROMI) with ==Robust Value-aware== model learning characterizing one-step value error over a dynamics-uncertainty set + Implicitly Differentiable Adaptive Weighting bi-level transition prioritization, paired with SAC; tops RAMBO on 11/12 D4RL MuJoCo datasets (+**18.6%** total normalized score) and 6/9 NeoRL.
- **[[2603.04289|IPD]]** — An ==Imaginary Planning Distillation== method for offline RL: a quasi-optimal value function + an ==uncertainty-aware ensemble world model== (Jensen-Shannon rollout filtering) augment suboptimal segments via ==MPC planning==, then distill into a Transformer policy replacing return-to-go; SOTA D4RL Gym/Kitchen/Adroit (**103.2** hopper-medium-replay).
- **[[2602.23770|MAGE]]** — A ==multi-scale autoregressive generation== method for offline RL: a flexible hierarchy of temporal abstractions captures long-horizon, sparse-reward structure where flat transformers lose global context and diffusion drifts; SOTA on Adroit dexterous manipulation + Franka Kitchen + maze at **27.30 ms/step** — fast enough for real-time robot control.
- **[[2602.14351|WIMLE]]** — An ==uncertainty-aware MBRL== method using ==Implicit Maximum Likelihood Estimation== to model multi-modal dynamics (avoiding regression-to-the-mean) + uncertainty-weighted synthetic-rollout reweighting; **>50%** sample-efficiency gain on Humanoid-run (DMC) + Myo-key-turn-hard across **40** continuous-control tasks spanning DMC / MyoSuite / HumanoidBench.
- **[[2602.13977|WoVR]]** — Stabilizes an action-conditioned WM for VLA post-training via ==dual-channel action injection== + first-frame anchoring + ==Keyframe-Initialized Rollouts== + masked ==GRPO== hallucination control + Policy-Aligned Co-Evolution; **69.2%** LIBERO (**+29.3pp** over OpenVLA-OFT), **91.7%** real-robot SR (**+30.0pp**) with zero extra online interaction.
- **[[2602.08032|Horizon-Imagination]]** — An ==on-policy imagination== method enabling ==parallel denoising== of multiple future observations for diffusion world models via stable discrete ==action sampling== + a decoupled Horizon schedule; matches autoregressive-rollout control at **half the denoising budget**, cutting Atari training time by **~30%**.
- **[[2602.01270|Mixture-of-World-Models]]** — A ==Mixture-of-World Models (MoW)== with task-adaptive VAEs + a hybrid Transformer dynamics model with ==expert routing== + gradient-based task clustering + harmonious/balance losses for visual MTRL; **110.4%** mean HNS on 26 Atari games at **50%** fewer params, new SOTA **74.5%** avg on Meta-World 50 tasks.
- **[[2512.08108|Action-Chunk-MBRL]]** — A scalable offline MBRL method via ==action-chunk dynamics + reward models== predicting n steps at once (fewer autoregressive calls, less compounding error) + ==flow action-chunk policies== with rejection sampling + one-step distillation; SOTA on long-horizon goal-conditioned OGBench manipulation, though it struggles on erratic contact-rich locomotion.
- **[[2512.04341|NEUBAY]]** — A model-based offline-RL method optimizing expected return under a ==posterior distribution over world models== (==N=100 ensemble==) for ambiguity-neutral performance, with ==epistemic-uncertainty== adaptive trajectory truncation; new SOTA on **7 of 33** D4RL/NeoRL tasks (walker-medium **95.5→106.4**, pen-cloned **74.1→91.3**), no explicit conservatism.
- **[[2510.24482|COMBRL]]** — A ==continuous-time optimistic MBRL== algorithm folding uncertainty-driven exploration into an ==intrinsic reward== (GP/BNN dynamics, ODE formulation) instead of OCORL's costly dynamics co-optimization; matches/beats OCORL at **~3x** lower compute, first sample-complexity bound for unsupervised continuous-time RL.
- **[[2510.12560|CoIRL-AD]]** — A competitive dual-policy framework training distinct IL + RL actors over a shared ==latent world model== for offline end-to-end driving, with ==step-aware group sampling== + adaptive reward-scored knowledge transfer to stabilize RL on expert data; lowest combined L2+Collision on nuScenes vs the LAW baseline, with strong long-tail generalization.
- **[[2510.12312|Deep-SPI]]** — Extends ==Safe Policy Improvement== to online deep RL via a ==neighborhood-operator== trust region + local reward/transition losses bounding true-vs-model return gap; matches/beats PPO and DeepMDPs on ALE-57 with provably lower transition loss, fixing confounded representation updates.
- **[[2510.03578|Latent-MoS]]** — Embeds ==Lie-group== symmetries into latent-ODE dynamics via a time-variant ==Mixture-of-Experts== over rotation/translation/scaling generators, capturing multi-scale "symmetry-breaking"; **15–97%** gains under **90%** data drop, MBPO-integrated power-grid control beats GP/swing-equation baselines.
- **[[2509.07945|ScaleZero]]** — A multitask world model with a ViT encoder + ==sparse Mixture-of-Experts== backbone (provably lower gradient-conflict bound) + ==Dynamic Parameter Scaling== curating active tasks via LoRA; **0.39** mean HNS on Atari-100k (vs UniZero **0.31**), **769.7** mean DMC-18, **−28.5%** environment interactions.
- **[[2509.00215|DMO]]** — ==Decoupled== model-based RL: a high-fidelity simulator generates trajectories (forward pass) while a learned ==differentiable dynamics model== supplies ==policy gradients== at the simulator's true states (backward pass), avoiding compounding error; **10x** sample efficiency vs PPO (**<4M** samples), **20%** less wall-clock, real Go2 transfer.
- **[[2506.08902|InFOM]]** — An ==intention-conditioned flow occupancy model==: a ==flow-matching== future-occupancy predictor conditioned on a latent user-intention variable + a TD-flow-matching loss + implicit GPI policy extraction; **1.8×** median return and **+36%** success over eight baselines across **40** state- and image-based RL tasks (**20×** on jaco).
- **[[2505.20922|DIMA]]** — Reframes multi-agent dynamics prediction as a ==sequential conditional-denoising== process (diffusion-inspired, permutation-invariant over per-agent actions) inside a CTDE actor-critic, achieving centralized consistency at ==linear per-agent complexity==; beats MAMBA/MARIE on MAMuJoCo + Bi-DexHands, stable to unseen horizon **H=25**.
- **[[2505.16394|Raw2Drive]]** — A dual-stream MBRL framework pairing a privileged BEV ==Dreamer-style world model== with a raw-sensor ==BEVFormer== world model aligned via spatial-temporal + abstract-state losses, borrowing reward/continue heads from the privileged stream (==Head Guidance==); first raw-sensor MBRL for E2E driving, **71.36** Driving Score / **50.24%** SR on Bench2Drive.
- **[[2503.10370|LUMOS]]** — A language-conditioned imitation framework training an ==actor-critic agent inside a learned world model== on unlabeled play data, guided by an ==intrinsic reward== matching expert demonstrations in latent space plus a ==latent planning network==; higher multi-stage long-horizon SR on CALVIN than prior language-conditioned IL, with zero-shot real-robot transfer.
- **[[2502.19544|Generalist-to-Specialist]]** — A ==GSA== adaptation: a scaled ==RSSM== pretrained on diverse reward-free multi-embodiment offline data, then RL-finetuned with ==Experience Rehearsal== + ==Execution Guidance==; **+102.8%** aggregate over from-scratch DrQ-v2/DreamerV3 across **72** visuomotor tasks at 150k online samples, matching baselines needing **3.3–6.7×** more.
- **[[2502.00466|EDELINE]]** — A diffusion-based world model adding a ==Mamba== Recurrent Embedding Module for unbounded history feeding a U-Net next-frame predictor (AdaGN + cross-attention) with dynamic loss harmonization; **1.87** mean HNS Atari-100k, **11.5** Crafter return at 1M steps (vs DreamerV3-XL **9.2**), **4.1x** over DIAMOND, robust in 3D ViZDoom.
- **[[2501.16443|OC-STORM]]** — An ==object-centric== world model using few-shot video segmentation (Cutie/SAM2) + a categorical VAE over object and visual latents + a spatial-temporal dynamics model for sample-efficient RL; **134.8%** mean HNS Atari-100k over STORM's **107.2%**, and **48.0%** Hollow Knight Mage-Lord win rate vs STORM's **5.0%**.
- **[[2501.06605|RoboHorizon]]** — An LLM-assisted multi-view world model built on a ==Recognize-Sense-Plan-Act== pipeline with ==LLM-generated dense staged rewards== and a ==Key-horizon Multi-View MAE== for keyframe-focused representation, driving model-based visual RL; **75.58%** short-horizon (+25.35% over MV-MWM) and **45.47%** long-horizon (+29.23%).
- **[[2410.11234|BA-MCTS]]** — A ==Bayes-Adaptive Monte Carlo Tree Search== for offline model-based RL combining deep-ensemble world models, a pessimistic Bayes-Adaptive MDP, and Continuous BAMCP planning with double progressive widening; **80.25** avg across D4RL MuJoCo, belief adaptation improving prediction on 10/12 tasks, validated on tokamak control.
- **[[2410.00564|JOWA]]** — A ==shared transformer backbone== for world dynamics + Q-value via ==distributional CQL== + ==parallelizable inference planning==; **78.9%** IQM human-normalized on 15 Atari games (+**71.4%** over baselines), steepest scaling curve **40M→150M** params, **64.7%** IQM on 5 novel games with only **5k** fine-tuning transitions — the action-centered scaling baseline.
- **[[2406.09976|RMBPO]]** — A ==Robust MBPO== that adds an ==adversarial auxiliary model== learning ==pessimistic transitions== within a ==KL uncertainty set== around the nominal model, solving a two-player zero-sum game; outperforms MBPO across MuJoCo tasks under distorted physics (mass/friction) and varying action-noise scales — worst-case-robust model-based RL.
- **[[2405.12399|DIAMOND]]** — A ==diffusion-based world model== operating directly in image space (EDM formulation, ~3 denoising steps) to preserve visual detail, with actor-critic trained entirely in imagined rollouts; new SOTA **1.46** mean HNS on Atari-100k (superhuman on 11/26 games) and scales to an interactive CS:GO neural game engine.
- **[[2404.18926|PCWM]]** — A Point Cloud World Model running model-based RL on partial ==XYZ-RGB point clouds==, encoding with ==PointConv== and adapting the ==RSSM== to drop reconstruction for multi-step reward + KL dynamics; superior Pick & Place sample efficiency, **76%** less viewpoint-shift degradation than RGB-D, and **25–32** episodes to adapt to new cameras vs **70–94**.
- **[[2310.06253|Objective-Mismatch-MBRL-Survey]]** — A survey taxonomizing the ==objective-mismatch== problem (WM accuracy fails to track policy quality) into ==distribution correction==, ==value-equivalence==, ==control-as-inference==, and ==differentiable planning== as four corners of one decision-aware MBRL design space — the conceptual map for "train pixel, deploy latent".
- **[[2206.02072|VSRL]]** — A ==value-equivalent sampling== method marrying ==rate-distortion theory== with approximate value-equivalence so a PSRL agent plans with a lossy, ==compressed value-equivalent MDP==; yields capacity-sensitive Bayesian regret bounds matching best-known PSRL state-dependence — the information-theoretic case for *deciding what to model*.
- **[[2103.10369|RH-UCRL]]** — A model-based deep RL method modeling agent + adversary jointly with a ==calibrated dynamics model== separating epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty + ==optimistic-agent / pessimistic-adversary== hallucinated control; sublinear robust regret + sample-complexity guarantees, beating RARL/RAP on worst-case return across adversarial MuJoCo — the robust-MBRL theory anchor.
- **[[1906.08253|MBPO]]** — A method using ==branched short-horizon rollouts== (k-step) from real replay states with a ==probabilistic dynamics ensemble== feeding SAC; an order of magnitude better sample efficiency than SAC/PPO at matched asymptotic performance on Humanoid/Ant — the canonical "when to trust your model" recipe the robust variants extend.
- **[[1903.00374|SimPLe]]** — A ==Simulated Policy Learning== loop alternating real data collection, training a stochastic discrete-latent video-prediction ==world model==, and PPO policy training inside it from real-state rollout starts; beats Rainbow/PPO on over half of 26 Atari games under a 100k-interaction budget, up to **10x** more sample-efficient on Freeway.

### 5. VLM-Integrated WAMs

VLMs provide semantic understanding; world models provide dynamics prediction. These papers combine both, with the integration strategy determining which capability dominates — reasoning, unified action, test-time imagination, or compact motion.

#### 5.1 Visual Chain-of-Thought

VLM predicts *visual* subgoals (future frames or scene states) before generating actions; the world model plans between subgoals. Reasoning happens at the image-token level rather than the action level.

- **[[2607.04988|InternVLA-A1.5]]** — A ==Mixture-of-Transformers== unifying VLM understanding, ==latent-querying foresight== (learnable tokens distilling a frozen video model, no pixel generation), and ==flow-matching== action; **98.9%** LIBERO, **84.8%** LIBERO-Plus, **76.4%** long-horizon MOF chemistry (vs π0.5's **29.3%**).
- **[[2604.26934|World2VLM]]** — Distills ==spatial imagination== from a generative world model (Stable Virtual Camera / HY-WorldPlay) into a VLM as a ==training-time teacher== via a ==bidirectional inverse+forward task suite== + SFT→==GRPO==; **+15.44–15.98pp** avg over base VLM across 4 spatial-reasoning benchmarks, no test-time WM calls.
- **[[2604.07957|WorldMAP]]** — A ==teacher-student distillation== framework: world-model teacher builds ==3D semantic-spatial memory== from imagined futures + ==Fast Marching Method on BEV cost map==, then trains a ==VLM trajectory student== on quality-filtered pseudo-labels; **ADE 42.06** / **FDE 38.87** on Target-Bench, **−18.0% ADE** and **−42.1% FDE** vs Gemini-3-Pro.
- **[[2603.14497|WorldVLM]]** — A hybrid driving stack where a ==VLM emits behavioral commands + justifications== conditioning the ==LAW== trajectory-predicting world model via a ==motion vector==; **+24% BERTScore F1 (0.67)** on justifications vs zero-shot, **L2 1.03m @3s**; ground-truth motion-vector conditioning drops L2 to **0.27m** and collisions to **0.10%**.
- **[[2602.12322|ForeAct]]** — A hierarchical ==visual foresight planner==: a VLM decomposes instructions into subtasks while a linear ==Diffusion Transformer== (32× compression, pretrained on **1M+** cross-embodiment episodes) imagines goal images that dual-guide a base VLA; **0.33 s** 640×480 foresight, **87.4%** avg over 11 real tasks (**+40.9%** vs vanilla VLA, **+30.3%** vs VLM-augmented).
- **[[2509.20021|Embodied-AI-LLM-WM-Survey]]** — A position survey proposing a joint ==MLLM + World-Model architecture==: MLLMs supply semantic decomposition, WMs supply physics-aware prediction + real-time adaptation; the conceptual roadmap for the LLM-to-WM transition in embodied AI.
- **[[2509.02722|VLWM]]** — A ==natural-language world state representation== trained on a ==Tree of Captions== from natural videos + ==dual-system planning==; **+3.2 SR / +3.9 MeanAcc** on VPA, **Elo 1261** in PlannerArena human eval (**+27%** S2 over S1), **BLEU-4 55.6** on RoboVQA, critic hits **98.4%** goal-achievement accuracy.
- **[[2507.23773|SimuRA]]** — A ==Policy + World Model + Critic== triplet orchestrated by LLMs, using ==natural language as discrete hierarchical latent space==; **32.2%** on FlightQA (**+124%** over autoregressive baseline), **+47.5%** fact-level accuracy on FanOutQA multi-hop, **23.0%** on WebArena (**+91.7%** over BrowsingAgent).
- **[[2506.23919|Goal-VLA]]** — A hierarchical zero-shot framework using an ==image-generative VLM== (Gemini 2.5 Flash-image) as an ==object-centric world model== to synthesize visual goal images refined by a ==Reflection-through-Synthesis== loop, then grounded to 3D object poses for training-free control; **59.9%** avg RLBench (vs OpenVLA 0.2%), **60%** real-world.
- **[[2601.02456|InternVLA-A1]]** — A ==Mixture-of-Transformers== fusing Understanding Expert + Generation Expert (==VAE tokenization + non-autoregressive parallel decoding==, ~**13 Hz** on RTX 4090) + Action Expert; **75.1%** real-world static (vs **60.6%** π0 / **70.7%** π0.5), **86.7%** dynamic (**+26.7%** over π0.5 on Express Sorting); removing generation expert drops **20%**.

#### 5.2 Unified Policy + World Model

Single framework that *jointly* trains policy and world model under a shared objective or shared latent — not a stitched pipeline.

- **[[2607.18709|RoboInter1.5]]** — Makes dense ==intermediate representations== the shared interface: **230k** human-verified episodes train a ==VLM planner== on IR ==VQA tasks==, whose IRs then regularize VLA action spaces and constrain ==latent rollouts== of the world model; real closed-loop **65.0→77.3%** ID and **38.3→58.3%** OOD, world-model PSNR **18.26→21.05**.
- **[[2607.14187|RxBrain]]** — A **6.2B** ==Mixture-of-Transformers== unifying language reasoning + ==flow-matching== imagination on a **50,177-hour** annotated planning corpus; extended to action via ==Gated Expert Fusion== reaches **87%** SR on household tasks (vs π0 **68%** / π0.5 **82%**) without action pretraining, though visual fidelity (**0.52**) is the bottleneck.
- **[[2607.11270|Lumo-2]]** — Drops explicit reasoning for a physically grounded ==latent world dynamics== space, aligned to vision and language by ==multi-stage modality pre-alignment==, with a ==temporal context buffer== and ==Block-wise Autoregression==; VSIBench **21.73→52.34%**, beats π0.5 and Fast-WAM across **22** real tasks, torso geodesic error **0.66°→0.34°**, **2.71×** faster.
- **[[2607.03941|WSA1]]** — A ==3D-centric World-Spatial-Action== paradigm unifying 2D visual thinking + action-caused 3D world prediction + ==3D inverse dynamics== via ==Mixture-of-Transformers== with ==bidirectional 3D world-action causal attention==; **80.3%** real-world SR (+**20pp** over π0.5), **98.2%** LIBERO, only **1,000h** real-robot data.
- **[[2606.27677|DiM-WAM]]** — Adds ==Diverse Historical Event Memory== (K parallel bounded memory banks with ==novelty-aware compression== + ==mass-weighted fusion== + ==task-progress-aware auxiliary loss==) to a joint video-action WAM for long-horizon temporal disambiguation; RMBench **34.8%→69.8%** full-task SR over a LingBot-VA baseline, real Franka **52.5%→90.0%**.
- **[[2606.21088|MV-WAM]]** — A dual-branch ==Diffusion Transformer== with ==manifold-aware dual prediction objectives== (velocity for video, x0 for action/value) + ==progress-valued rollback==, fixing the video-action ==manifold mismatch==; **55.7%** OOD RoboTwin-2.0 (**+29.3pp** over HALO), **77.5%** real dual-arm.
- **[[2606.17924|PearlVLA]]** — A VLA running an iterative ==action-plan refinement loop in latent space== where a ==plan-conditioned world query== probes a frozen latent world model for action-free future-observation latents, with an anchored residual update + an RL stage using group-relative process rewards; **98.7%** LIBERO, **76.3%** LIBERO-Plus, **52.9%** RoboCasa few-shot, at **27.5 Hz**.
- **[[2606.16533|Kairos]]** — A native ==Understanding-Generation-Prediction== stack unifying VLM + video DiT + action DiT via ==Hybrid Linear Temporal Attention== (gated-linear/sliding-window/dilated) + ==Cross-Embodiment Data Curriculum==; **90.8%** LIBERO-Plus SOTA, **96.1** RoboTwin-2.0, orders-of-magnitude faster inference than 14-28B rivals.
- **[[2606.12403|World-Pilot]]** — A method steering VLAs with ==World-Action priors== via Latent Steering (scene-evolution latent into VLM hidden states by cross-attention) + Action Steering (trajectory hypothesis as a prior token on the action denoiser); **84.7%** LIBERO-Plus zero-shot OOD (+4.2pp over ABot-M0), **70%** on a geometry-sensitive OOD real task where baselines stay below **30%**.
- **[[2606.09215|MotionWAM]]** — A ==dual-DiT== (Video DiT + Motion DiT) real-time humanoid WAM translating egocentric video dynamics into unified whole-body actions via ==one-shot imagination== + three-stage progressive learning (video pretrain → cross-embodiment post-train → whole-body FT); **76.1%** avg real SR (**+32pp** over GR00T-N1.7), **4.9 Hz** closed-loop.
- **[[2606.05979|WLA]]** — An ==autoregressive Transformer== (VLM-initialized) predicting next-state as both ==textual subtasks (World Expert)== and ==latent actions (Action Expert)== under one joint action+world+language loss, no test-time image prediction; **56.5%** on RMBench bimanual, **92.94%** RoboTwin 2.0, **98.6%** LIBERO at ~**40 ms** latency (~**40×** faster than prior WAMs).
- **[[2606.02800|Cosmos-3]]** — An ==omnimodal Mixture-of-Transformers== jointly processing + generating language/image/video/audio/action, with distinct Reasoner (autoregressive) and Generator (==diffusion==) pathways; strong across **48** Physical-AI benchmarks, SOTA open-source video gen, and Cosmos3-Nano-Policy-DROID hits **39.7%** on RoboLab while ranking **#1** on real-world RoboArena.

- **[[2605.10942|HarmoWAM]]** — A model that resolves the generalization-precision trade-off via ==dual action experts==: a ==predictive expert== on current-step latents and a ==reactive expert== on *future predicted frames*, with a ==Process-Adaptive Gating Mechanism==; **89%** in-domain average across six real-world tasks with only **7.9%** OOD drop — smallest gap among unified WAMs.
- **[[2605.00078|Being-H0.7]]** — A ==dual-branch latent world-action model== with a learnable explicit latent-reasoning space + ==future-informed dual-branch training== (deployable prior branch aligned to a privileged posterior branch) packed into one ==Mixture-of-Transformers==; **99.2%** LIBERO, **62.1%** RoboCasa, **3-4 ms/step** inference across three real robot platforms.
- **[[2604.09330|VAG]]** — A ==dual-stream flow-matching== model synchronously denoising video + action from one initial frame + instruction, cross-modally aligned by an ==adaptive 3D pooling== module (no extra params); **45%** AgiBot SR (vs two-stage VAG-Video+AnyPos **29%**), **+20pp** real-world SR as a synthetic-data generator — doubles as a direct World-Action policy.

- **[[2603.08572|MetaWorld-X]]** — A hierarchical world model handling ==negative interference== via ==Specialized Expert Policies (SEPs)== + a VLM-guided ==Intelligent Routing Mechanism (IRM)==, SEPs trained via ==energy-based imitation rewards==; 'Walk' converges in **0.5M steps** vs TD-MPC2's **1.8M**; **9/10** Walk/Run/Carry; **470.0** 'Door' return vs TD-MPC2's **285.0**.
- **[[2512.06628|MIND-V]]** — A hierarchical WM coupling a ==Semantic Reasoning Hub== + a ==Motor Video Generator== linked by a domain-invariant ==Behavioral Semantic Bridge==, with two-stage SFT→==GRPO RL== under a ==Physical Foresight Coherence reward==; **+76.7%** task SR and **+172.2%** user preference over the second-best baseline, and as guidance lifts OpenVLA-OFT **27.7%→43.5%**.
- **[[2602.09849|BagelVLA]]** — An 'Interleaved Planning' VLA factorizing execution into ==linguistic planning== → ==visual forecasting== → ==action generation== in one ==Mixture-of-Transformers==, with ==Residual Flow Guidance== for low-latency visual foresight; **4.405** Calvin ABC-D length (vs π0 **3.648**), **75.26%** RoboTwin Clean, **73.3%** real bimanual stack-cubes.

- **[[2507.01424|TriVLA]]** — A triple-system VLA composing ==Episodic Multimodal Perception (VLM)== + ==Episodic Dynamics Perception== (a 1.5B Stable Video Diffusion forecasting future scenes) + a Diffusion Policy in one forward pass; **4.37** avg task length on CALVIN ABC→D and **87.0%** LIBERO avg at **36 Hz**, holding **3.46** on 10% of CALVIN data.

#### 5.3 Driving WAMs

The unified policy + world model recipe instantiated for autonomous driving: ego-trajectory planning coupled to future-scene prediction in one model, benchmarked on the NAVSIM / nuScenes lineage (PDMS / EPDMS, L2 error, collision rate).

- **[[2607.10781|Training-Free Norm Injection]]** — Encodes driving norms as differentiable ==energy functions== over a frozen ==rectified-flow== world model's joint video+trajectory output, injected by an ==energy-descent step== at sampling time; braking deviation **13.6→1.3 m** over **35** clips, yet the video ignores the steer (**<2%** optical flow) — **38** bypassed single-stream blocks.
- **[[2607.10630|AWM]]** — Turns the world model into the adversary: a ==constrained min-max game== whose ==Adversarial Coalition Learning== stage (role-conditioned generation + ==counterfactual credit assignment==) builds scene-adaptive attackers the ego planner best-responds to under ==CVaR tail-risk==; **+2.60** nuPlan Test14-hard, **+5.81** InterPlan-LongTail, **−2.78** as an attacker.
- **[[2606.05645|Discrete-WAM]]** — A model that unifies world model, world-action policy, and hierarchical decision in a shared ==discrete token space== via ==discrete diffusion== token editing; **90.4 EPDMS** on NAVSIM-v2 (**+2.7** vs WAM-Flow, **+3.1** vs DreamerAD), FID **6.6** / FVD **80.0** world generation, with ==LoRA== adaptation preserving **90.0 EPDMS**.
- **[[2605.28544|DriveWAM]]** — Adapts a pretrained ==video diffusion transformer== into autoregressive ==joint video-action generation== for driving, with VLM ==scene-evolving guidance== via temporally-localized cross-attention + ==selective KV memory==; **90.1** PDMS NAVSIM, **12×** less KV memory over 300s rollouts.
- **[[2605.11550|DAWN-WAIM]]** — Formalizes ==World-Action Interactive Models (WAIMs)== where future world states and actions are ==recursively refined as coupled variables== (not parallel or predict-then-plan); a compact ==V-JEPA2 latent== + short explicit latent rollout feeds a world-conditioned diffusion action denoiser; **89.1 PDMS** NAVSIM v1, **0.33m** avg L2 nuScenes (vs WorldRFT's 0.47m).
- **[[2604.25329|ProDrive]]** — Couples a query-centric trajectory planner with a BEV world model via ==Ego-Environment Coupling==: planner-aware ego tokens condition future-scene prediction, and gradients flow back from the WM's predicted outcomes to the planner, making driving decisions proactive rather than reactive; **86.6** PDMS on NAVSIM v1 (**98.0** No-Collision, **93.7** TTC).
- **[[2604.09059|VLA-World]]** — A unified ==Vision-Language-Action World Model== for driving with an ==autoregressive pipeline== (perception → prediction → future-frame generation → thinking-with-visual-tokens → planning) under three-stage training ending in ==GRPO==; **L2 0.26m** / **0.08%** collision on ST-P3 (nuScenes), competitive **FID 9.8** future frames.
- **[[2604.04198|DriveVA]]** — A unified driving VLA jointly decoding future video + trajectories in ==one shared-latent Diffusion Transformer== built on ==Wan2.2-TI2V-5B== priors, trained with dense video-level supervision; **90.9 PDM** NAVSIM v1, zero-shot nuScenes L2 **−78.9%** / collision **−83.3%** vs PWM.
- **[[2603.27287|Uni-World-VLA]]** — An autoregressive driving VLA that ==interleaves future-frame prediction and trajectory planning== step-wise closed-loop, fusing ==monocular depth maps== (Depth Anything 3) via depth encoders + cross-attention, trained with Dynamic Focal Loss + L1; **PDMS 89.4** on NAVSIM (beating multi-sensor methods), **FVD 141.8** for 4s rollouts.
- **[[2603.24581|Latent-WAM]]** — A perception-free driving WAM: a ==Spatial-Aware Compressive World Encoder== distills geometric knowledge into 16 compact scene tokens, fed to a ==Dynamic Latent World Model== (causal Transformer + ==3D-RoPE==) autoregressively predicting future world states; **89.3** EPDMS NAVSIM v2 (+3.2 over prior SOTA) at only **104M** params.
- **[[2603.11041|DynVLA]]** — A driving VLA with a ==Dynamics Chain-of-Thought== paradigm: a ==Dynamics Tokenizer== learns compact discrete future-dynamics tokens (decoupled ego/environment, action + cross-view consistency), integrated via three-stage pretrain → SFT → ==GRPO RFT==; **PDM Score 91.7** NAVSIM / **88.34** Bench2Drive, **0.37s** latency (vs 3.04s textual CoT).
- **[[2602.06521|DriveWorld-VLA]]** — A unified latent-space driving WM tying a VLA to a World Model through the ==LLM's hidden states as a shared latent==, with a ==Diffusion Transformer== for action-conditioned "what-if" reasoning over candidate actions; **91.3 PDMS** NAVSIMv1 / **86.8 EPDMS** NAVSIMv2, **99.1%** no-collision, **0.16%** nuScenes collision rate.
- **[[2512.06112|WAM-Flow]]** — A driving VLA over ==discrete flow matching==: parallel coarse-to-fine denoising of ego-trajectory tokens (a metric-aligned tokenizer + triplet-margin loss) plus simulator-guided ==GRPO== for safety/comfort reward; SOTA **90.3** PDMS NAVSIM-v1 and **0.23%** nuScenes collision rate, with **4.67x** speedup at 1-step denoising.
- **[[2510.12796|DriveVLA-W0]]** — A driving VLA adding a ==world-modeling objective== (predict future scenes) for dense self-supervision + a lightweight ==Action Expert== in a ==Mixture-of-Experts==, instantiated for discrete-token and continuous-feature VLA families; SOTA NAVSIM v1/v2 from a single front camera, **−28.8%** ADE / **−15.9%** collision on 70M frames, **−63.1%** latency.

#### 5.4 Imagination & Test-Time Reasoning

World model used for *test-time* simulation: the agent runs imagined rollouts during deployment to evaluate candidate plans. Expensive but maximally flexible — the imagination budget adapts to task difficulty.

- **[[2606.18589|DREAM-Chunk]]** — A policy-agnostic ==test-time scaling== method adding a lightweight auxiliary ==latent world model== that, at inference, predicts the latent future of multiple sampled VLA action chunks and reactively picks the one whose dreamed latent best matches reality; up to **+55pp** SR (**10%→65%** Franka can-insertion) under perturbation, adding **~7 ms** latency.
- **[[2606.22729|STL-Guided-Diffusion]]** — A method letting ==action-only diffusion policies== satisfy ==Signal Temporal Logic== constraints at inference: a separate differentiable world model predicts future states from proposed actions, then STL-robustness gradients steer denoising + post-hoc gradient ascent; cuts uprightness violations **84.0%→4.0%** in can-transport at **100%** task success.

### 6. Efficient & Action-Centered WAMs

Full video generation at test time is **4.8x slower** than pure VLAs. These models keep WAM benefits while eliminating the inference bottleneck via three architectural strategies — train with video then strip it at deployment, plan in compact latent / action spaces, or add uncertainty layers so the agent knows when to fall back to cautious behavior.

#### 6.1 Training-Time Video, Test-Time Speed

Train with video co-training to absorb spatiotemporal priors; deploy with a slim action head — *no test-time video generation*. The dominant efficient-WAM recipe of 2026.

- **[[2608.04404|Faster-WAM]]** — Generates a reusable future context in one video-expert pass, then reuses it sparsely via ==SparseMoT== (video-action interaction restricted to a compact subset of stages) + ==Interval KV-Fusion==; **2.21x** faster than Joint-WAM (**252.95 ms**), **73.6%** LIBERO-Plus OOD (vs Fast-WAM's **49.1%**), **71.1%** real-world OOD.
- **[[2608.01880|Async-WAM-Deployment-Study]]** — An empirical comparison of six WAM deployment strategies (sync, async, weighted-blend, inference-time RTC, ==prefix-conditioned training-time RTC==); prefix-conditioning wins on precision-smoothness balance, completion score **96** on a dynamic conveyor task vs synchronous's **20**.
- **[[2608.01221|EndoWAM]]** — A World Action Model for endoscopic navigation pairing a ==future grounding== paradigm (reconstructing target regions in future latents via a Grounding DiT) with single-denoising-pass action decoding, dropping the video/grounding branches at inference; **80.2%** avg SR (**+53.1pp** over GR00T-N1.7), **90.1%** zero-shot generalization, **7.5 Hz** real-time control.
- **[[2608.00793|DynamicWAM]]** — A ==Dual-Path Motion Conditioning== WAM combining a ==History-Flow pathway== with a ==Kinematic Token pathway== via ==joint world–action attention== + ==Real-Time Chunking== async execution; **38.2%** SR on DOMINO Level 1 (vs InternVLA-A1.5 **29.3%**), **46.67%** avg across 12 real dynamic tasks (**+22.92pp** over π0.5).
- **[[2608.00725|SelfWAM]]** — A self-grounded WAM with ==asymmetric information flow== conditioning future visual prediction on a 'clean copy' of the demonstrated action + ==future robot self-mask prediction== reusing the video backbone, action-only inference unaffected; **92.62%** RoboTwin 2.0 / **95%** real-world beating Fast-WAM, only **+0.97%** latency overhead.
- **[[2607.13960|GigaWorld-Policy-0.5]]** — An action-centered WAM whose ==causal masking== permits action-only decoding at inference, splitting a ==Mixture-of-Transformers== into visual and action experts, tuned by an agentic ==AutoResearch== pipeline and a C++ runtime; **0.85** fruit-picking / **0.80** long-horizon SR (**+33%** relative), **85 ms** on an RTX 4090 (**53%** faster than FastWAM).
- **[[2607.05468|MECo-WAM]]** — Injects action-relevant ==4D geometric priors== via a training-only 4D expert (frozen VGGT) + ==decayed 4D read-mask attention== + ==action-aware temporal geometric distillation==, entirely removed at deployment; **98.2%** LIBERO, **92.62%** RoboTwin 2.0, **-39%** corrections vs Fast-WAM.
- **[[2607.04927|DSWAM]]** — A ==dual-system== World Action Foundation Model pairing a ==System 1 WAM executor== (video-supervised training, action-only inference via ==TensorRT== + async ==Real-Time Chunking==) with an optional ==System 2 VLM subtask planner==; **96.3%** real deformable-manipulation SR (+3.8pp over DeMaVLA), **73.8 ms** latency (**2.69x** speedup).
- **[[2607.02195|BRIDGE-WA]]** — Distills a frozen ==Wan2.2-5B== future-change teacher into three compact priors (==future tokens==, ==change maps==, ==motion-flow maps==) conditioning an action transformer via ==WORLDBRIDGE== layered attention, removing the teacher at inference; **+9.7pp** VLABench SR, **72.1%** LIBERO-Plus zero-shot.
- **[[2606.15768|LaWAM]]** — A two-stage recipe pretraining a ==Latent World Action Model== to predict future latents from inferred latent actions, then generating a compact ==latent visual subgoal== in one forward pass to guide an action expert via latent-action distillation + Knowledge Insulation; **98.6%** LIBERO, **92.6%** RoboTwin, **90%** real, at **187 ms/chunk** (**24×** faster).
- **[[2606.14048|WAM4D]]** — A 4D World-Action Model adding lightweight ==spatial register tokens== as future-depth readouts that distill geometric priors into the backbone via ==causal mixture attention== at train time, then dropping the geometric branch at deployment for fast 2D-to-action inference; **91.8%** RoboTwin 2.0 and **90%** real AstriBot S1 SR at **525 ms** + **9.71 GiB** VRAM.
- **[[2606.08962|C3ache]]** — A training-free ==Cross Inference Chunk Cache== that caches and reuses the accumulated DiT-block residual from a prior inference chunk to skip block computation on early denoising steps of World-Action Models; up to **2.5x** LIBERO and **1.84x** RoboTwin wall-clock speedup with negligible (+**0.20%**) success-rate change.
- **[[2606.05254|Flash-WAM]]** — A ==modality-aware distillation== method with distinct ==consistency functions== per stream (linear-gradient actions, standard LCM video); **23×** speedup (8.1 s → **348 ms**/chunk on L40S, under the **500 ms** real-time budget) retaining **81.41%** RoboTwin 2.0 vs **91.25%** teacher (naive joint LCM only **36.32%**), **50.0%** real-world on a Unitree G1.
- **[[2603.16666|Fast-WAM]]** — A ==Mixture-of-Transformer== that trains an action expert + video backbone under ==joint flow-matching== with ==structured attention==, then *removes* the video branch at deployment (~**190 ms/step**, **>4×** faster); it scores **97.6%** LIBERO and **91.8%** RoboTwin, and removing video co-training drops RoboTwin to **83.8%** — the canonical production recipe.
- **[[2603.17240|GigaWorld-Policy]]** — A ==unified diffusion Transformer== over action chunks + sparse future observations under ==flow matching==; action-only inference + ==curriculum pretraining==; **360 ms/step** on A100 (~**9× speedup** vs Motus's 3231 ms), **0.83** avg real-world SR (**+7%** over Motus, **+14%** over VLM-based VLAs), comparable with **10%** training data.
- **[[2512.15692|mimic-video]]** — A ==Video-Action Model== pairing a ==Cosmos-Predict2== generative video backbone with a lightweight ==action decoder== reading ==partially-denoised latent states==, trained via ==Conditional Flow Matching==; **56.3%** SIMPLER-Bridge / **93.9%** LIBERO, **10×** sample efficiency and **2×** faster convergence than VLA-conditioned decoders.
- **[[2506.18897|MinD]]** — A dual-system world model coupling a low-frequency video generator (==LoDiff-Visual==) with a high-frequency ==HiDiff-Policy== via a DiffMatcher + diffusion-forcing alignment loss, conditioning the policy on a single-step latent prediction for real-time control + implicit risk analysis; **63.0%** RL-Bench, **72.50%** real Franka, **11.3 FPS** (vs RoboDreamer 0.7–1.1).

- **[[2605.06247|CKT-WAM]]** — A parameter-efficient ==CKT module== compressing a teacher WAM's hidden states via ==learnable-query cross-attention== + ==sparsely-routed specialized adapters==, injected into a frozen student backbone; **86.1%** [[2510.13626|LIBERO-Plus]] with only **1.17%** trainable params; matches full fine-tune. - **[[2604.26182|LWM]]** — A method that *lifts* planning by composing a lightweight ==goal-conditioned policy== onto a ==frozen low-level world model== via a low-dimensional ==2D image-space waypoint== action space tractable for ==CEM search==; **3.8×** lower mean joint error (all-MJE **0.704m → 0.374m** vs PEVA-CEM **0.616m**), waypoint masking reaching **0.226m**.
- **[[2512.19133|WorldRFT]]** — A ==Spatial-aware World Encoder== injecting frozen ==VGGT== 3D priors + ==Hierarchical Planning Refinement== + ==GRPO RL fine-tuning==; **−21% L2** and **−83% collision rate** (**0.30%→0.05%**) over LAW on nuScenes, **PDMS 87.8** vision-only on NavSim closed-loop, **96.8% Drivable Area Compliance** — safety as active objective.

#### 6.3 Uncertainty-Aware & Self-Verifying

Introspection layers so the agent knows when to *distrust* its own predictions — and falls back to cautious behavior or queries an oracle.

- **[[2608.02578|CoWAM]]** — A selective intervention layer for bimanual WAMs formalizing synchronization, role-compatibility, and collision-avoidance as deterministic ==coordination contracts==, gated by an ==event-conditioned verifier==; **62.9%** closed-loop SR (**+9.6pp** over Selective Control), **93.3%** coordination-valid selection, **0.4%** harmful-intervention rate.
- **[[2607.15065|DriftWorld]]** — An action-conditioned world model on ==drifting generative models== (single forward pass, no diffusion) with ==action-accentuated negative-sample distribution== + ==DINOv2/v3== feature loss; **17×** faster than diffusion WMs (**30+ fps**), matching quality; boosts policy IoU **0.635→0.781**, hits **0.99** Pearson r² as offline policy-ranking simulator.
- **[[2607.14943|WA-LQR]]** — Locates robustness-relevant nuisance directions in WAM ==activation space== by ==mechanistic interpretability==, then closes a loop around them: activations project into a ==contrastive subspace== whose ==local linear dynamics== drive an ==LQR feedback controller== steering only on deviation; **+41%** SR for Cosmos-Policy 2B and DiT4DiT under OOD shift, no retraining.
- **[[2607.07196|Admissibility Ladder]]** — A five-level ==Verification, Validation & Accreditation== ladder (L0-L4) grading generative WMs' trustworthiness as closed-loop test oracles, from visual fidelity to ==action-robustness== to verdict-transfer; visual quality doesn't predict action-robustness (Epona **0.54** IEC/**3.2s** horizon vs Vista's **0.33**/**1.6s**).
- **[[2607.01060|RoboWorld]]** — A fast autoregressive video WM trained with ==STEP FORCING== (aligned denoising schedules + ==one-step self-forwarded priors== + anchor steps) paired with a ==task-progress-aware VLM judge==; Pearson **r=0.989** / Spearman **ρ=0.970** with RoboArena rankings at **~100 H100-hrs**, **15.31 FPS**.
- **[[2606.27326|MMBench2]]** — A reframing of world-model ==hallucination as a data-coverage problem==: three label-free internal predictors (Round-Trip Residual, Flow Instability, Inter-Seed Variance) correlate **~−0.80** Spearman with rollout error, with the MMBench2 dataset (65,600 trajectories, 210 tasks); coverage-aware training + targeted collection lift unseen-task MPC **0.276→0.325**.
- **[[2606.22363|RefFree-PhysConsist]]** — A ==reference-free relative assessment== combining ==3D consistency (DROID-SLAM reprojection error)== + ==photometric consistency (SEA-RAFT AEPE)== via Shannon-entropy weighting, plus spatio-temporal Artifact-Heatmap localization; filtering low-anomaly rollouts lifts OpenVLA SR **66.2%→74.6%**.
- **[[2606.18610|SC3-Eval]]** — Adapts a ==unified dynamics model== (UVA-style) with joint ==forward-inverse dynamics consistency== + ==cross-view inpainting== + ==uncertainty-driven early termination== for closed-loop VLA evaluation; **0.984** Pearson / **0.022** MMRV in-distribution, reproduces policy failure modes at **0.56** rate.
- **[[2606.05773|PiL-World]]** — A ==chunk-wise world model== for closed-loop VLA evaluation: a ==deterministic Action-to-Control Projection== + a ==Latent History Memory==; cuts the imagined-vs-real success gap **63.2% → 12.0%**, hits **0.94** Pearson with real policy ranking, lifts Hallucination-Free Ratio **41.5% → 70.1%** — evaluate policies in imagination, not on hardware.
- **[[2606.00267|StressDream]]** — A method ==steering diffusion video world models== for robust policy evaluation by optimizing the ==initial noise== toward high-impact outcomes via a ==differentiable test-time criterion== (VLM semantic + plausibility); reliably surfaces WM-imagination failures, and reweighting demos by stressed generations lifts real robot SR **39%→71%**.
- **[[2605.27491|GE-Sim-2.0]]** — A closed-loop ==action-conditioned multi-view diffusion== robot-manipulation simulator adding a Proprioceptive State Expert and a VLM World-Judge reward, with step-distillation for 100-frame rollouts in ~2.3s; tops the WorldArena leaderboard (+3.96 dB PSNR over Ctrl-World), **0.81** episode success-prediction accuracy, and **+15%** real-robot SR via filtered BC.
- **[[2605.08215|T3VF]]** — A ==test-time training== method for visual-foresight VLAs using predicted-vs-actual future-image correspondence as ==self-supervision==, updating only learnable query tokens with an ==adaptive update filter== keyed on action variance; **+2.8pp** LIBERO-Plus OOD on Mantis (**49.3%→52.1%**), **31.8%** on the Robot perturbation vs **28.6%** fixed-threshold.
- **[[2603.21017|Dream-Diffusion-Policy]]** — A ==Dream Diffusion Policy== co-optimizing a diffusion policy + diffusion world model over a shared ==3D visual encoder==, detecting OOD via a ==real-imagination discrepancy== and switching to acting on ==autoregressively forecasted latents== when vision is corrupted; **73.8%** OOD MetaWorld / **83.3%** real-world OOD SR vs DP3's **0.8%/3.3%**.
- **[[2504.16680|RWM-U]]** — An ==ensemble-based== Robotic World Model quantifying aleatoric + ==epistemic uncertainty==, paired with ==MOPO-PPO== penalizing PPO rewards by that uncertainty; trained fully offline, beats CQL/MBPO baselines and reaches **0.91** normalized reward on real ANYmal D, deploying on physical ANYmal D + Unitree G1.
- **[[2604.01985|WAV]]** — A World Action Verifier splitting checking into ==state plausibility== (a ==subgoal generator== on action-free video) + ==action reachability== (a ==sparse inverse dynamics model==), then a ==verification-guided self-improving cycle==; **2×** WM-learning sample-efficiency, **>22%** higher downstream reward across six tasks. Self-correcting WAM.
- **[[2604.11351|WM-DAgger]]** — An ==Eye-in-Hand Action-Conditioned World Model== that synthesizes OOD *recovery* trajectories via a ==Corrective Action Synthesis== module + ==DINOv2-embedding consistency filtering==, automating ==DAgger==; **96.7%** soft-bag pushing (vs BC's **30.0%**) and **63.3–76.7%** pick-and-place on unseen objects (vs BC's **0–10%**).
- **[[2602.22474|UPS]]** — Uncertainty-aware Policy Steering pairs a ==Dreamer-v3== latent world model with a VLM verifier calibrated by ==Conformal Prediction== + a ==Bayesian intent factorization== score, routing to execute / ask-clarification / ==residual-policy retraining==; **≥85%** coverage at the lowest human-intervention rate (**0.05–0.06**), **85%** SR after retraining.
- **[[2511.11520|Video-WM-Policy-Eval]]** — A method using an ==action-conditional video diffusion model== to evaluate policies without real-robot rollouts: roll out inside the video WM, then an ==off-the-shelf VLM== judges success; rollout-augmented training (**PSNR 18.7 → 20.6**), **Pearson r = 0.833–0.879** policy-ranking correlation in RoboMimic sim and **r = 0.687** against real-world Bridge.
- **[[2509.19555|AnySafe]]** — A runtime-adaptable ==latent safety filter== parameterizing image-based constraints inside a world model's latent space via a ==failure projector== + ==conformal==-calibrated threshold, no retraining per safety level; adapts to runtime image constraints on a real Franka and generalizes to interpolated unsafe sets where fixed-constraint filters fail.
- **[[2505.00779|UNISafe]]** — A latent ==Hamilton-Jacobi reachability== safety filter augmented with ==epistemic uncertainty== from a probabilistic ensemble (Jensen-Rényi Divergence) + ==conformal==-calibrated OOD detection to penalize uncertain states; failure rate **0.20** on vision block-plucking (vs **0.30** LatentSafe), **0.94** OOD balanced accuracy, real Jenga failures **0.82→0.08**.

### 7. Self-Evolving WAMs

WAMs that autonomously improve through experience, self-play, or co-evolution. See [[15_Self-Evolving-VLA-WAM#5. Self-Evolving WAMs]] for the full deep-dive on self-evolving mechanisms, VLA vs WAM comparison, failure modes, and research directions.

#### 7.1 Reflective Planning Loops

Generate, critique, regenerate. The agent improves through self-assessment of its own predictions — judging plan quality turns out to be easier than generating perfect plans first try.

- **[[2603.08403|SPIRAL]]** — The canonical generate → critique → regenerate template: agent generates a long-horizon video plan; ==CriticAgent== evaluates temporal coherence + action completeness, and failed plans regenerate; **58.72%** EgoPlan-Bench (+**3.94pp** over GPT-5.1, +**6.89pp** over Video-LLaMA) — *judging* is easier than *generating* perfect plans.
- **[[2602.20119|NovaPlan]]** — A hierarchical zero-shot planner pairing a ==closed-loop VLM planner== (==generate-then-verify tree search== + ==verify-and-recover== re-planning) with a low-level ==hybrid object/hand flow== executor switching between 6-DoF object trajectories and hand-pose guidance; **70%** four-layer block stacking (vs NovaFlow 30%), **100%** hidden-object search.
- **[[2502.05907|EvoAgent]]** — A three-part loop with continual world model: ==self-planning== proposes a plan via the WM, ==self-control== executes while monitoring prediction error, ==self-reflection== compares predicted vs actual outcomes and updates WM + policy. **+105%** on long-horizon tasks; the loop contributes **72%** of total gain (Minecraft).

#### 7.2 Self-Play & Autonomous Exploration

Agent generates its own training data via play or epistemic curiosity — no human demonstrations required. The "free data" recipe.

- **[[2606.28939|ReGuide]]** — Turns transient WM-guided test-time correction (==DINO-WM== dynamics + ==Phase-Conditioned Guidance== + ==two-threshold gating==) into reusable on-policy recovery data via a ==rollout-collect-train== loop; **1.2–7.7×** SR lift on Robomimic tasks, self-improves over iterations.
- **[[2605.03782|GLANCE]]** — Grounds a VLM agent's linguistic world model via ==cross-modal curiosity==: an ==alignment projector== maps its next-state hypothesis into a ==momentum-target== visual latent, and the prediction-observation gap becomes an intrinsic RL reward with ==Curriculum Exploration== rejuvenation; **0.86** vs **0.81** VAGEN-Full, **0.66** vs **0.25** on PrimitiveSkill.
- **[[2603.09030|PlayWorld]]** — An ==autonomous self-play data collection== framework with ==VLM Task Proposer + VLA Executer== + ==Stable-Video-Diffusion== finetuned via ==curriculum learning== on contact-rich play; captures failure modes absent in human data, **Pearson 0.8766** between predicted and real-world success, **+65%** real-world SR via in-model fine-tune.
- **[[2503.01584|SENSEI]]** — A method distilling a ==semantic intrinsic reward== from ==VLM== pairwise "interestingness" annotations into a ==DreamerV3== recurrent WM, combined with ==epistemic uncertainty== via a ==Go-Explore==-style adaptive policy; discovers meaningful interactions beating Plan2Explore and learns downstream tasks **orders of magnitude faster** on MiniHack/Robodesk.
- **[[2506.23468|NavMorph]]** — An ==RSSM-based WM== with ==Contextual Evolution Memory== for gradient-free online adaptation + feature-level (not pixel) future prediction; **+4.1% SR / +2.73% SPL** on RxR-CE unseen, **2.1× faster** test-time adaptation than gradient-based alternatives — navigation-specialized self-play in continuous 3D environments.

#### 7.3 RL-Driven & Co-Evolving

Continual RL on world-model dynamics; agent and environment co-evolve. The world model is itself a learning target, not just a frozen simulator.

- **[[2607.06018|RoboTALES]]** — A single-stage framework jointly training a ==Stable Video Diffusion== generator + action policy, with an ==LLM planner== decomposing sub-tasks and a ==VLM critic== reward-optimizing the generator via ==DDPO==; **64%** RoboCasa (vs VideoPolicy **57.5%** on 50 demos), **97%** LIBERO10.
- **[[2607.02431|WorldSample]]** — A closed-loop real-robot RL framework grounding an ==action-conditioned world model== with online rollouts to generate ==counterfactual trajectories==, gated by ==Policy-Paced Learning== (Q-aware sample selection + uncertainty-guided scheduling); SR **56%→82%**, physical steps cut **59%** (56K→23K).
- **[[2606.17906|WAM-RL]]** — A framework jointly optimizing the world model + action model online: the WM is refined by ==online video self-supervised fine-tuning== on successful rollouts (KL-stabilized) while the policy uses a dense ==reconstruction-based reward== from imagined-vs-executed similarity; LIBERO-Object **68%→82%** (vs **78%** actor-only RL), the WM learning post-failure recovery.
- **[[2606.22449|Self-Evolving-Cognitive-Framework]]** — A framework letting embodied agents continually refine internal ==causal world models== through interaction: it formalizes the environment as a time-indexed ==Structural Causal Model== where actions are interventions, with a ==structural evolution operator (Φ)== revising causal representations + a six-metric causal-epistemic benchmark.
- **[[2605.14274|CreFlow]]** — Post-trains a video-diffusion manipulation generator (Vidar) with an ==LTL-based compositional constraint monitor== (SAM3+IDM+VLM state-lifting), gated into a ==credit-aware NFT loss== + ==corrective reflow loss== (regress failures toward within-group success mean); **53.1%** RoboTwin avg (+**23.8pp**), beats EVA/DanceGRPO/DiffusionNFT.
- **[[2605.07794|NoiseGate]]** — Reframes per-latent denoising timestep in a joint video-action ==Mixture-of-Transformers WAM== as a learnable ==information-gating policy==, trained via ==GRPO== on sparse task-success reward through a lightweight ==Gating Policy Network==; **94.28%** RoboTwin avg (+**1.70pp** over Stage-1 WAM), beats Fast-WAM/Motus.
- **[[2603.25685|Persistent-Robot-World-Models]]** — An ==RL post-training== of action-conditioned video-diffusion WMs to fix ==exposure bias==: ==reward-contrasted denoising== for x0-prediction + an ==autoregressive protocol==; **+4.09 dB** PSNR / **−46.6%** LPIPS on external cameras (DROID), human-preferred **80%** of the time, stabilizes multi-step rollouts to **11 s**.
- **[[2603.19370|VAMPO]]** — A method that reframes the Video Prediction Model's multi-step ==denoising== as an ==MDP==, post-trained with ==GRPO== under a verifiable ==latent-consistency reward== + an ==Euler Hybrid sampler==; best task-completion + avg trajectory length on CALVIN ABC→D / L-CALVIN over VLM- and VPM-based SOTA. The canonical GRPO-on-WAM recipe.
- **[[2603.17808|EVA]]** — A ==GRPO post-training== aligning a video diffusion WM with robot ==executability==: a pretrained ==Inverse Dynamics Model== bridges generated frames to actions, building a ==dense reward== penalizing jerk + embodiment-limit violations (LoRA); kinematic plausibility **+20.9%** (to **91.4%**), **52.6%** sim avg over 21 bimanual tasks, real **64.0%** seen / **60.0%** OOD.
- **[[2602.09022|WorldCompass]]** — An ==RL post-training== of autoregressive video WMs via a ==clip-level rollout== + dual rewards (==Interaction-Following Score== from 3D foundation models + ==HPSv3 Visual-Quality Score==) under a DiffusionNFT-inspired optimizer; lifts composite-action following from ~**20%→55%** across HunyuanVideo-1.5 + Wan2.2 up to **381**-frame horizons.
- **[[2602.05842|RWML]]** — A ==Reinforcement World Model Learning== method: self-supervised ==GRPO== with a ==sim-to-real-gap reward== gives LLM agents an action-conditioned WM as a mid-training step; **+19.6pp** ALFWorld / **+7.9pp** τ2Bench self-supervised, **+6.9pp** on top of policy RL, far less catastrophic forgetting than SFT — semantic rewards beat token-fidelity SFT.
- **[[2509.23958|RLIR]]** — An ==RL with Inverse Rewards== method post-training WMs using an ==Inverse Dynamics Model== + a ==frame-level reward== optimized via ==GRPO== for both autoregressive (MineWorld) + diffusion (NFD) WMs; **+5–10%** action-classification accuracy and up to **+10%** visual quality (FVD/PSNR/VBench) — IDM rewards beat human-preference + pixel rewards.
- **[[2509.19080|World4RL]]** — A method that refines a BC policy by ==PPO inside imagined rollouts== of a frozen high-fidelity ==diffusion transition WM== (==two-hot action encoding==, sparse reward classifier); lowest FVD/FID/LPIPS vs NWM/iVideoGPT/DiWA, **67.5%** Meta-World avg and **93.3%** real Franka (**+25pp** over the BC start) — diffusion WMs give sharper imagined rollouts than RSSMs.
- **[[2508.03645|DiWA]]** — A method that fine-tunes ==diffusion policies entirely offline== via ==PPO== in a ==Dream Diffusion MDP== that embeds the denoising chain into a WM latent learned from play data, regularized with behavior cloning; first ==zero-shot real-world transfer== of an offline-WM-tuned diffusion policy, e.g. close-drawer **59.14%→91.95%** with zero physical interaction.
- **[[2507.09177|Online-Agent-OA]]** — A continual-RL method by ==MPC planning== over an ==online-learned world model== using ==Follow-The-Leader shallow models== with sparse-feature analytical updates (no replay, no forgetting) + ==CEM==; highest avg **72.93%** and lowest regret **27.62%** on the new Continual Bench, with no-regret guarantees.
- **[[2505.13934|RLVR-World]]** — A method that post-trains language + video WMs with ==Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards== over ==autoregressive token== predictions via ==GRPO==, directly optimizing task metrics; **+44.8%** text-game state accuracy, **+30.3 F1** web-page prediction, **−9.2%** robot-video LPIPS, cutting video repetition **48.6%→9.9%** in a few hundred steps.
- **[[2504.21024|WebEvolver]]** — A method co-training a web agent policy + ==dedicated world-model LLM== generating synthetic multi-step trajectories + ==WMLA inference-time multi-step lookahead==; **+10%** over OpenWebVoyager baseline, **51.37%** SR on WebVoyager and **24.53%** on Mind2Web-Live (from **18.86%**) at WMLA depth 2 — overcoming self-improvement plateaus via WM-generated diversity.

### 8. Cross-Paradigm Comparison

WAM paradigms encode different bets on the speed–robustness–sample-efficiency frontier. The five families don't compete on a single axis — they occupy orthogonal points in the design space, each dominating one constraint at the cost of others. This section frames the trade-offs so practitioners can pick the right paradigm for the deployment constraint that *actually binds*.

The 2026 frontier is hybridization: train with VideoGen objectives to absorb spatiotemporal priors, then deploy without test-time imagination (the [[2603.16666|Fast-WAM]] / [[2602.10098|VLA-JEPA]] recipe). The choice of paradigm has become a choice of *which axis to optimize at deployment*, not which is "best".

#### 8.1 Robustness-Optimized Paradigms

Maximize physics fidelity and zero-shot generalization at the cost of inference latency. The frontier when the bottleneck is *out-of-distribution* generalization, not throughput.

- **[[2602.15922|DreamZero]]** (VideoGen) — The slowest (~**7Hz**) but most robust WAM; **39.5%** on unseen tasks via cross-embodiment video priors. Defines the robustness ceiling.
- **[[2605.15153|Pelican-Unified]]** (VLM-Integrated) — A moderate-speed, high-robustness unified VLM + WAM; **64.7** VLM avg, **93.5%** RoboTwin; the unified-architecture entry on the robustness frontier.

#### 8.2 Speed-Optimized Paradigms

Maximize inference throughput for real-time control and production deployment. The frontier when the bottleneck is *latency per decision*, not absolute robustness.

- **[[2602.10098|VLA-JEPA]]** (Latent) — A fast latent-prediction WAM (~**10ms/step**); **97.2%** [[2306.03310|LIBERO]] in-distribution; the speed-quality Pareto baseline.
- **[[2603.16666|Fast-WAM]]** (Efficient) — A training-time-video, test-time-action-only WAM; ~**190ms/step** at deployment; the canonical production-ready recipe.

#### 8.3 Sample-Efficient Paradigms

Maximize what's learnable from *limited* data without VLM priors. The frontier when neither internet-scale video nor a pretrained VLM is available.

- **[[2301.04104|DreamerV3]]** ([[1912.01603|Dreamer]] Lineage) — The highest-sample-efficiency WAM; **150+** tasks with *fixed hyperparameters*; works without internet-scale data. The substrate when the data axis collapses to zero.

### 9. Open Problems & Failure Modes

WAMs convert visual or latent prediction into actionable policy — but their failure modes cluster into three distinct categories: *generative pathologies* (the model predicts physically impossible or trivially exploitable futures), *deployment-side weaknesses* (inference latency, adversarial robustness), and *uncertainty handling* (the model doesn't know when its own predictions are unreliable). Each category needs a different remediation path; conflating them leads to chasing the wrong fix.

#### 9.1 Generative & Prediction Pathologies

The cluster of failures rooted in the WAM's own prediction quality — hallucinated dynamics, exploited artifacts, and the coupled dynamics-reward bottleneck.

- **==Hallucinated dynamics==** — VideoGen WAMs can predict physically impossible futures (object teleportation, mass non-conservation). [[2603.23376|ABot-PhysWorld]] addresses this with ==Diffusion-DPO== over preference pairs scored by a physics critic.
- **==Artifact exploitation==** — Agents trained on WAM rollouts may exploit unrealistic visual artifacts in generated video (smooth gradients that don't exist in real cameras), leading to policies that fail at deployment. Physics-grounded training objectives mitigate but don't eliminate.
- **==Object-identity entanglement==** — Holistic WAMs fuse target identity with surrounding visual content; small scene changes flip target binding. [[2605.06481|OA-WAM]] — object-addressable attention with cached identity addresses; **+4.8pp** [[2510.13626|LIBERO-Plus]] geometric robustness over π0.5.
- **==Coupled dynamics-reward error==** — Existing return-gap theory treats learned dynamics + reward as one entity, hiding the bottleneck. [[2605.06732|Training-in-Imagination]] — decomposes the bound into separate dynamics and reward terms; reward error decays much faster (exponent **0.96**) than dynamics error (**0.11**), so for fixed budgets the dominant residual is dynamics.

#### 9.2 Deployment-Side Weaknesses

Problems exposed once a WAM leaves the training loop — inference latency, adversarial vulnerability, and the perturbation-robustness asymmetry vs. VLAs.

- **==Inference latency==** — WAMs are **≥4.8x** slower than VLAs ([[2603.22078|WAM-vs-VLA-Robustness]]); naive deployment misses real-time control loops. Use [[2603.16666|Fast-WAM]] for ==training-only video== (test-time speed) or latent-prediction variants (§3) to amortize cost.
- **==Adversarial jailbreaking==** — [[2604.05498|JailWAM]] shows WAMs are vulnerable to adversarial perturbations on action generation; small input shifts induce safety-violating rollouts. Need adversarial robustness training, currently underdeveloped relative to vision-only adversarial work.
- **==Visual perturbation robustness (positive)==** — Conversely, WAMs *outperform* VLAs on camera/light/background changes ([[2603.22078|WAM-vs-VLA-Robustness]]); spatiotemporal priors from video pretraining act as a regularizer. This asymmetry — robust to vision shift, vulnerable to action-space adversarial — suggests the two failure modes have orthogonal roots.

#### 9.3 OOD Detection & Uncertainty Handling

When should a WAM distrust its own predictions? Three complementary signals are emerging — each cheap on its own, but none yet integrated into a unified abstention policy.

- **==Prediction error monitoring==** — [[2603.04029|Self-Adapting-RL]] tracks the residual between predicted and observed next states; when the residual exceeds a threshold, the WAM flags the state as OOD and triggers targeted adaptation.
- **==Surprise filtering==** — [[2512.01119|WM-Surprise-Robustness]] distinguishes genuine OOD events (new physics) from sensor noise (camera glitch) by filtering prediction errors through a learned noise model.
- **==Forward-inverse asymmetry==** — [[2604.01985|WAV]] compares the forward model (predict next state from action) with the inverse model (infer action from state transition); disagreement reveals states where dynamics are poorly modeled.

#### 9.4 Adversarial & Poisoning Attacks

The attack surface unique to WAMs: because policies train on or read from world-model predictions, an adversary who corrupts those predictions — at data-collection time or at inference — propagates the fault straight into behavior. The axis: where the perturbation enters (poisoned training data vs. inference-time physical-condition channels) and what it compromises (synthetic-data quality, backdoored DRL policies, downstream planning safety).

- **[[2607.15207|BadWAM]]** — ==World-Action Drift Attacks== targeting the desynchronization between a WAM's imagined future and its executed action, generated ==black-box== by ==query-based online optimization== in action-only and stealthier ==imagination-preserving== flavors; closed-loop SR drops **>50%**, perturbations transfer across WAM variants, preprocessing defenses insufficient.
- **[[2606.16519|BadWorld]]** — An adversarial-attack framework showing autoregressive visual world models collapse under imperceptible perturbations via a ==label-free velocity attack== disrupting early-denoising velocity predictions + ==trajectory-adaptive bi-level optimization== over unknown user actions; 'Velocity-Min' drops Astra aesthetic **0.501→0.405**, raises MEt3R **+74.8%**.
- **[[2606.09499|World-Model-Poisoning]]** — A stealthy ==data poisoning== threat on robot-learning world models: Visual Prompt Hijacking injects malicious image-frame prompts so text-conditioned WMs emit dangerous trajectories, while Visual Transition Hijacking implants DRL backdoors; VPH hits **100%** target-class success at OOD angles, VTH backdoors persist in Dino-WM and Cosmos-Predict 2.5.
- **[[2602.18739|PhysAtt]]** — A white-box inference-time ==adversarial attack== (PhysCond-WMA) perturbing physical-condition channels (HDMap, 3D-box) of generative driving world models in two stages, steering reverse ==diffusion== via EMA target gradients; up to **0.55** attack success on DriveDreamer2 and **+26.7%** open-loop planning collision rate while preserving FID/FVD.

> [!star] Key Papers — WAM Failure Frontier
> - [[2605.06732|Training-in-Imagination]] — Decomposes return-gap into separate dynamics + reward error terms; the canonical evidence that dynamics-error (exponent **0.11**) dominates reward-error (**0.96**) at scale — invest the data budget in dynamics
> - [[2605.06481|OA-WAM]] — Object-addressable attention with cached identity; **+4.8pp** [[2510.13626|LIBERO-Plus]] geometric robustness — the first principled fix for holistic-WAM identity entanglement
> - [[2604.05498|JailWAM]] — Exposes adversarial attack surface on WAM action generation; the canonical "WAMs are not yet safe" evidence and the open-problem benchmark for robustness research

^key-papers-9

> [!tip] When to Use WAM vs VLA — and the Common Root of WAM Failures
> **Use WAM when** robustness to visual perturbations matters, physics-aware planning is needed, or real-world data is limited (world model enables imagination). **Use pure VLA when** inference speed is critical, tasks are simple enough for direct imitation, or in-domain data is abundant. The common root across §9.1–§9.3 failures is **calibration**: WAMs predict confidently in regimes where the prediction is unreliable. Three of seven problems above (hallucination, artifact exploitation, identity entanglement) are *training-time* miscalibration — the WAM doesn't know it's outside its training distribution. The remaining four (latency, adversarial, OOD detection, dynamics-reward decomposition) are *deployment-time* miscalibration — the WAM doesn't know when to abstain or hand off. Cross-reference [[07_Latent-World-Models#6. Open Problems]] (latent-space failure modes with the same calibration root) and [[08_Physics-Aware-Embodied-AI#8. Open Problems]] (physics-verifiability as the upstream constraint that *would* fix many of these if it generalized).
