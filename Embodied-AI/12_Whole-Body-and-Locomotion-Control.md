# Whole-Body & Locomotion Control — Deep Dive

> [!abstract] Overview
> Where the fingertip regulates a contact wrench the camera cannot see, the *whole body* regulates the same invisible budget at a different scale: ground-reaction forces, center-of-mass, and angular momentum that an aggressive arm reach or a moving base can spend into a fall. This note unifies that body-scale half of the contact-dynamics axis. **Whole-body control** (Part A) tracks how the field stopped treating locomotion and manipulation as separate controllers and learned single policies that allocate the coupled contact budget — unified/hierarchical controllers ([[2507.06905|ULC]], [[2602.06341|HiWET]]), balance and load-aware adaptation under OOD payloads ([[2606.03297|SplitAdapter]]), behavioral and motion-tracking foundation models ([[2504.11054|Meta-Motivo]], [[2511.07820|SONIC]]), cross-embodiment generalists, fall recovery, formal safety, adaptive compliance, and whole-body distributed tactile sensing. **Locomotion & agile skills** (Part B) push the body to the dynamic edge — terrain-aware perception, generative motion priors, autonomous contact-mode discovery, energy/noise/thermal constraints, and whole-body contact world models. **Humanoid manipulation & skill data** (Part C) attacks the binding constraint that is *not* architecture but *data*: motion retargeting, generative skill synthesis, and standardized learning workflows that manufacture whole-body demonstrations without scaling teleoperation. The recurring lesson rhymes with the fingertip thread exactly — **contact-relevant signals demand dedicated parameters, not a shared average** (factorize load-context from dynamics-context; split commander from tracker; pretrain on motion-tracking as the touch-SSL analog). For the contact-sensing / force-conditioned-manipulation half of the axis see [[11_Contact-Rich-and-Tactile-Control]]; for the RL-*methods* underpinning these controllers see [[03_Imitation-Learning-and-RL#6. RL for Locomotion, Navigation & Whole-Body Control]].

## Evolution Graph

The whole-body thread mirrors the touch thread's arc one scale up: task-specific gait controllers gave way to unified policies that regulate the locomotion–manipulation coupling, then to reward-free pretraining (motion tracking, unsupervised behavior) playing the role touch-SSL played for tactile, and finally to *manufactured* skill data (retargeting, generative priors) attacking the same data-scarcity bottleneck that throttles force-instrumented manipulation.

## Part A — Whole-Body Control

*Regulating the locomotion–manipulation coupling: unified vs hierarchical controllers, balance under disturbance, reward-free pretraining, cross-embodiment generalists, fall recovery, formal safety, adaptive compliance, and whole-body distributed tactile sensing.*

### 1. Whole-Body Control & Coordination

The defining tension of humanoid loco-manipulation is *coupling*: an aggressive arm reach destabilizes the gait, and a moving base drags the end-effector off target. A decoupled "walk, then manipulate" controller cannot resolve this — the two subsystems share a contact budget (ground-reaction forces, center-of-mass, angular momentum) that only a whole-body policy can allocate. This section tracks how the field stopped treating locomotion and manipulation as separate controllers and started learning a single policy that regulates the coupled contact dynamics.

Three axes of division emerge. **Unified and hierarchical controllers** decide whether to fuse everything into one policy or decompose into a high-level commander + low-level tracker. **Balance and load-aware adaptation** confronts the disturbances — external payloads, base oscillation — that the coupling injects. **Behavioral and motion-tracking foundation models** ask whether whole-body control, like vision-language before it, has a scalable pretraining objective that replaces per-task reward engineering.

#### 1.1 Unified & Hierarchical Whole-Body Controllers

The core architectural fork: a single end-to-end policy that jointly outputs locomotion + torso + arm commands, versus a two-level commander/tracker split that decouples global spatial reasoning from dynamic execution.

- **[[2608.10220|Confined-Space Whole-Body Planning]]** — Lifts particle guides to ==rigid-body volumes== via ==differentiable self-collision avoidance== (cone-program gradients through the implicit function theorem), then ==constrained SQP== trajectory optimization + residual RL tracking; real G1 **10/10** tilted-stairs/hole traversal at confinement ratio <1.5, spline baselines **0/10**.
- **[[2608.06375|ω-0]]** — A ==latent predictive World Action Model== generating whole-body action latents (not pixel video) via VLM pretrain → ==SONIC simulation-replay== human-to-humanoid pretrain → real fine-tune, with a ==diffusion Action DiT==; **81.8%** SR / **90.3%** progress over **11** real household loco-manip tasks (vs **43.6%** DiT4DiT).
- **[[2607.18016|POT-VLA]]** — A closed-loop humanoid VLA introducing ==Persistent Object Tokenization (POT)==, a role-indexed 3D object memory in the ==GR00T-N1.7== action-head, plus a ==geometric predicate supervisor== for verification/recovery; **71/80** real G1 loco-manip trials (vs **39/80** baseline), **9/10** under novel objects — mitigates "object-state divergence" in long-horizon tasks.
- **[[2607.06186|Calf-Integrated Quadruped Manipulator]]** — A quadruped loco-manipulation design integrating ==4-DoF calf-mounted manipulators== into a Go2's front calves, with a ==VLM skill-sequencing planner== + ==IK-based manipulation==; **70–74%** grasp SR under **10 mm** depth-noise, sim cabinet-opening, cooperative lifting, and object handover while keeping a four-foot stance.
- **[[2606.26855|Humanoid-DART]]** — A self-supervised ==iterative loco-manipulation skill-acquisition== framework bootstrapping a ==goal-conditioned Diffusion Transformer== with a physics-based RL tracker, re-labeling successful rollouts into an elite archive to grow the skill set from sparse demos; **96.6%** pick-and-place task-space coverage from 4 demos, real G1 push/kick/pick-and-place.
- **[[2606.26425|Nadia-Loco-Manip]]** — A ==robot-local runtime-editable behavior architecture== for fast resilient loco-manipulation, pairing a ==CRDT-synced operator interface== with concurrent action execution, reactive behavior trees, and behavior-time semantic perception (YOLO + FoundationPose); **14 s** door traversal, **11–12** consecutive door operations, behaviors authored in **<2 hr**.
- **[[2606.26201|OmniContact]]** — A hierarchical loco-manipulation framework using ==contact flow== (sparse body targets + binary contact signals) as the intermediate rep, with a rule-based ==CF-Gen== planner for chaining/replanning and a unified RL ==CF-Track== policy; **98.7%** Carry-Box / **82.5%** Push-Suitcase SR (**+40.9%**), 40-min endurance over 1000 skill rounds.
- **[[2606.25706|ASYNC-3PT]]** — An ==Asynchronous Time-indexed Conditioning== upper-body task-space tracker implicitly aligning robot motion with sparse low-frequency references via the full future trajectory + a time index, plus a kinematic ==MPC== completing references and self-guidance RL preventing drift; **99.50%** asynchronous SR, **6.90 cm / 6.83°** error, real G1.
- **[[2606.25591|WOLF-VLA]]** — A whole-body humanoid locomotion framework unifying ==optimal-control== motion synthesis with VLA learning: OC generates a large dynamically-consistent dataset training a GR00T-N1.5-pretrained transformer VLA via ==flow matching==; **99–100%** Walk-Forward SR with low ROM error, robust to visual distractors, far beating ACT (**1.4%**) and π0.5 (**0%**).
- **[[2606.23680|CoorDex]]** — A continuous dexterous loco-manipulation policy coordinating separate ==latent priors== for whole-body (29-DoF) and a ==wrist-stabilized== hand (20-DoF) within a ==residual RL== framework with a shared coordination trunk + separate residual heads; **0.55** WalkGrab (vs **0%** joint-space/body-only), **0.66** OpenFridge, **0.89** WalkPickTurn while moving.
- **[[2606.22174|OpenHLM]]** — An open-source ==empirical recipe== for whole-body humanoid loco-manipulation: a ==joint-based whole-body teleop interface== with real-time retargeting plus a π0.5-initialized VLA and heterogeneous data co-training; **89%** avg over 8 tasks from 40 demos, **87.5%** long-horizon (vs Ψ0 48.8% / GR00T N1.6 57.5%) at <half the demo time.
- **[[2606.19233|Mobile-Pedipulation]]** — A hierarchical control framework for ==non-prehensile object sliding== on a wheeled bipedal robot, with a ==Three Rigid Bodies== reduced-order model (retaining hip roll + multiple wheel-contact modes) extended with object dynamics + Coulomb-friction in ==NMPC==; real Tron1 scoots a 4 kg object **0.228 m** (up to 23 kg in sim).
- **[[2606.18772|HALOMI]]** — A ==humanoid loco-manipulation== system learning from robot-free human demos with bimanual grippers + an active neck, driven by a ==manifold-constrained whole-body controller== (BFM-Zero latent) generating stable motion from sparse head/hand targets + controller-aware reference adaptation; **90/85/80%** real SR, ego-alignment lifting bag **10% → 90%**.
- **[[2606.13222|Proprio-Visual-Self-Other]]** — A self-supervised framework giving humanoids ==self-other distinction== + a ==kinematics-free 3D self-model== from ==proprioceptive-visual correspondence== in multi-agent scenes, via contrastive alignment then self-masks as pseudo-supervision; **99.69%** self-distinction, **88.0%** reaching, **71.4%** collision-aware planning on a real humanoid.
- **[[2606.06493|HANDOFF]]** — A unified whole-body controller on a compact ==10-D command vector== built by ==context-based multi-teacher distillation== from motion-tracking, locomotion, and fall-recovery teachers into a soft ==MoE== head with action-sliced KL distillation; **0.31 m³** workspace at **97.7%** feasibility (vs 0.06 FALCON, 0.26 SONIC), **0.06 m/s** velocity error.
- **[[2606.05687|MPC-Guided]]** — An ==MPC-guided RL== framework for humanoid locomotion + forceful loco-manipulation pairing ==πnMPC== (a parallel-in-horizon batched GPU MPC solver) with a ==centroidal-dynamics MPC reward== using prediction-confidence weighting; robust real locomotion under **10 kg** payloads, pushing a **290 kg** cart (**829%** of body mass) in hardware.
- **[[2605.31343|Terrain-Aware]]** — ==TA-WBC== unifies a foot-centric multi-ring exteroceptive encoder with a ==Foot Contact Plane== spherical end-effector sampling method (decoupling manipulation targets from base fluctuation) + dual-policy distillation preventing catastrophic forgetting; **0.56%** contact rate on complex terrain, real Unitree B2 five-step-stair loco-manipulation.
- **[[2605.19981|CEER]]** — A ==compliant end-effector-root== task-space interface: an ==impedance-aware teacher-student== policy translates sparse EE-root commands into compliant whole-body actions, slotted under an LLM task manager + swappable mid-level planners; **3.3 cm** EE RMSE, **4.5×10³ m/s³** jerk (smoothest), real G1 whiteboard-writing + box-rotation.
- **[[2605.17300|HCLM]]** — A hierarchical ==cooperative loco-manipulation== framework for dual quadrupeds decoupling a high-level ==SE(3)-invariant Diffusion Policy== (relative-pose-only) from a low-level task-centric ==WBC== with kinematic MPC + cooperative admittance regulating internal force; **62.5%** SR under randomized yaws (vs **0%** world-frame), <2 cm EE deviation under 100 N impact.
- **[[2604.12852|PAINT-Transport]]** — A hierarchical cooperative-transport framework decoupling a High-Level intent estimator (inferring partner interaction wrench from arm-joint proprioception, no F/T sensor) from a terrain-robust Low-Level locomotion backbone via teacher-student ==KL distillation==; robust across 2-8 kg payloads, zero-shot decentralized 2-4 robot teams to 28 kg.
- **[[2603.13707|REFINE-DP]]** — A three-stage humanoid loco-manipulation framework ==jointly fine-tuning== a high-level ==Diffusion Policy== planner and a low-level RL whole-body controller via ==DPPO== (each denoising step as a decision step) with dual reward signals; **97–99%** sim SR (vs 50–70% baselines), **95%** from 50 trajectories, **50%** lower EE-orientation error.
- **[[2603.05410|PhysiFlow]]** — A ==bio-inspired multi-brain whole-body VLA==: a Neocortical Brain (semantic ==CVAE==), a Basal Ganglionic Brain (==conditional flow matching== at 50 Hz), and a Cerebellar Brain (==physics-aware tracking==), jointly fine-tuned by backpropagating tracking error into flow; **74.9%** avg SR over 9 tasks (**+9.9pp** over LeVERB), **5.3×** faster than DDPM, real G1.
- **[[2603.07095|ACLM]]** — An ==ADMM-based distributed MPC== for collaborative loco-manipulation, decomposing a centralized OCP into parallel robot-level subproblems coupled only through the shared payload via ==consensus on manipulation wrenches==, executed by a ==wrench-aware WBC==; up to **11.4×** speedup (4 robots), robust to ~**67%** cargo-mass error, rough-terrain transport.
- **[[2603.03733|X-Loco]]** — A ==synergetic policy distillation== framework consolidating three specialists (upright locomotion, fall recovery, whole-body coordination) into one generalist via ==Case-Adaptive Specialist Selection== + ==Specialist Annealing Rollout==; **0.939** locomotion / **0.871** coordination / **1.000** recovery SR, real G1 hybrid-terrain transitions.
- **[[2603.02856|Rhythm]]** — A dual-humanoid interactive WBC framework combining ==Interaction-Aware Motion Retargeting== (**0%** inter-penetration) with ==Interaction-Guided RL== (==MAPPO== + graph-based rewards) and LiDAR-fused sim-to-real sync; **86.7%** real 'Hug' SR on Unitree G1 pairs (vs **26.7%** baseline), resilient to disturbances.
- **[[2602.16705|HERO-Humanoid-EE-Control]]** — A modular ==loco-manipulation== system decoupling scene understanding + grasp planning from a learned ==residual-aware EE tracking policy==, with neural models correcting analytical forward kinematics + base odometry for sim-to-real; **90%** grasp on 10 objects, **73.3%** over 10 novel scenes, **2.44 cm** EE error (5.5× better translation).

#### 1.6 Safe & Robust Whole-Body Control

#### 1.7 Adaptive Compliance & Impedance Control

Where §1.6 enforces *hard* safety bounds, this thread makes the contact itself *soft*: the upper body yields to human and object contact with tunable, commandable compliance rather than tracking rigidly. The mechanism splits two ways — *learned* compliance baked into the tracking policy (interaction-force models, hindsight perturbation, compliant-motion augmentation) versus *retrieved* impedance, where a VLM picks stiffness/damping per scene. #### 1.10 Morphology–Control Co-Design

Most of this section optimizes the *controller* for a fixed body. These papers ask the inverse: jointly evolve the *morphology* and the policy, treating actuator ratios and link geometry as decision variables in a bi-level loop (outer design search, inner RL adaptation). The payoff is unlocking motions that no policy could achieve on the original hardware — fall recovery, chin-ups — by co-evolving body and brain.

- **[[2606.07424|Buoyancy-Co-Design]]** — A ==Gaussian Evolutionary Specialists== framework accelerating RL robot co-design by decoupling design-partitioning from policy learning: K specialists each own a competitively-assigned territory, a density router enabling zero-shot morphology evaluation in a Bayesian-opt loop; **+25.5%** sim traversal, **37%** less time, **3×** clearance on real BALLU.

## Part B — Locomotion & Agile Skills

*Pushing the body to the dynamic edge: terrain-aware perception, generative motion priors, autonomous contact-mode discovery, deployment-realism constraints (energy, noise, thermal), and whole-body contact world models.*

### 2. Legged Locomotion & Agile Skills

If §1 is about *coordinating* the body, §2 is about *pushing it to the dynamic edge* — gaps wider than a stride, table-tennis smashes, under-table contact plans. Agile skills expose a tension the static loco-manipulation controllers can defer: when the maneuver is at the limit of feasibility, the policy must *anticipate* the contact (where to look, where to plant, how to pre-load momentum) rather than react to it. This section tracks how perception, generative motion priors, and autonomous search push humanoid skills past the demonstration-limited frontier.

The papers divide by *how the agile skill is acquired*. **Terrain-aware perception** learns where to attend before committing a foothold. **Dynamic whole-body skills** scale sparse demonstrations into a generative motion library and add anticipatory intent. **Autonomous skill discovery** removes the human demonstrator entirely, searching the contact-mode space for feasible long-horizon plans.

#### 2.4 Constraint-Aware Locomotion — Energy, Noise & Thermal

A locomotion controller that only tracks velocity ignores the physical costs of every footfall: power draw, acoustic impact, and motor heat. This thread folds those costs into the objective as explicit constraints (Lagrangian penalties, control-barrier functions, tunable preference knobs), trading a sliver of tracking accuracy for gaits that are quieter, cooler, and more energy-efficient — the deployment-realism axis of legged control.

- **[[2604.23702|QuietWalk]]** — A physics-informed RL method for low-noise humanoid locomotion in which an ==inverse-dynamics-constrained PINN== estimates per-foot vertical GRFs from proprioception alone, and the frozen GRF predictor is folded *into the RL reward*; **7.17 dBA** mean-noise reduction across 4 surfaces and **4** footwear types — sim-to-real of the *reward signal*.
- **[[2510.09543|IMF-Reward]]** — A ==physics-informed== energy-efficient locomotion reward introducing the ==Impact Mitigation Factor== (actual vs locked-case contact impulse) as a direct RL signal in a unified AMP + handcrafted framework; **18–32%** lower Cost of Transport, **28–35%** lower peak mechanical power, **18–32%** lower cumulative joint torques by exploiting passive impact absorption.
- **[[2603.01631|Thermal-Aware-Locomotion]]** — A ==thermal-aware locomotion RL== framework giving the policy a whole-body ==motor-thermal model== and a ==CBF thermal-constraint reward== penalizing high motor temperatures, with randomized temperatures + eccentric payloads; a Unitree A1 with a 3 kg load runs **27 min** without thermal stop (4× the 7-min baseline) via lower peak/RMS torques.
- **[[2602.06445|ECO]]** — An ==energy-constrained RL== humanoid-walking framework formulating motor power |τ·q̇| as an explicit ==PPO-Lagrangian== inequality constraint plus a mirror-symmetry constraint for natural gait; **~6×** lower energy than MPC and **2.3×** lower than PPO on a real BRUCE humanoid at comparable speed, faster + more stable convergence than other constrained-RL.
- **[[2509.01765|PEGrad]]** — Frames energy efficiency as a subordinate objective by ==projecting== the energy policy-gradient onto the orthogonal subspace of the task-reward gradient with adaptive scaling, hyperparameter-free (no λ tuning); **64%** avg sim energy cut with no task loss, real Go2 walking **19.74%** more current-efficient than a tuned baseline.
- **[[2506.23114|Quiet-Quadruped]]** — A ==quiet-locomotion RL== framework (MUTE) minimizing foot-ground impact velocity via an ==asymmetric actor-critic== PPO with a continuous phase variable + forward contact-prediction and a tunable ==quiet factor β==; **~8 dBA** average noise reduction, β trading noise (77.85→73.18 dBA) against tracking, staying below 70 dBA over a 91.7 m mixed-surface run.
- **[[2503.05035|QuietPaw]]** — A ==noise-constrained safe-RL== quadruped framework (CNCP) conditioning behavior on a desired noise threshold ε via ==Successor Features== factorizing state from condition weights, trained with ==PPO-Lagrangian== per constraint; lowest cost violation + tracking error among conditioned policies, superior Pareto front, continuous real Go2 noise reduction as ε rises.
- **[[2502.10983|Quiet-Walking]]** — A sim-to-real ==quiet-gait RL== framework for a small home robot integrating ==adaptive PD gains==, foot-switch contact feedback, and a two-phase curriculum with foot-contact-velocity/acceleration penalties; **0.123 vs 0.417 m/s** foot-contact velocity vs baseline RL, quieter than commercial aibo controllers, tunable via domain randomization.

#### 2.5 Whole-Body Contact World Models

The body-scale analog of [[11_Contact-Rich-and-Tactile-Control#4.4 Tactile World Models — Forecasting Future Contact State|§4.4]]'s fingertip tactile world models: rather than forecast a *taxel* signal, these models predict future whole-body interaction state — environment contact or carried-object dynamics — in a compact latent, then plan over it. The payoff is the same anticipation argument as §2.2, but the substrate is an explicit world model the policy can roll out, letting a humanoid reason about bracing or balancing an occluded object before committing.

- **[[2606.09286|VAIC]]** — A two-stage vision-guided humanoid object-interaction framework with a recurrent ==Object Adaptation== module inferring dynamic object state from onboard depth + a decoupled velocity/interaction-phase command interface (no dense reference motion); **66.7/91.7/83.3%** SR on box/cart/skateboard interaction, generalizes to real hardware.
- **[[2602.11758|HAIC]]** — An agile humanoid-object-interaction controller with a ==Dynamics-Aware World Model== whose ==object adapter== infers object pose/velocity/acceleration from proprioceptive history, then ==geometric projection== renders predicted dynamic occupancy; **100%** skateboard glide / cart manip on real G1, robust across flat/slope/stair/mixed terrain with occluding objects.
- **[[2510.11682|Ego-Vision]]** — A demonstration-free ==ego-vision latent world model== predicting futures in a compressed latent (not pixels), paired with ==value-guided sampling MPC== using a learned surrogate value for reactive contact planning from egocentric depth; **25 Hz** wall-bracing / object-blocking / arch-traversal on real G1, ~**0.5M** data steps, OOD to unseen objects.
- **[[2505.12934|DiffusiveGRAIN]]** — Two ==U-Net diffusion== predictors forecast sand-avalanche/obstacle motion and the robot's own excavation-induced state change, with ==Effective Action Adjustment== correcting manipulator-vs-robot avalanche mismatch, planned via receding-horizon search; **80%** manipulation / **70%** loco-manipulation SR (vs 60%/20% GRAIN), robust to OOD rocks/slopes.

#### 2.7 Proprioceptive State Estimation & Odometry

#### 2.8 Legged Gait Learning & Control Methods

If §2.1 is about perceiving terrain and §2.2 about dynamic skills, this thread is the core engine underneath both: learning a stable, natural gait for legged robots — bipedal and quadrupedal alike — and the RL/control machinery that produces it. The papers vary the substrate — residual corrections over a kinematic generator, reduced-order-model guidance, symmetry-equivariant or multi-agent-limb decomposition, gait-conditioning, novel TD-learning objectives, and running at the speed limit — but share the goal of a deployable walking/running policy without a perception or manipulation task on top.

- **[[2607.24959|IFT-Contact-Differentiation]]** — An ==Implicit Function Theorem== method differentiates converged contact-solver residuals in MuJoCo MJX (no intermediate-step storage), feeding an ==optimizer-distillation== framework where full-horizon iLQR trains a policy guiding a ==short-horizon residual iLQR== online; **20×** lower memory, **28–98pp** SR gain on a Finger/Franka/A1 suite.

#### 2.9 LLM & VLM-Automated Reward Design

#### 2.10 Physics-Based Character Animation

The simulated-character ancestor of nearly every real-robot controller in §1 and §2.2: physics-based character animation learns to imitate human motion in a physics simulator, supplying the imitation-reward designs (Reference State Initialization, adversarial style priors, contact graphs) and motion-generation priors (diffusion, skill embeddings) that the hardware controllers then inherit. The thread runs from hand-tuned tracking rewards (DeepMimic) through adversarial style priors that drop the tracking objective (AMP, ASE) to unified inpainting + diffusion controllers and the large-scale generative motion priors that feed them — the lineage the §3 retargeting-and-data cluster ultimately serves.

> [!tip] Anticipation Beats Reaction at the Dynamic Edge
> Across this section the winning move is the same: *anticipate the contact before it happens.* [[2606.05880|TAGA]]'s gaze predicts where to look for the next foothold; [[2605.14417|DAJI]]'s 64D latent encodes the future physical transition before the body moves; [[2606.06139|MotionDisco]]'s search reasons over the whole contact-mode sequence rather than the next step. Reactive control suffices for quasi-static loco-manipulation (§1), but at the dynamic limit the latency of "sense → decide → act" is fatal — the maneuver is over before reaction completes. This is the locomotion echo of [[11_Contact-Rich-and-Tactile-Control#5.3 Deployment & Failure Recovery|§5.3]]'s millisecond-contact problem: when contact transitions outrun the reasoning loop, you must predict, not react. ## Part C — Humanoid Manipulation & Skill Data

*Manufacturing whole-body skill data without scaling teleoperation: motion retargeting into the feasible manifold, generative trajectory synthesis from priors, and standardized learning workflows that make manufactured data deployable.*

### 3. Humanoid Manipulation, Retargeting & Skill Data

The binding constraint on whole-body control is not architecture — it is *data*. Humanoid loco-manipulation demonstrations are extraordinarily expensive: teleoperating a high-DoF balancing robot is slow, dangerous, and embodiment-specific. This section tracks the three ways the field manufactures whole-body skill data without scaling teleoperation: retarget human motion into the robot's feasible manifold, generate physically-grounded trajectories synthetically, and standardize the learning workflow so policies actually transfer.

The axes are *where the data comes from*. **Motion retargeting** translates human (or human-demonstration) motion to the humanoid while respecting kinematics and dynamics. **Generative skill data** synthesizes whole-body trajectories from priors — diffusion, video-foundation-models, or composable primitives. **Learning workflows** wrap the whole loop so the manufactured data yields deployable controllers.

#### 3.1 Motion Retargeting & Human-to-Humanoid Transfer

The upstream data axis: cleanly map human motion onto the humanoid's feasible manifold. Naive optimization propagates jitter and self-collisions; these papers learn or constrain the mapping to produce policy-ready references.

- **[[2606.32009|Human-as-Humanoid]]** — A near-real-time pipeline converting ego-exo human video into 60-DoF action labels via ==staged IK retargeting== on the **PrimeU** human-aligned platform, training the ==PhysDex== VLA with ==Dual-Space Hierarchical Kinematic Constraint== supervision; **4.8–7.2×** demo throughput over teleop, zero-shot manipulation from **1,500 hr** human-only data.

- **[[2502.13013|HOMIE]]** — A semi-autonomous teleoperation system pairing a low-cost 3D-printed ==isomorphic exoskeleton cockpit== (direct joint-angle mapping, no IK) with an RL whole-body policy adding an ==upper-body pose curriculum + height-tracking reward== for balance under arm motion + squatting; 263/293 Hz arm/hand at ~$500, **73.3–80%** autonomous loco-manip from collected demos.

#### 3.5 Evaluation, Benchmarks & Human-Likeness Metrics

Manufactured data and learned controllers need verdicts: how human-like is the motion, how hard is a clip to imitate, how does a tracker rank across embodiments? This thread builds the measurement layer — egocentric WBC benchmarks, kinematics-only human-likeness scores, gait-divergence and actuation envelopes, and difficulty metrics that turn ad-hoc demos into reproducible, comparable evaluation.

- **[[2608.13555|HumanTracker]]** — A **153-hr** mocap benchmark across four diagnostic motion families with a standardized MuJoCo protocol, plus ==HumanScore==, a masked bidirectional Transformer reward model via ==Bradley-Terry== preference learning on **12K** annotated pairs; **0.9083** human Align Rate vs **0.8049** for MPJPE, needing **1-5 s** context to capture drift/recovery.
- **[[2606.17833|HumanoidArena]]** — An Isaac-Lab ==egocentric hierarchical WBC benchmark== decoupling perception-conditioned high-level policies from low-level ==General Motion Trackers==, with seven leg-critical HOI/HSI tasks + perturbation + cross-GMT protocols; Flow-Matching best (**36.1%** HOI / **58.8%** HSI), cross-GMT transfer dropping ~**40%** (often 0% retention).
- **[[2603.06181|Motion-Turing-Test]]** — A ==Motion Turing Test== evaluating humanoid human-likeness from kinematics alone (SMPL-X, no appearance bias), the **HHMotion** dataset (1,000 clips, 25-annotator Likert), and ==PTR-Net==, a pose-temporal regression baseline; **0.5813** MAE / **0.6841** Spearman ρ aligning with human judgment, real-robot motions scoring below human.
- **[[2602.21666|GDAF]]** — A ==Gait Divergence Analysis Framework== quantifying human-vs-humanoid biomechanical divergence across kinematics, kinetics, energetics over 28 walking speeds on a G1, with waveform-similarity, bilateral-symmetry + work-divergence metrics; robot shows higher asymmetry + knee-dominant power, GDAF cost falling 0.5106 → 0.2941 with speed.
- **[[2512.07248|MDS]]** — A ==Motion Difficulty Score== defining imitation difficulty from rigid-body dynamics (torque variation under pose perturbation) via spectral/variance/segment diversity, plus ==MD-AMASS== (30k clips with MDS); Spearman ρ up to **0.8203** with imitation error, enabling difficulty-aware benchmarking + curriculum learning + corruption detection.
- **[[2511.17925|Switch-JustDance]]** — A ==hardware-in-the-loop WBC benchmark== using a Nintendo Switch Just Dance game as a low-cost objective scorer, with GVHMR pose capture + GMR retargeting; the game score inversely correlates with PA-MPJPE (**r −0.76 to −0.42**, ICC 0.735), the best controller reaching ~1/3 of the human baseline, exposing jerk/agility gaps.
- **[[2511.06796|HLAS]]** — A ==Human-Level Actuation Score== framework quantifying robot actuation against human capability over six physically-motivated factors + ==Human-Equivalence Envelopes== enforcing simultaneous human torque + power within task-relevant (q, ω) bands, with reproducible protocols; diagnoses e.g. knee/hip actuation insufficient for sustained stair climbing.
- **[[2308.14636|Linear-Impactor]]** — A standardized ==disturbance-rejection testing== method proposing a pneumatic ==linear impactor== for repeatable external-force tests on legged robots, characterizing disturbances by ==impact momentum== not force; **<0.1 m/s** repeatability, a Digit Template-Model controller withstanding **26.376 kg·m/s** (~boxer's punch), exposing non-monotonic failure.
- **[[2305.14654|Barkour]]** — A standardized ==dog-agility-inspired obstacle-course benchmark== for quadruped agility (weave poles, A-frame, broad jumps; time-based scoring), with specialist + generalist (==Locomotion-Transformer==) PPO baselines + domain randomization; best **0.91** agility score (~20 s vs dogs' ~9 s), zero-shot sim-to-real, exposing the robot-vs-animal agility gap.

## Part D — Open Problems

*Where whole-body and locomotion control still fail.*

### 4. Open Problems & Failure Modes

Despite the architectural convergence on coupled whole-body policies (§1) and the data-manufacturing breakthroughs (§3), humanoid loco-manipulation has unresolved bottlenecks. The failures split cleanly into three categories: *data & generalization* (the corpora and cross-embodiment infrastructure that doesn't yet exist), *coupling & control* (regulating the shared contact budget under disturbance and at the dynamic edge), and *deployment & safety* (fall recovery, formal guarantees, and the sim-to-real gap for balance).

#### 4.1 Data & Cross-Embodiment Generalization

The dominant root cause mirrors the fingertip cluster's exactly: no OXE-scale corpus for whole-body loco-manipulation, and per-embodiment retraining that morphology randomization only partially escapes.

- **==No "OXE for humanoid loco-manipulation"==** — Teleoperating a balancing high-DoF humanoid is the most expensive data-collection regime in robotics, so there is no cross-embodiment corpus at visual-dataset scale. The 2026 escape hatch is to *manufacture* the data — retarget it ([[2603.22201|NMR]]), generate it from video priors ([[2606.05160|GRAIL]], [[2604.27711|ExoActor]]), or plan it from a seed set ([[2605.27724|HumanoidMimicGen]]) — but synthetic-to-real fidelity is still task-dependent.
- **==Cross-embodiment transfer caps below specialist==** — Morphology randomization ([[2602.05791|XHugWBC]], [[2509.02815|URMAv2]]) and embodiment scaling laws ([[2505.05753|Embodiment-Scaling-Laws]]) yield zero-shot generalists, but they plateau around **~85%** of specialist performance and need a few-shot adapter to close the gap. A truly embodiment-agnostic controller that matches per-robot specialists is unbuilt.
- **==Synthetic skill-data fidelity is uneven==** — [[2606.05160|GRAIL]]'s 4D HOI transfers at **90%** for stair-climbing, but generative-prior quality varies sharply by task; manufactured trajectories that are physically plausible in isolation can still violate the robot's true contact dynamics, and no standard validates this before training.

#### 4.2 Coupling & Control at the Dynamic Edge

The locomotion–manipulation coupling is the substrate, not a nuisance — and current controllers regulate it only within the regimes they were trained on. Disturbance, anticipation latency, and factorization choices remain open.

- **==Anticipation latency at the dynamic limit==** — Reactive control suffices for quasi-static loco-manipulation (§1), but at the dynamic edge ([[2604.01158|SMASH]], [[2606.05880|TAGA]], [[2605.14417|DAJI]]) the "sense → decide → act" loop is fatal: the maneuver finishes before reaction completes. Anticipatory latents and contact world models ([[2602.11758|HAIC]], [[2510.11682|Ego-Vision]]) help, but a general predict-don't-react substrate is still per-skill.
- **==Factorize vs unify the contact context==** — [[2606.03297|SplitAdapter]] shows factorizing load-context from dynamics-context beats a unified latent, echoing force-as-first-class-modality, but *which* signals deserve dedicated parameters (and where in the hierarchy) is decided case-by-case. No principled account of when to split a whole-body contact representation exists.
- **==Disturbance regimes stay narrow==** — Load- and force-adaptive controllers ([[2510.26280|Thor]], [[2603.08961|FAME]], [[2511.20275|HAFO]]) survive impressive payloads, but each is tuned to a disturbance envelope; transferring force-adaptive behavior across disturbance types (payload vs external pull vs terrain) without retraining is unsolved.

#### 4.3 Deployment & Safety

Whole-body deployment demands fall safety, formal guarantees, and a sim-to-real gap dominated by balance — sharper than vision-only policies face because a single misstep is catastrophic.

- **==Fall recovery generalization is narrow==** — Unified fall-safety policies ([[2602.16511|VIGOR]], [[2511.07407|Fall-Safety-Policy]], [[2511.18509|SafeFall]]) cover prevention/mitigation/recovery, but their coverage is bounded by the postures and terrains in training; open-set fall scenarios (novel obstacles, multi-contact entanglement) still defeat them.
- **==Formal safety vs learned performance==** — Safety filters ([[2605.25546|ISSf-CBF-WBC]], [[2505.11494|SHIELD-Humanoid]], [[2603.22703|Safe-Stoppability-Monitor]]) certify collision/stoppability bounds, but the guarantees rest on a bounded reduced-order/full-order mismatch that real dynamics can exceed; reconciling provable safety with the learned policy's full performance is an active tension.
- **==Sim-to-real gap is balance-dominated==** — Long-horizon balance instabilities are *masked* by short-episode sparse-reward benchmarks ([[2408.00342|MuJoCo-MPC-HumanoidBench]]) and surface only over multi-second episodes; the domain-randomization that closes the visual gap does not directly close the dynamics gap for balance, where the cost of a single failure is a fall.

**Whole-Body & Locomotion Failure Modes — Decision Matrix**

| Problem | Remediation Path |
|---|---|
| No OXE-scale humanoid loco-manip corpus | Manufacture data — [[2606.05160\|GRAIL]] (synthetic 4D HOI), [[2605.27724\|HumanoidMimicGen]] (seed-demo augmentation) |
| Cross-embodiment transfer caps below specialist | Morphology randomization + few-shot adapter — [[2602.05791\|XHugWBC]], [[2505.05753\|Embodiment-Scaling-Laws]] |

> [!tip] Whole-Body Bottlenecks Are Data-Scale + Coupling-Regulation
> Two roots explain most of the failures above. **(1) Data scale** — there is no humanoid-loco-manip analog of [[2310.08864|OXE]], because teleoperating a balancing high-DoF robot is the costliest data regime in robotics; the field's answer is to *manufacture* the data (§3), but synthetic-to-real fidelity and cross-embodiment transfer both plateau below specialist. **(2) Coupling regulation** — the shared contact budget (ground-reaction force, CoM, angular momentum) is the substrate, and current controllers regulate it only within their trained disturbance envelopes and reaction-latency budgets. Both roots rhyme with the fingertip cluster's data-scale + integration-scale bottlenecks — see [[11_Contact-Rich-and-Tactile-Control#5.
