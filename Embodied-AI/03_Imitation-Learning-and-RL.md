# Imitation Learning & RL for Robotics — Deep Dive

> [!abstract] Overview
> The real axis of robot policy learning is not imitation-learning versus reinforcement-learning — it is *where the learning signal comes from and how expensive it is*. **Demonstrations** are cheap but capped by the demonstrator's skill ([[2108.03298|Robomimic]], [[2202.02005|BC-Z]], [[2606.04269|Instant-Fold]]); **learned rewards** are scalable but mis-specifiable ([[2603.02115|Robometer]], [[2603.28730|SOLE-R1]], [[2511.14565|Masked-IRL]]); **environment interaction** is unbounded but unsafe ([[2505.22642|FastTD3]], [[2510.22512|TRL]], [[2010.15920|Recovery-RL]]). Modern recipes sit on the IL→RL bridge — clone a base policy, then refine it past the demo ceiling with a learned reward and a thin layer of interaction ([[2407.16677|ResiP]], [[2311.03351|Uni-O4]], [[2604.10962|ScoRe-Flow]]). This deep dive maps that signal-cost spectrum: foundations of learning from demonstrations (Part A), the methods that turn reward signals into optimized policies (Part B), and the capability frontier where these recipes meet locomotion, navigation, and whole-body control (Part C).

## Part A — Foundations: Learning from Demonstrations

*Demonstrations are the cheapest learning signal, but they cap policy quality at the demonstrator's skill. Part A covers the canonical empirical studies, the policy representations that extract more from each demo, and the recipes that push past the demo ceiling.*

### 1. Behavior Cloning & Imitation Learning Foundations

Behavior cloning is the default first move in robot learning: collect demonstrations, fit a supervised map from observations to actions, deploy. It is attractive because it sidesteps reward design and unsafe exploration entirely — but it inherits three structural weaknesses. Demonstrations are *non-Markovian* (human operators act on history the observation does not capture), they are *capped* by the demonstrator's own skill, and the *training objective* (action-prediction loss) does not correlate with the *evaluation objective* (task success). Every paper here is a response to one of those three weaknesses.

The first weakness drove the move from feedforward BC to history-dependent and long-context policy representations. The second drove the IL→RL bridge — keep the demonstration as a coarse prior, then refine it with interaction. The third remains the quiet crisis of the field: offline policy selection is unsolved, because the loss you minimize is not the metric you ship on. The sections below trace each response, ordered from the canonical baselines through the representations that extract more signal per demo to the refinement recipes that break the ceiling.

#### 1.1 Canonical BC & Empirical Studies

Reference IL/BC baselines, systematic empirical studies of what matters for demonstration learning, and single-task IL applications that don't yet generalize.

- **[[2112.01511|VINN]]** — Decouples visual representation learning from control: a ==ResNet-50== fine-tuned by ==BYOL== self-supervision on unlabeled robot experience, then ==non-parametric== ==k-NN locally-weighted regression== over those embeddings instead of a learned action head; **80%** real door-opening vs **0%** for end-to-end BC, holding **70%** under occlusion.
- **[[1905.11979|Causal Confusion]]** — Names the causal-misidentification failure of behavior cloning — more observation access can *hurt* — and fixes it with a ==graph-parameterized policy== spanning ==causal graph== hypotheses disambiguated by ==targeted interventions== over ==β-VAE== disentangled latents; recovers the true graph on MountainCar/Hopper with far fewer queries than DAgger or GAIL.
- **[[2607.04591|S2C]]** — A ==Simple-to-Complex== structured demonstration-collection strategy for VLA training via ==task decomposition== + ==progressive complexity scheduling==; lifts block sorting **0%→80.0%** and towel folding **0%→25.0%** over direct collection at comparable demo budgets.
- **[[2606.31106|AD Internal Prediction Probing]]** — Trains ==BC== and ==RL== driving policies on Waymo Open Motion Data, applying ==linear probing== + ==causal interventions== on internal surrounding-vehicle predictions; predictions stay imprecise (F1 **<10%**) and activate late, yet causal correction reliably shifts ego-planning in **36-40/58-64** cases.
- **[[2606.29898|CI-MSE]]** — **Critical Interval MSE** restricts offline validation error to VLM-identified ==critical intervals==, folds in inference-time ==Temporal Ensembling==/==Real-Time Action Chunking==, and aligns via ==Dynamic Time Warping==; Spearman ρ=**-0.87** with rollout success across 27 policies vs raw MSE's **-0.61**, holding under real-world and OOD conditions.
- **[[2606.29684|ES-Optimized Steering CNN]]** — Applies an ==(N+M) Evolution Strategy== with the ==1/5th success rule== to auto-tune CNN/DNN hyperparameters for PilotNet-style autonomous steering from just 2,957 images; the Optimized model cuts MAE **0.63→0.41°**, a Quarter-Size variant reaches **85%** VRAM reduction at **0.88°** MAE, exposing a pipeline-rate bottleneck at higher speeds.
- **[[2606.15587|Perfect-Demo-Makes]]** — Shows fluent human demos create a ==duration-sensitivity mismatch== starving critical alignment phases of signal, remedied by ==deliberate demonstrations== + ==critical-segment resampling== or by **STAIR**, a compact ==spatio-temporal dynamic feature== predicted by the VLM at inference; fluent-only success **52.2%→70.0%** combining both fixes.
- **[[2606.17317|CT-Warm-Start]]** — A transformer ==conditional behavior cloning== warm-starting method that generates initial guesses for the dominant Stage 2 of a two-stage ==Sequential Convex Programming== pipeline for space manipulators approaching tumbling targets; cuts SCP iterations **~28%** and Stage-2 runtime **~23%** at **≥99.3%** success, flow-matching decoders most sample-efficient.
- **[[2606.00383|BC-of-MPC]]** — A ==behavior cloning== study training MLPs/GRUs to mimic a hierarchical ==IK+MPC== expert from MuJoCo demos (state, target, MPC torques), finding deep MLPs beat RNNs since the immediate state suffices; **~1.1 ms**/step (**3×** faster than MPC), **25%** less CPU, **84.98%** success at 5 cm — but a 2.9 cm error gap shows BC lacks MPC's gradient correction.
- **[[2511.18617|AutoFocus-IL]]** — Uses a ==VLM== to auto-extract task-relevant objects into dense ==temporal multi-peak saliency maps==, regularizing ==behavior cloning== feature activations without human annotation to fight causal confusion; **+104%** CARLA unseen-route generalization (**12.20→30.17%**) and higher real-robot manipulation success under confounders.
- **[[2510.18085|R2BC]]** — ==Round-Robin Behavior Cloning== lets one human sequentially teleoperate single agents while teammates run their current policy, sidestepping impractical synchronized ==joint-action demonstrations==; matches/exceeds oracle joint-BC in sim and beats centralized BC **3.25–5.9×** on real Navigation/Block-Pusher robots.
- **[[2510.12403|Robot Learning Tutorial]]** — A tutorial bridging classical robotics to ==Reinforcement Learning==, ==Imitation Learning==, and ==Vision-Language-Action== policies (HIL-SERL, ACT, Diffusion Policy, flow matching, π0, SmolVLA) via the open-source **`lerobot`** library, with runnable end-to-end examples closing the accessibility gap.
- **[[2509.12026|RDM]]** — A method that recasts imitation as ==return distribution matching== — minimizing the Wasserstein distance between learner and expert *return* distributions; ==Risk-Sensitive BC== (unknown transitions) and ==RS-KT== (linear program, known transitions) beat risk-neutral BC, with sample complexity independent of state/action size.
- **[[2506.13915|Time-Optimal Quadrotor Trajectory Learning]]** — An ==LSTM encoder-decoder== ==imitation learning== surrogate for the model-based ==TOPPQuad== planner, predicting only squared-speed and yaw-cosine to reconstruct the trajectory, with ==noise-injection augmentation== checked by ==Backward Reachable Tubes==; **0.096 s** vs **10.656 s**, **0.074 m** deviation, **4.0%** failure.
- **[[2505.05787|Diffusion-Policy-Memorization]]** — A diagnostic study showing ==diffusion policies== reproduce memorized training action sequences even under OOD visual inputs; an explicit ==Action Lookup Table== (contrastive-embedding retrieval) matches **100%** in-distribution grasping at **~300×** faster inference and **100×** smaller memory — memorization, not generalization, drives wins.
- **[[2503.10334|Enhanced-View-Planning-Robotic]]** — An ==imitation learning== view-planning method for robotic harvesting that trains the ==Action Chunking with Transformer (ACT)== algorithm on expert demos to predict 6-DoF camera actions that actively de-occlude fruit; **86.7%** sim / **66.7%** real, ideal viewpoint in **3.1 s** vs RVP's **53.7 s** — action chunks beat single-step BC.
- **[[2502.12330|X-IL]]** — A modular open-source framework for ==exploring the imitation-learning design space== (interchangeable encoders, ==Mamba/xLSTM/Transformer== backbones, BC/diffusion/flow heads) over LIBERO + RoboCasa; xLSTM hits **92.3%** LIBERO and **53.6%** RoboCasa vs BC-Transformer's **40.0%** — the backbone and fusion, not the policy class, decide success.
- **[[2502.08452|Push-Group-Grasp-Diffusion]]** — A ==Diffusion Policy== imitation framework for multi-object delivery fusing pushing, grouping, and simultaneous grasping, predicting future pose + gripper actions from past visual + state observations; **85%** completion for 4 objects / **70%** for 6, **1.77** objects/grasp vs a rule-based **1.44**, fine-tuning to unseen shapes from 50 demos.
- **[[2405.05439|BC-Policy-Eval]]** — A statistical evaluation framework for ==behavior-cloning generalization== giving tight worst-case bounds (==UMA== binary, ==DKW== continuous) and computing the minimum real rollouts for a target confidence via ==Maximum Expected Shortage==; on hardware it separated RT-2 vs VC-1 at **95%** confidence — rigorous OOD evaluation over anecdotal averages.
- **[[2409.05865|Robot-Utility-Models]]** — A zero-shot generalist-policy framework combining diverse handheld ==Stick-v2== data collection, multi-modal ==VQ-BeT + Diffusion Policy==, and ==mLLM self-verification== with autonomous retries; **90%** average across 25 novel real environments (retries add **+15.6%**), zero-shot across arms and cameras — data diversity beats architecture.
- **[[2307.03659|Factor World]]** — Decomposes the IL generalization gap into 11 controllable environment factors on a new benchmark plus real-robot RT-1 trials; camera pose (**45.8%**) and table texture (**52.8%**) are hardest vs **91.7%** in-distribution, and ==random-crop==/==photometric== augmentation helps more than CLIP/R3M pretraining.
- **[[2303.00905|MOO]]** — Grounds a frozen ==OWL-ViT== VLM's object detection into a ==single-pixel mask== fed alongside images to an ==RT-1== behavior-cloned policy, letting the policy generalize to object categories never seen first-hand; **≈50%** higher success on novel objects and more robust to novel scenes/distractors.

#### 1.2 Structured & Long-Context Policy Representations

The feedforward observation→action map is the weak form of BC. These papers extract more signal per demonstration by structuring the policy — long action histories, geometric SE(3) intermediates — to handle the non-Markovian nature of human demos.

- **[[2608.05416|SpectraLDS]]** — A ==Nonlinear-to-LDS Distillation== pipeline: convex ==Observation Spectral Filtering== learns an implicit predictor, then ==SpectraLDS== distills it into an explicit recurrent ==Linear Dynamical System== at **<1.5%** error; on MuJoCo BC of a SAC expert it beats Diagonal LDS on **4/6** envs, **+1,700** reward points on Walker2d.
- **[[2607.27372|XM]]** — ==Explorative Modeling== factors the *training loop*, not the generation procedure: the model explores **K** candidate generations and trains only on the best match, unlocking ==generative expressivity== as a third pretraining axis; matches or beats Diffusion Policy on Robomimic BC with **1** network pass vs **100**.
- **[[2607.12992|ChunkFlow]]** — Fixes chunk-interface ==boundary jitter== in VLA action-chunking via training-time ==continuity losses== + a ==seam boundary loss==, parameter-free ==overlap-blending== inference, and a structure-preserving ==AWAC== fine-tuning stage; **93.4%** LIBERO-Long with the lowest action-variation and seam discrepancy on CALVIN at **4.43 ms** latency.
- **[[2607.07129|Object-Centric Neural Field LfD]]** — Learns ==object-centric neural fields== as compact per-object latents from raw images, paired with a temporal ==Mixture-of-Experts== that conditions trajectory segments on object latents for ==sequential compositionality==; **0.97** Cube Stacking success and **10/10** real box-in-drawer from just **4** demonstrations.
- **[[2607.06978|SPECTRA]]** — Represents skills as finite-horizon ==Fourier coefficients== (Spectral Movement Primitives) separating motion shape from a ==phase law==, with frame-aware ==GMM/GMR== context-conditioning and shape-preserving ==phase-only dynamic regulation==; converts a **1.452x**-over-limit motion to **0.980x** admissible with zero end-effector-path distortion.
- **[[2607.01051|AutoSpeed]]** — Learns annotation-free stage-adaptive motion speed end-to-end via ==cost-aware multi-target selective optimization==, using ==DCT== frequency-domain re-timing and ==Nonlinear Temporal Aggregation== to combine variable-speed action chunks; up to **1.7x** faster execution (**272->160** steps) with equal-or-better success on Meta-World/LIBERO-10.
- **[[2607.00534|STT-LfD]]** — Learns ==Spatiotemporal Tubes== capturing a demo's time-varying precision via ==Heteroscedastic Gaussian Processes==, paired with a ==closed-form, model-free feedback controller== for unknown Euler-Lagrange systems guaranteeing tube-bounded execution; **0.021 ms**/step (vs KMP+LQR's **15.94 ms**), **4.24 mm** error under **1 kg** load on a 7-DOF arm.
- **[[2606.30457|Behavior Prompting Policy]]** — Conditions a diffusion action-decoder on a full sensorimotor demonstration ("behavior prompt") via a ==Transformer prompt encoder== with cross-attention, paired with the **iPhUMI** ARKit handheld-teleop interface for wireless test-time prompting; **80.7%** error reduction over Goal-Image on unseen DrawAnything tasks.
- **[[2606.30101|SIR]]** — Integrates ==Scene Graphs== into ==Goal-Conditioned Imitation Learning== via a learnable ==sparsification module== pruning to minimal task-relevant sub-graphs, processed by a ==GNN== feeding diffusion/BC heads; **19.5%** RoboCasa success (vs **14.81%** image baselines) with near-zero distractor degradation, revealing dataset biases via the learned sub-graphs.
- **[[2606.29570|CSP]]** — The **Causal Spectral Policy** represents actions via ==Discrete Cosine Transform== into ==low-frequency== (coarse motion) and ==high-frequency== (fine control) bands, generated by a ==two-stage causal coarse-to-fine== predictor with a ==stop-gradient== decoupling; **91.9%** LIBERO-90, robust to larger action chunks, **90%** real thin-block stacking under noisy demos.
- **[[2605.15836|GAP]]** — An action-free ==Geometric Anchor Pre-training== warm-up regularizing a VFM's spatial adapter via ==centroid alignment==, ==spread==, and ==keypoint-diversity== losses on a simulated proxy task, fixing low-data "bottleneck collapse"; **62%** vs AFA's **46%** on Can-15-demos, generalizes across R3M/DINOv2/VC-1 backbones.
- **[[2505.18474|Canonical-Policy]]** — An ==SE(3)-equivariant imitation learning== method mapping 3D point clouds, states, and actions into a unique ==canonical frame== via de-centering + an ==SO(3)-equivariant rotation estimator==, compatible with diffusion policies; **+13.4%** over DP3 across 12 sim tasks, robust to unseen objects/layouts/viewpoints, non-zero success from **50** real demos.
- **[[2505.09561|PTP]]** — A ==Past-Token Prediction== regularizer that trains a policy to jointly predict past *and* future action tokens, fixing diffusion policies' under-use of action history; **+50%** average on long-context sim tasks, **70%** real success (**>4×** over no-history) and **>5×** training speedup — temporal structure the demo contains but BC discards.
- **[[2605.25829|OASIS]]** — A policy that aligns its intermediate representation with the action space by predicting a camera-frame ==SE(3) end-effector trajectory== via a 3D-aware encoder + ==Transformer== trajectory predictor; **97.6%** LIBERO and **89.2%** real (surpassing π0.5 by **7.6%**), matching baselines with **60%** fewer demos.
- **[[2510.02268|Know-Your-Camera]]** — A view-invariant ==imitation learning== method that conditions policies on explicit ==camera geometry== via per-pixel Plücker embeddings (early/late fusion), tested on randomized-background tasks that strip static cues; up to **+34.8%** success across IL models with fewer training views, validated on a real UR5.

- **[[2606.04269|Instant-Fold]]** — An in-context ==imitation learning== framework for deformable folding via a ==flow-matching Transformer== conditioned on auto-extracted keyframes from a *single* demo, with ==temporal contrastive pretraining== + 3D-ALiBi attention; **95.8%** context accuracy, **58.3%** held-out folding modes, **60.9%** zero-shot sim-to-real on 8 unseen garments.

#### 1.3 Imitation Beyond the Demo Ceiling

Refine a human-demonstration-cloned BC policy past the demonstrator's own skill via RL, on-policy interaction, or corrective feedback — excludes privileged-teacher/RL-teacher distillation pipelines that never clone a human demo.

- **[[2608.09138|SpeedTuning]]** — Bolts a lightweight ==speed policy== (==Rainbow DQN==) onto a frozen ==ACT== task policy, predicting a discrete per-chunk speed multiplier applied via ==linear temporal interpolation== to exceed teleop-capped demo pace; **>2.4x** speed-up across six ALOHA tasks, slowing only through contact-critical phases like pouring, holding success.

- **[[2607.01651|AutoSERL]]** — Replaces continuous human-in-the-loop supervision with three ==rule-based automated intervention== mechanisms (sliding-window, safety-recovery, termination) derived from a *single* demonstration for real-world RL; **100%** success (50/50) on six contact-intensive tasks, matching or beating HIL-SERL's training time.
- **[[2607.00033|CHORD (Contact Wrench Guidance)]]** — Represents human-demo contact as an embodiment-agnostic ==object-centric contact wrench space==, guiding ==RL== via a composite ==CWS reward== plus task/imitation rewards over a **4,739**-task bimanual dexterous benchmark; **82.12%** average success across **1,831** eval tasks, matching/beating ManipTrans and DexMachina.
- **[[2606.16888|LOPAL]]** — A ==local performance-aware active learning== method for imperfect demonstrations encoding a ==GMM with per-segment quality + phase== and synthesizing trajectories via ==Gaussian Mixture Regression== conditioned above-mean to combine the best segments and guide targeted corrections; up to **+27.31%** over GMR on real pipe inspection with fewer demos and lower workload.
- **[[2606.03268|EaDex]]** — A cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation method from single-RGB-D demos via ==MANO fitting + motion retargeting== plus hybrid ==RL== over task/imitation/BC/contact rewards; a ==contact-reward demo-annealing== mechanism decays reliance on noisy data, lifting success **23.5→36.5%** (**+55.3%** relative) across 9 tasks.
- **[[2606.03177|ConTrack]]** — Treats object-pose tracking as a hard constraint via ==Lagrangian relaxation== with an ==online dual controller== mixing task/style advantage, plus an ==adaptive mid-trajectory reset library== of policy-reachable states for stable long-horizon training; **0.899** mean progress vs ManipTrans's **0.743**, SPIDER's **0.444**, DexMachina's **0.246**.

- **[[2403.12910|YAY-Robot]]** — A hierarchical framework improving on-the-fly from ==language corrections==: verbal commands override a high-level policy feeding a ==language-conditioned BC== low-level policy, then ==DAgger-style== fine-tuning retrains on collected corrections; **+25–50%** during intervention and **+15–45%** autonomous gain across bimanual long-horizon tasks.
- **[[2403.06420|RLingua]]** — GPT-4-generated ==rule-based controller== guides ==DAgger==-style rollouts with exponentially decaying sampling, regularizing **TD3**'s actor via an imitation term; **0.8** success on PandaReach at **<25%** of TD3's steps, beats standard TD3 on **10/12** sparse-reward RLBench tasks it fails outright, real-Panda transfer.
- **[[2311.12996|RLIF]]** — Recasts interactive IL as RL by treating a ==human intervention== itself as an implicit **-1** reward (0 otherwise), trained via off-policy ==RLPD/SAC==; up to **5x** over DAgger/HG-DAgger with suboptimal experts, **100%** real Franka peg-insertion in 6 rounds, tighter suboptimality bound than DAgger.
- **[[2008.03285|Residual Hand Pose RL]]** — A ==residual RL== agent corrects noisy Inverse-Kinematics mappings of estimated hand poses for physics-based VR manipulation, trained with a hybrid ==GAIL== + task + 3D-pose-fidelity reward and a synthetic BigHand2.2M data-generation pipeline; **83%** door-opening success vs IK's 27.81%, converging **~2×** faster than RL-only.
- **[[2206.14349|Fleet-DAgger]]** — Formalizes ==Interactive Fleet Learning== (N robots, M humans) with **Return on Human Effort** as the efficiency metric and the **C.U.R.** (Constraint-Uncertainty-Risk) priority function for supervisor-to-robot allocation; up to **8.8×** higher ROHE than baselines at 100-robot/10-human scale and on 4 real ABB YuMi arms.
- **[[2203.15390|ReIL]]** — A budget-aware intervention IL+RL framework combining ==TD3== with a weighted ==Behavior-Cloning objective== and an intervention-penalizing reward (fixing value-overestimation), plus ==MimeticSNAIL==, a memory-aware policy; **20/20** CartPole success at **270.7** steps (vs IARL **16/20**), **0.247** vs HG-DAgger's **0.307** action error, no real-navigation degradation.
- **[[2111.07447|Self-Replay]]** — Records ==bottleneck poses== from one human demo per task stage, then autonomously collects training data via ==Random== or ==Active Self-Replay== to train stage-recognition/reaching/classification CNNs with zero further human effort; **70-80%** success on multi-stage tasks, **80%** at 4 stages, robust to distractors.
- **[[2110.03134|MIND MELD]]** — Meta-learns a personalized ==demonstrator embedding== via a ==Bidirectional LSTM== + ==mutual-information== subnetwork to correct suboptimal, style-varying human corrective feedback in robot-centric LfD; lifts correction quality **63%** on hold-out calibration, embeddings correlating with over/under-correction tendencies (p<**0.001**).
- **[[2109.08273|ThriftyDAgger]]** — A robot-gated intervention policy combining ==state novelty== (policy-ensemble variance) with a learned ==risk metric== (Q-based failure probability) and ==budget-aware threshold tuning== from a user-specified switching rate; **73%** autonomous peg-insertion (**49%** either ablated), **9.2** vs **3.0–5.1** fleet throughput over prior robot-gated methods.
- **[[1810.02890|HG-DAgger]]** — Gives the human expert ==uninterrupted control== during DAgger interventions (no stochastic switching) and *learns* a data-driven ==doubt threshold== from intervention moments rather than hand-tuning it; **zero** collisions/road-departures on a real test vehicle vs 1/1 for DAgger and 1/6 for BC.
- **[[1807.08364|EnsembleDAgger]]** — Extends DAgger with a ==Bayesian doubt rule==: an ensemble approximating a Gaussian Process triggers expert intervention when action ==discrepancy== or ==epistemic-uncertainty variance== exceeds a threshold; **zero failures** over 6 pendulum epochs, Pareto-dominates discrepancy-only on HalfCheetah — precedes [[2605.27114|VR-DAgger]].
- **[[1011.0686|DAgger]]** — ==Dataset Aggregation== rolls out the current policy, queries the expert for corrective labels on visited states, retraining one deterministic policy on the aggregate, reducing imitation to no-regret online learning; **zero** falls/lap by iteration 15 on Super Tux Kart vs SMILe's ~2 — precursor to the DAgger-variant family above.

#### 1.4 Non-Interactive Covariate-Shift Mitigation

DAgger-style correction (§1.3) requires an interactive expert to relabel states the policy visits. These papers instead mitigate covariate shift purely offline — synthesizing corrective observation-action pairs with a generative model, reshaping the state representation and injecting synthetic corrective labels, or jointly optimizing the policy with policy-compatible auxiliary trajectories — without any online expert queries.

- **[[2402.17768|Diffusion-DAgger]]** — Synthesizes OOD image-action pairs via a ==conditional diffusion model== conditioned on end-effector-pose perturbations, labeling actions from a future expert frame (k=3) to avoid "overshooting," replacing DAgger's human correction with automated data; **100%** pushing success vs BC's **30%** and SPARTN's **50%**, **90%** shirt-hanging vs **20%**.
- **[[2302.03086|DITTO]]** — Trains an actor-critic imitation agent entirely inside a learned ==RSSM world model=='s latent space via a ==non-adversarial intrinsic reward== (latent divergence to the nearest expert state), with no real-environment interaction; recovers or exceeds expert on all **5** Atari games and BipedalWalker from pixels.
- **[[2105.03019|Imitation-via-Simultaneous]]** — ==Collocation for Demonstration Encoding (CoDE)== jointly optimizes the policy + parameterized ==auxiliary trajectories== consistent with the policy class, bounding rollout deviation with no interactive expert; **10×** fewer demos to match BC+Noise, target error **0.01m vs 0.89m** (BC), collision rate **5.3% vs 49.0%** on obstacle-avoidance.
- **[[2101.06557|TrafficSim]]** — Trains a scene-level ==implicit latent variable model== (GNN joint actor) by unrolling ==closed-loop through a differentiable simulator== under a ==time-adaptive imitation + collision loss==, replacing an unavailable interactive expert; closed-loop unrolling cuts collisions **5.92%→1.28%**, scenario rate **0.50%** vs IDM's **1.19%**.
- **[[2011.06719|Grasping with Chopsticks]]** — Combats covariate shift in model-free IL for fine manipulation via an ==object-centric coordinate frame==, ==Gaussian noise-injected corrective labels== (no interactive expert), and a ==BC/k-NN ensemble== switching on state deviation; **80%** chopstick-grasping vs human's **82.7%**, noise injection alone lifts BC **37.3%→72.0%**.

> [!tip] The Loss You Minimize Is Not the Metric You Ship
> [[2108.03298|Robomimic]]'s most-cited finding is that BC validation loss does not correlate with task success — offline policy selection is an open problem because the supervised objective is a proxy for closed-loop performance. The two structural fixes are *temporal modeling* (BC-RNN, and the long-context policies in [[03_Imitation-Learning-and-RL#1.2 Structured & Long-Context Policy Representations]]) to handle non-Markovian demos, and *interaction* to ground the proxy in real success ([[03_Imitation-Learning-and-RL#1.3 Imitation Beyond the Demo Ceiling]]). For the dataset-scale view of what demonstrations to collect, see [[02_Dataset-Benchmark-Environment#1. Cross-Embodiment Scale Datasets]]; for [[2104.10218|Episodic-Memory-Manipulation]]'s single-demo FSM memory as an early answer to the same non-Markovian problem, see [[09_Robot-Memory#1.3 Episodic & Compression Memory for Manipulation]].

### 2. Scaling Demonstrations

*If demonstrations cap policy quality, scale the demonstrations — from cheap human and egocentric video, across embodiments via universal action representations, and through synthetic and co-training pipelines that manufacture data the real robot never collected.*

#### 2.1 Human & Egocentric Video

Human video is the cheapest demonstration source of all — orders of magnitude more abundant than teleoperation, but missing the action labels and embodiment alignment a robot policy needs. These papers bridge that gap with inverse dynamics, retargeting, and embodiment-agnostic observation spaces.

- **[[2607.05780|FORGE]]** — Decouples ==functional reasoning== (predicting ==2D keypoint trajectories== from action-free data via ==conditional flow-matching==) from ==grounded execution== on action-labeled data, bridging the tool-use perception-to-action gap; **>2x** unseen-tool success over Diffusion Policy (**0.36 vs 0.17** sim, **0.63 vs 0.22** real).
- **[[2607.03828|ObjRetarget]]** — Decouples human-video retargeting into ==task-adaptive arm-plane regularization== for anthropomorphic arm motion and ==polytope-cluster contact-aware retargeting== for multi-finger hand-object geometry; **75.8%** avg success on a RealMan dual-arm robot vs OKAMI's **61.6%** and ORION's **50.8%**, lowest object-slip distance.
- **[[2606.29517|CORE (Outcome Regularities)]]** — Trains a ==terminal outcome encoder== (contrastive + time-to-goal + terminal classification) on action-free video, clusters successful states via ==K-Means== into a task-level ==visual goal prototype==, then guides any ==backbone-agnostic== policy; **+2.5-11.1pp** on Meta-World/RoboTwin 2.0, **+16-17pp** real ARX R5 contact-rich tasks.
- **[[2606.11628|LUCID]]** — An embodiment-agnostic ==intent model== learned from unstructured internet human videos (object flow + palm-pose references) paired with a ==goal-conditioned RL== sensorimotor policy distilled in massively-parallel sim; **73%** real-world success vs **28%** for an open-loop video planner, transferring across a dexterous hand and parallel-jaw gripper.
- **[[2605.24934|HumanEgo]]** — A zero-shot robot-learning method from minutes of human egocentric video; ==hand inpainting== + virtual grippers + ==Interaction-Centric Tokens== give a viewpoint-invariant observation, fed to a ==flow-matching== transformer; **92.5%** real bimanual success from **30 min** of human data, **75.0%** from 15 min — beating a 30-min robot-teleop ACT baseline (**51.2%**).
- **[[2604.10677|LIDEA]]** — An alignment method that maps human, pseudo-robot, and robot views via ==dual-stage 2D feature distillation== (from ==DINOv3==) plus ==explicit 3D geometric alignment== into an embodiment-agnostic space, fed to a ==3D diffusion policy== (RISE-2); human demos substitute up to **80%** of robot demos and lift OOD Fold-Towel **36%→63%**.
- **[[2604.20841|DeVI]]** — A pipeline that generates 2D human-object-interaction video from text via an ==image-to-video diffusion model==, then extracts ==hybrid 3D-human + 2D-object imitation targets== for ==RL==; ==Visual HOI Alignment== cuts hand-joint pixel error **25.6→3.74** and contact distance **101→18.7 mm**; MPJPE **25–41 mm** vs baselines' **91–142 mm** on GRAB.
- **[[2604.07517|GraspDreamer]]** — Synthesizes ==human functional-grasp demonstrations== via visual generative models, extracts and metric-scale-corrects hand trajectories (HaMeR + ICP), then ==taxonomy-aware retargeting== (VLM-predicted grasp type + contact refinement) maps them to any robot hand; **>70%** real success on parallel-jaw and Allegro-hand functional grasping.
- **[[2505.17006|CoMo]]** — A method that learns ==continuous latent motion== from internet video via an ==inverse-dynamics model== with temporal-difference cues + ==temporal contrastive learning== to suppress static background; the latents serve as pseudo-action labels for joint policy training on action-less video + robot data; **+4.2pp** LIBERO (**75.9→80.1%**), lowest action-prediction MSE.
- **[[2511.16407|LAOF]]** — A method that constrains ==latent action learning== with ==optical flow== as an action-driven pseudo-supervision signal during video pretraining, using text-guided ==object-centric flow== to drop agent-irrelevant motion; unsupervised, **+4.2%** LIBERO / **+16%** PROCGEN over LAPO, and only **1%** action labels stabilizes convergence where LAPO fails.
- **[[2412.05718|RLZero]]** — A zero-supervision language/video→policy method via an ==Imagine-Project-Imitate== pipeline: a video model imagines the task, a VLM grounds it into the agent's observation space by retrieval, and a frozen ==Behavior Foundation Model== infers a closed-loop policy gradient-free; **83.2%** win vs GenRL, ~**25 s** (**~400×** faster), cross-embodied from YouTube.
- **[[2507.23523|H-RDT]]** — A model that pre-trains a ==Diffusion-Transformer== with ==flow matching== on **338K+** EgoDex human trajectories using a 48-D hand action, then modular-fine-tunes per robot; few-shot (1–5 demos) **41.6%** on dual-arm ARX5 vs RDT's **16.0%**, and **52%** real towel-folding — human pretraining as the bimanual prior.
- **[[2605.30350|DynaFLIP]]** — A ==Tri-modal-dynamics== pretraining method that distills image transitions, language, and 3D flow into a single-image encoder via a ==simplex-guided alignment== objective with cosine + ==InfoNCE== regularizers; as a frozen backbone it tops MetaWorld/RLBench/LIBERO and improves real UR3 robustness to OOD perturbations.
- **[[2409.08273|HOP]]** — Lifts hand-object interaction from in-the-wild video into 3D, retargets to robot trajectories via ==simulator-in-the-loop== optimization, pretrains a ==causal transformer== on next-action prediction; **2-5x** sample-efficiency gain finetuning with PPO/BC, **+30pp** real Grasp-and-Lift success, **3x** disturbance robustness.
- **[[2408.05485|CIA]]** — ==Contrast-Imitate-Adapt==: an interaction-aware alignment transformer learns task priors via temporal cycle-consistency, a ==trajectory GAN== imitates action priors, then ==GAN-inversion + CEM== latent-direction search refines from real interaction; **90%** Grasp / **97%** Close-refrigerator success vs **27-57%** for CQL/BC.
- **[[2407.18178|PianoMime]]** — Extracts 3D fingertip trajectories from YouTube piano videos (MediaPipe + MIDI-paired homography), trains song-specific ==residual-policy== IL+RL experts, then distills a generalist via a ==hierarchical policy== with an SDF-pretrained encoder; **F1>0.95** per-song, best generalization to unseen songs among tested architectures.
- **[[2405.20321|ORION]]** — Training-free object-centric imitation from a *single* human video: builds ==Open-world Object Graphs== via VLM segmentation/tracking (Grounded-SAM, Co-Tracker, FoundationPose), then warps object trajectories to the robot scene for SE(3) action optimization; **74.4%** avg success, **85.3%** on RGB-only video, beats hand-motion-imitation baselines.
- **[[2404.15709|ViViDex]]** — Refines noisy human-video hand/object trajectories into physically-plausible robot motion via a ==two-stage trajectory-guided RL reward==, then trains a vision-only policy on hand/target-centered point clouds with a ==3D Diffusion Policy==; **99%** seen / **50%** unseen SR in sim from just **1-3** videos per object, **80%** real Allegro-hand success.
- **[[2401.00025|ATM]]** — A two-stage method pretraining a ==Track Transformer== to predict future 2D ==any-point trajectories== from action-free video, then ==behavior-cloning== a policy on minimal action-labeled demos conditioned on the tracks as dense subgoals; **63%** LIBERO vs **37%** best baseline, cross-embodiment transfer, comparable success from only **4%** of labels.
- **[[2304.08488|VRB]]** — Learns ==contact points== and ==post-contact trajectories== as a robot-agnostic affordance representation from egocentric ==Epic-Kitchens== video (ResNet + deconv heatmaps + Transformer), bootstrapping IL, exploration, and goal-conditioned learning; **57%** vs **25%**-baseline offline-IL success, **3–10×** exploration gains.
- **[[2302.12422|MimicPlay]]** — Learns a high-level ==3D-aware latent planner== from abundant, cheap human play video, guiding a low-level ==Transformer visuomotor controller== trained on a small robot-teleop set via a ==GMM== + KL domain-bridging loss; **+23%** on long-horizon Kitchen tasks over robot-only training, **+35%** on unseen subgoal compositions.
- **[[2207.09450|WHIRL]]** — A ==Watch, Repeat, Improve== human-video imitation framework converting third-person demo hand poses into robot ==action priors==, refined via a ==CVAE residual policy== and an ==agent-agnostic video-alignment objective== (inpainting + action recognition); **20** real-world tasks, drawer **43%→83%** and door **40%→92%** over 3 iterations.
- **[[2204.03514|Habitat-Web]]** — A ==web-based teleoperation== platform collecting **92,000+** human demonstrations (AMT + Habitat-Sim WebGL) for ObjectNav and PICK&PLACE; ==behavior cloning== on human data hit **35.4%** SR (beating SOTA RL) at **7x** fewer unique steps, while RL scored **0%** on PICK&PLACE — human demos teach exploration strategies reward-shaping can't.
- **[[1707.03374|Context Translation]]** — Imitation from action-free third-person human video: a ==context translation model== predicts what the demo looks like in the learner's own context, and distances in its ==aligned feature space== become the RL reward; **66%** real Sawyer ladling from **60–135** videos where GAIL and third-person IL score **0%** — the ancestor of [[2106.03911|XIRL]].
- **[[2402.07127|Video-Based Manipulation Learning Survey]]** — The first survey dedicated to video-based manipulation learning, taxonomizing ==foundational perception==, ==RL==, ==IL==, ==hybrid RL-IL==, and ==multi-modal VLA== approaches with per-family trade-off tables; argues current benchmarks reward correlation, not the ==causal reasoning== a "Causal-CALVIN" protocol would test.

#### 2.2 Cross-Embodiment & Universal Action Representations

Demonstrations on one robot rarely transfer to another because action spaces are morphology-specific. These papers build morphology-invariant representations so a single corpus serves many embodiments.

- **[[2606.12965|EmbodiSteer]]** — A training-free ==cross-embodiment== deployment method that lifts a Cartesian ==diffusion policy== into the target robot's joint space via Jacobian updates, adding ==CBF whole-body collision guidance== as a QP per denoising step; **+28.5%** sim / **+36.7%** real success and **46.1%/90.0%** fewer collisions at **9.61 Hz**.
- **[[2606.01851|PHASOR]]** — A ==Phase-anchored== universal action representation built from per-body-part phase parameters via a ==differentiable FFT== plus a pose branch, with human-anchored ==hierarchical InfoNCE== alignment over lightweight robot adapters; **90.3%** R@1 human→robot retrieval, **1.62 mm** next-frame MPJPE, the only method to beat raw-kinematics teleop.
- **[[2502.16372|COMPASS]]** — A cross-embodiment mobility framework that ==imitation-learns== a base policy from one robot, ==residual-RL== fine-tunes per embodiment, then ==distills== specialists into one generalist via KL matching; residual specialists reach **5×–40×** higher success than the IL base and the generalist transfers zero-shot to NVIDIA Carter and Unitree G1 at **~80%** in clutter.

#### 2.3 Synthetic & Co-Training Data Pipelines

Close the data gap: manufacture synthetic demonstrations, curate/select from existing pools, or co-train real+sim mixtures.

- **[[2607.06442|SIEVE]]** — Segments demos by end-effector state flips, clusters ==V-JEPA2== embeddings into reusable ==visuo-motor primitives==, then allocates a data budget via ==logarithmic reuse utility== before picking ==medoid== trajectories per pattern; **56.3%** avg success on Bridge-V2 at **50%** of the data/steps, beating full-dataset training's **51.8%**.
- **[[2607.00351|ACT-VLA]]** — Repurposes ==Text Latent Interpolation== from an inference-time control into an offline compositional-data-synthesis engine, steering VLA latents to generate novel demonstrations under ==dual quality filtering==; **+52.7pp** LIBERO-Spatial-OOD and **+49.0pp** LIBERO-Goal-OOD on a π0.5 backbone, beating inference-time TLI and UniVLA/OpenVLA-OFT baselines.
- **[[2606.12365|Ambient-Diffusion-Policy]]** — An ==imitation learning== method that admits ==suboptimal data== into ==Diffusion Policy== training only at noise levels where it helps (high-noise global plans, low-noise primitives), set by a classifier-annotated t-range; beats filtering and co-training, **99.5%** maze and **83%** OXE table-cleaning vs co-training's **71%**.
- **[[2605.21710|PGDG]]** — A physically-grounded data-generation method from a *single* demo: ==spatial randomization== + control-point sampling generate recovery trajectories, a ==DPP + Goldilocks-Zone curator== selects informative ones, ==CEM local relabeling== fixes risky states; sim **38→93%**, real **35→82%**, fine-tunes ==GR00T N1.6== to **77.5%** vs **40%**.
- **[[2602.16444|RoboGene]]** — A ==diversity-driven agentic task generator== with ==LFU sampling== + ==multi-faceted LLM self-reflection== + memory-augmented refinement; **0.99** physical feasibility, **719** objects (**1.7×** expansion) / **118** skills, VLA pretrain reaches **40%** unseen dual-arm SR — the data-coverage complement to scene-level generators.
- **[[2505.09603|DataMIL]]** — A data-selection framework for ==imitation learning== adapting ==datamodels== to estimate each data point's influence on target-task performance via a validation-loss surrogate that avoids real rollouts; **61%** average real success, **+21pp** over STRAP, with cross-embodiment selection — optimize for policy gain, not similarity.
- **[[2601.15164|V-CAGE]]** — A ==context-aware generation + verification== pipeline for long-horizon synthetic data pairing ==LLM planning== with ==VLM-guided rejection sampling== to prune wrong trajectories and a prohibited-area map for plausibility; downstream policies reach **64.58%** average success (**+17.72%**) with **100%** for top checkpoints — verification kills silent failures.
- **[[2512.13100|OXE-AugE]]** — A large-scale cross-embodiment data engine that rebalances OXE by ==physics-based MuJoCo replay== augmenting 16 datasets with up to 9 new robot embodiments (4.4M trajectories), fusing learned + sim masks with auto base-position tuning; OpenVLA/Pi0 fine-tuned on it gain **+24%**/**+45%** on unseen robot-gripper configs, beating artifact-prone diffusion augmentation.
- **[[2503.24361|Sim-and-Real-Co-Training]]** — A simple recipe: ==BC co-training== on a weighted mixture of real + task-agnostic "Prior" sim and task-aware "Digital Cousin" sim data; **+38%** average over real-only across Panda + Fourier GR-1, with robustness to novel objects (**10%→80%** on Humanoid CupPnP) that persists even in data-rich regimes.
- **[[2503.13171|HybridGen]]** — A ==VLM-guided hybrid-planning== data-generation framework for ==imitation learning== combining pose adaptation, ==VLM-constrained optimization== for replanning, and a ==nearest-GRT== pose-only augmentation; **+5%** average over 18 task variants and **59.7%** on the hardest high-diversity D2 variants vs **49.5%** — semantic constraints over interpolation.
- **[[2501.04595|MobileH2R]]** — A ==4D imitation learning== framework for human-to-mobile-robot handover trained *exclusively* on synthetic data, fusing multi-camera point clouds + temporal flow to output coordinated base + arm actions from optimization-planned demos; **+26.3%** success and **−5.06 s** over non-end-to-end baselines, **80%** real simple / **63.3%** complex handover.
- **[[2304.08742|Behavior Retrieval]]** — A ==VAE state-action similarity metric== pre-trained on a large unlabeled prior dataset retrieves only transitions relevant to a handful of task-specific expert demos, then ==behavior-clones== on the combined set; **+30%** average sim success, **+20%** real (NutAssembly, Pickle, Soap), robust under distribution shift.
- **[[2509.01657|IWR]]** — Reframes few-shot-IL data retrieval as ==importance sampling==, replacing the L2-nearest-neighbor rule (shown equivalent to a degenerate zero-bandwidth KDE) with ==Gaussian KDE== ratios of target-vs-prior latent densities; **+4.4–5.8%** over Behavior/Flow Retrieval and SAILOR on Robomimic/LIBERO-10, **+30%** real Bridge V2 long-horizon SR.
- **[[2608.07895|MMPF]]** — A training-free auditor detecting ==Instruction-Trajectory Mismatches== via per-modality ==RBF-weighted kNN voting== + ==class-prototype softmax==, fused by ==reliability-weighted product-of-experts== gated on predictive entropy; **99.0%** AUROC / **100%** correction accuracy on LIBERO, **90.0%** vs **73.8%** real-robot success via correction-based curation.

## Part B — Methods: From Reward Signals to Policy Optimization

*Once demonstrations are exhausted, the learning signal becomes a reward — learned, inferred, or hand-designed — and the question becomes how to optimize a policy against it efficiently, stably, and safely.*

### 3. Reward Learning & Inverse RL

Reinforcement learning needs a reward, and for most real robot tasks no one can write one. The reward-learning literature attacks this from three directions: *learn* a reward model from data (often a VLM scoring progress), *infer* a reward from demonstrations or preferences (inverse RL), or *avoid* the reward entirely with goal-conditioned and contrastive objectives. The trade-off across the three is mis-specification risk versus annotation cost — a learned reward scales but can be hacked, an inferred reward is grounded in demos but expensive to collect, and a reward-free objective sidesteps the problem but gives up dense shaping.

The 2026 frontier is the VLM-as-reward-model: a vision-language backbone that emits a dense scalar progress estimate from raw video, turning the un-writable reward into a learned one. The risk it introduces is reward hacking — the policy exploits the reward model's perceptual errors rather than solving the task — which is why the strongest entries pair the reward model with verifiable supervision or trajectory-comparison structure.

#### 3.1 Learned & VLM-Based Reward Models

Use a learned or VLM-adapted reward: train a model to score trajectories or estimate task progress, or adapt a pretrained VLM/LLM to the job via prompting, in-context recalibration, or generated reward code, then use it as the RL reward. Scalable either way, but only as good as the model's robustness to hacking.

- **[[2607.13033|DenseReward]]** — An automated pipeline decomposes tasks into five ==canonical phases==, applying targeted perturbations to synthesize realistic ==failure trajectories== without manual annotation, training a dense per-timestep VLM reward model; **0.081 MAE** (vs **0.230–0.366** for prior reward models) and **+30–40pp** real-robot success from limited-rollout RL fine-tuning.
- **[[2607.05391|LLM-as-a-Verifier]]** — Extracts ==continuous reward signals== from LLM scoring-token logit distributions (vs discrete judge scores), scaled via granularity/repeated-evaluation/criteria-decomposition and a ==Probabilistic Pivot Tournament== O(Nk) ranker; **87.4%** RoboRewardBench preference accuracy and **1.8x** RL sample efficiency as a dense reward.
- **[[2607.01721|CoRe]]** — Decomposes RL rewards into LLM-searched ==Formal Rewards== (code candidates scored by preference alignment, not task completion) plus VLM ==Residual Rewards== from video-level preferences + per-frame importance weights; **99.0%** avg MetaWorld success at **3-40x** fewer preference labels than prior VLM-reward baselines.
- **[[2606.31377|STDR]]** — Segments unstructured expert videos into semantic stages via a ==VLM==, then an LSTM ==Stage Discriminator== (VAE-based OOD detection) + a ==FiLM-modulated Intra-stage Progress Estimator== combine into a dense, hack-resistant reward; beats Sparse/VIP/LIV across **14** MetaWorld/ManiSkill/Franka-Kitchen tasks, matching human-designed rewards on a real Franka.
- **[[2606.30698|VL-PR]]** — A Vision-Language Procedural Reasoning framework where an ==MLLM== infers real-time navigation context (cruise/bifurcation/retraction) to dynamically reweight a ==multi-component SAC reward== for endovascular guidewire navigation; **100%** coronary/renal and **97%** carotid success on a physical phantom, **48.4%** fewer steps than static-reward SAC.
- **[[2606.29834|STEAM]]** — Derives frame-level advantages from ==normalized temporal offsets== between expert-trajectory frames (incl. reversed pairs for regressions), with an ensemble of ==distributional offset predictors== aggregated by minimum to suppress OOD overestimation, feeding ==CFGRL==; **+59pp** success (**92.3%** bimanual towel folding) on real robots.
- **[[2606.28320|WARP-RM]]** — Self-supervised ==time-warp augmentation== (speed + reversal) of successful demos yields signed relative-progress labels for a ==categorical== reward model, whose terminal-frame velocity weights/filters ==WARP-BC== action chunks; **0/20→14/20** on the lowest-quality T-shirt-folding tier, up to **18x** throughput.
- **[[2606.24633|ReTVL]]** — A Retry-Supervised Value Learning framework that learns mistake-sensitive value functions for robot imitation by combining ==global progress calibration== with ==local pairwise preference learning== over sparsely-annotated ==retry keypoints==; VOC **0.987**, Drop AUC **0.797**, and **80%** real-robot success vs RECAP-BC's **62.5%** — retry events as local supervision.
- **[[2606.23640|Success-Visitation-Rewards]]** — A process-reward method (SVM) converting sparse outcome rewards into dense step-level feedback via a ==discriminator== over successful vs unsuccessful state-action ==visitation== plus a clipped ==log-ratio== reward that provably preserves the optimal policy; **80–90%** on LIBERO/RoboCasa, π0 finetuning at **~2×** fewer steps — online-IRL shaping.
- **[[2606.03963|AgenticRL]]** — A self-refining agent-guided RL method for UAV navigation: a ==multimodal GPT agent== generates and iteratively refines executable ==Python reward functions== from language + visual context + diagnostic feedback; **97%** sim / **91%** real success with **94%** sim-to-real accuracy and **71%** reward-refinement improvement.
- **[[2606.01036|Bad-Behavior-Rewards]]** — A position study showing embodied reward models systematically *over-reward* suboptimal behavior because datasets lack failures; injecting ==dense per-timestep reward labels on real failure videos== via in-context learning recalibrates a VLM reward model, +**~10%** accuracy on complex Tool-Use/Pour tasks — negative data is the fix.
- **[[2603.22574|GIFT]]** — A reward-generalization method grounding state similarity in ==high-level human intent== inferred by an ==LLM== from contrastive trajectories, then aligning novel test states to intent-equivalent training states via an ==intent-conditioned kernel== before a fixed reward; up to **+20%** win rate over vision/language baselines, generalizing on a real Franka.
- **[[2603.16065|LRM]]** — Specializes foundation VLMs into **Large Reward Models** producing dense, frame-level rewards via a ==tri-faceted structure== (==temporal contrastive==, ==absolute progress==, ==task completion==) trained on unlabeled robot/HOI/simulated video; **+15.3%** contrastive correlation, **-20.0%** progress MAE, **60.93%** ManiSkill3 success vs the **56.88%** SFT baseline.
- **[[2603.02115|Robometer]]** — A general-purpose reward model from ==trajectory comparisons==: a ==VLM backbone== trained with ==absolute progress + relative preference supervision== over the **1M-trajectory, 21-embodiment RBM-1M** dataset; OOD VOC Pearson **r=0.95**, lifts online RL success **2.5×** and IL **4.5×** via data filtering.
- **[[2603.28730|SOLE-R1]]** — A reward method using video-language reasoning *as the sole reward*: a VLM emits per-timestep ==spatiotemporal chain-of-thought== + dense scalar progress, trained via ==SFT then RLVR==; enables from-random-init on-robot RL reaching **≥50%** on **24** unseen tasks, with failures "signal-limited" rather than reward-hacked — hacking-resistance is the contribution.
- **[[2604.03037|ARM]]** — An Advantage Reward Model for long-horizon manipulation: a ==Tri-state Advantage Labeling== (Progressive/Regressive/Stagnant) feeds a ==temporal Transformer== predicting relative advantage, used in ==Advantage-Weighted BC==; dense-progress MSE **0.0014**, **2.5×** faster human + **20×** faster auto labeling, and **99.4%** bimanual towel-folding vs BC's **62.1%**.
- **[[2603.01694|MVR]]** — A ==Multi-view video reward shaping== method: a state-relevance model learned via ==paired-comparison + representation regularization== mitigates single-view occlusion bias, with ==state-dependent shaping== that decays VLM guidance as behavior aligns; best average rank (**1.67** HumanoidBench, **1.50** MetaWorld) over **19** tasks vs single-view rewards.
- **[[2603.00719|Keyframe-Lab-Rewards]]** — Auto-extracts ==kinematics-aware keyframes== from non-expert lab-robot demos via multi-view latent dynamics, then a ==diffusion-generated stage-wise reward== scores progress against them for HiL-RL on an Octo VLA backbone; **82%** avg success vs **42–47%** for HG-DAgger/Hil-ConRFT across 4 long-horizon lab tasks.
- **[[2602.11393|Visual-Motion-Pref-Modeling]]** — A reward method that learns a ==Motion-Prediction Reward== from egocentric human video via a ==DiT== with frozen ==DINOv2==, scoring robot actions by cosine similarity of predicted vs observed object motion; plugged into ==residual RL==, reaches **76.7%** Open-Microwave and **73.3%** Fold-Cloth on Franka while VIP shows unlearning.
- **[[2601.00675|RoboReward]]** — A general-purpose ==vision-language reward model== for robot RL trained on human-labeled 5-level progress scores with ==counterfactual relabeling + negative clipping== to synthesize failures; the 8B model hits **0.665** MAE beating 22 frontier VLMs and lifts real-robot success from **5–10%** to **50–80%** on unseen tasks — progress rewards over binary success.
- **[[2512.20675|VLM-Reward-Objectives]]** — A controlled re-evaluation of reward-model *learning objectives* (TCN, VIP, R3M, LIV) under a fixed SigLIP2+LoRA backbone; a simple ==triplet-loss baseline== wins on ranking accuracy (**68.88%** avg), while all objectives collapse on multi-step tasks (door-open VOC **6.70%**) — the objective, not the backbone, decides reward quality.
- **[[2605.22123|FLORA]]** — A reward-shaping method that learns ==invariant object-centric motion-flow rewards== from a few demos via a ==PBRS-MS== potential with milestone progress tracking and ==LLM-symbolic + Bayesian-optimization== potential discovery; **0.97** Spearman ρ, **96%** Meta-World success, near-**100%** real Peg-Insert/Box-Open with zero-shot OOD robustness.
- **[[2502.12599|Robotic-Wiping-Policy]]** — A quality-critical ==RL== method pairing a ==bounded reward== (checkpoint regions that cap cumulative quality reward, killing the 'forever' strategy) with an automated ==VLM curriculum== that diagnoses failures and tunes reward weights; **98%** navigation success and **243** force IAE vs **58%** for the naive baseline — bounded reward plus self-tuning.
- **[[2405.19988|Video-Language-Critic]]** — A language-conditioned ==reward model== trained on diverse video with a ==contrastive alignment + sequential ranking== objective (monotone progress) over a ViT + temporal-aggregation Transformer; lifts Meta-World success **58→78%**, **72%** on 6 unseen tasks (**>3×** efficiency), and **2×** efficiency on cross-embodiment transfer from Open X-Embodiment.
- **[[2404.17521|Ag2Manip]]** — Trains an ==agent-agnostic visual representation== (time-contrastive on human video with humans/robots inpainted out) and an ==agent-agnostic action proxy==, reward-shaped ==goal-conditioned PPO==; **78.7%** sim success vs **11.1-18.5%** for R3M/VIP/Eureka, real few-shot imitation **50%→77.5%**.
- **[[2306.00958|LIV]]** — Extends ==Value-Implicit Pre-training== with a ==combined VIP-image + VIP-language objective== (generalizing InfoNCE with temporal coherence) over a dual image/text encoder pre-trained on ==EpicKitchen==; zero-shot dense rewards with no robot data, and beats CLIP/R3M/VIP on language-conditioned BC across sim and real.
- **[[2605.22986|ASQ]]** — Diagnoses underspecified reward features from the ==empirical variance== of feature values across demonstrations, has an ==LLM== prune candidates and generate targeted queries, then reweights a ==weighted Maximum-Entropy IRL== fit with per-demo ==rationality vectors==; **M=0.021** normalized reward improvement over unguided baselines in a physical-robot user study.
- **[[2607.21655|Progress Reward Modeling Survey]]** — A ==three-step analytical framework== (interface, construction mechanism, data/benchmark protocol) unifying the progress-reward-modeling literature — input types, output forms, and construction routes (==frozen foundation models==, ==temporal supervision==, ==instruction tuning==); the reference taxonomy for this section.
- **[[2608.09853|RynnValue]]** — A value foundation model predicting ==temporal-distance cost-to-go== from timestamp-derived labels over **7,000+** hours / **3M** clips, with ==value-isolation attention== + ==random/shuffled temporal sampling== suppressing shortcuts; beats preference-supervised Robometer (**0.675** vs **0.655** Kendall's tau) and lifts real online RL success **52.5%→72.5%**.

#### 3.2 Inverse RL & Preference Learning

Recover the reward *implied* by demonstrations or preferences, then optimize it — grounded in expert behavior but historically unstable.

- **[[2607.14393|NEURO-LOOP]]** — An offline framework scoring rollouts from ==fNIRS== brain signals: an MLP classifies neural features into predicted trajectory optimality, recalibrating ==DDPG== rewards, ==Q-values==, or ==trajectory priorities==; Q-value augmentation gives the fastest learning on a Fetch Pick-and-Place task, with gains traced to the neural signal via noise-ablation.
- **[[2607.13938|DBF]]** — Embeds a ==Discrete-Time Control Barrier Function== into an ==Adversarial Imitation Learning== from Observation discriminator, jointly learning policy and barrier function from unlabeled expert observations with no safety labels; **0%** collisions on unseen obstacles (vs GAIL's **20.5%**), **65%** real success for DBF-MPAIL vs **0%** for non-DBF baselines.
- **[[2607.12466|PREC]]** — **Preference-based REward Clustering** factorizes reward learning into a population-level self-supervised ==trajectory encoder== and subpopulation decoders, clustering users via a 'leaky' ==Expectation-Maximization== algorithm from sparse, noisy binary preferences; highest Utilitarian/Egalitarian/Nash welfare on D4RL MuJoCo vs single-pooled and individualized policies.
- **[[2511.14565|Masked-IRL]]** — An IRL method that uses ==LLMs to disambiguate== ambiguous language instructions and generate ==binary state-relevance masks==, plus an ==implicit masking loss== that penalizes reward changes under perturbation of irrelevant state; **4.7×** fewer demos for comparable generalization, **+16.9%** mask-F1, and **44.8–59.4%** lower reward regret on real Franka.
- **[[2605.11020|TRIRL]]** — A ==Trust-Region Inverse RL== algorithm that reformulates ==MCE-IRL== as ==explicit dual ascent== in reward space with ==local policy updates== inside a reverse-KL trust region, plus a ==Lagrangian reward-correction== for monotonic improvement; **2.4×** aggregate IQM over GAIL/AIRL/LSIQ with transferable rewards under altered dynamics.
- **[[2605.00623|ENERGYFLOW]]** — An inverse-RL method unifying diffusion-policy modeling with reward recovery: a ==scalar energy function== whose negative action-gradient *is* the denoising field enforces a ==conservative vector field==, and a ==centered-shaping== reward trains downstream SAC to near-oracle; **93.8%** RoboMimic, **100%** real AGIBOT G1 — the hidden reward in a diffusion policy.
- **[[2507.21533|MPAIL]]** — A method unifying ==adversarial IRL== (AIRL, observation-only) with ==MPC== by embedding an ==MPPI planner== inside the adversarial loop, optimizing trajectories against the learned reward + bootstrapped value with online dynamics learning; reproduces a real expert at **0.17 m** cross-track error from a single partial demo, OOD-generalizing where GAIL fails.
- **[[2507.05906|Feature-vs-GAN-LfD-Survey]]** — A principled comparison of ==feature-based== (DeepMimic-style dense tracking rewards) vs ==GAN-based== (GAIL/AMP-style adversarial discriminators) motion imitation, debunking misconceptions (GANs don't auto-learn a distance metric) and showing both converge on ==structured motion representations== as the key to generalization.
- **[[2505.18595|MisoDICE]]** — A ==Multi-agent imitation from mixed-quality demos== method: LLM-generated preferences refined by offline MARL partition trajectories into expert/suboptimal, then a ==DICE== distribution-matching objective with ==value factorization + linear mixing== extracts a decentralized weighted-BC policy; highest average returns across SMACv1/v2 over BC and DICE baselines.
- **[[2502.18447|SRI]]** — ==Supervised Reward Inference== directly regresses behavior trajectories + query state to reward via a ==transformer/set-transformer== task encoder, sidestepping any assumed demonstrator behavior model; proven asymptotically Bayes-optimal, **0.822** goal proximity on gesture demos vs **~-0.001** for GAIL/AIRL/BC.
- **[[2406.16258|MEReQ]]** — Learns only a ==residual reward function== (the gap between a prior policy's implicit reward and the human's) via ==Max-Ent IRL + Residual Q-Learning==, plus ==pseudo-expert trajectories== from uncorrected rollouts; **1819** vs **4330** intervention samples to align on Highway-Sim.
- **[[2406.08472|RILe]]** — A ==reinforced imitation-learning== framework with a cooperative ==trainer-student== system concurrently learning an adaptive state-action reward and an imitation policy, the trainer guided by a ==discriminator== expert-likeness signal; up to **+470%** over GAIL under action noise (5154 vs 902 on Humanoid-v2) — dynamic shaping beats static GAIL/AIRL landscapes.
- **[[2206.00238|DARL]]** — A ==dynamics-agnostic adversarial imitation learning== method ==minimizing mutual information== between latent state-action embeddings and next-state, with a ==discriminator ensemble== over historical checkpoints fixing policy dependency; **≥92%** reward consistency, best in **15/16** MuJoCo dynamics-transfer tasks.
- **[[2106.03911|XIRL]]** — A self-supervised ==cross-embodiment inverse RL== method learning an embodiment-invariant task-progress encoder via ==Temporal Cycle-Consistency== on unlabeled multi-embodiment video, then a dense reward as negative L2 distance to a goal embedding; matches ground-truth sparse rewards same-embodiment and beats baselines on unseen embodiments, no frame correspondences.

#### 3.3 Reward-Free & Goal-Conditioned Signals

Skip the reward entirely: condition on goals and let contrastive or divide-and-conquer value learning supply the signal.

- **[[2604.05931|Saliency-Guided-Policy]]** — A visual unsupervised-RL method that decouples representation from the ==successor-representation== objective via a ==saliency-guided dynamics task== (Q-gradient saliency maps force dynamics-relevant encoding), plus a ==consistency policy== with classifier-free guidance for multimodal skills; up to **+33%** zero-shot over SOTA on 16 ExORL tasks.
- **[[2408.05804|Single-Goal-Contrastive-RL]]** — A reward-free method in which skills and exploration emerge from ==contrastive RL== conditioned on a *single target goal*, with an ==InfoNCE critic==; outperforms oracle multi-goal CRL, SAC+HER, and dense-reward SAC on MetaWorld + maze, with sequential grasp-pick-place emerging *before* the first success — no rewards, demos, or subgoals.
- **[[2503.14858|CRL]]** — A scaling study taking ==self-supervised contrastive RL== to **1024-layer** actor/critic networks with residual connections + LayerNorm in JaxGCRL; depth 4→64 yields **2×–50×** gains (continuing to 1024), unlocking emergent behaviors (a depth-256 Humanoid vaulting walls) — depth, not width, enables new goal-reaching.
- **[[2510.22512|TRL]]** — A ==Transitive RL== method for offline goal-conditioned learning via a ==divide-and-conquer Bellman update== with ==soft expectile regression== and in-trajectory subgoals + distance re-weighting; SOTA on long-horizon humanoidmaze-giant and best average across **10** OGBench environments / **50** tasks — divide-and-conquer beats TD and MC value learning.
- **[[2602.08557|CSRL]]** — A ==constrained state sampler== (Augmented Lagrangian NLP over contact/friction/quasi-static-equilibrium constraints) feeds ==Projected Interpolation Resets== + a phase-scheduled ==reset curriculum== into off-the-shelf TD7/HER; **96.5%** vs **4.4%** success on panda-sphere non-prehensile manipulation over random-drop resets.
- **[[2512.12046|Eik-QRL]]** — Derives the Eikonal constraint from ==Quasimetric RL=='s local-consistency loss for trajectory-free learning from i.i.d. state-goal pairs, then hierarchically composes it (==Eik-HiQRL==) with Implicit V-Learning for non-isotropic dynamics; SOTA on offline humanoidmaze stitch tasks, lower collision rates in pointmaze vs QRL.
- **[[2509.06782|Eikonal-Value-Learner]]** — Eik-HIQL regularizes HIQL's ==Goal-Conditioned Value Function== with an ==Eikonal PDE== penalty (unit gradient norm, 1-Lipschitz) derived from the HJB equation, encouraging a distance-field structure without needing dynamics; **>100%** gains on **7/31** OGBench navigation/locomotion tasks, weaker in contact-rich antsoccer/manipulation.
- **[[2505.14975|SAW]]** — A ==Subgoal Advantage-Weighted policy bootstrapping== method built on a *flat* goal-conditioned objective that drops the generative subgoal model, estimating subgoal expectations by ==advantage-weighted importance sampling== over in-trajectory waypoints plus AWR + bootstrapping; first non-trivial **35%** on humanoidmaze-giant (prior best **12%**), **73%** antmaze-giant.
- **[[2502.05454|TRA]]** — A ==Temporal Representation Alignment== method that augments goal-conditioned BC with two ==InfoNCE== objectives — a symmetric current↔future alignment encoding ==successor features== for behavior stitching, and a CLIP-style language↔future alignment; **+40%** OOD compositional success and **+45%** over AWR on instruction following, no explicit RL.
- **[[2410.20092|OGBench]]** — A benchmark for ==offline goal-conditioned RL== spanning 8 environments and 85 datasets (locomotion, manipulation, drawing) probing ==goal stitching==, long-horizon reasoning, and stochasticity, with JAX reference implementations of 6 GCRL algorithms; no single method dominates, and hierarchical ==HIQL== is strongest overall.

### 4. RL Algorithms, Efficiency & Policy Representations

The reward exists — now optimize against it. This section is about the *optimizer*: the algorithmic and engineering advances that make RL fast enough to train a humanoid in hours, the techniques that let expressive flow and diffusion policies admit policy gradients, and the methods that extend RL to language and multimodal agents. The unifying tension is between *expressiveness* and *optimizability* — Gaussian policies optimize trivially but cannot represent multimodal action distributions, while flow/diffusion policies are expressive but have ill-defined likelihoods that block standard policy gradients.

The 2025–2026 breakthrough on the efficiency axis is that *off-policy* RL — long considered too unstable for high-dimensional control — beats PPO in wall-clock time when paired with massive parallelism, large batches, and a distributional critic. The breakthrough on the expressiveness axis is deriving policy-gradient signals for flow policies without computing exact likelihoods, finally bringing diffusion-class policies into online RL.

#### 4.1a Throughput & Off-Policy Efficiency

Make RL fast and stable enough for real humanoids — through parallelism, large batches, and a distributional critic, the throughput half of this section's efficiency/optimizability tension.

- **[[1801.01290|SAC]]** — The canonical ==off-policy maximum-entropy actor-critic==: a stochastic policy trained against ==twin soft Q-functions== (min over the pair to curb overestimation) with the ==reparameterization trick== and ==tanh== action squashing; SOTA continuous control through Ant and Humanoid, far more sample-efficient than PPO and markedly more seed-stable than DDPG.
- **[[2607.12931|ExToken]]** — Fights ==action-mode collapse== in VLA-RL fine-tuning by conditioning the policy on discrete ==exploration tokens== from ==K-means clustering== of demo trajectory embeddings, with a REINFORCE-trained ==Token Selector== picking the relevant token; **98.2%** LIBERO (vs RLinf-GRPO's **96.8%**) and **+6.25%** real-robot performance over Evo-RL under limited data.
- **[[2606.31320|AutoSafe]]** — Blends a stochastic ==SAC== action with a deterministic safe-prior action via a state-dependent ==exponential-ramp mixing weight== λ(s), keeping negligible unsafe-region probability mass while preserving differentiable gradient flow; higher returns at far fewer violations than hard-intervention filters, deployed real-time on a Raspberry Pi cart-pole at **50 Hz**.
- **[[2606.20197|Stable-Transformer-MPC]]** — A ==Transformer-Actor-Critic MPC== where a Transformer predicts dynamic MPC cost coefficients from state/reference history, proven to satisfy global incremental ==δISS== and closed-loop ==Riemannian contraction== via a small-gain regularization loss; **0%** vs **30%** collision under max disturbance on a 3D drone — stability as a training constraint.
- **[[2606.20031|Neuromorphic-Reinforcement-Framework]]** — Trains a DQN with an SNN-friendly binary state for warehouse AGV pathfinding, then a two-stage ==ANN-to-SNN conversion== (scaling + hard-label distillation) deploys it on the ==SPECK2E== neuromorphic chip with a collision-safety layer; **11,281×** energy reduction, **0.061-0.079 ms**/decision, lossless conversion at 4 time-steps.
- **[[2606.19752|TSIL]]** — A Temporal Self-Imitation RL method making task-completion *speed* a self-supervisory signal via ==configuration-conditioned adaptive temporal targets== and an ==efficiency-weighted self-imitation== replay of only the fastest successes; **0.986** success and **0.674 s** completion across 15 Meta-World tasks, stabilizing training as an optimization anchor.
- **[[2606.15846|FlashNav]]** — A GPU-first navigation-RL framework with a ==vectorized bitmap simulator== and ==fully GPU-resident off-policy DRL== (FastTD3/SAC/DSAC) that trains a deployable policy in seconds; **14.9s** TurtleBot2 / **16.2s** Unitree Go2 to **100%** SR on an RTX 5090, with direct sim-to-real obstacle avoidance — nav training collapsed to under 20 seconds.

#### 4.8 Continual RL & Plasticity Preservation

Training that never stops has its own failure mode: under a non-stationary task stream the network loses plasticity, neurons go dormant, and the policy collapses. These papers ask what a continual learner should actually optimize, and how to keep the optimizer able to learn at all.

- **[[2607.24996|CPR]]** — ==Calibrated Partial Resets== replace all-or-nothing neuron reinitialization with a ==utility-scaled reset coefficient==, a decreasing function of gradient-based utility (==EMA== + ==layer-norm==) pulling low-utility neurons back toward init; no collapse across all **15** SlipperyAnt seeds over **400M** steps, best returns on SlipperyHumanoid and Continual MetaWorld.
- **[[2607.05609|Predictive Continual Learning]]** — Argues the field optimizes the wrong objective: swap forgetting-avoidance for ==Average Lifelong Error==, split into ==Instability== (bias from conflicting tasks) and ==Transient Error== (cold-start cost), exposing a ==Critical Task Duration== past which joint-task learning loses to independent training; its Window Algorithm beats both.

### 5. Offline, Model-Based & Skill-Based RL

This section covers the three ways RL escapes the cost of fresh environment interaction. *Offline RL* learns entirely from a fixed dataset, trading interaction for distribution-shift risk. *Model-based RL* learns a dynamics model and plans or trains inside it, trading real rollouts for model-error accumulation. *Skill-based and hierarchical RL* reuse a library of temporally-extended skills, trading low-level exploration for the cost of building the skill library. All three share a single bet: that structure — a dataset, a model, or a skill prior — can substitute for the unbounded-but-unsafe interaction that pure RL demands.

The recurring failure mode across all three is *staleness*: an offline dataset, a learned model, or a skill prior is a frozen snapshot of dynamics that may no longer hold at deployment. The strongest recent entries make the structure *adaptive* — a world model that updates toward the policy's own state distribution, or a model-based loop that tracks time-varying dynamics.

#### 5.1 Offline & Hybrid RL

Learn from a fixed dataset or transfer across robots — robust to dynamics shift, but only if the model or transfer accounts for it.

- **[[2608.01205|ReBRAC-v2]]** — Modernizes a behavior-regularized actor-critic with a ==normalizing-flow actor== and ==categorical critic ensemble==, via ==staged training== and ==multi-sample critic-guided inference==; **74.8** avg OGBench (**+22.5** over next-best), **90.2** D4RL AntMaze, **33.6** Adroit — matches or beats generative offline-RL policies without their complexity.
- **[[2605.00159|E²DT]]** — Turns ==Decision Transformers== from passive data consumers into active learners via a closed-loop ==k-Determinantal Point Process== experience-selection mechanism scoring quality and diversity in the DT's latent space, then ==debiased mixed replay==; **55.6%** Nut Assembly vs standard DT's **31.7%**, **82.1%** real-robot High-Shelf-Placement vs **52.4%**.
- **[[2512.08280|MPDiffuser]]** — A ==model-based compositional diffusion== framework for offline decision-making interleaving a conditional ==diffusion planner== with a decoupled ==diffusion dynamics model== under an ==alternating sampling== scheme, plus a reward/cost ranker; beats prior diffusion + offline-RL on D4RL/DSRL, adapts to novel dynamics by finetuning only the dynamics model, real Go2.
- **[[2505.13709|Policy-Driven-WM-Adaptation]]** — A method reformulating offline MBRL as a ==maximin== over (world model, policy) solved via ==Stackelberg dynamics==, dynamically adapting WM fidelity to ==policy-induced state visitation==; SOTA on **12** noisy D4RL tasks + **3** Tokamak-control tasks with robustness gains under adversarial perturbation.
- **[[2604.06943|Sustainable-Transfer-RL]]** — A ==Hybrid motion-force control== approach with ==SAC== auto-tuning controller parameters for peg-in-hole, studying cross-robot transfer; native **100%** (UR5e) / **97.98%** (Panda), zero-shot transfer **78.79–93.94%**, and fine-tuning restores **97.98%** with **135** steps vs **>1M** for native convergence — transfer as a sustainability lever.
- **[[2602.00629|OSO-DecQN]]** — An action-free offline-to-online RL method that pre-trains a ==state policy== by discretizing state differences via ==Decoupled Q-Networks== with conservative regularization, then a concurrent ==inverse-dynamics model== converts desired state changes into actions online; accelerates TD3/SAC convergence across D4RL + DMC over a transformer action-free baseline.
- **[[2412.18781|Offline-RL-Action-Perturbation-Eval]]** — A robustness study stress-testing offline (BCQ/TD3+BC/IQL) vs online (PPO/SAC/TD3) legged policies under random + ==Differential-Evolution adversarial== action perturbations; offline RL degrades far more, *medium*-coverage D4RL data beats expert data for robustness, and perturbation augmentation barely helps.
- **[[2311.03351|Uni-O4]]** — A unified RL method that joins offline + online learning under one on-policy ==PPO== objective (no added conservatism), with ==ensemble BC + disagreement== and an ==AM-Q== multi-step offline evaluator; SOTA on **14/20** D4RL tasks and stable offline-to-online fine-tuning reaching real-robot **1.62 m/s** locomotion.
- **[[2205.03353|How-to-Spend]]** — A ==policy-finetuning== study unifying kickstarting and offline RL: R-MPO/R-CRR mix a teacher's action distribution into the ==MPO==/==CRR== prior while training on mixed teacher-offline/student-online data; real stacking **81.5%** (R-MPO), offline CRR alone beats the teacher with no online interaction at higher budgets, ~1:1 ratio near-optimal.
- **[[2110.06169|IQL]]** — **Implicit Q-Learning** trains a value function via ==expectile regression== over in-dataset actions (never querying OOD actions) and extracts the policy by ==advantage-weighted regression==; excels on D4RL AntMaze trajectory-stitching, outperforming AWAC/CQL and fine-tuning cleanly online.
- **[[2106.02039|Trajectory Transformer]]** — Frames offline RL as ==autoregressive sequence modeling==: a ==Transformer decoder== over discretized state-action-reward tokens unifies policy, value, and dynamics, planned via ==beam search== (reward-to-go, likelihood, or future-state conditioning); matches/beats CQL/MBOP on D4RL, SOTA sparse-reward AntMaze combined with an IQL Q-function.
- **[[2106.01345|Decision Transformer]]** — Reframes offline RL as ==conditional sequence modeling==: a GPT-style Transformer predicts actions from states, actions, and ==returns-to-go== via supervised learning, no Bellman backups; matches/beats CQL on Atari from **1%** of DQN-replay data and on D4RL, fine-grained control via the target return — sibling to [[2106.02039|Trajectory Transformer]].
- **[[2103.06326|S4RL]]** — Folds ==state-based data augmentation== (Gaussian noise, adversarial perturbation, state mix-up) directly into the ==Q-learning== objective, averaging Q-values over augmented states to smooth the value function for offline RL; up to **3×** gains over CQL on dexterous MetaWorld/RoboSuite tasks, most robust under **5–25%** data scarcity.
- **[[2006.09359|AWAC]]** — Bridges offline pretraining and online fine-tuning via an ==advantage-weighted maximum-likelihood== actor update that implicitly constrains the policy without modeling the behavior policy, avoiding online-fine-tuning conservatism; solves dexterous pen rotation in **120K** timesteps where prior methods fail.
- **[[2006.04779|CQL]]** — ==Conservative Q-Learning== adds a Q-value regularizer minimizing ==out-of-distribution-action== values while maximizing in-distribution ones, provably lower-bounding true policy value; **2–5×** higher D4RL returns on multi-modal datasets and the only method to beat BC on AntMaze/Adroit/Kitchen — the canonical pessimistic-Q offline-RL baseline.
- **[[2005.13239|MOPO]]** — A ==Model-based offline RL== algorithm that penalizes reward by the ==uncertainty of a learned dynamics ensemble== (epistemic + aleatoric), letting the policy exploit the model only where it is trustworthy; beats model-free offline baselines on D4RL and generalizes OOD to **4016.6** avg return on halfcheetah-jump from a dataset capped at **1808.6**.

#### 5.2 Model-Based RL for Control

Learn a dynamics model and plan or adapt within it — sample-efficient, but model error compounds over the horizon.

- **[[2607.06535|Neural-ESO]]** — A dual-pathway architecture pairing a Lipschitz-constrained ==predictive neural network== with a corrective ==Extended State Observer== plus online ==Total Disturbance Retraining==; **0.37 cm** in-distribution quadrotor tracking error vs **10.95 cm** PD baseline, **53.4%** error reduction under severe OOD maneuvers.
- **[[2607.00215|ELMP]]** — BC-pretrains a tool-aware neural motion planner on point-cloud-conditioned kinematic-chain geometry, then fine-tunes via self-supervised ==Analytical Policy Gradients== through a differentiable kinematic rollout + ==SDF collision== loss; collisions **31.3%→8.9%**, **84.8%** avg success beating AIT*/MπNets at **60x** less adaptation data.
- **[[2607.00066|Endovascular RL-NMPC]]** — Decouples strategic endovascular navigation (a ==SAC== policy over YOLOv5/ResNet50 vessel features) from safety-constrained low-level execution (an ==NMPC== layer enforcing kinematic/curvature/vessel-boundary limits); **>96%** sim / **90%** in-vitro success, **29.3%** fewer steps, **zero** boundary violations on a physical phantom.
- **[[2604.25416|Biased Dreams]]** — Diagnostic study: Dreamer-style ==RSSM== latent world models show an ==attractor bias==, pulling imagined rollouts toward well-supported latent regions regardless of accuracy, so ==ensemble-disagreement uncertainty== decouples from true rollout error and systematically overestimates reward on DMC tasks — proprioceptive uncertainty guards do not transfer.
- **[[2604.02260|Time-Varying-MBRL]]** — An episodic ==MBRL== method for ==time-varying dynamics== via a ==variation-budget model in an RKHS==, with Full-Reset (R-OMBRL) and Sliding-Window (SW-OMBRL) data strategies + Bayesian-NN ensembles; proves sublinear ==dynamic regret== and adapts a real RC car to decaying throttle where stationary MBRL fails.
- **[[2504.18471|Action-Flow-Matching]]** — Learns a latent ==dynamics-regime== representation from prediction-vs-realized-transition discrepancies (no ground-truth model needed), then a ==conditional flow-matching== model via ==counterfactual simulation== refines planned actions to match that regime; **81.4%** UGV success (**+34.2%**), **6.6%** lower Crazyflie tracking error.
- **[[2603.13333|STL-SVPIO]]** — Reframes ==Signal Temporal Logic== control synthesis as variational inference, using ==Stein Variational Gradient Descent== over a swarm of control particles against differentiable STL robustness + differentiable physics; **92–100%** satisfaction on multi-agent long-horizon tasks, synthesizes a 7-DoF Franka reach and a Half Cheetah backflip directly from spec.
- **[[2510.21232|Confusing-World-Models]]** — Formalizes a ==confusing instance== (a nearby alternative model inverting optimal-vs-suboptimal policy ranking) and its ==suboptimality cost== (minimal KL divergence), found via ==adversarial training with Lagrangian relaxation==; well-trained models resist confusion far longer (**1506** vs **165** iterations), localizing to wall dynamics.
- **[[2510.06179|DiffMPC]]** — A GPU-native ==differentiable MPC== solver exploiting OCP time-sparsity via a custom ==symmetric-stair-preconditioned PCG== on the SQP-KKT system, implemented in JAX for RL/IL gradients; **>4×** faster than mpc.pytorch/trajax, **100%** vs **70%** simulated drift-through-puddles success, deployed on a real Toyota Supra.
- **[[2510.04280|PO-MPC]]** — A model-based RL framework recasting ==MPPI-based== sampling-policy learning as a ==KL-regularized== problem against a separate *learned intermediate prior* matching the online planner's distribution, cutting policy-mismatch and gradient variance; matches/beats TD-MPC2 and BMPC on DMC and learns high-dim HumanoidBench tasks where they fail — both as limiting cases.
- **[[2507.13088|ZipMPC]]** — Learns a ==context-dependent MPC cost== via ==differentiable MPC== so a short-horizon MPC imitates a long-horizon one, compressing upcoming track curvature into the cost function; imitation RMSE **0.068** vs **0.203** baseline, **68.5-87.9%** less optimization time, real-hardware racing where short-horizon MPC failed.
- **[[2505.15754|Temporally-Extended-Actions]]** — Optimizes both the primitive action and its continuous ==duration== in planning/MBRL via a learned ==Temporally-Extended Dynamics Function== plus a non-stationary ==Multi-Armed Bandit (UCB+EMA)== selecting max duration per episode; solves Mountain Car at duration≥4 vs primitive actions' **≥60**, up to **8×** faster MuJoCo MBRL.
- **[[2505.01059|Tensor-Planning]]** — A sampling-based ==MPC== framework (MTP) enhancing control-trajectory exploration via ==tensor sampling over randomized multipartite graphs== + spline interpolation, with a ==β-mixing== of global tensor and local CEM samples in vectorized JAX; lower cost and higher success than MPPI/PS on PushT, Cube-In-Hand, and G1-Standup at real-time rates.
- **[[2503.18871|Bootstrapped-MPC]]** — A model-based RL method (BMPC) learning the policy by ==expert imitation== (KL to the ==MPC== planner) and the value via model-based ==on-policy n-step TD-learning==, with ==lazy-reanalyze== buffer updates; cuts steps-to-solve **~300%** vs TD-MPC2 (360k→90k) at **3M** params on DMControl + HumanoidBench, nearly closing the policy–planner gap.
- **[[2502.02133|MPC-RL-Survey]]** — A survey classifying ==MPC-RL combinations== under an ==actor-critic== lens into nine sub-categories (MPC as expert actor / deployed policy / critic) and four architectural archetypes (Integrated, Hierarchical, Parallel, Parameterized); a meta-framework for when a planner should supply the actor, the policy, or the value estimate.
- **[[2305.17250|RaMP]]** — A transferable self-supervised RL method: an offline phase learns ==random-feature Q-basis functions== (each feature's cumulative return), then online a new reward is approximated as a linear combination and its Q-function reconstructed for ==MPC== planning; beats model-based and successor-feature transfer across manipulation + locomotion, robust to pixel inputs.
- **[[2212.02603|L2O-MPC]]** — A method that *learns the update rule* in sampling-based ==MPC== via a ==GRU-style learned optimizer== fed individual trajectory costs from Halton-sequence sampling, trained by ==imitation learning (DAGGER)== to mimic a high-sample expert; **100%** Franka-Reacher at **4×** fewer samples and only a **4%** drop at **8×** fewer on Franka-Obstacles vs MPPI.
- **[[2207.07560|SkiMo]]** — A model-based method that jointly learns a ==skill repertoire== + a ==skill-dynamics model== predicting the H-step outcome of a whole skill (not single steps), enabling ==MPC with CEM== in abstract skill space; solves long-horizon Maze + Kitchen with **~5×** fewer interactions than SPiRL and accurate prediction over **500** timesteps.
- **[[2010.13303|Trajectory-wise-MCL]]** — A model-based ==RL== method for dynamics generalization using a ==multi-headed dynamics model== where each head specializes via a ==trajectory-wise oracle loss== updating only the most accurate head over whole trajectory segments, with adaptive online planning; up to **~3×** over the next best on HalfCheetah, robust zero-shot on CrippledAnt/SlimHumanoid.
- **[[1909.11652|PDDM]]** — Online planning with an ==ensemble of deep dynamics models== (epistemic uncertainty) driving a refined ==MPC== optimizer combining ==reward-weighted refinement== and ==temporal action-smoothing filtering==; **100%** success on 90° Baoding-ball rotation on a real 24-DoF Shadow Hand within **2 hours** of real-world training, where model-free RL and prior MBRL fail.
- **[[1907.04202|VI-MPC]]** — A unified Bayesian stochastic-MPC framework recasting CEM/MPPI as ==moment matching== over the optimal-trajectory posterior, instantiated as ==PaETS== which models multimodal optimal actions with a ==GMM== + entropy regularization; matches/surpasses SAC across MuJoCo locomotion at **~10×** higher sample efficiency, beating PETS/MPPI.
- **[[1810.13400|Differentiable-MPC]]** — A method differentiating through ==MPC== via ==implicit differentiation of the KKT conditions== at the solver fixed point (backward pass = a modified ==LQR== problem, box-constraint aware), learning cost/dynamics end-to-end from a task loss; beats LSTM policies in imitation data-efficiency and out-performs SysId when expert dynamics are non-realizable.

#### 5.3 Skill-Based & Hierarchical RL

Reuse temporally-extended skills and memory to shorten the horizon — at the cost of building and maintaining the skill library.

- **[[2607.23726|HRL-SAC]]** — A two-level hierarchy running ==entropy-regularized SAC== at *both* levels — a ==meta-controller== picking sub-goals, a continuous low-level controller driven by an ==intrinsic reward== (negative L2 to sub-goal); **3.04×** extrinsic reward, **1.77×** success and **2.08×** search coverage over flat SAC, at **0.40M** vs **4.80M** environment steps.
- **[[2607.23515|LEACL]]** — Replaces hand-built curriculum specs with ==bi-level LLM prompting==: the LLM decomposes a long-horizon task into ==PDDL== subtasks, then emits ==parametric task spaces== and difficulty-ordered parameters feeding ==ADR==-style automatic curriculum learning under sparse rewards only; **99.8%** / **89.4%** on LIBERO+ tasks, beating dense-reward LEAGUE on **4/5**.
- **[[2607.08354|SkillPlug]]** — A plug-in ==unsupervised skill-mining== module adding a frozen ==latent skill library== + router to visuomotor policies via ==reconstruction==, ==KL regularization==, ==behavioral skill alignment==, and ==skill disentanglement== losses; DISCOVERSE jujube-pick **6.7%→94.0%**, few-shot LIBERO-Object **47.4%→79.6%** with **5** demos, only router/head fine-tuned.
- **[[2607.06323|LAMP (Dexterous Hand Manipulation)]]** — Learns a ==Latent Motion Prior Module== (history-conditioned, KL-regularized latent hand-action space from demos) shared between an IL policy predicting ==latent offsets== and online ==residual SAC== confined to local, contact-preserving corrections; dexterous IL **56.25%→98.75%** after RL, vs raw-joint's **15%** and PCA's **65%**.
- **[[2606.28323|DexCompose]]** — A two-stage pipeline reusing single-task dexterous policies: ==Finger Attribution== (LLM-agent + release tests) assigns ==finger-level action ownership==, then a ==Dual Residual Stabilizer== (PPO residuals per subspace) stabilizes Task A while adapting Task B, no retraining; **77.4%** composite success across 16 combinations, **+15.8pp** over FullRes.
- **[[2606.26428|Play2Perfect]]** — A two-stage dexterous framework pretrains a ==goal-conditioned play policy== (diverse objects, 6D pose control, randomized goals) task-agnostically in sim, then finetunes with sparse RL on CAD-defined assembly goals; solves all tasks in **2-5h** (**33×** faster than scratch), up to **9/10** real 2mm-clearance insertion, zero-shot sim-to-real.
- **[[2209.08959|TACO-RL]]** — A hierarchical offline framework in the [[1903.01973|Play-LMP]] lineage: a low-level policy learns latent skills from play via a ==Conditional Variational Auto-Encoder==, chained by a high-level ==Conservative Q-Learning== policy with ==hindsight relabeling==; **2.7** avg chain length vs LMP's **1.8** on CALVIN, **27%** two-task success (**10×** over LMP's **2.7%**).

- **[[2010.11944|SPiRL]]** — A skill-based method that learns a ==continuous skill embedding== + a ==state-conditioned skill prior== offline from unstructured data via a ==latent-variable model==, then regularizes ==max-entropy SAC== toward the prior for hierarchical downstream RL; reliably solves Maze/Block-Stacking/Kitchen where flat RL fails, robust to sub-optimal data.
- **[[2310.04582|PULSE]]** — Distills a robust ==PHC+== motion imitator into a ==probabilistic latent space== with a ==proprioception-conditioned prior==, exposed to downstream tasks via ==residual-action hierarchical RL==; **97.1%** AMASS-test imitation, faster convergence than ASE/CALM across generative + VR-tracking tasks without adversarial style rewards.
- **[[2511.01107|SLAP]]** — A ==Shortcut Learning for Abstract Planning== method that builds a two-level planning graph and learns ==PPO shortcut policies== between abstract states that generalize over objects via relevance-based object substitution; **32–73%** shorter plans and **100%** success on long-horizon manipulation where pure and hierarchical RL hit **0%**.
- **[[2510.20264|OpTI-BFM]]** — Online task-inference for pretrained ==Behavior Foundation Models== extending [[1606.05312|Successor Features]] zero-shot transfer: estimates reward from interaction via ==regularized least-squares== task-embedding and an ==Upper Confidence Bound== objective; near-Oracle performance within **~5 episodes** on DeepMind Control Suite.
- **[[2510.14968|RDD]]** — A Retrieval-Based Demonstration Decomposer aligning sub-tasks for a hierarchical VLM planner with the *low-level policy's* training data by casting decomposition as ==optimal partitioning== scored by ==retrieval similarity== to nearest-neighbor demos; **74.9%** RLBench (near expert **75.1%**), **77.9%** from one demo, mIoU **0.706** vs UVD's **0.506**.
- **[[2502.03752|SISL]]** — A ==Self-Improving Skill Learning== method for skill-based meta-RL: a decoupled ==skill-improvement policy== perturbs near-distribution trajectories to find better behaviors, with ==maximum-return relabeling== and a dynamic offline/online mixing coefficient; stays robust under noise (Kitchen σ=0.3 return **3.48** vs SiMPL's **0.81**) where baselines collapse.
- **[[2501.10395|t-DGR]]** — A lifelong-learning method via ==trajectory-based deep generative replay== (a ==diffusion== generator conditioned on timestep) + an ==AttentionTuner== that guides Transformer self-attention with human memory-dependency annotations; **81.9%** CW10 / **83.9%** CW20 and **99.8%** Mortar Mayhem vs **20.8%** vanilla, with **14–16×** less annotation effort.

> [!tip] Structure Substitutes for Interaction — Until It Goes Stale
> Offline datasets, learned models, and skill priors all buy sample efficiency by encoding structure that replaces unbounded interaction — but each is a *frozen snapshot* of dynamics that decays at deployment. The robust entries make the structure adaptive: [[2505.13709|Policy-Driven-WM-Adaptation]] updates the model toward the policy's visitation, [[2604.02260|Time-Varying-MBRL]] tracks drifting dynamics with bounded regret, and [[2003.01239|Evolutionary-Meta-Learning-Legged]] re-adapts in 50 real rollouts. The when-to-use rule: pick offline/model-based when interaction is expensive *and* dynamics are stable; add an adaptation loop the moment they drift. For online world-model adaptation as a sim-to-real mechanism see [[15_Sim-to-Real-Transfer#4. Real2Sim2Real Loops & Digital Twins]]; for the self-evolving framing where the model improves itself see [[16_Self-Evolving-VLA-WAM#5. Self-Evolving WAMs]]; and for [[2501.10395|t-DGR]]'s generative-replay answer to the same staleness problem, see [[09_Robot-Memory#6.2 Experience Distillation & Memory-Driven Evolution]].

## Part C — Capabilities & Frontier

*Where the IL→RL recipes meet hard embodied capabilities — legged locomotion, aerial navigation, and whole-body control — and where they still fail.*

### 6. RL for Locomotion, Navigation & Whole-Body Control

Locomotion and navigation are where RL most decisively beats imitation learning: the action spaces are high-frequency and contact-rich, demonstrations are hard to collect (you cannot teleoperate a backflip), and a well-shaped reward plus a fast simulator yields policies that transfer. This section organizes that capability frontier by embodiment regime — legged ground robots that must adapt online to terrain and hardware limits, aerial and navigation agents that must act under partial observability and perception loss, and whole-body humanoid controllers that must coordinate dozens of joints into stable, expressive motion.

The cross-cutting mechanism is *adaptation*: a locomotion policy is only as good as its ability to absorb a distribution it did not train on — new terrain, a damaged motor, a thermal limit, an occluded sensor. The strongest entries make adaptation a first-class architectural feature (long-context memory, residual correction, mixture-of-experts gating) rather than relying on domain randomization alone.

#### 6.1 Legged Locomotion & Adaptation

Legged policies must adapt online — to terrain, hardware limits, and morphology changes — beyond what domain randomization covers at training time.

- **[[2607.11624|SKooP]]** — Combines ==morphological symmetries== with a controlled ==Koopman autoencoder== inside an online actor-critic RL loop, feeding Koopman-predicted latent states as ==privileged observations== to a ==G-equivariant/invariant==-constrained critic; **8.49%** Symmetry Index and **82.77%**/**77.70%** success on trained/mirrored push-door tasks.
- **[[2607.07370|ABot-C0]]** — A quadruped ==Behavior Foundation Model== with a video-to-motion ==data pyramid==, ==DAgger-distilled Flow-Matching== tracking + ==Manifold-Calibrated Reference Conditioning==, and hardware-safe ==NP3O constrained RL==; unseen MPJPE **24.61→14.79mm** (scaling), **12.53mm** with MCRC, **zero** torque/velocity violations at **3.0 m/s**, **83.2%** all-terrain success.
- **[[2606.10288|MARCH]]** — A model-assisted ==RL== method for perceptive humanoid locomotion over sparse footholds: simplified dynamics generate safe ==reference trajectories==, a ==CLF-inspired PPO== teacher tracks them, and a depth-vision student (CNN + transformer + ==Mixture Density Network==) is distilled; **~50%** fewer teacher episodes, **12%** less torque, real G1 stepping-stones.
- **[[2606.04718|CoRe-MoE]]** — A multi-terrain humanoid-locomotion method via a two-stage ==asymmetric actor-critic MoE==: command velocity drives gait selection, a terrain-aware MoE refines actions, with a ==SwAV contrastive objective== forcing expert specialization; **99.13%** flat-terrain success, **94.3%** Up-Stairs (vs 84.6% without SwAV), zero-shot G1 to **2.5 m/s**.
- **[[2606.05143|HORIZON]]** — A ==recoverability-governed curriculum== expanding physical-domain ranges via a ==two-part recoverability gate== (feasibility + checkpoint-comparison) plus ==rollback/boundary refinement==; **28.1%** OOD-all success (vs **10.3%** wide DR), a four-domain core curriculum hits **36.8%** at **2.3×** lower cost, zero-shot real Go2 transfer under locked joints/payload.
- **[[2605.27046|Thermal-Aware-Residual]]** — A ==thermal-aware residual policy== over a nominal locomotion policy, with a whole-body ==lumped-parameter thermal model== giving real-time motor-temperature feedback and adaptive rewards scaling intervention with heat; overheating **70%→<10%**, a real Unitree A1 covers **650 m** with a 3 kg payload keeping motors **<50 °C**.
- **[[2509.23745|LocoFormer]]** — A generalist-locomotion method via massive-scale RL on procedurally-generated robots + aggressive DR, with a ==Transformer-XL== long-context policy retaining memory across trials; **0.96** normalized displacement on 10 unseen morphologies (few-shot 0.98), with zero-shot real adaptation to locked limbs, wheel failures, and payloads.
- **[[2508.00939|BarlowWalk]]** — A single-stage bipedal-locomotion method fusing ==Barlow Twins== self-supervised representation learning with an asymmetric actor-critic ==PPO== (privileged terrain critic, proprioception-only actor); a TRON1 robot clears **6** terrain types up to difficulty level **6**, trained in **4 hours** on one RTX 4090 with **4,096** parallel envs.
- **[[2505.09979|Diverse-Natural-Behaviors]]** — A quadrupedal controller pairing semi-supervised ==InfoGAIL== (latent skill distribution + ==RIM== regularization, preventing mode collapse) with ==Evolutionary Adversarial Simulator Identification==; **78%** success, **1.1 m/s** avg speed on a 7×10m course, EASI converging in **<10 min** from **80s** real data.
- **[[2405.10830|CTS]]** — Trains teacher (privileged) and student (proprioceptive) policies *concurrently* under a shared ==asymmetric actor-critic==, guiding the student with its own RL objective (not pure imitation) plus an encoder ==reconstruction loss==; **21.85%** lower tracking error and higher push-recovery than two-stage T-S on Unitree A1/Aliengo/LimX P1 biped.
- **[[2403.01928|ZSL-RPPO]]** — A direct ==Recurrent PPO== (POMDP, ==Gated Recurrent Policy Network==) that bypasses teacher-student imitation entirely, paired with extensive ==domain randomization==; **100%** zero-shot real success on stairs/deformable/oily terrain (vs T-S's 60%, RMA's 0%) on Unitree A1/Aliengo, no fine-tuning.
- **[[2107.03996|LocoTransformer]]** — An end-to-end vision-guided quadrupedal-locomotion method with a ==Transformer== fusing depth + proprioception via ==cross-modal self-attention==; **290.5–663%** fewer collisions in sim and **92%** farther with fewer collisions on a real Unitree A1, with attention focusing on obstacles — the foundational cross-modal locomotion transformer.
- **[[2201.08117|ANYmal Perceptive Locomotion]]** — A ==recurrent belief encoder== with attention fuses proprioceptive + exteroceptive input into a belief state, via ==teacher-student PPO== distillation + heavy ==domain randomization==; zero-shot sim-to-real, **zero falls** over a 2.2km alpine hike, **2×** faster obstacle courses than proprioceptive-only, robust to occlusion and snow.
- **[[2004.00784|Imitating Animals]]** — ==Motion retargeting== (IK) maps animal mocap onto a quadruped, ==PPO motion imitation== with domain randomization tracks it, then ==latent-space domain adaptation== (information-bottleneck encoding + AWR search over ~50 real episodes) transfers to hardware; a real Laikago hits **1.08 m/s** trotting, faster than its factory controller.
- **[[2003.01239|Evolutionary-Meta-Learning-Legged]]** — An ==ES-MAML== meta-learning method with a noise-tolerant gradient-free ==Batch Hill-Climbing== inner-loop operator for fast legged adaptation; on a real Minitaur, **+100%** forward velocity under a mass-voltage shift within **50** rollouts (150 s), beating gradient-based PG-MAML which shows negative adaptation.
- **[[1703.03400|MAML]]** — A ==model-agnostic meta-learning== algorithm learning an initialization maximally sensitive to task gradients via ==bi-level optimization== (inner gradient-descent adaptation, outer meta-gradient); **98.7%** 1-shot Omniglot and 1–3-step adaptation on MuJoCo locomotion — precursor to [[2003.01239|Evolutionary-Meta-Learning-Legged]]'s ES-MAML.

Aerial and navigation agents act under partial observability, perception loss, and tight collision constraints — demanding memory, active perception, and reward shaping beyond standard locomotion.

- **[[2606.29934|RoamFlow]]** — A ==MeanFlow==-based generative navigation policy modeling interval-level displacement for few-step trajectory generation, trained via offline IL then online clipped ==PPO== refinement with a ==trajectory evaluator== selecting the safest candidate; **68.7%** SR / **61.9%** SPL on Gibson (beats NavDP by **9.4pp**/**14.4pp**) at **19.6ms**, **100%** real Go2 success.

- **[[2605.14810|CaMeRL]]** — A collision-aware + memory-enhanced UAV-navigation method: a ==VAE== extracts safety-relevant latents from depth (supervised by collision-aware depth maps) and an ==LSTM== integrates temporal context for partial observability, trained with PPO; **0.77** success in ultra-small-obstacle vs MAVRL's **0.29**, real dense-forest flight at **1.4 m/s**.

- **[[2511.21083|Dual-Agent-VIO]]** — An RL method for adaptive visual-inertial odometry: a ==Select Agent== skips the visual pipeline on redundant frames from IMU signals while a ==Fusion Agent== adaptively weights IMU vs sparse VO under an uncertainty-aware reward; **39 FPS** (**1.77×** DPVO) at **4.37 GB** (**−45%** VRAM), competitive **0.092 m** ATE — RL as a compute-vs-accuracy scheduler.
- **[[2509.11481|RAPTOR]]** — A quadrotor ==foundation policy== distilling **1000** SAC teacher policies into a **2084**-parameter recurrent (GRU) student via ==Meta-Imitation Learning==, performing in-context emergent system identification from interaction history; **0.07–0.19 m** RMSE across 10 real quadrotors + 2 sims, R²=**0.949** thrust-to-weight prediction, 10-fold context extrapolation.

### 7. Open Problems & Failure Modes

Robot policy learning has reached the point where the median manipulation and locomotion task is solvable — but the failures have moved upstream, to the *signal* itself. Where early failures were about optimization (RL would not converge), the 2026 failures are about what the policy is optimizing *toward*: a demonstration distribution that caps quality, a learned reward that can be hacked, a frozen offline dataset that goes stale, an evaluation metric that does not predict deployment. The problems below cluster into three roots — *signal mis-specification* (the reward or demonstration is the wrong target), *distribution staleness* (the structure substituting for interaction decays), and *evaluation-deployment gap* (the metric you optimize is not the outcome you ship).

#### 7.1 Signal Mis-Specification

The learning signal itself is the wrong target — demonstrations cap quality, learned rewards get hacked, and the supervised loss does not track task success.

- **==The demo ceiling==** — Behavior cloning cannot exceed the demonstrator's skill, and offline policy selection is unsolved because validation loss does not correlate with success ([[2108.03298|Robomimic]]). The IL→RL bridge ([[2407.16677|ResiP]], [[2311.03351|Uni-O4]]) breaks the ceiling but reintroduces reward design and interaction cost — there is no free lunch on the signal-cost spectrum.
- **==Reward hacking in learned reward models==** — VLM reward models ([[2603.02115|Robometer]], [[2603.28730|SOLE-R1]]) supply dense progress but the policy exploits their perceptual blind spots; [[2603.28730|SOLE-R1]] mitigates this with verifiable rewards and [[2512.20675|VLM-Reward-Objectives]] shows the objective decides robustness — no reward model is hacking-proof under optimization.
- **==Multi-step reward collapse==** — Every learned reward objective evaluated in [[2512.20675|VLM-Reward-Objectives]] collapses on multi-step tasks (door-open VOC **6.70%**), even the triplet-loss winner; dense progress estimation degrades exactly where long-horizon tasks need it most.

#### 7.2 Distribution Staleness

The structure that substitutes for interaction — an offline dataset, a learned model, a skill prior — is a frozen snapshot that decays at deployment.

- **==Offline distribution shift==** — Offline RL underperforms IL on human data ([[2108.03298|Robomimic]]) and a frozen world model drifts from the policy's own state distribution; [[2505.13709|Policy-Driven-WM-Adaptation]] makes the model policy-aware but adaptive MBRL is not yet a default, and the trade-off between fidelity and deployment-relevance is unresolved.
- **==Time-varying dynamics==** — Hardware wears, terrain changes, motors heat ([[2605.27046|Thermal-Aware-Residual]]); [[2604.02260|Time-Varying-MBRL]] gives bounded dynamic regret and [[2003.01239|Evolutionary-Meta-Learning-Legged]] re-adapts in 50 rollouts, but continual online adaptation without forgetting remains brittle outside narrow settings.
- **==Catastrophic forgetting==** — Lifelong policy learning forgets prior skills; [[2509.04259|RL's-Razor]] shows on-policy RL forgets less (KL-minimal) and [[2501.10395|t-DGR]] uses generative replay, but the mechanism that makes RL forgetting-resistant does not transfer cleanly to the SFT and BC regimes most robot data still uses.

#### 7.3 Evaluation–Deployment Gap

The metric you optimize is not the outcome you ship — validation loss, sim success, and memory benchmarks all fail to predict real-world deployment.

- **==Validation loss ≠ task success==** — The oldest open problem ([[2108.03298|Robomimic]]): offline policy selection has no reliable proxy, so the field over-relies on expensive real-world rollouts to know which checkpoint to ship.
- **==Memory is the silent failure==** — [[2502.10550|MIKASA]] isolates memory as the limiting factor — PPO-LSTM/SAC/TD-MPC2 collapse on 5–9-item memory tasks and a real π0.5 gets **10%** on long-horizon occlusion; most benchmarks do not test memory, so policies look better than they deploy.
- **==Safety guarantees degrade in contact==** — Safety filters with formal guarantees ([[2605.26452|Koopman-CBF-SAC]], [[2605.09772|GP-Safe-Exploration]]) hold in smooth dynamics but [[2605.26452|Koopman-CBF-SAC]] degrades exactly in contact-rich locomotion where guarantees matter most — the certified set is fragile where deployment is hardest.

**Robot-RL Failure Modes — Decision Matrix**

| Problem | Remediation Path |
|---|---|
| BC capped by demonstrator skill | IL→RL refinement ([[2407.16677\|ResiP]], [[2311.03351\|Uni-O4]]) — reintroduces reward + interaction cost |
| Learned reward model gets hacked | Verifiable rewards ([[2603.28730\|SOLE-R1]]) or comparison structure ([[2603.02115\|Robometer]]) — not hacking-proof |
| Reward collapses on multi-step tasks | [[2512.20675\|VLM-Reward-Objectives]] (exposes collapse) — open; objective choice helps but does not solve |
| Frozen offline model drifts from policy | [[2505.13709\|Policy-Driven-WM-Adaptation]] — policy-aware WM; not yet default |
| Dynamics drift at deployment | [[2604.02260\|Time-Varying-MBRL]] + [[2003.01239\|Evolutionary-Meta-Learning-Legged]] — narrow settings only |
| Catastrophic forgetting across tasks | [[2509.04259\|RL's-Razor]] (RL) + [[2501.10395\|t-DGR]] (replay) — RL mechanism doesn't transfer to SFT/BC |
| No proxy for offline policy selection | Real-world rollouts — [[2108.03298\|Robomimic]] documents the gap; still unsolved |
| Memory failures unseen until deployment | [[2502.10550\|MIKASA]] (diagnoses) — most benchmarks omit memory |
| Safety guarantees fail in contact | [[2605.26452\|Koopman-CBF-SAC]] (degrades) + [[2605.09772\|GP-Safe-Exploration]] — fragile in contact-rich regimes |

> - [[2512.20675|VLM-Reward-Objectives]] — Exposed that every learned reward objective collapses on multi-step tasks, pinpointing where dense reward modeling still fails.
> - [[2502.10550|MIKASA]] — Isolated memory as a silent failure mode that standard benchmarks miss, explaining the deploy-time gap on long-horizon occluded tasks.
> - [[2509.04259|RL's-Razor]] — Established the KL-minimization principle behind why on-policy RL resists forgetting — and implicitly why SFT does not.

^key-papers-7

> [!tip] The Common Root Is a Mis-Specified Learning Signal
> All three clusters trace to one root: **the learning signal is a proxy that diverges from the deployment objective.** Demonstrations proxy skill but cap it; learned rewards proxy success but get hacked; offline datasets proxy dynamics but go stale; validation loss proxies deployment but does not predict it. The IL→RL spectrum is not a hierarchy where RL "wins" — it is a menu of proxies, each cheap in one currency (annotation, interaction, safety) and expensive in another. Progress comes from making the proxy *verifiable* ([[2603.28730|SOLE-R1]]'s RLVR), *adaptive* ([[2505.13709|Policy-Driven-WM-Adaptation]]), or *diagnosable* ([[2502.10550|MIKASA]]). This is the same mis-specified-target root that drives the sim-to-real reality gap — sysid error ≠ transfer quality — so cross-reference [[15_Sim-to-Real-Transfer#7. Open Problems]] for the simulator-fidelity angle and [[16_Self-Evolving-VLA-WAM#8. Open Problems & Failure Modes]] for the self-improvement loop where a mis-specified signal compounds across generations.

^insight-7
