## WAM — doc-level synthesis

### Capsule

WAM is a tightly-themed, internally honest agenda built on one durable first principle (the imagination's grounding lives at train time, and what you imagine should be a compact control-relevant latent, not pixels) carved into two clusters: A (substrate/encoding: density, force-modality, bottleneck-type) and B (training-time signal: discrete contact modes, self-evolution gating, forward-inverse calibration, data-engine filters). The cluster premises are correct and well-cited, and every direction is a clean, falsifiable A/B. But the agenda is uniformly **partially-pre-empted**: in six of seven units the *mechanism* is settled and conceded, leaving each bet to live on a narrow conjunction of qualifiers, and in nearly every case a single uncited 2026 paper (FAWAM 2606.08555 for A2/A, Pri4R 2603.01549 for A1, OmniVTA 2603.19201/ReTac-ACT for B1, EWAM 2606.12690 for B2, Fast-WAM/GeoSem-WAM 2603.16666/2606.03188 for B3, RoboCurate 2602.18742 for B4) sits on or inside the claimed-empty cell. The value therefore concentrates not in any mechanism but in the **measurement apparatus**: matched-capacity sweeps, executability-decoupled substrates, head-to-head protocols, and shared OOD/sensorless benchmarks. As a body of bets this is high-coherence, medium-novelty, and uniformly de-risk-before-commit. Grades cluster A-/B+ (A3 the cleanest, A2/B1/B4 the most eroded).

### Cross-direction pattern

**The mechanism is always conceded and the bet always survives only as a conjunction of adjectives that one uncited 2026 paper has already half-occupied — so the real contribution in every unit is the matched/controlled measurement, not the method.** Every direction follows the identical arc: (1) concede the mechanism is settled and cross-backbone (drop-head OOD, force-as-cause, semantic-over-reconstruction, structural-jump-at-contact, imagined-RL-improves-SR, forward-inverse asymmetry, engine-beats-real-collection); (2) narrow the live claim to a stacked tuple; (3) get caught by a dated off-card paper that holds most of the tuple. The recurring rescue is identical too: own the *protocol* (matched-capacity / iso-latency three-arm sweep, executability substrate, bounded-budget gate, generator-decoupled filter A/B), pre-register the null, and make a tie publishable.

### Sharpest intra-doc contradiction

**A2 (commit to a sensorless imagined-wrench rollout) vs the A-cluster Skeptic's own verdict that A2 is dead-on-arrival post-FAWAM.** The A2 card stakes its novelty on "future-tense + 6-DoF wrench + acted-on-in-rollout, no single paper holds all three"; the cluster-A review states FAWAM (2606.08555) holds the exact triple and its sensorless-leaning base ablation (73.75%) already books A2's H1/H3 result. The intra-A2 review then *corrects its own correction* — FAWAM's base encodes measured F/T at inference, so it is a with-sensor number, restoring A2's sensorless wedge. The contradiction is unresolved inside the doc: the same evidence (FAWAM-w/o-Res = 73.75%) is read as "A2 is overtaken" (cluster Skeptic, A2 Finding 2 first pass) and "A2's wedge survives, FAWAM is just the missing with-sensor baseline" (A2/A verdict topFix). A secondary, cleaner contradiction: B3's H5 bets IDM-reward beats pixel-reward, while WAM-RL's own ablation (cited in B3) found pixel-MSE is the *best* reconstruction reward — an internal contradiction inside a single direction's card.

### Recurring blind spots

- **Calibration/honesty under self-induced distribution drift.** A2 (imagined wrench confidently wrong at contact make/break), B2 (recovery policy off the imagined-failure manifold — the dangerous failures are the unimaginable ones), B3 (a mis-calibrated train-time WM fails *silently* — collisions/drops with no halt). No direction carries an uncertainty/abstention instrument; nobody asks what the policy does when its imagination is confidently wrong. This is the same FPR-bound-preserved-through-the-loop hole flagged across the vault's runtime-monitoring audits.
- **Statistical power / benchmark noise.** A1 (is a 5 pp LIBERO-Plus margin even resolvable above seed variance at matched ≥97.2% in-dist?), A3 (≥5 pp at matched capacity), B4 (RoboCasa-100 CIs can swallow a 15 pp inter-filter gap). Every threshold is a point estimate with no n or variance; several bets may be unfalsifiable in practice.
- **"Matched capacity / matched dim" never operationalized.** A1 (3DGS vs 2D-video vs sparse-3D heads differ in output dim and loss scale), A3 (continuous-d vs FSQ-N vs VQ-K carry unequal bits → measures capacity not bottleneck type). The confound that the whole doc is built to avoid (objective-not-quantizer, DC-MPC's real lesson) reappears one level down.
- **Label/provenance contamination as a masquerading confound.** B1 (sim make/break/slip boundaries may not survive transfer to a real sub-mm sensor), B4 (IDM-induced action-label noise masquerades as "the filter didn't transfer").

### Commit picks

- **A3 (bottleneck-type encoding sweep):** the cleanest, least-threatened unit — the settled half (semantic>reconstruction) is a near-certain publishable floor (H1), and the live half is genuinely unrun on closed-loop SR; cheapest experiment per unit novelty (encoder swap on frozen backbone), commit to the *protocol* and pre-register the null.
- **B3 (train-vs-runtime forward-inverse calibration), reframed:** the executability-decoupled crossover-map framing (RoboWM-Bench/Dream.exe, strong SC3-Eval control, one real arm) is a durable, protocol-owning contribution that survives the next pendulum swing — commit only as a crossover map, not as "train-time dominates."

### Reshape picks

- **A2 (imagined-wrench rollout):** threatened by FAWAM (2606.08555) — surviving novelty compressed to one adjective (sensorless-plan-over vs measured-reference); re-anchor against FAWAM-w/o-Res as a with-sensor ceiling, keep FD-VLA (61.1%) as the sensorless baseline, lead on calibration-under-drift.
- **B1 (explicit discrete tactile-mode latent):** threatened by OmniVTA/ReTac-ACT — continuous tactile WAMs and continuous gates already crack assembly/0.1mm; reframe as tactile-supervised mode *distillation* (train-time prior), move off saturating AutoMate/NIST to deformable WireCraft, fix the 3mm-not-0.1mm gate-ablation number.
- **A1 (renderable-3DGS dropped head):** threatened by Pri4R — dense form may *lose* to sparse metric-3D; re-aim from "3DGS beats video" to a three-arm geometry-vs-appearance sweep with a power analysis first.
- **B4 (kinematic filter transfers across generators):** threatened by RoboCurate — learned simulator-replay filter may transfer as well or better; reframe hero from "kinematic" to "generator-independent-ground-truth," add a learned-probe arm, hold the IDM constant, run a power calc before the 2×2.

### Doc grade

**B+** — coherent, honest, every bet falsifiable and protocol-rescuable; but uniformly partially-pre-empted with a shared calibration blind spot and pervasive unfalsifiable-margin risk, so the agenda's value is concentrated in measurement contributions rather than mechanism novelty, and most directions need reshaping before commit (A3 and reframed-B3 excepted).

### A (Cluster review)

Topic: the coherence + shared-bottleneck thesis of **Cluster A — WAM Substrate & Encoding** (A1 density / A2 modality / A3 encoding-type). The cluster claims these three are *orthogonal axes* of one representation question — "how dense the substrate is at train vs deploy (A1), which modality it spans (A2), and what its latent is trained to encode once dense-vs-sparse is fixed (A3)" — sharing one bottleneck: "the imagined state must be represented somewhere, and every substrate trades fidelity against deploy latency and OOD retention." Reviewed as a *cluster*: is the joint carved right, and is the shared bet sound?

#### Prompt 1 — Five Perspectives

**1. PRACTITIONER (builds/deploys policies daily).**
- *Core position*: The cluster's deploy-latency framing is the realest thing in it; A1's latency numbers ([[2606.17046|GAM]] 6.9 ms/~145 Hz vs [[2605.20752|GaussianDream]] 531 ms/chunk, [[2606.05254|Flash-WAM]] 348 ms) are the metrics a deployer actually cares about. But A1 and A2 both bury the cost that dominates a real lab: the *train-time* dense branch (3DGS render targets, synced 6-DoF wrench at 120 Hz) requires data infrastructure most groups do not have, and the "drop at deploy" trick assumes you already paid for it.
- *Strongest evidence*: [[2606.17046|GAM]]'s in-line geometry head holds 97.6% LIBERO with only a 12.1 pp LIBERO-Plus drop at 6.9 ms — proving you do not even need to *drop* a dense head to get OOD+speed, which quietly undercuts A1's whole "train-dense/deploy-light" premise (an in-line geometry head already wins both). [[2606.08555|FAWAM]] needs a physical ATI F/T sensor at 10 Hz to close its loop — A2's "sensorless at deploy" is the hard part and the card knows it (cross-sensor 60.3% is "not deployment-ready", its own Risk).
- *Only-this-perspective*: The A1 H1 three-arm sweep (no-head / 2D-video / 3DGS, matched capacity) is ~3× the training runs of a normal VLA paper; budget for the *sweep being the contribution* (a negative/tie result is still publishable), and pick A3 first because its ablations are cheap encoder swaps on a frozen backbone, not full WAM retrains.

**2. ACADEMIC (knows the peer-reviewed literature).**
- *Core position*: The cluster's orthogonality claim is the load-bearing and least-defended assertion. A1's own H5, A3's own H5, and the B3 cross-references all *test whether the axes interfere* — which concedes orthogonality is a hypothesis, not a given. If density washes out encoding (A1×A3) or wrench structure is just another encoding choice (A2⊂A3), the three "directions" are one direction with three knobs.
- *Strongest evidence*: A3's [[2605.06388|Semantic-LDM-WM]] (+9.8 pp closed-loop / +13.6 pp OOD, semantic-over-reconstruction) is a *clean controlled swap* (LDM held fixed, only encoder objective changes) — exactly the methodology the whole cluster should imitate. But [[2602.11832|JEPA-VLA]] already reproduced the cross-backbone swap (video-predictive +6.7% OOD), so A3's *settled* half is genuinely settled, and the live A3 bet narrows to one contested pair: [[2605.15725|DiLA]] (continuous wins generation) vs [[2603.05438|CompACT]] (discrete-FSQ wins planning) — and [[2503.00653|DC-MPC]] + [[2505.04999|CLAM]] already split *on control* (discrete>continuous on return; continuous>discrete on manip SR), so the "tie nobody has broken" is actually a four-way scatter where the answer plausibly *depends on the task*, not a single ≥5 pp winner.
- *Only-this-perspective*: The literature says A1 and A3 measure overlapping things — [[2606.17046|GAM]]'s in-line *geometry* head is simultaneously an A1 density point and an A3 encoding choice. The cluster needs a paper that *holds A1 fixed and varies A3* (and vice versa) to earn the orthogonality claim; without it, a reviewer reads three papers' worth of overlap.

**3. SKEPTIC (thinks the bet is wrong or already solved).**
- *Core position*: A2's "no single paper holds future-tense + 6-DoF wrench + acted-on-in-rollout" claim is **factually overtaken**. [[2606.08555|FAWAM]] (2026-06-07, NOT in the card's 27-row table) jointly predicts future end-effector 6-axis wrench trajectories $W_t$ inside a GE-Act world action model and the policy acts on the predicted-vs-sensed wrench discrepancy for closed-loop correction (63.75%→85%). So A2 is not whitespace; it is "do FD-VLA's sensorless distillation *and* FAWAM's future-wrench rollout at once" — a combination, not a category.

**4. ECONOMIST (follows money/incentives).**
- *Core position*: This cluster rides the 2026 WAM gold rush — the card cites ~97 systems across A1–A3, most posted within 4 months, many from labs (Meituan/[[2606.09337|TORL-VLA]], AGIBOT/τ0-WM, Shanghai AI Lab/[[2603.15169|ForceVLA2]]) with the GPU budget to brute-force the very sweeps A1/A3 propose. A3's "does the bottleneck-type answer depend on the task" and A2's "sensorless rollout vs sensored FAWAM" are *framing* questions a careful small lab can own before the gold rush settles them.

**5. HISTORIAN (has seen prior waves).**

#### Prompt 3 — Synthesis Briefing

The orthogonality of the three axes — the thing that justifies calling them three directions — is asserted, not demonstrated, and the cluster's own H5 rows concede it is a hypothesis.

(Lowest reliability as a *negative* — could be resolved either way by the H5 experiments.)

**(3) Hidden cross-perspective connection.** Economist and Historian independently arrive at the same defensive strategy: *bet on framing questions compute cannot brute-force.* Economist frames it as "industry will scoop the matched-capacity sweep"; Historian frames it as "teacher-form/latent-type gains are small and dataset-specific, the lasting contribution is the contingency map." **(4) Actionable insight for the researcher — DE-RISK, do not commit blind.** Do **not** commit to the cluster as a single bet, and do **not** lead with A1's full three-arm density sweep (most compute, most scoopable, premise dented by GAM). Instead: **(a)** Re-baseline A2 against [[2606.08555|FAWAM]] immediately — add it to the table, rewrite A2's thesis as *sensorless future-wrench rollout vs sensored FAWAM*, and make H1 a head-to-head against FAWAM (not FD-VLA). **(b)** Lead with **A3 reframed as a contingency map** — the cheapest experiments (encoder swaps on a frozen backbone) and the most scoop-resistant question. *Concrete next experiment*: run [[2603.05438|CompACT]]'s discrete-FSQ vs [[2605.15725|DiLA]]'s continuous vs a VQ arm at matched dim on the **same** closed-loop manipulation suite, but score the result *stratified by task type* (free-space vs contact-rich vs distractor-heavy) — predicting *not* a single ≥5 pp winner but a regime split (discrete favoring contact/return-style tasks per [[2503.00653|DC-MPC]], continuous favoring fine manip per [[2505.04999|CLAM]]). A clean contingency map is publishable even when the "tie-break" doesn't break. **(c)** Treat A1 as a follow-on only if A3 confirms encoding dominates density, and reframe it around *in-line vs dropped* dense heads (GAM vs GaussianDream), not 3DGS-vs-2D-video alone.

**(5) Frontier question that would change everything.** *Is the imagined substrate's reliability calibrated — i.e., does the WAM know when its imagined future (dropped-head latent, rolled-forward wrench) is wrong, and can the policy abstain/fall back on that signal?* Every Cluster-A direction optimizes mean OOD SR and is silent on the confidently-wrong tail (the blind spot). A WAM substrate that carries a calibrated uncertainty on its own imagination — and a policy that gates on it — would reframe the entire cluster from "which substrate retains the most OOD SR" to "which substrate *knows when it is hallucinating*," which is the property that actually governs safe deployment and the natural bridge to Cluster B's training-time-grounding (B3 calibration) thesis.

##### 1. Confidence scores (1–10) for the five key findings

**Finding 1 — Metric discipline (OOD + latency over saturated LIBERO) is the cluster's best feature. → 9/10**
Verified against the card: A1's Key-targets and Risks bind the bet to LIBERO-Plus OOD + deploy latency and explicitly retire headline LIBERO (line 218: "pure latent is already 97.2%... so headline LIBERO SR cannot distinguish methods"); A2 stakes the head-to-head on a shared ManiFeel suite (line 227); A3 scores on closed-loop SR + LIBERO-Plus at fixed latency (line 312). All three directions, all five FP bets, are falsified on OOD/latency, not in-dist SR. This is the cluster's strongest, most defensible property and the briefing reads it correctly. Docked one point only because "challenged by none" is slightly self-congratulatory: latency numbers in the table are cross-paper, cross-hardware (6.9 ms GAM vs 531 ms GaussianDream vs 348 ms Flash-WAM on unstated GPUs), so the latency axis is directionally sound but not yet a controlled metric.

**Finding 2 — A2's wedge narrowed from a category to a combination; FAWAM already does future-6-DoF-wrench-acted-on-in-loop. → 4/10**
This is the briefing's weakest finding *despite being ranked second*, and the over-reach the rest of the review hinges on. ### A3 (Direction review)

Topic: At matched architecture and deploy latency, the latent encoder's *bottleneck type* (continuous/semantic vs discrete-FSQ vs VQ/reconstruction) does NOT tie for closed-loop manipulation SR : one wins by ≥5 pp and settles the DiLA↔CompACT contradiction, and the recon-vs-semantic margin (Semantic-LDM-WM's +9.8 pp closed-loop / +13.6 pp OOD) reproduces with a *reconstruction/VAE arm* on a second non-LDM backbone (JEPA-VLA).

#### Prompt 1 — Five Perspectives

**1. PRACTITIONER (builds/deploys policies daily).**
- *Core position*: The bet is testable but the payoff is academic-shaped: a ≥5 pp closed-loop SR swing from bottleneck-type alone is plausible on LIBERO but the variable a deploying engineer actually fights is deploy latency and OOD retention, and there CompACT ([[2603.05438]]) already wins decisively (40× planning speedup, 16 tokens, 3× lower APE, 5.2× faster prediction). If discrete-FSQ even *ties* continuous on SR, the practitioner ships discrete for the latency alone : the SR tie-break is the wrong objective function.
- *Strongest supporting evidence*: CompACT's 8-16-token FSQ latent gives MPC in 5.78s vs 178.78s; StaMo ([[2510.05057]]) shows a 2-token state lifts OpenVLA +11.1% LIBERO with minimal overhead; OFlow ([[2604.17876]]) runs a semantic latent at 30 Hz with 69% real SR. These are the numbers a deploy decision turns on, and the card's H2 reports them only as a secondary "training stability" metric.
- *Only this perspective says*: Run H2 with a hard *iso-latency* constraint, not iso-dim. Matched latent dimension is the wrong control variable for deployment : a 256-dim continuous latent and a 16-token FSQ latent at "matched dim" are not matched in inference cost. Score SR *at fixed wall-clock planning budget*, which is the lever that decides what ships.

**2. ACADEMIC (knows the peer-reviewed literature).**
- *Core position*: The card's literature reading is unusually honest : it correctly concedes the static-vs-predictive split is settled cross-backbone (JEPA-VLA [[2602.11832]], VLA-JEPA [[2602.10098]]) and isolates the genuinely live axis (bottleneck type for control). But the "contradiction" it sells (DiLA vs CompACT) is partly an artifact of mismatched metrics, and the *real* contradiction the card under-weights is DC-MPC↔CLAM.
- *Strongest supporting evidence*: DiLA ([[2605.15725]]) scores continuous>discrete on **SSIM/LPIPS generation**; CompACT scores discrete>... on **planning latency**; they never share a metric, so they do not strictly contradict. The papers that DO share a control metric and DO contradict are DC-MPC ([[2503.00653]], discrete-FSQ ≥ continuous TD-MPC2 on **DMControl/Meta-World return**) vs CLAM ([[2505.04999]], continuous > discrete-VQ, 2-3× SR, "discretization significantly degrades fine control"). That is the sharp, same-axis, control-scored disagreement.
- *Only this perspective says*: The DC-MPC↔CLAM split is confounded by *what gets discretized* : DC-MPC discretizes the **state/observation latent** (no reconstruction, helps return); CLAM discretizes the **latent action** (hurts fine control). These are different objects. The card's H2 ablates the *state-latent* bottleneck type, so DC-MPC is the on-axis prior and predicts *discrete wins* : the card's "lead bet" may already lean the wrong way relative to its strongest control-grounded evidence.

**3. SKEPTIC (thinks it's wrong or already solved).**
- *Core position*: The honest falsifier in the card ("if recon, VQ, and continuous tie, bottleneck type is a free choice") is the *likely* outcome at matched capacity, and the field has a name for this : at sufficient latent dimension and a strong predictive objective, quantization type washes out. The ≥5 pp bar is a coin-flip dressed as a thesis.
- *Strongest supporting evidence*: FSQ-made-simple ([[2309.15505]]) showed FSQ ≈ VQ at matched budget; the card's own LGQ row ([[2602.16086]]) shows discretization-geometry differences live at rFID not control; and LARY ([[2604.11689]]) found the quantization bottleneck (codebook 64, dim 256) needs only "careful tuning" to avoid collapse : i.e., it's a hyperparameter, not a ceiling. The big swings in the literature (+7-15%) all come from the *static-vs-predictive* objective the card already concedes is settled, never from continuous-vs-discrete at matched objective.
- *Only this perspective says*: Pre-register the null. If H2 ties (<5 pp), that is publishable as "bottleneck type is a free choice for control once the objective is fixed" : but only if the experiment is designed so a tie is informative (matched capacity, matched objective, enough seeds to exclude a 5 pp difference). Without that the result is a non-finding either way.

**4. ECONOMIST (follows money/incentives).**
- *Core position*: This direction is cheap to run and benchmark-legible, which is exactly why it's tempting and exactly why it's crowded. LIBERO/LIBERO-Plus/RoboTwin are saturating; a controlled-ablation paper on "what the latent should encode" is a low-capex, high-citation-surface bet that many groups can run, so the whitespace is shrinking by the month.
- *Strongest supporting evidence*: The card cites 34 systems on this exact axis, six of them 2026-dated ablation/benchmark papers (Semantic-LDM-WM, JEPA-VLA, CompACT, LARY, DC-MPC variants). LARY (Meituan, 1.2M-video benchmark, open-sourced on HF/GitHub) is an industrial lab planting a flag on "evaluate latent-action representations" : the kind of move that commoditizes an ablation question fast.
- *Only this perspective says*: The defensible asset here is not the SR number, it's the *protocol* : the first matched-dim recon/VQ/continuous three-arm scored on closed-loop SR with an identifiability proxy ([[2511.08544]] LeJEPA). Own the controlled-comparison harness (like LARY owns the representation-probe harness) or get scooped on the headline number by whoever has more GPUs.

**5. HISTORIAN (has seen prior waves).**
- *Core position*: This is the pixel-vs-latent and VQ-vs-continuous cycle replaying inside world models. Each wave (VQ-VAE → continuous diffusion latents → FSQ) ended with "it depends on the downstream objective, not the quantizer," and the durable wins came from changing *what is predicted*, not *how it's binned*.
- *Strongest supporting evidence*: DreamerV2/V3's discrete categorical latents (the card's DC-MPC lineage) beat continuous on Atari but the *reason* was stochastic categorical dynamics + no reconstruction, not discreteness per se : DC-MPC's own ablation says classification + stochasticity (not the codebook) drove the gain. MWM ([[2206.14244]]) and the broader reconstruction-aux history show reward/task aux signal, not the bottleneck, moved control.
- *Only this perspective says*: The card's H2 risks rediscovering "the gain was the objective, not the quantizer." If the variance is all in the objective axis, the historian's prior wins and the lead bet collapses into H1.

I read the B4 card (`Mechanism/WAM.md` lines 678-771) and the Prompt-1/2/3 analyst file, and I independently verified the load-bearing external threat (RoboCurate 2602.18742) rather than trusting the synthesis. RoboCurate is real, on-target, and **not in the vault and not cited in the card** — it confirms the briefing's central worry but also caps how far that worry actually reaches.

##### 1. Confidence scores (1-10 reliability, with justification)

1. **The filter is load-bearing — 9/10.** Three independent in-vault ablations point the same way (WM-DAgger 96.7→46.7%, SAGE-Scene physics-critic-alone 7.8→1.9% collisions and 39.1 vs 13.2% SR, WorldArena r=0.825 perception vs 0.600 data-synth). This is the rare claim with a measured −50 pp ablation behind it. Docked one point only because all three measure *within* a single generator, so "load-bearing" is established but "load-bearing *and the same property across generators*" is not — and the briefing slightly leans on the former to imply the latter.

2. **Engine-beats-real-collection is settled, so the engine is not the contribution — 8/10.** Well-evidenced (RoboDream +26.2 pp 2.2×, AnchorDream, CRAFT, τ0-WM, plus the card's "five-plus papers"). The strategic move (disown the compute-moated engine, own the cheap filter) is correct and defensible. Half a grade of caution: "settled" is doing rhetorical work — the cited gains are heterogeneous (different embodiments, suites, baselines), so "engine beats real" is settled in *direction* but not as a single transferable effect size, which matters because B4's own ≥15 pp / ≥25 pp thresholds inherit that heterogeneity.

3. **The right axis is generator-independence-of-ground-truth, not kinematic-specifically — 7/10.** The Academic's reframe is genuinely the strongest contribution in the whole package and it is internally coherent: VISTA (analytic), CRAFT (success-replay), WM-DAgger (embedding), GE-Sim (VLM-judge), RoboCurate (simulator-replay) differ in *what ground truth they appeal to*, and "generator-independent ground truth transfers" is a cleaner thesis than "kinematic." Docked because it is a reframe-on-paper, not yet a result — and it quietly *weakens the novelty* it is sold as strengthening (see grade): once you absorb RoboCurate as a confirming instance, the headline becomes "many generator-independent filters transfer," which is a less surprising claim than the card's original differential.

4. **The analytic-kinematic filter is the transferable hero — 4/10.** This is the actual bet and it is the most exposed. I verified RoboCurate independently: it builds a *learned* simulator-replay action-verification probe, explicitly argues VLMs "cannot directly evaluate the generated actions themselves," and reports +70.1% GR-1 / +16.1% DexMimicGen / +179.9% real ALLEX humanoid. So a learned, generator-decoupled filter that beats video-plausibility filtering already exists. The briefing's medium-low rating is correct in sign. I rate it slightly *above* the analyst's framing on one technical point: RoboCurate is a *single-generator* result (its own neural-trajectory engine), so it pre-empts "*analytic*-is-the-only-transferable-type" but does **not** itself run a cross-*generator* filter-transfer 2×2 — the specific experiment B4 proposes is still unrun. The hero choice is wounded; the whitespace is dented, not closed.

5. **Success-replay does not transfer — 3/10.** Correctly flagged as the weakest leg, and the briefing is honest that it is "asserted, not shown." I push back on *both* sides here. The Skeptic's rebuttal ("CRAFT already shows 89.3% cross-embodiment") is itself a category slip: CRAFT's 89.3% is cross-*embodiment* transfer of the *policy/data*, not evidence that success-replay-*as-a-filter* survives a *generator* swap — those are different invariances. So finding #5 is weak as stated *and* its strongest challenger is slightly mis-aimed. Net: the differential's foil-failure is unestablished in either direction, which is exactly why it cannot anchor a paper yet.

Mean of the five: (9+8+7+4+3)/5 = **6.2**. Lowest: **3**.

##### 2. Weakest link

Finding #5 (success-replay does not transfer) at 3/10. It is the load-bearing leg of the *differential* narrative — without it, B4 degrades from "physics transfers, success-replay doesn't" to "several filters transfer," which is the analyst's own predicted "most likely real outcome." The specific information needed: a single clean datapoint where the **same generated corpus** (the card's fixed RoboDream corpus is the right substrate) is scored by CRAFT-style success-replay vs an analytic predicate vs a learned simulator-replay probe, **with the IDM/action-label pipeline held constant across arms**, and downstream SR read off RoboCasa's fixed 100-task suite. Until that exists, the differential is conjecture. Secondary verification need: confirm RoboCurate does *not* already contain a cross-*generator* arm (I confirmed it does not in the abstract/landing page, but the full ablation table should be read before committing — it is the difference between "novelty dented" and "novelty closed").

##### 3. Bias check

**The Skeptic over-dominated.** The CEO summary, the headline verdict ("one paper from collapse"), the de-risk ordering, and three of five finding-challenges all trace to a single artifact — RoboCurate — surfaced by the Skeptic and seconded by the Academic. That paper *is* the most important fact in the file, so the dominance is earned, not lazy. But it produced two distortions the briefing inherits uncritically: (a) it treats a single-generator learned-filter result as if it pre-empts a cross-generator transfer claim (overstated — see #4); (b) it imports CRAFT's cross-embodiment number as a foil-defeater when it is a different invariance (overstated — see #5). The Economist and Historian are present but ride along as color; the Practitioner's IDM/action-provenance point is the one genuinely independent contribution and it is under-weighted relative to its decision impact (it is the actual confound that could sink the pilot). Verdict: **Skeptic-dominant, with a correct but slightly over-extrapolated single source.**

##### 4. Missing perspective

A **Statistician / experimental-design** voice. Every threshold in this briefing — ≥15 pp transfer gap, "materially smaller" success-replay gap, "≥25 pp settled backdrop" — is a point estimate with no variance, n, or power analysis attached. The whole go/no-go hinges on whether a ~15 pp differential between two filter arms is real, yet RoboCasa-100 success rates over a handful of seeds routinely have confidence intervals wide enough to swallow 15 pp. This perspective changes the conclusion: the right first pilot deliverable may not be "does the differential survive" but "what n (tasks × seeds × rollouts) is needed to *detect* a 15 pp differential at all," and if that n is infeasible for an academic lab, the differential narrative is dead on power grounds regardless of whether it is true. None of the five perspectives questioned whether the experiment is *powered* to see the effect it is betting on. (A close-second missing voice: a **Reviewer-2 novelty** angle that asks whether "generator-independent filters transfer" — post-reframe — is surprising enough to clear a top venue, since the reframe trades fragility for blandness.)

##### 5. Overall grade and top fixes

**Grade: B+.**

What earns it: the briefing did the hard, honest thing — it found the one paper that threatens its own thesis, rated its own core bet medium-low, refused to commit, and proposed a falsifiable pilot with an IDM-constant control that directly closes the identified blind spot. The reliability ranking is well-calibrated (the order 1>2>3>4>5 matches my independent read), and the hidden-connection observation (IDM round-trip = where generator-coupling actually hides) is a real, non-obvious insight that reframes the experiment correctly.

What caps it below A-: (1) the briefing under-states that the Academic's reframe is a **novelty tax**, not just a save — absorbing RoboCurate as a confirming instance makes the headline blander, and the briefing presents this trade as pure upside; (2) it inherits two over-extrapolations from the Skeptic (single-generator RoboCurate treated as cross-generator pre-emption; CRAFT cross-embodiment treated as foil-failure) without flagging the category distinctions; (3) zero treatment of statistical power on a verdict that turns entirely on a 15 pp point estimate.

Top fixes, in order:
1. **Add the power calculation to the pilot spec.** Before the 2×2, compute the n (tasks × seeds × rollouts) needed to detect a 15 pp inter-filter differential at p<0.05; if infeasible, the differential narrative cannot be the headline regardless of truth. This is the single highest-leverage fix.
2. **State the reframe's cost explicitly.** "Generator-independent ground truth transfers" absorbs RoboCurate but lowers the novelty ceiling; the commit decision should weigh whether the *survived* claim still clears a top venue, not just whether it survives.
3. **Read RoboCurate's full ablation table before committing**, and correct the two category slips (single-generator ≠ cross-generator pre-emption; cross-embodiment ≠ filter-transfer-across-generators) so the de-risk rests on what the foil actually shows, not on an assumed failure.
4. **Promote the Practitioner's IDM-provenance confound from "control variable" to "first thing to measure."** If RoboDream's recoverable action labels are materially noisier than its rivals', the entire 2×2 is confounded before any filter runs — pilot week one should quantify IDM label noise per generator, not just hold it constant.
