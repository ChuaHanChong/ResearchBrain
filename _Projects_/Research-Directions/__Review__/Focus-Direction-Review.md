## Focus-Direction — doc-level synthesis

### Capsule
Focus-Direction is a single-bet document: it collapses the whole Embodied-AI research program into one publication wager, the "explicit-coupling loop." The thesis is that the off-diagonal inertia cross-term $\delta_{\text{base}}=M_{\text{base,arm}}\,\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ (an arm reach *is* a base/leg disturbance) should be an explicitly predicted, low-dimensional quantity rather than a residual the part-wise policy absorbs, and that this architectural move beats far larger data/compute budgets on a fixed budget, widening the OOD margin (~41%→~62%, gain growing under shift). As a body of bets the doc is *coherent and physically correct but strategically over-claimed*. The first-principles core (non-block-diagonal $M(q)$, value non-separable) is textbook-sound and the M0–6 falsifier (explicit head + aux-loss vs implicit baseline, sim-only, zero new data) is genuinely cheap, fast, and self-refuting — that is the doc's real asset. The value concentrates almost entirely in one narrow wedge: *anticipatory feedforward prediction of the arm→base self-reaction as a learned aux-loss target inside a VLA-scale policy*. Everything around it — the five-cluster "loop," the headline margin, the inter-cluster seams — is either borrowed, pre-empted, or admitted-unwired. Grade A−: a disciplined, honest, runnable bet whose framing oversells a few-point contribution as a 20-point one.

### Cross-direction pattern (recurring bottleneck)
Because this doc *is* one direction, the recurring tension runs across the five perspectives rather than across clusters, and it converges on a single bottleneck: **the grounding interface, not the architecture, is where the bet actually lives.** Practitioner (SEEC degrades 34.40% under unseen controllers; ADAPT needs FAST-LIO LiDAR odometry to get clean root velocity), Academic (the explicit term is controller-coupled; only the aux-loss-target framing is novel), and Historian (the explicit term wins only when the model is good, else domain randomization eats it) are independently circling the same mechanism: the explicit coupling term is only as good as the low-level WBC + dynamics model it is conditioned on. The cheap M0–6 sim falsifier sits *upstream* of this bottleneck; the actual risk is the M6–12 sim2real/inertia-grounding gate. This is the doc's shared weakness: every perspective agrees the physics is right and the falsifier is cheap, but the contribution's worth is determined entirely at the grounding interface that the falsifier does not exercise.

### Sharpest intra-doc contradiction
**Skeptic vs Academic/Practitioner on whether the whitespace still exists.** The Skeptic argues ADAPT (2606.16542, NTU, Jun 2026) already publishes the card's exact signature — a physics-grounded, sensor-free, explicit whole-body disturbance term fed into an RL policy that generalizes OOD (40N→60N, 4kg payload) where data-driven estimators fail — so the thesis is largely pre-empted, collapsing the program to a single-paper ablation. Academic/Practitioner counter that ADAPT *observes the realized external* disturbance via a momentum observer (post-hoc, reactive), whereas the bet is *anticipatory feedforward prediction of the self-induced arm→base reaction before the arm moves* — a different causal object. The clash is whether "predict-before vs observe-after" is a paper-sized delta or a footnote, and it is unresolved because the card never proves the anticipatory version beats the reactive observer. A second, internal contradiction sharpens it: Finding 1 calls the M0–6 falsifier "the right first move" while Finding 5 shows that an in-sim win is partly tautological (the sim that defines $\delta_{\text{base}}$ uses the same $M_{\text{base,arm}}$ the policy learns) — so the falsifier is "sound" and "circular" at once until the inertia target is perturbed off the policy's model.

### Recurring blind spots
- **Hardware contact dynamics.** Neither the card nor any of the five perspectives asks whether arm→base reaction is even the *dominant* unmodeled term on a real floating-base humanoid, or is swamped by foot-contact/friction, actuator-bandwidth limits, series-elastic compliance, and ground-reaction timing. This flips the failure mode from "wrong URDF" to "right URDF, wrong dominant physics" — passes in sim, dies on hardware for orthogonal reasons — and is exactly where a venue reviewer attacks. (The missing 6th perspective: a controls / sysID hardware realist.)
- **The circularity at the grounding step.** The M0–6 falsifier "runs in sim, zero new data," but the sim defining the target uses the inertia model the policy learns; an explicit win there is trivially recoverable and won't transfer. The bet truly lives at the M6–12 gate, not M0–6. No perspective fully argued this.
- **Headline conflation.** The 41%→62% margin measures HEX-implicit beating part-wise stacks, *not* explicit-vs-implicit; the card's own claimed delta is only ~+3pp. The borrowed margin dramatizes a few-point contribution.
- **Two-rate plumbing / latency.** A1 must run inside the WBC loop (500Hz–1kHz) while HEX/π0.5-class VLAs run at ~5–30Hz; the explicit term lives at a different clock than the policy consuming it. Only the Practitioner named this.
- **Deflation bias.** The Skeptic over-dominates (4 of 5 findings are Skeptic-led); the genuine strategic case for the bet (idea-bounded × humanoid-distinguishing, capital-light) is conceded in one CEO sentence then dropped. The synthesis lists what is wrong richly and what is worth betting on thinly.

### Commit
- **FOCUS (the explicit-coupling bet), as the *M0–6 falsifier plus benchmark*, not the five-cluster program** — the physics is textbook-correct, the falsifier is cheap/fast/decisive, and architecture-isolation results are under-supplied relative to the field's data-chasing incentive, so a clean ablation (and owning the predicted-vs-realized-coupling benchmark) is citable regardless of how the novelty argument lands.

### Reshape
- **FOCUS as the "41%→62%, five-cluster loop" program** — threatened on three fronts at once: (1) ADAPT (2606.16542) pre-empts the broad "physics-grounded coupling term generalizes OOD" claim, narrowing surviving novelty to "anticipate self-reaction vs observe realized disturbance"; (2) the headline margin is borrowed from a different comparison and the true delta is ~+3pp; (3) the inter-cluster seams are admitted-unwired by the card's own dashed-line framing, so four of five clusters are aspirational glue, not a pipeline. Reshape to: re-scope the headline honestly, add ADAPT's momentum observer as a *mandatory second baseline* in a three-way ablation (explicit-feedforward / ADAPT-observer / HEX-implicit) stratified by arm-acceleration quartile, and de-circularize M0–6 by defining $\delta_{\text{base}}$'s target from a *perturbed* inertia model so the sim win is non-trivial — that error-vs-margin curve *is* the contribution and *is* the verify harness.

### Doc grade
**A−** — a single, physically-grounded, intellectually honest bet with a genuinely self-falsifying cheap experiment; held back from A by an over-claimed headline, a novelty core that may have been pre-empted by an unread paper (ADAPT), an inherited blind spot on hardware contact dynamics, and a deflation-tilted synthesis that under-weighs why this is still worth a solo team's multi-year bet.

#### Prompt 1 — Five Perspectives

**1. PRACTITIONER** (builds/deploys daily)
- *Core position.* The falsifier (M0–6, sim-only, zero new data, explicit head + aux-loss vs implicit baseline on a fixed whole-body set) is genuinely cheap and the right first move — that part is well-designed. But the whole bet rides on a "half-decise" inertia model the card itself flags as the one absorbed risk, and on real humanoids $\hat M_{\text{base,arm}}$ is exactly the term URDFs get wrong (link masses off, harmonic-drive friction, payload of unknown mass in the gripper).
- *Strongest evidence.* SEEC (2509.21231) reports real-T1 EE accel 2.82 m/s² vs 3.57 IK but degrades 34.40% under *unseen controllers* — i.e., the explicit analytic term is controller-coupled; swap the low-level WBC and it erodes. ADAPT (2606.16542) needed FAST-LIO LiDAR-odometry to get root linear velocity clean enough for its observer, because IMU integration drifts — a hard, unglamorous deployment dependency the card never mentions.
- *Only-this-perspective.* The latency budget is unstated and load-bearing: the card promises "ms-latency residual head," but A1 must run *inside* the WBC loop (500Hz–1kHz) while HEX/π0.5-class VLAs run at ~5–30Hz. The explicit term lives at a different clock than the policy that consumes it; that two-rate plumbing, not the idea, is where this dies in deployment.

**2. ACADEMIC** (knows the peer-reviewed literature)
- *Core position.* The first-principles claim (non-block-diagonal $M(q)$, value non-separable) is textbook-correct and uncontroversial — model-based WBC (centroidal momentum, operational-space, inverse-dynamics MPC) has exploited it for 15 years. The genuinely novel slice is narrow and specific: *anticipatory feedforward prediction of the arm→base self-reaction, as a learned explicit auxiliary-loss target inside a VLA-scale policy* — which is not the same as "explicit coupling," a thing the field already does.
- *Strongest evidence.* HEX's own ablation is the card's strongest card and it is real: removing the UPP costs Pouring 11/12→6/12 (largest single drop) while 12M-frame pretraining is second-order — that *does* support "architecture > data on a fixed budget." But SEEC already does analytic-term+learned-residual under an L2 torque-matching loss; RAMBO (2504.06662) already does analytic-QP + RL residual *correcting base-acceleration targets*. The literature has the *machinery*; what is open is the *direction of prediction* and the *aux-loss-target* framing.
- *Only-this-perspective.* The "41%→62%, gain widens under shift" headline conflates two different systems. The 41.0→61.8 numbers are HEX-vs-baselines (an existing implicit MoE beating part-wise stacks) — they are NOT a measurement of *explicit-vs-implicit*. The card's own bet is only "≥+3pp into the mid-60s over HEX." The headline borrows HEX's margin to dramatize a contribution that, by the card's own falsifier, is a few points.

**3. SKEPTIC** (thinks it's wrong or already solved)
- *Core position.* The whitespace is thinner than claimed: ADAPT (2606.16542, NTU, June 2026) already feeds a *physics-grounded analytical whole-body disturbance estimate* (momentum observer, sensor-free, per-joint residuals including base lin/ang) into an RL whole-body policy as an explicit observation modality, and shows it *generalizes to OOD forces* (40N→60N, 4kg OOD payload) precisely where data-driven estimators fail. That is the card's thesis — physics-grounded explicit coupling term → OOD margin that widens under shift — already published.
- *Strongest evidence.* ADAPT's OOD result is the card's claimed signature: in-distribution 40N and OOD 60N both beat the proprioception-only baseline, with *emergent* postural compensation. The card's distinction — ADAPT *observes the realized* disturbance, the bet *predicts the self-induced* one *before* acting — is real but small, and the card never proves the anticipatory version *beats* the reactive observer. If a momentum observer at the next timestep is "good enough," A1 has no delta.
- *Only-this-perspective.* The seams the card admits are unwired (WAM-latent ≠ dynamics state; S2R recovers object physics not URDF inertia; verify metric checks imagination not realized coupling) mean four of the five clusters are *aspirational glue*, not a pipeline. Strip the rhetoric and the publishable unit is one paper: A1's explicit-vs-implicit ablation. The "five clusters reduce to one thesis" is a narrative, not a research program with wired hand-offs.

**4. ECONOMIST** (follows money/incentives)
- *Core position.* This bet is correctly *capital-light and idea-heavy* — a solo team's rational play when it cannot outspend the 12M-frame / 150K-trajectory data labs (HEX, MobileManiBench 2602.05233, EgoHumanoid 2602.10106 +51pp). Betting "architecture beats data on a fixed budget" is the only game where a solo researcher can win, because the data game is owned by Beijing Humanoid Center / AgiBot / NVIDIA-scale players.
- *Strongest evidence.* The asymmetry is visible in the card's own numbers: D2's +51pp from coupled demos vs A1's +3pp from architecture on fixed data. The field's incentive gradient points at data (everyone is building benchmarks — HumanoidArena 2606.17833, EBench 2606.18239, MobileManiBench), so an architecture-isolation result is *under-supplied* and therefore citable — but only if the ablation is airtight (matched controller/inputs/data, which HEX models well).
- *Only-this-perspective.* Benchmark risk: A1's headline metric (OOD success on a specific whole-body suite) has no agreed leaderboard, so the result is unfalsifiable-by-comparison and easy for reviewers to discount as "your sim, your numbers." The economically smart move is Milestone-4-first (ship the *causal-consistency benchmark* as the contribution) — owning the metric is worth more than winning on it, exactly the LIBERO/RoboTwin playbook.

**5. HISTORIAN** (has seen prior waves)
- *Core position.* This is the model-based/model-free pendulum swinging back: explicit-term + learned-residual is the 2020s reincarnation of feedforward computed-torque control bolted onto learning (RMA, then "analytic + residual," now "explicit coupling head"). Every wave that injected an analytic physics term into a learned policy got a real but bounded gain that domain randomization later partly closed.
- *Strongest evidence.* SEEC and RAMBO are the immediate precedents (analytic + RL residual); ADAPT is the momentum-observer lineage (Luca/De Luca's generalized-momentum disturbance observer, ~2005) reborn as an RL observation channel. Historically the "explicit term" wins when the model is good and *loses to brute-force DR + scale* when the model is wrong — which is exactly the card's stated failure mode ("a wrong URDF poisons it, explicit≈implicit").
- *Only-this-perspective.* The pixel-vs-latent and end-to-end-vs-modular cycles teach that the *interface* survives, the *implementation* churns. The durable contribution here is not the head but the *claim+benchmark* ("predicted coupling = realized coupling, measured") — the WAM/Dreamer lineage shows the metric outlives the architecture. Bet on the verify harness as the legacy, not the head.
