# Focus-Direction Research Plan: A Coupling-Aware World-Action Model (CAWM)

## 1. Abstract

> [!abstract] The bet
> World-Action Model whose latent **explicitly and anticipatorily predicts reaction wrench** of whole-body motion (both *self-induced* inertial reaction and *external* contact wrench), so humanoid policy plans against sensor-free coupling forecast instead of treating arm-to-leg, base-to-arm, force-under-load coupling as implicit residual. Trained dense, deployed latent on real G1/H1, grounded by differentiable system-ID, verified by causal-consistency metric. Working name **CAWM**. Bet: this one architectural term beats far larger data/compute on fixed budget, gain concentrated where each channel's cross-term largest. Thesis commits to **three** coupling channels (arm↔leg inertial, base↔arm kinematic, force-under-load external), so lift measured first on **balance / disturbance** (arm→leg test), **mobile-manipulation** (base↔arm anchor), **force-under-load / OOD-payload** (force-under-load anchor); remaining evaluation axes are **generality / robustness checks**, not co-equal contributions. Two **force** channels (arm↔leg self-induced, force-under-load external) are two **dominant** force-transmission pathways; share **one output wrench head** (legs compensate downstream) but solve **different prediction problems**: A's reaction computable from commanded $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ + inertia, C's exogenous wrench anticipated from context, load, contact state. Gravity/CoM folded into grounded model; arm-velocity-induced Coriolis cross-term predicted inside channel A (not separate channel). Three channels co-equal in explicit-coupling *bet* but differ in mechanism *depth*: A deepest (carries own reactive-observer and analytic-residual baselines), C rides A's shared wrench head, B thinner factoring-only claim, no reactive-observer comparator. Companions: [[Focus-Direction]] (thesis), [[Focus-Direction-Brief]] (one-page canvas pitch of full program), [[Focus-Direction-Paper-Code-Index]] (papers to code). Full rigor (pre-registration, fairness commitments, instrumentation) lives in `Focus-Direction-Research-Plan-detailed.md`, same section structure.

## 2. Introduction and motivation

> [!tip] First principles (the program bet)
> - **First principle:** generalized mass matrix non-block-diagonal, so every whole-body motion transmits low-dimensional, structured cross-term (reaction wrench, or kinematic feasibility constraint) between arm, base, legs; cross-term is quantity to predict, not data to collect.
> - **Assumption challenged:** field decouples whole-body control into arm controller plus balance controller, leaves coupling implicit or reactive.
> - **Bet:** making each coupling explicit, anticipatory, grounded, verified world-model output beats far larger data and compute on fixed budget, gain concentrated where cross-term largest; falsifies cheap via epsilon-curve.

Whole-body humanoid control conventionally **decoupled**: arm/manipulation controller plus balance/locomotion controller, coupling left implicit. But generalized mass matrix non-block-diagonal, so fast arm reach **is** base and leg balance disturbance; likewise *external* hand wrench propagates through $J_{\text{ext}}^{\top}$ to support legs, so force adaptation under load is whole-body equilibrium problem, not arm problem. Discarding those cross-terms makes whole-body policies brittle on aggressive motion and under load. Each cross-term low-dimensional, structured, quantity to **predict** (anticipatorily: from commanded arm motion for self-induced reaction, from estimate of external wrench for force-under-load), not data to collect. Program runs *same* explicit move across **three coupling channels in parallel**: two *force* channels (arm↔leg self-induced **A**, force-under-load external **C**), the two **dominant** force-transmission pathways, share one **output** wrench head (legs compensate) while solving different prediction problems (A computable from commanded $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$, C anticipated from exogenous context/load/contact); *kinematic* channel (base↔arm **B**) factors action; Gravity/CoM folded into grounded model; arm-velocity-induced Coriolis cross-term predicted inside channel A (not separate channel). Plan tests whether explicit prediction beats implicit-coupling and reactive-observer baselines on fixed budget, per channel.

> [!warning] Three fixed premises (literature facts, not relitigated here)
> 1. **[[2606.16542|ADAPT]] reactive, not anticipatory** (momentum disturbance-observer of *external* disturbance); it is reactive *estimate-then-react* foil for **both force channels** (self-induced A, external-wrench C). Closest *anticipatory* neighbor: [[2201.03871|ALMA]] (MPC-predicted *self-applied end-effector* wrench, arm's own manipulation force, known, fed as disturbance to *separate* RL locomotion policy, decoupled handoff), so it anticipates only that **self-induced known** wrench on quadruped, model-based. ALMA closest anticipatory precedent for both force channels by method (anticipatory wrench feedforward); by kind its wrench is applied **end-effector** force (nearest **C**, contact wrench), self-generated, model-computable (the **A**-like trait); because that self-applied wrench analytically known, surviving learned-anticipation wedge sharpest on **C** (unknown external wrench). Surviving wedge: anticipatory feedforward of each cross-term as learned WAM output in one coupled humanoid policy: *self-induced* inertial cross-term (A), *unknown external* wrench (C) that ALMA's self-induced-only scope excludes. ADAPT **mandatory baseline** on both force channels, ALMA decoupled-anticipatory comparator.
> 2. **Honest headline:** **41 to 62 figure borrowed** ([[2604.07993|HEX]]-implicit beating **monolithic generalist VLAs** (pi0.5 44.3 / GR00T N1.5 41.0) on unseen-scene generalization, not part-wise stacks, *not* explicit-vs-implicit) — field's proof-of-life, not this program's claim. Program's claim: two narrower wedge *types*, tested on each channel: **explicit-vs-implicit** wedge (concentrated where channel's cross-term largest, surviving model error; high arm-accel for A, first ~100 ms after step load for C; tested in de-risked core and again inside CAWM) and **explicit-vs-reactive** wedge (anticipatory feedforward beats reactive observer on force channels A, C, isolated by input-isolation control in ablation #1).
> 3. **Whitespace (re-verified against actual graph, snapshot at writing):** in large `graphify-out/graph.json` (~30331 nodes at writing, since grown), genuine zeros: **"reaction wrench" as compound (0), "causal-consistency as a verification metric" (~0)** (one "causal consistency" hit is reward term, blueprint-aware reward, not metric); these load-bearing zeros are what program rests on. Coupling and observer concepts clearly exist (snapshot: ~80 coupling-substring nodes = ~24 standalone coupling + ~56 decoupling, plus **3 "disturbance-observer"-compound nodes** by exact-substring match, 2 [[2606.16542|ADAPT]]'s own, 1 [[2603.27313|MetaTune]]'s; looser "disturbance"-and-"observer"-co-occurrence query returned **4**, 4th [[2509.18865|Bi-VLA]]'s "disturbance and reaction force observers", four-channel bilateral-control observer, not whole-body momentum disturbance-observer compound this plan compares against, so compound count stays 3; decoupling outnumbered standalone-coupling ~2.3:1, ~56 vs ~24); anticipatory-wrench node now exists ([[2201.03871|ALMA]]'s "anticipatory predicted-wrench observations"). Whitespace therefore **specific framing**: no node for *self-induced inertial cross-term as learned world-model output in one coupled policy*, plus two genuine zeros above.

## 3. Background and related work

Field defaults to **decoupling**: separate arm and balance controllers, coupling left implicit ([[2604.07993|HEX]]'s predictive MoE), emergent ([[2505.06776|FALCON]]'s shared observation), or one-directional ([[2509.21231|SEEC]], base-to-arm only); reactive observers ([[2606.16542|ADAPT]]) estimate disturbance after it manifests. Closest anticipatory neighbor [[2201.03871|ALMA]] feeds MPC-predicted *external* wrench sequence as anticipatory observation to *separate* RL locomotion policy, so distinction sharp: ALMA is the very two-controller decoupled handoff CAWM argues against, predicts self-induced applied arm wrench (force its own manipulation exerts, MPC-computed, not unknown external load, not self-induced inertial cross-term $M_{\text{base,arm}}\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ as learned output), not world-action model, no $\varepsilon$-curve, no robot-self sysID grounding, no causal-consistency metric. For **force-under-load** humanoid field is *reactive*: [[2505.06776|FALCON]] gets implicit compensation from torque-limit-aware force curriculum, [[2510.26280|Thor]] from force-augmented reward, [[2606.03297|SplitAdapter]] from history-estimated load context, [[2511.04679|GentleHumanoid]] / [[2505.20829|Unified-Force-Position-Control]] via learned impedance, [[2502.10894|UAN]] supplies high-load actuator model (113 N cart, 8 kg lift), [[2607.02332|HEFT]] handles heavy payload (24 kg) reactively via windowed payload curriculum; all *estimate-then-compensate from past* except [[2606.10818|IMPACT]], which predicts arm's external wrench feedforward from torque residuals sensorlessly but stays one-directional, arm-side; ALMA anticipates only *self-induced known* wrench on quadruped. World-action models imagine *scene* futures ([[2411.04983|DINO-WM]], [[2504.02792|UWM]], [[2603.14482|V-JEPA-2.1]]), yet [[2605.23856|JOPAT]] shows predicting explicit structured target beats pixels, [[2607.05966|iKCE]] shows such imagined rollouts stay kinematic, friction-invariant; differentiable system-ID recovers *object* physics ([[2603.01151|D-REX]], [[2104.02646|gradSim]]); structure-preserving sysID keeps recovered dynamics physically consistent ([[2606.09640|Physics-Aware-Sparse-EL]] holds inertia SPD, Coriolis consistent, [[2607.06824|CaLiSym]] enforces symplectic actuation/contact ports) yet still targets object or generic systems, not robot's own $M_{\text{base,arm}}$. Closest force-WAM [[2606.08555|FAWAM]] already forecasts end-effector wrenches jointly with actions, corrects on force residual, though sensor-bound, single-arm. No prior work makes self-induced arm-to-leg cross-term *or* unknown external wrench **explicit, anticipatory world-model output** carried in single coupled humanoid policy, nor binds **predicted-to-realized coupling** as metric. Full literature map: [[Whole-Body]], [[WAM]], [[Sim2Real]], [[Embodied-AI]], detailed plan.

## 4. Aims and hypotheses

Six clusters, one spine. Coupling core (co-equal): WB.A, WB.B, WB.C. Complementary general breakthroughs: WAM.A, S2R.B, EAI.B. One Aim, one core hypothesis per cluster; original H1-H4 mapped on, not dropped.

- **Aim WB.A (arm to leg):** make self-induced reaction wrench $\delta_{\text{base}} = M_{\text{base,arm}}\ddot q_{\text{arm}} + C_{\text{base,arm}}\dot q_{\text{arm}}$ explicit anticipatory prediction. **H-WB.A:** explicit beats implicit (MoE) and reactive (observer), margin concentrated at high arm acceleration, surviving model error (H1 on A).
- **Aim WB.B (base to arm):** factor action autoregressively (base to torso to arm) so base is predicted, coupled DoF. **H-WB.B:** coupled beats decoupled-base, margin concentrated on reach-extension tasks (H1 on B).
- **Aim WB.C (force-under-load):** estimate unknown external hand wrench, anticipate $J_{\text{ext}}^{\top}F_{\text{ext}}$, sharing WB.A's head. **H-WB.C:** anticipatory beats both reactive (observer/impedance) and implicit ([[2505.06776|FALCON]] force-curriculum), margin in first ~100 ms after step load; $J_{\text{ext}}$ identifiable in PR-0b sim check (planted contact-model / $J_{\text{ext}}$ unknowns, known ground truth), real-robot grounding validated downstream by PR-2 (OOD-mass SR + PR-4 force-plate self-consistency), since real true contact model unobservable (H1 + H4-C).
- **Aim WAM.A (predict):** carry all channels on train-dense / deploy-latent WAM, latent conditioned on dynamics state. **H-WAM.A:** lift positive across every WAM backbone (H2); exploratory, confound-aware gradient tested within-backbone, expected largest where native latent least control-relevant (H3).
- **Aim S2R.B (ground):** recover $M_{\text{base,arm}}$ (A) and contact model (C) from real interaction by differentiable system-ID; ground manipulated-object physics (B) via same engine's native object recovery, so all three clusters grounded. **H-S2R.B:** $M_{\text{base,arm}}$ identifiable in PR-0b sim check (planted $\tilde M$, known ground truth), real-robot grounding validated downstream by PR-2 (OOD-mass SR + PR-4 force-plate self-consistency), since real true inertia unobservable (H4-A).
- **Aim EAI.B (verify):** certify predicted-vs-realized coupling on existing harnesses + hardware. **H-EAI.B:** causal-consistency metric well-defined, discriminative, computed per channel (A / B / C), with three-way (prediction / grounding / execution) error decomposition; no public benchmark binds predicted-to-realized coupling.

Four breakthrough modifications: shared reaction-wrench imagination head ([[WAM|WAM·A2]]); coupling-conditioned latent ([[WAM|WAM·A3]]); differentiable-sysID grounding ([[Sim2Real|S2R·B2]]); causal-consistency verification ([[Embodied-AI|EAI·B1]]).

## 5. Approach

### Problem
Generalized mass matrix non-block-diagonal, so arm acceleration is base/leg balance disturbance (channel **A**, self-induced), external hand wrench propagates to support legs (channel **C**, external):
$$ \delta_{\text{base}} \;=\; M_{\text{base,arm}}(q)\,\ddot q_{\text{arm}} + C_{\text{base,arm}}(q,\dot q)\,\dot q_{\text{arm}}, \qquad \delta^{\text{ext}}_{\text{base}} \;=\; J_{\text{ext}}^{\top}(q)\,F_{\text{ext}} $$
Predict $\delta_{\text{base}}$ **anticipatorily** from *commanded* $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ (before it perturbs base); forward-model external reaction $\delta^{\text{ext}}_{\text{base}}$ from estimate of *unknown* $F_{\text{ext}}$ (before load fully felt). Both feed **one shared output wrench head** (legs compensate downstream), but **different prediction problems**: A computable from commanded $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ + inertia, C's exogenous wrench must be anticipated from context, load, contact state. Wedge is on timescales: reaction rises over ~20 to 60 ms, momentum observer lags ~6 ms plus one policy step, so anticipation wins only where rise-time long enough (pre-registered $\ge$ 31 ms, PR-0a). In MuJoCo exact target is `mj_fullM`, reachable through `humanoid-bench`'s `MjDataWrapper.__getattr__` passthrough (`env.data.qM` works directly); but `humanoid-bench` ships **zero** existing `mj_fullM`/`qM` usage, so authoring and confirming dense-densify-then-slice extraction on **H1 and G1 models is new Phase-0 task (to-be-confirmed at M0)**, not ready dependency. For falsifier, target comes from *perturbed* $\tilde M$.

### Architecture
```
     RGB(+depth) o_t                 proprio q,q̇ + cmd q̈_arm + est. F_ext
            │                                          │
            ▼                                          ▼
  Frozen DINOv2 encoder                   coupling-conditioned latent
            │                                          │
            └────────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                                 ▼
                            latent z_t
               ┌─────────────────┴─────────────────┐
               ▼                                   ▼
   latent dynamics predictor              SHARED WRENCH HEAD
               │                          δ_base (self-induced, A)
               ▼                          + J_ext^T·F_ext (external, C)
             z_t+1                        per contact mode
         ┌─────┴────────────┐                      │
         ▼                  ▼                      │
 dense video/3DGS     action head ◄────────────────┘
 head — TRAIN-TIME    (flow/diffusion)
 ONLY, dropped at     base→torso→
 deploy               arm (B)
```
*Dense teacher (train-time-only node) is dropped at deploy; deploy-time diagrams in [[Focus-Direction-Brief]] and canvas omit it by design.*

Coupling head is plug-in on any WAM backbone (backbone grid proves lift is the term, not backbone). Four added modules:
- **Coupling-conditioned latent:** carries proprioception + commanded $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$, not just scene appearance. So latent cannot ignore acceleration input and collapse wrench head to proprioception-only regressor, explicit conditioning auxiliary loss $\mathcal L_{\text{cond}}$ supervises latent to *reconstruct* proprioception + commanded $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ from $z_t$ (Seam 1's learning mechanism); ablation #3 measures wrench-head error, conditioning on vs off.
- **Shared wrench-imagination head:** predicts self-induced reaction $\delta_{\text{base}}$ (channel A) *and* external-wrench reaction $J_{\text{ext}}^{\top}F_{\text{ext}}$ (channel C), each $(6+n_{\text{leg}})$-vector per contact mode, plus mode classifier. External wrench $F_{\text{ext}}$ itself estimated, anticipated from proprioception, context, contact state (ADAPT-style observer), then forward-modeled; A and C **share this output head** but differ in prediction problem (A computable from own commands, C exogenous, anticipated). Contact modes discrete (sliding vs sticking, $n_{\text{modes}}=2$), labeled from simulator contact state in sim, from tactile/contact-force thresholding ([[2606.13877|ContactWorld]] modality) on hardware.
- **Autoregressive action head:** base-to-torso-to-arm factoring (base-arm anchor). Verified fork bases: `brs-algo` (WB-VIMA, `whole_body_decoding_order`) and [[2507.01961|AC-DiT]].
- **Dense teacher head** (video/3DGS): train only, dropped at deploy.

### Improving the coupling clusters (the co-equal core)

Three coupling clusters improved each as whole, co-equally: improving WB.A, WB.B, or WB.C means advancing all its directions together as one whole-body interface, per-direction recipes below are parts of that single advance.

#### WB.A (arm to leg)
Four directions are cluster's coordinated advances at different layers of one whole-body interface: upper-limb intent and lower-body support coupled before commands issue, so legs anticipate arm's inertial demand rather than absorbing late. WB.A1 anticipates arm-induced base reaction wrench, WB.A4 couples same intent into command vocabulary as feasible-by-construction latent, WB.A2 secures sim-to-real feasibility, WB.A3 secures workspace feasibility. WB.A2's SkillBlender-plus-HiWET blended hull and WB.A4's HALOMI behavior manifold ([[2511.04131|BFM-Zero]] latent) are alternative, non-stackable feasibility substrates cluster evaluates, not single stacked pipeline. Each recipe targets layer the next presumes: one whole-body interface advanced at four layers, not four point fixes.
- **WB.A1 CoReWA** = [[2604.07993|HEX]] + [[2509.21231|SEEC]] + [[2506.14278|Heavy-Limbs-WBC]]: forecast arm latents into anticipatory base reaction wrench feeding gate and controller.
- **WB.A2 BlendBridge** = [[2506.09366|SkillBlender]] + [[2602.08594|MOSAIC]] + [[2602.06341|HiWET]]: entropy-gated per-joint residual adapts frozen skill blend across sim-to-real gap.
- **WB.A3 ReachSched-WBT** = [[2602.06341|HiWET]] + [[2508.11275|Differentiable-Reachability-Maps]]: schedule base along reachability gradient, shrink margin with tracking residuals.
- **WB.A4 CoLA-WB** = [[2512.11047|WholeBodyVLA]] + [[2506.13751|LeVERB]] + [[2606.18772|HALOMI]]: loco latent as residual of manip code, projected onto feasible manifold.

#### WB.B (base to arm)
Three directions build one shared artifact: single mobile-manipulation policy denoising base, torso/arm, perception actions in same action chunk over one shared frame, with co-trained DynaMem-style voxel memory introduced by B2 and B3 as connective perception interface. B1 contributes base-first flow-matching coupling backbone (single arm-to-base feasibility edge) as reusable component the other two build on; its own deliverable is stratified margin map validating when that edge wins, not memory: B1 carries no DynaMem of its own (BRS, AC-DiT, InCoM only). B2 promotes memory-conditioned 3D look-at into same chunk so gaze reveals what base motion opens; B3 turns DynaMem's hard purge into differentiable in-policy write gate so scene the base and gaze act on stays self-correcting. Each closes loop the previous opened; once B1's validated backbone wrapped in B2/B3 memory interface, one perceive-move-update-act loop compounds into whole-body controller no single direction delivers alone. One caution on B2 specifically, not on B1's base-first bet: [[2608.02257|PanoVLA]] closes comparable gap (91.3%/73.4% vs. local-view 58.6%/30.0%) with passive 360-degree sensing, no gaze action — genuine alternative to B2's learned-gaze premise, still untested head-to-head against it.
- **WB.B1 KCFlow-Strat** = [[2503.05652|BRS]] + [[2507.01961|AC-DiT]] + [[2602.23024|InCoM]]: stratified test of when one arm-to-base feasibility edge beats unidirectional base-first.
- **WB.B2 LookAhead-WBC** = [[2603.03243|HoMMI]] + [[2405.07991|SPIN]] + [[2411.04999|DynaMem]]: memory-conditioned 3D look-at co-optimized with base in one diffusion chunk.
- **WB.B3 EchoPurge** = [[2511.18112|EchoVLA]] + [[2411.04999|DynaMem]] + [[2510.07134|TrackVLA++]]: differentiable in-policy purge gate plus cross-visit re-ID for self-correcting multi-room memory.

#### WB.C (force-under-load)
Improving WB.C means two recipes share ONE sensorless, proprioceptively-estimated load-mismatch signal fanned out to both locomotion controller and manipulation policy. That signal: PROWL's anticipatory load-mismatch forecast: WB.C1 produces it as wrench lookahead (predict and pre-brace), WB.C2 consumes same forecast to build conformal disturbance bound (certify and re-size shields), complementary points on anticipation-versus-safety spectrum. Together they turn reactive force-under-load whole-body control into one anticipatory, conditionally-certified loop off shared upstream signal. C1 now has first positive anchors: [[2603.03751|IO-WBC]] carries 18 kg load at 80% success where SOTA reactive WBC baseline manages 0% via proprioceptive-only anticipatory decoupling; [[2603.07095|ACLM]] extends same anticipatory-wrench move to multi-robot team robust to ~67% mass-model error, giving PROWL's OOD-load margin calibration baseline to beat, not just ADAPT to rebut.
- **WB.C1 PROWL** = [[2505.06776|FALCON]] + [[2201.03871|ALMA]] + [[2606.16542|ADAPT]]: momentum-observer residual forecasts hand wrench into FALCON's dual agents, legs pre-brace before loads peak; only ALMA's anticipatory-leaning mechanism is transferable claim, its headline numbers on quadruped, not the claim here.
- **WB.C2 PALADIN** = [[2605.25546|ISSf-CBF-WBC]] + [[2604.07457|CMP]] + [[2602.01515|RAPT]] (cross-check): PROWL's forecast conformally bounded into disturbance that re-sizes ISSf-CBF QP, narrows CMP's radius, RAPT's residual demoted to runtime cross-check.

### Improving the mechanism clusters (complementary general breakthroughs)

Three mechanism clusters are predict, ground, verify seams wired onto coupling; each also standalone general advance (network and training architecture, sim2real, evaluation). Each improved as whole, directions together, absorbs corresponding seam method.

#### WAM.A (predict)
Three directions build one shared artifact: predictive world-model latent trained dense, grounded, yet deployed lightweight, paired with calibrated residual gate deciding execute versus replan by comparing cached forecast to freshly encoded real observation. Each reuses cluster's train-teacher-then-discard-and-gate recipe (discardable train-only teacher following [[2607.05468|MECo-WAM]], whose decayed read-mask attention zeroes at deploy, no added inference cost) on different prediction target (scene geometry, contact wrench, action-recoverable dynamics), so robot inherits one latent whose visual, contact, control futures all calibrated, gateable. Upstream substrate CAWM's coupled controllers consume. Two results now back A3's density-vs-semantics orthogonality claim rather than threaten it: [[2608.05903|Robust-WAM]] shows semantic tokens added atop dense WAM raise OOD success instead of being washed out; [[2606.30988|MuSe]] shows mirror direction, future-video auxiliary lowering force-forecast error — substrate can carry both without competing for capacity.
- **WAM.A1 GROVER** = [[2605.20752|GaussianDream]] + [[2602.10098|VLA-JEPA]] + [[2605.06222|FFDC-WAM]]: shared motion code grounds leakage-free latent via discardable 3D-Gaussian teacher, deploy gates on calibrated residual.
- **WAM.A2 WrenchCast** = [[2606.09337|TORL-VLA]] + [[2606.13877|ContactWorld]] + [[2603.17851|DexViTac]]: rolls latent forward H steps, decodes sensorless 6-DoF wrench split into self-motion and contact, conformal trust gate.
- **WAM.A3 DUET-WM** = [[2605.06388|Semantic-LDM-WM]] + [[2503.00653|DC-MPC]] + [[2505.04999|CLAM]]: splits d=96 semantic latent into continuous flow channel plus FSQ residual channel, per-token gated, CLAM-grounded.

#### S2R.B (ground)
Four ground directions improve as one advance inside single shared artifact: differentiable physics twin whose backward pass and per-state correction residual read in one physically-commensurate trajectory-divergence unit, so each recipe contributes different grounded quantity to same calibrated interface, not own twin. B1 differentiates residual, B2 amortizes it, B3 gates on it, B4 certifies it as identifiability-calibrated inadequacy meter, each estimate trusted only up to divergence the others certify. Cluster-level artifact: residual-calibrated grounding pipeline emitting per-twin certificate of where reconstructed object and dynamics physics reliable. Pipeline's tools keep sharpening, not its claim: [[2608.04842|RORA]] recovers kinematic articulation (URDF joints) contact model needs, [[2608.06164|BendTwin]] upgrades deformable-contact model past axial-only spring-mass, [[2605.09954|JODA]] completes RORA's own stated gap, recovering joint-level dynamics, friction, damping, detents RORA lacks. [[2409.17992|LoopSR]] closest existing evidence for round-over-round grounding loop itself: real-hardware reward-vs-loop-round curve on legged locomotion, continual improvement across successive sim-real loops, not drift.
- **S2R.B1 SENSE-T** = [[2503.17973|PhysTwin]] + [[2511.04665|Real-to-Sim-GS]] + [[2603.01151|D-REX]]: one differentiable rollout yields per-channel analytic sensitivity of sim-real divergence, replacing discrete fidelity sweep.
- **S2R.B2 AMORTI-SIM** = [[2603.01151|D-REX]] + [[2603.23973|SLAT-Phys]] + [[2410.20357|Dynamics-as-Prompts]]: clutter-conditioned net predicts novel object's full constitutive vector in one pass, zero per-object loops.
- **S2R.B3 FideliGate** = [[2403.03949|RialTo]] + [[2504.03597|Real-is-Sim]] + [[2604.13645|CFG-ADDA]]: photometric correction residual becomes fidelity gate on fold-back demos, rounds monotonically improve real success.
- **S2R.B4 CLaRe** = [[2304.14369|NCLaw]] + [[2508.01112|MASIV]] + [[2603.22039|RAFL]]: two-head MPM splits constitutive law from starved residual, split becomes falsifiable extrapolation measurement.

#### EAI.B (verify)
Four EAI.B directions compose into one verify-and-retain layer around single shared artifact: predicted-versus-realized coupling residual emitted by whole-body wrench head, force-plate-grounded, decomposed by channel. B1 builds offline self-certifying action-counterfactual metric, B3 is its runtime lagged-agreement instance gating adaptive compute, B2 reads residual as per-failure correction signal, B4 protects corrected head under continual fine-tuning. They advance as one self-certifying causal-consistency metric plus cause-attributed, forgetting-free learning loop, not four separable tools. Two 2608-batch results sharpen what B1 must resist and can borrow: [[2607.15207|BadWAM]] is adversarial case, imperceptible perturbations desynchronizing still-plausible imagined future from executed action, dropping closed-loop success over 50% — exactly what causal-consistency metric exists to catch; [[2608.04653|CoCo-WorldModel]] is free machinery toward building one, training action-counterfactual consistency via inverse/zero-action rollout branches, shipping ARC/DE metric pair B1's forward-inverse recovery could reuse.
- **B1 CIC** = [[2606.05773|PiL-World]] + [[2606.18610|SC3-Eval]] + [[2605.29360|MiraBench]]: self-certifying action-counterfactual metric, U_cf-gated, rho=0.94/0.984 references.

### The cluster falsifiers (the decisive experiment)
**Three falsifiers run parallel, one per coupling cluster**, each *same* cheap ablation on lean `humanoid-bench` PPO substrate (same data/seeds/backbone), each testing **shared, load-bearing claim every direction in that cluster builds on** — pass de-risks whole cluster (directions advance together), fail voids cluster's shared bet, not one recipe. **Per-cluster go/no-go** (cluster can fail independently):
- **WB.A · arm↔leg (force), stratified by arm-acceleration quartile:** shared claim is *anticipatory coupling* (legs anticipate arm's inertial demand before commands issue), tested **explicit-feedforward (ours)** vs **implicit ([[2604.07993|HEX]]-MoE)** vs **reactive observer ([[2606.16542|ADAPT]])**. **Input-isolation control (decisive):** identical head, swap *only* input, planned $\ddot q_{\text{arm}}$ (anticipatory) vs observer's measured estimate (reactive); attributes any win to feedforward timing, not capacity. Pass validates interface WB.A1–A4 all build on.
- **WB.C · force-under-load (force, shares WB.A's head):** shared claim is *one anticipatory load-mismatch forecast* (WB.C1 produces, WB.C2 certifies), tested **explicit anticipatory wrench-reaction (ours)** vs **reactive ([[2606.16542|ADAPT]] external-wrench observer / [[2505.06776|FALCON]] curriculum / reactive impedance tracker)** vs **implicit**, under *step-applied unknown* external load, stratified by load magnitude and direction. **Headline-C metric:** CoM-excursion advantage in first ~100 ms after load onset, where reactive compensation lags ([[2201.03871|ALMA]] proved anticipation beats reactive for self-induced known wrench; C extends to unknown-external regime on humanoid).
- **WB.B · base↔arm (kinematic):** shared claim is *base-first coupling backbone with one arm-to-base feasibility edge* (WB.B2, B3 compound on it), tested autoregressive base→torso→arm vs flat-concatenation (factoring claim, no reactive-observer arm), per base-arm gate.

**De-circularization (headline $\varepsilon$-curve).** Supervise explicit head on $\delta^{\star}_{\text{base}}$ from *perturbed* $\tilde M$ (each link's mass/inertia scaled by $1\pm\varepsilon$); implicit baseline and observer run on true dynamics, no privileged target. If explicit head still wins under deliberately wrong model, win is inductive bias, not label leakage. Report margin **as function of $\varepsilon$** with bootstrap CIs. **Void unless** margin significantly $>0$ at small $\varepsilon$ ($\{0,0.05\}$) **and** degrades gracefully. Implementation specified as Phase-0 steps (coding later). Fairness (pre-registered): implicit = matched-capacity MoE on identical data/seeds; reactive = ADAPT observer at 500 Hz state-poll on true dynamics. Each force cluster gets own $\varepsilon$-curve: A perturbs $\tilde M$, C perturbs contact model / $J_{\text{ext}}$; same void gate (significant at small $\varepsilon$, non-increasing) applies per cluster.

## 9. Risks and mitigation

- **Explicit $\approx$ implicit:** three-way ablation + $\varepsilon$-sweep still definitive result (per channel).
- **Wrong / unidentifiable inertia or contact model:** gate Phase 2 on PR-0b; fall back to analytic term + broad DR (Seam 2 dropped) for affected channel.
- **Hardware-dominant physics:** instrument residual budget first (PR-0a'); scope down if $\delta_{\text{base}}$ dominated. Per upstream H8, decompose reaction at worst instant (max CoM excursion): if foot-contact / support-polygon-transition share dominates there, inertial term mis-specified, channel A reframes as contact-constrained hybrid-dynamics prediction (terrain/foot-ground contact is this pre-gate, not separate predicted channel).
- **Engine floor error, not just wrong model:** [[2608.05948|GAUGE]] shows physics engines given ground-truth-calibrated parameters still show order-of-magnitude errors on dynamic contact/deformation, so channel that clears grounding gate can still forward-simulate wrong; sharpens H8 — GAUGE's worst regime (dynamic contact) exactly H8's target.
- **Base↔arm factoring buys little, or its decode adds latency (channel B):** autoregressive base→torso→arm win may live only on reach-extension stratum (thin if task distribution rarely needs in-task base repositioning), sequential decode can cost latency. Report B margin **stratified by reach-extension vs fixed-base** (PR-1B), not pooled; parallelize/distill decode if latency bites; definitive autoregressive-vs-flat ablation still ships.
- **WAM too slow for contact:** async decoupling + quantization clear 30 Hz floor; report SR-vs-Hz Pareto honestly.
- **Force-under-load first-100 ms hostage to composed loop latency:** C headline is transient measured across estimate→forward-model→leg-torque loop; price whole onboard budget (observer settling + inference + actuator bandwidth); if it eats 100 ms window, move C headline to steady-state max-load number.
- **External-wrench / contact unidentifiable, or implicit (FALCON curriculum) already suffices:** that *is* the C falsifier; fall back to reactive/curriculum baseline, report definitive anticipatory-vs-reactive ablation.
