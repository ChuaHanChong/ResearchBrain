# VideoComposer: Compositional Video Synthesis with Motion Controllability

> [!summary] Summary
> VideoComposer extends compositional generation from images to videos, enabling fine-grained control over spatial and temporal elements through diverse conditions like text, sketches, and direct motion vectors. The model generates coherent videos with enhanced temporal consistency and precise motion control across various synthesis tasks.

## Problem

- Existing video generative systems offer limited controllability, lacking fine-grained command over spatial layouts, styles, and specific motions beyond basic text-to-video generation.
- Maintaining cross-frame consistency and coherence in synthesized videos becomes challenging when integrating multiple and diverse input conditions.
- There is a need for explicit and efficient mechanisms for direct motion control in video diffusion models, as current approaches often rely on implicit learning or limited structural guidance.

## Method

- The model develops a latent ==diffusion model== for video synthesis, extending the compositional generation paradigm from images to videos by decomposing videos into controllable conditions.
- It introduces a Spatio-Temporal Condition Encoder (STC-encoder) to process and integrate diverse sequential spatial and temporal conditions like depth maps, sketch sequences, and motion vectors.
- Motion vectors extracted from compressed videos are incorporated as an explicit, direct, and efficient control signal to guide fine-grained temporal dynamics within generated videos.

## Results

- VideoComposer successfully performs a wide array of compositional video generation tasks, including image-to-video animation, video inpainting, and sketch-to-video generation, guided by various combined conditions.
- It demonstrates enhanced motion controllability, quantitatively reducing motion control error (End-Point-Error) through the use of motion vectors and qualitatively showing superior performance in hand-crafted motion guidance.
- The model achieves improved temporal consistency, evidenced by higher average CLIP cosine similarity between consecutive frames and qualitatively producing more coherent and detailed videos compared to baselines.
- VideoComposer maintains competitive performance in standard text-to-video generation tasks on the MSR-VTT dataset, indicating that the compositional framework enhances overall video synthesis capabilities without degradation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The compositional generation paradigm, successful in image synthesis, can be effectively adapted to video by thoughtfully decomposing video content into independently controllable spatio-temporal conditions.
> - Explicit motion vectors from compressed video provide a direct and efficient mechanism for precise temporal control over object movements and dynamic patterns.
> - A unified Spatio-Temporal Condition Encoder is crucial for integrating diverse and sequential input conditions, ensuring temporal consistency and structural coherence in the synthesized video outputs.
