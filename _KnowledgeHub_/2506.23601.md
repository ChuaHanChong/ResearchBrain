# Semantic-guided Diverse Decoding for Large Language Model

> [!summary] Summary
> Semantic-guided Diverse Decoding (SemDiD) is an algorithm designed to generate multiple high-quality responses from large language models that are genuinely distinct in meaning, rather than just lexically varied. It enhances performance across reasoning, QA, and translation tasks by achieving superior coverage in Best-of-N strategies and accelerating RLHF training convergence.

## Problem

- Existing LLM decoding strategies produce lexically varied but often semantically redundant outputs, limiting their utility in applications requiring genuinely diverse perspectives.
- The theoretically ideal method of post-hoc clustering for semantic diversity is computationally prohibitive for practical LLM deployment due to high inference costs.
- Current probability-based quality assessments in decoding suffer from position and length biases, leading to suboptimal output selection and an inaccurate reflection of true quality.

## Method

- Introduces Semantic-guided Diverse Decoding (**SemDiD**), an extension of ==group beam search== that operates directly in the semantic embedding space to generate multiple diverse and high-quality answers.
- Employs three complementary mechanisms: directional guidance for initial divergence, inter-group repulsion for dynamic semantic separation, and a position-debiased probability assessment for refined quality.
- Balances quality and diversity objectives using an ϵ-constraint quality guarantee and a harmonic gain-based balancing function that adaptively prioritizes the weaker aspect of a candidate.

## Results

- Achieved superior coverage in Best-of-N evaluations, with up to a **5.2%** absolute improvement on MMLU-Pro+ and reaching **98.1%** on GSM8K with 25 samples, significantly outperforming baselines.
- Accelerated RLHF training convergence by up to **15%** and increased final accuracy/win rates by 1.8-**2.1%** on tasks like GSM8K and TLDR, indicating more efficient policy learning.
- Demonstrated the highest average pairwise semantic distance (**0.371**) while maintaining competitive n-gram diversity (**0.829**), confirming the generation of truly distinct reasoning paths with modest computational overhead (e.g., +**18%** latency for Qwen-2.5-**70B**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Achieving true semantic diversity, beyond mere lexical variation, is critical for unlocking the full potential of LLMs in applications like Best-of-N strategies and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).
> - Effective semantic guidance during decoding can be achieved by combining initial directional exploration with continuous inter-group repulsion, dynamically shifting emphasis as sequences develop.
> - Refined quality assessment through debiased probabilities and saturation thresholds is crucial for preventing low-quality outputs and ensuring fair comparisons between candidates of varying lengths.
