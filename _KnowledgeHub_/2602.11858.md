# Zooming without Zooming: Region-to-Image Distillation for Fine-Grained Multimodal Perception

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Ant Group developed "Zooming without Zooming" (ZwZ), a paradigm using Region-to-Image Distillation (R2I) to train multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for fine-grained perception. This approach internalizes "zooming" capabilities during training, enabling a single-pass inference that achieves competitive accuracy while being approximately 10 times faster than agentic "Thinking-with-Images" models.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) often lack fine-grained perception, struggling to discern subtle details like tiny text or specific attributes embedded within complex visual contexts.
- Current solutions using iterative "zooming" or agentic tool-use ("Thinking-with-Images") suffer from high inference latency, rendering them impractical for real-time applications.
- Existing benchmarks for fine-grained perception are often limited in scope, task coverage, or difficulty, hindering robust evaluation of MLLM capabilities.

## Method

- Developed ==Region-to-Image Distillation (R2I)==, an automated pipeline that synthesizes high-veracity, fine-grained VQA data by generating QA pairs from micro-crops using powerful teacher models, then distilling this supervision to full images with visual (bounding box overlay) and textual grounding.
- Trained student MLLMs using reinforcement learning (==DAPO==) on these augmented full-image QA pairs, teaching them to intrinsically "gaze" at relevant micro-evidence from the global context in a single forward pass.
- Introduced ==ZoomBench==, a hybrid-annotated benchmark featuring diverse perceptual dimensions and a dual-view evaluation protocol to rigorously assess fine-grained acuity and quantify the "global-regional zooming gap."

## Results

- ZwZ models, trained with **74K** R2I samples, demonstrated leading performance on various fine-grained benchmarks, surpassing larger open-source MLLMs and achieving competitive accuracy with state-of-the-art closed-source models, while performing approximately **10 times** faster than agentic baselines.
- Region-to-Image Distillation yielded higher-quality, more efficient training data, with models trained on **74K** R2I samples outperforming those trained on significantly larger public datasets.
- On the novel ZoomBench, ZwZ-8B reduced the "zooming gap" to **15.26%**, the smallest among tested models, and exhibited increased relative attention coverage within annotated key regions, confirming improved fine-grained attention.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The benefits of inference-time agentic "zooming" for fine-grained perception can be effectively internalized into an MLLM during training, enabling a single-pass inference that maintains accuracy without high latency.
> - Synthesizing fine-grained VQA data from micro-crops using powerful teacher models significantly improves data quality and reduces hallucination compared to direct global-to-global synthesis.
> - Visually augmenting training images with bounding box overlays for specific regions serves as crucial "privileged information," guiding the model's attention to learn intrinsic fine-grained perception.
