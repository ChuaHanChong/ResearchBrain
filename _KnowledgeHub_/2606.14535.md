# Spatially Conditioned Diffusion Policy: Learning Precise and Robust Manipulation with a Single RGB Camera

> [!summary] Summary
> The Spatially Conditioned Diffusion Policy (SCDP) enables precise and robust robotic manipulation using only a single RGB camera by leveraging evolving end-effector trajectories as dynamic attention anchors. This approach achieves an 87.2% average success rate across 54 simulation tasks and a 73.8% average success rate in real-world scenarios, outperforming multi-camera and depth-based baselines in many challenging manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Robotic manipulation via imitation learning often relies on multi-camera setups, including wrist-mounted cameras, for precise and contact-rich interactions.
- Achieving comparable performance with only a single global RGB camera is challenging due to the need to simultaneously capture fine-grained visual details and accurately identify task-relevant regions in complex scenes.
- Existing single-view visuomotor policies often infer visual relevance implicitly or rely on large, pre-trained models, which can be insufficient for fine-grained control and precise spatial attention.

## Method

- Spatially Conditioned Diffusion Policy (**SCDP**) introduces a novel ==spatial conditioning mechanism== within a diffusion-based visuomotor policy, operating solely with a single global RGB camera.
- It leverages intermediate end-effector trajectories as dynamic attention anchors, projecting them onto the image plane to explicitly query spatially selective, multi-scale visual features during the denoising process.
- The multi-scale image encoder and the spatial conditioning module extract both broad scene context and fine-grained local details around the predicted interaction points, which then conditions an action denoising network.

## Results

- SCDP achieved an **87.2%** average success rate across 54 diverse Meta-World and DexArt simulation tasks, outperforming Diffusion Policy (**54.9%**) and DP3 (**82.3%**) which used depth information.
- In real-world experiments, SCDP demonstrated an average success rate of **73.8%** across six tasks, showing robust performance in high-precision (e.g., USB insertion) and distractor-heavy scenarios with a single RGB camera.
- Ablation studies confirmed the importance of the multi-scale image encoder and dynamic reconstruction horizon, showing superior data efficiency and competitive performance compared to baselines using additional wrist cameras or depth sensors.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Evolving future end-effector trajectories, when dynamically projected onto the image plane, provide an effective and task-relevant sequence of attention anchors for visuomotor policies.
> - Explicitly sampling multi-scale visual features at these dynamic attention anchors allows the policy to simultaneously capture coarse scene context and fine local details, crucial for precise manipulation from a single-view.
> - Integrating this spatial conditioning into a conditional diffusion process enables robust action generation by focusing computational resources on predicted interaction regions, effectively reducing visual ambiguity and enhancing precision.
