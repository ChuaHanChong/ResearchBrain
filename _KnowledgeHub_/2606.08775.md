# Unifying Object-Centric World Models and Diffusion Policy: A Hierarchical Framework for Multi-Stage Robotic Tasks

> [!summary] Summary
> WorldDP introduces a hierarchical framework that unifies object-centric visual world models with diffusion policies to address multi-stage robotic manipulation. This approach achieves superior performance, with a 30% success rate on the complex Cube-Triple task for all three objects and a 20% completion rate for the Scene-Single-Composite task, surpassing existing methods.

## Problem

- Current visual world models struggle with long-horizon, multi-stage robotic tasks, often succeeding only on short-horizon or single-stage objectives.
- Existing world models frequently rely on patch-level features, which are suboptimal for capturing fine-grained details critical for robust robotic interaction.
- Single-tier planning in current methods faces computational and planning difficulties when applied to complex multi-stage tasks.

## Method

- **WorldDP** proposes a hierarchical framework combining an upper-tier ==object-centric== world model for high-level planning and a lower-tier goal-conditioned diffusion policy for execution.
- An Object-Centric Encoder (OCE), guided by SAM2 segmentation masks, extracts discrete object-centric state representations from raw image observations.
- The upper-tier planning utilizes a Particle Filter for multi-modal optimization and incorporates a contact predictor into the MPC cost function to generate precise, interaction-focused subgoals.

## Results

- WorldDP achieved a **30%** success rate for manipulating all three cubes in the Cube-Triple task and a **20%** full task completion rate for Scene-Single-Composite, outperforming all comparative baselines.
- The framework demonstrated competitive performance on simpler tasks, with a **72%** success rate on Cube-Single and an average of **74%** on the Scene-Single-Direct subtasks.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the Object-Centric Encoder, Particle Filter, and Contact Predictor individually contribute to the framework's enhanced effectiveness on complex tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining object-centric representations with a hierarchical planning structure effectively decomposes and executes complex multi-stage robotic tasks.
> - A robust low-level diffusion policy can efficiently track and execute subgoals generated by a high-level world model, enhancing overall task success.
> - Integrating a contact predictor and employing a Particle Filter for multi-modal optimization significantly improves the relevance and precision of generated subgoals for robotic manipulation.
