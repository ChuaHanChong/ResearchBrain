# MedAgentGym: A Scalable Agentic Training Environment for Code-Centric Reasoning in Biomedical Data Science

> [!summary] Summary
> MedAgentGym is presented as a scalable agentic training environment designed to enhance code-centric reasoning in large language model agents for biomedical data science. It offers a unified benchmark and interactive execution environment, successfully boosting open-source LLMs to perform comparably to proprietary models; for instance, Med-Copilot-14B achieved a 71.42% overall score after online reinforcement learning, representing a 51.30% gain over its base model.

## Problem

- Existing medical benchmarks focus on knowledge-intensive, narrative reasoning, rather than the computational and coding-intensive tasks crucial for modern biomedical data science.
- Proprietary LLMs are impractical for clinical use due to privacy restrictions and high costs, while open-source LLMs severely lack specialized biomedical coding capabilities.
- There is an absence of comprehensive, code-centric benchmarks and interactive training environments that provide executable sandboxes and feedback mechanisms for LLM agents in biomedical data science.

## Method

- Introduced **MedAgentGym**, a scalable, interactive ==training environment== comprising 72,413 task instances across 129 categories, derived from 12 authentic biomedical scenarios.
- Provided an isolated, executable Docker-based sandbox with interactive feedback, translating compile and runtime errors into natural language for LLM agents to iteratively generate, execute, and debug code.
- Utilized advanced agentic training paradigms, including supervised fine-tuning (SFT), offline reinforcement learning (DPO), and online reinforcement learning (PPO, GRPO), with scalable trajectory sampling to continuously refine agent performance.

## Results

- Benchmarking 29 LLMs on MedAgentGym revealed `gpt-4.1` scored **70.15%** in success rate, while many open-source models achieved 15-**30%**.
- Med-Copilot-**14B**, an open-source model trained with GRPO, attained an overall score of **71.42%**, performing on par with proprietary models like `gpt-4.1`.
- Training improved Med-Copilot-**14B**'s success rate by +**36.98%** with SFT, +**43.02%** with DPO, and +**51.30%** with GRPO, demonstrating the efficacy of the proposed training methodology and improved generalization to out-of-distribution tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A substantial performance gap exists between proprietary and open-source LLMs in code-centric biomedical reasoning, indicating a need for specialized training infrastructure.
> - Interactive feedback and debugging within an executable environment are essential for LLM agents to successfully tackle complex code-centric biomedical tasks.
> - Agentic reinforcement learning, particularly online methods like GRPO, significantly enhances open-source LLMs' biomedical coding proficiency by leveraging iterative execution and dynamic data generation.
