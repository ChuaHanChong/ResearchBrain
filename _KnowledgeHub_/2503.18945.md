# Aether: Geometric-Aware Unified World Modeling

> [!summary] Summary
> AETHER presents a unified framework for 4D dynamic reconstruction, action-conditioned video prediction, and goal-conditioned visual planning, enabling geometry-aware world modeling. The system achieves robust zero-shot generalization from synthetic data to real-world tasks, notably reducing Absolute Relative Error for depth estimation on KITTI to 0.056 and outperforming baselines in action-conditioned video prediction metrics.

## Problem

- Existing AI systems often lack a unified framework to seamlessly integrate 4D geometric reconstruction, future prediction, and goal-driven planning for comprehensive world understanding.
- The development of robust 4D world models is hindered by the limited availability of high-quality, densely annotated real-world dynamic scene data.
- Purely visual generative models may produce predictions that are visually plausible but physically inconsistent, lacking inherent geometric reasoning for real-world interaction.

## Method

- A unified, geometry-aware framework is proposed, built upon a pre-trained video ==diffusion model== (**CogVideoX-5b-I2V**), to jointly handle 4D reconstruction, action-conditioned prediction, and goal-conditioned visual planning.
- A novel, fully automatic synthetic RGB-D video data annotation pipeline generates high-quality 4D geometry knowledge, overcoming real-world data scarcity for training.
- Camera pose trajectories are represented as raymap videos, serving as a global, geometry-informed action space to condition predictions and planning.
- A two-stage training strategy, including latent space optimization and image space refinement with multi-modal losses (MS-**SSIM**, SSI, Pointmap), ensures robust learning and geometric consistency.

## Results

- Achieved state-of-the-art zero-shot video depth estimation on KITTI (Abs Rel of 0.056) and competitive performance on Sintel, outperforming prior specialized methods despite synthetic training.
- Demonstrated superior performance in action-free and action-conditioned video prediction against the CogVideoX baseline across VBench metrics, exhibiting better generalization on out-of-domain synthetic data.
- Showed that integrating a 4D reconstruction objective significantly improves visual planning capabilities, enhancing action-conditioned navigation and action-free visual path planning compared to models without depth information.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Jointly optimizing 4D geometric reconstruction, action-conditioned prediction, and goal-conditioned planning within a single framework enables synergistic learning and more physically reasonable outcomes.
> - High-quality synthetic data, generated via a robust annotation pipeline, can facilitate zero-shot generalization of complex world models from synthetic training environments to diverse real-world scenarios.
> - Representing actions through camera pose trajectories provides an effective and intuitive geometry-aware control mechanism for embodied AI tasks.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of AI systems capable of understanding, predicting, and interacting with the physical world is a cornerstone of current AI research. "World models" have emerged as a foundational paradigm, aiming to equip autonomous agents with the capacity to perceive, anticipate, and make informed decisions within their environments. AETHER directly addresses a critical challenge within this paradigm: the integration of geometric reconstruction and generative modeling to enable human-like spatial reasoning.

Historically, research in these areas has often progressed in silos:
- **Reconstruction:** Techniques like Structure-from-Motion (SfM) and Multi-View Stereo (MVS) have long focused on deriving 3D structures from images or videos. More recently, deep learning-based methods have advanced 3D vision, including dense depth and point map estimation, as well as camera pose estimation. The paper notes the emergence of diffusion models in 3D reconstruction (e.g., GeoWizard, DepthCrafter, DA-V, Matrix3D, PonderV2, UniPAD), which leverage rich 2D priors for 3D modeling.
- **Video Generation:** This field has seen rapid advancements, particularly with diffusion models (e.g., DDPM, latent diffusion, diffusion transformers). Recent large-scale models like Sora, Stable Video Diffusion (SVD), LTX Video, CogVideoX, and Hunyuan Video have significantly improved the quality, temporal consistency, and controllability of synthetic video generation.
- **World Models & Planning:** Early world models (e.g., Ha & Schmidhuber) introduced latent representations for decision-making. More recent work, such as Cat3D for 3D scene generation, Cat4D for dynamic 4D environments, and Genie 2 for interactive 3D worlds, has expanded these capabilities. Motion Prompting enables precise video generation control. These efforts highlight the evolution towards dynamic, interactive, and controllable AI agents.

The core limitation in the current landscape is the lack of a unified framework that seamlessly combines these three essential capabilities: high-fidelity 4D perception (reconstruction), accurate future prediction conditioned on actions, and effective goal-driven planning. Existing models often specialize in one or two aspects, or rely heavily on specific modalities (e.g., text prompts for video generation, or explicit 3D supervision for reconstruction). The scarcity of high-quality, dense 4D real-world data also presents a significant bottleneck.

AETHER distinguishes itself by:
1. **Unified Approach:** Being the first to bridge 4D reconstruction, action-conditioned prediction, and goal-conditioned planning within a single, geometry-aware framework.
2. **Geometric Awareness:** Intrinsic geometric modeling, achieved through direct processing of depth and camera pose, allows for more physically reasonable and interpretable predictions compared to purely visual generative models.
3. **Scalable Data Strategy:** Leveraging a novel synthetic data annotation pipeline to overcome real-world 4D data scarcity, coupled with zero-shot synthetic-to-real generalization.
4. **Novel Action Space:** Employing camera pose trajectories as a global, geometry-informed action representation, which is intuitive and effective for ego-view tasks like navigation and robotic manipulation.

By synergistically integrating these components, AETHER pushes the frontier towards more holistic and robust AI systems capable of truly understanding and interacting with the dynamic physical world.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of AETHER is to develop a **unified framework that enables geometry-aware reasoning in world models by jointly optimizing three core capabilities**:
1. **4D Dynamic Reconstruction:** To accurately perceive and represent the intricate spatial and temporal changes of the physical world. This includes estimating depth and camera poses from video sequences.
2. **Action-Conditioned Video Prediction:** To forecast how the environment will evolve under specific, explicitly defined actions (camera trajectories).
3. **Goal-Conditioned Visual Planning:** To determine the optimal sequence of actions required to achieve a given visual goal, based on an initial observation and a target image.

The motivation behind AETHER stems from the fundamental challenge of building AI systems that go beyond mere perception to achieve human-like spatial intelligence. As highlighted by Jeff Hawkins' quote, "Prediction is not just one of the things your brain does. It is the primary function of the neocortex." This emphasizes the critical role of anticipation and foresight in intelligence. World models embody this principle by allowing AI agents to simulate and understand environmental dynamics.

Specifically, the paper identifies several key motivations:
- **Bridging Disparate Fields:** The need to bridge the gap between geometric reconstruction (understanding the physical layout and motion) and generative modeling (synthesizing future states and planning) within a single, coherent framework.
- **Addressing Data Scarcity:** The limited availability of high-quality, densely annotated 4D real-world data poses a significant hurdle. AETHER aims to overcome this by relying entirely on synthetic data during training and demonstrating zero-shot generalization to the real world.
- **Enabling Actionable Intelligence:** By integrating prediction with planning, the goal is to develop world models that not only anticipate but also guide agents towards desired outcomes, making them truly "actionable."
- **Enhancing Physical Reasonability:** Purely generative models can sometimes produce visually plausible but physically impossible scenarios. By incorporating geometric awareness, AETHER aims to generate predictions and plans that are inherently more physically reasonable.
- **Unified Action Representation:** To explore effective, geometry-informed action spaces, such as camera pose trajectories, which are intuitive for many embodied AI tasks.

In essence, AETHER seeks to create a more comprehensive and intelligent world model that can perceive its surroundings in 4D, foresee future dynamics under control, and devise strategies to achieve goals, all within a single, synergistic architecture trained on scalable synthetic data.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

AETHER proposes a unified, multi-task world modeling framework built upon a pre-trained video generation model. Its methodology encompasses a robust synthetic data pipeline, specialized processing for depth and camera pose, and a sophisticated multi-stage training strategy.

#### 3.1. Base Model and Overall Framework

AETHER leverages **CogVideoX-5b-I2V** as its foundational video diffusion model. This choice provides strong generative priors for video synthesis. The framework extends the diffusion model by augmenting its input and output capabilities to handle geometry-aware information.

The core idea is to jointly train the model on three tasks: 4D dynamic reconstruction, action-conditioned video prediction, and goal-conditioned visual planning. This is achieved through **task-interleaved feature learning**, promoting synergistic knowledge sharing.

The target latent representation ($z_0$) for the diffusion process comprises three modalities: color video latents ($z_{c0}$), depth video latents ($z_{d0}$), and action latents ($z_{a0}$), which are channel-wise concatenated. The model is conditioned on corresponding color video conditions ($c_c$) and action conditions ($c_a$).

#### 3.2. Synthetic Data Annotation Pipeline

To address the scarcity of 4D data, AETHER employs a novel, robust, and fully automatic pipeline to annotate large-scale **synthetic RGB-D video data** (collected from AAA games, following DA-V and TheMatrix). This pipeline is crucial for obtaining high-quality 4D geometry knowledge, including camera extrinsics and intrinsics. It consists of four main stages:

1. **Dynamic Masking:** Utilizes semantic categories and Grounded SAM 2 to segment potentially dynamic objects. This is critical for distinguishing dynamic from static regions, which is vital for accurate camera parameter estimation. Robustness is enhanced by selecting low-uncertainty prompts and filtering frames with high mask-to-image ratios.
2. **Video Slicing:** Filters out unsuitable video segments (e.g., scene cuts, motion blur, insufficient SIFT features, large dynamic regions, or inaccurate optical flow from RAFT). This ensures that only temporally coherent and reconstruction-friendly clips are used. Robustness is achieved by using optical flow magnitude and forward-backward error as key metrics, alongside keypoint count.
3. **Coarse Camera Estimation:** DroidCalib performs an initial estimation of camera parameters using depth information from static regions.
4. **Tracking-based Camera Refinement:** Employs CoTracker3 to establish accurate long-term correspondences using SIFT and SuperPoint features from static regions. Bundle adjustment, optimized with Ceres Solver and Cauchy loss, minimizes reprojection errors, and forward-backward reprojection with dense depth further refines per-frame camera accuracy and inter-frame geometric consistency. A final filtering step removes erroneous estimations based on anomalous focal length, reprojection error, or geometric inconsistency.

#### 3.3. Depth Videos Process

To integrate depth information, raw depth values from synthetic RGB-D videos are transformed:
1. Depths are clipped to a predefined range ($[d_{min}, d_{max}]$).
2. A square root transformation followed by a reciprocal operation converts depths to **disparity**.
3. Disparity values are normalized in a scale-invariant manner to $$, then linearly mapped to $[-1, 1]$.
4. The single-channel normalized disparity map is replicated across three channels to be compatible with the VAE encoder of the base video diffusion model, yielding $z_d$.

#### 3.4. Camera Trajectories Process

Camera pose trajectories are represented as **raymap videos** to be compatible with the spatiotemporal framework of diffusion transformers (DiTs). This involves:
1. **Translation Scaling and Normalization:** The camera translation vector is scaled, normalized by the maximum disparity, and then passed through a signed $\log(1+\cdot)$ transformation to suppress large values.
2. **Raymap Construction:** Camera ray directions are computed from the intrinsic matrix, and ray origins are set to the transformed translation. These are combined into a 6-channel raymap.
3. **Resolution Downsampling:** The raymap is spatially downsampled by a factor of 8 and temporally rearranged by concatenating every 4 consecutive frames along the channel dimension, forming the action latent $z_a$.
An inverse process (Algorithm 1 in supplementary) is defined to recover camera parameters from generated raymaps.

#### 3.5. Model Training

AETHER's training follows a two-stage strategy:
- **Initialization:** The model is initialized with pre-trained CogVideoX-5b-I2V weights. New projection layers for depth and raymap channels are initialized to zero. An empty text embedding is used since text prompts are not required.
- **Data Augmentation:** The dataset consists of variable-length video clips and FPS. Training samples are randomly selected with varying frame counts and FPS, and RoPE coefficients are adjusted accordingly.
- **Conditional Input Masking:** A key aspect of multi-task learning is dynamic masking of conditional inputs to generalize across tasks:
 - For $c_c$ (color video conditions): Different probabilities are assigned for visual planning (observation + goal), video prediction (observation only), 4D reconstruction (full video), or masking all.
 - For $c_a$ (action conditions): Trajectory latents are either kept or fully masked with equal probability for action-conditioned or action-free tasks.
- **Stage 1: Latent Space Optimization:** The first stage minimizes the standard latent diffusion loss (MSE between predicted and actual noise in the latent space).
- **Stage 2: Image Space Refinement:** In this stage, generated latents are decoded into image space, and three additional loss terms are introduced:
 - **Multi-Scale Structural Similarity (MS-SSIM) loss:** For color video, to preserve perceptual quality and structural coherence.
 - **Scale- and Shift-Invariant (SSI) loss:** For depth videos, to align predicted depths with ground truth by optimizing for scale and shift parameters and enforcing gradient smoothness.
 - **Pointmap loss:** For raymaps, by projecting predicted disparity and raymaps into 3D pointmaps and minimizing their difference, ensuring 3D spatial alignment. This loss only back-propagates to raymap latents, not disparity.

The training utilizes a hybrid parallelization strategy (FSDP, Zero-2, DDP) on 80 A100-80GB GPUs for two weeks, using the AdamW optimizer and a OneCycle learning rate scheduler.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

AETHER demonstrates remarkable capabilities across its three core tasks, showcasing strong zero-shot generalization from synthetic training data to unseen real-world scenarios.

#### 4.1. 4D Reconstruction

AETHER's reconstruction performance is evaluated on zero-shot video depth estimation and camera pose estimation, with competitive or superior results compared to state-of-the-art domain-specific models.

- **Zero-Shot Video Depth Estimation (Table 1):**
 - **Sintel:** AETHER achieves the lowest Absolute Relative Error (Abs Rel) of 0.324 among reconstruction-based methods (e.g., MonST3R-GA: 0.378) and 0.314 among diffusion-based methods (e.g., ChronoDepth: 0.429), while maintaining competitive $\delta < 1.25$ scores.
 - **KITTI:** AETHER sets a new benchmark with an Abs Rel of 0.056 and $\delta < 1.25$ of 97.8, significantly outperforming previous SOTA like CUT3R (Abs Rel: 0.118, $\delta < 1.25$: 88.1). Similar strong performance is observed against diffusion-based methods.
 - **BONN:** AETHER shows competitive results, achieving the highest $\delta < 1.25$ (60.2) among diffusion-based methods. The paper notes that performance on BONN is comparatively weaker than on Sintel and KITTI, attributing this to BONN's indoor scene type, potential incompatibility with learned priors, and lower image quality with motion blur.
 - **Overall:** AETHER consistently outperforms or is comparable to prior approaches on real-world datasets, despite being trained solely on synthetic data, highlighting its robust generalization.

- **Zero-Shot Camera Pose Estimation (Table 2):**
 - Evaluated on Sintel, TUM Dynamics, and ScanNet datasets.
 - **Sintel:** Among feed-forward methods, AETHER achieves the best Absolute Translation Error (ATE) of 0.189 and Relative Translation Error (RPE Trans) of 0.054, with competitive Relative Rotation Error (RPE Rot).
 - **TUM Dynamics:** AETHER demonstrates the best RPE Trans (0.012) across all compared methods.
 - **ScanNet:** Performance on ScanNet is noted to be reduced, likely due to the domain gap from synthetic training data and the dataset's inherent imperfections like motion blur.
 - **Noise Mitigation:** A simple Kalman filter is applied to smooth generated camera trajectories, addressing noise likely caused by limited denoising steps.
 - **Overall:** AETHER's camera pose estimation is competitive with other feed-forward methods, which typically prioritize speed over the higher accuracy of optimization-based techniques.

#### 4.2. Generation and Planning

AETHER's generative capabilities are assessed through video prediction (action-free and action-conditioned) and visual planning, showing clear superiority over baselines.

- **Video Prediction without Action Conditions (Image-to-Video, Table 3):**
 - **Comparison:** AETHER versus CogVideoX-5b-I2V (baseline, with GPT-4o generated text prompts for fairness) on in-domain and out-domain synthetic validation sets. AETHER uses empty text prompts.
 - **Results:** AETHER consistently outperforms the baseline across all VBench metrics (subject consistency, background consistency, motion smoothness, dynamic degree, aesthetic quality, imaging quality, and weighted average). The improvement is more pronounced on out-domain data, suggesting better generalization from its geometry-aware training.

- **Action-Conditioned Video Prediction (Table 4):**
 - **Comparison:** AETHER (using raymap action conditions) versus CogVideoX (using GPT-4o generated detailed language descriptions of movements as action conditions).
 - **Results:** AETHER consistently outperforms CogVideoX across VBench metrics. A key qualitative difference is AETHER's ability to accurately follow action conditions and produce highly dynamic scenes, whereas CogVideoX often generates static scenes despite high visual quality. This validates the effectiveness of camera trajectories as an explicit, geometry-informed action space.

- **Visual Planning:**
 - **Action-Conditioned Navigation (Table 5):**
 - **Comparison:** AETHER versus AETHER-no-depth (an ablation model trained without the video depth objective).
 - **Metrics:** Pixel-wise reconstruction metrics (PSNR, SSIM, MS-SSIM, LPIPS).
 - **Results:** AETHER consistently outperforms AETHER-no-depth across all metrics, demonstrating the significant benefit of incorporating the reconstruction objective into the generative model for navigation tasks.
 - **Action-Free Visual Path Planning (Table 6):**
 - **Comparison:** AETHER versus AETHER-no-depth.
 - **Metrics:** VBench.
 - **Results:** The reconstruction objective substantially improves the model's visual path planning capabilities, enabling it to function as a "world model as an agent" from observation to goal.

- **Qualitative Visualizations:** Figure 1 showcases AETHER's ability to perform 4D reconstruction, action-conditioned prediction, and visual planning on unseen real-world data, highlighting the framework's versatility and strong visual coherence.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

AETHER represents a substantial leap towards developing more capable and generalist AI world models, with profound significance and potential impact:

1. **Unified World Modeling Paradigm:** AETHER is the first framework to successfully integrate three critical capabilities—4D geometric reconstruction, action-conditioned video prediction, and goal-conditioned visual planning—into a single, unified model. This unification moves beyond specialized AI systems, paving the way for more holistic intelligence in complex environments.
2. **Geometry-Aware Spatial Reasoning:** By inherently modeling 4D geometry (depth and camera poses), AETHER provides a foundation for physically reasonable world understanding. This contrasts with purely pixel-based generative models that might produce visually compelling but physically inconsistent outcomes. This geometric grounding is crucial for applications requiring real-world interaction and safety.
3. **Scalable Training with Synthetic Data & Zero-Shot Generalization:** One of AETHER's most impactful contributions is its demonstration of robust zero-shot generalization from training exclusively on synthetic 4D data to performing effectively on unseen real-world data. This breakthrough significantly mitigates the notorious challenge of acquiring vast amounts of expensive and scarce real-world 4D annotations, making the development of advanced world models more scalable and accessible. The robust automatic synthetic data annotation pipeline developed by the authors is a key enabler for this.
4. **Effective Action Representation:** The use of camera trajectory as a geometry-informed global action space is highly intuitive and effective, particularly for embodied AI tasks like robot navigation and manipulation. This representation allows for fine-grained control and planning within the visual environment.
5. **Benchmarking Performance:** AETHER achieves state-of-the-art or competitive performance in 4D reconstruction tasks (video depth and camera pose estimation) and superior performance in video prediction and visual planning tasks compared to specialized baselines. This quantitative validation underscores the efficacy of its multi-task learning approach and geometric integration.
6. **Inspiration for Future Research:** The authors explicitly state their hope that AETHER will "inspire the community to explore new frontiers in physically-reasonable world modeling and its applications." It serves as an effective "starter framework" for post-training world models with scalable synthetic data, opening new avenues for research in robotic control, autonomous driving, virtual reality, and advanced simulations.

**Limitations and Future Work:**
The authors acknowledge several limitations:
- **Camera Pose Estimation Accuracy:** Performance is less accurate compared to optimization-based methods, potentially due to incompatibilities between the raymap representation and prior video diffusion models. Future work could explore novel action representations.
- **Indoor Scene Performance:** Reconstruction quality in indoor scenes lags behind outdoor scenarios, likely because the training data predominantly features outdoor environments. Co-training with real-world data could address this domain gap.
- **Dynamic Scenes without Language Prompts:** Predictions in highly dynamic scenes without language prompts sometimes fail, suggesting a need to retain or enhance the language prompting capabilities of the base model or improve the model's intrinsic understanding of complex dynamics.

Despite these limitations, AETHER represents a significant step forward in unified, geometry-aware world modeling. Its ability to generalize from synthetic data to real-world tasks in a zero-shot manner, while achieving strong performance across diverse capabilities, positions it as a foundational work with the potential to accelerate the development of more intelligent and adaptable AI systems.
