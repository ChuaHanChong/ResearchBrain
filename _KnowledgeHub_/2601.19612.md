# Safe Exploration via Policy Priors

> [!summary] Summary
> SOOPER is a model-based reinforcement learning algorithm developed at ETH Zurich that ensures high-probability safety throughout online learning and achieves sublinear cumulative regret for optimal policy convergence. This method leverages conservative policy priors for safe exploration and has been validated on benchmarks and real-world robotic hardware, achieving approximately twice the reward of an initial prior while maintaining strict safety.

## Problem

- Deploying reinforcement learning agents in physical, real-world environments necessitates ensuring safety during online exploration, where catastrophic outcomes are unacceptable.
- Existing safe RL methods either offer strong theoretical guarantees but lack scalability to complex, high-dimensional problems, or they are scalable but fail to consistently ensure safety throughout the entire learning process.
- Ineffective utilization of prior knowledge (e.g., from offline data or simulators) to simultaneously ensure safety and guide efficient exploration.

## Method

- **SOOPER** combines pessimistic invocation of a ==conservative policy prior== during online data collection with optimistic planning in a learned world model.
- The algorithm continuously tracks predicted accumulated costs, falling back to a safe policy prior if an action from the current policy risks exceeding a predefined safety budget.
- The constrained optimization problem is reformulated into an unconstrained MDP for planning, which includes intrinsic rewards that encourage optimistic exploration and active expansion of the known safe policy set.

## Results

- SOOPER provides theoretical guarantees for high-probability safety throughout online learning (Theorem 1) and achieves sublinear cumulative regret for convergence to an optimal policy (Theorem 2).
- Empirically, the algorithm maintained safety (no constraint violations) and matched or outperformed state-of-the-art baselines in performance across safe transfer, vision-based control, and offline-to-online learning tasks.
- SOOPER successfully fine-tuned policies on real-world robotic hardware (a race car), achieving approximately twice the reward of an initial prior policy while strictly adhering to safety constraints during real-time online learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Conservative, suboptimal policy priors can be effectively integrated to provide high-probability safety guarantees during online learning, even under initial model uncertainty or distribution shifts.
> - An online cost-tracking mechanism, which switches to a safe prior when an uncertainty-penalized accumulated cost exceeds a budget, ensures safety throughout all learning episodes.
> - Reformulating the constrained problem as an unconstrained MDP with intrinsic exploration and expansion bonuses enables both scalability with deep RL and provable sublinear cumulative regret, ensuring good performance during and after learning.
