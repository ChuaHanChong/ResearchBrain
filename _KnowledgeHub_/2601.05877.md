# iReasoner: Trajectory-Aware Intrinsic Reasoning Supervision for Self-Evolving Large Multimodal Models

> [!summary] Summary
> The IREASONER framework enhances the implicit reasoning capabilities of Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) by introducing a trajectory-aware intrinsic reward for Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps within a fully unsupervised self-evolution setting. This approach yielded consistent performance gains across eight diverse multimodal reasoning benchmarks, particularly in visual mathematics.

## Problem

- Existing self-evolving LMMs primarily rely on "outcome-only" rewards, which provide insufficient supervision for the internal Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning process.
- Current methods for optimizing CoT in LMMs typically require labeled data, external judges, or offline supervision, limiting their applicability in unsupervised settings.
- Without direct feedback on intermediate steps, LMMs may develop unstable reasoning paths or rely on shortcuts, even if the final answer is correct.

## Method

- IREASONER integrates a novel ==trajectory-aware intrinsic reward== into a Proposer-Solver self-evolution framework that operates solely on unlabeled images.
- It quantifies ==CoT step agreement== by embedding intermediate claims from multiple Solver rollouts and comparing them to dynamically generated per-step prototypes using weighted cosine similarity.
- The Solver's training objective combines traditional answer-level consistency rewards with this new ==step-level agreement reward==, gradually introduced via a warmup schedule to foster stable learning.

## Results

- IREASONER achieved consistent performance gains across eight diverse multimodal reasoning benchmarks, with an average improvement of **+1.32 points** on general visual understanding and **+1.64 points** on visual mathematics tasks.
- The framework surpassed existing outcome-only self-evolution methods (e.g., EvoLMM) on all general visual understanding benchmarks, showing an average gain of **+0.50 points** on InfoGraphic-VQA and **+0.48 points** on AI2D.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical role of the reward warmup schedule, which led to the largest performance regression when removed, and demonstrated the complementary nature of answer stability and step-wise rewards.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Cross-rollout agreement on intermediate Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps can serve as a robust intrinsic reward signal, enabling step-level supervision without external labels or human intervention.
> - Combining outcome-level consistency with a continuously applied step-level agreement reward, introduced progressively, is more effective than discrete rewards for improving multimodal reasoning.
> - Early reasoning steps are particularly influential for overall consistency, and their consistency is emphasized through higher weighting in the intrinsic reward calculation.
