# Selftok: Discrete Visual Tokens of Autoregression, by Diffusion, and for Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Huawei's Media Technology Institute developed Selftok, a discrete visual tokenizer that represents images as autoregressive tokens derived from the reverse diffusion process. This approach facilitates purely discrete Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and enables effective visual Reinforcement Learning (RL), achieving state-of-the-art visual generation and reasoning capabilities.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with incompatible continuous autoregressive (cAR) methods for images or use conventional spatial discrete tokens that are not truly autoregressive.
- Non-autoregressive visual representations, particularly spatial tokens, violate the policy improvement optimality required for effective Reinforcement Learning (RL) in visual generation tasks.
- Large Language Models (LLMs) are facing data saturation with text, and there is a need for robust methods to integrate non-language modalities like images and videos into unified AI frameworks.

## Method

- ==Selftok==, a ==self-consistency tokenizer==, encodes images into discrete, non-spatial, autoregressive tokens by leveraging the recursive nature of the ==reverse diffusion process== in its ODE form.
- A purely discrete autoregressive VLM is constructed by initializing from a Llama3-8B LLM, expanding its vocabulary with Selftok's visual words, and training it using standard language modeling objectives.
- ==Visual Reinforcement Learning (RL)== is applied by framing visual generation as a finite ==Markov Decision Process==, validated by Selftok's AR structure that satisfies the Bellman equation, using both program-based and QA-based reward models for optimization.

## Results

- The Selftok tokenizer achieves superior image reconstruction quality on ImageNet-1k, reaching a PSNR of **26.30** and an LPIPS of **0.063** with **1024** tokens, outperforming established spatial and diffusion-based tokenizers.
- The Selftok-Zero model, optimized with visual RL, attains a GenEval score of **95** (surpassing SD3-Medium's **74**) and a DPG-Bench score of **85.57**, demonstrating substantial performance improvements (**+21** on GenEval, **+3.77** on DPG-Bench) in text-to-image generation and reasoning tasks.
- Selftok-based VLMs exhibit positive synergy between visual generation and comprehension and effectively mitigate hallucination issues, accurately generating rare object combinations that supervised models struggle with due to training data biases.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The reverse diffusion process exhibits an inherent recursive structure that can be directly mapped to an autoregressive causal graph, enabling the learning of naturally autoregressive visual tokens.
> - Autoregressive discrete visual tokens, unlike conventional spatial tokens, adhere to the Bellman equation, which is foundational for enabling optimal policy improvement in Reinforcement Learning for visual generation.
> - Discarding the spatial prior in visual tokenization and adopting an autoregressive structure allows for a minimalist, unified LLM-centric architecture for multimodal AI, simplifying integration and training.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) has highlighted the power of discrete autoregressive (AR) architectures, but their reliance on language data is approaching saturation. This paper situates itself at the critical juncture of expanding AI capabilities beyond text, recognizing the immense, yet largely untapped, potential of non-language data such as images and videos. The overarching goal in this landscape is to unify multiple modalities into a single, cohesive AI framework, mimicking the success of LLMs.

Current approaches to integrating vision into language models largely fall into two categories, both of which Selftok critiques:

1. **Hybrid Models:** Many Vision-Language Models (VLMs) adopt a hybrid approach, using discrete AR for language and continuous AR (cAR) for images. While proponents argue cAR minimizes compression loss, Selftok identifies several fundamental issues:
 - **Incompatibility with LLM Infrastructure:** cAR cannot directly leverage the well-established infrastructure and training paradigms of LLMs.
 - **Optimization Challenges:** cAR, using Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss, is less stable and harder to optimize than discrete AR models, which use Cross-Entropy (XE) loss. This often forces cAR models to abandon causal next-token prediction for bidirectional modeling, undermining the core AR philosophy.
 - **Reinforcement Learning (RL) Complexity:** cAR transforms the finite Markov Decision Process (MDP) of discrete tokens into an infinite MDP, complicating policy optimization for RL, a crucial post-training step for LLMs.
 - **Lack of Disentanglement:** Continuous representations struggle to achieve disentanglement of generative factors, requiring impractically large datasets. Discrete tokens, with their limited information bandwidth, act as a strong inductive bias for disentanglement.

2. **Conventional Spatial Tokens:** Even when discrete visual tokens are used, they typically encode images as spatial grids (patches). Selftok argues that this "spatial prior" is fundamentally misaligned with autoregressive modeling:
 - **Non-Autoregressive Dependencies:** Spatial pixels (causes) collectively form an image (effect). Observing any part during encoding introduces "spurious dependencies" (collider effect) among tokens, making them non-AR.
 - **Violation of RL Optimality:** Crucially, non-AR spatial dependencies violate the policy improvement optimality in RL. Later token predictions can anti-causally affect earlier, already optimized policies, leading to significantly less effective RL.

Selftok aims to resolve these critical issues by proposing a novel discrete visual tokenizer that is inherently autoregressive and non-spatial. By mirroring the causal structure of language, Selftok seeks to enable the seamless integration of visual data into the LLM paradigm, thereby unlocking true multimodal reasoning and effective visual reinforcement learning.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to **introduce Selftok (Self-consistency Tokenizer)**, a novel discrete visual tokenizer that fundamentally redefines how images are represented for multimodal AI. Unlike conventional methods that rely on spatial priors, Selftok completely discards this assumption in favor of an autoregressive token structure.

The core motivations driving the development of Selftok are multi-faceted:

- **Unifying Diffusion and Autoregression for VLMs:** A key motivation is to provide an "elegant and minimalist approach" to unify the powerful generative capabilities of diffusion models with the sequential processing strength of autoregressive models. By encoding images into Selftok tokens, the paper aims to train a Vision-Language Model (VLM) using a purely discrete autoregressive architecture, mirroring the successful paradigm of LLMs. This unification is sought without requiring additional modules or complex training objectives, simplifying the architecture and training process for multimodal foundation models.
- **Enabling Effective Reinforcement Learning (RL) for Visual Generation:** A significant long-standing challenge in visual AI has been the difficulty of applying effective RL to visual generation tasks. The paper theoretically demonstrates that Selftok's autoregressive prior satisfies the Bellman equation, a cornerstone of RL optimality, which spatial priors fail to do. This theoretical grounding motivates Selftok's design to unlock RL-based post-training for visual generation, akin to how RL has dramatically enhanced the reasoning capabilities of LLMs. This is presented as a crucial step towards advancing visual generation from mere data imitation to genuine reasoning.
- **Addressing the "Hallucination" Problem:** VLMs trained solely on supervised vision-language pairs often suffer from "hallucination," generating content that mimics training data distribution rather than truly understanding or reasoning about prompts. The motivation is that effective visual RL, facilitated by Selftok's AR properties, can overcome this by optimizing models directly for task-specific rewards, leading to more robust and accurate generations.
- **Achieving State-of-the-Art Reconstruction and Compression:** Beyond its primary architectural innovations, Selftok is also designed to be a highly performant tokenizer, aiming for a favorable trade-off between high-quality image reconstruction and compression rate. This ensures that the novel token structure does not compromise fundamental image representation fidelity.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The Selftok methodology centers on a novel discrete visual tokenizer that leverages the autoregressive nature of the reverse diffusion process. This tokenizer, in turn, underpins a unified VLM training pipeline and an effective visual Reinforcement Learning (RL) framework.

#### 3.1. Selftok: Self-consistency Tokenizer

The core idea is to encode an image $I$ into $K$ discrete tokens $V_K = [v_1, \dots, v_K]$ that adhere to an autoregressive (AR) prior, enabling high-quality reconstruction.

- **Autoregression and Recursion by Diffusion:** Selftok establishes a profound connection between the AR causal graph ($P(V_K) = P(V_{<i}) \cdot P(V_{\ge i} | V_{<i})$) and the recursive nature of the reverse diffusion process in its ODE form ($x_1 = x_t + \int_t^1 v_s(x_s)ds$). This means that the "second-half" tokens ($V_{\ge i}$) can be learned by reconstructing $x_1$ from an intermediate noisy state $x_t$, which itself encapsulates the "first-half" tokens ($V_{<i}$). This recursive formulation naturally imbues the tokens with an AR structure.
- **Causal Identification:** To ensure the learned $V_K$ truly possesses an AR structure, the training objective uses a re-parameterization of $x_t = \sigma(t) \cdot x_0 + \mu(t) \cdot x_1$. By framing $x_0$ as an instrumental variable, independent of the confounder $x_1$, the method ensures that $V_{\ge k(t)}$ learned from $x_t$ is an unbiased estimate.
- **Tokenizer Architecture:** Selftok employs a dual-stream transformer backbone, similar to MMDiT, comprising an image stream and a token stream.
 - **Encoder:** Takes VAE latent embeddings of images (from a pre-trained SD3 VAE) as input to the image stream. It uses co-attention to allow the token stream to learn from image features while preventing spurious dependencies. The output is $K$ continuous token embeddings. Token-aware Adaptive LayerNorm (AdaLN) differentiates token embeddings.
 - **Quantizer:** Compresses the continuous token embeddings through a bottleneck layer and quantizes them against a learnable codebook (2^15 words). A straight-through estimator is used for gradients. Quantization loss includes a commitment loss and an entropy loss to promote diverse codebook usage, complemented by EMA updates and dead-code reactivation to prevent underutilized words.
 - **Decoder:** A diffusion model initialized from SD3, configured as a dual-stream MMDiT. It reconstructs the image $x_1$ given a noisy input $x_t$ and a masked sequence of quantized token embeddings $V_{\ge k(t)}$. Its token stream weights are trained from scratch.
 - **Training Objective:** Jointly optimizes encoder and decoder parameters using an MSE loss, incorporating the diffusion re-parameterization and a re-weighting mechanism for token embeddings to balance gradient updates across tokens.
 - **Token Schedule $k(t)$:** A crucial component mapping continuous time $t$ to discrete token index $k(t)$. A custom schedule that allocates fewer tokens to smaller $t$ (early diffusion steps) is empirically found to be more effective, aligning with the diffusion model's trait that early noise has less impact on reconstruction.
- **One-step Renderer:** To accelerate inference, a separate one-step renderer $R(V_K)$ is trained *after* the tokenizer. It is initialized with decoder weights but replaces the $x_t$ dependency with learnable "canvas" tokens. This renderer is optimized with a combination of MSE, LPIPS (perceptual), and GAN losses for fast, high-quality, and non-random image reconstruction.

#### 3.2. Selftok-based Vision-Language Model (VLM) Training

The discrete, autoregressive nature of Selftok tokens enables a purely discrete AR VLM, initialized from a pre-trained LLM.

- **Model Initialization:** The VLM is initialized from Llama3-8B, with its vocabulary expanded to include the 32,768 Selftok visual words.
- **Training Objective:** Standard language modeling objective (maximizing log-likelihood of multimodal token sequences) with cross-entropy loss.
- **Two-Stage Training:**
 - **Stage 1: Cross-modality Alignment:** Focuses on aligning visual and language tokens using a diverse dataset of 530 million high-quality image-text pairs and text sequences. Four data formats (Text-to-Image, Image-to-Text, Image-Only, Text-Only) and multi-level captioning are used to enhance multimodal understanding.
 - **Stage 2: Cross-task Alignment (Supervised Fine-Tuning - SFT):** The model is fine-tuned on instruction-based datasets for text-to-image generation, image editing, and image understanding, using a "USER: <Instructions> ASSISTANT: <Answers>" format.
- **Inference with Adaptive Logit Adjustment:** During inference, an adaptive logit adjustment strategy is employed. Similar to classifier-free guidance, it adjusts logits when the token prediction entropy exceeds a threshold, indicating model uncertainty, thereby improving generation quality.

#### 3.3. Selftok-based Visual Reinforcement Learning (RL)

This is a critical innovation, enabling reasoning for visual generation.

- **Problem Formulation:** Visual RL is framed as a finite Markov Decision Process (MDP) for autoregressive tokens. The state $s_k$ is the sequence of generated tokens, the action is selecting the next token $v_{k+1}$ from the codebook, and the state transition is deterministic.
- **Bellman Equation Validity:** The paper theoretically proves that Selftok's AR structure allows the derivation of the Bellman equation, which is foundational for policy improvement optimality in RL. This is explicitly contrasted with non-AR spatial tokens, which violate this condition due to anti-causal dependencies.
- **Reward Models:**
 - **Program-based Reward:** For structured tasks (e.g., object counting, spatial relations), this model uses visual detectors (MM-Detection) to objectively evaluate generated image properties against the prompt, returning a score (e.g., 1 for correct, 0 for incorrect count).
 - **QA-based Reward:** For more complex and ambiguous prompts, powerful VLMs (InternVL, GPT-4o) are used. The prompt is decomposed into semantic tuples, questions are generated, and the VLM performs VQA on the generated image to provide an averaged reward score (0-1).
- **Policy Gradient:** A simplified version of GRPO is used. The policy network (the VLM) generates batches of outputs, rewards and advantages are calculated, and the policy parameters are updated using a loss function that includes a KL divergence term to maintain training stability. This is explicitly differentiated from diffusion-based DPO, which lacks access to true state-action trajectories.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research presents compelling evidence for Selftok's efficacy across tokenizer performance, VLM capabilities, and the groundbreaking application of visual RL.

#### 4.1. Selftok Tokenizer Performance

- **State-of-the-Art Reconstruction:** Selftok achieves superior reconstruction quality compared to existing spatial (e.g., VQGAN-LC, VAR, TiTok) and diffusion-based (e.g., FlowMo) tokenizers on the ImageNet-1k validation set, measured by rFID, PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS (Table 1, Figure 8). For instance, with 1024 tokens, Selftok achieves a PSNR of 26.30 and an LPIPS of 0.063, surpassing FlowMo-Hi (24.93 PSNR, 0.073 LPIPS) at a comparable token count.
- **Non-Spatial and Autoregressive Properties:** Qualitative visualizations (Figure 3) demonstrate that Selftok tokens are indeed non-spatial, representing the entire image generation path rather than localized patches. Crucially, empirical analysis of next-token prediction entropy (Figure 6) verifies Selftok's AR property, showing segmented decreasing trends that align with various shuffled generation orders, a characteristic not observed in spatial tokenizers.
- **Ablation Studies:** The studies confirm that increasing token count ($K$) and codebook size ($C$) improves reconstruction (Tables 3, 4). The custom token schedule and uniform time sampling yielded optimal performance (Table 2, Figure 9). The one-step renderer significantly accelerates reconstruction (from 8.2s to 0.31s per image) and improves reconstruction quality (e.g., 1024 tokens: PSNR 23.06 -> 26.26; LPIPS 0.119 -> 0.063) (Table 4, Figure 10).

#### 4.2. Selftok-based VLM Performance

- **Text-to-Image Generation (Selftok-SFT):** The Selftok-SFT model (after supervised fine-tuning) demonstrates strong text-to-image generation capabilities. On the GenEval benchmark, it scores 74, outperforming models like Emu3-Gen (54) and TokenFlow (55), and is competitive with advanced models like Infinity (73) and CogView4 (73) (Table 7). On DPG-Bench, Selftok-SFT achieves 81.8, exceeding Emu3-Gen (80.6) (Table 8). Qualitative examples show good adherence to complex text prompts and aesthetically pleasing images (Figure 12).
- **Image Editing (Selftok-SFT):** Selftok-SFT achieves competitive performance on the PIE-Bench dataset, showing a favorable trade-off between fidelity (e.g., Structure Distance, Background Preservation) and editability (CLIP Similarity) (Table 5). Qualitatively, it is comparable to leading models like GPT-4o, SeedEdit-1.0, and Gemini-2.0 in single-turn editing and maintains relatively better text-image alignment and identity preservation in multi-turn editing (Figures 13, 14).
- **Vision-Language Comprehension (Selftok-SFT):** Selftok scores 1381.3 on the MME benchmark, surpassing other unified models for both understanding and generation, such as Emu3 (1292.8) and VILA-U (1338.1). However, it acknowledges falling short of models specialized solely for comprehension (e.g., LLaVA, InternLM-XComposer2-VL) or those with dedicated comprehension branches (e.g., Janus-Pro) (Table 6).
- **Synergy between Generation and Comprehension:** Selftok-based VLMs consistently exhibit a positive synergy between visual generation and comprehension tasks, where dual-task training leads to performance gains for both. This contrasts with spatial VQ-tokens, which sometimes show negative synergy (Figure 15).

#### 4.3. Selftok-based Visual RL Performance (Selftok-Zero)

- **State-of-the-Art in Text-to-Image Generation:** Selftok-Zero, the model after visual RL, achieves groundbreaking performance. On GenEval, it scores 95 overall, surpassing all previous models including strong baselines like SD3-Medium (74) and HiDream-I1 (83) (Table 7). It shows significant gains across sub-tasks like Position (98) and Counting (90). On DPG-Bench, Selftok-Zero achieves 85.57, outperforming SD3-Medium (84.08) and Janus-Pro-7B (84.19) (Table 8).
- **Significant Enhancement from Visual RL:** The comparison between Selftok-SFT (supervised) and Selftok-Zero (RL-tuned) clearly demonstrates the power of visual RL. Selftok-Zero shows substantial improvements, e.g., a +21 gain in overall GenEval score, with remarkable increases in Position (+53) and Counting (+24). On DPG-Bench, an overall +3.77 increase is observed, with gains in Entity (+3.63) and Relation (+1.58).
- **Superiority over Spatial Tokens in RL:** Selftok's AR property proves critical for RL effectiveness. While Janus-Pro-7B (spatial tokens) initially outperformed Selftok-SFT, Selftok-Zero (AR tokens + RL) dramatically surpasses Janus-Pro-7B-Zero (spatial tokens + RL), highlighting that Selftok's design enables much more effective visual RL (Figure 17).
- **Addressing Hallucination:** Qualitatively, Selftok-Zero effectively overcomes hallucination issues, successfully generating rare object combinations (e.g., "yellow broccoli," "red dog") that Selftok-SFT struggled with due to training data biases (Figure 18, 22).
- **Program-based Reward Effectiveness:** Program-based rewards (used in GenEval) yield more substantial gains (+21 overall) compared to QA-based rewards (used in DPG-Bench, +3.77 overall), suggesting that precise, structured feedback is highly effective for visual RL.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Selftok represents a significant conceptual and practical advancement in multimodal AI, addressing foundational challenges that have hindered the seamless integration of vision into large language models and effective visual reinforcement learning.

The most profound significance lies in its **unification of diffusion models and autoregressive architectures within a single, minimalist LLM framework**. By conceiving of visual tokens not as spatial patches but as discrete representations of diffusion timesteps, Selftok bypasses the need for additional, modality-specific architectures or training objectives. This simplification offers a more elegant and unified approach to multimodal learning, promising to streamline the development of general-purpose AI. The ability to treat visual tokens identically to language tokens opens the door for truly symmetrical multimodal LLMs that can reason across modalities in a unified manner.

A critical impact of Selftok is its success in **enabling effective Reinforcement Learning (RL) for visual generation**. By theoretically demonstrating that its autoregressive visual tokens satisfy the Bellman equation, Selftok resolves a long-standing challenge that plagued prior visual tokenization methods. The empirical results vividly underscore this, with Selftok-Zero (RL-tuned) achieving unprecedented performance on visual generation benchmarks, vastly outperforming both supervised counterparts and RL-tuned models using spatial tokens. This breakthrough is analogous to the impact of RL on LLMs, suggesting that Selftok can unlock sophisticated **reasoning capabilities** in visual AI, moving it beyond mere pattern imitation to genuine understanding and generation based on abstract prompts. This directly addresses the pervasive **hallucination problem** in current VLMs, allowing models to generate novel and complex visual concepts not explicitly seen in training data.

The paper's vision for "one step closer to realizing a truly multimodal LLM" is particularly impactful. By aligning visual representations with the causal, sequential nature of language, Selftok provides a missing piece for developing AI that can process, understand, and generate across text and images with integrated intelligence.

**Potential Future Impacts and Ongoing Work:**

- **Scaling and Generalization:** The work's current resolution limitation (256x256) is being addressed with **Multi-resolution Selftok**, promising efficient scalability by leveraging lower-resolution pre-training, mirroring LLM training practices. This will be crucial for real-world applications.
- **World Models and Physics-aware AI:** The success of program-based rewards in visual RL motivates **Physics-aware Post-training**. This ambitious direction aims to incorporate fundamental physical laws into video generation, potentially addressing criticisms that large visual models lack a true world model and struggle with causal reasoning in dynamic environments.
- **Broader Multimodal Capabilities:** Investigating the **scaling laws of multimodal training with Selftok** will be key to unlocking emergent capabilities across modalities, moving beyond understanding and generation tasks to more complex, cross-modal reasoning.
- **Real-time Performance:** While the current token generation speed is a limitation for video, ongoing advancements in **real-time massive token generation** within the LLM community, combined with spatial-temporal compression, are expected to mitigate this, further amplifying Selftok's potential.

In conclusion, Selftok is a foundational contribution that redefines visual representation for a unified multimodal AI paradigm. By elegantly combining diffusion and autoregression, it not only pushes the boundaries of image generation and understanding but also lays crucial theoretical and empirical groundwork for effective visual reinforcement learning, paving the way for more reasoning-capable and truly multimodal AI systems.
