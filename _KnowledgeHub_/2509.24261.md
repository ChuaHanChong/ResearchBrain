# Risk-Sensitive RL for Alleviating Exploration Dilemmas in Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> An RL framework named RS-GRPO, developed by researchers from Tsinghua University, ETH Zurich, and ByteDance Seed, leverages risk-sensitive objectives to mitigate the exploration dilemma in large language model fine-tuning. This method consistently boosts multi-solution performance (Pass@k) and solution diversity on mathematical reasoning tasks, while preserving or enhancing single-solution accuracy (Pass@1).

## Problem

- Existing Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) methods often lead to an "exploration dilemma" in LLM fine-tuning, concentrating policy mass around limited, known solutions.
- This "sharpening" compromises solution diversity and Pass@k performance, preventing LLMs from discovering genuinely novel reasoning strategies beyond their pre-trained biases.
- Standard RL algorithms struggle to escape local optima in LLMs due to their sharply peaked initial policies, essentially distilling existing capabilities rather than expanding them.

## Method

- Introduced a ==Risk-Sensitive== Reinforcement Learning framework, instantiated as the **Risk-Sensitive GRPO** (RS-GRPO) algorithm, to promote risk-seeking exploration.
- The core of RS-GRPO is a risk-seeking objective function derived from an exponential utility, which dynamically re-weights optimization signals to emphasize high-reward outcomes.
- The algorithm acts as a "drop-in replacement" for existing GRPO-based RL methods by only modifying the advantage calculation, ensuring ease of integration and implementation.

## Results

- RS-GRPO consistently outperformed standard GRPO and base LLMs across six mathematical reasoning benchmarks and five different LLMs in Pass@k, sometimes by substantial margins.
- The method maintained or improved Pass@1 accuracy across tested models, demonstrating a superior balance between single-solution performance and multi-solution discovery compared to prior Pass@k optimization techniques.
- Analysis revealed RS-GRPO significantly increased the ratio of unique correct solutions found and improved accuracy on previously unsolved prompts, directly indicating enhanced solution diversity and exploration capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A risk-seeking objective function, controlled by the hyperparameter "beta", effectively encourages LLMs to explore low-probability regions of the solution space and discover novel reasoning paths.
> - The risk-sensitive advantage function dynamically amplifies learning signals for high-reward samples, particularly correct responses on challenging prompts, fostering deeper exploration.
> - Theoretical analysis demonstrates that, for a sufficiently large risk-sensitivity parameter, this approach can guarantee an increase in the probability of optimal actions, enabling escape from local optima.
