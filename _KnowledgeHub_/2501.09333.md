# Prompt-CAM: Making Vision Transformers Interpretable for Fine-Grained Analysis

> [!summary] Summary
> PROMPT-CAM introduces class-specific prompts into frozen Vision Transformers to accurately localize fine-grained visual traits that differentiate visually similar categories. The method demonstrates superior interpretability, achieving higher faithfulness scores and enabling human experts to recognize 60.49% of identified traits, even with a slight trade-off in classification accuracy.

## Problem

- Existing saliency map methods provide coarse, object-level explanations inadequate for identifying subtle, discriminative traits in fine-grained image recognition.
- Vision Transformers often lack class-specific attention mechanisms that focus on unique visual attributes crucial for distinguishing highly similar categories.
- Other interpretable AI methods for fine-grained analysis often require significant model architectural changes or extensive training, hindering practicality and compatibility with frozen pre-trained backbones.

## Method

- Introduces ==learnable, class-specific prompt tokens== injected into a frozen Vision Transformer backbone, particularly into the final Transformer layer (PROMPT-CAM-DEEP).
- Optimizes only these prompt tokens and a ==shared linear prediction head==, keeping the powerful ViT backbone fixed to ensure efficiency and stability.
- Employs a unique ==shared prediction head mechanism== to compel the model to generate localized patch features and class-distinctive attention weights for fine-grained trait identification.

## Results

- PROMPT-CAM achieved higher faithfulness scores with an insertion score of **0.61** and a deletion score of **0.09** on the CUB dataset, outperforming other saliency methods.
- Human evaluations confirmed superior trait identification, with participants recognizing **60.49%** of expert-identified traits in PROMPT-CAM's heatmaps, significantly higher than other interpretable models.
- The method demonstrates broad applicability across 13 diverse fine-grained datasets and various Vision Transformer backbones, consistently identifying biologically and taxonomically meaningful traits.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Constraining the prediction head with a shared weight vector forces the Vision Transformer to learn highly localized, class-specific attention to distinguish fine-grained traits.
> - Simple, learnable class-specific prompts injected into frozen pre-trained Vision Transformers can effectively enable fine-grained trait localization without complex architectural changes.
> - Prioritizing interpretability through architectural design, even with a slight trade-off in raw classification accuracy, leads to more faithful and human-understandable explanations for nuanced tasks.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work is strategically positioned at the intersection of several rapidly evolving fields: advanced Vision Transformers (ViTs), explainable and interpretable AI (XAI), and fine-grained visual categorization, with a strong emphasis on applications in scientific discovery.

**Evolution of Vision Transformers:** The paper builds upon the transformative success of Vision Transformers (ViTs), particularly those pre-trained on massive datasets, which have revolutionized computer vision. It specifically leverages the "remarkable capabilities" of self-supervised ViTs like DINO and DINOv2 in extracting localized and informative features, which are crucial for fine-grained analysis. This positions PROMPT-CAM as an effort to maximize the utility of these powerful backbones for tasks requiring deep understanding beyond mere classification.

**The Need for Interpretability in Fine-Grained Analysis:** While ViTs excel in performance, their "black-box" nature remains a challenge, especially in critical applications where understanding *why* a model makes a prediction is as important as the prediction itself. This is particularly true for fine-grained tasks where minute visual differences, or "traits," are determinative. The paper critically evaluates existing XAI methods like Class Activation Maps (CAMs), Grad-CAM, Layer-CAM, and even attention roll-out, finding them inadequate for fine-grained analysis. These methods often produce coarse or blurred heatmaps that highlight entire objects rather than specific, discriminative traits. This gap in existing XAI techniques forms a primary motivation for PROMPT-CAM.

**Prompting in Vision Transformers:** The work draws inspiration from the recent success of prompt engineering in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and its adaptation to vision tasks through methods like Visual Prompt Tuning (VPT). Prompting offers a parameter-efficient alternative to full fine-tuning, allowing pre-trained models to adapt to downstream tasks with minimal changes to the backbone. PROMPT-CAM ingeniously repurposes this concept by designing "class-specific prompts" not just for task adaptation, but explicitly for driving interpretable, fine-grained trait localization.

**Distinguishing from Existing Interpretable Models:** The paper also contrasts PROMPT-CAM with other interpretable models like ProtoPNet, ProtoTree, and INTR. While these methods aim for interpretability, they often require significant modifications to the model architecture or extensive training, making them less flexible or efficient. PROMPT-CAM aims for a "free lunch" approach by building on a frozen pre-trained ViT with minimal modifications, setting it apart in terms of simplicity and practical applicability.

**Application in Scientific Domains (Imageomics):** A crucial aspect of PROMPT-CAM's fit in the broader landscape is its explicit targeting of biological and ecological research. The ability to identify species-specific traits, compare commonalities between species, and even discover taxonomy keys directly addresses critical needs in biodiversity monitoring, conservation, and biological systematics. By bridging advanced AI with scientific interpretability, PROMPT-CAM contributes to the emerging field of "Imageomics," facilitating new forms of scientific discovery from visual data.

In essence, PROMPT-CAM is an elegant solution that combines the power of state-of-the-art ViTs with the efficiency of prompt tuning to deliver high-quality, fine-grained interpretability, explicitly addressing shortcomings of previous XAI methods and catering to demanding scientific applications.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of the PROMPT-CAM research is to enhance the interpretability of pre-trained Vision Transformers (ViTs) for fine-grained analysis, specifically aiming to identify and localize the subtle visual "traits" that differentiate visually similar categories.

The **key objectives** can be summarized as:

1. **Develop a simple and efficient method:** To make pre-trained ViTs interpretable without requiring extensive model redesign, complex training processes, or modification of the frozen backbone.
2. **Achieve fine-grained trait localization:** To move beyond coarse, object-level explanations provided by existing saliency maps and pinpoint specific visual attributes that are discriminative for a given class.
3. **Enable multi-functionality for analysis:** To support not just classification, but also trait localization, comparison of common traits between classes, and the identification of the most discriminative traits for a species.
4. **Demonstrate broad applicability:** To validate the method's effectiveness across a wide range of fine-grained classification datasets from diverse domains (animals, plants, objects).

The **motivation** for PROMPT-CAM stems from several critical limitations of current approaches:

- **Failure of Existing Saliency Maps:** Traditional saliency methods like Grad-CAM, while useful for general object localization, often produce "blurred, coarse heatmaps that highlight entire objects instead" for fine-grained tasks. They fail to zoom into the subtle features that distinguish, for instance, one bird species from another. Even ViT-specific variants like attention roll-out only mildly alleviate this issue.
- **Lack of Class-Specific Attention:** The inherent [CLS] token in ViTs, while aggregating image features, often focuses on common object parts (e.g., bird heads, wings) across different categories, rather than class-specific distinguishing traits. This "class-agnostic" behavior limits its utility for fine-grained interpretation.
- **Complexity of Other Interpretable Methods:** While "interpretable" methods (e.g., ProtoPNet, ProtoTree, INTR) provide more direct insights, they typically involve designing specific model architectures, modifying the pre-trained backbone, or requiring full fine-tuning. This complexity increases training time, computational cost, and reduces compatibility with rapidly evolving, state-of-the-art pre-trained models.
- **Need for Scientifically Meaningful Explanations:** Especially in domains like biology and ecology (central to the Imageomics initiative), researchers require AI models that can identify and localize *biologically meaningful traits*. This capability is essential for scientific discovery, species identification, and understanding evolutionary relationships, tasks for which current AI explanations are often insufficient.

PROMPT-CAM is thus motivated by the need for a practical, effective, and readily deployable solution that leverages the power of modern ViTs to provide truly fine-grained and class-specific interpretations, addressing a critical gap in both AI interpretability research and its application to scientific domains.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

PROMPT-CAM introduces a novel, yet remarkably simple, approach to make pre-trained Vision Transformers interpretable for fine-grained analysis. Its core idea revolves around injecting learnable, "class-specific" prompt tokens into a frozen ViT backbone and leveraging their attention mechanisms for trait identification and localization.

**A. Core Mechanism: Class-Specific Prompts**
The fundamental innovation is to introduce $C$ learnable $D$-dimensional vectors, denoted as $P = [p_1, \dots, p_C]$, where $C$ is the number of classes. These prompts are designed to become "class-specific" during training. Unlike the standard [CLS] token, which is class-agnostic, each $p_c$ learns to attend to image regions that are distinctive for its corresponding class $c$.

**B. Variants of Prompt Injection:**
The paper explores two main variants for injecting these prompts:

1. **PROMPT-CAM-SHALLOW:** In this simpler variant, the $C$ class-specific prompts are concatenated with the initial patch embeddings ($E_0$) and the [CLS] token ($x_0$) at the input of the *first* Transformer layer ($L_1$). These prompts then interact with image features throughout all subsequent layers. While straightforward, this approach can make prompts perform a "double duty" of both adapting the ViT and localizing traits, potentially leading to less focused attention.

2. **PROMPT-CAM-DEEP (Primary Approach):** This is the more sophisticated and preferred variant. It follows the design of VPT-Deep but decouples the roles of prompts:
 - **Class-Agnostic Prompts:** For the first $N-1$ Transformer layers ($L_1$ to $L_{N-1}$), standard class-agnostic prompts (similar to VPT-Deep) are injected. These prompts primarily serve to adapt the frozen ViT backbone to the downstream fine-grained task, helping to extract relevant high-level features.
 - **Class-Specific Prompts:** The $C$ learnable class-specific prompts ($P_{N-1} = [p_1^{N-1}, \dots, p_C^{N-1}]$) are injected *only into the input of the final Transformer layer* ($L_N$). This strategic placement ensures that these prompts attend to the most abstract and semantically rich patch features ($E_{N-1}$), which are typically more informative for differentiating fine-grained classes.

**C. Training Process:**
The training process is characterized by its simplicity:
- **Frozen Backbone:** The entire pre-trained Vision Transformer backbone (e.g., DINO, DINOv2) is kept completely frozen. This significantly reduces computational cost and prevents catastrophic forgetting.
- **Learnable Parameters:** Only the injected prompt tokens ($P$ or $P_i$) and a shared learnable vector $w \in \mathbb{R}^D$ (the prediction head) are optimized.
- **Prediction Head:** For each class $c$, an output vector $z_c^N$ is obtained after the final Transformer layer. A score $s[c]$ is computed as an inner product: $s[c] = w^\top z_c^N$.
- **Loss Function:** A standard cross-entropy loss is minimized, treating $s[c]$ as logits, to ensure the ground-truth class yields the highest score.
- **"Free Lunch" Philosophy:** This setup is described as an "almost free lunch" because it requires minimal code changes (building on VPT), no specialized loss functions, and fast training times due to the small number of trainable parameters.

**D. Trait Identification and Localization (Inference):**
During inference, PROMPT-CAM offers several functionalities for fine-grained analysis:
1. **Image Classification:** The class with the highest score $s[c]$ is predicted as the label $\hat{y}$.
2. **Trait Localization:** For a given class $c$ (e.g., the ground-truth or predicted class), its multi-head attention maps ($\alpha_{c,r}^{N-1}$) from the last Transformer layer ($L_N$) are visualized. Each of the $R$ attention heads can highlight a different set of patches, potentially corresponding to distinct traits.
3. **Discriminative Trait Ranking:** A greedy approach is used to identify the most discriminative traits. For a correctly classified image, attention heads are progressively "blurred" (by replacing their attention maps with uniform distributions) one by one, starting with the least important (i.e., the one whose blurring causes the least drop in classification confidence). The remaining, unblurred heads highlight traits sufficient for classification.
4. **Trait Comparison:** By visualizing an image using prompts from different classes (e.g., the ground-truth class prompt and another class's prompt), PROMPT-CAM can identify shared traits or explain misclassifications (e.g., why an image of class A might be mistaken for class B).
5. **Understanding Misclassifications:** If an image is misclassified ($\hat{y} \neq y$), comparing the attention maps of the true class $y$ with the predicted class $\hat{y}$ helps to understand why (e.g., traits of $y$ are occluded, or the object strongly resembles traits of $\hat{y}$).

**E. Unique Prediction Mechanism for Interpretability:**
Crucially, PROMPT-CAM's use of a *shared* vector $w$ in the prediction head (Equation 1) fundamentally differs from conventional classifiers that use class-specific weights $w_c$. This design discourages the ViT from encoding holistic, class-discriminative information in its patch features and instead forces it to:
1. Generate localized patch features ($v_j$) that highlight discriminative elements.
2. Generate distinctive attention weights ($\alpha_c$) for each class $c$, focusing on its unique traits.
This architectural choice is central to PROMPT-CAM's ability to produce meaningful and spatially resolved interpretations, even at the potential cost of a slight drop in raw classification accuracy compared to unconstrained models.

**F. Extensions:**
The methodology also extends to discovering hierarchical taxonomy keys, by training multiple sets of prompts for different nodes in a taxonomic tree, effectively re-discovering group-level characteristics.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The extensive empirical studies conducted on over a dozen diverse fine-grained datasets strongly validate PROMPT-CAM's superior interpretability and wide applicability.

1. **Faithfulness to Model Decisions:** PROMPT-CAM demonstrates high faithfulness, meaning it accurately highlights the image regions that the model genuinely relies on for its predictions. Quantitative evaluation using insertion and deletion scores on the CUB dataset shows PROMPT-CAM yielding higher insertion scores (0.61 vs. 0.52-0.56 for baselines) and lower deletion scores (0.09 vs. 0.13-0.27 for baselines). This indicates that PROMPT-CAM focuses more strongly on discriminative traits and that masking these highlighted regions significantly impacts classification, confirming its enhanced interpretability over standard post-hoc methods like Grad-CAM, Layer-CAM, Eigen-CAM, and attention roll-out.

2. **Superior Trait Identification (Human Assessment):** A human study comparing PROMPT-CAM with other interpretable methods like TesNet and ProtoConcepts revealed PROMPT-CAM's superiority in identifying traits recognizable by humans. Participants recognized 60.49% of expert-identified traits in PROMPT-CAM's heatmaps, significantly outperforming TesNet (39.14%) and ProtoConcepts (30.39%). This underscores PROMPT-CAM's effectiveness in generating explanations that align with human understanding of biological traits.

3. **Classification Accuracy vs. Interpretability:** While PROMPT-CAM shows a slight drop in classification accuracy compared to linear probing (e.g., 73.2% vs. 78.6% on CUB, and 98.8% vs. 99.2% on Birds-525), this is presented as a deliberate trade-off. The authors argue that this minor decline is a consequence of "constraining its capacity" to focus on localized, class-specific traits rather than "shortcuts" like global body shapes or backgrounds. Misclassifications by PROMPT-CAM are often explainable by the poor visibility or occlusion of key traits in the image, whereas linear probing might still classify correctly based on more holistic, less interpretable features.

4. **Qualitative Superiority over Interpretable Models:** Visual comparisons with other interpretable models (ProtoPFormer, INTR, TesNet, ProtoConcepts) demonstrate that PROMPT-CAM captures a more extensive and diverse range of distinct, fine-grained traits. Other methods often focus on narrower or repetitive sets of attributes, failing to provide a comprehensive understanding of what defines a category. PROMPT-CAM's multi-head attention mechanism effectively leverages the ViT's ability to attend to multiple distinct features simultaneously.

5. **Backbone Agnostic and Generalizable:** PROMPT-CAM is compatible with various pre-trained ViT backbones, including DINO, DINOv2, and BioCLIP (ViT-B and ViT-S scales). The attention maps consistently highlight key traits for species recognition regardless of the backbone used. Furthermore, PROMPT-CAM's effectiveness and generalizability were demonstrated across 13 diverse fine-grained datasets spanning animals (birds, dogs, moths, fish), plants/fungi (flowers, leaves, fungi), and objects (cars, food), confirming its broad applicability. BioCLIP showed particularly high accuracy on biology-specific datasets, leveraging its domain-specific pre-training.

6. **Detection of Biologically Meaningful Traits:** The method consistently identifies and localizes biologically significant traits. Examples include the red breast and white belly for Rose-breasted Grosbeak, the black head and yellow belly for Scott Oriole, and the red spot on the wing for Red-winged Blackbird. It can also identify common traits between species by comparing attention maps generated by different class prompts.

7. **Interpretability of Trait Manipulation:** A counterfactual analysis involving trait manipulation (e.g., removing the red spot from a Red-winged Blackbird) demonstrated that PROMPT-CAM truly relies on the identified traits for prediction. After manipulation, the model no longer highlights the original trait and consequently misclassifies the image to a visually similar species, showcasing its sensitivity to trait differences and strong interpretability.

8. **Discovery of Taxonomically Meaningful Traits:** PROMPT-CAM can be extended to detect taxonomically meaningful traits. By training it within a hierarchical framework (e.g., Order → Family → Genus → Species for fish), the model progressively shifts its focus from coarse-grained traits at higher taxonomic levels to fine-grained traits at lower levels. This suggests its potential to automatically identify and localize "taxonomy keys," which are critical for biological and ecological research.

In summary, PROMPT-CAM successfully delivers on its objectives, providing a simple, efficient, and highly interpretable method for fine-grained analysis, generating explanations that are both faithful to the model's decision-making and understandable to human experts, particularly in scientific contexts.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

PROMPT-CAM represents a significant advancement in the field of interpretable AI, particularly for fine-grained image recognition, with profound implications across scientific, industrial, and trust-building domains.

### 6. Enabling Fine-Grained Scientific Discovery

The most immediate and impactful contribution is to scientific research, especially in biology, ecology, and conservation (as championed by the Imageomics Institute). By accurately identifying and localizing subtle, species-specific traits, PROMPT-CAM provides a powerful tool for:
- **Automated Trait Discovery:** Helping biologists discover new diagnostic features for species identification.
- **Enhanced Species Monitoring:** Improving the accuracy and interpretability of automated species identification systems.
- **Understanding Biodiversity:** Gaining deeper insights into evolutionary relationships and how traits differentiate closely related species.
- **Refining Taxonomy:** Potentially aiding in the validation or refinement of existing taxonomic keys, as demonstrated by its ability to detect taxonomically meaningful traits across hierarchical levels.
This capability moves AI beyond simple classification to truly contribute to the scientific understanding of the natural world.

### 7. Bridging the Interpretability Gap for Advanced Vision Models

PROMPT-CAM effectively addresses a critical limitation of powerful pre-trained Vision Transformers (ViTs) – their black-box nature for fine-grained tasks. By providing clear, localized, and class-specific explanations, it makes these advanced models more transparent and trustworthy. Its superiority over traditional saliency maps (Grad-CAM, Layer-CAM) for fine-grained details is a key step forward in making AI explanations genuinely useful for nuanced visual analysis.

### 8. Simplicity and Accessibility as a "Free Lunch"

The "almost free lunch" aspect of PROMPT-CAM is highly significant. By requiring only minor modifications to Visual Prompt Tuning (VPT) and keeping the ViT backbone frozen, it drastically simplifies the implementation and training process. This ease of use and low computational overhead makes advanced interpretability accessible to a broader range of researchers and practitioners, accelerating the adoption of interpretable AI in various applications without demanding specialized hardware or deep expertise in model architecture. It also ensures compatibility with future generations of pre-trained ViTs.

### 9. Enhancing Trust and Accountability in AI Systems

In domains where AI decisions have real-world consequences (e.g., environmental policy, medical diagnosis, quality control), understanding *why* a model made a specific prediction is paramount. PROMPT-CAM's ability to explain its classifications based on human-understandable traits (e.g., "the red spot on the wing") fosters greater trust and accountability in AI systems, moving them closer to being reliable collaborators rather than opaque tools.

### 10. A New Paradigm for Interpretable AI Design

The paper's core design philosophy – intentionally constraining the model's capacity and prediction mechanism (via the shared $w$ vector) to enforce localized, trait-specific attention, even at the cost of a slight accuracy drop – presents a valuable paradigm for future interpretable AI research. It suggests that optimizing solely for accuracy might lead to "shortcuts" that sacrifice interpretability, and that purpose-built interpretability might require different design choices from the outset.

In conclusion, PROMPT-CAM is not just another interpretable method; it offers a practical, robust, and scientifically aligned solution that unlocks the fine-grained understanding capabilities of Vision Transformers. Its potential impact extends from accelerating biological discovery to establishing more trustworthy and transparent AI systems across diverse applications, solidifying its place as an important contribution to the AI landscape.
