# HOI-Diff: Text-Driven Synthesis of 3D Human-Object Interactions using Diffusion Models

> [!summary] Summary
> HOI-Diff generates 3D human-object interaction motion from a text prompt and object geometry by decomposing the task: a dual-branch diffusion model with a cross-attention communication module produces human and object motions, a separate affordance diffusion model predicts contact points, and classifier guidance corrects contact. On BEHAVE it cuts FID from 4.25 to 1.62.

## Problem

- Prior text-to-motion diffusion models synthesize isolated human motion in free space, ignoring the objects being manipulated, so generated objects float or interpenetrate.
- Methods handling dynamic objects either take object motion as input, grasp only small objects, or produce deterministic single-interaction outputs lacking text-controlled diversity.
- Existing datasets offer diverse interactions without detailed textual descriptions of action and contacting body parts, or text without dynamic object annotations.

## Method

- **Dual-branch diffusion model (DBDM)**: separate human and object ==MDM== transformers, human branch fine-tuned from a pretrained MDM, linked by a cross-attention **Communication Module** exchanging intermediate features.
- **Affordance prediction diffusion model (APDM)** denoises binary contact labels for eight body joints, their object-surface contact points, and an ==LLM==-derived static-object flag, conditioned on text and point cloud.
- **Affordance-guided interaction correction** applies ==classifier guidance== with contact, static-object, and smoothness objectives, perturbing human and object predicted means at each denoising step with separate guidance strengths.

## Results

- On BEHAVE, HOI-Diff reaches FID **1.62**, R-precision **0.46**, and Contact Distance **0.347**, beating MDM* (**4.25**, **0.38**, **0.448**) and InterDiff (**8.58**, **0.26**, **0.506**).
- On OMOMO with unseen test objects, it scores FID **8.76** and Contact Distance **0.326** versus PriorMDM*'s **9.87** and **0.523**, indicating generalization to novel geometry.
- Ablations: removing the Communication Module raises BEHAVE FID to **3.11**; a single joint motion-plus-affordance model drops contact-label AP from **78.54%** to **53.67%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing a hard generative task into modules that each reuse strong pretrained priors beats one monolithic model trained on a small paired dataset.
> - Predicting contact independently of generated motion lets the affordance module correct upstream errors and inject stochastic diversity instead of inheriting mistakes.
> - Test-time classifier guidance on analytic geometric objectives is a cheap way to enforce physical constraints a learned diffusion prior alone violates.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of 3D human motion generation has seen rapid advancements, particularly with the advent of deep learning and diffusion models. HOI-Diff specifically targets the challenging sub-area of *text-driven 3D human-object interaction (HOI) synthesis*, where humans not only move but also dynamically interact with objects based on semantic descriptions.

**Current State-of-the-Art and Limitations:**
- **General Human Motion Generation:** Diffusion models have achieved impressive results in generating isolated 2D images and 3D human motions (e.g., Human Motion Diffusion Model (MDM) and its successors). However, most of these methods focus on human motion in free space or with static environments, largely overlooking dynamic object interactions.
- **Scene- and Object-Aware Human Motion Generation:** Some works condition motion synthesis on scene geometry or static objects. While these produce realistic motions, they are limited to static objects and often a small set of predefined interactions (e.g., sitting, grasping a small object). They often overlook the movement of objects when humans interact with them.
- **Dynamic Object Interaction:** Methods like OMOMO can generate full-body motion from object motion, but they require the object motion as input, which HOI-Diff aims to generate. Other approaches like InterDiff predict whole-body interactions with dynamic objects but typically produce deterministic interaction types, lacking the diversity controllable by text. Concurrent works (e.g., CG-HOI, HOIAnimator, PhysHOI, InterDreamer) are emerging in text-driven 3D HOI synthesis for dynamic objects, but they often face challenges such as using SMPL parameters that can lead to unsmooth motion, reliance on initial states reducing diversity, or only generating static 3D HOIs.
- **Dataset Scarcity:** A significant bottleneck for text-driven 3D HOI generation has been the lack of high-quality datasets with diverse HOIs and detailed textual descriptions. Existing datasets either lack diversity or comprehensive text annotations.

**HOI-Diff's Contribution to the Landscape:**
HOI-Diff directly addresses these limitations by introducing a novel framework that:
1. **Generates both human and *dynamic object* motions** from text prompts.
2. **Enables diverse HOIs** with various interaction types and different object geometries, going beyond static objects or limited interaction sets.
3. **Ensures physical plausibility and semantic faithfulness** to the text, explicitly tackling challenges like floating objects and accurate contact.
4. **Mitigates data scarcity** by annotating the BEHAVE dataset with rich text descriptions, promising to release both code and annotations to the community.
5. **Adopts a modular design** to decompose the complex task into simpler sub-tasks, making the problem more tractable and improving overall performance. This contrasts with monolithic models attempting to solve everything at once.

By synthesizing physically plausible, semantically faithful, and diverse HOIs involving dynamic objects, HOI-Diff pushes the boundaries of generative AI for 3D content, providing a significant advancement in creating more realistic and controllable virtual characters and environments.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of this research is to **generate realistic 3D human-object interactions (HOIs) driven by textual prompts**. This ambitious goal is motivated by several challenges and potential applications:

- **Enabling Realistic Virtual Interactions:** The ability to synthesize natural and physically plausible 3D HOIs where virtual humans interact dynamically with objects is crucial for a wide range of applications, including augmented/virtual reality (AR/VR), video games, and filmmaking. Current methods often fall short in producing such nuanced and coherent interactions.
- **Addressing the Complexity of HOI Synthesis:** Generating HOIs involves intricate challenges:
 - **Variability of Object Shapes:** Objects come in diverse forms, making it difficult to generate semantically meaningful and physically accurate contact between the human and the object, often leading to undesirable "floating objects."
 - **Text-Faithfulness and Diversity:** For a given object (e.g., a chair), a human can interact in many ways (carry, sit on, push, pull). The generated HOIs must be faithful to the input text prompt while also exhibiting diversity in plausible interactions. Existing methods often predict deterministic interactions or offer limited diversity.
 - **Lack of High-Quality Data:** The development and evaluation of 3D HOI synthesis models are severely hampered by the scarcity of high-quality human motion datasets that feature diverse HOIs and, critically, detailed textual descriptions specifying interacting body parts and actions.
- **Improving upon Existing Methodologies:** Previous approaches either focus solely on human motion, static objects, or limited interaction types. Monolithic models that attempt to generate all aspects of HOI simultaneously often struggle with the inherent complexity. The motivation for a modular design is to break down this complexity, allowing each sub-task to be addressed more effectively, leading to better overall performance.
- **Achieving Physical Plausibility:** A core motivation is to ensure that the generated interactions are not only visually appealing but also physically sound, avoiding issues like penetration or unnatural movements. This requires explicit mechanisms to enforce contact constraints and motion smoothness.

In essence, HOI-Diff aims to unlock the potential for highly realistic and controllable virtual worlds by providing a robust, data-efficient, and physically grounded solution for text-driven 3D HOI generation.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

HOI-Diff adopts a novel modular design that decomposes the complex task of 3D HOI synthesis into three distinct, yet integrated, sub-tasks: (a) coarse 3D HOI generation, (b) affordance prediction, and (c) affordance-guided interaction correction. All modules leverage denoising diffusion models for their generative capabilities.

**Motion Representation:**
- **Human Motion (x_h):** A redundant representation (D_h = 263) is used, similar to HumanML3D, including pelvis velocity, local joint positions, velocities, rotations of other joints relative to the pelvis, and binary foot-ground contact labels.
- **Object Motion (x_o):** Assumes given object geometry (as a 512-point cloud, p ∈ R^(512×3)) and predicts its 6DoF poses (D_o = 6) over time.

**A. Coarse 3D HOIs Generation: Dual-Branch Diffusion Model (DBDM)**
The DBDM is designed to generate roughly coherent human and object motions conditioned on an input text prompt (d) and object geometry (p).
- **Architecture:** It consists of two Transformer-based diffusion models: a Human Motion Diffusion Model (M_h) and an Object Motion Diffusion Model (M_o).
- **Human Motion Model (M_h):** Fine-tuned from a pre-trained human MDM to leverage extensive prior knowledge of human movements, ensuring smoother and more realistic human motions.
- **Object Motion Model (M_o):** Trained from scratch to generate the object's 6DoF poses. Input and output linear layers are adapted for the object motion's specific dimensions.
- **Communication Module (CM):** A crucial cross-attention mechanism inserted between M_h and M_o (at the 4th transformer layer for M_h and the last for M_o) that allows for explicit feature exchange. This module encourages the human and object motions to be mutually coherent and interact meaningfully.
- **Training Objective:** Standard reconstruction loss minimizing the difference between the predicted clean motion and the ground truth HOI sequence.

**B. Affordance Prediction Diffusion Model (APDM)**
Recognizing that DBDM alone might struggle with physically plausible contact, APDM is introduced to predict precise contacting areas. This module is *independent* of DBDM's outputs, allowing it to correct potential errors from the coarse generation. It also stochastically generates contact points to enhance motion diversity.
- **Task:** Identify where the human and object should physically contact.
- **Interacting Parts:** Focuses on eight primary human body joints (pelvis, neck, feet, shoulders, hands) as potential interacting parts.
- **Output:** Predicts binary contact labels (y_h ∈ {0,1}^8) for these joints and their corresponding 3D contact points on the object surface (y_o ∈ R^(8×3)).
- **Object State Prediction (y_s):** Utilizes a Large Language Model (LLM), specifically GPT-3.5-turbo, to infer whether the object should be static (y_s = 1) or dynamic (y_s = 0) based on the textual prompt. This helps in correctly constraining static objects during interaction.
- **Architecture:** Transformer-based diffusion model for affordance prediction, taking text description (d) and object point cloud (p) as input. It denoises noisy contact labels, contact points, and object state to their clean versions.
- **Post-processing:** Predicted object contact points (y_o) are refined by mapping them to their nearest neighbors on the object's point cloud and then slightly offset outwards (d=0.05) along the normal to mitigate penetration during mesh reconstruction.

**C. Affordance-Guided Interaction Correction**
This module integrates the precise affordance information from APDM into the coarse motion generation process of DBDM using classifier guidance, ensuring accurate and close contact.
- **Mechanism:** Perturbs the predicted mean of human (μ_h_t) and object (μ_o_t) motions at each diffusion step based on the gradient of an analytic objective function, G.
- **Objective Function G:** Combines three terms:
 - **G_con (Contact Term):** Minimizes the distance between the human's global contact joints and the object's predicted contact points (derived from APDM's output).
 - **G_sta (Static Object Term):** If APDM (via LLM) predicts a static object (y_s = 1), this term penalizes deviations from the object's average pose, effectively immobilizing it.
 - **G_smo (Smoothness Term):** Penalizes temporal jittering in the object's motion, ensuring physically smooth movements.
- **Guidance Strengths (τ_1, τ_2):** Different strengths are applied for human (τ_1 = 1) and object (τ_2 = 100) motions, with a higher strength for objects to enforce stronger alignment with contact points due to sparser object motion features.
- **Iterative Perturbation:** To counter the diffusion model's bias in attenuating guidance signals, iterative perturbations (K=100) are applied in the final denoising step.

**Dataset:**
The authors address data scarcity by:
- **Annotating BEHAVE:** Manually labeling interaction types, subjects, and contact body parts, then using GPT-3.5 to rephrase and generate three diverse text descriptions for each HOI sequence. This results in 1451 HOI sequences with text.
- **Evaluation on OMOMO:** Also evaluates on OMOMO, which focuses on hand manipulation, using its train/test split to assess generalization to unseen objects.

This modular, diffusion-based approach, coupled with a novel affordance prediction mechanism and guided interaction correction, provides a comprehensive solution for generating high-quality, text-driven 3D HOIs.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experimental results validate the effectiveness of HOI-Diff across various quantitative metrics and qualitative observations, demonstrating its superiority over existing baselines and the importance of its modular design.

**A. Quantitative Results (on BEHAVE and OMOMO Datasets):**
HOI-Diff consistently outperforms adapted baseline methods (MDM†, MDM*, PriorMDM*, InterDiff) on both the annotated BEHAVE dataset and the OMOMO dataset, particularly showcasing strong generalization capabilities on unseen objects in OMOMO.

- **Human Motion Quality:**
 - **Fréchet Inception Distance (FID)** (Lower is better): HOI-Diff achieves significantly lower FID scores (1.62 on BEHAVE, 8.76 on OMOMO) compared to baselines, indicating that its generated human motions are more realistic and closer to the ground-truth distribution.
 - **R-Precision** (Higher is better): HOI-Diff shows higher R-precision (0.46 on BEHAVE, 0.31 on OMOMO), demonstrating better alignment between the generated motions and the input text prompts.
 - **Diversity** (Closer to real data is better): HOI-Diff achieves diversity scores (12.02 on BEHAVE, 8.13 on OMOMO) closer to real data (12.48, 9.98 respectively), indicating its ability to generate a wider range of varied and plausible motions.
 - **Foot Skating Ratio** (Lower is better): HOI-Diff exhibits lower foot skate ratios (0.182 on BEHAVE, 0.141 on OMOMO), implying more stable and physically realistic foot-ground contact, reducing unnatural sliding.

- **Human-Object Interaction Quality:**
 - **Contact Distance** (Lower is better): HOI-Diff achieves the lowest Contact Distance (0.347 on BEHAVE, 0.326 on OMOMO), quantitatively confirming its ability to produce physically plausible HOIs with accurate and close contact between humans and objects.
 - **Penetration Score (Pene)** (Lower is better): While not uniformly lowest, HOI-Diff maintains competitive penetration scores (0.51 on BEHAVE, 0.39 on OMOMO), indicating effective mitigation of body-object interpenetration.

**B. Qualitative Results:**
Visual comparisons highlight HOI-Diff's ability to generate realistic and smooth 3D HOIs with plausible contacts, capturing intricate details from text descriptions. Baselines often suffer from issues like floating objects, unrealistic movements, or inaccurate spatial relationships between humans and objects. HOI-Diff excels in reflecting the nature of interactive actions and specific body parts involved (e.g., "raising the trash bin with the right hand"). It also demonstrates the ability to generate diverse interactions for the same object using different body parts and contact points.

**C. Ablation Studies:**
Extensive ablation studies confirm the importance of each component in HOI-Diff's modular design:
- **Object MDM and Communication Module (CM):** Removing the object MDM and CM (`Ours w/o M_o & CM`) or just the CM (`Ours w/o CM`) significantly degrades performance across all metrics, emphasizing their critical role in generating coherent and realistic HOIs.
- **Pre-trained Human Motion Prior:** Training the human MDM from scratch (`Ours w/o pretrain`) rather than fine-tuning a pre-trained MDM leads to worse results, highlighting the benefit of leveraging existing strong motion priors for human motion realism.
- **Interaction Correction:** The full model with affordance-guided interaction correction consistently outperforms variants without it, particularly in contact accuracy metrics, demonstrating its effectiveness in achieving close and accurate human-object contact and alleviating floating objects. All three goal functions (G_con, G_sta, G_smo) within the interaction correction module contribute positively.
- **Modular vs. Joint Generation:** A variant that jointly generates human motion, object motion, and affordance within a single model (`Ours_joint`) performs significantly worse than the modular HOI-Diff. This confirms that disentangling motion generation and affordance estimation enhances their respective performances.
- **LLM for Object State Prediction:** The use of GPT-3.5 for object state prediction is shown to be highly accurate (95.6%) and efficient, outperforming an MLP-based prediction head significantly.

**D. User Study:**
A user study involving 40 participants showed a strong preference for HOI-Diff's generated results over baselines, with HOI-Diff favored in 89.6% against MDM*, 73.8% against PriorMDM*, and 95.3% against InterDiff, further confirming its perceptual quality and realism.

In summary, HOI-Diff not only achieves state-of-the-art quantitative performance but also produces qualitatively superior and perceptually realistic 3D HOIs, successfully addressing long-standing challenges in the field.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

HOI-Diff represents a significant advancement in text-driven 3D human-object interaction synthesis, with profound implications across several domains.

**A. Technical Significance:**
- **Novel Modular Framework:** The core contribution is its innovative modular design, which effectively decomposes the complex HOI generation task into more manageable sub-problems: coarse motion generation, affordance prediction, and guided interaction correction. This decomposition allows for specialized models to handle each aspect, leading to higher quality and more robust results than monolithic approaches.
- **Integration of Diffusion Models:** HOI-Diff demonstrates a sophisticated application of diffusion models for multi-modal generation (text-to-3D motion) and for distinct yet interconnected tasks (motion and affordance prediction).
- **Explicit Contact and Plausibility Enforcement:** The introduction of an independent Affordance Prediction Diffusion Model (APDM) and the subsequent affordance-guided interaction correction mechanism using classifier guidance is a key innovation. This explicitly addresses the critical challenge of ensuring physically plausible and semantically meaningful contact, moving beyond mere visual coherence.
- **Leveraging Pre-trained Models and LLMs:** The strategic fine-tuning of a pre-trained human motion diffusion model and the effective integration of a Large Language Model (GPT-3.5) for robust object state inference showcase intelligent model reuse and interdisciplinary integration.

**B. Data Contribution and Research Acceleration:**
- **Text-Annotated BEHAVE Dataset:** The release of the text-annotated BEHAVE dataset directly addresses the severe scarcity of high-quality data for text-driven 3D HOI generation. This resource is invaluable for future research, enabling the training and evaluation of new models and accelerating progress in the field.

**C. Potential Applications and Impact:**
- **AR/VR and Gaming:** HOI-Diff can dramatically enhance the realism and interactivity of virtual environments. Game developers and AR/VR content creators can generate highly dynamic and customizable character-object interactions from simple text prompts, leading to more immersive and believable experiences.
- **Animation and Filmmaking:** The ability to generate complex and diverse 3D HOI sequences can streamline animation workflows, allowing animators to rapidly prototype and create nuanced scenes that are difficult and time-consuming to hand-animate.
- **Robotics:** Generating physically plausible human-object interactions can be invaluable for training and simulating robotic agents. Robots could learn to understand and execute complex manipulation tasks by observing and replicating these text-driven interactions.
- **Human Behavior Understanding:** The model's capacity to synthesize realistic interactions could also offer insights into how humans interact with their environment, potentially aiding in the study of ergonomics or cognitive science.

**D. Future Directions:**
The paper acknowledges limitations, such as the current dataset's focus on single-phase interactions, suggesting future work could explore multi-phase HOIs, potentially guided by more advanced LLMs. The reliance on affordance estimation also points to integrating even more sophisticated 3D affordance models as a promising avenue.

**E. Societal Implications:**
On the positive side, this technology can significantly advance digital content creation, making virtual experiences richer and more accessible. However, as with all generative AI involving human representations, there are ethical considerations. While 3D poses might be less directly identifying than images, the ability to generate realistic human movements raises questions about potential misuse, though the authors note a lower risk compared to traditional image-based methods regarding personal privacy.

In conclusion, HOI-Diff makes substantial contributions to the field of 3D generative AI by providing a robust, modular, and highly effective solution for text-driven 3D human-object interaction synthesis. Its technical innovations, coupled with the release of valuable annotated data, are poised to significantly impact research and applications across various industries.
