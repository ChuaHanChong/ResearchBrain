# EXPO-FT: Sample-Efficient Reinforcement Learning Finetuning for Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> EXPO-FT, a reinforcement learning framework for vision-language-action (VLA) models, enables sample-efficient and reliable finetuning of pretrained VLA policies for real-world robotic manipulation tasks. The system achieved a perfect 30/30 success rate across eight challenging tasks with an average of 19.1 minutes of online robot interaction data.

## Problem

- Pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, despite strong generalization, lack the reliability needed for practical real-world robotic deployment.
- Existing reinforcement learning (RL) finetuning methods for VLAs are often sample-inefficient, fail to achieve high success rates, or are incompatible with expressive VLA policy architectures.
- Robot learning currently faces a gap in leveraging powerful VLA priors while simultaneously achieving the high sample efficiency and reliability required for real-world applications.

## Method

- Develops **EXPO-FT**, an off-policy ==reinforcement learning== framework that uses a lightweight edit policy to refine actions from a pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model without directly backpropagating through it.
- Adapts the RL framework to operate natively with temporally extended action chunks, aligning with the output format of modern VLA policies.
- Integrates human-in-the-loop interventions during online training to provide corrective actions and high-quality data, significantly boosting sample efficiency and guiding exploration.

## Results

- EXPO-FT achieved a perfect **30/30** success rate across all eight challenging real-world robotic manipulation tasks, demonstrating high reliability for deployment.
- The system consistently outperformed baseline methods, including HG-DAgger (22.**1/30** average) and supervised finetuning (20.**5/30** average), particularly on tasks requiring dynamic actions or high precision.
- Reliable performance was attained with an average of only 19.1 minutes of online robot interaction data per task, showcasing high sample efficiency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Leveraging a strong pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) prior within a sample-efficient reinforcement learning (RL) framework is essential for achieving high reliability in real-world robotics.
> - An "edit policy" that refines actions from a frozen VLA, combined with the VLA's native action chunking, allows for stable and targeted policy improvement without the computational burden of full VLA backpropagation.
> - Integrating human-in-the-loop feedback during online RL finetuning drastically improves sample efficiency and guides exploration towards reliable policy performance.
