# OGPO: Sample Efficient Full-Finetuning of Generative Control Policies

> [!summary] Summary
> Off-policy Generative Policy Optimization (OGPO) is an algorithm developed by researchers from Carnegie Mellon and collaborating institutions for sample-efficient full-policy finetuning of Generative Control Policies (GCPs). This approach, which decouples costly environment interactions from computationally cheap policy updates, consistently achieves high success rates across diverse robotic manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Policies derived from imitation learning often exhibit brittleness and lack robustness when deployed in environments with minor variations, leading to low real-world success rates.
- Existing finetuning methods for Generative Control Policies (GCPs) present a trade-off: sample-efficient approaches offer limited expressiveness, while methods for maximal improvement are highly sample-inefficient.
- Directly backpropagating gradients through the entire generative process of GCPs or through noisy Q-functions can lead to unstable or inaccurate updates, particularly in contact-rich manipulation tasks.

## Method

- **OGPO** introduces a ==bi-level MDP== framework that decouples off-policy critic learning for expensive environment interactions from on-policy policy extraction for computationally cheap action generation.
- It employs off-policy ==Temporal Difference== (TD)-learning to train Q-functions for the environment dynamics and applies ==zeroth-order Proximal Policy Optimization== (**PPO**)-style updates for the inner denoising MDP, using learned Q-functions as terminal rewards.
- The **OGPO**+ variants incorporate enhancements such as behavior cloning regularization from successful trajectories, conservative advantages with ensemble critics, and \u03c7\u00b2-regularization for pixel-based observations to mitigate critic over-exploitation and improve stability.

## Results

- OGPO achieved high success rates and demonstrated substantial sample efficiency improvements, requiring approximately 10 times fewer environment steps than on-policy baselines like DPPO on ROBOMIMIC tasks.
- The method successfully finetuned poorly-initialized behavior cloning policies to near full task success without requiring additional expert data in the online replay buffer, showcasing novel capability in real-world deployment scenarios.
- Ablation studies confirmed that OGPO's zeroth-order PPO extraction method was more stable and effective than direct backpropagation or other policy extraction variants (e.g., AWR/FPO-style), particularly on complex robotic tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling the optimization of computationally cheap generative action processes from expensive environment interactions enables both sample-efficient and expressive full-policy finetuning of GCPs.
> - Using zeroth-order PPO-style policy extraction over the denoising MDP provides a stable and effective way to update generative models, avoiding the instability associated with direct backpropagation through complex generative chains.
> - Regularization techniques like behavior cloning from successful trajectories and conservative advantages are critical for grounding policy learning, mitigating initial performance dips, and robustifying updates against imperfect critic estimates, particularly in sparse-reward settings.
