# Phys4D: Fine-Grained Physics-Consistent 4D Modeling from Video Diffusion

> [!summary] Summary
> Phys4D develops a three-stage training paradigm for video diffusion models, integrating physics-based simulation data and explicit depth/motion supervision to generate fine-grained physics-consistent 4D world representations. The approach improved Physics-IQ scores by up to 11.4 percentage points on various backbones while maintaining high video quality and demonstrating superior 4D geometry and motion consistency.

## Problem

- Existing video diffusion models frequently produce fine-grained physical inconsistencies (e.g., implausible geometry, unstable motion) because their training prioritizes visual appearance over physical laws.
- There is a scarcity of scalable, high-fidelity supervision data with dense, temporally aligned ground-truth geometry and motion for training physics-aware 4D models.
- Many current physics-aware video generation methods are limited in scalability and generalization due to operating at the image level, focusing on short-horizon dynamics, or relying on specialized 3D representations.

## Method

- A three-stage training paradigm is introduced, progressively injecting physical structure into pretrained video diffusion models: pseudo-supervised pretraining, physics-grounded supervised fine-tuning, and simulation-grounded reinforcement learning.
- A scalable physics-based simulation pipeline, built on Isaac Sim, generates diverse and richly annotated 4D datasets for dense ground-truth geometry and motion supervision.
- Lightweight auxiliary heads for depth and motion prediction are attached to the ==diffusion model==, trained with physics-aware losses like warp-based geometry-motion consistency, and reinforcement learning is used for long-horizon physical consistency.

## Results

- Phys4D consistently improved overall Physics-IQ scores across tested video diffusion backbones, with the CogVideoX-5b backbone showing an 11.4 percentage point increase (from 18.8% to 30.2%).
- The model achieved superior per-frame 3D geometry accuracy (e.g., AbsRel 0.2711 vs. 0.3929 for WAN + DAV2) and enhanced temporal geometry-motion consistency, evidenced by reduced depth warp errors and improved optical flow accuracy.
- Demonstrated better world-level 4D geometry consistency (lowest 4D Chamfer Distance of 0.4626) and improved long-horizon trajectory dynamics, including lower Worldline L2 Error and reduced failure rates.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large-scale physics-based simulation provides a scalable and accurate source for fine-grained 4D ground-truth data, essential for training physics-aware generative models.
> - A progressive, multi-stage training approach can effectively integrate explicit depth and motion supervision and reinforcement learning into pretrained video diffusion models to instill physical consistency.
> - Enforcing warp-based geometry-motion consistency during training is critical for coupling 3D structure and its temporal evolution, leading to more coherent 4D representations.
