# Pretrain Value, Not Reward: Decoupled Value Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> Decoupled Value Policy Optimization (DVPO) streamlines reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) for large language models by pretraining a global value model offline, which then guides policy optimization without further updates. This method matches or exceeds the performance of traditional RLHF approaches while reducing GPU memory usage by 30-40% and training time by 30-45%.

## Problem

- Traditional RLHF pipelines suffer from instability due to online critic training (critic drift) and high computational demands from concurrently loaded models.
- Existing methods often lack fine-grained token-level credit assignment, relying on sentence-level rewards that can lead to high variance and limit precise preference learning.
- The two-stage process of training a reward model and then a value model from fixed human preferences is informationally redundant and complex when no new rewards are introduced.

## Method

- Introduce Decoupled Value Policy Optimization (**DVPO**), which pretrains a ==Global Value Model== (GVM) offline to function as a universal, fixed critic.
- Train the GVM using Temporal Difference (TD) learning to capture policy-conditioned action-value functions across diverse trajectories and freeze its parameters.
- Optimize the policy using a modified PPO objective, leveraging the fixed GVM for stable advantage estimates, thereby decoupling actor and critic learning dynamics.

## Results

- DVPO achieved performance on par with or superior to PPO, DPO, ReMax, and GRPO on various LLM benchmarks (e.g., MT-Bench, Alpaca-Eval, Arena-Hard) for both base and instruction-tuned models.
- The method reduced GPU memory usage by 30-**40%** and training time by 30-**45%** compared to PPO, along with faster convergence rates (e.g., **75%** fewer interaction steps).
- The pre-trained GVM provided more robust and generalizable value estimates, outperforming PPO's online critic, especially under distribution shifts, and enabled finer-grained token-level feedback for policy learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Pretraining a value model directly from a fixed human preference dataset is informationally equivalent to the conventional reward model-critic training pipeline.
> - Decoupling value learning from policy optimization mitigates critic drift, enhancing the stability and predictability of policy updates.
> - A policy-agnostic, pre-trained Global Value Model can provide more accurate, finer-grained (token-level) and generalizable supervision than dynamically trained critics or models relying solely on sentence-level rewards.
