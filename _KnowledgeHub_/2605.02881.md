# MolmoAct2: Action Reasoning Models for Real-world Deployment

> [!summary] Summary
> MolmoAct2, developed by the Allen Institute for AI and the University of Washington, is a fully open-source action reasoning model designed for real-world robot deployment, enhancing generalizability and efficiency. It incorporates an embodied reasoning VLM backbone and a continuous action expert, achieving up to 87.1% success on real-world DROID tasks with unseen objects and a 2.42x speedup in control rate compared to unoptimized inference.

## Problem

- Existing frontier vision-language-action (VLA) models are often closed systems, hindering scientific reproduction and adaptation due to proprietary data and weights.
- Current general-purpose VLMs lack specialized embodied skills for robotic control, such as accurate spatial understanding and cross-view object tracking.
- Reasoning-augmented policies for robots suffer from prohibitive inference latency, making them unsuitable for real-time, closed-loop control in dynamic environments.

## Method

- Developed **Molmo2-ER**, an ==embodied reasoning VLM backbone==, specifically optimized for robot perception skills, and curated diverse, open robot demonstration datasets, including large-scale bimanual data.
- Created ==OpenFAST Tokenizer== for efficient discrete action representation and integrated a continuous action expert with a novel ==per-layer KV connection== to the VLM's visual-language attention state.
- Introduced **MolmoAct2**-Think, an adaptive-depth reasoning variant that regenerates depth codes only for dynamic scene regions, alongside inference optimizations like CUDA Graphs to reduce latency.

## Results

- Molmo2-ER achieved an average score of **63.8%** on embodied reasoning benchmarks, outperforming baseline VLMs on 9 out of 13 tasks and improving **17%** over its base Molmo2.
- MolmoAct2-DROID demonstrated **87.1%** average success on five real-world manipulation tasks with unseen objects (a 38.7 percentage point gain over baseline) and MolmoAct2-SO100/101 achieved **56.7%** success on novel pick-and-place tasks.
- Inference optimizations, including caching and CUDA Graphs, resulted in up to a **2.42x** speedup for MolmoAct2, boosting its control rate to 55.79 Hz, and MolmoAct2-Think saw a **1.58x** speedup to 12.71 Hz.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Specializing a VLM backbone for embodied perception skills, through a 'specialize-then-rehearse' approach on a curated corpus, significantly enhances the model's ability to learn robot actions.
> - A per-layer KV connection effectively conditions a continuous action expert on the VLM's deep, hierarchical visual-language context, enabling more grounded and precise robot control without replacing the backbone.
> - Adaptive depth reasoning, which selectively updates depth information only for changing scene areas, provides geometric grounding and interpretability while substantially reducing inference latency for real-time operation.
