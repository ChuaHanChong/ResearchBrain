# RoboRefer: Towards Spatial Referring with Reasoning in Vision-Language Models for Robotics

> [!summary] Summary
> RoboRefer introduces a 3D-aware Vision-Language Model that enhances spatial referring with reasoning for embodied robots. It achieves precise spatial understanding and multi-step reasoning by integrating a dedicated depth encoder, a two-stage supervised and reinforcement fine-tuning strategy, and a new large-scale dataset, outperforming existing models on complex spatial tasks.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with accurate understanding of complex 3D scenes and dynamic reasoning for instruction-indicated locations, often suffering from 'modality gaps' or 'modality interference'.
- Most research focuses on single-step spatial understanding, with multi-step reasoning (breaking down intricate instructions into sequential references) remaining largely underexplored, and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) often leading to memorization rather than generalized explicit reasoning.
- There is a critical scarcity of large-scale, high-quality datasets and benchmarks explicitly annotated for multi-step spatial reasoning, hindering the development and rigorous evaluation of models in this area.

## Method

- **RoboRefer** employs a disentangled VLM architecture with separate RGB and dedicated depth encoders to prevent modality interference, preserving pre-trained RGB encoder integrity while enhancing precise 3D perception.
- A two-stage fine-tuning approach combines ==Supervised Fine==-tuning (SFT) for foundational spatial understanding with Reinforcement Fine-tuning (RFT), guided by metric-sensitive process reward functions, to foster explicit, generalized multi-step spatial reasoning.
- The research introduces `RefSpatial`, a large-scale dataset (20M QA pairs, up to 5 reasoning steps) supporting both SFT and RFT, and `RefSpatial-Bench`, a challenging benchmark for multi-step reasoning in real-world cluttered scenes, addressing data and evaluation gaps.

## Results

- RoboRefer-8B-SFT achieved state-of-the-art performance on single-step spatial understanding benchmarks, with an average success rate of **89.6%**, surpassing Gemini-**2.5**-Pro by **5%** and baseline NVILA-2B by **21.7%**.
- On `RefSpatial-Bench`, RFT-trained RoboRefer-2B demonstrated superior multi-step reasoning, outperforming Gemini-**2.5**-Pro by **17.4%** average accuracy and showing a **9.1%** improvement in generalization to unseen spatial combinations over SFT-only models.
- RoboRefer enabled robust real-world robotic applications, achieving a **6.8%** absolute improvement in success rate over VLA baselines in simulation and demonstrating the ability to execute long-horizon, dynamic manipulation and navigation tasks on various real robots while adapting to real-time disturbances.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A dedicated depth encoder, separate from the RGB encoder, effectively prevents modality interference and enhances precise 3D spatial understanding without compromising the VLM's general visual knowledge.
> - Reinforcement Fine-tuning (RFT) with metric-sensitive process reward functions is crucial for teaching explicit, generalized multi-step spatial reasoning, overcoming the memorization tendencies of SFT for complex, sequential tasks.
> - A comprehensive data recipe, integrating 2D web images, 3D embodied videos, and simulated scenes with ground-truth reasoning processes, is essential for training models capable of both precise spatial understanding and robust multi-step reasoning across diverse environments.
