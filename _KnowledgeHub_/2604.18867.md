# Hierarchically Robust Zero-shot Vision-language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This research introduces a framework that enhances adversarial robustness in zero-shot Vision-Language Models by leveraging hyperbolic geometry to embed and align hierarchical class information. The method improves both clean and robust accuracy across 15 diverse datasets, consistently outperforming previous state-of-the-art techniques against various attack types, and demonstrates successful application in medical imaging.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) are susceptible to adversarial attacks, where subtle input perturbations can lead to confident but incorrect predictions.
- Existing adversarial fine-tuning methods for VLMs primarily focus on base-class attacks, resulting in degraded robustness against attacks targeting broader 'superclasses' (parent categories).
- Adversaries generated against superclasses are more generic and can effectively transfer to attack base classes, presenting a significant unaddressed vulnerability.

## Method

- Develops an ==adversarial fine-tuning== framework that projects class hierarchies into a ==hyperbolic embedding space== (==Poincaré ball model==) to explicitly represent multi-level semantic relationships.
- Introduces **Hierarchy-preserving Image-Text Alignment (HITA)** and **Hierarchy-aware Negative Set Augmentation (HNSA)** to robustly align image and text embeddings across all hierarchical levels.
- Incorporates novel loss components, including ==hierarchical norm gap constraints== and ==intra-class vicinity alignment==, to maintain hierarchical integrity and account for individual image variability within the hyperbolic space.

## Results

- Achieved average clean accuracy of **62.14%** and robust accuracy (PGD-20) of **44.34%** across **15 datasets**, outperforming state-of-the-art adversarial fine-tuning methods like FARE and AoS.
- Demonstrated superior robustness against image-level, text-level (BERT-Attack, GBDA), and bi-level (Co-Attack, SGA) adversarial attacks across various perturbation intensities.
- Successfully extended to other Vision-Language Models (BLIP, CoCa) for tasks like image-text retrieval and captioning, and significantly improved **robust AUC** scores for chest X-ray classification in medical imaging.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Hyperbolic geometry is intrinsically suited for low-distortion representation of class hierarchies, where vector norms within the Poincaré ball naturally correspond to hierarchical levels.
> - The varying margin sizes inherent to hyperbolic classifiers, which can grow exponentially, facilitate the generation of 'universal' adversarial perturbations that robustify models across different hierarchical levels.
> - Robustness against superclass-level adversaries is crucial for overall VLM security, as these generic attacks effectively transfer to and compromise base-class performance.
