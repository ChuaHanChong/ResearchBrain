# Distribution-Agnostic Robust Trajectory Optimization via Chance-Constrained Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework named Distribution-Agnostic Robust Trajectory Optimization via Chance-Constrained Reinforcement Learning (RCCRL) was developed to robustify spacecraft trajectories against various uncertainties by combining deterministic optimal control with reinforcement learning. The system demonstrated a 5.50% reduction in upper-tail fuel cost and significantly tighter terminal dispersion in an Earth-Mars transfer, while successfully adapting to different uncertainty distributions and maintaining sub-meter accuracy in a pinpoint rocket landing scenario.

## Problem

- Spacecraft trajectory design faces challenges from uncertainties like navigation errors and unmodeled perturbations, leading to mission compromises or failures.
- Traditional robust optimization methods rely on restrictive assumptions (e.g., Gaussian uncertainty, linear dynamics) that are inadequate for complex, non-Gaussian, or bounded uncertainties.
- Standard reinforcement learning (RL) optimizes expected return rather than probabilistic feasibility and often yields unstructured policies difficult to integrate into safety-critical aerospace systems.

## Method

- A Robust Chance-Constrained ==Reinforcement Learning== (RCCRL) framework was proposed, integrating an offline-computed deterministic nominal trajectory with an RL-learned affine robustification layer.
- The RL policy outputs a structured control law consisting of feedforward corrections and time-varying linear feedback gains to adapt the nominal path and reshape uncertainty ensembles.
- Probabilistic feasibility and terminal dispersion are enforced empirically using Monte Carlo rollouts, upper-tail quantile evaluations, and ensemble-level covariance penalties, avoiding analytical reformulations of chance constraints.

## Results

- In an Earth-Mars transfer, RCCRL achieved a **5.50%** reduction in 95th-percentile total impulse cost and 42-**78%** tighter terminal dispersion compared to a robust optimal control benchmark under Gaussian uncertainty, maintaining **100%** mission success probability.
- The framework adapted effectively to bounded uniform uncertainties, showing a modest **3.3%** increase in cost from its Gaussian performance while still outperforming the benchmark.
- For an atmospheric pinpoint rocket landing, RCCRL demonstrated less than **1%** fuel overhead relative to the deterministic baseline and maintained sub-meter terminal accuracy under both Gaussian and bounded uniform uncertainties, showcasing portability across different problem types.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling nominal trajectory design from robust augmentation allows leveraging established optimal control methods while using RL specifically for handling uncertainties in a structured manner.
> - A structured affine closed-loop control policy provides both interpretability for aerospace applications and the ability to systematically adjust the trajectory and contract uncertainty spreads.
> - Empirical, sample-based evaluation of probabilistic constraints enables the framework to handle distribution-agnostic uncertainties and highly nonlinear dynamics without restrictive analytical approximations.
