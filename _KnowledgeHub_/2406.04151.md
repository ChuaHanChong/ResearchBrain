# AgentGym: Evolving Large Language Model-based Agents across Diverse Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> AGENTGYM provides a framework for evolving Large Language Model-based agents across diverse environments by integrating initial behavioral cloning with a self-evolution method called AGENTEVOL. This approach enables agents to generalize and adapt to new tasks, demonstrating improved performance and efficiency in various simulated settings compared to imitation-only baselines.

## Problem

- Existing LLM-based agent approaches largely depend on costly expert-provided trajectories for behavioral cloning, limiting scalability and scope.
- Current self-improvement methods for LLM agents are often confined to isolated environments, resulting in specialized agents that struggle to generalize across diverse tasks and real-world scenarios.

## Method

- **AGENTGYM** is introduced as a comprehensive platform for training, evaluating, and evolving LLM-based agents across diverse environments including web navigation, embodied tasks, and tool use.
- The framework employs a two-stage learning process: initially equipping agents with basic instruction-following abilities through ==behavioral cloning== from expert trajectories (==AGENTTRAJ==), followed by continuous evolution.
- **AGENTEVOL**, a new evolution method, alternates between an exploration step where agents interact with environments to generate trajectories and a learning step where policies are updated based on a ==reward-weighted objective==.

## Results

- Agents evolved with AGENTEVOL consistently outperformed those trained solely with behavioral cloning, demonstrating performance improvements across various environments.
- Evolved agents exhibited enhanced generalization, showing improved performance even in environments not explicitly used during the evolution phase.
- The evolution process led to more efficient task completion, with agents requiring fewer interaction rounds and displaying improved reasoning and action selection, as evidenced in WebShop and ALFWorld tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining an initial phase of behavioral cloning with a subsequent self-evolution stage enables agents to acquire basic skills and then adapt to diverse, real-world tasks.
> - AGENTEVOL, a novel evolution method, effectively handles sparse rewards and maintains stability across varied environments by leveraging a weighted negative log-likelihood objective for policy updates.
> - A comprehensive framework with standardized APIs facilitates the integration of diverse environments and efficient data collection, promoting the development of generalist LLM-based agents.
