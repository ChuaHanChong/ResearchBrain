# BeautyGRPO: Aesthetic Alignment for Face Retouching via Dynamic Path Guidance and Fine-Grained Preference Modeling

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Sun Yat-sen University and vivo BlueImage Lab developed BeautyGRPO, a reinforcement learning framework for face retouching that effectively aligns outputs with human aesthetic preferences while preserving high visual fidelity. This system leverages a new fine-grained preference dataset and a multi-dimensional reward model, combined with Dynamic Path Guidance (DPG) to stabilize stochastic exploration, resulting in superior aesthetic quality and naturalness in retouched images as confirmed by quantitative metrics and user studies.

## Problem

- Existing supervised face retouching methods optimize for pixel-level reconstruction, often failing to capture subjective human aesthetic preferences and leading to rigid or unnatural results.
- Online reinforcement learning (RL) frameworks for image generation, when applied to face retouching, suffer from a "fidelity-exploration conflict," where stochastic noise for exploration introduces visual artifacts and compromises high-fidelity requirements.
- There is a lack of fine-grained reward models and suitable datasets for face retouching that can provide detailed perceptual feedback on subtle aesthetic aspects like skin texture, blemish removal, and identity preservation.

## Method

- Curated ==FRPref-10K==, a large-scale dataset of 10,000 high-resolution preference pairs with multi-dimensional aesthetic annotations collected via a hybrid VLM-Human pipeline, and trained a specialized reward model on this data.
- Developed Dynamic Path Guidance (DPG), an innovative mechanism that stabilizes SDE-based stochastic exploration by dynamically guiding the sampling trajectory towards high-fidelity anchors while introducing controlled noise to prevent "stochastic drift."
- Integrated the fine-grained reward model and DPG into an extended FlowGRPO framework, optimizing a flow-matching image editing policy to enable reward-driven aesthetic alignment beyond fixed supervised targets.

## Results

- BeautyGRPO consistently achieved superior scores across nearly all no-reference perceptual and aesthetic metrics (NIMA, MUSIQ, MANIQA, NRQM, TOPIQ, NIQE) on FFHQR and in-the-wild datasets, outperforming state-of-the-art specialized and general image editing models.
- A user study confirmed BeautyGRPO's strong alignment with human preferences, achieving significantly higher win rates (**63.25%**) compared to all baselines and demonstrating accurate alignment with human judgments on fine-grained aesthetic dimensions.
- The framework produced natural and realistic retouched images, effectively removing blemishes and preserving natural skin texture and identity features, avoiding the common "plastic-like" or over-smoothed appearance of other methods.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Achieving true aesthetic alignment in face retouching requires moving beyond pixel-level supervision to models that can actively learn from and respond to nuanced human preferences through reward signals.
> - The fidelity-exploration conflict in SDE-based online RL can be resolved by dynamically guiding stochastic trajectories with high-quality anchors, allowing for effective exploration without sacrificing visual stability.
> - A dedicated, fine-grained reward model, trained on multi-dimensional human preference data, is crucial for providing accurate and perceptually relevant feedback to guide reinforcement learning in subtle aesthetic tasks.
