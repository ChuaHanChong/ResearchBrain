# Lemon: A Unified and Scalable 3D Multimodal Model for Universal Spatial Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> LEMON, a unified transformer architecture, directly processes 3D point cloud patches and language tokens as a single sequence to achieve universal spatial understanding. This model sets new state-of-the-art results in 3D embodied interaction and scene spatial awareness benchmarks, outperforming existing 3D multimodal models and demonstrating competitive performance with 2D vision-language models.

## Problem

- Existing 3D Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) use fragmented architectures with separate, modality-specific encoders, leading to architectural imbalances and representational bottlenecks.
- 3D encoders typically suffer from limited pretraining on smaller datasets, hindering generalization and end-to-end optimization of 3D representations.
- The inherent irregularity, sparsity, and unordered nature of 3D point cloud data complicates its effective integration into deep learning models and large language models.

## Method

- A ==unified transformer architecture== processes both 3D point cloud patches and natural language tokens as a single, concatenated sequence, integrating 3D spatial information directly within the language model framework.
- A ==hierarchical spatial partitioning scheme== dynamically divides point clouds along Z, Y, and X axes into patches, which are then tokenized with specialized separator tokens to preserve spatial context and structure.
- A ==progressive three-stage training curriculum== systematically builds 3D spatial understanding, starting with object recognition, moving to object captioning, and culminating in scene spatial question answering.

## Results

- LEMON-7B achieved the highest GPT-4 score of **57.22** on 3D MM-Vet for embodied object QA and established new state-of-the-art on 3D-GRAND (**74.32%** binary accuracy), substantially outperforming previous 3D LMMs.
- The model demonstrated strong 3D object generative recognition (**59.20%** accuracy, comparable to GPT-4V) and significantly surpassed other 3D multimodal models in object captioning using semantic metrics like Sentence-BERT and SimCSE.
- Ablation studies confirmed the effectiveness of the unified architecture, the three-stage training curriculum, and the Z-Y-X spatial tokenization, with the model also showing robustness to sparse/noisy data and superior computational efficiency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Unified transformer architecture consistently outperforms modular designs, demonstrating that separate 3D encoders often create a representational bottleneck and can even degrade performance.
> - LEMON provides the first systematic evidence of power-law scaling behavior in 3D LMMs, indicating predictable performance gains with increased model size and training data.
> - The proposed Z-Y-X hierarchical spatial ordering of patches with explicit separator tokens is crucial for effective scene-level spatial reasoning and significantly outperforms other tokenization strategies.
