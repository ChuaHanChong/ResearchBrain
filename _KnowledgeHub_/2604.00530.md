# AceTone: Bridging Words and Colors for Conditional Image Grading

> [!summary] Summary
> AceTone introduces a multimodal framework that generates 3D Look-Up Tables (3D-LUTs) directly from text prompts or reference images for conditional image grading. This approach unifies photorealistic style transfer and instruction-guided grading, achieving improved performance on objective metrics and demonstrating higher user preference compared to existing methods.

## Problem

- Existing automated color grading methods often lack expressiveness, struggle to generalize across diverse creative intents, and produce outputs inconsistent with human aesthetic preferences.
- Current workflows are inefficient and fragmented, with photorealistic style transfer (PST) and instruction-guided grading (IGG) tasks typically requiring separate and incompatible model designs.
- Adversarial training paradigms commonly used for color grading models suffer from instability, mode collapse, and scalability issues, hindering consistent performance improvement.

## Method

- A 3D Vector Quantized Variational Autoencoder (==VQ-VAE==) tokenizes continuous 3D-LUTs into compact, perceptually accurate discrete representations (tokens).
- A Vision-Language Model (VLM) is pre-trained to autoregressively generate LUT tokens conditioned on multimodal inputs (text prompts or reference images).
- The model undergoes supervised fine-tuning for PST and IGG tasks, followed by reinforcement learning (Group Relative Policy Optimization - GRPO) using color similarity and aesthetic quality rewards to align outputs with human preferences.

## Results

- The 3D VQ-VAE tokenizer reconstructs LUTs with an average CIEDE2000 0E of 1.38, indicating perceptually minimal differences, while achieving a **99.98%** compression ratio.
- AceTone reduced LPIPS by approximately **40%** on the PST-50 benchmark compared to Neural Preset (**0.09** vs. **0.15**) and achieved higher aesthetic scores (3.29 vs. 3.03).
- On AceTone-Bench[Instruct], AceTone achieved a higher aesthetic score (3.54 vs. 3.19 for Qwen-Image-Edit) and a lower LPIPS (**0.22** vs. **0.33**), with user studies showing preference for AceTone's results in both PST (**41.4%**) and IGG (**46.9%**) tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Directly tokenizing 3D-LUTs enables a high-fidelity, compact discrete representation of color transformations, facilitating their integration and generation by Vision-Language Models.
> - A unified VLM architecture can effectively handle both reference-image and text-instruction guided color grading, streamlining previously disparate creative workflows.
> - Reinforcement learning, specifically GRPO, provides a stable and scalable mechanism to refine color grading outputs, effectively aligning them with aesthetic and perceptual preferences beyond what supervised fine-tuning alone can achieve.
