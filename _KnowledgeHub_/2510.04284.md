# Doctor-R1: Mastering Clinical Inquiry with Experiential Agentic Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Tsinghua University developed DOCTOR-R1, an AI doctor agent framework leveraging experiential agentic reinforcement learning to master strategic patient inquiry and empathetic communication in multi-turn clinical consultations. The 8B parameter model achieved higher average scores than proprietary models like GPT-4.1 on dynamic diagnostic benchmarks such as HealthBench and MAQuE, demonstrating enhanced communication quality and diagnostic accuracy.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) perform well on static medical knowledge tests but exhibit limitations in dynamic, open-ended clinical consultations.
- Existing AI doctor agents often lack strategic, multi-turn patient inquiry capabilities and empathetic communication essential for real-world clinical practice.
- Current reinforcement learning approaches for medical LLMs struggle with continuous learning from high-quality experiences and integrating differential diagnosis into dynamic workflows.

## Method

- Develops DOCTOR-R1, an AI doctor agent framework, which formalizes ==medical consultation== as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) within a closed-loop interactive environment.
- Utilizes a two-tiered reward architecture, including dense process rewards for conversational quality (with a safety-first veto system) and a terminal outcome reward for diagnostic accuracy.
- Implements an Experience Repository for continuous learning, featuring multi-stage retrieval of relevant experiences and selective storage of high-reward interactions to refine inquiry strategy.

## Results

- The **8B** parameter DOCTOR-R1 model achieved higher average scores (e.g., 36.29 on HealthBench) than proprietary models like GPT-4.1 and Grok-4 on dynamic inquiry benchmarks, demonstrating improved communication and diagnostic accuracy.
- DOCTOR-R1 prevented foundational knowledge degradation, scoring **83.50%** on MedQA and **85.00%** on MMLU medical topics, a substantial increase over its base model.
- Human evaluations by licensed physicians and patient experience surveys indicated DOCTOR-R1's superior clinical competence and patient-centric communication, with **83.87%** of retrieved experiences deemed "Clinically Helpful".

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Effective AI doctor agents require strategic, multi-turn patient inquiry and empathetic communication skills, beyond static knowledge retrieval.
> - Learning from high-quality past interactions, through selective storage and retrieval, is essential for continuously refining an agent's dynamic inquiry strategy.
> - A multi-objective reward architecture with safety prioritization and granular process feedback is crucial for training robust agents in complex clinical consultation environments.
