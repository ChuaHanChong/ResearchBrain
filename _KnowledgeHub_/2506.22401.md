# Exploration from a Primal-Dual Lens: Value-Incentivized Actor-Critic Methods for Sample-Efficient Online RL

> [!summary] Summary
> The Value-Incentivized Actor-Critic (VAC) method, a model-free online reinforcement learning algorithm, achieves near-optimal sample efficiency by reframing optimistic exploration through a primal-dual lens. This approach leads to a single, optimizable objective function that avoids the complex bi-level optimization of prior frameworks like Maximize to Explore (MEX).

## Problem

- Online reinforcement learning with complex function approximators struggles with sample efficiency due to the inherent exploration-exploitation trade-off.
- Theoretically sound exploration strategies, often based on optimism in the face of uncertainty, frequently suffer from prohibitive computational complexity or sub-par empirical performance in practical, large-scale settings.
- The Maximize to Explore (MEX) framework, while offering theoretical advantages, presents a bi-level optimization problem that hinders direct application of first-order methods and complicates practical implementation.

## Method

- Reinterpreted optimistic regularization by deriving the MEX objective from a ==regularized Lagrangian== of a constrained value maximization problem, viewed through a primal-dual lens.
- Formulated a new optimization problem that jointly optimizes the Q-function (critic) and policy (actor), constrained by the Bellman consistency equation rather than optimality.
- Developed the Value-Incentivized Actor-Critic (VAC) method, which uses a single objective function amenable to first-order optimization, unlike prior bi-level approaches.

## Results

- The VAC method achieved a near-optimal cumulative regret bound of O(dH2Tlog⁡(log⁡(∣A∣)Tδ)log⁡(1+T3/2d))O(dH^2\sqrt{T}\sqrt{\log(\frac{\log(|A|)T}{\delta})\log(1 + \frac{T^{3/2}}{d})})O(dH2T​log(δlog(∣A∣)T​)log(1+dT3/2​)​) for episodic linear Markov Decision Processes (MDPs).
- For infinite-horizon discounted MDPs, VAC demonstrated a near-optimal sample complexity of O(d2(1−γ)5ϵ2)O(\frac{d^2}{(1-\gamma)^5\epsilon^2})O((1−γ)5ϵ2d2​) to reach an ϵ\epsilonϵ-optimal policy, up to logarithmic and polynomial factors.
- The theoretical framework extends to general function approximation, yielding regret bounds that match or slightly improve upon existing methods under similar assumptions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The principle of optimism in exploration can be formally understood and derived from a primal-dual perspective, linking it to regularized Lagrangian formulations.
> - Shifting the optimization constraint from the Bellman optimality equation to the Bellman consistency equation significantly simplifies the resulting objective to a single-level problem.
> - A unified Lagrangian objective can effectively integrate exploration and exploitation by promoting Q-function and policy estimates that are both consistent with observed data and yield higher value functions.
