# ID-Crafter: VLM-Grounded Online RL for Compositional Multi-Subject Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> ID-CRAFTER, a framework from Xiamen University and collaborators, generates multi-subject videos with enhanced identity preservation and temporal consistency by combining hierarchical attention, VLM-grounded semantic understanding, and online reinforcement learning. The model achieved a total score of 57.05% on multi-subject video generation benchmarks, surpassing prior open-source and proprietary models.

## Problem

- Existing video generation models struggle to consistently preserve distinct identities of multiple subjects, leading to identity drift or leakage and semantic conflicts.
- Current approaches face limitations in controllability and applicability for complex multi-subject scenes due to reliance on sparse inputs.
- Maintaining semantic coherence and temporal consistency in multi-subject videos, especially with dynamic interactions, remains a significant challenge.

## Method

- The ID-CRAFTER framework utilizes a latent video diffusion transformer with ==Rectified Flow==, incorporating a dual text-encoder architecture combining T5 and Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct for semantic understanding.
- It integrates a Hierarchical Identity-Preserving Attention mechanism (intra-subject, gated inter-subject, cross-modal attention) within the Diffusion Transformer backbone to manage multi-subject conditioning.
- Post-training, the model undergoes online reinforcement learning using a Groupwise Policy Optimization (GRPO) algorithm with a composite reward function focused on fidelity and quality.

## Results

- The ID-CRAFTER **14B** model achieved a Total Score of **57.05%** on multi-subject video generation benchmarks, outperforming proprietary models like Kling 1.6 (**54.46%**) and existing open-source baselines.
- The model demonstrated superior identity preservation with a FaceSim score of **60.71%** and a NexusScore of **45.11%** for the **14B** model, exceeding previous state-of-the-art results.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical roles of hierarchical attention, VLM-based semantic understanding, and the online RL stage, with online GRPO improving FaceSim by **13.7%** and Aesthetics by **14.9%** relative to a supervised fine-tuning baseline.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Hierarchical identity-preserving attention effectively disentangles and preserves individual subject identities while modeling complex inter-subject relationships.
> - Leveraging a pretrained Vision-Language Model (Qwen2.5-VL) provides granular semantic understanding and dynamic guidance for complex multi-subject textual prompts and reference images.
> - Online Reinforcement Learning, specifically GRPO with a multi-faceted reward design, offers a stable method for optimizing complex perceptual rewards and balancing competing generation objectives like identity preservation and video quality.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of video generation has seen considerable advancements, particularly in synthesizing high-fidelity videos. However, a significant challenge remains in effectively integrating identity information from multiple distinct subjects into generated video content. Current approaches often struggle with semantic conflicts, leading to a degradation of individual subject identities and inconsistencies in interactions over time. This limits the controllability and applicability of video generation models for complex, multi-subject scenes.

Prior work in subject-driven image generation has explored tuning-based methods, which involve fine-tuning generative models on reference images, and tuning-free methods, which inject identity information during inference. While these have enabled high-fidelity image synthesis, extending them to the video domain introduces the additional complexity of maintaining temporal consistency alongside identity preservation. Existing subject-consistent video generation methods often enhance attention mechanisms or use adapter-based techniques to incorporate appearance cues. For multi-subject scenarios, some methods attempt to inject information into pretrained video diffusion models, but frequently encounter issues with semantic conflicts and identity degradation.

This research addresses these specific limitations by proposing a framework that aims to explicitly disentangle and preserve multiple subject identities while ensuring semantic coherence and temporal consistency in video generation. It integrates recent advancements in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for semantic understanding and online Reinforcement Learning (RL) for refining critical concepts, an approach that has seen limited application in subject-consistent video generation despite its success in enhancing other generative tasks like aesthetic alignment and text-rendering fidelity in text-to-image models. By tackling the inherent tension between individual identity preservation and dynamic scene coherence, this work seeks to advance the capabilities of multi-subject video synthesis beyond the current state-of-the-art.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop a framework capable of generating high-fidelity videos that effectively integrate identity information from multiple subjects, preserving their distinct identities and ensuring semantic coherence and temporal consistency. This objective is motivated by several identified shortcomings in existing video generation paradigms:

1. **Ineffective Multi-Subject Identity Integration**: Current models often fail to consistently preserve the identity of multiple subjects throughout a video. This leads to issues such as identity drift, where subjects change appearance, or identity leakage, where features from one subject inappropriately blend with another. The paper highlights that existing methods struggle to resolve semantic conflicts when multiple subjects are present, resulting in suboptimal performance in identity preservation and interactions.

2. **Limited Controllability and Applicability**: The reliance of many video generative models on sparse inputs, such as a single text prompt or an initial frame, significantly restricts their controllability, especially for complex scenes involving multiple characters or objects. The ability to precisely control the identities of multiple subjects is crucial for advanced applications.

3. **Semantic Coherence and Temporal Consistency Challenges**: Beyond identity, maintaining semantic coherence (alignment with the textual prompt and plausible interactions between subjects) and temporal consistency (smooth, natural motion and appearance across frames) in multi-subject videos presents a complex challenge that existing models do not fully resolve.

To address these motivations, the ID-CRAFTER framework is designed with specific goals:
- To disentangle complex inter-subject relationships and preserve individual attributes.
- To enhance generation fidelity for intricate multi-subject descriptions by leveraging advanced semantic understanding.
- To directly optimize the trade-off between identity preservation, video quality, and motion fluidity.

The successful achievement of these objectives is anticipated to unlock capabilities crucial for downstream applications, including subject-driven video synthesis, dynamic scene composition, and controllable product placement, thereby advancing personalized content creation, virtual storytelling, and advertising.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

ID-CRAFTER is a multi-subject video generation framework built upon a latent video diffusion transformer, specifically inspired by the Wan Video architecture, trained with Rectified Flow (RF). Given a textual prompt and a set of reference images, each depicting a unique subject, the model aims to generate a video that aligns with the prompt and preserves the identities of all subjects with high fidelity and temporal coherence.

#### 3.1. Foundation: Rectified Flow for Video Generation

The methodology is based on a latent video diffusion transformer that utilizes Rectified Flow (RF) for the generative process. A video clip is first encoded into a latent space using a Variational Autoencoder (VAE). RF defines a straight-line trajectory between a data sample and a prior distribution sample (Gaussian noise), allowing a video diffusion transformer to be trained to predict the velocity field along this trajectory. The VAE weights are kept frozen during training, and the model employs a dual text-encoder architecture combining T5 with Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct for semantic understanding.

#### 3.2. Core Architectural Innovations

ID-CRAFTER introduces two primary architectural innovations to manage multi-subject conditioning:

- **Hierarchical Identity-Preserving Attention Mechanism**: This mechanism is integrated within the Diffusion Transformer (DiT) backbone to hierarchically aggregate features from multiple subjects and modalities. It operates in three sequential stages:
 1. **Intra-subject attention**: Applied to each subject's token set individually to capture fine-grained, identity-specific details and consolidate features before cross-subject interactions.
 2. **Gated Inter-subject attention**: Employs a gated cross-attention mechanism where each subject's features query those of all other subjects. This models interactions between subjects while aiming to mitigate identity bleed.
 3. **Cross-modal attention**: The refined subject tokens and VLM-derived textual context tokens attend to the main video latent tokens. This stage injects semantic guidance, with the VLM features dynamically modulating video features at each DiT layer. This hierarchical design is implemented within the first 20 layers of the DiT backbone, based on the observation that identity features are most critical in early generation stages.

- **Semantic Understanding via Pretrained Vision-Language Model (VLM)**: The Qwen2.5-VL model (specifically the 7B variant) is employed as a sophisticated encoder for both textual prompts and reference images. Unlike conventional text encoders, the VLM provides a granular understanding of scene composition and cross-modal dynamics, transforming from a static encoder into a dynamic semantic guide for the Hierarchical Identity-Preserving Attention. The VLM processes the textual prompt and reference images, yielding semantically enriched tokens that are then concatenated with individual subject tokens to form the complete conditioning tokens for the generative process.

#### 3.3. Post-training with Online Reinforcement Learning (RL)

Following the initial flow matching training, an online reinforcement learning phase is incorporated to further refine the model for semantic alignment and identity preservation. This addresses the credit assignment problem in optimizing complex perceptual rewards for hierarchical attention layers.

- **Online GRPO Algorithm**: To overcome the limitations of offline methods (e.g., DPO) and the instability of standard policy-gradient algorithms (which require a separate value function), ID-CRAFTER utilizes a Groupwise Policy Optimization (GRPO) strategy. For each condition, a group of outputs is sampled from the current policy, and the policy model is optimized by maximizing an objective that involves a ratio of probabilities and an advantage term. The advantage term is computed using groupwise comparison, eliminating the need for a fragile value network. The deterministic generation process of flow matching is converted to a stochastic one by injecting noise at each step of the flow integration.

- **Reward Design**: The RL objective maximizes a composite reward function ($R_{total}$), defined as a weighted sum of fidelity and quality rewards:
 - **Fidelity Reward ($R_{fid}$)**: Ensures consistency between generated subjects and reference images. It combines a face-specific metric (mean and minimum ArcFace-based FaceSim scores) with a holistic subject consistency score (NexusScore from OpenS2V-Eval benchmark), balanced with a factor $\alpha$. A balance factor $\gamma$ is used within $R_{face}$ to weight average versus minimum FaceSim scores, found to be optimal at 0.5.
 - **Quality Reward ($R_{qual}$)**: Assesses aesthetic appeal and physical plausibility. It combines a standard Aesthetic Score with a NaturalScore (from the proposed benchmark) that uses a VLM to evaluate adherence to physical laws and common sense, balanced with a factor $\beta$. A strong penalty for unnatural generations is applied.
 - Empirical weights for $R_{fid}$ and $R_{qual}$ are set to 0.6 and 0.4, respectively, prioritizing identity preservation.

#### 3.4. Training Data Curation

To overcome limitations of paired training data (e.g., restricted diversity, "copy-plate" artifacts), a sophisticated data curation pipeline is developed:
- **OpenS2V-Nexus Dataset**: A foundational component providing a wide array of authentic scenes and actions, including subject-video pairs, masked subject images, and generated counterparts. Video shot detection is applied to ensure temporal coherence.
- **High-Fidelity Internal Collection**: Professionally shot videos with detailed annotations, supplemented with synthetically generated subject images to increase appearance diversity.
- **Subject Image Dataset**: A static collection of human-centric images and isolated clothing items to provide rich priors for appearance synthesis.
The pipeline leverages modern VLMs (e.g., QwenVL-72B) and advanced image editing models (e.g., Nano Banana) for high-quality reference image synthesis, including explicitly designed cross-subject compositions and fusion examples.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments conducted demonstrate that ID-CRAFTER achieves state-of-the-art performance in multi-subject video generation.

#### 4.1. Quantitative Comparison

ID-CRAFTER consistently outperforms existing open-source models across all evaluation metrics, as shown in Table 1.
- **Overall Performance**: The model establishes a new state-of-the-art, with the 14B (Large-scale) model achieving a Total Score of 57.05%, surpassing proprietary models like Kling 1.6 (54.46%) and Pika 2.1 (48.88%), and significantly outperforming open-source baselines such as VACE-14B (52.87%) and Phantom-14B (52.32%).
- **Identity Preservation**: ID-CRAFTER shows substantial improvements in FaceSim (60.71% for 14B model, compared to 55.09% for VACE-14B and 51.48% for Phantom-14B) and NexusScore (45.11% for 14B model), which are attributed to the hierarchical identity-preserving attention mechanism and VLM encoder.
- **Quality and Consistency**: The model also achieves high scores in Aesthetics (45.28%), Motion (40.34%), and NaturalScore (73.23%), indicating superior visual quality, temporal consistency, and physical plausibility.

#### 4.2. Qualitative Comparison

Qualitative comparisons (Figure 4) indicate that ID-CRAFTER generates videos with superior visual quality, temporal coherence, and identity preservation compared to leading open-source and proprietary methods. While baseline methods often exhibit artifacts or identity drift, ID-CRAFTER maintains stable representations of all subjects, accurately reflecting their appearance from reference images, even in longer or complex scenes.

#### 4.3. Ablation Studies and Analysis

- **Effect of Hierarchical Identity-Preserving Attention**: Replacing the hierarchical attention with a standard cross-attention mechanism ("w/o Hierarchical Attention") resulted in a significant 11.7% drop in FaceSim (from 58.12% to 51.34%) and a 7.8% drop in Total Score (Table 2). This confirms the mechanism's critical role in modeling subject-specific features and preventing identity degradation. Further ablation on individual stages (Table S.5) indicated that Stage 3 (cross-modal attention to VLM) was most critical for semantic alignment, while all three stages contributed to optimal performance.
- **Effect of VLM-based Semantic Understanding**: Replacing the dual-encoder setup with a single T5 encoder ("w/o VLM Encoder") led to an 18.2% decrease in Q-Align, a VLM-based visual scorer for perceptual quality, and an 8.2% drop in Total Score (Table 2). This demonstrates the VLM’s superiority in parsing complex multi-subject prompts and providing fine-grained semantic guidance.
- **Effect of Data Curation Pipeline**: Training the model on a baseline dataset without the curated data ("w/o Curated Data") resulted in lower scores across all metrics, with a 10.2% drop in Total Score and a 7.7% drop in Video Quality (Table 2). This variant also showed more severe "copy-plate" artifacts. This highlights the importance of the diverse and high-quality training examples from the curated pipeline in achieving robust and generalizable performance.
- **Effect of Online RL**: The online RL stage with GRPO significantly improved performance over the Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) baseline and an offline DPO approach (Table 3). The online GRPO increased FaceSim by 13.7% (relative to SFT baseline, from 58.12% to 66.10%), Aesthetics by 14.9%, and Q-Align by 16.8%. This validates that online optimization with the composite reward function enhances identity preservation and perceptual quality.
 - **Ablation on Reward Components**: Removing the fidelity term ($R_{fid}$) led to a severe drop in FaceSim (45.32%). Removing the quality term ($R_{qual}$) degraded Aesthetics. Removing the Natural Reward ($R_{nat}$) led to a degradation in Q-Align but a slight increase in FaceSim and Aesthetics, suggesting a potential reward hacking scenario without this component. This validates the multi-faceted reward design.

#### 4.4. Controllable Video Editing

The architecture enables zero-shot, identity-consistent video editing (Figure 6). This includes inserting new subjects, replacing existing ones, or altering backgrounds while maintaining temporal and semantic coherence.

#### 4.5. Human Preference Study

A human preference study involving 200 questionnaires from 30 participants confirmed the superior performance of ID-CRAFTER (Figure S.13). Participants showed a strong preference for ID-CRAFTER in Aesthetic Quality (54%), Identity Consistency (60%), Video Quality (43%), and Motion Naturalness (65%), reinforcing the automated metric results.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The ID-CRAFTER framework represents an advancement in multi-subject video generation by directly addressing the challenges of identity preservation, semantic coherence, and temporal consistency in complex scenes.

### 6. Significance

- **State-of-the-Art Performance**: The model establishes new benchmarks in multi-subject video generation, outperforming existing open-source and, in some metrics, proprietary models. This indicates a notable improvement in the fidelity and consistency of generated videos.
- **Novel Architectural Integration**: The integration of a hierarchical identity-preserving attention mechanism and a Vision-Language Model (VLM) into a latent diffusion backbone is a key contribution. This unique combination allows for effective disentanglement of complex intra- and inter-subject relationships and robust semantic guidance.
- **Pioneering Online Reinforcement Learning Application**: The work demonstrates the effective and stable application of online Reinforcement Learning (GRPO) to fine-tune a complex multi-subject video generation model. This approach is shown to be crucial for optimizing perceptual rewards and balancing competing generation objectives, outperforming offline RL methods.
- **Robust Data Curation**: The development of a large-scale, composite dataset through a sophisticated curation pipeline, incorporating both authentic and synthetically generated data, is vital for training robust and generalizable multi-subject video synthesis models, mitigating common issues like "copy-plate" artifacts.

**Potential Impact:**
- **Enhanced Controllability in Video Creation**: ID-CRAFTER's ability to preserve multiple subject identities with high fidelity offers enhanced control over video content, moving beyond single-subject limitations. This could significantly impact creative industries.
- **Advanced Personalized Content Generation**: The framework facilitates the creation of highly personalized video content for virtual storytelling, social media, and advertising, where specific subjects or characters need to be consistently portrayed in dynamic scenes.
- **Flexible Video Editing Capabilities**: The model's architecture naturally supports controllable video editing applications, allowing for zero-shot subject replacement, insertion, or background modification in existing videos while maintaining identity and temporal coherence.
- **Foundation for Future Research**: This work provides a foundation for future research in several areas, including incorporating physics-aware priors for more realistic interactions, mitigating biases from pretrained components, and achieving even more fine-grained controllable generation of attributes, actions, and inter-subject interactions.

**Limitations and Future Work:**
Despite its strengths, the paper acknowledges limitations in modeling highly complex interactions and fine-grained dynamics between subjects. Future work will focus on integrating physics-aware priors, addressing potential biases in pretrained components, and advancing fine-grained controllable generation of attributes, actions, and interactions. The ethical implications of generative AI are also recognized, with a focus on non-realistic subject datasets and support for synthetic media detection methods.
