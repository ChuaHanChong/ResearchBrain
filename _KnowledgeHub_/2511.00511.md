# ID-Crafter: VLM-Grounded Online RL for Compositional Multi-Subject Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> ID-CRAFTER, a framework from Xiamen University and collaborators, generates multi-subject videos with enhanced identity preservation and temporal consistency by combining hierarchical attention, VLM-grounded semantic understanding, and online reinforcement learning. The model achieved a total score of 57.05% on multi-subject video generation benchmarks, surpassing prior open-source and proprietary models.

## Problem

- Existing video generation models struggle to consistently preserve distinct identities of multiple subjects, leading to identity drift or leakage and semantic conflicts.
- Current approaches face limitations in controllability and applicability for complex multi-subject scenes due to reliance on sparse inputs.
- Maintaining semantic coherence and temporal consistency in multi-subject videos, especially with dynamic interactions, remains a significant challenge.

## Method

- The ID-CRAFTER framework utilizes a latent video diffusion transformer with ==Rectified Flow==, incorporating a dual text-encoder architecture combining T5 and Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct for semantic understanding.
- It integrates a Hierarchical Identity-Preserving Attention mechanism (intra-subject, gated inter-subject, cross-modal attention) within the Diffusion Transformer backbone to manage multi-subject conditioning.
- Post-training, the model undergoes online reinforcement learning using a Groupwise Policy Optimization (GRPO) algorithm with a composite reward function focused on fidelity and quality.

## Results

- The ID-CRAFTER **14B** model achieved a Total Score of **57.05%** on multi-subject video generation benchmarks, outperforming proprietary models like Kling 1.6 (**54.46%**) and existing open-source baselines.
- The model demonstrated superior identity preservation with a FaceSim score of **60.71%** and a NexusScore of **45.11%** for the **14B** model, exceeding previous state-of-the-art results.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical roles of hierarchical attention, VLM-based semantic understanding, and the online RL stage, with online GRPO improving FaceSim by **13.7%** and Aesthetics by **14.9%** relative to a supervised fine-tuning baseline.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Hierarchical identity-preserving attention effectively disentangles and preserves individual subject identities while modeling complex inter-subject relationships.
> - Leveraging a pretrained Vision-Language Model (Qwen2.5-VL) provides granular semantic understanding and dynamic guidance for complex multi-subject textual prompts and reference images.
> - Online Reinforcement Learning, specifically GRPO with a multi-faceted reward design, offers a stable method for optimizing complex perceptual rewards and balancing competing generation objectives like identity preservation and video quality.
