# UniDex: A Robot Foundation Suite for Universal Dexterous Hand Control from Egocentric Human Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> UniDex introduces a robot foundation suite that enables universal dexterous hand control by transforming egocentric human videos into a large-scale robot-centric dataset and leveraging a unified action space for diverse robot hands. The suite achieves 81% average task progress on real-world tasks, enables zero-shot skill transfer across different hand embodiments, and offers an efficient human-robot co-training setup that reduces data collection costs.

## Problem

- Collecting large, broadly usable pretraining datasets for dexterous hands is labor-intensive and costly due to the unique challenges of teleoperation.
- The wide variation in degrees of freedom (DoFs), morphology, and kinematics across different dexterous hands prevents effective data and policy transfer.
- Existing robot foundation policies and VLA models predominantly cater to parallel-jaw grippers, leaving dexterous manipulation underserved by generalist approaches.

## Method

- Develop UniDex-Dataset, a large-scale robot-centric dataset derived from egocentric human videos using a novel ==kinematic and visual retargeting pipeline==.
- Introduce UniDex-VLA, a 3D vision-language-action policy trained with a ==Function Actuator Aligned Space (FAAS)== that unifies control across diverse dexterous hand embodiments.
- Provide UniDex-Cap, a portable hardware-software setup for efficient ==human-robot data co-training==, transforming real-time human demonstrations into robot-executable trajectories.

## Results

- UniDex-VLA achieved **81%** average task progress on five challenging real-world tool-use tasks, showing a significant performance improvement over baselines, with pretraining being critical.
- The system demonstrated zero-shot skill transfer to unseen robot hands, achieving **60%** success on the Oymotion Hand and **40%** on the Wuji Hand for tasks trained on a different embodiment.
- UniDex-Cap enabled efficient human-robot co-training, with an approximate 2:1 human-to-robot data exchange rate for fine-tuning, which reduced the overall data collection cost by **5.2x**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Appropriately retargeted egocentric human videos can generate a large-scale, diverse, and robot-centric dataset, effectively mitigating the data scarcity in dexterous manipulation.
> - A unified Function
Actuator
Aligned Space (FAAS) allows for effective zero-shot skill transfer and control policies that generalize across highly diverse dexterous robot hands with varying DoFs and morphologies.
> - Combining large-scale pretraining with 3D vision-language-action policies provides strong motion priors and spatial awareness, enabling efficient adaptation to complex, real-world dexterous tasks.
