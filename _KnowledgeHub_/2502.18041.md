# Openfly: A comprehensive platform for aerial vision-language navigation

> [!summary] Summary
> OpenFly introduces a comprehensive platform for aerial vision-language navigation, providing a large-scale dataset of 100,000 trajectories generated through an automated toolchain and a keyframe-aware VLN model. The platform and model achieved up to a 14.0% increase in success rate on seen scenarios and demonstrated superior real-world performance compared to existing methods.

## Problem

- Existing aerial Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) datasets suffer from limited data diversity and small scale, hindering the development of generalizable models.
- High data collection costs are associated with manual UAV piloting and labor-intensive language instruction annotation.
- There is a need for efficient VLN models to process sequential aerial visual information while managing redundancy.

## Method

- OpenFly integrates diverse rendering engines, including ==Unreal Engine==, GTA V, Google Earth, and 3D Gaussian Splatting, to generate varied and realistic aerial environments.
- An automated data generation toolchain leverages Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Large Language Models (LLMs) to create 100,000 trajectories with corresponding instructions.
- The OpenFly-Agent, a keyframe-aware VLN model, employs keyframe selection and visual token merging strategies to efficiently process long visual sequences and reduce redundancy.

## Results

- OpenFly released the largest aerial VLN dataset to date, comprising 100,000 trajectories across 18 diverse scenes with varied lengths and flight heights.
- OpenFly-Agent achieved a **34.3%** success rate (SR) and **24.9%** success weighted by path length (SPL) on test-seen scenarios, representing a **14.0%** SR and **7.1%** SPL improvement over the next best method, NaVila.
- Real-world experiments demonstrated OpenFly-Agent's superior performance (**26.09%** SR) and better sim-to-real transfer compared to existing baselines, validating the platform's practical applicability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating diverse rendering technologies, including 3D Gaussian Splatting for real-to-sim reconstruction, is crucial for creating aerial VLN datasets with enhanced realism and environmental diversity.
> - Automating trajectory and instruction generation using VLMs and LLMs significantly scales dataset creation, overcoming the limitations of manual annotation.
> - Intelligent keyframe selection and visual token merging effectively handle redundant visual information in sequential aerial observations, improving navigation efficiency and performance.
