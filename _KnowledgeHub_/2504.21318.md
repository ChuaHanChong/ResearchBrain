# Phi-4-reasoning Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Microsoft researchers develop a 14B parameter language model specialized in reasoning through careful data curation and a combination of supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, achieving performance competitive with much larger models while maintaining efficiency through targeted training on complex reasoning tasks.

## Problem

- Existing reasoning-focused language models require massive parameter counts, limiting their practical deployment
- Difficult to maintain strong reasoning capabilities while reducing model size and computational requirements

## Method

- Carefully curated ==dataset combining web-sourced and synthetic reasoning problems== with high-quality responses
- ==Two-stage training approach== using supervised fine-tuning followed by reinforcement learning for math reasoning
- Specialized architecture modifications including ==longer context and reasoning block tokens==

## Results

- Outperforms significantly larger open-weight models like DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-**70B** on reasoning benchmarks
- Shows strong generalization to algorithmic problem solving and planning tasks
- Maintains high performance on general-purpose skills like instruction following and long-context QA
- Offers flexible accuracy vs token length tradeoffs between base and plus versions

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality data curation and targeted training can enable smaller models to match larger ones' reasoning ability
> - Combining supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning provides complementary benefits
> - Models exhibit strong generalization to unseen reasoning tasks not directly targeted in training

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work builds upon the growing body of research focused on developing and improving reasoning capabilities in large language models (LLMs). It directly addresses the challenges of scaling reasoning abilities while maintaining efficiency and practicality.

**Contextual fit in the research landscape:**

- **Reasoning-Focused LLMs:** The paper acknowledges the trend of developing LLMs specifically trained for complex reasoning tasks. It mentions prominent models like OpenAI's o1/o3, Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini, and DeepSeek-AI's DeepSeek-R1, highlighting the importance of reasoning in the current LLM landscape.
- **Distillation and Fine-Tuning:** The research leverages the technique of distilling knowledge from larger models into smaller ones through supervised fine-tuning. This approach is recognized as a viable strategy for creating efficient and high-performing reasoning models, referencing the DeepSeek-R1 Distill model.
- **Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning:** The paper incorporates reinforcement learning (RL) to further enhance the reasoning capabilities of the model, following the trend of using RL to refine LLMs after supervised fine-tuning.
- **Data-Centric AI:** The authors explicitly align their work with data-centric methods, emphasizing the importance of careful data curation and high-quality synthetic datasets for training smaller models to compete with larger counterparts.
- **Open-Weight Models:** The paper mentions multiple open-weight reasoning models, indicating a contribution to the open-source community and promoting transparency in research.
- **Evaluation Challenges:** The authors acknowledge the challenges in evaluating reasoning models, particularly with small benchmarks and non-deterministic behavior. This highlights the need for more rigorous evaluation practices.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind this research are:

- **Develop a High-Performing Reasoning Model:** To create a 14-billion parameter model, Phi-4-reasoning, capable of achieving strong performance on complex reasoning tasks.
- **Improve Reasoning Capabilities of Phi-4:** To enhance the reasoning abilities of the base model, Phi-4, by leveraging supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning.
- **Demonstrate the Power of Data Curation:** To emphasize the importance of meticulous data curation, prompt selection, and high-quality synthetic datasets in developing reasoning models.
- **Combine SFT and RL:** To explore the potential of combining supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for creating efficient and high-performing small language models with strong reasoning abilities.
- **Address Evaluation Challenges:** To highlight the need for more rigorous evaluation practices in assessing the performance and robustness of reasoning models.
- **Contribute to Open Research:** Provide details about data curation, training methodologies, and evaluations to promote transparency and foster further research in the field.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The researchers employed a multi-stage approach to develop and evaluate Phi-4-reasoning and Phi-4-reasoning-plus:

1. **Data Curation:**
 - Collected a diverse dataset of questions from web-based sources and synthetically generated problems.
 - Filtered prompts based on difficulty level and the extent to which they challenged the base model's capabilities.
 - Generated high-quality responses using o3-mini, including detailed reasoning traces.
 - Created alignment and safety prompts to enhance model safety and promote responsible AI practices.
2. **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):**
 - Fine-tuned the Phi-4 model on the curated dataset of prompt-response pairs.
 - Repurposed placeholder tokens for <think> and </think> tags to mark reasoning blocks.
 - Increased the maximum token length to accommodate longer reasoning traces.
 - Experimented with different training hyperparameters, data mixtures, and system messages.
3. **Reinforcement Learning (RL):**
 - Further trained Phi-4-reasoning using outcome-based reinforcement learning (GRPO algorithm).
 - Focused the RL training exclusively on mathematical reasoning problems.
 - Employed a rule-based reward model to incentivize correctness, penalize undesirable behaviors, and encourage proper response formatting.
4. **Evaluation:**
 - Evaluated the models on a wide range of reasoning tasks, including math, science, coding, algorithmic problem solving, planning, and spatial understanding.
 - Compared the models' performance against other state-of-the-art models.
 - Conducted detailed benchmark analyses to assess the robustness and generalizability of the models.
 - Examined the accuracy distribution on AIME 2025 to account for the stochastic nature of LLM generations.
 - Evaluated safety using Toxigen and other measures.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The main findings and results presented in the technical report are:

- **Strong Reasoning Performance:** Phi-4-reasoning and Phi-4-reasoning-plus demonstrated strong performance on complex reasoning tasks, outperforming significantly larger open-weight models such as DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B.
- **Competitive with Larger Models:** The models achieved performance levels approaching those of the full DeepSeek-R1 model on certain benchmarks.
- **Improvement over Base Model:** Both models showed significant improvements over the base model, Phi-4, across a broad set of reasoning tasks.
- **Generalization to Unseen Domains:** The models exhibited strong generalization to algorithmic problem solving and planning tasks that were not directly targeted during training.
- **Benefits on General-Purpose Tasks:** Improvements in reasoning contributed to non-trivial benefits on more general-purpose skills, such as instruction following, long-context question answering, and human preference alignment.
- **Accuracy vs. Token Length Tradeoffs:** The two models offered different token length vs. accuracy tradeoffs, with Phi-4-reasoning-plus achieving higher accuracy on math but using more tokens.
- **Importance of Data Curation:** The research emphasized the benefits of careful data curation and supervised fine-tuning for reasoning language models.
- **Potential of Reinforcement Learning:** The gains achieved through reinforcement learning highlighted the potential of combining distillation/SFT and reinforcement learning.
- **Evaluation Challenges:** The evaluation pointed to opportunities for improving how we assess the performance and robustness of reasoning models.
