# SANA-WM: Efficient Minute-Scale World Modeling with Hybrid Linear Diffusion Transformer

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA researchers developed SANA-WM, a 2.6B-parameter open-source world model capable of generating high-fidelity, one-minute 720p videos with precise 6-DoF camera control. The system achieves visual quality comparable to larger industrial baselines while significantly reducing data, training, and inference costs, demonstrating strong performance on a new 60-second world-model benchmark.

## Problem

- Existing minute-scale, action-conditioned video generation models are computationally expensive, requiring extensive resources and multi-GPU inference, thus limiting accessibility for many researchers.
- Common distillation approaches from short-video generators are suboptimal, as teachers often lack sufficient supervision for minute-scale scene persistence and accurate trajectory following.
- Generating one-minute, 720p videos presents technical challenges in managing token counts and maintaining long-context coherence within model architectures.

## Method

- SANA-WM employs a 2.6B-parameter Hybrid Linear Diffusion Transformer, using a high-compression LTX2 tokenizer and a two-stage generation pipeline for 60-second, 720p video outputs.
- It integrates a dual-branch camera control mechanism, featuring a coarse branch (Ray-Local UCPE) for global 6-DoF pose and a fine branch (Raw-Frame Plücker Mixing) for precise intra-stride motion.
- A robust data annotation pipeline produces a 213K-clip corpus with enhanced 6-DoF pose estimation and 3D Gaussian Splatting augmentation, alongside a progressive four-stage training strategy.

## Results

- SANA-WM (with refinement) achieved RotErr of 4.50 degrees and TransErr of 1.39 on simple trajectories, and 8.34 degrees and 1.39 on hard trajectories, demonstrating superior camera control compared to baselines.
- It produced VBench Overall scores of 80.62 (simple) and 81.89 (hard) at 720p with single-GPU inference, matching visual quality of 480p models requiring eight GPUs.
- The full SANA-WM pipeline generates 60-second 720p clips at 22.0 videos per hour on a single H100 GPU, with a distilled variant achieving a 39x speedup on a single RTX 5090.
