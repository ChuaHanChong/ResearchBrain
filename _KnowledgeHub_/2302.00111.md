# Learning Universal Policies via Text-Guided Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> UniPi proposes a universal policy representation as text-conditioned video generation, enabling agents to learn from internet-scale data and generalize across diverse tasks and environments. It demonstrates superior combinatorial generalization and multi-environment transfer in robot manipulation, achieving up to 60.1% accuracy on novel language prompts and effectively transferring knowledge to real-world robot tasks.

## Problem

- Difficulty in sharing knowledge and generalizing policies across diverse environments with unique state and action spaces.
- Challenge of designing precise, quantifiable reward functions for varied and complex real-world tasks.
- Limitations in leveraging vast, unstructured internet-scale video and text data for learning general-purpose decision-making agents.

## Method

- Formulates sequential decision-making as a ==Unified Predictive Decision Process (UPDP)== that uses images as a universal observation/action interface and text as a universal task specifier.
- Employs a universal video-based planner, instantiated as a ==text-to-video diffusion model==, to synthesize future visual trajectories (plans) given an initial observation and textual task description.
- Utilizes a separate, smaller ==task-specific inverse dynamics model== to translate the synthesized video plans into a sequence of executable actions for a given robot or environment.

## Results

- Achieved superior combinatorial generalization, outperforming baselines (e.g., Diffuser) significantly on novel language prompts for robot manipulation (e.g., **60.1%** vs. **12.5%** accuracy on novel "Place" tasks).
- Demonstrated strong multi-environment transfer, generalizing to unseen CLIPort tasks with substantial improvements over baselines (e.g., **51.6%** vs. **14.8%** on "Place Bowl").
- Pretraining on internet-scale video data improved the quality of generated video plans for real robots and enabled generalization to novel real-world commands and scenes, resulting in a **77.1%** success rate on robotic tasks with pretraining vs. **72.6%** without.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Casting sequential decision-making as text-conditioned video generation provides a universal policy representation that bypasses environment-specific state/action spaces and explicit reward functions.
> - Large-scale video diffusion models can effectively serve as universal planners, synthesizing coherent visual trajectories for diverse tasks and environments.
> - Decoupling a universal visual planner from a task-specific action adaptation module enables leveraging vast unstructured internet data for general planning and smaller datasets for precise action grounding.
