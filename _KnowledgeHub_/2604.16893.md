# EasyVideoR1: Easier RL for Video Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> EasyVideoR1 introduces a comprehensive reinforcement learning framework for large vision-language models, specifically designed for video understanding. It achieved a 1.47x throughput improvement in RL training and enhanced the average accuracy of a Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct model by +2.3 points across 10 video benchmarks, including a +6.6% gain on Video-Holmes and +6.7% on VideoMathQA.

## Problem

- Systematic application of Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) to video understanding faced challenges due to diverse task types and computational overhead.
- Existing RL training frameworks suffered from significant computational burden due to repeated and redundant video decoding and preprocessing, hindering efficiency.
- Reproducible and efficient evaluation of video understanding models was difficult due to sensitive hyperparameters and the sheer volume of high-dimensional video data.

## Method

- Developed **EasyVideoR1**, an ==RLVR== training framework incorporating video-friendly optimizations like ==offline preprocessing== and ==tensor caching==, significantly boosting throughput.
- Designed a comprehensive, ==task-aware reward system== with a unified routing mechanism supporting 11 distinct video and image problem types.
- Implemented an ==asynchronous multi-benchmark evaluation== framework that integrates 22 mainstream video-understanding benchmarks for efficient and reproducible accuracy assessment.

## Results

- RL training with EasyVideoR1 improved the average accuracy of the Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct model by **+2.3 points**, raising the average score from **62.1%** to **64.4%** across 10 video benchmarks.
- Achieved substantial accuracy gains in complex reasoning tasks (**+6.6%** on Video-Holmes) and mathematical reasoning tasks (**+6.7%** on VideoMathQA).
- Demonstrated a **1.47x** overall speedup in RL training throughput, with the reference-model forward pass time decreasing by **2.85x** due to offline video caching.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling video preprocessing from the training loop and caching frames provides substantial throughput improvements, particularly by reducing redundant operations for reference models.
> - A modular and unified reward system effectively handles the diverse scoring requirements of various video understanding tasks, enabling broad applicability.
> - Hybrid online-offline training and joint image-video training paradigms offer flexible and efficient strategies for leveraging diverse data sources to improve model learning.
