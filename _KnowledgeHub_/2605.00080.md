# World Model for Robot Learning: A Comprehensive Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey systematically reviews world models in robot learning, offering a robotics-centric definition and categorizing existing approaches by architectural coupling, functional roles, and application domains. It synthesizes current research, identifies key challenges, and outlines future directions for integrating predictive modeling into embodied AI.

## Problem

- Reactive Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies often struggle with complex tasks requiring long-horizon reasoning, effective temporal credit assignment, and robust performance in physical environments.
- The existing literature on world models for robot learning is fragmented, lacking a comprehensive and fine-grained taxonomy with a specific robotics-centered perspective.
- Previous discussions often overlook distinctions in major world-model paradigms, making it difficult to understand their architectural and functional roles in embodied agents.

## Method

- The paper introduces a ==multi-dimensional taxonomy==, categorizing world models based on their architectural coupling with robot policies, functional roles (e.g., as simulators or evaluators), and specific application domains.
- It establishes a clear, ==robotics-centered definition== of world models, emphasizing their utility for action and robust predictive quality for embodied agents over mere perceptual plausibility.
- The survey systematically analyzes the historical and ongoing evolution of world models, detailing how they are integrated with policies and utilized as learned simulators or evaluators across various robotic tasks.

## Results

- The survey identifies a clear architectural evolution from decoupled "predict-then-act" pipelines to tightly integrated unified, Mixture-of-Experts/Transformers (MoE/MoT), and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) backbones, as well as latent-space world modeling.
- World models have expanded their functional roles, increasingly serving as learned environments for reinforcement learning (RL) and robust evaluators for real-time decision-making in diverse robotic applications like navigation and autonomous driving.
- While competitive performance is observed across various integration styles on standard manipulation benchmarks, long-horizon tasks consistently present a greater challenge, with performance often highly dependent on specific benchmarks and task variations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The true value of a world model in embodied AI is contingent on its utility for action and physical consistency, not solely on visual realism or generic perceptual prediction quality.
> - World models are transforming from auxiliary components into integral elements of robotic intelligence, providing core capabilities such as foresight, imagination-driven planning, and data amplification.
> - Evaluation metrics for embodied world models must evolve beyond visual fidelity to comprehensively assess action faithfulness, physical consistency, controllability, and downstream task utility in both open-loop and closed-loop settings.
