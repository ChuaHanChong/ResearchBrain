# AutoSpatial: Visual-Language Reasoning for Social Robot Navigation through Efficient Spatial Reasoning Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> AutoSpatial enhances Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for social robot navigation by introducing a structured spatial grounding framework and an efficient hybrid data labeling strategy. This approach enables VLMs to interpret spatial relationships with greater precision and achieve up to 20.50% higher scores in action generation and improved social compliance compared to baseline models.

## Problem

- Traditional navigation methods often lack explicit spatial reasoning and an understanding of nuanced social norms, leading to limitations in dynamic social scenarios.
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) suffer from poor spatial grounding, lacking explicit metric representations of spatial relationships critical for precise navigation.
- There is a scarcity of domain-specific, large-scale annotated datasets tailored for complex social navigation scenarios, especially from a robot's perspective.

## Method

- A structured spatial grounding framework standardizes spatial descriptions, decomposing information into angular position, distance, and movement direction relative to the robot.
- An efficient data labeling strategy combines rule-based auto-generated VQA (==Visual Question-Answering==) pairs from the CODA dataset with targeted manual annotations for challenging social scenarios.
- A two-round VQA structure facilitates hierarchical learning, progressing from fine-grained individual perceptions (auto-labeled) to broader scene-level comprehension and social interaction analysis (manual annotations).

## Results

- AutoSpatial (AS-A72D1) achieved a basic spatial reasoning score of **0.710** on the CODA benchmark, outperforming LLaVA-M (**0.404**) and Vanilla-LLaVA (**0.376**).
- It demonstrated up to **10.71%** improvement in perception & prediction, **16.26%** in reasoning, **20.50%** in action, and **18.73%** in explanation scores compared to baseline models trained only on manual data.
- Qualitative analysis showed AutoSpatial providing superior spatial awareness with standardized terminology and generating specific, socially appropriate instructions like 'Stop and wait for the person in the orange shirt to clear the path,' unlike generic baseline responses.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The combination of auto-labeled and manual data creates a synergistic effect, proving more effective for comprehensive social navigation reasoning than either data source alone.
> - Structured spatial grounding significantly reduces ambiguity in spatial interpretations, providing VLMs with a consistent and precise understanding of object locations and movements.
> - A hierarchical two-round VQA structure enables the model to learn foundational spatial cues before integrating complex social dynamics and generating coherent explanations and actions.
