# FASTER: Value-Guided Sampling for Fast RL

> [!summary] Summary
> FASTER is a framework that improves the computational efficiency of diffusion-based reinforcement learning policies by filtering unpromising action candidates at the initial noise level. It achieves up to an 8x reduction in inference FLOPs and a 4.5x speedup in training time, enabling large Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models to maintain comparable task success rates to standard best-of-N sampling with significantly lower overhead.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art reinforcement learning algorithms, particularly those using generative diffusion models, achieve high performance through 'best-of-N' test-time scaling, which involves fully denoising multiple action candidates.
- This multi-candidate denoising process is computationally expensive, creating a bottleneck for real-time applications and large models like Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models due to increased latency and resource demands.
- Existing solutions, such as distillation, can be unstable due to non-stationary training targets or introduce additional training complexity and costs.

## Method

- **FASTER** models the denoising and selection of action candidates as a ==Markov Decision Process== where unpromising candidates are progressively filtered at each denoising step.
- A lightweight ==noise-level critic (Q_dn)== is learned to predict the expected return of denoising a specific initial noise sample, allowing for ==early filtering of candidates==.
- During inference, multiple initial noise candidates are sampled, scored by Q_dn, and only the highest-scoring candidate is fully denoised by the base ==diffusion policy==, significantly reducing computational cost.

## Results

- **FASTER-EXPO** consistently achieved top performance across challenging manipulation tasks in both online and batch-online settings, matching or exceeding state-of-the-art baselines.
- The framework delivered substantial computational savings: **8x reduction** in inference FLOPs, up to **4.5x reduction** in training update time, and **1.7x decrease** in inference latency, while maintaining comparable task success rates.
- FASTER was successfully applied to a **3.3B-parameter VLA** model, enabling efficient online RL fine-tuning with **8x less compute** for inference while matching base model performance on most held-out tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The performance gains observed from 'best-of-N' action selection in diffusion policies are largely determined by variance present at the initial noise level.
> - It is possible to effectively predict the downstream value of a fully denoised action directly from its initial noise representation using a specialized, lightweight critic.
> - A simplified single-step filtering approach at the initial noise level is sufficient for achieving significant computational benefits without sacrificing performance, validating the practical implementation over a full multi-step MDP.
