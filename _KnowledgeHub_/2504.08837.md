# VL-Rethinker: Incentivizing Self-Reflection of Vision-Language Models with Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> VL-Rethinker enhances the multimodal reasoning capabilities of Vision-Language Models through a direct reinforcement learning approach. The work introduces Selective Sample Replay to stabilize RL training and Forced Rethinking to incentivize adaptive self-reflection, resulting in new state-of-the-art performance on various multimodal benchmarks, including MathVista and MathVerse.

## Problem

- Text-only 'slow-thinking' LLMs excel in complex reasoning tasks (e.g., math), but this capability does not readily transfer to multimodal reasoning in VLMs, leaving a performance gap.
- Existing methods for multimodal reasoning often depend on resource-intensive, multi-stage pipelines involving supervised fine-tuning on detailed reasoning traces, often distilled from powerful teacher models.
- Standard reinforcement learning for VLMs, especially with binary rewards, faces training instability due to 'vanishing advantages,' which hinders the emergence of deep, explicit reasoning patterns.

## Method

- The VL-Rethinker project employs a two-stage reinforcement learning methodology using ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== on a base instruction-tuned VLM.
- ==Selective Sample Replay (SSR)== is introduced to address the 'vanishing advantages' problem in GRPO by maintaining a replay buffer that only stores and prioritizes samples with non-zero advantage signals.
- A ==Forced Rethinking== mechanism compels the model to generate self-reflection steps (e.g., 'Wait, does it seem right?'), and successful rethinking trajectories are reinforced with an additional Supervised Fine-tuning loss.

## Results

- VL-Rethinker-72B achieved new state-of-the-art results on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, scoring **80.4%** on MathVista and **63.5%** on MathVerse, outperforming OpenAI-o1 and other open-source models.
- The model also set new open-source state-of-the-art on multi-disciplinary benchmarks like MMMU-Pro (**55.9%**) and EMMA (**38.5%**), narrowing the gap with proprietary models.
- Ablation studies confirmed that Selective Sample Replay (SSR) effectively mitigates vanishing advantages, stabilizing training, while Forced Rethinking cultivates adaptive self-reflection, leading to a higher Pass@1 than passive prompting.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision-language models possess latent self-correction capabilities that can be activated and enhanced through targeted training.
> - Reinforcement learning, when properly stabilized with techniques like Selective Sample Replay, can directly incentivize complex reasoning and self-reflection in VLMs without relying on distillation from larger models or pre-generated reasoning traces.
> - Forcing a VLM to rethink during training, coupled with an SFT loss on successful attempts, enables the model to learn adaptive metacognitive behaviors, strategically engaging in self-correction only when necessary.
