# Self-Improving Loops for Visual Robotic Planning

> [!summary] Summary
> The Self-Improving Loops for Visual Robotic Planning (SILVR) framework enables in-domain visual robotic planners to continuously enhance their performance on novel tasks by iteratively learning from self-collected online experience. This approach yields up to 285% improvement in success rates on simulated MetaWorld tasks and substantial gains in real-world manipulation, outperforming baseline methods and demonstrating robustness to varying data quality.

## Problem

- Visual robotic planners struggle to generalize effectively to novel tasks or arbitrary robotic behaviors for which expert demonstrations are unavailable.
- Existing methods, even those leveraging large-scale offline datasets, lack continuous online adaptation capabilities to dynamically refine skills based on self-collected experience.
- Current online improvement techniques, such as reinforcement learning finetuning, often suffer from sample inefficiency and reliance on human-defined reward functions.

## Method

- **SILVR** introduces an ==iterative self-improvement loop== that fine-tunes an in-domain video model using successful trajectories gathered from online interactions.
- Integrates an optional ==internet-pretrained video prior== (e.g., ==AnimateDiff==) via ==Inverse Probabilistic Adaptation (IPA)== to enhance text-conditioned generalization during visual plan synthesis.
- An ==Inverse Dynamics Model (IDM)== translates synthesized visual plans into executable robotic actions, which are then deployed to collect new experience for iterative improvement.

## Results

- **SILVR** achieved significant success rate improvements, including up to **+285%** on 12 unseen MetaWorld tasks and substantial gains on real-world object pushing and drawer opening, over multiple self-improvement iterations.
- Consistently outperformed **DSRL** and **Behavior Cloning Improvement Loop** baselines in MetaWorld, showcasing higher **sample efficiency** for online self-improvement.
- The improved visual planner can be **distilled** into a lightweight action-predicting policy for efficient deployment, achieving comparable or superior performance while operating faster.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Iterative finetuning of a visual planner with self-collected online experience, particularly when composed with internet-scale video priors, leads to continuous performance gains on novel robotic tasks.
> - Visual planning-based self-improvement demonstrates superior sample efficiency compared to direct reinforcement learning finetuning of action-predicting policies for online adaptation.
> - The framework exhibits robustness to suboptimal initial training data and varying feedback mechanisms, including VLM-based filtering or no explicit success filtering, in enabling sustained improvement.
