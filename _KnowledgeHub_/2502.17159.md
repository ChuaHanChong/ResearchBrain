# RobustMerge: Parameter-Efficient Model Merging for MLLMs with Direction Robustness

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed RobustMerge, a training-free, data-free, and storage-free model merging method specifically for parameter-efficient tuning (PEFT) modules in Multimodal Large Language Models. This approach significantly enhances knowledge consolidation, leading to a 3.4% average performance improvement on seen generative tasks and a 4.5% gain on unseen tasks on a new multimodal benchmark compared to previous merging techniques.

## Problem

- Existing model merging methods, primarily designed for full fine-tuning, perform poorly when directly applied to parameter-efficient tuning (PEFT) modules.
- Advanced model merging techniques often rely on validation data or test-time adaptation, limiting their generalization to unseen tasks and practical applicability in data-agnostic scenarios.
- Efficiently consolidating knowledge from multiple task-specific PEFT-tuned MLLMs into a single model without extensive retraining or data access remains a challenge.

## Method

- Introduced a method combining ==magnitude-based parameter pruning== with ==complementary parameter scaling== for ==LoRA matrices== to preserve critical directions and reduce interference.
- Developed a ==cross-task normalization== mechanism for scaling coefficients to balance the influence of different tasks and improve generalization across diverse domains.
- The final merged PEFT module is formed by adaptively summing these pruned and scaled low-rank matrices, requiring no training, additional data, or extra storage.

## Results

- Achieved an average performance of **57.33%** on eight seen multimodal generative tasks and **37.99%** on four unseen tasks on the MM-MergeBench, representing a **3.4%** and **4.5%** average improvement respectively over adapted baseline methods.
- Demonstrated superior performance on general MLLM benchmarks (POPE, MME, MMBench) and for vision tasks using CLIP-ViT-B-32 and CLIP-ViT-L-14, with up to a **4.4%** average accuracy increase over previous merging techniques.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both parameter pruning with complementary scaling and cross-task normalization are vital components, significantly contributing to direction robustness and overall performance gains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Parameter-efficient tuning (PEFT) modules, such as LoRA, exhibit distinct singular value distributions and wider parameter ranges compared to full fine-tuning, rendering existing merging methods unsuitable.
> - Maintaining "direction robustness" by preserving the singular vector directions, particularly for smaller singular values, is essential for minimizing task interference and effective merging of PEFT modules.
> - Compensating for significant singular value gaps through adaptive inter-parameter scaling, combined with cross-task normalization, enhances stability and generalization to unseen tasks without explicit singular value decomposition.
