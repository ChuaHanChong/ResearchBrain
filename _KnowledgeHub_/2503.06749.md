# Vision-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in Multimodal Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Vision-R1 introduces a two-phase training methodology that enhances multimodal large language models' reasoning capabilities through a cold-start initialization using a novel human annotation-free dataset and subsequent reinforcement learning with Progressive Thinking Suppression Training. The 7B parameter model achieves competitive performance on multimodal math reasoning benchmarks, sometimes rivaling models with over 70 billion parameters, while exhibiting emergent human-like reasoning processes.

## Problem

- Extending reinforcement learning-driven self-emergent reasoning, as seen in text-only Large Language Models like DeepSeek-R1, to the multimodal domain is challenging.
- There is a scarcity of substantial, high-quality multimodal reasoning data that captures human-like cognitive processes such as questioning, reflection, and self-correction.
- Multimodal Large Language Models tend to "overthink," generating excessively long and often incorrect reasoning chains after initial training, hindering effective reinforcement learning optimization.

## Method

- A two-phase training methodology is proposed: ==cold-start initialization== via Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed by Reinforcement Learning (RL) training.
- A **200K** sample human annotation-free multimodal CoT dataset, =="Vision-R1-cold"==, is generated using =="Modality Bridging"==, which leverages existing MLLMs and a text-only reasoning LLM (DeepSeek-R1) to extract human-like CoT.
- RL training incorporates ==Progressive Thinking Suppression Training (PTST)==, which progressively relaxes constraints on the CoT length across stages (e.g., from 4K to 8K tokens) to mitigate "overthinking."

## Results

- Vision-R1-7B achieves **73.5%** accuracy on MathVista, comparable to leading closed-source models and surpassing open-source models with significantly more parameters (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-72B).
- The Vision-R1-cold dataset contains a higher frequency of human-like self-reflective indicators ("Wait," "Hmm," "Mistake") compared to existing multimodal Chain-of-Thought datasets.
- Ablation studies demonstrate Vision-R1's comprehensive approach (cold-start + PTST + GRPO) achieves **55.4%** average accuracy, outperforming RL-only baselines (**50.7%**) and cold-start only models (**44.5%**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Direct Reinforcement Learning is insufficient for activating complex multimodal reasoning; a "cold-start" initialization with high-quality data is necessary.
> - High-quality, human-like multimodal Chain-of-Thought data, free from manual annotation, can be effectively synthesized using a "Modality Bridging" technique by combining MLLMs and text-only reasoning LLMs.
> - "Overthinking" in MLLMs during RL training can be mitigated by progressively increasing the allowed Chain-of-Thought length, enabling the model to learn concise, correct reasoning before expanding to longer, more complex thought processes.
