# EmbodiedBench: Comprehensive Benchmarking Multi-modal Large Language Models for Vision-Driven Embodied Agents

> [!summary] Summary
> This paper presents EMBODIEDBENCH, a new comprehensive benchmark for evaluating Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs) as vision-driven embodied agents. Experiments with 24 MLLMs reveal that while models perform strongly on high-level planning tasks, they significantly struggle with low-level physical interactions like precise manipulation and long-horizon tasks, with GPT-4o achieving only 28.9% on manipulation.

## Problem

- Existing benchmarks for embodied agents are often tailored for Large Language Models (LLMs), lacking multimodal input capabilities necessary for evaluating Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs).
- Current evaluation frameworks primarily focus on high-level symbolic planning or specific domains, neglecting low-level physical interaction (e.g., precise manipulation and continuous navigation).
- There is a lack of fine-grained evaluation to diagnose specific capabilities (e.g., spatial awareness, visual perception) of MLLMs in embodied contexts, beyond overall task success.

## Method

- Introduces **EMBODIEDBENCH**, a comprehensive benchmark for vision-driven MLLM-based embodied agents, featuring **1,128 tasks** across four distinct environments (==EB-ALFRED==, ==EB-Habitat==, ==EB-Navigation==, ==EB-Manipulation==).
- Covers both ==high-level tasks== (household planning) and ==low-level atomic actions== (precise navigation, robotic arm manipulation) to assess MLLMs across hierarchical action levels.
- Provides a ==fine-grained capability-oriented evaluation== across six dimensions: commonsense reasoning, complex instruction understanding, spatial awareness, visual perception, long-term planning, and basic task solving.

## Results

- MLLMs achieved over **60%** success rates on high-level tasks (EB-ALFRED, EB-Habitat), but the best model (**GPT-4o**) scored only **28.9%** on average for low-level manipulation (EB-Manipulation).
- Removing visual input caused GPT-4o's EB-Navigation performance to drop from **57.7% → 17.4%**, demonstrating vision's criticality for low-level tasks.
- Providing detection boxes boosted EB-Manipulation performance by **+12.5%** for GPT-4o, and visual in-context learning improved manipulation tasks by **+16.7%** for Claude-3.5-Sonnet.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - While MLLMs show strong performance in high-level planning and reasoning for embodied tasks, they struggle significantly with low-level, fine-grained physical interaction and manipulation.
> - Visual input is indispensable for low-level embodied tasks (e.g., navigation and manipulation), whereas high-level tasks sometimes rely more heavily on an MLLM's textual understanding and reasoning.
> - Long-horizon planning remains a primary bottleneck for MLLM-based agents across all task complexities, indicating limitations in multi-step reasoning and memory.
