# ManiCM: Real-time 3D Diffusion Policy via Consistency Model for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> ManiCM introduces a real-time 3D diffusion policy for robotic manipulation by adapting consistency models to generate actions in a single inference step. This system achieves a 10-fold acceleration in decision latency, operating at approximately 17 milliseconds per step, while maintaining or improving task success rates across various simulation and real-world manipulation tasks compared to iterative 3D diffusion policies.

## Problem

- Diffusion models for robotic manipulation suffer from high inference latency due to their iterative denoising process, preventing real-time closed-loop control.
- Existing acceleration techniques for diffusion policies often lack generalizability across diverse task domains.
- High-dimensional 3D sensory inputs further exacerbate the computational inefficiency of 3D diffusion policies, leading to latencies of over 170ms per step for state-of-the-art methods.

## Method

- **ManiCM** leverages ==Consistency Models (CMs)== to distill the iterative 3D diffusion process into a single-step action generation.
- It processes sparse point clouds from an RGB-D camera and robot proprioception as conditions, encoding them into a compact 3D representation via an MLP.
- The model is specifically designed to predict the clean action sample directly, rather than noise, optimizing for the low-dimensional action space of manipulation tasks and improving convergence stability.

## Results

- Achieved an average runtime of **17.3** 
 **0.1** ms per step across 31 tasks, representing a 10-fold acceleration over the state-of-the-art 3D Diffusion Policy (DP3) which took **177.6** 
 **1.4** ms.
- Maintained competitive average success rates (**78.5** 
 **4.2%**) compared to DP3 (**77.5** 
 **3.8%**), with a **3.4%** absolute improvement in Metaworld Very Hard tasks.
- Demonstrated comparable real-world performance on a UR3e robot, achieving **10x** faster inference speeds (e.g., **16.8** ms for 'Transfer Piece') while maintaining success rates (**80%**) compared to DP3 (**70%** at **172.4** ms).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Consistency models can effectively accelerate 3D diffusion policies for robotic manipulation, enabling real-time operation without sacrificing performance.
> - Directly predicting the clean action sample, instead of noise, is more effective for consistency distillation in low-dimensional robotic action spaces, leading to faster convergence and higher success rates.
> - Significant acceleration (up to 10x) can be achieved by distilling the knowledge of an iterative diffusion teacher model into a single-step consistency model, outperforming simple model backbone reduction.
