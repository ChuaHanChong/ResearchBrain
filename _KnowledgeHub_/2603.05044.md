# WebFactory: Automated Compression of Foundational Language Intelligence into Grounded Web Agents

> [!summary] Summary
> WebFactory introduces an automated, closed-loop reinforcement learning pipeline to distill large language model intelligence into grounded web agent behaviors using high-fidelity offline environments. This approach enables scalable and reproducible training of GUI agents, achieving superior performance on offline and online benchmarks compared to models trained on human data and zero-shot foundation models.

## Problem

- LLMs possess extensive internet-scale knowledge but struggle to reliably translate abstract intent into precise, actionable GUI interactions due to a "semantic-to-action gap."
- Existing agent training paradigms face a dilemma between scalability and control, with human-crafted data being expensive and biased, and live web training introducing non-determinism, safety risks, and noise.
- Inefficient intelligence compression methods often focus on raw data volume rather than effectively leveraging an LLM's latent knowledge into actionable behaviors.

## Method

- Developed a high-fidelity, fully controllable ==offline web environment== that automatically generates realistic websites with LLM assistance, ensuring reproducibility and full observability for data generation.
- Implemented a knowledge-driven dual-track task generation system (operation and information-retrieval) with validators, coupled with a strong executor and filtering pipeline for scalable collection of high-quality interaction trajectories.
- Utilized a reinforcement learning framework with a unified action space and a per-step decomposed reward function (format and accuracy) for policy optimization from the generated trajectories.

## Results

- The knowledge-driven approach significantly improved task executability to **86.3%** and validity to **92.6%**, while trajectory generation success nearly doubled to **84.3%**, with a **38%** reduction in average steps.
- WebFactory-**3B**, trained on synthetic data, outperformed GUI-R1-**3B** (trained on human data) on internal offline operational tasks (**71.8%** TCR vs. **68.2%**) and showed a **162%** improvement over QwenVL2.5-**3B** on offline-to-online transfer (**53.4%** average TCR).
- The agent demonstrated strong generalization, achieving the highest success rate (**84.2%**) on GUI-Act-Web and substantially higher Type accuracy (**66.0%**) on the challenging GUI-Odyssey benchmark compared to other foundation models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - LLMs can act as architects for their own embodiment, driving the creation of training environments and tasks to efficiently compress their latent intelligence into grounded behaviors.
> - The efficiency of intelligence compression is a critical factor in agent development, potentially more impactful than raw data volume, as demonstrated by agents trained on synthetic data matching or exceeding those trained on human data.
> - Agents trained exclusively in controlled offline environments can achieve robust generalization, effectively transferring to complex, unseen real-world online platforms and diverse public GUI benchmarks.
