# MaskFocus: Focusing Policy Optimization on Critical Steps for Masked Image Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> MaskFocus, a reinforcement learning framework developed by researchers at the University of Science and Technology of China and Fudan University, optimizes masked generative models by focusing policy updates on critical image generation steps and employing dynamic routing for sampling. This approach yielded an overall GenEval score of 0.76, surpassing previous baselines, and enhanced image quality metrics like DEQA to 4.39 on the Meissonic model.

## Problem

- Applying reinforcement learning (RL) to masked generative models (MGMs) for image generation is computationally expensive, especially when optimizing over entire sampling trajectories.
- Prior RL methods for MGMs often use non-dynamic step selection strategies, leading to suboptimal exploration of the varying contributions of different generation steps and reduced performance.
- Conventional confidence-based sampling in MGMs can lead to deterministic outputs for simple regions, limiting image diversity and quality, while aggressive exploration strategies can destabilize training.

## Method

- Introduced **MaskFocus**, an RL framework built on ==Group Relative Policy== Optimization (GRPO), comprising two main components: Critical Step Selection (CSS) and Dynamic Routing Sampling (DR-Sampling).
- Critical Step Selection (CSS) identifies and focuses policy optimization on the 'K' steps with the highest 'information gain,' quantified as the absolute difference in cosine similarity between consecutive intermediate and final image embeddings.
- Dynamic Routing Sampling (DR-Sampling) balances exploration and exploitation by sorting samples by entropy, applying standard confidence-based sampling to high-entropy samples, and employing entropy-based dynamic temperature modulation for low-entropy samples.

## Results

- MaskFocus achieved an overall GenEval score of **0.76** for compositional generation, surpassing the Meissonic baseline (**0.54**) and the MaskGRPO baseline (**0.73**), with notable improvements in specific sub-tasks like Counting (**0.85**).
- The framework significantly improved image quality and human preference alignment, attaining a DEQA score of 4.39, a PickScore of 22.39, an HPS score of 35.52, and an ImageReward score of 1.09, demonstrating performance comparable to advanced diffusion models.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both Critical Step Selection and Dynamic Routing Sampling are crucial for performance; removing them led to drops in GenEval scores (e.g., to **0.72** without CSS) and image quality metrics (e.g., DEQA to 4.34 without CSS), indicating their efficacy in efficient RL training and enhanced exploration.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The contribution of each sampling step to the final image is non-uniform; early steps rapidly establish global structure, while later steps refine details, making targeted optimization of 'critical steps' more efficient.
> - Directly leveraging the probability distributions of all masked tokens (not just the sampled subset) provides a more effective and direct signal for policy optimization, aligning with the pre-training objective of MGMs.
> - A nuanced sampling mechanism that dynamically adjusts exploration based on the entropy of individual samples is essential to balance training stability with the need for diverse and high-quality outputs.
