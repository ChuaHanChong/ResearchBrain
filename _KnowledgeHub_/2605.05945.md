# MobileEgo Anywhere: Open Infrastructure for long horizon egocentric data on commodity hardware

> [!summary] Summary
> MobileEgo Anywhere introduces an open infrastructure for collecting long-duration, egocentric multimodal data using commodity LiDAR-enabled smartphones, alongside a comprehensive processing pipeline. The framework produces a 200-hour dataset featuring continuous 6 DoF camera poses, depth maps, 3D hand poses, and automatically generated hierarchical action labels, offering a scalable resource for Vision Language Action (VLA) model development.

## Problem

- Existing egocentric datasets for VLA models often contain short episode durations, hindering the learning of long-horizon temporal dependencies for complex robotic tasks.
- High hardware barriers, involving specialized and expensive equipment, limit the accessibility and scalability of high-fidelity egocentric data collection.
- Many datasets lack precise actionable modalities like continuous 6 DoF pose, depth, 3D hand pose, and sufficiently detailed action language labels required for VLA policies.

## Method

- A framework utilizing **MobileEgo Anywhere** — commodity ==LiDAR-enabled iOS devices== (iPhone Pro models) with an open-source mobile application for recording synchronized ==RGB-D streams==, IMU data, and ARKit-derived ==6 DoF camera poses==.
- An open-source Python processing pipeline that transforms raw mobile captures into training-ready formats, extracting robust ==3D hand trajectories==, atomic action labels, and hierarchical task instructions.
- Automated 3D hand pose estimation using ==WiLoR== and a ==VLM-based labeling system== for generating detailed, object-modified atomic action labels, further structured into hierarchical goals by a language model.

## Results

- Release of a **200-hour dataset** comprising **354 sessions** of diverse household activities, with continuous recordings up to **108 minutes**, including synchronized RGB-D, 6 DoF poses, 3D hand poses, and multi-level language annotations.
- ARKit's long-term pose tracking demonstrated minimal cumulative drift, remaining **below 1 cm** in most scenarios and **under 0.1%** of the total trajectory length.
- Validated 3D hand pose consistency with median bone length coefficient of variation **below 1%** for most bones and **over 99.99%** of joint angles falling within biomechanical limits; automated hierarchical instruction generation achieved distinct temporal scale separation with high cost-efficiency (**$1.29** for all sessions).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Commodity LiDAR-enabled smartphones provide sufficiently stable and accurate long-term 6 DoF pose tracking (e.g., <1.5 cm drift over hour-long whole-house activities) and depth maps for VLA applications.
> - Automated pipelines can generate more detailed and temporally consistent action labels and hierarchical task instructions than human annotators, at a low cost.
> - The proposed framework effectively bridges the gap between hardware accessibility and the demand for long-horizon, high-fidelity egocentric data by democratizing the collection process.
