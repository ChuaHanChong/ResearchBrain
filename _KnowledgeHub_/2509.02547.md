# The Landscape of Agentic Reinforcement Learning for LLMs: A Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey formally defines Agentic Reinforcement Learning (RL) for Large Language Models (LLMs) using Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes, distinguishing it from conventional LLM RL. It offers a detailed taxonomy of RL-enhanced agentic capabilities and application domains, providing a structured overview of the field and identifying future research directions.

## Problem

- The rapidly evolving field of Agentic Reinforcement Learning for LLMs lacked a unified conceptual framework, consistent terminology, and systematic categorization.
- Prior Reinforcement Learning approaches for LLMs often treated them as static generators for single-turn text outputs, not as adaptive agents in dynamic, multi-step sequential decision processes.
- LLM agents, while showing capabilities like tool use and planning, frequently relied on heuristic pipelines or supervised fine-tuning, missing a unified, adaptive learning mechanism.

## Method

- Formally defined Agentic RL using temporally extended ==Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs)==, contrasting it with conventional LLM RL (PBRFT) modeled as degenerate MDPs.
- Developed a comprehensive ==two-fold taxonomy==: one based on RL-optimizable agentic capabilities (e.g., planning, tool use, memory) and another on diverse application domains (e.g., code, math, embodied agents).
- Consolidated over **500** recent works, identifying key trends, methodologies, and advancements, alongside a compendium of open-source environments, benchmarks, and frameworks.

## Results

- Presented a formal distinction between conventional LLM RL (PBRFT, a degenerate MDP) and Agentic RL (a POMDP), clarifying the theoretical underpinnings of treating LLMs as interactive agents.
- Provided a systematic taxonomy categorizing existing research across **six** core agentic capabilities (e.g., Planning, Tool Use, Self-Improvement) and **seven** application domains (e.g., Code Agents, Embodied Agents).
- Compiled a comprehensive list of open-source environments and frameworks, offering practical resources for researchers, and outlined a roadmap for future research by identifying key challenges and opportunities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Agentic RL fundamentally shifts LLMs from static conditional generators to active, autonomous decision-makers capable of sustained interaction and adaptation within complex, dynamic environments.
> - Reinforcement learning acts as a critical mechanism for transforming heuristic agentic modules into adaptive, robust, and learnable behaviors, optimizing capabilities through environmental interaction.
> - Significant challenges for Agentic RL include ensuring trustworthiness (e.g., security, hallucination), efficiently scaling training and environments, and developing a mechanistic understanding of how RL installs or amplifies capabilities in LLMs.
