# High-Fidelity Simulated Data Generation for Real-World Zero-Shot Robotic Manipulation Learning with Gaussian Splatting

> [!summary] Summary
> RoboSimGS creates high-fidelity, physically interactive simulation environments from real-world images using 3D Gaussian Splatting for photorealism and Multi-modal Large Language Models to infer object articulation and physical properties. This approach enables successful zero-shot Sim2Real transfer for robotic manipulation tasks and significantly boosts policy performance when augmenting limited real-world datasets.

## Problem

- The prohibitive cost and labor of collecting large-scale real-world robot interaction data severely limit the scalability of generalist robot policies.
- Traditional Sim2Real approaches suffer from a significant "domain gap" in visual appearance and physical properties, hindering transferability to real robots.
- Prior Real2Sim2Real (R2S2R) methods, while offering photorealistic scene reconstructions, largely create static environments, lacking the physical interactivity needed for training complex, contact-rich manipulation tasks.

## Method

- A hybrid scene representation is employed, using 3D Gaussian Splatting for photorealistic backgrounds and explicit meshes for interactive objects.
- Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs like GPT-4o) are utilized to automatically infer complex articulation (e.g., joint types, limits) and physical material properties (e.g., density, stiffness) for interactive objects directly from multi-view renderings.
- A comprehensive scene augmentation strategy, including randomization of object poses, camera views, lighting conditions, and robot trajectories, is applied to generate diverse training data.

## Results

- Policies trained exclusively on RoboSimGS-generated data demonstrated successful zero-shot transfer across eight diverse real-world robotic manipulation tasks.
- Augmenting limited real-world datasets (e.g., 50 demonstrations) with synthetic RoboSimGS data (e.g., 50 demonstrations) consistently and substantially improved the performance of state-of-the-art visuomotor policies like Diffusion Policy and π0 across various tasks.
- RoboSimGS achieves a high data generation throughput, producing over 10,000 demonstrations per day on a single GPU, representing a 10-fold increase compared to manual teleoperation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating 3D Gaussian Splatting for high visual fidelity with MLLM-driven inference for physical interactivity effectively transforms static scene reconstructions into dynamic, interactive robot training environments.
> - Multi-modal Large Language Models can automate the complex and traditionally manual process of inferring articulated structures and physical properties of objects from visual input.
> - A holistic augmentation strategy, encompassing diverse environmental factors like camera views, lighting, and action trajectories, is essential for enabling robust Sim2Real transfer, beyond simple object randomization.
