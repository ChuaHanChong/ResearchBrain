# GuidedVLA: Specifying Task-Relevant Factors via Plug-and-Play Action Attention Specialization

> [!summary] Summary
> GuidedVLA introduces a framework for Vision-Language-Action models that explicitly guides action decoding through specialized attention heads, focusing on object grounding, skill recognition, and geometry perception. This approach enhanced average success rates on the LIBERO-Plus benchmark by 7.2%, on RoboTwin 2.0 by 13.25%, and demonstrated up to 22.1% improvement in real-world generalization tasks compared to baselines.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models implicitly learn task-relevant features from end-to-end supervision.
- This implicit learning causes overfitting to spurious correlations, shortcut learning, and causal confusion, hindering generalization to out-of-domain scenarios.
- Opaque decision-making in current VLA models makes diagnosing failures and understanding underlying factors challenging.

## Method

- **GuidedVLA** explicitly specializes subsets of ==attention heads== within the VLA action decoder to capture specific task-relevant factors.
- It guides these specialized heads with auxiliary supervision for ==object grounding==, ==skill recognition==, and integrates 3D information via a dedicated ==depth head==.
- A ==ControlNet-style residual adapter== facilitates seamless integration of these specialized heads into pre-trained VLM backbones, minimizing disruption.

## Results

- GuidedVLA achieved an average success rate of **75.4%** on the LIBERO-Plus benchmark, surpassing the π₀ baseline by **+7.2pp** and outperforming other state-of-the-art methods.
- The framework improved the average success rate on RoboTwin 2.0 from **77.38% to 90.63%**, showing a **+13.25pp** increase across 8 diverse manipulation tasks.
- Real-world experiments demonstrated enhanced generalization, with average success rates improving from **55.8% to 75.8%** in in-domain, **44.2% to 67.5%** in scene, and **57.5% to 79.2%** in lighting generalization settings.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicit guidance of attention heads toward task-relevant factors significantly improves VLA model robustness and generalization across various perturbations.
> - Specializing attention heads for distinct factors leads to decoupled, disentangled feature learning, enhancing interpretability compared to entangled representations.
> - The quality and fidelity of the factor-specific supervision directly impact the overall task success, demonstrating their causal importance.
