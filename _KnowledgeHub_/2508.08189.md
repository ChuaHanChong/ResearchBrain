# Reinforcement Learning for Large Model: A Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey maps the rapidly expanding landscape of visual Reinforcement Learning (RL) applied to large multimodal models, systematically categorizing over 250 recent works and detailing algorithmic advancements, evaluation methods, and persistent challenges. This work aims to provide a coherent guide for researchers and practitioners in this rapidly evolving interdisciplinary field.

## Problem

- The rapidly growing and fragmented research landscape at the intersection of reinforcement learning (RL) and large multimodal models (LMMs) lacks a comprehensive, up-to-date synthesis, especially for advancements since 2024.
- Significant challenges exist in applying RL to visual domains, including stabilizing policy optimization with complex reward signals, handling high-dimensional visual inputs, and designing scalable reward functions for long-horizon decision-making.
- There is a need for a structured overview to guide future research and practical deployment of RL techniques for visual tasks, covering areas from perception to generation and interaction.

## Method

- Conducted an extensive and systematic literature review, synthesizing over ==250 representative works==, predominantly focusing on research advancements since 2024.
- Formalized visual RL problems by casting text/image generation as an episodic ==Markov Decision Process== and detailing key alignment paradigms (==RLHF==, ==DPO==, ==RLVR==) and policy optimization algorithms (==PPO==, ==GRPO==).
- Organized the vast literature into a clear taxonomy across four thematic pillars: ==MLLMs==, ==Visual Generation==, ==Unified Model Frameworks==, and ==Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models==, analyzing algorithmic design, reward engineering, and benchmarking within each.

## Results

- A structured synthesis identifies RLHF and its variants, along with GRPO, as dominant approaches for aligning and optimizing large multimodal models, providing a clear map of algorithmic evolution.
- The survey successfully categorizes over **250** works into four key application areas, detailing their specific algorithmic designs, reward engineering strategies, and progress in benchmarking for each.
- A principled taxonomy for evaluating visual RL methods is introduced, differentiating between set-level, sample-level, and model-state metrics, alongside a clear articulation of critical open challenges and promising future research directions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - RLHF principles and its variants, such as Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR), are profoundly influencing reward signal design for visual RL.
> - Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) emerges as a particularly effective policy optimization algorithm for visual RL, offering stable, low-variance learning signals and reducing memory consumption by discarding the value network.
> - The field's research landscape is structured into four primary thematic areas: MLLMs, Visual Generation, Unified Models, and VLA Models, each presenting unique algorithmic adaptations and reward design considerations.
