# Scaling Synthetic Task Generation for Agents via Exploration

> [!summary] Summary
> AutoPlay introduces a scalable, automated pipeline that uses explicit environment exploration to synthesize diverse, feasible, and verifiable tasks for training multimodal large language model agents. This approach significantly enhances agent performance on UI interaction benchmarks, achieving up to 20.6% higher success rates on AndroidWorld and enabling robust reinforcement learning without human supervision.

## Problem

- Training MLLM-based interactive agents is hampered by the scarcity of high-quality, diverse, and verifiable task-demonstration data.
- Existing synthetic task generation methods often produce generic or hallucinated tasks due to a lack of grounding in actual environment dynamics.
- Human annotation for creating agent training data is prohibitively expensive and does not scale efficiently for complex, open-ended environments.

## Method

- A two-stage pipeline, **AutoPlay**, employs an MLLM-based '==Explorer Agent==' to systematically explore interactive environments and gather rich context through summarized memory.
- An 'Exploration-Conditioned Task Generator' MLLM synthesizes diverse and feasible tasks by combining collected exploration trajectories with predefined task guideline prompts.
- MLLM-based executor and verifier agents automatically generate and filter high-quality task execution demonstrations for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and provide reward signals for Reinforcement Learning (RL).

## Results

- AutoPlay-trained agents achieve substantial performance gains, with AutoPlay-**7B** improving success rates by **20.6%** on AndroidWorld and **7.7%** on OSWorld compared to its base MLLM.
- The framework enables effective Reinforcement Learning, with an AutoPlay-**3B** model trained via RL approaching the performance of the SFT-trained AutoPlay-**7B** model (**39.9%** vs. **40.1%** Pass@1).
- AutoPlay tasks demonstrate higher feasibility (executor completes **46.0%** of tasks) and lead to significantly improved downstream agent performance (**9.4%** Pass@1 higher than 'No Exploration' tasks).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicit and systematic environment exploration is crucial for generating a broad range of high-quality, feasible, and diverse synthetic tasks that significantly improve agent performance.
> - MLLM-based verifiers are effective in automatically filtering successful trajectories for SFT and serving as reward models for scalable RL training, reducing reliance on human annotation.
> - Training with AutoPlay's synthetically generated and verified data can lead to agents that outperform the expert policies used for initial data collection, demonstrating improved generalization and robustness.
