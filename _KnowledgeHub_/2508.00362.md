# A Whole-Body Motion Imitation Framework from Human Data for Full-Size Humanoid Robot

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework enables full-size humanoid robots to perform diverse and stable human motions by integrating contact-aware motion retargeting with nonlinear centroidal Model Predictive Control. It achieves a 100% success rate and lower tracking errors compared to baselines in challenging motions, demonstrating real-time performance on physical hardware.

## Problem

- Endowing full-size humanoid robots with diverse, stable, and anthropomorphic motions remains a challenge due to kinematic and dynamic discrepancies between humans and robots.
- Traditional model-based robot control methods often produce rigid motions, while data-driven approaches struggle with real-time stability and transfer from simulation to complex physical hardware.
- Existing motion retargeting techniques often fail to produce kinematically feasible or dynamically stable trajectories, and real-time computation of complex multi-rigid body dynamics is frequently unfeasible.

## Method

- A three-stage framework integrates contact-aware whole-body motion retargeting, a nonlinear centroidal ==Model Predictive Control (NMPC)==, and an optimization-based Whole-Body Controller (WBC).
- Motion retargeting uses a Graph Neural Network (GNN) with a comprehensive objective function to generate kinematically feasible and anthropomorphic reference trajectories, explicitly considering foot contacts.
- NMPC ensures real-time dynamic balance, accurate trajectory tracking, and robustness against disturbances by optimizing contact forces and joint velocities within physical and dynamic constraints.

## Results

- Achieved a **100%** success rate across challenging "Wave," "Yoga," and "Stride" motions, demonstrating superior robustness compared to linear MPC and WBC-only baselines.
- Exhibited consistently lower tracking errors, with CoM RMSE for "Yoga" motion at 0.0170m, significantly outperforming baseline methods of 0.0400m (LMPC+WBC) and 0.0747m (WBC).
- Successfully validated on a full-size physical humanoid robot, reliably executing diverse tasks such as upper-body movements, one-legged standing, and simple walking over multiple real-world trials.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The integration of contact-aware, data-driven motion retargeting with model-based nonlinear Model Predictive Control (NMPC) is crucial for achieving both anthropomorphic motion and real-time dynamic stability.
> - Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) can effectively handle morphological differences and generate kinematically feasible robot reference trajectories by incorporating contact awareness and specific loss functions.
> - Centroidal dynamics-based NMPC, utilizing a Real-Time Iteration scheme for efficiency, effectively maintains dynamic balance and robustness for complex whole-body motions on a full-size humanoid robot.
