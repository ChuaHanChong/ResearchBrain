# Genie Sim PanoRecon: Fast Immersive Scene Generation from Single-View Panorama

> [!summary] Summary
> Genie Sim PanoRecon presents a feed-forward pipeline that generates high-fidelity, geometrically consistent 3D Gaussian scenes from a single panoramic image within seconds. The method integrates a panoramic depth fusion strategy with a training-free depth injection module, enabling rapid and scalable creation of photo-realistic backgrounds for robotic manipulation simulations.

## Problem

- High demand for scalable, high-quality 3D scene data for robotics simulation and embodied AI, vital for policy training and synthetic data generation.
- Existing iterative 3D reconstruction methods require dense multi-view inputs and costly per-scene optimization, hindering industrial-level scalability.
- Feed-forward 3D reconstruction models designed for pinhole cameras struggle with 360° panoramic inputs, often leading to structural discontinuities and scale drifting at cubemap boundaries.

## Method

- **Genie Sim PanoRecon** is a ==feed-forward Gaussian-splatting pipeline==, which decomposes a single panoramic image into ==cubemap faces== for parallel processing.
- A ==Panoramic Depth Fusion module== combines global structural depth (from ==DA360==) with high-resolution local depth (from ==DepthPro==) using a ==Laplacian pyramid==.
- A ==Training-free Depth Injection== mechanism adapts a pre-trained monocular feed-forward ==3D Gaussian reconstruction== framework (**SHARP**) by replacing its internal depth predictions with the fused panoramic depth.

## Results

- Photo-realistic and geometrically consistent 3D Gaussian scenes are reconstructed from single panoramas in seconds, demonstrating efficiency and reduced production costs for simulation environments.
- Achieves high-fidelity reconstruction with accurate global spatial structures and sharp local details, successfully addressing seam artifacts and scale inconsistencies prevalent in prior panoramic methods.
- The pipeline is integrated into the Genie Sim platform, providing scalable and robust 3D background assets that are suitable for robotic manipulation simulation tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Fusing distinct depth sources, one for global structure and another for local detail, with a Laplacian pyramid effectively resolves scale inconsistencies and preserves fine-grained information in panoramic depth estimation.
> - Pre-trained monocular feed-forward 3D Gaussian models can be repurposed for panoramic inputs through a training-free depth injection, enabling efficient and geometrically consistent scene generation without per-scene optimization.
> - Applying anti-aliasing during the initial cubemap projection step is crucial for mitigating artifacts and noise, which improves the accuracy of feed-forward Gaussian initialization.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of robotics simulation and embodied intelligence is experiencing rapid growth, which has consequently generated a substantial demand for high-quality 3D scene data. Such data is critical for various applications, including policy training, sim-to-real transfer, and large-scale synthetic data generation. Simulation platforms require immersive environments that combine local photorealism with consistent global spatial structure.

Existing scene construction methods face significant limitations in meeting this demand for scalable production:
- **Iterative Optimization Approaches:** Methods like 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) can achieve high-fidelity reconstructions. However, they typically rely on dense multi-view image inputs, incurring high data acquisition costs. Furthermore, they necessitate tedious, per-scene optimization, which is time-consuming and exhibits limited generalization ability, thereby hindering industrial-level data production requirements.
- **Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction Schemes:** To improve efficiency, feed-forward models have emerged. These methods bypass per-scene training by rapidly generating scene representations through a single forward inference pass. However, most existing feed-forward models are designed for conventional pinhole camera images and are not directly compatible with 360° equirectangular inputs.
- **Panorama-Specific Feed-Forward Methods:** Current practices for panoramas often involve projecting equirectangular images into multiple independent cube-map faces and performing inference separately. While methods like PanSplat and PanoSplat3r adapt feed-forward 3DGS to panoramic images, they frequently struggle with high-resolution reconstruction and hole-filling due to their pixel-aligned architectures. Crucially, independent prediction across cubemap faces, without incorporating global geometric priors, tends to cause structural discontinuities and scale drifting at inter-face boundaries. This compromises the overall spatial continuity of the scene, rendering it unsuitable for applications requiring global geometric consistency, such as robot navigation and manipulation.

Genie Sim PanoRecon positions itself within this landscape by addressing the specific challenges of generating geometrically consistent and photo-realistic 3D scenes from single-view panoramas, particularly for robotic manipulation simulation. It leverages the efficiency of feed-forward Gaussian splatting while introducing mechanisms to overcome the geometric inconsistencies typically associated with multi-face panoramic reconstruction. The work builds upon recent advancements in high-definition monocular reconstruction, specifically extending SHARP, to incorporate panoramic input and depth-guided consistency, aiming for a scalable and low-cost solution for generating simulation backgrounds.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this work is to develop Genie Sim PanoRecon, a feed-forward Gaussian-splatting pipeline designed to generate high-fidelity, low-cost 3D scenes from single-view panoramas. This system is specifically tailored for applications in robotic manipulation simulation.

The motivation behind this development is rooted in several critical needs and limitations within the current landscape of robotics and embodied intelligence:
- **Demand for High-Quality 3D Data:** Research in robotics simulation and embodied intelligence requires vast amounts of high-quality 3D scene data. This data is essential for tasks such as training robotic policies, facilitating sim-to-real transfer, and generating large-scale synthetic datasets. The immersive environments needed for these applications must exhibit both local photorealism and consistent global spatial structure.
- **Inefficiency of Existing Methods:** Traditional 3D reconstruction techniques, particularly iterative optimization methods like 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), are limited by their reliance on dense multi-view inputs and time-consuming per-scene optimization. These factors make them unsuitable for industrial-level data production, where rapid and scalable scene generation is crucial.
- **Geometric Inconsistency in Panorama Reconstruction:** While feed-forward methods offer improved efficiency, their direct application to 360° panoramas often involves decomposing the panorama into independent cubemap faces. Processing these faces in isolation, without global geometric priors, leads to significant challenges, including structural discontinuities, scale inconsistencies, and visible artifacts at the boundaries between faces. Such inconsistencies undermine the global spatial continuity required for robust robot navigation and manipulation tasks.
- **Need for a Scalable and Low-Cost Solution:** The high cost of data acquisition and scene generation represents a bottleneck for large-scale synthetic data production. The authors aim to provide a low-cost and scalable solution specifically for generating 3D background assets for indoor desktop-scale robotic manipulation simulations. This approach seeks to output geometrically coherent, simulation-ready assets without the need for per-scene training.
- **Integration into an Embodied AI Platform:** The system is motivated by its potential integration into the Genie Sim platform, an LLM-driven simulation environment. This integration aims to support scalable backgrounds for manipulation tasks, thereby contributing to the efficient construction of data infrastructure for embodied intelligence.

In summary, the core motivation is to overcome the limitations of existing 3D scene generation techniques by creating an efficient, consistent, and scalable method for producing high-fidelity 3D environments from single panoramic images, directly addressing the needs of advanced robotics simulation.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The Genie Sim PanoRecon pipeline is a feed-forward Gaussian-splatting system that processes a single-view panorama to reconstruct a 3D scene. The methodology is designed to ensure geometric consistency and high fidelity while maintaining computational efficiency. The pipeline involves decomposing the panoramic input, processing it in parallel, and seamlessly reassembling the reconstructed components.

The approach consists of two main technical modules: Panoramic Depth Fusion and Training-Free Depth Injection.

**A. Panoramic Depth Fusion**
This module addresses the challenge of inconsistent depth scales and visible seam artifacts that can arise when a panoramic image is split into cubemaps for independent reconstruction. The strategy focuses on generating a high-resolution and scale-consistent depth map by combining global and local depth information.

1. **Global Depth Estimation:** An initial panoramic depth map is estimated using DA360. This method is chosen for its ability to preserve global structural consistency across the entire panorama. The estimated panoramic depth map is then projected into the six cubemap faces and upsampled to align with the desired resolution and maintain spatial consistency. While globally consistent, DA360's output may lack fine-grained details.
2. **Local Depth Estimation:** Concurrently, for each cubemap face, a high-resolution monocular depth map is predicted using DepthPro. DepthPro excels at capturing rich, fine-grained details within individual views but typically suffers from scale inconsistency across different predictions.
3. **Inverse-Depth Laplacian Pyramid Fusion:** To combine the strengths of both global and local depth estimates, a four-layer Laplacian pyramid is employed. This fusion mechanism specifically operates on the *inverse depth* maps. Inverse depth is used because it typically linearizes depth, making fusion operations more robust.
 - The low-frequency global scale cues derived from the DA360 inverse depth map are extracted.
 - The high-frequency texture details from the DepthPro inverse depth maps are integrated.
 - This multi-scale fusion process combines the accurate global scale provided by DA360 with the sharp local details from DepthPro, resulting in a fused panoramic depth map that is both high-resolution and scale-consistent across all cubemap faces.

**B. Training-Free Depth Injection**
This module modifies an existing feed-forward 3D Gaussian reconstruction framework to incorporate the fused depth maps and guide the Gaussian generation process.

1. **Base Framework Adaptation:** The work extends SHARP, a feed-forward monocular view synthesis method known for high-definition reconstruction and generalization. In standard feed-forward Gaussian frameworks, Gaussian parameters are inferred from the depth output and latent features of an internal depth estimation network (e.g., DPT head).
2. **Depth Map Substitution:** A key modification involves substituting the intermediate depth map predictions generated by SHARP's original depth estimation backbone with the precisely fused panoramic depth maps from the Panoramic Depth Fusion module. This allows the system to leverage the geometrically consistent and detailed depth information generated by the prior step.
3. **Revised Gaussian Initialization:** The corresponding Gaussian initialization scheme within SHARP is revised to directly utilize these external, predefined depth constraints. This steers the feed-forward network to generate 3D Gaussians that are geometrically coherent with the fused depth map, without requiring additional training or fine-tuning of the SHARP model itself for panoramic inputs.
4. **Scene Merging:** For each cubemap face, the modified SHARP pipeline generates a set of 3D Gaussians. After individual generation, Gaussians that lie outside the frustum of their respective cuboid face are removed. Finally, all valid Gaussians from the six processed cubemap faces are merged to construct the complete, globally consistent, panoramic 3D Gaussian scene.

Additionally, **anti-aliasing** is applied during the cubemap projection stage. This step mitigates stair-step artifacts and boundary noise, which is crucial for accurate feed-forward Gaussian initialization and smooth scene reconstruction. The entire system is designed to reconstruct photo-realistic scenes in seconds, providing a rapid solution for generating immersive environments.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research paper presents a series of qualitative evaluations and outlines the metrics used for quantitative assessment, demonstrating the effectiveness of the Genie Sim PanoRecon pipeline.

**A. Qualitative Evaluation**

1. **Novel View Synthesis:** A comparison was conducted with existing panoramic 3D reconstruction methods, PanSplat and PanoSplatt3R, using an identical panoramic input. The results, as depicted in Fig. 3, indicate that Genie Sim PanoRecon produces novel views with sharper geometry and a more photorealistic appearance. This suggests an improved ability to represent scene details and overall visual quality.
2. **Panoramic Depth Comparison:** The efficacy of the panoramic depth fusion strategy was demonstrated by comparing the individual depth sources with the fused output (Fig. 4).
 - DA360 was found to preserve global structural consistency but lacked fine-grained details, resulting in a smoother, less textured depth map.
 - DepthPro provided sharp, highly detailed local depth maps but suffered from scale inconsistencies across different views, making it unsuitable for a unified global reconstruction without correction.
 - The proposed inverse-depth Laplacian fusion method successfully combined these attributes, yielding depth maps that maintained accurate global constraints while also incorporating sharp local boundaries and intricate details.
3. **Effect of Anti-aliasing:** The study confirmed the importance of applying anti-aliasing during cubemap projection (Fig. 5). This step was shown to effectively mitigate stair-step artifacts and boundary noise, which in turn enabled more accurate initialization of the feed-forward Gaussians.
4. **Preservation of Fine-Grained Details:** Through the integration of high-frequency depth cues from DepthPro, the pipeline demonstrated the capability to capture intricate local structures (Fig. 6). This approach resulted in significantly sharper object boundaries and better preservation of small geometric details in the reconstructed 3D scenes, a notable improvement over methods relying solely on global depth priors.
5. **Accurate Spatial Geometry:** The reconstructed 3D Gaussian scenes, derived from single-view panoramas (including those generated by DiT360), exhibited highly coherent spatial structures and accurate geometric layouts (Fig. 7, 8, 9). The method maintained global consistency with minimal structural distortion, rendering the scenes suitable for use as robust background assets in indoor robotic manipulation simulations.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Genie Sim PanoRecon presents a significant contribution to the field of 3D scene generation for robotics simulation by offering a feed-forward pipeline that delivers high-fidelity, low-cost 3D environments from single-view panoramas.

The work addresses several key limitations of existing approaches:
- **Efficiency and Scalability:** It moves beyond the high data acquisition costs and time-consuming per-scene optimization inherent in traditional 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) methods. By employing a feed-forward approach, Genie Sim PanoRecon generates photo-realistic scenes in seconds, without requiring per-scene training. This drastically reduces the production cost and time associated with creating simulation environments, making it suitable for large-scale data generation needs in embodied intelligence.
- **Geometric Consistency in Panoramic Reconstruction:** A critical innovation is the panoramic consistent depth-guided feed-forward Gaussian-splatting pipeline. This design effectively mitigates the geometric inconsistencies, structural discontinuities, and scale drifting that typically arise when processing independent cubemap faces from a panorama. The depth-aware fusion strategy and training-free depth injection module ensure a globally coherent 3D scene, which is crucial for applications like robot navigation and manipulation that demand accurate spatial structures.
- **High Fidelity:** The fusion of global structural depth from DA360 with high-resolution local depth details from DepthPro, combined with the capabilities of SHARP, enables the reconstruction of scenes that maintain both accurate overall geometry and fine-grained visual details. This results in sharper geometry and more photorealistic appearances in novel views compared to prior panorama-specific methods.
- **Practical Integration:** The pipeline has been integrated into Genie Sim, an LLM-driven simulation platform. This integration directly supports the generation of scalable backgrounds for manipulation tasks, positioning Genie Sim PanoRecon as a practical tool for advancing robotic policy training and synthetic data generation within a broader embodied AI ecosystem.
- **Targeted Application:** The focus on indoor desktop-scale scenes with moderate parallax directly serves the immediate needs of robotic manipulation simulation, providing a low-cost and scalable solution for creating essential 3D background assets.

**Potential Impact:**
- **Accelerated Robotics Research:** By significantly lowering the cost and time required to generate high-quality 3D simulation environments, Genie Sim PanoRecon can accelerate research and development in robotics, particularly in areas like reinforcement learning, sim-to-real transfer, and synthetic data augmentation.
- **Enhanced Synthetic Data Generation:** The ability to rapidly convert generated or captured panoramas into explorable, high-fidelity 3D Gaussian scenes facilitates the large-scale construction of data infrastructure for embodied intelligence.
- **Broader Accessibility:** The method offers a pathway for researchers and developers without extensive 3D scanning or modeling resources to create complex and realistic simulation settings from readily available single-view panoramas.

**Limitations and Future Work:**
The authors acknowledge limitations, including the inherent inability of pixel-aligned feed-forward 3D Gaussian methods to complete unseen regions in novel views. The current scope is also confined to approximately 1m³ manipulation spaces with limited parallax, indicating that outdoor or larger-scale reconstruction would require further development. Future work includes rigorous measurement and validation for deployment to formats like USDZ and Isaac Sim to demonstrate end-to-end performance. Despite these limitations, the current work establishes a strong cost-performance point for immersive scene generation for specific robotic applications.
