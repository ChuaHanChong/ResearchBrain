# Audio Array-Based 3D UAV Trajectory Estimation with LiDAR Pseudo-Labeling

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Nanyang Technological University developed a self-supervised framework for 3D UAV trajectory estimation that leverages audio array data, using LiDAR to generate pseudo-labels for training. Their system achieved an average position error of 0.48 meters, maintaining performance in both ideal and dark environments, which is an improvement over visual and combined methods.

## Problem

- Current UAV tracking methods, primarily visual or RADAR/LiDAR, face limitations in terms of visibility, field of view, and cost-effectiveness for widespread deployment.
- The increasing use of UAVs presents challenges in public safety, privacy, and unauthorized airspace incursions, necessitating robust 3D trajectory estimation.
- Developing audio-based UAV tracking has been hindered by the difficulty and expense of collecting and annotating ground truth data in real-world scenarios.

## Method

- A self-supervised Teacher-Student Network framework is introduced, where a Teacher Network generates 3D pseudo-labels from LiDAR data.
- The Student Network, an Audio Perception Network, processes microphone array data by extracting time difference of arrival (TDOA) and intensity features from mel-spectrograms.
- Gaussian Process (GP) modeling is applied post-estimation to refine and smooth the predicted 3D UAV trajectories, enhancing spatiotemporal consistency.

## Results

- The framework achieved an average position error of **0.48** meters, surpassing established visual, audio, and multi-modal methods in 3D UAV trajectory estimation.
- Performance remained consistent in dark environments (0.48m APE), showcasing robustness against lighting conditions where visual systems severely degraded (e.g., VisualNet at 11.45m APE).
- The method exhibits high computational efficiency with **0.0065** GLOPs, making it suitable for practical deployment.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - LiDAR data can be effectively leveraged as a pseudo-ground truth source to train audio-based perception networks in a self-supervised manner.
> - An audio array, combined with specific convolutional feature extraction (TDOA and intensity), can accurately estimate 3D UAV trajectories across diverse environmental conditions.
> - Gaussian Process modeling significantly enhances the smoothness and precision of audio-derived 3D trajectory estimations.
