# Dynamics-Aligned Latent Imagination in Contextual World Models for Zero-Shot Generalization

> [!summary] Summary
> The Dynamics-Aligned Latent Imagination (DALI) framework extends model-based reinforcement learning to achieve zero-shot generalization in latent contextual environments by self-supervised inference of dynamics-aligned context representations. It demonstrated performance increases of up to 96.4% over context-unaware baselines in complex extrapolation tasks, encoding physically consistent environmental factors.

## Problem

- Reinforcement learning agents struggle with zero-shot generalization across diverse, unseen environmental conditions, particularly when critical context parameters are latent and unobservable.
- Existing contextual RL methods often rely on explicit context conditioning, which is impractical or impossible in real-world scenarios where ground-truth context is unavailable.
- Standard model-based RL architectures, such as DreamerV3, suffer from information bottlenecks in their recurrent states, limiting their ability to explicitly disentangle and adapt to diverse latent contexts efficiently.

## Method

- **DALI** extends ==DreamerV3== by introducing a self-supervised context encoder that infers dynamics-aligned latent context representations from short interaction histories.
- The context encoder is optimized using a forward dynamics loss to predict future observations and a cross-modal regularization loss to align with DreamerV3's stochastic state.
- Inferred latent contexts are integrated into the DreamerV3 world model and policy, either shallowly (DALI-S) or deeply (DALI-D), to explicitly condition dynamics and behavior for zero-shot generalization.

## Results

- DALI-S-χ achieved up to +**96.4%** performance gain over the context-unaware Dreamer-DR baseline in the Ball-in-Cup extrapolation task (Pixel modality, IQM **0.2730**).
- In Ball-in-Cup extrapolation, DALI-S-χ outperformed ground-truth context-aware baselines (cRSSM-S/D) by **33.8%** to **63.9%** in the Featurized modality (IQM **0.3720**).
- Counterfactual analysis demonstrated that perturbations to learned latent context dimensions resulted in physically plausible changes in imagined trajectories, such as altered gravity-string dynamics in Ball-in-Cup and modified actuator strength for locomotion in Walker Walk.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The recurrent state in standard world models like DreamerV3 acts as an information bottleneck, making efficient and rapid context inference challenging and requiring long interaction histories.
> - DALI's dedicated context encoder enables efficient context identifiability from short temporal windows, theoretically demonstrating a sample complexity gain of O(T/K) compared to standard approaches.
> - The learned latent context representations in DALI are interpretable and capture physically consistent mechanistic factors of the environment, enabling physically plausible counterfactual predictions.
