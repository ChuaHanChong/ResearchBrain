# TransferTraj: A Vehicle Trajectory Learning Model for Region and Task Transferability

> [!summary] Summary
> TransferTraj introduces a vehicle GPS trajectory learning model designed for strong region and task transferability, leveraging relative spatial encoding and context-aware expert networks. The model demonstrates superior performance in trajectory prediction, recovery, and travel time estimation across diverse geographical areas without requiring task-specific fine-tuning.

## Problem

- Conventional vehicle trajectory learning models are typically trained and maintained separately for each specific region and task, leading to high computational costs and significant data requirements.
- Existing transferable trajectory models primarily learn generalized embeddings but still require additional prediction modules and fine-tuning for downstream tasks, hindering true zero-shot task transfer.
- Transferring models across different geographical regions is challenging due to unique spatial features, road network topologies, and POI distributions, which are not consistently represented by existing standardization methods.

## Method

- A ==Region-Transferable Trajectory Encoder== (RTTE) processes trajectory points using relative spatial, temporal, POI, and road network modalities, fostering region-independent feature learning.
- RTTE incorporates a Trajectory Relative Information Extraction (TRIE) block for modeling relationships based on relative spatial distances and a Spatial Context Mixture-of-Experts (SC-MoE) block for context-aware pattern learning.
- A task-transferable input-output scheme unifies diverse generative tasks through a masking and recovery paradigm, where the model is pre-trained to reconstruct masked modalities or entire trajectory points.

## Results

- TransferTraj, without fine-tuning, outperformed existing state-of-the-art baselines by an average of **20.18%** for Trajectory Prediction, **17.87%** for Trajectory Recovery, and **7.94%** for Origin-Destination Travel Time Estimation.
- In zero-shot region transfer, the model achieved an average improvement of **83.70%** for Trajectory Prediction and **10.88%** for OD Travel Time Estimation when trained on one region and directly applied to another.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the Trajectory Relative Information Extraction (TRIE) block, Spatial Context Mixture-of-Experts (SC-MoE) block, and the inclusion of POI/road network modalities are critical for the model's performance and transferability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Relative spatial encoding, combined with region-independent semantic representations for POIs and road networks, enables the model to learn movement patterns that generalize across diverse geographical areas.
> - The Spatial Context Mixture-of-Experts (SC-MoE) dynamically adapts to varying local spatial contexts, allowing the model to capture diverse movement patterns and share analogous patterns across regions.
> - A unified input-output scheme with modality and trajectory point masking allows the model to be pre-trained for comprehensive spatio-temporal understanding, enabling direct application to various generative tasks without task-specific fine-tuning.
