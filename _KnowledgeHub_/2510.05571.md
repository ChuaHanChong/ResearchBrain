# Presenting a Paper is an Art: Self-Improvement Aesthetic Agents for Academic Presentations

> [!summary] Summary
> EvoPresent introduces a self-improving multi-agent framework that automatically generates academic presentations, including slides, videos, and scripts, by integrating narrative coherence with aesthetic-aware design. The system, leveraging a specialized multi-task reinforcement learning model called PresAesth, consistently outperforms existing automated methods in both content and visual quality, with PresAesth achieving an 18% lower Mean Absolute Error in aesthetic scoring compared to closed-source models.

## Problem

- Existing automated methods for academic presentation generation often produce presentations lacking narrative coherence and compelling storytelling.
- Current tools suffer from insufficient aesthetic quality and design flexibility, typically relying on fixed templates rather than nuanced visual design.
- Automated systems generally lack self-improvement mechanisms, requiring extensive manual intervention for iterative refinement and aesthetic correction.

## Method

- **EvoPresent** is a self-improvement ==multi-agent framework==, consisting of Storyline, Scholar, Design, and Checker agents, that iteratively refines presentation drafts.
- PresAesth, a multi-task reinforcement learning model, provides reliable aesthetic scoring, defect identification, and comparative feedback specifically for academic presentation visuals.
- A comprehensive EvoPresent Benchmark was developed, including 650 top-tier AI conference papers for generation quality and 2,000 human-annotated slide pairs for aesthetic awareness evaluation.

## Results

- EvoPresent consistently outperforms existing methods in presentation generation quality, reducing perplexity by approximately **17%** compared to GPT-4o alone and showing superior fine-grained evaluation scores.
- PresAesth demonstrates superior aesthetic awareness across all three tasks (scoring, defect adjustment, comparison), achieving an **18%** lower MAE for scoring compared to closed-source models and **87.8%** accuracy in comparison tasks.
- Qualitative comparisons and human preference evaluations confirm EvoPresent generates presentations that align closely with human-designed references and surpass other automated methods in overall quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality, domain-specific feedback, like that from PresAesth, is essential for efficient self-improvement in agentic AI systems, where initial performance does not guarantee strong self-correction abilities.
> - Multi-task reinforcement learning (GRPO) effectively enhances aesthetic awareness model performance and generalization, outperforming single-task training or supervised fine-tuning.
> - An inherent trade-off exists between optimizing visual design and ensuring content construction without redundancies, which the iterative multi-agent framework helps mitigate.
