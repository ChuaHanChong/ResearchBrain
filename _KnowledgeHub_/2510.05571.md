# Presenting a Paper is an Art: Self-Improvement Aesthetic Agents for Academic Presentations

> [!summary] Summary
> EvoPresent introduces a self-improving multi-agent framework that automatically generates academic presentations, including slides, videos, and scripts, by integrating narrative coherence with aesthetic-aware design. The system, leveraging a specialized multi-task reinforcement learning model called PresAesth, consistently outperforms existing automated methods in both content and visual quality, with PresAesth achieving an 18% lower Mean Absolute Error in aesthetic scoring compared to closed-source models.

## Problem

- Existing automated methods for academic presentation generation often produce presentations lacking narrative coherence and compelling storytelling.
- Current tools suffer from insufficient aesthetic quality and design flexibility, typically relying on fixed templates rather than nuanced visual design.
- Automated systems generally lack self-improvement mechanisms, requiring extensive manual intervention for iterative refinement and aesthetic correction.

## Method

- **EvoPresent** is a self-improvement ==multi-agent framework==, consisting of Storyline, Scholar, Design, and Checker agents, that iteratively refines presentation drafts.
- PresAesth, a multi-task reinforcement learning model, provides reliable aesthetic scoring, defect identification, and comparative feedback specifically for academic presentation visuals.
- A comprehensive EvoPresent Benchmark was developed, including 650 top-tier AI conference papers for generation quality and 2,000 human-annotated slide pairs for aesthetic awareness evaluation.

## Results

- EvoPresent consistently outperforms existing methods in presentation generation quality, reducing perplexity by approximately **17%** compared to GPT-4o alone and showing superior fine-grained evaluation scores.
- PresAesth demonstrates superior aesthetic awareness across all three tasks (scoring, defect adjustment, comparison), achieving an **18%** lower MAE for scoring compared to closed-source models and **87.8%** accuracy in comparison tasks.
- Qualitative comparisons and human preference evaluations confirm EvoPresent generates presentations that align closely with human-designed references and surpass other automated methods in overall quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality, domain-specific feedback, like that from PresAesth, is essential for efficient self-improvement in agentic AI systems, where initial performance does not guarantee strong self-correction abilities.
> - Multi-task reinforcement learning (GRPO) effectively enhances aesthetic awareness model performance and generalization, outperforming single-task training or supervised fine-tuning.
> - An inherent trade-off exists between optimizing visual design and ensuring content construction without redundancies, which the iterative multi-agent framework helps mitigate.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The paper addresses a significant and growing challenge in scholarly communication: efficiently and effectively disseminating academic research in an increasingly digital and visually-driven landscape. While the promotion of academic papers has become crucial for research visibility, existing automated methods for generating presentations (e.g., paper-to-slide, text-to-video) suffer from critical limitations. These include:

- **Limited Storytelling and Narrative Coherence:** Many approaches rely on direct content extraction, leading to presentations that lack a compelling, coherent narrative.
- **Insufficient Aesthetic Quality and Design Flexibility:** Existing systems often use fixed templates, resulting in visually unengaging or poorly designed slides and videos. Visual aesthetic evaluation is particularly underdeveloped for academic content, with current methods lacking dedicated metrics and robust aesthetic awareness.
- **Absence of Self-Improvement Mechanisms:** Most automated generation tools require significant manual intervention for refinement, as they lack the ability to iteratively assess and correct their own outputs.

This work positions itself directly against these shortcomings. Previous efforts, such as PPTAgent (Zheng et al., 2025) and PresentAgent (Shi et al., 2025), are noted for their reliance on direct content extraction and fixed templates, leading to limited narrative coherence and design flexibility. Paper2Poster (Pang et al., 2025) is cited for its use of VLM-based checkers, but these are deemed insufficient due to limited aesthetic and design perception. Furthermore, the broader field of aesthetic evaluation, especially for complex academic visuals, is characterized by a significant gap between model capabilities and human perception (Zhou et al., 2024b; Hwang et al., 2025).

EvoPresent aims to push the boundaries of AI-driven content generation by introducing a **self-improvement agent framework** that integrates narrative coherence, aesthetic-aware design, and realistic presentation delivery. It moves beyond static generation by embedding an iterative **draft–feedback–refinement loop**, a concept central to the development of more autonomous and intelligent AI systems. The introduction of **PresAesth**, a specialized multi-task reinforcement learning model for aesthetic evaluation, addresses the critical need for reliable aesthetic assessment in academic contexts, a gap not adequately filled by general visual language models (VLMs). By also providing the **EvoPresent Benchmark**, the authors contribute a much-needed comprehensive evaluation framework that supports systematic assessment of both generation quality and aesthetic awareness, fostering future research in this specific domain. In essence, this paper represents a significant step towards creating truly intelligent agents capable of not just *generating* content, but *refining* it to human-comparable standards of quality and engagement.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of this research is to develop an advanced, self-improving AI framework, named **EvoPresent**, capable of generating high-quality, engaging academic presentations (including slides, videos, and scripts) with minimal human intervention. This framework is designed to overcome the significant limitations of existing automated methods in academic content creation.

The core motivations driving this work are multifaceted:

1. **Enhance Research Visibility and Dissemination:** In an increasingly competitive academic landscape, effective promotion of research is vital. Engaging presentations are a key tool for this, but creating them is time-consuming. Automating this process while ensuring high quality can significantly boost research visibility.
2. **Address Limitations of Existing Generation Methods:** Current automated tools for paper-to-presentation conversion suffer from:
 - **Limited Storytelling:** They often perform direct content extraction, leading to fragmented narratives that fail to convey the paper's core message coherently.
 - **Insufficient Aesthetic Quality:** Reliance on fixed templates results in visually unappealing or generic designs, lacking the nuance and impact of human-designed presentations.
 - **Lack of Self-Adjustment:** Systems are typically static and require extensive manual revisions, negating the benefits of automation. The authors pinpoint this as a fundamental issue: "there is no way to improve it when you cannot evaluate it right."
3. **Advance Aesthetic Evaluation for Academic Content:** General aesthetic evaluation models (e.g., VLMs-as-judge) are often unreliable and inconsistent when applied to the specific, nuanced, and subjective domain of academic presentation aesthetics. There is a clear need for dedicated, comprehensive models that can reliably score, identify defects, and compare academic visual designs.
4. **Establish a Robust Evaluation Standard:** The lack of a comprehensive benchmark for assessing both the generation quality and aesthetic awareness of academic presentations hinders systematic progress. Creating such a benchmark is essential for fair comparison and meaningful advancement in the field.

To achieve these objectives, the research aims to:
- Introduce **EvoPresent**, a self-improvement agent framework that unifies coherent narratives, aesthetic-aware designs, and realistic presentation delivery through virtual characters.
- Develop **PresAesth**, a multi-task reinforcement learning (RL) aesthetic model designed for reliable aesthetic scoring, defect adjustment, and comparative feedback, specifically tailored for academic presentations and capable of learning effectively even from limited aesthetic training data.
- Propose **EvoPresent Benchmark**, a comprehensive benchmark comprising "Presentation Generation Quality" (based on 650 top-tier AI conference papers with multimodal resources) and "Aesthetic Awareness" (consisting of 2,000 slide pairs with varying aesthetic levels).

### 3. Methodology and Approach

EvoPresent adopts a sophisticated **self-improvement multi-agent framework** built around an iterative **draft–feedback–refinement loop**. This framework is underpinned by a novel multi-task aesthetic awareness model and evaluated using a custom-designed benchmark.

#### 3.1. EvoPresent Framework Workflow

The framework comprises four specialized agents that work sequentially and iteratively:

1. **Storyline Agent:** This initial agent is responsible for extracting and structuring the core content of a given academic paper. It uses tools like Marker (Paruchuri, 2025) to extract text and visuals, then constructs a thematic story framework, reorganizes text into coherent chapters, associates relevant visuals, and finally generates a complete presentation script.
2. **Scholar Agent:** This agent enhances the academic content and visual quality of the presentation. It identifies information gaps in the storyline and enriches them using external knowledge retrieval tools (e.g., ArXiv Metadata and Citation Parser - MCP (Blazick, 2025)). It also improves visual impact by generating relevant charts or diagrams using advanced models like GPT-4o (OpenAI, 2024) and Qwen-Image (Wu et al., 2025), ensuring visuals align with the text.
3. **Design Agent:** Once the storyline is defined, this agent focuses on visual presentation. It consists of two components:
 - **Layout Planner:** Generates an initial layout for each slide, ensuring element balance and correct aspect ratios based on text and image sizes. This step is crucial for stability before styling.
 - **Style Render:** Renders the overall visual design by selecting a suitable style from a predefined Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) library, applying visual elements like colors, fonts, and backgrounds while preserving layout flexibility. It also adjusts font sizes for hierarchy and adds dynamic effects for engagement. The use of HTML over PPTX provides greater flexibility and aesthetic control.
4. **Checker Agent:** This is the core of the self-improvement mechanism, integrated with the PresAesth model (described below). It operates in an iterative loop (Algorithm 1):
 - Evaluates the aesthetic score of the current slide using PresAesth.
 - If the score meets a predefined threshold, it terminates and generates the final presentation video.
 - Otherwise, it provides targeted feedback to the Layout Planner for refinement.
 - It also compares scores across iterations, reverting to better versions if quality degrades, ensuring stability.
 - Finally, finalized slides are paired with narration audio (generated by a text-to-speech system like OpenAI TTS), temporally aligned, and composed into a video with optional transitions.

#### 3.2. PresAesth: Multi-Task Aesthetic Awareness Model

PresAesth is a crucial component that provides reliable aesthetic perception and reasoning for the EvoPresent framework. It is designed to perform three core tasks:

1. **Scoring:** Evaluates the absolute aesthetic quality of a single slide image on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-10).
2. **Defect Adjustment:** Identifies specific design deficiencies (categorized into Composition & Layout, Typography, and Imagery & Visualizations) in a slide and provides concrete feedback for improvement.
3. **Comparison:** Given a baseline slide and two proposed revisions, it identifies which revision offers a superior improvement.

**Training PresAesth:**
- **Method:** Multi-task Reinforcement Learning (RL) using **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)** (Shao et al., 2024). GRPO is chosen for its efficiency in leveraging verified reward signals to capture subjective aesthetic evaluation, allowing the model to learn from comparative assessments rather than absolute labels, which are often limited.
- **Base Model:** Qwen-2.5-VL-7B (Bai et al., 2025) serves as the foundation.
- **Reward Function:** A comprehensive reward function guides training:
 - **Format Reward:** +1 if the model's output adheres to a strict format (reasoning within `<think>` tags, conclusion within `<answer>` tags), 0 otherwise, encouraging structured reasoning.
 - **Accuracy Reward (r_acc):** Task-dependent. For Comparison, it's 1 if the choice is correct, 0 otherwise. For Adjustment, it's 1 if the F1-score of identified deficiencies exceeds a threshold (α). For Scoring, it's 1 if the absolute error is within a tolerance (ζ).

#### 3.3. EvoPresent Benchmark

To systematically evaluate the framework and model, the authors introduce a comprehensive benchmark with two main components:

1. **Presentation Generation Quality:**
 - **Dataset:** 650 top-tier AI conference papers (ICLR, NeurIPS, etc.) from various domains, each with associated human-annotated slides, videos, and scripts. This offers a diverse and realistic set of academic resources.
 - **Evaluation Metrics:**
 - **Global Evaluation:** Objective metrics for content (Perplexity, ROUGE-L) and design (Layout Balance, Aesthetic Scores from PresAesth).
 - **Fine-Grained Evaluation:** A VLM-as-judge assesses presentations on a 1-5 scale across eight dimensions: Fidelity, Clarity, Narrative, Engagement (content); Elements, Layout, Hierarchy, Color (visual design).
2. **Aesthetic Awareness:**
 - **Dataset:** 2,000 slide pairs created by applying controlled perturbations (e.g., layout changes, style variations) to original slides, generating "poor," "base," and "good" variants. Each slide is human-annotated with quality ratings and defect labels.
 - **Evaluation Metrics:** Mean Absolute Error (MAE) for scoring, F1-score for defect adjustment, and Accuracy for comparison. This dataset supports joint training and evaluation of aesthetic perception and reasoning.

This holistic approach, from a multi-agent generation framework to a specialized aesthetic model and a robust evaluation benchmark, ensures a comprehensive and systematic investigation into the automated generation of high-quality academic presentations.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The extensive experiments conducted by the authors yielded several key findings that underscore the effectiveness and unique contributions of EvoPresent:

1. **Superior Presentation Generation Quality:**
 - EvoPresent consistently **outperforms existing end-to-end and multi-agent methods** across both content and visual design metrics. For instance, EvoPresent-4o reduced perplexity by approximately 17% compared to GPT-4o alone and showed significant improvements in fine-grained evaluation dimensions.
 - **Trade-off between Visual Design and Content Construction:** The results revealed an inherent trade-off, where models like Deepseek-R1 and GPT-5 might produce better visual quality but introduce content redundancies, leading to higher perplexity. EvoPresent, with its iterative refinement, mitigates this.
 - **Impact of Base Model Capability:** Models with stronger underlying capabilities, such as Claude-4-Sonnet, integrated into EvoPresent, performed exceptionally well in aesthetics, particularly in HTML rendering, demonstrating their ability to refine visual elements with greater precision.
 - **Content vs. Aesthetics:** Differences in content quality among most models were relatively small, highlighting that aesthetic and design capabilities are the primary differentiators and bottlenecks.

2. **Exceptional Aesthetic Awareness by PresAesth:**
 - **Consistently Superior Performance:** PresAesth achieved the best performance across all three aesthetic tasks (scoring, defect adjustment, comparison). Its MAE for scoring was, on average, 18% lower than closed-source models like GPT-4o and Claude-4-Sonnet, indicating a closer alignment with human judgments.
 - **Reliable Defect Detection:** While some models showed strength in individual defect categories, PresAesth demonstrated stable and superior overall performance in defect adjustment, reflecting more reliable detection capabilities.
 - **High Comparison Accuracy:** PresAesth achieved an impressive 87.8% accuracy in the comparison task, about 40% higher than other models, further providing structured reasoning that enhances feedback explainability.
 - **Benefit of Multi-task RL:** These results demonstrate that multi-task RL training fosters stronger generalization and significantly enhances performance in aesthetic awareness tasks, especially when compared to single-task RL or multi-task SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning).

3. **Enhanced Presentation Experience:**
 - **Video Performance:** Evaluated by Qwen-Omni-7B (simulating human viewing), EvoPresent consistently achieved the best scores across dimensions like comprehension difficulty, narrative coherence, temporal fluency, and conciseness.
 - **Content Delivery:** Human-like evaluation (using Qwen2.5-VL-32B and GPT-4o to answer questions based on slides/scripts) showed EvoPresent attaining higher accuracy in both verbatim and explanatory questions, effectively conveying core paper knowledge.

4. **Qualitative Advantages and Human Preference:**
 - Qualitative comparisons illustrated that EvoPresent addressed common design deficiencies (e.g., rigid layouts, boundary errors, poor whitespace, blurred text, text-heavy designs) prevalent in other methods, producing visuals most closely aligned with human-designed references.
 - Human preference evaluations with volunteers confirmed that EvoPresent outperformed other automated methods in generation quality and was competitive with human-generated presentations.

5. **Ablation Study Insights:**
 - **Importance of Core Agents:** The ablation study confirmed the critical role of each agent: the Scholar Agent for content coverage and narrative depth, the Design Agent for layout balance and readability, and the Checker Agent (with PresAesth) for detail alignment, visual consistency, and overall aesthetic improvement. Removing any agent significantly degraded performance.
 - **Multi-Task Generalizability:** Training with GRPO on individual tasks resulted in performance drops, indicating that aesthetic awareness tasks are interconnected. Multi-task GRPO significantly outperformed multi-task SFT, highlighting its ability to actively perceive aesthetics and generate targeted, actionable feedback.
 - **Effectiveness of Self-Improvement:** Using PresAesth as the checker module dramatically accelerated the self-improvement process, allowing the agent to improve aesthetic scores from 3.2 to over 8.0 within three iterations, whereas other checkers required more iterations for lower final scores. This finding emphasizes that **high-quality feedback is essential for agent self-improvement**, and an agent's initial high performance does not necessarily correlate with its self-correction ability.

In summary, the findings robustly support EvoPresent's capability to generate high-quality, aesthetically pleasing, and narratively coherent academic presentations through its innovative self-improvement framework and specialized aesthetic model.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The EvoPresent framework represents a significant advancement in the field of automated content generation, particularly within the domain of scholarly communication. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

1. **Paradigm Shift in Academic Presentation Generation:** EvoPresent introduces the first self-improvement multi-agent framework for generating realistic academic presentations. This moves beyond static, template-driven or direct-extraction methods to an iterative, intelligent system capable of learning and refining its outputs. This is a crucial step towards truly autonomous AI agents in content creation.
2. **Enhanced Research Dissemination and Visibility:** By automating the creation of high-quality, engaging presentations—comparable to human-designed ones—EvoPresent can dramatically reduce the time and effort researchers spend on preparing talks. This allows scientists to focus more on their core research while ensuring their findings are communicated effectively and reach a broader audience, thereby boosting research visibility and impact.
3. **Novel Aesthetic Awareness Capabilities:** The development of PresAesth, a multi-task reinforcement learning model for aesthetic evaluation, is a key technical contribution. It addresses a long-standing challenge in AI: reliably perceiving and evaluating complex, subjective aesthetics, especially in specialized domains like academic slides. Its ability to provide detailed defect adjustments and comparative feedback, even with limited data, sets a new standard for AI-driven visual design critique.
4. **Robust Evaluation Standard for Future Research:** The introduction of the comprehensive EvoPresent Benchmark, covering both presentation generation quality (multimodal content and design) and aesthetic awareness tasks, provides a much-needed standardized tool for the community. This benchmark will facilitate rigorous evaluation, enable fair comparisons between different methods, and accelerate future research in intelligent content generation and aesthetic AI.
5. **Insights into Agent Self-Improvement:** The findings from the ablation studies offer critical insights into the dynamics of agentic AI. The demonstration that high-quality feedback is paramount for efficient self-improvement, and that initial performance doesn't guarantee strong self-correction, has implications for designing more effective and robust autonomous agents across various domains.
6. **Broader Applicability:** While focused on academic presentations, the underlying principles of EvoPresent—iterative self-improvement guided by domain-specific aesthetic intelligence—could be extended to other forms of visual communication and content creation, such as marketing materials, educational modules, or public speaking aids.

In conclusion, EvoPresent not only provides a practical solution to a pressing need in academia but also makes significant theoretical and empirical contributions to the fields of multimodal AI, agentic systems, and computational aesthetics. Its potential to transform how scientific knowledge is shared and consumed is substantial.
