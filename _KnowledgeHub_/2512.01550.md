# NavForesee: A Unified Vision-Language World Model for Hierarchical Planning and Dual-Horizon Navigation Prediction

> [!summary] Summary
> NavForesee, developed by Amap, Alibaba Group, introduces a unified Vision-Language Model that integrates hierarchical language planning with dual-horizon predictive world modeling for embodied navigation. The model achieved a 66.2% Success Rate and 78.4% Oracle Success Rate on the R2R-CE Val-Unseen benchmark, demonstrating enhanced long-term planning and environmental foresight.

## Problem

- Embodied navigation agents struggle with long-horizon tasks due to limited context windows and planning capabilities, frequently leading to disorientation.
- Existing navigation models are predominantly reactive, lacking the predictive foresight to anticipate future environmental states or visual milestones.
- A disconnection exists between VLM-centric planning (prone to semantic hallucinations) and world model-based prediction (prone to semantic drift) in current approaches.

## Method

- **NavForesee** is a unified VLM (based on Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct) combining VLM-driven ==hierarchical language planning== and world model-based ==dual-horizon== visual prediction.
- A specialized dataset was created using Gemini 2.5 Pro to train hierarchical VLM planning, enabling instruction decomposition into sub-goals and identification of keyframe visual milestones.
- The architecture integrates learnable 'dream queries' for both short-term (fixed 5-step horizon) and long-term (adaptive, milestone-based) depth and semantic feature prediction.

## Results

- NavForesee achieved a **66.2%** Success Rate (SR) and **78.4%** Oracle Success Rate (OSR) on R2R-CE Val-Unseen, representing improvements of **1.1%** SR and **10.9%** OSR over previous methods.
- Ablation studies confirmed that hierarchical VLM planning, long-term prediction, and short-term prediction modules each contribute significantly to performance, with VLM planning causing the largest drop (SR to **48.8%**) when removed.
- The model demonstrated strong predictive capabilities, outperforming identity baselines in predicting environmental depth up to T+2 and high-level semantics up to T+3 (Cosine Similarity **0.62**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Unifying VLM-driven hierarchical planning with a world model's dual-horizon prediction creates a robust perception-planning/prediction-action loop, mitigating issues like semantic hallucinations and drift.
> - Explicit decomposition of long instructions into sub-goals and tracking mission progress through visual milestones significantly improves an agent's ability to maintain long-term task understanding.
> - Anticipating both immediate environmental dynamics (short-term) and key visual features of future milestones (long-term) is crucial for proactive decision-making and coherent, goal-oriented navigation.
