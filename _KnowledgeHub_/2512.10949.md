# Are We Ready for RL in Text-to-3D Generation? A Progressive Investigation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed AR3D-R1, the first RL-enhanced 3D autoregressive model, employing a systematic investigation of reward designs and algorithms with a novel hierarchical RL paradigm called Hi-GRPO. This approach generated 3D objects with improved geometric consistency, texture quality, and prompt alignment, demonstrating superior performance on both standard and a new reasoning-focused benchmark, MME-3DR.

## Problem

- Reinforcement Learning (RL) has been underexplored for text-to-3D generation, despite its success in Large Language Models (LLMs) and 2D image generation.
- Creating 3D content presents unique challenges, including high spatial complexity, strict requirements for global geometric consistency, and the need for fine-grained local textures.
- Existing RL techniques from 2D generation or LLMs cannot be directly adapted due to the high sensitivity of 3D generation to reward functions and algorithmic choices.

## Method

- A systematic investigation was conducted into various ==reward designs== (e.g., human preference, prompt alignment, 3D consistency) and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) variants for text-to-3D autoregressive generation.
- A new benchmark, MME-3DR, was developed to evaluate implicit reasoning abilities of 3D generative models, focusing on challenging categories like complex geometry and mechanical affordances.
- Hi-GRPO, a hierarchical RL paradigm, was proposed to optimize 3D generation from coarse global shapes to fine-grained local textures using step-specific reward ensembles, culminating in the AR3D-R1 model.

## Results

- The proposed AR3D-R1 model achieved superior quantitative performance, with a **29.3** CLIP score on Toys4K, outperforming current state-of-the-art text-to-3D models like Trellis (**26.8**) and ShapeLLM-Omni (**22.7**).
- RL training significantly improved performance across all five challenging categories of the MME-3DR benchmark, demonstrating enhanced implicit reasoning capabilities (e.g., 5-6 point overall CLIP score improvement for ShapeLLM-Omni).
- AR3D-R1 produced high-quality meshes with better geometry consistency, improved texture quality, and stronger prompt alignment, exhibiting a clear coarse-to-fine progression during inference.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Human preference rewards (e.g., HPS v2.1) are crucial for improving overall 3D generation quality, and general Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) enhance multi-view 3D consistency.
> - Token-level averaging in the RL loss computation is more beneficial for 3D autoregressive generation than sequence-level operations, with dynamic sampling and decoupled clipping improving training stability.
> - Current text-to-3D models consistently struggle with implicit reasoning tasks, a limitation effectively highlighted and measured by the newly introduced MME-3DR benchmark.
> - A hierarchical RL paradigm, Hi-GRPO, which progresses from coarse shape generation to fine texture refinement with tailored reward ensembles, aligns with human 3D perception and yields effective control over the generation process.
