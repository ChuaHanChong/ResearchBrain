# Generative to Agentic AI: Survey, Conceptualization, and Challenges

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey examines the evolution from generative AI to agentic AI, mapping key differences in capabilities like reasoning, memory, and tool usage while highlighting technical challenges and providing a framework for understanding AI agents' progression toward greater autonomy and environmental interaction.

## Problem

- Lack of clear understanding of how agentic AI differs from generative AI
- Need for unified conceptual framework to guide research and development in agentic AI systems
- Growing autonomy in AI agents raises unaddressed ethical and societal risks, including control, value alignment, and existential concerns as systems approach human-level intelligence

## Method

- Systematic survey and analysis of existing literature on both ==generative AI== and ==agentic AI==
- Development of clear ==conceptual framework== distinguishing key capabilities and limitations
- Identification of ==evolutionary pathway== from generative to agentic systems

## Results

- Provides structured comparison of generative vs agentic AI capabilities
- Identifies key technical challenges requiring further research and outlines a research agenda for advancing toward more capable AI agents
- Maps potential risks and limitations of current approaches

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Agentic AI builds upon generative AI by adding reasoning, memory, and environmental interaction capabilities
> - Progress toward agentic AI requires addressing challenges in error accumulation, interpretability, and safety
> - Multi-agent systems and agent specification are crucial for effective agentic AI development

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This paper addresses a critical and rapidly evolving area within artificial intelligence: the transition from generative AI (GenAI) to agentic AI. This work fits into the research landscape by:

- **Bridging the Gap in Understanding:** It acknowledges that while GenAI has gained widespread attention, the distinction between GenAI and Agentic AI remains less clear to many. The paper aims to address this gap by providing a comprehensive survey and conceptualization of the two paradigms.
- **Mapping the Evolution:** It contextualizes Agentic AI not as a sudden revolution, but as an evolutionary step building upon earlier GenAI systems. This helps researchers understand the historical progression and key milestones in the field.
- **Defining Agentic AI:** The paper provides a clear definition of Agentic AI, drawing on both academic and industry perspectives. This is important because a clear definition is needed to establish a shared understanding of the concept and facilitate further research.
- **Highlighting Novel Aspects:** It delves into the novel capabilities of Agentic AI, including reasoning, memory, tool usage, and interaction with the environment. This helps researchers identify areas where Agentic AI offers significant advantages over GenAI.
- **Identifying Challenges and Risks:** The paper also acknowledges the challenges and potential risks associated with Agentic AI, such as error accumulation, interpretability issues, safety concerns, and human alignment problems. This encourages researchers to address these challenges proactively.
- **Future research agenda:** By addressing the gaps between generative AI and Agentic AI, this paper proposes a future research agenda to guide scientists interested in expanding AI.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives of this research paper are:

- **To provide a clear and concise comparison of GenAI and Agentic AI.** This includes defining the key characteristics of each paradigm, highlighting how Agentic AI addresses the limitations of GenAI, and outlining the steps in the evolution of GenAI toward Agentic AI.
- **To offer a deep dive into the novel aspects of Agentic AI.** This includes exploring the concepts of reasoning, memory, tool usage, interaction, and agent specification.
- **To discuss the challenges that could serve as a future research agenda**
- **To caution against potential risks that can emerge when exceeding human intelligence.** This includes addressing concerns about control, value alignment, and existential risks associated with advanced AI.
- **To enable a broader audience** to better understand the novel risks and opportunities of Agentic AI.

The motivation behind this research is driven by:

- **The increasing importance of Agentic AI:** Agentic AI has the potential to transform many industries, and it's crucial to understand its capabilities and limitations.
- **The need for clarity:** Many people are still unfamiliar with Agentic AI, and there is a lack of consensus on its definition and scope.
- **The ethical and societal implications:** Agentic AI raises important ethical and societal questions that need to be addressed proactively.
- **Advance research and development:** The information provided can help guide researchers in further research and development.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The research paper employs a survey-based methodology, incorporating innovations to address the rapid growth of research. The methodology includes the following steps:

- **Meta-Survey:** Conducting a search for existing surveys on "Agentic AI," "LLM agents," and "Generative AI" to identify key papers, build on existing works, and improve prior works.
- **Outline Development:** Creating an outline for the paper, including chapter structures and basic content, unifying prior works while omitting less relevant aspects and introducing novel ones.
- **Conceptualization Refinement:** Revisiting the surveys to identify gaps in the conceptualization and refining the framework through backward search (examining cited works).
- **Forward Search:** Conducting forward searches, focusing on citations and publications in top venues and considering new preprints.
- **Targeted Searches:** Performing targeted searches on specific topics, such as evaluation, reasoning, planning, and RAG, to further refine the structure and content.
- **Recent Results:** Incorporating works from preprint servers and reputable blog posts after quality assessments.
- **Synthesis and Analysis:** Synthesizing the information gathered from various sources and analyzing it to provide a comprehensive overview of Agentic AI.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The main findings and results of this research paper are:

- **Clear Differentiation:** The paper provides a clear and concise comparison of GenAI and Agentic AI, highlighting their key differences in reasoning, interaction, execution capability, adaptability, and autonomy.
- **Evolutionary Perspective:** It frames Agentic AI as an evolutionary step building upon earlier GenAI systems, outlining the key milestones in this progression.
- **Novel Capabilities:** It identifies the novel capabilities of Agentic AI, including reasoning, memory, tool usage, and interaction with the environment, which enable it to tackle more complex tasks than GenAI.
- **Advantages and Limitations:** It acknowledges the advantages and limitations of Agentic AI, noting that it does not offer dramatic improvements over GenAI in all areas and that it can still make mistakes on relatively simple problems.
- **Challenges and Risks:** It highlights the challenges and potential risks associated with Agentic AI, including error accumulation, interpretability issues, safety concerns, human alignment problems, and control issues.
- **Agentic AI is not a magic bullet:** the study finds that Agentic AI does not offer dramatic improvements over GenAI in all areas.
- **Agent and Multi-agent Specification:** It discusses the crucial aspects of defining agents and multi-agent systems to develop an effective Agentic AI system.
