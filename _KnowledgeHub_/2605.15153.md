# Pelican-Unified 1.0: A Unified Embodied Intelligence Model for Understanding, Reasoning, Imagination and Action

> [!summary] Summary
> The WFM System Group at X-Humanoid introduces Pelican-Unified 1.0, a unified embodied intelligence model that integrates understanding, reasoning, imagination, and action within a single adaptive loop. The model achieves competitive specialist performance, scoring 64.7 on multimodal benchmarks and a 93.5% success rate on the RoboTwin dual-arm tasks, while also demonstrating enhanced compositional and zero-shot generalization on real robots.

## Problem

- Current embodied AI models are fragmented, relying on separate specialist modules for perception, language, world modeling, and action policies, which leads to semantic breaks and inefficiencies.
- Existing Vision Language Models (VLMs) offer strong understanding but lack direct action capabilities, while Vision-Language-Action Models (VLAs) often lack explicit future imagination, limiting generalization.
- World Models excel at imagining future visual states but struggle to steer imagination with task logic or language-grounded reasoning, preventing mutual strengthening of capabilities.

## Method

- **Pelican-Unified 1.0** implements a unified paradigm by integrating understanding, reasoning, imagination, and action as ==interdependent dimensions== within a single, ==end-to-end trainable intelligence loop==.
- A ==Vision-Language Model (VLM) backbone== serves as the unified encoder, processing multimodal context, generating a ==chain-of-thought reasoning trace==, and projecting to a dense, ==shared latent variable 'z'==.
- A ==Unified Future Generator (UFG)==, a ==diffusion transformer==, is conditioned on 'z' and jointly models and generates future visual observations (video) and robot actions using shared computational resources and a combined loss function.

## Results

- Achieved an average score of **64.7** on eight multimodal VLM benchmarks, outperforming comparable models and demonstrating significant improvements in embodied-oriented benchmarks (e.g., **+28.2pp** on Where2Place).
- Attained a **93.5%** average success rate on the RoboTwin 50-task dual-arm benchmark, ranking second among compared action methods and outperforming most specialized VLA and world-model baselines.
- Ranked first on the WorldArena imagination benchmark with an **EWM Score of 66.03**, showing high **3D Accuracy (98.13)** and **Motion Quality (62.69)** for generated future videos, and successfully performed unseen compositional and zero-shot generalization on real robotic platforms.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - True unification in embodied AI requires structurally shared representations, mutually constrained conditions, and co-evolution through a common training process, rather than modular assembly.
> - The VLM's chain-of-thought and the projected latent variable 'z' provide a central coupling mechanism, ensuring the task state is simultaneously semantic for reasoning, predictive for imagination, and actionable for control.
> - Jointly generating future videos and actions within the same diffusion transformer enables actions to be consequence-aware, imagination to be task-directed, and reasoning to be constrained by executability.
