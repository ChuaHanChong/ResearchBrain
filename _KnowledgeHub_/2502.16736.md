# Adaptive Conformal Guidance for Learning under Uncertainty

> [!summary] Summary
> AdaConG, a framework from researchers at the University of Maryland, North Carolina State University, and Adobe Research, integrates split conformal prediction directly into the training loop to dynamically modulate guidance signals based on their quantified uncertainty. This approach enhances learning robustness and improves performance across supervised, semi-supervised, and reinforcement learning tasks, achieving up to +10.89% accuracy gains in knowledge distillation under domain shift.

## Problem

- Machine learning systems often rely on noisy or unreliable guidance signals, such as pseudo-labels or expert demonstrations, which can degrade performance if blindly trusted.
- Existing methods for addressing uncertainty are often heuristic-based, poorly calibrated, or limited to post-hoc analysis, lacking dynamic integration into the learning process.
- The challenge lies in adaptively balancing reliance on imperfect guidance with the model's self-learning capabilities to prevent error propagation and improve generalization.

## Method

- **AdaConG** embeds ==split conformal prediction== directly into the machine learning training loop to provide statistically rigorous and model-agnostic uncertainty quantification for guidance signals.
- It quantifies guidance uncertainty based on the size of prediction sets generated by conformal prediction, then uses this measure to adaptively weight the influence of guidance loss in the overall objective function.
- The framework is applied and validated across diverse learning paradigms, including knowledge distillation (supervised), semi-supervised learning, and imitation-guided reinforcement learning.

## Results

- Achieved up to +**10.89%** improvement in Top-1 accuracy for knowledge distillation under domain shift, outperforming training from scratch and other uncertainty-aware methods.
- Consistently improved Top-1 accuracy in semi-supervised image classification across multiple baselines and datasets, with gains of up to +**5.98%** by mitigating pseudo-label noise.
- Demonstrated significantly faster convergence and over **6x** higher accumulated rewards in imitation-guided reinforcement learning in domain-shifted environments compared to baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating conformal prediction directly into the training process provides a principled, dynamic mechanism for managing uncertainty in guidance, moving beyond static or heuristic approaches.
> - Adaptively reducing reliance on highly uncertain guidance signals enhances robustness to noisy data, domain shifts, and suboptimal teachers.
> - The approach offers a generalizable solution for effective learning under uncertainty, applicable across various machine learning settings and tasks.
