# PaCo-RL: Advancing Reinforcement Learning for Consistent Image Generation with Pairwise Reward Modeling

> [!summary] Summary
> The PaCo-RL framework, developed by researchers from Xi’an Jiaotong University and A*STAR, introduces a specialized reward model and an efficient reinforcement learning algorithm to enhance visual consistency in generated images. This framework achieves superior human alignment on consistency benchmarks and significantly improves consistency in Text-to-ImageSet generation and image editing, reducing training time by half while maintaining performance.

## Problem

- Supervised methods for consistent image generation are hindered by the scarcity of large-scale, high-quality datasets and struggle to capture subjective human perceptual preferences.
- Existing reinforcement learning (RL) approaches for image generation lack specialized reward models designed to evaluate multi-image visual consistency, often relying on single-image metrics.
- Current RL algorithms for image generation are computationally expensive for high-resolution or multi-image tasks and prone to instability due to reward domination when optimizing multiple objectives.

## Method

- PaCo-Reward, a human-aligned ==consistency reward== model, was developed using a new pairwise consistency dataset (PaCo-Dataset) and a Vision-Language Model (VLM)-based generative formulation for consistency evaluation.
- PaCo-GRPO, an efficient RL algorithm, implements a resolution-decoupled training strategy that optimizes at lower resolutions to reduce computational cost and halve training time.
- PaCo-GRPO incorporates a log-tamed multi-reward aggregation mechanism that adaptively compresses fluctuating reward signals, preventing reward domination and ensuring stable, balanced optimization across multiple objectives.

## Results

- PaCo-Reward-**7B** achieved a **10.5%** gain in Accuracy and a **0.150** increase in Spearman's ρ on the ConsistencyRank benchmark, demonstrating superior alignment with human consistency preferences.
- Integrated into FLUX.1-dev, PaCo-RL improved Text-to-ImageSet generation consistency by average absolute gains of **0.117** (Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** evaluation) and **0.103** (Gemma-3-**4B**-IT evaluation).
- Resolution-decoupled training at **0.5x** resolution achieved comparable final performance in approximately half the training time (6 hours vs. 12 hours), and log-tamed aggregation prevented reward domination, leading to balanced improvements across all objectives.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - General-purpose multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are less effective at evaluating visual consistency than specialized, fine-tuned reward models, highlighting the need for domain-specific reward architectures.
> - Training RL models for image generation at moderately lower resolutions can significantly accelerate the training process without compromising final high-resolution performance, provided a minimum level of visual detail is retained for effective reward signals.
> - Adaptive reward aggregation mechanisms are crucial for stable multi-objective RL in generative tasks, preventing one reward signal from dominating and leading to suboptimal or unstable training when optimizing for diverse criteria like consistency and prompt alignment.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of image generation has demonstrated advancements in producing high-quality and diverse images from textual prompts. However, achieving *consistent* image generation—where identities, styles, and logical coherence are faithfully preserved across multiple images within a set or during editing—remains a significant challenge. This consistency is crucial for applications such as storytelling, character design, and image editing.

Existing approaches to consistent image generation primarily rely on supervised training. These methods encounter limitations due to the scarcity of large-scale datasets specifically curated for visual consistency. Furthermore, supervised models often struggle to accurately capture and generalize complex, subjective human perceptual preferences regarding consistency.

Reinforcement Learning (RL) has emerged as an alternative paradigm in generative AI, offering a mechanism to optimize generative models based on feedback from reward models (RMs). These RMs are often trained to encapsulate human preferences across various perceptual dimensions, enabling models to learn intricate visual criteria without extensive explicit supervision from meticulously labeled datasets. This "data-free" learning capability positions RL as a promising avenue for addressing the challenges in consistent image generation.

Despite the potential of RL, its application to consistent image generation faces two primary limitations within the current research landscape:
1. **Lack of Suitable Reward Models:** Existing reward models predominantly focus on general image quality, aesthetics, or prompt alignment. They are not explicitly designed to evaluate visual consistency, which often necessitates pairwise comparisons between images rather than single-image assessment. Current similarity metrics like CLIP or DreamSim can assess image similarity but may not fully capture the multifaceted nature of human consistency perception.
2. **Inefficient RL Algorithms:** Consistent image generation tasks are computationally demanding, particularly when dealing with high-resolution images or generating multiple images per prompt. Current RL algorithms for image generation tend to be computationally expensive, and optimizing multiple reward signals (e.g., consistency and prompt fidelity) can lead to instability due to reward domination.

This work, PaCo-RL, positions itself within this landscape by specifically addressing these two limitations. It aims to develop a specialized reward model tailored for visual consistency and an efficient RL algorithm designed to optimize generative models for consistent output, thereby unifying various consistent image generation tasks like Text-to-ImageSet generation and Image Editing under a single, efficient framework.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to advance consistent image generation by developing a comprehensive reinforcement learning framework, PaCo-RL. This framework is designed to overcome the limitations of existing supervised approaches and current RL methodologies in addressing the complexities of visual consistency.

The motivation for this work stems from several key observations and challenges:

1. **Deficiencies in Supervised Training:** Consistent image generation applications, such as storyboarding, character design, and product mockups, require maintaining identity, style, and logical coherence across a series of images. Supervised learning, while effective for many image generation tasks, is hampered in this domain by two main factors:
 - **Data Scarcity:** There is a significant lack of large-scale, high-quality datasets that precisely capture and annotate visual consistency across multiple images. Creating such datasets is labor-intensive and expensive.
 - **Subjectivity of Consistency:** Human perception of visual consistency is often complex and subjective, making it difficult to define and encode through explicit labels in supervised datasets.

2. **Unrealized Potential of Reinforcement Learning:** RL offers a promising pathway to address these challenges by allowing generative models to learn complex, subjective visual criteria directly from feedback. This feedback, typically provided by reward models, can implicitly capture human preferences without requiring extensive, pixel-level consistent annotations. This "data-free" learning paradigm is appealing for tasks where explicit supervision is difficult to acquire.

3. **Gaps in Current RL for Consistent Image Generation:** Despite the general promise of RL for image generation, its direct application to *consistent* image generation specifically encounters two core unresolved issues:
 - **Absence of Specialized Consistency Reward Models:** Existing reward models (e.g., those for aesthetics or text-image alignment) are not designed to evaluate the multi-image, relational aspects of visual consistency. A reward model capable of accurately assessing how well multiple images adhere to shared attributes (identity, style, logic) is a prerequisite for effective RL in this domain.
 - **Computational Inefficiency of RL Algorithms:** Consistent image generation often involves producing multiple images or high-resolution outputs, which significantly escalates computational costs during RL training. Current RL algorithms, while successful in single-image generation, become prohibitively expensive and can suffer from instability (e.g., reward domination) when extended to these more complex, multi-image scenarios requiring simultaneous optimization of several reward signals (e.g., consistency and prompt fidelity).

Therefore, the key objectives are to:
- **Construct a specialized, human-aligned consistency reward model (PaCo-Reward)** capable of evaluating visual consistency from pairwise image comparisons, designed to align with the autoregressive nature of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and leverage structured reasoning.
- **Develop an efficient and stable reinforcement learning algorithm (PaCo-GRPO)** that can optimize generative models for consistent image generation, specifically addressing the computational bottlenecks and reward instability inherent in multi-image, high-resolution tasks.

By achieving these objectives, the research aims to provide a practical and scalable solution for advancing consistent image generation, enabling generative models to produce coherent outputs that better align with human perceptual preferences across diverse applications.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The PaCo-RL framework consists of two main components: PaCo-Reward, a specialized consistency reward model, and PaCo-GRPO, an efficient reinforcement learning algorithm.

#### 3.1. PaCo-Reward: Pairwise Consistency Reward Modeling

PaCo-Reward is designed to address the lack of suitable reward models for evaluating visual consistency.

#### 3.1.1. PaCo-Dataset: Pairwise Consistency Dataset Construction

To train and evaluate consistency reward models, a new, large-scale, human-annotated dataset called PaCo-Dataset was constructed.
- **Scope:** The dataset covers 6 major categories and 32 subcategories of consistent image generation scenarios (e.g., character generation, design style generation, process generation), encompassing various types of visual consistency like identity, style, and logic.
- **Data Synthesis Pipeline:**
 1. **Prompt Generation:** 2,000 text prompts were initially generated using Deepseek-V3.1, from which 708 diverse prompts were selected using graph-based diversification on text embeddings.
 2. **Image Grid Generation:** For each selected prompt, FLUX.1-dev was used to generate `m x n` image grids exhibiting strong internal consistency. To increase diversity and manage costs, four grids were generated per prompt using different seeds.
 3. **Sub-figure Combinatorial Pairing:** Each `m x n` grid was divided into sub-figures. A `2 x 2` grid setting was chosen for its balance of quality and efficiency. Sub-figures were then exhaustively paired across the four grids generated from the same prompt, resulting in 33,984 unique ranking instances. Each instance comprised one reference image and four comparison candidates.
- **Data Annotation:**
 1. Six trained annotators ranked the four comparison images against the reference image based on visual consistency. A random subset of 3,136 instances was reserved as the ConsistencyRank benchmark.
 2. Rankings were converted into pairwise comparisons (positive and negative pairs) to provide efficient supervision for training.
 3. The dataset was augmented with 5,695 manually verified consistent pairs from ShareGPT-4o-Image.
 4. The final dataset contains 54,624 annotated image pairs (27,599 consistent, 27,025 inconsistent).
 5. Each pair was further augmented with CoT (Chain of Thought) reasoning annotations generated by GPT-5, enhancing interpretability and robustness against overfitting.

#### 3.1.2. Pairwise Reward Modeling Approach

PaCo-Reward reformulates consistency evaluation as a generative task to align with the autoregressive nature of Vision-Language Models (VLMs).
- **Generative Formulation:** Instead of using an extra regression head to output a scalar reward, the model predicts the likelihood of generating a "Yes" or "No" token to indicate consistency between two input images, given a shared prompt. This approach directly leverages the VLM's next-token prediction capabilities.
- **Enhancements:** Task-aware instructions and CoT-style reasons are incorporated into the input prompt to the VLM, aiming to improve generalization and interpretability.
- **Inference:** During inference, the predicted probability of the "Yes" token serves as the consistency score.
- **Training Objective:** A weighted likelihood objective, `L_PaCo`, is employed to balance the supervision signal from the binary decision token ("Yes"/"No") and the subsequent CoT reasoning sequence.
 - `L_PaCo = - [α log p(y0 | I) + ((1-α)/(n-1)) * sum(log p(yi | I))]`
 - `y0` represents the first token (binary answer), `yi` represents the i-th token of the reasoning sequence, and `α` is a weighting hyperparameter (set to 0.1) that controls the balance between these two components.

#### 3.2. PaCo-GRPO: Efficient and Stable RL Algorithm

PaCo-GRPO builds upon existing Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) methods for image generation (e.g., FlowGRPO, DanceGRPO) but introduces two core strategies to enhance efficiency and stability.

#### 3.2.1. Resolution-decoupled Training Strategy

- **Motivation:** Consistent image generation tasks often involve generating high-resolution images or multiple sub-figures, leading to a quadratic increase in computational cost with resolution in Transformer-based architectures. This makes RL optimization expensive, especially for sampling.
- **Mechanism:** During RL training, the generative model produces lower-resolution images (e.g., 512x512 or 384x384) for reward computation and optimization. However, the model retains the capability to generate full-resolution images (e.g., 1024x1024) during inference and evaluation.
- **Rationale:** Prior work indicates that even lower-quality images (e.g., fewer denoising steps) can provide effective reward signals. This strategy reduces the computational overhead of both sampling and training significantly.

#### 3.2.2. Log-tamed Multi-reward Aggregation Mechanism

- **Motivation:** In consistent image generation, multiple reward signals (e.g., visual consistency from PaCo-Reward, and prompt alignment from CLIP-T) must be simultaneously optimized. Naive weighted aggregation of these rewards often leads to reward domination, where one signal with large fluctuations overwhelms others, causing suboptimal or unstable training.
- **Mechanism:** This strategy adaptively compresses reward signals that exhibit large fluctuations.
 1. **Coefficient of Variation (CoV) Calculation:** For each reward component `Rk`, its coefficient of variation `hk = std(Rk) / mean(Rk)` is computed across a batch of samples.
 2. **Logarithmic Transformation:** If `hk` exceeds a predefined threshold `δ` (e.g., 0.2 or the mean CoV), a logarithmic transformation `log(1 + Rk)` is applied to that reward component. Otherwise, the original reward value is retained.
- **Benefits:** This transformation compresses large reward values, effectively mitigating reward domination while preserving the relative order of preferences among samples. This promotes balanced optimization across all reward objectives and enhances training stability.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments were designed to evaluate PaCo-Reward's alignment with human preferences and PaCo-GRPO's effectiveness in improving consistent image generation performance, efficiency, and stability.

#### 4.1. PaCo-Reward's Alignment with Human Preferences (RQ1)

- **ConsistencyRank Benchmark:** PaCo-Reward-7B demonstrated superior alignment with human perceptions of visual consistency compared to all baselines. It achieved a 10.5% gain in Accuracy and a 0.150 increase in Spearman's ρ over the original Qwen2.5-VL-7B. Notably, advanced multimodal large language models (MLLMs) like InternVL3.5-8B and the original Qwen2.5-VL-7B performed less effectively than traditional similarity-based methods such as CLIP-I and DreamSim on this benchmark, highlighting the necessity of specialized fine-tuning for consistency evaluation.
- **EditReward-Bench:** On the EditReward-Bench, PaCo-Reward-7B achieved competitive results, outperforming all open-source baselines and approaching the performance of proprietary models like GPT-5 in Prompt Following, Consistency, and Overall accuracy. These results suggest that PaCo-Reward generalizes effectively to model human preferences across diverse visual consistency tasks.

#### 4.2. Impact of PaCo-Reward in RL-based Image Generation (RQ2)

Integrating PaCo-Reward-7B into PaCo-GRPO demonstrated improvements in consistency for both Text-to-ImageSet generation and Image Editing tasks.

- **Text-to-ImageSet Generation (T2IS-Bench):** FLUX.1-dev, when enhanced with PaCo-Reward-7B, substantially outperformed the strongest open-source baseline (AutoT2IS) across all visual consistency metrics. It yielded average absolute gains of 0.117 when evaluated by Qwen2.5-VL-7B and 0.103 when evaluated by Gemma-3-4B-IT. Its performance was comparable to closed-source models such as GPT-4o, indicating a reduction in the performance gap between open-source and closed-source solutions.
- **Image Editing (GEdit-Bench):** PaCo-Reward-7B consistently improved Semantic Consistency (SC) and Prompt Quality (PQ) across various base models, including FLUX.1-Kontext-dev and Qwen-Image-Edit. Unlike some prior methods (e.g., EditReward for Step1X-Edit), PaCo-Reward-7B achieved a balanced improvement, enhancing consistency while preserving image quality.

#### 4.3. Efficiency and Stability of PaCo-GRPO Strategies (RQ3)

Ablation studies confirmed the effectiveness of PaCo-GRPO's two key components.

- **Resolution-decoupled Training:**
 - Training at 512x512 resolution (0.5x) achieved comparable final performance to 1024x1024 training within approximately 50 epochs, while requiring nearly half the training time (6 hours vs. 12 hours). This suggests that moderate resolution reduction significantly accelerates training without compromising final performance.
 - Lower-resolution training exhibited higher reward variance, which promoted exploration during RL optimization.
 - However, training at very low resolutions (256x256) resulted in failure, indicating that a minimum level of visual detail is necessary for reliable reward evaluation.
 - Pearson correlation analysis showed strong positive correlations (0.725–0.848) between evaluation metrics at 1x and 0.5x resolutions, confirming the reliability of reward signals under moderate resolution reduction.
- **Log-tamed Multi-reward Aggregation:**
 - Without log-tamed aggregation, the ratio of Consistency to Prompt Alignment rewards rapidly exceeded 2.5 after 50 epochs, indicating that the Consistency reward dominated the optimization process. This led to a degradation of Aesthetics and Prompt Following metrics, suggesting a "reward collapse" where the model produced low-quality, albeit highly consistent, image sets.
 - With log-tamed aggregation, the reward ratio was maintained below 1.8 throughout training, preventing any single reward from dominating. This resulted in balanced improvements across all objectives.

#### 4.4. Case Studies

Visualizations of training progression (e.g., a dentist's facial attributes, chalkboard font uniformity, or sketch-to-drawing sequences) demonstrated PaCo-RL's ability to progressively enhance diverse dimensions of visual consistency, including identity, style, and logical coherence.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The PaCo-RL framework offers a notable advancement in the field of consistent image generation, with several areas of significance and potential impact:

1. **Enhanced Consistency in Generated Content:** By introducing a specialized, human-aligned reward model for visual consistency, PaCo-RL enables generative models to produce image sets and edited images that are more coherent and aligned with human perceptual expectations. This addresses a critical limitation in current image generation systems, where individual image quality may be high but multi-image consistency often lacks.

2. **Improved Practicality of Reinforcement Learning for Generative Tasks:** The development of PaCo-GRPO's efficient strategies, particularly resolution-decoupled training and log-tamed multi-reward aggregation, significantly reduces the computational cost and improves the stability of RL training for complex image generation tasks. This makes RL a more practical and scalable approach for fine-tuning generative models, especially for scenarios involving high-resolution outputs or multiple images. The increased efficiency can accelerate research and development in this area.

3. **Reduced Reliance on Large-Scale Supervised Datasets:** PaCo-RL leverages the data-free learning paradigm of RL more effectively for consistency tasks. By learning from a specialized reward model rather than requiring extensive, perfectly consistent paired or grouped image datasets, the framework mitigates a major bottleneck associated with supervised training in this domain. This reduces the labor and cost associated with data collection and annotation for consistent generation.

4. **Broader Applicability and Creative Potential:** The ability to generate consistently styled characters, logically progressing storyboards, or coherently edited images unlocks new possibilities across various applications. This includes:
 - **Storytelling and Narrative Generation:** Creating visually cohesive sequences for comic books, animations, or advertising campaigns.
 - **Character and Product Design:** Maintaining consistent visual identities across different poses, scenarios, or product mockups.
 - **Image Editing and Manipulation:** Ensuring that edits preserve the overall style and identity of an image while accurately following instructions.
 - **Educational Content and Visual Explanations:** Generating sequential illustrations that logically depict processes or concepts.

5. **Foundation for Future Research:** The framework establishes a strong foundation for future research in several directions, including:
 - Exploring more sophisticated consistency dimensions beyond identity, style, and logic.
 - Developing more adaptive or personalized consistency reward models.
 - Applying the efficient RL strategies to other computationally intensive generative tasks.
 - Further bridging the gap between open-source and proprietary models in complex generative capabilities.

In summary, PaCo-RL represents a methodological contribution to consistent image generation by offering a comprehensive, efficient, and human-aligned RL framework. Its impact lies in improving the quality, practicality, and accessibility of generating coherent visual content across a range of applications.
