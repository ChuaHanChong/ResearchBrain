# What Is The Best 3D Scene Representation for Robotics? From Geometric to Foundation Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey provides a comprehensive analysis of 3D scene representation methods for robotics, categorizing them from traditional geometric approaches to neural and foundation models. It evaluates their performance and suitability across core robotic functionalities like perception, mapping, localization, navigation, and manipulation.

## Problem

- Traditional geometric 3D scene representations lack the density, continuity, semantic richness, and photorealism required for advanced embodied intelligence tasks in robotics.
- The rapid development of neural representations (e.g., NeRF, 3DGS) and large-scale Foundation Models necessitates a unified and critical understanding of their strengths, weaknesses, and optimal applications within diverse robotic modules.
- Robotic systems require distinct properties from scene representations for various tasks, making it challenging to identify the "best" method for specific perception, mapping, localization, navigation, or manipulation functionalities.

## Method

- The paper systematically classifies 3D scene representations into geometric, neural (NeRF, 3DGS), and Foundation Model categories, detailing their formulations and properties.
- It conducts a comparative analysis of these representations across key dimensions including data form, continuity, memory efficiency, photorealism, flexibility, and geometric capability.
- The study evaluates the performance and applicability of each representation type across five fundamental robotic modules: perception, mapping, localization, manipulation, and navigation.

## Results

- Geometric representations remain efficient and interpretable for explicit geometric accuracy in many robotic tasks, while neural methods excel in high-fidelity, photorealistic reconstruction and dense mapping.
- 3D Gaussian Splatting provides a significant leap in real-time photorealistic rendering with editable primitives, offering a faster alternative to NeRF for dynamic scene reconstruction and SLAM.
- Foundation Models demonstrate superior performance in open-vocabulary perception, language-grounded manipulation, and robust localization by leveraging powerful learned features and semantic priors, pushing robotics towards more cognitive and generalized capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A clear evolutionary trend exists from explicit geometric representations towards implicit neural and large-scale Foundation Models, driven by increasing demands for photorealism, continuous scene modeling, and semantic understanding in robotics.
> - Neural representations like NeRF and 3DGS offer unparalleled photorealism and differentiable scene modeling but often face challenges related to memory consumption, rendering speed, and explicit geometric accuracy without specialized regularization.
> - Foundation Models show promising capabilities for integrating high-level semantic reasoning, language-based priors, and generalization into 3D scene understanding, enabling robust open-set perception and complex task execution for robots.
