# MaMi-HOI: Harmonizing Global Kinematics and Local Geometry for Human-Object Interaction Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at South China University of Technology developed MaMi-HOI, a framework for 3D Human-Object Interaction generation that harmonizes global kinematics with local geometry by addressing 'Geometric Forgetting'. This framework enabled physically plausible interactions with precise surface contact and natural human motion, reducing trajectory end-point error by over 50% and improving downstream task success rate by 6.02 percentage points on long-term tasks.

## Problem

- Existing diffusion models for 3D Human-Object Interaction (HOI) generation struggle to balance natural human motion with precise physical contact, leading to 'Geometric Forgetting'.
- Joint generation methods often produce artifacts like object floating or interpenetration due to implicitly entangled states, lacking explicit control over global trajectories.
- Waypoint-guided approaches treat spatial constraints as soft guidance, causing deep networks to lose fine-grained object geometry and sacrifice contact accuracy for overall semantic continuity.

## Method

- The **MaMi-HOI** framework augments a diffusion backbone with a ==dual-adapter paradigm== (==GAPA== and ==KHA==) to reconcile macro-level kinematic fluidity with micro-level spatial precision.
- A **Geometry-Aware Proximity Adapter (GAPA)** explicitly re-injects dense object geometry via ==distance-aware cross-attention== and applies ==residual snapping corrections== to ensure precise contact.
- A **Kinematic Harmony Adapter (KHA)** implicitly modulates the generative flow using a ==spatial-aware copy mechanism== and ==zero-linear injection== to maintain kinematically natural global motion and adherence to sparse waypoints.

## Results

- **MaMi-HOI** achieved the **lowest errors** for start, end, and intermediate waypoints on the FullBodyManipulation dataset, demonstrating strict adherence to spatial conditions.
- The model recorded the **best FID** for human motion quality and the highest F1 score and Contact Percentage while maintaining low penetration scores for interaction quality.
- In long-term tasks within 3D scenes, MaMi-HOI reduced trajectory end-point error by over **50%** and improved the downstream task success rate by **6.02 percentage points** compared to baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - 'Geometric Forgetting' is a core problem in HOI diffusion models, where fine-grained geometric details crucial for accurate physical contact are lost in deeper network layers.
> - A synergistic, coarse-to-fine approach, explicitly separating global kinematic control and local geometric refinement, effectively overcomes the limitations of single-stream HOI generation models.
> - Hard-wiring geometric locality into attention mechanisms (GAPA) and applying zero-linear injection for kinematic modulation (KHA) provide robust inductive biases for high-fidelity HOI synthesis.
