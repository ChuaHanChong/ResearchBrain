# OmniVLA-RL: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Spatial Understanding and Online RL

> [!summary] Summary
> OmniVLA-RL introduces a vision-language-action (VLA) model for robotic manipulation, integrating enhanced spatial understanding, deep multimodal fusion, and a stable online reinforcement learning method. It achieved an average success rate of 97.6% on the LIBERO benchmark and demonstrated superior convergence stability and sample efficiency on LIBERO-Plus compared to prior RL approaches.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models often lack precise 3D spatial perception, hindering accurate object grasping and manipulation.
- Current multimodal fusion strategies in VLA models typically modify only peripheral components, limiting deep integration of linguistic, visual, and spatial information.
- Online reinforcement learning (RL) methods like PPO and GRPO exhibit instability and inefficiency when applied to VLA models, complicating policy refinement.

## Method

- **OmniVLA-RL** uses a ==Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT)== architecture with dedicated ==Spatial==, ==Reasoning==, and ==Action Experts== for deep and bidirectional multimodal fusion, addressing spatial understanding.
- **Flow-GSPO**, a principled online reinforcement learning method, reformulates deterministic ==Flow Matching== into a ==Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE)== process for stable policy exploration and refinement.
- A ==three-stage progressive training paradigm== is employed, including ==multimodal spatial perception pre-training==, ==action generation pre-training==, and ==online reinforcement learning== with Flow-GSPO.

## Results

- OmniVLA-RL achieved an average success rate of **97.6%** on the LIBERO benchmark, outperforming previous state-of-the-art methods across all four task suites.
- On the LIBERO-Plus benchmark, Flow-GSPO demonstrated a **70%** success rate within the initial **50 training steps**, indicating faster convergence and higher stability compared to PPO and GRPO.
- Ablation studies confirmed the Spatial Expert's criticality, with its removal causing an **8.3%** performance drop, and highlighted Flow-GSPO's contribution, providing a **39.1%** absolute success rate increase over supervised fine-tuning alone.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating a specialized Spatial Expert within a MoT backbone enhances granular spatial understanding and enables robust scene perception, crucial for precise robotic tasks.
> - Reformulating Flow Matching from a deterministic ODE to a stochastic SDE allows for effective integration with reinforcement learning, enabling exploration and policy optimization.
> - Action block-level optimization within Flow-GSPO improves RL stability and sample efficiency for continuous robotic trajectories by capturing sequence-level dependencies better than token-based approaches.
