# Robust Model-Based In-Hand Manipulation with Integrated Real-Time Motion-Contact Planning and Tracking

> [!summary] Summary
> A hierarchical framework integrates real-time contact-implicit motion planning with tactile-feedback tracking control for robust in-hand manipulation, enabling complex dexterous tasks like rotating objects and opening doors while maintaining high precision and adapting to external disturbances without requiring extensive training data.

## Problem

- Existing model-based methods struggle to balance real-time performance with accuracy/robustness in in-hand manipulation
- Contact-implicit methods, while fast, suffer from modeling errors that limit real-world performance
- Current approaches lack effective integration of planning and feedback control

## Method

- Two-level hierarchical framework combining contact-implicit ==MPC== planner with tactile-feedback tracking controller
- Smoothed contact-implicit dynamics model enables efficient planning while maintaining accuracy
- Integration of tactile feedback compensates for modeling errors during execution

## Results

- Successfully demonstrated on 5 challenging real-world manipulation tasks including rotating objects and opening doors
- Maintained manipulation precision despite external disturbances and varying object geometries
- Outperformed baseline methods in simulation studies for contact force tracking and motion accuracy
- Achieved real-time planning performance without requiring extensive training data

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Warm-starting the DDP solver with previous solutions enables real-time replanning capabilities
> - Explicit tracking of both planned motions and contact forces improves robustness
> - Hybrid force-motion control effectively handles multi-contact scenarios
