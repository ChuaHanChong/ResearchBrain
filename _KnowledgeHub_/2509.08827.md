# A Survey of Reinforcement Learning for Large Reasoning Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper systematically synthesizes advancements in Reinforcement Learning (RL) for Large Reasoning Models (LRMs), moving beyond human alignment to focus on enhancing intrinsic reasoning capabilities through verifiable rewards. It identifies key components, challenges, and future directions for scaling RL towards Artificial SuperIntelligence (ASI).

## Problem

- The rapid success of RL in transforming LLMs into LRMs faces significant hurdles concerning computational resources, optimal algorithm design, and scalable, high-quality training data.
- Existing RL applications for LLMs primarily focused on human alignment, neglecting the potential of RL to fundamentally improve complex reasoning abilities.
- A lack of a unified framework to understand and navigate the complex, rapidly evolving landscape of RL for reasoning models hinders systematic research and development.

## Method

- The authors conduct a comprehensive literature review, systematizing the field into foundational components: ==reward design==, ==policy optimization==, and ==sampling strategies==.
- The survey introduces and emphasizes =="Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)"== as a key paradigm for enhancing reasoning in tasks with ==objective feedback==.
- It identifies and discusses foundational problems and ongoing debates, such as RL's role in =="sharpening vs. discovery"== and the interplay between ==RL and Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)==.

## Results

- The survey demonstrates RL's broad impact across diverse applications, including coding, agentic tasks, multimodal understanding, robotics, and medical applications, achieving superior performance compared to non-RL methods.
- Analysis of foundational problems reveals that RL can enable "discovery" of new capabilities beyond mere "sharpening," and hybrid SFT+RL paradigms often yield the best generalization.
- Future research directions include continual RL, memory-based RL, and model-based RL, alongside architectural co-design and applications to pre-training and scientific discovery.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - RL with verifiable rewards (RLVR) provides a scalable path to improve complex reasoning in LLMs, especially for tasks like math and coding, by leveraging automated feedback.
> - Effective RL for LRMs relies on a careful combination of dense/unsupervised rewards, efficient off-policy optimization, and dynamic/structured sampling strategies.
> - The field is shifting from static datasets to dynamic environments and robust open-source infrastructure, which are crucial for scaling RL training for reasoning tasks.
