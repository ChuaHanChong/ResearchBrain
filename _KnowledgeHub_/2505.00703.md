# T2I-R1: Reinforcing Image Generation with Collaborative Semantic-level and Token-level CoT

> [!summary] Summary
> T2I-R1 introduces a text-to-image generation model that integrates a bi-level Chain-of-Thought process, combining semantic-level planning and token-level generation. Through Reinforcement Learning, the model improves image quality and prompt adherence, particularly for complex and reasoning-intensive prompts, achieving significant gains on T2I-CompBench and the WISE benchmark over its baseline and state-of-the-art diffusion models.

## Problem

- Existing Chain-of-Thought (CoT) applications are largely explored for text or visual understanding tasks, with limited exploration in visual generation.
- Unified Large Multimodal Models (ULMs) often decouple understanding and generation capabilities, preventing understanding prowess from directly benefiting image generation.
- Mainstream text-to-image (T2I) models lack explicit high-level semantic planning and struggle with compositional or reasoning-intensive prompts, leading to failures in complex scenarios.

## Method

- T2I-R1 introduces a ==bi-level Chain-of-Thought (CoT)==: a semantic-level textual reasoning phase for high-level planning and a token-level CoT for autoregressive image token generation.
- The model is trained using ==BiCoT-GRPO (Bi-level CoT Group Relative Policy Optimization)==, a Reinforcement Learning framework, on a Unified Large Multimodal Model (Janus-Pro).
- An ensemble of ==diverse vision experts== (Human Preference Model, Object Detector, VQA Model, Output Reward Model) provides a multi-faceted reward for comprehensive image evaluation and optimization.

## Results

- T2I-R1 improves performance by **13%** on T2I-CompBench and **19%** on the WISE benchmark compared to its Janus-Pro-7B baseline.
- The model outperforms state-of-the-art diffusion models like FLUX.1 on challenging benchmarks, leading in **five** out of **six** subtasks on T2I-CompBench and achieving the highest overall score on WISE by **4%**.
- Qualitative analysis shows T2I-R1 generates images that are more aligned with human intentions for complex and abstract prompts, demonstrating enhanced reasoning and robustness in unusual scenarios.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A bi-level Chain-of-Thought (CoT) process, encompassing semantic-level planning and token-level generation, is effective for improving text-to-image synthesis.
> - Reinforcement Learning, specifically BiCoT-GRPO, can effectively train a Unified Large Multimodal Model to fuse its understanding and generation capabilities for enhanced reasoning in T2I.
> - An ensemble of diverse vision experts as reward models is crucial for robustly evaluating and optimizing image generation, overcoming the challenges of subjective quality assessment.
