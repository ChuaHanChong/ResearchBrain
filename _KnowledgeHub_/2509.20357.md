# Language Models that Think, Chat Better

> [!summary] Summary
> Princeton University researchers developed Reinforcement Learning with Model-rewarded Thinking (RLMT), an approach that enables large language models to employ explicit reasoning for diverse, open-ended conversational tasks. An 8B parameter model trained with RLMT achieved a 58.7 score on AlpacaEval2 and 50.4 on WildBench, outperforming larger 70B models and, in some cases, even frontier models like GPT-4o on chat and creative writing benchmarks.

## Problem

- Existing Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) models, despite strong performance in specific domains like math and code, demonstrate limited generalization of their reasoning abilities to open-ended, subjective tasks.
- Large Language Models (LLMs) struggle to engage in robust, human-like "system 2 thinking" for diverse, general-purpose chat and creative problem-solving.
- Current LLM alignment methods like RLHF primarily optimize final outputs, often neglecting to explicitly encourage or optimize the internal reasoning processes that lead to high-quality responses.

## Method

- RLMT trains LLMs to generate an explicit ==Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning trace== prior to the final response, extending structured thinking to general conversational tasks.
- This approach leverages a ==preference-based reward model== to evaluate the final response, enabling supervision for thinking in subjective, open-ended domains where rule-based verification is impractical.
- The framework was evaluated with various online RL algorithms, with ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== demonstrating superior performance, and tested on models both with and without initial Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT).

## Results

- RLMT models consistently outperformed RLHF baselines by **1.5–4** points across benchmarks, achieving average gains of **3–8** points in chat and creative writing tasks.
- The top 8B RLMT model (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct with GRPO) scored **58.7** on AlpacaEval2 and **50.4** on WildBench, surpassing 70B instruction-tuned models by **9–22** points and outperforming GPT-4o on specific general chat and creative writing benchmarks.
- Applying RLMT directly to base models without prior Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) yielded substantial improvements, with Llama-3.1-8B-Base-RLMT outperforming its SFT-trained counterpart by **5.5** points on chat benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement Learning with Model-rewarded Thinking (RLMT) effectively bridges the generalization gap of prior reasoning-focused methods by applying explicit Chain-of-Thought training to subjective, open-ended domains.
> - The research indicates that superior performance in general chat and creative tasks is not solely dependent on model scale, as smaller models with RLMT can outperform significantly larger and frontier LLMs.
> - RLMT demonstrates the potential to simplify the LLM post-training pipeline, achieving strong results by directly training base models without extensive Supervised Fine-Tuning.
