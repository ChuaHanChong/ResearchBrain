# MM-HELIX: Boosting Multimodal Long-Chain Reflective Reasoning with Holistic Platform and Adaptive Hybrid Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> The MM-HELIX framework evaluates and enhances multimodal large language models' long-chain reflective reasoning through a novel benchmark and an adaptive hybrid policy optimization strategy. This approach achieved an 18.6% accuracy improvement on its own benchmark and a 5.7% gain in generalization across other mathematical and logic tasks.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) critically lack the capacity for long-chain reflective reasoning, which involves iterative trial, error, and correction.
- Existing MLLM benchmarks primarily focus on text-only or puzzle-like challenges, failing to adequately assess end-to-end reflective reasoning in visually-rich, multimodal contexts.
- Standard MLLM training paradigms like Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) often lead to catastrophic forgetting, while on-policy reinforcement learning struggles with sparse rewards in complex, multi-step tasks.

## Method

- Developed **MM-HELIX**, a novel ==multimodal benchmark== comprising 42 challenging tasks across Algorithms, Graphs, Puzzles, and Games, requiring visual observation, rule comprehension, and sequential reflective thought.
- Created MM-HELIX-100K, a large-scale instruction-tuning dataset of 100,000 high-quality reasoning trajectories with explicit reflection, using a Step-Elicited Response Generation (SERG) pipeline.
- Introduced Adaptive Hybrid Policy Optimization (AHPO), a training algorithm that dynamically integrates off-policy expert guidance from MM-HELIX-100K with on-policy exploration to learn and generalize reflective reasoning skills.

## Results

- Evaluations on MM-HELIX revealed that 23 leading MLLMs struggle with reflective reasoning, with GPT-5 achieving **58.1%** and the best open-source model reaching only **33.3%** accuracy.
- AHPO boosted the Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** baseline model's accuracy by +**18.6%** on the MM-HELIX benchmark, outperforming larger state-of-the-art models and achieving **24.9%**.
- AHPO demonstrated robust generalization, achieving an average performance gain of +**5.7%** across various out-of-domain mathematics and logic tasks (e.g., MathVision, LogicVista), while SFT alone resulted in a -**6.6%** degradation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Leading MLLMs, including GPT-5, exhibit a profound deficit in long-chain reflective reasoning, with top proprietary models achieving only up to 58.1% accuracy on MM-HELIX and open-source models performing substantially worse.
> - A significant modality gap exists, where performance on text-only problem representations is dramatically higher than on multimodal equivalents, indicating challenges in integrating and reasoning over visual and linguistic inputs.
> - Dynamically adapting the influence of offline expert data based on a model's real-time performance, as implemented in AHPO, is crucial for effectively cultivating complex, transferable reflective reasoning skills without experiencing catastrophic forgetting.
