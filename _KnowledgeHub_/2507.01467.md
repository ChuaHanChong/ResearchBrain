# Representation Entanglement for Generation: Training Diffusion Transformers Is Much Easier Than You Think

> [!summary] Summary
> Representation Entanglement for Generation (REG) introduces an image-class denoising paradigm, achieving up to 63x faster training for Diffusion Transformers while setting a new FID record of 1.8 on ImageNet 256x256 by structurally integrating a high-level class token with image latents.

## Problem

- High-fidelity image synthesis in diffusion models often requires extensive training iterations and computational resources.
- Previous methods, such as REPA, improve representation quality during training but lack an active mechanism to leverage discriminative information during the denoising inference process.
- Generative representations within diffusion models generally lag behind the discriminative power of dedicated, large-scale pretrained vision models like DINOv2.

## Method

- **REG** proposes an "==image-class denoising== paradigm" that injects Gaussian noise simultaneously into both image latents and a high-level class token extracted from a vision foundation model.
- The noised class token is spatially concatenated with patchified image latents, forming a unified input for Scalable Interpolant Transformer (SiT) blocks.
- The model is trained with dual objectives: a prediction loss for both the noised image latents and the noised class token, alongside an alignment loss for intermediate transformer layer features.

## Results

- REG achieved up to **63x** faster training convergence than the baseline SiT-XL/2 and **23x** faster than SiT-XL/2 + REPA, significantly reducing GPU hours required for training.
- A SiT-XL/2 + REG model attained a new record-low FID of 1.8 on ImageNet 256x256 at **4M** training steps without classifier-free guidance.
- These substantial improvements in training efficiency and generation quality were accomplished with negligible additional cost, specifically less than a **0.5%** increase in parameters, FLOPs, and inference latency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Structurally integrating a discriminative class token directly into the input and denoising process enables continuous, autonomous semantic guidance throughout the entire generative pipeline, including inference.
> - Jointly reconstructing image latents and their corresponding global semantics from noise leads to a more coherent and efficient generation process, improving both quality and convergence.
> - Leveraging rich, high-level discriminative signals from foundation models through entanglement significantly boosts generative model performance with minimal additional computational overhead.
