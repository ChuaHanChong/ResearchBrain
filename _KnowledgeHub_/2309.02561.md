# Physically Grounded Vision-Language Models for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> A physically grounded Vision-Language Model (VLM) was developed by fine-tuning InstructBLIP on PHYSOBJECTS, a novel dataset of common household objects annotated with physical concepts, to enable deeper understanding of material properties. This integration with LLM-based robotic planners led to a 31.3% increase in real-scene planning accuracy and a higher task success rate on a physical robot compared to ungrounded models.

## Problem

- Current Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with understanding physical object properties like material composition, fragility, or deformability, which are vital for nuanced robotic interaction.
- Robotic planners leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) lack deep physical grounding from visual input, limiting their ability to perform complex, context-aware manipulation tasks safely and effectively.
- Collecting extensive interaction data for physical property estimation is costly and challenging to scale, posing a practical hurdle for traditional robotic learning methods.

## Method

- ==Developed PHYSOBJECTS==, a new dataset of 225,466 object bounding boxes from household videos, annotated with 10 physical concepts using both automated and crowd-sourced preferences and labels.
- Fine-tuned the InstructBLIP VLM on PHYSOBJECTS, updating its Q-Former parameters to learn a score function for physical concepts, including material, fragility, and contents.
- Integrated the resulting physically grounded VLM (PG-InstructBLIP) with an LLM-based planner, enabling the LLM to interactively query the VLM about physical object properties for informed robotic task execution.

## Results

- The physically grounded VLM (PG-InstructBLIP) achieved an average test accuracy of **87.5%** on physical concepts from PHYSOBJECTS, a **21.3%** absolute improvement over the base InstructBLIP.
- When integrated into an LLM-based robotic planning framework, PG-InstructBLIP increased overall planning accuracy on unseen real scenes to **88.2%**, compared to **56.9%** for the base VLM.
- On a physical Franka Emika Panda robot, the system demonstrated a higher task success rate, completing 9 out of 10 manipulation tasks successfully compared to 4 out of 10 with the ungrounded VLM.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - VLMs can be effectively endowed with an understanding of physical object concepts from visual appearance through fine-tuning on targeted human-annotated datasets.
> - Integrating such physically grounded VLMs allows LLM-based robotic planners to move beyond basic object identification to perform more sophisticated, context-aware manipulation tasks.
> - This approach offers a scalable method for acquiring physical property knowledge, leveraging robust visual reasoning from pre-trained VLMs rather than relying solely on extensive, costly interaction data.
