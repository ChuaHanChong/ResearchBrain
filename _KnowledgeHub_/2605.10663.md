# Evolving-RL: End-to-End Optimization of Experience-Driven Self-Evolving Capability within Agents

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Xiaohongshu Inc. and Peking University developed Evolving-RL, a framework that jointly optimizes an LLM agent's capabilities for extracting and utilizing textual skills from past experiences. This approach fosters improved generalization to unseen tasks and enhances the agent's base policy by internalizing reusable procedural knowledge.

## Problem

- Large language models (LLMs) are static post-training and struggle to adapt continuously to novel, out-of-distribution (OOD) tasks and environments during deployment.
- Existing experience-driven self-evolving agents often decouple the optimization of experience extraction and utilization, leading to suboptimal performance or reliance on external quality control mechanisms.
- When reinforcement learning (RL) is applied to enhance experience utilization, it typically optimizes this phase in isolation, neglecting the intrinsic capability of the LLM to extract high-quality, reusable experience.

## Method

- Evolving-RL proposes an algorithmic framework that unifies the optimization of both experience extraction and utilization within a single, shared LLM policy.
- The framework employs an online skill generation and generalization-oriented evaluation process, producing coordinated supervisory signals for both the extractor and solver components.
- It utilizes Group-Normalized Policy Optimization (GRPO) for both components, incorporating a negative entropy coefficient in the extractor loss to bias skill generation towards more deterministic predictions and counter evaluation noise.

## Results

- Evolving-RL achieved a 96.0% overall success rate on ALFWorld and 30.87% action accuracy on Mind2Web (with skill injection), outperforming various prompt-based and RL-based baselines.
- On unseen ALFWorld tasks, the framework achieved an 88.6% success rate (with skills), representing approximately a 98.7% relative improvement over the GRPO (w/ skills) baseline.
- Ablation studies confirmed that co-evolution of extraction and utilization is critical for performance, showing that isolated optimization (solver-only or extractor-only) yields limited or overfit generalization gains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Jointly optimizing skill extraction and utilization within a shared policy is essential for robust generalization to out-of-distribution tasks, overcoming limitations of isolated optimization.
> - Evolving-RL inherently functions as an experience-augmented RL algorithm, allowing the policy to internalize reusable procedural patterns and improve its base capabilities even without explicit skill injection at inference time.
> - The framework improves the intrinsic quality of extracted skills, making them broadly reusable and transferable to other policies rather than just inducing a policy-specific communication protocol.
