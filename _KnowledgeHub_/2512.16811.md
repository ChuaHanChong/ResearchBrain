# GeoPredict: Leveraging Predictive Kinematics and 3D Gaussian Geometry for Precise VLA Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> GeoPredict augments Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models with predictive kinematic priors and 3D Gaussian geometry, enhancing robotic manipulation precision and 3D spatial reasoning. It achieved substantial success rate improvements, such as 10.1 percentage points on RoboCasa Human-50, by using these predictive modules exclusively during training to maintain inference efficiency.

## Problem

- Existing VLA models are primarily reactive and 2D-centric, limiting precise 3D spatial reasoning, object pose understanding, and collision avoidance.
- Current VLA models often lack physically consistent long-horizon control due to an absence of foresight regarding future robot and scene states.
- Directly integrating high-capacity 3D prediction modules with VLA backbones introduces significant computational overhead during inference, making them impractical for real-time robotic control.

## Method

- The framework jointly learns future robot ==keypoint trajectories==, providing a compact summary of past kinematics and an explicit prediction of future robot motion.
- It predicts future scene geometry using 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), which is refined using predicted robot keypoint tracks to concentrate modeling capacity in interaction regions.
- The predictive kinematic and 3D geometric modules serve purely as training-time supervision, shaping the VLA transformer's internal representations without adding computational burden during inference.

## Results

- Achieved an average success rate of **52.4%** on RoboCasa Human-50, an improvement of 10.1 percentage points over the baseline (**42.3%**), and surpassed other methods.
- Demonstrated an average success rate of **96.5%** across the four LIBERO suites, outperforming the UniVLA baseline (**95.2%**) and π0 baseline (**93.9%**).
- Showed substantial gains in real-world experiments, with an **85.0%** success rate for spatial generalization (vs. **60.0%** for baseline) and **95.0%** for geometry generalization (vs. **50.0%** for baseline).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating explicit predictive kinematic and 3D geometric priors (via 3D Gaussian Splatting) significantly improves VLA model performance in tasks requiring precise 3D manipulation.
> - Complex 3D world models can effectively provide supervision during training to instill intrinsic spatial reasoning into VLA policies, without needing to be executed at inference time.
> - Dynamically allocating geometric modeling capacity to task-relevant areas, such as using track-guided refinement for 3DGS, enhances precision and efficiency by focusing on critical interaction zones.
