# HOIGen-1M: A Large-scale Dataset for Human-Object Interaction Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from JD Explore Academy and academic partners introduce HOIGen-1M, a dataset of over 1 million high-quality human-object interaction videos with detailed captions, along with new evaluation metrics and a multi-expert captioning system that enables more accurate generation of interaction-focused videos.

## Problem

- Existing text-to-video models struggle to generate realistic human-object interactions due to limited training data
- Current video datasets lack sufficient quality, detailed captions, or focus on interactions for training HOI generation
- Existing evaluation benchmarks lack specific metrics to assess the physical plausibility and accuracy of human-object interactions in generated videos

## Method

- Created **HOIGen-1M** through multi-stage filtering of 80M source videos using quality metrics and human verification
- Developed **MoME** (Mixture-of-Multimodal-Experts) captioning system to generate accurate, detailed interaction descriptions
- Introduced **CoarseHOIScore** and **FineHOIScore** metrics to evaluate interaction accuracy in generated videos

## Results

- Fine-tuning existing text-to-video models on HOIGen-1M significantly improved their HOI generation capabilities
- MoME captioning system produced more accurate and detailed descriptions compared to single-model approaches
- New evaluation metrics revealed limitations in existing models' ability to generate physically plausible interactions

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Multiple expert models with cross-validation can reduce caption hallucination and improve description accuracy
> - Pixel-level evaluation of human-object contact and interaction stability provides better assessment than general metrics
> - Models show emergent capabilities in generating novel interactions not seen in training data

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research directly addresses a critical gap in the field of text-to-video (T2V) generation, specifically in the area of Human-Object Interaction (HOI). While T2V generation has made significant progress, existing models often struggle to accurately depict HOI due to the scarcity of large-scale, high-quality video datasets with precise captions that focus on these interactions.

The paper highlights the limitations of current large-scale video datasets like WebVid-10M and Panda-70M, which either lack sufficient quality, contain many videos without HOI, or have captions that are too brief and don't capture the nuances of human-object interactions. Existing HOI datasets for perception tasks are too small to support T2V model training.

This work contributes by:

- **Addressing a Data Scarcity Problem:** Creating HOIGen-1M, a significantly larger dataset specifically designed for HOI video generation.
- **Improving Caption Quality:** Developing a novel video description method (MoME) to generate more expressive and accurate captions for HOI videos, mitigating the hallucination problem common in large multimodal models.
- **Providing an Evaluation Framework:** Proposing new metrics (CoarseHOIScore and FineHOIScore) to assess the quality of generated HOI videos, which are currently lacking in existing evaluation benchmarks.

The paper positions HOIGen-1M as a crucial resource for advancing HOI video generation and enabling the development of T2V models that can accurately simulate the physical world, with potential applications in video creation, self-driving technology, and robotics. It builds upon existing work in T2V generation, HOI detection, and HOI reconstruction but provides a unique contribution by focusing on the specific challenge of *generating* videos of human-object interactions at scale. The research also fills a gap between datasets used for video understanding and video generation tasks. Datasets for video understanding typically focus on recognition, while datasets for video generation must focus on creating novel realistic content.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives of this research are:

- **To create a large-scale, high-quality dataset (HOIGen-1M) specifically for training T2V models to generate videos of Human-Object Interactions (HOI).** The dataset aims to overcome the limitations of existing datasets that are either too small, lack high-quality videos, or do not provide sufficiently detailed captions focusing on HOI.
- **To develop a robust video captioning method that can generate expressive and accurate captions for HOI videos.** This addresses the issue of caption quality in existing datasets, where captions are often too brief or lack specific details about the interactions.
- **To develop an automatic evaluation framework for assessing the quality of generated HOI videos.** This fills a critical gap in the field, as existing evaluation metrics are not specifically designed to evaluate the accuracy and realism of human-object interactions in generated videos.

The motivation behind these objectives stems from the recognition that accurate HOI generation is a fundamental aspect of building general simulators of the real world. Such simulators have broad applications across various domains, including:

- **Video Creation:** Enabling the generation of realistic and engaging video content with human interactions.
- **Self-Driving Technology:** Improving the ability of autonomous vehicles to understand and predict human behavior in complex environments.
- **Robotics:** Developing robots that can interact with objects and humans in a natural and intuitive way.

Ultimately, the motivation is to enable T2V models to generate more realistic and useful videos that accurately reflect the complexities of human-object interactions in the real world.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The research employs a multi-stage methodology involving data collection, processing, captioning, and evaluation:

- **Video Curation and Processing:**
 - **Data Collection:** The researchers collected a large pool of videos (80 million) from public datasets designed for HOI perception and general T2V generation.
 - **Metadata Filtering:** Videos were filtered based on metadata (duration, resolution, FPS) to retain only high-quality candidates.
 - **Optical Character Recognition (OCR):** Videos with excessive text overlays were removed.
 - **Aesthetic Score:** An aesthetic predictor was used to filter videos based on visual appeal.
 - **Motion Score:** Optical flow was used to filter videos with excessively high or low motion.
 - **LLM Evaluation:** A multimodal large language model (MLLM) was used to annotate each video with a caption, and another LLM was used to judge if the caption contained an interaction.
 - **Human Verification:** Human annotators manually checked the remaining videos to ensure they contained clear HOIs.
- **Video Captioning with MoME (Mixture-of-Multimodal-Experts):**
 - **Prompt Engineering:** A structured prompt was designed to encourage MLLMs to focus on object attributes, human movements, and detailed temporal descriptions of interactions.
 - **MoME Strategy:** Multiple MLLMs were used to generate captions for each video, and a decision expert (LLM) was used to detect hallucinations (inconsistencies) between the captions. A third expert was used to focus on disagreements between the first two experts and correct hallucinations to regenerate a caption.
- **Evaluation:**
 - **VBench:** The widely used VBench evaluation metric was employed to assess the quality of generated videos across various dimensions.
 - **Prompt Suite:** A diverse set of 306 prompts was created, covering various object and interaction types.
 - **CoarseHOIScore:** A HOI detector was used to predict possible HOI triplets in generated videos, and the score was based on the presence of these triplets.
 - **FineHOIScore:** A pixel-level approach was used to evaluate the contact between humans and objects and the stability of the interaction action.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The key findings and results of the research are:

- **HOIGen-1M Dataset:** A large-scale dataset of over one million high-quality HOI videos with detailed captions was successfully created.
- **Limitations of Existing T2V Models:** Experiments revealed that current T2V models, even those with billions of parameters, struggle to generate high-quality HOI videos. Commercial software performed only marginally better than open-source models.
- **Effectiveness of HOIGen-1M:** Fine-tuning open-source T2V models on HOIGen-1M significantly improved their performance in generating HOI videos, demonstrating the value of the dataset.
- **Effectiveness of MoME:** The proposed captioning method (MoME) was shown to generate more accurate and expressive captions, leading to improved HOI video generation.
- **Value of CoarseHOIScore and FineHOIScore:** These metrics provide a more specific and informative evaluation of HOI video generation than existing general-purpose metrics. Specifically, VBench alone, is not sufficient for evaluating the quality of generated interactions.
- **Out-of-Distribution Generation:** The model demonstrates a degree of "emergent capability", with only marginal changes in the HOIScore between videos that have been encountered in the training data and novel videos.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The HOIGen-1M dataset and the associated methods have the potential to significantly impact the field of T2V generation and related areas:

- **Advancement of T2V Models:** HOIGen-1M provides a valuable resource for training and evaluating T2V models, particularly for generating realistic and accurate videos of human-object interactions.
- **Improved Real-World Simulators:** The ability to generate realistic HOI videos is crucial for building general simulators of the real world, which have applications in various domains, including robotics, autonomous driving, and virtual environments.
- **New Evaluation Frameworks:** The proposed CoarseHOIScore and FineHOIScore metrics offer a more specific and informative way to evaluate the quality of generated HOI videos, potentially leading to the development of more sophisticated evaluation benchmarks.
- **Potential for further research:** The release of a new dataset to the scientific community may allow researchers to explore new approaches and build on the work accomplished.

The research also highlights the importance of data quality and the need for specialized datasets to address specific challenges in T2V generation. By focusing on HOI and developing methods to improve caption quality and evaluation, this work provides a valuable contribution to the advancement of the field.
