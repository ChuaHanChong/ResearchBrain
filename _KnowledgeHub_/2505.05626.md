# Perceiving Beyond Language Priors: Enhancing Visual Comprehension and Attention in Multimodal Models

> [!summary] Summary
> PERCEPTLLM, a multimodal large language model, integrates architectural disentanglement, auxiliary visual supervision, and input masking to enhance visual grounding and reduce reliance on language priors. This approach achieves superior performance on spatial reasoning benchmarks, outperforming larger models while maintaining inference efficiency.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) exhibit weak visual grounding, frequently generating plausible but factually incorrect responses by over-relying on linguistic patterns.
- Existing MLLMs struggle with fine-grained visual details, precise spatial reasoning, and object attribute identification due to sparse visual training signals and strong language priors.
- A single Transformer backbone faces a tension between optimizing for causal language modeling and rich spatial representation learning, hindering robust multimodal integration.

## Method

- Introduced ==VISUALLOSS==, an auxiliary loss leveraging powerful self-supervised vision encoders (I-JEPA/AIM-v2) to compel the LLM backbone to build rich, ==disentangled visual representations==.
- Implemented ==BLANKTOKENS==, a strategy that masks portions of input text during training, forcing the model to initiate and sustain responses based primarily on visual information.
- Developed a ==disentangled architecture== with independent weights for vision and text pathways within the LLM backbone, optimizing each modality's processing without incurring additional inference overhead.
- Generated a specialized synthetic dataset featuring programmatically arranged objects and spatially-focused questions to explicitly train for precise visual parsing and spatial reasoning.

## Results

- PERCEPTLLM demonstrated consistent reductions in next-token prediction loss on visually-dependent datasets, with the disentangled architecture alone contributing a notable drop from **3.48** to **3.20**.
- On the SpatialEval benchmark, the full PERCEPTLLM model achieved an average normalized accuracy improvement of **1.52** compared to the baseline, outperforming the larger Llama 3.2 **11B** model.
- The combination of all proposed techniques, including the advanced AIM-v2 encoder, yielded superior performance while maintaining a smaller model size (**8B** vs **11B**) and lower input resolution (**224x224** vs **560x560**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - MLLMs inherently develop a preliminary capability to associate semantic labels with image patches, indicating a foundation for enhanced visual understanding that can be explicitly guided.
> - Decoupling visual and textual processing with independent weights within the LLM backbone resolves the conflict between non-autoregressive spatial representation and autoregressive language generation, improving overall performance and efficiency.
> - Targeted, explicit visual supervision and the systematic weakening of language priors during training are crucial for MLLMs to move beyond superficial visual processing toward truly visually faithful reasoning.
