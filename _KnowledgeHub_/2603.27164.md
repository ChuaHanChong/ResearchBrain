# daVinci-LLM:Towards the Science of Pretraining

> [!summary] Summary
> The daVinci-LLM project from SII, SJTU, and GAIR developed a 3-billion-parameter language model using a fully-open, scientific approach to pretraining, achieving an overall average score of 51.72 which is comparable to 7-billion-parameter models like OLMo-3, and demonstrating superior performance in reasoning tasks such as MATH (62.80). This was accomplished through a hierarchical 'Data Darwinism' framework for data processing and a two-stage adaptive training curriculum.

## Problem

- The fundamental pretraining phase for large language models remains significantly under-explored due to a 'structural paradox' where well-resourced commercial entities lack transparency, while academic efforts with research freedom face computational limitations.
- Existing LLM development paradigms lack systematic, evidence-based methodologies for pretraining data processing, training dynamics, and design rationales, hindering scientific understanding and reproducibility.
- Post-training techniques are limited in overcoming foundational capability ceilings set during pretraining, underscoring the need for deeper scientific investigation into this initial phase.

## Method

- A fully-open research paradigm combines industrial-scale computational resources with complete research freedom to systematically investigate the foundational pretraining phase of large language models.
- A 3.09 billion-parameter **Qwen2**-based transformer model is trained using a ==Data Darwinism framework== (L0-L9 taxonomy) for ==hierarchical data curation== and a ==two-stage adaptive curriculum== spanning 8 trillion tokens.
- Comprehensive disclosure of model architecture, data processing pipelines, training processes, and over 200 controlled ablations are provided to advance pretraining as a scientific discipline.

## Results

- **daVinci-LLM-3B** achieved an overall average score of **51.72**, making it comparable to the 7-billion-parameter **OLMo-3** model and outperforming other parameter-matched baselines such as LLaMa-3.2-3B (**37.58**) and Qwen-2.5-3B (**51.44**).
- The model demonstrated particularly strong performance in reasoning-intensive domains, scoring **62.80** on MATH (over **23 points** higher than OLMo-3 7B) and **55.99** in average code generation, comparable to the larger OLMo-3 7B.
- Ablation studies confirmed that L4 generative refinement improved MATH by **7.00 points**, and L5 cognitive completion with synthetic QA data effectively steered targeted capability development in code and science reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Hierarchical data processing, from model-based filtering (L3) to generative refinement (L4) and cognitive completion (L5), systematically enhances model capabilities, with L5 synthesis acting as a high-precision tool for targeted development.
> - Adaptive training curricula are essential, as general knowledge saturates faster than reasoning abilities, necessitating shifts from rebalancing existing text data to introducing fundamentally new data formats like structured QA.
> - Data mixture design benefits from internal balance between reasoning domains and progressive intensification of structured QA, preventing over-specialization while enabling further capability gains across training stages.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of large language models (LLMs) has progressed through various paradigms, each with differing levels of transparency and accessibility. The current landscape is characterized by three tiers:
1. **Closed-source commercial models:** These models (e.g., GPT, Claude, Gemini) offer state-of-the-art capabilities but are only accessible via APIs, providing no insight into their pretraining data, methodology, or internal workings. This opacity limits scientific investigation and reproducibility.
2. **Open-weight models:** Projects like LLaMA and Qwen release model checkpoints but typically withhold critical pretraining details such as full data compositions, mixture ratios, and training dynamics. While this allows for some experimentation, a complete understanding of design rationales and negative results remains undisclosed.
3. **Fully-open academic efforts:** Initiatives like OLMo prioritize transparency and research freedom, openly sharing training specifications. However, these efforts often face significant computational resource limitations, which constrain the scale and systematic nature of pretraining experiments, making extensive ablations infeasible.

This paper identifies a "structural paradox" within this landscape: organizations with vast computational resources operate under commercial pressures that disincentivize transparent disclosure, while academic institutions possess research freedom but lack the necessary computational scale for comprehensive pretraining exploration.

daVinci-LLM positions itself at this intersection, aiming to combine industrial-scale resources with full research freedom. Its objective is to address the under-explored nature of the foundational pretraining phase, which the authors argue fundamentally determines a model's capability ceiling. The work aims to transform pretraining from an intuition-driven practice into an evidence-based scientific discipline by adopting a fully-open paradigm. This involves not only releasing model artifacts but also detailing complete data processing pipelines, full training processes, and systematic exploration results, including both successes and failures. This approach contrasts with existing paradigms by emphasizing comprehensive disclosure as a scientific methodology, thereby contributing to the accumulation of verifiable scientific knowledge in pretraining.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation for this work stems from the observation that the pretraining phase is critical for establishing a model's capabilities, yet it remains significantly under-explored due to a structural paradox in the LLM ecosystem. Post-training techniques are acknowledged to refine and align model behavior, but they are limited in their ability to overcome fundamental capability limitations set during pretraining. The authors contend that the community currently possesses limited means to systematically investigate the principles governing knowledge acquisition and organization during pretraining.

To address this gap, daVinci-LLM outlines several key objectives:

1. **Advance the "Science of Pretraining":** The overarching goal is to move pretraining beyond heuristic or proprietary practices towards a systematic, evidence-based scientific discipline. This involves treating openness as a scientific methodology, ensuring that all aspects of the pretraining process are transparent and reproducible.
2. **Overcome the Structural Paradox:** By leveraging computational resources capable of billion-parameter training alongside research freedom, the project aims to conduct the large-scale, systematic investigations that are currently infeasible for most academic institutions and undisclosed by commercial entities.
3. **Systematize Data Processing:** The authors recognize a lack of systematic methodology for data processing decisions. An objective is to introduce and apply the "Data Darwinism framework," an L0-L9 taxonomy, to categorize and guide data curation and quality enhancement, ranging from basic filtering to advanced knowledge synthesis.
4. **Disclose Complete Training Processes:** A core objective is to release comprehensive details of the training methodology, including the model architecture, hyperparameters, learning rate schedules, and data mixture compositions across all training stages. This includes making intermediate checkpoints and full training logs publicly available.
5. **Conduct Systematic Exploration through Ablations:** The project aims to convert pretraining design decisions into verifiable research questions. Through over 200 controlled ablations, the objectives are to empirically investigate:
 - How hierarchical data processing systematically enhances model capabilities (data processing depth).
 - How data strategies and curriculum should adapt during training (training dynamics).
 - How to balance targeted capability enhancement with general capability preservation (data mixture design).
 - Which evaluation protocols reliably measure base model pretraining progress (evaluation validity).
6. **Provide Transferable Methodological Foundations:** The work intends to contribute reusable research infrastructure, including the Data Darwinism framework, systematic exploration methods, and documented successes and failures, to enable the community to build upon its findings and collectively advance scientific knowledge in pretraining.
7. **Release Complete Research Materials:** A commitment to public release of model weights, intermediate checkpoints, processed datasets, complete curation toolkits (prompts and code), training logs, and ablation results is a key objective to facilitate reproducibility and further research.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology employed by daVinci-LLM is structured around three pillars: data, training recipe, and systematic exploration, all executed under a fully-open paradigm.

#### 3.1. Model Architecture

The model utilizes a **Qwen2-based transformer architecture** with **3.09 billion parameters**. It is a decoder-only causal language model trained with a standard causal language modeling objective. Key architectural features include:
- **Grouped-Query Attention (GQA)** with 2 key-value heads shared across 16 query heads.
- **SwiGLU activation function** in the MLP layers with an expansion ratio of approximately 5.4x.
- **RMSNorm** for pre-normalization.
- **Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE)** with a base frequency of 10000.
- A sequence length of 4096 tokens.
- A vocabulary size of 151936 tokens using the Qwen2 tokenizer.
- Training precision is **bfloat16**.
The design emphasizes depth (36 layers) with moderate hidden dimensions (2048) to balance parameter count, throughput, and performance.

#### 3.2. Data Strategy: Data Darwinism Framework

Recognizing the importance of data quality, the authors adopt the **Data Darwinism framework (L0-L9 taxonomy)** to systematically categorize and manage data processing operations. This framework describes data quality levels from basic acquisition (L0) to format normalization (L1), rule-based filtering (L2), lightweight model filtering (L3), generative refinement (L4), cognitive completion (L5), and higher-order synthesis (L6-L9).

The pretraining corpus, totaling approximately **7.58 trillion tokens**, is organized into four main categories, with each source annotated by its Darwin Level:
- **General:** Primarily **Nemotron-CC-v1** (4.28T tokens, L3), undergoing text extraction, English filtering, global deduplication, and ensemble classifier-based quality scoring.
- **Code:** A mix of **Self-Crawled GitHub** (187B tokens, L3), **Nemotron-Pretraining-Code-v1-non-synthetic** (220B tokens, L3), **Nemotron-Pretraining-Code-v1-synthetic-code** (171B tokens, L5), and **TxT360-Stack-Exchange** (20B tokens, L2). Processing includes license filtering, deduplication, and model-based quality assessment.
- **Science:** Comprises various subsets from **MegaMath** (L3-L5), **Nemotron-CC-Math-v1** (L4-L5), and **Darwin-Science** (L4-L5). This includes documents extracted from Common Crawl, refined versions using LLMs, and synthetically generated content with reasoning steps. Processing involves OCR, deduplication, rule-based filtering, educational value scoring, and LLM-driven generative refinement (L4) and cognitive completion (L5).
- **QA:** Includes **Nemotron-Pretraining-SFT-v1** (L5), **Llama-Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset** (L5), **MegaMath-QA-RS** (L5), **MegaScience-RS** (L5), **Darwin-Science-Book QA** (L5), and **Nemotron-CC-v1 Synthetic QA** (L5). These are high-quality, often synthetic, question-answer pairs spanning code, general knowledge, and science domains, frequently involving rejection sampling or LLM-based generation of reasoning paths.

#### 3.3. Training Methodology: Two-Stage Adaptive Curriculum

The pretraining spans **8 trillion tokens** using a multi-stage adaptive curriculum:

- **Stage 1: General Foundation Pretraining (6 Trillion Tokens)**
 - **Stage 1-1 (4T tokens):** Focus on foundation building. Progressive global batch size (GBS) scaling (1,024 to 4,096) with a constant learning rate (3e-4 after 2,000-step warmup). Data mixture is dominated by Nemotron-CC-v1 (68.2%).
 - **Stage 1-2 (2T tokens):** Reasoning enhancement. GBS maintained at 4,096. Learning rate follows cosine decay (3e-4 to 3e-5). Data mixture is rebalanced, reducing Common Crawl to 55.4% and increasing Code (+2.1%) and Science (+10.6%) proportions.
 Training stability is monitored through training loss and gradient norm, which demonstrated consistent convergence without significant issues.

- **Stage 2: Reasoning Capability Enhancement (2 Trillion Tokens)**
 - **Stage 2-1 (1T tokens):** Balanced foundation building. A balanced data mixture is introduced: 30% structured QA, 30% code, 30% L4/L5-processed scientific data, and 10% high-quality web text. Constant learning rate (3e-5 after 2,000-step warmup).
 - **Stage 2-2 (1T tokens):** QA-intensive enhancement. The QA concentration is increased to 70%, with other domains adjusted accordingly. Constant learning rate (3e-5).
This multi-stage approach is informed by observations of differential capability saturation rates, where general knowledge plateaus earlier than reasoning-intensive domains.

#### 3.4. Evaluation Protocol

Evaluations are conducted comprehensively at **5,000-step intervals** to track capability emergence and ensure benchmark stability. The model is assessed on **19 benchmarks** across three capability domains:
- **General:** MMLU, MMLU-Pro, AGIEval, HellaSwag, TriviaQA, RACE, WinoGrande, OpenBookQA, PIQA.
- **Code:** HumanEval, EvalPlus, MBPP.
- **Science:** GSM8K, GSM-Plus, MATH, GPQA, SuperGPQA, MMLU-STEM, MMLU-Pro-STEM.
Inference uses **greedy decoding**. Evaluation strategies include:
- **Perplexity-based (PPL) evaluation:** For multiple-choice or likelihood estimation tasks (e.g., PIQA, MMLU, GPQA-Main) with 0-shot or 5-shot settings.
- **Generative-based evaluation:** For tasks requiring complex reasoning, code generation, or Chain-of-Thought (CoT) outputs (e.g., MATH, MMLU-Pro, HumanEval, GSM8K) with 0-shot to 8-shot settings.

Baselines for comparison include OLMo-3 7B, OLMo-2 7B, Qwen-3.5-4B, Qwen-3-4B, Qwen-2.5-3B, LLaMa-3.2-3B, and Yulan-Mini-2.4B, providing both parameter-matched and capability-matched comparisons.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The daVinci-LLM project yielded several findings through its systematic exploration:

#### 4.1. Overall Performance

The daVinci-LLM-3B model achieved an overall average score of **51.72**, positioning it comparably to the OLMo-3 7B model despite having less than half its parameters. It significantly outperformed other parameter-matched baselines such as LLaMa-3.2-3B (37.58), Yulan-Mini-2.4B (44.82), and Qwen-2.5-3B (51.44). The model demonstrated particular strength in reasoning-intensive domains:
- **MATH:** Achieved 62.80, which is over 23 points higher than OLMo-3 7B (39.60).
- **Code Generation (Avg Code):** Scored 55.99, comparable to OLMo-3 7B (54.42).
- **Science Reasoning (Avg Science):** Reached 48.30, exceeding OLMo-3 7B (45.98).
General knowledge capabilities were maintained at a level comparable to larger baselines, indicating that specialized training did not lead to catastrophic forgetting.

#### 4.2. Data Processing Depth: From Filtering to Synthesis (Section 4.1)

Controlled ablations revealed that hierarchical data processing systematically enhances model capabilities:
- **L3 Model-Based Filtering (Code Data):** Applying model-based filtering (L3) to code data, based on quality scoring, resulted in modest but consistent improvements over rule-based filtering (L2). A notable gain of +3.40 was observed on the MBPP benchmark. This suggests that basic programming competence benefits from cleaner training examples.
- **L4 Generative Refinement (Math Data):** Using generative models (Qwen3-235B) for refinement (L4) on mathematical data (pruning noise, extracting formulas, reorganizing proofs) yielded substantial improvements, particularly a +7.00 increase on the MATH benchmark. This indicates that transforming content into a more structured, pedagogical format significantly aids complex, multi-step reasoning.
- **L5 Cognitive Completion (Synthetic QA):** The synthesis of new reasoning chains through domain-specific QA data (L5) demonstrated strong domain-specific steering. CodeQA significantly enhanced programming benchmarks, while CC-QA primarily strengthened science reasoning. This indicates that L5 synthesis acts as a high-precision tool for targeted capability development rather than a general booster, and strategic synthesis can be as effective as massive data scaling.

#### 4.3. Training Dynamics: Adaptive Data Strategies (Section 4.2)

The study of training dynamics underscored the necessity of adaptive data strategies:
- **Differential Saturation:** General knowledge benchmarks plateaued rapidly within the first 1T tokens of Stage 1 training, while reasoning-intensive domains (code and science) exhibited sustained growth through 4T tokens. This differential saturation motivated adaptive adjustments in data mixture.
- **Domain Proportion Adjustment Limits:** While rebalancing data mixture in Stage 1-2 (reducing general web data, increasing code/science) successfully amplified reasoning gains, further similar adjustments in a hypothetical Stage 1-3 yielded diminishing returns. This indicated a fundamental limitation of merely reallocating proportions among existing textual formats once they collectively approached saturation.
- **Transition to Structured QA:** Introducing structured QA data in Stage 2 (30% QA) substantially outperformed continued domain proportion adjustments with raw text. This established that fundamental data format shifts are necessary to overcome the saturation of unstructured text regimes and unlock advanced cognitive potential in reasoning-intensive domains. Adaptive mixture adjustment remained effective within Stage 2, triggering renewed growth in reasoning when QA concentration was further increased.

#### 4.4. Data Mixture Design: Balancing and Intensifying (Section 4.3)

Investigations into data mixture design revealed principles for balancing targeted enhancement with general capability preservation:
- **Domain Balance (Code and Science Composition):** In Stage 2, while aggressive concentration of reasoning data (code and science) was essential, internal balance between these domains proved critical. Configurations with balanced proportions (e.g., 30% code, 30% science) achieved superior overall performance compared to extreme specialization (e.g., 40% code, 20% science), suggesting that internal balance prevents over-specialization and maintains broader competence.
- **QA Concentration (Progressive Intensification):** The model's tolerance for high-concentration structured QA data was found to be stage-dependent. A conservative 30% QA ratio in Stage 2-1 was necessary to maintain stability and prevent performance degradation (e.g., in code, which collapsed at higher QA ratios in this phase). However, once a balanced foundation was established, an intensified 70% QA regime in Stage 2-2 could be applied successfully, leading to further performance gains across all clusters without catastrophic forgetting. This supports a progressive, adaptive mixture strategy.

#### 4.5. Evaluation Validity: PPL-based vs. Generative-based (Section 4.4)

The study found that the choice of evaluation protocol significantly influences conclusions about base model capabilities:
- **Different Capability Probes:** PPL-based evaluation, which assesses latent knowledge by measuring likelihood assignment, and generative evaluation (e.g., CoT), which requires active knowledge production and organization, probe different aspects of model capability.
- **Ranking Reversals:** Models with extensive QA pretraining, which trains them in explicit answer production, often exhibit amplified advantages and even ranking reversals under generative evaluation compared to PPL-based evaluation. For instance, on MMLU, Qwen-2.5-3B slightly underperformed OLMO-2-7B under PPL but outperformed it under generative evaluation, demonstrating a significant shift. This highlights that generative evaluation is more sensitive to a model's ability to operationalize knowledge.
- **Protocol Alignment:** The appropriate evaluation protocol depends on the intended deployment scenario (e.g., generative evaluation for chatbots, PPL for scoring functions). Transparent reporting of both protocols helps provide a more complete capability profile.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The daVinci-LLM project holds several implications for the advancement of large language model research:

1. **Advancing Pretraining as a Science:** By adopting a fully-open paradigm and conducting systematic, question-driven ablations, the work contributes to transforming pretraining from an empirical craft into a more rigorous scientific discipline. The explicit documentation of methodologies, data processing steps, and experimental outcomes, including negative results, fosters transparency and cumulative knowledge building within the community.
2. **Efficiency in Model Development:** The findings demonstrate that meticulous attention to data quality and adaptive training strategies can enable smaller models (3B parameters) to achieve performance comparable to or exceeding larger models (7B parameters) in specific domains. This suggests that "processing depth" and "adaptive curriculum" can serve as effective scaling mechanisms, offering a more resource-efficient alternative to simply increasing raw data volume or model size. This could have implications for democratizing access to high-performing LLMs by reducing the computational barrier.
3. **Transferable Methodologies and Resources:** The introduction and application of the Data Darwinism framework provide a systematic taxonomy for reasoning about and categorizing data quality, offering a reusable framework for future data curation efforts. The public release of complete datasets, processing pipelines, training logs, intermediate checkpoints, and ablation results offers a rich empirical substrate for researchers to reproduce results, conduct further investigations, and build upon established boundary conditions.
4. **Empirical Guidance for Pretraining Decisions:** The detailed ablation studies provide empirical insights into key pretraining decisions:
 - The value of hierarchical data processing (L3 filtering, L4 refinement, L5 synthesis) for systematic capability enhancement.
 - The necessity of adaptive data strategies, responding to differential capability saturation rates, and transitioning from domain proportion adjustments to fundamental data format shifts (e.g., structured QA).
 - Principles for data mixture design, emphasizing the importance of internal balance within reasoning domains and progressive QA intensification across training stages to prevent capability degradation.
 - An understanding of how evaluation protocols can shape perceived model capabilities, advocating for transparent reporting and protocol choice alignment with use cases.
These insights offer practical guidance for practitioners and researchers in designing more effective and targeted pretraining regimes.
5. **Fostering Open Collaboration:** The project's commitment to full transparency and open-sourcing of all research artifacts is intended to encourage broader community engagement, enabling collective progress in understanding and developing LLMs. This open approach can help accelerate innovation by allowing other researchers to leverage daVinci-LLM's foundations.
