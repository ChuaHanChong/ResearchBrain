# $π_\texttt{RL}$: Online RL Fine-tuning for Flow-based Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed πRL, an online reinforcement learning framework designed to fine-tune flow-based Vision-Language-Action models. This framework resolves issues with log-likelihood estimation and action stochasticity, achieving up to 31.0% performance gains and enabling zero-shot sim-to-real transfer for robotic manipulation.

## Problem

- Large-scale expert demonstration datasets for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) of VLA models are costly and time-consuming to collect.
- SFT-trained models tend to overfit expert demonstrations, limiting generalization and robustness in real-world scenarios.
- Flow-based VLA models, such as the π-series, face challenges in online RL due to their inherently deterministic action generation and intractable log-likelihood estimation for standard policy gradient algorithms.

## Method

- **πRL**, an ==online reinforcement learning== framework, was developed for fine-tuning ==flow-based Vision-Language-Action models== (**π0**, **π0.5**, **GR00T**).
- ==Flow-Noise==: Integrates a ==learnable noise network== into the flow matching denoising process, modeling it as a ==discrete-time Markov Decision Process (MDP)== for exact ==log-likelihood computation==.
- ==Flow-SDE==: Converts the deterministic ==ODE== denoising process into a ==Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE)== formulation to inject stochasticity and enable a ==two-layer MDP== for coupling denoising with environmental interaction.

## Results

- πRL yielded performance improvements up to **29.2%** for π0 and **31.0%** for π0.5 over SFT baselines across diverse robotic manipulation benchmarks (LIBERO, ManiSkill, MetaWorld, CALVIN).
- Few-shot SFT followed by πRL optimization with the π0.5 model achieved a **98.3%** success rate on LIBERO, surpassing the **96.9%** success rate of the full-dataset SFT baseline.
- A zero-shot sim-to-real transfer was successfully demonstrated, with an RL-tuned policy achieving a **40%** success rate in the real world compared to the SFT baseline's failure, utilizing a 3D Gaussian Splatting simulator to reduce the visual domain gap.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Flow-based VLA models can be effectively fine-tuned with online reinforcement learning by addressing the challenges of intractable log-likelihoods and deterministic action generation through methods like learnable noise injection or ODE-to-SDE conversion.
> - RL training enhances performance beyond supervised fine-tuning, demonstrating improved robustness and generalized action-level refinement that transfers to out-of-distribution scenarios without catastrophic forgetting.
> - Careful design of the critic architecture, optimal noise levels, and action chunk sizes are critical for stable and effective online RL training of flow-based VLA models.
