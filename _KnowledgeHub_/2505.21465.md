# ID-Align: RoPE-Conscious Position Remapping for Dynamic High-Resolution Adaptation in Vision-Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Peking University developed ID-Align, a method that strategically remaps position IDs in Vision-Language Models to mitigate performance degradation caused by the interaction between dynamic high-resolution adaptation and Rotary Position Embedding. The approach improved overall VLM performance by 0.82% across various benchmarks, with gains of 6.09% in relation reasoning tasks.

## Problem

- Dynamic high-resolution adaptation, widely used in state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models (VLMs), can inadvertently degrade performance when combined with Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE).
- RoPE's long-term decay property causes diminished attention between distant image and text tokens, leading to overlooked visual information.
- The large number of high-resolution image tokens disrupts the crucial spatial correspondence between high-resolution and lower-resolution thumbnail features.

## Method

- ID-Align introduces a ==position ID remapping approach== that assigns identical position IDs to spatially corresponding high-resolution and thumbnail image tokens.
- Text embeddings and thumbnail image embeddings retain their usual sequential position IDs.
- High-resolution tokens are assigned position IDs based on the ==interpolated position ID== of their corresponding thumbnail region, reducing the effective ==positional distance== between related multi-resolution tokens.

## Results

- ID-Align consistently improved LLaVA-Next's performance, with an overall average gain of **0.82%** across MMBench, MMStar, ScienceQA, and VQAv2 benchmarks.
- The method achieved a **6.09%** improvement in Relation Reasoning tasks, demonstrating enhanced understanding of complex visual relationships.
- Attention visualizations confirmed ID-Align leads to more focused attention between text and image tokens, and accurate spatial correspondence between high-resolution and thumbnail features, alongside more stable training dynamics.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) exhibits a practical "long-term decay property" where attention scores diminish with increased positional distance.
> - Combining dynamic high-resolution adaptation with RoPE creates two main issues: impeded image-text interaction and disrupted multi-resolution correspondence due to large positional distances between tokens.
> - Strategically remapping position IDs to assign identical IDs to spatially corresponding tokens across different resolutions effectively reduces these problematic positional distances.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have become increasingly important in artificial intelligence, combining the strengths of large language models with visual understanding capabilities. A popular approach to enhance VLM performance is dynamic high-resolution adaptation, where models process both high-resolution images and lower-resolution thumbnails simultaneously. This technique is employed by state-of-the-art models including LLaVA-Next, Qwen2-VL, InternVL3, and others.

*The dynamic high-resolution pipeline processes input images at multiple resolutions, creating both thumbnail and high-resolution features that are concatenated for improved visual understanding.*

However, researchers Bozhou Li and Wentao Zhang from Peking University have identified a critical issue: this approach can inadvertently hinder model performance when combined with Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE), a widely-used positional encoding method in modern language models. Their paper introduces ID-Align, a simple yet effective solution that strategically remaps position IDs to preserve crucial relationships between multi-resolution image tokens.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

#### 2.1. RoPE and Its Long-Term Decay Property

Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) has become the standard positional encoding method for many large language models due to its ability to capture relative positional relationships. A key characteristic of RoPE is its "long-term decay property" - attention scores between tokens tend to decrease as their positional distance increases.

The authors empirically validated this property by sampling query-key pairs from actual LLM layers and measuring their average inner product across different relative positions. Their findings confirm that RoPE indeed exhibits this decay behavior in practice, contradicting some theoretical arguments that suggested otherwise.

#### 2.2. The Dynamic High-Resolution Challenge

When dynamic high-resolution adaptation is applied, two significant problems emerge:

1. **Impeded Image-Text Interaction**: The large number of image tokens generated from high-resolution processing creates substantial positional distances between image and text tokens. Due to RoPE's long-term decay, distant image tokens receive diminished attention from text tokens, potentially causing important visual information to be overlooked.

2. **Disrupted Multi-Resolution Correspondence**: A fundamental assumption of dynamic high-resolution strategies is that high-resolution tokens should correspond spatially with their thumbnail counterparts. However, RoPE's decay property can weaken this crucial relationship, making it difficult for the model to effectively leverage both global context and fine-grained details.

*Comparison between (a) the original sequential position ID assignment method and (b) ID-Align's approach, which assigns identical position IDs to spatially corresponding tokens across resolutions.*

### 3. Methodology and Approach

#### 3.1. Core Principle

ID-Align addresses these issues through a strategic position ID remapping approach. Instead of assigning sequential position IDs to all tokens, the method assigns identical position IDs to spatially corresponding high-resolution and thumbnail image tokens.

The implementation follows these key steps:

1. **Text and Thumbnail Processing**: Text embeddings and thumbnail image embeddings receive sequential position IDs as usual, maintaining their relative order.

2. **High-Resolution Token Mapping**: Each high-resolution image token is assigned the same position ID as its corresponding thumbnail token. This correspondence is determined by spatial overlap between regions in the high-resolution and thumbnail images.

3. **Interpolation-Based Assignment**: The mapping is achieved through interpolation, where thumbnail position IDs are resized to match the high-resolution feature map dimensions.

#### 3.2. Technical Implementation

The position ID assignment process can be summarized as:

```python
# For text and thumbnail tokens
pos_id = sequential_assignment()

# For high-resolution tokens
pos_id = corresponding_thumbnail_pos_id()
```

This approach effectively reduces the positional distance between related tokens, leveraging RoPE's dependence on position ID differences rather than absolute sequence indices.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

#### 4.1. Performance Improvements

The authors evaluated ID-Align using the LLaVA-Next framework with different base models. When applied to Vicuna-1.5 7B, the method achieved consistent improvements across multiple benchmarks:

- **MMBench**: +1.63% (66.58 → 68.21)
- **MMStar**: +1.71% (36.61 → 38.32)
- **ScienceQA**: +1.29% (69.41 → 70.70)
- **VQAv2**: +0.65% (65.74 → 66.39)
- **Overall Average**: +0.82%

The most significant improvement was observed in **Relation Reasoning** tasks (+6.09%), highlighting ID-Align's effectiveness in understanding complex visual relationships and global information.

#### 4.2. Attention Distribution Analysis

Qualitative analysis through attention visualizations demonstrates ID-Align's effectiveness:

*Attention distribution analysis showing how ID-Align improves focus on spatially corresponding regions between high-resolution and thumbnail images, as well as between text and image tokens.*

- **High-Resolution to Thumbnail Attention**: Without ID-Align, attention from high-resolution regions is scattered across unrelated thumbnail areas. With ID-Align, attention focuses precisely on the corresponding spatial regions.

- **Text to Image Attention**: Similarly, text tokens show more focused attention on relevant image regions when ID-Align is applied, improving multimodal understanding.

#### 4.3. Training Dynamics

The learning curves reveal that models trained with ID-Align exhibit:
- Slightly lower training loss
- Notably lower gradient norms during training
- More stable convergence patterns

These improvements suggest that ID-Align not only enhances performance but also contributes to more stable training dynamics.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

#### 5.1. Addressing a Fundamental Bottleneck

ID-Align tackles a core architectural challenge that arises from the interaction between two widely-used techniques: dynamic high-resolution adaptation and RoPE. By identifying and resolving this specific bottleneck, the method enables VLMs to better leverage their multi-resolution processing capabilities.

#### 5.2. Practical Advantages

The method offers several practical benefits:

1. **Computational Efficiency**: ID-Align doesn't introduce new parameters or significantly alter the number of tokens, making it a lightweight solution.

2. **Broad Applicability**: Since dynamic high-resolution strategies are used across numerous state-of-the-art VLMs, ID-Align has wide potential for adoption.

3. **Compatibility**: The method can be combined with other RoPE modifications, offering opportunities for further improvements.

4. **Easy Integration**: The simple position ID remapping can be readily incorporated into existing VLM architectures.

#### 5.3. Enhanced Multimodal Understanding

The consistent improvements across diverse benchmarks, particularly in relation reasoning and global information tasks, demonstrate ID-Align's contribution to more sophisticated visual understanding. This is crucial for real-world applications requiring detailed analysis of complex visual scenes.

#### 5.4. Limitations and Future Work

The authors acknowledge several limitations that point to future research directions:

1. **Token Compression Techniques**: ID-Align may not be directly compatible with methods that compress visual tokens, as these could disrupt the spatial correspondence relationships the method relies on.

2. **Native Dynamic Resolution Support**: Vision Transformers that inherently support dynamic resolution might not benefit as much from this approach, suggesting the need for specialized adaptations.

3. **Theoretical Analysis**: While empirically validated, further theoretical analysis of the interaction between RoPE and multi-resolution features could provide deeper insights.

The work establishes a foundation for more sophisticated positional encoding strategies in multimodal models, particularly as VLMs continue to scale and incorporate even higher resolutions and longer contexts.

ID-Align represents a targeted solution to a specific but important challenge in modern VLMs, offering a practical path toward more effective multimodal understanding while maintaining computational efficiency and broad applicability.
