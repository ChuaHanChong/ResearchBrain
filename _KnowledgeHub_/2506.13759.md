# Discrete Diffusion in Large Language and Multimodal Models: A Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey provides a systematic overview of Discrete Diffusion Language Models (dLLMs) and Multimodal Language Models (dMLLMs), detailing their mathematical foundations, modeling innovations, and diverse applications. It highlights how these models achieve up to 10 times faster inference and offer enhanced control compared to traditional autoregressive models, establishing them as a promising paradigm for future generative AI.

## Problem

- Autoregressive (AR) models suffer from slow sequential inference, preventing parallel generation of output tokens.
- AR models exhibit limited fine-grained control over output attributes, often requiring complex prompt engineering.
- The static, one-pass perception of input contexts in AR models hinders dynamic, task-aware comprehension.

## Method

- ==Discrete diffusion models== reframe generation as an ==iterative denoising process== over discrete token sequences, enabling parallel decoding for faster inference.
- These models provide enhanced controllability over output properties by treating generation as a denoising or infilling task.
- ==Bidirectional attention== in discrete diffusion models allows for continuous revision of context perception throughout the generation process.

## Results

- The survey systematically consolidates fragmented research on discrete diffusion models, tracing their evolution and categorizing their mathematical frameworks, architectures, and techniques.
- It details sophisticated training paradigms, such as diverse masking strategies, reweighting techniques, distillation, and reinforcement learning, alongside innovative inference methods like unmasking, remasking, and various guidance mechanisms (up to **10x** faster inference).
- The work identifies critical future research directions, including the development of robust infrastructure, further optimization of inference efficiency, and improved security and privacy safeguards tailored for d(M)LLMs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Discrete diffusion models offer a viable alternative to autoregressive models, providing up to 10x faster inference speeds and improved control over generation.
> - The field is built on diverse mathematical foundations, including D3PMs, simplified masked diffusion, and continuous-time frameworks, each offering distinct approaches to the denoising process.
> - Discrete diffusion models have rapidly scaled and diversified across language and multimodal tasks, showcasing competitive performance and expanding applications in areas like reasoning, robotics, and drug discovery.
