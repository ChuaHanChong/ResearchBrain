# MotionCraft: Physics-based Zero-Shot Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Politecnico di Torino developed MOTIONCRAFT, a zero-shot video generation method that animates static images with physically plausible motion by warping the latent space of a pre-trained image diffusion model using optical flows derived from physics simulations. The system generates temporally coherent videos, achieving an average Motion Consistency of 0.8655, which is notably higher than the baseline's 0.6740.

## Problem

- Existing video diffusion models require extensive training data and computational resources, limiting accessibility and flexibility.
- Current text-to-video models struggle with fine-grained control over specific, physically accurate motion dynamics from simple text prompts.
- Zero-shot video generation methods often face challenges in achieving both temporal consistency and synthesizing novel, coherent content within dynamic scenes.

## Method

- Utilizes a pre-trained image diffusion model (==Stable Diffusion==) in a zero-shot, ==autoregressive== pipeline for frame-by-frame video generation.
- Generates ==optical flow== from physics simulators (e.g., fluid dynamics, rigid body motion, ==multi-agent== systems) to explicitly define precise motion dynamics.
- Applies this physics-based optical flow to warp the noise latent space of the diffusion model during the reverse DDIM sampling process.
- Integrates Multiple Cross-Frame Attention (MCFA) for enhanced long-range and short-range temporal consistency and Spatial Noise Map Weighting (Spatial-\eta) to selectively enable stochastic sampling for new content generation.

## Results

- Achieved an average Motion Consistency of **0.8655** (± **0.12**), significantly outperforming the Text2Video-Zero baseline (**0.6740** ± **0.23**), indicating better preservation of content details under motion.
- Qualitatively generated realistic and temporally coherent videos with prescribed fluid dynamics, rigid body motion, and multi-agent behaviors, successfully synthesizing new, plausible scene elements (e.g., new continents) without explicit prompting.
- Ablation studies confirmed that Multiple Cross-Frame Attention (MCFA) and Spatial Noise Map Weighting (Spatial-\eta) are essential for maintaining temporal consistency and accurately creating novel content, respectively.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The optical flow in pixel space shows a strong correlation with flow in the noise latent space of image diffusion models, enabling effective motion application within this abstract representation.
> - Decoupling content generation from motion dynamics by leveraging physics simulators for motion and a powerful image prior for content synthesis leads to more controllable and plausible video outputs.
> - Careful management of attention mechanisms (MCFA) and stochastic noise sampling (Spatial-\eta) within the latent space is critical for maintaining consistency and generating new, coherent content in zero-shot video generation.
