# Vision-OPD: Learning to See Fine Details for Multimodal LLMs via On-Policy Self-Distillation

> [!summary] Summary
> Vision-OPD is a regional-to-global self-distillation framework that enables Multimodal Large Language Models to perceive fine-grained details from full images in a single forward pass. A 9B Vision-OPD model achieved an average score of 79.68% on fine-grained visual understanding benchmarks, surpassing larger open-source models and competing with closed-source MLLMs, all while preserving general multimodal abilities.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models often fail to discern small, crucial details in complex full images, leading to inaccurate responses despite general understanding.
- A "regional-to-global perception gap" shows MLLMs are more accurate when fine-grained evidence is pre-cropped, indicating a difficulty in focusing within a global context rather than a lack of local recognition.
- Current approaches for fine-grained understanding, such as agentic models, suffer from high inference overhead or require extensive external supervision (teacher models, ground-truth labels, reward verifiers).

## Method

- A regional-to-global on-policy self-distillation framework, **Vision-OPD**, is proposed to transfer fine-grained perception from cropped views to full-image processing.
- The method uses the same base MLLM to create a "teacher" (conditioned on an evidence-centered crop) and a "student" (conditioned on the full image).
- The student learns by minimizing the token-level Jensen-Shannon divergence between its own and the EMA-regularized teacher's next-token distributions on student-generated rollouts, using an efficient top-K logits distillation.

## Results

- The 9B Vision-OPD model achieved an average score of **79.68%** across fine-grained visual understanding benchmarks, outperforming larger open-source MLLMs and competing with closed-source models like Gemini-3.1-Pro (**79.25%**).
- Vision-OPD consistently surpassed other training strategies (SFT, RLVR, OPSD) on fine-grained tasks while concurrently preserving or enhancing general multimodal capabilities, avoiding performance degradation.
- Training with Vision-OPD substantially reduced the "regional-to-global perception gap," indicating that models learned to effectively attend to fine-grained details directly from full images in a single forward pass.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - MLLMs exhibit a "regional-to-global perception gap," performing significantly better on cropped, evidence-centered images than on the corresponding full images.
> - An MLLM's own enhanced understanding of cropped regions can be leveraged as an internal, self-supervised signal for distillation, eliminating the need for external teachers or ground-truth labels.
> - On-policy self-distillation, particularly with Exponential Moving Average (EMA) teacher regularization and top-K logits distillation, effectively internalizes fine-grained perception into the MLLM's single-pass processing.
