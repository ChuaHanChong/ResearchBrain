# Intrinsically-Motivated Humans and Agents in Open-World Exploration

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at UC Berkeley's BAIR group compared human (adults, children) and AI agent exploration in the open-ended Crafter environment, finding human progress correlated with Entropy and Empowerment, but not Information Gain. Adults explored more efficiently than agents, and children's goal-oriented private speech showed a positive link to exploration success.

## Problem

- Artificial intelligence agents often lag behind human performance in open-ended exploration, despite incorporating intrinsic rewards inspired by information theory.
- It is unclear which specific information-theoretic intrinsic objectives (Entropy, Information Gain, Empowerment) effectively drive human curiosity and exploration.
- Previous computational studies examining human curiosity were confined to simplified environments, limiting generalizability to complex, real-world scenarios.

## Method

- The study directly compared exploration behaviors of adults, children, and various reinforcement learning agents (intrinsic, extrinsic, random) within the same complex, Minecraft-like 'Crafter' environment.
- It quantified human exploration progress using achievement and map coverage scores, and correlated these with three ==information-theoretic intrinsic objectives== (==Entropy==, ==Information Gain==, ==Empowerment==).
- Audio recordings of human participants were transcribed and analyzed using an LLM to classify ==private speech== utterances, specifically looking for expressions of goals or questions.

## Results

- Adults achieved higher exploration scores and higher levels of Entropy and Empowerment compared to children and intrinsically-motivated AI agents.
- The extrinsic Reward agent generally outperformed the intrinsic agents on most achievement-based metrics and achieved Mean Achievement Scores comparable to adults.
- Children's fraction of goal utterances showed a strong positive correlation (**ρ = 0.8**, **p = 0.005**) with their Mean Achievement Score, while adults' verbalizations did not correlate with performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Human exploration progress consistently correlated with Entropy and Empowerment, suggesting these are more indicative intrinsic objectives for effective exploration than Information Gain.
> - The temporal dynamics of Entropy (rapid initial increase, then plateau) and Empowerment (steady increase) suggest potential strategies for dynamically weighting intrinsic rewards in AI.
> - Children's goal-oriented private speech strongly correlated with their exploration success, indicating a functional role for explicit goal-setting in open-ended tasks.
