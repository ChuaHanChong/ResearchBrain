# DriveDreamer-Policy: A Geometry-Grounded World-Action Model for Unified Generation and Planning

> [!summary] Summary
> DriveDreamer-Policy introduces a geometry-grounded world-action model that unifies depth generation, future video generation, and motion planning for autonomous driving. This model achieved state-of-the-art planning performance on Navsim v1 (PDMS 89.2) and v2 (EPDMS 88.7), while also improving future video generation quality with a FVD reduction of 32.36 and enhancing depth accuracy (AbsRel 8.1).

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) planners often lack explicit modeling of how the future world evolves under different actions, limiting interpretability and reliability in safety-critical scenarios.
- Existing World-Action Models (WAMs) frequently lack explicit geometric grounding, relying on 2D appearance or latent representations, which can lead to geometrically inconsistent future scenarios for planning.
- Autonomous driving is fundamentally a 4D physical process, necessitating accurate 3D geometry evolving over time for tasks like occlusion reasoning and ensuring physically consistent motion.

## Method

- Developed **DriveDreamer-Policy**, a unified ==driving world-action model== that integrates a Large Language Model (==LLM==) with lightweight ==generative experts== for depth, video, and action prediction.
- Implemented a ==causal ordering== in the LLM's query processing (depth → video → action) to ensure subsequent generative experts leverage progressively richer contextual information, including explicit 3D geometry.
- Utilized ==conditional flow matching== for training the generative experts to produce continuous targets for depth maps, future video sequences, and action trajectories, enhancing generation quality and consistency.

## Results

- Achieved state-of-the-art planning performance on Navsim v1 (PDMS **89.2**) and Navsim v2 (EPDMS **88.7**), outperforming existing vision-based, VLA, and world-model-based approaches.
- Demonstrated substantial improvements in future video generation quality, reducing the Fréchet Video Distance (FVD) by **32.36** compared to prior world models (PWM), indicating more temporally consistent predictions.
- Improved depth accuracy with an Absolute Relative Error (AbsRel) of **8.1** and high threshold accuracies (δ₁ of **92.8**), attributed to LLM conditioning that resolves local ambiguities and promotes geometric consistency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicit geometric grounding, achieved through dedicated depth map generation, serves as an effective 3D scaffold that significantly improves the coherence and informativeness of future video predictions.
> - Jointly modeling depth, video, and actions within a single, modular architecture, guided by an LLM, leads to more robust and informed motion planning decisions.
> - The causal conditioning pathway and modular design allow for flexible operating modes, such as planning-only or imagination-enabled planning, while maintaining controllable latency for practical deployment.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

Autonomous driving systems have undergone a notable evolution, transitioning from early handcrafted modular stacks to end-to-end learning systems, and more recently, to advanced Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. These VLA models, often built upon Large Language Models (LLMs), introduce enhanced reasoning capabilities, common-sense knowledge, and instruction-following abilities. However, a limitation of many VLA planners is their primary focus on optimizing action outputs without explicitly modeling future world evolution under different actions. This approach can constrain interpretability and reduce reliability, particularly in safety-critical or rare scenarios where forward-looking reasoning about occlusions and potential hazards is crucial.

In parallel, driving world models have emerged as a significant area of research. These models aim to learn spatio-temporal traffic dynamics directly from extensive sensor data, enabling future observation forecasting (e.g., videos, BEV features). Such capabilities are beneficial for scalable simulation, evaluation of rare events, and controllable synthetic data generation.

Recognizing the complementary strengths of VLA models and driving world models, recent World-Action Models (WAMs) have sought to unify future world generation with motion prediction or planning within a single framework. However, a persistent challenge in many existing WAMs is the lack of explicit geometric grounding in their world components. These models often rely on 2D appearance prediction or latent representations, which may lead to visually plausible but not always maximally informative future scenarios for planning. Consequently, planning systems might not fully leverage structured safety cues such as geometric layout and free-space constraints.

The presented work addresses this gap by observing that autonomous driving is fundamentally a 4D physical process, where 3D geometry changes over time. Therefore, an effective world model should not only synthesize visual appearance but also accurately preserve geometric structure, which is essential for tasks like occlusion reasoning, distance estimation, and ensuring physically consistent motion. Depth-centric modeling is identified as a particularly suitable approach due to its compactness, direct link to geometry, and potential to serve as an explicit scaffold for both future image/video generation and planning decisions. Furthermore, recent advancements in depth foundation models suggest the feasibility of generating high-fidelity depth information without requiring extensive additional data or training dedicated estimators from scratch.

DriveDreamer-Policy positions itself at the intersection of these advancements by integrating depth generation, future video generation, and motion planning. It aims to develop a geometry-grounded world-action model that produces more coherent imagined futures and more informed driving actions, thereby bridging the reasoning and instruction-following capabilities of VLA models with the spatio-temporal modeling and crucial geometric grounding required for robust autonomous driving.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The central objective of the DriveDreamer-Policy research is to develop a unified driving world-action model that integrates depth generation, future video generation, and motion planning within a single, modular architectural framework. This model is designed to facilitate both robust decision-making and comprehensive world understanding for autonomous driving.

The motivation for this work stems from several limitations observed in preceding autonomous driving paradigms:

- **Deficiencies in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Planners:** While VLA models offer rich common-sense knowledge and instruction-following capabilities, most primarily optimize for immediate action outputs. They generally lack explicit mechanisms for modeling how the future world might evolve under alternative actions. This absence of forward-looking reasoning can limit interpretability and reduce reliability in complex, rare, or safety-critical situations where understanding potential future states and hazards (e.g., occlusions) is crucial.
- **Limitations of Existing World-Action Models (WAMs):** Although WAMs endeavor to unify future world generation and planning, many existing approaches primarily model 2D appearance or latent representations of the world. They often exhibit limited geometric grounding, which is considered an essential element for embodied systems operating in the physical world. This can lead to a disconnect where generated future scenarios might be visually plausible but lack the geometric fidelity necessary to inform precise planning decisions, thereby hindering the effective utilization of safety-relevant cues.
- **Fundamental Nature of Autonomous Driving as a 4D Physical Process:** The authors emphasize that autonomous driving fundamentally involves understanding and interacting with a 4D physical environment—3D geometry evolving over time. Therefore, an effective world model must not only synthesize visual appearance but also accurately preserve and utilize geometric structure. Such geometric information is critical for tasks like occlusion reasoning, accurate distance estimation, and ensuring physically consistent motion and interactions within the environment.
- **Leveraging Advances in Depth Modeling:** Recent progress in depth foundation models has made it practical to generate high-fidelity depth maps directly. This development presents an opportunity to explicitly incorporate depth representations into a world-action model, using depth as a compact, geometry-rich scaffold. The motivation is to investigate how this explicit geometric input can benefit both the generation of coherent future video predictions and the robustness of motion planning decisions within a unified system.
- **Achieving Modularity and Controllable Latency:** Beyond performance, a practical system requires efficient compute management. The design aims for a modular architecture that allows for flexible operating modes—such as planning-only, imagination-enabled planning, or full generation for simulation—while maintaining controllable latency, enabling its deployment in real-world driving contexts.

By addressing these motivations, DriveDreamer-Policy seeks to establish a more robust and interpretable framework for autonomous driving that fully leverages an understanding of both scene dynamics and physical geometry.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

DriveDreamer-Policy is designed as a unified driving world-action model that couples a large language model (LLM) with a set of lightweight generative experts. Its architecture jointly supports 3D world representation (depth), 2D world generation (video), and motion planning. The core training principle for the generative experts is conditional flow matching, which enables the generation of continuous targets by learning a time-dependent velocity field to transport a base distribution towards the data distribution along a predefined path.

**Overall Pipeline:**
The system begins with input processing via an LLM, followed by the generation of world and action embeddings. These embeddings then condition three modular generative experts: a depth generator, a video generator, and an action generator. The LLM handles multimodal understanding and provides compact state representations, while the experts produce modality-specific outputs, all mediated by a fixed-size query bottleneck. This design supports flexible operation modes, from planning-only to full generation.

**Input Processing:**
At each decision step, the model takes three primary inputs:
1. **Natural Language Instruction:** Tokenized using the LLM's standard tokenizer.
2. **Multi-view RGB Observations:** Each camera view is encoded by a vision encoder into a sequence of visual patch tokens.
3. **Current Action Context:** Embedded into action tokens via a lightweight action encoder.
These input tokens are augmented with three fixed-size groups of learnable query tokens—depth, video, and action queries—appended in a specific causal order. This structured input provides a stable and compact interface for downstream processing.

**Embeddings Generation with Large Language Model:**
All input and query tokens are processed by the LLM (specifically, Qwen3-VL-2B) to generate contextualized hidden states. A crucial aspect is the imposition of a **causal ordering** across the query groups:
- Depth queries are processed first.
- Video queries can attend to the context provided by depth queries.
- Action queries can attend to both depth and video query contexts.
This causal attention mask establishes a single-pass information flow, allowing subsequent generative experts to leverage progressively richer contextual information.

**World and Action Prediction with Generative Experts:**
1. **Depth Generator:**
 - **Purpose:** To generate a monocular depth map as an explicit 3D scaffold, capturing geometric structure and ambiguity.
 - **Architecture:** A pixel-space diffusion transformer. Generating depth in pixel-space is chosen for its lower dimensionality compared to RGB video and its ability to preserve boundary fidelity.
 - **Training:** Uses a flow-matching objective. Ground-truth depth (from Depth Anything 3, DA3) is corrupted with noise, and the denoiser learns to predict the denoising update.
 - **Conditioning:** The denoiser takes as input the concatenation of the noisy depth and the corresponding RGB image. It is further conditioned on the LLM's world depth embeddings via cross-attention, which guides the diffusion process to maintain global structural consistency while recovering fine-grained details.
2. **Video Generator:**
 - **Purpose:** To generate action-conditioned future video sequences.
 - **Architecture:** A text-image-to-video diffusion transformer (initialized from Wan-2.1-T2V-1.3B) operating in latent space.
 - **Process:** Current RGB images are first encoded into a compact latent representation using a VAE. This initializes a sequence of noisy video latents for the target prediction horizon.
 - **Conditioning:** Instead of standard text embeddings, the diffusion model is conditioned on the LLM's world video embeddings. These embeddings encapsulate language instructions, multi-view perception, action context, and importantly, upstream geometric cues derived from the depth queries. Cross-attention integrates these embeddings into the denoiser. Additionally, a lightweight visual condition extracted from the current image via a CLIP model is concatenated to the world video embeddings, ensuring grounding in current appearance and identity.
3. **Action Generator:**
 - **Purpose:** To produce a feasible future action sequence (trajectory).
 - **Architecture:** A standalone diffusion transformer.
 - **Conditioning:** It is conditioned on the action embedding generated by the LLM from the action query tokens. This embedding integrates instruction semantics, multi-view observations, and both geometric (from depth) and imagination (from video) cues.
 - **Trajectory Representation:** Trajectory states are parameterized using a continuous representation (x, y, cos θ, sin θ) to ensure smooth dynamics and avoid angular wrap-around issues.
 - **Flexibility:** The action generator can be activated independently for planning tasks, implicitly benefiting from the rich, predicted future world context learned through the overall architecture.

**Training Details:**
- **Depth Normalization:** Depth maps are log-transformed and then normalized to a stable range of [-0.5, 0.5] using per-map percentiles, with inverse transformation at inference.
- **Model Initialization:** Qwen3-VL-2B for the LLM, PPD for the depth generator, and Wan-2.1-T2V-1.3B for the video generator (adapted for image-to-video).
- **Resolution and Horizon:** Fine-tuned at a spatial resolution of 144 × 256 for depth and video, with a video training horizon of 9 frames.
- **Joint Multi-task Loss:** All components are trained simultaneously using a joint loss: $\mathcal{L} = \lambda_d \mathcal{L}_d + \lambda_v \mathcal{L}_v + \lambda_a \mathcal{L}_a$, with $\lambda_d = 0.1$ and other weights at 1.0.
- **Optimization:** Trained for 100k steps with a batch size of 32 on 8 NVIDIA H20 GPUs, using the AdamW optimizer with a learning rate of $1 \times 10^{-5}$.
- **Query Configuration:** Default configuration uses 64 depth-query tokens, 64 video-query tokens, and 8 action-query tokens. No additional datasets beyond Navsim training data or extra pre-training beyond the initialized backbones are used.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of DriveDreamer-Policy encompassed both closed-loop planning performance and world generation quality on the Navsim v1 and v2 benchmarks.

**Planning Performance Comparison:**
DriveDreamer-Policy was compared against state-of-the-art methods across three categories: vision-based end-to-end models, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) planners, and world-model-based planners.
- **Navsim v1:** The model achieved a Predictive Driver Model Score (PDMS) of 89.2, outperforming all considered baseline methods. It also demonstrated strong performance on critical planning sub-scores, including a Drivable Area Compliance (DAC) of 97.1 and an Ego Progress (EP) of 83.5.
- **Navsim v2:** On this extended benchmark, DriveDreamer-Policy obtained an Extended Predictive Driver Model Score (EPDMS) of 88.7, surpassing existing world-model-based approaches. It exhibited robust performance on sub-metrics such as Direction Compliance (DDC) of 99.5 and Lane Keeping (LK) of 97.6. These results indicate the model's ability to generate controlled and compliant planning trajectories.

**World Generation Performance Comparison:**
- **Video Quality:** For future video generation, DriveDreamer-Policy was compared against PWM. The model achieved an LPIPS of 0.20, PSNR of 21.05, and a Fréchet Video Distance (FVD) of 53.59. Notably, the FVD score represented a substantial improvement (32.36 lower) over PWM, indicating higher quality and more temporally consistent video predictions.
- **Depth Accuracy:** The model's depth generation capabilities were evaluated against PPD, a model from which its depth generator was initialized. DriveDreamer-Policy achieved an Absolute Relative Error (AbsRel) of 8.1 and high threshold accuracies ($\delta_1$ of 92.8, $\delta_2$ of 98.6, $\delta_3$ of 99.5). These metrics indicate lower depth error compared to both zero-shot and fine-tuned PPD variants. This improvement is attributed to the LLM conditioning, which aids in resolving local ambiguities and fostering more geometrically consistent depth estimations.

**Ablation Studies:**
1. **Ablations on World Learning for Planning:** Four variants were tested to determine the contribution of different world modeling modalities to planning performance:
 - **Action-Only:** A baseline without any explicit world modeling, achieving a PDMS of 88.0.
 - **Depth+Action:** Incorporating depth generation, raising PDMS to 88.5.
 - **Video+Action:** Incorporating video generation, resulting in a PDMS of 88.9.
 - **Depth+Video+Action (Full Model):** The complete proposed model, yielding the highest PDMS of 89.2.
 These results indicate that all forms of world learning contribute positively to planning performance, with the combined effect of both depth and video generation providing the most substantial gains, suggesting their complementary roles in providing comprehensive world information.
2. **Ablations on Depth Learning for Video Generation:** A comparison between a video-only variant and one where depth was jointly learned and causally conditioned on the video generator demonstrated the impact of depth on video quality.
 - **Video-Only:** Achieved an FVD of 65.82.
 - **Depth+Video:** Achieved an FVD of 53.59.
 This reduction in FVD signifies that incorporating depth learning improves video generation accuracy, reinforcing the idea that depth acts as an effective 3D scaffold for producing more coherent future video predictions.
3. **Ablations on Number of Queries:** The study investigated the effect of the query budget on performance. Reducing the number of query tokens (e.g., from 64 depth/video and 8 action to 32 depth/video and 4 action) led to a slight decrease in both planning (PDMS from 89.2 to 88.9) and world generation metrics (FVD from 53.59 to 57.97, AbsRel from 8.1 to 9.7). This suggests that a larger query budget provides higher-capacity slots for storing relevant context, which is beneficial for overall model performance.

**Qualitative Results:**
Visualizations demonstrated that DriveDreamer-Policy generates spatially stable depth maps, coherent future video frames, and effective planning actions. The depth-conditioned imagination was shown to mitigate common failure modes such as short-horizon collision risks and off-road drifts. Planning trajectories were observed to align well with human driving behavior and effectively manage speed. Comparisons across ablation variants further illustrated how world learning, particularly with both depth and video, contributes to safer and more human-like trajectories, enhancing safety margins, correcting maneuvers, and aligning with expert paths more closely than action-only approaches.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The DriveDreamer-Policy research introduces a significant contribution to the field of autonomous driving by bridging a critical gap in existing world-action models: the integration of explicit geometric grounding. By jointly performing depth generation, future video imagination, and motion planning within a single, modular framework, the work offers several layers of significance and potential impact:

- **Enhanced Geometric Grounding for World Models:** The explicit generation and utilization of depth maps provide an essential 3D scaffold for understanding the driving environment. This addresses a limitation of many prior world-action models that primarily relied on 2D appearance or latent representations, which often lacked the necessary geometric fidelity for robust physical interaction and decision-making. This capability is crucial for accurately perceiving occlusions, estimating distances, and ensuring physically consistent motion.
- **Improved Coherence and Informativeness of Imagined Futures:** The causal conditioning pathway (depth → video → action) ensures that future video predictions benefit from an explicit 3D understanding. This structured information flow enables the model to generate more coherent and geometrically plausible imagined futures, which are directly more informative for downstream planning tasks.
- **More Robust and Informed Motion Planning:** By leveraging both explicit scene geometry (depth) and predicted future dynamics (video), the model's action generator can produce more robust and informed driving actions. The ablation studies confirm that incorporating world learning, especially with both depth and video, consistently improves planning performance and safety margins. This is particularly impactful for safety-critical scenarios and rare events where anticipation and forward-looking reasoning are paramount.
- **Unified and Modular Architecture for Practical Deployment:** The design that couples a large language model with lightweight generative experts connected through a compact query interface offers flexibility. It enables various operating modes, such as planning-only (for low-latency control), imagination-enabled planning (when foresight is critical), or full generation (for offline simulation and data synthesis), while maintaining controllable latency. This modularity could facilitate easier integration into existing autonomous driving stacks and enable more efficient resource allocation.
- **Advancements in Autonomous Driving Benchmarking:** Achieving state-of-the-art performance on comprehensive benchmarks like Navsim v1 and v2, across both planning and world generation metrics, establishes a new benchmark for geometry-grounded world-action models. This sets a higher standard for future research and provides a robust baseline for comparison.
- **Potential for Scalable Data Generation and Simulation:** The model's ability to generate multimodal data, including depth, future video, and actions, holds significant potential for scalable simulation and the creation of diverse synthetic training data. This could alleviate challenges associated with collecting real-world data, especially for rare and complex scenarios, thereby accelerating the development and testing of autonomous systems.
- **Increased Interpretability:** By explicitly visualizing the model's predicted depth and future scene evolution, the approach contributes to greater interpretability of autonomous driving systems. Understanding what the model "sees" and "imagines" can be crucial for debugging, validation, and building trust in self-driving technologies.

In conclusion, DriveDreamer-Policy represents a step towards more capable and reliable autonomous driving systems by deeply integrating geometric understanding into the core of world-action modeling. Its architecture and performance highlight the importance of 3D contextual reasoning for both generating realistic future scenarios and executing safe, informed driving behaviors.
