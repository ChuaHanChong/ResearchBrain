# AffordSim: A Scalable Data Generator and Benchmark for Affordance-Aware Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> AffordSim introduces a simulation framework integrating open-vocabulary 3D affordance prediction into robotic manipulation data generation. It automatically produces semantically correct interaction trajectories for complex tasks, and enhances sim-to-real transfer through novel domain randomization, including 3D Gaussian Splatting for photorealistic backgrounds.

## Problem

- Existing simulation platforms for robotic manipulation lack object affordance information within their trajectory generation pipelines, leading to physically plausible but semantically inappropriate robot interactions.
- Current methods for grasp pose estimation in simulation are either labor-intensive (manual design) or generic (e.g., AnyGrasp), failing to consistently identify task-relevant functional regions for complex manipulation tasks.
- Standalone 3D affordance prediction models had not been systematically integrated into the simulation data generation process for robotic control.

## Method

- **AffordSim** integrates the ==VoxAfford== model for open-vocabulary 3D affordance detection, enabling per-point affordance scoring on object point clouds to guide manipulation.
- It employs an ==affordance-guided grasp selection== mechanism that combines VoxAfford predictions with kinematic feasibility, followed by GPU-accelerated collision-free motion planning.
- A VLM-powered pipeline automates task and scene generation from natural language descriptions, and comprehensive domain randomization, including novel ==3D Gaussian Splatting== backgrounds, enhances sim-to-real transfer.

## Results

- AffordSim successfully generated affordance-guided trajectories for four robot embodiments with high generation success rates ranging from **83**% to **95**%.
- On a **50**-task benchmark, affordance-guided trajectories (VoxAfford) achieved an average **61**% success rate, outperforming generic grasp estimation (**20**%) but indicating a gap for certain complex tasks compared to manual design (**87**%).
- Zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a real Franka FR3 robot showed **60**% success for grasping but only **10****-20**% for affordance-demanding tasks like mug hanging and pouring, confirming the inherent task difficulty and sim-to-real transferability.
- Policies trained with domain randomization improved average real-world success from **17**% (no DR) to **27**% and demonstrated robustness to visually altered real scenes.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating 3D affordance prediction directly into the simulation data generation process significantly improves the semantic correctness and task relevance of generated manipulation trajectories.
> - Tasks requiring fine-grained affordance understanding, such as pouring into narrow containers or mug hanging, pose substantial challenges for current imitation learning methods, even with affordance-aware data.
> - Domain randomization, particularly with photorealistic 3D Gaussian reconstructed backgrounds, is crucial for achieving robust zero-shot sim-to-real transfer of policies trained in simulation.
