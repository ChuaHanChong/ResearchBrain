# AffordSim: A Scalable Data Generator and Benchmark for Affordance-Aware Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> AffordSim is a simulation framework for robotic manipulation that integrates open-vocabulary 3D affordance prediction to guide grasp generation, leading to task-relevant data. It introduces a 50-task benchmark to evaluate affordance reasoning and employs comprehensive domain randomization, including 3D Gaussian Splatting for backgrounds, enabling policies trained on its data to achieve up to 25% average zero-shot success on a real robot.

## Problem

- Generating scalable training data for robotic manipulation tasks is challenging, as current methods rely on labor-intensive manual contact annotations or generic grasp estimators lacking task semantics.
- Generic grasp estimators often select stable but functionally irrelevant grasps on objects, leading to task failures, and do not inherently provide information for complex object-to-object interactions.
- Existing domain randomization techniques often use synthetic textures and simplified backgrounds, creating a noticeable appearance gap that hinders zero-shot sim-to-real transfer.

## Method

- **AffordSim** integrates a ==VLM-powered== module for automated scene generation and affordance query extraction from natural language descriptions.
- It employs an ==affordance-aware trajectory collection== pipeline that uses an ==open-vocabulary 3D affordance model== (**VoxAfford**) to predict functional regions, followed by a ==two-stage grasp selection== process to ensure task-relevant grasp planning.
- A comprehensive ==domain randomization== module, featuring ==cross-viewpoint background replacement== using ==3D Gaussian Splatting== from real photographs, is included to enhance sim-to-real transfer capabilities.

## Results

- On the 50-task benchmark, AffordSim's affordance-guided trajectory collection reaches per-tier success of **79%** (medium) and **64%** (hard) — versus generic AnyGrasp's **15%** and **3%** — recovering **93%** of the manual-annotation success rate without any per-object annotation.
- On the 20-task simulation benchmark, the strongest policies reach **56%** (Pi 0.5) and **54%** (VLA-adapter) overall; the Pi 0.5–BC gap widens to nearly **30** points on hard tasks, with failures concentrated in precise post-grasp alignment and long-horizon action chains.
- Zero-shot sim-to-real on a real Franka FR3 (10 trials/task, no fine-tuning) averages **24%** — Pi 0.5 **25%**, VLA-adapter **23%**, DP **8%**, ACT **3%**, BC **0%** — with a per-task gradient of placing up to **40%**, pouring **30%**, and hanging only **10%**, confirming the bottleneck is affordance difficulty, not a simulation artifact.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly integrating 3D affordance prediction directly into the data generation process is crucial for producing task-relevant manipulation trajectories, significantly outperforming generic grasp estimators on affordance-critical tasks.
> - The accuracy and generalization capabilities of the underlying 3D affordance prediction model are primary factors influencing overall task success, especially for tasks requiring fine-grained affordance understanding.
> - Advanced domain randomization, particularly the use of 3D Gaussian Splatting for realistic background replacement, effectively bridges the sim-to-real appearance gap, enabling successful zero-shot transfer for trained policies.
