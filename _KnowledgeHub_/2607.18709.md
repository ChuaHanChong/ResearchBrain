# RoboInter1.5: A Holistic Intermediate Representation Suite for Embodied World Modeling and Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> RoboInter1.5 introduces a comprehensive suite of data, benchmarks, and models that establish dense intermediate representations as a core interface for embodied AI. The framework demonstrates that these representations enhance VLM capabilities for manipulation, improve robotic policy generalization with up to 77.3% real-world success rates, and enable more reliable, long-horizon world modeling, boosting generative quality metrics like PSNR from 18.26 to 21.05.

## Problem

- Existing robot datasets are costly, embodiment-specific, and lack the dense, fine-grained annotations (Intermediate Representations - IRs) needed for advanced reasoning and robust execution.
- Current Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies and embodied world models struggle with generalization and reliability due to the scarcity of high-fidelity, structured IRs.
- There is a need for a unified, bidirectional interface to seamlessly connect high-level semantic understanding with low-level physical interaction and future state simulation in robotics.

## Method

- Developed **RoboInter-Data**, a large-scale dataset of 230k manipulation episodes featuring dense, per-frame, human-verified annotations for over ten types of manipulation-oriented ==intermediate representations (IRs)==.
- Introduced **RoboInter-VLM**, a ==VLM Planner== trained on specific ==VQA tasks== derived from RoboInter-Data to understand and generate these IRs, serving as a shared source for downstream modules.
- Designed **RoboInter-VLA** and **RoboInter-World** frameworks that leverage IRs to regularize low-level action spaces for robotic control and constrain ==latent rollouts== of open-world physical simulators for reliable, long-horizon prediction.

## Results

- The RoboInter-VLM Planner achieved substantial performance gains on third-party embodied reasoning benchmarks (e.g., **76.8%** on RoboRef It over RoboBrain2.0-7B) and high accuracy on custom RoboInter-VQA tasks (e.g., **75.1%** Object Grounding, **81.9%** Visual Trace generation).
- RoboInter-VLA models, conditioned on IRs, demonstrated improved open-loop action scores (mOLS up to **0.3543** with modular text F-CoT vs. **0.3086** for the vanilla baseline) and enhanced real-world closed-loop success rates (ID **77.3%** vs. **65.0%** for vanilla, OOD **58.3%** vs. **38.3%**).
- RoboInter-World, guided by IRs (Seg+Trace), showed a notable increase in generative quality (PSNR from **18.26** to **21.05** for a 14B model) and improved long-horizon physical realism, with its predicted latents also enhancing VLA action accuracy.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Intermediate representations function as a powerful bidirectional interface, both regularizing low-level action spaces for VLA policies and constraining latent rollouts of open-world physical simulators for embodied world models.
> - Dense, high-fidelity, manipulation-oriented IRs significantly enhance spatial-temporal reasoning and generation capabilities in VLMs, improve action execution in VLA policies, and enable more accurate and physically realistic long-horizon world modeling.
> - Visually encoding IRs (e.g., rendering object points and gripper traces into a 'control video') provides strong spatial priors that substantially improve the convergence and fidelity of embodied world models, outperforming direct numerical coordinate injection.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How this Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of embodied intelligence has seen advancements driven by large foundation models, leading to progress in vision-language-action (VLA) policies and embodied world models (WMs). However, a significant challenge remains in the form of existing robot datasets. These datasets are often expensive to curate, specific to particular embodiments, and frequently lack the fine-grained annotations necessary for generalizable reasoning, robust execution, or long-horizon environment dynamics simulation.

Current VLA policies, whether implicit or explicit, aim to bridge high-level reasoning with low-level control. Implicit methods directly regress actions, while explicit methods often employ chain-of-thought (CoT) planning using language. Simultaneously, embodied world models aim to simulate environmental evolution under physical interactions, transitioning from unconstrained visual hallucinations to reliable dynamics synthesis. Both domains increasingly leverage structured intermediate representations (IRs) to address the generalization gap and enhance reliability.

However, the effectiveness of this shared paradigm is constrained by the scarcity of high-quality, densely annotated IRs in existing datasets. Efforts to automate annotation often encounter limitations such as sensitivity to distribution shifts, lack of precise physical alignment, or temporal misalignment with actions. This absence of large-scale, high-fidelity IR annotations bottlenecks the development of both VLA models and embodied world models.

RoboInter1.5 positions itself as a resource designed to address these limitations by providing a unified suite of data, benchmarks, and models centered on dense, manipulation-oriented IRs. It builds upon previous work, RoboInter1.0, extending its scope and capabilities. The work aims to establish intermediate representations as a universal interface connecting semantic understanding, physical execution, and future simulation, distinct from treating them merely as interpretable signals.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations for developing RoboInter1.5 are:

1. **Address the limitations of existing robot datasets for generalizable robotics:** Current robot datasets are costly to curate, often tailored to specific robot embodiments, and lack the dense, fine-grained structural annotations essential for advanced reasoning, robust execution, and accurate physical world modeling. This deficit prevents VLA policies and generative world models from fully inheriting the broad semantic reasoning capabilities of web-scale multimodal foundation models.

2. **Establish intermediate representations as a unified, bidirectional interface:** The paper aims to move beyond treating intermediate representations merely as interpretable signals. Instead, it conceptualizes them as a "bidirectional interface" that can both regularize low-level action spaces (for VLA policies) and constrain the latent rollouts of open-world physical simulators (for embodied world models). This unification is intended to seamlessly connect semantic understanding, physical execution, and future simulation.

3. **Provide a comprehensive suite of data, benchmarks, and models for manipulation-oriented IRs:** The project's motivation is to curate and make available a holistic resource that facilitates research into IRs. This includes:
 - **RoboInter-Data:** A large-scale dataset featuring dense, per-frame annotations across a wide range of manipulation-oriented intermediate representations, combining scalability with high fidelity through human-in-the-loop verification. This aims to overcome the noise and limitations of purely automated annotation pipelines.
 - **RoboInter-VQA:** A benchmark and dataset for enhancing the spatial-temporal reasoning capabilities of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) in embodied scenes, specifically tailored for understanding and generating manipulation-oriented IRs.
 - **RoboInter-VLA:** An integrated framework for robotic control that investigates how IRs benefit action execution across implicit, explicit, and modular plan-then-execute paradigms, aiming to improve policy generalization and controllability.
 - **RoboInter-World:** A controllable embodied world model that leverages IRs as structured conditioning signals to enable reliable, long-horizon prediction of future world states, mitigating issues of visual hallucination in generative models.

4. **Advance methodological capabilities and evaluation for embodied world modeling:** Specifically, RoboInter1.5 extends the prior RoboInter1.0 by introducing:
 - **RoboInter-CV:** A novel long-horizon benchmark for embodied world modeling, explicitly designed to utilize intermediate representations as primary guidance for world models in complex manipulation tasks.
 - **RoboInter-World:** A new world model architecture specifically designed for intermediate representation guidance to achieve superior generalization and physical realism in video generation tasks.
 - **Extended Comprehensive Evaluation:** New experiments to rigorously evaluate world models and their ability to guide VLA models, providing insights into how IRs enhance performance and physical realism.

In essence, the overarching motivation is to bridge the gap between high-level semantic reasoning and low-level physical interaction in robotics by providing high-quality, structured intermediate representations, thereby fostering more generalizable, robust, and controllable embodied AI systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

RoboInter1.5 introduces a holistic suite encompassing data, benchmarks, and models, all centered on dense intermediate representations. The methodology is structured around several interconnected components:

#### 3.1. RoboInter-Data

This dataset forms the foundation of the suite.
- **Data Collection:** Raw manipulation data is gathered from diverse sources, including "In-the-Wild" settings (e.g., Droid, RH20T, OXE) for scene and instruction diversity, and "Table-Top" settings (e.g., RH20T) for high quality and skill diversity. This results in 230k manipulation episodes, primarily third-person videos.
- **Annotations & Check with RoboInter-Tool:** A semi-automatic GUI tool (RoboInter-Tool) is used for per-frame annotation:
 - **Task Decomposition & Key-frame Annotation:** Videos are segmented into clips based on 15 predefined primitive skills. ChatGPT provides preliminary language annotations, which human annotators refine for clip-level and video-level descriptions. Contact frames (robot arm contacting object) are recorded.
 - **Manipulated Object Recognition:** The manipulated object is identified, and annotations are fed to SAM2 for automatic segmentation and tracking. A re-annotation and inspection mechanism is used to ensure high fidelity.
 - **End-effector Locating:** Given the frequent lack of reliable camera parameters, a calibration matrix is estimated, and gripper detection and point tracking are employed to reconstruct accurate 2D end-effector traces.
- **Post-processed Annotations:** From the raw annotations, additional IRs are derived: grasp affordance boxes (from 2D end-effector at contact), contact points (pre-defined gripper key points), grasp poses (6D end-effector pose), target placement locations (object position at subtask end), and gripper bounding boxes (projected 3D anchor points).
- **Statistics:** RoboInter-Data comprises 230k episodes, 6 robot arm types, 571 scene types, and 15 primitive skills, with dense annotations including ~61M object grounding frames, ~70M gripper trace frames, 190k affordance/placement proposals, and 760k language clip annotations. All annotations are temporally synchronized with executed actions, robot states, and two-view visual observations.

#### 3.2. RoboInter-VQA

This component converts RoboInter-Data annotations into VQA tasks to train VLM capabilities.
- **VQA Task Construction:** Tasks are categorized by intermediate representation type (spatial vs. temporal) and target capability (understanding vs. generation).
 - **Spatial VQA (Understanding):** Includes selection tasks (object bounding box, grasp pose, scene-instruction matching) and judgment tasks (contact occurrence).
 - **Spatial VQA (Generation):** Five prediction tasks for generating spatial IRs (object bounding box, grasp pose, placement proposal, key points, gripper bounding box).
 - **Temporal VQA (Understanding):** Selection tasks for gripper movement directions, trace-description matching, subtask/primitive discrimination, execution stage identification, and judgment tasks for task success/next-step feasibility.
 - **Temporal VQA (Generation):** Tasks requiring trace generation and multi-step planning, conditioned on varying contextual completeness (e.g., past subtasks, overall instructions).
- **Statistics:** Approximately 1M spatial generation entries, 172k spatial understanding entries, 131k temporal generation entries, and 935k temporal understanding entries. Evaluation is conducted on a designated subset of 7,246 videos to prevent data leakage.

#### 3.3. RoboInter-CV

A dataset designed for long-horizon embodied world modeling.
- **Data Construction:** Derived from RoboInter-Data. Object points are sampled from segmentation masks and tracked, and rendered with gripper traces into a "control video" on a black canvas, aligned with original observations. Filters are applied to ensure validity of visual controls (non-trivial object masks, non-empty traces).
- **Data Statistics:** 65k clip-level samples from 16.9k unique episodes, each paired with control video, gripper trace, action sequence, language, subtask, RGB observation, and future latent chunk. These control videos, preserving object-centric spatial cues and gripper motion, serve as dense visual conditions for RoboInter-World.

#### 3.4. RoboInter-VLM Planner

The central VLM that learns to understand and generate IRs.
- **VLMs as Planner:** Adopts VLM architectures (e.g., Qwen-VL, LLaVA-One-Vision) supporting single- and multi-image inputs, comprising an LLM, a vision encoder, and an MLP projector. Trained with a co-training strategy using VQA formulation, optimized via cross-entropy loss for autoregressive generation.
- **Role:** Acts as a shared source of intermediate representations for downstream modules, either through implicit feature transfer, explicit joint reasoning, or modular conditioning.

#### 3.5. RoboInter-VLA: Plan-Then-Execute

A family of VLA models employing a plan-then-execute paradigm.
- **Executor Architecture:** Built on a Qwen2.5-VL backbone with a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) action head. It uses an information aggregator to compress hidden states and IRs into conditioning features. It consumes multi-view visual observations, language instructions, and IRs to produce multi-step action chunks via a diffusion loss.
- **Plan-Then-Execute Paradigms:** Three variants leverage the pretrained Planner:
 - **RoboInter-IC-E2E (Implicitly-Conditioned End-to-End):** Directly injects the VLM from the Planner into the Executor, using it as a stronger vision-language feature extractor.
 - **RoboInter-EC-E2E (Explicitly-Conditioned End-to-End):** Executor is initialized with the Planner's VLM and jointly optimizes reasoning and action generation.
 - **RoboInter-Modular (Modular Planner-to-Executor):** Planner and Executor are independent modules. Executor conditions on ground-truth IRs during training and Planner-predicted IRs during inference.
- **Flexible Chain-of-Thought (F-CoT):** Composed of multiple IRs (subtasks, skills, object boxes, affordance boxes, motion traces, etc.) in textual or visual form. F-CoT acts as VQA supervision for the Planner and action-aligned guidance for the Executor.

#### 3.6. RoboInter-World: Controlled Imagination

An embodied world model for future observation generation.
- **Denoising Model:** Given history frames and IR controls, it generates future observations in the latent video space. The denoising model $f_\theta(x_t, t | z_{\text{hist}}, c, a, u)$ predicts flow target, where $x_t$ is noisy future latent, $z_{\text{hist}}$ is history latent, $c$ is language, $a$ is action sequence, and $u$ is the control video (rendered IRs).
- **Control Video from Intermediate Representations:** Object points from segmentation masks and 2D gripper traces are explicitly rendered onto a blank canvas, temporally aligned with observations. This visual encoding exploits strong spatial priors in pretrained vision models, improving convergence and avoiding noise from direct numerical coordinate injection.
- **World Model Architecture:** A latent video diffusion transformer processes heterogeneous inputs:
 - Language and actions are encoded and concatenated for cross-attention context.
 - VAE-encoded control latent and a mask latent (distinguishing history/future) are concatenated with video latents.
 - Flattened spatio-temporal tokens, along with CLIP-based reference image tokens, are fed to the DiT module.
- **Control Protocol:** During training, a teacher-forcing setup with stochastic perturbations to IRs enhances robustness. During inference, two protocols are used: oracle-control (ground-truth IRs) and planner-control (Planner-generated IRs). The world model can also guide VLA models by feeding predicted image latents directly into the executor's action head.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of RoboInter1.5 encompasses benchmarking the VLM Planner, open-loop and closed-loop evaluations of the VLA Executor, and comprehensive assessment of the RoboInter-World model.

#### 4.1. Benchmarking the Planner (RoboInter-VLM)

- **Enhanced Grounding and Embodied Capability:** RoboInter-Qwen and RoboInter-LLaVAOV models demonstrated improved performance on third-party spatial reasoning benchmarks (Where2Place, RoboRef It) and temporal task planning (RoboVQA) compared to their base models and RoboBrain2.0. For instance, RoboInter-Qwen-7B showed gains of 76.8% on RoboRef It and 42.8% on RoboVQA over RoboBrain2.0-7B. Grounding performance on Refcoco also improved. General VLM capabilities remained stable, indicating that embodied VQA training specifically enhances manipulation-oriented reasoning without significant degradation of general abilities.
- **RoboInter-VQA Benchmark Performance:** On the custom RoboInter-VQA benchmark, general VLMs and closed-source APIs (e.g., GPT4o-mini, Gemini-2.5-flash) generally exhibited limited ability to generate accurate intermediate representations (typically below 40% accuracy) or future traces. In contrast, RoboInter-VLM variants consistently achieved higher scores across both spatial and temporal generation and understanding tasks. For example, RoboInter-Qwen-7B achieved 75.1% in Object Grounding and 81.9% in Visual Trace generation, outperforming baselines significantly.

#### 4.2. Open-Loop Evaluation of the Executor (RoboInter-VLA)

- **Planner Improves Action Generation:** In the "In-the-Wild" setting, RoboInter-IC-E2E, which implicitly incorporates the pretrained VLM Planner, achieved a higher mean Open-Loop Score (mOLS) (0.3218) than the Vanilla baseline (0.3086), indicating enhanced learning capability. Explicitly conditioned EC-E2E further improved mOLS (0.3340).
- **Modular Architectures and F-CoT:** RoboInter-Te-Modular (textual F-CoT) surpassed EC-E2E (0.3543 vs. 0.3340), suggesting that decoupling planning and execution can be beneficial. The Oracle+Executor, which uses ground-truth IRs, achieved the highest mOLS (0.3861), confirming the informativeness and stability of the annotations.
- **Convergence Speed:** In the "Table-Top" setting, RoboInter-Te-Modular and Oracle+Executor converged faster and reached higher performance, with EC-E2E eventually approaching their performance.

- **Ablations on Intermediate Representations:** Finer-grained IRs provided larger improvements. Coarse-grained representations (Subtask, Primitive Skill) offered marginal gains. Spatially grounded representations (Object Box, Gripper Box, Affordance) yielded substantial improvements. The 2D motion Trace provided the most significant enhancement, contributing to the strongest overall performance.

#### 4.3. Closed-Loop Real-World Evaluation of the Executor (RoboInter-VLA)

- **Improved Generalization:** In real-world closed-loop experiments on a Franka Research-3 arm, RoboInter-IC-E2E consistently outperformed the Vanilla baseline in both in-distribution (ID) and out-of-distribution (OOD) tasks (e.g., Object Collecting, Cup Stacking, Towel Folding, Clutter Cleaning). IC-E2E achieved an average ID success rate of 77.3% vs. Vanilla's 65.0%, and an OOD rate of 58.3% vs. 38.3%.
- **OOD Robustness of Explicit Conditioning:** RoboInter-EC-E2E, while having a slightly lower ID success rate than IC-E2E (68.3% vs. 77.3%), demonstrated better OOD performance and a smaller ID-to-OOD drop (8.3% vs. 19.0%), suggesting that explicit intermediate reasoning contributes to OOD robustness.

#### 4.4. Benchmarking on World Modeling (RoboInter-World)

- **Impact of Intermediate Representations:** Replacing raw action conditioning with dense intermediate representations (Inter, combining 2D traces and segmentation masks) substantially boosted generative quality metrics. For a 14B model, PSNR improved from 18.26 to 21.05, and LPIPS decreased from 0.171 to 0.102. This structural advantage was consistent across model scales (1.3B and 14B) and fine-tuning strategies (LoRA and Full Fine-Tuning).
- **Context and Prediction Horizons:** IRs (Seg+Trace) proved crucial for long-horizon predictions and scenarios with limited historical context. While action-only baselines performed adequately for short-term predictions with ample history, Seg+Trace provided essential geometric priors that prevented error accumulation and structural degradation over extended sequences, especially when historical context was sparse.
- **Modality Ablation Study:** Both segmentation masks (Seg) and 2D traces (Trace) independently outperformed action-conditioned baselines. Their combination (Seg+Trace) achieved the highest fidelity, indicating synergistic contributions: segmentation masks enforced object-centric spatial constraints, and 2D traces imposed temporal kinematics.
- **Evaluation of Control Protocols:** The planner-control protocol (using Planner-generated IRs) consistently and decisively outperformed action-conditioned baselines, even with inevitable noise. For instance, the 14B model achieved 20.17 PSNR with planner-control versus 18.26 PSNR with action-only. The oracle-control protocol (ground-truth IRs) provided the upper bound (21.06 PSNR).
- **Qualitative Analysis:** RoboInter-World, guided by IRs, demonstrated more accurate physical simulation of object deformations and gripper traces in long-horizon, zero-shot scenarios, surpassing action-guided baselines which suffered from stagnation or unnatural structural distortions.

#### 4.5. World Model guides VLA Executor

- **Actionable Physical Priors:** Conditioning the VLA policy on future latent features predicted by RoboInter-World resulted in consistent and substantial gains in action accuracy across all evaluation thresholds compared to a VLA baseline without a world model and a VLA with a standard world model baseline. For example, at 55K steps, VLA + RoboInter-W achieved an OLS@0.03 of 22.17%, compared to VLA Baseline (No WM) at 22.09% and VLA + I2V-Baseline (Pred) at 21.07%. This indicates that RoboInter-World's latent representations encode actionable physical priors that improve downstream manipulation.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

RoboInter1.5 presents a comprehensive and integrated approach to leveraging intermediate representations for embodied world modeling and robotic manipulation. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

1. **Addresses Critical Data Gaps:** By curating RoboInter-Data, a large-scale dataset with dense, per-frame, high-fidelity annotations across over ten types of intermediate representations, the work directly tackles the scarcity of structured, generalizable robotic data. This provides a robust foundation for training and evaluating advanced embodied AI systems, moving beyond embodiment-specific or sparsely annotated datasets.

2. **Establishes Intermediate Representations as a Core Paradigm:** The work redefines intermediate representations not just as interpretable signals, but as a "bidirectional interface." This conceptual shift has the potential to unify high-level semantic understanding with low-level physical interaction, enabling more principled development of generalist robot policies and simulators. The demonstration that IRs can both regularize action spaces and constrain world model rollouts highlights their fundamental role.

3. **Advances Embodied Reasoning and Control:**
 - **Improved VLM Capabilities:** RoboInter-VQA and RoboInter-VLM demonstrate that targeted training with structured IRs significantly enhances VLMs' spatial-temporal reasoning and generation capabilities for manipulation tasks, surpassing general VLMs and prior embodied VLMs. This can lead to more intelligent robotic planners.
 - **Enhanced Robotic Manipulation:** The RoboInter-VLA framework, through its implicit, explicit, and modular paradigms, shows that conditioning on IRs leads to superior action execution performance in both open-loop and closed-loop real-world settings. This suggests pathways to more robust, generalizable, and controllable robotic policies.

4. **Enables Reliable World Modeling:** RoboInter-World's ability to generate accurate, long-horizon future observations, guided by IRs, represents a step toward more reliable physical simulators. By mitigating visual hallucinations and structural degradation common in action- or language-only conditioned models, it can provide internal predictive capabilities essential for complex task planning and learning in dynamic environments. The control video mechanism, converting IRs into a visual format, offers an effective method for injecting geometric and temporal constraints.

5. **Fosters Synergistic Development:** The suite's integrated design, where the RoboInter-VLM Planner generates IRs that guide both RoboInter-VLA and RoboInter-World, promotes a synergistic development cycle. The demonstration that world-model-predicted latents can directly improve VLA performance illustrates the potential for future closed-loop learning systems where perception, planning, and action are tightly coupled through IRs.

Overall, RoboInter1.5 provides a comprehensive research platform and a significant data asset that aims to propel the development of more capable and generalizable embodied AI systems by placing dense intermediate representations at the core of perception, reasoning, action, and world modeling.
