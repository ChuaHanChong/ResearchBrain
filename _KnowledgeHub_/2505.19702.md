# Point-RFT: Improving Multimodal Reasoning with Visually Grounded Reinforcement Finetuning

> [!summary] Summary
> Point-RFT is a framework for multimodal reasoning that explicitly grounds Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps to specific visual coordinates, enhancing reasoning accuracy and interpretability. This approach achieves 90.04% accuracy on the ChartQA dataset, outperforming previous methods and generalizing well to out-of-domain visual reasoning tasks.

## Problem

- Vision-language models often suffer from visual hallucinations and inadequate integration of visual information when generating reasoning chains in multimodal tasks.
- Traditional text-only Chain-of-Thought (CoT) fails to leverage rich spatial and quantitative visual information, leading to factual inaccuracies in multimodal contexts.
- There is a lack of mechanisms to verify if textual descriptions in reasoning chains accurately reflect visual content, complicating error diagnosis.

## Method

- Introduces ==Point-RFT==, a two-stage training framework combining supervised format finetuning with reinforcement learning.
- Develops the ==Point-CoT dataset==, containing 71,000 diverse visual reasoning problems with step-by-step rationales explicitly grounded to ==visual coordinates== using GPT-4o for reasoning and Molmo-7B for localization.
- Utilizes ==Group-wise Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== for reinforcement finetuning, employing a dual reward system to optimize for both point-grounded CoT structure adherence and final answer accuracy.

## Results

- Achieved **90.04%** accuracy on the ChartQA dataset, representing a **19.16 percentage point** improvement over the format-finetuned baseline (**70.88%**) and **6.12 percentage points** over text-only reinforcement learning (**83.92%**).
- Demonstrated strong generalization capabilities by consistently outperforming alternative approaches on out-of-domain benchmarks including CharXiv, PlotQA, IconQA, and TabMWP.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both visual grounding and reinforcement finetuning are crucial for optimal performance and interpretability, validating the complete Point-RFT framework design.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly grounding reasoning steps to visual coordinates significantly reduces visual hallucinations and improves factual accuracy in multimodal AI.
> - Connecting textual reasoning to precise visual elements enables better error diagnosis, distinguishing between perception and reasoning failures.
> - Reinforcement learning with dual rewards (format adherence and accuracy) can effectively optimize for both structural adherence (visual grounding) and correct logical reasoning.
