# Chain of Event-Centric Causal Thought for Physically Plausible Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework called Chain of Event-Centric Causal Thought (CoECT) is introduced for physically plausible video generation, which explicitly models complex physical phenomena as a sequence of causally ordered and dynamically evolving events. This approach demonstrated an 8.19% improvement in Physical Commonsense Alignment on PhyGenBench and a 3.4% gain in combined Semantic Adherence and Physical Commonsense on VideoPhy compared to prior methods.

## Problem

- Current video diffusion models struggle to incorporate detailed physical laws from brief prompts, often failing to represent the temporal progression of physical events.
- Existing Physically Plausible Video Generation (PPVG) methods tend to simplify physical phenomena into static moments, neglecting the dynamic and causally ordered progression of events.
- Language alone often proves inadequate for conveying continuous causal relationships between events, and obtaining tightly aligned visual priors for specific physical phenomena is challenging, leading to issues with physical consistency and smooth transitions.

## Method

- The ==Physics-driven Event Chain Reasoning== (PECR) module of **CoECT** decomposes complex physical phenomena into causally ordered event units by grounding reasoning in physical formulas and computational analysis.
- The ==Transition-aware Cross-modal Prompting== (TCP) module bridges the inferred event chain to video generation by progressively synthesizing semantic and visual prompts for each event, preserving temporal progression.
- The framework uses an ==LLM== for progressive narrative revision and Qwen-Image-Edit for interactive keyframe synthesis, guided by evolving physical conditions and scene graphs to maintain coherence and consistency.

## Results

- The framework achieved an average Physical Commonsense Alignment (PCA) score of **0.66** on the PhyGenBench benchmark, which is an **8.19%** improvement over the previous state-of-the-art, PhysHPO.
- On the VideoPhy benchmark, the framework attained an overall score of **49.3%** for scenarios with both Semantic Adherence (SA=1) and Physical Commonsense (PC=1), surpassing PhysHPO by approximately **3.4%**.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the Physics Formula Grounding (PFG), Physical Phenomena Decomposition (PPD), and Interactive Keyframe Synthesis (IKS) components are critical for generating realistic physical progressions and maintaining structural fidelity in the produced videos.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly modeling complex physical phenomena as a sequence of causally ordered event units, grounded in physical formulas, is crucial for generating videos that accurately reflect real-world physics.
> - Decomposing physical processes into fine-grained events with dynamically evolving physical conditions and scene graphs allows for the generation of realistic and causally coherent temporal progressions.
> - Leveraging a dual-condition strategy through progressively revised semantic narratives and interactively synthesized, physically constrained keyframes effectively guides video diffusion models, ensuring both temporal consistency and visual plausibility across event transitions.
