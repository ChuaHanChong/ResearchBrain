# Do generative video models understand physical principles?

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Google DeepMind created Physics-IQ, a real-world video benchmark, demonstrating that state-of-the-art generative video models possess limited understanding of physical principles. This work also found that visual realism in generated videos does not correlate with an underlying comprehension of physics.

## Problem

- A scientific debate exists on whether advanced generative video models truly learn 'world models' that incorporate physical laws or if they are merely sophisticated 'pixel predictors'.
- Existing benchmarks for evaluating physical reasoning in AI frequently rely on synthetic data, introducing a distribution shift that hinders accurate assessment of models trained on real-world videos.
- There was a need for a rigorous, quantifiable methodology to empirically test the physical understanding of state-of-the-art generative video models in real-world scenarios.

## Method

- Developed Physics-IQ, an open-source benchmark consisting of 396 high-quality, 8-second real-world videos covering 66 distinct physical scenarios across five categories (solid mechanics, fluid dynamics, optics, thermodynamics, magnetism).
- Implemented an evaluation protocol where generative models predict video continuations from 3-second conditioning clips, assessing performance with four specialized physical understanding metrics combined into a single Physics-IQ score.
- Evaluated eight prominent generative video models, including Sora, Runway Gen 3, and **VideoPoet**, alongside a Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) evaluation to quantify visual realism independently.

## Results

- The best-performing model, VideoPoet (multiframe), achieved only 29.5% on the Physics-IQ score (normalized to 100% for physical variance), with Sora (i2v) scoring 10.0%, indicating a substantial deficit in physical understanding across all evaluated models.
- Sora (i2v) emerged as the leader in visual realism, with an MLLM score of 55.6% (lower indicates greater realism), yet it ranked lowest in physical understanding.
- Statistical analysis revealed no significant correlation between visual realism (MLLM scores) and physical understanding (Physics-IQ scores) across the models (Pearson’s r = -0.46, p=0.249).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current generative video models exhibit severe limitations in understanding fundamental physical principles, scoring substantially below the measured physical variance inherent in real-world events.
> - High visual realism in generated videos does not correlate with a deep understanding of physics, challenging the notion that producing visually convincing outputs implies an underlying grasp of physical laws.
> - The study highlights a significant gap in AI's ability to model intuitive physics from passive observation alone, suggesting that current next-frame prediction paradigms may be insufficient for achieving genuine physical comprehension.
