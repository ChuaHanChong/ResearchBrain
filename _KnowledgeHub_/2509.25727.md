# Boundary-to-Region Supervision for Offline Safe Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Harbin Institute of Technology and Westlake University developed the Boundary-to-Region (B2R) framework, which enhances offline safe reinforcement learning by addressing the "symmetry fallacy" in cost signal conditioning. B2R employs region-level supervision through CTG realignment, leading to superior safety compliance in 35 out of 38 DSRL tasks and competitive reward performance.

## Problem

- Existing sequence-model-based offline safe RL methods (e.g., Constrained Decision Transformer) treat 'return-to-go' (RTG) and 'cost-to-go' (CTG) symmetrically, overlooking CTG's role as a rigid safety boundary.
- This 'symmetry fallacy' leads to unreliable constraint satisfaction, especially when policies encounter out-of-distribution cost trajectories.
- Current methods suffer from sparse supervision, learning primarily from trajectories with costs close to the safety threshold, which limits robust generalization across the entire safe operational region.

## Method

- The ==Boundary-to-Region== (**B2R**) framework first filters the offline dataset, retaining only trajectories whose total cumulative cost does not exceed the deployment-time safety threshold.
- It then performs Cost-To-Go (CTG) realignment for each safe trajectory, adding a constant offset to ensure all initial CTG tokens align with the fixed safety budget, thereby providing dense, region-wide supervision.
- Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) are integrated to effectively encode the fine-grained temporal structure and relative dependencies of the realigned cost signals within the Transformer architecture.

## Results

- B2R demonstrated superior safety compliance, satisfying safety constraints (normalized cost < 1) in **35** out of **38** safety-critical tasks on the DSRL benchmark.
- The framework achieved competitive reward performance, securing the highest rewards in **20** of the **38** tasks and ranking first in average scores across all three benchmark suites.
- Ablation studies confirmed that CTG realignment significantly lowers cumulative costs, trajectory filtering ensures stable safety compliance, and RoPE improves both cost adherence and reward performance by better modeling temporal dynamics.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - There is a fundamental asymmetry between flexible reward targets (RTG) and rigid safety boundaries (CTG) that existing sequence-model-based offline safe RL methods often conflate.
> - Transforming sparse boundary-level cost supervision into dense, region-wide supervision through intelligent CTG realignment enables more robust and reliable learning of safety constraints.
> - Careful temporal encoding of cost dynamics, such as with Rotary Positional Embeddings, is crucial for sequence models to effectively leverage realigned cost signals and ensure fine-grained safety adherence.
