# Paying More Attention to Visual Tokens in Self-Evolving Large Multimodal Models

> [!summary] Summary
> VISE (Visual Invariance Self-Evolution) introduces an unsupervised framework that directly regularizes a Large Multimodal Model's (LMM) visual conditioning using geometric and semantic invariance rewards. This approach enhances visual reasoning, reduces hallucination, and improves performance across diverse vision-language tasks without requiring human annotation or external reward models.

## Problem

- Existing self-evolving LMMs primarily optimize for answer agreement or reasoning consistency, often overlooking whether the model genuinely attends to visual content.
- This leads to "visual under-conditioning," where models rely on statistical language priors, causing hallucination, modality bypass, and unstable visual interpretation.
- Prior multi-role self-evolving frameworks can be unstable, with one role potentially dominating or the solver overfitting, and often assume that self-consistency implies correct visual grounding.

## Method

- VISE directly regularizes the LMM's visual conditioning policy through two unsupervised, self-generated invariance-based reward signals.
- A Geometric Invariance Reward (R_geo) enforces spatial consistency of predicted bounding boxes under known image transformations (e.g., affine, flip).
- A Semantic Invariance Reward (R_sem) penalizes evidence-agnostic generation by requiring the model to recognize the absence of queried objects when their visual regions are perturbed or "ghosted."

## Results

- VISE achieved substantial improvements in image captioning, with gains of +**16.85** CIDEr on COCO for Qwen3-VL-2B, outperforming baselines by 4 to 7 times.
- It improved performance across all twelve VQA and reasoning benchmarks simultaneously at the 2B scale (e.g., +**4.19** on ScienceQA, +**3.72** on MMMU), with no observed task tradeoffs.
- The method consistently reduced object hallucination, decreasing Chair-I by -**5.00** points and Chair-S by -**5.45** points, while also improving POPE accuracy by +**1.02** at 2B.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Direct regularization of visual conditioning via invariance-based rewards is more effective for creating robust LMMs than indirect optimization for answer agreement or consistency.
> - Geometric invariance addresses localization instability, while semantic invariance ensures predictions are driven by actual visual evidence rather than language priors.
> - Strengthening visual grounding through these unsupervised rewards leads to broad, consistent performance gains across diverse vision-language tasks without task-specific tradeoffs.
