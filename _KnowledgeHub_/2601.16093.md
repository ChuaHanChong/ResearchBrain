# SAMTok: Representing Any Mask with Two Words

> [!summary] Summary
> Purdue University, ByteDance, and NUS researchers developed SAMTok, a framework that converts any image segmentation mask into two discrete language tokens, allowing multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to achieve pixel-wise understanding and generation through standard next-token prediction. This non-intrusive approach enhances MLLMs like QwenVL with state-of-the-art capabilities in various vision-language tasks, including interleaved text-mask generation and multi-round interactive segmentation, while simplifying their training process.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs struggle with fine-grained pixel-wise understanding and generation due to complex, fragmented architectures and specialized modules.
- Integrating mask capabilities requires complex co-training with disparate losses and intricate forward pipelines, making it hard to scale.
- Applying discrete reinforcement learning to mask generation is challenging because current segmentation outputs are continuous.
- Previous mask representations (e.g., special image tokens, RLE encoding) incur high inference costs, often requiring many tokens per mask.

## Method

- **SAMTok** uses a mask Variational Autoencoder with ==residual quantization== to represent any 2D mask as two discrete, special language tokens, effectively creating a 'new language' for masks.
- It integrates SAMTok into base MLLMs by treating mask tokens as part of the MLLM's vocabulary, allowing fine-tuning with only standard next-token prediction loss, without architectural modifications.
- The framework applies Reinforcement Learning using Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with a novel, purely textual answer-matching reward function for mask generation, leveraging its discrete token representation.

## Results

- SAMTok achieves high-fidelity mask reconstruction, preserving fine structures, and ablation studies confirm the superiority of Residual Quantization.
- The integrated MLLM (QwenVL-SAMTok) achieves new state-of-the-art performance on interleaved text-mask generation (GCG) and multi-round interactive segmentation (MR-RefCOCO/+/g, MR-PACO).
- It demonstrates strong results on text-to-mask tasks (GRES, RefCOCO/+/g, GroundingSuite) and mask-to-text tasks (DLC-Bench, MDVP-Bench, VideoRefer-D), often outperforming or matching specialized models.
- Reinforcement Learning with the textual answer-matching reward significantly boosts mask generation performance (e.g., +**6.8%** gIoU and +**18.9%** N-acc on GRES), marking the first successful optimization of mask generation using only textual rewards.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Representing complex 2D segmentation masks as just two discrete tokens provides a compact and efficient 'language' for MLLMs to process pixel-wise information.
> - Residual Quantization is effective for high-fidelity mask compression and reconstruction, enabling a compact codebook while preserving fine details.
> - MLLMs can acquire robust pixel-wise capabilities non-intrusively by unifying mask understanding and generation into a next-token prediction problem.
> - Discrete mask tokenization enables the successful application of reinforcement learning with a purely textual reward, simplifying optimization for mask generation.
