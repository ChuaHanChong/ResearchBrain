# DeepVerse: 4D Autoregressive Video Generation as a World Model

> [!summary] Summary
> SJTU, USTC, Tsinghua, and Shanghai AI Lab researchers develop DeepVerse, a 4D autoregressive world model that generates video sequences by explicitly incorporating depth maps and camera poses alongside visual observations through a composite state representation and geometry-aware memory mechanism, addressing core limitations of visual-only approaches including scale ambiguity and temporal drift by training on 10 million synthetic game frames with precise geometric annotations and achieving superior performance in VBench consistency metrics while enabling long-horizon predictions through a sliding window approach that maintains global coordinate alignment across extended sequences.

## Problem

- Existing world models rely on 2D visual observations, neglecting 3D geometric structures that lead to scale ambiguity, rapid error accumulation, and temporal inconsistency
- Autoregressive models suffer from cumulative prediction errors and "forgetting" of earlier scene details over long horizons
- Current interactive models fail to capture underlying physical dynamics and spatio-temporal relationships in complex environments

## Method

- Introduce composite 4D state representation combining visual observations with geometric information (depth parameterized as square root of disparity and camera viewpoint via raymap encoding)
- Develop geometry-aware memory mechanism that maintains historical 4D observations in global coordinate system and selectively retrieves spatially relevant states based on angular rotation and translation distances
- Implement token-wise concatenation architecture in MM-==DiT== diffusion framework with textual conditioning for versatile control

## Results

- Superior performance across VBench metrics (subject consistency, background consistency, motion smoothness) with Model 2 architecture achieving lower FVD scores
- Successful generation of coherent 4D sequences from diverse inputs including game images, real-world images, and AI-generated content with human-guided textual control
- Demonstrated ability to maintain spatial memory and generate extended sequences through sliding window inference with depth/camera parameter scaling for global consistency

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Token-wise concatenation of temporal states significantly outperforms channel-wise concatenation in mitigating autoregressive drift despite higher computational complexity
> - Explicit depth integration substantially enhances environmental comprehension and reduces temporal deterioration compared to raymap-only approaches
> - Geometry-aware spatial conditioning enables extended temporal coherence beyond fixed-duration chunks, preserving long-term spatial consistency

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

DeepVerse operates at the cutting edge of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) research, specifically within the domain of "World Models." World models are computational frameworks designed to learn the dynamics of an environment, enabling intelligent agents to predict future states, simulate complex physical interactions, and plan actions. These models are considered crucial building blocks for achieving truly intelligent systems that can understand and interact with the physical world.

The paper positions itself by identifying critical limitations in existing interactive world models and video generation approaches:
- **Visual-centric nature**: Most current interactive models primarily predict visual observations (e.g., RGB frames). However, the physical world is inherently 3D (or 4D if considering time), and videos are merely 2D projections. Neglecting underlying geometric structures (e.g., depth, camera pose) leads to fundamental issues like scale ambiguity, rapid error accumulation, and temporal inconsistency, especially over long prediction horizons.
- **Cumulative prediction errors and forgetting issues**: Standard autoregressive models, which predict future frames based on previous ones, often suffer from compounding errors, leading to "drifting" over time. They also struggle to maintain long-term consistency and "forget" earlier scene details.

DeepVerse directly addresses these challenges by making a significant conceptual and technical leap: it introduces the concept of an **autoregressive 4D world model**. It explicitly models the world not just visually, but also geometrically, by incorporating depth maps and camera poses alongside visual observations. This is a crucial distinction from prior work that might use 3D information for reconstruction but not integrate it directly into the autoregressive *generation* process. The authors claim DeepVerse is the "pioneering introduction of an autoregressive 4D world modeling paradigm."

The paper builds upon and differentiates itself from several related areas:
- **Neural World Simulation**: While existing models like UniSim, UniPi, Aether, and Cosmos have advanced interactive environments and incorporated 3D information (like Aether), DeepVerse aims for a more comprehensive 4D representation directly within an autoregressive generation framework.
- **Interactive Video Generation**: Methods like GameNGen, Oasis, and Genie have focused on controllable video generation. DeepVerse integrates control but emphasizes the geometry-aware aspect to enhance realism and consistency, especially over long durations.
- **3D/4D Representations**: The growing use of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs), Gaussian Splatting, and other 3D/4D representations is transformative. DeepVerse leverages these by parameterizing geometric information (depth, raymap) to enable its 4D state representation, but uniquely integrates this into an autoregressive world model, rather than just for reconstruction or view synthesis. The authors assert that "DeepVerse is the first to incorporate 4D representations into auto-regressive world models."

By explicitly grounding video forecasting in robust geometric representations and designing a geometry-aware memory mechanism, DeepVerse aims to fundamentally improve the fidelity, consistency, and long-term predictive capabilities of world models, thus contributing significantly towards more capable and physically intelligent AI systems.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the DeepVerse project are clearly articulated throughout the paper:

1. **Overcome Limitations of Visual-Centric World Models**: The core motivation stems from the recognition that existing world models predominantly focus on 2D visual observations. This neglect of the underlying 3D/4D physical world leads to inherent problems like scale ambiguity, rapid error accumulation, and temporal inconsistency in predictions. DeepVerse aims to move beyond this by explicitly modeling geometric structures.
2. **Explicitly Incorporate Geometric Predictions (4D Information)**: The central objective is to introduce and integrate "4D information" (visual observations, depth, and camera pose/viewpoint) into an autoregressive world modeling framework. This 4D representation is designed to provide a more complete and informative "state representation" ($\hat{s}_t = (v_t, g_t)$) that better approximates the true, hidden environment state.
3. **Enhance Prediction Accuracy and Reduce Drift/Temporal Inconsistency**: By leveraging geometric constraints, DeepVerse seeks to capture richer spatio-temporal relationships and underlying physical dynamics. This improved understanding is intended to significantly reduce the cumulative prediction errors ("drifting") and enhance temporal consistency, enabling the model to reliably generate extended, coherent future sequences.
4. **Address the "Forgetting Issue" in Long-Term Prediction**: Autoregressive models often struggle to retain long-term contextual information, leading to degraded performance over extended horizons. DeepVerse aims to mitigate this by introducing a "geometry-aware memory read-and-write mechanism." This module strategically retrieves geometrically relevant historical observations to condition current predictions, preserving long-term spatial consistency.
5. **Establish a Pioneering 4D Autoregressive World Modeling Paradigm**: The paper explicitly states its goal to be the "pioneering introduction of an autoregressive 4D world modeling paradigm," providing theoretical and practical guidelines for future development in interactive world models. This includes exploring optimal architectural configurations for incorporating historical and geometric information.
6. **Validate Effectiveness Across Diverse Scenarios**: A key objective is to demonstrate DeepVerse's capacity for high-fidelity, long-horizon predictions grounded in geometry-aware dynamics across various input types (game images, real-world images, AI-generated images) and control signals (textual conditions).

In essence, DeepVerse is motivated by the need for world models that possess a deeper, geometry-aware understanding of the dynamic physical world, moving beyond superficial visual predictions to enable more robust, consistent, and long-term simulation capabilities vital for advancing AGI.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

DeepVerse introduces a novel framework for 4D autoregressive video generation, centered on a composite state representation and a geometry-aware memory mechanism.

#### 3.1. Problem Formulation and 4D State Representation

DeepVerse redefines the environment state from a standard visual observation ($v_t$) to a richer **composite 4D state representation** ($\hat{s}_t = (v_t, g_t)$). Here, $v_t$ is the visual observation (RGB frame), and $g_t$ encapsulates geometric information, specifically camera viewpoint ($c_t$) and depth ($d_t$).
- **Depth ($d_t$)**: Parameterized as the square root of disparity ($e_t = \sqrt{1/d_t}$), allowing direct encoding by a pre-trained Variational Autoencoder (VAE).
- **Viewpoint ($c_t$)**: Parameterized using the raymap representation, which geometrically encodes camera orientation and position through ray direction vectors.
This $\hat{s}_t$ aims to better approximate the hidden physical state of the environment, forming a richer 4D data stream (3D space + time). The model then learns to predict future 4D states autoregressively: $P(\hat{s}_{t+1:t+k} | a_t, \hat{s}_t, \hat{s}_{t-m:t-1}, \psi(\hat{s}_{0:t-m-1}))$.

#### 3.2. Model Components

- **4D Representation**: The three modalities (RGB, depth, raymap) are channel-wise concatenated to form $\hat{s}_t$, ensuring compatibility with standard image latents for iterative future prediction.
- **General Control**: Unlike some prior work that introduces new modalities for control, DeepVerse utilizes **textual conditions**. This design choice preserves pre-trained model capabilities and offers a more versatile control paradigm, allowing for both direct text-to-controller key mapping and efficient fine-tuning.
- **Spatial Condition / Geometry-Aware Memory**: To address the "forgetting issue" and maintain long-term spatial consistency, DeepVerse introduces a novel mechanism. It maintains a **memory pool** of all historical 4D observations aligned to a global coordinate system. A selective mechanism ($\psi$) dynamically retrieves a historical state ($\hat{s}_i$) as a spatial condition if it is statistically correlated with the current state $\hat{s}_t$. This selection prioritizes geometrically relevant historical states based on spatial proximity (minimum angular difference in rotation and Euclidean distance in translation). This retrieved state is then encoded into a token sequence to condition the current prediction.

#### 3.3. Dataset Construction

DeepVerse is trained on a **large-scale synthetic dataset** to obtain precise geometric labels.
- **Data Collection**: Approximately 10 million frames of gameplay footage were collected. UI elements were systematically removed using ReShade.
- **Annotation**: An automatic camera annotation pipeline (inspired by Aether) was used to synthesize intrinsic/extrinsic camera parameters and ground-truth depth maps.
- **Filtering**: Data was filtered to exclude clips with excessive camera rotation or minimal movement to ensure high reconstruction quality and relevant dynamics.
- **Caption Annotation**: Textual descriptions were generated hierarchically:
 - Algorithmic derivation from camera movements/rotations (e.g., "walked forward").
 - Qwen-VL was used to annotate video clips with first-person descriptions for egocentric videos and third-person narratives for exocentric recordings.
 - CLIP and T5 were used to generate caption embeddings, consistent with SD3 methodology.
- **Training Preprocessing**: Global scene scaling and depth normalization were applied to ensure depth values are within a suitable range for VAE encoding while preserving autoregressive causality.

#### 3.4. Model Architecture (Diffusion Model)

DeepVerse is implemented as a **diffusion model** operating in an autoregressive paradigm, specifically using a flow matching methodology. The core architecture is based on MM-DiT (Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer). The authors explored two approaches for integrating historical information:
- **Model 1: Channel-wise Concatenation**: Concatenates temporally ordered latent states along the channel dimension before patchification. This reduces FLOPs but was found to exacerbate error accumulation in extended scenarios.
- **Model 2: Token-wise Concatenation (Final Choice)**: This approach performs individual patchification of temporal states, generating separate tokens for each timestep. This significantly increases token quantity but requires a 3D VAE for temporal compression. Despite higher computational complexity, Model 2 was empirically found to achieve superior performance in mitigating autoregressive model drift and enhancing visual performance.
The final architecture (Model 2) integrates noised latent, recent history, and spatial condition as concatenated tokens. Text embeddings (from T5 and CLIP) are injected via token-wise concatenation and AdaLN. Classifier-Free Guidance is employed during training with stochastic conditioning dropout and during inference with modality-specific guidance scales.

#### 3.5. Long-Duration Inference

DeepVerse employs a **sliding window approach** for long-duration reasoning. After generating a sequence, a segment of recent observations is used as conditioning for the next window. A critical scaling operation is applied to depth/camera parameters in the transitional segment to maintain global consistency across windows and prevent truncation artifacts, with `d_max` values recorded for rescaling. The entire procedure is formalized as an algorithm ensuring seamless sequence concatenation and global contextual information preservation.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments conducted validate DeepVerse's capabilities and the effectiveness of its proposed innovations.

#### 4.1. Architectural Comparison (Model 1 vs. Model 2)

- **Quantitative Analysis (Figure 4b)**: Evaluated using VBench metrics (subject consistency, background consistency, aesthetic quality, imaging quality, motion smoothness, dynamic degree) over 32, 64, 96, and 128 generated frames.
- **Result**: Model 2 (Token-wise Concatenation) consistently achieved superior performance across nearly all metrics compared to Model 1 (Channel-wise Concatenation).
- **Implication**: Despite higher computational complexity (average GFLOPs of 1280.9 vs. 1049.4 for Model 1), the token-wise concatenation effectively mitigates autoregressive model drift and yields superior visual performance. The aggregation of temporal features within single tokens in Model 1 was found to exacerbate error accumulation, validating the architectural preference for Model 2.

#### 4.3. Simulation Quality (Figure 6)

- DeepVerse demonstrated the ability to generate highly consistent 4D representations with exceptional visual fidelity, strictly adhering to input conditions.
- It successfully handles diverse initial visual inputs, including game images, real-world images, and AI-generated images.
- The model's versatile conditioning mechanism allows for human-guided manipulations via textual inputs, enabling control over generated sequences.
- Crucially, DeepVerse's 4D autoregressive generation distinguishes itself from conventional reconstruction-then-rerendering paradigms by simultaneously preserving viewpoint-object dynamics and predicting environmental interactions.

In summary, DeepVerse successfully demonstrates that explicitly incorporating 4D geometric information and leveraging a geometry-aware memory significantly enhances the performance of autoregressive world models in terms of visual quality, spatio-temporal consistency, and long-horizon prediction capabilities. The chosen token-wise concatenation architecture is empirically validated as superior for this task.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

DeepVerse represents a substantial step forward in the development of world models, addressing fundamental limitations that have hindered the progress of intelligent agents capable of complex, interactive understanding of dynamic environments. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

1. **Pioneering 4D Autoregressive World Modeling**: DeepVerse introduces and validates the "first interactive world model based on 4D autoregressive video generation." This paradigm shift, moving from purely visual prediction to explicit 4D (visual + geometric) representation, is a crucial conceptual and technical innovation. It provides a new blueprint for future world model architectures.

2. **Enhanced Physical Understanding and Realism**: By integrating precise geometric information (depth and camera pose/raymap) directly into the autoregressive generation process, DeepVerse enables the model to capture richer spatio-temporal relationships and better approximate underlying physical dynamics. This leads to significantly improved visual realism, reduced scale ambiguity, and more geometrically consistent predictions compared to traditional visual-centric models. This deeper understanding of the physical world is vital for true intelligence.

3. **Mitigation of Autoregressive Drift and Forgetting**: The core issues of error accumulation (drift) and loss of context (forgetting) in long-horizon autoregressive predictions are significantly alleviated. The geometry-aware memory mechanism allows the model to retrieve and utilize past spatial configurations, maintaining long-term consistency and coherence in generated sequences. This capability is paramount for sustained interaction and simulation.

4. **Foundation for Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)**: World models are recognized as essential building blocks for AGI. DeepVerse's ability to generate coherent, long-duration 4D simulations of dynamic environments, grounded in geometric understanding, brings AGI closer by enabling agents to:
 - **Imagine Future States**: Crucial for planning and decision-making in complex scenarios.
 - **Simulate Complex Physical Interactions**: Beyond superficial visual changes, DeepVerse models the underlying scene structure.
 - **Support Embodied AI**: A robust understanding of 3D/4D space is fundamental for tasks in embodied AI, such as navigation and manipulation, leading to more capable and robust robotic systems.

5. **Versatile Control and Applications**: The use of textual conditions for control makes DeepVerse highly versatile and user-friendly. Its ability to generate high-fidelity, controllable 4D experiences from diverse inputs (game images, real images, AI-generated images) suggests broad applications in:
 - **Game Development and Simulation**: Creating dynamic, interactive game environments.
 - **Virtual and Augmented Reality**: Generating realistic and consistent virtual worlds.
 - **Robotics Simulation**: Providing rich, physically consistent environments for training and testing robotic agents.
 - **Content Creation**: Enabling creators to generate complex animated scenes with fine-grained control.

**Limitations**: Despite its promising results, the paper acknowledges a key limitation: DeepVerse's generalization capability to real-world scenarios is currently limited due to its training exclusively on synthetic data. Future work will need to address this gap by incorporating real-world data or developing domain adaptation techniques to unleash its full potential in practical applications.

In conclusion, DeepVerse's pioneering integration of 4D representations and geometry-aware memory into an autoregressive world model marks a significant advancement. It provides a more robust and physically grounded approach to environmental simulation, pushing the boundaries of generative AI and laying important groundwork for the development of truly intelligent systems.
