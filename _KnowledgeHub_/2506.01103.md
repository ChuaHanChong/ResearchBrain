# DeepVerse: 4D Autoregressive Video Generation as a World Model

> [!summary] Summary
> SJTU, USTC, Tsinghua, and Shanghai AI Lab researchers develop DeepVerse, a 4D autoregressive world model that generates video sequences by explicitly incorporating depth maps and camera poses alongside visual observations through a composite state representation and geometry-aware memory mechanism, addressing core limitations of visual-only approaches including scale ambiguity and temporal drift by training on 10 million synthetic game frames with precise geometric annotations and achieving superior performance in VBench consistency metrics while enabling long-horizon predictions through a sliding window approach that maintains global coordinate alignment across extended sequences.

## Problem

- Existing world models rely on 2D visual observations, neglecting 3D geometric structures that lead to scale ambiguity, rapid error accumulation, and temporal inconsistency
- Autoregressive models suffer from cumulative prediction errors and "forgetting" of earlier scene details over long horizons
- Current interactive models fail to capture underlying physical dynamics and spatio-temporal relationships in complex environments

## Method

- Introduce composite 4D state representation combining visual observations with geometric information (depth parameterized as square root of disparity and camera viewpoint via raymap encoding)
- Develop geometry-aware memory mechanism that maintains historical 4D observations in global coordinate system and selectively retrieves spatially relevant states based on angular rotation and translation distances
- Implement token-wise concatenation architecture in MM-==DiT== diffusion framework with textual conditioning for versatile control

## Results

- Superior performance across VBench metrics (subject consistency, background consistency, motion smoothness) with Model 2 architecture achieving lower FVD scores
- Successful generation of coherent 4D sequences from diverse inputs including game images, real-world images, and AI-generated content with human-guided textual control
- Demonstrated ability to maintain spatial memory and generate extended sequences through sliding window inference with depth/camera parameter scaling for global consistency

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Token-wise concatenation of temporal states significantly outperforms channel-wise concatenation in mitigating autoregressive drift despite higher computational complexity
> - Explicit depth integration substantially enhances environmental comprehension and reduces temporal deterioration compared to raymap-only approaches
> - Geometry-aware spatial conditioning enables extended temporal coherence beyond fixed-duration chunks, preserving long-term spatial consistency
