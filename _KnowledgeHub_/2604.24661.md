# Agent-Centric Visual Reinforcement Learning under Dynamic Perturbations

> [!summary] Summary
> This research introduces the Agent-Centric Observations with Mixture-of-Experts (ACO-MoE) framework, a plug-and-play preprocessor that restores and extracts agent-centric foregrounds to robustify visual reinforcement learning agents. Evaluated on the new Visual Degraded Control Suite (VDCS) benchmark, ACO-MoE achieved 95.3% recovery of clean performance and state-of-the-art results on the DMC-GB benchmark.

## Problem

- Visual reinforcement learning agents suffer significant performance degradation when deployed in real-world settings with dynamic and heterogeneous visual perturbations (e.g., weather, sensor noise).
- Existing robustness methods, such as data augmentation or perturbation-invariant representation learning, often process corrupted pixel data, failing to recover information irrecoverably lost or distorted at the pixel level.
- Current benchmarks do not adequately address the impact of physical image degradations affecting foreground objects or evaluate scenarios involving non-stationary transitions between different perturbation types.

## Method

- The ==Visual Degraded Control Suite (VDCS)== is introduced, a new benchmark simulating dynamic, ==Markov-switching physical degradations== (e.g., rain, haze, noise) to reflect real-world complexities.
- **ACO-MoE** (==Agent-Centric Observations with Mixture-of-Experts==) is proposed as a frozen, plug-and-play preprocessing module, inserted before the RL encoder, compatible with both model-free and model-based RL algorithms.
- ACO-MoE utilizes a ==shared encoder==, a ==perturbation router==, and specialized ==dual-stream expert decoders== for RGB restoration and foreground mask prediction to construct clean, agent-centric observations on a uniform black background.

## Results

- ACO-MoE achieved an average score of **736.0** on the VDCS benchmark, outperforming the best baseline (SimGRL) by **78.5%** and recovering **95.3%** of DreamerV3's clean performance.
- Ablation studies confirmed the benefit of the Mixture-of-Experts (**10.9%** improvement) and the critical roles of both RGB restoration and foreground masking, with the router demonstrating over **99%** accuracy.
- The framework achieved state-of-the-art average performance on the DMC-GB `video-hard` (**809**, outperforming FTR by **6.2%**) and `color-hard` (**818**, outperforming SMG by **17.4%**) benchmarks, demonstrating broad generalization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reconstruction-based world models inevitably suffer from "representation contamination," where latent states encode nuisance corruption features alongside task-relevant information due to the reconstruction objective.
> - An ideal clean foreground observation acts as an approximate Information Bottleneck anchor, theoretically justified to achieve nuisance invariance and approximate task sufficiency by decoupling task-relevant information.
> - Using ACO-MoE as a frozen, pretrained preprocessor is crucial for maintaining stable input distributions to the downstream RL agent, preventing observation drift and ensuring training stability.
