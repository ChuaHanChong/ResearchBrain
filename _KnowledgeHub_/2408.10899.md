# All Robots in One: A New Standard and Unified Dataset for Versatile, General-Purpose Embodied Agents

> [!summary] Summary
> ARIO introduces a unified data standard and a large-scale, multi-modal dataset for embodied AI, integrating approximately 3 million episodes from real-world, simulated, and converted open-source data. This resource supports five sensory modalities and diverse robot types to facilitate the development of versatile, general-purpose embodied agents.

## Problem

- Existing large-scale robotic learning datasets often lack standardized formats, hindering efficient data processing and integration across different platforms and tasks.
- Current datasets suffer from insufficient data diversity, volume, and comprehensive sensory modality coverage, limiting the development of robust, general-purpose embodied agents.
- A critical gap exists in providing matched simulated and real-world data, which impedes research into effectively bridging the sim-to-real gap for practical robotics deployment.

## Method

- Developed ARIO, a new hierarchical data standard designed for unified collection, storage, and analysis of multi-modal, multi-embodiment data with timestamp-based synchronization.
- Constructed the ARIO dataset by combining new real-world teleoperated data, large-scale simulation generation, and the conversion of 62 existing open-source datasets into the ARIO standard.
- Integrated comprehensive sensory modalities (2D images, 3D vision, text, tactile, auditory) and diverse robot types (single-arm, bimanual, humanoid, mobile) to support versatile agent development.

## Results

- Successfully introduced the ARIO data standard, featuring a four-level hierarchical structure and a unified data format with timestamp alignment for comprehensive multi-modal, multi-robot data.
- Created the ARIO dataset, comprising approximately 3 million episodes across 258 series and 321,064 tasks, derived from converted open-source datasets (~2.3M episodes), new simulations (~703K episodes), and novel real-world collections (~3.6K episodes).
- The dataset supports five sensory modalities (image, 3D vision, audio, text, tactile) and includes a wide range of robot morphologies, control types, and manipulation/navigation skills, including complex bimanual and long-horizon tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A unified data standard is crucial for overcoming data fragmentation, simplifying data sharing, and enabling the development of scalable, general-purpose embodied AI foundation models.
> - Integrating diverse sensory modalities and robot embodiments within a single, standardized dataset significantly enriches the learning potential for robust and nuanced agent behaviors.
> - A multi-pronged data acquisition strategy—combining high-quality real-world collection, scalable simulation generation, and efficient conversion of existing resources—is effective for building large-scale, diverse embodied AI datasets.
