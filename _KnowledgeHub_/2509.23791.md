# CaRe-BN: Precise Moving Statistics for Stabilizing Spiking Neural Networks in Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Peking University researchers developed CaRe-BN (Confidence-adaptive and Re-calibration Batch Normalization) to stabilize Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) in online Reinforcement Learning. This method provides more precise normalization statistics, enabling SNNs to achieve faster convergence and higher returns, outperforming traditional ANNs by an average of 5.9% in continuous control tasks while preserving energy efficiency.

## Problem

- Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) inherently suffer from unstable gradient propagation during training, relying on Batch Normalization (BN) for stability.
- Traditional BN, which uses Exponential Moving Averages (EMA), struggles to precisely track the continuously shifting data distributions in non-stationary online Reinforcement Learning (RL) environments.
- Existing SNN-BN methods, designed for static supervised learning, are inadequate for the dynamic nature of online RL, leading to a negative feedback loop that hinders exploration and policy improvement.

## Method

- A ==Confidence-adaptive== update (Ca-BN) mechanism, inspired by the Kalman estimator, adaptively reweights batch statistics to minimize the mean-squared error of BN statistics under non-stationary RL dynamics.
- A periodic Re-calibration mechanism (Re-BN) corrects accumulated estimation errors by sampling multiple calibration batches from the replay buffer at fixed intervals.
- **CaRe-BN** is integrated into general online RL algorithms, applying Ca-BN during policy updates and Re-BN periodically, ensuring no additional inference overhead by fusing layers into synaptic weights for deployment.

## Results

- CaRe-BN consistently reduced the Wasserstein distance of BN statistics, leading to more precise estimates and higher exploration returns across MuJoCo tasks.
- SNNs with CaRe-BN achieved faster convergence, higher final returns, and significantly lower training variance across various spiking neuron models and RL algorithms, outperforming existing SNN-RL methods and setting a new state-of-the-art for SNN normalization.
- SNNs equipped with CaRe-BN demonstrated an average performance gain of **5.9%** over their ANN counterparts in continuous control, maintaining substantial energy efficiency (e.g., 16.48 nJ for SNNs vs. 1775.36 nJ for ANNs) with negligible training overhead.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Precise Batch Normalization statistics are critical for effective exploration and learning in online SNN-RL, directly influencing an agent's ability to select optimal actions.
> - Combining adaptive weighting of current batch statistics (Ca-BN) with periodic global corrections from a replay buffer (Re-BN) effectively addresses the non-stationarity challenges in SNN-RL.
> - Robust, SNN-specific normalization can enable SNNs to match or exceed the performance of Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) in dynamic RL settings while retaining their inherent energy efficiency.
