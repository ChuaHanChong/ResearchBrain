# Show-o2: Improved Native Unified Multimodal Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Show Lab and ByteDance researchers developed Show-o2, a native unified multimodal model capable of both understanding and generating text, images, and videos within a single architecture. The model achieves state-of-the-art or highly competitive performance across various multimodal benchmarks, supported by a novel dual-path visual representation and an efficient two-stage training strategy.

## Problem

- Existing large multimodal models (LMMs) primarily focus on text and images, often lacking comprehensive and native handling of dynamic video content.
- A challenge in achieving true unification of multimodal understanding and generation within a single, cohesive architecture, rather than assembling disparate specialized models.
- The difficulty of efficiently training powerful generative LMMs to cultivate visual generation abilities while preserving extensive pre-trained language model (LLM) knowledge without relying on prohibitively expensive, web-scale text corpora.

## Method

- Introduces a native unified multimodal architecture that leverages a ==3D causal VAE== for efficient visual encoding/decoding of both images and videos.
- Employs a ==dual-path Unified Visual Representation (UVR)== with ==Spatial (-Temporal) Fusion== to integrate both high-level semantic and low-level structural features for visual inputs.
- Utilizes a ==two-stage training recipe==: an initial visual generation pre-training phase, followed by full model fine-tuning, designed to efficiently impart visual generation skills while preserving the LLM's language knowledge.

## Results

- Achieves state-of-the-art or highly competitive performance on image understanding benchmarks (e.g., MME-p, GQA, MMMU-val) and image generation benchmarks (GenEval, DPG-Bench, OneIG-Bench).
- Outperforms models with more parameters (e.g., TokenFlow-XL **14B**) on several image understanding tasks and demonstrates strong text-to-video and image-to-video generation on VBench with a **2B** parameter model.
- The two-stage training successfully preserves LLM language knowledge, maintaining performance comparable to original Qwen2.5-1.5B and Qwen2.5-7B Instruct models on text-only tasks like MMLU and GSM8K.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The dual-path Spatial (-Temporal) Fusion mechanism is crucial for effectively integrating both high-level semantic and low-level structural features, significantly improving both multimodal understanding and generation.
> - A two-stage training strategy effectively cultivates strong visual generation capabilities and adapts visual components to the LLM's embedding space, critically maintaining the pre-trained LLM's language proficiency without requiring massive text-only datasets.
> - Unified visual representations, designed to be scalable for both images and videos, enable robust multimodal understanding and generation within a single, coherent framework.
