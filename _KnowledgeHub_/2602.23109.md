# Towards Intelligible Human-Robot Interaction: An Active Inference Approach to Occluded Pedestrian Scenarios

> [!summary] Summary
> Autonomous vehicles from Tongji University demonstrate enhanced safety and explainability in occluded pedestrian scenarios by employing a novel active inference framework. This framework, incorporating cognitively-inspired mechanisms like Conditional Reset and Hypothesis Injection, proactively manages uncertainty to achieve significantly reduced collision rates compared to baseline methods.

## Problem

- Autonomous driving systems face critical safety challenges from sudden appearances of occluded pedestrians, characterized by high uncertainty.
- Traditional rule-based or purely data-driven methods struggle with these safety-critical 'long-tail' corner cases due to data scarcity and lack interpretability.
- Existing models often exhibit reactive or data-dependent behaviors, failing to proactively reason about unobserved hazards in a human-like manner.

## Method

- Developed an active inference framework, formalized as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP), to enable human-like, belief-driven, proactive decision-making.
- Introduced a Conditional Reset mechanism for belief updating to ensure persistent vigilance for unobserved pedestrians under uncertainty.
- Integrated a Hypothesis Injection technique into the planning process to facilitate proactive reasoning by considering counterfactual 'what-if' scenarios.

## Results

- The proposed method achieved an overall collision rate of **5.3%**, significantly outperforming Rule-based (**41.3%**), PPO-LSTM (**27.5%**), and Reactive (**82.2%**) baselines.
- Ablation studies showed Conditional Reset prevented belief decay, leading to successful collision avoidance, while Hypothesis Injection reduced collision rates (e.g., from **9.5%** to **2.3%** in dynamic scenarios) by promoting proactive caution.
- The framework demonstrated real-time computational feasibility, with an average planning step time of **17.69** ms on a GPU, meeting the requirements for 10 Hz real-time control loops.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Maintaining a persistent belief in potential, unobserved hazards (via Conditional Reset) is crucial for autonomous agents to exhibit cautious and safe behavior.
> - Proactive planning that considers diverse, counterfactual 'what-if' scenarios (via Hypothesis Injection) significantly improves safety in high-uncertainty situations.
> - Active inference provides an interpretable, mechanism-inspired framework for human-like decision-making that effectively balances pragmatic goals with information-seeking in safety-critical human-robot interactions.
