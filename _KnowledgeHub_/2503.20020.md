# Gemini Robotics: Bringing AI into the Physical World

> [!summary] Summary
> Google DeepMind introduces the Gemini Robotics family of models, which extend the Gemini 2.0 multimodal model to physical robots, enabling them to perform a wide range of dexterous manipulation tasks. This research demonstrates enhanced embodied reasoning, robust generalization to novel conditions, and rapid adaptation to new tasks and robot embodiments through extensive real-world robot data and specialized fine-tuning.

## Problem

- Bridging the generalist capabilities of large multimodal models (LMMs) from digital domains to practical applications on physical robots.
- Overcoming the limitations of traditional, highly specialized robotic systems that lack robust generalization to diverse, unstructured real-world environments.
- Developing robotic AI that can acquire robust embodied reasoning (e.g., 3D understanding, intuitive physics) and directly translate this understanding into precise physical actions.

## Method

- The Gemini Robotics team at Google DeepMind developed the ==Gemini Robotics== family of models, extending the Gemini 2.0 multimodal model to physical robotics.
- ==Gemini Robotics-ER==, a Vision-Language Model (VLM), was developed to enhance embodied reasoning capabilities, including open-world 2D/3D perception, trajectory, and grasp prediction, evaluated on a new ERQA benchmark.
- Gemini Robotics, a ==Vision-Language-Action (VLA)== model, was built for low-latency real-time robot control, trained on thousands of hours of diverse real-world expert teleoperated robot action data alongside other multimodal data.

## Results

- Gemini 2.0 and Gemini Robotics-ER achieved state-of-the-art results on embodied reasoning benchmarks, including the newly introduced ERQA, with chain-of-thought prompting significantly boosting performance.
- Gemini Robotics demonstrated proficiency on **20** diverse dexterous tasks, surpassing state-of-the-art baselines, and showed robust generalization across visual, instruction, and action variations.
- Specialized Gemini Robotics achieved up to **79%** average success on six challenging long-horizon dexterous tasks (e.g., **55%** for origami fox), and demonstrated rapid adaptation to new tasks (over **70%** success with **100** demonstrations) and new robot embodiments (e.g., Apptronik Apollo humanoid).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Enhancing large multimodal models with "embodied reasoning" capabilities is crucial for enabling them to understand and interact with the physical world effectively.
> - A generalist Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model built on a powerful large multimodal model backbone and trained on diverse robot action data can achieve robust out-of-the-box dexterity and generalize to unseen visual conditions and instructions.
> - Such foundation models can be specialized and rapidly adapted with limited data for complex long-horizon tasks, new short-horizon tasks, and even completely different robot embodiments.
