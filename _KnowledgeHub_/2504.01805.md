# SpaceR: Reinforcing MLLMs in Video Spatial Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> SpaceR introduces a framework for improving Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) in video spatial reasoning, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmarks such as VSI-Bench by surpassing GPT-4o by 11.6% accuracy. This advancement is facilitated by a novel, large-scale dataset, SpaceR-151k, and a training approach called Spatially-Guided Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Reward (SG-RLVR), which includes a map imagination mechanism.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with inferring and reconstructing underlying 3D spatial layouts and relationships from sequences of 2D video frames.
- There is a scarcity of high-quality, verifiable training datasets specifically designed to foster and evaluate spatial reasoning in MLLMs.
- Current training strategies, primarily Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), are often insufficient for cultivating deep, abstract reasoning capabilities in MLLMs.

## Method

- Created ==SpaceR-151k==, a novel large-scale dataset (151,310 samples) tailored for video spatial reasoning, including 91k verifiable spatial reasoning QA pairs derived from 3D scene data and 60k general video understanding samples.
- Developed ==Spatially-Guided Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Reward (SG-RLVR)==, which extends the GRPO algorithm with specialized mechanisms and reward functions for spatial reasoning.
- Introduced a ==map imagination mechanism== that guides the MLLM to explicitly generate a cognitive map of object distributions and positions, and a `Map Reward` to quantitatively evaluate its accuracy, encouraging spatial thinking.

## Results

- SpaceR achieves state-of-the-art performance on spatial reasoning benchmarks, scoring **45.6%** accuracy on VSI-Bench and surpassing GPT-4o by **11.6%** accuracy.
- The SG-RLVR training paradigm consistently improves performance across all evaluated spatial reasoning and general video understanding benchmarks (VSI-Bench, STI-Bench, SPAR-Bench, Video-MME, TempCompass, LongVideoBench), demonstrating superior generalizability compared to SFT.
- Ablation studies confirm the critical contribution of the SpaceR-151k dataset, particularly its spatial reasoning component, and the map imagination mechanism for enhancing spatial reasoning and overall video understanding.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Reward (SG-RLVR) significantly outperforms Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) in fostering generalizable reasoning capabilities in MLLMs, especially for complex tasks like spatial understanding.
> - Explicitly guiding MLLMs to generate cognitive maps during their reasoning process, coupled with a reward for map accuracy, effectively enhances their ability to infer and represent 3D spatial layouts from video.
> - The creation of a large-scale, high-quality dataset specifically designed for spatial reasoning, derived from 3D scene reconstructions, is crucial for training MLLMs in this advanced cognitive ability.
