# CGL: Advancing Continual GUI Learning via Reinforcement Fine-Tuning

> [!summary] Summary
> The CGL framework enables Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models to adapt continuously to evolving Graphical User Interfaces by synergistically integrating Supervised Fine-Tuning and Reinforcement Learning. It achieves superior average Step-wise Accuracy (e.g., 82.33% on QwenVL2.5-3b-Instruct) and minimizes catastrophic forgetting with a near-zero or positive Forgetting Measure on the new AndroidControl-CL benchmark.

## Problem

- GUI agents trained with static paradigms struggle to adapt to dynamic, evolving software environments, leading to performance degradation and obsolescence.
- Current GUI agent training methods face a "stability-plasticity dilemma": Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) offers fast adaptation but suffers catastrophic forgetting, while Reinforcement Learning (RL) retains knowledge but adapts slowly.
- There is a lack of dedicated, standardized benchmarks tailored for continual GUI learning in dynamic environments, hindering systematic evaluation.

## Method

- The **CGL** framework integrates Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and ==Group Relative Policy== Optimization (GRPO) into a joint training objective for Multimodal Large Language Models.
- Error-Aware Routing dynamically injects SFT guidance using ground-truth demonstrations when GRPO encounters sparse rewards and struggles to find solutions.
- Entropy-Regulated Tuning adaptively controls the SFT loss weight based on policy entropy and training steps, balancing exploration during early learning and exploitation for convergence.
- Conditional Gradient Surgery resolves conflicts between SFT and GRPO gradients by projecting the SFT gradient orthogonally to the GRPO gradient when their cosine similarity is negative, preventing detrimental knowledge interference.
- A new benchmark, AndroidControl-CL, was established to enable rigorous evaluation of continual GUI learning, featuring functional super-class task splits and fine-grained bounding box annotations.

## Results

- CGL achieved the highest average Step-wise Accuracy (**82.33%** with QwenVL2.5-3b-Instruct) and Trajectory-wise Accuracy (**38.03%**) on the AndroidControl-CL benchmark, outperforming all SFT and RL baselines.
- CGL exhibited the smallest Forgetting Measure (e.g., -**0.02** with QwenVL2.5-3b-Instruct, -**0.52** with LLaVA-OneVision-0.5b), demonstrating effective mitigation of catastrophic forgetting and even positive backward transfer in some task orders.
- Ablation studies confirmed the synergistic contributions of Error-Aware Routing, Entropy-Regulated Tuning, and Conditional Gradient Surgery, with each module progressively improving overall performance in continual GUI learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Supervised Fine-Tuning provides plasticity for rapid new task adaptation but causes catastrophic forgetting, while Reinforcement Learning (specifically GRPO) ensures stability but adapts slowly in sparse-reward GUI environments.
> - Dynamically managing policy entropy through SFT weighting is crucial for escaping low-entropy local optima during initial learning and for consolidating knowledge during convergence to ensure long-term retention.
> - Explicitly resolving gradient conflicts between SFT and GRPO through conditional gradient surgery is essential to prevent knowledge interference and maintain optimization stability in hybrid continual learning systems.
