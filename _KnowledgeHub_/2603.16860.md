# DreamPlan: Efficient Reinforcement Fine-Tuning of Vision-Language Planners via Video World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> DreamPlan presents a framework for efficiently fine-tuning Vision-Language Model (VLM) planners for deformable object manipulation through an action-conditioned video world model. This approach nearly doubles the average success rate of VLM planners on challenging real-world tasks, achieving an average score of 0.60, and reduces inference time per decision from thousands of seconds to approximately 1.12 seconds.

## Problem

- VLM planners, despite strong semantic reasoning, lack task-specific physical understanding, leading to ineffective actions and low success rates in real-world manipulation, especially for deformable objects.
- Directly fine-tuning VLMs with real-world Reinforcement Learning (RL) is prohibitively expensive, unsafe, and extremely sample-inefficient.
- Existing high-fidelity simulators struggle to model complex deformable object dynamics, and integrating learned world models into online RL is computationally intensive due to repeated video generation.

## Method

- The framework utilizes a pre-trained VLM, **Qwen3-VL-8B**, which generates discrete, keypoint-based manipulation commands conditioned on visual inputs and a goal image.
- An ==action-conditioned video world model== (built on **CogVideoX-5B**) is trained on sub-optimal exploratory data, with enhanced conditioning via rendered robot arm trajectories and a focus on predicting object-only videos.
- An efficient reinforcement fine-tuning strategy employs ==Best-of-K sampling== to generate preference signals from world model rollouts, which are then used for policy updates via ==Odds Ratio Policy Optimization== (**ORPO**), decoupling expensive video generation from policy optimization.

## Results

- DreamPlan's action-conditioned world model reliably simulates deformable dynamics, generating physically plausible motions and achieving a higher PSNR of 26.25 for object-only predictions compared to 24.70 for full-scene predictions.
- VLM planners fine-tuned with DreamPlan achieved an average score of 0.60 on real-world deformable manipulation tasks (e.g., rope straightening, cloth folding), nearly doubling the performance of the strongest zero-shot baseline (Qwen3-VL-32B at 0.35) and showing absolute gains of 15-**40%** in success rates.
- The framework demonstrated superior inference efficiency, reducing decision time from thousands of seconds (for an explicit verification baseline) to approximately 1.12 seconds, while achieving better performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Sub-optimal exploratory data collected by a zero-shot VLM is sufficient to train a robust action-conditioned video world model capable of accurately simulating complex deformable object dynamics.
> - Conditioning a video diffusion model with rendered robot arm trajectories and focusing on object-only prediction significantly improves the physical plausibility and accuracy of predicted deformations.
> - Decoupling world model inference from policy optimization through Best-of-K sampling and ORPO enables highly efficient reinforcement fine-tuning, overcoming the computational bottleneck of direct online RL with generative models.
