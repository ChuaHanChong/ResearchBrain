# Geometric Entropy: When Trajectory Diversity Helps and Hurts in Imitation Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Geometric Entropy (H_G) is a task-agnostic, pre-training metric for trajectory-shape diversity in robot demonstrations. It isolates free-space transit segments, aligns them in a target-anchored frame that strips goal pose and workspace scale, then scores the covariance eigenspectrum via effective dimensionality plus total variance. Across simulation and real-robot manipulation, success versus H_G traces an inverted-U whose peak shifts lower as task mastery rises.

## Problem

- Data-scaling imitation learning work treats diversity coarsely as tasks, objects, or embodiments, never quantifying trajectory geometry as its own dataset attribute.
- Curation and synthesis methods optimize selection, robustness, or coverage, but offer no predictive criterion for whether to raise or lower geometric diversity.
- Naive spread measures on raw positions inflate diversity from extrinsic factors like randomized goal pose and workspace scale, mistaking placement variation for genuine strategy difference.

## Method

- Phase decomposition splits episodes at semantic gripper events into free-space transit segments versus contact-constrained fine manipulation, scoring only transit where strategy variation is learnable.
- Endpoint-anchored frame alignment: arc-length resample to 50 waypoints, translate terminal point to origin, rotate into target frame, divide by start-end displacement.
- Geometric Entropy sums log effective dimensionality (exponentiated Shannon entropy of the normalized covariance spectrum) and log total variance, aggregated across phases by trajectory-count weighting.

## Results

- H_G stabilizes within 50-100 successful demonstrations on StackCube-v1, and rises monotonically with approach radius (2.901 at r=0 to 3.667 at r=0.10) while single-mode k=1 lowers it to 2.997.
- Diffusion Policy shows inverted-U success on StackCube-v1 and PegInsertionSide-v1 with the peak shifting leftward as dataset size grows from 100 to 1000; fine-tuned pi-0.5 decays monotonically.
- Real ARX-arm ACT policies over 40 test episodes and three seeds peak at H_G around 3.3 on StackCube, while PlacePanda and OpenDrawer decline monotonically; variance, LogDet, participation ratio, and kNN entropy all alias or drift.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Match demonstration diversity to learner mastery rather than maximizing it; more data, easier tasks, or stronger pretrained priors all shrink the tolerable diversity budget.
> - Normalize away extrinsic context before measuring diversity, otherwise identical straight-line motions at randomized goal poses register as maximally diverse.
> - Spectral summaries that combine mode count with mode spread avoid the aliasing where marginal-variance proxies cannot separate many tight modes from one wide corridor.
