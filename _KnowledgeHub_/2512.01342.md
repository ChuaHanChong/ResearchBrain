# InternVideo-Next: Towards General Video Foundation Models without Video-Text Supervision

> [!summary] Summary
> InternVideo-Next, a two-stage self-supervised pretraining framework, learns general video representations from unlabeled data, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmarks like Kinetics-400 and SSv2. The model exhibits strong generalization in tasks requiring physical and 3D spatial intelligence, along with competitive performance in multimodal applications.

## Problem

- Current text-supervised video models are limited by noisy, biased captions, hindering their ability to capture non-semantic visual features like motion and 3D priors.
- Self-supervised Masked Video Modeling (MVM) faces challenges with pixel-level reconstruction converging poorly and latent prediction methods encouraging "shortcut learning" instead of genuine spatiotemporal dynamics.
- Existing MVM architectures struggle to reconcile the learning of fine-grained pixel details with the extraction of high-level semantic abstraction.

## Method

- Introduces **InternVideo-Next**, a two-stage pretraining framework utilizing a disentangled Encoder-Predictor-Decoder (EPD) architecture to form a "Latent ==World Model==."
- Stage 1 employs semantic-guided pixel reconstruction, using a conditional ==diffusion== decoder to balance fine-grained detail with semantics and leveraging a frozen image semantic model (SigLIP) for semantic alignment.
- Stage 2 focuses on semantically coherent latent prediction, using a multi-block masking strategy and a frozen teacher (initialized from Stage 1) to learn robust spatiotemporal dynamics and prevent shortcut learning.

## Results

- InternVideo-Next achieves state-of-the-art results among video-only models on action recognition benchmarks like Kinetics-400 (**78.1%**) and SSv2 (**59.4%**), outperforming video-text pretrained counterparts.
- The model demonstrates capabilities in physical and 3D spatial intelligence, achieving superior performance in depth estimation on ScanNet and KITTI, and higher mean IOU for object tracking on Waymo Open.
- It exhibits strong multimodal compatibility, achieving competitive zero-shot video-text retrieval and serving as an effective foundation encoder for video-LLM frameworks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The performance gap in self-supervised video understanding stems from architectural limitations, not inherent paradigm flaws, particularly regarding pixel-level reconstruction and latent prediction.
> - Disentangling the Encoder-Predictor-Decoder (EPD) structure enables the Predictor to function as a "Latent World Model," fostering a semantically consistent and detail-preserving latent space.
> - Integrating image-level semantic priors and a conditional diffusion decoder in Stage 1, combined with a frozen, semantically rich teacher for latent prediction in Stage 2, effectively bridges pixel-level fidelity with high-level semantic abstraction.
