# OmniH2O: Universal and Dexterous Human-to-Humanoid Whole-Body Teleoperation and Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> The OmniH2O system presents a universal framework for human-to-humanoid teleoperation and learning, enabling dexterous whole-body control of humanoid robots. The system integrates various real-world input modalities and facilitates the learning of autonomous policies from human demonstrations, demonstrating robustness across diverse tasks and environments.

## Problem

- Controlling humanoid robots is challenging due to their high degrees of freedom and inherent instability.
- Coordinating dexterous manipulation and stable locomotion simultaneously in humanoid robots remains difficult.
- A lack of universal frameworks for real-time human-to-humanoid teleoperation and learning from diverse input modalities.

## Method

- **OmniH2O** provides a universal framework incorporating human motion retargeting, a two-phase sim-to-real training pipeline, and universal real-world teleoperation.
- It uses kinematic pose as an intermediate representation to bridge various human input modalities (VR, RGB cameras, verbal instructions) to robot actuation.
- A two-phase training pipeline, involving reinforcement learning for a privileged teacher policy and knowledge distillation to a real-world deployable student policy, enhances robustness and sim-to-real transfer.

## Results

- The system demonstrated robust whole-body control capabilities, including recovery from physical disturbances and stable navigation across various outdoor terrains.
- OmniH2O effectively supports multiple control interfaces, including VR-based, RGB camera-based, and verbal instruction, enabling real-time teleoperation.
- Autonomous policies learned from the OmniH2O-6 dataset successfully performed six complex whole-body tasks, such as 'Catch-Release' and 'Rock-Paper-Scissors', with high reliability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Using kinematic pose as an intermediate representation provides a universal bridge between various human input modalities and robot actuation.
> - A two-phase training pipeline, combining privileged reinforcement learning with teacher-student distillation and domain randomization, effectively enables robust sim-to-real transfer for complex humanoid control.
> - Specialized motion data augmentation, including sequences with fixed lower body positions, enhances stability during dexterous manipulation tasks.
