# TOPPO: Rethinking PPO for Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning with Critic Balancing

> [!summary] Summary
> TOPPO reformulates Proximal Policy Optimization for Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning by introducing "Critic Balancing" modules to resolve critic-side gradient ill-conditioning. This approach achieved a 90.88% mean success rate on MT50 with 22.7 times fewer parameters than top baselines, significantly improving performance on challenging tail tasks.

## Problem

- On-policy PPO methods historically underperformed off-policy SAC variants in Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning (MTRL), often attributed to inherent algorithmic limitations.
- Existing MTRL approaches, including strong off-policy methods, typically failed on challenging "tail tasks" in benchmarks like Meta-World, achieving 0% success.
- A key, overlooked issue within PPO was critic-side gradient ill-conditioning, where dominant gradients from easy tasks biased the shared critic and hindered learning for harder tasks.

## Method

- Introduced **TOPPO** (Tail-Optimized PPO), a reformulation of PPO that incorporates a suite of ==Critic Balancing== modules.
- Integrated ==PopArt== for per-task value-target normalization to address critic-side gradient scale spread (D1).
- Applied ==LayerNorm-Critic (LN-c)== to improve hidden-feature conditioning and ==FairGrad-Critic (FG-c)== for fair gradient aggregation to mitigate co-linear collapse (D2) and unfair aggregation (D3), along with ==PCGrad-Actor (PCGrad-a)== for actor-side conflict.

## Results

- TOPPO achieved a **90.88 ± 1.59%** mean success rate on MT50 (Meta-World V2), surpassing larger state-of-the-art baselines by nearly **+2pp** while using **22.7 times fewer parameters**.
- It significantly improved performance on the worst-10 tail tasks of MT50, reaching **56.50 ± 7.76%** success, compared to **0%** for most non-ARS SAC methods and **29.3%** for ARS-LoRA.
- Ablation studies confirmed the additive benefits of each "Critic Balancing" module, with LayerNorm-Critic providing the largest individual gain and PopArt proving essential for robust performance on diverse reward scales.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Critic-side gradient ill-conditioning, characterized by up to 497x spread in gradient norms and high co-linearity, is the primary impediment to PPO's performance in MTRL, more so than actor-side issues.
> - PopArt's per-task value normalization and LayerNorm on critic hidden layers are crucial for stabilizing gradient scales and improving feature conditioning across tasks.
> - FairGrad (α=1α=1) provides scale-invariant gradient aggregation for the critic, theoretically absorbing PopArt's rescaling effects and ensuring fair updates across tasks.
