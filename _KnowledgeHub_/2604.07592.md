# Spatio-Temporal Grounding of Large Language Models from Perception Streams

> [!summary] Summary
> The Formally Explainable Spatio-Temporal Scenes (FESTS) framework, developed by Toyota Motor North America Research & Development, enables enhanced spatio-temporal reasoning in LLMs through automatically generated, verifiable supervision. This approach allowed a 3-billion-parameter LLM to achieve an 87.5% Frame F1 on complex spatio-temporal tasks, exceeding GPT-4.1's 84.8% on the proposed benchmark.

## Problem

- Frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) and Visual Language Models (VLMs) demonstrate limitations in fine-grained spatial reasoning, metric distance comprehension, and accurate temporal ordering for dynamic 3D environments.
- Existing spatio-temporal reasoning benchmarks often rely on simple queries like multiple-choice QA, lacking the depth and verifiability needed for robust real-world reasoning in complex scenarios.
- Manually annotating large datasets for complex spatio-temporal events is prohibitively expensive, posing a scalability challenge for training data critical to advanced reasoning capabilities.

## Method

- The **FESTS** (Formally Explainable Spatio-Temporal Scenes) framework automatically generates verifiable spatio-temporal supervision by matching complex queries against perception logs, yielding ==(query, frames, match, explanation) tuples==.
- The ==Spatial Regular Expression (SpRE)== language is extended with universal and existential quantifiers (==q-SpRE==) to enable persistent object tracking and the expression of more complex, multi-object relationships over time.
- A multi-stage fine-tuning approach, combining ==Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)== on query-answer pairs and ==Reinforcement Learning (RL)== with a custom ==hierarchical reward function==, is applied to a target LLM.

## Results

- A **3-billion-parameter** LLM, fine-tuned with the Q-SFT+RL pipeline, achieved an overall Frame F1 score of **87.5%** and an Exact Match score of **64.5%** on the FESTS benchmark.
- The fine-tuned Q-SFT+RL model slightly surpassed GPT-4.1's overall Frame F1 of **84.8%** on the same benchmark, demonstrating competitive performance with a model two orders of magnitude smaller.
- Performance gains scaled positively with increasing query lengths, with the Q-SFT+RL model achieving an **82.7%** Frame F1 score for the longest (16-frame) input sequences, marking a **17-point** increase over SFT alone.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Automatically generated, self-verifiable spatio-temporal supervision, derived from formal methods, can substantially improve LLM reasoning capabilities without the need for manual, crowd-sourced labeling.
> - Extending formal pattern matching languages with quantifiers is crucial for enabling robust and nuanced spatio-temporal reasoning, supporting persistent object tracking and complex logical conditions over time.
> - Targeted, logically-grounded fine-tuning can imbue relatively small LLMs (3 billion parameters) with expert spatio-temporal reasoning capabilities that are competitive with, or even surpass, much larger frontier models.
