# Matrix-Game: Interactive World Foundation Model

> [!summary] Summary
> Skywork AI developed Matrix-Game, a 17-billion-parameter interactive world foundation model that generates game worlds from a reference image and user actions. The model achieves superior action controllability and physical consistency, outperforming previous models on a new comprehensive benchmark for Minecraft environments.

## Problem

- Existing interactive video datasets are scarce and lack fine-grained action annotations for training robust world models.
- Prior world models struggle to accurately simulate physical dynamics and achieve fine-grained, frame-by-frame controllability in generated environments.
- A lack of standardized, objective evaluation benchmarks hinders consistent comparison and progress in interactive world modeling research.

## Method

- Developed **Matrix-Game**, a **17-billion-parameter** multi-modal ==Diffusion Transformer==, for interactive image-to-world generation, conditioned purely on a reference image and discrete/continuous actions.
- Curated **Matrix-Game-MC**, a large-scale dataset of **2,700 hours** of unlabeled Minecraft videos and **1,200 hours** of action-labeled gameplay, with a ==two-stage progressive training strategy==.
- Introduced **GameWorld Score**, a unified benchmark assessing models across visual quality, temporal quality, action controllability, and physical rule understanding.

## Results

- **Matrix-Game** surpassed prior open-source Minecraft world models across all eight dimensions of the GameWorld Score benchmark, showing **0.95** keyboard accuracy and **0.76** object consistency.
- Fine-grained action control was demonstrated with over **88%** accuracy for individual keyboard actions and over **89%** accuracy for mouse-based camera movements across eight directions.
- Double-blind human evaluations confirmed Matrix-Game's superiority, achieving a **96.3%** win rate in overall quality and maintaining strong visual and temporal coherence in long-duration autoregressive generations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Conditioning a diffusion model purely on a reference image and user actions allows for intuitive, fine-grained control and fosters spatial intelligence in world generation.
> - Scaling both the model size (17 billion parameters) and the curated interactive video dataset significantly improves generalization and understanding of physical rules across diverse virtual environments.
> - A two-stage progressive training approach, leveraging unlabeled data for broad world understanding before fine-tuning with action-labeled data, improves model stability and interactive performance.
