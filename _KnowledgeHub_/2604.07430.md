# HY-Embodied-0.5: Embodied Foundation Models for Real-World Agents

> [!summary] Summary
> Tencent Robotics X and HY Vision Team introduced HY-Embodied-0.5, a family of foundation models designed for real-world embodied agents that enhance fine-grained visual perception and complex embodied reasoning. The models achieved leading performance on 16 out of 22 embodied benchmarks and demonstrated robust control in real-world robotic tasks like precision plug-in packing (85% success) and mug hanging (75% success).

## Problem

- General Vision-Language Models (VLMs) often lack the fine-grained visual perception necessary for robust physical interaction and decision-making in the real world.
- Mainstream VLMs are not inherently optimized for embodied prediction, interaction, and planning in dynamic physical environments.
- A significant functional gap exists between the broad capabilities of general VLMs and the specialized requirements of embodied intelligence for real-world agents.

## Method

- Developed **HY-Embodied-0.5**, a family of foundation models with architectural innovations like **HY-ViT 2.0** for efficient visual encoding and ==Modality-Adaptive Computing== with ==Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT)==.
- Compiled a diverse, high-quality corpus of over **100 million** vision-language training samples, including visual perception, embodied-centric, and spatial-centric data.
- Employed a ==multi-stage training pipeline== (pre-training, mid-training, post-training with Supervised Fine-tuning, Reinforcement Learning, ==iterative self-evolving training==, and ==large-to-small on-policy distillation==).

## Results

- The efficient **HY-Embodied-0.5 MoT-2B** model achieved an average score of **58.0%** across 22 embodied-relevant benchmarks, leading on **16** tasks.
- The powerful **HY-Embodied-0.5 MoE-A32B** model achieved an average score of **67.0%**, surpassing frontier models like Gemini 3.0 Pro (**63.6%**) and Seed 2.0 (**66.2%**).
- In real-world robot control tasks, the VLA model (based on MoT-2B) achieved **85%** success for precision plug-in packing, **80%** for tableware stacking, and **75%** for mug hanging.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Modality-adaptive architectures like Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) can efficiently integrate visual and linguistic processing while maintaining performance for embodied tasks.
> - A comprehensive data strategy, combining diverse visual perception, embodied, and spatial understanding data, is crucial for developing robust embodied intelligence.
> - Iterative self-evolving post-training strategies and on-policy distillation effectively enhance complex reasoning abilities and enable knowledge transfer to compact models suitable for edge deployment.
