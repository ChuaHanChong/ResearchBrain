# Mogao: An Omni Foundation Model for Interleaved Multi-Modal Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Mogao, an omni foundation model from ByteDance Seed, enables the causal, multi-turn generation of interleaved text and images by seamlessly integrating autoregressive and diffusion models. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across multimodal understanding and text-to-image generation benchmarks, while also demonstrating emergent capabilities like zero-shot image editing.

## Problem

- Integrating text-based autoregressive models with image-based diffusion models effectively without architectural misalignment or compromising individual strengths.
- The challenge of creating unified visual representations that are optimal for both high-fidelity image generation and robust semantic understanding.
- Enabling truly interleaved multi-turn generation of both text and images, where any previous output can condition subsequent outputs across modalities.

## Method

- A hybrid architecture that causally models sequential distributions of modalities, using ==next token prediction for text and rectified flow matching for images==.
- Employs a ==dual visual encoder== (ViT for understanding, VAE for generation) and a ==deep-fusion architecture== with modality-specific MLPs and projectors to mitigate task interference.
- Introduces ==Interleaved Rotary Position Embedding (IL-RoPE)== for improved 3D positional encoding and ==Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF)== for efficient training on interleaved data.

## Results

- Mogao-7B achieved an overall score of **0.89** on the GenEval benchmark, outperforming Janus-Pro-7B (**0.80**) and SD3-Medium (**0.74**) in text-to-image generation.
- It obtained the highest average score of **57.4** across six diverse multimodal understanding benchmarks (POPE, MME-P, MMBench, SEEDBench, GQA, MMMU), surpassing other unified models.
- The model demonstrated emergent zero-shot image editing capabilities, including adding, removing, and modifying elements, and applying style transfers through interleaved multi-turn interactions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality multimodal understanding and generation can be harmonized within a single foundation model by carefully designing architectural components, such as dual encoders and deep-fusion with modality-specific paths, to mitigate task interference.
> - Training on diverse, large-scale interleaved multimodal data enables emergent capabilities like zero-shot image editing and compositional generation, even without explicit task-specific fine-tuning.
> - Efficient training strategies, including Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF) and optimized positional embeddings (IL-RoPE), are crucial for scaling and practically realizing interleaved multimodal generation models.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

#### 1.1. Evolution of Multimodal Models

The field of artificial intelligence has seen rapid advancements in large language models (LLMs) and text-to-image (T2I) generation models. Initially, these models operated largely in isolation, processing single-modal inputs (text for LLMs, text for T2I) and generating single-modal outputs. For instance, LLMs like GPT-3 excel at understanding and generating human-like text, while diffusion models such as Stable Diffusion and DALL-E have achieved remarkable photorealism in image synthesis from text prompts.

The natural progression has been towards **unified multimodal models**, aiming to bridge the gap between these modalities. Early efforts focused on **visual understanding** by extending LLMs to Large Multi-Modal Models (LMMs or VLMs). These models, exemplified by Flamingo or LLaVA, typically convert visual features into discrete or continuous tokens, enabling LLMs to process images as additional "text" input for tasks like visual question answering (VQA) or image captioning. However, these models primarily perform text generation conditioned on visual inputs.

Simultaneously, researchers explored unifying **visual generation** within an autoregressive (AR) framework, treating image patches as tokens to be generated sequentially, similar to text (e.g., Emu series, Chameleon). While conceptually simple, AR-based image generation often struggles with high-quality synthesis, suffering from issues like low training efficiency, exposure bias, and suboptimal image tokenization compared to diffusion models.

#### 1.2. Challenges in Unified Multimodal Systems

The core challenge in creating truly unified models lies in harmonizing the distinct paradigms of text and image processing:
- **Architectural Misalignment:** Autoregressive models (for text) and diffusion models (for images) operate on fundamentally different principles. Integrating them effectively without compromising the strengths of either is complex. Hybrid architectures have emerged, sharing transformer backbones but applying separate generation mechanisms (e.g., Transfusion, Show-O). However, these often face **task interference** where parameters optimized for one modality or task (e.g., understanding) might degrade performance on another (e.g., generation).
- **Representation Mismatch:** Visual representations optimized for understanding (e.g., those from Vision Transformers like ViT or SigLIP for semantic content) are often suboptimal for high-fidelity image generation, which typically relies on autoencoders like VAEs to compress images into a latent space. Finding a unified visual representation that serves both purposes well is a persistent challenge (e.g., JanusFlow, SEED).
- **Interleaved Generation Complexity:** While some models can generate *single-turn* multimodal outputs (e.g., an image from text, or text from an image), the ability to handle **arbitrarily interleaved sequences** of text and images—where previous outputs of any modality can serve as conditions for subsequent outputs of any modality in a multi-turn conversation—represents a significant leap towards more human-like interaction. This requires robust causal modeling across modalities and the ability to maintain coherence and identity across multiple generated visual elements.
- **Training Efficiency and Data:** Training large multimodal models requires massive, diverse datasets. Curating high-quality, interleaved multimodal data and developing efficient training strategies to handle variable-length, mixed-modality sequences are critical bottlenecks.

#### 1.3. Mogao's Position

Mogao directly addresses these challenges by proposing an "omni foundation model" designed for **interleaved multi-modal generation**. It positions itself as an advancement over existing unified models by:
- **Seamlessly integrating AR and Diffusion:** It uses a unified transformer backbone but employs modality-specific processing (e.g., distinct MLPs and QKV layers), seeking to mitigate task interference without sacrificing the strengths of either.
- **Dual Vision Encoders:** It strategically leverages both ViT (for understanding) and VAE (for generation) representations, integrating them cleverly within its architecture to improve contextual awareness for generation and robust understanding.
- **Causal Interleaved Generation at the Modal Level:** Unlike many models limited to single-turn outputs, Mogao is designed to model sequential distributions of modalities causally, allowing any generated modality to condition subsequent generations.
- **Efficient Training and Data Strategy:** It introduces an efficient training pipeline, including novel techniques like Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF), on a large-scale, in-house interleaved dataset, which is crucial for unlocking its emergent capabilities like zero-shot image editing.

In essence, Mogao aims to push the boundary from merely "unified" models (which combine understanding and generation) to "omni" models capable of truly fluid, multi-turn, multi-modal interaction, mirroring human communication.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the development of Mogao can be summarized as follows:

1. **Advance Beyond Single-Modal Generation:** The core motivation is to move beyond the limitations of contemporary unified models that are typically constrained to single-modal outputs (e.g., generating only an image from text, or only text from an image), even if they can process multi-modal inputs. Mogao aims to enable the generation of **interleaved sequences of both text and images**.

2. **Enable Human-like Multimodal Interaction:** To achieve Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and facilitate more natural, human-like interaction with AI systems, models must be capable of interpreting and generating content in arbitrarily interleaved sequences of text and images. This means seamlessly switching between modalities in both input and output, maintaining coherence and context.

3. **Harness Strengths of Both AR and Diffusion Models:** The authors seek to combine the robust text generation and understanding capabilities of autoregressive (AR) large language models with the high-quality image synthesis prowess of diffusion models. The goal is to create a hybrid architecture that leverages the best of both paradigms without succumbing to their respective weaknesses (e.g., AR's struggles with image quality, or task interference in shared-parameter models).

4. **Mitigate Task Interference:** A significant challenge in unified models is the conflict between visual understanding and generation tasks when sharing model parameters. Mogao aims to decouple these tasks within its architecture to enhance performance across both domains.

5. **Develop a Practical and Efficient Foundation Model:** The research emphasizes the development of a "practical omni-modal foundation model." This implies a focus not only on achieving state-of-the-art performance but also on computational efficiency during both training and inference, making the model scalable and deployable for real-world applications. This includes innovations in training strategies and data handling.

6. **Unlock Emergent Multimodal Capabilities:** By enabling causal interleaved generation, the authors anticipate and demonstrate emergent capabilities such as zero-shot image editing and compositional generation, which are difficult to achieve with models trained solely on paired text-image data.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Mogao employs a sophisticated hybrid architecture that integrates autoregressive (AR) modeling for text and rectified flow matching for images, allowing for causal, interleaved generation of both modalities.

#### 3.1. Core Unified Framework

Mogao's foundational concept is to model the sequential distributions at the modal level in a causal manner: $P_\theta(x) = \sum_{t=0}^T \log P_\theta(x_{t+1} | x_0, \ldots, x_t)$, where $x_t$ can be either an image ($x_{img}$) or a text sequence ($x_{txt}$). This allows any preceding modality to condition subsequent generations.
- **Text Generation (Understanding):** For text, Mogao uses a Next Token Prediction (NTP) loss, similar to standard LLMs, where the model predicts the next text token conditioned on the historical sequence (text and visual features).
- **Image Generation:** For images, it adopts a rectified flow matching loss, a variant of diffusion models, to predict the velocity field for image denoising. This process generates high-quality images from noisy latents.
- **Overall Training Loss:** The total loss is a weighted combination of NTP loss and flow matching loss, optimized simultaneously.
- **Modality Switching:** During inference, special `<|vision_start|>` and `<|vision_end|>` tokens are used to signal the model to switch between image and text generation modes.

#### 3.2. Architectural Innovations

Mogao introduces several key technical improvements to its transformer-based architecture:

##### 3.2.1. Improved Dual Visual Encoder

Recognizing the different requirements of visual representations for understanding (semantic) and generation (fidelity), Mogao employs a dual visual encoder strategy, building upon ideas from JanusFlow:
- **ViT for Understanding:** A Vision Transformer (ViT, specifically SigLIP from Qwen2-VL) is used to extract robust semantic representations for visual understanding tasks. Text tokens primarily attend to these ViT tokens for contextual alignment.
- **VAE for Generation:** A Variational Autoencoder (VAE from Flux) encodes images into a low-dimensional latent space for the diffusion process, crucial for high-quality image synthesis.
- **Simultaneous Extraction (Conditioning):** When an image acts as a condition, Mogao simultaneously extracts *both* ViT and VAE visual representations and appends them to the historical sequence.
- **Selective Attention (Generation):** For image generation, noisy VAE tokens attend to *all* tokens (ViT, VAE, text) in the historical sequence, allowing the model to leverage both semantic and detailed visual context.

##### 3.2.2. Deep-Fusion Architecture

Mogao builds upon a pre-trained LLM backbone (Qwen2.5-3B, extended to 7B trainable parameters through modifications) and incorporates a "deep-fusion" design inspired by MMDiT:
- **Unified Self-Attention:** A single self-attention layer processes both visual and text sequences.
- **Modality-Specific MLPs and Projectors:** Crucially, to mitigate task interference, Mogao employs *distinct* Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs) in the feed-forward network (FFN) and linear projectors in the attention blocks for processing visual versus text modalities. ViT tokens are specifically routed to the "text branch" due to their semantic alignment.
- **Timestep Modulation:** Timestep embeddings (for diffusion) modulate visual feature maps in each block via an AdaLN layer.
- **Benefits:** This deep fusion allows the LLM's text representations and image features to interact at every layer, enabling high-quality image generation *without needing an external text encoder*. It also naturally inherits the LLM's long-context capabilities, vital for extended interleaved sequences.

##### 3.2.3. Interleaved Rotary Position Embedding (IL-RoPE)

To handle the 3D positional relationships (Temporal 'T', Height 'H', Width 'W') in interleaved multimodal sequences, Mogao proposes an improved RoPE variant:
- **Position ID Design:** For images, spatial H/W IDs are reset from 0 for each image instance, ensuring consistent spatial understanding regardless of position in the sequence. The temporal ID increases for each token, but all tokens within an image share the same temporal ID.
- **Frequency Allocation:** Unlike prior 3D-RoPE designs that might disproportionately allocate frequencies (e.g., higher to temporal, lower to spatial), IL-RoPE interleaves frequency assignments for {T, H, W} across the embedding dimensions. This ensures a balanced capture of both long-range and local semantic information for all dimensions, crucial for detailed image synthesis.

##### 3.2.4. Multi-modal Classifier-free Guidance

Mogao extends the standard Classifier-free Guidance (CFG) mechanism to a dual-coefficient approach for interleaved generation:
- **Problem:** In interleaved scenarios, a standard single CFG coefficient can lead to issues like image repetition, as the influence of preceding *image* context might be too strong or weak compared to preceding *text* context.
- **Solution:** Mogao introduces two distinct CFG coefficients: $\gamma$ for the general (cross-modal) condition (empty condition) and $\gamma_{img}$ specifically for the intra-modal image condition (visual-only condition). This allows fine-grained control over the influence of text and image history on the generated image, enhancing precision and coherence.

#### 3.3. Data Curation and Training Strategy

##### 3.3.1. Data Composition

Mogao is trained on a diverse mixture of large-scale data:
- **Text-only Data:** Inherited from DouBao LM datasets.
- **Visual Understanding Data:** Inherited from DouBao VLM datasets.
- **Image Generation Data:** Used for training SeedDream, crucial for high-quality image synthesis.
- **In-house Interleaved Multi-modal Data:** A core contribution. This ten-million-scale dataset is curated from publicly available websites and videos. For native interleaved content, original order is preserved. For video clips, a VLM is used to caption sampled frames, which are then intertwined with captions to create interleaved sequences.

##### 3.3.2. Training Phases and Efficiencies

The training is structured in three stages:
1. **Stage 1 (Low-resolution Unified):** Unified training on multimodal understanding and text-to-image data (4:1 ratio), with images up to 256x256 resolution. All parameters except visual encoder are trainable.
2. **Stage 2 (High-resolution Unified):** Continues unified training with the same data ratio, increasing image resolution to 512x512 for higher quality.
3. **Stage 3 (Multi-modal Interleaved):** Incorporates the interleaved multimodal generated data for training. Some understanding (20%) and T2I (40%) datasets are still included to maintain performance. Image resolution remains 512x512, and Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF) is employed.

**Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF):** This innovation addresses the train-inference discrepancy in interleaved generation where all visual elements are designated for prediction during training, even if they are noisy inputs. ECTF introduces a "clean-noise decoupling" via a dynamic causal attention mask. This means clean images with interleaved sentences are processed separately from noisy images, eliminating redundant computation of ahead items under perturbed conditions, thus improving computational efficiency and conditional consistency.

**Native Resolution:** Throughout training, Mogao uses a native resolution strategy, converting input images into a variable number of visual tokens while preserving aspect ratios, which improves efficiency and visual understanding compared to fixed-resolution padding.

**Global Batch Reduced Loss:** An efficient approximation method for calculating unbiased gradients across multiple GPUs when training with mixed-source multimodal data with variable token weights, minimizing communication overhead.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Mogao demonstrates state-of-the-art performance across multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and novel interleaved multi-modal generation capabilities.

#### 4.1. Interleaved Multi-Modal Generation

- **Qualitative Excellence:** Figure 2 visually showcases Mogao's ability to produce high-quality, coherent interleaved outputs, exhibiting strong ID preservation (e.g., maintaining the same puppy or frog character across different scenes) and instruction-following abilities within complex, multi-turn interactions involving both text and images. The model effectively generates accurate images based on both preceding text and image context.
- **Zero-Shot Image Editing:** Despite not being explicitly trained on task-specific image editing datasets, Mogao exhibits impressive emergent capabilities in zero-shot image editing (Figure 6). It can successfully execute various editing instructions, including adding, removing, and modifying elements, changing actions, adjusting perspective and layout, and applying artistic style transfers (e.g., to Van Gogh painting, pencil drawing, Ghibli comics). This highlights its robust generalization from interleaved training data.

#### 4.2. Text-to-Image Generation Performance

- **Benchmark Superiority:** Mogao-7B achieves comparable or superior performance to leading multi-modal unified models like Janus-Pro-7B and even strong generation-only models like SD3-Medium on standard text-to-image benchmarks:
 - **GenEval:** Mogao achieves an overall score of 0.89, outperforming Janus-Pro-7B (0.80) and SD3-Medium (0.74), particularly excelling in object position understanding.
 - **DPG-Bench and GenAI-Bench:** Consistent strong performance across these benchmarks, further validating its robust generation capabilities.
- **Human Evaluation:** In human evaluations on the Bench-240 dataset, Mogao-7B significantly outperforms advanced unified models like Janus-Pro 7B and Emu3-Gen 8B across three critical dimensions: Aesthetics, Text-Image Alignment, and Structural Correction (Figure 7). This suggests Mogao produces outputs that are not only quantitatively accurate but also subjectively pleasing and practical for real-world scenarios.
- **Aesthetic Quality:** Figure 1 showcases Mogao's remarkable aesthetic performance in image generation, demonstrating strong composition, color manipulation, and the ability to generate poster-style images with inherent text rendering.

#### 4.3. Multi-Modal Understanding Performance

- **State-of-the-Art (SOTA) in Understanding:** Despite its focus on generation, Mogao also achieves competitive, and often state-of-the-art, results on various multimodal understanding benchmarks (Table 4):
 - It achieves SOTA on POPE, MME-P, SEED, and MMMU benchmarks.
 - It obtains the highest average score (57.4) across six diverse benchmarks (POPE, MME-P, MMBench, SEEDBench, GQA, MMMU), surpassing other unified models like Janus-Pro-7B (57.1) and TokenFlow-XL (54.4).
- This strong performance in understanding, alongside generation, validates Mogao's success in integrating both tasks effectively within a single model.

#### 4.4. Ablation Studies

Comprehensive ablation studies rigorously validate the effectiveness of Mogao's key architectural designs:
- **IL-RoPE vs. M-RoPE:** Experiments confirmed that the proposed Interleaved Rotary Position Embedding (IL-RoPE) significantly improves image generation quality and accelerates loss convergence compared to prior frequency allocation strategies (Figure 8, Exp. A vs. Exp. B), attributing this to more balanced long-range and local semantic information capture.
- **Dual Visual Encoder:** The addition of high-level semantic representations from ViT (used for understanding) significantly enhances multimodal understanding abilities (Table 5, Exp. B vs. Exp. C) and also contributes to lower image generation loss, by aiding the model in learning high-level semantics during the diffusion process.
- **Deep-Fusion Architecture:** Decoupling understanding and generation within the backbone using modality-specific MLPs and linear projectors (Deep-Fusion) leads to a significant reduction in flow matching loss for image generation and further improvements in multimodal understanding scores (Figure 8 & Table 5, Exp. C vs. Exp. D). This confirms that separating parameters for tasks with differing representational needs is beneficial.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Mogao represents a significant step forward in the development of general artificial intelligence and multimodal interaction, offering several key areas of significance and potential impact:

1. **True Interleaved Multimodal Communication:** Mogao's ability to perform causal, multi-turn, interleaved generation of text and images is a critical advancement towards more natural and human-like AI systems. This enables dynamic and contextually rich interactions that go beyond simple text-to-image or image-to-text translations, paving the way for more sophisticated multimodal dialogues and creative workflows.

2. **Harmonized Understanding and Generation:** By effectively integrating autoregressive text generation and diffusion-based image synthesis within a single, efficient architecture, Mogao demonstrates that high performance in both multimodal understanding and generation can be achieved simultaneously. This addresses a long-standing challenge of task interference in unified models. The deep-fusion architecture and dual visual encoders are crucial to this success, showing a path to optimize for disparate modal requirements.

3. **Emergent Capabilities and Practical Applications:** The demonstrated emergent capabilities, particularly zero-shot image editing and compositional generation, are highly significant. These functionalities, arising directly from the interleaved training paradigm without explicit task-specific fine-tuning, highlight the power of broad, general-purpose multimodal foundation models. This opens doors for practical applications in creative industries (e.g., design, content creation, visual storytelling), education, and enhanced user interfaces where intuitive image manipulation and contextual generation are desired.

4. **Efficiency and Scalability:** Innovations like Native Resolution, Global Batch Reduced Loss, and especially Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF) contribute to a more computationally efficient training and inference pipeline for large-scale multimodal models. This efficiency is paramount for industrial adoption and for scaling these models further, making advanced multimodal AI more accessible.

5. **Blueprint for Future Foundation Models:** Mogao serves as a compelling proof-of-concept for the "omni-modal" foundation model paradigm. Its success in integrating diverse modalities and tasks, coupled with robust performance, provides a strong inspiration and a potential architectural blueprint for future research into general-purpose AI systems capable of seamlessly interacting across multiple sensory data types. This work suggests that unified models, especially those capable of true interleaved generation, hold greater potential than single-modal approaches for advancing AI.
