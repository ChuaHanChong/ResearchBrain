# Mogao: An Omni Foundation Model for Interleaved Multi-Modal Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Mogao, an omni foundation model from ByteDance Seed, enables the causal, multi-turn generation of interleaved text and images by seamlessly integrating autoregressive and diffusion models. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across multimodal understanding and text-to-image generation benchmarks, while also demonstrating emergent capabilities like zero-shot image editing.

## Problem

- Integrating text-based autoregressive models with image-based diffusion models effectively without architectural misalignment or compromising individual strengths.
- The challenge of creating unified visual representations that are optimal for both high-fidelity image generation and robust semantic understanding.
- Enabling truly interleaved multi-turn generation of both text and images, where any previous output can condition subsequent outputs across modalities.

## Method

- A hybrid architecture that causally models sequential distributions of modalities, using ==next token prediction for text and rectified flow matching for images==.
- Employs a ==dual visual encoder== (ViT for understanding, VAE for generation) and a ==deep-fusion architecture== with modality-specific MLPs and projectors to mitigate task interference.
- Introduces ==Interleaved Rotary Position Embedding (IL-RoPE)== for improved 3D positional encoding and ==Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF)== for efficient training on interleaved data.

## Results

- Mogao-7B achieved an overall score of **0.89** on the GenEval benchmark, outperforming Janus-Pro-7B (**0.80**) and SD3-Medium (**0.74**) in text-to-image generation.
- It obtained the highest average score of **57.4** across six diverse multimodal understanding benchmarks (POPE, MME-P, MMBench, SEEDBench, GQA, MMMU), surpassing other unified models.
- The model demonstrated emergent zero-shot image editing capabilities, including adding, removing, and modifying elements, and applying style transfers through interleaved multi-turn interactions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality multimodal understanding and generation can be harmonized within a single foundation model by carefully designing architectural components, such as dual encoders and deep-fusion with modality-specific paths, to mitigate task interference.
> - Training on diverse, large-scale interleaved multimodal data enables emergent capabilities like zero-shot image editing and compositional generation, even without explicit task-specific fine-tuning.
> - Efficient training strategies, including Efficient Complete Teacher Forcing (ECTF) and optimized positional embeddings (IL-RoPE), are crucial for scaling and practically realizing interleaved multimodal generation models.
