# MARK: Memory Augmented Refinement of Knowledge

> [!summary] Summary
> A memory-augmented framework called MARK enables continuous learning and knowledge refinement in Large Language Models through specialized agents managing domain insights, user interactions, and response refinement, achieving improved accuracy and reduced hallucinations while maintaining coherence across multi-turn conversations without requiring model fine-tuning.

## Problem

- Traditional LLMs struggle with incorporating evolving domain knowledge and adapting to user-specific contexts
- Current approaches like RAG rely on fixed knowledge sources requiring manual updates
- LLMs face challenges with hallucination and maintaining consistency over multiple interactions

## Method

- **MARK** introduces ==specialized memory agents== for managing domain insights, user questions, and LLM responses
- Implements ==Memory Builder Service (MBS)== and ==Memory Search Service (MSS)== for knowledge extraction and retrieval
- Uses ==Memory Relevance Scoring== to prioritize information based on ==recall count==, ==recency==, and ==feedback==

## Results

- Demonstrated reduction in hallucinations through memory-based refinements
- Achieved improved efficiency with lower average token count per response
- Maintained response coherence and knowledge adaptation across multi-turn, multi-user interactions
- Successfully integrated domain expert knowledge and user feedback into the system's responses

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Memory augmentation significantly improves response accuracy while reducing token usage
> - Dynamic memory refinement enables continuous learning without model fine-tuning
> - Structured memory architecture helps maintain coherence across multiple conversation turns

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work falls under the broader research area of improving Large Language Models (LLMs) for domain-specific applications. It addresses the limitations of traditional LLMs in handling evolving domain knowledge and adapting to user-specific contexts. The research draws inspiration from the "Society of Mind" concept, proposing an architecture of specialized agents working together to manage and refine knowledge.

Here's how it fits into the research landscape:

- **Addressing LLM Limitations:** The paper directly tackles the well-known challenges of LLMs, such as hallucination, difficulty in incorporating new information, and lack of long-term memory. This aligns with a significant trend in LLM research aimed at making these models more reliable and adaptable.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Alternatives:** The paper positions MARK as an alternative or enhancement to RAG. While RAG is a common approach, it relies on fixed knowledge sources that require manual updates. MARK proposes a more dynamic and automated approach to knowledge refinement, potentially overcoming the limitations of RAG.
- **Memory-Augmented LLMs:** The core concept of MARK aligns with the growing interest in memory-augmented LLMs. This field explores various ways to equip LLMs with external memory modules to improve their ability to retain and utilize information over time. MARK contributes a specific architecture and methodology for memory management within this space.
- **Agentic AI:** The paper leverages the concept of "agentic AI," where specialized agents perform specific tasks within a larger system. This approach is becoming increasingly popular in AI research, as it allows for modularity, specialization, and improved control over complex AI systems.
- **Knowledge Management:** The research touches upon knowledge management principles, aiming to create a system that can continuously learn, prioritize relevant information, and discard outdated insights. This aligns with the broader field of knowledge engineering and aims to bring these principles to the realm of LLMs.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The key objectives and motivations behind this research are:

- **Enable Continuous Learning for LLMs:** The primary motivation is to enable LLMs to continuously learn and adapt to evolving domain knowledge without requiring costly and time-consuming fine-tuning.
- **Bridge the Gap between Domain Experts and AI Systems:** The paper aims to bridge the gap between the deep, nuanced understanding of domain experts and the knowledge available to AI systems. This is particularly important in specialized fields like healthcare, law, and manufacturing.
- **Improve Accuracy and Reduce Hallucinations:** A key objective is to reduce hallucinations and inconsistencies in LLM responses, particularly in high-risk domains where accuracy is critical.
- **Personalize AI Assistants:** The research aims to improve virtual assistants by enabling them to remember user preferences and ensure coherent responses over time, leading to a more personalized and user-friendly experience.
- **Address Limitations of RAG:** The authors identify limitations in RAG-based systems, such as dependence on fixed knowledge sources and lack of dynamic adaptation, and propose MARK as a way to overcome these issues.
- **Scalable Solution:** The authors also address that the proposed solution, MARK, is a scalable agentic memory design framework that enhances LLM adaptability and performance using agentic AI and memory injections.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology and approach employed in this research involve:

- **Memory-Augmented Architecture:** The core of the approach is the MARK framework, which consists of a society of specialized agents:
 - **Residual Refined Memory Agent:** Stores and retrieves domain-specific insights.
 - **User Question Refined Memory Agent:** Captures user-provided facts, abbreviations, and terminology.
 - **LLM Response Refined Memory Agent:** Extracts key elements from responses for refinement and personalization.
- **Knowledge-First Approach:** The framework prioritizes a "knowledge-first" approach, where domain knowledge is prioritized and then refined through memory injection.
- **Memory Builder Service (MBS) and Memory Search Service (MSS):** MBS extracts and stores memories, while MSS retrieves relevant memories based on similarity scores and relevance ranking.
- **Memory Relevance Scoring (MRS):** A scoring mechanism is used to re-rank retrieved memories based on recall count, recency, similarity score, and feedback score. This allows the system to prioritize relevant and up-to-date information.
- **Experimental Evaluation:** The authors conducted a series of four experiments to evaluate the effectiveness of MARK:
 - **Baseline Evaluation:** Assessing the performance of a chatbot without memory refinement.
 - **Memory Construction through User Interaction:** Analyzing how well the chatbot retains and applies user corrections.
 - **Knowledge Refinement through Injected Memory:** Evaluating the chatbot's ability to integrate SME-driven updates and user feedback.
 - **Multi-Turn, Multi-User Interaction and Response Evaluation:** Assessing MARK in a realistic multi-user environment.
- **Dataset:** The MedMCQA dataset, a large-scale medical question-answering dataset, was used for evaluation.
- **Evaluation Metrics:** The authors introduced novel heuristic metrics to evaluate the chatbot's performance:
 - Information Coverage Score (ICS)
 - Key Point Coverage Score (KPCS)
 - Average Information Capture Score (AICS)
 - Additional Metrics: Token Efficiency, Turn-based Performance

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The main findings and results of the research are:

- **Memory Augmentation Improves Accuracy:** The experiments demonstrated that memory augmentation significantly improved the accuracy of the chatbot's responses. Specifically, memory injection led to a substantial increase in correct responses and a reduction in errors.
- **Structured Memory Enhances Domain Knowledge Retention:** The results validated the effectiveness of structured memory in enhancing domain knowledge retention.
- **Dynamic Memory Refinement is Important:** The findings underscored the importance of dynamic memory refinement in high-stakes domains and enterprise AI.
- **Memory Relevance Scoring (MRS) is effective in proritizing Knowledge:** The MRS system takes into account recall count, recency, similarity score, and feedback score to allow the system to prioritize relevant and up-to-date information
- **Reduction in Hallucinations:** Memory-based refinements help mitigate hallucinations, leading to more reliable responses.
- **Improved Efficiency:** The average token count per response was significantly lower with memory augmentation, indicating improved efficiency in responses.
- **Multi-Turn Coherence:** The multi-turn experiments showed that MARK can maintain coherence and adapt to evolving knowledge across sessions.
