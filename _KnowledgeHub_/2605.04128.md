# Awaking Spatial Intelligence in Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> JoyAI-Image, a unified multimodal foundation model from Joy Future Academy, integrates spatial intelligence into multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and instruction-guided image editing. It achieves performance improvements in spatial reasoning benchmarks, accurate long-text rendering, and fine-grained spatial image editing, enabling applications like novel view reasoning and 3D reconstruction.

## Problem

- Existing unified multimodal models exhibit weak interaction between visual understanding, generation, and editing capabilities, limiting cross-task information flow.
- Current models often lack robust spatial intelligence, struggling with fine-grained spatial understanding, precise geometric manipulation, and viewpoint consistency.
- The inability to deeply understand and manipulate physical world geometry restricts applications in areas like visual-language-action systems and controllable visual synthesis.

## Method

- **JoyAI-Image** combines a ==spatially enhanced Multimodal Large Language Model== (MLLM) with a Variational Autoencoder (**VAE**) and a ==Multimodal Diffusion Transformer== (**MMDiT**) to unify understanding, generation, and editing.
- A progressive training paradigm fine-tunes the MLLM for spatial understanding, trains the **MMDiT** for high-fidelity generation, and optimizes the full framework for precise instruction-based editing.
- The model utilizes a multi-faceted data suite, including a 3D box-centric ==OpenSpatial engine== for advanced spatial understanding data and 3D-driven frameworks for spatial editing.

## Results

- JoyAI-Image-Und improved spatial understanding scores by an average of 5.3 points over its base MLLM, matching proprietary models like Gemini-2.5-Pro, with notable gains in AllAngles (+11.5 points).
- In text-to-image generation, the model achieved 0.963 on LongText-Bench-EN/ZH and a Word Accuracy of 0.8739 on CVTG-2K, demonstrating precise long-text rendering and character-level fidelity.
- For image editing, JoyAI-Image showed substantial improvements in spatial editing, reducing Object Moving Score error from 0.373 to 0.652 and Viewpoint Error from 0.802 to 0.290 compared to baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Systematically integrating spatial intelligence across architectural design, data construction, and training processes significantly enhances overall visual performance and cross-task interaction in unified multimodal models.
> - A bidirectional collaborative paradigm, where stronger spatial understanding informs generation and editing, and generative transformations provide feedback for perception, leads to more robust and adaptable systems.
> - Leveraging 3D-grounded data generation, such as the OpenSpatial Engine and Blender-rendered multi-view corpora, enables models to acquire a deeper understanding of real-world geometry and viewpoint consistency.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of JoyAI-Image is situated within the evolving landscape of multimodal artificial intelligence, particularly concerning Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) and diffusion models. Recent advancements in these areas have enabled the creation of unified models capable of jointly performing image understanding, generation, and editing. This trend signifies a shift from designing task-specific computational pipelines towards developing general-purpose visual intelligence systems. The inherent advantage of such unification lies in the potential for enhanced coordination across various visual tasks, allowing improvements in one area, such as understanding, to mutually benefit others, like generation and editing, through integrated architectural designs, data curation, and training methodologies.

Despite progress in integrating these visual tasks into a single framework, current unified models exhibit limitations. A primary challenge is the weak interaction between their perception and generation capabilities; visual understanding is often not fully leveraged to guide grounded generation or editing, and generative transformations rarely provide substantive feedback for perception and reasoning processes. Furthermore, existing systems often lack robust spatial intelligence necessary for comprehending and manipulating real-world scenes. The physical world is characterized by complex elements such as object layouts, relative geometries, dynamic viewpoint changes, and the requirement for cross-view consistency. Current models tend to show limitations in fine-grained spatial understanding and geometrically precise manipulation. These identified weaknesses restrict the controllability of generation and editing tasks and impede the expansion of unified visual models towards broader spatial intelligence, which has implications for applications like visual-language-action systems and comprehensive world models. JoyAI-Image aims to address these identified limitations by systematically enhancing spatial intelligence within a unified multimodal framework.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to introduce JoyAI-Image, a unified multimodal foundation framework designed to enhance overall visual performance by systematically strengthening spatial intelligence. This overarching goal is motivated by several specific considerations:

- **Strengthening Cross-Task Interaction:** A key motivation is to move beyond loosely coupled perception and generation modules by building an understanding-driven visual framework. JoyAI-Image aims for stronger cross-task coupling, where visual understanding is fully exploited to guide grounded generation and editing, and generative transformations provide feedback to perception and reasoning. This bidirectional loop is intended to enable more coherent and context-aware multimodal processing.
- **Awakening Spatial Intelligence:** The research is motivated by the recognized deficit in spatial intelligence within existing unified models. JoyAI-Image seeks to inject spatial awareness into the entire training and reasoning pipeline, rather than treating it as an isolated or late-stage component. This involves developing spatially grounded data construction, task design, and supervision strategies to foster spatial awareness jointly with understanding, generation, and editing capabilities. The goal is to move beyond general visual competence towards a robust understanding of object layouts, relative geometry, viewpoint changes, and cross-view consistency in the physical world.
- **Enabling Controllable Visual Synthesis:** The limitations in spatial intelligence directly constrain the controllability of image generation and editing. By enhancing geometric awareness and precise manipulation, the model aims to achieve more controllable generation, particularly in areas like viewpoint-controllable synthesis and geometry-preserving transformations.
- **Supporting Advanced Downstream Applications:** The lack of strong spatial intelligence hinders the application of unified visual models in advanced domains such as visual-language-action systems and world models. By improving the model’s ability to reason about structure, transformation, and physical geometry, JoyAI-Image seeks to lay a practical foundation for these applications, which require grounded visual reasoning under dynamic conditions.
- **Developing a Versatile Capability Suite:** The objective includes delivering a comprehensive set of capabilities through a unified instruction-following framework. This involves harmonizing understanding, generation, and editing objectives through a multi-stage curriculum and leveraging a diverse data suite. The aim is to balance broad-domain robustness with pinpoint spatial control, encompassing spatial understanding, typography-enhanced generative synthesis, general and spatial editing, and view-assisted reasoning.

In summary, the motivation behind JoyAI-Image is to create a more integrated and spatially intelligent unified multimodal model that addresses the weaknesses of current systems, thereby expanding their capabilities for complex real-world interactions and advanced AI applications.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

JoyAI-Image is structured as a unified framework for image understanding, text-to-image generation, and instruction-based editing. The architecture integrates a spatially enhanced Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM), a Variational Autoencoder (VAE), and a Multimodal Diffusion Transformer (MMDiT).

#### 3.1. Architecture Overview

- **Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM):** This component functions as the "cognitive brain," responsible for general scene parsing and intricate spatial reasoning. It also acts as an intent-explanation mediator, interpreting interleaved instructions and reference signals to guide downstream generative tasks. The MLLM is built upon the Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct model and is further fortified for precise geometric awareness and multi-view structural consistency through dedicated data and specialized training. It operates in two modes: standalone understanding (decoding representations to text) and generative conditioning (parsing inputs to guide the diffusion process for text-to-image generation and instruction-based editing). High-dimensional hidden states from the MLLM’s final layer are extracted to serve as primary conditioning signals for the MMDiT.
- **Variational AutoEncoder (VAE):** The Wan-2.1-VAE is employed for latent encoding, bridging pixel-level data and the latent manifold. It performs spatio-temporal compression, designed to preserve fine-grained structures and high-frequency details, such as small text rendering, during reconstruction.
- **Multimodal Diffusion Transformer (MMDiT):** A 16B-parameter MMDiT serves as the core generative engine. Its dual-stream architecture facilitates deep cross-modal fusion, processing MLLM-derived priors and VAE-encoded latent representations. This design supports both denoising-based generation and multimodal-conditioned editing. The MMDiT's rotary positional embeddings are aligned by replacing MSRoPE with a standard MRoPE for backbone efficiency.

#### 3.2. Training Paradigm

The model is trained using a progressive, three-stage paradigm:
1. **MLLM Fine-tuning:** The MLLM is initially fine-tuned to establish robust visual-spatial understanding capabilities.
2. **MMDiT Training:** The MMDiT is trained from scratch for high-fidelity image generation, leveraging the MLLM-derived priors as conditioning signals.
3. **Instruction-based Editing Optimization:** The entire framework is then optimized for precise, instruction-based image editing.

#### 3.3. Advanced Spatial Understanding

- **Data Acquisition and Synthesis:** A core element is **OpenSpatial**, an automated data engine that synthesizes spatially-grounded QA pairs from a unified, 3D box-centric representation. OpenSpatial scales beyond labor-intensive 3D scans by employing a 3D lifting mechanism that converts unconstrained web videos into high-fidelity training data. This process generates OpenSpatial-3M, a dataset of 3 million entries covering Spatial Measurement, Spatial Relationship, Camera Perception, Multi-view Consistency, and Scene-Aware Reasoning across 19 sub-tasks. The engine ingests various sources, including 3D indoor scans and web-scale video sequences, normalizing all assets into a canonical coordinate system. It uses a 3D box-centric design as a geometric anchor, distilling scene-level 3D oriented bounding boxes (OBBs) into frame-level object attributes to create a unified object-frame index for both single-view and multi-view QA.
- **Dataset Composition:** The training corpus comprises approximately 11.3 million samples, with non-uniform, per-dataset sampling to mitigate scale imbalances.
 - **General Understanding (54.25%):** Covers general VQA, mathematics, document/chart understanding, language, multi-task instruction following, and OCR, primarily from single-view samples.
 - **Spatial Understanding (29.65%):** Composed of OpenSpatial (3.3M samples for fine-grained spatial skills, including multi-view supervision) and a VST Subset (49.4K samples for camera motion).
 - **Prompt Enhancement (11.98%):** Includes Instruction Rewriting (transforming concise descriptions into detailed instructions) and Spatial Editing (mapping spatial instructions to visual transitions).
 - **Others (2.89%):** Curated in-house multi-modal understanding data.
- **Training for Spatial Understanding:** Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is performed on Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct using a full-parameter training setup. A dynamic sequence packing strategy handles varying input lengths, and a decoupled learning rate schedule applies a smaller update rate to the vision encoder. An online knowledge distillation objective (KL divergence penalty) retains original pre-trained model capabilities, selectively applied to general-purpose datasets to avoid hindering spatial knowledge acquisition.

#### 3.4. Text-to-Image Generation

- **Data Pipeline:** A progressive, multi-stage system optimizes data quality and coverage.
 - **Data Filtering:** A multi-stage pipeline with progressively tightened criteria (208p to 1024p) removes unsuitable samples (e.g., broken files, low resolution, duplicates, NSFW content). It employs an in-house Image Quality Assessment (IQA) operator, combining statistical properties (Brightness, Entropy, Saturation, Sharpness Variance) with learned perceptual indicators (NIQE, CLIP-IQA, MUSIQ), and a caption-based content filter leveraging VLM-generated captions to detect undesirable content.
 - **Captioning:** A multi-level and OCR-aware captioning system uses Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct as a bilingual backbone. Four caption types are generated (Short, Long, Extended Long, Structured JSON) to capture varying granularities. OCR-aware captioning uses a two-stage framework where an OCR-specialized model extracts text tokens, which are then fused with visual features for caption generation, followed by fine-grained post-filtering to ensure fidelity. A text-rendering system converts LLM prompts into structured layout blueprints, rendered via HTML/CSS, and grounded by a VLM for spatially aware captions.
 - **Rebalancing:** A "Caption → Embedding → Tag → Retrieval → Rebalance" flow addresses long-tail distributions in web-crawled datasets. It uses a hierarchical classification tree, embedding-based tagging, adaptive diversity sampling, and a stratified sampling strategy to balance categories.
 - **Annotating:** A human-in-the-loop annotation pipeline provides multi-dimensional quality scores (Aesthetics, Information Density, Style Purity) for selecting SFT data, with quality control measures and iterative refinement.
 - **Multi-view Generation:** A 1M-scale Blender-rendered multi-view corpus supports controllable text-to-image generation under explicit viewpoint constraints. It focuses on object-centric and scene-centric settings, producing both dense and structured captions using Gemini-3-Flash.
- **JoyAI-Image Model Training for Generation:**
 - **Pre-Training:** Utilizes a flow matching objective to predict the velocity field between noise and clean data. It follows a progressive multi-resolution schedule (208P, 512P, 1024P), introducing multi-view data at the high-resolution stage to encourage 3D-aware understanding.
 - **Continue Training:** Refines the model with a high-quality subset of data to reduce distribution entropy, stabilize generation behaviors, suppress artifacts, and improve visual consistency.
 - **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** Enhances complex text rendering (bilingual, dense text, structured typography) and multi-view generation (consistent subjects across viewpoints) using a task-oriented high-quality dataset.
 - **Reinforcement Learning (RL):** Employs the Flow-GRPO framework, using a group-wise normalized advantage. Multiple reward models (aesthetic and text-image alignment) provide complementary supervision.

#### 3.5. Image Editing

- **Image Editing Data:** The corpus is a capability-oriented mixture comprising open-domain editing, spatial editing, and specialized general editing.
 - **General Editing Data Engine:** Supports diverse tasks (IP-preserving, text rendering, in-context generation, portrait retouching, local transformations) through open-source datasets, expert-model distilled data, in-house text editing data, and multi-image editing data.
 - **Open-domain Editing Data Engine:** Constructs editing pairs from video-derived sources, capturing realistic visual transformations (human motion, object displacement, pose variation, illumination shifts), with multimodal language models performing difference-based annotation.
 - **Spatial Editing Data Engine:** A 3D-driven framework generates paired editing samples with explicit spatial transformations. This includes Static-Camera Object Transformation (object-level manipulation under a fixed camera, with inpainting for realism) and Dynamic-Camera Viewpoint Transformation (viewpoint control by varying camera poses in a fixed 3D scene). A unified instruction interface converts structured spatial transformations into prompt templates.
 - **Data Curation and Quality Control:** A unified pipeline filters for visual fidelity, semantic meaningfulness, and instruction-image alignment, complemented by model-based quality assessment and manual inspection.
 - **Instruction Refinement and Unified Data Representation:** All data sources are converted into a standardized format (source image, references, natural-language instruction, target image, metadata) with instruction refinement to reduce ambiguity.
 - **Data Balancing:** A balanced training mixture avoids over-representation of dominant edit types across task categories, granularity, input conditions, and semantic domains.
- **JoyAI-Image-Edit Model Training:**
 - **Pre-Training:** Initializes the MMDiT-based architecture with large-scale image generation and reconstruction priors, then exposes it to coarse-grained editing supervision from image-text and video-related data.
 - **Continue Training:** Adapts the model to high-quality image editing using a curated mixture of general and specialized data, improving instruction following, content preservation, identity/structure consistency, and visual aesthetics.
 - **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** Refines performance on sensitive editing aspects such as instruction fidelity, local controllability, text accuracy, spatial precision, and reference consistency.
 - **Post-Training:** Optimizes for higher edit fidelity, visual quality, and alignment with human preferences using DiffusionNFT. Reward models (Gemini-3-Flash for instruction-following and consistency, HPSv3 for naturalness) guide the optimization, with an instruction-following-prioritized mixing strategy.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of JoyAI-Image demonstrated competitive or state-of-the-art performance across understanding, generation, and editing tasks, with a particular emphasis on spatial intelligence.

#### 4.1. Spatial Understanding

- **Benchmark Performance:** JoyAI-Image-Und achieved an average score of 64.4 on spatial understanding benchmarks, representing a 5.3-point improvement over its base model (Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, 59.1). This score matched the performance of the proprietary Gemini-2.5-Pro model.
- **General Capabilities:** The model maintained competitive results on general multimodal benchmarks such as MMBench, MMStar, OCRB, and MathVista, indicating its versatility without compromising foundational visual-language capabilities.

#### 4.2. Text-to-Image Generation

- **Long-Text Rendering:** On the LongText-Bench benchmark, JoyAI-Image achieved 0.963 for both English (LongText-Bench-EN) and Chinese (LongText-Bench-ZH), outperforming existing methods and demonstrating consistent high accuracy across languages.
- **Word Accuracy and Edit Distance:** On CVTG-2K, the model attained the highest Word Accuracy of 0.8739, surpassing prior methods like Z-Image (0.8671) and GPT Image 1 (0.8569). It also achieved a competitive normalized edit distance (NED) score of 0.9369, indicating high overall string-level fidelity.
- **Bilingual Generation and General T2I:** JoyAI-Image showed competitive bilingual generation on OneIG (0.542 for English, 0.521 for Chinese) and a strong overall score of 88.05 on the DPG benchmark, indicating robust general text-to-image generation capability.
- **Compositional and Reasoning Capabilities:** On the T2I-CoReBench, JoyAI-Image achieved an overall score of 68.7, with the best performance on the composition split (mean score of 94.2) and a competitive second rank on the reasoning split (mean score of 55.9).
- **Qualitative Results:** Qualitative comparisons demonstrated more accurate and complete text rendering, improved multilingual consistency, stronger visual coherence, better layout fidelity, and enhanced aesthetic quality in stylized scenarios compared to several existing T2I models.

#### 4.3. Image Editing

- **General Instruction-Based Editing:**
 - **GEdit:** JoyAI-Image-Edit achieved competitive results, improving the overall G_O score from 7.943 to 8.290 on GEdit-Bench-EN and from 7.887 to 8.208 on GEdit-Bench-CN, surpassing FireRed-Image-Edit. It also achieved the best G_SC among open-source models while maintaining strong G_PQ.
 - **ImgEdit:** The model (with prompt enhancement, w/ PE) achieved the best overall score of 4.57, performing well on challenging categories like "Extract."
 - **RL Benefits:** The RL-trained version consistently outperformed its SFT baseline on GEdit and ImgEdit, demonstrating improved instruction alignment and perceptual quality, as well as narrowing performance gaps across languages.
- **Spatial Editing:**
 - **SpatialEdit-Bench:** JoyAI-Image-Edit significantly outperformed leading image editing approaches and recent video world models. It improved the Moving Score from 0.373 to 0.652 and the Rotation Score from 0.505 to 0.646. Viewpoint Error was reduced from 0.802 to 0.290, and Framing Error from 0.684 to 0.568. This resulted in a large gain in Object Overall Score (from 0.439 to 0.649) and a significant reduction in Camera Overall Error (from 0.743 to 0.429).
- **Human Evaluation:** Human evaluation across dimensions of Semantic Following, Consistency, Naturalness, and Overall showed JoyAI-Image-Edit was preferred over Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 and Flux.2 [DEV], particularly in instruction faithfulness and edit consistency. It trailed slightly behind Nano-Banana-2, especially in Naturalness.
- **Qualitative Results:** Qualitative comparisons indicated stronger instruction following, better content preservation, and more faithful spatial control, especially for challenging viewpoint, camera manipulation, and object rotation tasks, compared to several strong baselines.

#### 4.4. Applications Demonstrating Spatial Intelligence

- **Thinking with Novel Views (TwNV):** Implementing the TwNV paradigm, where an MLLM Planner outputs camera motion instructions for a generative Synthesizer (JoyAI-Image-Edit) to produce novel views for an MLLM Reasoner, proved effective for spatial reasoning. JoyAI-Image-Edit, as the Synthesizer, improved GPT-5's overall accuracy from 68.8% to 71.7% and yielded a 5.7 percentage point gain in multi-object relationship tasks. This paradigm showed consistent improvements across various reasoner models, with a "Small-Model Dividend" indicating more pronounced relative gains for smaller models (e.g., 7.8% for Qwen3-VL-32B).
- **Reconstruction with Novel Views:** Novel views generated by JoyAI-Image-Edit from a single input image significantly improved 3D reconstruction quality (denser, more structurally complete point clouds and camera poses) when processed by a 3D reconstruction model (VGGT). This demonstrates the model's ability to preserve cross-view geometric structure, validating its 3D-aware scene manipulation capabilities.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The development of JoyAI-Image represents a step towards unified multimodal foundation models with enhanced spatial intelligence. Its significance and potential impact span several key areas:

- **Advancement in Unified Multimodal AI:** The work demonstrates that a tightly coupled framework for visual understanding, generation, and editing can achieve strong performance across diverse visual tasks. By allowing perception and generation to interact through a shared multimodal interface, JoyAI-Image moves beyond systems where these capabilities are loosely combined. This integration facilitates mutual benefits, allowing improvements in one modality to bolster the others.
- **Pioneering Spatial Intelligence in Foundation Models:** JoyAI-Image makes a contribution by systematically strengthening spatial intelligence within a unified model. Rather than treating spatial capability as an isolated component, the research integrates spatially grounded data construction, task design, and supervision throughout the entire pipeline. The achieved improvements in geometry-aware reasoning, controllable spatial editing, and novel-view-assisted reasoning suggest a practical foundation for models that can genuinely reason about the structure, transformation, and geometry of the physical world.
- **Practical Framework for General-Purpose Models:** The scalable training recipe, which includes unified instruction tuning, long-text rendering supervision, spatially grounded data, and both general and spatial editing signals, provides a practical methodology for developing unified multimodal models with broad general-purpose capabilities. This recipe could inform future efforts in building versatile and robust visual AI systems.
- **Enabling Next-Generation AI Applications:**
 - **Visual-Language-Action Systems and Robotics:** The enhanced spatial understanding and controllable editing capabilities are directly applicable to building more capable visual-language-action systems and robotic platforms. These systems require a nuanced understanding of physical space, object interactions, and the ability to plan and execute actions based on visual input. JoyAI-Image’s capacity for geometry-aware manipulation and novel-view synthesis could enable robots to better perceive their environment, simulate potential changes, and interact with objects more precisely.
 - **World Models:** The ability to generate geometrically consistent novel views and facilitate improved 3D reconstruction directly contributes to the development of more accurate and robust world models. Such models are crucial for simulating environments, predicting outcomes of actions, and enabling advanced AI agents to learn and reason within complex virtual or real-world settings.
 - **Content Creation:** The model’s strengths in long-text rendering, compositional fidelity, and precise instruction-guided editing offer advanced tools for digital content creators, potentially streamlining workflows for generating complex visual narratives, advertising, and artistic works with high fidelity and control.
- **Addressing Model Limitations and Generalizability:** The "Thinking with Novel Views" paradigm and the observation of a "Small-Model Dividend" suggest that explicit view synthesis can serve as a compensatory mechanism for smaller, parameter-constrained models. By offloading 3D spatial modeling to an external generative workspace, these models can leverage synthesized evidence to achieve spatial intelligence that exceeds their native capacities, potentially broadening the accessibility and applicability of spatial reasoning capabilities across diverse computational resources.

In conclusion, JoyAI-Image represents a significant contribution to the field by demonstrating a viable path towards building truly spatially intelligent visual foundation models, moving beyond broad competence to a deeper, geometry-aware understanding and manipulation of the visual world.
