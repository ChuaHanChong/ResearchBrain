# Awaking Spatial Intelligence in Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> JoyAI-Image, a unified multimodal foundation model from Joy Future Academy, integrates spatial intelligence into multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and instruction-guided image editing. It achieves performance improvements in spatial reasoning benchmarks, accurate long-text rendering, and fine-grained spatial image editing, enabling applications like novel view reasoning and 3D reconstruction.

## Problem

- Existing unified multimodal models exhibit weak interaction between visual understanding, generation, and editing capabilities, limiting cross-task information flow.
- Current models often lack robust spatial intelligence, struggling with fine-grained spatial understanding, precise geometric manipulation, and viewpoint consistency.
- The inability to deeply understand and manipulate physical world geometry restricts applications in areas like visual-language-action systems and controllable visual synthesis.

## Method

- **JoyAI-Image** combines a ==spatially enhanced Multimodal Large Language Model== (MLLM) with a Variational Autoencoder (**VAE**) and a ==Multimodal Diffusion Transformer== (**MMDiT**) to unify understanding, generation, and editing.
- A progressive training paradigm fine-tunes the MLLM for spatial understanding, trains the **MMDiT** for high-fidelity generation, and optimizes the full framework for precise instruction-based editing.
- The model utilizes a multi-faceted data suite, including a 3D box-centric ==OpenSpatial engine== for advanced spatial understanding data and 3D-driven frameworks for spatial editing.

## Results

- JoyAI-Image-Und improved spatial understanding scores by an average of 5.3 points over its base MLLM, matching proprietary models like Gemini-2.5-Pro, with notable gains in AllAngles (+11.5 points).
- In text-to-image generation, the model achieved 0.963 on LongText-Bench-EN/ZH and a Word Accuracy of 0.8739 on CVTG-2K, demonstrating precise long-text rendering and character-level fidelity.
- For image editing, JoyAI-Image showed substantial improvements in spatial editing, reducing Object Moving Score error from 0.373 to 0.652 and Viewpoint Error from 0.802 to 0.290 compared to baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Systematically integrating spatial intelligence across architectural design, data construction, and training processes significantly enhances overall visual performance and cross-task interaction in unified multimodal models.
> - A bidirectional collaborative paradigm, where stronger spatial understanding informs generation and editing, and generative transformations provide feedback for perception, leads to more robust and adaptable systems.
> - Leveraging 3D-grounded data generation, such as the OpenSpatial Engine and Blender-rendered multi-view corpora, enables models to acquire a deeper understanding of real-world geometry and viewpoint consistency.
