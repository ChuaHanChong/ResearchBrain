# Any2Caption:Interpreting Any Condition to Caption for Controllable Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Any2Caption introduces an "any-condition-to-caption" paradigm, leveraging Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to interpret diverse inputs like text, images, depth maps, poses, and camera movements into detailed, structured captions. This approach consistently enhances the controllability and visual quality of videos generated by existing text-to-video models across various conditions.

## Problem

- Text-only prompts are often insufficient to capture the complex, fine-grained user intent required for controllable video generation.
- Current video generation models struggle to accurately interpret and synthesize multiple heterogeneous multimodal inputs (e.g., images, poses, camera motion) simultaneously, limiting fine-grained control.
- The direct integration of diverse multimodal conditions into diffusion model backbones can be inefficient and less interpretable for complex reasoning.

## Method

- **Any2Caption** proposes an MLLM-based framework that decouples the complex task of condition interpretation from video synthesis, acting as a universal translator.
- It processes any combination of input conditions (text, image, video, depth, pose, camera) using specialized encoders and an **MLLM** backbone (**Qwen2-LLM**) to generate a unified, rich, structured textual caption.
- A large-scale instruction-tuning dataset, **Any2CapIns**, and a two-stage progressive mixed training strategy are used to teach the **MLLM** to interpret conditions into specific six-component captions.

## Results

- Any2Caption achieves a Structural Integrity of 91.25% for generated captions and high lexical/semantic matching (e.g., ROUGE-L 48.63, BERTSCORE 91.95), demonstrating accurate condition interpretation.
- Structured captions generated by Any2Caption consistently improve overall video quality and enhance adherence to diverse non-text conditions across various state-of-the-art controllable video generation methods.
- The model shows strong performance in handling multiple combined conditions and generalizes effectively to 'unseen' conditions (e.g., style, segmentation), improving motion smoothness and aesthetic quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling the interpretation of diverse input conditions from the video generation process, by translating conditions into rich structured captions via MLLMs, effectively enhances controllability.
> - A structured caption format, comprising six granular components (dense, main object, background, camera, style, action), is crucial for providing disentangled and effective guidance to video generators.
> - Leveraging the robust vision-language comprehension of MLLMs allows for a 'plug-in' module that can seamlessly integrate with any off-the-shelf text-to-video generator without architectural modifications or retraining.
