# CoEnv: Driving Embodied Multi-Agent Collaboration via Compositional Environment

> [!summary] Summary
> The CoEnv framework enables multiple heterogeneous robots to collaboratively execute complex manipulation tasks by integrating real-world observations with a physics simulator into a compositional decision-making environment. It leverages VLM-driven action synthesis and collision-aware sim-to-real transfer to achieve an overall 49% success rate across five challenging real-world multi-agent benchmarks.

## Problem

- Complex, long-horizon manipulation tasks often exceed the capabilities of single-agent robotic systems.
- Existing multi-agent systems struggle with fine-grained spatial coordination, collision avoidance, and temporal reasoning, often relying on isolated viewpoints or high-level textual planning.
- Traditional robot learning through real-world experimentation is prone to damage and resource-intensive, while current simulation methods often lack robust multi-agent dynamics and effective sim-to-real transfer.

## Method

- **CoEnv** introduces a ==compositional environment== that unifies real-world scene reconstruction with a physics simulator, creating a shared decision-making space for multi-agent collaboration.
- Employs a ==VLM-based hierarchical planner== to decompose tasks and synthesize actions, utilizing both ==interactive== (closed-loop, adaptive) and ==iterative== (code-based, full-trajectory) execution modes.
- A ==collision-aware sim-to-real transfer== mechanism verifies swept collision volumes for interpolated robot trajectories before real-world execution, enhancing safety and reliability.

## Results

- **CoEnv** achieved an overall task success rate of **49%** across five real-world multi-agent manipulation tasks involving up to three heterogeneous robots, with the "Cube Stacking" task reaching **75%** success.
- Ablation studies demonstrated that adaptive camera control and checkpoint verification are critical, with their removal reducing the average success rate from **50%** to **30%** and **20%** respectively.
- The framework provides a scalable pipeline for generating high-quality multi-agent manipulation data, with the iterative mode exhibiting significantly higher throughput (e.g., **17.5** episodes/session for "Cube Stacking") compared to the interactive mode.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating a high-fidelity simulator with real-world perception offers a safe and effective unified decision-making space for complex multi-agent coordination.
> - VLM-driven planning, combined with interactive and iterative execution modes, provides complementary strengths for generating precise and adaptable multi-agent actions.
> - Adaptive camera control for enhanced observability and checkpoint verification for early error detection are crucial for robust performance in long-horizon multi-agent tasks, especially under occlusion.
