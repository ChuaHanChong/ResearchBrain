# MWM: Mobile World Models for Action-Conditioned Consistent Prediction

> [!summary] Summary
> MWM (Mobile World Models) improves visual planning for robot navigation by training a world model to generate action-conditioned future observations with enhanced consistency and faster inference. The system achieves a 4x speedup in rollout time while improving multi-step rollout quality and leads to better goal-image navigation performance in real-world scenarios.

## Problem

- Existing world models for embodied navigation often produce visually plausible but 'action-conditioned inconsistent' rollouts, where imagined trajectories deviate from true physical paths, undermining Model Predictive Control (MPC).
- Diffusion models, while good for visual generation, typically have high inference latency (many denoising steps) which is impractical for real-time MPC on robots, and current acceleration methods do not explicitly preserve action-conditioned consistency.

## Method

- A two-stage ==Structure-First, Consistency-Refine== training pipeline is introduced, pretraining a ==diffusion model== for scene structure and then post-training to mitigate error accumulation and improve action-conditioned consistency.
- ==Inference-Consistent State Distillation (ICSD)== is designed to enable efficient, few-step diffusion inference while explicitly preserving consistency, by bridging the gap between truncated training states and inference-time denoising endpoints.
- **MWM** integrates with ==Model Predictive Control (MPC)== using the ==Cross-Entropy Method (CEM)== to search for optimal action sequences, evaluated by a terminal-frame perceptual objective against a goal image.

## Results

- **MWM** (with 5 DDIM steps) demonstrates superior action-conditioned consistency on the SCAND dataset, achieving lower **LPIPS (0.495)** and **DreamSim (0.337)** at 16s horizon compared to NWM (25 DDIM steps, LPIPS **0.569**, DreamSim **0.373**).
- It achieves a **4x speedup** in average rollout time (**2.3s** vs. **9.6s** for NWM) on an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 GPU, while maintaining or improving visual fidelity (lower FID scores).
- On real-world goal-image navigation tasks with the AIRBOT MMK2 platform, MWM achieves a higher Success Rate of **0.30** and lower Navigation Error of **2.16**, outperforming baselines like NoMaD (SR **0.08**, NE **2.88**) and NWM (SR **0.20**, NE **3.18**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing world model training into distinct structural learning and consistency refinement stages effectively balances visual fidelity with multi-step autoregressive accuracy.
> - Explicitly training for action-conditioned consistency using self-forcing and perceptual losses significantly reduces error accumulation in multi-step rollouts.
> - Introducing an 'inference-consistent state' during distillation helps preserve sequence consistency when accelerating diffusion models, addressing the mismatch between training and efficient inference.
