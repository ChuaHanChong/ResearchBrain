# RobustVLA: Robustness-Aware Reinforcement Post-Training for Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> RobustVLA introduces an online reinforcement learning post-training method designed to enhance the resilience of pre-trained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models against environmental perturbations. This approach achieved an 82.1% success rate under joint observation and action perturbations on the LIBERO benchmark, surpassing existing state-of-the-art baselines.

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models exhibit limited generalization in Out-of-Distribution (OOD) scenarios due to environmental disturbances like observation noise and action perturbations.
- Existing online reinforcement learning (RL) post-training methods primarily focus on task performance, lacking explicit mechanisms to guarantee robustness against these real-world uncertainties.
- Unaddressed environmental perturbations can amplify over time, significantly degrading VLA model performance and reliability in real-world robotic deployment.

## Method

- **RobustVLA** integrates two theoretically derived regularization terms, ==Jacobian regularization== and ==smoothness regularization==, into the ==Proximal Policy Optimization== (**PPO**) objective for online fine-tuning.
- Jacobian regularization penalizes the model's sensitivity to observation noise, while smoothness regularization controls the magnitude of policy updates to mitigate action perturbation effects.
- An adaptive noise scheduling mechanism based on ==curriculum learning== dynamically adjusts the levels of injected observation and action noise during rollouts to progressively build robustness.

## Results

- RobustVLA achieved an **82.5%** average success rate under various observation perturbations and a **54.8%** success rate under action perturbations on the LIBERO benchmark, outperforming all baselines.
- Under challenging joint perturbations (image rotation and action noise), RobustVLA-C obtained the highest success rate of **82.1%**, demonstrating superior robust and stable decision-making in complex OOD scenarios.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both Jacobian and smoothness regularization terms are essential and complementary, contributing to more robust latent representations and stable action distributions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Performance deviation under observation perturbations is primarily governed by the VLA model's Jacobian sensitivity, indicating that controlling this sensitivity is key for visual robustness.
> - Return degradation under action perturbations is driven by cumulative policy drift from online updates, highlighting the importance of smooth and stable policy evolution.
> - A combined strategy of Jacobian regularization, smoothness regularization, and curriculum-based noise exposure is crucial for simultaneously optimizing task performance and robustness in dynamic environments.
