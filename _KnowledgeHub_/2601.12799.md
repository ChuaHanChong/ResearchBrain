# FRoM-W1: Towards General Humanoid Whole-Body Control with Language Instructions

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Fudan University and Shanghai Innovation Institute developed FRoM-W1, an open-source framework enabling humanoid robots to execute diverse natural language instructions with whole-body and hand movements. The system achieved a Fréchet Inception Distance of 0.229 for motion generation and demonstrated up to 15% improvement in motion tracking accuracy on physical robots.

## Problem

- Humanoid robots are limited by hard-coded or task-specific control policies, hindering versatility and natural interaction.
- A semantic gap exists between abstract natural language instructions and the concrete, low-level actions required for robot control.
- Reliably generating and executing stable, expressive whole-body motions, including intricate hand movements, on physical humanoid platforms is difficult.

## Method

- A two-stage framework, **FRoM-W1**, comprising H-GPT for language-driven motion generation and H-ACT for robot execution.
- H-GPT utilizes a LLaMA-3.1-based model, enriched with Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning and a VQ-VAE motion tokenizer, to generate hand-inclusive human motions from text.
- H-ACT employs an RL-based motion imitator with a novel ==Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning (RFT)== step and a modular deployment framework (RoboJudo) for stable and accurate physical execution on various humanoid robots.

## Results

- H-GPT demonstrated superior human motion generation with an FID of **0.229** on HumanML3D-X, and its Chain-of-Thought mechanism improved performance for rephrased and noisy instructions.
- The Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning within H-ACT consistently enhanced motion tracking accuracy by up to **15%** and increased success rates for Unitree H1 and G1 robots in simulation.
- The full FRoM-W1 framework successfully enabled physical Unitree H1 and G1 robots to execute complex, abstract language instructions with integrated whole-body and hand movements, showcasing its real-world applicability and modular deployment.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Chain-of-Thought reasoning significantly improves the generalization and semantic consistency of language-driven motion generation, especially for complex or rephrased instructions.
> - A novel Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning (RFT) strategy during inference effectively adapts pre-trained robot control policies, leading to improved tracking accuracy and stability for specific generated motions.
> - Open-sourcing a modular, two-stage framework for language-to-motion generation and robot execution fosters reproducibility and accelerates research in embodied AI and humanoid control.
