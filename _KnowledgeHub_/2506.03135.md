# OmniSpatial: Towards Comprehensive Spatial Reasoning Benchmark for Vision Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> OmniSpatial, a new benchmark based on cognitive psychology, evaluates Vision-Language Models on complex visual-spatial reasoning tasks including dynamic reasoning, spatial logic, interaction, and perspective-taking. It revealed that state-of-the-art models achieve over 56% accuracy, still more than 30 percentage points below human performance, with particular struggles in geometric reasoning and non-egocentric perspectives, while demonstrating improvements with explicit geometric cues and multi-view imagination.

## Problem

- Existing benchmarks for Vision-Language Models (VLMs) primarily assess basic spatial relations, with advanced models reaching over 90% accuracy, indicating saturation and insufficient challenge.
- Current VLM evaluations fail to capture the breadth and depth of human-like spatial cognition, which involves complex, dynamic, context-dependent, and 3D structural reasoning, as well as perspective-taking.
- The absence of a comprehensive and cognitively-informed benchmark hinders the identification of specific VLM limitations in advanced spatial understanding, thus impeding the development of capable embodied AI.

## Method

- Introduced **OmniSpatial**, a comprehensive visual-spatial reasoning benchmark organized by a taxonomy of four dimensions (dynamic reasoning, complex spatial logic, spatial interaction, perspective-taking) with 50 fine-grained subcategories.
- Constructed the **OmniSpatial** dataset with 8.4K manually curated question-answer pairs, sourcing images and video frames from diverse real-world contexts, public tests, and existing datasets to ensure variety and minimize bias.
- Developed two strategies to enhance VLM spatial reasoning: **PointGraph**, which integrates explicit ==scene graph== cues, and **SpatialCoT**, which augments visual inputs with ==3D novel views== to stimulate spatial imagination.

## Results

- Proprietary VLMs like ChatGPT o3 and Gemini-2.5-pro achieved the highest scores at over **56%** on OmniSpatial, but a significant gap of over **30 percentage points** remains compared to human accuracy (**92.6%**).
- Models performed stronger in Dynamic Reasoning and Spatial Interaction categories, but showed considerable weaknesses in Complex Spatial Logic (**30-40%** accuracy) and Perspective-Taking, particularly with allocentric or hypothetical viewpoints.
- PointGraph and SpatialCoT strategies consistently boosted VLM performance, especially in Dynamic Reasoning and Perspective-Taking; supervised fine-tuning on the OmniSpatial-train dataset yielded a **+7.82** average point gain for a baseline model, outperforming template-style data training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - State-of-the-art VLMs, including proprietary models like ChatGPT o3 and Gemini-2.5-pro, achieve an average success rate of just over 56% on OmniSpatial, remaining significantly below human performance (92.6% accuracy).
> - VLMs demonstrate substantial limitations in complex spatial logic, such as pattern recognition and geometric reasoning (scoring 30-40%), and struggle with non-egocentric perspective-taking tasks.
> - Providing explicit structured object representations (PointGraph) and stimulating spatial imagination through multi-view inputs (SpatialCoT) consistently improves VLM accuracy in various spatial reasoning tasks, indicating the value of direct geometric cues and richer spatial context.
