# Turning Sand to Gold: Recycling Data to Bridge On-Policy and Off-Policy Learning via Causal Bound

> [!summary] Summary
> By applying a novel causal upper-bound loss optimization (SUFT) within Deep Reinforcement Learning, researchers bridge the gap between on-policy and off-policy learning through the efficient reuse of discarded value network outputs. This leads to substantial gains in sample efficiency and agent performance across diverse DRL benchmarks, with negligible computational cost.

## Problem

- Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) agents face high computational and resource demands due to extensive training steps and large experience replay buffers.
- On-policy DRL algorithms are sample-inefficient, discarding collected data after each update for stable convergence.
- Off-policy DRL algorithms improve sample efficiency by reusing data but often introduce learning instability from policy divergence, requiring large buffers and extensive training to stabilize.

## Method

- A novel theoretical ==causal upper bound== on the factual (on-policy) loss is derived from the Neyman-Rubin potential outcomes framework, using observed counterfactual loss and an estimated treatment effect.
- This causal bound is integrated into DRL algorithms by mapping factual/counterfactual losses to on-policy/off-policy losses and the estimated treatment effect to a SUFT Off-Policy Evaluation (OPE) term.
- Previously discarded value network outputs (Q-values or V-values) are stored in the experience replay buffer and reused to compute the SUFT OPE term, enhancing loss optimization with minimal computational and storage overhead.

## Results

- The SUFT method introduces negligible computational (approx. **0.8%** of training time) and memory overhead (approx. **0.003%** additional space per experience).
- Agents augmented with SUFT achieved substantial performance gains, with SUFT DQN showing a **383.19%** mean reward ratio improvement on Atari 2600 games and SUFT SAC reaching **224.53%** on MuJoCo, consistently outperforming baselines.
- SUFT enables high performance with significantly smaller replay buffers, with SUFT DQN using a **4K** buffer outperforming a **100K** buffer baseline DQN by **437.05%**, demonstrating a drastic reduction in memory requirements.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The factual (on-policy) loss in DRL can be effectively upper-bounded by the observed counterfactual (off-policy) loss combined with an estimated treatment effect, offering a principled theoretical bridge between on-policy and off-policy learning.
> - Storing and reusing previously discarded value network outputs (Q or V values) from the experience replay buffer provides a computationally negligible method to quantify and leverage the estimated causal effect between behavior and target policies.
> - The proposed causal bound optimization (SUFT) significantly improves DRL performance by explicitly accounting for policy divergence, outperforming simpler solutions like larger replay buffers or general inertia regularization.
