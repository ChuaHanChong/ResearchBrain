# NoisyRollout: Reinforcing Visual Reasoning with Data Augmentation

> [!summary] Summary
> NoisyRollout introduces a data augmentation method for training Vision-Language Models (VLMs) using Reinforcement Learning, applying controlled visual noise during RL rollouts to enhance reasoning capabilities. This approach improves robustness and generalization, achieving superior performance on five out-of-domain benchmarks and demonstrating exceptional sample efficiency compared to existing methods.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) leveraging Reinforcement Learning (RL) for reasoning struggle with effective policy exploration, as methods like increasing decoding temperature often lead to superficial diversity.
- VLMs possess inherent limitations in visual perception, such as hallucinations, which can propagate and hinder subsequent reasoning steps during RL training.
- Existing RL adaptations, often directly borrowed from Language Models, do not specifically address the unique visual-centric challenges faced by multimodal reasoning agents.

## Method

- A ==hybrid rollout strategy== is implemented where the old policy generates responses from both clean and moderately distorted images for each training sample.
- All rollouts (from both clean and noisy inputs) are combined into a single group to compute a unified baseline reward and normalized advantages, using ==reward discrepancies as implicit contrastive signals==.
- A ==noise annealing schedule== gradually reduces the distortion strength of visual noise over training steps, ensuring stability while maintaining diverse exploration signals.

## Results

- NoisyRollout achieved state-of-the-art out-of-domain generalization, outperforming vanilla GRPO and other leading RL-tuned models across five benchmarks, including MathVerse (**53.2%**), MathVision (**28.5%**), and HallusionBench (**72.1%**).
- The method demonstrated exceptional sample efficiency, achieving strong performance with only **2.1K** training samples from Geometry3K, requiring significantly less data than comparable state-of-the-art models.
- It exhibited robust and scalable performance, consistently improving over vanilla GRPO for both **7B** and **32B** parameter models, and maintaining gains across different training datasets and varying data scales.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Injecting controlled visual noise during RL rollouts creates targeted perceptual diversity, leading to more robust reasoning paths and improved generalization.
> - Reward differences between clean and noisy rollouts provide implicit contrastive signals, which help refine the model's visual perception by reducing negative exploration space.
> - An annealing schedule for noise strength is critical for stable training, enabling a smooth transition from broad exploration early on to more 'on-policy' learning as the VLM's capabilities improve.
