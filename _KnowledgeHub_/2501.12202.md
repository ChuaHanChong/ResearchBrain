# Hunyuan3D 2.0: Scaling Diffusion Models for High Resolution Textured 3D Assets Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Hunyuan3D 2.0 introduces an open-source, two-stage diffusion model pipeline for generating high-resolution, textured 3D assets from conditional inputs. Its components, Hunyuan3D-DiT for 3D shape generation and Hunyuan3D-Paint for texture synthesis, achieve competitive performance compared to state-of-the-art models in shape reconstruction, texture quality, and overall asset generation.

## Problem

- The traditional pipeline for creating digital 3D assets is complex, time-consuming, and resource-intensive, often requiring specialized expertise.
- The field of 3D generation has experienced slower progress compared to 2D image and video generation, lacking robust, large-scale, open-source foundational models.
- Existing 3D generation methods struggle with generating high-fidelity shapes and textures that are consistent and detailed.

## Method

- **Hunyuan3D 2.0** proposes a ==two-stage pipeline== for 3D asset generation, decoupling ==shape generation== (Hunyuan3D-DiT) from ==texture map synthesis== (Hunyuan3D-Paint).
- ==Hunyuan3D-DiT== utilizes a ==latent diffusion model== with an ==Importance Sampled Point-Query Encoder== and a ==dual/single-stream transformer== architecture to generate high-fidelity 3D shapes conditioned on an input image.
- ==Hunyuan3D-Paint== generates high-resolution, seamless, and multi-view consistent texture maps, incorporating ==image delighting==, a ==double-stream Reference-Net==, and a ==multi-task attention== mechanism.

## Results

- Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE achieved superior shape reconstruction with a V-IoU of **93.6%** and S-IoU of **89.16%**, surpassing existing baselines.
- Hunyuan3D-DiT generated shapes with higher adherence to conditional inputs, reflected by a Uni3D-I score of **0.3151**, outperforming several state-of-the-art models.
- Hunyuan3D-Paint produced the best generative texture quality and semantic alignment, achieving the lowest CMMD (**2.318**) and FID_CLIP (**26.44**) and the highest CLIP-score (**0.8893**).
- The end-to-end Hunyuan3D 2.0 system demonstrated improved overall textured 3D asset quality and condition following, registering the lowest CMMD (**3.193**) and FID_CLIP (**49.165**), alongside higher user satisfaction in evaluations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling 3D shape and texture generation simplifies the overall problem, allowing for specialized models and greater flexibility in asset creation workflows.
> - Incorporating an importance sampling strategy in the shape VAE's encoder effectively captures fine-grained geometric details, leading to more accurate 3D shape reconstruction.
> - Combining image delighting, a frozen pre-trained reference network, and a multi-task attention mechanism ensures high-resolution, geometrically consistent, and semantically aligned texture generation across multiple views.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The creation of digital 3D assets is fundamental to numerous modern applications, including gaming, film production, physical simulation, and embodied AI. However, the conventional pipeline for 3D asset creation is recognized as complex, time-consuming, and resource-intensive, often requiring specialized expertise in various digital content creation software. This challenge has driven interest in the automated generation of high-resolution digital 3D assets.

Despite the rapid advancements in image and video generation fueled by diffusion models, the field of 3D generation has experienced slower progress in the era of large-scale models and extensive datasets. Existing works, such as 3DShape2Vectset, Michelangelo, and CLAY, have contributed to shape generation, with CLAY demonstrating the potential of diffusion models in this domain. However, a significant gap remains in the availability of robust, large-scale, open-source foundational models for 3D generation, similar to the impact of Stable Diffusion in image generation or LLaMA in language models. The absence of such foundational models is seen as a limiting factor for the broader prosperity of the 3D domain.

Hunyuan3D 2.0 positions itself within this landscape by addressing the need for a comprehensive and scalable 3D synthesis system. It aims to overcome the stagnation in 3D generation by introducing two open-source foundation models for shape and texture synthesis, thereby providing a robust baseline to accelerate future research and development in the open-source 3D community. The work also emphasizes decoupling shape and texture generation, a strategy found effective in managing the complexity of 3D asset creation.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of Hunyuan3D 2.0 is to establish an advanced, large-scale 3D synthesis system capable of generating high-resolution textured 3D assets from conditional inputs. This overarching goal is driven by several motivations and broken down into specific objectives:

- **Addressing the Challenges of Manual 3D Asset Creation:** The current process for creating 3D assets is characterized by its complexity, time investment, and high cost, requiring specialized skills at various stages like sketch design, digital modeling, and texture mapping. The project seeks to automate and simplify this process significantly.
- **Advancing 3D Generative Modeling:** Recognizing the relatively slow pace of 3D generation compared to 2D image and video generation, the team aims to push the boundaries of 3D synthesis by leveraging and scaling diffusion models. This involves developing models capable of generating intricate geometry and vibrant textures.
- **Establishing Open-Source Foundational Models:** A key motivation is to fill the identified void in the open-source 3D community for large-scale foundational generative models. By publicly releasing Hunyuan3D 2.0, including its code and pre-trained weights, the project intends to provide a strong baseline and foster collaborative development and research in 3D generation.
- **High-Fidelity Shape Generation:** A specific objective is to develop a shape generative model, Hunyuan3D-DiT, that can create geometry accurately aligned with a given condition image. This model is designed to lay a solid foundation for subsequent applications requiring precise 3D forms.
- **High-Resolution Texture Synthesis:** Another core objective is to create a texture synthesis model, Hunyuan3D-Paint, capable of producing high-resolution, vibrant, and geometrically consistent texture maps. This model is designed to be versatile, supporting texturing for both newly generated meshes and pre-existing or hand-crafted meshes.
- **Enhancing User Accessibility and Workflow:** Beyond the core generative models, a practical objective is to build Hunyuan3D-Studio, a user-friendly production platform. This platform is intended to democratize 3D asset creation, manipulation, and animation, making it accessible to both professional creators and amateur users without requiring extensive technical expertise.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Hunyuan3D 2.0 employs a two-stage generation pipeline: first, creating a bare mesh, and then synthesizing a texture map for it. This modular approach is designed to decouple the complexities of shape and texture generation while offering flexibility for various applications.

#### 3.1. Generative 3D Shape Generation (Hunyuan3D-DiT)

The shape generation component of Hunyuan3D 2.0 adopts a latent diffusion model architecture. It comprises two main sub-components:

- **Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE:** This variational autoencoder (VAE) is responsible for compressing 3D shapes (represented as polygon meshes) into a sequence of continuous tokens in a latent space and decoding them back into mesh form.
 - **Representation:** It leverages "vector sets" for 3D shape representation, similar to 3DShape2VecSet and Michelangelo.
 - **Encoder:** An "Importance Sampled Point-Query Encoder" extracts features from point clouds sampled from the 3D surface. In addition to uniform sampling, an importance sampling method is used to concentrate points on mesh edges and corners, aiming to capture fine-grained details more effectively. These point clouds are then processed through Fourier positional encoding, linear projections, cross-attention, and self-attention layers to produce a hidden shape representation, which is then projected to predict the mean and variance of the latent shape embedding.
 - **Decoder:** The decoder reconstructs a 3D neural field, specifically a Signed Distance Function (SDF), from the latent shape embedding. This SDF can then be converted into a triangle mesh using the marching cubes algorithm.
 - **Training Strategy:** The model is trained using a combination of a reconstruction loss (Mean Squared Error between predicted and ground-truth SDF on sampled points) and a KL-divergence loss to ensure a compact and continuous latent space. A multi-resolution strategy is also employed, varying the length of the latent token sequence during training to manage computational cost and reconstruction quality.

- **Hunyuan3D-DiT:** This is a flow-based diffusion model designed to generate high-fidelity 3D shapes conditioned on an input image.
 - **Network Structure:** Inspired by FLUX, it utilizes a dual- and single-stream transformer architecture. Dual-stream blocks process latent tokens and condition tokens separately but allow interaction via attention. Single-stream blocks concatenate tokens and process them with spatial and channel attention. Timestep embeddings are used for modulation, but positional embeddings for the latent sequence are omitted, as the latent token content is responsible for defining 3D position/occupancy.
 - **Condition Injection:** A pre-trained DINOv2 Gaint image encoder extracts conditional image tokens from 518x518 input images. Image preprocessing involves background removal, object resizing and repositioning, and filling the background with white to enhance the effective resolution and focus on the object.
 - **Training & Inference:** The model is trained using a flow matching objective with an affine path and conditional optimal transport schedule. During inference, a random start point from a Gaussian distribution is iteratively refined using a first-order Euler ordinary differential equation (ODE) solver.

#### 3.2. Generative Texture Map Synthesis (Hunyuan3D-Paint)

Hunyuan3D-Paint is designed to generate high-resolution, seamless, and multi-view consistent texture maps for a given 3D mesh and image prompt. Its methodology is divided into three stages:

- **Pre-processing:**
 - **Image Delighting Module:** To prevent shadows and illumination from being baked into the texture maps, an image-to-image module converts the input reference image to an unlit state. This module is trained on paired images rendered under random HDRI and even white light.
 - **View Selection Strategy:** A geometry-aware greedy search algorithm selects 8 to 12 viewpoints for texture generation. This process starts with 4 fixed orthogonal viewpoints and then iteratively adds novel viewpoints that maximize coverage of unseen geometric surface areas in the UV space, aiming to minimize post-processing inpainting requirements.

- **Hunyuan3D-Paint Model:** This component focuses on geometry-conditioned multi-view image generation.
 - **Double-stream Image Conditioning Reference-Net:** This network conditions the generation process on the reference image. It directly feeds the noiseless VAE features of the reference image into a reference branch (with timestep 0) to preserve image details. The weights of this reference-net are frozen from a Stable Diffusion 2.1 base model to act as a soft regularization, preventing style drift towards the training dataset's rendered image distribution and improving performance on real-world images.
 - **Multi-task Attention Mechanism:** To ensure both image alignment and multi-view consistency, two additional parallel attention modules are introduced alongside the original self-attention: a reference attention module (integrating reference image features) and a multi-view attention module (ensuring consistency across generated views).
 - **Geometry and View Conditioning:** View-invariant geometric conditions (canonical normal maps and coordinate maps) are encoded using a VAE, then concatenated with latent noise and fed into the diffusion model's input convolution layer. Additionally, learnable camera embeddings, mapping unique integers per viewpoint to feature vectors, are injected into the multi-view Diffusion model to provide explicit viewpoint information.

- **Texture Baking:**
 - **Dense-view Inference:** The model is trained with a view dropout strategy, randomly selecting a subset of viewpoints from a larger pre-set pool. This enhances 3D perception and generalization, enabling dense-view inference during the generation stage to alleviate later inpainting needs.
 - **Single Image Super-resolution:** A pre-trained super-resolution model is applied to each generated multi-view image to enhance texture quality, maintaining inter-view consistency.
 - **Texture Inpainting:** For any remaining uncovered texels in the UV texture map, an inpainting approach is used. Existing UV textures are projected to vertex textures, and uncovered texel colors are queried as a weighted sum of colors from connected, textured vertices, with weights inversely proportional to geometric distances.

#### 3.3. Implementation Details

Hunyuan3D-Paint is initialized from a Stable Diffusion 2 v-model checkpoint and trained on a large, self-collected 3D dataset with multi-view images rendered under even white light. The training incorporates variations in reference image azimuth and elevation to improve robustness. The system also supports general text- and image-to-texture capabilities by leveraging external T2I models (like ControlNet and IP-Adapter) to generate geometry-aligned input images from user prompts, facilitating texture "re-skinning."

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Hunyuan3D 2.0 was systematically evaluated across three dimensions: 3D shape generation, texture map synthesis, and end-to-end textured 3D asset generation. Comparisons were made against various state-of-the-art open-source and closed-source models.

#### 4.1. 3D Shape Generation

- **Shape Reconstruction (Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE):**
 - **Metrics:** Volume Intersection over Union (V-IoU) and Surface Intersection over Union (S-IoU).
 - **Results:** Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE demonstrated superior reconstruction performance, achieving a V-IoU of 93.6% and an S-IoU of 89.16%, outperforming baselines such as 3DShape2VecSet, Michelangelo, and Direct3D. The effectiveness of the importance sampling strategy in capturing fine-grained details was highlighted.
 - **Visual Quality:** Qualitative comparisons showed that Hunyuan3D-ShapeVAE could faithfully recover complex shapes with intricate surface details and produced clean 3D spaces without floating artifacts.
- **Shape Generation (Hunyuan3D-DiT):**
 - **Metrics:** ULIP-T/I and Uni3D-T/I (measuring similarity between generated mesh and image/text prompts).
 - **Results:** Hunyuan3D-DiT generated shapes that exhibited higher adherence to conditional inputs, achieving metrics such as a Uni3D-I score of 0.3151, surpassing Michelangelo, Craftsman 1.5, Trellis, and several closed-source models.
 - **Visual Quality:** Visual comparisons indicated that Hunyuan3D-DiT successfully synthesized detailed surface bumps, clear human faces, logo texts, and complex layouts, while maintaining the completeness of the generated bare meshes.

#### 4.2. Texture Map Synthesis (Hunyuan3D-Paint)

- **Metrics:** CLIP Maximum-Mean Discrepancy (CMMD), CLIP-version of Fréchet Inception Distance (FID_CLIP), CLIP-score, and LPIPS (perceptual similarity). Lower CMMD, FID_CLIP, and LPIPS, and higher CLIP-score indicate better performance.
 - **Results:** Hunyuan3D-Paint achieved the best generative quality and semantic alignment among compared methods, including TEXTure, Text2Tex, SyncMVD, Paint3D, and TexPainter. It reported the lowest CMMD (2.318) and FID_CLIP (26.44), and the highest CLIP-score (0.8893).
 - **Visual Quality:** Qualitative results demonstrated Hunyuan3D-Paint's ability to produce text-conforming textures for various objects (e.g., fish, rabbit), generate seamless and clean texture maps (e.g., football), and synthesize intricate texture patterns (e.g., castle, bear).
 - **Applications:** The model successfully demonstrated "texture re-skinning," generating different seamless and detailed texture maps for the same mesh based on varying prompts.

#### 4.3. Textured 3D Assets Generation (End-to-end Hunyuan3D 2.0)

- **Metrics:** CMMD, FID_CLIP, FID_Incept, and CLIP-score, evaluated on renderings of the generated textured 3D assets.
 - **Results:** The end-to-end Hunyuan3D 2.0 system outperformed Trellis and three closed-source models in overall textured 3D asset quality and condition following. It registered the lowest CMMD (3.193), FID_CLIP (49.165), and FID_Incept (282.429), and the highest CLIP-score (0.809).
 - **Visual Quality:** Visual comparisons showcased Hunyuan3D 2.0's capability to synthesize detailed surface bumps and accurate texture maps for complex text, handle intricate actions for characters (e.g., penguin), and generate elaborate structures with vivid textures (e.g., mountain).
- **User Study:** A user study involving 50 volunteers evaluating 300 generated results indicated that Hunyuan3D 2.0 achieved higher user satisfaction, particularly in terms of overall visual quality and adherence to image conditions, compared to the baseline models.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Hunyuan3D 2.0 represents a notable contribution to the field of 3D generative AI, with several implications for research and application:

- **Advancement in 3D Generative AI:** The system demonstrates a significant step forward in the capability of diffusion models for 3D asset generation, particularly in producing high-resolution, high-fidelity textured meshes. Its performance, surpassing both open-source and closed-source state-of-the-art models in quantitative and qualitative evaluations, indicates progress in the field.
- **Open-Source Foundation Model:** By publicly releasing Hunyuan3D 2.0, including its code and pre-trained weights, the project addresses a critical gap in the open-source 3D community. This initiative aims to provide a robust, large-scale foundational model that can serve as a baseline for future research, fostering innovation and accelerating development in 3D generation algorithms.
- **Democratization of 3D Content Creation:** The development of Hunyuan3D-Studio, a versatile and user-friendly platform, has the potential to lower the barrier to entry for 3D content creation. Features like Sketch-to-3D, Low-polygon Stylization, and 3D Character Animation can streamline the 3D production pipeline, making advanced 3D asset creation and manipulation accessible to a broader audience, including both professionals and amateurs.
- **Broadening Application Scope:** High-quality, automated 3D asset generation is crucial for various industries. In gaming and film, it can enhance creative expression and immersive experiences. For physical simulation and embodied AI, it provides essential building blocks for developing more realistic and capable virtual environments and robotic systems. The ability to generate complex geometries and detailed textures efficiently can significantly impact these domains.
- **Modular and Flexible Design:** The two-stage pipeline, decoupling shape and texture generation, offers inherent flexibility. This allows Hunyuan3D-Paint to texture not only newly generated meshes but also existing or hand-crafted meshes, expanding its utility in diverse workflows.
- **Robustness and Detail Preservation:** The meticulous design choices, such as importance sampling in ShapeVAE, dual-stream transformers in DiT, image delighting, and multi-task attention in Paint, contribute to the system's ability to preserve fine-grained details and maintain consistency across generated views and conditions.
