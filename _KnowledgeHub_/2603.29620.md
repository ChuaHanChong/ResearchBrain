# Unify-Agent: A Unified Multimodal Agent for World-Grounded Image Synthesis

> [!summary] Summary
> Unify-Agent introduces a unified multimodal agent framework for world-grounded image synthesis, actively acquiring and integrating external knowledge to overcome factual inaccuracies and identity drift in generative models. It achieves a 22-point improvement in overall score on the FactIP benchmark compared to its base model, demonstrating superior factual and visual fidelity for knowledge-intensive concepts.

## Problem

- Existing Text-to-Image (T2I) models struggle to faithfully depict real-world, knowledge-intensive concepts (e.g., specific IPs, historical figures), often suffering from factual inaccuracies or "identity drift".
- Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) rely on static parametric knowledge from pre-training, which is insufficient for less common or frequently updated real-world targets, leading to hallucinations.
- Prior agentic T2I systems are brittle, multi-stage pipelines with decoupled components (LLM planner, retrieval tools, image generator), leading to cascading errors and poor evidence integration.

## Method

- Developed **Unify-Agent**, a unified multimodal agent built on the ==Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT)== architecture (**Bagel**) that integrates visual understanding and continuous image generation.
- Implemented an end-to-end ==agentic pipeline== during inference, featuring prompt understanding, ==multimodal evidence searching== (textual and visual), ==evidence-grounded recaptioning==, and visual synthesis.
- Curated a specialized data pipeline of 143K high-quality agent trajectories, including multimodal research traces and structured, generation-oriented recaptions, for fine-tuning the unified model.

## Results

- Unify-Agent achieved a **22-point** overall score improvement on the FactIP benchmark (**73.2** vs. 50.9 for base model) and the best WiScore (**0.77**) on the WiSE benchmark among Unified MLLMs.
- It set a new state-of-the-art overall score (**4.08**) among open-source models on KiTTEN, demonstrating superior text and entity alignment.
- Ablation studies confirmed that multimodal evidence acquisition (text and image search) and evidence-grounded recaptioning are essential for improving factual relevance and image quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A tightly coupled, unified multimodal architecture integrating reasoning, search, and generation within a single model significantly improves factual and identity-consistent image synthesis.
> - Transforming raw retrieved textual and visual evidence into a structured, generation-oriented "recaption" is crucial for effective knowledge integration and downstream synthesis.
> - In a unified model, the interplay between low-level generative latents (VAE) and high-level semantic visual representations (ViT) during recaptioning enhances the interpretation of visual references, improving overall grounding.
