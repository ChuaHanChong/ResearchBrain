# Thinker: A vision-language foundation model for embodied intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> UBTECH Robotics introduces Thinker, a 10-billion parameter vision-language foundation model engineered for embodied intelligence. The model achieves state-of-the-art performance on robotic task planning benchmarks like Robovqa and Egoplan-bench2, demonstrating enhanced egocentric perspective, temporal reasoning, and spatial understanding through specialized data and a two-stage fine-tuning approach.

## Problem

- General-purpose vision-language models (VLMs) often confuse third-person and first-person perspectives, hindering their application in embodied AI.
- Existing VLMs struggle with temporal reasoning, frequently overlooking critical information towards the end of video sequences, essential for understanding action flows.
- Lack of tailored training data for robot-specific tasks results in deficiencies in spatial grounding from an egocentric viewpoint and effective language instruction understanding for physical actions.

## Method

- Developed **Thinker**, a 10-billion parameter VLM with a unified architecture for vision, language, and time, designed to process images, videos, and complex language instructions.
- Implemented a two-stage training strategy: first, building foundational embodied capabilities with diverse datasets, then fine-tuning for complex industrial planning tasks using the proprietary Industroplan-200K dataset.
- Constructed extensive, purpose-built datasets for visual grounding, ego-view reasoning, robotic manipulation planning, and industrial tasks, crucially incorporating joint video and last-frame inputs to enhance temporal understanding.

## Results

- Thinker-7B achieved state-of-the-art performance on the Robovqa benchmark, with an average BLEU score of 63.5, surpassing general-purpose VLMs like GPT-4V (26.8 average BLEU) by a substantial margin.
- The model secured the highest overall accuracy of **58.21%** on the Egoplan-bench2 benchmark, leading in three out of four specific domains including Daily life (**63.78%**), Work (**54.95%**), and Recreation (**61.20%**).
- These findings confirm Thinker-7B's superior ability to parse fine-grained spatiotemporal cues and effectively decompose complex, long-range planning tasks into coherent, actionable responses for robotic applications.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Specialized training with large-scale, robotics-tailored datasets and a multi-stage fine-tuning process is crucial for adapting general VLMs to the complexities of embodied intelligence.
> - Jointly incorporating key frames alongside full video inputs significantly improves temporal reasoning and the model's ability to focus on critical, task-relevant information.
> - Creating an egocentric coordinate system for spatial understanding, coupled with explicit training on task planning, enables VLMs to bridge the gap between abstract AI and practical robotic action.
