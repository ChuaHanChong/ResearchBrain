# VOiLA: Vectorized Online Planning with Learned Diffusion Model for POMDP Agents

> [!summary] Summary
> VOiLA introduces a framework for online planning under uncertainty, integrating learned task-agnostic POMDP models with a vectorized online planner. The approach achieves significant data efficiency and superior generalization to unseen environments, successfully transferring models trained purely in simulation to a physical quadruped robot for real-world tasks.

## Problem

- Developing accurate, hand-crafted POMDP models for complex, stochastic real-world robotic systems is often impractical.
- Many reinforcement learning (RL) methods suffer from data inefficiency and limited generalization to novel environments or tasks.
- Expressive generative models like diffusion models are computationally intensive, making their direct use prohibitive for real-time online planning.
- Traditional online POMDP solvers often lack the scalability to leverage modern parallel computing architectures for real-time performance in complex tasks.

## Method

- **VOiLA** learns task-agnostic POMDP transition, observation, and likelihood models from offline simulated data using conditional diffusion models.
- Computational efficiency is addressed by distilling the trained diffusion models into compact, feedforward generators for fast sampling during online planning.
- These learned and distilled models are integrated into a massively parallel, GPU-accelerated online POMDP planner (VOPP), enhanced with vectorized progressive widening for continuous observation spaces.

## Results

- VOiLA demonstrated superior data efficiency, using 50K transitions compared to 2.6M-32M for baselines on FloorPositioning and 750K for Recurrent SAC on LeggedNavigation.
- The framework achieved enhanced generalization, maintaining 72-**80%** success rates on unseen LeggedNavigation environments, while Recurrent SAC's performance dropped significantly (e.g., to 10-**12%**).
- Distilled transition and observation samplers achieved approximately **5.9** × 10^4 samples per second, providing up to 283 times higher throughput than original diffusion models.
- VOiLA enabled successful sim-to-real transfer, with a physical quadruped successfully completing a target-finding task in 10 out of 10 real-world runs using simulation-trained models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Learning reusable, task-agnostic POMDP models from data improves data efficiency and generalization across environments compared to learning task-specific policies.
> - Distilling expressive diffusion models into compact generators is essential for achieving the computational throughput required for real-time online planning.
> - Combining data-driven learned models with a principled, massively parallel online POMDP planner enables robust decision-making in high-dimensional, partially observable environments.
> - POMDP models learned entirely from simulated data can be sufficiently robust for direct sim-to-real transfer to physical robots without additional real-world training.
