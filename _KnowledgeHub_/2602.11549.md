# Native Reasoning Models: Training Language Models to Reason on Unverifiable Data

> [!summary] Summary
> Native Reasoning Training (NRT) is a framework that trains language models to reason using only question-answer pairs, circumventing the need for human-annotated reasoning traces or external verifiers. This method, which intrinsically rewards reasoning that increases the model's confidence in correct answers, boosted average performance by 10.2 points over SFT on Llama-3.1-8B across nine benchmarks, achieving a 76.0 score on GSM8K compared to SFT's 29.0.

## Problem

- Traditional Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) depend on expensive human-annotated reasoning data and external verifiers, limiting scalability and applicability.
- RLVR's reliance on objective external verifiers restricts its use to domains like mathematics and coding, leaving many tasks with subjective or unverifiable correctness unaddressed.
- Existing verifier-free reinforcement learning approaches often suffer from instability, mode collapse, or introduce biases through proxy reward mechanisms like LLM judges.

## Method

- Native Reasoning Training (**NRT**) treats reasoning as a ==latent variable==, employing reinforcement learning to explore and intrinsically reward reasoning traces that enhance the model's predictive confidence in ground-truth answer tokens.
- The framework optimizes using off-policy RL with importance sampling, generating both trace policy gradients and weighted token prediction gradients to refine trace generation and answer prediction simultaneously.
- NRT introduces robust reward aggregation functions (e.g., Geometric Mean, Weighted Sum based on inverse or log-probability) and reward stabilization techniques to prevent policy collapse and ensure stable learning.

## Results

- NRT-WS (
− log p) achieved an average score of 56.2 on the Llama-3.1-**8B** model across nine benchmarks, outperforming the SFT baseline by 10.2 points and the strongest prior method (RLPR) by 5.4 points.
- The method demonstrated substantial performance gains in complex reasoning tasks, with NRT-WS (
− log p) increasing the Llama-3.1-**8B** model's GSM8K score from 29.0 (SFT) to 76.0.
- NRT effectively mitigated mode collapse and policy degradation, maintaining diverse and high-quality reasoning processes throughout training, and improved prediction confidence for high-entropy tokens by up to **15%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Principled reward shaping, particularly weighted-sum aggregation that targets difficult tokens, is crucial for cultivating robust reasoning abilities without explicit trace supervision.
> - Language models can intrinsically learn to resolve their own predictive uncertainties by generating reasoning that specifically boosts confidence in previously high-entropy, hard-to-predict tokens.
> - NRT enables the autonomous emergence of a structured reasoning vocabulary and format, allowing models to clearly differentiate between internal thought processes and final answers.
