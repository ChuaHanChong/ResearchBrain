# MARBLE: Multi-Aspect Reward Balance for Diffusion RL

> [!summary] Summary
> MARBLE, a gradient-space optimization framework developed by researchers at Zhejiang University and HiThink, enables a single diffusion model to be simultaneously optimized across multiple reward objectives, efficiently balancing competing signals. This approach improves overall quality across five training rewards and four held-out metrics while maintaining training speed comparable to single-reward baselines.

## Problem

- Existing diffusion Reinforcement Learning (RL) methods primarily optimize a single scalar reward, limiting the ability to align models with multi-faceted human preferences.
- Current multi-reward strategies are inefficient (training separate models), labor-intensive and prone to catastrophic forgetting (sequential fine-tuning), or suboptimal (naive weighted-sum aggregation due to signal dilution).
- Gradient interference, a known challenge in multi-task learning, had not been effectively addressed for multi-reward fine-tuning of diffusion models.

## Method

- **MARBLE** decomposes reward signals by maintaining ==independent advantage estimators== and computing policy gradients for each reward, preserving reward-specific supervision.
- Harmonizes per-reward gradients using a ==Quadratic Programming (QP) solver== to find a balanced update direction, adaptively allocating optimization emphasis without manual reward weighting.
- An ==amortized gradient harmonization== technique, combined with ==EMA smoothing== of balancing coefficients, reduces computational overhead and stabilizes the optimization process.

## Results

- MARBLE achieved simultaneous improvements across **five training rewards** (PickScore, HPSv2, CLIPScore, OCR accuracy, GenEval) and demonstrated strong performance on **four held-out metrics** (Aesthetic Score, ImageReward, UniReward, Composite Score), outperforming previous multi-reward baselines.
- The amortized MARBLE variant trained with **0.97x** the speed of a single-reward baseline with a modest **1.14x** GPU memory increase, demonstrating practical efficiency.
- Ablation studies confirmed that **gradient normalization** and **adaptive coefficients** are critical for balanced convergence, and that amortization with EMA smoothing significantly enhances stability and performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Naive weighted-sum reward aggregation leads to a 'specialist sample phenomenon' and 'dilution of supervision,' where strong signals for one reward are diluted by uninformative signals from others, often resulting in gradient anti-alignment.
> - Gradient-space harmonization, by operating on normalized per-reward gradients, effectively mitigates inter-reward interference and facilitates balanced progress across diverse objectives.
> - Amortization and EMA smoothing of balancing coefficients are crucial for making gradient-space multi-reward optimization practical and stable for diffusion RL, maintaining efficiency while preventing transient batch-level fluctuations from disrupting learning.
