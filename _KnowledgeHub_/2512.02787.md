# Diagnose, Correct, and Learn from Manipulation Failures via Visual Symbols

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Beihang University, Southern University of Science and Technology, and Shanghai Jiao Tong University developed ViFailback, a framework for diagnosing, correcting, and learning from real-world robotic manipulation failures. It introduces explicit visual symbols for efficient data annotation and provides multimodal corrective guidance, resulting in up to a 23.8% increase in task success rates for a leading VLA model in real-world experiments.

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models lack robust capabilities to diagnose, learn from, or recover from manipulation failures, especially under out-of-distribution conditions.
- Existing large-scale failure datasets are predominantly simulated, creating a 'sim-to-real gap' that limits their utility for real-world robotic applications.
- Annotating real-world robotic failure data, including complex failure categories and detailed corrective guidance, is time-consuming and labor-intensive with current methods.

## Method

- A framework named **ViFailback** was introduced, leveraging ==seven explicit visual symbols== (e.g., arrows, crosshairs, ON/OFF labels) to efficiently annotate real-world robotic manipulation failures and provide ==multimodal corrective guidance==.
- A dataset, **ViFailback**, comprising **5,202** real-world trajectories and **58,128** VQA pairs, and a benchmark, **ViFailback**-Bench (Lite and Hard versions), were constructed for fine-grained failure analysis.
- **ViFailback**-8B, a VLM finetuned on the **ViFailback** dataset, was integrated as an ==external supervisor== with a VLA model (π0.5) to deliver textual and ==visual symbol-based corrective actions== in real-world robot execution.

## Results

- **ViFailback-8B** achieved **93.70%** average accuracy on the closed-ended ViFailback-Bench Lite, a **+39.14%** performance gain over the best baseline model (Gemini-2.5-Pro).
- On the open-ended ViFailback-Bench Hard, **ViFailback-8B** reached **72.64%** average accuracy, outperforming its zero-shot counterpart by **+39.6%** and all baseline models.
- Integrating **ViFailback-8B** with a π0.5 VLA model in real-world robotic experiments led to an average increase in task success rates of **+20.6%** (Visual Symbols-Following) to **+23.8%** (Point-based Motion Control) across unseen manipulation tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Existing state-of-the-art VLMs exhibit significant limitations in robotic manipulation failure diagnosis and correction, particularly in open-ended reasoning and multi-step guidance generation.
> - Utilizing explicit visual symbols significantly streamlines the annotation of complex real-world failure data, making it more practical to collect and scale such datasets.
> - Multimodal corrective guidance, combining textual explanations with precise visual symbols, offers a more actionable and interpretable form of feedback for VLA models, enabling effective policy recovery.
