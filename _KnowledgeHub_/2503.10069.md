# SmartWay: Enhanced Waypoint Prediction and Backtracking for Zero-Shot Vision-and-Language Navigation

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework for Vision-and-Language Navigation in Continuous Environments (VLN-CE), SmartWay enhances waypoint prediction via a DINOv2 encoder and occupancy-aware loss, while its zero-shot MLLM-based navigator incorporates a history-aware prompting system and a novel backtracking mechanism. The system achieves an Oracle Success Rate of 51% in simulated zero-shot VLN-CE and a Success Rate of 36% on a physical robot in real-world scenarios.

## Problem

- Existing waypoint predictors in Vision-and-Language Navigation in Continuous Environments (VLN-CE) often lack robust spatial awareness, leading to predictions of waypoints that are obstructed or suboptimal.
- Current Large Language Model (LLM) and Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) navigators for VLN-CE struggle with continuous motion control, direct visual interpretation, and lack effective historical reasoning and crucial backtracking capabilities.
- There is a need to bridge the sim-to-real gap and address data scarcity for real-world VLN-CE deployment, as supervised models often lack sufficient generalization.

## Method

- ==An Occupancy==-aware Waypoint Predictor leverages DINOv2 for richer visual features, a masked cross-attention mechanism for feature fusion, and an occupancy-aware loss to ensure predicted waypoints are physically traversable.
- A zero-shot MLLM-based Navigator (utilizing gpt-4o) employs a "History-aware Single-expert Prompt System" that integrates multi-scale turning options, semantic object recognition (via RAM), and expanded historical information.
- A novel and explicit backtracking mechanism is integrated into the MLLM's action space, enabling the agent to detect navigational failures and revert to previous, known-good locations to mitigate error propagation.

## Results

- The enhanced waypoint predictor achieved an **87.26%** %Open rate (indicating navigable waypoints) and the lowest Hausdorff distance (**1.96**), demonstrating superior waypoint quality in simulation.
- SmartWay achieved a new state-of-the-art in zero-shot VLN-CE in simulation with an Oracle Success Rate of **51%**, a Success Rate of **29%**, and an SPL of **22.46%** on the R2R-CE benchmark, significantly outperforming previous zero-shot methods.
- In real-world deployment on a Turtlebot 4, SmartWay achieved a **24%** Success Rate, which increased to **36%** with the inclusion of the backtracking mechanism, outperforming a supervised baseline and reducing collision rates.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining powerful self-supervised vision encoders (DINOv2) with explicit spatial awareness (occupancy-aware loss) significantly improves waypoint quality and navigability.
> - Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) can be effectively leveraged for zero-shot Vision-and-Language Navigation in continuous environments, especially when provided with rich historical context and semantic information.
> - An explicit backtracking mechanism is crucial for improving the robustness and error recovery capabilities of MLLM-based navigation agents in complex, unfamiliar environments.
