# Refining Hybrid Genetic Search for CVRP via Reinforcement Learning-Finetuned LLM

> [!summary] Summary
> A reinforcement learning framework, RFTHGS, was developed to fine-tune a 14B-parameter LLM, enabling it to autonomously generate high-performance crossover operators for the Hybrid Genetic Search (HGS) algorithm, leading to an improvement in Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP) solutions that surpasses both expert-designed operators and larger commercial LLMs.

## Problem

- Combinatorial Optimization Problems (COPs) like Vehicle Routing Problems (VRPs) are NP-hard and traditionally require extensive domain expertise and manual effort for heuristic design.
- Existing LLM-based VRP solvers often generate suboptimal or infeasible solutions and depend on large, computationally expensive general-purpose LLMs, which struggle to match the performance of specialized solvers.
- A persistent performance gap exists between LLM-driven approaches and advanced conventional or deep learning-based solvers for large-scale combinatorial optimization problems.

## Method

- **RFTHGS**, an iterative reinforcement learning framework, was introduced to fine-tune a smaller, open-source LLM (Qwen-14B) specifically for generating effective crossover operators within the ==Hybrid Genetic Search== (HGS) algorithm for CVRP.
- A multi-tiered, curriculum-based reward function was designed, progressively guiding the LLM to first generate compilable and executable code, then to optimize for superior solution quality compared to expert-designed operators.
- An anti-plagiarism cache utilizing Abstract Syntax Trees (ASTs) and incremental compilation were implemented to promote operator diversity and accelerate the evaluation process during training.

## Results

- The RFTHGS-fine-tuned LLM consistently generated crossover operators that achieved superior performance on CVRPLIB benchmarks (up to 1000 nodes) compared to expert-designed operators and a diverse set of state-of-the-art baselines, including GPT-4o and advanced conventional solvers.
- RFTHGS achieved a **100%** successful compilation rate for generated operators, a notable improvement over commercial LLMs like GPT-4o, which had a **19%** compilation rate, indicating higher reliability in code generation.
- Training dynamics showed stable convergence, with generated operator performance surpassing expert-designed baselines around step 200 and further improving, validating the effectiveness of the multi-tiered curriculum learning approach.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Smaller, specialized LLMs, when fine-tuned via reinforcement learning with a focused objective, can generate algorithmic components that outperform those created by human experts for complex optimization tasks.
> - A progressive, multi-faceted reward system effectively trains LLMs to produce functional and high-quality code, leading to significant enhancements in solver performance.
> - The fine-tuned LLM demonstrates robust generalization capabilities, maintaining high performance on significantly larger problem instances (up to 1000 nodes) than those seen during training (n<400), and across different execution budgets.
