# UniT: Unified Multimodal Chain-of-Thought Test-time Scaling

> [!summary] Summary
> The UniT framework, developed by Meta Superintelligence Labs and Stanford University, introduces iterative reasoning and refinement for unified multimodal models. It extends chain-of-thought test-time scaling to the multimodal domain, yielding an average 10.34% improvement in compositional generation tasks and a 225.19% increase in human preference for multi-turn editing, while proving 2.5 times more compute-efficient than parallel sampling.

## Problem

- Existing unified multimodal models operate in a single-pass mode, lacking inherent mechanisms for self-evaluation, reflection, or iterative refinement of outputs.
- This single-pass limitation hinders performance on complex multimodal tasks that inherently require multi-step reasoning and self-correction, such as compositional generation or multi-turn editing.
- The systematic application of iterative, sequential test-time scaling, which has benefited text-only models, was not yet extended to unified multimodal models.

## Method

- An ==agentic framework== is employed to automatically synthesize high-quality ==multimodal chain-of-thought trajectories==, encompassing prompt generation, initial image creation, iterative reflection by a VLM, and refinement by an image editing model.
- These synthesized multi-round trajectories are used to fine-tune a unified multimodal model (==Bagel==), enabling it to internalize complex multimodal reasoning, verification, and refinement patterns.
- At inference time, the single trained **UniT** model autonomously performs all multi-round planning, generation, reflection, and refinement, guided by techniques like ==Nested Classifier-Free Guidance==.

## Results

- UniT models demonstrate emergent generalization, performing effectively on inference chains averaging **4.7 rounds** after training on shorter **3.6-round trajectories**.
- Sequential chain-of-thought scaling consistently outperforms parallel sampling, achieving better performance across tasks (OneIG-Bench, CompBench, ImgEdit, MIRA) with **2.5× less computational cost**.
- The framework yields substantial improvements in multimodal tasks, including a **10.34%** alignment increase on OneIG-Bench, a **225.19%** human preference improvement for multi-turn editing, and a **53.33%** gain in out-of-distribution visual reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Sequential chain-of-thought reasoning is a more scalable and computationally efficient test-time scaling strategy for multimodal tasks compared to parallel best-of-N sampling.
> - Unified multimodal models can be trained to internalize cognitive behaviors (verification, subgoal decomposition, content memory) that enable iterative self-correction and multi-round reasoning.
> - Models trained on shorter reasoning trajectories can effectively generalize and extrapolate to longer, more complex inference chains at test time, indicating an emergent problem-solving capability.
