# Flow-GRPO: Training Flow Matching Models via Online RL

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from MMLab, Tsinghua University, Kuaishou Technology, and Shanghai AI Lab developed Flow-GRPO, a framework that integrates online policy gradient reinforcement learning into flow matching models. This method significantly enhances capabilities in compositional image generation, visual text rendering, and human preference alignment, achieving up to 95% GenEval accuracy on SD3.5-M by addressing challenges of determinism and sampling efficiency through an ODE-to-SDE conversion and denoising reduction.

## Problem

- Flow matching generative models struggle with composing complex scenes involving multiple objects and attributes, as well as accurately rendering text within images.
- Applying online reinforcement learning (RL) to these models is challenging due to their deterministic ODE-based generation process, which conflicts with RL's need for stochastic exploration.
- The high computational cost of generating numerous high-quality samples for online RL training makes the process prohibitively slow for large flow models.

## Method

- The iterative denoising process of flow matching models is formulated as a ==Markov Decision Process (MDP)==, and ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== is applied for its memory efficiency.
- The deterministic Flow-ODE is converted into an equivalent ==Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE)== to enable stochastic sampling for RL exploration, preserving the original model's marginal probability density function.
- A =='Denoising Reduction' strategy== uses significantly fewer denoising steps during training sample collection (e.g., 10 steps) to accelerate online RL, while retaining full steps (e.g., 40 steps) for high-quality inference.

## Results

- Flow-GRPO-tuned Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium (SD3.5-M) improved GenEval accuracy from **63%** to **95%** for compositional generation, surpassing GPT-4o's **84%** performance.
- Visual text rendering accuracy for SD3.5-M increased from **59%** to **92%**, and the framework demonstrated strong generalization to unseen compositional elements and different flow-matching backbones.
- The Denoising Reduction strategy achieved over a **4x** speedup in training sample collection, and KL regularization effectively mitigated reward hacking, preserving image quality and diversity during optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Online policy gradient RL, when properly integrated, can effectively enhance compositional reasoning and text rendering abilities of flow matching models.
> - Injecting stochasticity into deterministic flow models via an ODE-to-SDE conversion provides the necessary exploration for online RL without sacrificing the original model's probabilistic properties.
> - Informative reward signals for online RL can be derived from lower-quality, few-step generated samples, allowing for drastic improvements in training efficiency for large generative models.
