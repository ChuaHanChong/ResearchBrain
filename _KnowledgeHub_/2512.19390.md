# TwinAligner: Visual-Dynamic Alignment Empowers Physics-aware Real2Sim2Real for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Peking University researchers developed TwinAligner, a unified Real2Sim2Real system that precisely aligns both visual and dynamic properties between real and simulated environments. The system enables policies trained purely in simulation to achieve strong zero-shot generalization to the real world and provides reliable cross-environment policy evaluation for robotic manipulation.

## Problem

- High cost and logistical challenges of collecting sufficient, diverse, and high-quality real-world data for robot learning.
- The persistent "Sim2Real gap," characterized by visual and dynamic discrepancies, prevents policies learned in simulation from reliably transferring to the real world.
- Lack of a robust, unified framework that simultaneously addresses both visual and dynamic alignment for physics-aware robotic manipulation tasks.

## Method

- A "Mesh-GS Digital Twin" module reconstructs objects, scenes, and robots using a combination of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) for photorealistic rendering and SDF/mesh for accurate collision geometry.
- A "Visual-Dynamic Real2Sim Alignment" module aligns simulation viewpoint and rigid physics parameters (friction, mass, center of mass, robot controller) using gradient-free optimization, robust to rendering errors.
- The aligned environment supports "Sim2Real Policy Learning" for zero-shot generalization to the real world and allows for trustworthy cross-environment evaluation of robotic policies.

## Results

- Achieved superior object digital twin quality with a PSNR of 38.03 for rendering and consistent, watertight meshes across diverse object types, outperforming baselines.
- Demonstrated significantly improved dynamic alignment, reducing average ADD to 1.39 cm and ADD-S to 0.78 cm for object trajectories, showing better consistency with real-world physics than previous methods.
- Enabled strong zero-shot Sim2Real generalization with policies trained in TwinAligner achieving higher success rates (e.g., 10/15 for Pushing Milk Box) across four manipulation tasks, including articulated objects, compared to other Real2Sim2Real baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining 3DGS with SDF reconstruction effectively balances high-fidelity rendering with watertight, simulator-friendly collision geometry for diverse objects and scenes.
> - Gradient-free optimization for dynamic parameter alignment provides robustness to visual errors (like relighting) and handles physically disturbed objects more effectively than differentiable simulation methods.
> - A unified approach that meticulously aligns both visual appearance and physical dynamics across objects, scenes, and robots is crucial for enabling effective physics-aware manipulation in a Real2Sim2Real pipeline.
