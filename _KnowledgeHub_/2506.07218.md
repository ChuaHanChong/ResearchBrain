# Perception-R1: Advancing Multimodal Reasoning Capabilities of MLLMs via Visual Perception Reward

> [!summary] Summary
> Perception-R1 enhances the multimodal reasoning abilities of Multimodal Large Language Models by explicitly integrating a novel visual perception reward into the Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards framework. This approach yields new state-of-the-art performance on various multimodal benchmarks while achieving remarkable data efficiency, using only 1,442 training samples.

## Problem

- Existing Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) methods for MLLMs primarily focus on logical reasoning and neglect the foundational aspect of multimodal perception.
- MLLMs trained with accuracy-only RLVR can "guess" correct answers without genuinely understanding the visual context, leading to brittle reasoning.
- Reward sparsity in accuracy-only RLVR training makes it difficult for MLLMs to learn and correct underlying visual perception flaws.

## Method

- Perception-R1 introduces a ==visual perception reward== to explicitly guide MLLMs towards improved multimodal perception during RLVR training.
- High-quality =="visual annotations"== are curated from powerful proprietary MLLM Chain-of-Thought trajectories, serving as ground-truth references for critical visual elements.
- A =="judging LLM"== assesses the consistency between the MLLM's generated response and these visual annotations, contributing to an enhanced reward function that includes format, accuracy, visual perception, and repetition penalty components.

## Results

- Achieves new state-of-the-art performance across numerous multimodal reasoning benchmarks, including MathVista, MathVerse, MMMU, and MMStar.
- Demonstrates statistically significant improvements in multimodal perception capabilities, particularly on vision-only subsets of MathVerse and MMMU-Pro, validating the effectiveness of the visual perception reward.
- Obtains SOTA results using an exceptionally small dataset of only **1,442** training samples, showcasing remarkable data efficiency compared to previous methods.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Multimodal perception is a critical bottleneck for robust MLLM reasoning, often overlooked by existing accuracy-only RLVR methods.
> - Explicitly rewarding accurate visual perception, grounded in verifiable annotations, significantly improves MLLM reasoning capabilities and robustness.
> - Intelligent reward design, by incorporating richer, perception-focused signals, can dramatically enhance data efficiency in MLLM development.
