# Learning Action Manifold with Multi-view Latent Priors for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> A new Vision-Language-Action (VLA) framework enhances robotic manipulation by improving monocular spatial perception through a Geometry-Guided Gated Transformer and boosting action learning efficiency via Action Manifold Learning. This approach achieved an average success rate of 98.6% on LIBERO and 85.7% on LIBERO-Plus, outperforming prior methods in robustness and generalization.

## Problem

- Monocular Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models struggle with 3D spatial perception due to inherent depth ambiguity and geometric inconsistency from single RGB camera inputs, limiting manipulation precision.
- Current generative action prediction methods for VLA models rely on indirect, high-dimensional statistical targets (noise or velocity), imposing a heavy optimization burden and leading to inefficient and less robust policy learning.

## Method

- Introduced a Geometry-Guided Gated Transformer (G
3T) that synthesizes multi-view latent features and adaptively fuses them with monocular geometric priors to enhance spatial perception.
- Developed Action Manifold Learning (AML), an action expert that directly predicts clean action chunks on a hypothesized low-dimensional action manifold, using a velocity-consistent loss.
- Integrated these components into a modular VLA framework that leverages a powerful Vision-Language Model (Qwen3-VL) for semantic feature extraction and an AML-based action expert.

## Results

- Achieved an average success rate of 98.6% on the LIBERO benchmark and 85.7% on LIBERO-Plus under zero-shot perturbations, significantly outperforming existing state-of-the-art VLA methods.
- Demonstrated improved depth estimation accuracy with G
3T, showing lower Absolute Relative Error and Root Mean Squared Error compared to monocular VGGT features.
- Showcased AML's efficiency, maintaining robust performance with minimal denoising steps and improved graceful degradation with increasing action chunk sizes, alongside higher success rates in real-world bi-manual manipulation on RoboTwin 2.0 (86.06%).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Fusing synthesized multi-view latent representations with monocular geometric priors through an adaptive gating mechanism can effectively resolve monocular depth ambiguity and improve 3D spatial awareness.
> - Directly predicting clean action chunks, rather than abstract noise or velocity targets, by aligning with an "Action Manifold" simplifies the optimization landscape and enhances learning efficiency for high-dimensional actions.
> - A synergistic integration of enhanced spatial perception and efficient action learning significantly improves the robustness, precision, and generalization capabilities of VLA models in complex robotic manipulation tasks.
