# Revisiting LRP: Positional Attribution as the Missing Ingredient for Transformer Explainability

> [!summary] Summary
> Tel Aviv University researchers develop Positional-Aware LRP (PA-LRP), a framework for Transformer explainability that explicitly attributes relevance to positional encoding components alongside token embeddings, addressing a fundamental flaw in existing Layer-wise Relevance Propagation methods that violate conservation properties by ignoring positional information, achieving consistent performance improvements across NLP (7-51% AU-MSE reduction on IMDB classification, 3-8% gains on LLaMA-3 zero-shot tasks) and vision tasks (4-point average improvement on ImageNet perturbation tests, 2-3% gains in segmentation metrics) by introducing specialized LRP rules for various positional encoding types (learnable, sinusoidal, RoPE, ALiBi) and reformulating the input space as position-token pairs with per-layer positional sinks, with empirical analysis revealing that positional relevance constitutes 9-22% of total model relevance in vision transformers, demonstrating that structural and spatial cues carry distinct explanatory value complementary to semantic attributions.

## Problem

- Existing LRP methods for Transformers ignore positional encoding during relevance propagation, violating the conservation property and losing significant relevance information (9-22% of total relevance)
- Current explainability approaches fail to attribute the distinct structural and spatial information encoded in positional components, leading to incomplete and potentially unfaithful explanations
- State-of-the-art methods like AttnLRP produce fragmented attribution maps that miss holistic object-level patterns captured by positional encoding

## Method

- Reformulate input space as ==position-token pairs== with separate "sinks" for positional relevance at each layer to prevent information loss during backward propagation
- Design specialized ==LRP rules== for different positional encoding types: standard LRP-ε for input-level PE (learnable/sinusoidal), matrix multiplication rules for attention-level PE (RoPE), and addition rules for bias-based PE (ALiBi)
- Aggregate final relevance by summing positive contributions from both semantic embeddings and all per-layer ==positional components== using multi-sink approach

## Results

- Consistent improvements across diverse tasks: **14.5-51%** AU-MSE reduction on IMDB classification with LLaMA models, **3.2%** AUAC and **7.7%** AU-MSE gains on zero-shot question answering
- Superior segmentation performance on ImageNet with **1.8-3%** improvements in pixel accuracy and mIoU across DeiT models, demonstrating better object boundary detection
- Empirical validation of theoretical claims showing positional relevance constitutes **9.22-22.39%** of total model relevance in vision transformers, confirming conservation property violations in existing methods

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Positional encoding carries substantial and unique relevance signal that complements semantic attributions, with "PE Only" explanations often matching full AttnLRP baseline performance
> - Positional relevance captures broader spatial and structural patterns (entire objects, sequential relationships) while semantic relevance focuses on specific discriminative features
> - Conservation property violation in existing methods represents a significant theoretical flaw that impacts explanation faithfulness, particularly when positional cues are dominant
