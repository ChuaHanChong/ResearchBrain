# RoboBrain 2.0 Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> RoboBrain 2.0, an embodied vision-language foundation model from BAAI, integrates perception, reasoning, and planning to empower intelligent interaction in physical environments. It demonstrates state-of-the-art performance across numerous spatial and temporal reasoning benchmarks, including object placement prediction and multi-robot planning, establishing a new baseline for embodied AI capabilities.

## Problem

- Existing embodied AI models face limitations in accurately understanding spatial relationships, identifying object affordances, and modeling relative and absolute spatial positions in dynamic physical environments.
- A lack of robust temporal modeling hinders current models from performing multi-stage, cross-agent temporal planning and executing robust closed-loop control in interactive scenarios.
- Many models struggle to extract causal logic from complex human instructions and align it with dynamic environmental states, restricting their generalization to open-ended embodied tasks.

## Method

- The system employs a heterogeneous encoder-decoder architecture, utilizing a lightweight vision encoder for dynamic-resolution images/videos and a Qwen2.5-VL-initialized LLM decoder for core reasoning, supporting diverse outputs including text, spatial coordinates, and reasoning traces.
- A massive, meticulously curated training dataset is constructed, spanning general MLLM VQA, detailed spatial understanding (visual grounding, object pointing, affordance, 3D/2D spatial concepts), and complex temporal tasks (ego-view planning, multi-robot coordination, closed-loop interaction).
- A progressive three-stage training strategy is implemented, moving from foundational spatiotemporal learning to embodied spatiotemporal enhancement, and culminating in Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning with Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) using the Reason-RFT framework.

## Results

- The 32B variant of RoboBrain 2.0 consistently achieved state-of-the-art or near state-of-the-art results across most spatial reasoning benchmarks, including BLINK (83.63), RoboSpatial (72.43), RefSpatial-Bench Location (54.00), and Where2Place (73.59), often surpassing proprietary models like Gemini and GPT-o4-mini.
- On temporal reasoning benchmarks, RoboBrain 2.0 (7B and 32B variants) demonstrated outstanding performance, achieving SOTA in Multi-Robot Planning (81.50 for 7B), Ego-Plan2 (57.23 for 32B), and RoboBench (72.16 for 7B).
- The lightweight 7B variant also exhibited strong competitive performance across benchmarks, showcasing its versatility and potential for deployment in resource-constrained environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Unifying perception, reasoning, and planning within a single vision-language foundation model is effective in overcoming long-standing bottlenecks in spatial understanding, temporal modeling, and causal reasoning for embodied AI.
> - Developing a diverse and high-quality embodied dataset, encompassing fine-grained spatial and complex temporal aspects, alongside a multi-stage training curriculum, is crucial for achieving advanced embodied intelligence.
> - Leveraging robust, scalable infrastructure for both large-scale training (FlagScale) and reinforcement fine-tuning (VeRL) significantly contributes to the efficient development and deployment of complex multimodal foundation models.
