# Spatial-SSRL: Enhancing Spatial Understanding via Self-Supervised Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Spatial-SSRL introduces a self-supervised reinforcement learning framework that enhances the spatial understanding capabilities of Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs). This approach utilizes intrinsic signals from ordinary RGB and RGB-D images to generate verifiable rewards, achieving average accuracy gains of 4.63% on spatial benchmarks for its 3B model while preserving general visual abilities.

## Problem

- Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) struggle with robust 2D and 3D spatial understanding, limiting their application in real-world scenarios like robotics and autonomous systems.
- Existing supervised fine-tuning (SFT) methods for spatial tasks are costly, rely on potentially noisy human annotations, and lack scalability.
- Current reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) approaches are often confined to specific environments and require complex engineering, limiting broad applicability.

## Method

- A ==two-stage self-supervised reinforcement learning== paradigm is implemented, utilizing five automatically generated ==pretext tasks== for spatial understanding.
- ==Depth-free tasks== (shuffled patch reordering, flipped patch recognition, cropped patch inpainting) are designed for 2D spatial consistency from RGB images.
- ==Depth-based tasks== (regional depth ordering, relative 3D position prediction) leverage RGB-D data for 3D scene comprehension, with GRPO used to optimize LVLM policy using accuracy and format rewards.

## Results

- Spatial-SSRL models achieved average accuracy gains of **+4.63%** (3B model) and **+3.89%** (7B model) across seven spatial understanding benchmarks.
- General visual question answering (VQA) and fine-grained perception capabilities were preserved or improved, with the 3B model showing an average gain of **+2.02%** across four general VQA benchmarks.
- The framework enhanced the LVLMs' ability to generate coherent spatial reasoning, outperforming baselines when explicit reasoning was required.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Intrinsic consistency signals within ordinary RGB/RGB-D images can serve as naturally verifiable, scalable, and cost-effective ground truth for self-supervised reinforcement learning.
> - A diverse set of complementary 2D and 3D self-supervised tasks leads to more holistic spatial intelligence, rather than single-task optimization.
> - Self-supervised reinforcement learning can cultivate genuinely productive spatial reasoning in LVLMs, enabling better performance even when models are prompted to generate explicit reasoning chains.
