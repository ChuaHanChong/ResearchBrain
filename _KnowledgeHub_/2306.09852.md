# Actor-Critic Model Predictive Control: Differentiable Optimization meets Reinforcement Learning for Agile Flight

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at the University of Zurich developed Actor-Critic Model Predictive Control (AC-MPC), a framework integrating a differentiable MPC solver into an actor-critic reinforcement learning architecture, improving sample efficiency and robustness for agile quadrotor flight. The system successfully transfers from simulation to real-world, achieving high-speed drone racing performance comparable to pure model-free methods while better handling disturbances and dynamic changes.

## Problem

- Model-free Reinforcement Learning (RL) suffers from high sample inefficiency, lacks robustness to out-of-distribution scenarios, and provides few safety guarantees.
- Traditional Model Predictive Control (MPC) requires extensive manual engineering of cost functions and parameters, leading to conservative assumptions and suboptimal performance at extreme limits.
- Combining the strengths of RL and MPC while mitigating their individual limitations for agile, robust, and generalizable flight in dynamic environments remains a challenge.

## Method

- Introduced **Actor-Critic** ==Model Predictive Control== (AC-MPC), embedding a differentiable MPC solver as the actor policy within a Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) reinforcement learning framework.
- A "Neural Cost Map" learns the quadratic cost function coefficients for the MPC solver, providing explicit prior knowledge of quadrotor dynamics and control constraints.
- Proposed Model Predictive Value Expansion (MPVE) to enhance critic training and sample efficiency by reusing short-horizon state-action predictions from the differentiable MPC.

## Results

- AC-MPC demonstrated superior robustness to strong wind gusts (**83.33%** success rate vs. **6.5%** for AC-MLP) and out-of-distribution initial conditions (**90.37%** vs. **74.78%** SR for AC-MLP).
- The framework exhibited high robustness to significant variations in quadrotor mass (up to **+27%**), inertia (up to 17%**80%**), and body rate limits (up to 17%**40%**) without requiring retraining.
- Achieved successful zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on complex drone racing tracks, matching state-of-the-art model-free RL performance at speeds up to 21 m/s while maintaining predictive properties.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Constraining the MPC's quadratic cost matrix (Q) to be diagonal was empirically crucial for successful learning, outperforming more complex matrix representations.
> - The critic's learned value function strongly correlates with the learned MPC cost terms, suggesting the critic effectively learns an approximation of the infinite-horizon cost-to-go, which informs local MPC optimization.
> - Embedding prior knowledge through the differentiable MPC significantly reduces the system's sensitivity to exploration hyperparameters, allowing AC-MPC to learn effectively with less exploration compared to pure model-free RL.
