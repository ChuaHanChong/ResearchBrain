# Embodied-R: Collaborative Framework for Activating Embodied Spatial Reasoning in Foundation Models via Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> A collaborative framework from Tsinghua University enables foundation models to perform embodied spatial reasoning by combining large vision-language models for perception with reinforcement learning-trained small language models for reasoning, achieving comparable performance to state-of-the-art multimodal models while reducing computational costs through key frame extraction and logical consistency rewards.

## Problem

- Current foundation models lack human-like spatial cognition abilities needed for embodied reasoning in physical environments
- Existing multimodal models face limitations with input token limits, frame redundancy, and poor generalization for embodied video understanding

## Method

- ==Collaborative framework== combining large VLMs for perception with small LMs for reasoning
- RL training with novel ==logical consistency reward== to improve reasoning quality
- ==Key frame extractor== to reduce computational costs by eliminating redundant frames
- Three-stage training schedule optimizing RL performance

## Results

- Achieved **comparable or better** performance than proprietary models like OpenAI-o1 and Gemini-2.5-Pro
- Demonstrated strong generalization on out-of-distribution datasets like EgoSchema and MVBench
- Reduced computational costs while maintaining accuracy through key frame selection
- RL training improved reasoning quality and mitigated reward hacking through logical consistency

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Small LMs can match large model performance when trained with RL and logical consistency rewards
> - Frame extraction and collaborative approach enable efficient processing of embodied video
> - RL training leads to better out-of-distribution generalization compared to supervised fine-tuning
