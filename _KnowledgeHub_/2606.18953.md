# Object-Centric Residual RL for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real VLA Enhancement

> [!summary] Summary
> An object-centric residual reinforcement learning framework enhances the robustness of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, achieving zero-shot sim-to-real transfer by training a corrective policy exclusively in simulation. The approach leverages a domain-invariant observation space and robustness training, leading to a 34 percentage point improvement in real-robot task success rates across various manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, while generalizable, often exhibit brittleness in precise physical interactions due to compounding execution errors from imitation learning.
- Applying traditional reinforcement learning (RL) directly to modern VLA architectures (e.g., diffusion models) is difficult due to their non-differentiable iterative processes.
- Existing residual RL approaches struggle with sim-to-real transfer, either losing performance through distillation from privileged simulator states or facing substantial visual domain gaps with image-based policies, while real-world RL is resource-intensive and risky.

## Method

- A residual RL framework is introduced where a lightweight corrective policy is learned in simulation and combined with a frozen, real-world base VLA action.
- A ==domain-invariant observation space==, consisting of 6-DoF object poses, robot proprioception, and the base VLA action, is used for the residual policy to bridge the sim-to-real gap.
- Robustness to real-world perception noise and failures is achieved by injecting random noise into object poses and applying pose dropout during simulation training of the residual policy.

## Results

- The sim-trained residual policy achieved zero-shot transfer to a real robot, improving the average task success rate by 34 percentage points (from **42%** to **76%**) across five manipulation tasks.
- Ablation studies confirmed that object pose dropout and noise injection were critical for robust sim-to-real transfer, and the object-centric observation space outperformed image-based and distillation baselines.
- The framework enabled a VLA self-improvement loop: supervised fine-tuning of the base VLA with residual-corrected real-robot rollouts increased its average success rate from **42%** to **59%**, demonstrating the generation of higher-quality training data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A domain-invariant observation space composed of object poses, proprioception, and base VLA actions enables zero-shot sim-to-real transfer for residual policies, bypassing visual domain adaptation challenges.
> - Training the residual policy with object pose noise injection and dropout mechanisms significantly improves its robustness to real-world pose estimation inaccuracies and failures, which is crucial for successful deployment.
> - Aligning the action distributions of simulation and real-world VLAs (by training both on identical teleoperation replays) ensures the sim-trained residual policy's corrections are relevant to the real-world VLA's behavior.
