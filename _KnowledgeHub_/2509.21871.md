# Unlocking the Essence of Beauty: Advanced Aesthetic Reasoning with Relative-Absolute Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> The Aes-R1 framework equips multimodal large language models with the ability to provide both accurate aesthetic scores and multi-dimensional, human-aligned critiques for images. It leverages an automatic aesthetic reasoning data pipeline (AesCoT) and a Relative-Absolute Policy Optimization (RAPO) algorithm to achieve state-of-the-art performance and strong generalization across diverse image datasets.

## Problem

- Existing MLLM-based Image Aesthetic Assessment (IAA) methods primarily offered numerical scores without explicit linguistic rationales, hindering interpretability and human alignment.
- Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) for IAA was data-intensive, prone to overfitting, and struggled with generalization, often learning implicit aesthetic patterns.
- Direct application of Reinforcement Learning (RL) to IAA faced challenges in defining an appropriate reward proxy for subjective aesthetic evaluation and ensuring robust aesthetic reasoning.

## Method

- Developed **AesCoT**, an automatic pipeline for constructing high-quality, interpretable ==aesthetic reasoning== data by prompting powerful MLLMs to generate multi-dimensional critiques across five predefined aesthetic dimensions.
- Introduced Relative-Absolute Policy Optimization (RAPO), a novel RL algorithm that combines a relative rank reward and an absolute error reward to simultaneously optimize for ordinal consistency and accurate score calibration.
- Implemented a two-stage training approach, beginning with a cold-start supervised fine-tuning phase on AesCoT data to instill basic aesthetic cognitive behaviors, followed by reinforcement learning with RAPO.

## Results

- Aes-R1 achieved state-of-the-art performance on five benchmark datasets, attaining an average PLCC of **0.6337** and SRCC of **0.6186**, improving over **0.2** in both metrics compared to the backbone model.
- The framework demonstrated strong generalization capabilities on out-of-distribution datasets (PARA, AADB), matching or exceeding models pretrained on large-scale human-annotated corpora with only **15K** training samples.
- Qualitative analysis confirmed Aes-R1's ability to provide nuanced, multidimensional critiques and assign scores closer to ground truth, offering balanced assessments unlike baseline models that often over-appraised images.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A dual reward function combining relative ranking and absolute error effectively captures the multifaceted nature of human aesthetic judgment, leading to both consistent ordering and calibrated absolute scores.
> - Moderate initial supervised fine-tuning (cold-start) is crucial for instilling robust aesthetic reasoning in MLLMs before RL, preventing superficial explanations without inhibiting policy exploration.
> - Automatic pipelines like AesCoT can efficiently generate high-quality, explainable aesthetic reasoning data, addressing the scarcity and cost of manual expert annotations and accelerating research.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The research addresses the growing demand for sophisticated Image Aesthetic Assessment (IAA) within the broader field of multimodal large language models (MLLMs). Traditionally, vision tasks like Image Quality Assessment (IQA) have focused on objective metrics such as sharpness and clarity. However, with the proliferation of creative design and social media, there's a critical need for models that can evaluate images based on subjective, high-level aesthetic criteria, aligning with human perception.

Current MLLM-based IAA methods primarily rely on supervised fine-tuning (SFT) using datasets annotated with Mean Opinion Scores (MOSs). While MLLMs demonstrate strong cross-modal understanding, SFT-based approaches often suffer from several limitations:
1. **Lack of Explainability:** They typically provide only a score, without explicit linguistic rationales that connect visual elements to aesthetic judgments. This limits their utility in applications requiring interpretable feedback.
2. **Implicit Reasoning:** Models learn aesthetic patterns implicitly, making it difficult for them to align with multidimensional aesthetic criteria.
3. **Data Intensity and Overfitting:** SFT requires extensive, high-quality, and often expensive-to-annotate data. Furthermore, as highlighted by the authors, prolonged SFT can lead to models learning biases, overfitting to curated datasets, and reduced entropy, hindering generalization and further optimization.

Reinforcement Learning (RL) has emerged as a promising, efficient, and scalable alternative for goal-driven tasks with MLLMs. However, direct application of RL to IAA faces its own set of challenges:
1. **Absence of Aesthetic Reasoning:** Without prior aesthetic knowledge or specific corpus pre-training, end-to-end RL can fail to activate robust aesthetic reasoning patterns, leading to superficial or generic explanations.
2. **Intricate Reward Modeling:** Defining an appropriate reward proxy for subjective aesthetic scores is complex, as there are no unified criteria for aesthetic preferences. Previous attempts with binary rewards or purely rank-based rewards have shown deficiencies, either in score calibration or in discerning fine-grained differences. For instance, rank-based rewards might separate high and low-quality images but fail to accurately calibrate their absolute scores, while scalar rewards can suffer from peak mismatch (as illustrated in Figure 1).

This paper positions itself as a solution to these combined challenges by integrating the strengths of interpretable reasoning, robust reward modeling, and efficient RL optimization. It aims to develop an IAA framework that not only accurately predicts aesthetic scores but also provides human-aligned, multidimensional aesthetic critiques, moving beyond simple numerical predictions to a deeper understanding of visual beauty.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the Aes-R1 framework are to overcome the limitations of existing IAA methods, particularly in the context of MLLMs:

1. **Enable Human-Aligned Aesthetic Reasoning and Explainability:** Current MLLMs often lack the ability to generate structured, explainable aesthetic critiques alongside numerical scores. The authors aim to equip MLLMs with aesthetic cognitive behaviors, allowing them to produce reliable, multi-dimensional explanations before issuing a score, thereby enhancing interpretability and alignment with human judgment.
2. **Address the Scarcity of High-Quality Aesthetic Reasoning Data:** Generating expert-level aesthetic rationales is labor-intensive and costly. A key motivation is to develop an efficient and scalable method for constructing high-quality aesthetic reasoning data that can be used for training.
3. **Develop an Effective and Robust Reward Mechanism for RL in IAA:** Direct application of RL is hindered by the difficulty of modeling an appropriate reward proxy for subjective aesthetic evaluation. The goal is to design a reward function that can simultaneously enforce ordinal consistency (relative ranking) and calibrate absolute aesthetic scores, reflecting the multifaceted nature of human aesthetic judgment.
4. **Improve Efficiency and Generalization of IAA Models:** By leveraging RL, the authors aim to develop an IAA model that is data-efficient, can be trained with limited supervision, and demonstrates robust generalization capabilities across various datasets, including out-of-distribution scenarios.
5. **Bridge the Gap Between Objective Metrics and Human-like Aesthetic Reasoning:** Ultimately, the project seeks to move beyond purely numerical aesthetic prediction towards a more holistic framework that combines quantitative evaluation with rich, qualitative critique, enabling MLLMs to "unlock the essence of beauty" in a more human-like manner.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The Aes-R1 framework is a comprehensive two-stage pipeline designed to achieve advanced aesthetic reasoning. It comprises two main innovations: the **AesCoT data construction pipeline** and the **Relative-Absolute Policy Optimization (RAPO)** algorithm.

#### 3.1. AesCoT: Aesthetic Reasoning Data Construction Pipeline

Recognizing the scarcity and cost of expert-annotated aesthetic reasoning data, the authors developed AesCoT as the first automatic pipeline for constructing high-quality aesthetic reasoning data. The process involves:
1. **Input:** Starting with original image-score pairs (e.g., from AVA, TAD66K, FLICKR-AES datasets).
2. **Prompting a Powerful MLLM:** The continuous aesthetic score is intentionally concealed from the powerful closed-source MLLM (specifically, GPT-4.1). Instead, the MLLM is prompted to provide a thoughtful aesthetic analysis across five predefined dimensions: Light and Shadow, Mood and Narrative, Composition, Color, and Exposure. These dimensions are chosen to cover core elements of visual aesthetics. The MLLM is guided to match the overall aesthetic level (bad, fair, good) associated with the hidden score.
3. **QA Pair Generation:** The generated aesthetic analysis (Chain-of-Thought critique) is then concatenated with the ground-truth score to form aesthetic reasoning pairs.
4. **Data Filtering:** A crucial step to ensure data quality. The generated outputs are rigorously filtered to eliminate:
 - **Score Leakage:** Instances where the critique indirectly reveals the hidden score.
 - **Reasoning Inconsistencies:** Cases where the logical steps in the critique conflict with the given aesthetic score.
 - **Factual Errors:** Descriptions of elements not present in the image or misinterpretations of visual features.
 This filtering is performed through automated checks (e.g., model judgments for inconsistencies) and nuanced human audits.
This pipeline results in the AesCoT dataset (released in 3K and 10K versions), which provides high-quality, interpretable multimodal aesthetic reasoning data, significantly reducing manual labor costs.

#### 3.2. Relative-Absolute Policy Optimization (RAPO)

RAPO is a novel reinforcement learning algorithm designed to optimize MLLM policies for IAA by addressing the subjective and multifaceted nature of human aesthetic judgment. It operates in two stages:

**Stage 1: Cold-Start Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)**
- Before applying RL, the MLLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct backbone) is first fine-tuned on the constructed AesCoT dataset for a single epoch.
- This "cold-start" phase is crucial to instill basic aesthetic cognitive behaviors in the MLLM, teaching it to generate structured explanations (the reasoning trajectory `c`) before producing the final aesthetic score `s`. This prevents the model from generating superficial explanations during the subsequent RL phase, which can lead to reward-hacking. The authors found that moderate SFT (e.g., 1 epoch) is optimal, as excessive SFT can reduce entropy and hinder RL's benefits.

**Stage 2: Reinforcement Learning with RAPO**
- After the SFT cold-start, the model undergoes reinforcement learning using RAPO. RAPO addresses the complexity of aesthetic evaluation by jointly optimizing two complementary facets of human judgment: relative preference and absolute magnitude.
- **Reward Function Design:** RAPO's total reward function (`r`) combines two components:
 - **Relative Rank Reward (`r_rank`):** This component encourages the model to correctly rank images based on their comparative appeal. It's based on the FRank method, which is bounded, continuous, and differentiable. For each image, it calculates a pairwise comparison probability against other images in the batch, aligning with ground-truth preference labels. This provides a stable signal for optimizing ordinal consistency.
 - **Absolute Error Reward (`r_abs`):** This component explicitly calibrates predicted scores toward the ground-truth Mean Opinion Scores (MOSs). It uses a continuous exponential decay function, where the reward decreases as the absolute difference between the predicted score and the ground truth increases. This ensures that the model's scores are not only ordered correctly but also accurately reflect the intrinsic aesthetic merit on an absolute scale.
- **Policy Optimization:** RAPO utilizes policy gradient methods, maximizing the expected cumulative reward. It adapts the DAPO (Distributed Advantage Policy Optimization) approach, using an advantage function calculated by normalizing rewards within a group of sampled outputs. This involves a higher clipping threshold and reduced KL penalty terms to foster exploration and stabilize training, aiming to maintain appropriate policy entropy.

By integrating these two stages and the dual reward system, Aes-R1 aims to achieve a balanced model that can both provide nuanced aesthetic critiques and assign accurate, well-calibrated aesthetic scores, aligning closely with human perception.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The extensive experiments conducted demonstrate the superior performance, robustness, and generalization capabilities of the Aes-R1 framework.

#### 4.1. Quantitative Results

- **State-of-the-Art Performance:** On a combined training set of 15K samples (from AVA, TAD66K, FLICKR-AES), Aes-R1 significantly outperforms all baseline methods, including vanilla MLLMs, hand-crafted methods, deep-learning based models, and other MLLM-based approaches. It achieves the highest average PLCC (Pearson Linear Correlation Coefficient) of 0.6337 and SRCC (Spearman Rank-order Correlation Coefficient) of 0.6186 across five benchmarks (TAD66K, AVA, FLICKR, PARA, AADB). This represents an improvement of over 0.2 in both metrics compared to the backbone model.
- **Superiority of RL-based Methods:** The results highlight the general superiority of efficient and scalable RL-based methods over SFT-based methods for IAA, particularly when combined with an appropriate reward structure.
- **Effectiveness of Relative-Absolute Reward Design:** The direct comparison with VisualQuality-R1 (which uses only rank reward) reinforces the rationale behind RAPO. VisualQuality-R1 shows high SRCC (0.5930), indicating strong relative ordering, but a much lower PLCC (0.4429), demonstrating its failure to calibrate absolute scores. Aes-R1, with its combined relative-absolute reward, achieves the closest alignment with ground-truth score distributions, featuring a well-matched peak and reduced tail deviation (Figure 1).
- **Robust Generalization (OOD Scenarios):** Aes-R1 exhibits strong out-of-distribution (OOD) performance on PARA and AADB datasets when trained on the combined dataset. Further validation in a single-dataset setting (trained on AVA only) shows that Aes-R1 achieves comparable performance to models like ArtiMuse, which are pretrained on large-scale human-annotated corpora. This indicates that RL with appropriate feedback can achieve strong cross-task generalization without requiring extensive manually annotated data.

#### 4.2. Ablation Studies

- **Reward Function Analysis:** Ablations on reward function design (binary, absolute error, relative rank) clearly validate RAPO's combined approach:
 - Binary reward performs poorly, as expected, due to its sparse signal.
 - Absolute error reward significantly outperforms binary, providing continuous and precise signals for score distribution.
 - Rank-only reward yields high SRCC but depressed PLCC, confirming its inability to calibrate absolute scores.
 - The Aes-R1 configuration (Error + Rank) consistently achieves the best average PLCC and SRCC, demonstrating the synergistic effect of combining both objectives for comprehensive aesthetic evaluation and improved OOD generalization.
- **Analysis of SFT and RL Interaction:** Experiments varying the SFT cold-start duration reveal critical insights:
 - Training RAPO without any SFT (Aes-R1-Zero) results in superficial explanations, despite reasonable scoring accuracy, highlighting the need for aesthetic prior.
 - Moderate SFT (1 epoch) is found to be optimal. It provides a strong prior, stabilizes output formats, and maximizes downstream performance, yielding the best overall results (0.6337/0.6186 PLCC/SRCC).
 - Excessive SFT (2 or 10 epochs) leads to reduced average token entropy, indicating overfitting and narrowed policy exploration, which ultimately limits the gains from RL and results in poorer generalization.
 - RAPO effectively maintains appropriate policy entropy during RL training, preventing entropy collapse and fostering stable exploration, which is crucial for maximizing performance gains.

#### 4.3. Qualitative Results (Case Study)

- A case study comparing Aes-R1 with other MLLMs on an image (chick emerging from an egg) clearly demonstrates Aes-R1's superior qualitative reasoning.
- Baseline models (GPT4.1, Qwen 2.5-7B-VL, Q-insight, VisualQuality-R1) consistently over-appraise the image, assigning inflated scores (0.82–0.91) and focusing only on positive attributes without critical assessment.
- In contrast, Aes-R1 assigns a score (0.59) closer to the ground truth (0.613) and provides a more balanced critique. It highlights both strengths (composition, lighting, color, technical execution) and weaknesses (lack of dynamic angle, stronger emotional narrative, or more dramatic lighting).
- This qualitative analysis underscores Aes-R1's ability to offer nuanced, multidimensional judgments and critique-aware explanations, a significant step beyond simple numerical predictions.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The Aes-R1 framework represents a significant advancement in the field of Image Aesthetic Assessment, with several key significances and potential impacts:

1. **Bridging Quantitative and Qualitative Aesthetic Understanding:** Aes-R1 successfully integrates numerical score prediction with interpretable, multi-dimensional aesthetic critiques. This "dual capability" moves MLLMs closer to human-like aesthetic reasoning, where judgments are often accompanied by explanations of *why* an image is beautiful or lacking. This is crucial for applications requiring not just a score, but actionable feedback.
2. **Enhancing Interpretability and Trust in AI-driven Aesthetics:** By generating explicit Chain-of-Thought rationales, Aes-R1 makes the aesthetic judgment process transparent. This interpretability can foster greater trust in AI systems for creative professionals (photographers, designers), content creators, and platform managers who rely on automated aesthetic evaluations.
3. **Efficient and Scalable Data Generation:** The AesCoT pipeline offers an automatic and cost-effective method for constructing high-quality aesthetic reasoning data. This addresses a major bottleneck in the field, as manually annotating expert-level critiques is prohibitively expensive. The release of AesCoT-3K and AesCoT-10K will likely accelerate future research in interpretable IAA.
4. **Robust and Data-Efficient Reinforcement Learning for Subjective Tasks:** The Relative-Absolute Policy Optimization (RAPO) algorithm provides a robust solution for applying RL to highly subjective tasks like IAA. Its innovative dual-reward mechanism (relative ranking and absolute error) effectively navigates the complexities of human preferences, leading to well-calibrated scores and consistent ranking, even with limited training data (only 15K samples).
5. **Strong Generalization Capabilities:** Aes-R1's demonstrated performance on out-of-distribution datasets and its ability to achieve strong cross-task generalization without massive pre-training on human-annotated corpora are highly impactful. This suggests that the framework can be reliably deployed in diverse, real-world scenarios where unseen image distributions are common.
6. **Foundation for Future Research and Applications:** Aes-R1's success provides a robust foundation for future research in several directions:
 - **Personalized Aesthetics:** Extending the framework to incorporate individual or cultural aesthetic preferences.
 - **Generative AI Feedback:** Providing sophisticated aesthetic feedback for image generation models, guiding them to produce more aesthetically pleasing outputs.
 - **Content Curation and Recommendation:** Improving automated content selection and recommendation systems in social media, e-commerce, and creative platforms.
 - **AI-powered Photography and Design Tools:** Integrating Aes-R1 into tools that offer real-time aesthetic advice or suggestions to users.

In conclusion, Aes-R1 pushes the boundaries of MLLM capabilities in aesthetic assessment. By enabling models to both quantify beauty and articulate its essence through reasoned critiques, it opens new avenues for AI to interact with and understand the nuanced world of human perception and artistic expression.
