# LLaDA-V: Large Language Diffusion Models with Visual Instruction Tuning

> [!summary] Summary
> LLaDA-V introduces a purely diffusion-based Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) capable of end-to-end training and sampling across modalities. It achieves competitive performance against and often surpasses autoregressive MLLMs like LLaMA3-V across various multimodal benchmarks, demonstrating enhanced data scalability in several key domains.

## Problem

- Most state-of-the-art Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are built upon autoregressive (AR) language models, limiting exploration of alternative probabilistic modeling approaches.
- Previous attempts at purely diffusion-based MLLMs suffered from limited language modeling capacity, performing suboptimally compared to AR-based counterparts.
- The potential of discrete language diffusion models, like LLaDA, to handle visual inputs and multimodal instruction following remained largely unexplored.

## Method

- LLaDA-V integrates a ==large language diffusion model (LLaDA-8B-Instruct)== with a ==vision tower (SigLIP 2)== via a two-layer MLP connector.
- It employs a ==masked diffusion process== for training, learning to predict masked tokens in responses given clean image features and prompts, differing from autoregressive next-token prediction.
- Inference uses an ==iterative refinement process== starting from a fully masked sequence, employing low-confidence re-masking, and is trained via a ==three-stage strategy== for language-image alignment, visual instruction tuning, and multimodal reasoning enhancement.

## Results

- LLaDA-V achieved state-of-the-art performance among existing hybrid and pure diffusion MLLMs across **18** diverse multimodal tasks.
- Outperformed LLaMA3-V on **11** of 18 tasks, particularly excelling in multidisciplinary knowledge, mathematical reasoning, multi-image, and video understanding benchmarks.
- Demonstrated superior data scalability on MMMU and MMMU-Pro, with LLaDA-V trained on **1M** samples outperforming LLaMA3-V with **9M** samples on MMMU-Pro.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Purely diffusion-based MLLMs can achieve competitive performance against strong autoregressive MLLMs, challenging the dominant paradigm.
> - LLaDA-V demonstrates superior data scalability in certain domains like multidisciplinary knowledge and mathematical reasoning compared to its autoregressive counterpart, LLaMA3-V.
> - Fully bidirectional attention across all tokens in the diffusion process proved more effective for multimodal understanding than a dialogue causal mask.
