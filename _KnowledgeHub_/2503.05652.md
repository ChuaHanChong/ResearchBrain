# BEHAVIOR Robot Suite: Streamlining Real-World Whole-Body Manipulation for Everyday Household Activities

> [!summary] Summary
> The BEHAVIOR ROBOT SUITE (BRS) is an integrated framework enabling real-world whole-body manipulation for everyday household activities using the Galaxea R1 robot. It combines a low-cost, intuitive teleoperation interface with a novel learning algorithm to achieve robust and safe performance on diverse tasks, often surpassing human teleoperation in contact-rich scenarios.

## Problem

- Household robotics lacked comprehensive systems for complex, real-world tasks requiring whole-body mobile manipulation, such as bimanual coordination and extensive reach.
- Collecting scalable, high-quality demonstration data for high-DoF mobile manipulators was challenging due to limitations of existing teleoperation interfaces, often leading to embodiment mismatch or poor user experience.
- Learning algorithms struggled to model coordinated whole-body actions for high-dimensional robots, resulting in amplified end-effector drift, out-of-distribution states, and safety violations.

## Method

![[2503.05652-method.png]]
*WB-VIMA architecture: egocentric colored point cloud and proprioception fuse via multi-modal visuomotor attention, then an autoregressive whole-body action decoder denoises base, torso, and arm actions hierarchically within the embodiment space.*

- Developed the **BEHAVIOR ROBOT SUITE (BRS)**, an integrated system built around the ==Galaxea R1== robot, a wheeled dual-arm manipulator with a ==4-DoF torso==, designed for extensive reach and bimanual capabilities.
- Introduced **JoyLo**, a cost-effective, ==puppeteering-based teleoperation== interface utilizing Nintendo Joy-Cons, offering intuitive, simultaneous whole-body control and haptic feedback for high-quality data collection.
- Proposed **WB-VIMA**, a transformer-based visuomotor policy that employs ==autoregressive whole-body action decoding== and ==multi-modal observation attention== to learn coordinated and safe actions from collected data.

## Results

- WB-VIMA achieved an average sub-task success rate of **88%** and an average entire-task success rate of **58%** across **five** challenging real-world household tasks, with **13×** and **21×** greater performance than DP3 and RGB-DP respectively and a near-zero safety violation rate.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both autoregressive whole-body action decoding and multi-modal observation attention are critical for WB-VIMA's performance, with their removal leading to significant degradation.
- JoyLo delivered superior data collection performance, achieving a **5×** higher task success rate and **23%** shorter median completion time in user studies vs. VR controllers, while also providing higher data quality (lowest singularity ratio).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Autoregressive decoding of whole-body actions, by predicting mobile base, torso, and arm movements hierarchically, is essential for mitigating error propagation and achieving coordinated movements in high-DoF mobile manipulators.
> - Dynamic fusion of multi-modal observations (colored point clouds and proprioception) via attention mechanisms allows visuomotor policies to extract salient features and maintain robust perception, preventing overfitting to single sensory inputs.
> - A direct-mapping, kinematic-twin teleoperation interface with haptic feedback (JoyLo) significantly enhances data quality and collection efficiency for complex mobile manipulation tasks, outperforming IK-based and VR methods.
