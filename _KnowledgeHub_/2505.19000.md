# VerIPO: Cultivating Long Reasoning in Video-LLMs via Verifier-Gudied Iterative Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> VerIPO cultivates deep, consistent, long-term reasoning in Video-LLMs by introducing a Verifier-guided Iterative Policy Optimization (VerIPO) algorithm. This approach uses a GRPO-Verifier-DPO training loop, leveraging a small LLM to dynamically curate high-quality preference data, resulting in improved accuracy and reasoning length on video reasoning benchmarks while reducing inconsistencies and repetitions.

## Problem

- High-quality, long Chain-of-Thought (Long-CoT) video datasets are scarce and costly, posing a data preparation bottleneck for Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT).
- Existing online RL methods for Video-LLMs often produce shallow, inconsistent, or repetitive reasoning chains and show unstable performance, failing to cultivate deep, long-form reasoning.
- Video-LLMs struggle to generate long, coherent, and contextually consistent step-by-step reasoning for complex video understanding tasks.

## Method

- Introduces ==VerIPO==, a Verifier-guided Iterative Policy Optimization algorithm, structured around a ==GRPO-Verifier-DPO training loop==.
- Utilizes a =="Rollout-Aware Verifier"== (a small LLM) to critically assess the quality, consistency, and repetition of online generated reasoning chains.
- The verifier dynamically constructs high-quality contrastive preference datasets, which are then used by ==DPO== for efficient, targeted policy refinement.

## Results

- VerIPO consistently outperforms strong baselines, including instruction-tuned LMMs and other RL-trained thinking models, on various video reasoning benchmarks, such as a **5.6%** improvement over Video-R1 on Video-MMMU.
- The Verifier-guided DPO and iterative training significantly enhance "Acc-True" (correct answers with consistent reasoning) and increase average response length, while effectively reducing contextual inconsistencies and repetitive outputs.
- An initial "Reasoning Activation" phase using text and image data proves more beneficial for generalizing to video tasks than cold-starting with video-specific Long-CoT data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - An iterative, verifier-guided reinforcement learning framework can effectively cultivate deep, long-term, and consistent reasoning in Video-LLMs without requiring large, pre-existing Long-CoT datasets.
> - Dynamically assessing and curating preference data from online model rollouts using a small LLM verifier is crucial for improving reasoning quality and addressing issues like inconsistency and repetition.
> - Combining the broad exploration capabilities of GRPO with the efficient, targeted refinement of DPO, guided by the verifier, leads to stable and superior performance gains in long reasoning.
