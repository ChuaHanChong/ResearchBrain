# WMNav: Integrating Vision-Language Models into World Models for Object Goal Navigation

> [!summary] Summary
> WMNav introduces a data-free knowledge distillation framework that leverages degradation-prompt diffusion models to synthesize domain-related degraded images for multi-weather image restoration, achieving efficient lightweight student models with performance comparable to data-present methods.

## Problem

- Zero-Shot Object Goal Navigation (ZSON) agents face costly and risky physical interactions with unknown environments, requiring extensive exploration.
- Existing VLM/LLM-based navigation methods often lack anticipatory planning, reacting to immediate observations rather than proactively planning based on predicted future states.
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) can suffer from hallucinations, providing unreliable information in complex planning tasks, necessitating robust mitigation strategies.

## Method

- **WMNav** integrates ==Vision-Language Models (VLMs)== into a world model framework, enabling agents to predict future states and outcomes of actions without direct physical interaction.
- A Curiosity Value Map (CVM) is introduced as a dynamic, quantitative memory strategy to store and update the likelihood of a target's presence, preventing redundant exploration.
- The navigation task is structured through Subtask Decomposition and a Two-stage Action Proposer, enhancing planning efficiency and mitigating VLM hallucination for more reliable action selection.

## Results

- WMNav achieved state-of-the-art performance in ZSON, with **58.1%** Success Rate (SR) and **31.2%** Success Rate Weighted by Inverse Path Length (SPL) on the HM3D benchmark, and **45.4%** SR and **17.2%** SPL on the MP3D benchmark.
- Ablation studies confirmed that Subtask Decomposition, the Curiosity Value Map, and the Two-stage Action Proposer each consistently improved both SR and SPL, highlighting their critical contributions.
- The framework benefits from larger and more advanced VLM backbones (e.g., Gemini **1.5** Pro) for higher performance, but remains robust with smaller proprietary and open-source models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - VLMs, pre-trained on vast human perspective images, possess sufficient common sense knowledge of indoor layouts and object relationships to function effectively as predictors within a world model for embodied tasks.
> - Anticipatory planning through a VLM-driven world model significantly reduces the need for constant, costly physical exploration, leading to more efficient and robust navigation.
> - Quantitative memory (Curiosity Value Map) combined with structured subtask planning helps alleviate VLM hallucination, forcing the VLM to generate more rigorous and reliable predictions.
