# Taming Diffusion Probabilistic Models for Character Control

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, the University of Hong Kong, and Tencent AI Lab developed a framework leveraging diffusion probabilistic models for real-time character control. Their Conditional Autoregressive Motion Diffusion Model (CAMDM) produces high-quality, diverse, and controllable character animations that respond seamlessly to user input at over 60 frames per second.

## Problem

- Traditional methods for real-time character animation struggled to produce diverse and natural-looking motions while maintaining precise control and computational efficiency.
- Adapting generative diffusion models for interactive character control faced challenges including high computational cost (requiring many denoising steps), difficulty in maintaining precise control, lack of motion diversity, and issues with smooth style transitions.
- Previous deep learning approaches for character control often lacked motion diversity and sometimes produced artifacts, while probabilistic models struggled with trajectory tracking and real-time performance.

## Method

- The authors introduce a ==Conditional Autoregressive Motion Diffusion Model (CAMDM)== that generates each animation frame sequentially based on past motion, user-specified style, future trajectory guidance, and facing direction.
- The model employs a transformer-based architecture with Separate Condition Tokenization (SCT) to process distinct conditioning information and a technique called Classifier-Free Guidance on Past Motion (CFG-PM) for smooth style transitions.
- A Heuristic Future Trajectory Extension (HFTE) technique is incorporated to improve motion diversity and ensure smooth trajectory following by intelligently extending predicted future paths.

## Results

- The system generates natural animations with fewer artifacts, superior to previous methods in motion quality, control precision, and diversity.
- Characters accurately follow user-specified trajectories and maintain proper orientation while smoothly transitioning between animation styles, even without explicit transition data.
- The approach achieves real-time performance at over 60 frames per second on consumer-grade hardware by using only 8 denoising diffusion steps during inference.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Separate Condition Tokenization (SCT) effectively balances the influence of diverse control signals, preventing any single feature from dominating the generation process.
> - Classifier-Free Guidance on Past Motion (CFG-PM) enables smooth and natural transitions between animation styles by dynamically reducing the influence of past motion during style changes.
> - Heuristic Future Trajectory Extension (HFTE) enhances motion diversity and smoothness by intelligently extending predicted trajectories, mitigating jitter and promoting natural movements.
