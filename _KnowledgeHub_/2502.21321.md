# LLM Post-Training: A Deep Dive into Reasoning Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This paper presents a comprehensive survey of post-training methodologies for Large Language Models (LLMs), focusing on techniques designed to enhance their reasoning capabilities, factual accuracy, and alignment. It systematically categorizes methods into fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and test-time scaling, providing a structured overview of their roles, challenges, and future directions in LLM optimization.

## Problem

- Pre-trained Large Language Models (LLMs) often struggle with factual accuracy, generating 'hallucinations' or logically inconsistent outputs.
- LLM reasoning is primarily based on statistical patterns rather than explicit logical inference, leading to limitations in complex reasoning tasks.
- Despite vast pre-training, LLMs may not fully align with human intent, preferences, or ethical considerations, requiring further refinement for reliable deployment.

## Method

- The paper systematically surveys and categorizes existing post-training methodologies for LLMs, focusing on their roles in enhancing reasoning capabilities.
- It organizes techniques into three primary, interconnected stages: ==fine-tuning== (e.g., instruction, CoT, PEFT), ==reinforcement learning== (e.g., RLHF, DPO, RLAIF), and ==test-time scaling== (e.g., CoT prompting, Tree-of-Thoughts, MCTS).
- The survey highlights the specific contributions of each technique in refining LLM behavior, improving robustness, and ensuring adaptability in various applications.

## Results

- The survey established a unified taxonomy categorizing LLM post-training methods into Fine-tuning, Reinforcement Learning, and Test-Time Scaling.
- It provided a detailed review of numerous algorithms and strategies within each category, tracing their evolution and application for improving LLM reasoning and alignment.
- The analysis identified emergent research trends, including increased RL applications, the rise of self-rewarding models, and the growing importance of compute-optimal scaling strategies at inference time.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Post-training techniques are crucial for refining LLMs beyond pre-training, addressing issues like factual accuracy, logical consistency, and alignment with human intent.
> - Fine-tuning, reinforcement learning (RL), and test-time scaling (TTS) are interconnected optimization strategies, each playing a distinct role in enhancing LLM performance and reasoning capabilities.
> - Key challenges in LLM post-training include catastrophic forgetting during fine-tuning, reward hacking in RL, and computational trade-offs associated with test-time scaling methods.
