# LLMs Improving LLMs: Agentic Discovery for Test-Time Scaling

> [!summary] Summary
> The AutoTTS framework automates the discovery of Test-Time Scaling (TTS) strategies for Large Language Models (LLMs) by reframing the design problem as an algorithmic search. Discovered strategies consistently achieve superior accuracy-cost trade-offs, reducing token consumption by up to 69.5% compared to handcrafted baselines while often improving peak accuracy and demonstrating robust generalization across various LLMs and benchmarks.

## Problem

- Test-Time Scaling (TTS) strategies for Large Language Models (LLMs) are conventionally developed through manual design and heuristic tuning.
- This reliance on human intuition restricts the exploration of complex computation allocation policies and is prone to human cognitive biases.
- Handcrafted methods often result in strategies that are specific to certain tasks or models, leading to sub-optimal accuracy-cost trade-offs across a broader spectrum.

## Method

- **AutoTTS** formulates ==Test-Time== Scaling (TTS) as a controller synthesis problem, where an explorer LLM iteratively proposes and refines code-defined policies.
- It utilizes an offline replay environment, pre-collecting reasoning trajectories to enable cheap, deterministic evaluation of candidate controllers without real-time LLM calls.
- The framework incorporates beta parameterization to simplify the search space for hyperparameters and provides fine-grained execution traces as feedback for robust discovery.

## Results

- AutoTTS discovered strategies consistently achieved a superior accuracy-cost Pareto frontier, outperforming strong handcrafted baselines across various LLM models and benchmarks.
- The discovered controllers significantly reduced token consumption by up to **69.5%** compared to baselines while maintaining or often improving peak accuracy.
- Strategies demonstrated robust generalization capabilities, transferring effectively to held-out benchmarks, different LLM families (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B), and diverse tasks (GPQA-Diamond).
- The discovery process itself was efficient, costing approximately $39.9 and 160 minutes, and yielded a sophisticated "Confidence Momentum Controller" with non-obvious mechanisms.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Automating TTS strategy discovery through agentic search can uncover more sophisticated and effective computation allocation mechanisms than manual design.
> - Decoupling LLM generation from controller evaluation via an offline replay environment is crucial for making the discovery process computationally affordable.
> - Constraining complex controller logic to a single scalar hyperparameter (beta parameterization) helps manage search complexity and prevents overfitting.
> - Providing fine-grained execution traces as feedback, beyond just scalar outcomes, significantly aids the explorer LLM in diagnosing and refining proposed strategies.
