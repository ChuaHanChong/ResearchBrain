# ChronoMagic-Bench: A Benchmark for Metamorphic Evaluation of Text-to-Time-lapse Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers introduced ChronoMagic-Bench and ChronoMagic-Pro, a specialized benchmark and large-scale dataset, respectively, to evaluate text-to-time-lapse video generation. This work also developed new metrics, MTScore and CHScore, demonstrating that existing T2V models generally struggle with generating videos depicting significant, long-duration physical changes but can improve with fine-tuning on the new dataset.

## Problem

- Existing Text-to-Video (T2V) evaluation primarily focuses on general video quality and text relevance, neglecting the unique challenges of 'time-lapse' or 'metamorphic' video generation.
- Current benchmarks and metrics are not designed to accurately quantify metamorphic amplitude (degree of change over time) or temporal coherence (logical progression) crucial for time-lapse videos.
- Large-scale T2V datasets predominantly contain general videos with limited metamorphic content and short durations, hindering models from learning and reproducing complex physical transformations.

## Method

- Proposed **ChronoMagic-Bench**, a new benchmark featuring 1,649 diverse prompts and reference videos categorized across physical, biological, human creation, and meteorological phenomena.
- Developed two novel automatic metrics: MTScore for metamorphic amplitude (including coarse-grained retrieval and fine-grained GPT-4o variants) and CHScore for temporal coherence, based on a video tracking model.
- Created ChronoMagic-Pro, the first large-scale dataset of 460K high-quality 720p time-lapse video-text pairs with detailed, time-aware captions, specifically curated for high physical content and metamorphic amplitude.

## Results

- Evaluation of **14 open-source** and **4 closed-source** T2V models on **ChronoMagic-Bench** revealed that models trained on general videos often produce limited metamorphic amplitude, generating general actions instead of significant state changes.
- Fine-tuning **OpenSoraPlan v1.1** on **ChronoMagic-Pro** using a 'Magic Training Strategy' resulted in improved performance across visual quality (UMT-FVD), text relevance (UMTScore), and metamorphic amplitude (MTScore, GPT4o-MTScore), validating the dataset's efficacy.
- Human preference studies confirmed the strong alignment of MTScore, CHScore, and GPT4o-MTScore with human judgments, while the UMTScore showed low correlation for text relevance in time-lapse videos.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Most contemporary T2V models, both open-source and closed-source, generally lack the ability to generate time-lapse videos with substantial and physically accurate changes over extended durations.
> - Models specifically trained with time-lapse videos (e.g., MagicTime, CogVideoX-2B) exhibit notably superior metamorphic amplitude compared to those trained solely on general video datasets.
> - The newly developed MTScore and CHScore metrics demonstrate strong consistency with human perception, providing reliable automated evaluation for metamorphic amplitude and temporal coherence.
