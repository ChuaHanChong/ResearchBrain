# Articulated Kinematics Distillation from Video Diffusion Models

> [!summary] Summary
> UCLA and NVIDIA researchers develop a framework that combines skeleton-based animation with video diffusion models to generate high-fidelity character animations from text prompts, achieving improved 3D consistency and physical plausibility through articulated kinematics distillation while maintaining compatibility with physics-based simulation systems.

## Problem

- Current text-to-4D generation methods struggle with shape distortion and physical accuracy due to high degrees of freedom in neural deformation fields
- Video diffusion models lack inherent 3D understanding, leading to inconsistencies in generated content

## Method

- Combines skeleton-based animation with video diffusion models using dual mesh-==3D Gaussian== representation
- Implements differentiable rendering and ==Score Distillation== Sampling to optimize joint angles based on video model guidance
- Incorporates non-uniform ground rendering and physics-based motion tracking for improved physical plausibility

## Results

- Outperforms existing methods like TC4D in both automated VideoPhy scores and human evaluations
- Generates more expressive motions including alternating leg movements and realistic gaits
- Successfully maintains geometric structure during deformations while enabling diverse motion generation
- Demonstrates compatibility with physics simulation environments for further motion refinement

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Skeleton-based representation reduces degrees of freedom while preserving structural consistency
> - Ground rendering cues help reduce foot-skating and ground penetration issues
> - Physics-based refinement enhances motion realism through trajectory optimization

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work sits at the intersection of several active research areas:

- **Text-to-3D Generation:** AKD builds upon existing text-to-3D methods for initial asset creation, which focus on generating static 3D models from text prompts.
- **Video Generation and Diffusion Models:** The core of AKD relies on pre-trained video diffusion models, which have shown remarkable progress in generating realistic and diverse videos from text descriptions. However, these models often lack inherent 3D understanding, leading to inconsistencies in generated content.
- **Text-to-4D (Dynamic 3D) Generation:** Recent research aims to leverage video generation models to create consistent 4D animations, where objects deform and move over time. Existing approaches often use neural deformation fields, which are flexible but can be difficult to optimize and maintain structural integrity.
- **Skeleton-Based Animation:** This is a traditional approach in computer graphics, offering precise control over character motion through articulated skeletons. However, it typically requires manual work from digital artists and struggles to capture the complexity of real-world dynamics.
- **Physics-Based Simulation:** This domain focuses on creating physically plausible motion by simulating interactions between objects and their environment. Integrating physics into generative models is a challenging but important direction for improving realism.

**How AKD Fits In:**

AKD addresses the limitations of current text-to-4D generation methods by combining the strengths of skeleton-based animation and video diffusion models. By using a skeleton-based representation, AKD reduces the degrees of freedom (DoFs), making optimization more efficient and preserving structural consistency. The video diffusion model provides diverse motion knowledge, while the skeleton ensures anatomical plausibility and compatibility with physics-based simulation. The work differentiates itself from other text-to-4D methods by specifically addressing problems with shape distortion and physical accuracy that are often seen in other works.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

- **Objective:** To develop a framework for generating high-fidelity character animations from text prompts by merging the strengths of skeleton-based animation and video diffusion models.
- **Motivation:**
 - **Streamline Animation Authoring:** Traditional 3D animation pipelines are time-consuming and require extensive manual work. Text-to-video models offer a promising avenue for automating this process.
 - **Overcome Limitations of Video Generation Models:** Existing video generation models struggle to generate high-fidelity dynamics for real-world objects, often failing to preserve 3D structure consistency and producing physically implausible motions.
 - **Address Challenges of Text-to-4D Generation:** Current text-to-4D methods based on neural deformation fields have a large number of DoFs, making optimization challenging and resulting in suboptimal quality.
 - **Bridge Generative Models and Skeleton-Based Animation:** The goal is to combine the knowledge of diverse motions from generative models with the low-DoF control, structural permanence, and physical grounding offered by skeleton-based animation.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The AKD framework consists of the following key components:

- **Rigged 3D Asset:** A static 3D model with an embedded articulated skeleton. The authors use an off-the-shelf text-to-3D generator, but assets from other sources can also be used.
- **Dual Mesh-3DGS Representation:** The asset is represented as both a mesh and a set of 3D Gaussian kernels. The mesh is used to compute skinning weights, while the 3D Gaussians support differentiable deformations and rendering.
- **Linear Blend Skinning (LBS):** This technique is used to deform the Gaussian kernels based on the transformations of the bones in the skeleton.
- **Differentiable Rendering:** The deformed Gaussian kernels are rendered into a video sequence using a differentiable rasterizer.
- **Video Diffusion Model:** A pre-trained video diffusion model (CogVideoX-5B) provides guidance for adjusting the joint angles of the skeleton to produce text-aligned motion.
- **Score Distillation Sampling (SDS):** This technique is used to distill motion knowledge from the video diffusion model into the skeleton animation. The SDS gradient is backpropagated through the rendering pipeline to optimize the joint angles.
- **Non-Uniform Ground Rendering:** AKD incorporates a checkerboard ground in the rendered images to provide the diffusion model with clues about the interaction between the asset and the ground, reducing foot-skating and ground penetration.
- **Optimization:** A loss function is used to optimize the joint angles, consisting of an SDS loss, a smoothness penalty, and a ground penetration penalty.
- **Physics-Based Motion Tracking:** The generated motion can be further refined by projecting it onto a physically plausible trajectory using a physics-based simulator. A PD controller applies torques to the joints to minimize the difference between the simulated and synthesized motion.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

- **Superior 3D Consistency:** AKD produces results with better 3D shape consistency compared to existing text-to-4D methods, such as TC4D.
- **More Expressive Motions:** AKD generates more expressive and diverse motions, including alternating leg movements and realistic gaits.
- **Enhanced Physical Plausibility:** The incorporation of non-uniform ground renderings and physics-based motion tracking enhances the physical plausibility of the generated animations.
- **Compatibility with Physics-Based Simulation:** The skeleton-based formulation allows the generated motion to be easily transferred into simulation environments.

**Quantitative Comparisons:**

The authors compare AKD with TC4D using both automated video scores (VideoPhy) and a user study.
- **Automated Metric:** VideoPhy scores demonstrate that AKD outperforms TC4D in both Semantic Adherence (SA) and Physical Commonsense (PC).
- **User Study:** Human evaluators preferred AKD in terms of Motion Amount, Physical Plausibility, and Text Alignment.

**Qualitative Comparisons:**

Visual comparisons show that AKD effectively maintains the geometric structure of each part within the assets during deformations, while TC4D often produces blurry artifacts and fails to generate alternating leg movements.

**Ablation Studies:**

Ablation studies demonstrate the importance of each component in the AKD framework, including ground rendering, ground penalty loss, and smoothness loss.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

- **Novel Approach to Text-Driven Animation:** AKD introduces a novel framework for animating 3D assets from text prompts, combining the strengths of skeleton-based animation and video diffusion models.
- **Improved Quality of Generated Animations:** AKD produces animations with better 3D consistency, more expressive motions, and enhanced physical plausibility compared to existing methods.
- **Potential for Automation and Scalability:** AKD offers an alternative to laborious motion authoring in traditional animation pipelines, potentially leading to increased automation and scalability.
- **Applications in Various Fields:** The generated animations can be used in various fields, including game development, film production, virtual reality, and robotics.
- **Inspiration for Future Research:** This work can inspire future research in generating labeled data for robotics applications.

**Limitations and Future Work:**

The authors acknowledge several limitations of AKD, including:

- **Suboptimal Visual Quality:** Improving the visual quality of the generated assets can help reduce the gap between rendered outputs and the realistic video distributions encoded in video models.
- **Dependence on Video Model's Ability:** The diversity of motions produced by AKD largely depends on the video model's ability to synthesize desired motions.
- **Focus on Articulated Motion:** AKD may not be suitable for other types of deformation, such as soft-body dynamics.
- **Manual Rigging:** The pipeline assumes a manually rigged skeleton, which can be time-consuming.

Future work can focus on addressing these limitations by:

- **Improving the Visual Quality of Generated Assets**
- **Leveraging Automatic Rigging Techniques:** Using techniques such as RigNet to generalize to diverse character types.
- **Exploring More Efficient Sampling Techniques:** accelerating the generation process.
- **Exploring Better Video Priors:** Improving the motion diversity that can be captured with more advanced video models.

**Overall Assessment:**

"Articulated Kinematics Distillation from Video Diffusion Models" presents a significant contribution to the field of text-to-4D generation. By combining skeleton-based animation with video diffusion models, AKD overcomes the limitations of existing methods and produces high-quality animations with improved 3D consistency, physical plausibility, and expressiveness. The work has the potential to streamline animation authoring and inspire future research in related areas.
