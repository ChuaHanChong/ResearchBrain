# SenseNova-U1.5: Towards Native Unified Visual Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> SenseNova-U1.5 is an 8B Mixture-of-Transformers native unified multimodal model that understands, reasons about and generates images without a vision encoder or VAE. It replaces patch-wise MLP decoding with a spatially coupled Pixel-Shuffle convolutional decoder enabling native 4K synthesis, and trains four specialized RL experts merged by on-policy velocity distillation, reaching leading open-source results across generation, text rendering, editing and interleaved benchmarks.

## Problem

- Most systems perceive images via pretrained vision encoders but generate via VAEs, splitting understanding and generation into separate representational spaces that hinder coordinated visual creation.
- SenseNova-U1's lightweight interface decoded each visual token as an independent RGB patch, producing seams, texture discontinuities and geometric inconsistencies that worsen at high resolution.
- Jointly optimizing aesthetics, typography, infographics and editing rewards inside one policy entangles competing objectives with different scales and rollout dynamics, diluting task-specific gains.

## Method

- A ==spatially coupled decoder== restores 2D topology from backbone states and upsamples through ==Pixel Shuffle== stages of 2, 2 and 8 with 3x3 convolutions, plus resolution-dependent noise-scale conditioning extended to 4096x4096.
- A unified objective couples autoregressive language modeling, ==pixel-space flow matching== with resolution-adaptive noise and x-prediction, and an ==LPIPS perceptual loss==, all inside one native ==Mixture-of-Transformers== backbone.
- ==Specialize-then-unify== post-training trains aesthetic, OCR, infographic and editing RL experts with tailored rewards, then hard-routes each capability into one student via ==on-policy velocity-field distillation==.

## Results

- Best open-source overall on GenEval at **0.92** and on Qwen-Image-Bench at **60.22** English and **60.13** Chinese with prompt enhancement, versus **48.28** and **45.99** for SenseNova-U1.
- Leads text rendering with CVTG-2K average **0.948** and LongText-Bench **0.988** English and **0.989** Chinese; BizGenEval hard-split average rises from **39.7** to **72.2** with prompt enhancement.
- Tops open models on editing and interleaved tasks: ImgEdit **4.59**, GEdit-Bench-EN **8.26**, WeEdit **7.46**, and VBVR-Pro-Bench **68.2** overall against Nano-Banana-Pro's **56.4**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Compact per-token visual compression stays viable if decoding restores local spatial interaction; artifacts often blamed on compression actually come from independent per-patch prediction.
> - Heterogeneous rewards are better routed to separate experts and consolidated by on-policy distillation than summed into one score, which preserves their distinct scales and semantics.
> - Structure learned through understanding transfers to creation: chain-of-thought lifts knowledge-heavy generation, yet helps spatial edits little, so reasoning pays mainly for implicit transformations.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The work on SenseNova-U1.5 is situated within the evolving field of multimodal artificial intelligence, specifically focusing on "native unified multimodal models." This paradigm aims to integrate visual understanding, reasoning, and generation within a single, end-to-end framework, departing from conventional architectures that typically employ separate pre-trained vision encoders (VEs) for perception and variational autoencoders (VAEs) for generation. Traditional systems operate in distinct representational spaces, which can limit the seamless coordination between perception, reasoning, and generation tasks.

SenseNova-U1.5 builds upon prior work such as SenseNova-U1 and NEO-unify, which demonstrated the feasibility of encoder-free and VAE-free architectures. These earlier models directly learned from pixels and words, unifying perception, reasoning, and pixel-space generation. However, SenseNova-U1 faced limitations, particularly in spatial coherence during high-resolution image formation, due to its patch-wise independent reconstruction of visual tokens.

The current research positions itself as an advancement in addressing these limitations, aiming to enhance the visual interface for improved spatial continuity and to refine post-training strategies for more diverse visual creation capabilities. It also relates to advancements in reinforcement learning (RL) for diffusion models and on-policy distillation (OPD) for consolidating specialized expert capabilities into a unified model, which are key components of its training methodology. By pursuing a "native unification" approach, the paper seeks to demonstrate that a compact, shared visual representation can efficiently support both visual perception and creation, fostering a synergistic relationship between different forms of visual intelligence.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of SenseNova-U1.5 is to develop an 8-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Transformers (8B-MoT) native unified multimodal model capable of understanding, reasoning about, and generating visual content within an encoder-free and VAE-free architecture. This overarching goal is motivated by several identified limitations of existing visual generation systems:

- **Architectural Divide and Representational Disparity:** Most contemporary systems use pre-trained vision encoders (e.g., CLIP, EVA-CLIP) for understanding and variational autoencoders (VAEs) for generation. This architectural separation means understanding operates in a semantic abstraction space, while generation occurs in a pixel-level fidelity space. This fundamental divide restricts the seamless integration and coordination of perception, reasoning, and generative tasks, leading to complex pipelines and potential inefficiencies.
- **Spatial Coherence in Native Models:** Previous native unified models, such as SenseNova-U1, demonstrated the feasibility of an encoder-free and VAE-free paradigm. However, their visual interface often reconstructed each visual token as an independent RGB patch. This factorization limited information exchange between neighboring patches during image formation, resulting in noticeable seams, texture discontinuities, and geometric inconsistencies, particularly at higher resolutions. A key motivation for SenseNova-U1.5 is to overcome this limitation by introducing spatially coherent reconstruction, enabling high-resolution generation up to 4K without such artifacts.
- **Optimization Challenges for Diverse Visual Tasks:** Visual creation encompasses a wide array of distinct capabilities, including aesthetic synthesis, bilingual text rendering, infographic design, and fine-grained image editing. Each of these tasks often requires specialized reward signals, rollout dynamics, and optimization strategies during post-training. Attempting to optimize them jointly within a single policy can lead to entangled or competing objectives, potentially diluting task-specific performance gains. This motivated a "specialize-then-unify" strategy in SenseNova-U1.5, where dedicated experts are first trained for specific capabilities and then their strengths are consolidated into a single unified policy.
- **Transfer of Multimodal Understanding to Visual Creation:** The research aims to explore whether structural knowledge and visual planning capabilities acquired through general multimodal understanding and reasoning can naturally transfer to complex visual creation tasks. This involves assessing the model's ability to generalize to long, compositional, and highly structured visual instructions, even with limited exposure to fixed generation templates during training.

In essence, SenseNova-U1.5 seeks to advance the concept of native unified modeling beyond architectural simplification to demonstrate how a compact, shared visual representation can efficiently support a broad spectrum of visual intelligence tasks, fostering a deeper interaction and reinforcement among understanding, reasoning, and creation.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

SenseNova-U1.5 employs a multi-faceted methodology, encompassing architectural refinements, a staged training procedure, and advanced post-training techniques for capability consolidation.

#### 3.1. Model Architecture

SenseNova-U1.5 retains an 8B-MoT (Mixture-of-Transformers) native unified multimodal architecture that is encoder-free and VAE-free.
- **Near-Lossless Visual Interface:** Images or noise-corrupted visual inputs are directly transformed into compact token sequences without external encoders. This involves two convolutional projections downsampling the input, yielding one visual token for each 32x32 image region. Sinusoidal positional embeddings are used, and special `<img>` and `</img>` tokens delimit visual blocks. Text is tokenized conventionally, and both modalities are projected into a shared hidden space.
- **Resolution-Aware Noise Conditioning:** To support high-resolution generation up to 4K (4096x4096), the model incorporates a resolution-dependent noise-scale embedding, `NSEmb(·)`, combined with the diffusion timestep representation. This provides explicit awareness of both diffusion time and resolution-specific noise statistics.
- **Spatially Coupled Decoder:** A significant architectural shift from SenseNova-U1 is replacing the patch-wise MLP output head with a lightweight spatially coupled decoder. Hidden states from the backbone are restored to their 2D topology and progressively reconstructed into a full-resolution RGB image using Pixel Shuffle stages and 3x3 convolutions. This allows neighboring token regions to exchange information, improving spatial continuity, reducing seams, and enhancing local fidelity.
- **Native Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT):** The model integrates understanding and generation within a single Transformer backbone. Clean image-text context and noise-conditioned visual states are interleaved. Text tokens follow causal attention, while image tokens (both clean and noise-conditioned generation tokens) within their blocks attend bidirectionally. Generation tokens also attend to all preceding clean multimodal context. Crucially, understanding and generation branches have separate attention projections, normalization layers, and feedforward modules, dynamically routed by token type, allowing specialized computation while maintaining a shared attention interface for cross-stream communication.

#### 3.2. Unified Training Objectives

SenseNova-U1.5 is trained with a unified objective combining three complementary signals:
- **Autoregressive Language Modeling (L_AR):** Optimizes the conditional likelihood of target text sequences, fostering semantic understanding and multimodal reasoning.
- **Pixel-Space Flow Matching (L_Flow):** Learns the continuous transformation from noise to clean images directly in RGB space. It uses a resolution-adaptive noise construction and supervises the predicted clean endpoint against the true clean image.
- **Perceptual Supervision (L_Perc):** Introduced via an LPIPS (Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity) loss to enhance structural consistency and local visual coherence beyond pixel-space fidelity.
The overall loss function `L` combines these components with weighting coefficients (λ_AR, λ_Flow, λ_Perc).

#### 3.3. Training Procedure

The training procedure is structured in multiple stages:
1. **Stage 1: Generation Pre-Training:**
 - Initializes the generation branch, training it with pixel-space flow matching while keeping the understanding branch frozen.
 - Starts with text-to-image data at resolutions 256x256 to 1024x1024.
 - Extends to higher-resolution (512x512 to 4096x4096) text-to-image data, enhancing 4K generation.
 - Introduces image-editing and interleaved-generation tasks, broadening generative capabilities, and incorporates the LPIPS perceptual loss.
2. **Stage 2: Unified Mid-Training:**
 - Jointly optimizes both understanding and generation branches, allowing cross-branch information exchange through shared attention.
 - Uses a mixed corpus including text-only, multimodal understanding, text-to-image, image-editing, and interleaved image-text data.
 - Loss weights are set to preserve understanding while emphasizing generative objectives.
3. **Stage 3: Unified Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):**
 - Further fine-tunes the model on a curated collection of high-quality instruction-following data, using a similar task composition and loss coefficients as Stage 2. This strengthens instruction adherence and consolidates capabilities.

#### 3.4. Post-Training: Specialize-Then-Unify Strategy

This stage is central to SenseNova-U1.5's methodology for addressing diverse visual tasks.
1. **Stage 4: Multi-Expert Reinforcement Learning:** Four specialized experts are trained independently for distinct capabilities, each with tailored data, rewards, sampling strategies, and regularization.
 - **Aesthetic Expert:** Interleaves aesthetic-preference (HPSv3++) and typography (bilingual OCR) data, routing to task-specific rewards to avoid mixing scales. Uses Coefficients-Preserving Sampling (CPS) for stochastic exploration and focuses optimization on early denoising stages.
 - **OCR Expert:** Specializes in accurate bilingual text rendering using a normalized multiset intersection-over-union (IoU) score from PaddleOCR. Employs Precise sampling and GRPO-Guard for stable optimization under sparse high OCR rewards.
 - **Editing Expert:** Balances instruction adherence, preservation of unedited content, and visual quality of edited regions. Utilizes a multi-dimensional VLM-based reward framework (instruction fulfillment, edit execution, overall visual quality, text-editing quality, unedited-region preservation) and a progressive sliding-window strategy for coarse-to-fine optimization.
 - **Infographic Expert:** Undergoes specific mid-training with real and synthetic infographic data, then refined in three stages: OCR reward for text, Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) for visual quality, and a final stage alternating OCR and aesthetic rewards with GRPO.
2. **Stage 5: Multi-Expert On-Policy Distillation (OPD):** Consolidates the four specialized experts into a single unified model using on-policy velocity-field distillation.
 - Each training sample is hard-routed to its corresponding frozen expert.
 - The student model generates its own trajectory, and both student and expert are evaluated at the same student-generated state, timestep, and condition.
 - A distillation loss `L_OPD` matches the student's velocity estimate to the expert's, with gradients stopped to prevent backpropagation through the full sampling trajectory.
 - The query distribution for timesteps is progressively shifted during training from early high-noise states (global structure) to late low-noise states (fine details, text fidelity).
 - Classifier-free guidance (CFG) is used for velocity optimization, with domain-specific guidance scales.
 - Specific modules (understanding branch, final generation layers, output head) are frozen to stabilize training.

#### 3.5. Data Construction

SenseNova-U1.5 significantly expands its training corpus, emphasizing diversity, quality, high-resolution content, complex instruction following, and fine-grained editing.
- **Image Generation Data:** Approximately 59M additional text-image pairs from 78 sources, with substantial fractions exceeding 1024x1024 (88.2%) and 2048x2048 (64.4%) resolutions. Captions are constructed at multiple granularities, and bilingual Chinese-English coverage is strengthened, especially for text-rich images. Targeted synthesis for rare concepts and complex layouts is also included.
- **Image Editing Data:** Approximately 38M examples covering general, infographic, reference-conditioned, and spatially controlled editing. Includes structured prompt enhancement and chain-of-thought examples for complex multi-target/multi-constraint edits.
- **Interleaved Data:** Majority from lifestyle-oriented trajectories (~44%), infographic data (~29%), video-derived sequences (~19%), and reasoning-intensive examples (~8%). All are converted to a unified trajectory format.
- **RL Training Data:** Specific datasets for each expert:
 - **Aesthetic:** 280K prompts (HPSv3++, Pick-a-Pic, Cosmos3PE, internal prompts).
 - **OCR:** 60K bilingual text-rendering prompts, including expanded short-text prompts.
 - **Infographic:** Reuses OCR corpus and 120K filtered preference pairs from Linear-DPO.
 - **Image Editing:** 120K examples (80% local, 20% global editing), spanning various edit types and resolutions, with multi-stage curation for quality and diversity.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

SenseNova-U1.5 was evaluated across a range of benchmarks for general understanding, image generation, image editing, and interleaved generation.

#### 4.1. General Understanding

- **Multimodal Understanding:** SenseNova-U1.5 demonstrated consistently strong performance across diverse multimodal benchmarks (MMMU, MathVista, MMBench, MMStar, InfoVQA, OCRBench-v2, AI2D, HallusionBench, BabyVision, TiR). It preserved or improved performance compared to SenseNova-U1, indicating that enhanced generative capabilities did not compromise visual understanding. It achieved competitive results with strong modular baselines like Qwen3-VL and compared favorably with other encoder-free models such as Gemma4-12B.
- **Language Understanding:** The model retained strong language understanding and instruction-following abilities, achieving high scores on MMLU-Pro (86.67), C-Eval (90.41), IFEval (93.35), and IFBench (69.00). This suggests that the introduction of visual generation and editing capabilities did not dilute its underlying linguistic competence.

#### 4.2. Image Generation

- **General Generation:**
 - **Qwen-Image-Bench:** SenseNova-U1.5 achieved the best overall performance among evaluated open-source models on both English (60.22 with prompt enhancement) and Chinese (60.13 with prompt enhancement) subsets, narrowing the gap with leading closed-source systems.
 - **GenEval:** It outperformed other open-source models (0.92 overall), with particular improvements in counting, position, and attribute binding, indicating enhanced compositional consistency.
 - **GenEval2:** With prompt enhancement, it substantially surpassed all open-source baselines, showing stronger compositional reasoning for complex constraints, especially in attribute, counting, and verb-related generation.
 - **OneIG-Bench:** Exhibited a balanced generation profile across English and Chinese, with strong bilingual text-centric generation, competitive alignment, reasoning, and style scores.
 - **DPG-Bench:** Achieved an overall score of 88.11, positioning it among top-performing open-source models with strong semantic grounding and compositional fidelity across various prompt components.
- **Text-centric Generation:**
 - **CVTG-2K:** Demonstrated leading overall performance (0.948 average), with strong gains in dense text rendering, maintaining word accuracy above 0.95 even in challenging multi-region settings.
 - **LongText-Bench:** Achieved leading open-source performance in both English and Chinese, showing robust accuracy as text length and layout complexity increased.
- **Complex Infographic Generation:**
 - **IGenBench:** Achieved the strongest open-source performance, which improved further with prompt enhancement, indicating more reliable infographic generation and structured visual planning.
 - **BizGenEval:** Substantially improved over SenseNova-U1, achieving leading open-source performance with prompt enhancement, especially in knowledge-intensive cases.
- **Reasoning-centric Generation:**
 - **WISE:** Performed competitively without chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning, and CoT yielded substantial improvements, establishing leading open-source performance, particularly in knowledge-intensive domains (cultural, biology, chemistry).

#### 4.3. Image Editing

- **General Editing:**
 - **ImgEdit:** Achieved leading overall performance across diverse edit types, with substantial gains over SenseNova-U1, indicating stronger edit execution, source-image preservation, and visual quality.
 - **GEdit-Bench:** Delivered the strongest overall performance, particularly in semantic consistency, reflecting reliable identification of intended edits and preservation of semantic context.
 - **WeEdit:** Substantially outperformed other open-source models and remained competitive with proprietary systems, demonstrating robust text-centric editing and improved coordination between semantic editing and visual reconstruction.
 - **OmniRef-Bench:** Achieved leading open-source performance in both objective and MLLM-based evaluations, with pronounced gains in style consistency, background preservation, subject, and pose consistency for multi-reference editing.
- **Reasoning-centric Editing:**
 - **RISEBench:** Outperformed evaluated open-source baselines without CoT, and explicit reasoning further improved performance significantly, especially for causal, logical, and temporal edits.

#### 4.4. Interleaved Generation

- **Interleaved Generation:**
 - **OpenING:** SenseNova-U1.5 with CoT achieved leading overall performance, demonstrating strong image-text coherency, human alignment, and multi-step consistency for open-ended interleaved generation.
 - **VBVR-Pro-Bench:** Achieved new state-of-the-art results across cognitive faculties, showcasing its ability to reason directly through interleaved text-image generation and generalize effectively to out-of-domain tasks.
- **Unified Reasoning:**
 - **Uni-MMMU-GaU and RealUnify-GEU:** Achieved leading performance on RealUnify-GEU and remained competitive on Uni-MMMU-GaU. These findings suggest that generation in SenseNova-U1.5 serves not only as an output modality but also as an intermediate representation supporting multimodal reasoning and understanding.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

SenseNova-U1.5 presents a substantial step toward the realization of native unified multimodal systems, wherein visual understanding and generation are integrated within a singular computational substrate. The extensive evaluations demonstrate that this direction can be scaled beyond basic image synthesis. The model's ability to simultaneously sustain strong understanding capabilities while supporting high-fidelity generation, complex editing, interleaved generation, and reasoning-intensive visual tasks within a compact native representation highlights the efficacy of its architectural and training innovations.

The work suggests that the primary advantage of multimodal unification lies not merely in architectural simplification but in the synergistic interaction of capabilities. The paper posits that reasoning can enhance the creation and transformation of visual content, while generation can become an integral component of the model's internal processes for solving visual problems. This approach blurs the traditional boundaries between perception and creation, indicating a trajectory towards foundation models where seeing, reasoning, and generating are not loosely coupled functions but rather emergent expressions of a shared visual intelligence.

The open-sourcing of training code, including supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and on-policy distillation, is expected to facilitate further research and development in this area, contributing to the broader scientific community's understanding and advancement of truly unified multimodal AI systems. This initiative suggests a future for multimodal models that aim not just to combine different modalities but to develop a coherent mechanism for interpreting, imagining, and interacting with visual information.
