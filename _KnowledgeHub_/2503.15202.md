# A Unified Framework for Real-Time Failure Handling in Robotics Using Vision-Language Models, Reactive Planner and Behavior Trees

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Lund University and ABB Robotics developed a unified framework that combines Vision-Language Models, reactive planning, and Behavior Trees to achieve real-time robotic failure handling through both proactive verification and dynamic execution monitoring. The system demonstrated a 100% task success rate on a physical ABB YuMi robot for various manipulation tasks, including peg-in-hole and object sorting, by effectively detecting, identifying, and correcting failures as they occur or before execution.

## Problem

- Robotic systems face frequent execution failures in dynamic environments due to unforeseen circumstances, limiting their autonomy and reliability.
- Traditional predefined failure recovery strategies lack adaptability to novel or unexpected failure modes.
- Modern learning-based methods often lack interpretability and verifiability, making them unsuitable for safety-critical robotic applications, and struggle with data requirements and generalization.

## Method

- A unified framework integrating ==Vision-Language Models== (VLMs) for context-aware reasoning, a reactive planner for dynamic Behavior Tree (BT) generation, and BTs for structured execution policies.
- Employs a three-phase failure handling process (detection, identification, correction) consistently across pre-execution verification and real-time execution monitoring.
- Leverages dynamically updated ==scene graphs== and execution history to maintain real-time situational awareness and enable adaptive BT modifications, including skill suggestions.

## Results

- The framework achieved a **100%** task success rate on the REFLECT benchmark in AI2-THOR simulations and across multiple real-world manipulation tasks with an ABB YuMi robot.
- Demonstrated **100%** success rates in failure detection, identification, and correction during real-world experiments, significantly outperforming pre-execution only approaches (**31.25%** task success).
- Proactive pre-execution checks reduced computational overhead and execution time compared to purely reactive methods by preventing foreseeable failures, while still enabling full real-time adaptability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A synergistic combination of pre-execution plan verification and real-time reactive failure monitoring is essential for robust and efficient robotic autonomy in dynamic environments.
> - Vision-Language Models (VLMs) effectively bridge high-level reasoning with low-level control, enabling accurate context-aware failure detection, identification of root causes, and suggestion of corrective actions.
> - Behavior Trees offer a modular, interpretable, and reactive policy representation that facilitates dynamic adaptation and incremental recovery, avoiding computationally expensive full replanning.
