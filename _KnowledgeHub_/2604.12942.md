# RMGS-SLAM: Real-time Multi-sensor Gaussian Splatting SLAM

> [!summary] Summary
> RMGS-SLAM presents a real-time multi-sensor (LiDAR-Inertial-Visual) SLAM system for generating photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splatting maps. It achieves the best overall localization accuracy (ATE RMSE) and superior rendering quality (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) across challenging large-scale outdoor datasets, maintaining global consistency through explicit loop closure and efficient mapping via cascaded Gaussian initialization.

## Problem

- Integrating computationally intensive 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) for photorealistic mapping into real-time SLAM while maintaining low-latency pose estimation.
- Achieving long-term global consistency and suppressing accumulated drift in large-scale 3DGS maps due to the lack of explicit loop closure mechanisms in existing systems.
- Improving the quality and accelerating the convergence of 3D Gaussian optimization by addressing suboptimal initialization strategies in current LiDAR-assisted methods.

## Method

- Developed a tightly coupled ==LiDAR-Inertial-Visual (LIV)== SLAM framework for ==non-blocking dense mapping==, executing pose estimation and Gaussian initialization in parallel with global map optimization.
- Introduced a **cascaded 3D Gaussian initialization** strategy that combines ==feed-forward model predictions== with ==voxel-PCA== derived geometric priors to improve primitive quality and accelerate optimization.
- Implemented a **Gaussian-based loop closure** method for large-scale scenes, utilizing ==Gaussian GICP registration== for loop constraint estimation and ==GTSAM-based pose graph optimization== for global consistency.

## Results

- **RMGS-SLAM** achieved the best overall localization accuracy (ATE RMSE) and superior rendering quality (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) across all tested sequences compared to existing 3DGS-based SLAM systems.
- Demonstrated near-real-time performance, with Real-time factors close to **1** on most sequences, successfully balancing computational demands for pose estimation, continuous mapping, and loop closure.
- Validated the effectiveness of cascaded Gaussian initialization via voxel-PCA and the Gaussian-based loop closure mechanism, which significantly improved rendering quality and global consistency in large-scale scenes.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Tightly integrating LiDAR, IMU, and camera data enables robust real-time ego-motion estimation and high-quality photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) reconstruction.
> - A cascaded Gaussian initialization strategy, leveraging both learned predictions and geometric priors from voxel-PCA, significantly improves the quality of initial 3D Gaussians and accelerates optimization convergence.
> - Explicit loop closure through Gaussian-based GICP registration and pose graph optimization effectively maintains long-term global consistency and reduces drift in large-scale 3DGS maps.
