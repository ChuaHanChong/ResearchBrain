# SaPaVe: Towards Active Perception and Manipulation in Vision-Language-Action Models for Robotics

> [!summary] Summary
> SaPaVe presents an end-to-end framework for robots to perform active manipulation by jointly learning semantic active perception and active-view execution with data efficiency. The framework, developed by researchers at Peking University and Beihang University, utilizes a decoupled training strategy and Universal Spatial Knowledge Injection, achieving superior success rates in real-world active manipulation tasks and robust generalization across various out-of-distribution scenarios.

## Problem

- Existing active perception methods often lack semantic understanding and fine-grained continuous camera control.
- Current Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are sensitive to viewpoint shifts, as they are often trained on fixed, near-optimal views, limiting their robustness in active-view execution.
- Augmenting VLA models with continuous camera movement is data-intensive and can lead to conflicts between learning camera control and manipulation due to a unified action space.

## Method

- A decoupled architecture with a lightweight ==Camera Adapter== (LoRA) for the VLM and separate action decoders for camera and manipulation actions.
- A two-stage, bottom-up training strategy: first, learning semantic ==active perception== using a large-scale dataset (ActiveViewPose-200K), then fine-tuning for active manipulation with hybrid data.
- Integration of Universal Spatial Knowledge Injection to enhance 3D geometric consistency and view-invariant understanding for action guidance.

## Results

- SaPaVe achieved an **84.3%** average success rate on semantic active perception tasks (ActiveViewPose-200K), outperforming generalist VLMs like Gemini-2.5-Pro (**72.7%**).
- In real-world active manipulation, SaPaVe demonstrated a superior average success rate of **85.00%**, significantly exceeding existing VLA models such as π0 (**45.00%**) and GR00T-N1 (**53.75%**).
- The framework exhibited robust generalization to out-of-distribution scenarios, maintaining high success rates (e.g., 85-**90%** for unseen objects in occluded pick-and-place tasks) in real-world settings.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Direct training on specialized data for semantic camera control (ActiveViewPose-200K) is more effective for precise active perception than relying on emergent properties of generalist VLMs.
> - Decoupling camera and manipulation action learning prevents interference and is crucial for robust active-view execution and data-efficient skill acquisition.
> - Integrating 3D geometric knowledge significantly enhances spatial awareness, improving robustness to viewpoint changes and occlusions in active manipulation tasks.
