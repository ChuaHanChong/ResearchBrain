# IBISAgent: Reinforcing Pixel-Level Visual Reasoning in MLLMs for Universal Biomedical Object Referring and Segmentation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Zhejiang University and Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory developed IBISAgent, an agentic Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) framework for universal biomedical object referring and segmentation. It reformulates segmentation as a multi-step Markov Decision Process, allowing an MLLM to iteratively refine masks by generating text-based click actions for an external segmentation tool, significantly outperforming existing MLLMs on medical benchmarks with an 85.58% IoU, 92.21% DSC, and 96.39% F1-score on an in-domain test set.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs struggle with fine-grained, pixel-level understanding and precise segmentation critical for clinical diagnosis despite strong image-level comprehension.
- Current MLLM-based segmentation methods face issues like catastrophic forgetting, limited generalization, and interference with natural language capabilities due to architectural modifications or implicit tokens.
- These methods often lack iterative refinement capabilities, relying on single-pass segmentation that is insufficient for complex biomedical image analysis.

## Method

- Reformulates biomedical segmentation as a vision-centric, ==multi-step Markov Decision Process==, enabling an MLLM to iteratively refine masks.
- Trains the MLLM to act as an agent, generating textual reasoning and ==text-based click actions== that invoke an external interactive segmentation tool for mask updates.
- Employs a two-stage training strategy: ==cold-start supervised fine-tuning== using automatically generated trajectories, followed by agentic reinforcement learning with fine-grained, rule-based rewards.

## Results

- IBISAgent achieved state-of-the-art performance, with an **85.58%** IoU, **92.21%** DSC, and **96.39%** F1-score on an in-domain test set, significantly surpassing other MLLMs for biomedical segmentation.
- Qualitative analysis revealed that the model generates correct, coherent textual reasoning and precisely grounded, high-quality masks through adaptive, step-by-step refinement.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical contributions of the agentic formulation, the two-stage training protocol (SFT and RL), and the novel fine-grained reward signals to the overall performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - An agentic, multi-step reasoning framework allows MLLMs to achieve genuine pixel-level visual understanding and adaptive action planning for segmentation.
> - Decoupling the MLLM from architectural modifications for segmentation preserves its inherent language capabilities and prevents catastrophic forgetting.
> - The use of specific, fine-grained rewards during reinforcement learning is essential for fostering robust decision-making and efficient iterative mask refinement.
