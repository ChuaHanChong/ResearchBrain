# MedLoc-R1: Performance-Aware Curriculum Reward Scheduling for GRPO-Based Medical Visual Grounding

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and Emory University developed MedLoc-R1, a framework that introduces a performance-aware curriculum reward scheduling mechanism for Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)-based medical visual grounding. This approach effectively addresses reward sparsity, leading to superior localization accuracy and enhanced training stability across diverse medical imaging datasets.

## Problem

- Medical images present challenges like low signal-to-noise, blurred boundaries, and small lesions, making precise visual grounding difficult.
- Existing GRPO-based visual grounding methods use fixed Intersection-over-Union (IoU) thresholds, causing severe reward sparsity and training stagnation due to vanishing policy gradients.
- Conventional curriculum learning strategies, focused on data reordering, are not directly applicable to reinforcement learning where task difficulty is governed by reward design.

## Method

- **MedLoc-R1** dynamically adjusts the ==IoU reward== criterion within GRPO using a performance-aware curriculum reward scheduling mechanism.
- A sliding-window tracks recent training dynamics through mean reward, reward standard deviation, and IoU margin to assess the model's current readiness.
- A composite update criterion, combined with adaptive step-size decay strategies, progressively increases the IoU threshold only when sufficient accuracy, stability, and capability are demonstrated.

## Results

- MedLoc-R1 consistently surpassed all baselines across HAM10000, HEEL, and TN3K datasets, particularly showing increased accuracy at stricter A@**0.8** thresholds (e.g., +43.18 A@**0.8** on HEEL over VLM-R1).
- The framework demonstrated faster convergence and more stable performance growth, scaling effectively across Qwen2.5-VL model variants (**3B**, **7B**, **32B**).
- Qualitative results showed MedLoc-R1 produced more accurate bounding boxes and generated coherent, semantically rich reasoning traces, enhancing interpretability in medical contexts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Dynamically adapting the reward criterion based on the model's performance effectively mitigates reward sparsity and stabilizes GRPO training in medical visual grounding.
> - A multi-faceted performance tracking system (mean reward, standard deviation, IoU margin) provides robust signals for intelligently scheduling difficulty progression.
> - Integrating curriculum learning directly into the reward design of reinforcement learning frameworks improves fine-grained localization and interpretability without adding model complexity.
