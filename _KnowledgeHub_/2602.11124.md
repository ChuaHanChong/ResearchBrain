# PhyCritic: Multimodal Critic Models for Physical AI

> [!summary] Summary
> PhyCritic introduces a multimodal critic model trained with a two-stage Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) pipeline, leveraging self-referential finetuning to enhance its ability to evaluate and reason about physical AI tasks. The model achieves 68.0% accuracy on the new PhyCritic-Bench for physical judgment, outperforming existing open-source models by over 12 percentage points, and also improves the base model's physical reasoning capabilities.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal critic models lack sufficient "physics awareness" to accurately evaluate responses in physical AI tasks, often failing to distinguish physically plausible from impossible reasoning.
- Current Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with nuanced physical correctness, causal validity, and adherence to environmental constraints in complex, interactive scenarios.
- A general framework for multimodal critics optimized for physical AI was missing, leading to unreliable evaluations for embodied AI systems.

## Method

- A two-stage Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) pipeline is proposed, starting with a 'Physical Skill Warmup' to build foundational perception and reasoning, followed by 'Self-Referential Critic Finetuning'.
- During finetuning, the critic model is explicitly instructed to first generate its own internal reasoning and prediction for a given problem before evaluating candidate responses, grounding its judgment on this self-prediction.
- A new benchmark, **PhyCritic**-Bench, was introduced for rigorous evaluation, alongside a high-quality critic training dataset derived from diverse robotics and embodied datasets.

## Results

- PhyCritic-7B achieved state-of-the-art open-source performance on the PhyCritic-Bench with **68.0%** accuracy, surpassing baselines like Eagle-2.5-8B by 12.0 percentage points.
- The model demonstrated improved physical reasoning as a policy, achieving **63.9%** accuracy on CosmosReason1-Bench and strong performance on CV-Bench (**79.7%** average, **83.9%** 3D score) and EgoPlanBench2 (**42.3%**).
- PhyCritic also generalized effectively to general-domain judging, outperforming its Qwen2.5-VL base model by +4.1 and +1.9 points on VL-RewardBench and Multimodal-RewardBench, respectively, while being highly data-efficient (4,058 training samples).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A self-referential learning framework, where a critic first solves the problem itself before judging, significantly enhances the accuracy and physical consistency of multimodal evaluations.
> - The two-stage RLVR pipeline effectively separates foundational skill acquisition from critic judgment training, leading to complementary improvements in physical reasoning and evaluation capabilities.
> - Training a model to be a physics-aware critic also improves its performance as a policy for solving physical tasks, demonstrating a synergistic relationship between evaluation and problem-solving skills.
