# Spatial-MLLM: Boosting MLLM Capabilities in Visual-based Spatial Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> Tsinghua University researchers develop Spatial-MLLM, a framework that enhances multimodal large language models' spatial reasoning from 2D videos by combining a standard visual encoder with a spatial encoder initialized from a visual geometry foundation model (VGGT), achieving state-of-the-art performance on VSI-Bench and substantially outperforming existing video MLLMs on ScanQA and SQA3D through a dual-encoder architecture, space-aware frame sampling strategy, and reinforcement learning training on their new Spatial-MLLM-120k dataset covering diverse spatial understanding tasks.

## Problem

- Existing video MLLMs rely on CLIP-based visual encoders trained on image-text pairs, which capture semantic content but lack spatial awareness needed for 3D reasoning
- Current 3D MLLMs require additional 3D or 2.5D data (point clouds, depth maps) that limit their applicability in scenarios with only 2D video available
- MLLMs struggle with visual-based spatial intelligence tasks including object counting, distance estimation, and spatial relationship understanding from monocular video

## Method

- **Spatial-MLLM** ==dual-encoder architecture== combining a 2D visual encoder for semantic features with a spatial encoder initialized from ==VGGT visual geometry foundation model== for 3D structural information
- ==Space-aware frame sampling strategy== that reformulates frame selection as a ==maximum coverage problem== over ==voxel grids== to identify spatially informative frames under input length constraints
- Two-stage training pipeline with ==SFT== on ==Spatial-MLLM-120k dataset== followed by ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== reinforcement learning for enhanced ==chain-of-thought spatial reasoning==

## Results

- Achieves state-of-the-art performance on VSI-Bench, surpassing both proprietary and open-source MLLMs including models with significantly larger parameter counts
- Substantially outperforms video-input models on ScanQA and SQA3D benchmarks, even exceeding some task-specific models and approaches using 3D/2.5D input data
- Ablation studies confirm effectiveness of dual-encoder architecture, space-aware frame sampling, and GRPO training components for spatial reasoning enhancement

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Visual geometry foundation models pretrained on pixel-point pairs can effectively recover implicit 3D structural information from 2D video inputs when properly integrated with semantic encoders
> - Frame sampling based on spatial informativeness rather than temporal uniformity significantly improves performance by focusing computational resources on geometrically relevant content
> - Reinforcement learning with cold-start initialization and task-dependent reward modeling enhances long-chain spatial reasoning capabilities beyond supervised fine-tuning alone
