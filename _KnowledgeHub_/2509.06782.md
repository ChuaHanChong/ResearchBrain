# Physics-informed Value Learner for Offline Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Purdue University developed Physics-informed Eikonal-regularized Hierarchical Implicit Q-Learning (Eik-HIQL), which integrates a novel Eikonal PDE-based regularizer into Goal-Conditioned Value Function (GCVF) estimation for offline reinforcement learning. This framework significantly improves GCVF accuracy and generalization by encouraging a distance-like cost-to-go structure, outperforming state-of-the-art baselines on navigation and locomotion tasks within the challenging OGbench.

## Problem

- Existing Offline GCRL algorithms struggle to accurately estimate Goal-Conditioned Value Functions (GCVFs) from limited and potentially biased offline datasets, leading to suboptimal policies.
- Learned GCVFs often exhibit poor generalization to unseen state-goal pairs, particularly in long-horizon tasks, which limits real-world applicability.
- Many current methods lack structural priors, making them susceptible to data limitations and yielding less robust or interpretable value functions.

## Method

- Introduced a novel ==Eikonal PDE==-based regularizer, inspired by the Eikonal equation, into the GCVF estimation loss of the Hierarchical Implicit Q-Learning (HIQL) framework.
- The regularizer encourages the GCVF to approximate a distance field by penalizing deviations from a unit gradient norm with respect to the state, effectively promoting a 1-Lipschitz continuous value function.
- Designed as a model-free approach, the Eikonal regularizer avoids the need for explicit system dynamics and potential numerical instabilities associated with model-based methods.

## Results

- Eik-HIQL consistently outperformed its non-regularized counterpart (HIQL) and other state-of-the-art baselines, showing performance improvements exceeding **100%** in 7 out of 31 tasks on the OGbench, particularly in large navigation and locomotion scenarios.
- The method generated GCVFs that more accurately reflected the underlying maze geometry, leading to improved goal-directed policy performance compared to baselines.
- Eik-HIQL showed limited advantages in interactive, contact-rich domains such as antsoccer and manipulation, suggesting the Eikonal regularizer's smoothness assumptions are less applicable where dynamics are discontinuous.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Physics-informed regularization, specifically derived from the Eikonal PDE, can effectively inject a geometric inductive bias into GCVF estimation, significantly enhancing its accuracy and generalization.
> - A simple constant speed profile (S(s)=1) within the Eikonal regularizer is highly effective, outperforming more complex, obstacle-aware profiles by promoting a consistent 1-Lipschitz GCVF without introducing unnecessary complexity.
> - The Eikonal regularizer demonstrates broad compatibility with various temporal-difference-based GCRL algorithms, indicating its potential as a general plug-in enhancement for offline value learning.
