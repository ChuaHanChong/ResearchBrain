# OpenHOI: Open-World Hand-Object Interaction Synthesis with Multimodal Large Language Model

> [!summary] Summary
> OpenHOI introduces a framework for generating physically plausible 3D hand-object interaction sequences for unseen objects and open-vocabulary instructions. It integrates a 3D Multimodal Large Language Model for instruction understanding and affordance reasoning with an affordance-driven diffusion model and a training-free physical refinement stage, achieving state-of-the-art performance across various metrics.

## Problem

- Existing 3D hand-object interaction (HOI) synthesis methods struggle with generalization to 'unseen' objects and are limited to closed datasets.
- Prior approaches often fail to respond to free-form, open-vocabulary language commands and generate long-horizon, multi-stage manipulation sequences coherently.
- Many generated HOI sequences lack physical plausibility, exhibiting interpenetration or unnatural motion, and existing 3D MLLMs mainly focus on perception, not dynamic interaction synthesis.

## Method

- A two-stage framework: (1) Instruction Decomposition and ==Affordance Reasoning== using a fine-tuned 3D MLLM (ShapeLLM backbone) to generate spatial affordance maps and temporal sub-task sequences.
- (2) Affordance-driven HOI Diffusion for motion synthesis, conditioned on MLLM outputs and object point clouds, employing a transformer-based denoising diffusion model.
- A training-free physical refinement strategy applied during diffusion sampling, utilizing spherical Gaussian constraints to minimize hand-object penetration, ensure precise affordance contact, and maintain smooth motion transitions for long-horizon tasks.

## Results

- OpenHOI achieved superior quantitative performance over state-of-the-art methods across GRAB and ARCTIC datasets, showing lower MPJPE, FOL, and FID, particularly on unseen object splits (e.g., MPJPE of **51.34** vs. Text2HOI's **60.67** on unseen GRAB).
- The framework demonstrates strong generalization capabilities, synthesizing realistic and coherent long-horizon HOI sequences for completely unseen objects and complex, open-vocabulary instructions.
- Ablation studies confirmed the necessity of each core component, including affordance awareness, classifier-free guidance, and physical/motion in-between refinement, for achieving robust, physically plausible, and temporally coherent interactions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining 3D MLLMs for semantic understanding and affordance reasoning with diffusion models for motion synthesis effectively bridges 3D perception and dynamic interaction generation in an open-world setting.
> - Explicit affordance grounding from an MLLM, refined through a coarse-to-fine tuning strategy, is critical for achieving semantically meaningful and physically plausible hand-object interactions with novel objects.
> - Training-free physical refinement, guided by task-specific losses during diffusion sampling, provides a robust mechanism to enhance physical realism and temporal coherence without introducing distribution shifts or requiring re-training.
