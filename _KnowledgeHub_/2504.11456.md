# DeepMath-103K: A Large-Scale, Challenging, Decontaminated, and Verifiable Mathematical Dataset for Advancing Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> DeepMath-103K is a meticulously curated mathematical dataset containing 103,000 challenging, decontaminated, and verifiable problems, designed to advance reasoning in Large Language Models. Models trained with this dataset achieve state-of-the-art performance on mathematical benchmarks and demonstrate improved generalizable reasoning across various scientific domains.

## Problem

- Existing mathematical datasets often lack sufficient difficulty to push the reasoning capabilities of advanced LLMs, plateauing at moderate problem levels.
- Widespread data contamination from evaluation benchmarks within training datasets leads to unreliable performance metrics and an overestimation of model capabilities.
- Many current datasets lack verifiable answers, which is crucial for the effective application of rule-based reward functions in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR).
- A significant portion of existing datasets suffer from limited novelty and diversity, recycling problems from common sources and hindering the development of truly generalizable reasoning.

## Method

- The **DeepMath-103K** dataset was constructed with ==103,000 mathematical problems==, focusing on difficulty levels 5-9, primarily sourced from less structured platforms like Math StackExchange to ensure novelty.
- A rigorous decontamination pipeline was implemented using embedding similarity search and an LLM-Judge to remove any overlaps with 17 comprehensive mathematical and STEM benchmarks.
- To ensure verifiability for RLVR, a two-stage process involved GPT-4o for question filtering and standardization, followed by generating three DeepSeek-R1 solutions per problem and verifying consistency with a rule-based checker.

## Results

- DeepMath series models achieved state-of-the-art pass@1 accuracy, with DeepMath-Zero-Math-**7B** improving by +12.7 on AIME24 and +12.1 on AIME25 compared to strong baselines.
- DeepMath-Omn-**1.5B** established new SOTA performance for **1.5B**-scale models, reaching 64.0 on AIME24 and 57.3 on AIME25, outperforming larger proprietary models like o1-mini.
- Models trained on DeepMath-**103K** consistently outperformed baselines on the GPQA-Diamond benchmark, demonstrating enhanced reasoning generalization across biology, physics, and chemistry.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality, challenging, and decontaminated mathematical data is critical for advancing LLM reasoning beyond current limitations, enabling state-of-the-art performance and robust evaluation.
> - Rigorous data curation, particularly in preventing benchmark contamination and ensuring answer verifiability, is essential for reliable reinforcement learning and trustworthy model assessment.
> - Training on diverse and complex mathematical problems fosters generalizable reasoning skills that extend beyond mathematics to other scientific domains, as evidenced by performance on interdisciplinary STEM benchmarks.
