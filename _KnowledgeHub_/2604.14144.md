# SpatialEvo: Self-Evolving Spatial Intelligence via Deterministic Geometric Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Zhejiang University and StepFun developed SpatialEvo, a self-evolving framework for 3D spatial reasoning that leverages deterministic geometric environments to provide objective, noise-free ground truth. This approach enables visual-language models to continuously improve their spatial understanding without relying on human annotations or biased pseudo-labels, achieving superior performance on spatial reasoning benchmarks while retaining general visual capabilities.

## Problem

- Developing large-scale spatial reasoning capabilities for visual-language models (VLMs) is hindered by the high cost and static nature of geometric annotations.
- Existing self-evolving methods for reasoning tasks typically construct biased pseudo-labels from model predictions, which can reinforce errors rather than correct them.
- There is a need for a scalable paradigm that can continuously adapt to a model's evolving weaknesses and generate more challenging examples without proportional increases in human effort.

## Method

- Introduces **SpatialEvo**, a self-evolving framework that uses a ==Deterministic Geometric Environment (DGE)== to programmatically compute exact, noise-free ground truth for **16** categories of spatial reasoning tasks directly from unannotated 3D scene assets.
- Implements ==Spatially-Grounded Policy Co-Evolution==, where a single VLM acts as both a ==Questioner== (generating physically valid spatial questions) and a ==Solver== (deriving precise answers against DGE-verified ground truth).
- Incorporates an ==adaptive curriculum learning scheduler== that dynamically adjusts the training distribution to concentrate resources on the model's weakest task categories, fostering an endogenous curriculum.

## Results

- **SpatialEvo** achieved the highest average scores on spatial reasoning benchmarks, scoring **51.1** (3B) and **54.7** (7B), outperforming baseline methods that use static data tuning or self-supervised RL.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical role of the Deterministic Geometric Environment (DGE) and its physical grounding, with replacing DGE ground truth with majority-voting pseudo-labels causing the largest degradation (**5.1-point** average drop).
- The framework maintained competitive performance on general visual understanding benchmarks (e.g., **MMStar**, **RealWorldQA**), demonstrating that specialized spatial training does not degrade broader cognitive capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Ground truth for 3D spatial reasoning is a deterministic consequence of underlying geometry, allowing objective, noise-free supervision directly from the environment, unlike many other reasoning domains.
> - A shared-parameter VLM can effectively co-evolve by taking on dual roles of Questioner and Solver, with the DGE providing objective physical feedback to correct hallucinations and probe cognitive boundaries.
> - Dynamically adjusting training focus to areas of model weakness through an adaptive scheduler leads to more efficient and targeted learning, enabling an emergent curriculum without manual design.
