# SketchAgent: Language-Driven Sequential Sketch Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> SketchAgent introduces a method for language-driven, sequential sketch generation that leverages off-the-shelf multimodal large language models without additional training. The system generates sketches stroke-by-stroke, enabling real-time interactive collaboration and achieving human-like drawing aesthetics with high recognition rates.

## Problem

- Traditional sketch generation methods are limited by training data, lack generalizability, and often produce static or non-sequential output.
- Existing vision-language model (VLM)-based approaches are slow, generate sketches in a single step (lacking interpretable intermediates), and often produce mechanical-looking vector graphics.
- Multimodal Large Language Models (LLMs) struggle with fine-grained spatial reasoning, which is crucial for creating and editing visual content like sketches.

## Method

- Leverages off-the-shelf, frozen multimodal LLMs (e.g., Claude3.5-Sonnet) to generate sequential sketches, without requiring additional training or fine-tuning.
- Introduces a ==structured 50x50 grid-based canvas== and a precise =="sketching language"== (specifying points and t-values) to enhance the LLM's spatial reasoning abilities.
- Employs a ==cubic Bezier curve fitting process== to convert LLM-specified points into smooth, freehand-like strokes, ensuring a natural, human-like appearance.
- Uses a comprehensive prompting strategy, including a system prompt, ==Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning==, and ==in-context learning (ICL) examples==, to guide the LLM's sequential generation and support interactive editing.

## Results

- Achieved a Top-1 CLIP accuracy of **0.23** and Top-5 accuracy of **0.44** on 50 QuickDraw categories, approaching human-level recognition rates (human sketches **0.27** Top-1, **0.49** Top-5).
- User studies showed SketchAgent's drawings were perceived as more human-like (**74.90%** preference over direct prompting) and achieved **45.32%** human-likeness when compared to actual human drawings.
- Generated sketches in approximately **20 seconds**, enabling real-time interactive editing with **92%** accuracy for instructions and effective human-agent collaborative sketching with high recognizability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - It is possible to achieve language-driven, sequential sketch generation using off-the-shelf multimodal LLMs without any specific training or fine-tuning, by carefully structuring prompts and outputs.
> - Augmenting LLMs with a structured "sketching language" and a grid-based canvas can significantly improve their spatial reasoning for visual creation tasks.
> - The stroke-by-stroke generation process of SketchAgent allows for meaningful intermediate steps, real-time interaction, and effective human-AI collaborative sketching, mimicking human creative workflows.
