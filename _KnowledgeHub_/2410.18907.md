# SkillMimicGen: Automated Demonstration Generation for Efficient Skill Learning and Deployment

> [!summary] Summary
> SkillMimicGen, developed by NVIDIA, is an automated data generation system that expands a small set of human demonstrations into large, diverse datasets for robot manipulation. It significantly improves data generation success rates by separating and adapting skills and motions, enabling training of robust policies that show strong sim-to-real transfer capabilities, achieving up to 95% success on complex tasks in simulation and successful real-world execution.

## Problem

- Collecting sufficient high-quality human demonstration data for robot manipulation is time-consuming, expensive, and requires specialized equipment, leading to data scarcity.
- Previous automated data generation methods, such as MimicGen, struggled to produce high-quality, collision-free demonstrations for complex tasks and to handle significant scene variations or obstacles.
- The bottleneck of data acquisition limits the development of robust and generalizable manipulation skills for robots across diverse environments.

## Method

- SkillMimicGen proposes a novel automated data generation system that segments human demonstrations into discrete "skill" (contact-rich manipulation) and "motion" (free-space movement) segments.
- It adapts skill segments to new contexts using geometric transformations and employs motion planning to generate collision-free paths for motion segments connecting adapted skills.
- A trial-and-error generation loop within simulation executes and validates the synthesized trajectories, only saving successful demonstrations to build a large and diverse training dataset.

## Results

- SkillMimicGen achieved significantly higher success rates in generating valid demonstrations compared to previous approaches (e.g., 65% vs. 14% for the Coffee task, 92% vs. 72% for Nut Assembly in simulation).
- Policies trained on SkillMimicGen-generated data scaled effectively with dataset size, achieving up to 95% success rates on complex manipulation tasks with 5000 generated demonstrations.
- The system demonstrated successful zero-shot sim-to-real transfer, enabling policies trained entirely in simulation to perform complex tasks like Nut Assembly and kitchen tasks on real-world robots.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing robot manipulation tasks into distinct "skill" (contact-rich) and "motion" (free-space) segments allows for specialized and more effective adaptation and planning for each part.
> - Combining learned policies for complex, contact-rich skills with traditional motion planning for collision-free navigation between skills (Hybrid Skill Policy) leverages the complementary strengths of both approaches.
> - Automated generation of training data through a validated simulation loop ensures that only high-quality, successful demonstrations are included, which is crucial for training robust robotic policies.
