# FlowPolicy: Enabling Fast and Robust 3D Flow-based Policy via Consistency Flow Matching for Robot Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> FlowPolicy introduces a novel framework for robot manipulation that employs conditional consistency flow matching with 3D visual representations, enabling real-time policy generation. The method achieves a 7x speedup in inference time over state-of-the-art 3D diffusion policies while maintaining or improving success rates across 37 robotic tasks.

## Problem

- Diffusion models, despite their performance in complex robotic trajectory generation, suffer from slow, multi-step inference, making them unsuitable for real-time robot manipulation.
- Existing 2D-based imitation learning methods often lack the rich spatial understanding required for precise 3D manipulation tasks.
- The inherent efficiency-quality trade-off in advanced visuomotor policies limits their practical deployment in real-world robotic applications.

## Method

- **FlowPolicy** integrates conditional consistency flow matching into a 3D visual representation backbone, adapting the architectural strengths of 3D ==Diffusion Policy (DP3)==.
- It leverages 3D point clouds, derived from single-view depth observations, as robust visual conditional inputs for the policy, providing essential spatial information.
- The framework employs a two-segment Consistency Flow Matching approach, which enables one-step policy generation by directly predicting the target action trajectory from noise, bypassing iterative sampling.

## Results

- FlowPolicy achieved an average inference time of 19.9ms per step (NFE=1), demonstrating a more than 7-fold speedup compared to the state-of-the-art 3D Diffusion Policy (DP3) which took 145.7ms.
- The method obtained an average success rate of **70.0%** across 37 Adroit and Metaworld tasks, outperforming or matching DP3 (**68.7%**) and significantly surpassing 2D-based baselines.
- FlowPolicy exhibited superior learning stability, faster convergence, and better robustness to varying numbers of expert demonstrations compared to diffusion-based policies, particularly breaking through performance bottlenecks on hard tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Conditional consistency flow matching can effectively model complex, high-dimensional action distributions for 3D robot manipulation with a single-step inference process.
> - Combining the efficiency of consistency flow matching with robust 3D visual representations (point clouds) successfully resolves the critical efficiency-quality trade-off in visuomotor policies.
> - The 'two-segment' consistency flow matching strategy enhances the quality and flexibility of action generation for robotic tasks without requiring additional distillation steps common in other consistency-based models.
