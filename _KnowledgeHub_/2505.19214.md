# Omni-Perception: Omnidirectional Collision Avoidance for Legged Locomotion in Dynamic Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) developed Omni-Perception, an end-to-end reinforcement learning framework enabling legged robots to perform omnidirectional collision avoidance by directly processing raw, spatio-temporal LiDAR data. The system achieved 70% success avoiding aerial obstacles and 90% for moving humans in real-world tests, significantly outperforming a native robot system, and incorporates a custom high-fidelity LiDAR simulator and a novel hierarchical perception network.

## Problem

- Legged robots often lack robust spatial awareness and rapid reactive collision avoidance in complex, dynamic 3D environments due to reliance on proprioceptive feedback or limited depth-camera-based perception.
- Existing exteroceptive methods (e.g., depth cameras) are prone to lighting sensitivity, narrow fields of view, noise, and struggle with complex 3D obstacles like overhangs or aerial clutter.
- Direct integration of raw LiDAR point clouds into end-to-end learning policies for legged robots has been largely unexplored due to data dimensionality, computational demands, and lack of high-fidelity, scalable LiDAR simulations.

## Method

- An end-to-end reinforcement learning policy leverages raw, spatio-temporal LiDAR point clouds for robust omnidirectional collision avoidance and velocity tracking in dynamic 3D environments.
- A custom, high-fidelity, GPU-accelerated LiDAR simulation toolkit, based on ==NVIDIA Warp== and Taichi, enables scalable and realistic training with support for diverse LiDAR models and efficient dynamic object rendering.
- The Proximal-Distal Risk-Aware Hierarchical Network (PD-RiskNet) processes point clouds by partitioning them into proximal (near-field) and distal (far-field) subsets, each processed by a dedicated GRU, to extract risk-aware spatial embeddings.

## Results

- The custom LiDAR simulator accurately reproduced real-world Livox Mid-360 scan patterns and outperformed Isaac Sim in efficiency, supporting 4096 environments with 16k rays at 220 ms rendering time where Isaac Sim ran out of memory.
- An ablation study showed PD-RiskNet achieved a **76.7%** success rate in dynamic obstacle avoidance simulation, significantly outperforming simpler point cloud processing methods (e.g., FPS + GRU at **30%** success).
- Real-world deployment on a Unitree Go2 robot demonstrated **70%** success in avoiding aerial obstacles and **90%** for moving humans, compared to **0%** success for the native Unitree system in both categories.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Directly processing raw, spatio-temporal LiDAR data within an end-to-end RL framework significantly enhances a legged robot's spatial awareness and reactive agility for omnidirectional collision avoidance.
> - A hierarchical perception architecture, such as PD-RiskNet, is effective for efficiently interpreting high-dimensional LiDAR data by separately analyzing near-field and far-field information for risk assessment.
> - Developing a custom, highly optimized LiDAR simulation environment with realistic noise modeling and efficient parallel raycasting is critical for scalable training and successful sim-to-real transfer for complex LiDAR-based robotic tasks.
