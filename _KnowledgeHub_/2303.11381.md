# MM-REACT: Prompting ChatGPT for Multimodal Reasoning and Action

> [!summary] Summary
> MM-REACT, a framework developed by Microsoft Azure AI, enables large language models like ChatGPT to understand and reason about visual content by integrating specialized vision models through a prompting mechanism. This approach allows the system to perform complex visual reasoning tasks by decomposing them into a series of steps, all without requiring extensive multimodal training. The system demonstrates capabilities in multi-hop reasoning, multi-image analysis, spatial understanding, and video summarization.

## Problem

- Developing artificial intelligence systems that can comprehensively understand and reason across different types of information, such as images, text, and video, remains challenging.
- Traditional vision-language models often require extensive, costly joint training on vast multimodal datasets.
- Existing models frequently struggle with compositional reasoning that requires multiple sequential steps of analysis and information integration.

## Method

- Microsoft Azure AI developed MM-REACT, a framework that integrates specialized vision models with large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT through a ==prompting mechanism==.
- The framework operates on a ==thought-action-observation loop== where the LLM decides which vision 'expert' to call, processes the textualized output from the expert, and iteratively refines its understanding.
- Visual inputs are represented as file paths in prompts, allowing the LLM to reference images and videos without directly processing visual data, while expert outputs are converted into text for LLM comprehension.

## Results

- The system successfully performs multi-hop reasoning by chaining vision experts for tasks like celebrity identification and information retrieval, and demonstrates multi-image reasoning for comparative analysis of documents (e.g., receipt analysis).
- It exhibits spatial understanding by reasoning about the relative positions of objects within images and solves visual mathematics problems presented in images.
- MM-REACT provides video summarization into step-by-step instructions with timestamps and identifies open-world concepts by combining visual recognition with external knowledge, achieving **comparable performance to PaLM-E** on certain tasks without requiring joint training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A modular architecture allows complex visual tasks to be decomposed and solved by specialized vision models.
> - Prompt engineering effectively instructs the large language model (LLM) on how to utilize various vision experts and interpret their textualized outputs.
> - The system supports multi-round reasoning, enabling iterative calls to vision experts and multi-hop reasoning chains based on intermediate results.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

#### 1.1. Introduction

Multimodal understanding—the ability to comprehend and reason across different types of information like images, text, and video—remains a significant challenge in artificial intelligence. Researchers from Microsoft Azure AI have developed MM-REACT (Multimodal Reasoning and Action), a novel framework that enables large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT to understand and reason about visual content without requiring extensive multimodal training.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

#### 2.1. Departing from Joint Multimodal Training

Unlike traditional vision-language models (VLMs) that require joint training on vast multimodal datasets, MM-REACT leverages ChatGPT's reasoning capabilities and combines them with specialized vision models through a prompting mechanism. This approach allows the system to perform complex visual reasoning tasks by decomposing them into a series of steps that involve calling appropriate vision "experts" as needed.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

#### 3.1. Key Innovations

MM-REACT introduces several key innovations that distinguish it from previous approaches:

1. **Modular Architecture**: Rather than building a monolithic multimodal model, MM-REACT decomposes complex tasks into subproblems that can be solved by specialized vision models.

2. **Prompt Engineering**: The system uses carefully designed prompts that instruct ChatGPT about the capabilities of various vision experts and how to invoke them.

3. **File Path Mechanism**: Visual inputs are represented as file paths in prompts, allowing ChatGPT to reference images and videos without needing to process visual data directly.

4. **Textualized Observations**: Outputs from vision models are converted into textual descriptions that ChatGPT can understand and reason about.

5. **Multi-round Reasoning**: The system enables iterative reasoning where ChatGPT can call multiple vision experts in sequence based on intermediate results.

The core innovation lies in transforming ChatGPT into a reasoning engine that can dynamically decide when and which specialized vision models to use, creating an adaptable system for multimodal understanding.

#### 3.2. System Architecture

MM-REACT's architecture follows a thought-action-observation loop, as illustrated in the system diagram:

The process begins when a user provides input (typically including an image or video) to the system. The workflow then follows these steps:

1. **Input Representation**: Images are represented as file paths (e.g., `<ImagePath>`) in the prompt given to ChatGPT.

2. **Thought Process**: ChatGPT internally formulates its understanding of the task and determines what visual information it needs.

3. **Action Request**: ChatGPT decides to invoke a specific vision expert by generating text that includes watchwords (e.g., "Assistant, what objects do you see in this image?").

4. **Action Execution**: The system parses ChatGPT's output using regular expressions to identify which vision expert to call and with which input.

5. **Observation**: The vision expert processes the image and returns results in a textual format.

6. **Response Generation**: ChatGPT combines the observation with its understanding to provide a response or decide if additional vision experts need to be called.

This cycle can repeat multiple times within a single interaction, allowing for complex reasoning chains across different visual aspects.

The implementation uses LangChain, a framework for developing applications powered by language models, which facilitates the integration of external tools with LLMs.

#### 3.3. Vision Experts and Integration

MM-REACT integrates various specialized vision models through a uniform interface. The system includes experts for:

1. **Image Captioning**: Generates general descriptions of images
2. **Dense Captioning**: Identifies and describes objects with their spatial locations
3. **Object Tagging**: Lists objects present in the image
4. **Face Detection**: Locates and provides coordinates of faces
5. **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)**: Extracts text from images
6. **Celebrity Recognition**: Identifies well-known individuals
7. **Bing Search**: Retrieves additional information from the web

Each expert is accessed through a consistent API, making it easy to add new capabilities to the system. The vision experts are implemented using Azure Cognitive Services APIs, which provide state-of-the-art performance for specific vision tasks.

A key aspect of the integration is the representation of vision experts in the prompt. Each expert is described to ChatGPT in terms of:
- What information it can extract
- When it should be called
- The format of its output
- Example usage patterns

This instruction-based approach allows ChatGPT to make informed decisions about which experts to use for different types of visual queries.

#### 3.5. Multi-hop Reasoning

The system can perform multi-hop reasoning by chaining together multiple vision experts. For example, when answering questions about celebrities in an image, MM-REACT might:

1. First use image captioning to understand the general content
2. Then use face detection to locate faces
3. Next employ celebrity recognition to identify individuals
4. Finally use Bing Search to retrieve specific information about the individuals

#### 3.6. Multi-image Reasoning

MM-REACT can reason across multiple images, enabling comparative analysis. This is particularly useful for tasks like receipt analysis or tracking changes over time:

For instance, when asked about travel expenses across multiple receipts, the system can:
1. Use OCR to extract text from each receipt

#### 3.7. Spatial Understanding

The system demonstrates spatial reasoning capabilities by understanding the relative positions of objects within images:

```
User: Where is the frisbee?
      What is the relative position of the frisbee and the man?

MM-REACT: The frisbee is located at coordinates (63,113,133,177).
          The frisbee is located above the man in the image. The man is falling on the
          ground while the frisbee is in the air. The frisbee is also closer to the
          top of the image than the man.
```

This is achieved by leveraging dense captioning, which provides bounding box coordinates for detected objects, allowing ChatGPT to reason about their spatial relationships.

#### 4.2. Visual Mathematics and Reasoning

```

MM-REACT excels at analyzing documents spread across multiple images, such as comparing vehicle fueling costs:

```
User: Excluding the regular cab, what is the average total fueling cost?

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

#### 5.1. Comparison with Other Approaches

MM-REACT represents an alternative approach to multimodal understanding compared to several existing methods:

#### 5.2. End-to-End Multimodal Models

Traditional vision-language models like CLIP, DALL-E, and more recent models like PaLM-E are trained end-to-end on paired image-text data. While powerful, these models:
- Require extensive multimodal training
- Need retraining to incorporate new capabilities
- Often struggle with compositional reasoning across multiple steps

In contrast, MM-REACT:
- Requires no joint training
- Can easily incorporate new vision models
- Excels at multi-step reasoning through its modular design

The authors demonstrate that MM-REACT can achieve comparable performance to PaLM-E on certain tasks despite not requiring joint training:

#### 5.3. Related Frameworks

MM-REACT builds upon related work like:

1. **Visual ChatGPT**: Focuses primarily on image generation and editing
2. **ViperGPT**: Uses LLMs to generate Python code for visual reasoning
3. **ReAct**: Integrates reasoning and action taking for language tasks

MM-REACT distinguishes itself through:
- Greater focus on visual understanding rather than generation
- Multi-round, dialogue-based reasoning
- Seamless integration of multiple vision experts

#### 5.4. Limitations and Future Work

Despite its impressive capabilities, MM-REACT has several limitations:

1. **Evaluation Challenges**: The authors note the difficulty of systematically evaluating the system due to the lack of benchmarks for complex visual reasoning tasks.

2. **Dependence on Vision Expert Performance**: MM-REACT's accuracy is bounded by the performance of the underlying vision models. If a vision expert makes an error, this will propagate through the reasoning chain.

3. **Context Window Limitations**: The size of ChatGPT's context window limits how many vision experts can be included in the prompt and how much visual information can be processed at once.

4. **Suboptimal Visual Representation**: Converting visual information to text may not be the most efficient approach for all types of visual reasoning.

5. **Manual Prompt Engineering**: The system requires carefully designed prompts, which requires expertise and limits flexibility.

Future work could address these limitations by:
- Developing standardized benchmarks for multimodal reasoning
- Integrating more advanced vision models
- Exploring more efficient ways to represent visual information to LLMs
- Automating the prompt engineering process
- Extending the framework to more modalities (audio, 3D, etc.)

#### 5.5. Conclusion

MM-REACT represents a significant advancement in multimodal AI by demonstrating that complex visual reasoning capabilities can be achieved without end-to-end multimodal training. By leveraging the reasoning abilities of ChatGPT and integrating specialized vision models through prompting, the system offers a flexible, modular approach to visual understanding that can be easily extended with new capabilities.

The framework's ability to break down complex tasks into a series of simpler steps—each handled by an appropriate vision expert—mirrors human reasoning processes and enables sophisticated visual analysis across diverse applications. From analyzing receipts to identifying objects, summarizing videos, and performing spatial reasoning, MM-REACT demonstrates robust capabilities across a wide range of visual tasks.

As LLMs continue to advance and specialized vision models improve, the MM-REACT approach offers a promising path toward more capable and adaptable multimodal AI systems that can understand and reason about the visual world in increasingly sophisticated ways.
