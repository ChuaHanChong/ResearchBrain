# MM-REACT: Prompting ChatGPT for Multimodal Reasoning and Action

> [!summary] Summary
> MM-REACT, a framework developed by Microsoft Azure AI, enables large language models like ChatGPT to understand and reason about visual content by integrating specialized vision models through a prompting mechanism. This approach allows the system to perform complex visual reasoning tasks by decomposing them into a series of steps, all without requiring extensive multimodal training. The system demonstrates capabilities in multi-hop reasoning, multi-image analysis, spatial understanding, and video summarization.

## Problem

- Developing artificial intelligence systems that can comprehensively understand and reason across different types of information, such as images, text, and video, remains challenging.
- Traditional vision-language models often require extensive, costly joint training on vast multimodal datasets.
- Existing models frequently struggle with compositional reasoning that requires multiple sequential steps of analysis and information integration.

## Method

- Microsoft Azure AI developed MM-REACT, a framework that integrates specialized vision models with large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT through a ==prompting mechanism==.
- The framework operates on a ==thought-action-observation loop== where the LLM decides which vision 'expert' to call, processes the textualized output from the expert, and iteratively refines its understanding.
- Visual inputs are represented as file paths in prompts, allowing the LLM to reference images and videos without directly processing visual data, while expert outputs are converted into text for LLM comprehension.

## Results

- The system successfully performs multi-hop reasoning by chaining vision experts for tasks like celebrity identification and information retrieval, and demonstrates multi-image reasoning for comparative analysis of documents (e.g., receipt analysis).
- It exhibits spatial understanding by reasoning about the relative positions of objects within images and solves visual mathematics problems presented in images.
- MM-REACT provides video summarization into step-by-step instructions with timestamps and identifies open-world concepts by combining visual recognition with external knowledge, achieving **comparable performance to PaLM-E** on certain tasks without requiring joint training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A modular architecture allows complex visual tasks to be decomposed and solved by specialized vision models.
> - Prompt engineering effectively instructs the large language model (LLM) on how to utilize various vision experts and interpret their textualized outputs.
> - The system supports multi-round reasoning, enabling iterative calls to vision experts and multi-hop reasoning chains based on intermediate results.
