# PhysMaster: Mastering Physical Representation for Video Generation via Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from The University of Hong Kong and Kuaishou Technology developed PHYSMASTER, a framework that enhances video generation models by teaching them to adhere to physical laws. It learns implicit physical representations from input images via a specialized PhysEncoder, optimized through a three-stage reinforcement learning pipeline using Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), resulting in more physically plausible and efficient video generation than existing methods.

## Problem

- Modern video generation models frequently fail to adhere to fundamental physical laws, leading to visually implausible object behaviors in generated content.
- Current generative models struggle to effectively integrate physical knowledge because standard loss functions prioritize visual fidelity over physical correctness, and there is no direct way to supervise physical representations.
- Existing physics-aware approaches are either limited to specific simulated physical categories or face issues with the scalability and accuracy of feedback mechanisms like expensive human annotation.

## Method

- Introduces **PhysEncoder**, a specialized module designed to extract ==implicit physical representations== directly from an input image, leveraging a ==DINOv2 encoder== and a ==trainable physical head==.
- **PhysMaster** utilizes a top-down optimization strategy based on ==RLHF==, specifically ==Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)==, to refine PhysEncoder's ability based on the physical plausibility of the final generated videos.
- Employs a ==three-stage training pipeline== that first fine-tunes the base ==Image-to-Video (I2V)== diffusion model and PhysEncoder, then applies DPO to the I2V model, and finally uses the refined I2V model's feedback to optimize PhysEncoder's physical head.

## Results

- **PhysMaster** achieves **state-of-the-art** performance on a 'free-fall' proxy task, showing superior trajectory accuracy (competitive **L2/Chamfer Distance**) and significantly higher **IoU** for shape consistency compared to specialized rigid-body models.
- It outperforms general video generation and specialized physics-focused models on diverse open-world physical scenarios, improving both semantic adherence and physical commonsense scores while being up to **70×** faster than previous physics-aware methods.
- Human preference studies confirm that videos generated by the final PhysMaster model are **significantly preferred** over baselines in terms of adherence to physical laws across various real-world scenarios.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Learning an implicit physical representation from an initial frame, rather than relying on explicit simulations, can serve as an effective guiding signal for generative models to achieve physics-awareness.
> - A multi-stage DPO approach, sequentially optimizing the generative model and then the physical encoder, is crucial for effectively learning robust physical cues and representations.
> - The PhysEncoder demonstrates the ability to acquire a deeper understanding of physical properties, as evidenced by feature clustering that differentiates objects based on external forces and material properties.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of video generation has witnessed rapid advancements, with models increasingly capable of producing visually stunning and realistic videos (Brooks et al., 2024; Kuaishou, 2024; Yang et al., 2024c). However, a significant limitation persists: these models frequently fail to adhere to fundamental physical laws. While they excel at sophisticated "pixel prediction" and "appearance fitting" based on training data, they often struggle with common-sense physics, leading to videos where objects might deform implausibly, interact incorrectly, or defy gravity (Kang et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025a; Meng et al., 2025). This lack of physical plausibility severely curtails their potential to evolve from mere content creators into sophisticated "world models" that genuinely comprehend and simulate physical principles.

The current research landscape faces two primary challenges in achieving physics-aware video generation. First, the standard Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss, commonly used for training generative models, prioritizes visual fidelity over physical correctness, making it difficult to directly supervise physical performance. Second, models struggle to logically extract relevant physical knowledge from input text or images and translate it into effective guidance for generation.

Existing approaches to physics-aware video generation can be broadly categorized:
- **Simulation-based methods** (Lv et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024b) attempt to integrate physics-based simulation results to guide video generation. While these methods can produce accurate physical dynamics within their simulated domains, they are often constrained to a limited range of simulable physical processes and modalities. This specificity limits their generalizability to diverse and complex real-world phenomena.
- **Simulation-free methods** (Xue et al., 2024; Furuta et al., 2024) rely either on extensive post-training on physics-rich datasets to implicitly learn physical priors or on reinforcement learning (RL) frameworks that align generated videos with human preferences. The former approach is highly dependent on matching similar training samples and struggles with novel scenarios, while the latter, often employing Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), faces challenges with expensive human annotation (due to rater variability) or scalable but often inaccurate AI evaluators. These methods, in essence, find it hard to truly abstract and understand the underlying physics of the world, hindering generalization.

PhysMaster positions itself as a novel, generalizable solution within this landscape. Instead of relying on explicit simulations or solely on appearance-driven supervision, it proposes to learn a **physical representation** from the input image. This representation acts as a crucial "bridge" to guide generative models towards physics awareness. By adopting a top-down optimization strategy using RLHF, PhysMaster aims to overcome the limitations of prior methods by enabling the model to effectively capture implicit physical cues and inject them into the video generation process, thereby facilitating a more comprehensive and generalizable understanding of physical laws. The "plug-in" nature of PhysMaster further enhances its utility, allowing existing video generation models to gain physics-awareness without extensive architectural changes.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to **enhance the physical plausibility and realism of videos generated by modern video generation models**. The authors aim to transition these models from being merely visually impressive "content creators" to more sophisticated "world simulators" that inherently understand and adhere to physical laws.

The key motivations driving this work stem from the observed shortcomings of current video generation models:
- **Lack of Physical Adherence:** Despite significant advancements in visual quality, generated videos frequently exhibit physically implausible behaviors, such as objects defying gravity, passing through each other, or deforming unrealistically. This fundamental flaw limits their applicability in scenarios requiring realistic physical interactions.
- **Inadequate Physical Knowledge Integration:** Existing models struggle to effectively incorporate physical knowledge into their generation process. Traditional loss functions prioritize visual fidelity, and there's no straightforward mechanism to supervise or extract an abstract "physical representation" that can guide dynamics.
- **Limitations of Current Solutions:** As discussed, simulation-based methods lack generalizability to diverse phenomena, while simulation-free methods either overfit to specific data or suffer from the scalability and accuracy issues of feedback mechanisms. The authors seek a more generalizable and scalable solution that can facilitate a broad understanding of physics.
- **Developing a "World Model":** The ultimate long-term motivation is to enable video generation models to function as rudimentary "world models" – AI systems that can predict and simulate the future state of an environment based on its current observation and understood physical laws. Achieving physical plausibility is a critical step towards this ambitious goal.

To address these motivations, the core objective of PhysMaster is to **learn an effective physical representation from an input image and leverage it to guide an image-to-video (I2V) generation model**. The authors specifically focus on the I2V task, hypothesizing that an initial frame contains rich physical priors (object configurations, materials, potential interactions) that can dictate subsequent physical evolution. The main challenge then becomes defining and optimizing this "physical representation" without direct supervision, which PhysMaster tackles using a novel reinforcement learning-based, top-down optimization strategy.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

PhysMaster introduces a novel framework for enhancing the physics-awareness of video generation models, primarily targeting the Image-to-Video (I2V) task. Its methodology is built upon a transformer-based diffusion model (DiT) and a custom-designed `PhysEncoder`, optimized through a three-stage training pipeline utilizing Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF), specifically Direct Preference Optimization (DPO).

#### 3.1. Physical Representation Learning

The core of PhysMaster lies in learning a **physical representation** from an input image.
- **Base Architecture:** PhysMaster is built upon a transformer-based Diffusion inpainting Transformer (DiT) model (Peebles & Xie, 2023). This model utilizes a 3D Variational Autoencoder (VAE) to convert videos and initial frames into a latent space and a T5 encoder (Raffel et al., 2020) for extracting text embeddings (`c_text`).
- **PhysEncoder Design:** The authors devise `PhysEncoder` to extract implicit physical representations from the initial input image. This encoder is structured similarly to Depth Anything (Yang et al., 2024a), comprising a DINOv2 (Oquab et al., 2023) encoder (initialized with pretrained weights for semantic perception) and a trainable physical head. `PhysEncoder` takes the first frame of a video as input and generates physical embeddings (`c_phys`).
- **Conditioning the DiT Model:** The extracted `c_phys` is then concatenated with image embeddings (`c_image` from VAE) and text embeddings (`c_text`) before being fed into the DiT model. This integration allows the physical representation to act as an extra condition, guiding the generation process.

#### 3.2. Top-Down Optimization Strategy

A key challenge is the lack of a well-established definition or direct supervision for a "physical representation." PhysMaster addresses this by adopting a **top-down optimization strategy** using reinforcement learning, where `PhysEncoder`'s ability is optimized based on the physical plausibility of the *final generated videos*.

#### 3.3. Three-Stage Training Pipeline

The training pipeline is structured into three distinct stages to progressively enhance physics-awareness:

- **Stage I: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) for DiT and PhysEncoder:**
 - **Objective:** To equip the base I2V model with initial capabilities to predict subsequent frames and to adapt `PhysEncoder` for physical condition injection.
 - **Process:** Both the DiT model and `PhysEncoder` (specifically its trainable physical head) are fine-tuned using the Flow Matching objective (Lipman et al., 2022). The DiT model learns to regress velocity fields (`v_theta`) conditioned on `c_text`, `c_image`, and `c_phys` by minimizing the L2 loss against ground-truth velocities. This stage provides `PhysEncoder` with a preliminary ability to guide the generation model.

- **Stage II: Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) for DiT:**
 - **Objective:** To align the DiT model's output distribution with physically more plausible outcomes, thereby improving its intrinsic physics-awareness before it provides feedback to `PhysEncoder`.
 - **Process:** Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) (Hu et al., 2021) is applied to fine-tune the DiT model using DPO (Rafailov et al., 2023). A preference dataset is constructed by generating video pairs (one preferred `x_w`, one rejected `x_l`) using the Stage I model with different seeds, given the same prompt and initial frame. The DPO objective (specifically Flow-DPO from Liu et al., 2025b) maximizes the probability of generating preferred videos while minimizing rejected ones, leveraging a reference model (the Stage I DiT).

- **Stage III: DPO for PhysEncoder:**
 - **Objective:** To leverage the refined generative feedback from the Stage II DiT model to optimize `PhysEncoder`'s physical representation.
 - **Process:** In this crucial stage, *only the physical head of `PhysEncoder` is trained*, while the DiT model's weights (from Stage II) are kept frozen. The DPO objective (Eq 3) is applied again. By directing the DiT model (which is now better at physics) to generate more accurate physical dynamics, `PhysEncoder`'s representation is iteratively refined with more profound physical knowledge. This process makes `PhysEncoder` learn implicit physical cues by observing how its guidance affects the physical plausibility of the generated videos.

#### 3.4. Task Formulation

The methodology is evaluated on two types of tasks:
- **Proxy Task ("free-fall"):** A specialized task involving objects dropping and colliding. This scenario is chosen for its clear, fundamental physical principles (e.g., energy/momentum conservation), diverse object properties, ease of simulation (Kubric dataset), and deterministic nature, which facilitates automatic construction of preference data for DPO.
- **General Open-World Scenarios:** To demonstrate generalizability, PhysMaster is applied to large-scale datasets (WISA-80K) covering 17 types of real-world physical events (Dynamics, Thermodynamics, Optics). For these complex scenarios, human annotators provide pairwise labels for DPO data construction and evaluation, and domain-specific prefixes are added to text prompts to condition the model on the type of physics involved.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

PhysMaster demonstrates significant improvements in physics-awareness for video generation across both specialized proxy tasks and general open-world scenarios.

#### 4.1. Evaluation on Proxy Task ("Free-Fall")

- **Quantitative Comparison:** On the "free-fall" proxy task using the PisaBench real-world unseen subset, PhysMaster outperforms specialized rigid-body motion models like PhysGen (Liu et al., 2024b) and PISA (Li et al., 2025). PhysMaster achieves superior overall performance, marked by competitive L2 distance and Chamfer Distance (CD) for trajectory accuracy, and notably higher Intersection over Union (IoU) for shape consistency. This indicates that PhysMaster not only predicts accurate object movements but also maintains the integrity of object shapes throughout the dynamics.
- **Qualitative Comparison:** Visual comparisons (Figure 2) reinforce these findings, showing PhysMaster's advantage in preserving shape consistency and achieving accurate trajectories compared to baselines, which often exhibit implausible object interactions or deformations.
- **Ablation Study:** Conducted on the synthetic PisaBench subset, the ablation study (Table 1) validates the efficacy of the multi-stage training pipeline:
 - **Effectiveness of Stages:** SFT (Stage I) provides an initial capability. DPO for DiT (Stage II) further steers generation towards physically plausible distributions. Critically, DPO for PhysEncoder (Stage III) is shown to significantly enhance the model's physics-awareness, leading to the best performance in terms of L2, CD, and IoU.
 - **Importance of PhysEncoder:** While SFT alone for PhysEncoder in Stage I did not immediately yield better results than SFT on DiT alone, the subsequent DPO stages unlock PhysEncoder's potential, demonstrating that DPO is essential for learning effective physical representations.
 - **DPO Strategy:** Sequential DPO (Stage II for DiT, then Stage III for PhysEncoder) outperforms solely optimizing PhysEncoder or joint DPO of both in Stage III, particularly in generalizing to "unseen" scenarios. This suggests that separately aligning the DiT model first creates a more stable foundation for PhysEncoder to learn from.
- **PCA Analysis:** Visualization of Principle Component Analysis (PCA) on physical features extracted by `PhysEncoder` (Figure 4) provides qualitative evidence of its learning. In Stage III, `PhysEncoder`'s features exhibit clear clustering for objects under similar external forces (e.g., in mid-air vs. on the ground) and better differentiation between materials, confirming its ability to acquire a deeper understanding of physical properties and dynamics.

#### 4.2. Generalization on General Open-World Scenarios

- **Quantitative Comparison:** PhysMaster demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on a wide range of real-world physical phenomena evaluated using the VIDEOPHY benchmark (Bansal et al., 2024). It surpasses both general video generation models (e.g., HunyuanVideo, CogVideoX-5B, Cosmos, Wan2.1-T2V) and specialized physics-focused models (PhyT2V, WISA) in both semantic adherence (SA) and physical commonsense (PC) scores (Table 3).
 - **Efficiency Advantage:** Crucially, PhysMaster achieves this superior performance with remarkable efficiency, being approximately 70x faster than PhyT2V and 8x faster than WISA. It generates a 5-second video in just 26 seconds on a single A800 GPU, making it a highly practical solution.
- **Qualitative Comparison:** Figure 5 showcases PhysMaster's superior ability in challenging open-world scenarios involving complex rigid-body movements and fluid dynamics, producing more physically coherent videos than baselines.
- **Ablation Study:** The ablation study for general open-world scenarios (Table 4) reiterates findings from the proxy task:
 - `PhysEncoder` combined with DPO significantly boosts SA and PC scores compared to a baseline model or one without `PhysEncoder`.
 - DPO is vital for `PhysEncoder` to learn useful guiding physical representations, as SFT alone proves insufficient.
- **User Study:** A human preference study (Table 5) confirms that videos generated by the final PhysMaster model (Stage III) are significantly preferred over those from earlier stages or the base model in terms of adherence to physical laws across different real-world scenarios, validating the perceived physical realism by human annotators.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

PhysMaster represents a significant step forward in the field of video generation, primarily by addressing the long-standing challenge of physical implausibility. Its core contributions and potential impact are multifaceted:

- **Novelty in Physical Knowledge Integration:** The research introduces a novel and effective paradigm for incorporating physical knowledge into video generation models. By learning a dedicated "physical representation" via a specialized `PhysEncoder` and optimizing it through a top-down reinforcement learning strategy, PhysMaster moves beyond explicit simulations or mere data-fitting. This approach is highly innovative in tackling the implicit nature of physical understanding for generative AI.
- **Enhanced Physical Realism and Plausibility:** PhysMaster significantly improves the physical accuracy of generated videos, as demonstrated by state-of-the-art performance on diverse benchmarks. This enhanced realism makes generated content more believable and useful for various applications.
- **Generalizability Across Diverse Physics:** Unlike many prior methods limited to specific physical categories or motion modalities, PhysMaster shows strong generalization capabilities across a wide range of physical phenomena, from rigid-body dynamics to fluid mechanics. Its "plug-in" nature allows it to infuse general physical properties into existing I2V models without requiring domain-specific retraining for every new physical interaction.
- **Efficiency and Practicality:** Achieving state-of-the-art results while also demonstrating superior computational efficiency makes PhysMaster a highly practical solution for real-world applications. Its ability to generate high-quality, physics-aware videos quickly is a crucial advantage for industrial deployment.
- **Stepping Stone Towards "World Models":** By enabling generative models to comprehend and adhere to physical principles, PhysMaster brings the field closer to developing genuine "world models." These models, capable of accurately predicting and simulating future states of the world, are fundamental for advanced AI applications, including robotics, autonomous systems, and scientific discovery.
- **Foundation for Future Research:** The framework provides a strong foundation for future research in physics-aware generative AI. The concept of learning implicit physical representations through preference-based optimization can be extended to other generative tasks and modalities, potentially leading to more intelligent and physically grounded AI systems.
- **Potential for Broader Applications:** The enhanced physical realism and efficiency open doors for more sophisticated applications, such as creating realistic training environments for AI agents, developing advanced visual effects, and generating educational content that accurately depicts physical laws.

**Limitations and Future Directions:**
The paper acknowledges a key limitation: the reliance on human annotators for constructing preference datasets in real-world scenarios, which is costly and time-consuming. While PhysMaster is shown to be effective even with small human-labeled datasets, scaling this to encompass the full complexity of physical reality remains a challenge. Future work could explore more scalable alternatives for physical feedback, such as improved AI evaluators with better physics knowledge, or hybrid approaches that combine sparse human feedback with synthetic physical data. Despite this, PhysMaster provides a compelling and generalizable solution, demonstrating the immense potential of leveraging reinforcement learning to master physical representations for video generation.
