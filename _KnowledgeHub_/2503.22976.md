# From Flatland to Space: Teaching Vision-Language Models to Perceive and Reason in 3D

> [!summary] Summary
> A 3D-driven data generation pipeline yields SPAR-7M, a large-scale 2D spatial dataset, and SPAR-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating Vision-Language Models. Training VLMs on SPAR-7M enables them to develop robust 3D spatial perception and reasoning from 2D inputs, achieving state-of-the-art performance on existing spatial benchmarks and competitive results on 3D tasks, without requiring architectural modifications.

## Problem

- Current Vision-Language Models (VLMs) demonstrate limited ability to perceive and reason about the 3D physical world from 2D inputs.
- Integrating explicit 3D representations into VLMs faces challenges due to data scarcity, architectural complexity, and integration difficulties.
- Existing 2D-based spatial datasets and benchmarks lack the precision, diversity, or comprehensive scope needed to robustly train and evaluate fundamental 3D spatial understanding in VLMs.

## Method

- Developed a novel ==3D-driven data generation pipeline== that transforms 3D scene data into diverse and precisely annotated 2D spatial question-answering (QA) pairs.
- Constructed **SPAR-7M**, a large-scale dataset containing over ==7 million QA pairs== across ==33 spatial task types==, supporting single-view, multi-view, and video inputs.
- Created **SPAR-Bench**, a rigorously validated benchmark subset of SPAR-7M, for comprehensive evaluation of spatial perception and reasoning in Vision-Language Models.

## Results

- Models pretrained on SPAR-mix achieved **state-of-the-art** performance on 2D spatial benchmarks (e.g., **89.08** on CV-Bench 3D), outperforming baselines while maintaining general 2D capabilities.
- VLMs trained with SPAR-7M showed competitive results on 3D-specific tasks like spatial QA (**90.7 CIDEr** on ScanQA) and 3D grounding (**48.8 Acc@0.25** on ScanRefer), comparable to or surpassing models using explicit 3D inputs.
- Probing experiments revealed a significant reduction in Average Position Error (**1.05m vs. 2.22m**) for BEV coordinate prediction from multi-view RGB, indicating the learning of implicit 3D representations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision-Language Models can develop robust 3D spatial perception and reasoning capabilities from 2D inputs alone, eliminating the need for explicit 3D representations or architectural changes during inference.
> - A scalable 3D-to-2D data generation pipeline can effectively bridge the gap between abundant 2D images and precise 3D spatial ground truth, providing rich supervision for VLMs.
> - Training on sufficiently diverse and accurate 2D spatial data allows VLMs to implicitly learn and utilize 3D structures, evident in improved bird's-eye-view coordinate predictions.
