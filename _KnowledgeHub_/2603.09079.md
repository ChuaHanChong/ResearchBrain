# GST-VLA: Structured Gaussian Spatial Tokens for 3D Depth-Aware Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework for Vision-Language-Action models, GST-VLA, introduces structured 3D Gaussian spatial tokens to encode geometric details and a Depth-Aware Chain-of-Thought mechanism for explicit 3D reasoning. This approach achieves an 83.1% average success rate on the LIBERO-Pro benchmark, improving precision insertion tasks by 9.2 percentage points over previous methods.

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models commonly process visual observations as 2D patch tokens, lacking intrinsic geometric properties like depth, surface orientation, and confidence crucial for precise robotic manipulation.
- Existing depth augmentation methods often provide pixel-uniform scalar depth values that fail to encode surface orientation or geometric confidence and allocate representational capacity inefficiently across a scene.
- VLA models typically lack explicit intermediate spatial verification steps, making their 3D scene interpretation opaque and challenging to inspect or debug before an action is executed.

## Method

- Developed a Gaussian Spatial Tokenizer (GST) to represent 3D scenes using anisotropic Gaussian primitives that capture position, surface orientation, and geometric confidence, adaptively allocating tokens to relevant regions.
- Introduced a Depth-Aware Chain-of-Thought (DA-CoT) mechanism that compels the VLM to explicitly generate structured 3D geometric targets (e.g., object centroids, grasp affordances, SE(3) waypoints) before action decoding.
- Utilized a composite training objective and a three-stage protocol to synergistically train the GST, VLM reasoning core, and a flow-matching action expert, ensuring geometric calibration and precise action generation.

## Results

- GST-VLA achieved an 83.1% average success rate on the LIBERO-Pro benchmark, surpassing SpatialVLA (76.8%) and improving precision insertion tasks by 9.2 percentage points over DepthVLA.
- The framework demonstrated enhanced generalization on the SimplerEnv benchmark, achieving an 80.2% average task progress, which is a 5.4 percentage point improvement over DepthVLA.
- Ablation studies confirmed that core components like 3D Fourier Positional Encoding and spatial attention pooling within GST, as well as the SE(3) motion plan in DA-CoT, each contributed meaningfully to the overall performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Encoding 3D scenes with anisotropic Gaussian primitives, which capture position, surface orientation, and geometric confidence, significantly enhances a model's ability to perform precise manipulation tasks.
> - Explicitly supervising intermediate 3D geometric reasoning (DA-CoT) makes the model's scene interpretation verifiable and interpretable, improving debugging and potentially enabling human-robot collaboration.
> - The accuracy of the explicit 3D reasoning outputs directly correlates with the success rate of downstream robotic tasks, indicating its role as a strong predictor and diagnostic tool for action quality.
