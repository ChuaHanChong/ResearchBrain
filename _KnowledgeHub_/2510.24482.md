# Sample-efficient and Scalable Exploration in Continuous-Time RL

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at ETH Zürich developed COMBRL, a continuous-time model-based reinforcement learning algorithm that achieves scalable and sample-efficient exploration by integrating uncertainty-aware intrinsic rewards. The method yields improved asymptotic returns and superior generalization on control tasks while reducing computational cost by approximately three times compared to prior optimistic continuous-time approaches.

## Problem

- Most reinforcement learning algorithms operate in discrete time, which misaligns with inherently continuous-time real-world systems, leading to potential information loss and control precision limitations.
- Existing continuous-time model-based RL methods often lack robust, principled exploration mechanisms or are computationally prohibitive due to intensive co-optimization of policies and plausible dynamics.
- A unified framework is needed to address both reward-driven and unsupervised exploration effectively in continuous-time environments, balancing task performance with global model accuracy.

## Method

- Introduces **COMBRL** (==Continuous-Time Optimistic== Model-Based RL), an episodic algorithm that uses probabilistic models (e.g., Gaussian Processes, Bayesian Neural Networks) to learn unknown ODE dynamics and quantify epistemic uncertainty.
- Employs an optimistic planning objective where policies maximize a weighted sum of extrinsic reward and an intrinsic bonus directly derived from model uncertainty, guided by a tunable weight

## Results

- Achieved higher asymptotic returns on continuous control tasks (Pendulum, MountainCar) with Gaussian Process dynamics and iCEM planning, matching or exceeding OCORL's performance at approximately three times lower computational cost.
- Demonstrated superior zero-shot generalization to unseen downstream tasks in the unsupervised setting, indicating a more globally accurate system model by prioritizing uncertainty reduction.
- Integrated with the time-adaptive control framework (TaCoS), COMBRL-TaCoS achieved competitive or superior returns with fewer environment interactions compared to other time-adaptive baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - COMBRL, a continuous-time model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) algorithm, significantly improves both sample efficiency and scalability for systems governed by ordinary differential equations (ODEs).
> - Its optimistic planning objective incorporates an intrinsic reward directly derived from model uncertainty, allowing flexible control between exploitation, balanced exploration, and purely unsupervised exploration.
> - Theoretical guarantees include sublinear regret for reward-driven tasks and the first known sample complexity bound for unsupervised continuous-time reinforcement learning, validating its efficiency.
