# DriveWAM: Video Generative Priors Enable Scalable World-Action Modeling for Autonomous Driving

> [!summary] Summary
> DriveWAM introduces a world-action model for autonomous driving from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and Voyager Research, Didi Chuxing, that leverages a pretrained video diffusion transformer for unified future video and ego-action generation. It integrates VLM-based semantic guidance and selective memory management, achieving a Predictive Driver Model Score of 90.1 on NAVSIM v1 and lower trajectory prediction errors on PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles compared to existing methods.

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for autonomous driving often lack inherent temporal dynamics and motion priors due to reliance on static image-text pretraining.
- Existing world-action models struggle to integrate high-level semantic planning (e.g., route intent, right-of-way) into their action prediction capabilities.
- Autoregressive driving policies face challenges with unbounded memory growth or suboptimal information eviction when managing historical context for long-horizon rollouts.

## Method

- A pretrained ==video diffusion transformer== is adapted into an ==autoregressive video-action policy==, formulating driving as joint generation of future video latents and ego actions with a ==flow-matching objective==.
- A frozen VLM provides ==scene-evolving driving guidance== by generating chunk-specific semantic intent, which is injected via ==temporally localized cross-attention== to maintain causal consistency.
- A ==selective KV memory== mechanism maintains bounded, modality-aware memory pools for historical video and action tokens, using a relevance-redundancy criterion to retain critical information during long-horizon inference.

## Results

- Achieved a Predictive Driver Model Score (PDMS) of **90.1** on NAVSIM v1, demonstrating competitive planning performance with high scores in safety and comfort metrics.
- Recorded lower trajectory prediction errors on the PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles benchmark, with an Average Displacement Error (ADE) of **0.47 meters** and Final Displacement Error (FDE) of **1.35 meters** at a 3-second horizon, outperforming baselines.
- Selective KV memory reduced KV memory usage by over **12 times** and attention GFLOPs by a similar factor for a **300-second** rollout, while largely closing the accuracy gap to full KV caching.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Repurposing pretrained video diffusion transformers enables robust world-action modeling for autonomous driving by leveraging inherent spatio-temporal generative priors for joint video and action prediction.
> - Integrating causally consistent, VLM-generated semantic guidance effectively bridges the gap between low-level dynamic prediction and high-level driving intentions, enhancing overall policy robustness.
> - Efficient and intelligent memory management (selective KV memory) allows autoregressive policies to maintain high performance in long-horizon scenarios while significantly reducing computational and memory overhead.
