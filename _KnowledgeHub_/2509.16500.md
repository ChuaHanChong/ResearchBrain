# RLGF: Reinforcement Learning with Geometric Feedback for Autonomous Driving Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the University of Macau and Li Auto Inc. developed RLGF, a system that employs reinforcement learning with geometric feedback to enhance the structural accuracy of synthetic autonomous driving videos. This approach substantially reduced geometric errors and boosted 3D object detection performance on generated data by an absolute 12.7% mAP.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art diffusion models generate visually realistic autonomous driving videos but contain subtle geometric distortions.
- These geometric inaccuracies severely limit the utility of synthetic data for critical downstream 3D perception tasks, leading to a performance gap (e.g., 3D object detection mAP of 25.7 on synthetic vs. 35.5 on real data).
- Traditional pixel-wise loss functions and general preference-based reinforcement learning methods fail to enforce the precise, multi-scale geometric consistency required for autonomous driving.

## Method

- **RLGF** (Reinforcement Learning with Geometric Feedback) refines pre-trained ==video diffusion== models by integrating perception-model-driven geometric constraints as reward signals.
- It introduces Latent-Space Windowing Optimization, efficiently applying rewards within intermediate denoising steps on noisy latent features to guide geometry formation progressively.
- A Hierarchical Geometric Reward (HGR) system provides multi-level feedback (vanishing points, lane topology, depth, 3D occupancy) using specialized latent-space perception networks and a Micro-Decode Module.

## Results

- RLGF reduced geometric errors in synthetic videos, including a **21%** decrease in Vanishing Point Error and a **57%** reduction in Depth Error on the DiVE baseline.
- The framework significantly improved 3D object detection performance on synthetic data, boosting mAP from 25.75 to 31.42 for the DiVE baseline, an absolute increase of **12.7%**.
- Geometric improvements were achieved while maintaining or slightly improving visual realism, with comparable Fréchet Video Distance (FVD) scores.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Geometric structures in generated videos emerge progressively during diffusion, allowing targeted feedback at intermediate latent states to efficiently correct specific inaccuracies.
> - Integrating explicit, quantifiable geometric and scene-level rewards derived from dedicated autonomous driving perception models, rather than subjective preferences, is crucial for achieving task-aware synthetic data.
> - Current generative models prioritize 2D appearance, overlooking critical 3D scene structure, which necessitates dedicated geometric evaluation metrics like GeoScores.
