# Pure Vision Language Action (VLA) Models: A Comprehensive Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Lanzhou University, NUS, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences conducted a comprehensive survey classifying pure Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models into five paradigms based on action generation strategies, reviewing over three hundred studies to map the field's advancements and future directions for generalized robotics. The survey identifies critical challenges in data scarcity and real-time inference, alongside opportunities for world modeling and causal reasoning to bridge the perception-understanding-action gap in robotics.

## Problem

- Traditional robotics faced limitations in adaptability and intuitive human interaction due to reliance on pre-programmed instructions.
- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) were primarily passive observers, capable of understanding but not actively interacting or making decisions in the physical world.
- The rapidly growing field of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models lacked a consolidated, systematic review and taxonomy to guide research and development.

## Method

- Systematically review over **300** recent studies focusing on =="pure Vision-Language-Action (VLA) methods"== to synthesize insights into this emerging field.
- Develop a structured taxonomy that classifies VLA approaches based on their ==action-generation strategies== (e.g., ==autoregression==, ==diffusion==, ==reinforcement==, ==hybrid==).
- Detail the motivations, core strategies, implementations, and application domains of various VLA models, along with an overview of foundational ==datasets== and ==simulation platforms==.

## Results

- The survey established a comprehensive taxonomy for VLA models, detailing the innovations and limitations of autoregression-based, diffusion-based, reinforcement-based, hybrid, and specialized approaches.
- Critical foundational resources were cataloged, including major real-world datasets like OXE, simulation platforms such as CARLA and Isaac Gym, and various robotic hardware.
- Key challenges to VLA development were identified, notably data scarcity, architectural heterogeneity, real-time inference constraints, and the need for more robust evaluation methods.
- Future opportunities were outlined, focusing on advancements in world modeling, causal reasoning, virtual-real integration, and developing trustworthy VLA systems for societal embedding.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Pure VLA models extend Vision-Language Models from passive understanding to active physical interaction and decision-making, unifying perception, language, and executable control.
> - A structured taxonomy identifies autoregression-based, diffusion-based, reinforcement-based, hybrid, and specialized methods as core paradigms for action generation in VLA.
> - Significant challenges include the scarcity of diverse real-world robotic data, architectural complexity, real-time inference constraints, and limitations in current evaluation benchmarks.
