# Off-Policy Value-Based Reinforcement Learning for Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> ReVal introduces an off-policy value-based reinforcement learning framework for large language models that interprets LLM logits as Q-values and utilizes a replay buffer for efficient experience reuse. This approach achieved an average of 4.3x speedup in convergence and an 18% reduction in wall-clock training time on mathematical reasoning tasks while outperforming on-policy baselines.

## Problem

- On-policy reinforcement learning methods for large language models (LLMs) suffer from sample inefficiency, requiring frequent, costly data generation rounds, especially for long and complex reasoning tasks.
- Directly applying traditional value-based off-policy RL to LLMs is challenging due to the vast token vocabulary (action space), sparse rewards, and the computational/memory overhead of separate Q-networks.
- Existing value-based interpretations for LLMs, such as TBRM, did not satisfy "Calibrated Initialization," causing unstable policy drift when reward signals were absent.

## Method

- **ReVal**, an ==off-policy value-based RL== framework, directly interprets an LLM's logits as its ==Q-function==, eliminating the need for a separate value network and maintaining a single-model architecture.
- A new ==Bellman objective== is introduced that ensures ==Calibrated Initialization==, preventing policy drift in the absence of rewards, and effectively integrates multi-temporal feedback.
- A ==FIFO replay buffer== is used to decouple data collection from policy updates, enabling efficient reuse of historical trajectories for multiple gradient updates per collected sample.

## Results

- ReVal achieved an average of **4.3x** speedup in convergence compared to the on-policy GRPO baseline, demonstrating enhanced sample efficiency, particularly for harder tasks.
- The framework outperformed GRPO and TBRM on various mathematical reasoning benchmarks, with up to a **4.5%** improvement on out-of-domain tasks like GPQA for the DPSK-R1-Distill-**1.5B** model.
- In limited-rollout scenarios, ReVal reduced the number of data generation rounds by **19%** and total wall-clock training time by **18%**, translating into tangible cost savings.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Off-policy learning with a replay buffer dramatically improves sample efficiency and optimization speed, especially critical for complex LLM reasoning tasks where generating valid trajectories is expensive.
> - Interpreting LLM logits as Q-values allows for a low-cost, single-model value-based RL approach that avoids the overhead of separate value networks while leveraging pre-trained knowledge.
> - Ensuring "Calibrated Initialization" in the Bellman objective prevents spurious policy drift when reward signals are absent, contributing to stable and effective learning.
