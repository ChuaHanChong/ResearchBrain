# WMPO: World Model-based Policy Optimization for Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> WMPO introduces a framework for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policy optimization that uses a pixel-space video-generative world model to enable reinforcement learning entirely in imagination, overcoming the need for real-world interactions. The method facilitates self-correction and efficient task execution, achieving superior sample efficiency and robustness in both simulation and real-world robotic tasks.

## Problem

- VLA models trained via imitation learning (IL) are brittle, lack self-correction, and struggle with out-of-distribution states, leading to compounding errors.
- Applying reinforcement learning (RL) directly to real robots for VLAs is severely constrained by high sample complexity, making it impractical, unsafe, and costly.
- Many existing model-based RL approaches use latent world models, which creates a representational mismatch with powerful VLA foundation models pretrained on pixel-rich images.

## Method

- A high-fidelity, pixel-space ==video-generative world model==, based on a modified ==OpenSora architecture==, is developed to generate imagined trajectories conditioned on VLA policy actions.
- A lightweight, learned ==reward model== is trained on real trajectories to provide binary success/failure feedback for the automated evaluation of imagined rollouts.
- An on-policy ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== algorithm is applied within the imagined environment, leveraging ==dynamic sampling== to enable scalable and principled policy updates.

## Results

- WMPO achieved superior sample efficiency and overall performance in simulation, outperforming established RL baselines by up to **+15.2 points** in mean success rate.
- Qualitative analysis revealed emergent self-correction capabilities and more efficient, smoother task execution, with successful imagined trajectories being significantly shorter.
- The framework demonstrated robust generalization to novel disruption scenarios (spatial, background, texture changes) and showed stable, substantial improvements in a lifelong learning setup.
- Deployed on a real Cobot Mobile ALOHA platform, WMPO achieved a **70%** success rate on a fine-grained manipulation task, surpassing a base policy (**53%**) and offline DPO (**60%**) over **30** trials.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Operating a world model in pixel space is crucial for aligning imagined trajectories with the rich visual features that VLA foundation models are pre-trained to understand.
> - Fine-tuning the world model on real rollout trajectories collected from the policy itself enables accurate simulation of policy-specific behaviors, including failure modes, which is essential for effective RL.
> - Decoupling on-policy RL from physical interaction, by performing it entirely within a learned world model, allows for scalable learning of complex behaviors like self-correction and efficient execution.
