# Visual Spatial Tuning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from HKU, ByteDance Seed, and Tsinghua University developed Visual Spatial Tuning (VST), a framework that cultivates human-like visuospatial abilities in general Vision-Language Models without architectural modifications, utilizing extensive spatial datasets and a progressive training pipeline. The approach achieves state-of-the-art spatial understanding across various benchmarks and improves Vision-Language-Action models by 8.6% on robotic manipulation tasks, while preserving general multi-modal capabilities.

## Problem

- Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) demonstrate limitations in capturing precise spatial relationships and understanding 3D environments from visual inputs.
- Existing methods to improve VLM spatial awareness often involve additional expert encoders, which introduce computational overhead and can degrade general capabilities.
- Prior spatial datasets for VLMs typically focus on narrow aspects of spatial understanding or are limited to supervised fine-tuning, lacking diversity and comprehensive training strategies.

## Method

- A comprehensive ==Visual Spatial Tuning (VST)== framework is proposed, integrating spatial perception and reasoning into general VLM architectures.
- Two large-scale, diverse datasets, ==VST-Perception (VST-P)== and ==VST-Reasoning (VST-R)==, were created, covering single-image, multi-image, and video spatial tasks.
- A ==three-stage progressive training pipeline== is employed, involving Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT), Chain-of-Thought (CoT) cold start, and Reinforcement Learning (RL) using the GRPO algorithm.
- The spatial-enhanced VST model is adapted for ==Vision-Language-Action (VLA)== tasks by discretizing the action space and fine-tuning the VLM for robotic manipulation.

## Results

- VST models consistently achieved state-of-the-art performance across various spatial benchmarks, with VST-7B-RL reaching an average spatial score (S-AVG) of **61.2%**.
- The VST-7B-RL model demonstrated superior 3D object detection, achieving **44.2%** AP@15 on SUN RGB-D without a specialized 3D encoder, outperforming existing general and specialized models.
- The framework successfully preserved general multi-modal capabilities, and VST-enhanced Vision-Language-Action models showed an **8.6%** improvement in average success rate on the LIBERO robotic manipulation benchmark.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Comprehensive spatial intelligence, encompassing both perception and reasoning, can be effectively cultivated in general VLM architectures without relying on specialized 3D encoders.
> - Constructing large-scale, diverse datasets that cover single-image, multi-image, and video-based spatial tasks is essential for holistic visuospatial understanding.
> - A progressive training pipeline, starting with supervised fine-tuning for perception and advancing to reinforcement learning for reasoning, proves effective for instilling complex spatial abilities.
