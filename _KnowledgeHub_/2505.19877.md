# Vad-R1: Towards Video Anomaly Reasoning via Perception-to-Cognition Chain-of-Thought

> [!summary] Summary
> The paper introduces Video Anomaly Reasoning (VAR) as a new task, moving beyond mere detection to deep, human-like understanding of anomalous video events through a Perception-to-Cognition Chain-of-Thought (P2C-CoT). Researchers at Sun Yat-sen University developed Vad-R1, an MLLM-based framework that significantly improves both anomaly reasoning and detection, surpassing existing models on the new Vad-Reasoning dataset and VANE benchmark.

## Problem

- Traditional Video Anomaly Detection (VAD) methods can identify when/where an anomaly occurs but lack the ability to understand and explain *why* an event is anomalous or its implications.
- Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) applied to VAD offer superficial descriptions, lacking the structured, step-by-step analytical reasoning humans use for anomaly interpretation.
- A scarcity of VAD datasets with structured reasoning annotations limits the development and evaluation of models capable of deep anomaly reasoning.

## Method

- Developed a structured Perception-to-Cognition Chain-of-Thought (P2C-CoT) template to guide MLLMs through a four-step process (Global Perception, Local Perception, Shallow Cognition, Deep Cognition) for comprehensive anomaly analysis.
- Constructed Vad-Reasoning, a new dataset with over 8,000 videos, featuring high-quality step-by-step P2C-CoT annotations for supervised fine-tuning and weak video-level labels for reinforcement learning.
- Proposed Anomaly Verification Augmented GRPO (AVA-GRPO), a novel reinforcement learning algorithm with a self-verification mechanism to effectively train MLLMs for open-ended VAR using weak labels.

## Results

- Vad-R1 achieved state-of-the-art performance on the Vad-Reasoning test set for both anomaly reasoning (e.g., BLEU score improvements of 0.088 to 0.127 over proprietary MLLMs) and anomaly detection (Accuracy, F1, mIoU).
- P2C-CoT prompting alone increased Recall by +0.265 and F1 by +0.133 for a base MLLM, demonstrating the direct benefits of structured reasoning for detection.
- LLM-guided evaluation showed Vad-R1 outperformed proprietary models like GPT-4o in Reasonability, Detail, and Consistency of anomaly explanations, indicating superior semantic quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Structured, step-by-step reasoning (P2C-CoT) is crucial for enhancing both anomaly detection and deep understanding in MLLMs, outperforming direct answering and random reasoning.
> - A two-stage training strategy combining Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) with Reinforcement Learning (RL) is most effective for acquiring and refining complex multimodal reasoning capabilities.
> - The proposed Anomaly Verification Reward within AVA-GRPO enables effective learning from limited weak labels by self-verifying the model's anomaly predictions, pushing beyond simple pattern matching.
