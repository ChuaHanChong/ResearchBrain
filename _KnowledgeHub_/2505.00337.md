# T2VPhysBench: A First-Principles Benchmark for Physical Consistency in Text-to-Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers introduce T2VPhysBench, a benchmark for evaluating physical consistency in text-to-video generation models across 12 fundamental laws, revealing that current state-of-the-art models achieve below 0.60 compliance scores even with detailed physics-specific prompts, while demonstrating particular weakness in handling conservation laws.

## Problem

- Existing text-to-video models lack systematic evaluation of their ability to adhere to physical laws
- Current benchmarks focus on visual fidelity and compositional ability but overlook physical plausibility

## Method

- Development of **T2VPhysBench** with 7 prompts for each of 12 fundamental physical laws
- Implementation of a manual evaluation protocol using three annotators to assess physical consistency
- Testing of models with varying levels of physics-specific hints and counterfactual prompts

## Results

- All evaluated models scored below 0.60 on physical compliance across all law categories
- Models that perform well on standard prompts are easily misled by counterfactual scenarios
- Performance degradation observed in some cases when more detailed physics hints were provided
- Three-annotator evaluation protocol achieved consistent scoring across physical law categories

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Conservation laws prove more challenging for models than Newtonian mechanics
> - Adding detailed physics-specific hints to prompts does not significantly improve performance
> - Models rely on pattern matching rather than true understanding of physical constraints
