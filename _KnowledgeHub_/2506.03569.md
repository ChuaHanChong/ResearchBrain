# MiMo-VL Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Xiaomi's LLM-Core division develops MiMo-VL-7B, a compact vision-language model that achieves state-of-the-art performance across 50+ multimodal benchmarks through a four-stage training process consuming 2.4 trillion tokens and novel Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL) framework, with the resulting MiMo-VL-7B-RL model outperforming Qwen2.5-VL-7B on 35 out of 40 tasks and surpassing much larger models (up to 78B parameters) on complex reasoning benchmarks like OlympiadBench (59.4 score) and GUI grounding tasks like OSWorld-G (56.1 score), while achieving 95.4% on MATH500 and maintaining the highest Elo rating among open-source VLMs in user preference evaluations, accomplished through strategic incorporation of high-quality Chain-of-Thought reasoning data directly into late pre-training stages rather than only post-training fine-tuning.

## Problem

- Existing compact vision-language models lag behind larger proprietary models on complex multimodal reasoning and agentic tasks
- Current VLM training approaches primarily incorporate reasoning capabilities through post-training fine-tuning rather than pre-training
- Limited availability of comprehensive, standardized evaluation frameworks for fair VLM comparison across diverse tasks
- Challenge of integrating multiple reward signals (verifiable rewards and human feedback) in reinforcement learning for VLMs

## Method

- ==Four-stage pre-training architecture== with strategic data progression from basic alignment to complex reasoning integration
- ==Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL)== framework combining rule-based verifiable rewards with human feedback through "Reward-as-a-Service" system
- Direct incorporation of high-quality synthetic reasoning data with long ==Chain-of-Thought== into Stage 4 pre-training rather than only fine-tuning
- Comprehensive open-source evaluation suite covering **50+** multimodal benchmarks with standardized prompts and protocols

## Results

- MiMo-VL-7B-RL achieves **59.4** on OlympiadBench, surpassing models up to **72B** parameters and setting new standards for multimodal reasoning
- Outperforms specialized GUI models on OSWorld-G (**56.1** score) despite being a general-purpose VLM, demonstrating strong agentic capabilities
- Maintains excellent text-only performance (**95.4%** MATH500, **85.1%** DROP) while excelling across diverse multimodal tasks including video understanding and document analysis
- Achieves highest Elo rating among open-source VLMs in user preference evaluation, approaching proprietary model performance levels

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Incorporating reasoning data with long Chain-of-Thought directly into late pre-training stages yields substantial performance gains (+9 MMMU, +14 OSWorld-G, +16 OlympiadBench) compared to post-training only approaches
> - On-policy reinforcement learning variants demonstrate superior scaling properties and training stability compared to vanilla GRPO, showing continuous improvement without early saturation
> - Task interference remains a significant challenge in mixed RL objectives, with disparities in response length requirements across different task types limiting unified optimization
> - Compact 7B parameter models can achieve competitive performance with much larger models through sophisticated training methodologies and high-quality data curation

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of MiMo-VL-7B models is situated at the forefront of multimodal AI research, specifically within the rapidly advancing domain of Vision-Language Models (VLMs). VLMs are recognized as foundational components for next-generation AI systems, enabling agents to perceive, reason about, and interact with the visual and digital world. This work aligns with several key trends in the broader research landscape:

- **Emergence of Powerful Open-Source VLMs:** The AI community is witnessing a strong push towards open-sourcing powerful foundational models, democratizing access to cutting-edge AI capabilities. MiMo-VL-7B directly contributes to this trend by releasing state-of-the-art models at a relatively compact 7B parameter scale, making them more accessible for researchers and developers with limited computational resources compared to models with tens or hundreds of billions of parameters. This challenges the notion that only massive, proprietary models can achieve top-tier performance.
- **Focus on Complex Reasoning and "Agentic" AI:** Beyond basic image captioning or visual question answering, the field is moving towards VLMs that can perform sophisticated multimodal reasoning, solve complex STEM problems, and act as autonomous agents in digital environments (e.g., Graphical User Interfaces or GUIs). MiMo-VL-7B explicitly targets these "thinking" and "agentic" capabilities, demonstrated by its strong performance on reasoning benchmarks like OlympiadBench and GUI-centric tasks like OSWorld-G. This reflects the industry's drive towards more intelligent and versatile AI systems capable of executing multi-step tasks.
- **Advanced Training Methodologies:** The paper highlights innovative training strategies, specifically a four-stage pre-training process consuming 2.4 trillion tokens and a novel Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL) framework. This reflects the increasing sophistication in VLM training, moving beyond simple supervised fine-tuning to incorporate diverse data recipes, progressive training stages, and reinforcement learning techniques for fine-tuning and alignment. The emphasis on high-quality reasoning data with Chain-of-Thought (CoT) during pre-training is a notable insight, suggesting a shift in data curation strategies to explicitly foster deeper reasoning abilities early in the model's development.
- **Multimodal Integration:** The model’s architecture seamlessly integrates a Vision Transformer (ViT) with an LLM, facilitated by an MLP projector. The training data covers a wide array of modalities, including images, video, text, OCR, grounding, and GUI interactions. This holistic approach to multimodal integration, rather than focusing on a single modality, is crucial for building truly general-purpose VLMs that can understand and respond to complex real-world inputs.
- **Benchmarking and Reproducibility:** The contribution of a comprehensive evaluation suite covering over 50 tasks with prompts and protocols is a significant contribution to the research community. As VLMs become more complex, standardized and open evaluation frameworks are essential for fair comparison, reproducibility, and tracking progress, addressing a common challenge in the rapidly expanding AI landscape where many models are released without fully transparent evaluation methods.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The core objectives and motivations behind the MiMo-VL project, as articulated in the technical report, are multi-fold:

- **Develop a Compact Yet Powerful VLM:** The primary objective is to create a vision-language model, MiMo-VL-7B, that is both "compact" (7 billion parameters) and "powerful," delivering state-of-the-art performance across a wide spectrum of visual understanding and multimodal reasoning tasks. This aims to challenge the assumption that only models with significantly larger parameter counts can achieve top-tier capabilities.
- **Achieve State-of-the-Art Performance:** The ambition is to surpass existing benchmarks and models of comparable scale, and even outcompete much larger models on challenging reasoning and application-specific tasks. This includes setting new standards in general visual understanding, complex multimodal reasoning (e.g., OlympiadBench), and specific application areas like GUI grounding (e.g., OSWorld-G).
- **Advance Reasoning Capabilities:** A key focus is on enhancing the model's capacity for complex logical and scientific reasoning. This is pursued through dedicated data curation (synthetic reasoning data with long Chain-of-Thought) and training methodologies (incorporating this data into pre-training and using Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards).
- **Enable Agentic Applications:** A significant motivation is to empower VLMs for practical "agentic" applications, particularly in interacting with Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs). The model's strong performance on GUI grounding tasks indicates a drive towards developing models that can act as intelligent assistants in digital environments.
- **Open-Source for Community Advancement:** A crucial objective is to open-source the model checkpoints (MiMo-VL-7B-SFT and MiMo-VL-7B-RL) and a comprehensive evaluation suite. This is motivated by a desire to promote transparency, foster reproducibility, and contribute to the broader advancement of the vision-language research field.
- **Explore and Validate Novel Training Methodologies:** The project aims to validate the effectiveness of its unique four-stage pre-training recipe and the Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL) framework. Through this, the authors seek to identify and share key insights into VLM training, such as the importance of early reasoning data incorporation and the benefits and challenges of mixed RL objectives.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The development of MiMo-VL-7B involves a sophisticated, multi-stage training paradigm designed to imbue the model with robust visual perception, advanced reasoning, and precise grounding capabilities.

#### 3.1. Architecture

MiMo-VL-7B comprises three main components:
- **Vision Encoder:** A native-resolution Vision Transformer (ViT), specifically initialized with Qwen2.5-ViT, is used to process visual inputs while preserving fine-grained details.
- **Projector:** A Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) projector efficiently maps the visual encodings from the ViT into the latent space of the Large Language Model (LLM), enabling cross-modal alignment.
- **Large Language Model (LLM):** The LLM backbone is initialized with MiMo-7B-Base, leveraging its inherent strong reasoning capabilities to handle textual understanding and reasoning.

#### 3.2. Pre-Training (Four Stages)

The model undergoes a meticulously designed four-stage pre-training process, consuming an massive 2.4 trillion tokens, with strategically adjusted dataset distributions and trainable components at each stage:

- **Stage 1: Projector Warmup:** The ViT and LLM are frozen, and the randomly initialized MLP projector is warmed up using image-caption pairs. This initial phase ensures the projector learns effective visual concept mapping, providing stable gradients for subsequent stages.
- **Stage 2: Vision-Language Alignment:** The ViT is unfrozen, and interleaved image-text data is introduced. This strengthens the alignment between vision and language modalities and enhances the ViT's performance and robustness by exposing it to more complex visual contexts.
- **Stage 3: General Multimodal Pre-training:** All model parameters become trainable. A highly diverse array of data and tasks, including OCR, grounding, video, and GUI data, is incorporated (1.4 trillion tokens). Small quantities of QA, instruction-following, reasoning, and text-only data are also included to bolster general multimodal capabilities and preserve the LLM's textual intelligence.
- **Stage 4: Long-context Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** This final pre-training stage focuses on enhancing the model's ability to handle long-context inputs. The training sequence length is extended from 8K to 32K tokens. Additional data types such as long pure text, high-resolution images, extended videos, and crucially, a significantly increased proportion of long-form synthetic reasoning data, are introduced. This stage is particularly emphasized for realizing the model's advanced reasoning aptitude, especially for complex STEM problems.

#### 3.3. Post-Training (Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning - MORL)

Following pre-training, MiMo-VL-7B undergoes a post-training phase using a novel Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL) framework, integrating diverse reward signals. This phase yields MiMo-VL-7B-RL.

- **Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR):** This component utilizes rule-based reward functions for continuous self-improvement across multiple verifiable tasks:
 - **Visual Reasoning:** Compiling 80K STEM questions (K-12 level) with numerical or symbolic answers, validated by a rule-based Math-Verify library.
 - **Text Reasoning:** Incorporating challenging text-only mathematical reasoning data, also verified by Math-Verify.
 - **Image Grounding:** Including general and GUI grounding tasks, with rewards calculated using GIoU for bounding boxes and point-in-box checks for point predictions.
 - **Visual Counting:** Rewards defined by the accuracy of counting predictions.
 - **Temporal Video Grounding:** Localizing video segments based on natural language queries, with rewards computed via Intersection over Union (IoU) of temporal segments.

- **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF):** This complements RLVR by aligning model outputs with human preferences and mitigating undesirable behaviors.
 - **Query Collection:** Diverse multimodal and text-only queries are gathered, screened, clustered, and balanced (Chinese/English, helpfulness/harmlessness). MiMo-VL-7B and other VLMs generate responses, which are then pairwise ranked by an advanced VLM to train the reward models.
 - **Reward Model:** Two specialized reward models are developed: a text-only model (initialized from MiMo-7B) and a multimodal model (built upon MiMo-VL-7B), both trained using the Bradley-Terry objective.

- **Mixed On-Policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL):** The MORL framework integrates both rule-based and model-based rewards into a unified "Reward-as-a-Service" (RaaS) system.
 - **On-Policy RL Recipe:** A fully on-policy variant of GRPO (General Reinforcement Learning for Policy Optimization) is adopted, performing single-step policy updates after response rollout, without a clipped surrogate objective. Enhancements like dynamic sampling, easy data filtering, and re-sampling are integrated.
 - **Reward-as-a-Service (RaaS):** A reward router dynamically selects the appropriate reward function based on the query type, with reward models deployed as standalone services for low-latency computation. All rewards are normalized to a range.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The MiMo-VL technical report presents compelling results that validate the effectiveness of their training approach, positioning MiMo-VL-7B as a leading open-source VLM.

#### 4.1. State-of-the-Art Performance

- **General Visual Understanding:** MiMo-VL-7B-RL achieves impressive scores, outperforming Qwen2.5-VL-7B on 35 out of 40 evaluated tasks. For instance, it scores 66.7% on MMMU (MMMU-val), surpassing much larger models like Gemma 3 27B. On document and chart understanding, MiMo-VL-7B-RL excels with 56.5% on CharXiv-RQ, significantly outperforming Qwen2.5-VL by 14.0 points and InternVL3 by 18.9 points.
- **Multimodal Reasoning:** MiMo-VL-7B-RL demonstrates outstanding performance on complex multimodal reasoning tasks, scoring 59.4 on OlympiadBench. This is a remarkable achievement as it surpasses models with up to 72B parameters (e.g., QVQ-72B-Preview, Qwen2.5-VL-72B, InternVL3-78B) and even some proprietary models mentioned in the benchmark. It also shows strong performance on MathVision (60.4%) and MathVerse (71.5%).
- **GUI Grounding for Agentic Applications:** The model sets a new standard for GUI grounding, achieving 56.1 on OSWorld-G. This performance is particularly noteworthy as it even outperforms specialized GUI models like UI-TARS, despite MiMo-VL being a general-purpose VLM. This highlights its potential for developing autonomous agents.
- **Video Understanding:** MiMo-VL-SFT and RL models show superior video understanding, with MiMo-VL-SFT achieving 53.1% on Video-MMMU and MiMo-VL-RL obtaining an impressive 50.0% mIoU on Charades-STA.
- **Text-only Benchmarks:** The models maintain strong performance on pure text benchmarks (e.g., MATH500 at 95.4%, DROP at 85.1%), indicating that the multimodal training does not degrade core language understanding and reasoning abilities inherited from MiMo-7B-Base.
- **User Preference and Elo Rating:** MiMo-VL-7B-RL achieves the highest Elo rating among all evaluated open-source VLMs on Xiaomi's in-house user preference evaluation, closely approaching proprietary models like Claude 3.7 Sonnet. This demonstrates superior user experience and practical performance.

#### 4.2. Key Insights from Development

The authors highlight several critical observations from their development process:
- **Importance of Reasoning Data in Pre-training:** Incorporating high-quality, broad-coverage reasoning data with long Chain-of-Thought (CoT) directly into later pre-training stages (especially Stage 4) is crucial. This significantly enhanced model performance across reasoning benchmarks (e.g., +9 on MMMU, +14 on OSWorld-G, +16 on OlympiadBench for MiMo-VL-7B-SFT). The authors observe continuous performance improvements without saturation in this stage, leading to more detailed and profound reasoning outputs.
- **Benefits of On-Policy RL:** The chosen on-policy variant of GRPO demonstrated robust training stability and continuous performance improvement with more data, unlike vanilla GRPO, which showed early saturation. This suggests that on-policy RL is more effective for scaling performance with increasing training data.
- **Challenges of Task Interference in MORL:** Despite the overall benefits of Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL), achieving stable and simultaneous improvements across diverse task domains (reasoning, perception, grounding, human preference) remains challenging. Disparities in response length growth (longer CoT for reasoning vs. shrinking for grounding/counting) and task difficulty levels contribute to interference, making it difficult to match the performance of standalone RL on individual tasks. This indicates a limitation that warrants further research.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The MiMo-VL technical report presents a significant contribution to the field of multimodal AI, with several key areas of significance and potential impact:

- **Advancing Open-Source VLM Capabilities:** The open-sourcing of MiMo-VL-7B-SFT and MiMo-VL-7B-RL, along with their state-of-the-art performance at a relatively compact 7B parameter scale, significantly pushes the frontier for accessible, high-performance VLMs. This is crucial for democratizing AI research and application development, enabling a broader community to build upon and innovate with powerful multimodal foundational models without requiring immense computational resources.
- **Setting New Performance Benchmarks:** By outperforming larger open-source models and even competing with proprietary models on challenging reasoning and GUI interaction tasks, MiMo-VL sets new benchmarks. Its exceptional performance on OlympiadBench demonstrates its advanced scientific and logical reasoning capabilities, while its leadership on OSWorld-G highlights its potential for practical agentic applications in digital environments. This validates the effectiveness of Xiaomi's sophisticated training methodology.
- **Pioneering Training Methodologies:** The detailed four-stage pre-training process, particularly the strategic incorporation of high-quality, long Chain-of-Thought reasoning data directly into later pre-training stages, offers valuable insights for the VLM training paradigm. This finding challenges conventional wisdom and suggests new avenues for embedding complex reasoning capabilities early in model development, rather than relying solely on post-training fine-tuning.
- **Contribution to Reinforcement Learning in VLMs:** The novel Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL) framework, integrating verifiable rewards and human feedback, provides a robust approach to further enhance VLM capabilities. While acknowledging challenges like task interference, the successful application of this framework demonstrates its potential for refining model alignment and performance across diverse objectives. The insight into the continuous scaling of on-policy RL compared to vanilla GRPO is also valuable for future RL-based VLM training.
- **Facilitating Reproducibility and Research:** The decision to open-source a comprehensive evaluation suite covering 50+ tasks, complete with prompts and protocols, is a major step towards enhancing transparency and reproducibility in VLM research. This allows other researchers to rigorously compare their models against MiMo-VL-7B and build upon a standardized evaluation framework, fostering a more collaborative and rigorous research environment.
- **Enabling Future AI Applications:** The strong performance in GUI grounding and multimodal reasoning opens doors for more advanced AI applications, such as highly capable digital assistants, automated web navigation agents, sophisticated data analysis tools that can interpret charts and documents, and intelligent systems for STEM education and research. MiMo-VL's agentic capabilities suggest a future where AI can interact with computing environments in a more intelligent and autonomous manner.

In conclusion, the MiMo-VL project by Xiaomi marks a significant milestone in open-source multimodal AI, showcasing that compact models can achieve state-of-the-art performance through meticulous data curation, innovative multi-stage training, and advanced reinforcement learning techniques. Its contributions extend beyond model release to crucial methodological insights and open evaluation resources, poised to accelerate further research and development in the VLM domain.
