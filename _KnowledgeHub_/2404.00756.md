# Recover: A Neuro-Symbolic Framework for Failure Detection and Recovery

> [!summary] Summary
> The Recover framework, developed by researchers at Samsung AI and the University of Plymouth, introduces a neuro-symbolic approach for online failure detection and recovery in robotic systems. This framework integrates symbolic AI's structured reasoning with Large Language Models' flexible planning, achieving 100% accuracy in failure detection through its ontology and successfully recovering from approximately 70% of detected failures in simulated environments.

## Problem

- Traditional model-based or policy-based robotic control struggles with the complexity and unpredictability of dynamic, real-world environments.
- Pure Large Language Model (LLM) approaches for robotics often lack grounding and suffer from hallucinations, leading to unreliable failure detection and recovery.
- Robots require the ability to identify failures, understand their causes, generate appropriate recovery strategies, and adapt to changing conditions in real-time without extensive retraining.

## Method

- The **Recover** framework employs a ==neuro-symbolic== approach, integrating ==symbolic knowledge representation== via a comprehensive ontology (==OntoThor==) with Large Language Models for flexible planning.
- It operates through an ==online, cyclical process==: action execution, environment observation, symbolic explanation (==scene graph==, audio classification), ==ontological reasoning== for subgoal verification and recovery strategy generation, LLM-based planning, and plan execution.
- ==OntoThor== provides structured knowledge of objects, properties, relationships, and a detailed ==taxonomy of failures== with associated logical rules for detection and guidance for recovery.

## Results

- OntoThor's logical rules achieved **100%** accuracy in failure detection, outperforming LLM-based subgoal verification which achieved approximately **50%** accuracy.
- The framework demonstrated an approximately **70%** recovery rate for detected failures and effectively identified **100%** of safety issues, recovering from **93%** of them.
- Recover significantly reduced monetary costs compared to pure LLM-based methods while achieving a **59%** task completion rate for simple tasks and **33%** for complex tasks after recovery.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Neuro-symbolic integration effectively combines structured reasoning from ontologies with flexible planning from Large Language Models, mitigating limitations of individual approaches like LLM hallucinations.
> - A comprehensive failure taxonomy and a dedicated ontology (OntoThor) enable accurate, rule-based failure detection and guide context-aware recovery strategies.
> - The online, cyclical process of observation, explanation, reasoning, and planning allows for real-time adaptation and recovery during robotic task execution.
