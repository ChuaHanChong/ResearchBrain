# RLFTSim: Realistic and Controllable Multi-Agent Traffic Simulation via Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning

> [!summary] Summary
> RLFTSim introduces a reinforcement learning framework that fine-tunes pre-trained multi-agent traffic simulators to enhance realism and instill controllable behaviors. The method achieves state-of-the-art realism with a WOSAC RMM of 0.7867 and effectively distills diverse goal-conditioned maneuvers without relying on human feedback.

## Problem

- Existing multi-agent traffic simulators, particularly rule-based and open-loop learning-based models, suffer from a 'sim-to-real gap' and accumulate errors in closed-loop deployment, leading to unrealistic behaviors.
- The WOSAC Realism Meta-Metric (RMM) is too sparse and population-based to serve as an effective, sample-efficient reward signal for direct reinforcement learning.
- A lack of robust methods for distilling controllable, goal-conditioned behaviors into multi-agent simulators while maintaining high realism limits their utility for targeted scenario testing in autonomous driving development.

## Method

- **RLFTSim** fine-tunes a pre-trained ==imitation learning== model using reinforcement learning, employing a novel Meta-metric Leave-One-Out (MLOO) reward signal to provide dense, low-variance feedback for realism alignment.
- The framework incorporates Goal-Conditioned Fine-Tuning (GCFT) which integrates goal information via positional encoding or agent token concatenation, enabling the generation of specific maneuvers.
- Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) is utilized to address reward sparsity in goal-reaching tasks, augmenting training data with achieved goals to improve sample efficiency.

## Results

- RLFTSim achieved state-of-the-art realism on the WOSAC private test split with an RMM of **0.7867**, surpassing its SMART-tiny base model (**0.7824**) and other fine-tuning methods.
- The MLOO reward significantly outperformed other reward formulations (e.g., minADE, RLOO) in realism alignment, exhibiting empirically confirmed quadratic variance reduction and greater stability for RL training.
- Goal-Conditioned Fine-Tuning variants improved goal passing rates and enabled the generation of diverse, novel maneuvers (e.g., U-turns, specific turns) while largely preserving realism, with the positional encoding indication for goals maintaining higher realism (**0.7819** RMM with soft goals) compared to direct concatenation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The MLOO reward mechanism provides an unbiased gradient estimator and achieves quadratic variance reduction (O(1/N^2)), significantly improving the stability and sample efficiency of RL training for realism alignment.
> - Distilling controllability into learned traffic simulators is achievable by combining realism-aligned RL fine-tuning with goal-conditioned observations and a mixed reward signal, allowing agents to perform diverse, targeted maneuvers.
> - The framework is model-agnostic, demonstrating its ability to enhance realism and controllability across different base simulator architectures, including discrete-token and continuous-action models.
