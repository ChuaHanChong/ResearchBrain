# RoboFlamingo-Plus: Fusion of Depth and RGB Perception with Vision-Language Models for Enhanced Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> RoboFlamingo-Plus integrates depth data with RGB visual inputs into Vision-Language Models for robotic control, significantly improving zero-shot generalization capabilities on the CALVIN benchmark with a 10-20% performance lead over RGB-only methods.

## Problem

- Most Vision-Language Model (VLM)-based robotic control systems rely primarily on RGB visual data, neglecting crucial depth information.
- This reliance limits robustness and stability, particularly when dealing with varying lighting, occlusions, or novel 3D environments.
- Prior VLM frameworks like RoboFlamingo faced challenges in executing long-term tasks and generalizing to unseen environments due to their RGB-only perception.

## Method

- **RoboFlamingo-Plus** integrates pre-processed depth data alongside RGB inputs into the ==RoboFlamingo VLM== architecture for language-conditioned robotic control.
- It utilizes a frozen Vision Transformer (ViT) for encoding both RGB and depth information, followed by a fine-tuned Perceiver Resampler to compress and fuse these multimodal features.
- The solution employs a feature fusion decoder with fine-tuned gated cross-attention layers to align linguistic instructions with the combined visual-depth embeddings, outputting actions via an LSTM and MLP policy head.

## Results

- RoboFlamingo-Plus showed slight performance gains in imitation learning in familiar environments compared to the original RoboFlamingo.
- The model achieved a substantial 10-**20%** performance lead over RoboFlamingo in zero-shot generalization across novel visual-depth environments and enriched language instructions on the CALVIN benchmark.
- Ablation studies revealed that normalizing depth values based on the default dataset's range improved task success rates compared to a broader normalization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating depth information significantly enhances a robot's 3D spatial understanding and robustness, especially in novel or dynamic environments.
> - Effectively leveraging large pre-trained VLMs for robotics can be achieved by selectively fine-tuning specific components like the Perceiver Resampler and cross-attention mechanisms, rather than retraining the entire backbone.
> - The normalization strategy for depth data, particularly using statistics from a specific dataset range, is crucial for maintaining the fidelity and utility of spatial cues for the model.
