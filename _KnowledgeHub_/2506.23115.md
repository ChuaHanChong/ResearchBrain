# MoCa: Modality-aware Continual Pre-training Makes Better Bidirectional Multimodal Embeddings

> [!summary] Summary
> MoCa is a two-stage framework that transforms pre-trained causal Vision-Language Models (VLMs) into robust bidirectional multimodal embedding models by applying modality-aware continual pre-training on unlabeled data and heterogeneous contrastive fine-tuning. The MoCa-7B model achieved a state-of-the-art 71.5 average Precision@1 on the MMEB benchmark and strong performance on ViDoRe-v2, demonstrating improved generalization and scalability.

## Problem

- Many state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models (VLMs) are designed with causal attention for generation, which is suboptimal for embedding tasks requiring comprehensive context understanding.
- Current multimodal embedding methods heavily rely on large, costly labeled image-text pairs, limiting scalability and preventing the effective use of vast amounts of readily available unlabeled multimodal data.
- Existing VLM-based embedding approaches often use a single contrastive objective on relatively narrow datasets, which can lead to models that overfit and struggle to generalize to diverse multimodal scenarios beyond simple image-caption pairs.

## Method

- MoCa adapts pre-trained causal VLMs to a ==bidirectional attention mechanism== by removing causal masks and applying mean pooling for embedding extraction.
- It introduces ==Modality-aware Continual Pre-training (MCPT)== with a joint denoising objective (Masked Language Modeling for text and Masked Autoencoding for images) on massive unlabeled multimodal data.
- The framework incorporates ==Heterogeneous Contrastive Fine-tuning (HCFT)== using a broad range of supervised data, including long-form multimodal documents, curated pairs, and text-only pairs, along with task-aware batching.

## Results

- MoCa-7B achieved a state-of-the-art average Precision@1 score of **71.5** on the Massive Multimodal Embedding Benchmark (MMEB), surpassing prior models like mmE5 (**69.8**) and VLM2Vec (**62.9**).
- MoCa-3B, a smaller model, achieved an average **67.5** on MMEB and **59.8** NDCG@5 on ViDoRe-v2, demonstrating that the framework enables smaller models to match or exceed larger baselines.
- Ablation studies confirmed the individual contributions of Modality-aware Continual Pre-training (MLM and MAE) and Heterogeneous Contrastive Fine-tuning (diverse data types and task-aware batching) to the overall performance gains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Transforming causal VLMs into bidirectional encoders via continual pre-training with joint reconstruction (MLM+MAE) on unlabeled data significantly enhances multimodal embedding quality and scalability.
> - Leveraging diverse heterogeneous data sources and task-aware batching during contrastive fine-tuning is crucial for improving model generalization and robustness across various multimodal tasks.
> - The benefits of large-scale unlabeled continual pre-training can enable smaller models (e.g., 3B) to achieve performance comparable to or exceeding larger models (e.g., 7B) trained with less comprehensive methods.
