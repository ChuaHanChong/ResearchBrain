# FP3: A 3D Foundation Policy for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> FP3 (3D Foundation Policy) is the first large-scale 3D foundation policy model for robotic manipulation, which leverages point cloud data to achieve robust generalization across diverse tasks, objects, and environments. The model demonstrated a 100% success rate on household manipulation tasks like pouring water and folding towels, even with challenging variations such as novel objects or lighting changes.

## Problem

- Traditional robotic manipulation systems struggle with generalizing to novel scenarios, objects, and environments due to their reliance on limited 2D visual information.
- Current robot foundation models based on 2D images lack explicit 3D information, are sensitive to visual appearance changes, and have difficulty reasoning about occlusions and spatial constraints.
- Achieving precise and robust physical interaction in complex 3D environments requires a deeper understanding of object geometries and spatial relationships than 2D representations can provide.

## Method

- **FP3** (3D ==Foundation Policy==) is introduced as the first large-scale 3D foundation policy model for robotic manipulation, utilizing point clouds for explicit geometric information.
- The architecture employs a multimodal diffusion transformer, integrating point cloud features from third-person and wrist-view stereo cameras, natural language instructions via CLIP, and proprioceptive robot state.
- Training involves a pre-training phase on the DROID dataset (60,000 trajectories) followed by a parameter-efficient fine-tuning phase using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) for new tasks.

## Results

- FP3 achieved a **100%** success rate across four challenging household manipulation tasks (folding towels, cleaning tables, standing up cups, pouring water) after fine-tuning with only 80 demonstrations.
- The model demonstrated superior generalization, maintaining high success rates in 'in-the-wild' scenarios such as different lighting conditions (**100%**), novel objects (**100%**), and background changes (**100%**).
- Ablation studies confirmed that 3D representation is critical, with performance dropping from **100%** to **30%** average success rate when using 2D images instead of point clouds.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - 3D point cloud representations explicitly encode geometric information, making them more robust to lighting and viewpoint changes compared to 2D image observations for robotic manipulation.
> - A two-stage training approach with large-scale pre-training on a diverse dataset and subsequent parameter-efficient fine-tuning with LoRA enables data-efficient adaptation to new tasks.
> - Integrating multimodal inputs (point clouds from multiple views, language instructions, and proprioception) within a diffusion transformer architecture is crucial for comprehensive understanding and precise action generation.
