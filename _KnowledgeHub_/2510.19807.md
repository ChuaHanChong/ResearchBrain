# Scaf-GRPO: Scaffolded Group Relative Policy Optimization for Enhancing LLM Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and The University of Hong Kong introduce Scaf-GRPO, a progressive training framework that uses in-prompt hierarchical guidance to help Large Language Models overcome the 'learning cliff' in reinforcement learning from verifier rewards. This approach significantly boosts reasoning accuracy on challenging math and scientific benchmarks, improving pass@1 scores by up to a relative 44.3% on AIME24 for Qwen2.5-Math-7B.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) training with Reinforcement Learning from Verifier Rewards (RLVR) suffer from a "learning cliff" where all attempts on difficult problems yield zero rewards, stalling progress.
- Existing guidance methods, which often provide partial correct solutions (prefixes) from a teacher, introduce distributional mismatches and may restrict the model's exploratory flexibility.
- Policy optimization algorithms like GRPO collapse to zero advantage for these difficult problems, making them "invisible" to the learning gradient.

## Method

- **Scaf-GRPO** integrates a "==guidance exemption== period" to allow initial autonomous exploration and identify
- true-hard
- problems that genuinely require assistance.
- When a true-hard problem is encountered, Scaf-GRPO conducts a progressive search through a three-tiered hint hierarchy (knowledge, planning, solution) to find the minimal effective hint.
- The identified hint is injected directly into the user prompt (in-prompt scaffolding) to augment failed trajectories in the batch, restoring a non-zero reward signal for GRPO's policy update.

## Results

- Scaf-GRPO improved Qwen2.5-Math-**7B**'s pass@1 on AIME24 by a relative **44.3%** (from **30.0%** to **43.3%**) and achieved a **9.2%** relative improvement over the leading prefix-based method, LUFFY.
- The framework demonstrated strong generalization, boosting average performance by **10.3%** on Llama-3.2-**3B**-Instruct and **5.9%** on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-**1.5B** compared to vanilla GRPO.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both the initial guidance exemption period and the progressive, hierarchical nature of hints were essential for optimal performance, with the full framework outperforming variants by up to **9.2%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Minimal, hierarchical in-prompt guidance effectively reactivates the learning process on problems where LLMs previously received consistent zero rewards, avoiding learning stagnation.
> - Allowing an initial period of unguided exploration is crucial for the model to develop foundational skills and differentiate between truly intractable problems and those solvable with more training.
> - Injecting hints directly into the prompt ensures policy consistency by processing both problem and hint under a unified policy, mitigating distributional mismatches seen in prefix-based methods.
