# VideoCon: Robust Video-Language Alignment via Contrast Captions

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from UCLA and Google Research developed VideoCon, a dataset of video-language misalignments and natural language explanations (NLEs), to improve the robustness of video-language alignment models. Finetuning with VideoCon led to a generative model that achieved 84.6 ROC-AUC on entailment, state-of-the-art performance on text-to-video retrieval (e.g., 15.2 mAP on SSv2-Temporal), and accurate NLE generation, demonstrating enhanced fine-grained and temporal understanding.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art video-language models lack robustness to semantically plausible manipulations of video captions (e.g., altering entities, actions, or temporal order), which undermines their trustworthiness.
- Simply scaling up video-language pretraining datasets has shown diminishing returns for improving fine-grained understanding and robustness, indicating a need for higher quality, targeted data.
- Current models struggle with fine-grained details like temporal relationships and object counting, and often cannot provide natural language explanations for misalignments.

## Method

- Developed **VideoCon**, a novel dataset containing diverse, semantically plausible contrast captions and natural language explanations (NLEs) for video-text misalignments.
- Utilized a Large Language Model (LLM, PaLM-2) to scalably generate contrast captions across seven categories (e.g., object, action, event order flip) and corresponding NLEs for filtered, temporally-challenging video-caption pairs.
- Finetuned a generative video-language model (mPLUG-Owl-Video) on the **VideoCon** dataset using a multitask learning approach for both video-language entailment prediction and NLE generation.

## Results

- The finetuned model, Owl-Con, achieved an ROC-AUC of **84.6** on the VideoCon (LLM) entailment task, significantly outperforming Owl-Base (**57.2**) and atemporal baselines (**67.0**).
- Owl-Con established new state-of-the-art performance on text-to-video retrieval tasks, with **15.2** mAP on SSv2-Temporal and **11.4** mAP on SSv2-Events, and 41.1% accuracy on the ATP-Hard video question answering dataset.
- The model demonstrated strong capabilities in generating Natural Language Explanations, achieving a Q2 entailment score of **0.50** and PaLM-2 entailment accuracy of 65.4% for in-domain NLEs, while also generalizing to human-generated explanations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Targeted training with carefully constructed "hard negative" contrast captions is crucial for building robust fine-grained and temporal video-language understanding, outperforming random negative sampling.
> - LLMs can effectively generate high-quality, diverse contrastive data and natural language explanations, providing a scalable strategy to create challenging benchmarks and training examples.
> - Enhancing video-language alignment robustness through contrastive training and NLE generation directly translates to improved zero-shot performance on downstream tasks requiring fine-grained temporal and causal reasoning.
