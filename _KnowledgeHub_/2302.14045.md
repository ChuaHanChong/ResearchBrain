# Language Is Not All You Need: Aligning Perception with Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Microsoft researchers developed KOSMOS-1, a Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) capable of processing arbitrarily interleaved images and text. The model achieves strong zero-shot performance in perception-language tasks, such as 84.7 CIDEr on COCO for image captioning, and demonstrates rudimentary nonverbal reasoning with 22% accuracy on Raven IQ tests.

## Problem

- Traditional Large Language Models (LLMs) are limited to text data, lacking the inherent ability to perceive and interpret visual information from the real world.
- Achieving Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) necessitates a convergence of language, multimodal perception, action, and world modeling, which current text-only LLMs cannot natively support.
- Existing multimodal AI approaches often lack a unified framework for handling arbitrarily interleaved multimodal inputs and struggle with consistent cross-modal transfer and instruction following.

## Method

- **KOSMOS-1**, a ==Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM)== built on a Transformer-based causal language model (==MAGNETO==), was developed to process both text and image inputs in a unified sequence.
- A pre-trained ==CLIP ViT-L/14== model, with its last layer fine-tuned and a ==Resampler== module, was integrated as the vision encoder to efficiently incorporate visual information into the Transformer backbone.
- The model was trained from scratch on a massive, ==web-scale multimodal corpus== comprising monomodal text, cross-modal image-caption pairs, and ==interleaved image-text== data, followed by language-only instruction tuning.

## Results

- Achieved competitive or superior zero-shot performance on perception-language tasks, including **84.7 CIDEr** on COCO for image captioning and **51.0%** accuracy on VQAv2, outperforming smaller Flamingo models.
- Demonstrated nascent nonverbal reasoning abilities with **22%** accuracy on custom Raven IQ tests (**26%** with language-only instruction tuning) and robust OCR-free language understanding, reaching **72.9%** accuracy on Rendered SST-2 with Multimodal Chain-of-Thought prompting.
- Maintained strong performance on language-only tasks and exhibited significant cross-modal transfer benefits, with language-only instruction tuning boosting VQA accuracy by **+4.3 pp** and multimodal pre-training enhancing visual commonsense reasoning in language-only contexts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A unified Transformer-based architecture effectively aligns multimodal perception with language models, enabling them to process and understand interleaved text and image inputs in a cohesive manner.
> - Language-only instruction tuning, surprisingly, transfers and significantly enhances instruction-following capabilities in multimodal contexts, indicating a generalized understanding of instructions.
> - Multimodal pre-training fosters cross-modal knowledge transfer, where visual information improves language-only commonsense reasoning and learning in one modality benefits others, leading to a more grounded understanding of the world.
