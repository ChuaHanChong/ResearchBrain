# AdaPower: Specializing World Foundation Models for Predictive Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> AdaPower introduces a lightweight adaptation framework that transforms World Foundation Models (WFMs) into specialist models for predictive robotic manipulation. This approach enhances zero-shot generalization and long-horizon consistency by adding minimal trainable parameters, leading to improved performance in both simulated and real-world tasks.

## Problem

- World Foundation Models (WFMs) excel at simulating visual dynamics but lack the control-oriented precision required for robotic manipulation tasks.
- Current methods for adapting WFMs to robotics are computationally expensive, involve lengthy adaptation cycles, and do not fully leverage pre-trained generalist policies.
- Deployment of WFMs in closed-loop robotic control suffers from test-time distribution shifts and long-horizon inconsistency, where prediction errors accumulate.

## Method

- **AdaPower** employs a parameter-efficient adaptation strategy by introducing minimal trainable layers (less than 10% of the base WFM's parameters) into a frozen WFM backbone.
- A Temporal-Spatial Test-Time Training (TS-TTT) module is integrated to allow self-supervised optimization during inference, adapting to novel distribution shifts by leveraging the low-rank properties of 4D video features.
- A Memory Persistence (MP) module is introduced to maintain historical context via cross-attention with object-centric features, ensuring long-horizon consistency and combating error accumulation during multi-step rollouts.

## Results

- AdaPower achieved an average success rate of **41.5%** on ten unseen LIBERO manipulation tasks, demonstrating a **41%** improvement over baseline VLA models and outperforming other adapter methods.
- Qualitative evaluations confirmed AdaPower generates visually stable and physically consistent long-horizon trajectories for unseen tasks, unlike other methods that suffer from error accumulation and prediction degradation.
- The framework universally empowered various pre-trained VLA policies, increasing CogACT's success rate by **41%** and π0's by **27%**, and showed over **30%** improvement in average success rate in real-world manipulation experiments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Specializing large, pre-trained World Foundation Models for robotic control can be achieved efficiently with a small fraction of trainable parameters, preserving generalist knowledge.
> - Test-time adaptation tailored for the spatiotemporal characteristics of video data (TS-TTT) significantly improves model generalization to unseen environments and objects.
> - Integrating historical context through a memory module is crucial for maintaining physical consistency and preventing error propagation in long-horizon, autoregressive predictions.
