# ReLaX: Reasoning with Latent Exploration for Large Reasoning Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Hong Kong Polytechnic University and Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory introduce ReLaX, a framework that counters "entropy collapse" in Large Reasoning Models by promoting heterogeneity in their latent dynamics, rather than just token-level entropy. By employing Dynamic Spectral Dispersion based on Koopman operator theory, ReLaX improved average multimodal reasoning accuracy by up to 8.3 percentage points and consistently outperformed token-level exploration methods.

## Problem

- Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) in Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) often leads to 'entropy collapse,' where policies become over-deterministic, limiting exploration and resulting in suboptimal performance.
- Existing token-level exploration methods are limited as they address superficial symptoms, struggle to maintain high token entropy, and conflict with RL's natural tendency towards low-entropy policies.
- For Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs), token-level feedback can be misaligned with underlying cross-modal computations, reducing its effectiveness in reflecting deeper processing.

## Method

- **ReLaX**, a framework that integrates a novel metric for ==latent dynamics== into the policy optimization process of RLVR, intervenes at the level of internal computational flexibility rather than token-level stochasticity.
- Utilizes Koopman operator theory and Dynamic Mode Decomposition (DMD) to linearize the complex, nonlinear latent dynamics of LRMs, making them tractable for analysis.
- Introduces Dynamic Spectral Dispersion (DSD), a metric quantifying the heterogeneity of latent dynamics, which is then integrated into the GRPO objective with advantage shaping and adaptive KL regularization to guide exploration.

## Results

- ReLaX-VL-**3B** and ReLaX-VL-**7B** achieved absolute improvements of 8.3 and 5.3 percentage points, respectively, in average mean@1 accuracy across 7 multimodal reasoning benchmarks, setting a new performance level for **7B**-scale models.
- ReLaX consistently outperformed existing token-level exploration methods across both multimodal and text-only mathematical reasoning tasks, demonstrating better generalization across model families and more robust, semantically grounded reasoning.
- Ablation studies confirmed that DSD-based regularization, advantage shaping, and adaptive KL regularization are crucial for maintaining diverse latent dynamics, preventing entropy collapse, and ensuring stable, high-reward policy optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Entropy collapse in LRMs is a symptom of rigid internal computations within the latent space, which lose flexibility under RLVR, suggesting a deeper problem than token-level over-determinism.
> - Koopman operator theory provides a robust framework for linearizing and analyzing the high-dimensional, nonlinear latent dynamics of LRMs, allowing for a principled quantification of their exploration capacity.
> - Guiding exploration by promoting heterogeneity in latent dynamics (quantified by DSD) offers a more effective and stable approach to the exploration-exploitation tradeoff than interventions in the discrete token space.
