# 3D Dynamics-Aware Manipulation: Endowing Manipulation Policies with 3D Foresight

> [!summary] Summary
> The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology researchers developed "3D Dynamics-Aware Manipulation," a framework providing robotic policies with "3D foresight" by integrating 3D world modeling through current depth, future RGB-D, and 3D flow predictions. This approach enhances policies' spatial awareness and robustness, achieving higher success rates in depth-critical manipulation tasks across diverse simulations and real-world experiments with minimal inference latency.

## Problem

- Existing robotic manipulation policies primarily rely on 2D visual world models, leading to an insufficient representation of crucial depth information.
- Robust manipulation tasks often involve significant depth-wise movement, precise distance guidance, and intricate obstacle avoidance, for which 2D-centric models are inadequate.

## Method

- The **3D Foresight Manipulation** framework utilizes a ==causal transformer architecture== to integrate multimodal inputs and jointly model language, vision, proprioception, 3D world dynamics, and robotic actions.
- It employs three complementary ==self-supervised learning== tasks: ==current depth estimation==, ==future RGB-D prediction==, and ==3D flow prediction== to explicitly teach 3D dynamics.
- A ==preprocessing pipeline== automatically annotates depth and 3D flow using state-of-the-art ==monocular depth estimators== and ==3D trackers== for datasets lacking ground-truth 3D information.

## Results

- "3D Foresight" improved task completion on CALVIN, achieving **4.08** (D0D) and **4.23** (ABC0D) average solved tasks with pretraining, and increased the average success rate on LIBERO to **95.3%**.
- It outperformed 2D foresight baselines by larger margins (e.g., **+0.11** in CALVIN D0D and **+0.17** in LIBERO) and showed superior performance in real-world depth-critical tasks, like "stack two cups" (**70%** success vs. **35%** for 2D foresight).
- The integration of 3D foresight incurred a negligible increase in inference latency, adding only **6 ms** compared to a 2D world-modeling baseline (**112 ms** vs. **106 ms**), demonstrating practical efficiency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly learning 3D dynamics provides policies with "3D foresight," significantly enhancing spatial awareness and enabling more accurate action planning in 3D space beyond superficial 2D pixel dynamics.
> - The proposed self-supervised learning objectives (current depth, future RGB-D, and 3D flow prediction) are complementary and mutually beneficial, leading to a more calibrated and effective world model.
> - Shared 3D dynamics between scene transformation and low-level robotic actions can be effectively leveraged to improve policy understanding of physical interactions and achieve robust behaviors.
