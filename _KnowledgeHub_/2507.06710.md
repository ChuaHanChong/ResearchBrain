# Spatial-Temporal Aware Visuomotor Diffusion Policy Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> A new visuomotor imitation learning method, 4D Diffusion Policy (DP4), integrates a dynamic Gaussian world model to capture 3D spatial structures and 4D spatiotemporal dynamics. This approach significantly improves task success rates and generalization across diverse simulated and real-world robotic manipulation tasks without increasing inference time.

## Problem

- Existing visual imitation learning methods primarily rely on behavior cloning, limiting their ability to understand underlying physical rules and generalize to novel scenarios.
- Current approaches struggle to inherently capture the complex 3D geometry and dynamic 4D spatiotemporal relationships within interactive environments.
- The lack of explicit spatial-temporal awareness hinders robust object interaction and environment-aware decision-making for robots in varied real-world settings.

## Method

- DP4 introduces a dynamic Gaussian world model, built using 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), to provide structured supervision for learning 3D spatial and 4D spatiotemporal perceptions.
- It implements multi-level 3D spatial awareness from single-view RGB-D inputs to reconstruct scene geometry and 4D spatiotemporal awareness to predict future environmental states.
- A denoising diffusion framework is conditioned on these learned structured spatial-temporal representations to generate optimal, physically-grounded action trajectories.

## Results

- DP4 achieved an 84.7% overall success rate on Adroit simulation tasks, a 16.4% improvement over the 3D Diffusion Policy (68.3%).
- It demonstrated an 82.5% overall success rate on DexArt simulation tasks, outperforming 3D Diffusion Policy (68.5%) by 14.0%.
- In real-world robotic tasks, DP4 obtained a 54.6% overall success rate, an 8.6% improvement compared to 3D Diffusion Policy (46.0%), confirming practical applicability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly modeling 3D spatial structures and 4D spatiotemporal dynamics through a dynamic Gaussian world model offers richer supervision than pure behavior cloning.
> - Integrating Gaussian Splatting allows for efficient and high-quality representation and prediction of dynamic 3D scenes for robotic policy learning.
> - Conditioning diffusion policies on learned spatial-temporal representations significantly enhances generalization and robustness in complex robotic manipulation tasks.
