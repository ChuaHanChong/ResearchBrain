# VideoREPA: Learning Physics for Video Generation through Relational Alignment with Foundation Models

> [!summary] Summary
> VideoREPA introduces a representation alignment method to inject physics understanding from video foundation models into pre-trained text-to-video diffusion models. This approach, which does not require external physics simulators or specialized datasets, improved the Physical Commonsense (PC) score of a CogVideoX-5B baseline by 24.1% on the VideoPhy benchmark, reaching 40.1.

## Problem

- Text-to-video (T2V) diffusion models often generate videos lacking physical plausibility, exhibiting inconsistencies in object behavior and motion dynamics.
- Current T2V models possess significantly weaker implicit physics understanding compared to video foundation models (VFMs) like VideoMAEv2.
- This gap results in issues such as unrealistic object deformation, irrational movements, and implausible interactions.

## Method

- Proposes **VideoREPA**, a representation alignment method for injecting physics understanding from video foundation models (VFMs) into pre-trained text-to-video (T2V) diffusion models.
- Employs a Token Relation Distillation (TRD) loss that aligns relational patterns (spatial and temporal) between tokens from VFMs and T2V models, providing soft guidance.
- The method finetunes pre-trained ==latent diffusion== models by combining the standard diffusion loss with the TRD loss, without requiring external physics simulators or specialized datasets.

## Results

- VideoREPA-5B achieved a Physical Commonsense (PC) score of 40.1 on the VideoPhy benchmark, marking a 24.1% improvement over the CogVideoX-5B baseline (32.3).
- Showed improved physical consistency across interaction types, including 42.9% for Solid-Solid, 16.7% for Solid-Fluid, and 20.6% for Fluid-Fluid interactions.
- Surpassed other physics-aware methods on open-domain datasets and improved the PC score by 4.57 points on the VideoPhy2 benchmark.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Enhanced physics understanding directly improves the generation quality and physical consistency of videos produced by T2V models.
> - Aligning relational structures between tokens through "soft" guidance (Token Relation Distillation) is effective for knowledge transfer to pre-trained diffusion models without degrading their existing capabilities.
> - Video foundation models possess implicit physics understanding that can be distilled into generative models, bridging the gap between generation and comprehension.
