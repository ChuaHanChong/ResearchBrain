# Thinking with Drafts: Speculative Temporal Reasoning for Efficient Long Video Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> SpecTemp introduces a dual-model framework for long video understanding, employing a lightweight draft MLLM for rapid frame selection and a powerful target MLLM for iterative reasoning. This approach enhances inference efficiency by 10-23% across various video lengths while achieving competitive or improved accuracy compared to existing methods.

## Problem

- Long videos present significant computational challenges for multi-modal large language models (MLLMs) due to their extended temporal contexts and the context window limitations of current models.
- The 'thinking-with-frames' paradigm, while effective for complex spatio-temporal reasoning, creates an efficiency bottleneck by accumulating extensive multi-modal contexts with high visual redundancy.
- Analysis revealed that over 90% of visual tokens in existing MLLM contexts contribute minimally to reasoning, indicating substantial computational inefficiency.

## Method

- **SpecTemp** proposes a cooperative dual-model design consisting of a lightweight 3B ==Draft MLLM== for dense visual perception and a powerful 7B Target MLLM for high-level temporal reasoning and verification.
- It implements an iterative speculation-verification process where the Draft MLLM rapidly selects a compact set of salient frames from densely sampled regions, which the Target MLLM then verifies and integrates into its accumulated reasoning trace.
- The framework is optimized through a two-stage process: supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on the SpecTemp-80K dataset to build foundational capabilities, followed by reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT) using specialized reward signals for both models.

## Results

- SpecTemp reduced inference latency by approximately 14-**19%** on short-form videos and 10-**23%** on long-form videos, compared to baseline MLLMs like Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** and VideoChat-R1.5, while maintaining or improving accuracy.
- The framework achieved substantial accuracy improvements on long-form benchmarks, including a +**12.0%** gain on Video-Holmes and +**8.0%** on MLVU over Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** (average 14.5 frames vs 16 frames).
- Ablation studies confirmed that the dual-model collaboration, specialized training strategies (SFT+RL), and custom reward components are essential for achieving optimal efficiency and performance, outperforming single-model or uniform sampling baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling temporal perception from high-level reasoning through a cooperative dual-model design can effectively mitigate the efficiency bottleneck in long video understanding without compromising accuracy.
> - Speculative decoding principles, traditionally used for LLM inference acceleration, can be adapted to the multi-modal domain to achieve reasoning-level acceleration in complex video tasks.
> - A custom-built, large-scale dataset (SpecTemp-80K) with synchronized coarse and fine-grained annotations is crucial for effectively training iterative, dual-level reasoning systems in video understanding.
