# X-Token: Projection-Guided Cross-Tokenizer Knowledge Distillation

> [!summary] Summary
> X-Token introduces a projection-guided cross-tokenizer knowledge distillation framework that enables efficient knowledge transfer between language models with different tokenizers. It consistently outperforms prior methods like GOLD, achieving an average gain of +3.82 points with a Qwen3-4B teacher and +0.5 points with a Phi-4-mini teacher, and demonstrates benefits in multi-teacher distillation.

## Problem

- Traditional knowledge distillation methods rely on shared tokenizers, limiting the choice of teacher models and hindering multi-teacher distillation across diverse tokenizer families.
- Existing logit-distribution-based cross-tokenizer methods, like GOLD, suffer from "uncommon-token failure" where critical tokens (e.g., multi-digit numerals) receive degraded supervision.
- These methods also exhibit "over-conservative matching" due to strict string-equality criteria, overlooking functionally equivalent token pairs and losing useful alignment signals.

## Method

- A dynamic programming-based span alignment method identifies text-consistent chunks across teacher and student token sequences, providing robust positional correspondence.
- A sparse projection matrix, W, is deterministically constructed to map student-token probabilities into the teacher's vocabulary space, bridging incompatible vocabularies.
- Two complementary loss formulations, Projection-Guided KL (P-KL) and Hybrid Loss with Relaxed Matching (H-KL), are introduced to address specific shortcomings of prior hybrid loss designs, selected based on token coverage audit.

## Results

- X-Token consistently outperformed the state-of-the-art GOLD method, showing an average gain of +3.82 points with a Qwen3-4B teacher and +0.5 points with a Phi-4-mini teacher.
- P-KL dramatically improved GSM8k accuracy by approximately 6-fold (from 2.56 to 15.54) with a Qwen3-4B teacher, outperforming even same-tokenizer KD from a weaker teacher.
- Multi-teacher distillation, combining Phi-mini (H-KL) with Llama-3B (standard KL), yielded the highest overall average accuracy of 40.48, exceeding the best single-teacher cross-tokenizer run by +1.3 points.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - P-KL effectively resolves "uncommon-token failure" by projecting the student's entire distribution into the teacher's vocabulary space, ensuring all tokens receive identity-aware supervision.
> - H-KL mitigates "over-conservative matching" by expanding the "common" token set through relaxed matching using the projection matrix W, capturing broader semantic correspondences.
> - Optimal distillation performance depends on dynamically selecting either P-KL or H-KL based on whether critical tokens are adequately covered by the common set for a given teacher-student tokenizer pair.
