# PSR: Scaling Multi-Subject Personalized Image Generation with Pairwise Subject-Consistency Rewards

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Zhejiang University and Huawei Inc. developed a scalable framework for multi-subject personalized image generation, introducing a novel data generation pipeline and a reinforcement learning (RL) strategy with Pairwise Subject-Consistency Rewards (PSR). Their method achieved state-of-the-art performance on a new comprehensive benchmark, PSRBench, demonstrating superior subject consistency, semantic alignment, and aesthetic quality in generating images with multiple specified subjects.

## Problem

- Existing personalized image generation models struggle to maintain subject consistency and adhere to complex textual prompts in multi-subject scenarios.
- A significant lack of high-quality, large-scale multi-subject personalized image datasets hinders the training of robust generative models.
- Current training strategies, like supervised fine-tuning, optimize at a global image level, failing to ensure fine-grained, subject-specific consistency when multiple subjects are present.

## Method

- A scalable ==multi-subject== data generation pipeline was developed, leveraging single-subject personalization models and Large Language Models (LLMs) to synthesize diverse and consistent training data.
- A novel Pairwise Subject-Consistency Reward (PSR) was introduced within a reinforcement learning (RL) framework to explicitly optimize for subject identity preservation in multi-subject outputs.
- A scalable frame-wise positional encoding scheme was devised to extend single-subject models, enabling them to handle varying numbers of input reference images without imposing fixed spatial biases.

## Results

- The 'Ours (PSR)' model achieved state-of-the-art performance on the PSRBench benchmark, with overall scores of **0.673** for subject consistency, 1.124 for aesthetic preference, and **0.783** for semantic alignment.
- The RL stage with PSR led to a substantial improvement in subject consistency (from **0.559** to **0.673** compared to SFT-only) and restored overall aesthetic quality, demonstrating the effectiveness of the targeted reward mechanism.
- A user study showed that images generated by 'Ours (PSR)' were preferred across all metrics (subject consistency, semantic alignment, and aesthetic preference) over outputs from other state-of-the-art methods.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly calculating consistency rewards at a pairwise subject level during RL fine-tuning significantly improves subject identity preservation in generated images, surpassing global image-level optimization.
> - A data generation pipeline that synthesizes multi-subject data by re-contextualizing single-subject instances can overcome data scarcity and produce high-quality, consistent training examples.
> - Employing a virtual temporal offset for positional encoding, instead of spatial offsets, enables a single-subject model to generalize effectively to multi-subject inputs without introducing unwanted spatial priors.
