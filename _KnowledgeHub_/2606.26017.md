# G2DP: Diffusion Planning with Spatio-Temporal Grid Guidance

> [!summary] Summary
> G2DP introduces a diffusion-based motion planner that uses differentiable spatio-temporal grid guidance to integrate dense environmental constraints directly into trajectory generation for autonomous vehicles. This approach achieves state-of-the-art performance among imitation-based planners on nuPlan, improving reactive driving scores by 8.4 points on Test14-hard and showing significant gains in collision avoidance (+10.15 points) and progress (+10.5 points) on interPlan and DeepScenario benchmarks.

## Problem

- Autonomous driving motion planners struggle to generate safe and efficient trajectories in complex, dynamic environments due to the limitations of traditional rule-based and existing learning-based methods.
- Diffusion-based generative models for motion planning, while capable of diverse trajectory generation, lack explicit mechanisms to guarantee safety and compliance with driving rules in closed-loop execution.
- Current guidance strategies for diffusion planners are either sparse (entity-centric geometric queries) or post-hoc (rule-based refinement), leading to limited situational awareness, rigidity, or deviations from learned behaviors.

## Method

- Proposes **G2DP**, a grid-guided diffusion planner that integrates a ==Diffusion Transformer (DiT)== for trajectory generation with a U-Net for spatio-temporal occupancy grid prediction.
- Constructs a differentiable spatio-temporal cost volume by fusing predicted future occupancy grids with route desirability maps, providing a continuous cost landscape.
- Employs classifier guidance to inject dense, analytical gradients derived from the fused cost volume directly into the diffusion model's denoising loop, proactively steering trajectory generation towards safe and progress-optimal pathways.

## Results

- G2DP achieved state-of-the-art performance among purely imitation-based planners on the nuPlan benchmark, with its history-free variant (G2DPext†^	ext{\dagger}ext†) improving reactive driving scores by **8.4** points on Test14-hard.
- Demonstrated robust zero-shot generalization on the interPlan benchmark, outperforming Diffusion Planner by **9.65** points overall and improving collision avoidance by **10.15** points and Time-to-Collision by **10.75** points.
- In a zero-shot evaluation on the DeepScenario urban dataset, G2DP improved collision avoidance by approximately **11.0** points and progress by **10.5** points compared to Diffusion Planner, showcasing robustness to distribution shifts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Densely represented spatio-temporal cost volumes, fusing probabilistic occupancy with route progress, enable more proactive and continuous steering of generative planning compared to sparse geometric guidance.
> - Injecting energy-based guidance during the later stages of the diffusion denoising process is crucial for refining macroscopic trajectory topology towards safety and progress without disrupting the model's initial behavioral intent.
> - A Top-K aggregation strategy for the ego vehicle's footprint on the cost grid provides a robust and differentiable mechanism for calculating trajectory costs, balancing safety coverage with robustness to noise.
