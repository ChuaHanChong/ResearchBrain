# Ovis-U1 Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Ovis-U1, a 3-billion-parameter unified model from Alibaba Group, integrates multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and image editing capabilities, achieving competitive performance across these tasks through a six-stage unified training approach that simultaneously optimizes all components.

## Problem

- Developing AI models capable of seamlessly integrating and operating across multiple modalities (text, image) for both understanding and generation tasks.
- Endowing multimodal understanding models with image generation capabilities without relying on frozen or decoupled components.
- Designing an effective unified training approach that allows for collaborative improvement across understanding and generation tasks, rather than separate or sequential optimization.

## Method

- Introduced ==Ovis-U1==, a 3-billion-parameter unified model architecture built on Qwen3-1.7B, incorporating a visual encoder, a ==diffusion transformer (MMDiT)== as a visual decoder, and a novel ==bidirectional token refiner==.
- Implemented a ==six-stage progressive unified training procedure==, updating the LLM and other components using a diverse dataset that includes multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and various image editing data.
- Utilized architectural elements like an ==adaptive visual encoder== for arbitrary image resolutions and a learnable CLS token in the refiner for improved cross-modal interaction.

## Results

- Ovis-U1 achieved an average score of **69.6** on OpenCompass Multi-modal Academic Benchmarks for understanding, outperforming other open-source models in its parameter range.
- Demonstrated strong generative capabilities, scoring **0.89** on GenEval and **83.72** on DPG-Bench for text-to-image generation.
- Secured competitive results in image editing, with a score of **4.00** on ImgEdit-Bench and **6.42** on GEdit-Bench-EN, comparable to larger proprietary models in some aspects.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training a unified model from a language model, with collaborative optimization across understanding and generation tasks, improves performance in both domains.
> - A bidirectional token refiner enhances interaction between visual and textual embeddings, contributing to better conditional guidance in generation and reducing reliance on external models like CLIP.
> - Progressive training stages, particularly incorporating image editing data late in the pipeline, can synergistically boost overall multimodal capabilities.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of Ovis-U1 directly addresses one of the most significant and rapidly evolving trends in artificial intelligence: the convergence of multimodal understanding and generation into unified models. Historically, AI models have often specialized in single modalities (e.g., text understanding, image generation). However, the recent advent of powerful unified models, epitomized by OpenAI’s GPT-4o (2025), has demonstrated the immense potential of systems capable of seamlessly integrating and operating across multiple modalities, particularly text and vision.

GPT-4o’s ability to perform complex visual tasks—such as image editing, multi-view synthesis, style transfer, object detection, and depth estimation—through natural language dialogue marked a significant paradigm shift. This has inspired a wave of research into creating AI models that can not only comprehend diverse inputs (text, image) but also generate outputs in different modalities (text, image) and manipulate existing data based on instructions. Ovis-U1 positions itself directly in this competitive and rapidly advancing landscape.

The paper highlights two fundamental questions arising from this shift:
1. **How can a multimodal understanding model be endowed with image generation capabilities?** This involves designing visual decoders that can seamlessly interact with existing MLLMs. Ovis-U1 addresses this by incorporating a diffusion-based visual decoder and a bidirectional token refiner.
2. **How can a unified model be effectively trained on both understanding and generation tasks?** Many previous approaches either focused on one task or used frozen MLLMs for generation, limiting the synergistic potential. Ovis-U1 proposes a novel "unified training approach starting from a language model," which collaboratively enhances performance across both understanding and generation. This challenges the common practice of using pre-trained and frozen MLLMs as backbones for generation tasks, arguing that end-to-end unified training yields superior results.

By openly releasing Ovis-U1, Alibaba aims to accelerate the collective progress in AI, particularly in developing general-purpose AI systems. This contributes to the growing ecosystem of open-source large models that can serve as foundations for further research and application development, contrasting with proprietary models like GPT-4o. The project's ambition is to provide a competitive, compact (3-billion-parameter) model that pushes the boundaries of multimodal AI, offering an alternative to larger, more resource-intensive proprietary systems. It competes directly with other emerging open-source unified models such as BAGEL, UniWorld-V1, and OmniGen/OmniGen2, particularly in the 3B-parameter range.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the Ovis-U1 project are multi-faceted:

- **Develop a Unified Multimodal AI Model:** The core objective is to create a single, cohesive AI model capable of performing diverse tasks across modalities, specifically multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and image editing. This moves beyond task-specific models towards more versatile, general-purpose AI.
- **Achieve State-of-the-Art Performance within a Compact Footprint:** The authors aim for Ovis-U1, with its 3-billion-parameter architecture, to achieve performance comparable to or surpassing leading models, including larger ones and those from proprietary sources (like GPT-4o), across understanding, generation, and editing benchmarks. This emphasizes efficiency and practical deployment viability.
- **Investigate and Validate Unified Training Strategies:** A key motivation is to explore and demonstrate the effectiveness of a novel unified training approach. Unlike some prior models that use frozen MLLMs for generation, Ovis-U1 is trained from a language model on both understanding and generation tasks simultaneously. The hypothesis is that this collaborative training enhances performance in both domains, a critical research question addressed directly by this work.
- **Enhance Modality Interaction:** The project seeks to improve the interaction between textual and visual embeddings within the model. This is driven by the introduction of specific architectural components like the bidirectional token refiner and advanced visual decoders designed for better feature alignment and synthesis.
- **Open-Source Contribution:** A significant stated objective is to open-source Ovis-U1. This is motivated by a desire to accelerate AI development within the broader community, foster collaborative research, and contribute to the creation of advanced multimodal reasoning and manipulation systems that are accessible for further innovation.
- **Address Practical Challenges in Multimodal AI:** By supporting a diverse range of tasks—from high-fidelity image generation to precise image editing and nuanced image understanding—Ovis-U1 aims to provide a robust solution for real-world multimodal challenges, enhancing precision and flexibility in handling complex tasks.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Ovis-U1 is a 3-billion-parameter unified model designed to integrate multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and image editing capabilities. Its methodology is characterized by a novel architecture and a sophisticated multi-stage unified training procedure.

#### 3.1. Architecture

The Ovis-U1 architecture (totaling 3.644 billion parameters, including a 1.72B LLM and 1.046B visual decoder) builds upon the existing Ovis series by augmenting it with generative components.

- **LLM & Text Tokenizer:** The core language model is initialized from **Qwen3-1.7B**. Crucially, unlike previous approaches that fix a multimodal LLM (e.g., Qwen-VL) as the backbone, Ovis-U1's LLM is trained from a *language model* and updated using both visual understanding and generation data, promoting a true unified learning.
- **Visual Encoder & Adapter:** The visual encoder is based on **Aimv2-large-patch14-448**, adapted to handle arbitrary image resolutions through interpolation of positional embeddings and the incorporation of 2D Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE). It also utilizes a variable-length sequence attention mechanism for efficient batch processing. An adapter, using a probabilistic tokenization scheme, bridges the visual encoder with the LLM, converting visual features into a weighted average from a learnable embedding table.
- **Visual Decoder & VAE:** For image generation, Ovis-U1 employs a diffusion transformer as its visual decoder, specifically **MMDiT (Multimodal Diffusion Transformer)** with RoPE. This 1-billion-parameter decoder is initialized randomly and trained from scratch. A frozen **SDXL VAE** (Variational AutoEncoder) is used to encode images into latents and decode latents back to images, managing the computational load on the decoder. The visual semantic embedding (from the LLM) is concatenated with text embeddings, and context image tokens (visually detailed embeddings from the VAE encoder) are fed into the decoder's visual stream.
- **Refiner:** A novel **bidirectional token refiner** is introduced to enhance the interaction between visual and textual embeddings, especially for conditional guidance in generation. It consists of two stacked transformer blocks with a modulation mechanism. To capture comprehensive information, it concatenates features from the LLM's last and second-to-last layers. Furthermore, to replace the role of external models like CLIP for global feature capture, a learnable `[CLS]` token is incorporated into the refiner's input.

#### 3.2. Data Composition

Ovis-U1 is trained on a diverse and extensive multimodal dataset, categorized into three types:

- **Multimodal Understanding Data:** A blend of public datasets (COYO, Wukong, Laion, ShareGPT4V, CC3M) and in-house data, rigorously preprocessed to filter noise, enhance caption quality, and balance data ratios.
- **Text-to-Image Generation Data:** Primarily derived from Laion5B (samples with aesthetic score > 6), with detailed descriptions for each image generated using the Qwen model to create the Laion-aes6 dataset, augmented by JourneyDB.
- **Image+Text-to-Image Generation Data (Editing and Control):** This specialized category includes:
 - **Image Editing Data:** OmniEdit, UltraEdit, SeedEdit.
 - **Reference-image-driven Generation Data:** Subjects200K, SynCD (subject-driven), StyleBooth (style-driven).
 - **Pixel-Level Controlled Generation Data:** MultiGen 20M for tasks like canny-to-image, depth-to-image, inpainting, and outpainting.
 - **In-House Data:** Additional datasets covering style-driven generation, content removal, style translation, de-noise/de-blur, colorization, and text rendering.

#### 3.3. Training Procedure (Six-Stage Progressive Pipeline)

The model undergoes a carefully orchestrated six-stage training process, distinct from methods that use pre-trained and fixed MLLMs.

- **Stage 0: Visual Decoder Pretraining:** Initializes the randomly weighted visual decoder and refiner to develop basic text-to-image generation capabilities using T2I data.
- **Stage 1: Adapter Pretraining:** Trains the randomly initialized adapter to align visual and textual embeddings, using understanding, T2I generation, and image editing tasks.
- **Stage 2: Visual Encoder Alignment:** Fine-tunes both the visual encoder and adapter, again across all three task types, to further refine cross-modal alignment.
- **Stage 3: Understanding Learning:** The visual encoder, adapter, and crucially, the LLM parameters are trained on understanding tasks. After this stage, these parameters are fixed to preserve understanding ability.
- **Stage 4: Generation Learning:** The refiner and visual decoder are trained to align with the *optimized* text and image embeddings resulting from Stage 3. This leverages the refined understanding capabilities for better generation.
- **Stage 5: Generation Fine-tuning:** The final stage fine-tunes the decoder specifically for text-to-image and image editing tasks, incorporating image editing data to enhance manipulation capabilities.

This progressive, unified training approach is a cornerstone of Ovis-U1's methodology, enabling collaborative improvement across understanding and generation, unlike models that decouple these processes or rely on frozen components.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Ovis-U1 demonstrates strong performance across multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and image editing tasks, especially given its relatively compact 3-billion-parameter size.

- **Overall Performance (Table 3):** Ovis-U1 achieves competitive scores against state-of-the-art unified models, including larger ones and those from major labs. Its combined parameter count (2.4B for understanding components + 1.2B for generative components) is efficient.
- **Multimodal Understanding (Table 4):** Ovis-U1 achieves an average score of **69.6** on the OpenCompass Multi-modal Academic Benchmarks. This surpasses other leading open-source models in the 2-3B parameter range, such as Ristretto-3B (67.7) and SAIL-VL-1.5-2B (67.0). This result is significant as it validates the hypothesis that unified training, integrating generation tasks, can indeed *enhance* understanding performance, countering the common trade-offs seen in other unified models that might underperform their MLLM backbones.
- **Text-to-Image Generation (Tables 5 & 6):**
 - On **GenEval**, Ovis-U1 scores **0.89**, outperforming competitors like BAGEL (0.82), UniWorld-V1 (0.80), and OmniGen2 (0.80) when using raw prompts, and even surpassing GPT-4o's reported 0.84 on this benchmark (though GPT-4o's performance might differ with rewritten prompts). Its high scores across object, counting, and position metrics indicate strong compositional generation capabilities.
 - On **DPG-Bench**, it achieves **83.72**, which is highly competitive, especially in Relation (93.35) and Entity (90.08) generation, showing strong semantic understanding and coherence.
- **Image Editing (Tables 7 & 8):**
 - For **ImgEdit-Bench**, Ovis-U1 scores **4.00**, leading among most compared models (e.g., OmniGen2 at 3.44, BAGEL at 3.20), and is remarkably close to GPT-4o's reported 4.20. It excels particularly in "Replace" (4.45) and "Add" (4.13) tasks.
 - On **GEdit-Bench-EN**, it achieves **6.42**, demonstrating strong overall editing capabilities, being competitive with BAGEL (6.519) and other specialized editing models.
- **Ablation Studies:**
 - **Refiner Design (Table 9):** The ablation studies highlight the importance of the token refiner. Concatenating features from the last and second-to-last LLM layers for the refiner yielded better results than using only the last layer. The "clip-free" approach using a learnable `[CLS]` token showed promising results, particularly on larger datasets, suggesting potential for further improvement and reducing reliance on external models.
 - **Unified Training Impact on Understanding (Table 10):** The comparison with a baseline Ovis model (without unified training) showed a notable **1.14-point improvement** in the average OpenCompass score for Ovis-U1. This empirically confirms the benefits of integrating generation and editing tasks for improving understanding capabilities.
 - **Unified Training Impact on Generation (Tables 11 & 12):** Generation performance consistently improved across the progressive training stages (Stage 1, 4, 5). The inclusion of image editing data in Stage 5, in particular, led to a 0.77 improvement on DPG-Bench, underscoring the synergistic benefits of multimodal training data.
- **Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG):** The model shows robustness to variations in CFG settings for image editing, with optimal settings varying by benchmark.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Ovis-U1 represents a significant advancement in the field of unified multimodal AI, particularly for open-source models within a constrained parameter budget. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

- **Pioneering Unified Training Paradigm:** The most significant contribution of Ovis-U1 is its empirical validation of the "unified training" approach, where understanding and generation capabilities are mutually enhanced through collaborative training, rather than treating them as separate tasks or relying on frozen MLLM backbones. This challenges existing norms and provides a new blueprint for developing more capable general-purpose multimodal AI.
- **Democratization of Advanced Multimodal AI:** By open-sourcing a model with competitive state-of-the-art performance across understanding, generation, and editing, Alibaba contributes immensely to the broader AI community. This lowers the barrier to entry for researchers and developers, enabling wider experimentation, replication, and further innovation in multimodal AI, akin to how open-source large language models have accelerated NLP research.
- **Efficiency and Scalability:** Achieving top-tier performance with only 3 billion parameters (roughly 2.4B for understanding and 1.2B for generation) demonstrates remarkable efficiency. This makes Ovis-U1 more accessible for deployment in resource-constrained environments or for researchers without access to supercomputing clusters, expanding the practical applicability of unified models.
- **Versatility in Applications:** Ovis-U1's combined capabilities (understanding, T2I generation, image editing) make it a highly versatile tool. It can power a wide range of real-world applications, from creative content generation and professional image manipulation to sophisticated visual question answering systems, all controllable via natural language. This versatility streamlines workflows that previously required multiple specialized models.
- **Foundation for Future AGI Research:** The paper explicitly frames Ovis-U1 as a step towards "artificial general intelligence (AGI)." By demonstrating robust multimodal reasoning and manipulation within a single framework, it lays foundational groundwork for more holistic AI systems that can interact with and understand the world more akin to humans. The outlined future work (scaling, better data, architectural innovations, reinforcement learning) further emphasizes this long-term vision.
- **Innovation in Model Architecture:** The introduction of the bidirectional token refiner with learnable `[CLS]` tokens to replace external CLIP models and the adaptive visual encoder for arbitrary resolutions showcases architectural innovations that improve cross-modal interaction and practical utility.

In conclusion, Ovis-U1 is a compelling demonstration of what can be achieved in unified multimodal AI with thoughtful architectural design and, critically, a collaborative training paradigm. Its open-source release makes it a valuable asset for the AI community, pushing the boundaries of what compact, versatile AI models can accomplish.
