# Exploring Data Scaling Trends and Effects in Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at ByteDance Seed developed data-centric strategies for Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) to mitigate reward hacking and improve response diversity in large language models. Their approach, combining a hybrid reward system, intelligent prompt selection, and early-stage task prioritization, improved overall performance by 1.4 points on challenging test sets and led to specific gains of up to 3.9 points in STEM tasks.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) trained with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) often suffer from "reward hacking," where they optimize for reward scores without truly improving performance or aligning with human preferences.
- RLHF training can lead to a deterioration of model response diversity, resulting in less varied and creative outputs as the model prematurely converges on coarse distinctions.
- Simply increasing the quantity of RLHF training data does not reliably improve performance, indicating data-driven bottlenecks beyond scale.

## Method

- Implemented a hybrid reward system combining objective ==Reasoning Task Verifiers== (RTVs) and a Generative Reward Model (GenRM) with ground truth, alongside a conventional Bradley-Terry model.
- Developed Pre-PPO, a prompt selection method, to identify and prioritize challenging training prompts with lower initial reward scores that are less susceptible to reward hacking.
- Applied strategic task prioritization during early RLHF training, focusing initially on mathematical and coding tasks which benefit from robust ground-truth assessments.

## Results

- The proposed data-centric approach consistently outperformed baselines, achieving a 1.4-point overall improvement on challenging test sets and up to 3.9-point gains in STEM tasks.
- Strategies scaled positively to larger models (**150B** parameters) and enhanced generalization, particularly on more difficult and out-of-distribution prompts.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both Pre-PPO (sustaining learning beyond baseline plateau) and early task emphasis (accelerating learning in coding/math) contributed to the gains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reward models vary in susceptibility to hacking; Reasoning Task Verifiers (RTVs) are highly resistant, while Generative Reward Models (GenRM) with ground truth are robust, and traditional preference models are more vulnerable.
> - Effective Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) training requires prioritizing data quality and strategic data utilization over mere quantity, as increasing low-quality data can degrade performance.
> - Learning fine-grained distinctions first, particularly from tasks with clear ground truth, helps mitigate the negative impact of prematurely optimizing for coarse-grained patterns from less robust reward signals.
