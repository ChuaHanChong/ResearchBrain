# Identity-Preserving Image-to-Video Generation via Reward-Guided Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Huazhong University of Science and Technology and Alibaba introduced IPRO, a reinforcement learning framework that significantly enhances facial identity preservation in image-to-video generation without modifying the foundational diffusion model's architecture. The approach led to improvements in FaceSim by up to 45.9% while maintaining overall video quality.

## Problem

- Maintaining consistent human identity in image-to-video (I2V) generation is challenging, especially with varying expressions, movements, or small input faces, leading to identity degradation and drift.
- Existing identity preservation methods, often designed for text-to-video (T2V), are architecturally intrusive for I2V or suffer from exposure bias when using supervised finetuning.
- A general-purpose solution is needed that improves identity fidelity in I2V foundation models without compromising their original capabilities or requiring per-scene/per-person fine-tuning.

## Method

- The ==Identity-Preserving Reward==-guided Optimization (**IPRO**) framework optimizes diffusion models via a differentiable facial reward function, training on-policy to mitigate exposure bias.
- A novel Facial Scoring Mechanism (FSM) computes rewards by comparing generated faces to a multi-view reference pool from the ground-truth video, allowing for natural variations while preserving identity.
- KL-divergence regularization is incorporated to stabilize training and prevent 'reward hacking' by constraining the optimized model's deviation from the original model's distribution.

## Results

- IPRO consistently improved Face Similarity (FaceSim) across foundation models, with increases of up to **45.9%** for an in-house I2V model (**0.4769** to **0.6960**) and **20.1%** for Wan 2.2 **27B**-A14B (**0.5780** to **0.6942**).
- The method achieved superior FaceSim (**0.6942**) compared to adapted state-of-the-art T2V identity-preserving methods (e.g., MoCA† **0.5820**, Concat-ID† **0.6056**) and other RL approaches like DPO (**0.6284**).
- Ablation studies demonstrated that the Facial Scoring Mechanism and KL-divergence regularization effectively mitigated reward hacking from **58%** to **10%** while maintaining high FaceSim, and ArcFace embeddings significantly outperformed CLIP for facial identity scoring.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement learning, guided by a differentiable identity reward, can effectively enhance identity preservation in I2V models without altering their architecture, directly addressing long-term consistency and exposure bias.
> - Using a multi-view reference pool for facial scoring allows generated subjects to resemble ground-truth identities while accommodating natural variations in pose and expression, preventing rigid 'copy-paste' artifacts.
> - Truncating gradient backpropagation to the final sampling steps efficiently optimizes fine-grained identity details, and KL-divergence regularization is essential to balance identity fidelity with maintaining overall video quality and preventing unnatural generated content.
