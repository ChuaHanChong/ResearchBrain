# A Survey on Self-Evolution of Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper by Tao et al. provides a structured overview of self-evolution approaches for Large Language Models by proposing a four-phase iterative cycle. It categorizes existing methods for experience acquisition, refinement, updating, and evaluation, offering a foundational document for understanding and advancing autonomous LLM development while also outlining key challenges.

## Problem

- Current Large Language Model (LLM) training relies heavily on costly and labor-intensive human or external model supervision, hindering scalability.
- LLMs are largely static post-training, struggling to autonomously adapt, acquire new knowledge, or refine skills without external intervention.
- The field of self-evolution in LLMs, despite rapid growth, lacked a systematic organization and clear understanding of the relationships between various self-improvement methods.

## Method

- The authors propose a comprehensive conceptual framework outlining an iterative self-evolution cycle for LLMs, comprising experience acquisition, refinement, updating, and evaluation phases.
- They systematically categorize diverse self-evolution objectives, distinguishing between fundamental LLM abilities and agent-specific capabilities, along with various evolution directions.
- The paper summarizes and analyzes existing literature within each module of the proposed framework, providing a detailed taxonomy of methods for task evolution, solution evolution, feedback collection, experience refinement, and model updating.

## Results

- A novel conceptual framework for LLM self-evolution was established, defining an iterative cycle with four phases: experience acquisition, experience refinement, updating, and evaluation.
- A detailed taxonomy and analysis were provided for existing methods within each phase, including various approaches for task and solution evolution, feedback collection, experience filtering and correcting, and in-weight or in-context model updating.
- The survey systematically outlined critical challenges and open problems for self-evolving LLMs, such as the need for diverse objectives, higher autonomy, theoretical grounding, and improved safety and superalignment.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Self-evolution for LLMs involves an iterative cycle of experience acquisition, refinement, updating, and evaluation, mirroring human experiential learning.
> - A comprehensive taxonomy categorizes diverse methods for each self-evolution phase, from task and solution generation to feedback mechanisms, data refinement, and in-weight or in-context model updating.
> - Critical open problems include establishing theoretical grounding for self-generated data, addressing the stability-plasticity dilemma, and developing systematic evaluation for continuously evolving models.
