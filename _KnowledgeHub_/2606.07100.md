# LARA: Latent Action Representation Alignment for Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> The LARA (Latent Action Representation Alignment) framework jointly optimizes Latent Action Models (LAMs) and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models by explicitly aligning their intermediate latent representations. This approach enhances VLA model performance and generalization capabilities across various robotic manipulation benchmarks, achieving improved success rates and better adaptation to novel tasks and embodiments.

## Problem

- VLA models for robotic manipulation face challenges due to the scarcity of large-scale, high-quality labeled robot action data.
- Existing approaches decouple the training of Latent Action Models (LAMs) and VLA policies, leading to LAMs capturing spurious visual changes and VLAs being constrained by static or suboptimal LAM representations.
- This data scarcity and decoupled training limit VLA models' generalization to novel tasks or environments and can lead to overfitting.

## Method

- **LARA** proposes a joint optimization framework that explicitly aligns the intermediate latent representations of a VLA diffusion model with the continuous latent actions generated by an online Latent Action Model (LAM).
- A cosine similarity loss, often with a projection head, is used to link the VLA's internal features to the LAM's latent space, enabling reciprocal refinement during training.
- The framework employs a three-stage training pipeline: initial LAM pre-training on unlabeled videos, LARA joint pre-training on robot demonstrations for embodiment-agnostic representations, and LARA joint post-training for task-specific fine-tuning.

## Results

- LARA (full) achieved an **88.6%** average success rate on the LIBERO benchmark, an improvement of **12.1%** over OpenVLA (**76.5%**), and a **65.2%** success rate on SIMPLER-ENV, **3.8%** better than Moto-GPT (**61.4%**), using only OXE-constrained pre-training.
- As a post-training enhancement, LARA improved a state-of-the-art model, GR00T-N1.6, by **5.56%** on the real-world G1-Real benchmark (**76.0%** vs **72.0%**), demonstrating enhanced generalization to novel embodiments.
- LARA-aligned LAMs provided pseudo-labels that improved downstream Moto-GPT performance by **15.7%** on SIMPLER-ENV (**49.5%** vs **42.8%**), indicating a significant enhancement in the quality and action-centricity of latent representations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Joint optimization and explicit latent action representation alignment provide bi-directional regularization, making LAMs more action-centric by grounding them with real robot actions and grounding VLA policies with forward dynamics priors.
> - Aligning the VLA's internal representations with the LAM's latent actions helps the VLA generate actions that lead to predictable and desired future states, reducing the 'hallucination' of ineffective trajectories.
> - LARA functions as a versatile, 'plug-and-play' framework, capable of enhancing existing diffusion-based VLA models, refining LAMs for improved pseudo-label generation, or serving as a comprehensive pre-training method.
