# TraPO: A Semi-Supervised Reinforcement Learning Framework for Boosting LLM Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> TRAPO is a semi-supervised reinforcement learning framework designed to enhance large language model reasoning by efficiently leveraging a small labeled dataset to guide learning on a larger unlabeled dataset. The framework achieves strong performance, often surpassing fully supervised models, by selectively incorporating unlabeled samples whose learning dynamics align with those of verified examples.

## Problem

- Traditional Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) methods for LLMs incur prohibitively high annotation costs for ground-truth labels.
- Purely unsupervised RLVR methods, while alleviating annotation burdens, are prone to model collapse, where they reinforce internally consistent but factually incorrect reasoning paths.
- Simple integration of supervised and unsupervised losses in semi-supervised RLVR yields only marginal benefits, lacking an effective mechanism to bridge labeled and unlabeled data.

## Method

- TRAPO introduces a ==semi-supervised reward== function that combines supervised rewards for labeled data with unsupervised proxy rewards for unlabeled data.
- It uses 'pass rate trajectories' to track the learning dynamics of individual questions across training epochs and a 'Trajectory-based Cosine Similarity' (TCS) to identify reliable unlabeled samples.
- Only unlabeled samples with TCS scores above a threshold or within a top percentile, indicating learning dynamics similar to labeled data, are included in the GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization) training objective.

## Results

- With only **1K** labeled and **3K** unlabeled samples, TRAPO achieved **42.6%** accuracy on ID benchmarks, outperforming the best unsupervised method (**38.3%** with **45K** unlabeled) by **4.3%**.
- Using **4K** labeled and **12K** unlabeled samples (**10%** of the full labeled dataset), TRAPO attained **45.6%** ID and **59.7%** OOD accuracy, surpassing a fully supervised model trained on the entire **45K** labeled samples (**45.5%** ID, **57.3%** OOD).
- TRAPO consistently demonstrated strong generalization to out-of-distribution tasks, is robust across different LLM architectures (e.g., DeepSeek-R1-Distill, LLaMA-3.1-**8B**-Instruct), and its trajectory-based selection improves various semi-supervised baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Minimal external supervision is critical to stabilize and anchor consistency-based training on unlabeled data, preventing the reinforcement of incorrect reasoning patterns.
> - Leveraging the 'learning dynamics' of the model through pass rate trajectories provides a robust method to connect labeled and unlabeled data, outperforming simple loss function combinations.
> - Reliable unlabeled samples exhibit learning dynamics that align with those of validated labeled samples, and quantifying this similarity allows for intelligent selection of data for training.
