# 3DGen-Bench: Comprehensive Benchmark Suite for 3D Generative Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This research introduces 3DGen-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark for 3D generative models, which establishes the first large-scale human preference dataset for evaluating text-to-3D and image-to-3D outputs across five distinct criteria. The work also develops automated scoring models, 3DGen-Score and 3DGen-Eval, that demonstrate superior correlation with human judgments, with 3DGen-Score achieving an average pairwise alignment of 0.725 for text-to-3D and 0.767 for image-to-3D tasks.

## Problem

- The rapid advancement in 3D generative models has outpaced the development of robust and human-aligned evaluation methodologies.
- Existing 3D evaluation metrics (e.g., CLIP similarity) are insufficient as they lack 3D-specific prior knowledge and fail to capture the multifaceted aspects of 3D asset quality.
- There is a critical absence of large-scale human preference datasets in the 3D domain, which are essential for training automated evaluators aligned with human perception, unlike in 2D image and language generation.

## Method

- A comprehensive benchmark suite, **3DGen-Bench**, was developed, featuring a diverse ==model zoo== of 19 3D generative models and a substantial set of 1,020 text and image prompts.
- A large-scale ==human preference dataset== was collected through ==multi-criteria human voting== (68,400 comparison votes, 56,100 absolute scores) across five key dimensions of 3D asset quality.
- Two human-aligned automated scoring models, ==CLIP-based== **3DGen-Score** and ==MLLM-based== **3DGen-Eval**, were trained using a ==two-stage strategy== on the collected human preference data.

## Results

- Human evaluations established leaderboards, identifying MVDream as the top performer for text-to-3D and Wonder3D for image-to-3D, demonstrating specific strengths and weaknesses across models.
- 3DGen-Score consistently achieved higher pairwise alignment with human judgments (average **0.725** for text-to-3D, **0.767** for image-to-3D) and superior Kendall's Tau ranking correlation compared to all baseline metrics.
- Both 3DGen-Score and 3DGen-Eval showed strong generalization to unseen models and external datasets, and 3DGen-Score successfully functioned as a reward model to optimize the MVDream generative model, enhancing 3D asset quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Human preference, systematically collected across multiple dimensions, serves as a highly effective and reliable foundation for evaluating 3D generative models.
> - Automated evaluators can be trained to accurately predict human preferences for 3D assets, offering scalable and efficient alternatives to manual evaluations.
> - Integrating human preference-aligned metrics as reward functions can effectively guide the optimization of 3D generative models, leading to improved output quality and coherence.
