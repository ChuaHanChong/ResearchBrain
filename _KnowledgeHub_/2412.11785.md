# InterDyn: Controllable Interactive Dynamics with Video Diffusion Models

> [!summary] Summary
> InterDyn introduces a framework that synthesizes realistic, controllable videos of interactive dynamics from an initial image and dynamic control signal, leveraging the implicit physics knowledge within large video diffusion models without explicit 3D reconstruction or physics simulations. It achieves high spatio-temporal coherence and realistic object motion for human-object interactions.

## Problem

- Traditional methods for dynamic interaction prediction relied on computationally expensive and error-prone "reconstruct-simulate-render" pipelines with explicit 3D geometry and physics simulators.
- Existing generative models often focused on synthesizing static state transitions, failing to capture continuous, temporally extended dynamics and force propagation between interacting objects.
- Generalizing physically plausible interaction generation from simplified synthetic environments to complex real-world scenarios remained a significant challenge due to the limitations of explicit modeling.

## Method

- **InterDyn** extends a frozen, pre-trained Stable Video Diffusion (SVD) model with a trainable **ControlNet-like** branch, allowing fine-grained control over dynamic interactions.
- The control branch processes dynamic control signals, primarily binary masks (e.g., hand masks), and integrates them into the SVD's latent space via zero-initialized convolutions.
- The system is trained on datasets like **CLEVRER** (for object collision) and **Something-Something-v2** (for human-object interaction) to learn implicit physical dynamics.

## Results

- On the CLEVRER dataset, InterDyn successfully generates realistic force propagation and demonstrates counterfactual reasoning by producing distinct, physically plausible outcomes from altered control signals.
- In human-object interaction tasks (Something-Something-v2), InterDyn surpasses baselines like CosHand and DynamiCrafter in spatio-temporal dynamics (FVD, KVD) and achieves the highest Motion Fidelity, capturing continuous post-interaction effects.
- The framework generates diverse and complex interactions, including articulated object motion, fluid dynamics, and deformable object behaviors, while maintaining high visual quality and robustness to noisy control signals.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large video diffusion models, trained on extensive real-world video data, implicitly learn sophisticated "physics knowledge" and can function as neural renderers and implicit physics simulators.
> - Continuous, interactive dynamics, including force propagation and post-interaction effects, are crucial for realistic scene understanding and generation, moving beyond static state predictions.
> - The model demonstrates robustness to simple control signals like binary masks, indicating its capacity to infer complex interactive motions and details (e.g., individual fingers) from sparse input.
