# VisualPRM: An Effective Process Reward Model for Multimodal Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Shanghai AI Laboratory and partner institutions introduce VisualPRM, a process reward model that evaluates step-by-step reasoning in multimodal tasks, improving MLLM performance through test-time scaling while introducing VisualPRM400K dataset and VisualProcessBench for comprehensive evaluation of multimodal reasoning capabilities.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs show significant gaps in reasoning capabilities compared to proprietary models
- Test-time scaling for MLLMs lacks effective critic models and evaluation resources
- Current open-source MLLMs struggle to accurately assess step-wise correctness in reasoning

## Method

- Developed ==VisualPRM== to evaluate correctness of each step in multimodal reasoning processes
- Created ==VisualPRM400K dataset== with **400,000** step-by-step solutions and correctness annotations
- Established ==VisualProcessBench== to evaluate PRMs and MLLMs on error detection in reasoning steps

## Results

- VisualPRM significantly improves MLLM reasoning across different model families and scales
- Demonstrates effectiveness in both multimodal and text-only reasoning tasks
- Provides new benchmark resources for evaluating and advancing multimodal reasoning capabilities

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Process reward models (PRMs) outperform outcome reward models (ORMs) and self-consistency methods
> - Value-based PRMs perform better than advantage-based approaches
> - Averaging step scores yields superior performance compared to selecting maximum scores
