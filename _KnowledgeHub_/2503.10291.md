# VisualPRM: An Effective Process Reward Model for Multimodal Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Shanghai AI Laboratory and partner institutions introduce VisualPRM, a process reward model that evaluates step-by-step reasoning in multimodal tasks, improving MLLM performance through test-time scaling while introducing VisualPRM400K dataset and VisualProcessBench for comprehensive evaluation of multimodal reasoning capabilities.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs show significant gaps in reasoning capabilities compared to proprietary models
- Test-time scaling for MLLMs lacks effective critic models and evaluation resources
- Current open-source MLLMs struggle to accurately assess step-wise correctness in reasoning

## Method

- Developed ==VisualPRM== to evaluate correctness of each step in multimodal reasoning processes
- Created ==VisualPRM400K dataset== with **400,000** step-by-step solutions and ==correctness annotations==
- Established ==VisualProcessBench== to evaluate PRMs and MLLMs on error detection in reasoning steps

## Results

- VisualPRM significantly improves MLLM reasoning across different model families and scales
- Demonstrates effectiveness in both multimodal and text-only reasoning tasks
- Provides new benchmark resources for evaluating and advancing multimodal reasoning capabilities

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Process reward models (PRMs) outperform outcome reward models (ORMs) and self-consistency methods
> - Value-based PRMs perform better than advantage-based approaches
> - Averaging step scores yields superior performance compared to selecting maximum scores

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research directly addresses the burgeoning field of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), which seeks to integrate visual and textual information processing for more sophisticated AI systems.
- **Addressing Reasoning Gaps:** The introduction clearly states that while MLLMs have made strides in perception and recognition, a "large gap remains in reasoning capabilities" compared to proprietary models. This sets the stage for the paper's contribution: improving MLLM reasoning through a novel approach.
- **Building on Existing Techniques:** The paper references established techniques for enhancing LLM reasoning, such as:
 - Data collection and construction strategies
 - Offline preference optimization
 - Online reinforcement learning
 - Test-Time Scaling (TTS)
- **Filling a Gap: TTS for MLLMs:** The authors identify that Test-Time Scaling (TTS), where a model generates multiple responses and selects the best one, is "largely underexplored" for MLLMs. This becomes the central focus of their work.
- **Addressing Limitations of Existing Critic Models:** The paper acknowledges the critical role of "critic models" in TTS for evaluating response quality but notes that existing open-source MLLMs struggle to serve effectively as critics due to a lack of sufficient training data.
- **Multimodal Process Reward Models (PRMs):** The research situates itself within the context of Reward Models (RMs), differentiating between Outcome Reward Models (ORMs) that assign a single score and Process Reward Models (PRMs) that evaluate step-by-step reasoning.
- **Multimodal PRM Underexplored:** The paper highlights that multimodal PRMs are "largely underexplored," justifying the creation of their VisualPRM400K dataset and the VisualPRM model itself.
- **Benchmarks for Reward Models:** The authors note that while benchmarks exist for evaluating RMs, there is a "lack of a multimodal process benchmark," which motivates the creation of their VisualProcessBench.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives of this research are:
- **Improve MLLM Reasoning Abilities:** The overarching goal is to enhance the reasoning capabilities of MLLMs, specifically in multimodal tasks.
- **Develop an Effective Critic Model for TTS:** Address the lack of suitable critic models for Test-Time Scaling (TTS) in MLLMs.
- **Create a Multimodal Process Reward Model (PRM):** Design and train a PRM, VisualPRM, that can effectively evaluate the correctness of each step in a multimodal reasoning process.
- **Construct a Multimodal Process Supervision Dataset:** Generate a dataset, VisualPRM400K, to train the PRM, providing step-by-step solutions and correctness annotations.
- **Establish a Benchmark for Evaluating PRMs:** Develop VisualProcessBench, a benchmark to evaluate the ability of PRMs and MLLMs to detect erroneous steps in multimodal reasoning.
- **Demonstrate the Effectiveness of VisualPRM in BoN Evaluation:** Show that VisualPRM can serve as a critic model in Best-of-N (BoN) evaluation, improving the performance of various MLLMs.

The motivation stems from the recognition that MLLMs, while strong in perception, lag in reasoning, and that TTS, a promising technique, is hindered by the lack of good critic models and evaluation resources.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The research employs a multi-faceted methodology:

- **Dataset Creation (VisualPRM400K):**
 - Leverage question prompts from an existing dataset (MMPR v1.1).
 - Generate step-by-step solutions using MLLMs (InternVL2.5 series).
 - Develop an automated data pipeline based on Monte Carlo sampling to assess the "expected accuracy" of each step.
- **Benchmark Construction (VisualProcessBench):**
 - Collect questions from established multimodal reasoning benchmarks (MMMU, MathVision, etc.).
 - Generate solutions using leading MLLMs (GPT-4o, Claude-3.5-Sonnet, etc.) to ensure solution diversity.
 - Employ human experts (with university degrees) to manually annotate the correctness of each step in the solutions, focusing on detecting *all* errors, not just the first.
- **Model Development (VisualPRM):**
 - Formulate the PRM training as a multi-turn chat task.
 - Train the model to predict the correctness of each step in a reasoning process, given the image, question, and previous steps.
 - Explore both value-based and advantage-based PRM modeling approaches.
- **Experimental Evaluation:**
 - Utilize VisualPRM as a critic model in Best-of-N (BoN) evaluation to improve MLLM reasoning performance.
 - Evaluate VisualPRM and other MLLMs on VisualProcessBench to assess their ability to detect erroneous steps.
 - Conduct ablation studies to analyze the effects of different components (e.g., BoN, PRM modeling methods).

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The key findings of the research are:

- **VisualPRM Improves MLLM Reasoning:** VisualPRM significantly enhances MLLM reasoning abilities across different model families and scales, achieving substantial performance gains on various multimodal reasoning benchmarks.
- **PRMs Outperform ORMs and Self-Consistency:** In BoN evaluation, PRMs consistently outperform Outcome Reward Models (ORMs) and Self-Consistency.
- **MLLMs Struggle to Assess Step-Wise Correctness:** Existing open-source MLLMs struggle to accurately assess step-wise correctness, as revealed by evaluations on VisualProcessBench.
- **Value-Based PRMs Outperform Advantage-Based PRMs:** Value-based PRMs demonstrated better performance than advantage-based PRMs.
- **Averaging Step Scores Yields Better Performance:** Averaging the scores of each step for the final response score performs better than selecting the maximum score.
- **VisualPRM Enhances Text Reasoning Abilities:** Testing on text reasoning benchmarks reveals that the approach is also useful on text only inputs.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

This research has several significant contributions and potential impacts:

- **Advancement of MLLM Reasoning:** It provides a practical and effective approach (VisualPRM) for improving the reasoning abilities of MLLMs, addressing a critical limitation in current models.
- **Effective Test-Time Scaling for MLLMs:** Demonstrates the feasibility and effectiveness of Test-Time Scaling (TTS) for MLLMs using a process-oriented reward model.
- **Valuable Datasets and Benchmarks:** The creation of VisualPRM400K and VisualProcessBench provides valuable resources for the research community, enabling further exploration and evaluation of multimodal PRMs.
- **New Insights into PRM Design:** The ablation studies offer insights into the design and training of effective PRMs for multimodal tasks, including the choice of modeling approach (value-based vs. advantage-based) and score aggregation methods.
- **Potential for Real-World Applications:** Improved MLLM reasoning can lead to more capable AI systems in various applications, such as visual question answering, robotics, autonomous driving, and education.
- **Opens New Research Directions:** The research inspires further investigation into multimodal PRMs, critic model design, and the application of TTS to MLLMs.

In summary, this paper presents a well-executed study that addresses a key challenge in the field of MLLMs. By developing a novel PRM, creating valuable datasets and benchmarks, and conducting thorough evaluations, the authors make a significant contribution to advancing the reasoning capabilities of multimodal AI systems.
