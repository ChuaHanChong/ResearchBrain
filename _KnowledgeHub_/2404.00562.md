# Text2HOI: Text-guided 3D Motion Generation for Hand-Object Interaction

> [!summary] Summary
> Text2HOI generates 3D hand-object interaction motion from a text prompt and a canonical object mesh alone, needing no object trajectory or initial hand pose. A VAE predicts a text-guided, scale-variant contact map over object points; a Transformer diffusion model denoises left-hand, right-hand and object motion conditioned on that map; a feed-forward refiner removes penetration. It beats text-to-motion baselines on accuracy, FID and physical realism.

## Problem

- Text-to-motion generators model human bodies without object interaction context, so generated sequences lack the semantics and contact structure that manipulation requires.
- Prior hand-object motion synthesis assumes stationary grasping or requires object pose sequences and initial hand poses as input, which users cannot supply.
- Labeled 3D hand-object sequence datasets cover few object categories, hand types and scales, so generative models trained directly on them fail physically.

## Method

- ==Contact map prediction== network: a PointNet-plus-CLIP **VAE** decodes an N-point contact probability map from normalized point cloud, text feature, object scale and a latent, giving category-agnostic geometry.
- **Text2HOI** diffusion generator: a Transformer encoder with frame-wise and agent-wise positional encoding predicts clean motion directly, trained with ==distance-map== and ==relative-orientation== geometric losses.
- Hand refinement network: a non-diffusion Transformer taking joints, contact map, deformed point cloud and a distance attention map, trained with ==penetration== and ==contact losses== in one forward pass.

## Results

- On GRAB, accuracy rises from **0.5127** for the strongest baseline to **0.9218**, FID drops **0.6023** to **0.3017**, and physical realism reaches **0.8839** versus **0.8084** ground truth.
- On H2O and ARCTIC, accuracy reaches **0.8295** and **0.9205** with FID **0.1744** and **0.1329**, all improving on T2M, MDM and IMoS baselines.
- Ablations: dropping the refiner lowers realism **0.8839** to **0.8312**; removing contact loss collapses realism to **0.1467**; a diffusion-based refiner scores only **0.1682** realism.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing generation into a category-agnostic contact prior then motion synthesis transfers to unseen objects, sidestepping scarcity of labeled interaction sequences.
> - Refinement of already-generated motion is better done by a single feed-forward pass than by re-noising through a diffusion process, being both faster and more accurate.
> - Contact conditioning, not penetration penalties alone, drives physical plausibility; the method still ignores forces, limiting true physical understanding of manipulation.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research paper addresses a critical and challenging problem at the intersection of natural language processing, 3D computer vision, and human-computer interaction: generating realistic 3D motions from textual descriptions.

The broader research landscape for 3D motion generation has seen significant advancements, particularly in text-guided *human body* motion synthesis. Existing works like T2M, MDM, and IMOS have successfully demonstrated the generation of diverse and expressive full-body human movements from text prompts. These models often leverage techniques such as VAEs, diffusion models, and Transformer architectures to map linguistic instructions to complex kinematic sequences.

However, a significant gap exists when it comes to *hand-object interaction*. The authors explicitly state that "the context of object interaction has been often missing, which significantly limits the expressiveness in the semantics of the generated motion sequence." Prior work in hand and object motion generation, such as those focusing on grasping stationary objects, typically has limitations:
- They often focus solely on grasping, not dynamic manipulation.
- They may require pre-defined object trajectories or initial hand poses as input, which are frequently unavailable in real-world scenarios or user prompts.
- Some methods rely on action labels rather than free-form text, limiting expressiveness.
- Optimizing for physical plausibility (like contact and penetration) often involves computationally expensive post-processing or iterative optimization stages.

Text2HOI positions itself as the **first text-guided work** to generate sequences of 3D hand-object interaction without requiring object trajectory or initial hand pose. This is a crucial distinction. It aims to bridge the gap between high-level semantic commands (text) and low-level physical interactions in 3D. By tackling the complexity of hand-object dynamics directly from text, this work pushes the boundaries of generative AI in embodied agents and interactive simulations. It moves beyond simple human motion generation to sophisticated, physically informed interaction generation.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of Text2HOI is to **generate realistic and diverse sequences of 3D hand-object interaction motions directly from a text prompt and a canonical object mesh**, critically, *without requiring additional inputs such as object trajectory or initial hand pose*.

The key motivations driving this research stem from several challenges and opportunities:

- **Addressing Data Scarcity:** A major hurdle in learning diverse 3D hand-object interactions is the extreme scarcity of high-quality, labeled datasets. Existing datasets are limited in terms of interaction types, object categories, scales, and associated textual descriptions. A generative model trained on such limited data would struggle to generalize and produce physically and semantically plausible motions. The authors explicitly aim to overcome this by proposing a decomposed framework that learns general geometry representations from augmented data.
- **Enhancing Expressiveness and Semantics:** While text-guided human motion generation has advanced, the lack of object interaction context severely limits the semantic expressiveness of generated motions. Hands naturally interact with objects in daily life, and incorporating this interaction is vital for creating truly natural and semantically rich animations.
- **Enabling Broader Applications:** The ability to generate complex, physically plausible hand-object interactions from text opens doors to numerous applications:
 - **Robotics:** For designing and planning robot hand movements for complex manipulation tasks.
 - **Computer Graphics and Animation:** Creating more realistic and diverse character animations for games, virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), and film without requiring laborious manual keyframing or motion capture for every interaction.
 - **Simulation:** Developing more sophisticated training simulations, such as surgical training or industrial operations, where precise hand-object dynamics are critical.
 - **Interactive Control:** Providing more intuitive control interfaces for virtual characters or robots.
- **Ensuring Physical Plausibility:** Generating motions that respect physical constraints (e.g., correct contacts, no penetration) is paramount for realism. The authors are motivated to produce motions that are not only semantically correct but also physically sound.

In essence, the paper seeks to enable a new paradigm where users can simply describe a desired hand-object interaction in natural language, and the system automatically generates a high-fidelity 3D animation of that interaction, robustly handling diverse objects and scenarios.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Text2HOI proposes a novel three-stage compositional framework to tackle the challenges of text-guided 3D hand-object interaction generation, specifically addressing data scarcity and ensuring physical plausibility.

**Overall Framework (Decomposition Strategy):**
The core idea is to decompose the complex task of interaction generation into two manageable subtasks: "object contact map generation" and "hand-object motion generation." This decomposition allows dedicated models to learn more generalized representations. A final refinement stage then enhances physical realism.

**Stage 1: Contact Map Prediction (Section 3.1)**
This initial stage focuses on identifying potential contact regions on the object surface given a text prompt and the object's geometry.
- **Input:** A text prompt `T` (processed into CLIP features `f_CLIP(T)`) and a canonical 3D object mesh `M_obj` (sampled as a normalized point cloud `P_norm`), along with the object's scale `s_obj`. A Gaussian random noise vector `z_contact` is also introduced to enable diversity.
- **Model:** A VAE-based network `f_contact`. This network uses PointNet to extract local and global geometric features from the object's point cloud.
- **Output:** A 3D contact map `ˆm_contact` (a probability map indicating potential hand-object contact regions) and global object features `F_obj`.
- **Key Design:** The network learns a category-agnostic local geometry representation, making it applicable to general and unseen objects. It also inherently produces scale-variant contact probabilities (e.g., wider contact regions for smaller objects).
- **Training:** Uses a combination of binary cross-entropy loss, Dice loss (for segmentation-like contact prediction), and Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence loss (for the VAE component).

**Stage 2: Text-to-3D Hand-Object Motion Generation (Section 3.2)**
This stage generates the actual 3D motion sequences for both hands and the object, heavily guided by the predicted contact map and text.
- **Motion Representation:** 3D hand-object motion `x_0` is represented as a sequence of pose and translation parameters for left hand (MANO model, 99D), right hand (MANO model, 99D), and object (translation, rotation in 6D representation, articulation angle, 10D).
- **Model:** A Transformer-based diffusion model `f_THOI`.
- **Diffusion Process:** Follows the Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) framework.
 - **Forward Process:** Gradually adds Gaussian noise to the original motion `x_0` to produce noised motion `x_t`.
 - **Backward Process:** The Transformer model `f_THOI` learns to denoise `x_t` to reconstruct `ˆx_0` (the clean motion). Crucially, unlike conventional diffusion models that predict noise, `f_THOI` is designed to directly estimate the final sample `ˆx_0` at each step, which facilitates the application of explicit geometric losses.
- **Conditional Inputs:** The model conditions its denoising on:
 - Text features `f_CLIP(T)`.
 - Predicted contact map `ˆm_contact`.
 - Global object features `F_obj`.
 - Object's scale `s_obj`.
 - Diffusion time-step `t`.
- **Positional Encoding:** Novel frame-wise and agent-wise positional encodings are applied to the Transformer inputs. Frame-wise encoding helps the model understand temporal relationships, while agent-wise encoding differentiates between left hand, right hand, and object, crucial for multi-agent interaction.
- **Training Losses:**
 - `L_diff`: Standard L2 loss for reconstructing `x_0` from `x_t`.
 - `L_dm` (Distance Map Loss): Encourages alignment between estimated and ground-truth distances between hand joints and object points, particularly focusing on regions where contact is expected (within a threshold `τ`). This is vital for realistic contact.
 - `L_ro` (Relative Orientation Loss): Minimizes the difference in 3D relative rotation between hands and the object, promoting physically plausible manipulation.
- **Data Augmentation:** To combat data scarcity, the authors manually annotated text labels for existing 3D motion datasets (H2O, GRAB, ARCTIC), creating a richer training resource.

**Stage 3: Hand Refinement Network (Section 3.3)**
This final stage refines the generated hand motions to address physical inaccuracies like penetration.
- **Model:** A Transformer-based network `f_ref`. Unlike `f_THOI`, it does *not* use a diffusion mechanism; it's a single feed-forward pass, making it fast and efficient for refinement.
- **Input:** The denoised hand output from `f_THOI` (`ˆx_0,hand`), corresponding hand joints (`ˆJ_hand`), predicted contact map (`ˆm_contact`), deformed object point cloud (`ˆP_def`), and a distance-based attention map (`m_att`).
- **Output:** Refined hand motions `˜x_hand`.
- **Training Losses:**
 - `L_simple`: Basic L2 loss to align with ground truth.
 - `L_penet` (Penetration Loss): Penalizes hand vertices that penetrate the object surface, specifically targeting unwanted intersections.
 - `L_contact` (Contact Loss): Encourages hand joints to remain close to the object surface where contact is desired, further improving interaction realism.

**Auxiliary Modules:**
- **Hand-type selection:** Uses CLIP's cosine similarity to input text (`T`) and predefined hand type templates (e.g., "left hand," "right hand," "both hands") to determine which hand(s) are active, enabling selective masking of inputs, outputs, and losses.
- **Motion length prediction:** A dedicated network `f_Length` predicts the appropriate sequence length `ˆL` from the text prompt `T`.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experimental evaluation of Text2HOI demonstrates its superior performance compared to existing text-to-human motion generation baselines adapted for hand-object interaction.

**Quantitative Results (Table 1):**
The method was rigorously evaluated on three datasets: H2O, GRAB, and ARCTIC, using standard metrics: Accuracy (action classification), Frechet Inception Distance (FID, for distribution similarity), Diversity, Multimodality, and Physical Realism (a custom metric based on contact and penetration).
- **Overall Outperformance:** Text2HOI consistently outperforms all baseline methods (T2M, MDM, IMOS) across almost all metrics on all three datasets.
- **Physical Realism:** Text2HOI achieves the highest scores in Physical Realism, indicating that its generated hand-object interactions are significantly more physically plausible than those from baselines. For instance, on GRAB, Text2HOI scores 0.8839 compared to the next best (MDM) at 0.7382, and on ARCTIC, 0.8760 vs. IMOS at 0.7569. This highlights the effectiveness of its contact generation, geometric losses, and refinement module.
- **Accuracy and Semantic Alignment:** High Accuracy (top-3) scores demonstrate the model's ability to generate motions that are well-aligned with the semantic content of the text prompts (e.g., 0.9218 on GRAB compared to 0.5127 for MDM).
- **Diversity and Multimodality:** Text2HOI shows strong performance in Diversity and Multimodality, indicating that it can generate a wide range of distinct and varied motions for a given text prompt, reflecting the natural variability in human interaction. Its diversity scores are very close to the Ground Truth (GT) distribution, suggesting a comprehensive understanding of motion space.
- **FID (Lower is Better):** Lower FID scores (e.g., 0.1744 on H2O, 0.3017 on GRAB) indicate that the distribution of generated motions is closer to the real data distribution, signifying higher quality and realism.

**Qualitative Results (Figures 4, 5, S1-S5, S8-S10):**
- **Realistic Interaction:** Visual comparisons (Fig. 4) demonstrate that Text2HOI produces hand-object interactions that appear much more natural and physically correct, with hands appropriately grasping and manipulating objects according to the text prompt (e.g., "Use a hammer with the right hand"). Baselines often show disconnected or unnatural interactions.
- **Text-Alignment and Diversity:** The generated motions closely align with the input text prompts, even for complex or abstract actions (e.g., "Fly an airplane with the right hand," "Close a microwave with both hands," Fig. 5). The model can also generate diverse interpretations for the same prompt (Fig. S8).
- **Generalizability to Unseen Objects:** A crucial finding is the method's applicability to unseen objects (Fig. 5), showcasing its strong generalization capabilities, a direct benefit of the category-agnostic contact map prediction.
- **Scale Variance:** The predicted contact maps and subsequent motions adapt appropriately to different object scales (Fig. S5), demonstrating sensitivity to object properties.

**Ablation Studies (Table 2, Figures S1-S3):**
The ablation studies conducted on the GRAB dataset confirm the critical contribution of each proposed component:
- **Positional Encoding:** Both frame-wise and agent-wise positional encodings significantly improve performance across all metrics, validating their role in helping the Transformer understand the complex spatio-temporal structure of hand-object interactions.
- **Geometric Losses (L_dm, L_ro):** The distance map loss (`L_dm`) and relative orientation loss (`L_ro`) are crucial for improving the model's understanding of 3D relationships between hands and objects, leading to better physical realism and overall accuracy.
- **Conditional Inputs (ˆm_contact, s_obj):** Removing the predicted contact map (`ˆm_contact`) or object scale (`s_obj`) conditions leads to noticeable drops in performance, particularly in accuracy and physical realism. This confirms their importance as strong priors for appropriate hand poses and interaction.
- **Hand Refinement Network (f_ref):** The refinement module (`f_ref`) yields substantial gains, most notably in Physical Realism. Ablations show that the penetration loss (`L_penet`) and contact loss (`L_contact`) within the refiner are independently effective and combine to ensure highly realistic, penetration-free interactions (Fig. S3). The choice of a non-diffusion-based refiner also proves to be more efficient and performant (Table S1).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Text2HOI represents a significant step forward in the field of 3D content generation, particularly for interactive and embodied AI. Its significance and potential impact are multifaceted:

- **Pioneering Text-Guided Hand-Object Interaction:** As the first work to directly generate complex 3D hand-object interaction sequences from text, Text2HOI opens up a new avenue of research. It moves beyond simple human motion generation to a more nuanced and semantically rich form of content creation that incorporates environmental and interaction contexts.
- **Addressing the Data Bottleneck:** By cleverly decomposing the problem into contact prediction and motion generation, and by learning category-agnostic geometric representations, Text2HOI effectively mitigates the severe data scarcity issue inherent in 3D hand-object interaction datasets. This approach enables the model to generalize to unseen objects, which is a critical capability for real-world applications.
- **Enhanced Physical Plausibility and Realism:** The integration of a dedicated contact map prediction module, specific geometric losses (distance map, relative orientation), and a fast, non-diffusion-based refinement network is a major methodological contribution. This ensures that the generated motions are not just semantically accurate but also physically sound, minimizing common artifacts like penetration and ensuring realistic contact, which is paramount for believable simulations and animations.
- **Broadening Applications Across Industries:** The capabilities of Text2HOI have transformative potential across various domains:
 - **Computer Graphics and Animation:** Animators can rapidly prototype complex hand-object interactions, reducing manual labor and accelerating content creation for games, films, and virtual environments. This could democratize 3D animation by allowing text-based descriptions to drive character behavior.
 - **Robotics:** The ability to generate complex manipulation trajectories directly from natural language instructions could revolutionize robot programming, making robots more intuitive to control and enabling them to perform intricate tasks in unstructured environments.
 - **Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR/AR):** Text2HOI can facilitate the creation of highly interactive and immersive VR/AR experiences, where virtual hands realistically manipulate virtual objects based on user input or narrative cues.
 - **Training and Simulation:** From surgical training simulators requiring precise hand movements to industrial assembly simulations, the framework can generate realistic scenarios for skill development and procedural learning.
- **Foundation for Future Research:** The public release of the model and the newly labeled datasets (augmented from H2O, GRAB, and ARCTIC) is a substantial contribution to the research community. This provides a strong baseline and valuable resources for future work, encouraging further advancements in text-to-3D generation, human-robot interaction, and embodied AI. The limitations section points to future work on incorporating forces, which further highlights the extensibility of this foundational contribution.

In conclusion, Text2HOI pushes the frontier of generative AI by enabling intuitive and realistic creation of dynamic 3D hand-object interactions from textual descriptions, laying a robust foundation for next-generation applications in virtual content creation, robotics, and immersive technologies.
