# NoRD: A Data-Efficient Vision-Language-Action Model that Drives without Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Applied Intuition developed NORD, a Vision-Language-Action model for autonomous driving that achieves competitive performance without explicit reasoning or extensive data by adapting Dr. GRPO for reinforcement learning post-training. This approach reached a PDM score of 85.6 on NAVSIM and an RFS of 7.709 on WaymoE2E, utilizing significantly less training data and demonstrating improved runtime efficiency.

## Problem

- Existing end-to-end Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for autonomous driving incur high costs due to extensive data collection, detailed reasoning annotations, and increased training/inference latency.
- Standard Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) post-training methods often require large datasets with reasoning traces to achieve high performance, limiting scalability.
- Current RL post-training algorithms like GRPO exhibit a 'difficulty bias,' failing to effectively optimize weaker SFT policies on complex driving scenarios with sparse and high-variance reward signals.

## Method

- NORD (==No Reasoning for Driving==) is a Vision-Language-Action model built on Qwen-2.5VL-3B-Instruct, designed for data and token efficiency by eliminating reasoning annotations.
- It employs a two-stage training pipeline: initial Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on limited data to create a '==weak SFT policy==,' followed by Reinforcement Learning (RL) post-training.
- The RL post-training phase utilizes Dr. GRPO, a modified policy optimization algorithm that removes the standard deviation term from advantage calculation to mitigate 'difficulty bias' and enable effective learning from high-variance, challenging scenarios.

## Results

- NORD achieved a PDM score of 85.6 on the NAVSIM benchmark and, in a best-of-N configuration, outperformed the reasoning-based AutoVLA-BoN with a PDM score of 92.4 (vs. 92.1).
- On the WaymoE2E benchmark, NORD obtained an RFS of 7.709 and an ADE at 3 seconds of 1.2504, outperforming models that used 12-**17x** more training data and often relied on reasoning.
- The Dr. GRPO modification improved the PDM score of the NORD-BASE policy by +**11.68%** (from 76.66 to 85.62), a substantial gain compared to the marginal +**0.67%** achieved with standard GRPO.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Standard GRPO's 'difficulty bias,' which disproportionately penalizes high-variance scenarios, is a major impediment to optimizing weaker SFT policies for complex driving tasks.
> - Dr. GRPO successfully addresses this bias by allowing 'hard' scenarios to contribute sufficient gradient signals, leading to substantial performance gains in data-efficient, reasoning-free VLA models.
> - High-performance autonomous driving VLAs can be developed without explicit language-based reasoning or extensive datasets, shifting the learning burden to robust RL fine-tuning and challenging the perceived necessity of reasoning traces.
