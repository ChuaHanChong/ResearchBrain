# How to Enable LLM with 3D Capacity? A Survey of Spatial Reasoning in LLM

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Tsinghua University and HKUST-GZ present a comprehensive survey of methods for enabling spatial reasoning in Large Language Models (LLMs), organizing approaches into image-based, point cloud-based, and hybrid modalities while analyzing current limitations and future directions for integrating 3D understanding capabilities.

## Problem

- LLMs lack inherent ability to understand and reason about 3D spatial information
- Fundamental mismatch between sequential text processing and continuous 3D spatial structures
- Scarcity of high-quality 3D datasets compared to text corpora

## Method

- Systematic categorization of approaches based on input modality (==image==, ==point cloud==, ==hybrid==)
- Analysis of different ==alignment strategies== for connecting language and 3D data
- Review of architectural modifications and training strategies for spatial reasoning

## Results

- Comprehensive taxonomy for understanding diverse approaches to **3D-capable LLMs**
- Identification of key technical challenges across different modalities
- Analysis of current limitations in dataset quality, computation, and cross-scene generalization
- Roadmap of future research directions in perception, fusion, and real-world applications

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Image-based methods offer data availability advantages but struggle with depth perception
> - Point cloud approaches provide better geometric fidelity but face computational challenges
> - Hybrid methods can leverage complementary strengths through careful architectural design
> - Three main alignment strategies emerge: direct, step-by-step, and task-specific

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This survey paper is positioned within the rapidly evolving research area of multi-modal AI, specifically focusing on endowing Large Language Models (LLMs) with the ability to understand and reason about 3D spatial data. This area is a confluence of several key fields:

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** LLMs have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in language understanding, generation, and reasoning.
- **Computer Vision (CV):** The field has made significant advances in 3D scene understanding, object recognition, and spatial perception.
- **Multi-Modal Learning:** This area focuses on integrating information from different modalities (e.g., text, images, 3D data) to create more comprehensive and intelligent systems.
- **Robotics and Autonomous Systems:** Enhancing spatial reasoning in LLMs directly benefits robotics and autonomous systems, enabling them to interact with the physical world more effectively.

The paper acknowledges the existing survey by Ma et al. [2024b], but justifies its contribution by highlighting the rapid progress in this area, necessitating an up-to-date overview of the latest breakthroughs. This places the paper as a timely and relevant contribution to the research community, providing a current snapshot of the field. The taxonomy proposed in the paper, categorizing methods based on input modality (image-based, point cloud-based, hybrid), provides a structured way to understand the diverse approaches being pursued. The work contributes by distilling the latest research and organizing it under a clear framework that benefits researchers entering or working in the field.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The key objectives and motivations of this survey paper are:

- **Address the Need for an Updated Overview:** The rapid advancement of LLMs in 3D understanding tasks necessitates a current survey to capture recent developments.
- **Provide a Structured Taxonomy:** To categorize and organize existing research into three primary groups based on their approach to incorporating 3D data into LLMs.
- **Comprehensive Review:** To systematically review current research progress on LLMs for spatial reasoning tasks, covering data representations, architectural modifications, and training strategies.
- **Highlight Limitations and Future Directions:** To identify the remaining limitations of existing works and suggest potential directions for future research.
- **Bridge the Gap:** To address the growing interest in enabling LLMs with a “3D capacity,” enabling them to interpret, reason, and generate three-dimensional representations based on natural language.
- **Challenges:** To address integrating 3D capacity into LLMs facing several key challenges, including the scarcity of high-quality 3D datasets compared to abundant text corpora, the fundamental mismatch between sequential text data and continuous 3D spatial structures, requiring specialized architectural adaptations; and the intensive computational requirements for processing 3D data at scale.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The paper employs a survey-based methodology, involving the following steps:

- **Literature Review:** A comprehensive review of existing research papers, articles, and preprints related to LLMs and 3D spatial understanding.
- **Taxonomy Development:** Creation of a novel taxonomy to categorize existing methods based on input modalities (image-based, point cloud-based, and hybrid modality-based).
- **Systematic Review:** Detailed analysis of representative methods within each category, covering data representations, architectural modifications, and training strategies.
- **Critical Analysis:** Identification of current limitations, challenges, and potential future research directions.
- **Synthesis and Organization:** Consolidation of findings into a structured and coherent report.

The authors use a systematic approach to categorize and analyze the existing literature, focusing on:
- **Image-based:** methods that derive 3D understanding from 2D images
- **Point cloud-based:** methods working directly with 3D representations
- **Hybrid modality-based:** methods combining multiple data streams.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The main findings and results of the survey can be summarized as follows:

- **Taxonomy:** The proposed taxonomy provides a useful framework for understanding the diverse approaches to integrating LLMs with 3D spatial data.
- **Image-based Spatial Reasoning:** These methods offer advantages in terms of data availability and leveraging pre-trained 2D models, but face challenges related to depth perception, occlusion, and viewpoint dependency.
- **Point cloud-based Spatial Reasoning:** These methods work directly with 3D data, offering greater geometric fidelity, but face challenges in handling unstructured data and computational complexity. The paper identifies three main alignment strategies: Direct, Step-by-step, and Task-specific.
- **Hybrid Modality-based Spatial Reasoning:** These methods combine multiple data streams, leveraging complementary strengths, but require careful architectural design and training strategies to ensure effective fusion. Tightly and loosely coupled architectures are reviewed.
- **Limitations:** The survey highlights several limitations, including the scarcity of high-quality 3D datasets, computational challenges, the difficulty of aligning sequential text with continuous 3D structures, and the need for improved spatial reasoning capabilities in LLMs.
- **Future Directions:** The survey identifies promising research directions, including:
 - Enhancing 3D perception and representations.
 - Improving multi-modal fusion and instruction understanding.
 - Developing cross-scene generalization and robust evaluation metrics.
 - Expanding applications in robotics, medical imaging, and other fields.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The survey paper has several significant contributions and potential impacts:

- **Comprehensive Overview:** It provides a comprehensive and up-to-date overview of a rapidly evolving research area, serving as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners.
- **Structured Understanding:** The proposed taxonomy provides a structured way to understand the diverse approaches to integrating LLMs with 3D spatial data.
- **Identification of Challenges and Opportunities:** The survey highlights the key challenges and limitations in the field, as well as promising directions for future research, guiding future research efforts.
- **Promotion of Interdisciplinary Research:** By highlighting the intersection of NLP, computer vision, robotics, and other fields, the survey promotes interdisciplinary research and collaboration.
- **Advancement of AI Capabilities:** The integration of LLMs with 3D spatial understanding has the potential to significantly enhance the capabilities of AI systems in various applications, including robotics, autonomous systems, medical imaging, and virtual reality.
- **Industrial Application:** This work will likely guide the deployment of 3D capable LLMs in real-world applications.

In conclusion, this survey paper makes a valuable contribution to the research community by providing a comprehensive and structured overview of the emerging field of LLMs with 3D spatial reasoning capabilities. The paper identifies key challenges and opportunities, guiding future research efforts and potentially leading to significant advancements in AI and related fields.
