# DrEureka: Language Model Guided Sim-To-Real Transfer

> [!summary] Summary
> DrEureka automates sim-to-real transfer for robotics by leveraging large language models to synthesize reward functions and configure domain randomization. This framework enabled a quadruped robot to achieve 34% higher forward velocity compared to human-designed policies and successfully taught a quadruped to balance and walk on a yoga ball, a previously unsolved task.

## Problem

- Bridging the 'reality gap' between robot training in simulation and deployment in the real world due to imperfect simulation models.
- The difficulty and expert knowledge required to manually design effective reward functions for reinforcement learning that promote safe and robust robot behaviors.
- The manual and often tedious tuning of domain randomization parameters needed to create policies robust to real-world variations.

## Method

- A three-stage framework uses LLMs to synthesize safety-regularized reward functions in code based on task descriptions.
- A 'Reward-Aware Physics Prior' (RAPP) is constructed by evaluating trained policies in perturbed simulations to identify feasible ranges for physics parameters.
- LLMs are then prompted with these RAPP ranges to automatically configure domain randomization parameters, determining which parameters to randomize and their distributions.

## Results

- DrEureka-trained policies achieved approximately 34% higher forward velocity and 20% greater distance traveled for quadruped locomotion compared to human-designed policies across various terrains.
- The framework successfully enabled a novel task: a quadruped robot balancing and walking on a yoga ball, demonstrating robust sim-to-real transfer for complex, unsolved challenges.
- For dexterous manipulation, the best DrEureka policy resulted in a 300% increase in in-hand cube rotations compared to human-designed policies.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large language models can effectively reason about physical systems and robotics when provided with task context and physical parameter ranges.
> - Explicit safety instructions during LLM prompting lead to the generation of more robust and deployable robotic policies.
> - The framework intelligently determines which simulation parameters to randomize and their distributions based on task requirements, outperforming uniform randomization.
