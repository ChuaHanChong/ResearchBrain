# Hadamax Encoding: Elevating Performance in Model-Free Atari

> [!summary] Summary
> The Hadamax encoder, developed by researchers at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, significantly boosts the performance of pixel-based model-free reinforcement learning agents on the Atari-57 benchmark by approximately 80% over the PQN baseline. This novel architecture, combining max-pooling, Hadamard representations, and GELU activations, allows PQN to surpass Rainbow-DQN's performance much faster while maintaining computational efficiency.

## Problem

- Neural network architectures in model-free reinforcement learning have remained relatively simple despite advancements in other deep learning domains.
- Scaling pixel-based RL architectures often leads to training instability or only modest performance improvements, especially in high-dimensional Atari environments.
- A more effective and stable encoder design is needed to unlock substantial performance benefits in model-free RL without complex algorithmic changes.

## Method

- The **Hadamax** encoder is introduced, a novel pixel-based convolutional architecture that enhances network capacity through specific design choices.
- It incorporates max-pooling for feature compression, Hadamard representations for rich multiplicative interactions between parallel hidden layers, and GELU activation functions.
- The encoder is integrated into the Parallelised Q-Network (PQN) algorithm and evaluated on Atari, with no changes to the base algorithm or hyperparameters.

## Results

- The Hadamax encoder achieved an approximately **80%** performance increase over the PQN baseline on the Atari-57 benchmark, setting a new state-of-the-art for model-free performance.
- Hadamax-PQN outperformed Rainbow-DQN, reaching comparable performance levels nearly three times faster (at **90M** vs. **260M** frames) with substantially reduced computational resources.
- The encoder's effectiveness generalized to other model-free algorithms, boosting C51 performance by ~**70%** on Atari-10 and DQN/Rainbow by 20-**30%** on Atari-3.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Significant performance gains in model-free RL are achievable through targeted architectural innovation rather than solely relying on algorithmic changes or increased network depth.
> - Hadamard representations effectively increase the network's capacity and effective rank in deeper layers, supporting stable training even with zero-saturating activation functions like GELU.
> - Max-pooling is crucial for efficient feature compression and selection, while the synergistic combination of max-pooling, Hadamard representations, and GELU is key to the encoder's performance.
