# Accelerating Diffusion Planners in Offline RL via Reward-Aware Consistency Trajectory Distillation

> [!summary] Summary
> RACTD (Reward-Aware Consistency Trajectory Distillation) accelerates diffusion planners in offline reinforcement learning by enabling single-step action trajectory generation through distilling a reward-aware student from a teacher diffusion model. The method achieves up to a 142x speedup in inference time and improves average performance on D4RL Gym-MuJoCo benchmarks to 96.4.

## Problem

- Diffusion models in reinforcement learning suffer from slow inference speeds due to their iterative sampling process, hindering real-time applications.
- Existing consistency-based RL methods struggle to effectively learn from suboptimal and heterogeneous offline datasets, or involve complex and unstable multi-network concurrent training.
- Classifier-guided diffusion methods require training noise-aware reward models, which is complex and can lead to inaccurate guidance from corrupted inputs.

## Method

- **RACTD** employs a decoupled training framework involving a pre-trained ==teacher diffusion planner==, an independent reward model, and a student consistency model.
- The student model is trained with a multi-objective loss function combining consistency trajectory distillation (CTM and DSM loss) with an explicit negative reward objective.
- The reward objective utilizes a frozen, independently trained reward model operating on noise-free inputs to steer the student towards high-reward action modes in a single sampling step.

## Results

- RACTD achieved the highest average score on D4RL Gym-MuJoCo benchmarks (96.4 for offline, 101.5 for online model selection), outperforming existing baselines.
- The method demonstrated substantial inference time speedups, including a **142x** speedup over its EDM teacher model and a **43x** speedup over Diffuser on MuJoCo tasks, all with single-step sampling.
- It surpassed the Diffuser baseline on D4RL Maze2d tasks, achieving an average score of 133.4 compared to Diffuser's 119.5, with significantly fewer sampling steps (up to **256x** reduction).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating a reward objective directly into the consistency trajectory distillation process enables a single-step planner to effectively select and generate high-reward behaviors from multimodal distributions learned by a teacher diffusion model.
> - Decoupling the training of the reward model and the student consistency model simplifies the overall framework, avoids the complexity of noise-aware reward modeling, and enhances training stability.
> - Consistency trajectory distillation, when combined with reward guidance, allows the student to retain the teacher's capacity for complex, long-horizon planning while achieving substantial speedups.
