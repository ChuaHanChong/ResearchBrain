# PhysVid: Physics Aware Local Conditioning for Generative Video Models

> [!summary] Summary
> PhysVid improves the physical realism of videos generated by Text-to-Video models by introducing a local, physics-aware conditioning scheme. This approach uses Vision Language Models to annotate contiguous video chunks with physics-grounded descriptions, outperforming larger baseline models on physical plausibility benchmarks like VideoPhy and VideoPhy2.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art generative video models frequently produce outputs that violate fundamental physical laws despite high visual fidelity.
- Existing physics-aware methods often apply conditioning globally, limiting their ability to capture fine-grained physical phenomena evolving over shorter temporal segments.
- Global text conditioning can lead to spatiotemporal misalignment or static attention maps, struggling to interpret temporal logic for intricate local interactions.

## Method

- **PhysVid** segments videos into contiguous temporal chunks and uses a ==Vision Language Model (VLM)== to generate physics-grounded descriptions for each chunk during training.
- It integrates ==chunk-aware cross-attention layers== with ==Rotary Positional Embeddings== into the generative video model, enabling cross-modal position awareness for localized textual conditioning.
- During inference, an LLM generates both positive and ==counterfactual (negative) local physics prompts==, which are then used with ==Classifier-Free Guidance== to steer generation towards physical accuracy.

## Results

- PhysVid achieved a Physical Commonsense (PC) score of **0.32** on the VideoPhy benchmark, representing an approximate **33%** relative gain over the larger Wan-14B baseline (PC **0.24**) with a significantly smaller model (**1.7B** vs **14B** parameters).
- On the VideoPhy2 benchmark, PhysVid (PC **0.64**) outperformed the Wan-1.3B (**0.61**) and Wan-14B (**0.59**) baselines by approximately **5%** and **8%** respectively, consistently across various subcategories.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both the chunk-aware cross-attention mechanism and the application of counterfactual guidance contribute to the observed improvements in physical commonsense scores.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Many critical physical phenomena manifest and evolve distinctly over local, temporally contiguous segments of a video, making chunk-level conditioning effective for enforcing physical laws.
> - Vision Language Models can extract detailed, physics-grounded annotations from generic video data, allowing for generalizable physics awareness without requiring pre-existing specialized labels.
> - A chunk-aware cross-attention mechanism can effectively align localized physics descriptions with corresponding video segments, enhancing spatiotemporal consistency in generated content.
> - Negative physics prompts (counterfactual guidance) at inference time further improve physical commonsense by actively steering the model away from physically implausible scenarios.
