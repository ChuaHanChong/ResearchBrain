# Hybrid Latent Reasoning with Decoupled Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> The HyLaR framework enhances multimodal large language models by integrating continuous visual latent reasoning with discrete text generation to overcome early semantic collapse. It introduces Decoupled Policy Optimization (DePO), a novel reinforcement learning algorithm that addresses optimization challenges in hybrid action spaces. This approach yields performance improvements of 7.33% on V* and 7.00% on HRBench-4K, and reduces hallucination by 7.11% on HallusionBench.

## Problem

- Conventional MLLMs suffer from 'early semantic collapse,' prematurely compressing high-bandwidth visual signals into discrete text tokens and losing fine-grained details.
- Existing 'Think-with-Images' paradigms, which use external tools for re-perception (e.g., zooming), introduce rigid bottlenecks, potential errors, and increased inference latency.
- Optimizing hybrid discrete-continuous action spaces for MLLMs faces challenges due to variance mismatches in importance sampling ratios and geometric mismatches (Euclidean assumptions on hyperspherical MLLM representations) with standard reinforcement learning.

## Method

- **HyLaR** seamlessly interleaves discrete text generation with ==continuous visual latent representations==, employing control tokens (<|canvas_start|>, <|canvas_end|>) to define an internal ==canvas mode== for visual working memory.
- A two-stage training process begins with ==Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)== to align continuous internal states with compressed visual features, followed by ==Reinforcement Learning (RL)== using the novel **Decoupled Policy Optimization (DePO)** algorithm.
- **DePO** models continuous latent actions using the ==von Mises–Fisher (vMF) distribution==, applies ==decoupled PPO surrogate losses== with separate, tighter clipping ranges for continuous updates, and uses ==closed-form KL regularization== to respect hyperspherical geometry.

## Results

- HyLaR-7B achieved notable performance gains on high-resolution perception and visual search benchmarks, with improvements of **+7.33%** on V*, **+7.00%** on HRBench-4K, and **+14.50%** on HRBench-8K (FCP) compared to Qwen2.5-VL-7B.
- The model enhanced general VQA accuracy across several benchmarks (e.g., **+8.00%** on MMVP, **+2.30%** on MMStar) and reduced visual hallucination by **7.11%** on HallusionBench.
- Ablation studies confirmed DePO's significant outperformance over other RL algorithms and the critical role of decoupled clipping ranges and vMF distribution for optimal and stable learning in the hybrid latent space.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Retaining visual information in a continuous latent space through an internal 'canvas mode' effectively mitigates early semantic collapse, allowing for finer-grained perception during reasoning.
> - Standard reinforcement learning algorithms are suboptimal for hybrid discrete-continuous MLLM action spaces due to inherent variance and geometric mismatches, necessitating a tailored optimization approach like DePO.
> - Modeling continuous latent representations with von Mises–Fisher (vMF) distributions, combined with decoupled trust-region clipping, provides stable and geometrically sound optimization for MLLMs' hyperspherical latent spaces.
