# MuLoCo: Muon is a practical inner optimizer for DiLoCo

> [!summary] Summary
> Mila researchers demonstrate that Muon optimizer significantly outperforms AdamW as the inner optimizer in DiLoCo distributed training frameworks, with MuLoCo achieving 8x communication reduction through 2-bit quantization and error feedback while maintaining faster convergence than standard DiLoCo on 220M parameter transformer pre-training, establishing that Muon updates are more resilient to aggressive compression and providing the first systematic evaluation of how inner optimizer choice affects communication efficiency in distributed large language model training.

## Problem

- Communication bottleneck in distributed LLM training creates major scalability limitations as networks require frequent synchronization of large parameter updates
- Previous DiLoCo implementations used AdamW without systematic investigation of how inner optimizer choice affects compression effectiveness and communication efficiency
- Lack of comprehensive evaluation of error feedback mechanisms combined with different optimizers in communication-constrained distributed training scenarios

## Method

- Replace AdamW with ==Muon optimizer in DiLoCo framework== to create MuLoCo, leveraging Muon's superior optimization properties for distributed settings
- Implement systematic ==compression schemes including quantization and top-k sparsification== with ==error feedback== to accumulate information lost during compression
- Conduct thorough hyperparameter optimization and ablation studies across different compression levels and error feedback configurations

## Results

- MuLoCo with **2-bit** quantization and error feedback achieves **8x** communication reduction while converging faster than uncompressed DiLoCo with AdamW
- MuLoCo without compression matches data-parallel baseline performance while providing substantial communication savings compared to traditional distributed training
- Error feedback consistently improves performance across all compression schemes, with MuLoCo showing superior robustness to aggressive quantization levels

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Muon optimizer produces updates that are inherently more compressible and resilient to quantization compared to AdamW updates
> - Error feedback mechanisms consistently improve convergence for both optimizers but provide greater benefits when combined with Muon
> - Communication efficiency and optimization performance are not mutually exclusive when proper optimizer-compression combinations are used
