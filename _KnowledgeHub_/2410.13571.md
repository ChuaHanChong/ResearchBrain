# DriveDreamer4D: World Models Are Effective Data Machines for 4D Driving Scene Representation

> [!summary] Summary
> DriveDreamer4D enhances 4D driving scene representation by utilizing autonomous driving world models to generate diverse synthetic training data for 4D Gaussian Splatting. The system improves visual quality and spatiotemporal coherence for renderings from novel and complex ego-vehicle trajectories, making closed-loop simulation more robust.

## Problem

- Existing 4D driving scene representation methods (e.g., NeRF, 3DGS) struggle to accurately render complex vehicle maneuvers or from out-of-distribution viewpoints due to training data bias towards simple forward-driving scenarios.
- Autonomous driving 'world models' are effective at generating diverse 2D driving videos but lack the 3D/4D spatiotemporal coherence required for high-fidelity physical simulation and reconstruction.
- A critical need exists for robust and realistic closed-loop simulation capabilities to evaluate end-to-end autonomous driving systems, which current sensor simulation methods cannot adequately provide.

## Method

- A Novel Trajectory Generation Module (**NTGM**) synthesizes diverse ego-vehicle trajectories and corresponding 2D video frames using an autonomous driving world model, ensuring physical plausibility and consistency with scene elements.
- A Cousin Data Training Strategy (**CDTS**) combines real-world and generated synthetic data in temporally-aligned pairs to optimize the 4D ==Gaussian Splatting== (4DGS) model.
- The 4DGS optimization includes a specific loss function that excludes depth supervision for synthetic novel trajectory data and incorporates a perception regularization loss to ensure perceptual coherence between real and synthetic views.

## Results

- DriveDreamer4D significantly improves spatiotemporal coherence, achieving average relative gains of 22.6% in Novel Trajectory Agent IoU (NTA-IoU) and 1.8% in Novel Trajectory Lane IoU (NTL-IoU) against PVG baseline.
- Visual quality is substantially enhanced, with a 32.1% relative reduction in FID score compared to PVG for lane change scenarios, indicating fewer artifacts and higher fidelity.
- User studies consistently show strong preference for DriveDreamer4D, with over 80% win rates against baselines, validating the perceived quality and realism of its renderings under complex maneuvers.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Autonomous driving world models can serve as effective 'data machines' to synthesize diverse, high-fidelity training data for 4D scene representation models, overcoming data scarcity for complex maneuvers.
> - Excluding depth supervision for synthetic novel views is crucial for 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) optimization, as projected LiDAR depth from novel views is inherently incomplete and can degrade performance.
> - The Cousin Data Training Strategy (CDTS), which pairs real and synthetic data with perceptual regularization, effectively merges these data sources to enhance both visual quality and spatiotemporal consistency in 4DGS.
