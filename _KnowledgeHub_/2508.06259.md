# SIFThinker: Spatially-Aware Image Focus for Visual Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> The SIFThinker framework enhances multimodal large language models by intrinsically integrating dynamic visual attention with 3D spatial awareness, mimicking human perception. Researchers from Tsinghua University and ByteDance developed this approach, which achieved state-of-the-art results across spatial intelligence, fine-grained visual perception, and grounding benchmarks, including an 8.01% improvement over ChatGPT-o3 on the SAT (Static) benchmark.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with complex visual tasks that require fine-grained perception and robust spatial understanding, often processing images uniformly.
- Current MLLMs lack the dynamic attention shifts and inherent 3D spatial awareness characteristic of human visual perception, hindering accurate interpretation of 2D projections.
- Existing 'think-with-images' methods often rely on fragmented reasoning steps, external tools, or outcome-based supervision, leading to less coherent and intrinsic multimodal reasoning.

## Method

- SIFThinker introduces a unified framework that merges ==dynamic visual perception==, including attention correction and region focusing, with ==3D spatial awareness== via explicit depth information.
- A novel dataset, ==SIF-50K==, was created using a reverse-expansion–forward-inference strategy to provide process-level supervision for spatially-aware visual focus through interleaved image-text chains of thought.
- The model is trained in two stages: supervised fine-tuning for structured reasoning, followed by reinforcement learning (==GRPO-SIF==) with a composite reward function that includes reasoning format, progressive answer accuracy, correction-enhanced grounding via Hierarchical IoU, and depth consistency.

## Results

- SIFThinker achieved state-of-the-art performance on SpatialBench, surpassing SpatialBot by **7.82%** and SSR by **11.17%**, and outperformed ChatGPT-o3 by **8.01%** on the SAT (Static) benchmark.
- The framework demonstrated superior visual perception, outperforming SEAL by **5.75%** on V*Bench-Attribute and showing comparable overall performance to ChatGPT-o3 on VisCoT s.
- Improved grounding capabilities were observed in Referring Expression Comprehension and Open-Vocabulary Detection, with a **45.9%** gain over VisRL on OVDEval, while maintaining strong performance on general VLM benchmarks (e.g., **~4%** improvement on MMBench).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The inherent integration of dynamic, human-like attention with explicit 3D spatial awareness significantly boosts MLLMs' capabilities for complex visual reasoning and grounding.
> - Process-level supervision using interleaved image-text chains of thought with depth-enhanced bounding boxes is critical for training MLLMs to perform intrinsic, fine-grained visual focus.
> - A reinforcement learning paradigm with specifically designed reward functions, particularly those encouraging iterative correction and depth consistency, effectively refines a model's visual grounding and spatial understanding.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The research on SIFThinker addresses a critical limitation in current Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs): their struggle with complex visual tasks that demand fine-grained perception and robust spatial understanding. While MLLMs have made remarkable strides in integrating visual and textual information, they often process entire images uniformly, failing to mimic the dynamic attention shifts and 3D spatial awareness inherent in human visual perception.

The broader research landscape for visual reasoning in MLLMs can be categorized into several approaches:
- **Traditional Methods:** These models typically process an entire image without dynamic attention, hindering their ability to pinpoint prompt-relevant regions or infer 3D relationships from 2D projections.
- **Visual Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning:**
 - **Pure-Text-Thought Methods:** These approaches (e.g., R1-V, UniVG-R1) generate purely textual reasoning chains, neglecting intermediate visual signals and dynamic attention.
 - **Intermediate-Thought Methods:** Models like V*, VisCoT, and VisRL predict fine-grained visual cues (e.g., bounding boxes) in a fragmented, stepwise manner, then perform inference. This often severs the continuity of reasoning and can lead to weaker interpretability.
 - **Multi-Modal-Thought Methods ("Think-with-Images"):** More recent efforts, such as Cogcom and ChatGPT-o3, simulate dynamic attention by adaptively zooming or using external specialized models/tools. However, these methods often rely on external capabilities, making them less "intrinsic" to the MLLM's core reasoning process and potentially less coherent. Other works (e.g., GRIT, Chain-of-Focus) have explored interleaving language and explicit bounding boxes or iterative cropping, but often lack 3D spatial awareness or rely on outcome-based supervision.
- **Spatial Intelligence in MLLMs:** Recognizing the limitations of MLLMs trained primarily on RGB images without explicit spatial cues, works like SpatialRGPT, SpatialVLM, SSR, and SpatialBot have attempted to enhance 3D perception. They achieve this by incorporating depth images, constructing specialized datasets, or leveraging depth estimation tools. However, these methods often focus solely on reasoning, without deeply integrating spatial awareness with visual grounding – two processes that are fundamentally interdependent in human perception.

SIFThinker distinguishes itself by proposing an **intrinsic and unified framework** that merges dynamic visual perception (attention correction and region focusing) with 3D spatial awareness (depth information) into a single, coherent reasoning pipeline. Unlike prior "think-with-images" methods, SIFThinker inherently supports interleaved image-text reasoning by simulating human-like attention shifts using depth-enhanced bounding boxes directly within the model's autoregressive structure. It moves beyond fragmented or tool-dependent approaches, positioning itself as a significant advancement in developing MLLMs capable of more human-like, spatially-aware visual reasoning.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop **SIFThinker**, a spatially-aware "think-with-images" framework that mimics the intricate dynamics of human visual perception for complex visual reasoning tasks.

The key motivations and specific objectives driving this work are:
1. **Bridging the Gap with Human Perception:** The authors observe that human visual perception is dynamic, involving progressive focusing, attention correction, and an inherent understanding of 3D spatial relationships. Current MLLMs largely fall short in these aspects, often processing images uniformly or in fragmented steps. The motivation is to imbue MLLMs with these human-like capabilities.
2. **Enabling Dynamic Visual Perception:** To allow MLLMs to dynamically shift and correct their attention to prompt-relevant regions throughout the reasoning process, moving beyond static or externally-guided focus mechanisms.
3. **Integrating 3D Spatial Awareness:** To overcome the limitation of MLLMs primarily trained on 2D RGB images, by explicitly incorporating 3D spatial cues (depth information) directly into the visual perception and reasoning pipeline, enabling a deeper understanding of visual scenes.
4. **Developing Coherent Interleaved Reasoning:** To create an intrinsic mechanism for coherent image-text interleaved reasoning, where visual grounding and answer generation are unified, rather than separated or reliant on external tools.
5. **Constructing a Tailored Dataset:** To facilitate the training of such a framework, a novel dataset (SIF-50K) is needed that provides process-level supervision in the form of interleaved image-text chains of thought, simulating spatially-aware visual focus.
6. **Designing an Effective Training Paradigm:** To propose a reinforcement learning paradigm, GRPO-SIF, that integrates depth-informed visual grounding and utilizes novel reward functions to guide the model to dynamically correct and focus its attention on relevant regions, ultimately leading to more accurate answers.

In essence, the motivation is to create an MLLM that not only "thinks about" images but also actively "thinks with" them, dynamically perceiving and reasoning with spatially-grounded visual information, much like humans do.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

SIFThinker is designed as a sophisticated, two-stage pipeline that integrates spatially-aware visual focus into multimodal reasoning. Its core components are data generation, a warm-up phase, and a reinforcement learning stage.

**A. Data Generation: SIF-50K Dataset**
To enable process-level supervision for spatially-aware visual focus, SIFThinker introduces a novel dataset, SIF-50K, comprising approximately 50,000 samples. This dataset is constructed by adapting existing benchmarks like Flickr30k, Visual7W, GQA, Open Images, VSR, Birds-200-2021 (for fine-grained reasoning), and TallyQA (for multi-instance counting), all of which provide ground-truth bounding box annotations.

The data generation employs a **reverse-expansion–forward-inference strategy**:
1. **Reverse Expansion (Algorithm 1):** For each (Question, Image, Ground-truth Bounding Boxes, Answer) tuple, the process iteratively expands and merges the ground-truth bounding boxes. This generates a sequence of increasingly broader regions. Crucially, it also includes randomly selected bounding boxes from outside the current regions to simulate exploring other parts of the image, and uses an early termination mechanism to ensure a diverse set of regions. This process mimics how humans might start with a specific focus and then broaden their attention before narrowing it down again.
2. **Depth Estimation:** The DepthAnythingV2 model is used to generate a depth map for the input image, providing the crucial 3D spatial context.
3. **Forward Inference for CoT Generation:** A powerful Multimodal Large Language Model (Doubao-1.5-vision-pro) is then used to generate a detailed, interleaved image-text Chain-of-Thought (CoT). This CoT is conditioned on the original question, answer, the generated depth map, and the sequence of expanded bounding boxes. The output R_cot includes interleaved text explanations and depth-enhanced bounding box annotations, providing process-level supervision.

**B. Spatially-Aware Image Focus Training Paradigm**
The training of SIFThinker proceeds in two stages:

1. **Warm-start Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT):**
 - The model (M_SFT) is initially fine-tuned on the full SIF-50K dataset. This stage primarily teaches the model to adopt the structured reasoning format with explicit, focused regions and interleaved image-text generation. The objective is to bias the model towards producing coherent chains of thought that incorporate visual information dynamically.

2. **Reinforcement Learning (RL) with GRPO-SIF:**
 - This is the core stage where the model's grounded behaviors are further refined and optimized, yielding the final model (M_RL). It operates on a smaller, curated subset of 200 samples from SIF-50K for efficient progressive learning.
 - **RL Formulation:** SIFThinker builds upon the Group-Relative Policy Optimisation (GRPO) framework. The model M_SFT acts as a policy (π_θ) that generates output sequences (o_i) based on the input (Q, I, D_I). The policy is updated by maximizing an objective function that incorporates a ratio between the new and old policies, a KL divergence regularization to a reference model, and a **group-normalized advantage**. This advantage is calculated based on a task-specific reward (R) for each generated completion.
 - **Composite Reward Function:** The task reward (R) is a combination of four novel components, designed to jointly encourage spatially-aware, grounded reasoning:
 - **Reasoning-format Reward (r_format):** Assigns a high reward (1.0) if the model adheres strictly to a predefined output format, which requires `<think><area>...</area><text>...</text></think><answer>...</answer>`. The `<area>` tag must contain JSON-formatted bounding boxes with depth information, while `<text>` provides explanations grounded in that region. This ensures structural compliance.
 - **Progressive-answer-accuracy Reward (r_ans,t):** Leverages an external Vision-Language Model (Doubao-1.5-vision-pro) as a judge to assess the quality and correctness of the predicted answer (s_t). The reward is designed to encourage not just a correct final answer, but also progressive improvement, defined as `s_t + (s_t - mean{s_1,t-1..., s_N,t-1})`.
 - **Correction-enhanced Grounding Reward (r_bbox):** This reward component focuses on the accuracy and refinement of the bounding boxes generated during reasoning. It uses a novel **Hierarchical Intersection over Union (HIoU)** metric, which combines a Global IoU (GIoU) for overall spatial consistency of multiple objects and a Pairwise IoU (PIoU) for matched individual objects. The `r_bbox` rewards the final grounding accuracy (`s_end = HIoU(B_end, B_gt)`) and crucially, a "correction-aware improvement" term (`s_end - s_init`), where `B_init` is the first non-full-image bounding box and `B_end` is the final predicted box. This encourages iterative refinement of the focused region.
 - **Depth-consistency Reward (r_depth):** This reward ensures that the depth values associated with the generated bounding boxes are consistent with the actual input depth map. It assigns a reward (1.0) only if the absolute error between the generated depth (d_i) and the ground-truth depth (`d_gt_i`) for each box `B_i` is below a threshold (`|d_i - d_gt_i| / d_gt_i <= T`). This penalizes depth hallucinations and reinforces accurate spatial awareness.

**C. Inference:**
During inference, given a question and an image, SIFThinker autoregressively generates a spatially-aware, interleaved image-text reasoning chain, dynamically focusing on relevant regions with associated depth information, and then provides a final answer.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

SIFThinker demonstrates significant performance improvements across a wide range of benchmarks, outperforming state-of-the-art open-source methods and even challenging leading proprietary models like ChatGPT-o3 in several key areas.

**A. Spatial Intelligence (Table 1):**
- **SpatialBench:** SIFThinker significantly outperforms previous methods that focus on spatial understanding. Using the same base model (Bunny-8B), it surpasses SpatialBot by 7.82% (64.3 vs. 59.6) and SSR by 11.17% (74.5 vs. 67.1).
- **SAT (Static) & CV-Bench:** On larger-scale benchmarks, SIFThinker achieves substantial gains of 11.15% on SAT (Static) and 3.97% on CV-Bench over its Qwen2.5-VL-7B base model.
- **Comparison with ChatGPT-o3:** SIFThinker achieves a comparable average score to ChatGPT-o3 on SpatialBench (74.6 vs. 74.8) and notably *outperforms* o3 by 8.01% on SAT (Static) (72.8 vs. 67.4), highlighting its superior capability in static spatial perception. The authors attribute these gains to the deep integration of depth perception and spatial grounding via reasoning over relevant regions.

**B. Visual Perception (Table 2 & 3):**
- **Visual Understanding (VisCoT s & V*Bench):**
 - **VisCoT s:** SIFThinker consistently outperforms other methods designed for fine-grained perception. For instance, it surpasses VisCoT-LLaVA-1.5-7B by 11.76% and VisRL-Qwen2.5-VL-7B by 8.89%.
 - **V*Bench:** On this challenging benchmark for fine-grained perception on high-resolution images, SIFThinker outperforms the state-of-the-art SEAL by 5.75% on the Attribute subset and 1.70% on the Spatial subset.
 - **Comparison with ChatGPT-o3:** SIFThinker achieves comparable overall performance to ChatGPT-o3 on VisCoT s (0.760 vs. 0.762) and *surpasses* o3 by 7.04% on V*Bench-Attribute (0.791 vs. 0.739), demonstrating the effectiveness of its intrinsic visual information integration and dynamic attention.
- **Grounding Capability (Table 3):**
 - **Referring Expression Comprehension (REC):** SIFThinker consistently outperforms previous generalist models with comparable parameters (e.g., VisRL by 1-3% on average across RefCOCO, RefCOCO+, RefCOCOg splits) and often surpasses specialist models like Grounding-DINO and UNINEXT.
 - **Open-Vocabulary Detection (OVD) on OVDEval:** SIFThinker shows promising robustness in multi-object scenarios, outperforming VisRL by 45.9% (37.8 vs. 25.9), which is attributed to the effectiveness of the proposed Hierarchical IoU (HIoU).
- **Self-correction Ability (Figure 4):** Visualizations illustrate SIFThinker's ability to correct its focus. Given an initial area, it can judge, search, and ultimately attend to the prompt-relevant region. It also successfully detects multiple relevant objects in complex scenes, a task where VisRL and VLM-R1 often yield only single-object predictions.

**C. General VLM Benchmarks (Table 4):**
SIFThinker not only excels in specialized tasks but also maintains or improves performance on widely used general VLM benchmarks (MME, MMBench, SEED-I, VQA v2, POPE). It consistently outperforms both VisCoT (fine-grained perception focused) and SpatialBot (spatial reasoning focused) under the same base models. For example, it achieves approximately 4% improvements on MMBench across different base model settings, demonstrating its robustness and broad applicability, especially where depth information can be beneficial.

**D. Ablation Study (Table 5):**
The ablation studies confirm the crucial contributions of each proposed component:
- **Training Strategy:** The "think-with-images" CoT-SFT (0.582) significantly outperforms direct VQA-SFT (0.536), validating the benefit of structured reasoning. The subsequent RL stage provides a substantial additional gain of 30.58% over CoT-SFT alone (0.760 vs. 0.582), highlighting its effectiveness in refining grounding and reasoning.
- **Reward Functions:** Removing the `r_bbox` (correction-enhanced grounding reward) or `r_ans,t` (progressive-answer-accuracy reward) leads to noticeable performance drops, emphasizing their importance.
- **Depth Information:** Removing the depth input (`w/o D_I`) causes a significant performance degradation (0.760 to 0.655), underscoring the critical role of 3D spatial awareness. Removing `r_depth` (depth-consistency reward) also reduces performance, confirming the need for accurate depth grounding.
- **Correction-enhanced Grounding Reward:** Specifically, removing the `(s_end - s_init)` term (the improvement component) from `r_bbox` reduces performance, validating the importance of encouraging iterative correction.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

**Significance:**
SIFThinker represents a significant advancement in multimodal large language models by introducing a pioneering framework that inherently integrates adaptive visual focus with 3D spatial awareness. By mimicking human-like dynamic attention and perception rooted in a 3D understanding of the world, SIFThinker moves beyond the limitations of prior MLLMs that often rely on fragmented reasoning, external tools, or lack explicit spatial cues. The development of the SIF-50K dataset through a novel reverse-expansion–forward-inference strategy provides crucial process-level supervision, a key enabler for this intrinsically coherent reasoning. Furthermore, the innovative GRPO-SIF reinforcement learning paradigm, with its hierarchical IoU and specialized reward functions (format, progressive answer accuracy, correction-enhanced grounding, and depth consistency), effectively guides the model to refine its visual attention and spatial understanding iteratively. The demonstrated state-of-the-art performance across diverse and challenging benchmarks, including spatial intelligence, fine-grained visual perception, and visual grounding, firmly establishes SIFThinker as a robust and highly capable method. Its ability to surpass or match leading proprietary models like ChatGPT-o3 in specific, critical tasks underscores its effectiveness and the validity of its architectural and training choices.

**Potential Impact:**
The development of SIFThinker holds substantial potential impact across various domains:
- **Enhanced MLLM Capabilities:** It pushes the boundaries of MLLMs, paving the way for models that possess more profound and human-like visual and spatial reasoning abilities. This could lead to a new generation of multimodal AI systems that are more intuitive and effective in understanding complex visual information.
- **Applications in Embodied AI and Robotics:** The intrinsic spatial awareness and dynamic focusing capabilities of SIFThinker are directly applicable to robotics, autonomous navigation, and other embodied AI scenarios. Robots equipped with SIFThinker could interpret complex scenes more accurately, enabling safer and more intelligent interactions with their environment.
- **Augmented and Virtual Reality:** Improved spatial understanding and fine-grained perception are crucial for AR/VR systems to seamlessly blend digital content with the real world and provide more immersive experiences.
- **Complex Image Analysis:** In fields requiring detailed visual inspection and analysis, such as medical imaging, manufacturing quality control, or scientific image interpretation, SIFThinker could provide more accurate and contextually rich insights.
- **Foundation for Future Research:** SIFThinker provides a strong methodological foundation and benchmark for future research into intrinsic visual attention mechanisms, the deeper integration of 3D perception into MLLMs, and advanced reinforcement learning techniques for multimodal reasoning.

**Limitations and Future Work:**
The authors acknowledge a key limitation: SIFThinker is currently trained on single-image scenarios. This means it may face challenges in generalizing to dynamic spatial scenarios that require reasoning across multiple images or video streams, which are common in real-world interactive environments. Extending the framework to such dynamic settings is highlighted as an interesting and important direction for future work, promising even higher practical impact. This future research could involve incorporating temporal reasoning alongside its current spatial and visual grounding capabilities.
