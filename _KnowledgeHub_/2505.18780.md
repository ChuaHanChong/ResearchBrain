# DreamPolicy: A Unified World-model Policy for Scalable Humanoid Locomotion

> [!summary] Summary
> Humanoid robots gain versatile locomotion capabilities across diverse and unseen terrains through a unified world-model policy. This approach leverages an autoregressive diffusion-based world model to guide behavior, leading to robust performance and effective zero-shot transfer to physical hardware.

## Problem

- Achieving versatile humanoid locomotion with a single, unified policy that generalizes robustly across diverse, unseen, and composite terrains.
- Limitations of policy distillation, which often averages diverse skills, preventing effective generalization and organic skill composition on novel terrains.
- The heavy reliance of learning-based locomotion methods on manually engineered reward functions and their tendency to overfit to specific training conditions.

## Method

- Collection of a diverse dataset of humanoid locomotion skills by training multiple specialized reinforcement learning policies on primitive terrains.
- Development of a terrain-aware autoregressive diffusion world model, trained on aggregated offline data, to synthesize adaptable multi-step future state trajectories.
- Formulation of unified policy learning as a ==Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning== problem, where the diffusion-generated trajectories serve as dynamic guidance, coupled with a hybrid reward mechanism for kinematic tracking and motion manifold regularization.

## Results

- Demonstrated superior zero-shot generalization on four novel terrains, outperforming the strongest baseline by up to **27%** in success rate, and achieved substantial improvements (up to **37.98%** higher success rates) on complex combined terrains.
- Successful zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a Unitree G1 humanoid robot, exhibiting robust locomotion across diverse real-world terrains like stairs, slopes, and uneven surfaces.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical role of both the diffusion world model for generalizing to out-of-distribution states and Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning for physically feasible and robust policy execution.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Diffusion models can effectively serve as terrain-aware world models, directly synthesizing multi-step future state trajectories that act as dynamic objectives for humanoid policy learning.
> - Aggregating data from specialized policies allows a diffusion world model to learn structured, terrain-conditioned motion priors, enabling dynamic skill composition and robust generalization.
> - Formulating policy learning with diffusion-guided future tracking and online motion manifold regularization substantially reduces the need for extensive, task-specific reward engineering.
