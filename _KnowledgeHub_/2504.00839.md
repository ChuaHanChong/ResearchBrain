# Context-Aware Human Behavior Prediction Using Multimodal Large Language Models: Challenges and Insights

> [!summary] Summary
> This research systematically evaluates pre-trained Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) for context-aware human behavior prediction from a third-person robot perspective. Researchers found that MLLMs can predict complex, multi-label human activities up to 3 seconds into the future, with the optimal configuration of GPT-4o achieving 66.1% accuracy and 92.8% semantic similarity using MLLM-generated scene captions and In-Context Learning.

## Problem

- Traditional data-driven methods for human behavior prediction struggle with generalization to unseen environments, new activities, and longer prediction horizons.
- Standalone Large Language Models (LLMs) lack visual grounding, leading to "hallucinations" when applied to real-world visual tasks.
- Applying general-purpose MLLMs for complex prediction tasks is challenging due to input sequence limitations, sensitivity to prompt design, and the high cost of task-specific fine-tuning.

## Method

- A modular framework was developed to systematically benchmark pre-trained MLLMs, investigating various input types, ==In-Context Learning (ICL)== examples, and autoregressive prediction strategies for human behavior prediction.
- Different visual context representations were utilized, including single images, sequences of images, and MLLM-generated scene captions, alongside historical interaction labels.
- The approach incorporated In-Context Learning to direct general-purpose MLLMs to the specific prediction task without fine-tuning, and explored autoregressive prediction by generating intermediate activity labels.

## Results

- The highest prediction accuracy of **66.1%** and a semantic similarity of **92.8%** were achieved by GPT-4o using MLLM-generated scene captions, 15 In-Context Learning examples, and autoregressive prediction.
- Visual context in the form of image sequences and scene captions consistently outperformed single images and text-only (blind) inputs, confirming the benefit of temporal and descriptive visual information.
- Autoregressive prediction provided only marginal improvements (approximately **1%** average accuracy gain), indicating it serves as a minor refinement rather than a major performance booster for the tested MLLMs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Incorporating visual context, particularly MLLM-generated scene captions, consistently improves human behavior prediction accuracy compared to purely text-based inputs.
> - In-Context Learning (ICL) substantially enhances MLLM performance for this task, with prediction accuracy generally improving as the number of ICL examples increases, up to a saturation point.
> - Pre-trained MLLMs are capable of predicting non-trivial human activity sequences from a third-person view, handling scenarios with multiple concurrent labels and changes in activity over a 3-second horizon.
