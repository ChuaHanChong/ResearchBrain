# Image Diffusion Preview with Consistency Solver

> [!summary] Summary
> Google DeepMind researchers developed ConsistencySolver, a learnable, high-order ODE solver trained with Reinforcement Learning, enabling a "Diffusion Preview" paradigm for rapid, consistent low-step previews in diffusion models. This framework reduces user interaction latency by up to 55% while delivering high fidelity and strong consistency between previews and full-step generations.

## Problem

- Diffusion models suffer from slow inference due to computationally intensive reverse differential equation solving, hindering interactive applications.
- Existing acceleration methods either yield low-quality, inconsistent previews (training-free solvers) or require costly retraining that can degrade the original model (distillation methods).
- Creative users require rapid iteration and consistent visual feedback, which current slow and unreliable generation processes fail to provide.

## Method

- Introduced the "**Diffusion Preview**" framework, a two-stage workflow where users quickly evaluate low-step previews before committing to full-step, high-fidelity generation.
- Developed ConsistencySolver, a lightweight, trainable, high-order ODE solver designed to adapt its integration strategy for few-step diffusion sampling.
- Optimized ConsistencySolver's policy network using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with non-differentiable perceptual similarity rewards, maximizing alignment between low-step previews and full-step reference images without altering the base diffusion model.

## Results

- ConsistencySolver achieved superior or competitive performance, e.g., an FID of 20.39 at 5 steps on Stable Diffusion, outperforming training-free solvers and matching or exceeding distillation methods while showing higher consistency across metrics.
- On instructional image editing (FLUX.1-Kontext), it reached an Edit Reward of **0.86** and Depth consistency of 25.18 at 5 steps, improving editing accuracy and adherence to instructions.
- The Diffusion Preview paradigm, evaluated through user studies, reduced average inference time by up to **55%** on the LAION dataset, demonstrating practical efficiency gains and maintaining high user satisfaction (**94.2%** on GenEval prompts).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A learnable, adaptive ODE solver, trained with RL and perceptual rewards, can effectively bridge the gap between low-step efficiency and high-step fidelity in diffusion models.
> - Consistency between low-step previews and final high-quality outputs is a critical factor for user satisfaction in interactive generative AI workflows, often more crucial than distribution-level metrics alone.
> - The optimal sampling dynamics learned by ConsistencySolver can generalize across different diffusion model architectures and scales without retraining, suggesting transferable knowledge in acceleration strategies.
