# Towards Better Optimization For Listwise Preference in Diffusion Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Penn State University and TikTok Inc. developed Diffusion-LPO, a framework that extends Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to directly leverage listwise human preferences for text-to-image diffusion models. This approach consistently enhanced visual aesthetics and prompt alignment, yielding up to a 12% improvement in PickScore win rate over pairwise DPO on general text-to-image generation tasks.

## Problem

- Existing Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) methods for diffusion models, such as DPO, predominantly rely on less informative pairwise human preference data.
- Human preferences are often inherently expressed as richer, more precise ranked lists among multiple options, but current DPO implementations do not fully utilize this structural information.
- Naive decomposition of listwise data into pairwise comparisons can introduce redundancy, treat all pairs equally, and potentially lead to an underestimation of aggregate reward for negative samples.

## Method

- Proposed **Diffusion-LPO**, a novel framework that extends ==Direct Preference Optimization== (DPO) to directly model listwise human preferences for text-to-image diffusion models.
- Leveraged the Plackett-Luce (PL) model to probabilistically define rankings and derived an objective function that ensures a higher-ranked item is preferred over all its lower-ranked alternatives within a given list.
- Adapted the Diffusion-DPO surrogate for tractability, approximating the log-likelihood ratio with a denoising error term, which allows the framework to generalize standard Diffusion-DPO when the list size is two.

## Results

- Diffusion-LPO consistently improved text-to-image alignment and image editing, achieving over a **12%** increase in PickScore win rate compared to pairwise Diffusion-DPO on SD1.5 and further gains on SDXL.
- Demonstrated enhanced personalized preference alignment, increasing win rates from **71.1%** to **72.3%** for held-in users and substantially from **70.3%** to **80.2%** for held-out users, indicating better generalization.
- Ablation studies confirmed Diffusion-LPO's theoretical advantage over Group-Pairwise DPO (GP-DPO), showing significant performance gains across all metrics (e.g., **80.4%** vs. **52.3%** PickScore win rate), validating the importance of direct listwise optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Listwise preference modeling (Diffusion-LPO) offers a theoretically superior optimization signal compared to merely decomposing rankings into pairwise comparisons (Group Pairwise DPO), which understates the effective margin of negatives.
> - Significant listwise preference information can be effectively extracted and utilized from existing pairwise datasets (like Pick-a-Pic) by constructing Directed Acyclic Graphs from user preferences.
> - The listwise optimization approach is generalizable, enhancing performance not only for Diffusion-DPO but also for other DPO-based methods, as demonstrated with DSPO-LPO.
