# Habitat-Matterport 3D Dataset (HM3D): 1000 Large-scale 3D Environments for Embodied AI

> [!summary] Summary
> The Habitat-Matterport 3D (HM3D) dataset provides 1,000 large-scale, high-fidelity indoor environments, featuring 34-91% fewer reconstruction artifacts and 20-85% higher visual fidelity compared to prior datasets. Training embodied AI agents on HM3D resulted in superior generalization and up to 100% success on the Gibson-test dataset, empirically validating the data scaling hypothesis.

## Problem

- Existing 3D reconstruction datasets (e.g., Matterport3D, Gibson) were limited in scale, often containing fewer than 600 scenes, hindering robust agent generalization.
- Prior datasets suffered from significant reconstruction artifacts like missing surfaces, holes, or untextured regions, creating unrealistic visual and geometric cues for embodied agents.
- Visual fidelity of rendered images from existing datasets often showed a domain gap compared to real-world captures, complicating the transfer of learned policies to physical robots.

## Method

- Acquired **HM3D**'s 1,000 real-world indoor spaces from diverse global locations using ==Matterport Pro2 depth sensors==, ensuring a broad range of architectural styles and furnishings.
- Employed Matterport's proprietary ==3D reconstruction pipeline==, followed by over 800 hours of rigorous ==human curation== to ensure high completeness, diversity, and physical integrity of the scenes.
- Anonymized personally identifiable information and explicitly packaged the dataset to be ==Habitat-ready== for seamless integration with the high-performance ==Habitat simulator==, including ==navigation meshes==.

## Results

- HM3D offers **1,000** building-scale scenes (**112.5k m²** navigable space), representing **1.4 to 3.7 times** larger coverage than previous building-scale datasets like MP3D and Gibson.
- The dataset exhibits a **34-91%** reduction in reconstruction defects and **20-85%** higher visual fidelity (measured by FID and KID metrics) compared to other photorealistic 3D datasets.
- Agents trained exclusively on HM3D achieved **100%** success on the Gibson-test dataset and outperformed the next-best agents by **3-4 SPL points** on the challenging MP3D-test dataset for PointGoal navigation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Scaling the quantity and quality of 3D environment datasets empirically correlates with improved embodied AI agent performance and generalization, showing a strong near-linear relationship (Pearson 
 = 0.88) between navigable area and PointNav SPL.
> - High-fidelity and near-complete 3D environments significantly reduce the sim-to-real gap, enabling agents to learn more robust and transferable skills that generalize exceptionally to unseen environments.
> - Training on a sufficiently large and diverse dataset allows agents to achieve pareto-optimal performance, consistently outperforming agents trained on smaller or lower-quality datasets across various evaluation benchmarks.
