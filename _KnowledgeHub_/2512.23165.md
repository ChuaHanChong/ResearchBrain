# Evaluating Parameter Efficient Methods for RLVR

> [!summary] Summary
> This research systematically evaluates various Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods within the Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) paradigm for large language models. It finds that structural PEFT variants like DoRA consistently outperform standard LoRA and full fine-tuning, achieving up to 46.6% average accuracy on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, while SVD-based initialization strategies suffer catastrophic failure due to spectral misalignment.

## Problem

- Training large language models (LLMs) with Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) is computationally intensive and resource-demanding due to their immense parameter counts.
- The optimal PEFT architecture for the distinct optimization dynamics of RLVR was largely unexplored, with standard LoRA being a default choice without rigorous validation.
- A lack of comprehensive understanding existed regarding which PEFT methods are best suited for RLVR given its sparse reward signals and unique gradient properties, potentially leading to suboptimal choices.

## Method

- Conducted the first comprehensive, large-scale empirical evaluation of over ==12 diverse PEFT methods== categorized by their design paradigm (structural, initialization, efficiency-oriented) within the RLVR framework.
- Utilized the ==Decoupled Clip and Dynamic sampling Policy Optimization (DAPO)== algorithm for RLVR training, alongside GRPO and Dr. GRPO for robustness checks, on SFT-trained DeepSeek-R1-Distill models (1.5B and 7B).
- Evaluated performance on a suite of mathematical reasoning benchmarks (e.g., MATH-500, AIME, AMC, HMMT) using ==strict outcome-based binary rewards== and Pass@1 in k metrics.

## Results

- DoRA achieved the highest overall average accuracy of **46.6%** on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, outperforming full-parameter fine-tuning (**44.9%**) and standard LoRA (**42.5%**).
- SVD-based initialization methods like PiSSA collapsed to near-zero accuracy (**0.2%**), while MiLoRA significantly underperformed at **18.0%**, attributed to optimization instability and spectral misalignment.
- Extensive ablation studies across batch sizes, RLVR algorithms, learning rates, and LoRA ranks confirmed the robustness of these findings and their generalizability to larger 7B models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Structural PEFT variants, such as DoRA, AdaLoRA, and MiSS, consistently surpass standard LoRA and even full-parameter fine-tuning, indicating their enhanced ability to handle the complex policy shifts in RLVR.
> - SVD-based initialization strategies (PiSSA, MiLoRA) catastrophically fail because they force updates into principal subspaces, misaligning with RLVR's intrinsic 'off-principal' optimization dynamics.
> - There exists an 'expressivity floor' for PEFT in RLVR; extreme parameter reduction methods (e.g., VeRA, IA^3, Rank-1 LoRA) create information bottlenecks that severely limit the model's ability to acquire complex reasoning behaviors.
