# RDPO: Real Data Preference Optimization for Physics Consistency Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Fudan University and Shopee Inc. developed RDPO, an annotation-free framework that enhances the physical realism and action coherence of generated videos. The method distills physical priors directly from real-world videos using a reverse-sampling mechanism to automatically construct preference pairs, leading to substantial improvements in physical consistency and overall video quality.

## Problem

- Despite advancements in visual quality, current video generation models often fail to faithfully reproduce real-world physics, resulting in physically implausible content.
- Existing preference optimization methods for improving physical consistency are limited by the high cost, subjectivity, and time-consuming nature of human annotation.
- Developing accurate and generalizable AI reward models to detect diverse physical violations in high-dimensional video data remains a challenging open problem.

## Method

- **RDPO** leverages a novel mechanism to reverse-sample real video sequences with a pre-trained generator, automatically constructing statistically distinguishable preference pairs based on physical correctness.
- The framework employs an annotation-free approach to generate preferred samples by ==denoising== noisy latents derived from real videos, and less preferred samples from pure Gaussian noise.
- A multi-stage iterative training schedule progressively guides the generator from learning macroscopic motion patterns to refining finer physical interactions, often interleaved with Supervised Fine-tuning (==SFT==).

## Results

- RDPO, particularly when combined with SFT, substantially improved metrics for physical consistency (Physics IQ) and overall video quality on both LTX-2B and Shopee-MUG-V-10B baselines.
- The annotation-free RDPO method surpassed DPO trained on human-annotated data in several key metrics, despite requiring significantly less data acquisition effort, and demonstrated complementary benefits when combined with human labels.
- Human evaluations and qualitative examples confirmed that RDPO-enhanced models produced videos with visibly superior physical consistency, motion naturalness, and realism across diverse scenes and actions, from human kinematics to fluid dynamics.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Real-world videos inherently contain a wealth of accurate physical information that can be directly distilled into a preference signal, eliminating the need for costly human annotations or explicit reward models.
> - Reverse-sampling from real video latents provides a statistically effective way to create 'preferred' samples that implicitly embed real-world physics, forming annotation-free training data.
> - A progressive, coarse-to-fine training curriculum, combined with interleaved SFT, stabilizes and enhances the learning of complex physical dynamics, leading to more robust and accurate video generation.

## Detailed Report

### 1. Executive Summary

The research paper "RDPO: Real Data Preference Optimization for Physics Consistency Video Generation" introduces an innovative, annotation-free framework designed to significantly enhance the physical realism and action coherence of artificially generated videos. Authored by researchers from Fudan University and Shopee Inc., RDPO addresses a critical challenge in video generation: while visual quality has advanced considerably, faithfully reproducing real-world physics remains elusive. Traditional preference optimization methods, which can improve physical consistency, are hampered by the high cost and subjectivity of human annotation or the difficulty of developing accurate reward models for physics.

RDPO tackles these limitations by distilling physical priors directly from real-world videos. It leverages a novel mechanism that reverse-samples real video sequences with a pre-trained generator to automatically construct statistically distinguishable preference pairs based on physical correctness. A multi-stage iterative training schedule then guides the generator to progressively adhere to physical laws. The framework demonstrates substantial improvements in physical consistency and overall video quality across various benchmarks and human evaluations, showcasing its potential to scale and reduce reliance on manual supervision for achieving high-fidelity, physically plausible video content.

### 2. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of video generation has experienced rapid advancements, largely driven by the emergence of **diffusion models** and **transformer-based architectures**. These models have led to remarkable improvements in visual quality, diversity, and temporal coherence of generated videos, opening doors for applications in entertainment, education, and simulation. Major models like Imagen Video, Make-a-Video, and LTX-Video demonstrate the impressive capability to synthesize high-resolution and coherent video content from text prompts.

Despite these breakthroughs, a significant frontier remains: achieving **physical consistency**. Existing video generators often struggle to faithfully reproduce complex real-world physics, such as accurate fluid dynamics, object interactions, or kinematic plausibility. Models might generate superficially plausible motions, but subtle or quantitative physical laws are frequently violated (e.g., incorrect water volume increase when pouring). This limitation prevents generated videos from being truly realistic or useful for simulations requiring high physical fidelity.

To address this, **post-training techniques**, particularly **preference optimization methods** inspired by Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), have gained traction. These methods aim to fine-tune pre-trained generative models to align better with desired attributes, including physical correctness. However, current preference-based approaches face two major limitations:
1. **Costly Human Annotation:** Building large-scale datasets of human preferences, especially for complex physical phenomena requiring expert judgment, is expensive, time-consuming, and subject to inter-rater variability.
2. **Inaccurate Reward Models:** Developing robust and generalizable reward models that can reliably detect diverse physical violations in high-dimensional video data remains an open research problem. These models are difficult to train accurately and can sometimes be "gamed" by generators.

RDPO positions itself as a solution to these challenges by proposing an **annotation-free** preference optimization framework. Instead of relying on human labels or explicit reward models, RDPO directly distills physical priors from real-world videos, presenting an efficient and scalable pathway to imbue video generators with a deeper understanding of physical laws. This work builds upon the foundational success of diffusion models and DPO, extending their capabilities to tackle the nuanced problem of physical consistency without the typical associated data acquisition costs.

### 3. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to **significantly improve the physical consistency and realism of generated videos** by enabling generative models to better adhere to real-world physical laws.

The motivation stems from the following critical observations and challenges in the current video generation landscape:
1. **Gap in Physical Realism:** Despite remarkable progress in visual quality and temporal coherence, current video generation models frequently produce content that violates fundamental physical laws. This 'superficial plausibility' limits their utility in demanding applications like advanced simulations or realistic virtual environments. For instance, a model might show water being poured, but fail to maintain the correct volume-to-height relationship.
2. **Limitations of Existing Post-Training Methods:** While preference optimization techniques (like DPO or RLHF) have shown promise in refining model outputs, their application to physics consistency is hindered by severe practical constraints:
 - **Prohibitive Annotation Costs:** Collecting human-annotated preference data for physical correctness is extremely expensive, time-consuming, and requires specialized domain expertise, leading to subjectivity and inconsistency.
 - **Undeveloped Reward Models:** Creating a reliable and generalizable AI reward model that can accurately detect complex physical violations across diverse scenarios is an unsolved problem.
3. **Untapped Potential of Real Data:** Real-world videos inherently contain a wealth of accurate physical information. The existing paradigm often involves training models on these videos for general generation, but not explicitly for distilling specific physical priors in a preference learning context.

RDPO's core motivation is to overcome these limitations by **automating the construction of preference training data** using real-world videos and a pre-trained generator. By treating real footage as the "gold standard" for physical dynamics, RDPO aims to guide the generator towards greater physical fidelity without any manual human intervention or the need for a separate, complex reward model. This novel approach seeks to unlock an efficient and scalable pathway for enhancing the physical realism of generated video content.

### 4. Methodology and Approach

RDPO (Real Data Preference Optimization) is an annotation-free framework designed to improve the physical consistency of generated videos. Its methodology builds upon the principles of Rectified Flow and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), adapting them for video generation by cleverly leveraging real-world data.

#### 4.1. Preliminaries

- **Rectified Flow (RF):** Rectified Flow establishes approximately straight trajectories between a data distribution (e.g., real videos) and a prior distribution (e.g., Gaussian noise). It learns a velocity field that guides an Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE) from a noisy sample to a clean data sample. The loss function encourages the learned velocity to align with the constant velocity of a straight trajectory between two points. For generation, a sample from the prior is drawn, and the ODE is integrated backward to produce a sample approximating the data distribution.
- **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO):** DPO is a method for fine-tuning generative models to align with human preferences without explicitly training a reward model. Given preference pairs (x_w, x_l), where x_w is preferred over x_l, DPO directly optimizes the generative model's likelihood to generate x_w more often than x_l, relative to a fixed reference model. This effectively steers the model's output towards preferred characteristics.

#### 4.2. Real Data Preference Optimization (RDPO)

RDPO addresses the limitations of costly human annotations and unreliable reward models by automatically constructing preference data from real videos.

##### 4.2.1. Annotation-Free Pairwise Data Generation

This is the cornerstone of RDPO. The process is inspired by image-to-image editing, where a noisy real image is denoised rather than synthesizing from pure noise. RDPO extends this to video:
1. **Reverse Sampling from Real Videos:** The core idea is to take a real video (x_real_0), inject noise at an intermediate diffusion step `t` using the forward diffusion process (f(x_real_0, t)), creating a noisy latent `x_t`.
2. **Preference Pair Construction:**
 - **x_preferred:** The noisy latent `x_t` (derived from a real video) is then denoised back to a clean video (π_θ(x_t, t)). Because this process starts from a latent that has "seen" real-world physical information, the resulting video, `x_preferred_0`, tends to exhibit stronger physical consistency. The key insight is that by reducing the number of reverse sampling steps (i.e., starting closer to the real data distribution in the latent space), the generated videos gradually transition from the model's inherent distribution towards real-world physics consistency.
 - **x_model:** For comparison, a standard "less preferred" sample (`x_model_0`) is generated by denoising from pure Gaussian noise (`x_T`) using the same pre-trained generator (π_θ(x_T, T)).
3. **Rejection Sampling for Latent Selection:** To ensure stability and relevance, RDPO doesn't simply use any `x_t` derived from `x_real_0`. It performs rejection sampling:
 - For a given real video, noise is injected multiple times (K times) at step `t`.
 - For each noisy latent `x_t`, its L2 distance to a `x_model_t` (a noisy latent generated by the model from pure noise) is calculated.
 - The `x_t` that is closest in latent space to `x_model_t` is selected as `x_preferred_t`. This strategy ensures that while real-world physical information is injected, the resulting latent remains aligned with the model’s native generative manifold, preventing drastic out-of-distribution outputs and improving training stability.
4. **Statistical Preference:** Empirically, `x_preferred_0` generated this way consistently exhibits superior physical consistency compared to `x_model_0`, thus forming an automatic preference pair (`x_preferred_0`, `x_model_0`).

##### 4.2.2. Preference Optimization Objective

Once the annotation-free preference pairs are generated, RDPO fine-tunes the video generation model using a modified DPO loss, specifically referred to as **Flow-DPO loss**:
$$L_{RDPO}(\theta) = -\mathbb{E}_{c,x_{preferred},x_{model}} \left[ \log \sigma \left( \beta \left( \log \frac{\pi_\theta(x_{preferred}|c)}{\pi_{ref}(x_{preferred}|c)} - \log \frac{\pi_\theta(x_{model}|c)}{\pi_{ref}(x_{model}|c)} \right) \right) \right]$$
Here, $\pi_\theta$ is the current fine-tuned model, $\pi_{ref}$ is a fixed pre-trained reference model, $\sigma(\cdot)$ is the sigmoid function, $\beta$ is a hyperparameter, and $c$ is the text prompt (condition). Minimizing this loss encourages the model to increase the likelihood of generating the physically preferred video (`x_preferred`) relative to the less preferred one (`x_model`), thereby implicitly learning to obey physical laws.

##### 4.2.3. Progressive Training Strategy

To effectively and stably absorb real-world dynamics, RDPO employs a coarse-to-fine curriculum:
1. **Phase 1 (Coarse):** Training begins with a large diffusion step for reverse sampling (heavy noise injection). This encourages the model to learn macroscopic motion patterns and global structures first.
2. **Phase 2 (Fine):** As the model's outputs improve, the noise level is progressively reduced (smaller reverse sampling steps). This phase focuses on refining finer physical interactions and textural details.
3. **Interleaving Data:** Crucially, later phases continue to interleave data synthesized in previous phases. This prevents new, finer details from overwriting the larger-scale dynamics learned earlier, ensuring a balanced and comprehensive improvement. This iterative approach allows the model to gradually align its generative distribution with the complex manifold of real-world physics.

The overall training process also interleaves RDPO fine-tuning with Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT) on real data, further stabilizing the training and leveraging the strengths of both approaches.

### 5. Main Findings and Results

The effectiveness of RDPO was validated through extensive experiments on two different video generation baselines: LTX-Video(0.9.1)-2B and Shopee-MUG-V-10B, using both public benchmarks and human evaluations.

#### 5.1. Experiments on the LTX-2B Baseline

These experiments utilized the open-source LTX-Video-2B model and the WISA dataset for training, with VBench and Physics IQ for evaluation, ensuring reproducibility.

##### 5.1.1. Effectiveness of RDPO Training

- **Comparison:** The study compared the baseline LTX-2B, Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT) alone, RDPO alone (w/o sft), and RDPO combined with SFT (w. sft).
- **Results (Table 1):** RDPO alone significantly improved metrics related to physical realism (Physics IQ) and most aspects of overall video quality compared to the baseline and SFT. The combination of RDPO with SFT (RDPO w. sft) yielded the best overall performance, demonstrating that RDPO is particularly effective in enhancing motion smoothness and physical accuracy, while SFT benefits visual quality attributes. The combined approach effectively integrates their respective strengths.

##### 5.1.2. RDPO vs. Human-Annotated DPO

- **Comparison:** RDPO's annotation-free approach was compared against DPO trained on human-annotated data, and a hybrid model combining both. 8,000 RDPO pairs were automatically generated, while 1,000 human-labeled pairs were created, noting that human annotation was ten times slower.
- **Results (Table 2):** RDPO (Annotation-Free) surpassed human-annotated DPO on several key metrics (Physics IQ, Total Score), despite its significantly lower data acquisition cost. The hybrid model (RDPO Mix Human-label) achieved the strongest results across most metrics, indicating that RDPO and human preferences are complementary sources of supervision.

##### 5.1.3. Impact of Progressive Iterative Training

- **Analysis:** The study monitored RDPO's performance across different reverse-sampling steps and progressive training stages (iter1, iter2, iter3).
- **Results (Figure 3, Table 3):**
 - **Reverse-step Length:** Longer reverse steps (more noise) were more effective in earlier training rounds (iter1), suggesting the need for rich, high-noise guidance initially.
 - **Later Rounds Efficiency:** In later rounds (iter2), fewer reverse steps were sufficient, indicating that the model's distribution had moved closer to the real-data manifold, improving training efficiency.
 - **Monotonic Improvement:** Physics IQ scores improved monotonically over the three progressive stages (iter1: 25.21, iter2: 25.31, iter3: 25.66), and the total VBench score either rose or remained high, confirming the effectiveness of the coarse-to-fine progressive curriculum.

#### 5.2. Experiments on the Shopee-MUG-V-10B Baseline

For the larger Shopee-MUG-V-10B model, experiments focused on more challenging tasks involving intricate human interactions and everyday scenes, using a curated Daily Physics Interactions (DPI) dataset.

##### 5.2.1. Human and Automatic Evaluation

- **Human Evaluation (Figure 4):** Human annotators consistently preferred the Shopee-MUG-V model fine-tuned with RDPO across all four criteria: Overall Preference, Visual Quality, Motion Naturalness, and Physical Consistency. The most significant improvements were observed in Physical Consistency.
- **Automatic Metrics (Table 4):** Quantitative comparisons corroborated human evaluations, showing that Shopee-MUG-V-RDPO outperformed the base model across all automatic video quality metrics, including Subject Consistency, Background Consistency, Motion Smoothness, and Imaging Quality.

#### 5.3. Statistical Validation of RDPO’s Annotation-Free Pairwise Data

- **Human Study:** A dedicated human evaluation study confirmed the efficacy of RDPO's annotation-free pair generation. Humans assessed RDPO-generated video pairs for physical consistency.
- **Results (Figure 6):** The accuracy of RDPO's automated pair labeling (agreement with human judgment) improved as fewer reverse sampling steps were used to generate the "positive" samples. This statistically validates that RDPO effectively constructs useful preference data that aligns with human perception of physical realism, especially when "positive" samples are closer to the real-world latent distribution.

#### 5.4. Qualitative Observations

- **Visual Comparisons (Figures 5, 7-10):** Qualitative examples consistently demonstrated that RDPO-enhanced models produced videos with significantly improved physical consistency and motion realism. This included better human kinematic coherence (e.g., coordinated head-body movements), more natural complex actions (e.g., dancing), accurate fluid simulations (e.g., liquid accumulation, realistic streams), coherent particle effects (e.g., fireworks), and plausible deformable body dynamics and human-object interactions. The enhancements were evident across both LTX and Shopee-MUG-V baselines, underscoring RDPO's robustness.

### 6. Significance and Potential Impact

The introduction of Real Data Preference Optimization (RDPO) marks a significant step forward in the field of video generation, offering several impactful contributions:

1. **Annotation-Free Paradigm:** RDPO’s most profound impact is its ability to bypass the costly, time-consuming, and subjective nature of human annotation for preference learning. By automatically generating preference pairs directly from real-world videos, RDPO democratizes access to robust fine-tuning for physical consistency, making it scalable and more efficient. This paradigm shift can accelerate research and development in video generation, particularly for nuanced attributes like physics that are challenging for human annotators.
2. **Enhanced Physical Fidelity:** The core innovation of distilling physical priors directly from real data enables generated videos to adhere more faithfully to real-world physics. This translates to not just visually appealing but also physically plausible content, crucial for applications requiring high realism, such as:
 - **Advanced Simulations:** Creating realistic training environments for robotics, autonomous vehicles, or scientific research.
 - **Virtual and Augmented Reality:** Generating more immersive and believable virtual worlds.
 - **Content Creation:** Producing higher-quality visual effects, animations, and narrative content where physical consistency is paramount.
3. **Scalability and Robustness:** The method's effectiveness across different model scales (2B and 10B parameters) and diverse benchmarks (VBench, Physics IQ, human evaluation) demonstrates its robustness. As generative models continue to grow in complexity and fidelity, human annotation will become increasingly difficult for detecting subtle physical inaccuracies. RDPO, by leveraging objective real-world data, provides a more sensitive and objective optimization signal, making it highly valuable for future, larger foundation models.
4. **Complementary to Existing Methods:** The finding that RDPO can surpass human-annotated DPO and achieve synergistic benefits when combined with it suggests that RDPO is not merely a replacement but a powerful complementary tool. It can augment traditional preference learning by providing a strong baseline or additional, low-cost training data.
5. **New Pathway for Prior Learning:** RDPO introduces an efficient pathway to learn and leverage physical priors inherent in real-world video without repeatedly training on these videos in a conventional manner. This "distillation" of physics directly into a preference signal is a novel approach for embedding complex physical understanding into generative models.
