# RDPO: Real Data Preference Optimization for Physics Consistency Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Fudan University and Shopee Inc. developed RDPO, an annotation-free framework that enhances the physical realism and action coherence of generated videos. The method distills physical priors directly from real-world videos using a reverse-sampling mechanism to automatically construct preference pairs, leading to substantial improvements in physical consistency and overall video quality.

## Problem

- Despite advancements in visual quality, current video generation models often fail to faithfully reproduce real-world physics, resulting in physically implausible content.
- Existing preference optimization methods for improving physical consistency are limited by the high cost, subjectivity, and time-consuming nature of human annotation.
- Developing accurate and generalizable AI reward models to detect diverse physical violations in high-dimensional video data remains a challenging open problem.

## Method

- **RDPO** leverages a novel mechanism to reverse-sample real video sequences with a pre-trained generator, automatically constructing statistically distinguishable preference pairs based on physical correctness.
- The framework employs an annotation-free approach to generate preferred samples by ==denoising== noisy latents derived from real videos, and less preferred samples from pure Gaussian noise.
- A multi-stage iterative training schedule progressively guides the generator from learning macroscopic motion patterns to refining finer physical interactions, often interleaved with Supervised Fine-tuning (==SFT==).

## Results

- RDPO, particularly when combined with SFT, substantially improved metrics for physical consistency (Physics IQ) and overall video quality on both LTX-2B and Shopee-MUG-V-10B baselines.
- The annotation-free RDPO method surpassed DPO trained on human-annotated data in several key metrics, despite requiring significantly less data acquisition effort, and demonstrated complementary benefits when combined with human labels.
- Human evaluations and qualitative examples confirmed that RDPO-enhanced models produced videos with visibly superior physical consistency, motion naturalness, and realism across diverse scenes and actions, from human kinematics to fluid dynamics.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Real-world videos inherently contain a wealth of accurate physical information that can be directly distilled into a preference signal, eliminating the need for costly human annotations or explicit reward models.
> - Reverse-sampling from real video latents provides a statistically effective way to create 'preferred' samples that implicitly embed real-world physics, forming annotation-free training data.
> - A progressive, coarse-to-fine training curriculum, combined with interleaved SFT, stabilizes and enhances the learning of complex physical dynamics, leading to more robust and accurate video generation.
