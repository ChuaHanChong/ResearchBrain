# PriorVLA: Prior-Preserving Adaptation for Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> PriorVLA introduces a framework for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models that preserves and leverages broad pretrained priors during downstream adaptation, preventing prior forgetting. The approach integrates explicit scene and motor priors via learnable query interfaces and dual action experts, achieving improved performance, especially in out-of-distribution generalization and few-shot learning, across various simulation and real-world robotic manipulation tasks with reduced trainable parameters.

## Problem

- Full fine-tuning of pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for downstream tasks can lead to "prior forgetting," where generalizable knowledge from pretraining is lost.
- Existing VLA adaptation methods primarily focus on modifying or restricting components, lacking explicit mechanisms to preserve and leverage pretrained forward-pass representations (scene and motor priors) as usable sources for the adapted policy.

## Method

- **PriorVLA** uses ==Dual Action Experts (DAE)==: a frozen "Prior Expert" (original Action Expert) to preserve motor priors and a trainable "Adaptation Expert" for task specialization, both operating on the same noisy action trajectory.
- Introduces ==Expert Queries (EQ)== as learnable token interfaces: ==Scene Queries== capture VLM scene priors, ==Motor Queries== capture frozen Prior Expert motor priors, and ==Action Queries== integrate these priors into the Adaptation Expert.
- The framework optimizes approximately ==25% of the VLA model parameters== (Adaptation Expert, Expert Queries, VLM's vision encoder) using a standard ==flow-matching MSE objective==, keeping other components frozen.

## Results

- Achieved **77% ID** and **53% OOD** success on RoboTwin 2.0, demonstrating gains of **+10pp** and **+11pp** over the \u03c0



_{0.5} baseline in Easy (ID) and Hard (OOD) settings, respectively.
- Reached an average success rate of **99.1%** across LIBERO suites, surpassing strong VLA baselines like OpenVLA-OFT (**97.1%**) and \u03c0



_{0.5} (96.9%).
- On real-world robot tasks, PriorVLA achieved **+16pp OOD** success in standard data and **+22pp OOD** success in few-shot settings compared to \u03c0



_{0.5}, while updating 25% fewer parameters than full fine-tuning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly decoupling prior preservation (via a frozen expert) from task specialization (via a trainable expert) effectively maintains broad pretrained motor knowledge during adaptation.
> - Learnable query interfaces provide a crucial mechanism for capturing and integrating relevant scene and motor priors from frozen pretrained components into the adapting policy.
> - The value of a pretrained prior source stems from its learned knowledge rather than simply providing an additional architectural branch or trainable parameters.
