# MARC: Memory-Augmented RL Token Compression for Efficient Video Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> MARC introduces a "retrieve then compress" framework for visual language models, leveraging a visual memory retriever and reinforcement learning to achieve a 95% reduction in visual tokens for video understanding tasks. This method maintains performance comparable to models processing 64 times more frames while reducing GPU memory usage by 72.4% and overall model generation latency by 15.9%.

## Problem

- Visual Language Models (VLMs) encounter substantial computational overhead for video processing due to high frame rates and long durations, leading to increased inference costs, latency, and GPU memory consumption.
- Existing training-free token compression methods for video often result in considerable information loss, causing a noticeable degradation in VLM performance.
- The high computational demands of VLMs hinder their practical deployment in real-world, resource-constrained, and latency-sensitive applications like real-time surveillance or autonomous driving.

## Method

- A ==Visual Memory Retriever== (VMR) first segments video into semantically coherent, event-based fragments and retrieves the most query-relevant ones, reducing redundant information before compression.
- A memory-aware temporal compression layer applies iterative merging within and across selected segments, prioritizing intra-segment coherence to reduce tokens.
- Compression Group Relative Policy Optimization (C-GRPO), an RL-based distillation strategy, trains a low-token "student" model to match the reasoning quality of a high-token "teacher" model, with correctness-gated rewards for performance retention.

## Results

- MARC achieved a mean performance of 42.20 across six diverse benchmarks, nearly identical to the 42.21 mean accuracy of the 64-frame Qwen2.5-VL-**3B** baseline, while reducing visual tokens by **95%**.
- The method demonstrated a **72.4%** reduction in GPU peak memory usage (from **41.63 GB** to **11.48 GB**) and a **15.9%** reduction in overall model generation latency.
- MARC substantially outperformed existing training-free compression strategies, showing mean accuracy improvements up to **65%** over baselines like DynamicVLM and **31%** over MovieChat.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Pre-selecting task-relevant, event-level video segments through a VMR is highly effective in focusing downstream processing and mitigating information loss during subsequent compression.
> - Reinforcement learning, specifically the C-GRPO framework, can successfully distill complex reasoning capabilities from uncompressed teacher models to aggressively compressed student models, enabling robust performance under extreme token reduction.
> - Aggressive token compression, reducing video input to a single-frame token count, can maintain VLM performance levels comparable to models processing significantly more frames, challenging the necessity of full-context processing.
