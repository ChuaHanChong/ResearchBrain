# General Flow as Foundation Affordance for Scalable Robot Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Tsinghua University and affiliated institutes developed "General Flow," a language-conditioned 3D flow prediction model trained on large-scale human RGBD video datasets, to enable scalable robot learning. The framework achieves an 81% average success rate in zero-shot human-to-robot skill transfer across 18 diverse real-world manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Robot learning struggles with acquiring versatile manipulation skills at scale due to the high cost and difficulty of collecting diverse real-world robot data.
- A significant "sim-to-real" domain gap hinders the effective transfer of policies learned in simulation to physical robots.
- Existing flow-based methods for robot learning often suffer from limited scalability or are confined to simulation environments, making real-world deployment challenging.

## Method

- Introduces "==General Flow==," a universal 3D flow prediction target representing future trajectories of points on objects, inspired by Gibson’s theory of affordances.
- Leverages large-scale, cross-embodiment RGBD human video datasets to directly extract 3D flow labels, circumventing the sim-to-real gap.
- Develops ScaleFlow, a language-conditioned 3D flow prediction model trained on this data, incorporating scale-awareness and robustness augmentations like Hand Mask Augmentation.

## Results

- The ScaleFlow model achieves superior quantitative performance in 3D flow prediction, with an Average Displacement Error (ADE) of 3.55cm and Final Displacement Error (FDE) of 4.70cm on a held-out test set.
- The framework successfully achieves an **81%** average success rate in zero-shot human-to-robot skill transfer across 18 diverse manipulation tasks in 6 real-world scenes.
- It demonstrates robust generalization to novel object instances (e.g., an atypical "Safe") and adaptability to variations in gripper position, grasp manner, and segmentation imperfections.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training on diverse, large-scale human video data endows the General Flow model with emergent properties like robustness to hand occlusion and label noise.
> - The model exhibits language-driven semantic control, generating distinct actions for identical visual inputs based solely on language instructions.
> - ScaleFlow dynamically adapts predicted trajectory lengths based on the spatial context of the object, showing "scale spatial adaptation."
