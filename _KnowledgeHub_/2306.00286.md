# Efficient Deep Learning of Robust Policies from MPC using Imitation and Tube-Guided Data Augmentation

> [!summary] Summary
> A new Imitation Learning strategy, Sampling Augmentation (SA), efficiently trains robust deep neural network policies by distilling knowledge from Model Predictive Control (MPC) experts. SA enables zero-shot sim-to-real and lab-to-real transfer with 100% success rates on complex tasks using as few as one demonstration, significantly outperforming baselines in data and training efficiency.

## Problem

- Existing Imitation Learning (IL) methods for Model Predictive Control (MPC) often require a large number of demonstrations, leading to high data collection costs and prolonged training.
- Policies trained with current IL techniques exhibit limited robustness to unseen disturbances, model errors, and sim2real/lab2real gaps due to covariate shifts.
- Updating or adapting learned policies to changes in the MPC expert or new tasks is challenging due to the need for extensive retraining.

## Method

- Developed Sampling Augmentation (SA), an IL strategy that leverages Robust Tube **==MPC==** (RTMPC) to generate additional training samples within the expert's disturbance-invariant set (tube).
- For linear RTMPC, SA samples states within an approximated tube and efficiently computes corresponding actions using the ancillary controller's linear feedback law.
- For nonlinear RTMPC, SA utilizes parametric sensitivities of the NMPC ancillary controller to efficiently approximate and generate augmented actions, avoiding repeated expensive NMPC solves.

## Results

- SA methods achieved **100%** success rates in disturbed environments with as few as one demonstration (**3x** more sample-efficient) for both linear trajectory tracking and nonlinear acrobatic maneuvers.
- Policies trained with SA demonstrated robust zero-shot sim-to-real and lab-to-real transfer on physical multirotors under unseen disturbances, such as wind, slung loads, and drag boards.
- SA significantly reduced wall-clock training time (e.g., **64.8%** reduction over DAgger+DR for trajectory tracking), and the learned policies achieved 180-280 times faster inference on onboard hardware compared to the MPC expert.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The inherent robustness properties of RTMPC, particularly its disturbance-invariant sets (tubes), can be effectively exploited to guide data augmentation for learning robust policies.
> - Sampling within the RTMPC tube allows for efficient generation of diverse training data that covers states likely to be encountered under bounded uncertainties, significantly improving robustness and sample efficiency.
> - Parametric sensitivity analysis of the ancillary Nonlinear MPC (NMPC) in nonlinear RTMPC enables computationally efficient data augmentation without solving the full NMPC for each augmented sample.
