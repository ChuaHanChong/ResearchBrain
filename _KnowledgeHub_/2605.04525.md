# HDFlow: Hierarchical Diffusion-Flow Planning for Long-horizon Tasks

> [!summary] Summary
> HDFlow introduces a hierarchical planning framework for long-horizon robotic tasks that leverages diffusion models for strategic high-level subgoal generation and rectified flow models for fast, low-level trajectory execution. This hybrid approach, coupled with manifold-aware energy-based guidance, improves planning efficiency and achieves higher success rates on diverse manipulation and locomotion benchmarks.

## Problem

- Long-horizon robotic manipulation tasks suffer from error accumulation and sparse reward signals, making planning challenging.
- Purely diffusion-based planners are computationally intensive due to iterative denoising, hindering real-time low-level robotic control.
- Existing conditional diffusion models for planning often lack explicit mechanisms to evaluate plan quality, leading to infeasible or suboptimal trajectories, especially in high-dimensional latent spaces.

## Method

- A hybrid hierarchical architecture combines diffusion models for high-level subgoal generation with rectified flow models for efficient low-level trajectory synthesis.
- An Energy-Based Model (EBM) is trained to provide dense, success-oriented guidance to the high-level diffusion planner.
- Manifold-aware guidance, including a projection step, ensures generated latent subgoals remain on the feasible data manifold.

## Results

- HDFlow achieved higher success rates on FurnitureBench tasks, such as **55%** on `cabinet` (low randomization), outperforming existing imitation learning and diffusion-based planners.
- The hybrid architecture improved computational efficiency while maintaining high success rates (e.g., **68%** success at 88 ms/step on `lamp`), validating the choice of generative models.
- Demonstrated robust real-world performance with a Franka robot and achieved competitive or superior success rates across 18 RLBench and various OGBench tasks, showcasing strong generalization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Different generative models are optimally assigned to hierarchical levels: diffusion for exploratory high-level strategy and rectified flow for efficient low-level execution.
> - An Energy-Based Model providing dense reward signals can effectively guide high-level diffusion planners toward successful strategies in sparse-reward settings.
> - Manifold-aware projection combined with EBM guidance helps maintain the feasibility of generated plans within the latent space, preventing deviation.
