# SRPO: Enhancing Multimodal LLM Reasoning via Reflection-Aware Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> SRPO enhances multimodal large language models by integrating explicit self-reflection and self-correction capabilities through a two-stage training framework. The approach achieves state-of-the-art performance among open-source models, scoring 78.5% on MathVista with SRPO-32B, and showing competitive results against leading closed-source models across diverse reasoning benchmarks.

## Problem

- Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs) struggle with complex reasoning and self-correction, often producing redundant or erroneous steps, unlike unimodal LLMs.
- Existing reflection methods for MLLMs are often superficial, failing to generate meaningful feedback or truly revise reasoning due to limitations set during initial pre-training.
- Traditional supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning methods for MLLMs activate existing knowledge but do not foster genuine acquisition of new cognitive behaviors or extend reasoning beyond pre-training limits.

## Method

- SRPO introduces a two-stage training framework combining ==reflection-oriented== supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reflection-aware reinforcement learning (RL).
- The SFT stage injects foundational self-reflection knowledge by using a curated dataset of 10,000 multimodal samples that teach the model to revise initial responses using generated reflections.
- The RL stage refines the model's abilities using an enhanced Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with a novel reward function that explicitly incentivizes proper reflection formatting, concise yet informative reflections, and the effectiveness of reflection in correcting or preserving answers.

## Results

- SRPO-**32B** achieved state-of-the-art performance among open-source MLLMs, scoring **78.5%** on MathVista and consistently outperforming other open-source reasoning models across diverse mathematical benchmarks.
- The framework consistently surpassed existing open-source MLLMs on general reasoning benchmarks (e.g., MMMU-Pro, MMMU, EMMA) and challenging cross-disciplinary tasks (e.g., +5.1 points on Physics over MM-Eureka-**7B**).
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical importance of both the reflection-oriented SFT for cold-start initialization and the reflection-aware RL components, particularly the effectiveness reward (IeffI_{eff}Ieff), for robust performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly integrating reflection-oriented SFT and a reflection-aware RL reward mechanism is crucial for enabling genuine self-reflection and self-correction in MLLMs, extending their cognitive boundaries.
> - A sophisticated reward function that directly evaluates the *effectiveness* of reflection (e.g., correcting errors, preserving correct reasoning) and controls for brevity is vital for fostering meaningful self-improvement.
> - Structured reflection patterns (e.g., 'initial solution → reflection → refined solution') provide substantial benefits over simpler iterative thinking, leading to significant improvements in reasoning quality.
