# VLM2Vec-V2: Advancing Multimodal Embedding for Videos, Images, and Visual Documents

> [!summary] Summary
> VLM2Vec-V2, developed by researchers from Salesforce Research and collaborating universities, introduces a unified multimodal embedding model capable of processing and aligning videos, images, and visual documents with text. The model achieves the highest overall score of 58.0 on the newly introduced MMEB-V2 benchmark, which expands evaluation to 78 tasks across these diverse visual modalities.

## Problem

- Existing state-of-the-art multimodal embedding models predominantly focus on natural images and text, limiting their applicability to real-world scenarios involving diverse visual content.
- There is a lack of unified support for dynamic content like videos and structured visual forms such as documents (PDFs, slides) within a single multimodal embedding framework.
- A comprehensive and standardized benchmark was missing to effectively evaluate embedding models across images, videos, and visual documents.

## Method

- The paper introduces ==VLM2Vec-V2==, a unified multimodal embedding model trained using an ==instruction-guided contrastive learning== approach on the Qwen2-VL backbone.
- A new comprehensive benchmark, ==MMEB-V2==, is developed with 9 meta-tasks and 78 specific tasks to evaluate multimodal embeddings across text, images, videos, and visual documents.
- Sophisticated data sampling strategies, including on-the-fly batch mixing and ==interleaved sub-batching==, are employed during multi-task training to handle heterogeneous data sources.

## Results

- VLM2Vec-V2 achieves the highest overall average score of **58.0** across all 78 datasets in the MMEB-V2 benchmark, outperforming existing generalist models like GME and previous VLM2Vec versions.
- It demonstrates strong performance on image tasks (average **64.9**), competitive results on video tasks (average **34.9**), and significantly improved performance on visual document retrieval (**65.4**) compared to other generalist VLMs.
- Ablation studies confirm that training with all three modalities (images, videos, visual documents) consistently leads to the best overall performance and that increased training steps further improve results.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Comprehensive multi-modal training across images, videos, and visual documents is crucial for achieving broad generalization in embedding models.
> - Instruction-guided contrastive learning with a robust Vision-Language Model backbone (Qwen2-VL) enables effective alignment of diverse visual and textual data into a unified embedding space.
> - Data sampling strategies like interleaved sub-batching and specific LoRA ranks are important for optimizing performance across heterogeneous modalities during training.
