# FRMD: Fast Robot Motion Diffusion with Consistency-Distilled Movement Primitives for Smooth Action Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> The FRMD framework accelerates robot motion generation by integrating consistency-distilled models with movement primitives, allowing for single-step, real-time action generation. This approach yields motions 10 times faster than prior diffusion methods while achieving an average 64.8% task success rate and significantly smoother trajectories on manipulation benchmarks.

## Problem

- Existing diffusion-based robot motion generation often produces non-smooth, jerky motions lacking temporal consistency.
- The iterative denoising process in conventional diffusion models leads to high inference latency, making them impractical for real-time robotic control.

## Method

- **FRMD** integrates ==Consistency Models (CMs)== with Probabilistic Dynamic Movement Primitives (ProDMPs) to ensure both fast and smooth action generation.
- A teacher Movement Primitive Diffusion (MPD) model, which generates ProDMP parameters over multiple steps, is distilled into a student FRMD model using consistency distillation.
- The student FRMD model learns to directly predict ProDMP weight vectors from noisy inputs in a single inference step, leveraging skip connections and an EMA target network for stable training.

## Results

- FRMD achieved a substantially reduced average inference time of 17.2ms, making it 10 times faster than MPD (168.6ms) and 7 times faster than Diffusion Policy (119.7ms).
- It demonstrated the highest average task success rate of **64.8%** across 12 Meta-World and ManiSkill manipulation tasks, slightly outperforming its MPD teacher (**64.1%**) and significantly surpassing Diffusion Policy (**50.1%**).
- The framework generated significantly smoother trajectories with fewer non-smooth transitions, for instance, reducing them from 82 for Diffusion Policy to 21 for FRMD in the `PlugCharger-v1` task.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining classical movement primitives for structured motion with modern generative diffusion models can address both temporal consistency and expressiveness in robot control.
> - Consistency distillation effectively accelerates diffusion models, enabling single-step inference for real-time applications without compromising performance.
> - Generating low-dimensional movement primitive parameters instead of raw action sequences inherently ensures smoother and more temporally consistent robot trajectories.
