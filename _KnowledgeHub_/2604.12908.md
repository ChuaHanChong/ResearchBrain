# Robotic Manipulation is Vision-to-Geometry Mapping ($f(v) \rightarrow G$): Vision-Geometry Backbones over Language and Video Models

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework, Vision-Geometry-Action (VGA), redefines robotic manipulation as a direct vision-to-geometry mapping using a pretrained 3D world model backbone, achieving a 98.1% success rate on the LIBERO benchmark and enhanced zero-shot generalization to novel viewpoints in real-world scenarios.

## Problem

- Robotic manipulation models often rely on 2D-centric vision-language or video backbones, which are misaligned with the 3D geometric nature of physical actions.
- Existing methods struggle with an "information bottleneck" where 3D inputs are projected to 2D representations before being decoded back to 3D actions.
- These models tend to overfit visual patterns rather than learning true 3D dynamics, limiting robust generalization in complex environments.

## Method

- Develop the **VGA** (==Vision-Geometry-Action==) model, utilizing a pretrained ==3D world model== (**VGGT**) as its backbone to enable direct vision-to-geometry mapping.
- Incorporate a ==Progressive Volumetric Modulation (PVM)== module for structured integration of 3D representations into the action head.
- Implement a ==joint training strategy== to predict actions and auxiliary 3D properties (camera parameters, depth maps) from shared 3D representations.

## Results

- The VGA model achieved a **98.1%** average success rate on the LIBERO benchmark, outperforming 2D-centric VLA, 3D-VLA, and World Action Model baselines.
- Exhibited enhanced zero-shot generalization, achieving a **6%** higher success rate than leading baselines for out-of-distribution camera viewpoints in real-world scenarios.
- Ablation studies validated the contributions of Progressive Volumetric Modulation (PVM) and joint training, confirming their roles in improving geometric information flow and representation fidelity.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Robotic manipulation is fundamentally a vision-to-geometry mapping, best addressed by a native 3D representation backbone rather than 2D-centric priors.
> - A direct 3D reasoning pipeline, facilitated by components like Progressive Volumetric Modulation (PVM), bypasses the limitations of processing 3D data through 2D-centric latent spaces.
> - Pretrained 3D world models provide essential high-quality 3D representations, and parameter-efficient tuning methods are vital for maintaining their integrity during adaptation to control tasks.
