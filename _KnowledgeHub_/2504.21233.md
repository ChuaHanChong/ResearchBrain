# Phi-4-Mini-Reasoning: Exploring the Limits of Small Reasoning Language Models in Math

> [!summary] Summary
> Microsoft researchers demonstrate that a carefully designed multi-stage training recipe combining synthetic data distillation, supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning enables a 3.8B parameter language model (Phi-4-Mini) to outperform reasoning models nearly twice its size, achieving a 3.2-point improvement over DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B on Math-500.

## Problem

- Small language models (SLMs) struggle to match the reasoning capabilities of larger models
- Existing approaches for improving SLM reasoning lack a comprehensive, systematic training methodology

## Method

- ==Four-stage training recipe== combining distillation, fine-tuning, preference learning, and reinforcement learning
- Novel implementation techniques including ==packing mode== for efficiency and ==temperature annealing== for stable RL

## Results

- Outperforms **7B-8B** parameter models on math reasoning benchmarks despite using only **3.8B** parameters
- Exceeds DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B by **3.2 points** on Math-500
- Demonstrates stable learning curves and consistent improvements across multiple evaluation metrics
- Successfully maintains reasoning capabilities while reducing model size and computational requirements

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality Chain-of-Thought data is crucial for developing reasoning capabilities in small models
> - Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards provides more stable improvements than direct preference optimization
> - Each stage of the training recipe contributes measurably to overall performance gains
