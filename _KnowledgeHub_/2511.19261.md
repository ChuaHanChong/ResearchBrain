# LAST: LeArning to Think in Space and Time for Generalist Vision-Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> The LAST framework enhances generalist Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with advanced 3D spatial understanding and long video comprehension by integrating "visual thinking trajectories" through external visual tools. This extension of Chain-of-Thought reasoning yields substantial performance increases on relevant benchmarks for both powerful proprietary models and fine-tuned open-source VLMs.

## Problem

- Generalist Vision-Language Models (VLMs) demonstrate limitations in tasks requiring robust 3D spatial reasoning and comprehension of long video sequences.
- Existing solutions for 3D and long video understanding are often specialized, relying on additional 3D inputs or task-specific architectures, hindering the development of unified general-purpose VLMs.
- Traditional, language-centric Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning is insufficient for visually complex tasks, as it "lacks visual engagement" and can lead to incorrect reasoning when deep visual understanding is required.

## Method

- Introduces =="visual thinking trajectories"== to extend Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, enabling VLMs to dynamically interact with visual information for spatial and temporal analysis.
- Leverages a curated set of ==external visual tools==, including frame selection, object tracking, temporal grounding, image grounding, and depth estimation, to generate intermediate visual observations and augment VLM reasoning.
- The approach is implemented via zero-shot prompting for proprietary models (e.g., GPT-4o) and through a ==two-stage data curation and fine-tuning pipeline== for open-weight models (e.g., LAST-7B based on Qwen2.5-VL-7B).

## Results

- GPT-4o with LAST achieved up to a **15.8%** accuracy gain on EgoSchema (from **69.6%** to **85.4%**) and an **11.4** points CIDEr improvement on ScanQA (from **65.4%** to **76.8%**), outperforming some specialized 3D models.
- The fine-tuned LAST-7B model, built on Qwen2.5-VL-7B, showed an **8.3%** accuracy increase on VSI-Bench (from **33.0%** to **41.3%**) and a **14-point** gain on EgoSchema (from **55.0%** to **69.0%**) with 16 frames.
- LAST consistently outperformed text-only CoT, which often resulted in performance degradation (e.g., EgoSchema baseline **69.6%** vs. **62.7%** with text CoT, while LAST achieved **85.4%**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating external visual tool use directly into a VLM's reasoning process allows it to effectively acquire advanced spatial and temporal understanding from sequential 2D image observations without specialized architectures.
> - Purely textual Chain-of-Thought reasoning is inadequate and often degrades performance on visually complex spatial and temporal tasks, underscoring the critical need for visually-engaged reasoning.
> - A dedicated two-stage data curation process, generating visual thinking trajectories, can successfully fine-tune open-source VLMs to achieve significant performance gains on advanced visual reasoning benchmarks.
