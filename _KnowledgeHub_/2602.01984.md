# Enhancing Multi-Image Understanding through Delimiter Token Scaling

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Sogang University, KAIST, and Tübingen University developed a method to improve multi-image understanding in Large Vision-Language Models by scaling the hidden states of image delimiter tokens. This approach effectively mitigates cross-image information leakage and strengthens intra-image interactions, yielding consistent performance improvements across various benchmarks and models without incurring any additional computational cost.

## Problem

- Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) exhibit degraded performance when processing multiple images due to an inability to accurately distinguish and reason across distinct visual inputs.
- A primary issue is "cross-image information leakage," where information from one image blends with others, hindering reliable multi-image understanding.
- Existing image delimiter tokens, despite their presence, prove insufficient in fully preventing this leakage, and their mechanistic function was not well understood.

## Method

- Empirically analyzed the ==attention mechanisms of existing image delimiter tokens== in LVLMs to understand their function and limitations in separating distinct visual contexts.
- Identified two key properties of effective delimiters: ==localized attention absorption== from their corresponding image and reinforcement of intra-image interactions through an "image tagging" mechanism.
- Proposed =="delimiter token scaling"==, a simple, training-free method that scales the hidden states of delimiter tokens by a factor to strengthen their boundary function.

## Results

- The method consistently improves multi-image understanding, reducing cross-image attention by approximately **50%** while maintaining intra-image performance across diverse LVLMs (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL3, LLaVA-OneVision) and benchmarks.
- Delimiter token scaling incurs no additional training or inference costs, demonstrating superior computational efficiency and resource footprint compared to previous training-free approaches.
- The technique successfully generalizes beyond multi-image settings, yielding performance improvements in text-only multi-document summarization and multi-table question answering tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Image delimiter tokens are indispensable for LVLMs to distinguish individual images, but they are imperfect and allow significant cross-image information leakage.
> - Delimiter tokens function as localized attractors of attention, primarily receiving strong attention from tokens within their corresponding image, distinguishing them from global sink tokens.
> - This localized attention enables delimiters to act as an "image-specific tag," which provides a common bias to strengthen intra-image interactions.
