# Quality-Driven Curation of Remote Sensing Vision-Language Data via Learned Scoring Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Nanjing University developed `ScoreRS`, the first open-source learned scoring model and preference dataset specifically designed for assessing the quality of remote sensing (RS) vision-language data. This model enables quality-driven data curation, leading to improved VLM performance, with fine-tuning on top 30% of `ScoreRS`-ranked data yielding up to 7% higher classification accuracy and `ScoreRS` as a Best-of-N selector pushing LHRS-Bench accuracy past 70%.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) face significant challenges in interpreting Remote Sensing (RS) images due to substantial domain shifts and a scarcity of large-scale, high-quality RS-specific vision-language data.
- Current methods for creating RS vision-language data (e.g., rule-based or synthetic generation) often introduce quality issues such as informational sparsity, semantic inconsistency, or hallucinations.
- A robust, automated framework for systematically assessing and curating the quality of synthetically generated RS vision-language data is lacking, hindering performance improvements in RS-specific VLMs.

## Method

- A comprehensive ==quality framework== was established for RS vision-language data, defining five crucial dimensions: relevance, specificity & detail, completeness, clarity & fluency, and semantic richness.
- The first large-scale, RS-specific preference datasets were meticulously constructed for both image-captions (ICPD: 72K pairs) and vision instructions (VIPD: 59K pairs) using diverse VLM generations and GPT-4o for quality judgments.
- A novel parameter-wise learned scoring model, `**ScoreRS**`, was developed and trained using a three-stage progressive strategy on these preference datasets to automatically assess the quality of RS vision-language data.

## Results

- Fine-tuning CLIP-ViT-L/14 with the top **30%** of data ranked by `ScoreRS` achieved a **7%** improvement in average classification accuracy and **2%** in average retrieval recall on the RemoteCLIP dataset, surpassing full-data training.
- Integrating `ScoreRS` as a reward model in Reinforcement Learning for Qwen2VL-**7B**-RS improved performance by over **5%** on VG-DIOR and **2%** on LHRS-Bench compared to the base model.
- As a Best-of-N selector at test time, `ScoreRS` consistently enhanced VLM performance across multiple models and tasks, enabling the challenging LHRS-Bench dataset accuracy to exceed **70%** for the first time.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The quality of vision-language data, rather than just its volume, is a critical bottleneck for advancing Vision-Language Models in the remote sensing domain.
> - A learned scoring model, trained on human-aligned preference data generated with advanced LLMs, can effectively learn and predict fine-grained quality distinctions unique to RS imagery and text.
> - Quality-driven data curation, by filtering and selecting high-quality subsets using `ScoreRS`, can lead to superior VLM performance, validating the "less is more" principle in the context of RS data.
