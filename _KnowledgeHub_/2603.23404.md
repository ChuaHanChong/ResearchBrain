# Unleashing Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models via Textual Representation Guided Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Tsinghua University and Shanghai AI Lab introduce TRACE, a prompting method enabling multimodal large language models to construct and reason over structured, text-based allocentric representations of 3D environments from egocentric video. This approach consistently improves spatial reasoning accuracy on benchmarks like VSI-Bench, showing gains of up to 7.54% for Gemini 3 Pro without requiring architectural changes.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with 3D spatial reasoning from egocentric video, failing to construct structured, allocentric abstractions of environments.
- Existing MLLMs often over-fixate on 2D visual cues and implicit spatial correlations, leading to inaccuracies in complex spatial question answering.
- Prior solutions, such as large-scale supervised fine-tuning or integrating additional geometric modalities, often face limitations in scalability, generalization, or increased system complexity.

## Method

- ==TRACE (Textual Representation of Allocentric Context from Egocentric Video)== is a prompting strategy that guides MLLMs to generate a detailed, structured, text-based ==allocentric representation== of a 3D environment.
- The generated representation comprises a ==Room Aligned Meta Context==, a ==Camera Trajectory== (observer's path with positions and headings), and an ==Entity Registry== (detailed attributes and spatial relations of observed objects).
- MLLMs perform ==single-pass inference== where they first explicitly generate the schema-compliant TRACE as a 'spatial cache' and then leverage this structured textual context, alongside the video input, to derive the final answer.

## Results

- TRACE consistently improved spatial reasoning accuracy on VSI-Bench, with gains of **+7.54%** for Gemini 3 Pro, **+3.10%** for Qwen2.5-VL-72B, and **+1.63%** for MiMo-VL-7B-SFT over direct baselines.
- The method outperformed other prompting strategies (e.g., Chain-of-Thought, Tree-of-Thought, Cognitive Map) on both VSI-Bench and OST-Bench, showing particular superiority for compact MLLM backbones.
- Ablation studies demonstrated the critical role of TRACE's components: removing the entity registry resulted in a **5.24%** performance drop, and removing camera trajectory led to a **1.92%** drop.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - MLLMs can be effectively guided to construct and reason over structured allocentric representations of 3D spatial environments from 2D visual observations using only textual prompting.
> - A cognitively inspired, structured textual representation—detailing meta-context, camera trajectory, and entity registry—provides explicit geometric and relational grounding, significantly enhancing MLLM spatial reasoning.
> - Decompositional analysis reveals that MLLM bottlenecks in 3D reasoning can stem from both 3D visual perception (generating the spatial description) and language-based spatial reasoning (parsing the description), with visual perception often posing a greater challenge for smaller models.
