# FOSP: Fine-tuning Offline Safe Policy through World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> FOSP (Fine-tuning Offline Safe Policy through World Models) is a two-stage framework for safely learning and adapting policies from offline data to novel online environments, especially for vision-based robotic control. This method achieved near-zero safety constraint violations and improved task success rates with few-shot fine-tuning on real-world Franka manipulator tasks.

## Problem

- Offline Reinforcement Learning policies often suffer from out-of-distribution (OOD) issues, leading to performance degradation or safety constraint violations in novel environments.
- Online fine-tuning of offline-pretrained safe policies frequently experiences initial performance drops and safety compromises.
- Balancing task performance with strict safety adherence is challenging in high-dimensional, vision-based tasks, particularly during the offline-to-online transition.

## Method

- **FOSP** utilizes a ==two-stage framework==: offline pretraining to learn a robust and safe policy from static data, followed by online fine-tuning for efficient and safe adaptation to novel environments.
- It extends a model-based RL ==world model (DreamerV3)== with a ==cost decoder== to predict future rewards and crucial safety costs from high-dimensional visual observations.
- The framework incorporates ==in-sample optimization== for conservative value estimation, a ==Reachability Estimation Function (REF)== for robust safety constraint enforcement, and a ==safe policy expansion== mechanism for stable offline-to-online transfer.

## Results

- **FOSP** demonstrated superior safety performance in simulations (Safety-Gymnasium), achieving **nearly zero** constraint violations while maintaining competitive reward performance compared to other safe RL algorithms.
- On real-world Franka manipulator 'SafeReach' tasks, **FOSP** successfully adapted to unseen safety regions and target objects with few-shot fine-tuning (**40 gradient steps**), significantly improving success rates and rapidly reducing constraint violations.
- Ablation studies confirmed that in-sample optimization, safe policy expansion, and the Reachability Estimation Function are critical components, each contributing to FOSP's ability to balance reward maximization and safety adherence.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating a cost decoder into a world model allows for joint prediction of rewards and safety costs, enabling effective learning of safe environmental dynamics from vision-only offline data.
> - Combining in-sample optimization for conservative Q-value estimation with a Reachability Estimation Function (REF) provides robust safety guarantees by unifying mixed constraints and mitigating OOD action issues.
> - A safe policy expansion approach facilitates a stable transition from offline to online, allowing the agent to safely explore with an adapting policy while benefiting from the prior safety knowledge of the frozen offline policy.
