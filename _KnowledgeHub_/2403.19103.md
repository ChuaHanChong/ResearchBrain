# Automated Black-box Prompt Engineering for Personalized Text-to-Image Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> PRISM introduces an iterative VLM-driven algorithm that automatically generates human-interpretable and transferable text prompts for black-box text-to-image (T2I) models. The system achieves superior generalizability and visual accuracy across various T2I generators, including DALL-E and Midjourney, while producing prompts that are easily understandable and editable by users.

## Problem

- Controlling text-to-image models requires labor-intensive manual prompt engineering, which is sensitive to phrasing and demands significant human expertise.
- Existing automated prompt generation methods often lack transferability across different T2I models or necessitate white-box access to proprietary models.
- Many automated approaches produce non-interpretable 'soft tokens' or embeddings, hindering human understanding, refinement, or creative editing of the generated prompts.

## Method

- ==PRISM== employs an iterative, VLM-driven approach, using a =='Prompt Engineer Assistant' (GPT-4V)== to propose and refine textual prompts by analyzing feedback.
- A VLM-based =='Judge Model' (GPT-4V)== evaluates visual similarity between generated and reference images with nuanced, flexible criteria, guiding the prompt refinement process without needing white-box access.
- The algorithm refines a ==distribution of candidate prompts in parallel streams==, leveraging the VLM's in-context learning and reasoning abilities to improve prompt quality and interpretability over iterations.

## Results

- PRISM demonstrated superior performance and generalizability across closed-source T2I models (DALL-E 2, DALL-E 3, Midjourney) compared to baselines that often require white-box access.
- The method produced fully human-readable and editable prompts, achieving low mean negative log-likelihood scores and enabling intuitive user modifications for creative applications.
- PRISM maintained high visual accuracy (CLIP-I and DINO scores) in generated images for personalized T2I and image inversion tasks, while also significantly reducing unsafe prompt generation (**0.1-6.9%** failure rate on DALL-E safeguards).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision-Language Models can effectively function as iterative optimizers and reasoning engines for complex, black-box creative tasks like prompt engineering, mimicking human problem-solving strategies.
> - An iterative feedback loop between a T2I generator, a VLM-based prompt refiner, and a VLM-based judge enables the generation of highly transferable and human-interpretable prompts for black-box models.
> - Refining a distribution of candidate prompts over multiple parallel streams allows for broader exploration of the solution space and enhanced robustness compared to optimizing a single prompt.
