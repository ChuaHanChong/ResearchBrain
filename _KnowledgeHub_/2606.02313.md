# Towards Precise Intent-Aligned VLA Aerial Navigation via Expert-Guided GRPO

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Zhejiang University and Differential Robotics developed an efficient reinforcement learning framework for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) aerial navigation, allowing unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to achieve precise, intent-aligned flight. The framework increased overall success rate by 29.5% and intent alignment score by 60.9% compared to SFT baselines in simulation, demonstrating zero-shot transfer to real-world tasks.

## Problem

- VLA models for aerial navigation largely rely on supervised fine-tuning (SFT), which is limited by high data acquisition costs, poor generalization to out-of-distribution scenarios, and inability to capture nuanced human intent.
- Direct application of reinforcement learning (RL) to 3D UAV navigation is hindered by inefficient exploration in vast continuous state/action spaces and the difficulty of learning complex, intent-aligned behaviors from sparse rewards.
- Computational bottlenecks arise from the sequential coupling of simulation and VLA inference in online RL training, leading to hardware underutilization and slow data collection cycles.

## Method

- Expert-Guided Group Relative Policy Optimization (EG-GRPO) is proposed, integrating few-shot expert data into the online RL loop to mitigate exploration inefficiency and provide stable gradient signals for intent alignment.
- A high-fidelity, vectorized UAV simulation platform is developed to support high-throughput policy rollouts in instruction-conditioned environments, integrating precise physics and collision detection.
- An efficient heterogeneous parallelization pipeline decouples environment simulation and VLA model inference, leveraging a double-buffer paradigm to reduce rollout time by approximately 43.5%.

## Results

- The EG-GRPO framework improved overall success rate by **29.5%** (from **26.1%** to **55.6%**) and intent alignment score by **60.9%** (from 4.50 to 7.24) compared to SFT baselines in simulation.
- Policies trained in simulation demonstrated successful zero-shot transfer to real-world UAVs, executing complex commands like S-shaped bypassing and speed-modulated object navigation without additional fine-tuning.
- Ablation studies showed that EG-GRPO contributed an additional 1.54 IAS and **16.7%** SR gain on difficult tasks over GRPO alone, and heterogeneous parallelization reduced average rollout time by **43.5%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating few-shot expert demonstrations into a group-relative policy optimization framework (EG-GRPO) effectively addresses sparse reward challenges and provides stable learning signals for intent-aligned behaviors in complex 3D environments.
> - Heterogeneous parallelization of simulation and VLA inference drastically improves the efficiency and scalability of online reinforcement learning for robotics, significantly reducing hardware idle times and rollout durations.
> - Instruction-conditioned, LLM-based reward models, validated by human experts, can provide crucial fine-grained, trajectory-level feedback necessary for training VLA policies to align with qualitative human intents.
