# EgoAERO: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from a Single Egocentric Video without Object Assets

> [!summary] Summary
> EgoAERO introduces a framework that enables learning dexterous manipulation policies from a single egocentric RGB-D human demonstration, eliminating the need for object assets. It reconstructs contact-consistent hand-object trajectories from raw video and achieves a 49.5% success rate in simulation, demonstrating comparable performance to CAD-based methods.

## Problem

- Traditional methods for collecting human demonstration data for dexterous robot manipulation require specialized hardware or intrusive setups.
- Existing large-scale egocentric datasets lack crucial 3D object information (6-DoF pose, geometry, contact state) necessary for robot learning.
- Many hand-object datasets rely on pre-scanned CAD models or multi-view capture systems, limiting scalability and applicability to novel objects.

## Method

- **EgoAERO** integrates MLLM-guided semantic preprocessing, robust ==asset-free object tracking== under occlusion, neural object field reconstruction, and ego-motion compensation to generate contact-consistent 6-DoF hand-object trajectories.
- A two-stage residual policy learning approach transfers reconstructed human trajectories to robot policies, first tracking hand motion, then learning residual corrections based on object state and contact.
- An online quality assessment mechanism evaluates the physical usability of egocentric demonstrations, facilitating the creation of the large-scale, asset-free EgoDex-R dataset.

## Results

- EgoAERO achieved a **49.5%** success rate in simulation with object rotation error of **9.7**° and translation error of **0.82** cm, outperforming ablations that lacked object reconstruction or adaptive contact optimization.
- Its asset-free reconstruction produced trajectories with comparable or slightly superior performance (**44.7%** success rate, **10.9**° E_r, **0.68** cm E_t) to CAD-based annotations on the HOI4D dataset.
- The framework successfully transferred reconstructed human hand-object trajectories to a physical Unitree G1 robot with an Inspire Hand, demonstrating real-world dexterous manipulation capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Asset-free reconstruction of hand-object trajectories from egocentric RGB-D video is achievable by combining robust tracking, neural object fields, and ego-motion compensation.
> - Adaptive contact optimization is crucial for refining reconstructed hand-object interactions, ensuring physical plausibility and significantly improving manipulation performance.
> - A two-stage residual policy learning paradigm effectively bridges the gap from human demonstrations to robot control, enabling stable hand tracking and object-contact adherence.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of robotic dexterous manipulation increasingly leverages human demonstrations as a data source for policy learning. Traditional methods for collecting such data, including teleoperation, Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) systems, and data gloves, often involve specialized hardware or intrusive setups. Egocentric RGB-D devices represent a less intrusive alternative for capturing natural hand motions, object interactions, and contact-rich manipulation in everyday environments. This makes egocentric demonstrations a potentially scalable data source for robot learning.

However, existing egocentric datasets present challenges for direct use in robot learning. Large-scale datasets like EPIC-KITCHENS and Ego4D typically lack critical 3D information such as the manipulated object's 6-DoF (degrees-of-freedom) pose, geometry, and contact state, which are essential for object-conditioned rewards, contact constraints, and task replay. While other datasets such as H2O, HOI4D, HOT3D, HO-3D, DexYCB, OakInk, and ARCTIC provide 3D hand-object annotations, they often rely on pre-scanned CAD models, multi-view capture systems, or known object assets. This dependency significantly limits their scalability and applicability to arbitrary, novel objects encountered in natural environments.

EgoAERO addresses these limitations by proposing a framework that operates without object assets. It aims to bridge the gap between naturally collected, asset-free egocentric RGB-D videos and the structured 3D hand-object trajectories and robot policies required for dexterous manipulation, thus contributing to more scalable and generalizable robot learning from human demonstrations.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop **EgoAERO**, a framework capable of learning dexterous manipulation policies from a single egocentric RGB-D human demonstration *without requiring object assets*. This objective is motivated by the inherent challenges of using existing egocentric datasets for robot learning and the desire to leverage the naturalness and scalability of egocentric data collection.

Specific key objectives include:
- **Asset-Free Trajectory Reconstruction:** To reconstruct contact-consistent hand-object trajectories from raw egocentric RGB-D data. This involves developing methods for robust object tracking and geometry reconstruction, even under significant hand occlusion and with unknown objects.
- **Egocentric Motion Handling:** To effectively compensate for egocentric camera motion (head movement) to provide stable hand-object states in a unified coordinate frame.
- **Contact Consistency:** To optimize the reconstructed hand-object interactions to ensure physical plausibility and contact consistency, which is crucial for successful robot manipulation.
- **Policy Learning and Transfer:** To convert the reconstructed human hand-object trajectories into executable dexterous robot manipulation policies using a robust learning paradigm.
- **Data Quality Assurance:** To design an online mechanism for assessing the quality of egocentric demonstration data, enabling efficient data collection and improving the utility of generated datasets.
- **Dataset Construction:** To construct a large-scale, asset-free egocentric dataset (EgoDex-R) that provides structured, contact-consistent hand-object annotations suitable for dexterous policy learning.

The motivation stems from the recognized potential of egocentric RGB-D devices to capture natural human manipulation in everyday settings with minimal intrusion. However, the absence of crucial 3D object pose, geometry, and accurate contact information, coupled with the reliance on object-specific assets in most existing datasets, restricts their direct utility for robot learning. By developing an asset-free framework, EgoAERO seeks to overcome these barriers, making it feasible to train robots on a wider variety of tasks and objects encountered in the real world, thereby enhancing the scalability and generalizability of dexterous robot manipulation.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

EgoAERO is designed as an end-to-end framework, encompassing asset-free hand-object trajectory reconstruction, two-stage policy learning, and real-world robot execution.

#### 3.1. Asset-free Egocentric Hand-Object Reconstruction

This module converts raw egocentric RGB-D video into structured, contact-consistent hand-object trajectories.

- **MLLM-Guided Semantic Preprocessing:** To address ambiguity in complex scenes, EgoAERO first utilizes a Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM). By analyzing keyframes and a task description, the MLLM identifies the target manipulated object and any related contextual objects. It then generates text prompts for SAM3 (Segment Anything Model 3) to obtain frame-wise segmentation masks, and selects an initial seed frame for subsequent tracking. The MLLM's role is semantic initialization and pipeline configuration, not direct 3D pose or geometry estimation.

- **Asset-free Object Tracking and Reconstruction under Egocentric Hand Occlusion:**
 - **Coarse Object Pose Initialization:** For each frame, a coarse 6-DoF object pose (\(\tilde{T}_t\)) is estimated. This involves back-projecting the visible object region into a local point cloud and establishing local RGB-D correspondences between the current and previous frames. RANSAC is employed for robust rigid pose estimation, mitigating errors from hand occlusion and low-texture surfaces.
 - **Keyframe Memory-pool Pose Optimization:** To achieve stable tracking despite occlusion and partial visibility, EgoAERO maintains a memory pool (P) of reliable historical keyframes. For the current frame, a subset of relevant keyframes is selected, and a local pose graph is constructed. Object poses within this graph are jointly optimized using a combination of cross-frame RGB-D feature alignment (E_feat), geometric consistency (E_geo), online object geometry adherence (E_sdf), silhouette consistency (E_mask), and pose-prior regularization (E_pose). This multi-constraint optimization provides temporally stable 6-DoF object trajectories.
 - **Neural Object Field Guided Coarse-to-Fine Mesh Reconstruction:** Due to incomplete observations in egocentric videos, a coarse-to-fine strategy is used. An online neural object field (\(\Omega_\Theta\)) is maintained in the object's canonical frame, fusing keyframe observations to produce a temporally consistent coarse mesh (\(M_{coarse}^O\)). This is then combined with SAM3D and original RGB-D observations to recover finer surface details (\(M_{sam}^O\)), which is aligned to the coarse mesh to yield the final object mesh (\(M^O\)).

- **Egocentric Hand Pose Estimation:** EgoAERO initializes hand poses using camera-frame MANO (Model of Human Hands) results from HaWoR. A lightweight correction is then applied to the global hand translation using RGB-D depth information. This process projects MANO vertices onto the RGB-D image and adjusts the global translation to align the hand mesh more accurately with the observed depth.

- **Ego Motion Compensation:** The egocentric camera, typically head-mounted, introduces head motion into hand-object trajectories. EgoAERO estimates the camera trajectory using an RGB-D SLAM backend, minimizing the impact of dynamic hand regions by down-weighting hand pixels. All hand-object states are then transformed into a stable, unified table frame. Lightweight temporal smoothing is applied to the object trajectory and hand root translation without imposing strict environmental constraints.

- **Adaptive Contact Optimization:** To mitigate issues like fingertip floating or penetration caused by occlusions and pose estimation errors, EgoAERO applies a conservative, geometry-level contact correction. This process identifies an active manipulation window and constructs three contact regions (thumb pulp, dynamically selected opposing fingertip, thenar region). It first estimates a constrained whole-hand translation to correct global misalignment, followed by small local geometric offsets to the thumb and opposing fingers to enhance fingertip contact. The correction trajectory is temporally smoothed, boundary tapered, and includes penetration push-back. This module refines contact consistency without modifying MANO parameters, object trajectory, or geometry.

#### 3.2. Two-stage Residual Policy Learning

EgoAERO converts the reconstructed hand-object trajectories into executable robot policies using a two-stage residual learning approach.

- **Stage I: Hand Trajectory Tracking:** A first-stage policy (\(\pi_I\)) is trained to stably track the reconstructed human wrist pose and finger keypoint trajectories using a dexterous robot hand. Kinematic retargeting provides an initial robot hand trajectory, but the policy is supervised directly by the human hand reference trajectory. The reward function includes terms for wrist tracking (position, orientation, velocity), finger imitation, and action smoothness. This stage provides a stable hand control prior.

- **Stage II: Object-Contact Residual Policy Learning:** A residual policy (\(\pi_R\)) is learned to apply small corrections (\(\Delta a_R\)) to the base actions produced by the first-stage policy. This stage incorporates object states (reference trajectory, current pose/velocity, geometry encoding), hand-object distance, and simulated contact forces as additional feedback. The reward function combines the hand imitation term from Stage I with object trajectory tracking, contact constraints, and a residual regularization term, enabling the robot to maintain stable contact and follow the object's reference trajectory.

#### 3.3. Egocentric Demonstration Data Collection and Quality Assessment

EgoAERO includes a closed-loop data collection pipeline.

- **Online Ego Data Quality Assessment:** During data collection, a provisional hand-object trajectory is reconstructed and assessed for physical usability. The assessment evaluates tracking stability, contact consistency, residual penetration, and temporal jitter. It verifies if stable hand-object contact can be recovered through small, local corrections without altering the object trajectory or hand articulation. Based on these metrics, a decision is made to "accept," "repairable accept," or "recapture" the demonstration.

- **EgoDex-R Dataset:** Using this online assessment loop, EgoAERO facilitated the creation of EgoDex-R, a large-scale egocentric dexterous manipulation dataset. Collected with FastUMI Ego and without object CAD assets, EgoDex-R comprises approximately 4.3 million RGB-D frames, 5,600 manipulation sequences, and over 1,000 object instances across more than 200 task categories. It provides structured outputs including contact-consistent hand-object trajectories, object pose and geometry, depth maps, SLAM poses, and contact quality annotations, supporting various robot learning tasks.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of EgoAERO was conducted through simulation and real-world experiments, focusing on its ability to drive dexterous manipulation and the performance of its asset-free reconstruction compared to CAD-based methods.

#### 4.1. Simulation Experiment Results

Experiments were performed in Isaac Gym, evaluating object rotation error (E_r), object translation error (E_t), mean joint position error (E_j), mean fingertip position error (E_ft), and success rate (SR).

- **Evaluation on EgoDex-R:**
 - **"Only Hand Pose" (Ablation):** This baseline, using only hand motion without object geometry and pose supervision, yielded a low success rate of 9.8% and high errors (E_r: 28.6°, E_t: 4.72 cm, E_j: 3.35 cm, E_ft: 2.48 cm). This indicates that hand motion alone is insufficient for successful dexterous object manipulation without explicit object state and contact information.
 - **"w/o Adaptive Contact Optimization" (Ablation):** Incorporating asset-free object reconstruction significantly improved performance, achieving a success rate of 36.2% and reduced errors (E_r: 15.4°, E_t: 1.36 cm, E_j: 2.93 cm, E_ft: 2.18 cm). This demonstrates the contribution of object reconstruction to reducing object tracking errors and improving task success.
 - **"EgoAERO" (Full Pipeline):** The full EgoAERO framework achieved the highest success rate of 49.5% and the lowest errors across all metrics (E_r: 9.7°, E_t: 0.82 cm, E_j: 2.48 cm, E_ft: 1.65 cm). This result indicates that the adaptive contact optimization module further enhances hand tracking quality and contact stability, leading to improved overall manipulation performance.

- **Comparison on HOI4D (Asset-Free EgoAERO vs. CAD-Based):**
 - EgoAERO's asset-free reconstruction from raw RGB-D input was compared against trajectories reconstructed using existing object CAD assets from the HOI4D dataset.
 - EgoAERO achieved comparable performance (E_r: 10.9°, E_t: 0.68 cm, E_j: 2.44 cm, E_ft: 1.58 cm, SR: 44.7%) to the raw CAD-based annotations (E_r: 10.4°, E_t: 0.73 cm, E_j: 2.32 cm, E_ft: 1.69 cm, SR: 43.3%).
 - On certain metrics, EgoAERO even slightly outperformed the CAD-based data. The authors suggest this may be due to EgoAERO's adaptive contact optimization, which improves the physical consistency of hand-object demonstrations by reducing fingertip floating, missing contacts, and local penetrations, a step not explicitly performed on the raw HOI4D perception-level annotations. These results suggest that EgoAERO can reconstruct physically plausible and effective hand-object demonstrations without requiring object CAD assets.

#### 4.2. Real-World Experiment

EgoAERO was evaluated on a physical robot platform comprising a Unitree G1 humanoid robot and an Inspire Hand.
- The reconstructed hand-object trajectory from a single egocentric demonstration was successfully transferred, and the learned two-stage residual policy generated dexterous hand and wrist trajectories for execution.
- The G1 arm tracked the policy-generated wrist motion, while the Inspire Hand executed finger commands.
- These experiments qualitatively verified that EgoAERO can transform natural egocentric human demonstrations into contact-consistent robot trajectories capable of execution on physical dexterous hardware.

#### 4.3. EgoDex-R Dataset

The construction of EgoDex-R, a large-scale egocentric dataset, is itself a key outcome. It contains 4.3 million RGB-D frames, 5,600 manipulation sequences, and over 1,000 unique object instances. Crucially, it provides contact-consistent hand-object trajectories with comprehensive annotations (object pose, geometry, depth, SLAM, contact quality), distinguishing it from previous datasets that often lack these structured outputs or rely on object assets.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

EgoAERO represents a notable advancement in the field of dexterous robot manipulation by addressing a significant challenge: learning from natural human demonstrations without prior knowledge or assets of the manipulated objects.

**Key significance points include:**
- **Asset-Free Learning:** EgoAERO is presented as the first framework to learn dexterous manipulation from a *single egocentric RGB-D human demonstration without requiring object assets*. This capability eliminates the need for expensive and time-consuming CAD modeling or 3D scanning, which traditionally limits the scalability of robot learning to novel or unknown objects.
- **Enhanced Data Utility:** The framework's ability to reconstruct **contact-consistent** hand-object trajectories is critical. Many existing egocentric datasets lack accurate contact information, which is fundamental for stable and robust robotic grasping and manipulation. EgoAERO's adaptive contact optimization module addresses this, providing more physically plausible demonstrations for downstream policy learning.
- **Robust Trajectory Reconstruction:** The multi-component reconstruction pipeline, including MLLM-guided semantic preprocessing, robust object tracking under occlusion, neural field-guided coarse-to-fine mesh reconstruction, and ego-motion compensation, contributes to producing high-quality and reliable 6-DoF hand-object trajectories from noisy and incomplete egocentric video data.
- **Efficient Data Collection:** The introduction of an **online quality assessment mechanism** for egocentric demonstration data is significant. It enables a closed-loop data collection process where demonstrations can be immediately evaluated, repaired, or recaptured, reducing wasted effort and improving the efficiency of acquiring high-quality datasets.
- **New Dataset Contribution:** The creation of **EgoDex-R**, a large-scale egocentric dataset with structured, contact-consistent annotations collected *without object assets*, provides a valuable resource for the research community. This dataset can accelerate research in imitation learning, residual policy learning, and simulation replay for dexterous manipulation.
- **Demonstrated Transferability:** Both simulation and real-world robot experiments validate EgoAERO's capacity to translate human demonstrations into executable policies on physical dexterous hardware. The performance achieved on HOI4D, comparable to or exceeding CAD-based reconstructions, further underlines the effectiveness of the asset-free approach for practical robot learning.

**Potential Impact:**
EgoAERO has the potential to significantly impact the development of dexterous robots by:
- **Lowering the Barrier to Entry:** Making it easier and more scalable to acquire training data for robot manipulation by reducing reliance on specialized equipment and prior object knowledge.
- **Accelerating Research:** Providing a robust framework and a rich dataset that can serve as a foundation for future research into generalizable and autonomous dexterous manipulation.
- **Enabling Real-World Applications:** Facilitating the deployment of dexterous robots in unstructured environments where they need to interact with novel or previously unseen objects, which is a common scenario in service robotics, manufacturing, and logistics.

While the current work primarily focuses on single-hand manipulation and requires simulation training for task-specific policies, it lays a groundwork for future research into multi-hand interactions, robustness against environmental challenges (e.g., severe occlusion, reflective objects), cross-task generalization, and reducing policy learning costs.
