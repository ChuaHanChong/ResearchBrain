# Kwai Keye-VL Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Kuaishou Group's Keye Team developed Kwai Keye-VL, an 8-billion-parameter multimodal foundation model that achieves leading performance in short-video understanding while retaining robust general vision-language capabilities. It significantly outperforms existing models on video-centric benchmarks and a new real-world short-video dataset (KC-MMBench).

## Problem

- Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle to fully comprehend dynamic, information-dense short-form videos, often optimized for static images.
- Current MLLMs typically fail to capture the rich temporal, contextual, and sequential information inherent in video content, including event sequences and audio integration.
- A lack of tailored benchmarks exists for rigorously evaluating MLLMs on real-world short-video scenarios.

## Method

- Introduces Kwai Keye-VL, an 8-billion-parameter MLLM with a native-resolution Vision Transformer using ==2D and 3D Rotary Position Embeddings== for unified processing of text, image, and video.
- Constructs a massive, diverse corpus exceeding **600 billion** tokens, including MLLM-recaptioned images, synthesized OCR data, and extensive video data with interleaved ASR.
- Employs a sophisticated multi-stage training methodology, combining a four-stage progressive pre-training with a two-phase post-training that includes ==Mix-Mode Reinforcement Learning== and Iterative Alignment.

## Results

- Achieves leading performance on short-video benchmarks, including **57.6%** on Video-MMMU (an **8.7%** absolute improvement) and **66.1%** on MMVU.
- Outperforms the second-best model by over **10%** on the newly released Kuaishou Community Multimodal Benchmark (KC-MMBench), scoring **68.03%**, validating its practical value for commercial applications.
- Demonstrates competitive or state-of-the-art results on general vision-language tasks such as MMMU (**71.4%**) and AI2D (**86.7%**), while also showing lower hallucination rates.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A multi-stage training approach combining extensive pre-training and reasoning-focused post-training (including Chain-of-Thought and Reinforcement Learning) effectively cultivates comprehensive multimodal capabilities.
> - Training on Chain-of-Thought reasoning data mutually enhances both reasoning and non-reasoning abilities, leading to improved general perceptual performance.
> - The model can spontaneously select appropriate reasoning depths (Auto-Think mode), demonstrating adaptive cognitive processing for varying task complexities.
