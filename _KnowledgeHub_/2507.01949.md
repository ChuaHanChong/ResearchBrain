# Kwai Keye-VL Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Kuaishou Group's Keye Team developed Kwai Keye-VL, an 8-billion-parameter multimodal foundation model that achieves leading performance in short-video understanding while retaining robust general vision-language capabilities. It significantly outperforms existing models on video-centric benchmarks and a new real-world short-video dataset (KC-MMBench).

## Problem

- Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle to fully comprehend dynamic, information-dense short-form videos, often optimized for static images.
- Current MLLMs typically fail to capture the rich temporal, contextual, and sequential information inherent in video content, including event sequences and audio integration.
- A lack of tailored benchmarks exists for rigorously evaluating MLLMs on real-world short-video scenarios.

## Method

- Introduces Kwai Keye-VL, an 8-billion-parameter MLLM with a native-resolution Vision Transformer using ==2D and 3D Rotary Position Embeddings== for unified processing of text, image, and video.
- Constructs a massive, diverse corpus exceeding **600 billion** tokens, including MLLM-recaptioned images, synthesized OCR data, and extensive video data with interleaved ASR.
- Employs a sophisticated multi-stage training methodology, combining a four-stage progressive pre-training with a two-phase post-training that includes ==Mix-Mode Reinforcement Learning== and Iterative Alignment.

## Results

- Achieves leading performance on short-video benchmarks, including **57.6%** on Video-MMMU (an **8.7%** absolute improvement) and **66.1%** on MMVU.
- Outperforms the second-best model by over **10%** on the newly released Kuaishou Community Multimodal Benchmark (KC-MMBench), scoring **68.03%**, validating its practical value for commercial applications.
- Demonstrates competitive or state-of-the-art results on general vision-language tasks such as MMMU (**71.4%**) and AI2D (**86.7%**), while also showing lower hallucination rates.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A multi-stage training approach combining extensive pre-training and reasoning-focused post-training (including Chain-of-Thought and Reinforcement Learning) effectively cultivates comprehensive multimodal capabilities.
> - Training on Chain-of-Thought reasoning data mutually enhances both reasoning and non-reasoning abilities, leading to improved general perceptual performance.
> - The model can spontaneously select appropriate reasoning depths (Auto-Think mode), demonstrating adaptive cognitive processing for varying task complexities.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) has witnessed rapid advancements, building upon the successes of Large Language Models (LLMs) by extending their formidable linguistic capabilities to the visual domain. Models like GPT-4o, Gemini, LLaVA, Qwen-VL, and InternVL have demonstrated remarkable proficiency in tasks involving static images, such as visual question answering, detailed image captioning, and object grounding.

However, a significant limitation persists in their ability to fully comprehend dynamic, information-dense video content, especially short-form videos which have become a dominant medium on platforms like Kuaishou. Existing MLLMs, primarily optimized for single image-text pairs, often struggle to capture the rich temporal, contextual, and sequential information inherent in videos, including causal relationships, event sequences, and the integration of audio (via ASR). The challenge is not merely recognizing objects but understanding the narrative and interactions over time.

Kwai Keye-VL directly addresses this critical gap. By meticulously engineering an 8-billion-parameter model with a strong emphasis on video data and an innovative training recipe, this work aims to push the boundaries of video understanding in MLLMs. It positions itself as a leader in *short-video understanding*, a niche yet highly impactful area given the prevalence of such content. While maintaining robust general-purpose vision-language abilities, its core differentiator is its specialized focus on and leading performance in video-centric tasks. This work contributes to the evolving MLLM landscape by:
- **Bridging the Static-Dynamic Gap:** Moving MLLMs beyond static image comprehension to tackle the complexities of dynamic video.
- **Emphasizing Short-Form Video:** Recognizing the unique characteristics and commercial importance of this dominant content format, which requires specific design and training considerations.
- **Pioneering Advanced Reasoning in Video:** Introducing sophisticated reasoning capabilities (e.g., Chain-of-Thought, agentic thinking) that are adapted for video contexts, allowing the model to "think" about dynamic patterns.
- **Contribution of Data and Benchmarks:** Developing a massive, high-quality video-centric dataset and releasing a new benchmark (KC-MMBench) tailored for real-world short-video scenarios, thereby fostering further research and providing a more relevant evaluation tool for the community.
- **Industry-Leading Application:** Directly applying state-of-the-art AI research to solve real-world problems for a major social media platform, demonstrating the practical value of advanced MLLMs.

In essence, Kwai Keye-VL signifies a strategic shift in MLLM development towards more comprehensive visual intelligence that can handle the intricacies of the "video era," aiming to set a new standard for models in this domain.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the development of Kwai Keye-VL are clearly articulated throughout the paper:

- **Bridge the Gap in Video Understanding:** The most pressing motivation is to overcome the limitations of existing MLLMs, which perform remarkably on static images but "often fall short in comprehending dynamic, information-dense short-form videos." The paper explicitly aims to "bridge this gap."
- **Achieve Leading-Edge Performance in Short-Video Understanding:** The core goal is to engineer a multimodal foundation model that delivers "leading-edge performance in short-video understanding" specifically. This includes comprehending sequences of events, causal relationships, and overall narratives, integrating information from video frames and audio (via ASR).
- **Maintain Robust General-Purpose Vision-Language Abilities:** While specializing in video, Kwai Keye-VL is designed to simultaneously "maintain robust general-purpose vision-language abilities," ensuring it remains highly competitive across a broad spectrum of image-based tasks.
- **Enable Advanced Reasoning for Dynamic Patterns:** The work is driven by the need for a model that not only "sees" the world but also "thinks" about its dynamic patterns. This involves stimulating and enhancing complex cognitive abilities like Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning and agentic thinking within multimodal contexts.
- **Enhance User Experience and Applications on Video-Centric Platforms:** For Kuaishou Group, the motivation is deeply rooted in practical applications. A more intelligent video understanding model is crucial for "enhancing user experience and enabling more intelligent applications in content creation, recommendation, and e-commerce on video-centric platforms."
- **Address Data and Training Deficiencies:** The authors are motivated to tackle the challenges of constructing massive, high-quality, and diverse multimodal datasets with a strong video emphasis, and to devise an innovative multi-stage training recipe that effectively leverages this data for robust vision-language alignment and advanced reasoning.
- **Contribute a Tailored Benchmark:** Recognizing the limitations of public benchmarks for real-world short-video scenarios, a key objective is to "develop and release the KC-MMBench" to provide a more rigorous and relevant evaluation standard.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The development of Kwai Keye-VL-8B is built upon two core pillars: a massive, high-quality dataset and an innovative multi-stage training recipe, complemented by a robust architecture and training infrastructure.

#### 3.1. Model Architecture

Kwai Keye-VL adopts a classic MLLM architecture comprising three main components:
- **Vision Encoder:** Initializes with `SigLIP-400M-384-14`. A key upgrade is the implementation of a **native-resolution Vision Transformer (ViT)** to preserve image structural integrity and details without complex splicing operations. It extends fixed-length learnable position embeddings into resolution-adaptive ones via interpolation and integrates **2D Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE)** to enhance extrapolation for varying resolutions. NaViT packing with FlashAttention further optimizes ViT training across diverse resolutions. The ViT is continuously pre-trained with SigLIP loss on 500B tokens of open-source and in-house data.
- **MLP Projector:** Randomly initialized and fully pre-trained in Stage 1 to map visual tokens to the language decoder's input space.
- **Language Decoder:** Initializes with `Qwen3-8B`, providing universal world semantic knowledge.
- **Unified Processing for Video:** The model supports a dynamic resolution strategy for video frames (128-768 tokens per frame, max 24576 vision tokens). Crucially, it uses **3D RoPE** for unified processing of text, image, and video information, establishing a one-to-one correspondence between position encoding and absolute time for precise temporal perception.

#### 3.2. Data Construction Strategy

A large-scale, diverse, and high-quality corpus exceeding **600 billion tokens** is assembled from public and proprietary in-house data, with rigorous filtering and deduplication (pHash & minHash) to prevent benchmark leakage. Data categories include:
- **Image Caption Data:** Filtered using CLIP scores, and re-captioned with advanced MLLMs (Qwen2.5-VL 72B, Tarsier2, GPT-4o, Gemini1.5-pro) for precision and fine-grained understanding.
- **OCR & VQA Data:** Collected from open-source datasets (Latex-Formula, hand-write, street views, charts, rich-text) and enhanced with synthesized in-house Chinese data via MLLM generation and font rendering tools for robustness.
- **Grounding & Counting Data:** Utilizes RefCoCo, VisualGenome, TolokaVQA, PixMo, with CLIP-based filtering for accurate point, bounding box, and polygon annotations.
- **Interleaved Text-Image Data:** Large amounts of academic PDF and structured knowledge (STEM) data are processed to enhance multi-modal context modeling, general knowledge preservation, and vision-language alignment through in-context learning.
- **Video Data:** Collected from diverse open-source and large-scale in-house sources, with key pipelines:
 - **Interleaved video-ASR:** Speech-to-text (Qwen2.5-Omni) integrated with visual frames.
 - **Video recaption:** MLLMs generate captions under different FPS settings (0.5/1/2).
 - **Frame-level OCR annotation:** Ensures detail capture in each frame.
 - **Reasoning-enhanced tasks:** Frame-level re-ordering and multiple video matching to grasp temporal progression and semantic connections.

#### 3.3. Training Methodology

The training methodology involves a sophisticated multi-stage approach:

**A. Pre-Training (Four-Stage Progressive Strategy)**
1. **Stage 0 (Vision Encoder Pre-training):** Continuous pre-training of the SigLIP-initialized ViT with native dynamic resolution, 2D RoPE, and NaViT packing using SigLIP contrastive loss, adapting it to the internal data distribution.
2. **Stage 1 (ViT-LLM Alignment):** Freezes ViT and LLM parameters, focuses on optimizing the MLP projection layer with large-scale datasets to establish robust cross-modal feature alignment.
3. **Stage 2 (Multi-task Pre-training):** Unfreezes all parameters for end-to-end optimization on a diverse set of multi-task data (Image Captioning, OCR, Grounding, VQA, interleaved data), enhancing fundamental visual understanding.
4. **Stage 3 (Annealing with Model Merging):** Fine-tunes the model on a curated set of high-quality data to address insufficient exposure from Stage 2. A **homogeneous-heterogeneous merging technique** averages weights of models annealed with different data mixtures to reduce bias and enhance robustness.

**B. Post-Training (Two Phases)**
This phase is designed to cultivate comprehensive capabilities, from foundational performance to sophisticated reasoning.

- **Phase I: No-Reasoning Training (Establishing Foundational Performance)**
 1. **Step I.1: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** Over 5 million multimodal QA samples are used, ensuring task diversity (TaskGalaxy framework), challenge (MLLM-generated reasoning paths filtered by complexity), and reliability (human-annotated captions). Dynamic learning rate with annealing is employed.
 2. **Step I.2: Mixed Preference Optimization (MPO):** Refines performance using a diverse dataset: 400k open-source, 50k re-constructed preference (from MM-RLHF, MMPR), 10k self-improvement (RFT data targeting SFT weaknesses), 90k text-only, and 30k human-annotated samples. MPO algorithm optimizes overall non-reasoning performance.

- **Phase II: Reasoning Training (Core Breakthrough for Complex Cognition)**
 1. **Step II.1: CoT Cold-Start:** Initializes Chain-of-Thought (CoT) capabilities with a mixed dataset (330k non-reasoning, 230k reasoning, 20k automatic reasoning, 100k agentic reasoning, and diverse video data).
 - **Non-Thinking/Instruct data:** Reinforces foundational capabilities.
 - **Thinking data:** Over 70k multimodal samples for complex perception/reasoning (math, science, charts, OCR). High-frequency/commonly traversed thinking paths are constructed using MLLM-sampled CoT, evaluation by another model, frequency scoring, and synthesis.
 - **Auto-Think data:** Trains the model to autonomously determine when to engage its reasoning module ("think + answer" vs. direct answer) based on prompt complexity.
 - **Agentic data:** 100k samples enable "think with image" capabilities via code generation (image operations, calculations) using Qwen 2.5 VL-72B, Gemini-2.5-pro, GPT-4o, and external sandbox validation.
 - **Video data:** Diverse daily, movie, social, gaming, educational videos (24k Thinking, 80k Non-Thinking) with tasks like action classification, temporal relationships, VQA, and frame re-ordering. Individual sampling of 16 and 32 frames per example enhances robustness.
 - **Training strategy:** Mixed training supports `/think`, `/no_think`, `/auto_think`, and `/agentic_think` functionalities.
 2. **Step II.2: Mix-Mode RL:** Employs Reinforcement Learning using the GRPO algorithm to further enhance multimodal perception, reasoning (MMPR, MM-Eureka), text-based mathematical reasoning, and agentic reasoning (DeepEyes). A key focus is on **RL for short video understanding**, using ground-truth/annotated labels to align outputs with desired value orientations and improve video reasoning.
 3. **Step II.3: Iterative Alignment:** Addresses issues like repetitive outputs and flawed reasoning via rejection-sampling data. The Stage II.2 model samples multiple responses, which are scored and ranked using a hybrid system (rule-based for objective qualities like repetition/instruction adherence; model-based for subjective cognitive aspects). MPO algorithm iteratively refines the model's output and its ability to select reasoning modes.

#### 3.4. Training Infrastructure

Optimizations include:
- **Optimized Hybrid Parallelism:** Combines Data Parallelism (DP) with ZeRO optimizer and Sequence Parallelism (SP) for efficient scaling and computation-communication overlap.
- **Dynamic Load Balancing:** Global greedy balancing strategy reassigns samples by FLOPs to minimize hardware idle time for variable input sizes.
- **Sample-level Auto-Resume:** Joint checkpointing of training and I/O states for automatic resumption from interruption, enhancing stability.
- **Post-training Enhancements:** vLLM compatibility for rapid sampling, random dispatch strategy for reward models during RL.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Kwai Keye-VL-8B demonstrates strong performance across various benchmarks, particularly excelling in video understanding and showing competitive results on general vision-language tasks.

#### 4.1. Zero-shot Image Classification of ViT

- The native-resolution ViT, incorporating 2D RoPE, shows competitive performance on ImageNet benchmarks compared to the base SigLIP model. While a simple 1D interpolation caused slight performance degeneration, 2D RoPE largely mitigated this, demonstrating the effectiveness of the spatial encoding enhancement.

#### 4.2. Public Benchmarks Evaluation

- **General Vision-Language Tasks:** Keye-VL (Thinking mode) achieves competitive or state-of-the-art (SOTA) results, surpassing other models on MMMU (71.4%), AI2D (86.7%), ZeroBench (15.2%), and MMVP (79.0%), and showing lower hallucination rates on HallusionBench (67%).
- **Mathematical Reasoning Tasks:** Keye-VL significantly outperforms Qwen2.5-VL 8B and InternVL3-8B, ranking second only to MiMo-VL 7B-RL on most math benchmarks.
- **Auto-Think Mode:** Keye-VL in Auto-Think mode remains highly competitive. It achieves SOTA on MMVP and occasionally surpasses Thinking mode on BLINK and VLMs are Blind, indicating its ability to spontaneously select the appropriate reasoning depth and mitigate over-thinking.
- **Video-Centric Scenarios:** Keye-VL demonstrates superior capabilities. It substantially outperforms other open-source models on public video benchmarks like Video-MMMU (57.6% in thinking mode, an 8.7% absolute improvement) and MMVU (66.1%). On LongVideoBench, Auto-Think mode even surpasses Thinking mode (64.8% vs 62.8%), suggesting efficient information extraction.

#### 4.3. Kuaishou Community Multimodal Benchmark (KC-MMBench)

- A newly developed and open-sourced benchmark tailored for real-world short-video scenarios (e.g., product classification, hot video aggregation, comment understanding).
- Keye-VL-8B achieves an average accuracy of **68.03%** on KC-MMBench, significantly outperforming the second-best MiMo-VL 7B-RL (57.62%), underscoring its practical value for commercial applications.

#### 4.4. Internal Human Evaluations

- **Superior User Experience:** Comprehensive human evaluations against Keye-VL-8B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL3-8B, and MiMo-VL-7B-RL show Keye-VL delivers a superior user experience on both video and image-text tasks.
- **Video Subset Performance:** Keye-VL-8B achieves the highest overall composite score of 3.33 on the video subset. It shows outstanding performance in **Comprehensiveness (4.36)** and **Creativity (3.75)**, reflecting strong video understanding, description, and narrative abilities. It maintains competitive correctness, relevance, and fluency.
- **Image Subset Performance:** Keye-VL-8B also ranks highest on the image subset with a composite score of 3.81, leading in **Correctness (4.05)** and **Comprehensiveness (4.49)**. Its strong performance in Multi-Image Analysis (3.15) is attributed to training on short video data, demonstrating effective generalization of temporal understanding to spatial/logical relationships across static images.
- **Fine-grained Video Capabilities:** Keye-VL-8B achieves top scores in Visual Element Recognition (3.89), Prior Knowledge-Based Question Answering (2.78), and Robustness (3.50), establishing a solid foundation for perceptual and cognitive pathways in video understanding.

#### 4.5. Analysis of Kwai Keye-VL's Limitations

Despite its strengths, the authors identify several limitations:
- **Core Visual Perception:** Non-negligible error rates in OCR (especially Chinese, dense/stylized text) and fine-grained recognition (specific species, clothing details, subtle object variations). Information completeness can also be an issue, occasionally omitting secondary details.
- **Temporal Understanding in Video Processing:** Instability in describing coherent temporal action sequences (coarse vs. fine-grained), weak perception of cinematic language (camera movements), and areas for improvement in precise event localization, temporal ordering, object tracking, and motion trajectories.
- **Higher-Order Cognitive and Reasoning:** Reliability declines on problems requiring rigorous logical chains or complex mathematical calculations. Factual inaccuracies or omissions may occur with specialized domain knowledge (IPs, professional disciplines). Creative generation, while generally good, can be generic for high originality tasks.

#### 4.6. Discussion Insights

- **Mutual Enhancement:** Training on CoT-Mix data (Step II.1) significantly improves both Thinking (8.22% on MMMU) and Non-Thinking (5.67% on MMMU) modes, indicating that exposure to long CoT reasoning data enhances general perceptual and reasoning abilities.
- **Performance Gain from RL:** Mix-Mode RL (Step II.2) leads to a comprehensive and synchronous improvement, with an average gain of 1.44%/2.17% in Non-Thinking/Thinking modes across 10 benchmarks.
- **Auto-Think Mode Analysis:** Keye-VL demonstrates the ability to spontaneously select reasoning modes. It more frequently chooses Thinking mode for challenging tasks like MathVista and MMStar, while largely sticking to Non-Thinking for simpler tasks like OCRBench.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Kwai Keye-VL represents a significant advancement in the field of Multimodal Large Language Models, particularly for its specialized focus on and demonstrated proficiency in short-video understanding. Its potential impact spans both academic research and commercial applications:

#### 5.1. Advancing Multimodal AI Research

- **Pioneering Video-Centric MLLMs:** Keye-VL's dedicated architectural design (3D RoPE for temporal information), massive video-heavy dataset, and multi-stage training recipe (especially CoT Cold-Start, Mix-Mode RL for video) set a new precedent for how MLLMs can be effectively trained for dynamic visual content. It provides a blueprint for overcoming the inherent challenges of video data.
- **Sophisticated Reasoning Capabilities:** The introduction of "mix-mode" training, including "thinking," "non-thinking," "auto-think," and "agentic" modes, along with iterative alignment, significantly enhances the model's cognitive abilities. This allows MLLMs to not only comprehend but also *reason* about complex visual information, and critically, to *decide when and how to reason*. The mutual enhancement between reasoning and non-reasoning data is a valuable insight for future model development.
- **Data Construction and Decontamination:** The paper's meticulous approach to constructing a 600 billion token dataset, including MLLM re-captioning, synthetic data generation for Chinese OCR, and specific video tasks, offers valuable insights for data engineers. The rigorous data decontamination strategies are crucial for ensuring fair evaluation and highlighting potential data leakage issues in existing public datasets.
- **Open-Sourcing Benchmarks:** The development and release of **KC-MMBench** is a significant contribution to the research community. It provides a much-needed benchmark tailored to real-world short-video scenarios, enabling more relevant and robust evaluation of MLLMs beyond traditional image-centric tasks. This encourages further research and development in a commercially vital domain.

#### 5.2. Commercial and Practical Impact (for Kuaishou and beyond)

- **Enhanced User Experience on Video Platforms:** For Kuaishou, a model with superior short-video understanding is transformative. It can lead to more accurate content recommendations, intelligent search capabilities, improved content moderation, and richer interactive experiences for users engaging with short videos.
- **Applications in E-commerce:** The demonstrated ability in tasks like Standard Product Unit (SPU) and Category Property Value (CPV) (part of KC-MMBench) highlights its direct applicability in e-commerce, enabling more precise product attribute extraction, categorization, and comparative analysis from video or image listings.
- **Content Creation and Management:** Keye-VL can assist content creators with automated captioning, summarization, and content analysis, potentially streamlining workflows and enhancing content quality.
- **New Interaction Paradigms:** The "think with image" and "auto-think" capabilities pave the way for more intuitive and intelligent multimodal assistants that can adapt their reasoning process to the complexity of the user's query and the visual content.

#### 5.3. Future Directions Identified

The authors acknowledge limitations, which also point to key areas for future research:
- **Video Encoder Optimization:** Further architectural optimization for video encoders and more efficient video encoding strategies are needed to maximize temporal understanding.
- **Refining "Think with Image" and Perception:** While advanced, the "think with image" ability is still preliminary and fine-grained perception (e.g., OCR, specific object differentiation) needs improvement. This suggests a need for more nuanced visual reasoning and higher-fidelity visual processing.
- **Robust Reward Modeling:** The reliance on MLLMs as direct reward models for RL introduces reliability and computational cost limitations. Developing more reliable and efficient reward modeling strategies is an open research question with broad implications for RL-based MLLM training.

In conclusion, Kwai Keye-VL is a pioneering effort in pushing MLLMs into the dynamic realm of video. Its comprehensive methodology and impressive performance, especially in short-video understanding, mark a significant step towards more intelligent and context-aware multimodal AI, with direct and substantial implications for the burgeoning video-centric digital landscape.
