# Stable Virtual Camera: Generative View Synthesis with Diffusion Models

> [!summary] Summary
> A generalist diffusion model, STABLE VIRTUAL CAMERA (SEVA), synthesizes realistic and 3D-consistent novel views from flexible input configurations and arbitrary camera trajectories. It establishes new state-of-the-art performance across 10 diverse datasets, generating high-quality, temporally smooth videos up to 30 seconds without requiring intermediate 3D representation distillation.

## Problem

- Existing generative novel view synthesis methods often struggle to simultaneously achieve large viewpoint changes and maintain temporal smoothness in generated sequences.
- Many NVS models are constrained by rigid input/target view configurations, limiting flexibility for real-world scenarios.
- Ensuring 3D consistency typically requires complex post-processing steps like NeRF distillation, adding computational overhead.

## Method

- **SEVA** is a ==multi-view diffusion model== built on ==Stable Diffusion 2.1==, incorporating ==3D and 1D self-attention== to process multiple camera views.
- A ==two-stage curriculum training== recipe enabling the model to handle ==large context windows== and various input-output view configurations.
- A flexible ==procedural two-pass sampling== strategy for inference, which manages diverse input/output frame counts and uses a ==memory bank== for long-range consistency.

## Results

- Achieves new state-of-the-art performance across **10** diverse datasets, including quantitative gains of **+6.0 dB PSNR** on LLFF (P=3) and **+0.6 dB PSNR** over CAT3D on Mip360 (P=3).
- Generates high-quality, photorealistic novel view videos up to **30 seconds** with minimal temporal flickering, maintaining 3D consistency across various camera movements.
- Generated views, when distilled into 3D Gaussian Splatting, result in higher 3D reconstruction quality (higher PSNR, lower LPIPS) compared to baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A diffusion-based approach can synthesize 3D-consistent novel views and smooth trajectories without requiring explicit 3D representation distillation.
> - Flexible procedural sampling strategies, including a memory bank of spatially nearest anchors, are critical for maintaining consistency over long and complex camera paths.
> - The model exhibits robust zero-shot generalization to varying input view counts (especially semi-dense) and different image resolutions/aspect ratios.
