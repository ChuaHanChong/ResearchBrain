# Grounding Everything in Tokens for Multimodal Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> GETok enhances Multimodal Large Language Models' 2D spatial reasoning by introducing a lexical spatial representation with learnable grid and offset tokens for native object grounding. The method achieves state-of-the-art performance in referring expression segmentation and demonstrates improvements in complex real-world driving tasks.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs struggle with precise 2D spatial grounding due to the loss of fine-grained spatial information during image tokenization.
- Current spatial representations (text-based, patch-based, 1D bin-based) are inefficient, ambiguous, lack unified handling for various formats, and often require architectural modifications.
- MLLMs typically make one-shot spatial predictions without an inherent mechanism for iterative self-correction, limiting precision in complex scenarios.

## Method

- GETok augments MLLM vocabulary with learnable ==Grid Tokens== for coarse spatial anchoring and ==Offset Tokens== for fine-grained, iterative refinement and self-correction, directly integrating into autoregressive architectures.
- A novel ==greedy mask-to-token conversion algorithm== and an offset-aware dataset construction method are used for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) without altering the MLLM architecture.
- A self-improving Reinforcement Learning (RL) framework employs ==two-stage GRPO optimization== with ==geometry-aware reward functions== to leverage GETok's structured 2D lattice for stable policy learning and efficient exploration.

## Results

- GETok-RL achieved state-of-the-art performance in Referring Expression Segmentation, showing a **+4.5%** gain over GETok-SFT on ReasonSeg and RefCOCO datasets, with offset tokens improving performance by **+1.0%** (SFT) and **+1.5%** (RL).
- In a real-world driving case study, GETok outperformed traditional visual prompts, improving traffic sign color recognition by **+12.24%** and achieving a **+10%** F1-score gain for curved lane polyline detection over coordinate-based methods.
- A **32x32** grid with offset tokens achieved comparable or superior performance to a **64x64** grid while significantly reducing token length and vocabulary overhead, demonstrating the efficiency of the offset mechanism.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - GETok's 2D grid tokens natively encode spatial topology, outperforming 1D text coordinates and bin tokens by enabling smoother, topology-aware attention maps and reducing format brittleness.
> - Offset tokens provide fine-grained precision and iterative self-correction with minimal vocabulary overhead, achieving performance comparable to higher-resolution grids at a significantly reduced computational cost.
> - The structured 2D lattice of GETok creates a low-entropy action space, making it highly amenable to GRPO, leading to accelerated convergence and consistently higher reward levels in RL for spatial reasoning tasks.
