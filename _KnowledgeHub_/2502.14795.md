# Humanoid-VLA: Towards Universal Humanoid Control with Visual Integration

> [!summary] Summary
> This paper introduces Humanoid-VLA, the first Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model designed for humanoid robots, enabling autonomous interaction and context-aware motion generation by integrating language, egocentric vision, and control. It addresses data scarcity through a self-supervised data augmentation strategy, outperforming prior text-to-motion models in quality and diversity, and achieving high success rates in real-world object interaction tasks on a Unitree G1 robot.

## Problem

- Current humanoid control frameworks are largely reactive, responding to explicit external inputs and lacking autonomous interaction capabilities.
- Existing methods often rely on third-person observations or lack integrated egocentric visual context, limiting context-aware motion generation from the robot's own perspective.
- The development of universal humanoid control models, especially those integrating vision and language, faces significant data scarcity of synchronized first-person visual, textual, and whole-body motion data.

## Method

- Proposes **Humanoid-VLA**, the first ==Vision-Language-Action (VLA)== model for humanoids, built on a two-stage framework: ==Language-Motion Pre-Alignment== and ==Vision-Conditioned Fine-Tuning==.
- Introduces a ==self-supervised data augmentation== strategy using ==compositional motion quantization== and automatic instructional prompt generation to generate large-scale pseudo-annotated training data.
- Integrates the VLA model's generated kinematic motion sequences with a ==goal-conditioned Reinforcement Learning (RL)== policy to control a humanoid robot with physically plausible joint torques.

## Results

- Humanoid-VLA outperformed baselines in text-to-motion synthesis, achieving an FID of **0.467** on HumanML3D (**47.5%** improvement over MDM) and **4.466** motion diversity on Humanoid-S (**6%** better than MDM).
- The integrated RL policy achieved robust motion imitation in physics simulations with consistently low errors, including mean position errors below **40mm** and pose accuracy at **1.18mm**.
- Demonstrated strong performance on a real Unitree G1 robot with high success rates (e.g., **10/10** for "Turn to an object," **9/10** for "Hold an object," "Kick a ball") in various real-world object interaction tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A self-supervised data augmentation pipeline is effective in significantly strengthening motion-language alignment and addressing data scarcity for humanoid VLA models.
> - Integrating egocentric visual perception allows humanoids to move beyond reactive control, enabling autonomous interaction and context-aware motion generation.
> - Combining large language models for motion generation with a goal-conditioned RL policy for whole-body control leads to robust and physically consistent humanoid behaviors.
