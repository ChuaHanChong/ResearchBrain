# WorldCompass: Reinforcement Learning for Long-Horizon World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> WorldCompass is a reinforcement learning post-training framework that significantly improves interactive video-based world models' ability to accurately follow explicit actions and maintain visual quality over long sequences. It boosts composite action accuracy from approximately 20% to 55% and enhances overall visual fidelity across various generative tasks.

## Problem

- Existing video-based world models learn action-following implicitly from pixel-level supervision during pre-training, leading to difficulties in accurately executing complex or long-horizon explicit interaction signals.
- Current reinforcement learning (RL) frameworks for generative models are not directly applicable to autoregressive, interactive, and long-horizon video generation due to fundamental architectural differences.
- Models often struggle with maintaining long-term consistency, preventing drift, and accumulating errors when responding to complex action sequences, which limits their utility for embodied AI and interactive applications.

## Method

- Introduces **WorldCompass**, an RL post-training framework featuring a novel clip-level rollout strategy for efficient and fine-grained feedback in autoregressive video generation, mitigating sparse reward signals.
- Employs two complementary reward functions: an Interaction Following Score (IF Score) that measures action adherence using 3D foundation models, and a Visual Quality Score (VQ Score) based on human preference models (HPSv3) to ensure both accuracy and aesthetics.
- Utilizes an efficient RL optimization algorithm inspired by DiffusionNFT, with negative-aware fine-tuning and practical optimizations like timestep subsetting, Best-of-N sample selection, and progressive clip length training to enhance computational feasibility.

## Results

- Achieved substantial gains in interaction following accuracy for complex composite actions, improving from approximately **20%** to **55%** in base world models.
- Demonstrated consistent enhancements in HPSv3 visual quality scores and improved spatial geometric consistency across various scenarios and generation lengths (up to 381 frames).
- Showed strong generalizability across different base model variants (HunyuanVideo-1.5-8B and Wan2.2-5B) and video horizons, validating the robustness and broad applicability of the WorldCompass framework.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The proposed clip-level rollout strategy is crucial for providing dense, consistent feedback, leading to substantial improvements in interaction following for long-horizon video generation compared to sparse, sequence-level rollouts.
> - Combining complementary reward functions for interaction fidelity and visual quality is essential to prevent reward hacking and ensure balanced, stable improvements, as optimizing for one objective alone can degrade the other.
> - Sampling rollout data from different initial noises, as utilized by the DiffusionNFT-inspired approach, provides the necessary diversity for learning robust action control, outperforming methods that rely on SDE sampling from the same initial noise.
