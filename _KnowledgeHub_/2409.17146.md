# Molmo and PixMo: Open Weights and Open Data for State-of-the-Art Vision-Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This work introduces Molmo, a family of state-of-the-art open-weight Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and PixMo, a comprehensive suite of VLM-independent training datasets. Molmo-72B achieves leading performance on academic benchmarks and human preference evaluations, demonstrating that high-performing VLMs can be built entirely from truly open data and models without relying on proprietary systems for data generation.

## Problem

- Many open-weight Vision-Language Models (VLMs) rely heavily on synthetic data generated by proprietary VLMs, hindering true scientific understanding and reproducibility of VLM development.
- Collecting large-scale, high-quality multimodal training data is expensive and difficult, often leading to reliance on less transparent or less effective methods.
- Lack of truly open, foundational VLM resources creates barriers to independent research and development outside of large tech companies.

## Method

- Develops ==Molmo==, a family of open-weight VLMs with a streamlined architecture featuring a pre-trained vision encoder, an LLM, and an optimized connector using ==overlapping multi-crop vision input==.
- Introduces ==PixMo==, a novel suite of seven multimodal datasets collected entirely without relying on proprietary VLMs, utilizing innovative methods like oral descriptions for dense captions.
- Employs a simplified two-stage training pipeline (pre-training on dense captions, fine-tuning on a mix of PixMo and academic datasets) with specific optimizations like ==text-only dropout==.

## Results

- Molmo-72B achieves state-of-the-art academic benchmark scores among all tested models and ranks **2nd** in human preference (Elo score) just behind GPT-4o.
- Molmo models significantly outperform other VLMs, including proprietary ones, on counting tasks (e.g., CountBenchQA) and an in-the-wild clock reading benchmark.
- The most efficient model, MolmoE-1B, based on a **1B-7B** mixture-of-experts LLM, approaches the performance of GPT-4V on academic benchmarks and human preference.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality human-annotated data, collected without reliance on proprietary VLMs, performs comparably to data generated by powerful commercial models for VLM training.
> - Architectural choices like overlapping multi-crops for vision input and text-only dropout during pre-training significantly improve VLM performance.
> - Targeted, VLM-independent synthetic data can effectively imbue models with robust, specialized skills, as demonstrated by leading performance in clock reading and counting.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) has seen explosive growth, with proprietary models like OpenAI's GPT-4o, Google's Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Sonnet leading in performance and capabilities. These closed-source models offer impressive image description, complex visual question answering, and multimodal reasoning. However, their proprietary nature—lack of publicly released model weights, training data, or code—creates significant barriers to scientific understanding, reproducibility, and independent research.

In response, the research community has developed numerous open-weight models, such as LLaVA, Qwen2-VL, and InternVL2. While these models make their weights public, they often suffer from a critical limitation: their competitive performance heavily relies on **synthetic data generated by proprietary VLMs**. For instance, models are frequently trained on datasets like ShareGPT4V, which uses GPT-4V to create detailed image captions. This practice, often referred to as "distillation," means that even supposedly "open" models are effectively dependent on and derive their capabilities from closed-source systems. This creates a circular dependency, preventing the community from gaining foundational knowledge about *how to build performant VLMs from scratch* without relying on proprietary "black box" systems.

Molmo and PixMo directly address this critical gap. The authors explicitly state their work aims to provide "foundational knowledge about how to build performant VLMs from scratch." By releasing Molmo's model weights, training code, and, crucially, **PixMo datasets collected *without* the use of external VLMs (especially proprietary ones)**, this research offers a truly independent and reproducible pathway for VLM development. This is a significant departure from the prevailing trend of VLM distillation and represents a major contribution to open science and the democratization of advanced AI research. It allows researchers to explore the underlying principles of VLM training, data biases, and architectural choices in a transparent and verifiable manner.

Furthermore, the paper tackles the well-known challenge of collecting high-quality, large-scale multimodal data. Such data is expensive and difficult to obtain through traditional crowdsourcing methods. PixMo's innovative data collection strategies demonstrate novel approaches to acquiring detailed, diverse annotations efficiently and without relying on generative AI, paving the way for future open datasets.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the Molmo and PixMo project are multifaceted, driven by a commitment to open science and addressing critical limitations in current VLM development:

- **To Develop State-of-the-Art Open VLMs:** The overarching goal is to create a family of Vision-Language Models (Molmo) that achieve competitive performance—ideally state-of-the-art within their class—while being fully open in terms of model weights, training data, and source code.
- **To Foster Foundational Understanding:** A core motivation is to provide the scientific community with the necessary knowledge and resources to understand *how to build high-performing VLMs from scratch*. This directly counters the prevailing trend where many strong "open" models rely on synthetic data generated by proprietary VLMs, which hinders true scientific exploration and reproducibility.
- **To Create High-Quality, VLM-Independent Open Data:** A key objective is the collection and release of a novel suite of multimodal datasets (PixMo). Crucially, these datasets are designed to be high-quality, diverse, and collected entirely *without* relying on proprietary or other external VLMs for data generation, ensuring their foundational integrity. This includes:
 - **Detailed Image Captions (PixMo-Cap):** For robust pre-training.
 - **Free-Form Image Q&A (PixMo-AskModelAnything):** For comprehensive fine-tuning.
 - **Innovative 2D Pointing Data (PixMo-Points):** To enable grounding, counting, and visual explanation capabilities.
 - **VLM-Independent Synthetic Data:** To augment skills without proprietary dependencies (e.g., PixMo-Docs, PixMo-Clocks).
- **To Achieve Competitive Performance with Proprietary Models:** The authors aim to demonstrate that models trained exclusively on truly open data can rival or even surpass the performance of larger, proprietary VLMs and existing open models that rely on distillation. This validates the effectiveness of their open approach and the quality of PixMo data.
- **To Enable New Capabilities and Research Directions:** By introducing novel data types like 2D pointing, the project seeks to unlock new VLM capabilities (e.g., precise spatial grounding, chain-of-thought counting, agent control) and encourage future research into these areas.
- **To Provide Extensive Ablation Studies:** The project aims to rigorously evaluate various model and data design choices, offering valuable insights to the broader community on how these factors influence VLM performance, thereby accelerating collective understanding.

In essence, the motivation is to democratize cutting-edge VLM research by providing truly open, high-quality foundational components that allow for independent scientific inquiry, rather than merely replicating or distilling capabilities from closed systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The Molmo and PixMo project employs a comprehensive methodology encompassing novel data collection, a streamlined model architecture, and a carefully tuned training pipeline.

#### 3.1. Data Collection (PixMo Datasets)

The cornerstone of this work is PixMo, a collection of seven datasets (three human-annotated, four synthetically generated without VLM assistance) designed to provide high-quality, VLM-independent training data:

- **PixMo-Cap (Dense Captions for Pre-training):**
 - **Challenge:** Directly asking annotators for 200+ word captions often yields poor quality, is time-consuming, and risks VLM-copying.
 - **Innovation:** Annotators describe images orally for 60-90 seconds (modality switching trick). This produces more detailed descriptions faster and provides an audio receipt to verify human annotation.
 - **Processing:** Audio is transcribed by a standard speech-to-text system. A *language-only* LLM then summarizes/enhances transcripts, ensuring no VLM generates visual content.
 - **Scale:** 712k images with 1.3M transcripts/captions (average 196 words).

- **PixMo-AskModelAnything (Free-form Image Q&A for Fine-tuning):**
 - **Innovation:** Human annotators interactively edit responses generated by a *language-only* LLM. The LLM's input for answering is derived from non-VLM OCR and the dense captions from PixMo-Cap.
 - **Purpose:** Ensures high-quality, accurate, and free-form responses for diverse real-world questions.
 - **Scale:** 162k Q&A pairs on 73k images.

- **PixMo-Points (2D Pointing Data for Fine-tuning):**
 - **Innovation:** Annotators identify objects/expressions by pointing at them and then point to *each instance* in the image. This is significantly faster than bounding boxes/segmentation masks.
 - **Capabilities Enabled:** Grounds language in images with 2D points, enables counting by pointing, and supports visual explanations.
 - **Scale:** Over 2.3 million grounding annotations across 223k images. Includes "not present" cases.

- **VLM-Independent Synthetic Datasets (Complementary Skills):**
 - **PixMo-CapQA:** 214k Q&A pairs generated by a *language-only* LLM prompted to ask/answer questions based *only* on ground-truth captions from PixMo-Cap.
 - **PixMo-Docs:** 2.3M Q&A pairs for charts, documents, tables, and diagrams. Generated by prompting a *language-only* LLM to create code (e.g., Matplotlib, LaTeX) that renders these visuals. The LLM then generates Q&A from privileged access to the *code*, not the image.
 - **PixMo-Clocks:** 826k examples of synthetic clocks (real watch bodies + diverse watch faces) matched with time-telling Q&A.
 - **PixMo-Count:** 36k counting Q&A pairs using a standard *non-VLM* object detector to identify objects, with manual verification for validation/test sets.

#### 3.2. Model Architecture (Molmo)

Molmo follows a standard yet optimized VLM design, connecting a pre-trained vision encoder to a decoder-only LLM via a connector:

- **Components:**
 - **Vision Encoder:** Primarily OpenAI's ViT-L/14 336px CLIP, but also tested with SigLIP and fully open MetaCLIP.
 - **LLM:** Family of models using various scales and openness levels: OLMoE-1B-7B (efficient), OLMo-7B, Qwen2 7B, and Qwen2 72B.
 - **Connector:** Pools and projects patch features into the LLM's embedding space.

- **Key Architectural Innovations:**
 - **Overlapping Multi-Crop Strategy:** Divides images into multiple square crops (e.g., 12 crops for high-resolution tasks) that *overlap*. This ensures central patches have context from neighboring areas, mitigating context loss at crop borders (Figure 3).
 - **Vision-Language Connector:** Concatenates features from the third-to-last and tenth-from-last ViT layers. Uses multi-headed attention pooling (mean of patches as query) to aggregate 2x2 patch windows into single vectors, outperforming simple stacking.
 - **Arranging Vision Tokens:** Sequenced left-to-right, top-to-bottom (low-res full image first, then high-res crops), with special tokens for start/end of sequences and row transitions.
 - **Text-only Dropout:** Applied to LLM's text tokens during pre-training (not vision or prompt tokens). This encourages reliance on the visual input, improving captioning and downstream performance.

#### 3.3. Training Pipeline

- **Simplified Two-Stage Training:**
 - **Pre-training:** Trained on PixMo-Cap (dense captions and transcripts). Unlike many prior works, Molmo *does not* include a separate, explicit stage for tuning only the vision-language connector on web-scale data. Instead, it applies a higher learning rate with a shorter warmup for connector parameters, allowing them to adjust quickly.
 - **Fine-tuning:** Trained on a mix of all PixMo datasets and existing academic datasets (VQA v2.0, TextVQA, ChartQA, etc.). Datasets are sampled proportionally to the square root of their size, with pointing data significantly up-weighted due to slower learning.
- **Prompting and Style Tags:** Task-specific style tags (e.g., "vqa2:") are used for academic datasets to elicit desired answer styles, preventing them from influencing free-form user interactions. PixMo-AskModelAnything, -CapQA, -Points, -Count, and -Cap do not use style tags.
- **Multi-Annotated Images Efficiency:** For images with multiple annotations, all text tokens for an image are arranged in one long sequence. Attention masks ensure tokens for each annotation attend to image tokens and each other, but not to other annotations. This avoids redundant image encoding, significantly reducing training time.
- **Pointing Output Format:** Model outputs points as plain-text (x,y) coordinates normalized between 0-100, ordered top-down, left-to-right, and numbered for counting. This enables a unique chain-of-thought counting approach.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The Molmo project demonstrates significant achievements in developing highly capable, truly open VLMs, with comprehensive evaluation across academic benchmarks and human preference studies.

#### 4.1. Overall Performance and Competitiveness

- **State-of-the-Art Open Models:** The Molmo family emerges as state-of-the-art within the class of open-weight and open-data models.
- **Rivaling Proprietary Models:**
 - The best-in-class **Molmo-72B** (based on Qwen2 72B) achieves the highest academic benchmark score among all models tested, and ranks second in human preference (Elo score), just behind GPT-4o.
 - It outperforms several large proprietary models, including Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro and Flash.
 - The most efficient model, **MolmoE-1B**, based on the OLMoE-1B-7B mixture-of-experts LLM, surprisingly nearly matches GPT-4V on both academic benchmarks and human preference.
 - Molmo-7B-O (OLMo-7B based) and Molmo-7B-D (Qwen2 7B based) comfortably perform between GPT-4V and GPT-4o.

#### 4.2. Specific Strengths and Capabilities

- **Counting:** Molmo models significantly lead all other evaluated models (both open and proprietary) on counting tasks (CountBenchQA and PixMo-Count), a direct result of the novel PixMo-Points data and the model's chain-of-thought point-and-count ability.
- **Real-World QA and Captioning:** Molmo excels at answering questions about natural images, matching or outperforming all models on the zero-shot RealWorldQA benchmark and achieving state-of-the-art results on the highly competitive VQA v2.0.
- **Clock Reading:** Molmo models (MolmoE-1B, Molmo-7B-O, Molmo-7B-D, Molmo-72B) dramatically outperform other VLMs, including proprietary ones, on an in-the-wild clock reading benchmark, despite being trained solely on synthetic PixMo-Clocks data.
- **Pointing:** Molmo models at all scales demonstrate strong performance on a new pointing benchmark developed using SAM.
- **OCR-centric tasks:** While surpassing other open models and some proprietary ones, Molmo slightly trails Qwen2-VL on benchmarks like ChartQA, DocVQA, InfographicVQA, and TextVQA.
- **Reasoning tasks:** Molmo lags on more complex reasoning tasks (MMMU, MathVista), suggesting its current training mix could benefit from more reasoning-focused data.

#### 4.3. Ablation Study Insights (Key Findings)

- **Vision Encoder Choice:** Fully open MetaCLIP performs similarly to proprietary CLIP and SigLIP, indicating the feasibility of building entirely open VLMs without sacrificing performance on the vision backbone. Even DINOv2 (image-only pre-trained) is competitive.
- **Data Quality (PixMo-Cap):** Scaling PixMo-Cap data linearly improves captioning and downstream task performance. Critically, human-annotated PixMo-Cap data performs comparably to captions generated by GPT-4o on the *same set of images*, demonstrating the high quality of human data collection without VLM distillation. Adding noisy web-scale data (LAION) did not improve performance.
- **Fine-tuning Data Impact:** PixMo supervised fine-tuning datasets significantly improve benchmark performance beyond just academic datasets, particularly for document-heavy and counting tasks. Human evaluation also confirms PixMo-Cap and PixMo-AskModelAnything are vital for user preferred responses.
- **Architectural Choices:**
 - Overlapping crops (Figure 3) yield significant improvements by preventing context loss.
 - Text-only dropout during pre-training improves captioning by encouraging reliance on visual tokens.
 - Length conditioning in captions enhances pre-training quality and downstream results.
 - Attention pooling for patch features outperforms simple stacking.
- **Counting Strategy:** The chain-of-thought "point-then-count" strategy performs significantly better than direct counting. Training with spatially ordered points (top-down, left-to-right) is crucial. Plain-text coordinates for points work best.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The Molmo and PixMo project represents a monumental step forward for the Vision-Language Model research community, offering significance across several dimensions:

#### 5.1. Advancing Open Science and Reproducibility

- **Breaking the Distillation Cycle:** Molmo is arguably the first state-of-the-art VLM that provides not just open weights and code, but also *truly open training data collected without any reliance on proprietary VLMs or their distilled synthetic data*. This breaks a pervasive dependency in the field, allowing for genuinely independent and foundational research into VLM capabilities.
- **Full Transparency:** The release of model weights, comprehensive training code, and all underlying datasets enables full reproducibility and detailed scientific scrutiny of how high-performing VLMs are built. This fosters trust, accelerates error identification, and facilitates community-driven innovation.

#### 5.2. Democratization of VLM Research

- **Lowering Entry Barriers:** By making high-quality models and data freely accessible, Molmo and PixMo significantly lower the barrier to entry for researchers, academics, and smaller institutions who lack the vast resources of large tech companies to collect such data or develop competitive proprietary models. This fosters a more inclusive and diverse research landscape.
- **Enabling New Research Avenues:** With a fully open stack, researchers can now more easily experiment with VLM architectures, explore different pre-training and fine-tuning strategies, and investigate VLM biases and safety aspects without commercial restrictions.

#### 5.3. Novel Data Collection Methodologies

- **Innovation in Data Sourcing:** PixMo introduces and validates innovative, scalable, and cost-effective data collection techniques. The "modality switching trick" for dense captioning (speech-to-text), human-in-the-loop LLM editing for Q&A, and efficient 2D pointing annotation offer valuable blueprints for future open dataset initiatives. These methods provide high-quality data while mitigating the risks of VLM-generated hallucinations and ensuring human grounding.
- **Addressing Data Scarcity:** The creation of PixMo addresses a critical need for diverse, high-quality multimodal training data that does not rely on proprietary models for its generation, inspiring further efforts in this crucial area.

#### 5.4. Expanding VLM Capabilities and Applications

- **Enhanced Grounding and Counting:** The novel PixMo-Points dataset and its integration allow Molmo to achieve leading performance in spatial grounding and precise counting, enabling more natural and effective human-VLM interaction.
- **Foundations for AI Agents:** The ability to point and ground language in images has profound implications for developing more capable AI agents, such as robots interacting with physical environments or web agents navigating user interfaces. Molmo's success on AndroidControl points to this potential.
- **Specialized Expertise:** Molmo's exceptional performance on niche tasks like clock reading (due to PixMo-Clocks) demonstrates the power of targeted, VLM-independent synthetic data to imbue models with specific, robust skills.

#### 5.5. Providing Empirical Insights for VLM Design

- **Comprehensive Ablations:** The extensive ablation studies offer invaluable empirical insights into which architectural components, training strategies, and data characteristics contribute most significantly to VLM performance. This includes findings on multi-crop strategies, dropout mechanisms, length conditioning, and the critical role of human-annotated data. These findings serve as practical guidelines for future VLM development.

In summary, Molmo and PixMo represent a landmark contribution by pushing the boundaries of open-source AI. It not only delivers a highly competitive VLM but, more importantly, provides a complete, transparent, and reproducible pipeline that liberates VLM research from its current dependence on proprietary systems, truly accelerating the scientific understanding and democratic development of multimodal AI.
