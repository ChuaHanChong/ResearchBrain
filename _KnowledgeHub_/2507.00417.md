# ASTRO: Teaching Language Models to Reason by Reflecting and Backtracking In-Context

> [!summary] Summary
> AI at Meta and University of Washington researchers introduce ASTRO, a three-stage framework that teaches large language models to reason by reflecting and backtracking in-context. It instills search-like reasoning via synthetic data generation, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning, leading to absolute performance gains of up to 26.9% on challenging mathematical reasoning benchmarks.

## Problem

- Open-source large language models (LLMs) often lack the advanced search-like reasoning capabilities (e.g., self-reflection, backtracking) observed in state-of-the-art proprietary models.
- Current methods for enhancing LLM reasoning often rely on distillation from already strong models or external inference-time scaffolds, rather than enabling LLMs to internalize and generate complex search procedures themselves.
- It has been unclear how to robustly enhance the reasoning capabilities of general-purpose LLMs, such as the Llama 3 family, which may not inherently demonstrate such advanced behaviors.

## Method

- Generates a synthetic dataset of high-quality 'long Chain-of-Thoughts' (CoTs) that encode diverse search behaviors, including ==self-reflection and backtracking==, derived from ==Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) trajectories== and linearized into natural language.
- Applies Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) to a target LLM (Llama-3.1-70B-instruct) on this curated dataset for a single epoch to instill ==autoregressive search priors==.
- Utilizes a modified ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== for Reinforcement Learning (RL) to further refine and optimize the SFT-tuned model's reasoning capabilities, leveraging verifiable binary rewards.

## Results

- Llama-3.1-70B-ASTRO-RL achieved significant absolute performance gains on mathematical benchmarks: **81.8%** on MATH-500 (**+16.0%** over baseline), **64.4%** on AMC 2023 (**+26.9%**), and **30.0%** on AIME 2024 (**+20.0%**).
- ASTRO-RL models consistently outperformed strong baselines, including Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct and other Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct variants fine-tuned with Step-KTO and SPOC.
- Control experiments confirmed that explicitly infused search priors from SFT are essential, as ASTRO-RL models consistently and significantly outperformed 'Direct-RL' models across all benchmarks, which lacked these priors.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly teaching search priors, such as self-reflection and backtracking, during Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) is crucial for the efficacy and performance ceiling of subsequent Reinforcement Learning (RL) in reasoning tasks.
> - The models learn to generate longer, more detailed Chain-of-Thought (CoT) traces and increase their frequency of backtracking during RL training, which directly correlates with improved problem-solving performance.
> - The framework enables LLMs to internalize complex search procedures, allowing them to autoregressively generate full search trajectories, including recoveries from errors.
