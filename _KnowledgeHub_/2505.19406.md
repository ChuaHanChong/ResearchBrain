# Unveiling the Compositional Ability Gap in Vision-Language Reasoning Model

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and Tencent Hunyuan Research systematically investigated and addressed the compositional ability gap in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) using ComPABench, a novel diagnostic benchmark. Their proposed RL-Ground training strategy, which incorporates visual captioning and fine-grained progress rewards, improved multimodal compositional task performance to 52.8% for a 7B model and demonstrated robust out-of-distribution generalization.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle to truly compose different skills to solve complex, multi-step problems requiring both visual perception and reasoning.
- There is a fundamental limitation in current VLMs regarding cross-modal composition (transferring text-learned reasoning to visual inputs) and cross-task integration (combining independently learned visual reasoning skills).
- Current VLM training paradigms often fail to ensure compositional abilities generalize to novel, out-of-distribution task objectives, indicating a lack of true understanding.

## Method

- Development of **ComPABench**, a ==diagnostic benchmark== with precisely controlled synthetic tasks (geometric, spatial, and compositional) in both pure-text and multimodal formats to systematically evaluate compositional abilities.
- Introduction of RL-Ground, a reinforcement learning approach that integrates 'Caption-Before-Thinking' to promote visual-to-text alignment and 'Fine-Grained Progress Reward' to provide dense supervision at intermediate reasoning steps.
- Comparison of RL-Ground against Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and standard Reinforcement Learning (RL) approaches using Qwen2.5-VL models to assess their impact on compositional reasoning.

## Results

- A dramatic cross-modal performance gap was observed: models trained purely on text achieved near-perfect accuracy (**99.8%**), but performance dropped catastrophically (to **4.2%**) when tested on corresponding multimodal tasks.
- The proposed RL-Ground method improved multimodal compositional task performance to **52.8%** for a **7B** model, significantly outperforming standard SFT (as low as **0.6%**) and other RL methods (**31.2%**).
- RL-Ground consistently maintained high performance across out-of-distribution conditions, demonstrating robust generalization to tasks with altered objectives, unlike SFT which showed brittleness.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A substantial 'modality gap' exists, indicating that reasoning abilities acquired from text-only training do not automatically transfer to visual inputs in current Vision-Language Models.
> - Standard Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) can severely impair compositional abilities through catastrophic forgetting, despite improving performance on individual tasks.
> - Reinforcement Learning (RL), specifically with fine-grained progress rewards and explicit visual grounding (e.g., captioning before thinking), demonstrates effectiveness in improving multimodal compositional reasoning and out-of-distribution generalization.
