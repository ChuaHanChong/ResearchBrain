# PhysVLM: Enabling Visual Language Models to Understand Robotic Physical Reachability

> [!summary] Summary
> PhysVLM integrates robotic physical reachability into Visual Language Models (VLMs) using a novel Space-Physical Reachability Map (S-P Map) and a dual-branch architecture. The model achieves enhanced performance in embodied visual reasoning and task planning by enabling robots to consider physical constraints, outperforming baseline models on new benchmarks.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) deployed in robotics often produce impractical or unsafe actions due to a lack of intrinsic understanding of a robot's physical reachability.
- Existing approaches for understanding physical constraints are often task-specific or focus on environmental modeling, lacking a unified, generalizable integration with VLMs for diverse robotic platforms.
- A significant gap exists in large-scale datasets that explicitly incorporate robotic physical parameters to train VLMs for physical reachability understanding.

## Method

- Proposed the ==Space-Physical Reachability Map== (S-P Map), a robot-agnostic, unified spatial representation that abstracts complex robot kinematics into a visually interpretable format.
- Developed **PhysVLM**, a dual-branch VLM architecture that processes egocentric RGB images and S-P Maps through separate encoders, integrating both visual and physical reachability features into a language decoder.
- Constructed Phys100K, a large-scale multi-robot dataset for physical reachability question-answering, and introduced EQA-phys, a new benchmark for evaluating physical reachability in embodied QA tasks.

## Results

- PhysVLM-3B achieved an average score of **71.0%** on the EQA-phys benchmark, outperforming GPT-4o by **14%** (**57.0%**) and other embodied VLMs by 17-**20%**.
- The model demonstrated strong zero-shot generalization, scoring over **63%** on real-world UR3 and XArm6 robots, indicating the S-P Map's effectiveness in transferring reachability understanding to unseen platforms.
- In real-world task planning scenarios with unreachable objects, PhysVLM-3B achieved a **48.4%** success rate, significantly outperforming baselines (**21.5%** to **35.8%**) by planning to approach initially unreachable objects.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The S-P Map effectively abstracts complex robot kinematics into a generalized, image-like representation, allowing VLMs to reason about physical reachability independently of specific robot configurations.
> - Integrating physical reachability information via a dedicated feature encoder significantly enhances a VLM's ability to generate feasible and safe robotic task plans, bridging the gap between high-level perception and low-level physical constraints.
> - The constructed Phys100K dataset and EQA-phys benchmark provide essential resources for training and evaluating models that require an understanding of physical reachability, driving advancements in embodied AI.
