# Refining Few-Step Text-to-Multiview Diffusion via Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Wuhan University, Sun Yat-sen University, Tencent, and Xiaomi introduce MVC-ZigAL, a reinforcement learning framework designed to enhance few-step Text-to-Multiview (T2MV) diffusion models. This framework significantly improves both per-view fidelity and cross-view consistency, achieving superior performance over state-of-the-art baselines on benchmarks like MATE-3D while maintaining fast generation speeds.

## Problem

- Few-step Text-to-Multiview (T2MV) diffusion models, while fast, suffer from compromised per-view fidelity and a lack of cross-view consistency.
- Existing reinforcement learning (RL) finetuning methods for diffusion models primarily optimize for single-image quality, neglecting the critical cross-view coordination required for T2MV.
- The constrained policy space and tightly clustered reward values in few-step generation lead to weak learning signals, limiting the effectiveness of standard RL finetuning.

## Method

- **MVC-ZigAL** introduces a ==multiview-aware== Markov Decision Process (MDP) and a joint-view reward function to model the collective quality and consistency of all generated views.
- A novel advantage learning strategy, MV-ZigAL, utilizes self-reflection-based zigzag multiview sampling (ZMV-Sampling) to generate stronger reference trajectories and amplify learning signals.
- A multiview-constrained policy optimization framework balances per-view fidelity and cross-view consistency through a Lagrangian dual formulation, integrating adaptive primal-dual updates and a self-paced constraint threshold curriculum for stable training.

## Results

- MVC-ZigAL consistently outperformed adapted T2MV policy optimization baselines (MV-PG, MV-DPO, MV-RDL) across all HyperScore metrics and PickScore, demonstrating superior T2MV generation quality.
- The framework significantly enhanced geometric consistency and detail, effectively mitigating issues like the 'multi-face' problem, and achieved quality comparable to **50**-step models at just 4 or 8 inference steps.
- On the out-of-distribution MATE-3D benchmark, MVC-ZigAL surpassed state-of-the-art T2MV diffusion baselines and other methods, achieving higher scores across HyperScore subcategories, ImageReward, HPSv2, and PickScore with fewer function evaluations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reformulating the T2MV denoising process as a multiview-aware MDP with a joint-view reward function is crucial for optimizing multiview consistency.
> - Leveraging 'self-reflection' through zigzag sampling to create advantage signals from refined trajectories effectively addresses the issue of weak learning signals in few-step generation regimes.
> - A constrained optimization approach, rather than simple weighted sum of rewards, more effectively balances the often conflicting objectives of individual view quality and overall multiview geometric consistency.
