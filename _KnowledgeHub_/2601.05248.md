# LaST$_{0}$: Latent Spatio-Temporal Chain-of-Thought for Robotic Vision-Language-Action Model

> [!summary] Summary
> LaST0, a Vision-Language-Action model, was developed to enhance robotic manipulation by employing a Latent Spatio-Temporal Chain-of-Thought within a dual-system Mixture-of-Transformers architecture. It demonstrates 15.4 Hz inference speed and achieves up to an 82% mean success rate on RLBench tasks, outperforming explicit Chain-of-Thought approaches.

## Problem

- Existing explicit Chain-of-Thought (CoT) VLA models suffer from high inference latency due to autoregressive generation, hindering real-time robotic manipulation and temporal consistency.
- Explicit reasoning, especially linguistic CoT, struggles to faithfully capture 'ineffable' physical attributes (e.g., precise spatial relationships, forces) crucial for robust physical interaction.
- Maintaining temporal consistency and coherence in actions for long-horizon robotic manipulation tasks is challenging for current methods.

## Method

- Introduced **LaST0**, a ==dual-system Vision-Language-Action model== based on a ==Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT)== architecture.
- Developed a ==Latent Spatio-Temporal Chain-of-Thought (LaST CoT)== that captures fine-grained physical and robotic dynamics through ==compact latent embeddings== from ==2D visual, 3D geometric, and robot proprioceptive states==.
- Implemented ==asynchronous frequency coordination== with a ==slow reasoning expert== generating LaST CoT at a low frequency and a ==fast acting expert== producing high-frequency actions conditioned on the most recent latent reasoning output.

## Results

- Achieved a mean success rate of **82%** across 10 RLBench manipulation tasks and a state-of-the-art **98.1%** on the LIBERO benchmark, consistently outperforming baselines.
- Demonstrated an inference speed of **15.4 Hz** on an NVIDIA 4090 GPU, **14x** faster than explicit CoT methods (e.g., CoT-VLA at **1.1 Hz**).
- Validated across real-world setups (Franka single/dual-arm, AgileX mobile, TienKung 2.0 humanoid hands), achieving a **72%** mean success rate on Franka platforms and showing improved long-horizon task coherence.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reasoning directly in a compact, continuous latent space can overcome the inference latency and representational bottlenecks of explicit linguistic or visual CoT for robotic manipulation.
> - Integrating multimodal latent features (2D visual, 3D geometric, and robot proprioceptive states) provides a richer, physically-grounded understanding of robot-environment interactions.
> - A dual-system Mixture-of-Transformers architecture effectively coordinates low-frequency deliberative reasoning with high-frequency reactive action generation, enhancing overall system robustness and efficiency.
