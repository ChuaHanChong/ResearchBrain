# Nudging Beyond the Comfort Zone: Efficient Strategy-Guided Exploration for RLVR

> [!summary] Summary
> The NUDGERL framework enhances reasoning in large language models (LLMs) trained with Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) by introducing strategy-guided exploration and an inter-intra group advantage mechanism. This approach achieves superior performance, outperforming brute-force methods with an 8x reduction in rollout budget and exceeding oracle-guided baselines across mathematical reasoning tasks.

## Problem

- Existing RLVR exploration is fundamentally bottlenecked, as policy improvements are limited to trajectories that have already been sampled.
- Brute-force scaling of rollouts to discover rare, correct reasoning paths is computationally prohibitive and exhibits diminishing returns.
- Objective-based exploration (e.g., entropy regularization) lacks precise control over what is explored, often yielding unproductive trajectories, while oracle supervision is expensive and restricts diversity.

## Method

- **NUDGERL**'s ==Strategy Nudging== generates lightweight, heuristic text contexts to condition policy rollouts, forcing exploration of diverse reasoning paths with ==context dropout== for generalization.
- ==Inter-Intra Group Advantage== assigns credit based on both intra-context (relative to the same context) and inter-context (relative to global average) reward signals, balancing exploration among different strategies.
- A ==distillation-augmented RL objective== combines a PPO-style RL term with an advantage-weighted distillation term to effectively transfer context-conditioned strategies to the context-free base policy.

## Results

- NUDGERL achieved the best average pass@1 performance on Qwen3-4B and Olmo3-7B models using only **8 rollouts**, outperforming GRPO with **32 and 64 rollouts** (e.g., **0.489** pass@1 vs. **0.487** at 32 rollouts on Qwen3-4B).
- The framework consistently surpassed POPE, an oracle-prefix baseline, demonstrating the efficacy of structured exploration over injecting privileged solution signals.
- Strategy Nudging significantly increased the number of distinct reasoning approaches discovered and showed faster, more stable pass@1 improvement during training compared to GRPO variants.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Structured, lightweight strategy-level contexts can efficiently induce diverse and semantically meaningful reasoning trajectories, bypassing the limitations of brute-force sampling or expensive oracle supervision.
> - A robust credit assignment mechanism that disentangles intrinsic trajectory quality from context-specific biases is crucial for effective learning when policy rollouts are conditioned on diverse prompts.
> - Distilling effective behaviors discovered through context-conditioned exploration into a context-free base policy is essential to bridge the train-test gap and enable practical inference.
