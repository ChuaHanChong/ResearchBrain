# WorldDreamer: Towards General World Models for Video Generation via Predicting Masked Tokens

> [!summary] Summary
> WorldDreamer introduces a world model that redefines general video generation as an unsupervised visual sequence modeling task by predicting masked visual tokens using a Transformer-based architecture. This model achieves versatile video creation and editing, including image-to-video, text-to-video, and action-to-video, with significantly faster inference speeds than existing methods.

## Problem

- Existing world models are largely confined to specific, structured environments (e.g., gaming, robotics), limiting their ability to generalize to diverse "general world" scenes.
- Current visual dynamics models, often relying on RNNs or diffusion, face challenges in effectively capturing nuanced motion and physics across a wide array of general world scenes.
- A need exists for world models that can integrate multiple input modalities (text, action) for diverse, controlled video generation and editing tasks.

## Method

- **WorldDreamer** proposes a Transformer-based general ==world model== that frames world modeling as an unsupervised visual sequence modeling task through masked visual token prediction.
- It utilizes a Spatial Temporal Patchwise Transformer (STPT) with localized spatial attention and spatial-wise ==cross-attention== for effective multimodal prompt integration.
- The model employs a dynamic masking strategy and parallel sampling during inference, enabling significantly faster video generation by predicting multiple tokens concurrently.

## Results

- Demonstrates proficiency across multiple video generation and editing tasks, including image-to-video, text-to-video, video inpainting, video stylization, and action-to-video generation.
- Generates high-fidelity, cinematically smooth videos from single images and aligns generated videos with text prompts across various styles.
- Achieves video generation in approximately 10 steps, resulting in an estimated **3x** to **20x** faster inference speed compared to diffusion or autoregressive methods, generating a 24-frame, 192x320 video in about 3 seconds on an A800 GPU.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Framing world modeling as an unsupervised visual sequence modeling task, similar to large language models' masked token prediction, is an effective paradigm for learning general visual dynamics.
> - A Transformer architecture combining localized spatial-temporal patchwise self-attention with spatial-wise cross-attention can efficiently learn complex spatio-temporal dynamics and integrate multimodal conditioning.
> - A dynamic masking strategy with consistent masks across frames, coupled with parallel sampling, enables substantial acceleration of video generation, moving beyond sequential token prediction.
