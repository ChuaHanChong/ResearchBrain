# Translate Policy to Language: Flow Matching Generated Rewards for LLM Explanations

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework trains large language models to provide natural language explanations for decisions made by various AI agents, utilizing Rectified Flow models to generate distributional rewards that effectively capture nuanced human judgments. The approach yields explanations enabling higher prediction accuracy of agent actions, outperforming other methods by up to 18.2% on diverse tasks, and demonstrates improved logical soundness and lower cognitive load in human evaluations.

## Problem

- Existing natural language explanation methods for AI agents are highly task- and agent-specific, limiting their generalizability across diverse applications.
- Human judgments regarding the quality of AI explanations are inherently subjective, pluralistic, and probabilistic, a complexity not adequately captured by traditional scalar reward models.
- Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback (RLAIF) struggles with the inherent noise and potential inaccuracy of proxy rewards generated by auxiliary LLMs, requiring better management of these proxy errors.

## Method

- A framework trains an EXPLANATION LLM to generate ==natural language explanations== for any agent decision based solely on contextual information, without prior knowledge of the specific action.
- Generative Continuous Normalizing Flows (CNFs), specifically Rectified Flow, are employed as a reward model to capture the probabilistic nature of human judgments and denoise proxy rewards from other LLMs.
- The Rectified Flow model's architecture incorporates cross-attention mechanisms to selectively integrate linguistic and contextual cues from the decision context and generated explanation.
- The EXPLANATION LLM is fine-tuned using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with dense, sentence-level rewards derived from the flow-matched reward model.

## Results

- The proposed method achieved a **1.9%** to **18.2%** higher accuracy across various RL tasks (SMAC, AI2-THOR) and LLM benchmarks (MMLU, MathQA) compared to existing baselines.
- Human evaluations validated the quality of generated explanations, showing **25.7%** higher accuracy on MathQA than the best baseline and being rated as more logically sound and less cognitively demanding by **60%** of participants.
- Ablation studies demonstrated that the cross-attention mechanism in the reward model and the use of dense, sentence-level rewards are crucial, with their removal significantly reducing performance (e.g., over **5%** accuracy drop).
- The Rectified Flow reward model successfully generalized to distinguish between true and false negative explanations, and the framework maintained robust performance across different LLM backbones and under noisy reward conditions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Continuous Normalizing Flows can effectively model the probabilistic and pluralistic nature of human judgments, providing a mechanism to denoise LLM-generated proxy rewards and theoretically bound errors.
> - Training an explanation LLM to infer decisions from context alone, rather than directly stating them, fosters genuine reasoning and promotes a more generalizable explanation capability.
> - Cross-attention within the flow-based reward model is essential for its ability to effectively process linguistic and contextual cues, allowing it to generalize to negative samples and assign accurate reward distributions.
> - Providing dense, sentence-level rewards during RL fine-tuning offers more effective guidance to the EXPLANATION LLM compared to sparse, end-of-explanation rewards, leading to higher quality outputs.
