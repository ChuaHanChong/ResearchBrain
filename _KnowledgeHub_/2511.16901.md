# R-AVST: Empowering Video-LLMs with Fine-Grained Spatio-Temporal Reasoning in Complex Audio-Visual Scenarios

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers introduce R-AVST, the first dataset specifically designed for fine-grained audio-visual spatio-temporal reasoning in complex scenarios, and AVST-Zero, a reinforcement learning-based Video-LLM. AVST-Zero demonstrates competitive and often superior performance across new spatio-temporal reasoning tasks, outperforming existing models on the R-AVST benchmark.

## Problem

- Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with fine-grained spatio-temporal reasoning in complex, real-world audio-visual events, often operating in simpler video scenarios.
- Current audio-visual datasets are fragmented, lacking comprehensive integration of fine-grained temporal boundaries, spatial localization, and rich audio-visual dynamics for diverse event types.
- Existing Video-LLMs often rely on extensive high-quality labeled data and lack sufficient exploration capacity, while reinforcement learning approaches for Video-LLMs lack dedicated reward designs for complex audio-visual spatio-temporal tasks.

## Method

- Constructed ==R-AVST==, a novel dataset comprising 5,237 untrimmed videos with fine-grained temporal and spatial annotations for 27,253 objects across over 100 audio-visual event types, generating 8,166 task-specific question-answer (QA) pairs.
- Defined three core tasks for audio-visual spatio-temporal reasoning: ==Audio-Visual Temporal Reasoning (AVTR)==, Audio-Visual Spatial Reasoning (AVSR), and Audio-Visual Spatio-Temporal Reasoning (AVSTR).
- Developed AVST-Zero, a Video-LLM fine-tuned using Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) combined with a ==multi-dimensional reward system== tailored for format, object, temporal, and spatial accuracy in audio-visual contexts.

## Results

- R-AVST is the first dataset to combine fine-grained temporal boundaries and spatial localization for over **100** types of audio-visual events in untrimmed videos, offering a robust benchmark.
- AVST-Zero (7B) achieved the highest mean temporal IoU (m tIoU) of **47.96%** on Audio-Visual Temporal Reasoning (AVTR) and superior joint grounding with **46.04%** m tIoU and **8.59%** mean visual IoU (m vIoU) on Audio-Visual Spatio-Temporal Reasoning (AVSTR).
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical contribution of each multi-dimensional reward component and the GRPO fine-tuning strategy, with GRPO improving AVTR m tIoU from **42.84%** (SFT) to **48.17%** (AVST-Zero).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A comprehensive dataset like R-AVST, uniquely combining fine-grained temporal and spatial annotations with diverse audio-visual events, is crucial for advancing models beyond simple video understanding.
> - Reinforcement learning, specifically GRPO with a carefully designed multi-dimensional reward system, significantly enhances Video-LLM performance in fine-grained spatio-temporal reasoning tasks over supervised fine-tuning.
> - Accurate spatio-temporal reasoning for both audible and visible objects in complex real-world scenarios requires a holistic integration of audio and visual cues, revealing their interdependencies.
