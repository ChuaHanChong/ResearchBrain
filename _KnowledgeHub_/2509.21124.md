# Expanding Reasoning Potential in Foundation Model by Learning Diverse Chains of Thought Patterns

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Peking University and Meituan developed the CoTP framework, a dual-granularity approach to select high-value Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data during mid-training, enhancing the “reasoning potential” of foundation models. This method substantially improved performance on challenging mathematical reasoning tasks and elevated the upper bound of downstream reinforcement learning.

## Problem

- Reinforcement learning (RL) performance in large reasoning models is often limited by the inherent reasoning capacity of the foundation model, leading to unstable performance.
- Existing mid-training methodologies use Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data indiscriminately, without identifying which specific types or characteristics most effectively improve model reasoning capabilities.

## Method

- Defined "==reasoning potential==" as the inverse of the expected number of attempts required to solve a problem, providing a quantifiable metric for improvement.
- Constructed a high-value core reference set of CoT data by abstracting atomic reasoning patterns and applying importance weighting.
- Developed a dual-granularity data selection algorithm, CoTP, using Weighted Dynamic Time Warping on both reasoning pattern chains and token entropy chains to select high-value CoT data for mid-training.

## Results

- Models mid-trained with CoTP data achieved a **9.58%** average improvement on AIME 2024 and 2025, and an overall **6.39%** gain on challenging mathematical reasoning benchmarks compared to baseline approaches.
- The CoTP framework increased the upper bound of downstream Reinforcement Learning (RL) performance by an average of **7.81%** compared to models trained on general long-CoT data.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both token-level entropy features and importance weighting of reasoning patterns are critical for effectively selecting high-value data and improving model performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Enhancing a foundation model's intrinsic reasoning potential during mid-training is crucial for improving downstream RL performance and overall problem-solving efficacy.
> - Effective data curation, focusing on diverse and high-value reasoning patterns, is more impactful than merely increasing the volume of mixed reasoning data.
> - A dual-granularity representation combining abstract reasoning patterns and fine-grained token entropy captures the essential characteristics of effective CoT sequences.
