# Open-Qwen2VL: Compute-Efficient Pre-Training of Fully-Open Multimodal LLMs on Academic Resources

> [!summary] Summary
> Open-Qwen2VL presents a 2B-parameter multimodal LLM pre-trained using 220 A100-40G GPU hours, which outperforms Qwen2-VL-2B on several benchmarks. The project provides a fully open-source training pipeline, data filtering techniques, and pre-training data to promote reproducibility and accessibility.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) developed by major tech companies are often only partially open, with critical training details (data filtering, mixture strategies, codebases) remaining undisclosed, hindering reproducibility.
- Pre-training SOTA MLLMs requires vast computational resources, creating a significant barrier for academic institutions and smaller research groups with limited GPU access.
- The lack of transparency in MLLM pre-training pipelines makes it difficult for the community to understand underlying mechanisms or build upon advancements effectively.

## Method

- Developed a **2B**-parameter MLLM (Open-Qwen2VL) using a Qwen2.5-1.5B LLM backbone and a SigLIP-SO-400M vision encoder, employing a ==low-to-high dynamic image resolution== strategy for efficiency.
- Employed a rigorous data strategy including ==MLLM-based data filtering (MLM-Filter)== to curate high-quality multimodal caption data and ==multimodal sequence packing== to maximize context window utilization.
- Released a fully open-source training codebase, detailed data filtering techniques, and all pre-training and supervised fine-tuning data to ensure full reproducibility and community access.

## Results

- Open-Qwen2VL was pre-trained using only **220** A100-40G GPU hours, representing **0.36%** of the multimodal pre-training tokens used by Qwen2-VL.
- The instruction-tuned Open-Qwen2VL outperforms Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct on several multimodal benchmarks, including MMBench, SEEDBench-img, MMStar, and MathVista.
- Multimodal sequence packing significantly improved few-shot multimodal in-context learning, yielding **+3%** to **+12%** performance improvements on VQA datasets compared to 0-shot reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - MLLM-based data filtering (MLM-Filter) introduces beneficial, diverse data distributions that complement conventional CLIP-filtered data, leading to improved performance.
> - Low-to-high dynamic image resolution and multimodal sequence packing significantly enhance pre-training efficiency and improve multi-image in-context learning capabilities.
> - Achieving competitive MLLM pre-training and performance is feasible with academic-level computing resources (e.g., 8x A100-40G GPUs) when employing compute-efficient methodologies.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) is at the forefront of AI research, demonstrating remarkable capabilities in understanding and reasoning across different modalities, primarily images and text. These models, exemplified by GPT-4o, Claude-3.7-Sonnet, InternVL, DeepSeek-VL2, and Qwen2-VL, are advancing rapidly, enabling applications that can comprehend complex visual information, charts, and documents.

Historically, Vision-Language Models (VLMs) were trained from scratch on image-text caption data, typically with smaller model sizes. MLLMs, in contrast, usually build upon a pre-trained text-only LLM and then undergo continuous pre-training on diverse, large-scale multimodal data. This paradigm shift has unlocked emergent capabilities, but it comes with significant challenges, especially concerning accessibility and reproducibility.

The current landscape is characterized by a critical dichotomy:
1. **State-of-the-Art (SOTA) Models:** Many SOTA MLLMs are developed by major tech companies (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Alibaba, Microsoft). While they might release model checkpoints (often instruction-tuned versions), their "killer secrets" – the precise data filtering techniques, multimodal data mixture strategies, sequence packing methods, and the training codebases – remain largely closed-source. This lack of transparency severely hinders the academic community and smaller research groups from reproducing results, understanding the underlying mechanisms, or building upon these advancements effectively. Table 1 in the paper starkly illustrates this "partial openness" of leading MLLMs like MM1, Llama-3.2-Vision, and Qwen2-VL.
2. **High Computational Costs:** Pre-training SOTA MLLMs typically requires vast computational resources (e.g., millions of GPU hours, exabytes of data), making it largely exclusive to well-funded industry labs. This creates a "resource divide," limiting academic institutions with "limited GPUs" from actively participating in foundational MLLM research.

**Open-Qwen2VL addresses these critical gaps by:**
- **Championing "Fully Open-Source":** It redefines and commits to a higher standard of openness by releasing not just model checkpoints, but also the complete training codebase, detailed data filtering techniques, and all pre-training and supervised fine-tuning data. This is a direct challenge to the prevalent "partially open" practices and aims to foster true reproducibility and collaboration.
- **Demonstrating Compute-Efficiency:** The paper provides a concrete example of how SOTA MLLM pre-training can be achieved with significantly fewer resources (220 A100-40G GPU hours on 8xA100-40G GPUs) compared to models like Qwen2-VL (trained on 0.36% of its tokens). This aims to democratize access to foundational MLLM research for academic institutions.
- **Innovating on Efficiency Methods:** It contributes practical techniques like low-to-high dynamic image resolution, multimodal sequence packing, and the validation of MLLM-based data filtering (MLM-Filter) as key enablers for efficiency and performance, setting new benchmarks for efficient pre-training pipelines.

By tackling both the openness and computational cost issues, Open-Qwen2VL positions itself as a significant contribution towards making advanced MLLM research more accessible, transparent, and collaborative for the broader AI community, shifting the paradigm from closed, resource-intensive development to an open, efficient, and reproducible research ecosystem.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The core objectives and motivations behind the Open-Qwen2VL project are multifaceted, aiming to address critical barriers in the advancement and democratization of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs).

**Primary Objectives:**

1. **Develop a Compute-Efficient MLLM:** The foremost goal is to pre-train a 2B-parameter MLLM, named Open-Qwen2VL, using a significantly reduced computational footprint. This includes optimizing every stage of the pipeline to minimize GPU hours, thereby making SOTA MLLM pre-training feasible with "academic-level computing resources" (specifically, 8x A100-40G GPUs).
2. **Achieve State-of-the-Art Performance:** Despite the focus on efficiency, the project aims for Open-Qwen2VL to outperform or be highly competitive with existing partially-open SOTA MLLMs of similar scale, particularly Qwen2-VL-2B, on a diverse set of multimodal benchmarks.
3. **Establish a "Fully Open" Standard:** A crucial objective is to redefine and fully implement "fully open-source" for MLLMs. This entails the complete release of:
 - The entire training codebase.
 - Detailed data filtering techniques and scripts.
 - All pre-training and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) data used in development.
 This commitment aims to facilitate easy and convenient reproduction by the community.

**Motivation:**

1. **Address Reproduction Barriers:** The abstract explicitly states that "The reproduction of state-of-the-art multimodal LLM pre-training faces barriers at every stage of the pipeline." These barriers include the lack of public access to high-quality data filtering methods, multimodal data mixture strategies, effective sequence packing techniques, and robust training frameworks. Many SOTA MLLMs, while releasing checkpoints, "hide such technical details in their technical reports," making true reproduction impossible. Open-Qwen2VL directly confronts this by opening up all aspects of its work.
2. **Democratize MLLM Research:** A major driving force is the recognition that "recent state-of-the-art MLLMs are neither 'fully-open' to the community for reproduction nor compute-friendly to academic institutions with limited GPUs." This project seeks to dismantle the perception that "research on pre-training is not only a game for giant tech companies." By demonstrating that advanced training pipelines and data filtering can overcome compute limitations, the authors aim to encourage and empower the academic community to engage in foundational MLLM research, even with modest resources.
3. **Validate Efficient Methodologies:** The motivation also includes proving the efficacy of specific compute-efficient techniques. The paper is motivated to show that "low-to-high dynamic image resolution and multimodal sequence packing significantly enhance pre-training efficiency" and that "carefully curated [data] using both MLLM-based filtering techniques... and conventional CLIP-based filtering methods, substantially improv[es] data quality and training efficiency."
4. **Foster Collaborative Advancement:** By providing a comprehensive, fully-open solution, the authors intend to foster a more collaborative and accelerated research environment. Researchers can directly build upon this work, experiment with different components, and contribute to the collective understanding and improvement of MLLMs without having to "reinvent the wheel" or overcome prohibitive resource barriers.

In essence, Open-Qwen2VL is motivated by a vision of a more accessible, transparent, and democratized MLLM research landscape, where innovation is driven by ingenuity and collaboration rather than solely by massive computational power.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Open-Qwen2VL employs a suite of innovative and compute-efficient methodologies across its entire pre-training and fine-tuning pipeline to achieve its objectives of high performance with limited resources and full openness.

#### 3.1. Model Architecture

- **LLM Backbone:** Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct LLM, a relatively compact yet capable language model, serves as the textual foundation.
- **Vision Encoder:** SigLIP-SO-400M, a performant vision model, is used to encode visual information.
- **Visual Projector:** An Adaptive Average-Pooling Visual Projector, consisting of an Adaptive Average-Pooling layer followed by a two-layer MLP. This is a critical component for efficiency.
 - **Low-to-High Dynamic Image Resolution:** During the pre-training stage, the projector scales down the SigLIP's 729 output visual patches to a more compact 144 visual tokens, significantly reducing the visual token count processed by the LLM. For the Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) stage, the resolution is scaled up to the full 729 visual tokens, ensuring high-resolution understanding in the final model. This strategy aims to reduce computational burden during the extensive pre-training phase without compromising downstream SFT performance.
- **Parameter Freezing:** To save computation, the parameters of the vision encoder are frozen during both pre-training and SFT stages. Only the projector and LLM backbone parameters are trainable. While acknowledged that training the vision encoder can enhance capabilities, this choice prioritizes efficiency given academic resource constraints.

#### 3.2. Data Strategy and High-Quality Data Filtering

- **Focus on Caption Data:** Open-Qwen2VL exclusively focuses on image-text caption data for pre-training, explicitly avoiding interleaved data. This decision is made to control data scale, ensure pre-training efficiency, and prioritize single-image reasoning capabilities, as some studies suggest interleaved data can reduce zero-shot single-image performance.
- **Dataset Choices:** Four widely used image-text caption datasets are chosen for pre-training:
 1. **CCS-CLIP (8.5M pairs):** Curated from CC3M-CC12M-SBU using CLIP-based filtering.
 2. **LAION-CLIP (15M pairs):** A subset of LAION-400M, filtered with a strict CLIP image-text cosine similarity threshold (0.3).
 3. **DataComp-DFN (15M pairs):** Curated from DataComp-Medium using the top-1 independent data filter, Data-Filtering-Network (DFN). The authors downloaded the original DataComp-Medium and selected the DFN-curated subset.
 4. **DataComp-MLM-Filter & DFN (19.9M pairs):** This is a key novel contribution. It combines DataComp-DFN data with an additional 8M high-quality data curated by an **MLLM-based data filtering method, MLM-Filter**. Specifically, the Semantic Understanding (SU) quality metric from MLM-Filter (using `mlm-filter-qwen2.5-1.5b-gpt4o`) is applied with a high threshold (85/100). The union of MLM-Filter and DFN-curated data, after deduplication, results in 19.9M pairs. This MLLM-based filtering is hypothesized to introduce a different and beneficial data distribution.
- **Data Curation and Accessibility:** The paper details how these datasets were downloaded (using `img2dataset` for CCS and LAION, and official scripts for DataComp), processed, and converted into WebDataset format, explicitly stating efforts to make this data preparation fully reproducible and open-source.

#### 3.3. Multimodal Sequence Packing

- **Motivation:** Standard batching with padding leads to "heavy compute-waste and training inefficiency" due to varied sequence lengths.
- **Approach:** Multimodal sequence packing regroups image-text caption data into sequence groups that closely match the 4096 context length.
- **Algorithm (Algorithm 1):**
 1. Compute the "multimodal length" (text tokens + 144 visual tokens per image) for each image-text sample.
 2. Sort samples by length in descending order.
 3. Use a First-fit-decreasing (FFD) bin packing algorithm to group samples into "bins" such that the total length of each bin is closest to the maximum context length (4096), minimizing padding.
 4. Concatenate `input_ids` and Pillow-format images within each packed sequence.
 5. Insert an `<image>` placeholder token at the beginning of each image-text caption and use `<|im_end|>` as a separator between captions.
- **Storage:** Packed sequences are stored in Pickle files for efficient loading of mixed data types (Pillow images, PyTorch tensors).
- **Impact:** This technique generates pseudo image-text interleaving data structures, which is shown to enhance multi-image in-context learning capabilities.

#### 3.4. Training Infrastructure and Codebase

- **Base Codebase:** The authors developed their training codebase by heavily modifying Prismatic-VLM, which originally supported only single-image instructions.
- **Modifications:** Significant modifications were made to the dataloader and batch preparation to accommodate multimodal packed sequences with multiple images per sequence.
- **Distributed Training:** Retained and utilized Prismatic-VLM's Fully-Sharded Distributed Parallel (torch-FSDP) trainer. The authors highlight that FSDP-based implementation is approximately 17% faster per training step compared to DeepSpeed-Zero3, aligning with previous findings.
- **Full Pipeline Support:** The open-source codebase is claimed to be the first comprehensive solution supporting all stages of MLLM training, from data preparation and filtering to pre-training, SFT, and evaluation.

#### 3.5. Training Stages

- **Pre-training:** Performed for one epoch on the selected packed multimodal sequences (best mixture identified through ablations).
- **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** The base MLLM is then fine-tuned on a large-scale visual instruction dataset. Initially LLaVA-665k, then scaled up to MAmmoTH-VL-10M for further performance enhancement. Custom data loading for MAmmoTH-VL-10M was implemented to manage CPU memory limits.

This holistic approach, focusing on architectural efficiency, rigorous data curation, advanced packing techniques, and an optimized training framework, enables Open-Qwen2VL to achieve strong performance within academic resource constraints while upholding a high standard of openness.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research yields several significant findings, demonstrating the efficacy of compute-efficient pre-training methods and the value of a fully open-source approach.

#### 4.1. Compute Efficiency

- **Remarkable GPU Hour Reduction:** Open-Qwen2VL was pre-trained on 29 million image-text pairs (which translates to approximately 5 billion packed multimodal tokens) using only 220 A100-40G GPU hours on an 8x A100-40G setup at UCSB.
- **Token Efficiency Comparison:** This is a minuscule fraction, specifically 0.36%, of the 1.4 trillion multimodal pre-training tokens used for Qwen2-VL, a direct state-of-the-art competitor. This demonstrates an exceptional level of data and compute efficiency.
- **SFT Cost:** The subsequent supervised fine-tuning on LLaVA-665k instructions required an additional 48 A100-40G GPU hours.

#### 4.2. Performance Against State-of-the-Art (SOTA)

- **Outperformance over Qwen2-VL-2B:** The final instruction-tuned Open-Qwen2VL notably outperforms the partially-open SOTA MLLM Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct (2B-parameter model) on several critical multimodal benchmarks, including MMBench, SEEDBench-img, MMStar, and MathVista (Table 4).
- **Competitive General Performance:** Open-Qwen2VL demonstrates competitive performance across a broad range of benchmarks when compared to other 2B-parameter SOTA MLLMs like InternVL2.5-2B-MPO and DeepSeekVL-2-Tiny. For instance, it achieves the highest MMBench score (80.9) among the compared models.
- **Areas for Improvement (OCR VQA):** While strong generally, Open-Qwen2VL shows relatively weaker results in OCR VQA tasks (AI2D and TextVQA). The authors attribute this to the pre-training data not including OCR-specific datasets like SynthDoG or LAIONCOCO-OCR, suggesting a specific data gap rather than a fundamental model limitation.

#### 4.3. Ablations on Data Mixture Strategies

- **Impact of MLLM-based Filtering:** Ablation studies (Table 3) reveal that mixing a small amount (5M) of high-quality data curated by an efficient MLLM-based filter (MLM-Filter, specifically the Semantic Understanding metric) significantly enhances model performance, achieving a +0.5 average improvement across benchmarks.
- **Data Distribution Diversity:** This finding supports the hypothesis that MLLM-based data filters introduce a different and beneficial data distribution into the pre-training set, providing new knowledge that complements CLIP-filtered data.
- **Homogeneity of CLIP/DFN Data:** Simply mixing more CLIP-filtered or DFN-curated web-crawled data (CCS-CLIP, DataComp-DFN, LAION-CLIP) does not necessarily lead to better performance, possibly due to high data homogeneity between them.

#### 4.4. Scaling Effects of Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)

- **General Performance Improvement:** Scaling up the visual SFT data from LLaVA-665k to MAmmoTH-VL-10M (Figure 2) generally leads to remarkable improvements in model performance across various multimodal benchmarks.
- **Convergence and Continued Improvement:** Most benchmarks (POPE, MMMU, MMBench, SEEDBench) show performance convergence around 8M SFT instructions, with little to no further improvement for the final 2M data. However, TextVQA and MathVista performance show a steady improvement with increased data scale, suggesting these out-of-distribution tasks benefit more from larger, diverse SFT data.
- **Potential Degradation:** For general-purpose knowledge-based benchmarks (MMMU, SEEDBench, MMStar), slight performance degradation is observed in the final 6M instruction tuning data, indicating a potential saturation or overfitting beyond a certain SFT data scale for these tasks.

#### 4.5. Impact of Multimodal Sequence Packing

- **Enhanced In-Context Learning:** Experiments on the base (non-SFT) MLLM demonstrate that training with packed multimodal sequences significantly enhances few-shot multimodal in-context learning capabilities. On VQA datasets (GQA, VQA-v2, VizWiz, OKVQA, Text-VQA), 8-shot in-context learning yielded +3% to +12% performance improvements compared to 0-shot reasoning (Table 5). This confirms the necessity and significance of sequence packing for improving model capabilities beyond just efficiency.

#### 4.6. Impact of Unfreezing Vision Encoder Parameters

- **Mixed Results for Trainable Vision Encoder:** An ablation study on SFT (Table 6) shows that making the vision encoder parameters trainable (instead of frozen) generally leads to better *average* performance. However, it can also result in significant performance degradation on specific benchmarks like MMMU. This suggests a nuanced trade-off between overall improvement and performance on specific complex tasks, especially when the vision encoder was frozen during pre-training due to resource constraints.

In summary, Open-Qwen2VL successfully demonstrates that a combination of smart architectural choices, highly efficient data processing (MLM-Filter, sequence packing), and optimized training frameworks can enable the development of competitive MLLMs even with limited academic resources, while also setting a new standard for open-source releases.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The Open-Qwen2VL project carries significant implications for the future of Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) research and development, particularly for the academic community.

#### 5.1. Democratization of MLLM Research

- **Breaking the Resource Barrier:** The most profound impact is the demonstration that state-of-the-art MLLM pre-training is not exclusively a domain for well-resourced "giant tech companies." By achieving competitive performance with only 220 A100-40G GPU hours on academic-level hardware (8xA100-40G GPUs), Open-Qwen2VL provides a tangible blueprint for smaller research groups and academic institutions. This empowers a broader base of researchers to engage in foundational MLLM work, fostering diverse perspectives and accelerating innovation.
- **Encouraging Academic Contributions:** The work explicitly encourages the academic community to focus on "pre-training data and pipeline research even with very limited computing resources." This shifts the focus from raw compute power to ingenuity in data curation, model architecture, and training methodologies, where academia often excels.

#### 5.2. Redefining and Elevating Open-Source Standards

- **True Reproducibility:** By committing to a "fully open" standard—releasing not just model checkpoints but the entire training codebase, detailed data filtering techniques, and all pre-training and supervised fine-tuning data—Open-Qwen2VL sets a new benchmark for transparency in MLLM research. This level of openness is critical for true reproducibility, allowing other researchers to verify findings, debug issues, and build directly upon the work without hidden "killer secrets."
- **Accelerated Community Development:** A truly open ecosystem fosters collaboration and rapid iteration. Researchers can leverage the provided data, code, and insights to experiment with new ideas, compare approaches, and collectively push the boundaries of MLLM capabilities much faster than in a closed environment.

#### 5.3. Validation of Compute-Efficient Methodologies

- **Practical Techniques for Efficiency:** The paper validates the effectiveness of specific techniques like low-to-high dynamic image resolution, multimodal sequence packing, and MLLM-based data filtering. These methods are proven to significantly enhance pre-training efficiency and model capabilities, offering practical solutions for resource-constrained environments. This provides concrete methodological contributions that can be adopted and further improved by the broader community.
- **Insights into Data Curation:** The finding that MLLM-based data filtering (MLM-Filter) can introduce beneficial, diverse data distributions is a significant insight for future data curation efforts, moving beyond reliance solely on CLIP-based filtering.

#### 5.4. Foundation for Future Research

- **Open Baseline:** Open-Qwen2VL serves as a strong, fully-open baseline model and pipeline for future research. Researchers can directly experiment with new data mixtures, different visual encoders, improved sequence packing algorithms, or novel fine-tuning strategies without incurring the enormous initial cost of building a foundational MLLM from scratch.
- **Catalyst for Data and Pipeline Innovation:** By demonstrating that efficiency is achievable, the work encourages a deeper dive into optimizing every part of the MLLM lifecycle, from data acquisition and cleaning to model architecture and distributed training strategies.

In conclusion, Open-Qwen2VL is more than just a new MLLM; it is a statement and a significant step towards making advanced multimodal AI research more inclusive, transparent, and collaborative. Its emphasis on efficiency and full openness challenges the status quo and provides a robust foundation for the next generation of MLLM innovations driven by a wider, more diverse research community.
