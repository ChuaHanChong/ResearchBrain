# Open-Qwen2VL: Compute-Efficient Pre-Training of Fully-Open Multimodal LLMs on Academic Resources

> [!summary] Summary
> Open-Qwen2VL presents a 2B-parameter multimodal LLM pre-trained using 220 A100-40G GPU hours, which outperforms Qwen2-VL-2B on several benchmarks. The project provides a fully open-source training pipeline, data filtering techniques, and pre-training data to promote reproducibility and accessibility.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) developed by major tech companies are often only partially open, with critical training details (data filtering, mixture strategies, codebases) remaining undisclosed, hindering reproducibility.
- Pre-training SOTA MLLMs requires vast computational resources, creating a significant barrier for academic institutions and smaller research groups with limited GPU access.
- The lack of transparency in MLLM pre-training pipelines makes it difficult for the community to understand underlying mechanisms or build upon advancements effectively.

## Method

- Developed a **2B**-parameter MLLM (Open-Qwen2VL) using a Qwen2.5-1.5B LLM backbone and a SigLIP-SO-400M vision encoder, employing a ==low-to-high dynamic image resolution== strategy for efficiency.
- Employed a rigorous data strategy including ==MLLM-based data filtering (MLM-Filter)== to curate high-quality multimodal caption data and ==multimodal sequence packing== to maximize context window utilization.
- Released a fully open-source training codebase, detailed data filtering techniques, and all pre-training and supervised fine-tuning data to ensure full reproducibility and community access.

## Results

- Open-Qwen2VL was pre-trained using only **220** A100-40G GPU hours, representing **0.36%** of the multimodal pre-training tokens used by Qwen2-VL.
- The instruction-tuned Open-Qwen2VL outperforms Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct on several multimodal benchmarks, including MMBench, SEEDBench-img, MMStar, and MathVista.
- Multimodal sequence packing significantly improved few-shot multimodal in-context learning, yielding **+3%** to **+12%** performance improvements on VQA datasets compared to 0-shot reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - MLLM-based data filtering (MLM-Filter) introduces beneficial, diverse data distributions that complement conventional CLIP-filtered data, leading to improved performance.
> - Low-to-high dynamic image resolution and multimodal sequence packing significantly enhance pre-training efficiency and improve multi-image in-context learning capabilities.
> - Achieving competitive MLLM pre-training and performance is feasible with academic-level computing resources (e.g., 8x A100-40G GPUs) when employing compute-efficient methodologies.
