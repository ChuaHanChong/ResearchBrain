# CollabVLA: Self-Reflective Vision-Language-Action Model Dreaming Together with Human

> [!summary] Summary
> CollabVLA is a self-reflective Vision-Language-Action framework that empowers robots to introspect, articulate uncertainty, and proactively seek human guidance for complex manipulation tasks. This approach demonstrates a superior balance of success rate, interpretability, and efficiency in both simulation and real-world experiments by blending VLM-based reasoning, diffusion-based action generation, and adaptive human collaboration.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models often suffer from domain overfitting and degraded multimodal grounding when fine-tuned on scarce robot data.
- Prior VLA approaches frequently result in non-interpretable "black-box" policies, and explicit reasoning methods are often superficial, lacking deep reflective understanding for failure diagnosis.
- Many methods that use auxiliary generative models for explicit reasoning or subgoal generation introduce high latency, making real-time deployment challenging for embodied agents.

## Method

- Introduces a VLM-based framework that couples an ==InternVL2.5== backbone with a ==Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)== adaptation and a ==diffusion-based action model (DiT)== to unify reasoning, reflection, and action generation.
- Employs a ==two-stage training recipe==: an =="Action Grounding"== stage to align multimodal perception with motor actions, followed by a =="Reflection Tuning"== stage to integrate explicit reflective reasoning and human interaction capabilities.
- Utilizes a concise =="reflect-ask/act" two-pass inference loop==, where the model generates a reflection and adaptively decides whether to query a human for guidance or directly execute an action.

## Results

- Achieved state-of-the-art performance in multimodal understanding (**88.6** on CONREF) and VQA tasks, demonstrating preserved general VLM capabilities while integrating robotic control.
- Attained the best success rate across all **8** subtasks in the Simpler-Collab simulation benchmark with high efficiency (Time/Dream score of **36/1.9**), outperforming other collaborative and explicit-reasoning baselines.
- Demonstrated leading performance in real-world robotic manipulation on DOBOT CR5 and UR5 arms across **5** task families, improving mean success rate and score compared to strong baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating self-reflection and proactive, just-in-time human guidance into VLAs leads to improved task success and robustness, especially in ambiguous or challenging real-world scenarios.
> - An adaptive Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) design within the VLM effectively prevents task interference between multimodal perception/reflection and action generation, preserving general VLM capabilities.
> - Strategic and sparse human queries offer a more efficient and effective alternative to computationally intensive internal generative models for planning or self-correction, significantly reducing latency and internal "dream counts".
