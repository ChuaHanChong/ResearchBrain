# DataFlex: A Unified Framework for Data-Centric Dynamic Training of Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> A unified framework named DataFlex facilitates data-centric dynamic training for large language models, consolidating disparate methods for data selection, mixture, and reweighting into a single, extensible system. It demonstrates consistent improvements in MMLU accuracy and perplexity over static baselines, alongside enhanced runtime efficiency compared to original implementations of integrated algorithms.

## Problem

- The landscape of data-centric LLM training methods is fragmented, with diverse strategies implemented in isolated codebases, leading to inconsistent interfaces and protocols.
- Systematic evaluation and fair comparison between different data-centric algorithms are challenging due to varied experimental settings and difficulty in reproducing results.
- Integrating diverse data-centric algorithms into scalable LLM pipelines is complicated by the lack of a unified abstraction for managing model-dependent operations like embeddings or gradients.

## Method

- **DataFlex** introduces a unified framework built upon **LLaMA-Factory**, featuring a ==three-layered modular design== to dynamically control data usage during LLM training.
- Specialized ==Trainer modes== (Select, Mix, Weight) abstract different data-centric paradigms, interacting with pluggable ==strategy Components== to manage algorithm-specific logic.
- The framework standardizes model-dependent operations and supports large-scale distributed training environments, including ==DeepSpeed ZeRO-3==, with mechanisms for ==distributed gradient collection==.

## Results

- Data-centric methods integrated within **DataFlex** consistently outperformed static baselines, with LESS achieving **0.452** MMLU accuracy (vs. **0.394** static) on Mistral-7B and Reweight reaching **0.453** (vs. **0.319** static) on Llama-3.2-3B.
- Dynamic data mixture strategies (DoReMi, ODM) improved MMLU accuracy and corpus-level perplexity on SlimPajama datasets; DoReMi achieved **26.04%** MMLU accuracy on SlimPajama-6B compared to **25.27%** for the static baseline.
- **DataFlex** demonstrated improved runtime efficiency, reducing latency by up to **7.09%** for online selection (LESS) and **1-3.5%** for offline selection (TSDS) compared to their original implementations, while also exhibiting parallel scalability across multiple GPUs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A modular, unified framework can effectively integrate diverse data-centric strategies for LLM training, simplifying development and enabling systematic comparison.
> - Decoupling algorithmic logic from the core training loop through pluggable components enables high extensibility and rapid iteration for new data-centric methods.
> - Dynamic data management strategies (selection, mixture, reweighting) consistently enhance LLM performance, demonstrating improvements in accuracy and perplexity over static data approaches.
