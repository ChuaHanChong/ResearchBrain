# From Seeing to Experiencing: Scaling Navigation Foundation Models with Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from UCLA developed the Seeing-to-Experiencing (S2E) framework, which scales navigation foundation models by combining large-scale offline video pre-training with reinforcement learning, leading to policies with enhanced interactive capabilities and zero-shot generalization across diverse robotic platforms. The work also introduces NavBench-GS, a photorealistic 3D benchmark for evaluating navigation policies in dynamic environments.

## Problem

- Current navigation foundation models, pre-trained on passive offline data, lack causal reasoning, counterfactual understanding, and real-time reactivity to dynamic elements in complex environments.
- These models primarily 'see' the world without truly 'experiencing' it through active interaction, leading to limitations in adapting to unforeseen circumstances.
- Existing reinforcement learning approaches for generalizable navigation suffer from poor sampling efficiency and lack strong inductive priors when trained in constrained synthetic environments, hindering real-world transfer.

## Method

- The ==Seeing-to-Experiencing== (S2E) framework was introduced, integrating offline pre-training on real-world videos with online reinforcement learning in simulated environments.
- Anchor-Guided Distribution Matching (AGDM) was employed during pre-training to robustly learn and represent the complex, multimodal nature of navigation trajectories.
- A Residual-Attention Module (RAM) was designed for reinforcement learning post-training, allowing the model to acquire reactive behaviors by adaptively tuning interaction-centric cross-attention layers while freezing generalizable perceptual components.

## Results

- S2E-Full (pre-training + RL) achieved a **21%** improvement in success rate over the pre-trained S2E-BC model on the NavBench-GS benchmark, particularly in obstacle and obstacle-pedestrian scenarios.
- The framework demonstrated robust zero-shot generalization and performance when deployed on two distinct physical robotic platforms (wheeled and quadruped robots) in real-world scenarios.
- S2E-Full consistently outperformed state-of-the-art navigation foundation models across all four NavBench-GS task categories, showing superior interactive and reactive capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement learning is a more effective mechanism than solely scaling offline data for enhancing navigation foundation models, overcoming the diminishing returns observed in pure behavior cloning approaches.
> - The targeted adaptation facilitated by the Residual-Attention Module (RAM) enables efficient injection of interactive capabilities without catastrophic forgetting of pre-trained knowledge or significant representation drift to synthetic data.
> - Modeling multimodal action distributions through Anchor-Guided Distribution Matching (AGDM) during pre-training provides a robust and structured backbone, improving the model's ability to capture diverse navigation behaviors.
