# Robustness without Wrinkles: Parallel Simulation and Robust MPC for Certified Deformable Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> The CORD-SLS framework enables real-time, robust, and certifiably safe manipulation of high-dimensional deformable objects like ropes and cloth under uncertainty. It integrates a GPU-parallel differentiable simulator with contact smoothing, a robust output-feedback Model Predictive Control (MPC) algorithm based on System Level Synthesis (SLS), and uncertainty calibration using Conformal Prediction, demonstrating up to 700 times faster simulation and 100% safety rates in hardware experiments.

## Problem

- Manipulating deformable objects presents challenges due to their high-dimensional, nonlinear dynamics and the absence of compact state representations, making planning and control complex.
- Existing deformable object control methods often lack formal robustness or safety guarantees, especially in contact-rich scenarios and under uncertainties from perception or dynamics.
- Current differentiable simulators for deformable objects struggle with discontinuous gradients arising from contact dynamics, which hinders efficient gradient-based planning and optimization.

## Method

- A GPU-parallel differentiable simulator for ropes and cloth was developed, featuring contact smoothing to provide informative, non-vanishing gradients through contact interactions.
- A robust output-feedback ==Model Predictive Control== (**MPC**) algorithm, based on System Level Synthesis (SLS), was implemented with GPU-parallel acceleration to synthesize controllers that account for process and observation disturbances.
- Conformal Prediction (CP) was employed to calibrate data-driven uncertainty bounds for both dynamics and perception models, providing high-probability safety guarantees for the true system state.

## Results

- The GPU-parallel differentiable simulator achieved up to 700 times faster forward simulation and 2000 times faster dynamics Jacobian computation compared to prior differentiable simulators.
- CORD-SLS demonstrated **100%** safety rates and low goal errors across various contact-rich rope and cloth manipulation tasks, with MPC updates for 300 DoF cloth systems executing in under one second (e.g., **0.758** s/step for folding).
- Hardware validation confirmed successful execution of obstacle avoidance for ropes and cloth folding tasks, maintaining safety despite real-world sensor noise and model discrepancies, using a CP-calibrated perception error bound of **0.03** m.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating a highly efficient, differentiable simulator with contact smoothing effectively resolves gradient discontinuity issues, enabling robust gradient-based planning for contact-rich deformable manipulation.
> - GPU-parallel System Level Synthesis (SLS) can be extended to output-feedback control for high-dimensional deformable systems by leveraging associative operators for efficient observer and controller recursions.
> - Calibrating uncertainty bounds with Conformal Prediction allows for the derivation of explicit reachable tubes and probabilistic safety guarantees, bridging the gap between model predictions and real-world uncertainties.
