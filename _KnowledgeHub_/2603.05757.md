# EmboAlign: Aligning Video Generation with Compositional Constraints for Zero-Shot Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> EmboAlign integrates video generative models (VGMs) with vision-language models (VLMs) to achieve zero-shot robotic manipulation. The system addresses physical implausibility in VGM outputs and retargeting errors by enforcing VLM-derived compositional constraints, achieving an average success rate of 68.3% across six real-robot tasks, a 43.3 percentage point improvement over existing methods.

## Problem

- Video generative models (VGMs) produce visually coherent but often physically implausible motions, leading to 'physical hallucinations' that hinder precise robotic manipulation.
- Converting pixel-space video motions from VGMs into robot actions via geometric retargeting introduces cumulative errors due to imperfect perception, resulting in execution failures.
- Purely constraint-based manipulation methods suffer from initialization sensitivity, struggling to find feasible solutions for complex, long-horizon tasks without good initial trajectories.

## Method

- The framework automatically extracts task-specific ==compositional constraints== from natural language instructions using a ==Vision-Language Model (VLM)==.
- It filters physically implausible video rollouts generated by a ==Video Generative Model (VGM)== using a ==two-tier scoring system== based on visual plausibility and VLM-derived spatial constraint satisfaction.
- Selected video rollouts are refined into precise, robot-executable trajectories through a ==nonlinear optimization== process that minimizes VLM-derived constraint violations while adhering to the VGM's motion prior.

## Results

- EmboAlign achieved an average success rate of **68.3%** across six distinct real-robot manipulation tasks.
- The system demonstrated a **43.3 percentage point** improvement in average success rate compared to both constraint-only (**21.7%**) and video-only (**25.0%**) baseline methods.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both constraint-guided video selection and constraint-based trajectory optimization are essential, with the full EmboAlign system achieving the highest success rate of **68.3%** compared to selection-only (**48.3%**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The integration of VGMs (for diverse motion priors) and VLMs (for structured physical reasoning) effectively overcomes the individual limitations of each, enabling more robust zero-shot robotic manipulation.
> - Explicitly enforcing VLM-derived compositional constraints at both the video selection and trajectory optimization stages is crucial for significantly enhancing physical plausibility and precision in robot actions.
> - The quality of the underlying video generative model directly impacts the overall success rate of such integrated systems, suggesting that advancements in VGMs can further improve performance.
