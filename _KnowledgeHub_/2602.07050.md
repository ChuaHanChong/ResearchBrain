# Interpreting Physics in Video World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Analyzing video world models like V-JEPA 2 and VideoMAE-v2, researchers identified a "Physics Emergence Zone" where representations of physical concepts are constructed, observing an early emergence of speed and acceleration magnitude before motion direction. The study reveals that core physical variables are encoded in high-dimensional, orthogonal subspaces using a circular population code rather than a compact, shared "physics engine" representation.

## Problem

- Despite strong performance on intuitive physics benchmarks, the internal mechanisms of how video world models understand and represent physical information remain opaque.
- A lack of deep interpretability for video models hinders understanding where, how, and in what form physical variables are encoded.
- The cognitive science debate on whether intuitive physics in humans is based on a 'physics engine' or 'heuristic-based' reasoning lacks empirical evidence from advanced AI systems.

## Method

- The research employed a multi-faceted interpretability methodology, including layerwise probing, subspace geometry analysis, targeted attention ablations, and activation steering on V-JEPA 2 and VideoMAE-v2.
- Synthetic datasets with ground-truth physical quantities (e.g., motion direction, speed) and intuitive physics benchmarks were used to precisely quantify representations.
- Generalized Linear Models and orthogonal probing sequences characterized individual neuron tuning and the effective dimensionality of physical encodings.

## Results

- Physical representations peak in the middle layers (post-PEZ) and degrade towards the output, with the PEZ being specific to tasks requiring global spatiotemporal coherence.
- Motion direction is encoded in a high-dimensional (40-80 independent features) circular population code, where individual neurons exhibit sinusoidal tuning curves.
- Causal interventions revealed that steering motion direction requires coordinated manipulations across dozens of orthogonal dimensions, indicating complex, distributed control over these physical features.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A "Physics Emergence Zone" exists at approximately one-third depth of the encoder, marking a sharp transition where global physical concepts like motion direction and intuitive physics judgments become accessible.
> - Scalar physical quantities (speed, acceleration magnitude) emerge in earlier layers, while motion direction emerges later at the PEZ, paralleling biological vision hierarchies.
> - Representations for physics judgments and motion direction occupy nearly orthogonal high-dimensional subspaces, suggesting models do not use a compact, shared 'physics engine' but rather distributed, task-specific features built on local spatiotemporal attention.
