# Towards Fast Safe Online Reinforcement Learning via Policy Finetuning

> [!summary] Summary
> The paper introduces Marvel, an offline-to-online (O2O) framework for Safe Reinforcement Learning that employs policy finetuning, addressing challenges of inaccurate Q-estimations and Lagrangian mismatch. Marvel combines Value Pre-Alignment (VPA) and an Adaptive PID (aPID) controller, leading to policies that achieve higher rewards while satisfying safety constraints with significantly fewer online interactions across ten safety environments.

## Problem

- Learning safe policies from scratch in online Safe Reinforcement Learning (RL) is often costly, time-consuming, and risky due to extensive environmental interactions.
- Offline Safe RL, while avoiding online interaction, often suffers from lower performance, strong reliance on dataset quality, and vulnerability to out-of-distribution actions.
- Existing offline-to-online (O2O) techniques from standard RL are not directly applicable to Safe RL due to erroneous Q-estimations (from objective mismatch and cost sparsity) and Lagrangian mismatch.

## Method

- Proposed **Marvel**, a ==policy-finetuning based offline-to-online (O2O)== Safe RL framework, that integrates ==Value Pre-Alignment (VPA)== and an ==Adaptive PID (aPID)== control mechanism.
- **VPA** re-aligns offline-pretrained reward and cost ==Q-functions== to match online learning objectives and promote exploration, using only the offline dataset.
- **aPID** replaces standard ==dual ascent== for Lagrange multiplier updates during online finetuning, dynamically adjusting gains to efficiently manage safety constraints and stabilize training.

## Results

- Marvel consistently achieves superior or comparable performance across ten Bullet Safety Gym and Safety Gymnasium environments, obtaining higher rewards while effectively maintaining costs below safety thresholds.
- The framework significantly reduces the required online interactions, achieving near-optimal performance within hundreds of gradient updates (e.g., **120,000 interactions**) compared to **millions** for baselines.
- Ablation studies confirm the critical role and synergy of both VPA and aPID for Marvel's efficiency, stability, and robust constraint satisfaction, with the adaptive nature of aPID being particularly crucial.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Pre-aligning Q-functions with VPA is crucial for overcoming the inherent conservatism and inaccuracies of offline-trained Q-values, enabling effective online finetuning and exploration.
> - An adaptive PID controller is essential for rapidly and precisely managing safety constraints during online finetuning, especially after a more optimistic, VPA-driven warm start.
> - The synergistic combination of VPA and aPID effectively addresses both erroneous Q-estimations and Lagrangian mismatch, providing a robust and efficient pathway for fast safe online learning.
