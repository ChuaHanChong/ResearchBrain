# VE2VF: Vision-Enabled to Vision-Free Distillation via Real-world Reinforcement Learning for Robust Contact-Rich Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at TU Wien developed VE2VF, a real-world human-in-the-loop reinforcement learning framework that distills a vision-enabled teacher policy into a vision-free student for robust contact-rich manipulation. This approach achieved a 95.0% overall success rate across various tasks, including those with visual disturbances and unseen geometries, demonstrating superior generalization compared to vision-enabled baselines which failed on out-of-distribution tasks.

## Problem

- Contact-rich manipulation tasks are challenging due to complex, discontinuous dynamics and the need for high precision and adaptability in uncertain environments.
- Vision-enabled Reinforcement Learning (RL) policies tend to overfit to specific visual conditions, leading to brittleness and poor generalization when environmental appearances change.
- Training robust vision-free policies from scratch using only proprioceptive inputs for complex contact-rich tasks is sample-inefficient and difficult without visual guidance.

## Method

- A two-stage, real-world Human-in-the-Loop ==Reinforcement Learning== (HIL-RL) framework, **VE2VF**, is used, incorporating a teacher-student distillation process.
- A vision-enabled teacher policy is trained first using rich multimodal inputs (images + proprioceptive state) and HIL-RL to efficiently acquire expert reactive behaviors.
- The knowledge from the teacher is then distilled into a vision-free student policy that relies solely on proprioceptive inputs (pose, twist, wrench), utilizing task-relative pose representations to enhance robustness and prevent spatial overfitting.

## Results

- VE2VF achieved the highest overall success rate of **95.0%** across all evaluated task categories, including disturbed training tasks (**100%** success) and unseen out-of-distribution tasks (e.g., **100%** on USB insertion).
- The vision-enabled teacher policy, while achieving **100%** success on training tasks, completely failed on all out-of-distribution tasks (**0%** success) and significantly degraded under disturbed visual conditions.
- Fine-tuning the VE2VF policy for a challenging task (DSUB insertion) with distillation for 10 minutes resulted in **100%** success, whereas fine-tuning without distillation yielded **0%** success.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision-enabled policies, while effective for initial skill acquisition, exhibit poor generalization and completely fail when visual conditions deviate from training or tasks are out-of-distribution.
> - Distilling an expert vision-enabled teacher into a vision-free student effectively transfers robust reactive behaviors, overcoming visual overfitting while retaining the benefits of efficient visually-guided exploration during training.
> - Combining all proprioceptive modalities (pose, twist, wrench) is crucial for the robustness of the distilled vision-free policy, as learning from proprioception alone without distillation proves inefficient and prone to failure.
