# ReWatch-R1: Boosting Complex Video Reasoning in Large Vision-Language Models through Agentic Data Synthesis

> [!summary] Summary
> Alibaba Group researchers developed ReWatch-R1, a large vision-language model designed to improve complex video reasoning through an agentic data synthesis pipeline and an enhanced reinforcement learning framework. The model achieved state-of-the-art performance across five challenging video reasoning benchmarks and demonstrated improved logical consistency in its reasoning processes.

## Problem

- Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) struggle with complex, multi-step temporal video reasoning tasks despite advancements in image understanding.
- Existing open-source video datasets suffer from poor temporal granularity, lack complexity, and often contain unfaithful Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data, creating a data bottleneck.
- Current reinforcement learning (RL) approaches for video reasoning often rely on coarse final-answer rewards, failing to ensure factual grounding and verifiable intermediate reasoning steps.

## Method

- A multi-stage ==agentic synthesis== pipeline generates the ReWatch dataset, comprising 10k detailed captions, 170k high-difficulty QA pairs, and 135k video-grounded CoT traces.
- A baseline LVLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) is post-trained with Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on the ReWatch dataset to instill foundational video-text alignment and structured reasoning.
- Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Reward (RLVR) is applied, incorporating a novel Observation & Reasoning (O&R) reward mechanism that explicitly evaluates the factual accuracy of observations and the logical sufficiency of reasoning steps, alongside final answer correctness.

## Results

- ReWatch-R1 achieved state-of-the-art performance on five challenging video reasoning benchmarks: VCR-Bench, MINERVA, Video Holmes, VideoMathQA, and CG-AV-Counting.
- The multi-agent synthesized ReWatch-CoT data proved intrinsically superior, with the SFT-only model outperforming many other SOTA models, and the full ReWatch-R1 with O&R reward further boosting performance.
- Ablation studies confirmed the ReWatch-QA dataset's high video dependency (**29.4%** text-only accuracy vs. **68.9%** for baseline data), demonstrating its effectiveness in forcing genuine video understanding and providing a potent reward signal.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality, video-grounded Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data, synthesized through an agentic pipeline, is critical for LVLMs to learn verifiable, evidence-linked reasoning processes directly from video content.
> - The Observation & Reasoning (O&R) reward mechanism effectively guides reinforcement learning to reduce hallucinations and ensure logical consistency by incentivizing both correct outcomes and factually supported intermediate steps.
> - Combining agentic data synthesis with process-oriented reinforcement learning allows LVLMs to internalize sophisticated 'think-and-act' capabilities, leading to more efficient and integrated video understanding during inference.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) has experienced rapid advancements, particularly in image understanding and reasoning. The training paradigm involving Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) combined with Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Reward (RLVR) has been instrumental in boosting image reasoning capabilities. However, this paper identifies a critical gap: the application of these advanced paradigms to complex *video* reasoning remains underdeveloped.

Existing LVLMs, even those incorporating SFT+RLVR for video, frequently underperform on high-difficulty benchmarks that demand multi-step temporal reasoning, such as understanding causality, tracking states over time, and counting events across long video sequences. This deficiency is primarily attributed to a **data bottleneck**. Current open-source video datasets suffer from several limitations:
1. **Poor temporal granularity**: Captions are often holistic and untimestamped, erasing crucial temporal structure.
2. **Lack of complexity**: Questions are typically simple and perception-based, answerable from short clips or even textual priors without genuine video understanding.
3. **Unfaithful Chain-of-Thought (CoT)**: Synthesized CoT data, often bootstrapped from simple QA datasets, tends to rely on commonsense knowledge or process of elimination rather than being faithfully grounded in the visual evidence of the video.

These data limitations prevent SFT from effectively teaching video-grounded reasoning, and the subsequent RL phase struggles to provide a reliable reward signal for process correctness, leading to models that hallucinate or fail to establish logical fidelity.

Prior efforts in video reasoning include:
- **Reinforcement Learning (RL) for Video**: Several recent models have adopted RL/RFT (Reinforced Fine-Tuning) to enhance video reasoning. However, these often use final-answer accuracy as the primary reward and inherit the limitations of weakly grounded CoT and shortcut-prone QA, resulting in coarse rewards that focus on outcomes rather than verifiable intermediate steps.
- **Agentic Methods**: Paradigms like ReAct, which integrate reasoning with tool use, have been extended to long video understanding. These methods enable models to dynamically interact with video during inference to generate grounded reasoning chains. While promising, many are training-free (relying on inference-time tool use) and thus do not internalize these reasoning abilities within the base model. Other agent-based approaches synthesize CoT data for SFT but often generate fixed tool-use trajectories, lacking the iterative "think-and-act" capability seen in humans. The "think with video" paradigm, which dynamically injects video segments, faces challenges related to high context length demands and complex training.

The current work by Alibaba Group directly addresses these limitations by proposing a comprehensive solution. It introduces a novel dataset, ReWatch, explicitly designed to foster advanced video reasoning through high-fidelity, temporally dense, and challenging data. Furthermore, it enhances the RLVR framework with a new **Observation & Reasoning (O&R) reward mechanism** that uniquely evaluates not just the final answer but also the factual grounding and logical sufficiency of the intermediate reasoning steps. By combining sophisticated agentic data synthesis with process-oriented reinforcement learning, ReWatch-R1 aims to overcome the data starvation and reward signal deficiencies that have plagued previous attempts at complex video reasoning, establishing a new paradigm for building inherently more capable and verifiable multimodal models.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of this research is to **significantly boost complex video reasoning capabilities in Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs)**. This ambition is rooted in a clear and pressing motivation: the current struggle of LVLMs to perform multi-step temporal tasks that require deep video understanding, despite advancements in image reasoning.

The core motivation stems from the **critical data bottleneck** identified by the authors. Existing video reasoning datasets lack the necessary characteristics to effectively train and evaluate LVLMs for complex tasks. Specifically, the data often lacks:
- **Challenging, multi-hop questions**: Most questions are simple and can be answered with superficial observation or even textual shortcuts, rather than requiring intricate temporal analysis.
- **High-quality, video-grounded Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data**: The reasoning traces provided are often unfaithful to video content, relying on external knowledge or elimination, thereby failing to teach models how to derive conclusions directly from visual evidence.

To overcome these limitations, the paper sets forth several key objectives:
1. **Introduce ReWatch, a large-scale, high-fidelity dataset**: This dataset is specifically designed to foster advanced video reasoning by providing:
 - **Temporally dense and detailed video descriptions (ReWatch-Caption)**: To serve as a high-fidelity foundation for complex reasoning by capturing precise temporal events.
 - **High-difficulty, video-dependent question-answer pairs (ReWatch-QA)**: To ensure questions cannot be answered by simple perception or textual priors, forcing genuine video understanding.
 - **Video-grounded Chain-of-Thought (ReWatch-CoT)**: To teach models verifiable, evidence-linked reasoning processes directly from video content.
2. **Develop a novel multi-stage agentic synthesis pipeline**: This pipeline is designed to automatically generate the three components of the ReWatch dataset, simulating a human-like "re-watching" process to create rich, structured reasoning data efficiently.
3. **Propose an innovative Observation & Reasoning (O&R) reward mechanism**: To augment the Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Reward (RLVR) framework. This reward aims to explicitly evaluate not only the final answer's correctness but also the factual support of intermediate observations and the logical sufficiency of the reasoning process, thereby directly penalizing hallucination and promoting verifiable evidence-linked reasoning.
4. **Post-train a strong baseline LVLM (ReWatch-R1)**: Using the ReWatch dataset and the enhanced RLVR framework to achieve state-of-the-art performance on a suite of challenging video reasoning benchmarks.

In essence, the authors are motivated to bridge the gap between existing LVLM capabilities and the demands of true complex video understanding by supplying the necessary high-quality, process-oriented training data and a sophisticated reward signal that encourages models to "think" in a video-grounded manner.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology employed in this research centers around a novel two-stage framework: first, the synthesis of a high-quality, large-scale video reasoning dataset called **ReWatch** using an agentic pipeline, and second, the **post-training** of a strong baseline LVLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) using this dataset, incorporating a new reward mechanism for reinforcement learning.

#### 3.1. ReWatch Dataset Construction: A Multi-Stage Agentic Pipeline

The ReWatch dataset is designed to be large, high-fidelity, high-difficulty, and video-grounded. It comprises 10k captions, 170k QA pairs, and 135k CoTs, synthesized through a three-stage pipeline:

**Stage 1: Hierarchical Video Captioning (ReWatch-Caption-10k)**
- **Goal**: To generate temporally dense and semantically rich video descriptions, mitigating hallucination often seen with LVLMs processing long videos.
- **Process**:
 1. **Semantic Segmentation**: A dedicated LVLM (`M_seg`, based on Gemini2.5-Flash) partitions long videos into `k` semantically coherent segments at a low frame rate, preserving event integrity.
 2. **Detailed Description Generation**: For each segment, a powerful LVLM (`M_cap`, also Gemini2.5-Flash) processes it at a high frame rate to generate detailed descriptions, including distinct events and their *relative* timestamps.
 3. **Timestamp Realignment**: Relative timestamps are converted to absolute timestamps by adding the segment's start time.
- **Outcome**: `C_detail(V)`, a union of all timestamped descriptions, providing precise temporal and semantic grounding.

**Stage 2: High-Difficulty QA Pair Generation (ReWatch-QA-170k)**
- **Goal**: To create challenging QA pairs that necessitate fine-grained video analysis, preventing reliance on shortcuts.
- **Process**:
 1. **Contrastive QA Generation**:
 - A lightweight LLM (`M_sum`, Gemini2.5-Flash-Lite) generates a concise summary (`C_sum`) from `C_detail`.
 - A QA generator (`M_qa`, Gemini2.5-Flash) creates raw QA pairs `(Q, A)` that are explicitly answerable from `C_detail` but *not* from `C_sum`. This contrastive approach ensures questions probe fine-grained, video-dependent details.
 - Ten pre-defined question types guide generation for diversity.
 2. **Three-Layer Filtering**: A cascade of filters ensures quality and genuine video-dependency:
 - **Filter 1 (Answer Verification, `F1`)**: A verifier (`M_verify`, GPT-4.1) confirms the factual correctness of `A` based on `C_detail`.
 - **Filter 2 (Text Bias Elimination, `F2`)**: A set of LLMs (`M_probe`, including Qwen3 and Qwen2.5-VL) checks if `Q` is answerable from general knowledge (without video). Questions easily answerable are filtered out.
 - **Filter 3 (Summary Bias Elimination, `F3`)**: Similarly, `M_probe` checks if `Q` is answerable using only `C_sum`.
 3. **QA Rewrite**: The 85k passing QA pairs are rewritten into 170k multiple-choice questions by an LLM (`M_rewrite`, Gemini2.5-Flash).
- **Outcome**: Challenging QA pairs that require genuine video understanding rather than textual priors or general knowledge.

**Stage 3: Multi-Agent Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Synthesis (ReWatch-CoT-135k)**
- **Goal**: To generate video-grounded CoT traces that explicitly document information retrieval and verification, simulating a human's "re-watching" process.
- **Process**: A novel Multi-Agent ReAct framework is used:
 1. **Reasoner Agent (`A_R`)**: A powerful LLM (`Gemini2.5-Flash` in "Thinking" mode) generates thoughts (`T`) and decides on actions (`Act`) based on the question and interaction history.
 2. **Observer Agent (`A_O`)**: A powerful LLM (`GPT-4.1`) executes the actions by querying the `C_detail` (video caption) to return observations (`Obs`).
 3. **Core Actions**: Two key actions simulate visual lookup: `segment_retrieval(query)` (finds event timestamp) and `segment_query(timestamp)` (retrieves detailed description for a timestamp).
 4. **Conversion**: The structured execution trajectory (sequence of T, Act, Obs) is converted into a natural language CoT string (`R`) with explicit `<action>` and `<observation>` tags by an LLM (`M_convert`, Gemini2.5-Flash-Lite), making it suitable for SFT and reward calculation.
- **Outcome**: High-quality, verifiable CoT data that explicitly links reasoning steps to video-grounded evidence.

#### 3.2. Post-Training on ReWatch Dataset

The training proceeds in two stages on a base LVLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B):

**Stage 1: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)**
- **Objective**: To instill foundational video-text alignment, direct QA abilities, and structured step-by-step reasoning.
- **Multi-task Training**: The model is trained on `ReWatch-Caption`, `ReWatch-QA`, and `ReWatch-CoT` concurrently, using distinct instruction prompts to differentiate between response modes (e.g., "thinking" vs. "non-thinking").
 - `L_Cap`: Teaches generation of `C_detail` from videos.
 - `L_QA`: Teaches concise answer generation for direct-answer prompts.
 - `L_CoT`: Teaches generation of full reasoning traces for "think-step-by-step" prompts.
- **Outcome**: ReWatch-R1-SFT, a versatile model proficient in both direct answering and complex reasoning, serving as the initial policy for RL.

**Stage 2: Reinforcement Learning (RLVR) with Observation & Reasoning (O&R) Reward**
- **Algorithm**: GRPO (Generalized Reinforcement Learning for Policy Optimization).
- **Motivation for O&R Reward**: Traditional RL for video reasoning often uses only final answer accuracy (`r_acc`), which overlooks whether reasoning is truly grounded in video content, leading to hallucinations. The O&R reward addresses this by evaluating the *process* of reasoning.
- **O&R Reward Components**:
 1. **Observation Reward (`r_obs`)**: Assesses the factual accuracy of intermediate video observations (`Obs_i`) within the generated CoT by comparing them against the detailed video caption (`C_detail`) using a judge model (`M_judge`, Qwen3-30B).
 2. **Reasoning Reward (`r_rea`)**: Evaluates the sufficiency and validity of the reasoning process. An inference LLM (`M_infer`, Qwen3-30B) attempts to answer the question solely based on the model's parsed actions (`Act_i`) and observations (`Obs_i`). If `M_infer` can provide a correct answer, the reasoning process is deemed valid and sufficient.
 3. **Accuracy Reward (`r_acc`)**: Standard reward for the correctness of the final answer.
 4. **Format Reward (`r_fmt`)**: A binary reward (1 or 0) for adhering to the desired output format (e.g., proper tagging of `<action>`, `<observation>`, `<answer>`).
- **Final Reward Function**: `r_O&R = r_acc * (1 + r_obs + r_rea) + r_fmt`. This formula explicitly incentivizes both correct outcomes and verifiable, video-grounded reasoning steps.
- **Outcome**: ReWatch-R1, a refined LVLM that performs appropriate, video-grounded reasoning, reducing hallucinations and improving logical consistency.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments conducted demonstrate the superior performance and effectiveness of the ReWatch-R1 model and the underlying methodology.

1. **State-of-the-Art Performance in Video Reasoning**:
 - ReWatch-R1 achieves state-of-the-art (SOTA) performance on five challenging video reasoning benchmarks (VCR-Bench, MINERVA, Video Holmes, VideoMathQA, CG-AV-Counting).
 - It substantially outperforms other SOTA LVLMs of comparable size, both in 192-frame and 384-frame settings. For instance, the ReWatch-R1 + O&R variant achieved an average accuracy of 35.51% (192 frames) and 35.78% (384 frames) across these benchmarks, significantly higher than competitors.

2. **Criticality of High-Quality CoT Data**:
 - The SFT-only version, ReWatch-R1-SFT, already surpasses most other models (e.g., Video-R1-SFT, LongVideoReason-SFT) trained with the same configuration, demonstrating the intrinsic superiority of the ReWatch-CoT data synthesized by the multi-agent framework.

3. **Reinforcement Learning Unlocks Further Potential**:
 - The RL phase further boosts performance, with ReWatch-R1 improving upon its SFT-only counterpart. This indicates that while SFT teaches the *form* of CoT, RL, guided by the O&R reward, instills the *spirit* of logical and factually grounded reasoning.

4. **Efficacy of "Thinking" Mode**:
 - Untrained base models experience a performance *drop* when forced into a "thinking" (CoT) mode due to induced hallucinations (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-7B drops from 30.71% to 27.54% average accuracy).
 - In stark contrast, the fully trained ReWatch-R1 excels with CoT, confirming that the methodology successfully teaches the model *how to reason* rather than just producing CoT-like text.

5. **Ablation Studies Confirm Data and SFT Importance**:
 - **SFT is indispensable for RL**: Training without an SFT stage leads to a catastrophic performance drop, highlighting the need for a strong initial policy.
 - **High-quality CoT data is vital**: Replacing ReWatch-CoT with data from Video-R1 significantly degrades performance, validating the superiority of the multi-agent synthesized CoT.
 - **High-quality QA data for RL is crucial**: RL training with only baseline QA data (Video-R1-QA, LongVideoReason-QA) yields lower performance compared to using the challenging ReWatch-QA data, confirming that robust QA provides a more potent reward signal for genuine reasoning.

6. **Dataset Complexity and Video Dependency**:
 - A quantitative comparison of ReWatch-QA and Video-R1-QA shows ReWatch-QA elicits more profound reasoning, requiring nearly double the reasoning steps and significantly longer responses.
 - Critically, Video-R1-QA exhibits a high "Text-Only Accuracy" (68.9%), meaning questions are often solvable without video content. ReWatch-QA's "Text-Only Accuracy" is only 29.4% (near random guessing for multiple-choice), proving the effectiveness of the three-layer filtering in ensuring genuine video dependency.

7. **Evolution of Reasoning Process (Efficiency and Accuracy)**:
 - During SFT, the model learns a structured reasoning format, increasing both action counts and accuracy.
 - During RL, accuracy continues to improve, but the average number of actions *decreases*. This indicates that RL refines the policy for greater efficiency, pruning redundant steps and focusing on critical actions. The model transitions from learning reasoning's *form* (SFT) to mastering its *function* with efficiency (RL).

8. **Impact on Prompting Methods**:
 - During SFT, the "non-thinking" (direct answer) mode improves rapidly, while the "thinking" (CoT) mode develops slowly, suggesting SFT primarily teaches format.
 - The subsequent RL phase acts as a catalyst for the "thinking" mode, leading to a dramatic performance leap, ultimately surpassing the "non-thinking" mode in all tasks. This empirically proves the superiority of explicit, step-by-step reasoning, cultivated by the SFT-RL regimen, for complex video tasks.

9. **Synergistic Improvement in General Video Understanding**:
 - ReWatch-R1 achieves SOTA performance on four general video understanding benchmarks (MMVU, LVBench, VideoMME, VideoMMMU) among comparable models. This indicates that specialized training for complex reasoning not only avoids catastrophic forgetting but also enhances the model's foundational understanding abilities, likely due to the multi-task learning design.

10. **Superior Long-Video Reasoning**:
 - ReWatch-R1 demonstrates a significant advantage in reasoning tasks for long videos (>20 minutes), substantially outperforming other models.
 - While all models show performance degradation on longer videos ("Long Video Tax"), ReWatch-R1 exhibits a more attenuated rate of decline, indicating greater resilience and robustness in handling extended temporal dependencies.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The work presented in "REWATCH-R1: BOOSTING COMPLEX VIDEO REASONING IN LARGE VISION-LANGUAGE MODELS THROUGH AGENTIC DATA SYNTHESIS" marks a significant stride in the field of multimodal AI, with several far-reaching implications and potential impacts:

1. **Advancement in Complex Video Reasoning**: By achieving state-of-the-art performance on a suite of challenging video reasoning benchmarks, ReWatch-R1 demonstrates that LVLMs can move beyond superficial, perception-based video understanding to engage in deeper, multi-hop temporal reasoning. This pushes the frontier of what multimodal models can accomplish with video data.

2. **Addressing the Data Bottleneck through Agentic Synthesis**: The introduction of the ReWatch dataset and its novel multi-stage agentic synthesis pipeline is perhaps the most profound contribution. Data quality and quantity are often the primary bottlenecks in advancing AI research. This work provides a scalable, efficient, and robust framework for generating high-quality, video-grounded, and complex training data (temporally dense captions, challenging QA, and verifiable CoT). This approach could inspire new data generation paradigms across various AI domains, reducing reliance on costly and time-consuming manual annotation for complex tasks.

3. **Refined Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards**: The proposed Observation & Reasoning (O&R) reward mechanism is a crucial innovation for RL in multimodal contexts. By explicitly penalizing hallucination and rewarding the factual grounding and logical sufficiency of intermediate reasoning steps, it offers a more nuanced and effective way to guide model learning. This process-oriented reward system can be generalized to other complex reasoning tasks where verifiable steps are important, enhancing trust and reliability in AI-generated reasoning.

4. **Internalized Agentic Capabilities**: Unlike many existing agentic methods that primarily involve inference-time tool use, ReWatch-R1 demonstrates that combining agentic *data synthesis* with *RLVR* can internalize sophisticated reasoning abilities directly into the base multimodal model. This makes the model inherently more capable of "thinking" and acting in a video-grounded manner without external tool calls during inference, leading to more efficient and integrated intelligence.

5. **Broadening Applicability of LVLMs**: Enhanced complex video reasoning capabilities have immense potential across numerous real-world applications:
 - **Video Content Analysis**: More accurate and detailed content indexing, summarization, and retrieval for vast video libraries in media, entertainment, and surveillance.
 - **Intelligent Assistants**: Developing more sophisticated AI assistants that can understand and respond to complex queries about video content, whether in personal archives or streaming services.
 - **Education and Training**: Creating AI tutors that can analyze instructional videos, answer detailed questions, and provide step-by-step explanations based on visual demonstrations.
 - **Autonomous Systems**: Improving situational awareness and decision-making for robots, drones, and self-driving cars by enabling deeper understanding of dynamic environments from video streams.
 - **Medical Imaging**: Potentially assisting in the analysis of medical videos (e.g., surgical footage, diagnostic scans) for identifying subtle events or complex causal relationships.
 - **Security and Monitoring**: Enhancing threat detection and anomaly recognition by enabling AI to understand intricate event sequences in surveillance footage.

6. **Guidance for Future Research**: This work lays a strong foundation for future research avenues, including:
 - Exploring even more sophisticated multi-agent architectures for data synthesis.
 - Developing novel reward functions that incorporate deeper cognitive principles for RL.
 - Investigating the transferability of these reasoning skills to other modalities or cross-domain video tasks.
 - Improving the efficiency and scalability of training these highly capable models.

In conclusion, ReWatch-R1 represents a significant step forward in making LVLMs truly proficient in understanding and reasoning about complex video content. By strategically tackling the data bottleneck and refining reinforcement learning techniques, this research opens up new possibilities for AI applications and advanced intelligent systems.
