# Exploiting Both Domain-specific and Invariant Knowledge via a Win-win Transformer for Unsupervised Domain Adaptation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Beijing Institute of Technology, Tsinghua University, and Inceptio Tech developed WinTR, a Transformer-based framework for unsupervised domain adaptation that simultaneously leverages domain-specific and domain-invariant knowledge. This method achieved state-of-the-art results on VisDA-2017 (90.1%), Office-Home (77.2%), and DomainNet (46.8%) benchmarks, outperforming previous Transformer and CNN-based approaches.

## Problem

- Existing Unsupervised Domain Adaptation (UDA) methods struggle to simultaneously leverage domain-specific information and transfer domain-invariant knowledge without compromise.
- Traditional UDA often relies on a shared classifier that biases towards the source domain, leading to sub-optimal performance on the target domain.
- Many UDA approaches built on CNNs face limitations in transferability due to strong inductive biases, motivating the exploration of Vision Transformers.

## Method

- Introduces ==WinTR==, a Vision Transformer-based framework employing two distinct ==classification tokens ([src] and [tgt])== with a domain-wise mask to learn separate source-oriented and target-oriented representations.
- Utilizes two separate ==domain-specific classifiers== (g_s and g_t) for the source and target domains, trained with ground truth and pseudo-labels respectively.
- Implements ==bi-directional cross-domain knowledge transfer== through single-sided contrastive feature alignment with stop-gradient regularization, and refines target pseudo-labels using source-oriented target features.

## Results

- Achieved new state-of-the-art average accuracy on VisDA-2017 with **90.1%** (WinTR-B), surpassing CDTrans-B (**88.4%**) and CNN-based FixBi (**87.2%**).
- Obtained **77.2%** average accuracy on the Office-Home dataset (WinTR-S), outperforming CDTrans-S (**74.7%**) and other strong baselines.
- Reached **46.8%** average accuracy on the challenging DomainNet dataset (WinTR-B), a substantial improvement over CDTrans-B (**37.0%**) and the DeiT-B backbone (**34.6%**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision Transformers exhibit superior inherent transferability compared to CNNs, making them a strong backbone for UDA tasks.
> - Explicitly disentangling and exploiting both domain-specific and domain-invariant knowledge via dedicated tokens and classifiers within a Transformer architecture leads to a synergistic "win-win" outcome in UDA.
> - Source-oriented target features, supervised by clean source labels, retain finer structural relationships that are beneficial for refining noisy target pseudo-labels.
