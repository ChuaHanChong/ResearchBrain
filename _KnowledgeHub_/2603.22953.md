# Cluster-Wise Spatio-Temporal Masking for Efficient Video-Language Pretraining

> [!summary] Summary
> ClusterSTM, a framework developed by researchers at Harbin Institute of Technology, introduces a novel masking strategy that combines intra-frame clustering with temporal-density-based token selection and a video-text relevance reconstruction objective. This approach enhances video-language pretraining efficiency by mitigating visual information loss and temporal leakage, achieving state-of-the-art performance with improvements like 1.4% Recall@1 on MSRVTT for zero-shot text-to-video retrieval.

## Problem

- Large-scale video-language model pretraining is computationally expensive, hindering widespread application and further development.
- Existing masked visual modeling strategies for video-language tasks suffer from significant visual information loss at high masking ratios due to short training schedules.
- Masked video modeling faces temporal information leakage, where masked tokens are easily reconstructed from adjacent frames, undermining the effectiveness of representation learning.

## Method

- **ClusterSTM** proposes a masking strategy that uses ==intra-frame clustering== to select semantically diverse tokens and ==temporal-density-based filtering== to ensure strong temporal correlation among retained tokens, even with high masking ratios.
- A ==video-text relevance reconstruction== objective is introduced, training the student model to reconstruct high-level video-text relevance matrices for masked regions, generated by a teacher model (**SigLIP**).
- The pretraining objective integrates this ==masked relevance modeling loss== with standard ==video-text contrastive==, ==video-text matching==, and ==masked language modeling== losses.

## Results

- ClusterSTM achieved new state-of-the-art results in zero-shot text-to-video retrieval, improving Recall@1 on MSRVTT by **1.4%**, ActivityNet by **0.8%**, and MSVD by **1.6%**.
- It also established new state-of-the-art performance in fine-tuned text-to-video retrieval (e.g., MSRVTT R@1 increased by **1.2%** to **49.7%**) and video captioning (e.g., MSRVTT CIDEr increased by **0.5** to **64.4**).
- Ablation studies confirmed the cluster-wise spatio-temporal masking and the video-text relevance reconstruction target are critical components, with the latter providing the most substantial performance gains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Intra-frame clustering enables the preservation of holistic visual content by ensuring selected tokens represent diverse semantic regions within each frame.
> - Leveraging temporal density for token selection effectively mitigates temporal information leakage by favoring tokens with strong semantic correlation across adjacent frames.
> - Reconstructing high-level video-text relevance, rather than low-level visual features, directly fosters robust multimodal semantic alignment, leading to better performance in cross-modal tasks.
