# Thinking with Camera: A Unified Multimodal Model for Camera-Centric Understanding and Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Puffin, a unified multimodal model, integrates camera-centric understanding and generation by interpreting camera parameters as a "first-class modality" within a linguistic reasoning framework. The model demonstrates superior performance in both estimating camera parameters from images and generating visual content with precise viewpoint control, achieving enhanced spatial intelligence.

## Problem

- Camera understanding (e.g., pose estimation) and camera-controllable generation (e.g., text-to-image with viewpoint control) were traditionally treated as isolated tasks, limiting mutual benefits.
- Existing large multimodal models (LMMs) struggled with precise geometric control, often ignoring or misinterpreting abstract numerical camera parameters, leading to spatially inconsistent outputs.
- A scarcity of large-scale, high-quality datasets linking vision, language, and detailed camera parameters hindered progress in multimodal spatial intelligence.

## Method

- Developed ==Puffin==, a unified model combining a ==geometry-aligned vision encoder==, a large language model, and a diffusion model, connected by an adaptive module.
- Introduced the =="thinking with camera" paradigm==, which processes camera parameters as language, maps them to professional photographic terms, and uses structured textual reasoning to bridge the modality gap.
- Constructed ==Puffin-4M==, a 4 million vision-language-camera triplet dataset with detailed camera parameters, scene captions, and spatial reasoning annotations, built through a multi-stage process.

## Results

- Puffin significantly outperforms state-of-the-art specialized methods in camera understanding, achieving lower median angular errors and higher AUC scores on benchmarks like MegaDepth and Puffin-Und.
- Demonstrates high spatial consistency and realism in camera-controllable image generation, drastically outperforming powerful multimodal models (e.g., GPT-4o) and specialized generators like PreciseCam.
- Exhibits versatile adaptability to downstream cross-view tasks, including spatial imagination, world exploration, and photography guidance, extending LMM capabilities to tasks like **3D object insertion**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating camera parameters as a "first-class modality" and leveraging a linguistic reasoning paradigm ("thinking with camera") enables LMMs to robustly interpret and generate scenes with precise geometric control.
> - Pixel-wise continuous camera maps are critical for fine-grained spatial control during image generation, preventing geometric distortions and inverted spatial illusions under challenging camera configurations.
> - Jointly optimizing camera understanding and generation within a unified framework leads to mutual enhancement, with auxiliary generation losses implicitly improving geometric perception for understanding tasks.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The research on spatial intelligence in machines, particularly concerning camera interactions with the physical world, typically segregates into two distinct objectives: camera understanding and camera-centric generation. Camera understanding, which involves inferring camera geometry (e.g., intrinsic and extrinsic parameters) from images, is fundamental for tasks such as 3D reconstruction, spatial awareness, and navigation in environments pertinent to robotics, augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and autonomous driving. Concurrently, camera-centric generation focuses on synthesizing visual content by controlling camera parameters, allowing for viewpoint manipulation and scene composition.

Historically, these two areas have been studied in isolation, with specialized models developed for each task. Recent advancements in large multimodal models (LMMs) and unified multimodal models have demonstrated success in general image understanding and generation by aligning visual and linguistic modalities. However, these models typically operate under simplified camera assumptions (e.g., fixed front-view, predefined fields-of-view) and struggle with the abstract, non-intuitive nature of numerical camera parameters. This creates a "modality gap" where LMMs may prioritize semantic alignment over precise spatial control or geometric detail, leading to spatially inconsistent outputs.

This work addresses this fragmentation by proposing a unified framework that seamlessly integrates camera-centric understanding and generation. It extends the LMM paradigm to the spatial domain, explicitly incorporating camera geometry as a first-class modality. The paper introduces a novel approach to bridge the modality gap by conceptualizing "camera as language," allowing LMMs to perform spatially grounded reasoning using photographic terminology. This integration aims to overcome the limitations of both isolated specialized models and existing LMMs, offering a more holistic approach to spatial intelligence.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to unify camera-centric understanding and generation within a single, cohesive multimodal framework. This unification is termed "Puffin" and aims to extend spatial awareness along the camera dimension, allowing machines to interpret and create scenes from arbitrary viewpoints.

The motivation behind pursuing this objective is multi-faceted:

- **Bridging Isolated Fields:** Current research typically treats camera understanding (e.g., calibration, pose estimation) and camera-controllable generation as separate problems. This segregation leads to fragmented solutions and hinders the development of comprehensive spatial intelligence systems that can both perceive and interact with their environment coherently. The authors contend that these two tasks are complementary, with understanding decoding the world's geometry and generation encoding it into visual content.
- **Addressing Modality Gap in LMMs:** While LMMs have advanced general image understanding and generation, they are constrained in handling precise camera parameters. Numerical camera values (e.g., "20° roll," "35mm lens") are abstract and non-intuitive, creating a modality gap with the semantic-focused nature of vision-language models. Existing LMMs often neglect or misinterpret these cues, resulting in a lack of precise spatial control in generation and coarse geometric understanding. The motivation is to overcome this by treating camera information as a form of language that LMMs can inherently reason with.
- **Enhancing Spatial Reasoning:** Traditional vision-based methods for camera understanding often rely on low- or mid-level geometric patterns, which can limit their ability to capture holistic spatial concepts or generalize across diverse environments. Similarly, image generation models struggle to faithfully simulate diverse spatial distributions due to biases in training data (e.g., preference for near-level shots) and the inherent difficulty of altering perceived gravity through roll adjustments. The paper aims to improve spatial reasoning by aligning visual cues with photographic terminology and integrating geometric context, enabling more robust and accurate camera parameter prediction and scene synthesis.
- **Enabling Versatile 3D Applications:** A unified model that masters both camera understanding and generation can unlock a range of advanced 3D applications. These include improved virtual 3D object insertion, enhanced spatial imagination (predicting unseen views), world exploration, and providing intelligent photographic guidance, which are currently challenging to achieve with fragmented or spatially naive models.
- **Establishing Comprehensive Benchmarks:** The scarcity of large-scale datasets that integrate vision, language, and camera modalities limits progress in multimodal spatial intelligence. A key motivation is to construct such a dataset and establish a benchmark to facilitate standardized evaluation and encourage further research in this unified domain.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The Puffin model unifies camera-centric understanding and generation within a multimodal paradigm, extending spatial awareness along the camera dimension.

#### 3.1. Puffin Architecture

Puffin combines autoregressive modeling for understanding tasks and diffusion-based generation. It uses a geometry-aligned vision encoder, a large language model (LLM), and a diffusion model, connected by a learnable module.

- **Geometry-Aligned Vision Encoder:** To address the limitations of standard LMM vision encoders (which yield condensed features lacking geometric fidelity), Puffin employs a geometry-aligned vision encoder. This encoder is distilled from both semantic (e.g., CLIP, SigLIP) and vision-centric (e.g., DINO, SAM) teachers. This design ensures that rich geometric features are preserved alongside strong semantic understanding, crucial for spatial analysis. This encoder is then aligned with an LLM (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct).
- **Camera Understanding Module:** This is formulated as a question-answering task, where the model generates text containing a scene description, spatial reasoning, and estimated camera parameters (roll, pitch, FoV).
 - **Thinking with Camera Paradigm:** This novel mechanism treats "camera as language" to bridge the modality gap. It guides the LMM to align spatially grounded visual cues with professional photographic terminology while reasoning over geometric context.
 - **Spatially Grounded Visual Cues:** The model leverages inherent physical laws and spatial regularities (e.g., sky, ground, object scale) that implicitly encode vertical regularities for pitch or spatial composition for FoV. These cues are embedded into "thinking captions."
 - **Professional Photographic Terms:** Numerical camera parameters are mapped to quantized, semantic photographic terms (e.g., "close-up," "tilt-up," "Dutch angle") (Table A1). These terms are more aligned with LMMs' knowledge and serve as intermediate supervisory signals.
 - **Geometric Context:** Camera parameters (roll, pitch, FoV) are decoupled, allowing the model to link specific visual cues (e.g., sky for pitch, foreground-background ratio for FoV) with corresponding photographic terminology. The final parameters are predicted after this structured spatial reasoning, which is enclosed in `<think>` and `<answer>` tags in the model's output.
- **Camera-Controllable Generation Module:** This module synthesizes images based on textual descriptions and precise camera parameters.
 - **Thinking for Generation:** Similar to understanding, the model employs a "thinking" process. It infers potential visual cues from captions and uses this textual reasoning as a semantic planning stage. Numerical parameters are translated into photographic terms to guide image synthesis.
 - **Flexible and Faithful Control:** Beyond discrete camera tokens derived from numerical parameters, Puffin incorporates pixel-wise camera maps (specifically, a Perspective Field, Equation 1) as continuous latent representations. These dense maps encode local geometric context (orientation, displacement cues) at each pixel, providing fine-grained spatial control to the diffusion model.
 - **Diffusion Model Integration:** A connector module, comprising six transformer layers with 64 learnable queries, acts as an adaptive interface between the LLM and the diffusion model (SD3-Medium). This module extracts and restructures LLM hidden representations, projecting them into conditioning signals for the diffusion model, ensuring semantic and geometric understanding guides generation.

#### 3.2. Puffin-4M Dataset Construction

To support the unified framework, the authors constructed Puffin-4M, a large-scale dataset of 4 million vision-language-camera triplets.

- **Panoramic Data Collection and Preprocessing:** Approximately 200K high-quality panoramic images were collected from public datasets and online platforms, with resolutions up to 10K. Geometric correction techniques (line segmentation, vanishing point estimation) were applied to align panoramas with gravity and improve structural consistency.
- **Perspective Image Generation:** Perspective images were synthesized from the panoramas using a pinhole camera model. Roll, pitch (within [-45°, 45°]), and vertical FoV (within [20°, 105°]) were uniformly sampled. Corresponding camera parameters were converted into pixel-wise Perspective Fields (camera maps) encoding up-vector and latitude angle (Equation 1).
- **Scene and Spatial Reasoning Captioning:**
 - **Scene Captions:** Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct generated semantically rich captions, which were then refined into shorter, vivid descriptions using Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.
 - **Spatial Reasoning Captions:** A two-step strategy was used: (1) mapping numerical camera parameters to professional photographic terms (Table A1), and (2) using these terms to retrieve and prompt relevant visual concepts from an LMM's (Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct) prior knowledge, generating explicit spatial reasoning captions.
- **Cross-View Extension:** The dataset was enriched with cross-view data for tasks like spatial imagination, world exploration, and photographic guidance. For cross-view images, the yaw angle was also sampled (0-360°). Photographic guidance involved defining aesthetic criteria (viewpoint creativity, subject emphasis, compositional balance, spatial harmonization), and an LMM (Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct) was used to score and suggest optimal pitch/yaw offsets from an initial view.

#### 3.3. Training Recipe

Puffin is trained using a multi-stage strategy (Table 1):
1. **Stage I - Alignment:** Align the vision encoder with the LLM (training MLP projector for understanding) and bridge the LLM with the diffusion model (training learnable queries and connector for generation). Backbones remain frozen.
2. **Stage II - SFT:** Supervised fine-tuning of the entire framework, unfreezing all modules except the VAE.
3. **Stage III - SFT w/ Thinking:** Joint optimization incorporating spatial reasoning captions, enabling the model to learn textual reasoning alongside understanding and generation.
4. **Stage IV - Instruction Tuning:** Adapt the model to diverse cross-view tasks (spatial imagination, world exploration, photographic guidance) using specific instruction prompts. The vision encoder is frozen during this stage.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research presents several key findings across camera understanding, camera-controllable generation, and the unified framework's capabilities.

#### 4.1. Camera Understanding

- **Quantitative Performance:** Puffin demonstrates superior performance compared to specialized learning-based (e.g., GeoCalib, ParamNet, UVP) and traditional camera calibration methods (e.g., SVA). On the MegaDepth and Puffin-Und datasets, Puffin achieves lower median angular errors and higher AUC scores for roll, pitch, and FoV estimation (Table 3). For instance, on Puffin-Und, Puffin's median errors are 0.41° (roll), 0.74° (pitch), and 1.21° (FoV), significantly outperforming GeoCalib (0.92°, 2.18°, 5.04°).
- **Robustness:** The model shows robustness in challenging scenarios, including architectural scenes, indoor environments, and cases with limited geometric features or significantly tilted camera poses (Figure A5).
- **Impact of "Thinking with Camera":** Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of the "thinking with camera" paradigm. By aligning spatially grounded visual cues with photographic terms, performance consistently improves across all parameters, particularly for pitch and FoV, which rely more on broader contextual priors (Table 5).
- **Vision Encoder Choice:** Directly fine-tuning existing LMMs (e.g., InternVL3, Qwen2.5-VL) for camera understanding underperforms, indicating that their vision encoders often lack sufficient geometric fidelity. Puffin's geometry-aligned vision encoder, coupled with the LLM, effectively integrates geometric perception and contextual understanding (Table 5).

#### 4.2. Camera-Controllable Generation

- **Quantitative Performance:** Puffin significantly outperforms state-of-the-art specialized generation models (PreciseCam) and general multimodal models (GPT-4o, Qwen-Image, Nano Banana) in generating images with accurate camera parameters. On the Puffin-Gen benchmark, Puffin achieves substantially lower mean and median errors for up-vector, latitude, and gravity estimation, along with a better FID score (69.46 vs. PreciseCam's 90.91) for visual quality (Table 4).
- **Spatial Consistency and Diversity:** Unlike general LMMs that produce high-quality but spatially inconsistent images, Puffin generates images with spatially consistent layouts under specific camera configurations. It also avoids the monotonous stylized outputs often produced by PreciseCam, demonstrating better generalization across diverse scenarios and camera settings (Figure 6, A7).
- **Challenges in Roll Simulation:** An in-depth analysis reveals that existing generation models struggle more with roll simulation compared to pitch. This is attributed to aesthetic biases in training data (photographers prefer near-level shots) and the fundamental way roll alters perceived gravity, which is harder for models to simulate accurately (Figure 7).
- **Impact of Camera Map:** The inclusion of pixel-wise camera maps as a continuous representation of camera geometry is crucial for precise geometric encoding. Without it, generated images exhibit severe geometric distortions and inverted spatial illusions under challenging configurations (Figure 8, right).

#### 4.3. Unified Training and Applications

- **Mutual Benefits of Unified Training:** Jointly optimizing the LLM and diffusion model for both understanding and generation tasks yields mutual benefits. Training the understanding component in isolation underperforms, as the generation process provides auxiliary diffusion loss that implicitly enhances geometric perception. While generation gains are less pronounced, improvements are observed in challenging scenarios like FoV simulation, which benefits from holistic spatial understanding (Figure 9).
- **Multi-turn Interleaved Capability:** Puffin demonstrates the ability to engage in multi-turn interleaved conversations, performing coherent generation based on previous understanding, and accurate understanding based on its own generated images (Figure A3).
- **Downstream Applications:** The instruction-tuned Puffin model supports various cross-view applications:
 - **Virtual 3D Object Insertion:** Accurately predicting camera parameters enables seamless integration of 3D objects into images.
 - **Spatial Imagination:** The model can imagine and describe target views given an initial view and camera parameters.
 - **World Exploration:** It can generate target views, incorporating yaw deviations and conditioning on camera maps and initial views.
 - **Photographic Guidance:** Puffin can suggest camera parameter adjustments for improved photographic aesthetics (Figure 10, A9, A10, A11).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

This work presents a significant advancement in multimodal spatial intelligence by unifying camera-centric understanding and generation. The core significance and potential impacts are:

- **Paradigm Shift for Spatial Intelligence:** By demonstrating the feasibility and benefits of a unified framework for camera understanding and generation, the research proposes a paradigm shift from isolated problem-solving to a holistic approach. This unification means machines can both decode and encode camera geometry, leading to more robust and interactive spatial reasoning.
- **Bridging the Modality Gap:** The novel "thinking with camera" mechanism, which interprets abstract camera parameters as language and aligns them with photographic terminology and spatially grounded visual cues, effectively bridges the modality gap between camera geometry and vision-language models. This enables LMMs to achieve a fine-grained, physically interpretable understanding of space.
- **Foundation for Advanced Multimodal Models:** Puffin establishes a strong foundation for future multimodal models that require precise spatial awareness and control. By integrating geometry deeply into the LMM architecture, it pushes the boundaries of what these models can achieve in real-world environments.
- **Creation of a Comprehensive Dataset and Benchmark:** The construction of Puffin-4M, a large-scale dataset of 4 million vision-language-camera triplets with spatial reasoning annotations and cross-view capabilities, is a substantial contribution. It provides a standardized resource for training and evaluating camera-centric multimodal models, addressing a critical gap in existing datasets.
- **Enabling Diverse Real-World Applications:** The demonstrated ability to generalize to diverse downstream tasks such as virtual 3D object insertion, spatial imagination, world exploration, and photography guidance highlights the practical utility and versatility of the unified model. This has direct implications for advancements in fields like AR/VR, robotics, autonomous navigation, virtual content creation, and professional photography tools.
- **Advancing Human-Machine Interaction:** A model that can "think with camera" allows for more intuitive and precise control over visual content, mirroring human perception and photographic intent. This could lead to more natural and effective human-machine interfaces for spatial tasks.
- **Open Science Contribution:** The commitment to releasing the code, models, dataset pipeline, and benchmark facilitates further research and development in multimodal spatial intelligence, fostering community collaboration and accelerating progress in the field.

In conclusion, Puffin represents a step towards creating machines that can reason about and interact with spatial information in a manner that integrates both perception and generation, moving closer to a comprehensive understanding of the physical world through the lens of a camera.
