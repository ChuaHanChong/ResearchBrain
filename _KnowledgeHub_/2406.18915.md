# Manipulate-Anything: Automating Real-World Robots using Vision-Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> MANIPULATE-ANYTHING is a Vision-Language Model-driven framework that automates real-world robot manipulation by generating high-quality demonstration data and enabling zero-shot task execution. It achieved up to 22% higher average zero-shot success rates in simulation and yielded behavior cloning policies that performed comparably to human-trained policies, with only a 0.27% average performance difference.

## Problem

- Robotics faces a significant "dearth of data" for training, with traditional human teleoperation being costly and unscalable.
- Prior VLM-based approaches for robotic manipulation often rely on privileged state information or hand-designed skills, limiting real-world applicability and object generalization.
- Developing robust and generalizable robotic policies for diverse, unstructured environments remains a challenge.

## Method

- A modular framework leveraging VLMs and LLMs for autonomous task plan generation, multi-viewpoint action generation, and sub-task verification.
- Integrates multi-viewpoint processing to enhance VLM reasoning, allowing selection of optimal perspectives for object interaction and task verification.
- Implements a VLM-based sub-task verification module with an error recovery mechanism to detect failures and trigger re-planning for robustness.

## Results

- Achieved an average task-averaged margin of up to **22%** higher zero-shot success rates over strong baselines in simulation, covering all 14 RLBench tasks without privileged state information.
- Behavior cloning policies trained on MANIPULATE-ANYTHING data performed comparably to those trained on human-scripted demonstrations (**0.27%** average difference) and significantly outperformed policies from other automated data generation methods.
- Demonstrated an average zero-shot success rate of **38.57%** across 7 real-world tasks, outperforming a baseline by **38%**, and showing improved robustness for trained policies in physical environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Automated generation of robotic demonstration data using VLMs can produce trajectories comparable to or even superior to human-collected data for behavior cloning.
> - Vision-Language Models can enable robust zero-shot manipulation directly on physical robots without privileged state information or a library of hand-designed skills.
> - Incorporating error recovery through sub-task verification and multi-viewpoint reasoning is crucial for enhancing robustness and success rates in real-world robotic tasks.
