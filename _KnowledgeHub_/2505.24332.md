# DeepDiver: Adaptive Search Intensity Scaling via Open-Web Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Huawei Language Model Lab introduces DeepDiver, an RL-driven framework, and WebPuzzle, a new open-web benchmark, enabling large language models to dynamically adjust their web search intensity for complex information-seeking. DeepDiver-enhanced 7B-parameter models achieved 37.6-38.1% accuracy on WebPuzzle, matching or exceeding the performance of significantly larger models like the 671B-parameter DeepSeek-R1 (37.1%) and outperforming existing Wiki-based methods.

## Problem

- Large language models (LLMs) struggle with complex information-seeking in open-web environments due to limitations in existing prompting and supervised fine-tuning methods.
- Current benchmarks, largely based on structured, 'clean' datasets like Wikipedia, fail to accurately assess LLMs' ability to navigate noisy, inconsistent, and conflicting information from the live internet.
- Prior reinforcement learning (RL) approaches for LLMs are predominantly trained and evaluated on well-structured datasets, limiting their generalizability to realistic, open-ended information-seeking scenarios.

## Method

- The research introduces ==WebPuzzle==, a novel 24,000-sample training and 275-sample human-annotated test dataset, specifically designed to evaluate LLMs' information seeking on the live internet.
- **DeepDiver**, an RL-driven search and reasoning framework, is developed to cultivate Search Intensity Scaling (SIS) in LLMs, allowing dynamic adjustment of web search frequency and depth based on problem complexity.
- The framework utilizes a curriculum-based approach, combining cold-start supervised fine-tuning (SFT) with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) for iterative RAG and a two-stage reward system (loose then strict graders, plus extra search call rewards).

## Results

- DeepDiver-enhanced **7B** models achieved 37.6-**38.1%** accuracy on WebPuzzle, improving significantly over cold-start SFT (27.9-**30.3%**) and matching or exceeding the performance of much larger models like the **671B**-parameter DeepSeek-R1 (**37.1%**).
- Increased search intensity (more search calls and reasoning rounds) during RL training correlated with performance improvements, with DeepDiver models averaging significantly more search and reasoning rounds per example than SFT baselines.
- DeepDiver demonstrated strong generalization capabilities on out-of-distribution wiki-based problems and open-ended tasks (e.g., **32.72%** on ProxyQA), often outperforming prior Wiki-based methods even when evaluated across language barriers (e.g., **26.1%** on WebPuzzle-en vs. DeepResearcher **15.0%**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement learning can foster emergent adaptive behaviors like Search Intensity Scaling (SIS) in LLMs, enabling them to proactively use external tools to compensate for internal knowledge limitations, even after direct search incentives fade.
> - A carefully designed curriculum, transitioning from a loose to a stricter reward function, is crucial for stabilizing early RL training and effectively overcoming performance plateaus in complex, real-world information-seeking tasks.
> - Training on an open-web dataset like WebPuzzle promotes strong generalization, allowing models to perform well not only on complex real-world queries but also on out-of-distribution wiki-based problems and open-ended generation tasks.
