# EmotionThinker: Prosody-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Explainable Speech Emotion Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> EmotionThinker is a framework that enables explainable speech emotion reasoning by reformulating emotion understanding as a deep reasoning problem. It integrates prosody-aware learning with reinforcement learning, achieving an overall average accuracy of 68.89% for emotion recognition and generating high-quality, acoustically grounded explanations with an average reasoning score of 3.98.

## Problem

- Current SpeechLLMs and SER systems primarily offer categorical emotion predictions without providing interpretable explanations for their decisions.
- A scarcity of high-quality, fine-grained acoustic annotations and Chain-of-Thought style reasoning datasets exists for training explainable emotion models.
- Existing foundation models exhibit limited sensitivity to critical prosodic cues, hindering reliable emotion reasoning, and standard rule-based RL rewards are insufficient for supervising complex, open-ended reasoning processes.

## Method

- Construction of ==EmotionCoT==-35K, a 35,000-sample, prosody-aware Chain-of-Thought dataset, using an automated pipeline to extract speaker traits, prosodic features, and transcriptions, then prompting GPT-4o for reasoning traces.
- Prosody-centric Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) of the Qwen2.5-Omni-7B backbone to create **EmotionThinker**-Base, enhancing its perception of word-level stress, prosodic attributes, and comparative prosodic patterns.
- Application of Reinforcement Learning (RL) using GRPO-PTR (Progressive Trust-aware Reasoning Reward) with a custom-trained reasoning reward model and a trustworthiness weight to supervise both outcome accuracy and the quality of generated explanations.

## Results

- EmotionThinker achieved state-of-the-art emotion recognition accuracy, with an overall average of **68.89%** across IEMOCAP, MELD, RAVDESS, and SAVEE datasets, outperforming the next best model by approximately **3%**.
- The framework produced superior emotion reasoning quality, obtaining an average GPT-based rating of 3.98 across four dimensions (Factual Alignment, Interpretative Quality, Caption Completeness, Fluency/Structural Clarity), surpassing all baselines.
- Prosody-centric SFT significantly improved the model's sensitivity to acoustic features, with pitch perception accuracy rising from **25.71%** to **75.11%** in dedicated tests, validating the effectiveness of the training approach.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly enhancing a model's prosody perception through targeted SFT is crucial for accurate and perceptually grounded speech emotion reasoning.
> - A custom-trained reasoning reward model, coupled with a trustworthiness weight, effectively guides RL toward generating high-quality, factually aligned explanations and mitigates reward hacking.
> - Progressive introduction of complex reasoning rewards stabilizes RL training, allowing the model to first build foundational task performance before optimizing for open-ended reasoning quality.
