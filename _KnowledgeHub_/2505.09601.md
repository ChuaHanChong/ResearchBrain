# Real2Render2Real: Scaling Robot Data Without Dynamics Simulation or Robot Hardware

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from UC Berkeley and Toyota Research Institute introduce Real2Render2Real (R2R2R), a framework that generates diverse robot training data from smartphone videos without requiring dynamics simulation or robot hardware, achieving 51 demonstrations per minute compared to 1.7 via human teleoperation while matching teleoperation-trained policy performance.

## Problem

- Scaling robot learning datasets is bottlenecked by slow, costly human teleoperation and inaccurate physics simulations
- Current robot datasets are orders of magnitude smaller than those used for language and vision models

## Method

- Convert smartphone scans and demonstration videos into 3D assets and trajectories using ==Gaussian Splatting==
- Generate diverse robot executions through trajectory interpolation and parallelized rendering
- Synthesize physically grounded observation-action pairs without dynamics simulation

## Results

- Generates demonstrations **30x** faster than human teleoperation (51 vs **1.7** demos/minute)
- Matches or exceeds performance of policies trained on human teleoperation data
- Success rates scale predictably with dataset size across multiple manipulation tasks
- Demonstrates effectiveness on object picking, multi-object interactions, and articulated manipulation

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Treating objects as kinematic rather than dynamic bodies avoids complex contact modeling challenges
> - Trajectory interpolation enables adaptation to new start/end poses while preserving semantic intent
> - Visual diversity in rendering improves policy learning but excessive appearance perturbation can be harmful
