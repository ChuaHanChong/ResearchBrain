# CaMeRL: Collision-Aware and Memory-Enhanced Reinforcement Learning for UAV Navigation in Multi-Scale Obstacle Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> CaMeRL, a reinforcement learning framework, facilitates robust UAV navigation in environments with obstacles of diverse scales by integrating collision-aware perception and temporal memory, demonstrating superior success rates across a range of obstacle sizes, including ultra-small and extra-large scenarios, and successful real-world deployment.

## Problem

- Existing learning-based UAV navigation methods inadequately address the challenges posed by obstacles of widely varying scales, focusing more on obstacle density or number.
- Small obstacles are difficult for onboard depth sensors to reliably detect and avoid due to their fine granularity, leading to potential collisions.
- Large obstacles frequently occlude the UAV's field of view, causing partial observability and hindering effective pathfinding and detouring decisions.

## Method

- The framework incorporates a Collision-aware Representation Learning module that uses a VAE to extract safety-relevant latent features from raw depth images, supervised by pre-processed collision-aware depth maps.
- A Memory-enhanced Representation Learning module, using an LSTM, processes sequential latent vectors to integrate temporal context, mitigating issues caused by partial observability from large obstacles.
- A policy network, trained with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), uses the combined collision-aware and memory-enhanced observations to generate high-level UAV control commands.

## Results

- CaMeRL consistently achieved the highest success rates in all simulated test environments, notably improving performance in ultra-small obstacle environments (0.77 success rate, compared to MAVRL's 0.29) and extra-large obstacle environments (0.72 success rate, compared to MAVRL's 0.44).
- Ablation studies confirmed that both the collision-aware representation and the temporal memory modules synergistically contribute to the enhanced navigation performance across different obstacle scales.
- The system successfully demonstrated real-world navigation on a physical UAV in a dense forest environment featuring multi-scale obstacles, avoiding both slender poles and thick tree trunks at an average speed of approximately 1.4 m/s.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly designing mechanisms for both fine-grained collision awareness and broader spatial context reasoning (via temporal memory) is critical for unified robust navigation across multi-scale obstacle environments.
> - Utilizing a collision-aware preprocessing step to generate supervision targets for representation learning effectively guides the model to encode safety-relevant features without needing runtime computation for safety margins.
> - The optimal temporal interval for memory integration plays a crucial role, with moderate intervals providing the best balance of historical context and relevance for decision-making.
