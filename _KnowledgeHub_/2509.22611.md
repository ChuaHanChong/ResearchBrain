# Quantile Advantage Estimation: Stabilizing RLVR for LLM Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Quantile Advantage Estimation (QAE) introduces a K-quantile baseline to dynamically regulate policy entropy in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) for Large Language Models, preventing both entropy collapse and explosion. It consistently improved pass@1 accuracy across various LLM sizes on mathematical reasoning benchmarks while stabilizing training dynamics.

## Problem

- Policy instability in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) for LLMs, characterized by both entropy collapse (premature sharpening) and entropy explosion (uncontrolled stochasticity).
- The mean-baseline used in value-free RL algorithms improperly penalizes "negative-advantage samples," leading to inefficient exploration and stalled learning.
- Existing token-level entropy controls are one-sided, addressing only collapse or explosion without providing comprehensive, sustained stability or performance gains.

## Method

- Introduced ==Quantile Advantage Estimation== (**QAE**) which replaces the conventional mean reward baseline with a group-wise K-quantile baseline to compute advantages.
- Developed a two-regime gate mechanism where hard queries (low success rate) receive positive advantages for correct responses, while easy queries (high success rate) receive negative advantages for incorrect responses.
- The hyperparameter K acts as a direct control for balancing exploration and exploitation, enabling dynamic regulation of policy entropy.

## Results

- QAE consistently improved pass@1 scores across various Qwen3 LLM sizes (e.g., +**21.5%** on AIME
								’24 for Qwen3-**8B**-Base when integrated with Clip-Higher).
- The method stabilized policy entropy throughout training, preventing early spikes (explosion) and premature collapse, which led to sustained performance gains beyond plateaus observed in baseline methods.
- QAE produced sparse credit assignment, with approximately **80%** of sampled responses receiving zero advantage, indicating an efficient focus on the most informative samples for learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Both entropy collapse and its symmetric counterpart, entropy explosion, are critical challenges for stable and scalable RLVR, stemming from a fundamental baseline-design flaw rather than merely token-level heuristics.
> - Entropy regulation can be more effectively achieved by modifying the advantage estimation baseline (from mean to K-quantile) rather than solely relying on token-level controls.
> - Theoretical analysis demonstrates that the K-quantile baseline provides "two-sided" entropy safety, curbing excessive growth in low-success regimes and maintaining sufficient entropy in high-success regimes.
