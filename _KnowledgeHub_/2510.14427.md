# Deep Compositional Phase Diffusion for Long Motion Sequence Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework called Deep Compositional Phase Diffusion generates extended human motion sequences that maintain semantic alignment and smooth transitions by modeling motion in a periodic latent frequency domain. The method outperforms baselines on realism and text alignment metrics across compositional generation, long-term sequencing, and motion inbetweening tasks.

## Problem

- Current models struggle to maintain continuity and naturalness at transition boundaries when concatenating multiple semantically defined motion clips, leading to abrupt artifacts.
- Existing phase-based motion models often rely on fixed-length convolution schemes, which are unstable for variable-length motions and can lose critical semantic and transitional information.
- Generating long motion sequences composed of hundreds or thousands of segments poses scalability challenges, as sequential generation methods can accumulate errors and parallel methods struggle with global coherence.

## Method

- The **Compositional Phase Diffusion** framework simultaneously generates multiple ==motion clips== from sequential semantic instructions, ensuring smooth transitions by operating within a latent motion frequency domain.
- An Action-Centric Periodic Autoencoder (ACT-PAE) encodes variable-length motion segments into a unified, periodic latent space, capturing intrinsic phase dynamics without fixed-window slicing.
- Semantic Phase Diffusion (SPDM) and Transitional Phase Diffusion (TPDM) modules denoise phase parameters to align motions with text conditions and adjacent segments, respectively, combining predictions via a Phase Mixing Equation.

## Results

- Achieved the best overall FID (1.807) and MMD (4.711) scores on compositional motion pair generation, demonstrating superior realism and text alignment compared to baseline methods.
- Showed competitive performance in long-term motion generation on an extended sequence (302,298 frames, 168 minutes), obtaining the best overall FID (**0.909**), indicating improved transition-aware motion quality.
- Outperformed baselines across all evaluated metrics (L2-Vel, L2-Rot6D, NPSS, RMS-Jerk) in both unconditional and conditional motion inbetweening tasks, generating smoother and more realistic intermediate motions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Representing variable-length motion segments in a unified, periodic latent phase space allows for holistic processing, preserving complete semantic and transition-aware information.
> - Explicitly modeling and integrating motion phase information throughout a diffusion process effectively addresses both semantic alignment and kinematic continuity at segment boundaries.
> - The parallel application of semantic and transitional guidance through distinct diffusion modules, combined with a phase mixing mechanism, enables the generation of long-term, coherent motion sequences with global consistency.
