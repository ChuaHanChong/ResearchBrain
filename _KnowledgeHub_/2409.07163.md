# Mamba Policy: Towards Efficient 3D Diffusion Policy with Hybrid Selective State Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Mamba Policy proposes an efficient 3D diffusion policy for robotic manipulation, utilizing a hybrid Mamba-Attention UNet architecture. This design significantly reduces computational demands, cutting model parameters by 81.2% and FLOPs by 90.0% compared to prior 3D diffusion policies, while enhancing task success rates across benchmark tasks and demonstrating effectiveness in real-world scenarios.

## Problem

- Existing 3D diffusion policies require extensive computational resources due to large backbone architectures, such as UNets with hundreds of millions of parameters.
- These large models pose challenges for efficient long-horizon prediction and are impractical for deployment on resource-constrained hardware.
- High computational demands limit the widespread application of advanced visuomotor policies in real-world robotic systems.

## Method

- **Mamba Policy** introduces a novel X-==Mamba UNet== architecture that integrates a hybrid Mamba (selective state space model) and Attention mechanism.
- The core XMamba block combines FiLM (Feature-wise Linear Modulation) for perception-action fusion with MambaAttn refinement for efficient sequence processing.
- The policy uses a conditional diffusion model with a DDIM solver to iteratively denoise a randomly initialized action sequence.

## Results

- Achieved generally higher average success rates across Adroit, MetaWorld, and DexArt datasets compared to existing 3D diffusion policies like DP3.
- Demonstrated an **81.2%** reduction in model parameters and a **90.0%** reduction in FLOPs, alongside an **86.2%** decrease in GPU memory usage, compared to DP3.
- Successfully enabled a Franka arm to perform a real-world grasping and placement task, confirming practical applicability in a physical environment.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating Mamba models within a diffusion policy framework significantly reduces model parameters (81.2%) and FLOPs (90.0%) while maintaining or improving performance.
> - A hybrid architecture combining Mamba's efficient long-range dependency capturing with Attention's refinement capabilities is effective for 3D robotic manipulation.
> - The Mamba Policy demonstrates improved robustness and efficiency in handling longer action horizons, which is crucial for complex robotic tasks.
