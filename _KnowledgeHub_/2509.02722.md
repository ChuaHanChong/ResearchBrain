# Planning with Reasoning using Vision Language World Model

> [!summary] Summary
> The Vision Language World Model (VLWM) introduces a foundation model for language-based world modeling from natural videos, enabling high-level task planning. It leverages natural language as an abstract world state representation and achieves state-of-the-art performance on visual planning benchmarks and human-preferred plans in evaluations.

## Problem

- Traditional world models are limited to low-level, continuous control and struggle with high-level task planning that requires semantic and temporal abstraction.
- Existing LLMs lack direct grounding in sensory experience, leading to hallucinations in real-world planning, while VLMs are not designed for action-conditioned world state prediction.
- Generating future observations (e.g., raw pixels) is computationally inefficient for long-horizon roll-outs and often captures task-irrelevant details.

## Method

- A ==Vision Language World Model (VLWM)== is developed, using natural language as an abstract world state representation, trained on a large corpus of natural videos.
- A two-stage data generation pipeline compresses raw video into a =="Tree of Captions"== and extracts structured goal-plan trajectories using LLM self-refinement.
- A ==dual-system planning approach== combines efficient reactive plan generation (System-1) with reflective reasoning via cost minimization using a ==self-supervised critic== (System-2).

## Results

- Achieved new state-of-the-art performance on Visual Planning for Assistance (VPA) benchmarks, with absolute gains of **+3.2%** in Success Rate and **+3.9%** in Mean Accuracy.
- Secured the highest Elo score (**1261**) in human evaluations with PlannerArena, demonstrating superior plan quality, and System-2 planning improved Elo by **+27%** over System-1.
- Attained a state-of-the-art BLEU-4 score of **55.6** on the RoboVQA benchmark, and the self-supervised critic model achieved **98.4%** accuracy in goal achievement detection on in-domain data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Natural language provides an efficient, semantically rich, and interpretable interface for abstract world states, effectively bridging the gap between perception and reasoning.
> - Combining fast reactive planning with a deliberative, cost-minimizing reflective system significantly improves plan quality and robustness in complex, long-horizon tasks.
> - Self-supervised training of a critic model enables objective evaluation of candidate plans without relying on explicit, engineered reward functions or perfect demonstrations.
