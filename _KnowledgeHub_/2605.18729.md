# Robo-Cortex: A Self-Evolving Embodied Agent via Dual-Grain Cognitive Memory and Autonomous Knowledge Induction

> [!summary] Summary
> A self-evolving embodied agent framework, Robo-Cortex, addresses "experiential amnesia" in autonomous navigation by converting interaction history into generalizable decision heuristics, demonstrating improved task success and generalization across diverse environments in simulation and preliminary real-world tests.

## Problem

- Embodied agents frequently suffer from "experiential amnesia," failing to abstract generalizable strategies from past interactions.
- Existing trajectory-driven or reactive policies often lead to weak, local improvements that struggle to transfer to unseen or novel environments.
- Current methods typically do not explicitly induce transferable embodied heuristics that abstract knowledge beyond individual episodes or specific environmental layouts.

## Method

- A multimodal Imagine-then-Verify planning loop provides robust, short-horizon decision-making, simulating outcomes and validating action plans.
- A Dual-Grain Cognitive Memory system manages experience with Short-term Reflective Memory (SRM) for within-episode reflection and Long-term Principle Memory (LPM) for cross-episode principle abstraction.
- An Autonomous Knowledge Induction (AKI) mechanism continuously distills recurring success and failure patterns from interaction history into reusable, natural-language heuristics for strategy evolution.

## Results

- Robo-Cortex consistently outperformed state-of-the-art baselines on Image-Goal Navigation, Active Recognition, and Active Embodied Question Answering tasks, achieving **41.26%** SR and **31.66%** SPL on IGNav.
- The adaptive Robo-Cortex++ variant further improved performance, reaching **45.07%** SR and **35.06%** SPL on IGNav through continual online heuristic updating.
- Heuristics autonomously induced from "seen" environments demonstrated effective generalization to "unseen" environments, yielding **48.61%** SR on IGNav and **23.02%** SR on AR, and preliminary real-world tests showed SRM's ability to recover from navigation errors.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating multimodal decision-making, reflective memory, and cross-episode heuristic induction into a unified framework enables continuous cognitive strategy evolution.
> - Dual-grain memory, encompassing both local trajectory analysis and abstracted principles, facilitates adaptation across short-term and long-term temporal scales.
> - Automatically inducing and consolidating natural-language heuristics from past experiences can generate transferable strategic knowledge that enhances future perception, reasoning, and action.
