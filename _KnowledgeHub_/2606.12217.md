# Making Foresight Actionable: Repurposing Representation Alignment in World Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> The research introduces Action-Grounded Representation Alignment (AGRA) to overcome the "action-grounding gap" in World Action Models (WAMs), where visually plausible predictions fail to guarantee accurate robot actions. AGRA regularizes the world-action interface by aligning a video diffusion model's intermediate features with spatially coherent semantic representations from a frozen foundation visual encoder, significantly boosting real-world manipulation success rates from 34% to 80% and enhancing out-of-distribution generalization.

## Problem

- World Action Models (WAMs) frequently generate visually plausible future predictions but produce inaccurate low-level control actions, indicating an "action-grounding gap."
- Representations learned by video diffusion models, optimized for pixel reconstruction, do not inherently align with the requirements for generating precise robotic actions.
- Action decoders in WAMs often distribute attention across task-irrelevant regions, rather than focusing on critical interaction areas, leading to erroneous action predictions.

## Method

- Introduced ==Action-Grounded Representation Alignment== (**AGRA**), an auxiliary objective to regularize the interface between the World Action Model's visual features and the action decoder.
- AGRA aligns intermediate hidden states from selected layers of the video diffusion model with spatially coherent semantic features from a frozen foundation visual encoder like DINOv2.
- The alignment uses a negative cosine similarity loss between projected world model hidden states and the DINOv2 target features, thereby making the world model's representations more "action-grounded."

## Results

- AGRA achieved an **80%** success rate in in-distribution real-world manipulation tasks, a substantial improvement over the baseline WAM's **34%**.
- The method consistently improved out-of-distribution generalization by 27-**32%** across semantic, instance-level, and attribute variations.
- Analysis of action-head attention and causal interventions confirmed that AGRA precisely localized the action decoder's attention to critical hand-object interaction regions and reduced sensitivity to task-irrelevant information.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Features optimized for visual reconstruction in video diffusion models are not intrinsically organized for robust, low-level action control, leading to an "action-grounding gap" in World Action Models.
> - Aligning a world model's intermediate features with self-supervised visual encoders (e.g., DINOv2) can improve the actionability of representations by enhancing spatial coherence and focusing the action decoder on relevant regions.
> - The optimal alignment for semantic grounding often occurs in shallower layers of the generative model, allowing deeper layers to model motion and fine-grained dynamics effectively.
