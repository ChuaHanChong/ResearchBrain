# VideoRFT: Incentivizing Video Reasoning Capability in MLLMs via Reinforced Fine-Tuning

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework named VIDEORFT, developed by researchers from Beijing Institute of Technology and Shenzhen University, enables multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to perform human-like, visually grounded video reasoning through reinforcement fine-tuning. The approach leverages a novel data curation pipeline and a specialized semantic-consistency reward, achieving state-of-the-art performance and outperforming proprietary models like GPT-4o on several video reasoning benchmarks.

## Problem

- Current video MLLMs are predominantly "answer-driven," providing outputs without explicitly revealing their internal reasoning processes, limiting transparency and interpretability.
- Applying Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) to video reasoning is hampered by a scarcity of high-quality Chain-of-Thought (CoT) datasets with complex temporal, causal, and logical structures.
- A major challenge in cross-modal video reasoning is ensuring that generated reasoning steps are faithfully aligned with and supported by visual evidence, preventing hallucinations.

## Method

- A ==multi-expert-driven, cognition-inspired pipeline== automatically curates large-scale, high-quality video Chain-of-Thought (CoT) annotations, resulting in the ==VideoRFT-CoT-102K== and ==VideoRFT-RL-310K== datasets.
- A novel rule-based reward system for Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) is introduced, comprising format, accuracy, and a unique ==semantic-consistency reward==.
- The semantic-consistency reward explicitly guides MLLMs to produce visually grounded reasoning by measuring cosine similarity between video description tokens and visual features, activated only for factually correct answers.

## Results

- VIDEORFT achieved state-of-the-art performance on six challenging video reasoning benchmarks, surpassing its base model (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) by up to **+7.2%** and outperforming GPT-4o on VSI-Bench by **+2.8%**.
- Diagnostic experiments confirmed that the cross-modal CoT refinement stage effectively mitigates visual hallucinations, and the full two-stage SFT and RL training paradigm, including the semantic-consistency reward, significantly improved performance over individual components.
- The generated VideoRFT-CoT-102K dataset exhibits more elaborate and video-centric reasoning processes, with broader distribution and longer average token lengths compared to existing video Chain-of-Thought datasets.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality, cognitively-inspired Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data, curated through cross-modal refinement, is essential for training MLLMs to perform robust video reasoning and mitigate visual hallucinations.
> - Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) with a carefully designed reward system can effectively incentivize MLLMs to generate explicit, visually grounded reasoning steps for complex video content.
> - A conditional semantic-consistency reward, activated only when an answer is factually correct, is crucial for promoting visually faithful reasoning while preventing the reinforcement of visually plausible but incorrect explanations.
