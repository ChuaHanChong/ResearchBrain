# Kimi K2: Open Agentic Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> Moonshot AI developed Kimi K2, a 1 trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts large language model, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance in agentic intelligence tasks, including software engineering and tool use. The model, which incorporates the novel MuonClip optimizer for training stability and a multi-stage reinforcement learning framework, establishes a new benchmark for open-source LLMs.

## Problem

- Large language models faced limitations in performing complex, interactive agentic behaviors, primarily due to reliance on static training data for planning and autonomous action.
- Scaling LLM pre-training encountered challenges related to token efficiency and training instability, particularly when using token-efficient optimizers like Muon.
- Generating high-quality, diverse data for advanced agentic capabilities, such as multi-tool use, was difficult and costly to obtain from natural human-generated datasets.

## Method

- Kimi K2 utilizes a 1.04 trillion-parameter ==Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)== transformer architecture with 384 experts, enabling efficient scaling for agentic tasks.
- The novel ==MuonClip optimizer==, featuring a ==QK-Clip mechanism==, ensures stable pre-training on 15.5 trillion tokens by preventing attention logit explosions and loss spikes.
- A multi-stage post-training process incorporates a large-scale ==agentic data synthesis pipeline== for tool use and a comprehensive reinforcement learning framework with verifiable rewards and a self-critique rubric reward.

## Results

- Kimi K2-Instruct achieved state-of-the-art results in agentic benchmarks, scoring **71.6%** on SWE-bench Verified (Agentic-Multi-Attempt) and **66.1%** micro Pass@1 on Tau2-Bench, surpassing open-source baselines.
- The model was ranked as the top open-source LLM and **5th** overall on the LMSYS Arena (July 17, 2025) based on over 3,000 user votes, indicating strong real-world preference.
- Kimi K2-Base set new state-of-the-art records for open-source models on **10 out of 12** English language benchmarks (e.g., MMLU **87.79%**), and led across all coding and most mathematical reasoning benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The QK-Clip mechanism within the MuonClip optimizer is crucial for stabilizing ultra-large-scale LLM pre-training, preventing attention logit explosions and enabling stable learning over trillions of tokens.
> - Large-scale agentic data synthesis, combined with a sophisticated reinforcement learning framework incorporating both verifiable and self-critique rubric rewards, can effectively imbue LLMs with complex interactive and reasoning capabilities.
> - Scaling sparse MoE architectures by increasing the number of experts while optimizing attention heads proves effective for improving performance and inference efficiency under fixed computational budgets.
