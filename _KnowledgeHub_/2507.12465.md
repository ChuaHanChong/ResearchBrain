# PhysX-3D: Physical-Grounded 3D Asset Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> PhysX-3D introduces PhysXNet, the first comprehensive dataset for physics-grounded 3D assets, and PhysXGen, an end-to-end framework capable of generating 3D objects with fine-grained, part-level physical properties from single image prompts. The approach successfully integrates physical knowledge into 3D generation, achieving improved geometric and aesthetic quality while significantly enhancing physical property prediction over baselines.

## Problem

- Current 3D generative models predominantly focus on geometric structures and visual appearances, neglecting crucial physical properties essential for real-world interaction.
- Existing 3D datasets lack comprehensive, part-level annotations for physical attributes like absolute scale, material composition, functional affordances, and precise kinematic parameters.
- This omission creates a critical bottleneck for deploying generated 3D assets in physical domains such as robotics, embodied AI, and high-fidelity simulations.

## Method

- Developed PhysXNet, a pioneering dataset with over 26,000 3D objects, providing fine-grained, part-level annotations for absolute scale, material, affordance, kinematics, and function descriptions.
- Created a scalable human-in-the-loop annotation pipeline, leveraging Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and expert refinement, to efficiently convert existing geometry-focused assets into physics-enabled ones, complemented by PhysXNet-XL (6M procedurally generated objects).
- Proposed PhysXGen, a feed-forward, dual-branch generative framework based on diffusion models, designed to generate 3D assets from image prompts while simultaneously predicting plausible physical properties by integrating physical knowledge into pre-trained 3D structural latent spaces.

## Results

- PhysXGen consistently outperformed baseline methods, achieving better geometry/appearance (e.g., PSNR 24.53 vs. 24.31) and significantly improved physical property generation across absolute scale (error 7.24 vs. 13.21), material (score 13.01 vs. 8.63), affordance (score 11.30 vs. 7.23), and kinematics.
- The PhysXNet dataset, with 26,000 objects, established itself as the first comprehensive physics-grounded 3D dataset, while PhysXNet-XL expanded its scale to over 6 million procedurally generated and annotated 3D objects.
- Qualitative evaluations confirmed that PhysXGen generates plausible and consistent physical-grounded 3D assets from single image prompts, demonstrating superior stability and accuracy in distinguishing part-level properties compared to fragmented approaches.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating explicit physical properties into 3D generation can enhance both the structural and aesthetic quality of generated assets, demonstrating that physical grounding is not solely an add-on.
> - A dual-branch architecture, which fuses structural guidance with the primary physical generation module, effectively leverages the inherent correlations between geometric and physical attributes for more consistent outputs.
> - Scalable human-in-the-loop annotation, supported by VLMs and procedural generation techniques, provides an efficient strategy to overcome the data scarcity challenges for complex, multi-modal 3D physical properties.
