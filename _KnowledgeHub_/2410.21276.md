# GPT-4o System Card

> [!summary] Summary
> OpenAI's GPT-4o is a multimodal AI system integrating text, audio, and visual capabilities, achieving average audio response times of 320 milliseconds while maintaining the text and code performance of GPT-4 Turbo. The system also demonstrates improved understanding in non-English languages and enhanced visual processing.

## Problem

- Limited real-time conversational interaction due to slow audio processing in existing AI models.
- AI systems often lack native multimodal integration, requiring separate components for different data types.
- Addressing safety concerns and potential misuse of powerful AI, especially with new modalities like synthetic voices.

## Method

- Developed a single, ==end-to-end multimodal model==, **GPT-4o**, processing and generating text, audio, and images.
- Leveraged a diverse training dataset encompassing text, code, images, audio, and video.
- Employed a comprehensive ==safety framework==, including extensive ==red teaming== (over 100 external red teamers in 45 languages) and third-party assessments, for risk identification and mitigation.

## Results

- GPT-4o achieves average audio response times of **320 milliseconds**, comparable to human conversation.
- Demonstrated improved performance in non-English languages, narrowing the gap between English and historically underrepresented languages.
- Classified as "medium risk" under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, with specific mitigations for risks like unauthorized voice generation and persuasive capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Achieving near human-level audio response times (320ms) is possible in a natively multimodal AI model.
> - Integrated multimodal training enhances performance in traditionally challenging areas, such as non-English languages and visual understanding.
> - Rigorous, multi-faceted safety evaluations are essential for advanced AI deployment, categorizing the model's risk level (e.g., medium risk for GPT-4o) and guiding mitigation.

## Detailed Report

### 1. Introduction

OpenAI's GPT-4o represents a significant advancement in multimodal artificial intelligence, integrating text, audio, and visual capabilities into a single system. Unlike traditional academic papers, the GPT-4o System Card is a collaborative effort involving numerous teams at OpenAI, encompassing expertise in language processing, multimodal integration, platform development, safety engineering, and deployment.

This comprehensive report outlines the capabilities, limitations, and safety considerations of GPT-4o, providing transparency into how the system was developed, evaluated, and prepared for responsible deployment. The system card reflects OpenAI's continued investment in building powerful AI systems while simultaneously working to ensure they are aligned with human values and beneficial to society.

### 2. GPT-4o Capabilities and Advances

GPT-4o maintains the text and code performance of GPT-4 Turbo while introducing significant improvements in audio and visual understanding capabilities. The model exhibits enhanced performance across several key dimensions:

1. **Speech-to-Speech Capabilities**: GPT-4o can process and generate audio with remarkably fast response times, averaging around 320 milliseconds, comparable to human conversation speed. This represents a major step toward more natural human-computer interaction.

2. **Improved Language Performance**: The system demonstrates enhanced capabilities in non-English languages, narrowing the performance gap between historically underrepresented languages and English.

3. **Multimodal Integration**: GPT-4o effectively processes and generates information across multiple modalities (text, audio, image), enabling more versatile applications and use cases.

4. **Visual Processing**: The model shows improved visual perception capabilities, allowing it to better understand and interpret image content.

The speech-to-speech functionality in particular represents a significant advance, as it allows for more natural conversational interaction while maintaining the reasoning capabilities of large language models.

### 3. Development Approach and Methodology

The development of GPT-4o involved a multifaceted approach combining extensive training data, rigorous evaluation methodologies, and proactive risk assessment:

1. **Data and Training**: The model was trained on a diverse dataset comprising text, code, images, audio, and video from publicly available sources and proprietary data partnerships.

2. **Evaluation Methodology**: OpenAI developed innovative evaluation approaches, including:
 - Converting existing text-based evaluations to speech-to-speech format using text-to-speech (TTS) systems
 - Creating new evaluations specific to audio capabilities
 - Assessing performance across multiple languages and accents

3. **Red Teaming**: Over 100 external red teamers speaking 45 languages evaluated model snapshots at different development stages, providing critical feedback on potential vulnerabilities.

4. **Third-Party Assessment**: Independent organizations including METR and Apollo Research conducted additional evaluations to validate OpenAI's findings and identify potential risks.

This methodology reflects a comprehensive approach to model development that balances capability advancement with safety considerations.

### 4. Safety Evaluations and Risk Mitigation

OpenAI conducted extensive safety evaluations of GPT-4o through their Preparedness Framework, which assesses risks across multiple categories:

1. **Risk Identification**: The team systematically identified potential risks associated with GPT-4o's capabilities, focusing on:
 - Unauthorized voice generation or cloning
 - Speaker identification without consent
 - Generation of harmful, misleading, or copyrighted content
 - Biased inferences based on audio characteristics

2. **Mitigation Strategies**: Several methods were implemented to address identified risks:
 - Post-training alignment with human preferences
 - Integration of classifiers to block specific types of generations
 - Filters to detect and prevent outputs containing music or copyrighted content
 - Safety guardrails to prevent harmful use cases

GPT-4o shows significant improvements in handling diverse languages and modalities:

1. **Underrepresented Languages**: The model was evaluated on historically underrepresented languages including Amharic, Hausa, Northern Sotho, Swahili, and Yoruba, showing improved performance in reading comprehension and reasoning compared to previous models.

2. **Accent Recognition**: Extensive testing was conducted with speakers from various countries and accents to ensure the model performs consistently regardless of accent or dialect.

3. **Audio-Visual Integration**: GPT-4o demonstrates the ability to process and reason about multimedia content, integrating information from text, audio, and visual inputs simultaneously.

### 7. Societal Impact and Ethical Considerations

The system card addresses several important societal and ethical considerations related to GPT-4o:

1. **Anthropomorphism and Emotional Reliance**: The more natural speech capabilities of GPT-4o may increase user tendency to anthropomorphize the system or develop emotional reliance, raising questions about appropriate boundaries for human-AI relationships.

2. **Health Applications**: The model shows potential for beneficial health applications, such as improving access to health information and clinical documentation, but also raises concerns about accuracy and appropriate use in medical contexts.

3. **Scientific Understanding**: GPT-4o demonstrates capabilities in scientific reasoning and explanation, as shown in examples of it analyzing complex scientific concepts:

4. **Persuasive Capabilities**: Studies indicate that AI systems like GPT-4o can influence opinions and beliefs, highlighting the importance of transparency about AI-generated content and responsible deployment.

5. **Accessibility and Inclusion**: While GPT-4o improves performance on underrepresented languages, continuing work is needed to ensure equitable access and performance across diverse linguistic and cultural contexts.

These considerations reflect OpenAI's awareness of the broader implications of deploying advanced AI systems and their commitment to addressing potential societal impacts proactively.

### 8. Limitations and Future Directions

The system card transparently acknowledges several limitations of GPT-4o:

1. **Safety Robustness**: The model's safety mechanisms can be less robust when processing audio with perturbations such as low-quality input or background noise.

2. **Over-refusals**: GPT-4o may exhibit excessive caution in non-English conversations, refusing to answer legitimate queries due to misinterpreted safety concerns.

3. **Information Accuracy**: Like other large language models, GPT-4o can generate inaccurate information or perpetuate conspiracy theories when prompted.

4. **Non-English Audio**: The model has limitations in non-English language performance, particularly in audio outputs that may exhibit non-native accents.

5. **Adversarial Vulnerabilities**: Potential remains for adversarial techniques that could circumvent safety measures or exploit model limitations.

Future work will focus on addressing these limitations, improving robustness across languages and modalities, enhancing safety mechanisms, and continuing to develop evaluation methodologies that effectively capture model capabilities and risks.

### 9. Conclusion

The GPT-4o System Card represents a comprehensive effort to document the capabilities, limitations, and safety considerations of a significant advancement in multimodal AI. Through detailed technical evaluations, risk assessments, and societal impact analyses, OpenAI demonstrates a commitment to responsible AI development and deployment.

GPT-4o's integration of text, audio, and visual capabilities marks an important step toward more natural and intuitive human-computer interaction, with potential benefits across various domains from education to healthcare. At the same time, the thorough safety evaluations and mitigation strategies reflect recognition of the responsibility that comes with developing increasingly powerful AI systems.

By providing this level of transparency about their development process and evaluation findings, OpenAI contributes valuable information to ongoing discussions about AI safety, ethics, and governance. The GPT-4o System Card serves not only as documentation for this specific model but also as a template for how AI developers can approach the responsible development and release of advanced AI technologies.
