# Lance: Unified Multimodal Modeling by Multi-Task Synergy

> [!summary] Summary
> The Intelligent Creation Lab at ByteDance developed Lance, a lightweight unified multimodal model integrating image and video understanding, generation, and editing within a single framework. Leveraging multi-task synergistic training, the model achieves competitive performance across diverse benchmarks, including an 11.3% relative improvement on MVBench for video understanding and top-tier scores in image and video generation and editing.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal models were specialized, with understanding models (MLLMs) excelling at semantics but not visual synthesis, and generative models producing high-fidelity visuals but lacking integrated reasoning.
- Prior Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) struggled with misaligned visual representation requirements, as understanding tasks benefit from high-level semantics while generation tasks need low-level continuous representations.
- Current UMMs often had limited task coverage, being confined to text-image domains or partial task combinations, with diverse generation-oriented tasks like editing typically treated as downstream fine-tuning rather than systematically integrated.

## Method

- Introduces **Lance**, a lightweight native unified multimodal model featuring a dual-expert architecture, initialized from Qwen2.5-VL 3B, with dedicated streams for understanding (LLM_UND) and generation (LLM_GEN).
- Employs a unified interleaved multimodal sequence by tokenizing text, semantic visual inputs (from a ViT encoder), and ==VAE== latent tokens (for generation), all processed through a single context modeling framework.
- Utilizes Modality-Aware Rotary Positional Encoding (MaPE) to reduce positional ambiguity among heterogeneous visual token groups and implements a four-stage multi-task training paradigm with capability-oriented objectives and adaptive data scheduling.

## Results

- Achieved an overall score of 0.90 on GenEval for image generation and the highest Total Score of 85.11 on VBench among unified models with 3B parameters for video generation, demonstrating strong compositional ability and semantic fidelity.
- Secured the highest Avg/G_O score (7.30) on GEdit-Bench for multimodal editing, producing visually coherent edits for images and accurate multi-attribute modifications with preserved motion dynamics for videos.
- Attained the highest overall score (62.0) on MVBench for multimodal understanding, representing an approximate **11.3%** relative improvement over the second-best unified model, and surpassed most specialized understanding models with fewer parameters.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Multi-task synergistic training promotes significant positive transfer across diverse image and video understanding, generation, and editing tasks, leading to mutual enhancement of capabilities.
> - A balanced architectural approach, combining unified interleaved context modeling with decoupled capability pathways (dual-stream experts), effectively reconciles task-specific requirements for semantic reasoning and visual synthesis.
> - Modality-Aware Rotary Positional Encoding (MaPE) is crucial for stabilizing training and improving performance by distinguishing and correctly aligning heterogeneous visual token groups within the shared multimodal sequence.
