# Unlocking Location Intelligence: A Survey from Deep Learning to The LLM Era

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper provides a comprehensive review of geospatial representation learning, covering advancements from deep learning to large language models (LLMs). It introduces a novel three-dimensional taxonomy to systematically organize research and highlights the evolving landscape of Location Intelligence.

## Problem

- The rapidly evolving field of Location Intelligence (LI) lacked a systematic and up-to-date review that encompasses both deep learning and emerging Large Language Model (LLM) paradigms.
- Existing surveys often had limited scope, focusing on specific data types or methodologies, and failed to adequately integrate multimodal approaches or recent breakthroughs like Transformers and LLMs.
- There was no clear, structured taxonomy to categorize and compare the diverse research approaches in geospatial representation learning, hindering a holistic understanding of the field's progress.

## Method

- The authors conducted a comprehensive literature review of ==geospatial representation learning==, tracing its evolution through the deep learning and LLM eras.
- A novel ==three-dimensional taxonomy== was introduced to categorize representative works based on methodology (single, dual, multiple view), modality, and downstream tasks.
- The survey framed geospatial representation learning within a complete pipeline, analyzing it from data, methodological (location/region representation learning), and application perspectives.

## Results

- Identified spatial data (POIs, satellite imagery) as the most prevalent data modality (**67%**) in geospatial representation learning, followed by mobility data (**18%**).
- Showcased a clear progression from single-view to dual-view and multi-view multimodal integration methods, significantly enhancing representation learning for various geospatial tasks.
- Demonstrated an expanding application landscape for learned representations in predicting socioeconomic indicators, managing regions and POIs, and advanced location sensing.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Location Intelligence has experienced two transformative phases: first driven by deep neural networks for structured data, and now by LLMs for cross-modal reasoning and unstructured geo-textual data.
> - The field is increasingly moving towards multimodal integration, with contrastive learning and Graph Neural Networks being key techniques for combining diverse geospatial data modalities.
> - While LLMs are emerging enhancers, the GeoAI domain currently lacks dedicated foundation models, unified benchmarks, and large-scale, comprehensive multimodal geospatial datasets.
