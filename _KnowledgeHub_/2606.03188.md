# GeoSem-WAM: Geometry- and Semantic-Aware World Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from HKUST(GZ) developed GeoSem-WAM, a framework that enhances World Action Models for robotic control by incorporating geometry- and semantic-aware predictive supervision during training, achieving an average success rate improvement of 6.6% over baseline models in real-world robot manipulation tasks and reaching 98.55% on the LIBERO benchmark.

## Problem

- Existing World Action Models (WAMs) primarily rely on RGB-based future prediction, limiting their explicit understanding of environmental structure, spatial relations, and high-level semantics.
- The appearance-based supervision in current WAMs often lacks the geometric reasoning and object-level semantic awareness essential for embodied agents in complex real-world scenarios.
- Latent representations learned by these models are frequently insufficient for robotic tasks requiring a deeper grasp of environmental structure and object-level semantics.

## Method

- **GeoSem-WAM** augments World Action Model (WAM) training by incorporating multi-modal predictive supervision for future RGB, geometry, and semantic information into a unified framework.
- A DPT-style dense prediction head, extended with 3D reassemble and fusion modules, applies geometry and semantic supervision to intermediate video ==latent== tokens.
- The framework maintains efficient inference by utilizing structured predictive supervision only during training, discarding auxiliary branches at test time to directly predict actions from current observations.

## Results

- GeoSem-WAM achieved an average success rate of **98.55%** on the LIBERO benchmark and **92.52%** on the RoboTwin 2.0 dataset, outperforming Fast-WAM baselines.
- In real-world experiments, the framework improved the average success rate from **88.9%** to **95.4%** (+**6.6%**) on a Franka Emika Panda robot across various manipulation tasks, demonstrating enhanced robustness in generalization settings.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both geometry-aware (+**0.61%**) and semantic-aware (+**0.51%**) supervision positively contribute to performance, with their combination yielding the highest improvement (+**1.02%**) on LIBERO.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The core value of World Action Models (WAMs) primarily derives from learning robust, dynamics-aware latent representations through predictive supervision during training, rather than explicit test-time future imagination.
> - Augmenting WAM supervision with explicit geometry and semantic prediction compels the model to acquire physically grounded motion and spatial-semantic layouts, enriching its latent representations.
> - This structured multi-modal supervision consistently improves action prediction accuracy, robustness, and scene understanding in embodied agents without compromising efficient inference.
