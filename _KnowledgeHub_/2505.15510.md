# Visual Thoughts: A Unified Perspective of Understanding Multimodal Chain-of-Thought

> [!summary] Summary
> This research introduces "visual thoughts" as a unified concept to explain the effectiveness of Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (MCoT) reasoning in Large Vision-Language Models. The study defines and analyzes four distinct expressions of visual thoughts, demonstrating that their clarity and conciseness are critical for MCoT performance and that they function as a cache, facilitating deeper visual information flow within the models.

## Problem

- The precise mechanisms and underlying reasons for Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (MCoT) effectiveness in Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) remain largely unclear.
- A lack of unified theoretical framework exists to explain why different MCoT paradigms (Textual-MCoT vs. Interleaved-MCoT) enhance LVLMs in distinct ways.
- There is an absence of systematic understanding regarding the optimal MCoT paradigm for various tasks or how to derive generalizable insights across diverse applications.

## Method

- Proposed =="visual thoughts"== as intermediate, logic-driven cross-modal representations that act as a cache for distilled visual information, bridging raw pixels and linguistic rationales.
- Categorized visual thoughts into four distinct strategies based on expression modality: ==Natural Language (N-LANG)==, ==Structured Language (S-LANG)== for Textual-MCoT, and ==Edited Image (E-IMG)==, ==Generative Image (G-IMG)== for Interleaved-MCoT.
- Conducted extensive experiments across four state-of-the-art LVLMs and various multimodal benchmarks, coupled with detailed internal attention and information flow analysis to verify the concept and mechanisms.

## Results

- All four distinct visual thought expressions consistently enhanced LVLM performance, with image-form VTs outperforming text-form VTs in complex tasks, showing up to a **47.83%** improvement on CoMT-Selection.
- Textual-MCoT is more effective for coarse-grained perception tasks, while Interleaved-MCoT excels in scenarios requiring fine-grained visual operations and complex manipulations.
- Internal analysis revealed that models shift attention significantly to visual thoughts, which then convey visual information more deeply into transformer layers and are primarily queried by input questions, optimizing visual data for reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Visual thoughts are essential for MCoT's effectiveness, functioning as a distilled visual information cache rather than a direct replica of the original image.
> - The clarity and conciseness of visual thought expressions are strongly and positively correlated with reasoning accuracy, enabling more effective information retrieval and utilization.
> - Visual thoughts facilitate deeper and more sustained visual information propagation into transformer layers compared to direct image input, acting as a vital intermediary for sophisticated cross-modal interaction.
