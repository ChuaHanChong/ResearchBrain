# Real-Time Iteration Scheme for Diffusion Policy

> [!summary] Summary
> A new Real-time Iteration Scheme for Diffusion Policy (RTI-DP) significantly reduces inference latency for diffusion-based robotic policies by orders of magnitude without requiring model retraining. The method enables real-time performance and often surpasses full-step denoising policies in task success across various manipulation benchmarks.

## Problem

- Diffusion policies require hundreds of iterative denoising steps, leading to high inference latency unsuitable for real-time robotic tasks like contact-rich or high-speed manipulations.
- Existing acceleration methods for diffusion policies, such as distillation or architectural redesign, typically involve high computational costs for retraining or can compromise policy quality and stability.
- The slow inference time limits the responsiveness of diffusion-based control, resulting in sluggish robot movements and hindering practical deployment in real-world scenarios.

## Method

- The **Real-Time** ==Iteration Scheme== for Diffusion Policy (RTI-DP) warm-starts the denoising process for subsequent time steps using action sequences predicted in the previous step, applying only a reduced number of denoising steps.
- It leverages the spatiotemporal consistency inherent in physical systems, where actions and states evolve smoothly, making prior predictions effective initial guesses for current ones.
- A scaling-based mechanism is introduced to robustly handle discrete actions within the continuous denoising framework, either by clipping for pre-trained policies (`RTI-DP-clip`) or preprocessing training data (`RTI-DP-scale`).

## Results

- RTI-DP achieved order-of-magnitude speedups, reducing prediction times from hundreds/thousands of milliseconds to tens of milliseconds (e.g., 25 ms for state-based Push-T, 36 ms for image-based Push-T).
- The method consistently matched or exceeded the performance of standard Diffusion Policy with full denoising, for instance, `RTI-DP-scale` achieved a **1.00**/**0.93** (Max/Avg) score for state-based Push-T compared to DP's **0.92**/**0.91**.
- RTI-DP demonstrated superior performance over other state-of-the-art acceleration methods like Streaming Diffusion Policy (SDP) and Consistency Policy (CP) in balancing inference speed with task success rates across diverse benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Physical systems exhibit strong spatiotemporal consistency, allowing previous action predictions to serve as highly informative warm starts for subsequent real-time control iterations.
> - The reverse diffusion process demonstrates local contractivity, theoretically ensuring that errors from warm-started initial guesses diminish exponentially, leading to high-quality action predictions even with truncated denoising steps.
> - Optimal control principles, specifically the Real-Time Iteration scheme, can be effectively adapted to accelerate generative models like diffusion policies, bridging the gap between powerful planning capabilities and real-time execution.
