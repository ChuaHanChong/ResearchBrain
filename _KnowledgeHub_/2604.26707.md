# CurEvo: Curriculum-Guided Self-Evolution for Video Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, La Trobe University, and Beijing Institute of Computer Technology and Applications introduce CurEvo, a framework for video understanding that leverages curriculum learning to guide iterative self-improvement without human supervision. The model achieves consistent accuracy gains of up to +4.05% on various VideoQA benchmarks by progressively structuring learning from perceptual grounding to higher-level reasoning.

## Problem

- Traditional video understanding methods, particularly for VideoQA, rely heavily on large-scale human annotations, which can lead to models exploiting superficial correlations.
- Existing self-evolving video understanding frameworks lack explicit control over the learning process, resulting in variable quality of self-generated supervision and unstructured progression.
- The multi-level cognitive nature of video understanding (perception, semantic recognition, reasoning) amplifies the complexity of effectively organizing and reusing self-generated supervision.

## Method

- **CurEvo** introduces an iterative feedback loop integrating ==Curriculum-Guided Self-Evolution==, ==Multi-Dimensional Question Generation==, and ==Type-Adaptive Evaluation==.
- Generates questions across three cognitive dimensions: ==structural perception== (self-supervised), ==semantic recognition== (confidence-based), and ==reasoning understanding== (LLM-inferred confidence).
- Employs ==adaptive sampling ratios== and ==sample weighting== to dynamically adjust learning emphasis, with type-adaptive evaluation to filter unreliable Q&A pairs.

## Results

- **CurEvo** consistently improved accuracy across **7 Video-LLM backbones** and **4 VideoQA benchmarks**, with gains up to **+4.05%** (LLaVA-OneVision on NExT-QA).
- Ablations confirm all core modules contribute significantly; iterative refinement is fundamental.
- Over iterations, retained samples progressively shifted from perception toward recognition and reasoning, indicating effective curriculum progression.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Structuring self-generated supervision into a progressive curriculum, spanning perception to reasoning, provides a more stable and effective path for autonomous video understanding.
> - Dynamically adjusting the emphasis on different cognitive dimensions during training, based on the model's evolving competence, is crucial for balanced skill acquisition.
> - Robust quality assessment of self-generated questions and pseudo-labels via a type-adaptive evaluation mechanism is essential for ensuring reliable supervision and preventing overfitting to noise.
