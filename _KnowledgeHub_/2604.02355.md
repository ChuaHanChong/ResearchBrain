# From Broad Exploration to Stable Synthesis: Entropy-Guided Optimization for Autoregressive Image Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Entropy-Guided Group Relative Policy Optimization (EG-GRPO) is introduced as an enhanced fine-tuning strategy for autoregressive text-to-image (T2I) models that quantifies token-level uncertainty using entropy and dynamically reallocates optimization efforts. This approach significantly reduces generative instability and uncertainty, leading to state-of-the-art performance on compositional generalization benchmarks like T2I-CompBench, where it improves metrics such as Color (84.11), Shape (60.88), and Texture (77.38).

## Problem

- Balancing the generation of diverse outputs (exploration) with high-fidelity, reward-aligned images (exploitation) in text-to-image (T2I) generation.
- Ensuring stability and consistency of generated content across repeated sampling in T2I models.
- Lack of systematic understanding of how Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning and Reinforcement Learning (RL) interact to influence uncertainty and generation stability in autoregressive T2I.

## Method

- An entropy-based analysis was performed to quantitatively investigate the role of ==token-level uncertainty== in both textual CoT reasoning and subsequent image token generation.
- Entropy-Guided Group Relative Policy Optimization (EG-GRPO) was developed as a token-level modification to the standard GRPO objective, guided by principles of uncertainty focus and confidence protection.
- EG-GRPO dynamically categorizes tokens by entropy, suppressing reward-driven updates for low-entropy tokens and adding an entropy bonus to high-entropy tokens to prioritize uncertainty reduction.

## Results

- EG-GRPO achieved competitive performance on T2I-CompBench, surpassing baselines in Color (84.11), Shape (60.88), and Texture (77.38), and showed improvements on the WISE benchmark in Culture (49.00) and Science (46.33).
- The method systematically reduced both the mean and variance of entropy for textual CoT and image tokens compared to T2I-R1, fostering more confident and stable token generation.
- EG-GRPO demonstrated superior convergence efficiency, achieving higher reward scores earlier in the training process with only a marginal increase in computational overhead (**1.35%** step time, **0.38 GB** GPU memory).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Chain-of-Thought (CoT) expands the generative exploration space, while Reinforcement Learning (RL) (specifically GRPO) subsequently contracts it towards narrower, higher-reward, lower-entropy regions.
> - A strong negative correlation exists between final image reward and both the mean and variance of image-token entropy, implying that reducing generative uncertainty and instability is crucial for high-quality images.
> - The intrinsic uncertainty of textual CoT directly influences downstream image quality, with lower-entropy CoTs correlating with higher-reward images, highlighting the importance of coherent semantic planning.
