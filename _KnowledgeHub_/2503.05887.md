# MatchMaker: Automated Asset Generation for Robotic Assembly

> [!summary] Summary
> MatchMaker is an automated pipeline that generates diverse, simulation-compatible assembly asset pairs by combining a Vision-Language Model for semantic understanding, a 3D generative diffusion model for shape completion, and a clearance specification module. This approach yielded a higher asset diversity score of 0.43 compared to existing datasets and enabled policies trained on these assets to achieve an 82% success rate in real-world robotic assembly tasks.

## Problem

- Developing generalist robotic assembly agents is hindered by the scarcity of diverse, high-quality, simulation-compatible assembly asset pairs.
- Existing datasets often contain geometrically incompatible assets with interpenetrations, making them unsuitable for high-fidelity physics simulations and real-world manufacturing.
- Manual curation of assembly assets is labor-intensive and unscalable, severely limiting dataset size and diversity for modern robot learning paradigms.

## Method

- An automated three-stage pipeline, **MatchMaker**, generates simulation-compatible assembly asset pairs by formulating the problem as shape completion.
- A ==Vision-Language Model (VLM)== identifies semantic properties and assembly axes for robust contact surface extraction from an input asset.
- A 3D generative latent diffusion model (BrepGen) then generates the mating counterpart, using the extracted contact surfaces as fixed geometric constraints, followed by a clearance specification module to ensure penetration-free and user-defined clearances.

## Results

- MatchMaker generated assets with a diversity score of **0.43**, significantly surpassing existing datasets like AutoMate (**0.19**) and Assemble Them All (**0.24**).
- Automated repair of 100 incompatible assets for policy learning took less than 1 minute per asset, demonstrating substantial efficiency gains over manual methods which required approximately 40 hours for 100 assets.
- Policies trained on MatchMaker-generated assets successfully transferred to a physical robot, achieving an average success rate of **82%** across 5 distinct real-world assembly tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Framing paired assembly asset generation as a constrained shape completion problem, guided by extracted contact surfaces, enables automated and diverse asset creation.
> - Vision-Language Models can provide high-level semantic reasoning and assembly axis identification to effectively guide lower-level geometric processing for contact surface extraction.
> - The integration of a B-rep based 3D generative model with a precise, physics-informed clearance specification step is essential for producing simulation-compatible and physically realizable assembly assets.
