# Thinking with Reasoning Skills: Fewer Tokens, More Accuracy

> [!summary] Summary
> Thinking with Reasoning Skills (TRS) from Qiyuan Tech, Tsinghua University, The University of Hong Kong, and Peking University introduces a training-free framework that distills complex reasoning into compact skill cards to guide large language models, significantly reducing inference token usage and cost while maintaining or improving accuracy on math and coding tasks. It achieved up to a 53.8% cost reduction on Doubao Seed for mathematical reasoning and a +4.1 percentage point accuracy increase on GPT-OSS-120B for coding, particularly excelling on harder problems.

## Problem

- Large language models (LLMs) generate substantial 'thinking' tokens during complex reasoning, leading to high inference costs and latency in practical deployment.
- Current LLM reasoning methods are inefficient, often requiring models to re-derive solution logic from scratch for each new problem, even when similar patterns exist.
- Attempts to reduce reasoning tokens frequently result in an efficiency-accuracy trade-off, where compressing deliberation can lead to lower correctness, especially on challenging problems.

## Method

- An ==offline phase== constructs a skill library by distilling LLM-generated reasoning traces from source problems into concise, structured ==skill cards== (Trigger, Do, Avoid, Check, Risk) and retrieval keywords.
- An ==online phase==, upon receiving a new query, retrieves the most relevant skill cards from the library using sparse, dense, or hybrid retrieval methods.
- Retrieved skill cards are injected into the target LLM's prompt with lightweight arbitration instructions, guiding its reasoning process and minimizing redundant exploration without modifying the model itself. **TRS** is fully training-free.

## Results

- On math reasoning, TRS reduced costs by up to **53.8%** (Doubao Seed) and **17.5%** (Gemini-3-Flash), while improving accuracy by up to **+1.8pp** (GPT-4o-mini).
- For coding tasks, the framework increased pass@1 accuracy by up to **+4.1pp** (GPT-OSS-120B) and reduced costs by up to **6.3%** (GPT-4o-mini).
- TRS consistently maintained or exceeded baseline accuracy on harder problem instances, outperforming other token-reduction methods that experienced significant accuracy degradation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Distilling complex reasoning into compact, structured skill cards effectively serves as 'procedural shortcuts' that guide LLMs, minimizing redundant deliberation and improving efficiency.
> - The TRS framework successfully breaks the common efficiency-accuracy trade-off, demonstrating that significant token reduction can be achieved while preserving or enhancing accuracy, particularly on difficult problem instances.
> - Reasoning skills are transferable across different LLM models and related task domains, enabling modular knowledge reuse and offering a pathway to enhance the efficiency of less powerful models using expertise from more capable ones.
