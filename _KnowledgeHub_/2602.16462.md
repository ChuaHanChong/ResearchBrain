# Reactive Motion Generation With Particle-Based Perception in Dynamic Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> The SMART framework enables robot manipulators to generate reactive, collision-free motions in dynamic and unstructured environments by tightly integrating a real-time particle-based occupancy map with an uncertainty-aware Model Predictive Path Integral planner. This approach demonstrated higher success rates, reaching 96% for fast-moving obstacles, and more consistent collision avoidance compared to baseline methods in both simulated and real-world experiments.

## Problem

- Reactive motion generation for high-dimensional manipulators often assumes static environments or lacks real-time global mapping and dynamic obstacle propagation.
- Most reactive planning approaches for manipulators do not explicitly model dynamic obstacle characteristics like velocity and uncertainty, leading to collisions or over-conservative behaviors.
- There is a limited unified integration of real-time sensor feedback with motion generation for manipulators operating in dynamic and uncertain settings.

## Method

- A framework named SMART integrates a Global Dual-Structure Particle-based (G-DSP) map with a Dynamic Obstacle-Aware STORM (D-STORM) planner for real-time, reactive motion generation.
- The G-DSP map models obstacle positions, velocities, and uncertainties using a particle-based continuous occupancy representation, optimized via tensor operations on GPUs.
- The D-STORM planner extends ==MPPI== to incorporate dynamic robot-obstacle propagation and uncertainty-aware collision costs, ensuring proactive avoidance strategies.

## Results

- The G-DSP map yielded smoother velocity estimations with lower RMSE and higher occupancy estimation accuracy (e.g., AUC of **0.910** at 0.02m voxel resolution) compared to other particle-based maps.
- The D-STORM planner achieved a **96%** success rate for collision avoidance with fast-moving obstacles (**0.2** m/s), outperforming baseline planners (73-**87%** success rates).
- The integrated SMART framework demonstrated significantly higher success rates and consistent collision-free motion in dynamic simulations, and successfully avoided parcels and human interaction in real-world UR5 robot experiments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Tightly integrating a global particle-based continuous occupancy map (G-DSP) with an MPPI-based planner (D-STORM) enables robust reactive motion generation for manipulators in dynamic, unstructured environments.
> - Explicitly modeling obstacle dynamics (velocity, uncertainty) within both real-time perception and motion planning modules allows for more proactive and safer collision avoidance.
> - Tensor optimization and GPU acceleration are crucial for achieving real-time performance in high-dimensional manipulator tasks by efficiently handling batch operations in both mapping and stochastic control.
