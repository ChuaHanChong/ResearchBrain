# EDELINE: Enhancing Memory in Diffusion-based World Models via Linear-Time Sequence Modeling

> [!summary] Summary
> EDELINE enhances diffusion-based world models by integrating linear-time State Space Models (Mamba) to process unbounded historical sequences, improving memory capacity and visual prediction fidelity. The unified architecture achieves a mean Human Normalized Score of 1.87 on Atari 100k and significantly outperforms prior models in memory-demanding environments like Crafter and ViZDoom, demonstrating more consistent and accurate long-term imagination.

## Problem

- Existing diffusion-based world models like DIAMOND rely on a fixed, short context window of past observations and actions, limiting their ability to capture long-term dependencies in complex or partially observable environments.
- Prior diffusion-based world models often use separate networks for observation, reward, and termination prediction, preventing efficient representation sharing and leading to suboptimal learning.
- The inability to retain long-term memory results in inaccurate and inconsistent predictions over extended imagination horizons, especially in visually challenging 2D and 3D environments.

## Method

- **EDELINE** introduces a unified architecture that integrates a Mamba-based ==Recurrent Embedding Module== (REM) to process unbounded history and generate deterministic hidden states.
- A Next-Frame Predictor, a U-Net-like diffusion model, generates high-fidelity next observations conditioned on both recent frames and the Mamba-generated hidden embedding via Adaptive Group Normalization and Cross-Attention blocks.
- Reward and Termination Predictors, implemented as MLPs, are directly conditioned on the shared Mamba-generated hidden embedding, and a dynamic loss harmonization technique is applied to balance multi-task learning objectives.

## Results

- EDELINE achieves a mean Human Normalized Score (HNS) of 1.87 on the Atari 100k benchmark, surpassing existing model-based RL baselines and showing significant gains in memory-intensive games.
- In the Crafter environment, EDELINE obtains an average return of 11.5 within **1M** environment steps, outperforming DreamerV3 XL (9.2) despite using significantly fewer parameters and delivering a **4.1x** improvement over DIAMOND.
- It demonstrates superior performance in ViZDoom, maintaining consistent predictions in 3D environments (e.g., DeadlyCorridor, PredictPosition) where DIAMOND's predictions become inconsistent due to limited memory.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating State Space Models (specifically Mamba) as a recurrent embedding module allows diffusion-based world models to process unbounded observation-action histories, overcoming fixed-context limitations.
> - Conditioning the next-frame diffusion predictor with both historical embeddings (from Mamba) and diffusion time, alongside cross-attention mechanisms, improves visual fidelity and context awareness.
> - Unifying reward and termination prediction with the observation prediction through shared hidden states from the recurrent module, combined with dynamic loss harmonization, leads to more efficient multi-task learning.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work is positioned at the intersection of Model-Based Reinforcement Learning (MBRL) and generative modeling, specifically focusing on World Models. World models are a foundational component of modern RL, aiming to simulate environment dynamics to facilitate agent planning and reasoning, which significantly enhances sample efficiency compared to model-free RL.

The landscape of world models has evolved primarily along two paradigms:
1. **Latent-Space Models:** Approaches like Dreamer and DreamerV3 learn compact latent representations of the environment, using recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to predict future states within this compressed space. While efficient for policy optimization, this compression can lead to information loss, compromising generality and visual reconstruction quality, especially in environments requiring fine-grained visual detail.
2. **Generative Models:** More recently, generative models, particularly diffusion models, have been integrated into world modeling. DIAMOND was a significant breakthrough in this area, demonstrating superior visual fidelity in predictions for environments like Atari 100k. However, prior generative models, including DIAMOND, suffer from a critical limitation: they typically rely on fixed-length observation-action windows (e.g., the last four frames in DIAMOND) for conditioning, which inherently limits their memory capacity. This fixed context makes them struggle in environments requiring long-term temporal dependencies or reasoning over prolonged sequences of observations, such as partially observable settings. Furthermore, existing frameworks often segregate prediction tasks (e.g., separate networks for reward and termination signals from observation modeling), potentially leading to suboptimal representation sharing and optimization conflicts.

**State Space Models (SSMs)** have recently emerged as a powerful alternative to traditional RNNs and Transformers for sequence modeling. SSMs, especially the Mamba architecture, offer linear-time complexity, efficient parallel processing, and robust capabilities for modeling long-term dependencies, overcoming the quadratic complexity of Transformers and the gradient stability issues of older RNNs. Recent works like R2I and Drama have begun exploring SSMs in world models to address long-term memory challenges.

EDELINE synthesizes these two advanced areas: diffusion models for high-fidelity visual prediction and State Space Models (specifically Mamba) for efficient long-term memory. By integrating Mamba-based recurrent embedding modules with diffusion models, EDELINE directly addresses the fixed-context limitations and architectural fragmentation seen in prior diffusion-based world models like DIAMOND. This positions EDELINE as a significant advancement, aiming to combine the best of both worlds: the visual richness of diffusion models with the unbounded memory and temporal reasoning capabilities of SSMs, all within a unified framework. This approach is distinct from generative game engines (e.g., GameGAN, Genie), which train generative models from pre-collected datasets without RL-in-the-loop learning.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation behind EDELINE is to overcome the inherent limitations of existing diffusion-based world models, thereby enhancing the sample efficiency and performance of reinforcement learning agents.

The key motivations and objectives can be summarized as follows:

- **Addressing Memory Limitations in Diffusion World Models:** Current diffusion-based world models, exemplified by DIAMOND, rely on a fixed, short context window of past observations and actions (e.g., 4 frames). This fixed context severely limits their ability to capture long-term dependencies and make accurate predictions in scenarios that require extensive historical context, especially in partially observable environments. EDELINE aims to extend this memory capacity to process unbounded observation-action sequences.
- **Enhancing Prediction Consistency in Complex Environments:** The inability to retain long-term memory leads to inaccurate and inconsistent predictions over extended imagination horizons, particularly in visually challenging 2D environments (e.g., Atari games with memory elements), and even more so in complex 3D first-person environments (e.g., ViZDoom) that demand sophisticated spatiotemporal reasoning. EDELINE seeks to improve this consistency.
- **Unifying World Model Architecture:** Existing diffusion-based world models often use separate neural networks for different predictive tasks, such as observations, rewards, and termination signals. This architectural segregation prevents efficient representation sharing and can lead to suboptimal overall learning and optimization conflicts. EDELINE aims to create a unified framework where these predictions are conditioned on shared, rich hidden states.
- **Leveraging State Space Models for Efficiency and Memory:** To address the long-sequence dependency issues, the paper leverages State Space Models (SSMs), specifically Mamba, known for their linear-time complexity and robust memorization capabilities. The objective is to integrate SSMs as a "Recurrent Embedding Module" to process long histories efficiently.
- **Balancing Multi-task Learning Objectives:** World models inherently involve multi-task learning (predicting observations, rewards, terminations). Disparities in the scale and nature of these objectives can cause one task (e.g., high-dimensional visual prediction) to dominate the learning process. EDELINE aims to employ dynamic loss harmonization to effectively balance these objectives.

In essence, EDELINE's core objective is to develop a **unified world model architecture** that integrates the high-fidelity visual generation of diffusion models with the efficient long-term memory capabilities of State Space Models, addressing the shortcomings of prior work to achieve superior performance across diverse and challenging RL benchmarks.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

EDELINE (Enhancing Diffusion-basEd World Models via LINEar-Time Sequence Modeling) introduces a unified framework that integrates State Space Models (SSMs) with diffusion-based world models. The architecture consists of three principal components: a Recurrent Embedding Module (REM), a Next-Frame Predictor, and a Reward/Termination Predictor.

1. **Recurrent Embedding Module (REM) - `f_ϕ`:**
 - **Purpose:** To process the entire history of observations and actions (`o_0, a_0..., o_t, a_t`) to generate a hidden embedding `h_t`. This module is crucial for overcoming the fixed-context window limitation of previous diffusion world models.
 - **Architecture:** Implemented using **Mamba**, a selective State Space Model.
 - **Rationale for Mamba:** Chosen over self-attention-based Transformers (due to quadratic complexity and inefficiency) and traditional RNNs like LSTMs/GRUs (due to gradient instability and limited long-term dependency learning). Mamba offers linear-time complexity with respect to sequence length, efficient parallel processing, and robust memorization capabilities via its state-space formulation, making it suitable for processing theoretically unbounded sequences.
 - **Functionality:** Computes `h_t = f_ϕ(h_{t-1}, o_t, a_t)`, recursively updating the hidden state.

2. **Next-Frame Predictor - `p_ϕ`:**
 - **Purpose:** To generate high-fidelity predictions of the subsequent observation (`o_{t+1}`) using a denoising diffusion process.
 - **Architecture:** A U-Net-like denoising network (`D_ϕ`).
 - **Conditioning:** Conditions generation on the last `L` frames *and* the hidden embedding `h_t` from the REM, along with the diffusion time `τ`.
 - **Key Innovations beyond DIAMOND:**
 - **Adaptive Group Normalization (AGN):** Integrated within each residual block, parameters are conditioned on *both* the hidden embedding `h_t` and diffusion time `τ`. This extends DIAMOND's approach, which only conditioned AGN on `τ` and action embeddings, enabling more context-aware feature normalization.
 - **Cross-Attention Blocks:** Inspired by Latent Diffusion Models (LDMs), these blocks are introduced to fuse spatial-temporal features from the U-Net with the abstract dynamics encoded in `h_t`. `h_t` and `τ` serve as context vectors, while U-Net feature maps generate the query. This mechanism, absent in DIAMOND, facilitates deeper integration of learned dynamics.
 - **Loss:** Optimized using a mean squared error (MSE) loss, `L_obs(ϕ)`, based on the denoising objective (Eq. 2).

3. **Reward / Termination Predictor - `p_ϕ`:**
 - **Purpose:** To estimate the reward (`r_t`) and episode termination signal (`d_t`).
 - **Architecture:** Implemented as multilayer perceptrons (MLPs).
 - **Integration:** Crucially, these predictors directly utilize the deterministic hidden embedding `h_t` from the REM as their conditioning input. This represents a significant architectural unification compared to DIAMOND, which used a separate recurrent neural network for these tasks. This approach enables efficient representation sharing across all predictive tasks, allowing REM to learn dynamics encompassing all relevant environmental aspects.
 - **Loss:** Optimized via negative log-likelihood (cross-entropy) losses, `L_rew(ϕ)` and `L_end(ϕ)` (Eq. 3).

**World Model Training Strategy:**
- **Parallel Hidden Embedding Computation:** Leverages Mamba's parallel scan algorithm to efficiently compute hidden embeddings across all timesteps of a trajectory segment.
- **Random Target Timestep Sampling for Observation Loss:** To mitigate the high computational cost of applying the observation loss at every timestep (due to the recurrent nature and diffusion process), a single target timestep `j` is randomly sampled within a trajectory segment (after a burn-in period `B`) for computing `L_obs`. This allows for computational efficiency comparable to DIAMOND while retaining long-term memory benefits.
- **Unified Multi-task Loss Harmonization:** The total world model loss `L(ϕ)` combines `L_obs(ϕ)`, `L_rew(ϕ)`, and `L_end(ϕ)`. It adopts a dynamic loss harmonization technique inspired by HarmonyDream, using adaptive weights `w_o` and `w_r` (Eq. 5) to balance the high-dimensional observation modeling loss with the scalar reward modeling loss, addressing scale disparities.

**Agent Behavior Learning:**
- To ensure fair comparison and focus on the world model's efficacy, the agent architecture (policy `π_θ` and value `V_θ` networks) adopts the same optimization framework as DIAMOND. This includes REINFORCE with a value baseline and Bellman error optimization using λ-returns, following established model-based RL paradigms. The agent uses the world model to generate imagined trajectories for policy optimization.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

EDELINE demonstrates superior performance across a diverse set of reinforcement learning benchmarks, highlighting its enhanced memory capabilities, improved visual fidelity, and unified architecture.

1. **Atari 100k Benchmark:**
 - **Quantitative Performance:** EDELINE achieves a mean Human Normalized Score (HNS) of 1.87, a median HNS of 0.82, and an Interquartile Mean (IQM) HNS of 0.94. These scores significantly surpass existing model-based RL baselines that do not use look-ahead search techniques. It achieves superhuman performance in 13 out of 26 games.
 - **Comparison to DIAMOND:** EDELINE shows particular strength in memory-intensive Atari environments like BankHeist and Hero, where its improved memorization capabilities lead to substantial performance gains over DIAMOND.
 - **Underlying Reason:** The superior performance is attributed to EDELINE's ability to retain the visual fidelity advantages of diffusion-based models while integrating enhanced memory via SSMs, allowing it to select precise visual details crucial for reward.

2. **Crafter Benchmark:**
 - **Memory Demanding Environment:** Crafter is a procedurally generated survival environment known for requiring wide and deep exploration, long-term reasoning, and credit assignment, making it an ideal test for memory-enhanced architectures.
 - **Quantitative Performance:** EDELINE achieves an average return of 11.5 ± 0.9 within a 1M environment step budget. This significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines like DreamerV3 XL (9.2 ± 0.3), despite EDELINE having a much smaller parameter count (11M vs. 200M).
 - **Comparison to DIAMOND:** EDELINE delivers a 4.1x improvement over DIAMOND (2.8 ± 0.5) with a comparable parameter count (11M vs. 10.4M), showcasing the substantial benefits of its enhanced memory mechanism in complex, long-horizon tasks.
 - **Qualitative Evidence:** Appendix D.11 provides qualitative analysis showing EDELINE's superior ability to remember environmental features (e.g., trees, crafting tables) and maintain consistency when revisiting previously explored areas, which is critical for effective planning.

3. **ViZDoom Experiments:**
 - **Challenging 3D Environment:** ViZDoom, a first-person 3D environment, presents increased complexity for memory retention and imagination consistency.
 - **Performance:** EDELINE demonstrates superior performance over DIAMOND in scenarios demanding sophisticated 3D scene modeling (e.g., DeadlyCorridor) and spatiotemporal prediction (e.g., PredictPosition).
 - **Qualitative Evidence:** In DeadlyCorridor, EDELINE's SSM effectively integrates information from the entire history, allowing it to maintain consistent predictions of the agent's position, enemies, and task-critical objects (like armor). In contrast, DIAMOND's predictions become blurry and inconsistent due to its 4-frame memory constraint, failing to accurately model crucial visual elements and the overall scene structure. This highlights how enhanced spatial awareness from extended memory directly contributes to EDELINE's superior performance in 3D.

4. **Ablation Studies (Appendices D.9):**
 - **REM Architecture Choice:** Mamba consistently shows more stable learning curves and superior final performance compared to traditional linear-time sequence models like GRU and LSTM, especially in memory-intensive tasks.
 - **Cross-Attention in Next-Frame Predictor:** The inclusion of cross-attention mechanisms significantly improves performance, especially in environments requiring rich contextual information processing, by enabling effective fusion of hidden embeddings with visual features.
 - **Effect of Harmonizers:** Dynamic loss harmonization using harmonizers is crucial. Removing them leads to significant performance degradation, as the model struggles to balance observation and reward modeling, failing to retain precise, reward-relevant visual details.

5. **Linear Probing and Generation Quality (Appendices D.6, D.7):**
 - **Linear Probing:** EDELINE's unified hidden representations effectively encode information for both reward and observation prediction. It significantly outperforms DIAMOND in observation feature prediction (57.3% average reduction in loss) while maintaining comparable reward prediction performance.
 - **Generation Quality (Pixel-wise MSE):** EDELINE achieves lower pixel-wise MSE than DIAMOND in 20 out of 27 environments, with an average 8.2% reduction in pixel-level error. This confirms its superior visual generation quality due to the longer observation history provided by Mamba.

6. **Training Time Profile (Appendix D.8):**
 - Despite its architectural innovations, EDELINE maintains comparable total training time to DIAMOND. Its world model updates are approximately 26.8% faster due to the unified architecture and Mamba's parallel scan, offsetting the slightly higher computation time for actor-critic updates (due to more complex inference during imagination).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

EDELINE represents a significant step forward in the field of Model-Based Reinforcement Learning, particularly for environments that demand complex temporal reasoning and high-fidelity visual prediction. Its core significance lies in effectively addressing fundamental limitations of prior diffusion-based world models, paving the way for more capable and sample-efficient AI agents.

The potential impacts of EDELINE's contributions are multifaceted:

- **Enhanced Sample Efficiency:** By providing more accurate and consistent environmental dynamics models over extended horizons, EDELINE allows RL agents to learn policies with significantly less real-world interaction. This is crucial for applications where data collection is expensive, time-consuming, or risky, such as in robotics, autonomous driving, or complex simulations.
- **Improved Performance in Memory-Demanding Tasks:** EDELINE's Mamba-based Recurrent Embedding Module fundamentally improves the memory capacity of world models beyond fixed context windows. This is particularly impactful for partially observable environments, long-horizon tasks, and procedurally generated worlds (like Crafter) where agents need to recall past events or previously visited locations for effective planning and decision-making. Its strong performance in ViZDoom, a 3D first-person environment, further validates its ability to handle complex spatiotemporal dependencies.
- **Unified and Efficient Architecture:** The integrated approach to observation, reward, and termination prediction, conditioned on shared hidden states from the REM, leads to more efficient representation sharing and a more streamlined training process. This architectural elegance not only boosts performance but also simplifies the design of future world models.
- **Advancement in Generative World Models:** By synergistically combining the visual prowess of diffusion models with the temporal modeling strength of SSMs, EDELINE pushes the boundaries of what generative world models can achieve. The qualitative improvements in prediction fidelity, especially for reward-critical elements and consistent scene understanding, demonstrate a deeper comprehension of environment dynamics.
- **Foundational for Future AI Systems:** More robust and accurate world models are foundational for developing truly intelligent agents that can learn, plan, and generalize in complex, dynamic environments. EDELINE's advancements could contribute to more realistic and interactive AI simulations, safer autonomous systems, and more agile robotic agents that can operate effectively in unstructured settings.
- **Catalyst for Further Research:** The success of integrating SSMs with diffusion models could inspire further research into hybrid architectures that combine the strengths of different deep learning paradigms. Exploring more efficient diffusion processes or novel ways to integrate world models with policy optimization could be future directions.

While the paper acknowledges the inherent computational requirements of diffusion-based models, EDELINE demonstrates that significant memory enhancements and performance gains can be achieved without drastically increasing overall training time compared to prior SOTA diffusion-based models. This balance between capability and practicality makes EDELINE a highly promising contribution to the field of artificial intelligence.
