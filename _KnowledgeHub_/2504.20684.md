# Identifying Uncertainty in Self-Adaptive Robotics with Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> This research evaluates the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) to systematically identify uncertainties in self-adaptive robotic systems. An empirical study with 10 LLMs across four industrial-scale use cases showed that practitioners agreed with 63% to 88% of the LLM-identified uncertainties, including some previously overlooked ones.

## Problem

- Identifying and managing uncertainties in increasingly complex self-adaptive robots is challenging due to dynamic environments, evolving configurations, and complex interactions.
- Current practices for uncertainty identification often rely on practitioners' intuition and experience, which can be unsystematic and limited by incomplete knowledge of unpredictable operational contexts.
- A lack of comprehensive understanding exists regarding all factors influencing uncertainty across the entire robotic software engineering lifecycle.

## Method

- A novel, systematic LLM-prompting method was developed, instructing LLMs to assume specific roles and leverage robotic requirements to identify uncertainties.
- Ten advanced LLMs (e.g., GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, ==Gemini Pro== 1.5) were selected and used with self-consistency prompting to generate uncertainty responses for four industrial-sized robotic case studies.
- Practitioners from each case study evaluated the anonymized LLM-generated responses using a Likert scale and discussed their findings, followed by quantitative and qualitative data analysis.

## Results

- Practitioners showed substantial agreement, ranging from **63%** to **88%**, with the uncertainties identified by the LLMs across all four industrial case studies.
- Specific LLMs like GPT-4o, Claude **3.5** Sonnet, and LLama **3.3** 70B exhibited high agreement and low disagreement in respective case studies.
- Inter-rater reliability (Cohen's Kappa) and bootstrap confidence intervals confirmed high consistency and reliability in practitioner evaluations of LLM responses.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - LLMs demonstrated a strong understanding of specific robotic domain knowledge and generated logical, contextually relevant responses for identifying uncertainties.
> - LLMs identified realistic uncertainties, some of which were previously unconsidered by practitioners, showcasing their ability to augment human expertise.
> - Practitioner feedback revealed fragmented understanding of uncertainties across different software engineering lifecycle phases, suggesting LLMs could bridge these knowledge gaps.
