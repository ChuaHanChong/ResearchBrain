# ProDrive: Proactive Planning for Autonomous Driving via Ego-Environment Co-Evolution

> [!summary] Summary
> ProDrive introduces a framework for proactive autonomous driving planning that tightly couples a query-centric trajectory planner with a BEV world model, enabling ego-environment co-evolution. The system achieves a Predictive Driver Model Score (PDMS) of 86.6 on the NAVSIM benchmark, demonstrating improved safety and efficiency by anticipating future scene evolution and interactions.

## Problem

- Current autonomous driving planners are predominantly reactive, making decisions based only on immediate sensor observations.
- This reactive nature often leads to myopic decisions, safety-critical failures, and suboptimal efficiency in dynamic and complex driving environments.
- Existing integrations of world models are frequently auxiliary or loosely coupled, failing to deeply influence the planner's internal decision-making process with future scene dynamics.

## Method

- **ProDrive** jointly trains a ==query-centric trajectory planner== (Ego Module) and a ==BEV world model== (Environment Module) end-to-end.
- Implements ==Ego-Environment Coupling== by injecting ==planner-aware ego tokens== into the world model to condition future scene predictions on the ego vehicle's intended actions.
- Maintains ==unbroken gradient flow== from the world model's future outcome assessments back to the planner, letting predicted states and rewards shape decision-making during training.

## Results

- **ProDrive** achieved **PDMS 86.6** on NAVSIM v1, outperforming reactive and other world-model-integrated baselines.
- Strong safety: **NC 98.0**, **DAC 95.4**, **TTC 93.7**.
- Enhanced efficiency: **Ego Progress 80.7**; ablations confirm world model, ego token injection, and proactive gradient feedback are all necessary.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Bidirectional interaction between a planner and a world model, where each informs the other, is crucial for enabling proactive and safer autonomous driving.
> - Injecting rich, planner-aware semantic context (ego tokens) into the world model significantly enhances the accuracy of future scene prediction and improves the collaborative optimization of the system.
> - Maintaining an end-to-end gradient path from future-aware reward signals (e.g., collision avoidance, progress) back to the planner is vital for learning anticipatory behaviors and making globally optimal decisions.
