# Mesh-Pro: Asynchronous Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization for Artist-style Quadrilateral Mesh Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Mesh-Pro introduces an asynchronous online reinforcement learning framework and the Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization (ARPO) algorithm for generating high-quality artist-style quadrilateral meshes. The method achieves a 3.75x speedup in training and establishes new state-of-the-art performance in geometric fidelity, integrity, and topological quality on 3D mesh generation benchmarks.

## Problem

- Existing reinforcement learning methods for 3D mesh generation, primarily offline DPO, suffer from low training efficiency due to static datasets and inability to dynamically update policies.
- Implicit reward modeling in DPO leads to limited generalization, struggling to capture complex reward functions and generate high-quality out-of-distribution artist-style meshes.
- Prior mesh tokenization schemes and rule-based reward signals often result in geometric artifacts, inconsistent ordering, and fail to fully capture artistic quality standards like clean edge flow and structural integrity.

## Method

- A novel asynchronous online reinforcement learning framework is designed for ==3D mesh generation==, eliminating GPU idle times and enabling continuous policy and data updates, speeding up training by 3.75x.
- The Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization (ARPO) algorithm is introduced, balancing training efficiency and generalization by using explicit advantage values to guide ranking-based preference optimization.
- A diagonal-aware mixed triangular-quadrilateral tokenization scheme defers face type decisions for stable generation, complemented by a robust multi-faceted reward system including ray-based geometric integrity and topological rewards.

## Results

- The asynchronous online RL framework demonstrates a **3.75x** speed improvement over synchronous RL for 3D mesh generation, enhancing post-training efficiency and stability.
- Mesh-Pro achieves new state-of-the-art quantitative performance across multiple metrics, including lowest Chamfer Distance (**0.028** vs QuadGPT's **0.059**), lowest Broken Ratio (**22%** vs QuadGPT's **50%**), and highest Quad Ratio (**81%** vs QuadGPT's **78%**).
- Qualitative evaluations show Mesh-Pro consistently generates topologically sound, geometrically faithful, artist-style quadrilateral meshes, outperforming existing methods and significantly improving performance in downstream tasks like UV unwrapping and animation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Asynchronous online reinforcement learning significantly enhances training efficiency for autoregressive 3D mesh generation by concurrently updating policies and collecting data, effectively mitigating issues of varying token lengths.
> - ARPO's combination of ranking preference optimization with explicit advantage guidance enables faster, more stable convergence while achieving superior generalization compared to previous RL algorithms like DPO and GRPO.
> - A comprehensive reward function incorporating ray-based geometric integrity and topological structure, combined with a 'generate-then-decide' tokenization, is essential for producing artist-style, high-fidelity quadrilateral meshes.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The generation of high-quality 3D meshes is a cornerstone for numerous applications, including gaming, virtual reality, and robotics. Historically, 3D mesh generation has evolved through several paradigms, each with its inherent strengths and limitations.

Early approaches often relied on continuous neural representations (e.g., vecsets) or sparse voxels, which, when combined with iso-surfacing algorithms, typically produced dense, topologically unstructured triangular meshes. While some techniques aimed to accelerate field prediction for quadrilateral generation, they were often not end-to-end generative frameworks and frequently lacked the intentional edge flow highly valued by professional artists. The visual fidelity and realism of 3D models are heavily dictated by mesh quality, making the generation of structured, high-quality meshes a significant challenge.

More recently, autoregressive models have emerged as a powerful paradigm, capable of operating directly on raw mesh data. Innovations like Meshtron and QuadGPT have demonstrated strong modeling capabilities for generating structured meshes, including native quadrilateral representations. However, these supervised learning-based approaches often introduce undesirable artifacts such as holes, non-manifold surfaces, tiny facets, and disorganized topology, failing to meet the stringent quality standards of professional artists.

To address these quality deficiencies, reinforcement learning (RL) has gained traction, inspired by its profound success in text and image generation. Previous attempts in 3D mesh generation, such as DeepMesh, Mesh-RFT, and QuadGPT, have employed RL to fine-tune pre-trained networks. These methods typically leverage offline Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), guided by human-constructed preference pairs or rule-based reward signals, to improve topology and align mesh quality with artistic standards.

Mesh-Pro directly tackles the critical limitations of these existing RL-based approaches. It identifies two major shortcomings of offline DPO in 3D mesh generation: (1) **Low Training Efficiency:** Offline methods are constrained by static datasets, preventing dynamic policy updates and leading to inefficient training. While asynchronous RL frameworks exist for text, they are not directly suitable for the unique challenges of autoregressive 3D mesh generation, particularly the highly varying token lengths. (2) **Limited Generalization:** DPO's implicit reward modeling, while smooth and stable, struggles to capture complex reward function distributions, resulting in poor generalization to out-of-distribution data. Explicit reward modeling methods like Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) offer better generalization but often suffer from slow convergence and low exploration-exploitation efficiency when applied to 3D.

In this context, Mesh-Pro presents a significant advancement by introducing an asynchronous online RL framework and a novel algorithm, Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization (ARPO). This work aims to overcome the efficiency and generalization bottlenecks of previous RL methods, pushing the state-of-the-art in generating artist-style quadrilateral-dominated meshes with superior diversity, fidelity, and topological quality. It seeks to bring RL-driven 3D generation closer to the transformative impact seen in other generative AI domains.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of Mesh-Pro is to significantly enhance both the training efficiency and the generation quality of reinforcement learning in 3D mesh generation, particularly for artist-style quadrilateral meshes. This ambitious goal is driven by addressing several key problems identified in the current research landscape:

1. **Improve Training Efficiency of RL for 3D Mesh Generation:**
 - **Motivation:** Existing RL methods for 3D mesh generation predominantly rely on offline Direct Preference Optimization (DPO). This offline nature means training policies cannot dynamically adapt to new data, leading to low training efficiency. Furthermore, while online RL offers dynamic updates, synchronous online RL in autoregressive 3D mesh generation is prohibitively expensive. The highly varying token lengths of 3D meshes cause long waiting times between policy updates and significant GPU idle periods, making synchronous training impractical at scale.
 - **Objective:** Design and implement the first asynchronous online RL framework specifically tailored for 3D mesh generation. This framework aims to dramatically improve post-training efficiency by eliminating idle times and enabling continuous policy and data updates, thereby overcoming the limitations of both offline and synchronous online methods.

2. **Enhance Generalization and Quality of RL Algorithms for 3D Meshes:**
 - **Motivation:** DPO, despite its fast and stable convergence due to implicit reward modeling, struggles to explicitly capture complex reward function distributions. This limitation results in poor generalization, especially to out-of-distribution data, which is crucial for generating diverse artist-style meshes. While explicit reward modeling methods like Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) offer stronger generalization, empirical evidence shows they often suffer from low exploration-exploitation efficiency and slow convergence in the context of 3D generative models, primarily due to the complexity of reward distributions and the capabilities of pre-trained foundation models.
 - **Objective:** Develop a novel RL algorithm, Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization (ARPO), that achieves a superior trade-off between training efficiency and generalization. ARPO aims to combine the fast and stable convergence benefits of ranking preference optimization with the enhanced generalization capabilities provided by explicit advantage function guidance, thereby generating higher-quality quadrilateral meshes.

3. **Address Limitations in Mesh Representation and Reward Signals:**
 - **Motivation:** Prior methods for mixed triangle-quad mesh tokenization (e.g., QuadGPT) often use a leading special token to declare face type, which forces a premature commitment and precludes a truly consistent canonical ordering. This can lead to geometric artifacts and structural defects. Additionally, current rule-based reward mechanisms for RL fine-tuning may not fully capture all aspects of geometric integrity and topological quality desired by artists.
 - **Objective:** Propose Mesh-Pro, a comprehensive framework that integrates the asynchronous ARPO with a novel diagonal-aware mixed triangular-quadrilateral tokenization scheme. This new tokenization strategy defers face type decisions and enforces a global canonical order for more stable generation. Furthermore, Mesh-Pro aims to incorporate a robust ray-based reward mechanism for geometric integrity, complementing topological rewards and ensuring the generation of geometrically complete and structurally sound meshes.

By tackling these motivations, Mesh-Pro aims to significantly advance the field of 3D mesh generation, enabling the creation of high-fidelity, topologically consistent, and artist-preferred quadrilateral meshes with unprecedented efficiency.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Mesh-Pro proposes a comprehensive approach that integrates a novel mesh tokenization scheme, an asynchronous online reinforcement learning framework, a new RL algorithm (ARPO), and a multi-faceted reward design. The overall architecture consists of an initial pre-training phase followed by an RL post-training phase.

#### 3.1. Mesh Generation Pre-Training

- **Diagonal-Aware Mesh Tokenization:** Mesh-Pro introduces a novel tokenization scheme for mixed triangle-quad meshes to address limitations in prior methods.
 - **Canonical Representation:** Vertex coordinates are normalized, n-bit quantized, and lexicographically sorted to establish a consistent global order.
 - **"Generate-then-Decide" Strategy:** The model first generates a three-vertex base triangle. It then decides whether to terminate (forming a triangle, indicated by a padding token) or append a fourth vertex.
 - **Explicit Diagonal Encoding:** For quadrilaterals, the internal diagonal's orientation (flag ∈ {0, 1, 2}) is explicitly encoded as an offset added to the fourth vertex’s index. This defers the triangle-vs-quad decision to the last position, reducing predictive burden and resolving issues of inconsistent ordering in previous methods.
- **Model Architecture and Training:** The Hourglass Transformer architecture, known for its ability to capture global patterns and local details by progressively compressing sequences, is adopted. The model is pre-trained autoregressively to reconstruct input meshes using a negative log-likelihood loss on the tokenized sequence.

#### 3.2. Asynchronous Online RL Framework

To overcome the training efficiency issues of offline and synchronous RL, Mesh-Pro designs the first asynchronous online RL framework for 3D mesh generation.
- **Parallel Operation:** Rollout workers continuously generate training data by interacting with the environment (generating meshes), while trainer workers constantly sample data from a rollout replay buffer for policy updates.
- **Dynamic Policy Updates:** Updated model weights are periodically saved and fetched by rollout workers, ensuring that rollouts are generated using policies that are not too stale. Outdated rollout data in the buffer are promptly discarded.
- **Efficiency Gains:** This asynchronous design eliminates significant idle waiting times between rollout and trainer workers, dynamically updating both the policy and data to improve RL efficiency by 3.75× compared to synchronous RL.
- **Pre-Start Stage:** A crucial "pre-start" stage is introduced for stable asynchronous training. Rollout workers first collect an initial set of samples (S1) before trainers begin. The first policy checkpoint is saved after N1 training steps, allowing the model to adapt to the reward distribution. Subsequent updates save new policies more frequently (every N2 steps, where N2 < N1).
- **Policy Update Frequency Trade-Off:** Hyperparameters (N1, N2, buffer sizes S1, S2, GPU counts T, batch size B) are carefully controlled to balance the rate of policy updates with sufficient data for learning complex reward distributions.
- **Truncated Training:** To handle the varying lengths of 3D meshes efficiently, the framework employs truncated training. Each complete mesh is randomly truncated T times into segments (Mt), and each segment, along with the input point cloud (P) and its corresponding reward (Rt), forms a rollout data sample. Trainers use these truncated samples for policy updates.

#### 3.3. ARPO (Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization)

ARPO is a novel RL algorithm designed for a better trade-off between training efficiency and generalization.
- **Addressing Prior Limitations:** It aims to overcome the slow convergence of explicit advantage methods (like GRPO) and the poor generalization of implicit methods (like DPO).
- **Ranking-based Optimization with Advantage Guidance:** ARPO samples a group of rollout data and leverages a ranking-based optimization strategy. It explicitly uses advantage values, calculated from rewards, as a weighting mechanism to guide the model in learning the underlying reward distribution.
- **Objective Function:** Derived from a KL-regularized preference optimization objective and the Plackett–Luce model, ARPO minimizes an advantage-weighted negative preference. The advantage (A(k)) is calculated based on the difference between a sample's reward (R(k)) and the minimum reward within the group, normalized by the sum of these differences. This assigns greater weight to "high-dominance" samples and reduces the impact of noise from "low-dominance" samples.
- **Mechanism:** ARPO acts as a fast and stable ranking strategy model. By explicitly guiding with an advantage function, it achieves a deeper understanding and modeling of reward distributions, leading to stronger generalization.
- **Integration with Asynchronous Framework:** ARPO is seamlessly integrated into the asynchronous online RL. Rollout workers store groups of samples in the replay buffer, and trainer workers use the ARPO loss to learn better policies based on timely datasets.
- **Truncated Asynchronous ARPO Training:** ARPO loss is applied to truncated mesh segments (y_i|m:m+w) to ensure the RL policy learns to make locally optimal decisions that lead to globally superior topology.

#### 3.4. Reward Design

A robust, multifaceted reward function guides Mesh-Pro towards high-quality, artist-style outputs.
- **Combined Reward:** The total reward R(Mt) is a conditional sum of a topological reward (R_topo) and a geometric integrity reward (R_ray), with a check for Hausdorff distance consistency (R_hd). If integrity or consistency thresholds are not met, the reward is 0.
- **Ray Casting Integrity Reward (R_ray(Mt)):** Rays are cast towards the mesh from multiple directions. Mesh quality is assessed by comparing the normal direction of the surface hit by each ray with the ray's direction. Incomplete or inconsistent regions (e.g., broken surfaces) result in "bad faces." If the number of bad faces (N_bf) exceeds a threshold (θ_ray), the total reward is zero, prioritizing structural integrity. This method is more robust than boundary-edge-based rewards, especially for multi-component objects.
- **Topological Reward (R_topo(Mt)):** Inspired by QuadGPT, this reward emphasizes structured edge flow. It quantifies "quad rings" (closed loops of faces) and "quad lines" (open strips of faces) identified via an edge-based traversal algorithm. R_topo is computed as a weighted sum: w_qr * N_qr + N_ql^2. This promotes clean, structured quad layouts.
- **Hausdorff Distance Consistency Reward (R_hd(Mt)):** Ensures geometric fidelity between the generated mesh and the input point cloud. If the Hausdorff distance (D_hd) exceeds a threshold (θ_hd), the total reward is zero, maintaining alignment with the input.

This comprehensive methodology allows Mesh-Pro to generate high-quality, artist-style quadrilateral meshes by optimizing both efficiency and generalization in the RL training process, guided by a robust tokenization and a sophisticated reward system.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Mesh-Pro demonstrates significant advancements in 3D mesh generation through both qualitative and quantitative evaluations.

#### 4.1. Efficiency and Performance of Asynchronous Online RL

- **Speed Improvement:** The proposed asynchronous online RL framework is shown to be approximately **3.75 times faster** than synchronous RL for the same number of training steps and meshes, primarily by eliminating idle GPU time and dynamic policy/data updates.
- **Training Stability:** The "Pre-Start" stage is crucial for enhancing the stability of asynchronous RL training, leading to higher-quality meshes and consistent performance improvement over training steps.
- **Superiority over Offline RL:** Asynchronous online RL consistently achieves better performance across all evaluated metrics compared to offline RL.

#### 4.2. Effectiveness of ARPO

- **Generalization vs. Efficiency Trade-off:** ARPO achieves a better balance between training efficiency and generalization compared to existing RL algorithms for 3D mesh generation.
 - **Superior Generalization:** Explicit advantage guidance in ARPO improves the model's generalization ability, leading to higher quality generated meshes.
 - **Faster and Stable Convergence:** Compared to GRPO, which suffers from inefficient exploration-exploitation and slow convergence due to complex reward distributions, ARPO exhibits faster and more stable convergence. It also outperforms DPO in generalization while maintaining similar training stability.
- **Mesh Quality:** Visual comparisons confirm that meshes generated using ARPO are significantly superior in quality to those produced by DPO and GRPO. Loss curves further illustrate ARPO's faster and more stable convergence.

#### 4.3. State-of-the-Art Qualitative Results

- **Artist-Style Mesh Generation:** Mesh-Pro consistently generates topologically sound and geometrically faithful reconstructions. This is a stark contrast to existing methods, which frequently produce meshes with severe topological artifacts such as broken geometry, irregular topology, holes, and fragmented surfaces, particularly in challenging domains like out-of-distribution meshes and artist-created assets (Figure 5, 8, 15, 16).
- **Downstream Task Performance:** The high-quality, native quad-dominant topology produced by Mesh-Pro significantly improves performance in downstream tasks such as UV unwrapping, texture painting (less distortion, better consistency), and animation (smoother, more controllable deformation) (Figure 17).

#### 4.4. Quantitative State-of-the-Art Performance

- Mesh-Pro sets new benchmarks on both dense meshes (from Hunyuan3D 2.5) and artist meshes (from Toys4k dataset).
 - **Geometric Fidelity:** Achieves the lowest Chamfer Distance (CD ↓) and Hausdorff Distance (HD ↓) (e.g., CD of 0.028 on dense meshes vs. QuadGPT's 0.059, HD of 0.090 vs. QuadGPT's 0.163).
 - **Geometric Integrity:** Attains the lowest Broken Ratio (BR ↓) (e.g., 22% on dense meshes vs. QuadGPT's 50%), indicating robust structural integrity. The ray-based reward is crucial here, ensuring minimal defects.
 - **Topological Quality:** Demonstrates the highest Quad Ratio (QR ↑) (e.g., 81% on dense meshes vs. QuadGPT's 78%), reflecting superior structured quad layouts fostered by the topological reward.
 - **Perceptual Quality:** Achieves the highest User Study (US ↑) scores (e.g., 5.2 on dense meshes vs. QuadGPT's 4.5), confirming that Mesh-Pro outputs are perceived as closer to artist-level standards. Additional quantitative metrics like Normal Consistency (NC ↑) and F1 Score (F1 ↑) also show SOTA performance (Table 8).

#### 4.5. Ablation Studies

- **Impact of Diagonal-Aware Tokenization:** The novel tokenization generates more stable meshes with significantly lower structural broken ratios compared to prior methods. When combined with asynchronous ARPO, it shows superior geometric consistency and topological soundness (Table 5).
- **Contribution of Reward Components:** Each component of the reward function is vital. Removing the ray reward (R_ray) substantially increases the mesh broken ratio, while removing the topology reward (R_topo) diminishes overall output quality, underscoring their individual effectiveness and the importance of ensuring mesh integrity first.
- **Data Scaling Potential:** Performance improves with larger training data scales, indicating that asynchronous ARPO has strong data scaling potential (Table 4).

In summary, Mesh-Pro presents a robust and efficient solution for generating high-quality, artist-style quadrilateral meshes, outperforming existing methods by a significant margin across multiple objective and subjective metrics. Its innovations in asynchronous RL, preference optimization, tokenization, and reward design synergistically contribute to its state-of-the-art performance.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The Mesh-Pro research marks a significant stride in the field of 3D content generation, with potential impacts across several dimensions:

- **Transformative Advance in 3D Mesh Generation Quality:** By addressing the persistent challenges of generating artist-style quadrilateral meshes with high fidelity and topological quality, Mesh-Pro fills a critical gap in 3D content creation pipelines. The ability to produce clean, structured quad-dominant meshes with desirable edge flow directly caters to the needs of professional artists and designers in industries like gaming, animation, and industrial design. This can lead to faster content creation cycles, reduced manual labor for mesh clean-up, and higher-quality assets.

- **Pioneering Asynchronous Online RL for 3D:** The introduction of the first asynchronous online RL framework specifically tailored for 3D mesh generation is a foundational contribution. It solves the efficiency bottlenecks associated with varying token lengths in autoregressive 3D models, making large-scale RL training for 3D tasks practically feasible. This innovation could inspire a wave of research into online RL applications for other complex 3D generation problems, moving beyond the limitations of offline methods.

- **Novel RL Algorithm (ARPO) with Broad Applicability:** Advantage-guided Ranking Preference Optimization (ARPO) offers a compelling solution for balancing training efficiency and generalization in complex reward landscapes. While developed for 3D meshes, the underlying principles of leveraging ranking preferences with explicit advantage guidance for robust and efficient learning could be applied to other generative AI domains beyond 3D, potentially enhancing fine-tuning processes for large language models, image generation models, and other sequence-to-sequence tasks where human preferences or complex rewards are involved.

- **Enhanced Downstream Workflow Integration:** The demonstrated superior performance in downstream tasks such as UV unwrapping, texture painting, and animation is a testament to the practical value of Mesh-Pro's outputs. High-quality, topologically sound meshes inherently simplify these subsequent stages of the 3D asset pipeline, reducing distortions and enabling more fluid animations, thereby streamlining the entire content production workflow.

- **Inspiration for Future RL-Driven 3D Generation:** The authors explicitly express hope that Mesh-Pro will "inspire RL-driven 3D generation toward transformative advances akin to RL-driven text generation." This reflects a broader vision where sophisticated RL techniques can unlock new levels of autonomy and quality in 3D content creation, potentially leading to fully AI-generated virtual worlds or highly detailed assets from minimal human input.

- **Industrial Relevance and Commercial Potential:** The involvement of Tencent Hunyuan, a major industrial AI lab, underscores the work's direct relevance to real-world applications and commercial viability. The focus on "artist-style" meshes indicates a clear understanding of market demands in professional 3D content creation. Mesh-Pro's ability to reliably meet strict quality standards, even with minor, auto-repairable defects, positions it as a valuable tool for industrial asset production, potentially setting new standards for AI-assisted 3D modeling.

In conclusion, Mesh-Pro does more than just advance the state-of-the-art in 3D mesh generation; it lays a crucial foundation for the next generation of RL-powered 3D creation tools, promising significant impacts on both academic research and industrial practice.
