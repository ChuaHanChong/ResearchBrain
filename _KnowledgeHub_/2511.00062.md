# World Simulation with Video Foundation Models for Physical AI

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA developed Cosmos-Predict2.5 and Cosmos-Transfer2.5, a new generation of world foundation models, to advance simulation fidelity for Physical AI. These models demonstrate improved realism, prompt alignment, and control across applications like synthetic data generation, robot policy learning, and autonomous driving simulation, often achieving comparable or superior performance with fewer parameters than previous models.

## Problem

- Training embodied agents for Physical AI is costly, time-consuming, and poses safety risks in real-world environments.
- Existing video generative models often lack the precise control over object dynamics, interactions, and physical consistency needed for robust Physical AI applications.
- Prior open-source video generative models designed for Physical AI required advancements in data quality, architectural design, and training methodologies.

## Method

- A refined 7-stage video curation pipeline, processing over 200 million videos, was developed to generate high-quality general and domain-specific datasets for Physical AI.
- The core model architecture, built on flow-matching, unifies **Text2World**, **Image2World**, and **Video2World** generation, incorporating a Physical AI-specialized VLM ([**Cosmos-Reason1**]) as the text encoder.
- A multi-stage training paradigm includes progressive resolution, Supervised Fine-tuning (==SFT==), Model Soup merging, Reinforcement Learning (RL) with a VLM-based ==reward model==, and timestep distillation for accelerated inference.

## Results

- Cosmos-Predict2.5-14B achieved human preference scores on par with Wan2.2 27B-A14B using half the parameters, and Cosmos-Transfer2.5-2B outperformed its **3.5x** larger predecessor (Cosmos-Transfer1-7B) on control adherence and video quality.
- Robot policies augmented with synthetic data from Cosmos-Transfer2.5-2B achieved 24 out of 30 successes in real-robot tasks, a significant improvement over baseline policies (1/30 and 5/30 successes).
- Multi-view driving models demonstrated up to a **2.3x** boost in visual quality (FVD/FID scores) and up to 60% improvement in 3D-cuboid and lane detection metrics, indicating enhanced consistency and adherence to control signals.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Rigorous, VLM-based data curation is essential for creating high-fidelity and physically consistent world models tailored for Physical AI tasks.
> - Combining a unified model architecture with a progressive training strategy, SFT, model merging, and RL post-training effectively enhances both general and specialized Physical AI simulation capabilities.
> - Replacing generic text encoders with a Physical AI-specialized VLM provides richer context grounding and finer control, leading to improved human preferences and benchmark performance, even with reduced model size.
