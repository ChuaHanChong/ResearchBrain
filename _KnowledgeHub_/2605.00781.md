# Map2World: Segment Map Conditioned Text to 3D World Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Map2World introduces a text-conditioned framework for generating large-scale 3D worlds from user-defined segment maps, overcoming limitations of rigid layouts and content inconsistencies. It employs a multi-diffusion strategy for seamless structural coherence and a detail enhancer network that leverages latent space learning for fine-grained realism, achieving higher quality and completeness compared to prior methods like SynCity, with a GPTscore of 7.93/10 and a World Quality score of 7.76.

## Problem

- Existing 3D world generation methods struggle with limited high-quality, world-level datasets, leading to domain-specific or incomplete outputs.
- Current approaches often rely on rigid, grid-based layouts, limiting user control over complex, irregularly shaped environments.
- Scaling individual 3D assets to large worlds results in contextual disconnectedness, inconsistent object scales, and visible seams between generated regions.
- Maintaining fine-grained detail while ensuring global coherence across large 3D scenes is challenging due to data scarcity for training detail enhancement in 3D.

## Method

- A ==multi-diffusion strategy== with ==latent fusion== is implemented to expand spatial regions in the structured latent space, enabling seamless connections and consistent scaling across overlapping 3D windows.
- Segment-map-guided latent fusion, using dynamically weighted text prompts and segment masks, allows for flexible, arbitrary-shaped world layouts with soft boundaries that evolve during denoising.
- A ==detail enhancer network== is proposed to generate fine details by learning coarse-to-fine relationships within the structured latent space, conditioned on both coarse and adjacent cube latents to maintain global coherence.
- The pipeline leverages pre-trained 3D asset generators (**TRELLIS**) and fine-tunes its structured latent decoder (D_L) to robustly generalize across diverse domains with limited world-level training data.

## Results

- Map2World successfully generates 3D worlds from arbitrary-shaped segment maps with associated text prompts, demonstrating seamless transitions and connected structures between regions.
- It outperforms existing methods like SynCity on grid-type segmentation maps, achieving a higher GPTscore of 7.93/10 (vs 7.48/10) and a World Quality score of 7.76 (vs 7.25), particularly excelling in world completeness and coherence.
- Ablation studies confirmed that spectral parameterization for initial latent optimization stabilized convergence at high learning rates, and the detail enhancer design yielded the best image quality metrics (PSNR, LPIPS, FID) compared to alternatives.
- The detail enhancer can be applied recursively to progressively upscale resolution, consistently generating sharper details and textures while maintaining harmony with surrounding elements.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Fusing velocity predictions from overlapping diffusion windows effectively maintains the latent prior of the base generator while ensuring smooth transitions and global consistency for large-scale scenes.
> - Optimizing the initial sparse latent in the spectral domain (using 3D FFT) significantly stabilizes the scale-aware generation process, enabling better control over the generated scene's structure.
> - Detail enhancement can be achieved by training a network to learn latent space relationships between coarse and fine scene representations, leveraging existing asset priors without requiring new explicit 3D detail data.
> - Conditioning the detail enhancer on both the truncated coarse latent and adjacent generated latents ensures that added details are harmonized with both the local context and overall scene structure.
