# PeRL: Permutation-Enhanced Reinforcement Learning for Interleaved Vision-Language Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> PeRL enhances Vision-Language Models for complex multi-image reasoning by employing a permutation-enhanced reinforcement learning framework, explicitly mitigating positional bias. The approach achieves state-of-the-art results on several multi-image benchmarks, including 76.39 on Mantis-Eval and 58.53 on BLINK, while maintaining competitive performance on single-image tasks.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) often struggle with complex multi-image reasoning tasks, failing to understand relationships across multiple visual inputs like spatial or temporal connections.
- Current VLMs exhibit 'positional bias,' where their predictions are sensitive to the arbitrary order of input images, leading to inconsistent or incorrect responses despite unchanged underlying semantic content.
- Reinforcement learning (RL) approaches for enhancing VLM reasoning have primarily been confined to single-image contexts and do not adequately address the complexities of multi-image scenarios.

## Method

- **PeRL** employs a ==multi-stage data preprocessing== pipeline that filters and rephrases questions, balances sample difficulty using 'Rollout Filtering', and performs 'Semantic Variation Analysis' to handle image permutations.
- The approach utilizes a permutation-enhanced reinforcement learning framework built upon Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) to optimize the VLM's policy for correct responses.
- During training, PeRL generates diverse samples by randomly permuting image orders for multi-image inputs and adjusts corresponding answers to maintain semantic consistency, enforcing order-invariant learning during policy updates.

## Results

- PeRL achieves state-of-the-art performance on multi-image benchmarks, scoring **76.39** on Mantis-Eval, **58.53** on BLINK, and **54.23** on MMIU, substantially outperforming prior models.
- The model maintains competitive performance on general-purpose single-image mathematical reasoning tasks, with scores of **73.00** on MathVista and **49.56** on MathVerse.
- PeRL demonstrates robust generalization capabilities on out-of-domain multi-image mathematical reasoning benchmarks (Remi and MV-MATH), indicating its ability to implicitly learn inter-image relationships.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Permutation-enhanced GRPO significantly improves training stability and convergence compared to naive GRPO, forcing the model to learn more robust, order-invariant representations by exposing it to positionally varied cases.
> - A multi-stage data preprocessing pipeline, including 'Rollout Filtering' for difficulty balancing and 'Semantic Variation Analysis' for permutation-based semantic consistency, is crucial for efficient and effective reinforcement learning in multi-image settings.
> - Combining multi-image instruction examples with single-image RL examples during training yields synergistic benefits, enabling the model to learn both intra-image and inter-image reasoning capabilities effectively.
