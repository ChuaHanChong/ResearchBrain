# PhysBench: Benchmarking and Enhancing Vision-Language Models for Physical World Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> A new comprehensive benchmark, PhysBench, evaluates Vision-Language Models' (VLMs) understanding of the physical world, revealing an average accuracy of approximately 40% across 75 models. The proposed PhysAgent framework enhances VLMs' physical reasoning by 18.4% for GPT-4o on this benchmark, directly improving robotic manipulation task performance.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with understanding real-world physical interactions and dynamics, leading to operational errors in embodied AI agents.
- Existing benchmarks for physical comprehension are limited, focusing on vision-only, simplistic scenarios, or common-sense VQA rather than deep physical perception and dynamic understanding.
- There is a lack of comprehensive tools to diagnose the specific factors (e.g., perception, knowledge, reasoning) contributing to VLMs' limitations in physical world understanding.

## Method

- Introduction of **PhysBench**, a comprehensive human-annotated benchmark with 10,002 interleaved video-image-text entries covering object properties, relationships, scene understanding, and physics-based dynamics, for evaluating 75 diverse VLMs.
- Development of PhysAgent, a novel framework that enhances VLMs' physical understanding by integrating specialized Vision Foundation Models (e.g., Depth Anything, SAM) and explicit physical knowledge memory into a three-step reasoning process.
- Validation of PhysAgent's impact through application in embodied AI, demonstrating improved performance of a MOKA-based robotic manipulation system across five fundamental tasks.

## Results

- 75 representative VLMs achieved an average accuracy of approximately **40%** on PhysBench, with the best model (GPT-4o) reaching only **49.49%**, highlighting a substantial gap from human performance.
- PhysAgent significantly boosted zero-shot performance, improving GPT-4o's accuracy by **18.4%** overall on PhysBench and by **49.5%** in the challenging 'Scene' category, outperforming standard prompting methods and neuro-symbolic baselines.
- Integrating PhysAgent or fine-tuning with PhysBench data led to improved success rates and reduced reasoning and execution errors in five fundamental robotic manipulation tasks, directly validating enhanced physical understanding in embodied agents.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current VLMs possess a profoundly limited understanding of the physical world, achieving an average accuracy of approximately 40% on PhysBench, significantly below human performance (95.87%).
> - VLM struggles are primarily due to perceptual inaccuracies and a lack of explicit physical knowledge in their training data, rather than solely reasoning deficiencies.
> - Scaling VLM parameters, increasing general training data, or providing more video frames does not consistently improve physical comprehension, indicating fundamental limitations in current VLM architectures for this domain.
