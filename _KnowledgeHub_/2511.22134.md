# DualVLA: Building a Generalizable Embodied Agent via Partial Decoupling of Reasoning and Action

> [!summary] Summary
> DualVLA introduces a post-training framework that partially decouples reasoning and action learning to address "action degeneration" in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. The framework achieved a 61.0% average success rate on the SimplerEnv benchmark, an 8.0% improvement over its baseline, and increased real-world task success to 60.0% while also introducing a new VLA Score for comprehensive evaluation.

## Problem

- Existing reasoning-capable Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models suffer from "action degeneration," where enhancing reasoning capabilities often leads to degraded manipulation performance.
- Embodied reasoning data frequently contains repetitive, low-entropy segments that can dilute crucial visuomotor learning, causing models to overfit linguistic patterns.
- Current VLA evaluation metrics, primarily task success rates, are too coarse to capture the nuances of reasoning quality, action smoothness, or the alignment between reasoning and action.

## Method

- A dual-layers data pruning strategy identifies and retains only "action-critical" reasoning content by considering both scene event changes and kinematic keyframes.
- A dual-teacher adaptive distillation approach provides differentiated supervision, using a specialist VLA (action teacher) for robot data and the reasoning VLA's initial state (reasoning teacher) for multimodal data.
- ==The VLA Score== evaluation framework leverages a powerful VLM (GPT-4o) as an "intelligent judge" to provide fine-grained, multi-dimensional assessment across reasoning, action, intention, and reason-action alignment.

## Results

- Achieved a **61.0%** average success rate on the SimplerEnv benchmark, representing an **8.0%** improvement over the InstructVLA-G baseline and outperforming other top models.
- Increased success rates from **45%** to **60.0%** in challenging real-world dual-arm robotic manipulation tasks, demonstrating robust generalizability.
- The VLA Score evaluation revealed that specialist VLAs bottleneck in action modeling, while reasoning VLAs struggle with effective action execution, a gap DualVLA effectively bridges with a **20%** inference speedup.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Partially decoupling the learning objectives for reasoning and action at both the data and loss levels is crucial for building generalizable embodied agents that balance cognitive and physical proficiency.
> - Selective data pruning, guided by both semantic events and kinematic changes, effectively reduces data redundancy, focusing training on meaningful reasoning and mitigating action degeneration.
> - An adaptive dual-teacher distillation strategy enables balanced skill acquisition by providing specialized supervision for action execution and general multimodal reasoning, resolving conflicts from mixed-modality training.
