# Delving into RL for Image Generation with CoT: A Study on DPO vs. GRPO

> [!summary] Summary
> A systematic study compares Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) for autoregressive image generation with Chain-of-Thought reasoning. The research demonstrates that DPO excels in-domain, while GRPO offers superior out-of-domain generalization, and highlights the critical influence of reward model generalization and tailored scaling strategies on performance.

## Problem

- Applying reinforcement learning (RL) and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning to autoregressive image generation presents challenges distinct from text-based LLMs, such as ensuring text-image consistency and aesthetic quality.
- Image generation demands robust generalization across widely varying prompt styles, from detailed descriptions to concise templates, creating a significant domain gap.
- A comprehensive, systematic investigation into the characteristics and scaling behaviors of different RL algorithms like DPO and GRPO within the visual CoT domain was lacking.

## Method

- The study systematically investigates the application of GRPO and DPO for ==autoregressive image generation==, using Janus-Pro as the baseline model.
- It evaluates performance on both in-domain (T2I-CompBench) and out-of-domain (GenEval) benchmarks to assess efficacy and generalization.
- The research explores the impact of various reward models (human preference, VQA-based, metric rewards) and three scaling strategies: sampled images per prompt, in-domain training data volume, and iterative training paradigms.

## Results

- Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) consistently outperforms GRPO on in-domain tasks (T2I-CompBench) by an average of **11.53%**.
- Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) consistently exhibits better generalization capabilities on out-of-domain tasks (GenEval), surpassing DPO by an average of **1.14%**.
- Iterative DPO training substantially enhances in-domain performance but can significantly reduce out-of-domain generalization after two iterations, while increasing in-domain data diversity for DPO consistently improves both in-domain and out-of-domain performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is more sensitive to the specific characteristics and biases of the chosen reward model, especially concerning generalization performance.
> - The intrinsic generalization capabilities of reward models directly correlate with the generalization performance of both GRPO and DPO.
> - Scaling strategies exhibit distinct trade-offs: GRPO achieves efficient in-domain gains by scaling sampled images per prompt, while iterative training for DPO boosts in-domain performance but can significantly reduce out-of-domain generalization.
