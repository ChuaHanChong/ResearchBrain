# MAL: Motion-Aware Loss with Temporal and Distillation Hints for Self-Supervised Depth Estimation

> [!summary] Summary
> The Motion-Aware Loss (MAL) module improves self-supervised monocular depth estimation in dynamic environments by addressing motion-induced errors during training with temporal and distillation hints. This plug-and-play module reduced Absolute Relative Error by up to 10.8% for DualRefine on the CityScapes benchmark, without increasing inference time.

## Problem

- Self-supervised depth estimation methods degrade significantly in dynamic scenes due to the violation of the static scene assumption, leading to inaccurate depth predictions.
- Moving objects introduce errors in two key stages: the image reprojection loss calculation and the feature matching process within multi-frame student networks.
- Existing solutions for dynamic scenes often increase inference complexity by modifying the network's forward pass or utilize incomplete distillation strategies that do not fully leverage teacher knowledge.

## Method

- A plug-and-play Motion-Aware Loss (**MAL**) module is introduced, integrating two components: Temporal Hints and Distillation Hints, operating solely during the training phase.
- Temporal Hints utilize instance segmentation and motion tracking to rectify moving objects in reconstructed images, reducing motion-induced errors in the image reprojection loss.
- Distillation Hints expand the distillation domain to the entire image and generate a more accurate target depth map by fusing teacher and student predictions based on lower reprojection loss, enhancing knowledge transfer and correcting feature matching errors.

## Results

- On the CityScapes benchmark, MAL improved the Absolute Relative Error (AbsRel) for ManyDepth by **10.1%** (from **0.114** to **0.103**) and for DualRefine by **10.8%** (from **0.111** to **0.099**).
- Integrating MAL into DynamicDepth, a method already optimized for dynamic scenes, still yielded noticeable improvements, reducing Squared Relative Error (SqRel) by **5.34%** (from **1.011** to **0.957**).
- Ablation studies confirmed that both Temporal Hints and Distillation Hints contribute to the overall performance, with the full MAL module achieving the best results (AbsRel **0.103**, SqRel **1.073** for ManyDepth on CityScapes).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Robust self-supervised depth estimation in dynamic scenes requires mitigating errors stemming from both image reprojection inconsistencies and unreliable feature matching processes.
> - Motion-induced errors can be effectively addressed at the loss computation level during training, allowing for significant performance gains without incurring additional computational overhead during inference.
> - The Temporal and Distillation Hints are complementary, with Temporal Hints refining the photometric loss and Distillation Hints correcting student network errors in feature matching, both synergistically contributing to improved accuracy.
