# Set-of-Mark Prompting Unleashes Extraordinary Visual Grounding in GPT-4V

> [!summary] Summary
> Set-of-Mark prompting enhances GPT-4V's fine-grained visual grounding by augmenting images with unique alphanumeric marks, enabling the model to precisely refer to and output specific image regions. This approach allows GPT-4V to achieve performance on tasks like referring segmentation that often surpasses fine-tuned specialist models in a zero-shot setting.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art large multimodal models (LMMs) like GPT-4V exhibit weak fine-grained visual grounding, struggling to precisely pinpoint or refer to specific objects or regions within an image.
- LMMs cannot easily produce accurate spatial outputs such as coordinates for specific objects or parts within an image.
- Existing visual prompting methods often struggle with referring to multiple objects or are not 'speakable' by LLMs, limiting their utility for true multimodal grounding.

## Method

- The proposed ==Set-of-Mark (SoM) Prompting== technique augments input images with unique, speakable ==alphanumeric marks== generated for semantically meaningful regions.
- Image regions are automatically partitioned using a suite of state-of-the-art open-vocabulary segmentation models, including ==MaskDINO, SEEM, and SAM==, which provide rich granularity.
- A clever mark allocation algorithm ensures unique mark placement within each region, minimizing overlaps, and these marked images are then used with either plain or interleaved text prompts to enable precise human-AI interaction.

## Results

- Set-of-Mark (SoM) prompting dramatically improves GPT-4V's performance on Referring Expression Comprehension (REC) from **25.7** ACC@0.5 to **86.4** ACC@0.5 on RefCOCOg.
- GPT-4V + SoM achieves **75.6 mIoU** for Referring Expression Segmentation (RES) on RefCOCOg, outperforming state-of-the-art fine-tuned specialist models like PolyFormer (**67.2 mIoU**) in a zero-shot setting.
- The method demonstrates strong performance across various tasks, including phrase grounding (**89.2** Recall@1 on Flickr30K) and video object segmentation (**78.8** J&F on DAVIS2017), consistently outperforming other open-source LMMs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Powerful LMMs like GPT-4V possess latent fine-grained visual grounding abilities that can be unlocked by simple, speakable visual prompting techniques.
> - Set-of-Mark bridging visual and language prompting by making visual cues 'speakable' allows for seamless, symbolic integration of visual and textual information in prompts and responses.
> - The quality of initial image partitioning using off-the-shelf segmentation models serves as a bottleneck, indicating that GPT-4V's grounding capability is even stronger when provided with more precise regions.
