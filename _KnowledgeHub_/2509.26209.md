# Diversity-Incentivized Exploration for Versatile Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> The DIVER framework enhances Large Language Model reasoning by incentivizing global sequence-level diversity through intrinsic rewards, moving beyond token-level exploration. This approach leads to improved performance on mathematical and general reasoning benchmarks, demonstrating stronger generalization to out-of-domain tasks.

## Problem

- Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) for Large Language Models (LLMs) struggles with exploration due to vast state-action spaces and sparse rewards in complex reasoning tasks.
- Existing LLM exploration methods primarily focus on 'local token-level' diversity, which is insufficient for 'far-sighted, deep exploration' required to discover novel solution patterns.
- Combinatorial explosion in textual state-action spaces leads to deficient exploration and suboptimal sample efficiency, hindering LLMs' reasoning capabilities.

## Method

- **DIVER** introduces a framework that integrates global ==sequence-level diversity== as an intrinsic reward within the GRPO algorithm for LLMs.
- It employs two computationally efficient metrics: Textual Diversity (TD) for general text dissimilarity and Equational Diversity (ED) for mathematical formula differences.
- A potential-based reward shaping mechanism, formally proven to preserve optimal policy invariance, combined with conditional and balanced shaping, is used to integrate diversity incentives and mitigate reward hacking.

## Results

- DIVER achieved an average of 43.0 on six mathematical benchmarks, surpassing OpenReasoner-Zero by +2.0 points, and an average of 58.4 on challenging out-of-domain tasks, outperforming OpenReasoner-Zero by +6.8 points (e.g., +12.5 on GPQA).
- The framework consistently outperformed other exploration RL methods, such as Entropy-RL, by +1.2 points on in-domain tasks and +2.4 points on out-of-domain tasks, with notable gains of +4.6 points on OlympiadBench.
- DIVER demonstrated superior deep exploration, exhibiting more pronounced performance gains at higher Pass@k values (e.g., +6.7 points over Entropy-RL at Pass@32 on AIME25) and its solutions were ranked as 'best' in **76.4%** of cases by an advanced LLM judge.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Global sequence-level diversity is positively correlated with enhanced LLM reasoning capacity and significantly improves generalization to out-of-domain tasks.
> - Potential-based reward shaping can effectively incorporate intrinsic diversity rewards into RL without altering the optimal policy of the original MDP.
> - Mitigating reward hacking by applying diversity incentives only to correct responses and dynamically balancing exploration vs. exploitation is crucial for successful intrinsic reward learning in LLMs.
