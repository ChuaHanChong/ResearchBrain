# Grounding Creativity in Physics: A Brief Survey of Physical Priors in AIGC

> [!summary] Summary
> This comprehensive survey systematically reviews current approaches that integrate physical principles into AI-generated content, focusing on 3D and 4D synthesis, to enhance physical realism. It categorizes existing methods and provides a comparative analysis of their performance in modeling various material behaviors.

## Problem

- Current AIGC models for 3D and 4D content often lack physical realism, producing artifacts like implausible deformations or unstable dynamics.
- This deficiency limits the applicability of generated content in demanding fields such as engineering simulation, robotics, and advanced animation.
- Existing surveys in 3D and 4D generation do not systematically explore the integration of physics priors.

## Method

- The authors conduct a systematic review, compiling and analyzing research on physics-aware generative models for 3D and 4D content.
- A novel taxonomy is introduced, categorizing methods by spatiotemporal granularity (static 3D, dynamic 3D, 4D) and underlying representations (vision-based, **NeRF-based**, ==Gaussian Splatting==-based).
- A critical comparative analysis is presented, evaluating methods on their strengths, limitations, and suitability for various materials and motion dynamics, including quantitative results where applicable.

## Results

- A comprehensive taxonomy for physics-grounded AIGC was established, distinguishing between static 3D, dynamic 3D (vision, NeRF, GS), and 4D generation paradigms.
- Quantitative comparisons on the Synthetic PAC-NeRF dataset indicate Unleashing performs robustly for fluid/plasticine materials, while GIC shows lower errors for elasticity in materials like Torus and Bird.
- 4D generation research demonstrates a rapid progression towards inferring and integrating complex physical properties for continuous spatiotemporal dynamics, moving beyond simple animation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating differentiable physics engines, such as MPM or FEM, is crucial for embedding physical consistency into generative AI pipelines.
> - The field is transitioning from purely appearance-driven content creation to a more holistic approach that explicitly incorporates the laws of physics, especially for 4D generation.
> - Current physics-aware generative models often demonstrate specialized performance, with no single method universally excelling across all material types and physical properties.
