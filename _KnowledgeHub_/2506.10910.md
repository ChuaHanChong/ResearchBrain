# Magistral

> [!summary] Summary
> Mistral AI introduces Magistral, its inaugural reasoning model, which leverages a custom Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) pipeline to significantly enhance the reasoning abilities of its base models. Magistral Medium, trained purely with RL, achieved a nearly 50% increase on AIME '24 (pass@1), while Magistral Small demonstrated that RL effectively boosts distilled models and both models showed surprising improvements in multimodal reasoning despite text-only training.

## Problem

- Traditional large language models (LLMs) often struggle with multi-step logical deduction and complex problem-solving tasks.
- Developing scalable and efficient reinforcement learning pipelines for LLMs, particularly from scratch, poses significant engineering and algorithmic challenges.
- Ensuring that specialized reasoning training does not degrade other essential LLM capabilities like instruction following and function calling.

## Method

- Developed Magistral, Mistral AI's first reasoning model, using a custom-built, scalable ==Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)== pipeline.
- Employed a modified ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== algorithm with key changes like eliminating KL divergence and relaxing the trust region's upper bound to foster exploration.
- Implemented a meticulous reward system considering formatting, correctness (math/code verification), length penalties, and a novel ==language consistency reward== for multilingual reasoning.

## Results

- Magistral Medium achieved a nearly **50% increase** on AIME '24 (pass@1), improving from **26.8% to 73.6%**, and a **30% increase** on LiveCodeBench (v5), from **29.1% to 59.4%**.
- Magistral Small, a **24B model**, demonstrated that RL provided substantial performance boosts even after distillation (SFT) from a larger teacher model, achieving **70.7%** on AIME '24 pass@1 with SFT+RL.
- Despite being trained on text-only data, Magistral models showed improved performance on multimodal reasoning benchmarks like MathVista, MMMU, and MMMU-Pro.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Pure Reinforcement Learning (RL) alone can achieve substantial reasoning performance gains on large models without initial distillation from other reasoning models.
> - Text-only RL training not only preserves but surprisingly improves multimodal reasoning capabilities, suggesting deep generalization of learned reasoning patterns.
> - Detailed ablations revealed that controlling exploration via the PPO-like clip-higher threshold was more stable than entropy bonuses, and specific batching strategies influenced training stability.
