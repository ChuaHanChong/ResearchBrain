# R1-Reward: Training Multimodal Reward Model Through Stable Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> R1-Reward introduces StableReinforce, a reinforcement learning framework for training multimodal reward models (MRMs) that integrates explicit reasoning. The method improves training stability and enables MRMs to achieve new state-of-the-art performance across multiple benchmarks while demonstrating human-like reasoning and self-correction.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal large language models (MLLMs) face challenges in alignment with human preferences, and their reward models (MRMs) often lack long-term reasoning capabilities.
- Directly applying standard reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms like PPO to train reward models typically leads to significant training instability and potential collapse due to issues like unstable advantage normalization and unconstrained policy updates.
- Traditional reward models primarily output scalar values, lacking transparency and failing to leverage rich human feedback or an explicit reasoning process before making judgments.

## Method

- **R1-Reward** introduces ==StableReinforce==, a refined RL algorithm featuring 'Pre-CLIP' to prevent extreme policy ratio changes and an 'Advantage Filter' to mitigate outliers during advantage normalization, ensuring stable training.
- A novel, multi-component reward function is proposed, which includes a formatting reward, a result reward based on ground truth, and a consistency reward using an external MLLM to supervise the coherence between internal reasoning and the final answer.
- A progressive training strategy is employed, starting with supervised fine-tuning using GPT-4o-generated thought processes for 200K preference samples, followed by RL training focused on 'difficult' samples that require deeper reasoning.

## Results

- R1-Reward establishes new state-of-the-art performance on multimodal reward benchmarks, achieving **71.92%** accuracy on VL-Reward Bench (a **9.3%** improvement over previous best open-source models) and a **14.3%** improvement on Multimodal Reward Bench.
- The StableReinforce algorithm demonstrates significantly faster and more stable policy loss convergence compared to baseline RL methods, while also reducing response length by approximately **15%** during training.
- The model exhibits qualitative 'Aha moments' and self-reflection, correcting calculation errors and re-evaluating questions, demonstrating activation of genuine reasoning and self-correction capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement learning can be effectively applied to train multimodal reward models, improving their long-term reasoning capabilities beyond traditional supervised methods.
> - Algorithmic refinements like Pre-CLIP and Advantage Filter are crucial for achieving stable reinforcement learning training in the context of reward modeling, addressing common issues like divergence and numerical instability.
> - Designing a multi-component reward function that supervises both the final outcome and the internal 'thought process' (consistency reward) enables the model to develop explicit, interpretable reasoning and self-correction abilities.
