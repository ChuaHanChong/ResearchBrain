# V$^{2}$-SAM: Marrying SAM2 with Multi-Prompt Experts for Cross-View Object Correspondence

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework called V-SAM adapts the SAM2 foundation model for cross-view object correspondence, particularly in ego-exo settings, by integrating geometry-aware and appearance-guided multi-prompt experts. It achieved state-of-the-art results on Ego-Exo4D, with 46.3 IoU for Ego2Exo and 49.6 IoU for Exo2Ego, and showed strong generalization on DAVIS-2017 and HANDAL-X.

## Problem

- Identifying and associating the same object across different camera viewpoints is challenging due to drastic variations in viewpoint, appearance, scale, and shape, especially in ego-exo scenarios.
- Large single-view segmentation models like SAM2 rely on precise spatial prompts, which become problematic when target object positions shift significantly between views, hindering direct application in cross-view settings.
- Existing referring-based SAM variants often struggle with severe appearance changes and can reduce SAM2's inherent localization precision by removing spatial prompts.

## Method

- Developed V-SAM, a unified framework that adapts SAM2 for cross-view object correspondence by retaining its core components (Image Encoder, Prompt Encoder, Mask Decoder).
- Introduced a Cross-View Anchor Prompt Generator (V-Anchor) that uses DINOv3 features and stratified sampling to establish geometry-aware, coordinate-based prompts across views.
- Designed a Cross-View Visual Prompt Generator (V-Visual) with a learnable Visual Prompt Matcher (VPMatcher) to capture appearance-based cues, and integrated these into a multi-expert training scheme with a Post-hoc Cyclic Consistency Selector (PCCS).

## Results

- V-SAM achieved new state-of-the-art performance on Ego-Exo4D, scoring **46.3** IoU for Ego2Exo and **49.6** IoU for Exo2Ego, surpassing the previous best by 3.7 and **5.5** IoU points, respectively.
- The framework demonstrated superior performance across all metrics on DAVIS-2017 for video object tracking and strong zero-shot generalization on HANDAL-X with **77.2** IoU, outperforming prior methods by a large margin.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical contribution of both the V-Anchor module for geometric localization and the VPMatcher for appearance alignment, with the multi-expert combination and PCCS yielding the highest overall performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - SAM2's spatial prompting capability can be effectively leveraged for cross-view scenarios by generating robust, geometry-aware anchor points through dense feature matching and spatial stratification.
> - Combining distinct experts for geometry-aware (V-Anchor) and appearance-guided (V-Visual) prompts effectively addresses the diverse challenges of cross-view variations, leading to enhanced robustness.
> - Dynamically selecting the most reliable expert's prediction using a lightweight point-level cyclic consistency check (PCCS) during inference improves overall performance and adaptability without significant computational overhead.
