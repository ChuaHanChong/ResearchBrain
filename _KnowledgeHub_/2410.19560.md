# Connecting Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture with Contrastive Self-supervised Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> This work introduces C-JEPA (Contrastive-JEPA), integrating Variance-Invariance-Covariance Regularization (VICReg) into the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (I-JEPA) to address issues of representation collapse and inaccurate mean patch learning. The combined framework achieves superior performance and faster convergence across various vision tasks, including a 1.0% increase in ImageNet-1K fine-tuning accuracy and a 0.8 AP_box improvement on COCO compared to I-JEPA.

## Problem

- The Exponential Moving Average (EMA) mechanism in I-JEPA is often insufficient to prevent "entire collapse" of learned representations, leading to trivial features.
- I-JEPA's prediction mechanism struggles to accurately learn the mean of patch representations within masked target blocks, impacting feature quality.

## Method

- Introduces **C-JEPA** by integrating ==VICReg=='s variance, invariance, and covariance regularization terms directly into the I-JEPA loss function.
- VICReg's variance and covariance terms explicitly encourage diverse and decorrelated features, effectively preventing model collapse.
- The invariance term of VICReg helps to accurately align the mean of patch representations across different augmented views, addressing a key I-JEPA limitation.

## Results

- C-JEPA achieves faster and better convergence compared to I-JEPA, consistently yielding higher performance metrics with fewer pre-training epochs.
- Demonstrates improved ImageNet-**1K** classification, achieving **73.7%** (+**0.8%**) linear probing and **84.5%** (+**1.0%**) fine-tuning top-1 accuracy for ViT-B/16 over I-JEPA.
- Outperforms I-JEPA across diverse downstream tasks, showing gains of **0.8** AP_box and **0.8** AP_mask on COCO, 1.1 mIoU on ADE20K, and 1.7 (J&F)_m on DAVIS video object segmentation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicit regularization strategies, such as VICReg's variance and covariance terms, are more robust than EMA in preventing collapse in joint-embedding predictive architectures.
> - Combining the strengths of predictive learning from masked images (JEPA) with robust collapse-prevention and feature diversity mechanisms (VICReg) leads to a more stable and effective self-supervised learning framework.
> - Theoretical analysis using the Neural Tangent Kernel (NTK) framework provides insights into the necessity of stop-gradient and predictor in JEPA, further justifying the need for explicit regularization for stable, non-collapsing learning.
