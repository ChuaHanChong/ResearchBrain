# GR-2: A Generative Video-Language-Action Model with Web-Scale Knowledge for Robot Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at ByteDance Research developed GR-2, a generalist robot agent that combines large-scale video pre-training with robot action generation using a GPT-style transformer model. The system achieved a 97.7% success rate on multi-task tabletop manipulation, an average of 79.0% on industrial bin-picking, and established new state-of-the-art on the CALVIN benchmark for language-conditioned tasks.

## Problem

- Developing robotic systems capable of understanding natural language instructions and performing a wide range of manipulation tasks.
- Enabling robots to generalize effectively to unseen environments, novel objects, and new tasks with limited specific training data.
- Addressing the challenge of generating precise robot action trajectories from observations, where multiple valid actions may exist for a given state.

## Method

- A **GR-2** ==GPT-style transformer== architecture that processes tokenized representations of text instructions, video frames, and robot actions.
- A two-stage training process involving large-scale ==video-language pre-training== on **38 million** diverse video clips, followed by ==fine-tuning== on robot-specific video-language-action data.
- Integration of a ==conditional Variational Autoencoder (cVAE)== within the fine-tuning stage to generate diverse and robust action trajectories.

## Results

- Achieved a **97.7%** success rate on simple multi-task tabletop manipulation and maintained **75%** success with only **50 demonstrations** per task, showing data efficiency.
- Demonstrated an average success rate of **79.0%** on industrial bin-picking tasks, a significant improvement over its predecessor GR-1 (**35.9%**), including with unseen objects and in cluttered environments.
- Established a new state-of-the-art on the CALVIN benchmark, achieving **98.6%** success for completing one task and an average task completion length of **4.64** for longer sequences.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large-scale video pre-training on diverse human activity data enables strong generalization capabilities for subsequent robot manipulation tasks.
> - A unified GPT-style transformer architecture can effectively integrate language, video, and robot action modalities for complex control.
> - The use of a conditional Variational Autoencoder (cVAE) helps in generating robust action trajectories by addressing the one-to-many mapping inherent in robot control.
