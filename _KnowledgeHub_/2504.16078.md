# LLMs are Greedy Agents: Effects of RL Fine-tuning on Decision-Making Abilities

> [!summary] Summary
> Google DeepMind researchers systematically analyze three key failure modes of LLMs in decision-making tasks (greediness, frequency bias, and knowing-doing gap), demonstrating how reinforcement learning fine-tuning with chain-of-thought reasoning can improve exploration and decision-making capabilities across multi-armed bandits, contextual bandits, and game-playing environments.

## Problem

- LLMs exhibit poor exploration and premature commitment to suboptimal actions in decision-making scenarios
- There exists a gap between LLMs' ability to reason about decisions and their actual action choices
- Smaller LLMs tend to copy frequent actions regardless of rewards, showing strong frequency bias

## Method

- Implement ==reinforcement learning fine-tuning== (RLFT) with chain-of-thought reasoning
- Introduce exploration mechanisms like try-all and exploration bonus
- Leverage expert data and increased generation budgets for better decision-making

## Results

- Successfully identified and quantified three specific LLM failure modes in decision-making
- Demonstrated improved exploration and decision-making through RLFT across multiple environments
- Showed that expert data significantly enhances decision-making performance
- Established the importance of both classic RL and LLM-specific exploration techniques

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Chain-of-thought reasoning is critical for effective RLFT and decision-making
> - The knowing-doing gap can be narrowed through careful reward shaping
> - Exploration mechanisms significantly improve performance compared to greedy approaches
> - Increased "thinking time" (generation budget) leads to better decisions
