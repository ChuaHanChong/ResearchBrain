# Pelican-VL 1.0: A Foundation Brain Model for Embodied Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> Pelican-VL 1.0, developed by the WFM System Group at X-Humanoid, introduces a family of Vision-Language Models (7B to 72B parameters) and the Deliberate Practice Policy Optimization (DPPO) framework to advance embodied intelligence. This system achieves a 20.3% performance uplift from its base model, surpassing other open-source models by 10.6%, and demonstrates robust real-world applications including zero-shot manipulation and multi-robot collaboration.

## Problem

- General-purpose Vision-Language Models (VLMs) lack robust embodied cognition, struggling with real-world spatial, temporal-causal, and interactive properties.
- Existing embodied AI strategies are fragmented, either relying on brute-force data scaling without effective learning frameworks or using architectural refinements with limited dataset scale.
- A fundamental gap exists between the impressive digital perception capabilities of VLMs and their ability to translate this into effective interaction with the physical world.

## Method

- Introduces **Pelican-VL** 1.0, a family of large-scale (7B to 72B parameters) open-source Vision-Language Models serving as a foundation brain for embodied intelligence.
- Proposes Deliberate Practice Policy Optimization (DPPO), a metacognitively inspired training framework, which iteratively cycles between an RL-based "Exploratory Grounding" phase and an SFT-based "Targeted Remediation" phase.
- Employs a unified preference learning paradigm that synergistically integrates Supervised Fine-Tuning and Reinforcement Learning, guided by a multi-modal and multi-task reward system and difficulty-aware sampling.

## Results

- Pelican-VL 1.0 achieved a **20.3%** performance uplift from its base model and outperformed 100B-level open-source models by **10.6%** on various benchmarks, demonstrating stability against catastrophic forgetting.
- The model showed significant improvements in specific embodied abilities, including a **25.7%** increase in spatial understanding and a **15.1%** increase in temporal reasoning, with an average **15.4%** improvement across nine reasoning dimensions compared to its backbone.
- Demonstrated robust real-world applications such as zero-shot object manipulation with multi-view affordance generation, closing the sensorimotor loop for delicate grasping, multi-robot collaboration via function calls, and long-horizon task planning in domestic environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly addressing and improving specific weaknesses in embodied cognition through targeted, iterative refinement (DPPO metaloop) is more effective than solely scaling data or architectural complexity.
> - A unified preference learning framework can effectively bridge the gap between SFT's knowledge consolidation and RL's exploratory discovery, leading to stable and robust skill acquisition in embodied agents.
> - Comprehensive evaluation using a fine-grained, 9-dimension capability taxonomy reveals imbalances in existing benchmarks and highlights the need for a more holistic approach to assessing embodied intelligence.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) inherently requires systems that can perceive, reason, and interact with the physical world – a concept central to "embodied intelligence." Recent advancements in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated impressive digital perception capabilities, but a fundamental challenge remains in translating this to robust embodied cognition. General-purpose VLMs, typically trained on passive, internet-scale data, exhibit critical deficiencies in understanding complex spatial relationships, inferring temporal-causal chains, and making valid judgments about real-world interactive properties.

The paper identifies two prevailing strategies in embodied AI to address this gap:
1. **Data Scaling:** This "brute force" approach, exemplified by efforts like Google's Gemini Robotics, NVIDIA's GR00T N1, and ByteDance's GR3, leverages massive foundation models adapted through extensive data pyramids. While effective in pushing performance boundaries, this paradigm often lacks a sophisticated framework to effectively manage, refine, and consolidate learning from vast, heterogeneous, and often noisy datasets.
2. **Architectural Refinement:** Models like Figure AI's Helix and Wall-OSS adopt hierarchical architectures, employing a large VLM for high-level reasoning and a smaller, reactive policy for low-level action generation. This approach introduces vital layered reasoning but typically operates on smaller, more specialized datasets, limiting its generalization capabilities.

Pelican-VL 1.0 emerges as a significant effort to **unify these two paradigms**. It acknowledges massive data scale as a prerequisite but couples it with a novel, intelligent, adaptive learning mechanism called Deliberate Practice Policy Optimization (DPPO). The authors explicitly aim to create the "general-purpose Embodied Foundation Model itself," rather than merely developing specialized Vision-Language-Action (VLA) or Vision-Language-Navigation (VLN) models as downstream applications. By releasing the largest open-source embodied VLM to date, Pelican-VL 1.0 directly contributes to democratizing advanced embodied AI research, positioning itself as a foundational resource for the community. Furthermore, the work addresses a critical fragmentation in post-training algorithms by proposing a synergistic integration of Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL), which are often treated as distinct and sequential phases in other research.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation behind Pelican-VL 1.0 is to **bridge the fundamental gap between digital-world VLM perception and real-world embodied cognition**. The authors recognize that current general-purpose VLMs fall short in critical areas essential for physical interaction, such as robust spatial understanding, long-horizon temporal reasoning, and intuitive physical causality.

The explicit mission statement of the X-Humanoid group, "To embed powerful intelligence into various embodiments," directly translates into the following key objectives for Pelican-VL 1.0:

- **Develop a Large-Scale, Open-Source Embodied Multimodal Brain Model:** To create a family of models (7 billion to 72 billion parameters) that serves as a powerful, accessible foundation for embodied AI.
- **Integrate Data Power with Intelligent Adaptive Learning Mechanisms:** To combine the benefits of massive data scaling with a sophisticated training framework that can effectively learn from and refine this data, moving beyond simple "brute force" scaling.
- **Overcome Limitations of Current Embodied AI Strategies:** To offer a unified solution that addresses the shortcomings of both data-scaling (lack of effective learning frameworks) and architectural-refinement (limited dataset scale) paradigms.
- **Establish a Novel Training Framework (DPPO):** To introduce a metacognitively inspired learning algorithm that enables continuous diagnosis, targeted refinement, and robust consolidation of embodied skills.
- **Achieve State-of-the-Art (SOTA) Performance:** To demonstrate performance on par with or exceeding leading proprietary systems on critical embodied benchmarks, including real-world hardware experiments.
- **Demonstrate Real-World Applicability:** To validate the model's capabilities in complex, practical scenarios such as contact-rich tactile manipulation, task-oriented affordance reasoning, multi-robot collaboration, and long-horizon planning.
- **Empower the Research Community:** By open-sourcing the model checkpoints, inference codebase, and training toolchain, the authors aim to facilitate widespread adoption, customization, and further advancement of embodied AI.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Pelican-VL 1.0 is a family of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) scaled from 7 billion to 72 billion parameters, designed to serve as a foundational "brain model" for embodied intelligence. The core of its innovative approach lies in its **Deliberate Practice Policy Optimization (DPPO)** training framework, inspired by human metacognition.

#### 3.1. Training Framework: Deliberate Practice Policy Optimization (DPPO)

DPPO is operationalized as a **metaloop**, an iterative RL-Refine → Diagnose → SFT cycle that dynamically alternates between two synergistic phases:

1. **Exploratory Grounding (RL Phase):**
 - **Objective:** To enhance weak or brittle embodied abilities through exploratory interaction and to discover "hard cases" – regions of the policy's distribution where competence is low.
 - **Algorithm:** Employs a Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)-based reinforcement learning paradigm. GRPO is chosen for its ability to leverage richer, rank-based preference signals compared to binary DPO.
 - **Multi-Modal and Multi-Task Reward System:** A sophisticated, rule-based reward function guides learning across six core objectives: affordance reasoning, counting and distance estimation, causal and temporal reasoning, task success evaluation, task planning, and task prediction. A "format reward" ensures structural validity of model outputs, crucial for practical deployment.
 - **Rollout Logging and Difficulty-Aware Sampling:** During GRPO training, all rollout trajectories are logged and assigned a "difficulty score" (1 - SuccessRate). Samples with high difficulty scores (representing weakly mastered or ambiguous abilities) are automatically prioritized for the subsequent SFT phase.
 - **RL Stopping Criterion:** The RL phase terminates when a "Task Saturation" indicator (TS(t) ≥ 0.7) signals that learning efficiency has plateaued, preventing unnecessary computation and overfitting.

2. **Targeted Remediation (SFT Phase):**
 - **Objective:** To consolidate and generalize the knowledge discovered during the RL phase, thereby expanding the model's embodied competence.
 - **Data Construction and Knowledge Infusion:** The SFT dataset ($D_{SFT}$) is constructed from three complementary sources: $D_{weak}$ (hard or incorrectly solved rollout samples from the RL phase), $D_{assoc}$ (related embodied samples retrieved from the main dataset based on identified weaknesses), and $D_{gen}$ (additional data synthetically generated by other VLMs to enrich contextual and linguistic diversity). This ensures targeted knowledge infusion.
 - **Supervised Consolidation:** The model undergoes standard Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) using this enhanced dataset ($D_{SFT}$), maximizing the likelihood of high-quality demonstrations. This integrates localized RL improvements globally into the policy distribution, mitigating catastrophic forgetting and promoting generalization.

#### 3.2. Theoretical Underpinning: Unified Preference Learning

The DPPO framework is theoretically grounded in a **Unified Preference Learning (PL)** paradigm. The authors posit that SFT and RL (instantiated as GRPO) can be seen as specific instances of maximizing the log-likelihood of observed preference data under a policy-conditioned probabilistic model. SFT, optimized on positive exemplars, acts as a knowledge enhancer, rapidly grounding the policy. GRPO, operating on ranked preference samples (both superior and inferior outcomes), drives weakness detection and refinement by learning from suboptimal variations. This unified perspective provides a principled explanation for the synergy between the two phases.

#### 3.3. Data Curation

Pelican-VL 1.0 is trained on a diverse and extensive data pool designed for embodied AI:
- **Scale:** 231 million images, 29,000 hours of video, 231 million open-ended Q&A pairs, 9 million grounding annotations, and 2 million multiple-choice questions. A total of 4 billion training tokens were distilled into 1.3 million SFT instances and 0.5 million RL instances.
- **Capability Areas:** The data is categorized into four key areas: Physical, Spatial, and Numerical Reasoning; Perception, Grounding, and Multi-Object Consistency; Temporal, Functional, and Scene Understanding; and Decision Making and Task Planning.
- **Metaloop Data Selection:** To address data scarcity, natural world data (e.g., YouTube videos from SpatialVID) is leveraged. General-purpose VLMs (Qwen3VL-Plus, InternVL3.5-38B) are used for diverse QA generation, followed by filtering via multi-model voting and human review to ensure high-quality weakness samples are injected into the training loop, tailored to the model's current bottlenecks.

#### 3.4. Computational Infrastructure

To support the training of a 72B-scale multi-modal model with RL, the authors developed a specialized infrastructure:
- **Context Parallelism:** Optimized to scale context length by distributing input context across multiple GPUs, allowing processing of vast temporal and spatial information crucial for embodied agents.
- **Modified VERL Framework:** An extended framework enabling 72B-scale multi-modal RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback, generalized to preference learning) training on complex, mixed-modality data (text-only, image-text, video-text concurrently). This required addressing extreme parallelism (data, pipeline, tensor), multi-modal fusion at scale, and inference optimization.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Pelican-VL 1.0 demonstrates significant advancements in embodied intelligence, achieving state-of-the-art performance across various benchmarks and showcasing robust real-world applications.

#### 4.1. Benchmark Performance and Evolution

- **Overall Performance Uplift:** Pelican-VL 1.0 achieves a 20.3% performance uplift from its base model and outperforms 100B-level open-source models by 10.6%, positioning it on par with leading proprietary systems.
- **Training Stability and Catastrophic Forgetting:** Evaluations on MVBench show that Pelican-VL 1.0 maintains stable general capabilities throughout the metaloop training process, indicating successful prevention of catastrophic forgetting. This is attributed to the strategic infusion of general-domain knowledge during performance plateaus.
- **Embodied Abilities Enhancement:** The model shows continuous and significant improvements across various embodied benchmarks (RefSpatialBench, VSI-Bench, Where2Place, COSMOS), validating the effectiveness of the Metaloop's adaptive learning cycles.
- **SOTA Comparison:** Pelican-VL 1.0 (72B) achieves superior performance among models ≤100B parameters, particularly in embodied domains, and demonstrates competitive or superior results against >100B proprietary models (e.g., GPT-5, Gemini2.5-Flash) with significantly fewer computational resources.

#### 4.2. Benchmark Analysis with a Novel Capability Taxonomy

To provide a more granular evaluation, the authors introduced a unified taxonomy of nine core capability dimensions for embodied understanding:
1. Physical & Causal Reasoning
2. Perception & Object Grounding
3. Quantitative & Numerical Reasoning
4. Spatial & Geometric Reasoning
5. Temporal & Sequential Reasoning
6. Affordance & Function Reasoning
7. Multi-Object & Scene Consistency
8. Scene & Action Understanding
9. Decision & Task Planning

- **Limitations of Existing Benchmarks:** Analysis using this taxonomy revealed that most public embodied datasets disproportionately emphasize spatial, numerical, and semantic reasoning, while critical competencies like physical feasibility, affordance perception, and long-horizon decision planning are severely underrepresented.
- **Pelican-VL's Balanced Capability Profile:** Pelican-VL 1.0, trained with DPPO, not only achieves SOTA performance but also exhibits a comprehensively balanced capability profile across all nine dimensions, effectively remedying the imbalances observed in other open-source models (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct).
- **Specific Performance Gains:** Compared to its backbone model, Pelican-VL 1.0 achieved a 25.7% increase in spatial understanding (Spatial-Physical) and a 15.1% increase in temporal reasoning (CoT-Reasoning). Overall, it showed an average improvement of 15.4% across all nine reasoning dimensions compared to the Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct base model. Notably, it outperformed large-scale proprietary models in "Decision and Task Planning" and "Scene and Action Understanding."

#### 4.3. Function Calling

Pelican-VL 1.0 demonstrates competitive performance on the Berkeley Function-Calling Leaderboard. After fine-tuning on relevant datasets and incorporating multi-turn dialogue data, the model exhibited robust tool-invocation capabilities without degrading its general conversational abilities, proving crucial for real-world robotic interaction.

#### 4.4. Real-World Downstream Applications

1. **Zero-shot Object Manipulation with Affordance:** Deployed on a tabletop single-arm robotic platform, Pelican-VL 1.0 achieved SOTA affordance generation and enabled zero-shot object manipulation. It uses a hierarchical control structure that involves multi-view visual perception, embodied planning (decomposing high-level objectives), and function calling for execution. Multi-view consistency in affordance generation allows 2D predictions to be triangulated into accurate 3D action targets for the robot.
2. **Closing the Sensorimotor Loop: Proactive Prediction and Reaction Adaptation:** Pelican-VL 1.0 is the first VLM to close the sensorimotor loop by integrating its physical reasoning capabilities with online friction estimation and adaptive grasp control. This allows for human-like grasping of delicate and compliant objects through a continuous cycle of proactive force prediction (from Pelican-VL 1.0), real-time tactile adaptation (via a particle filter for friction estimation), and memory updates.
3. **Embodied Function Call for Multi-Robot Collaboration:** The model was successfully implemented in a multi-robot system comprising a wheeled humanoid, a bipedal humanoid, and an industrial manipulator. It coordinated cross-robot collaboration through multi-turn function calls, demonstrating an industry-first long-horizon planning capability. An example showcased an industrial light bulb inspection pipeline, where Pelican-VL 1.0 decomposed system-level tasks into behavior-level plans for each robot, generating parameterized action function calls.
4. **Long-Horizon Task Reasoning and Planning:** In a real domestic environment, Pelican-VL 1.0 successfully executed a complex, multi-stage natural-language instruction (e.g., "put shoes on rack, throw garbage in trash, put clothes in washing machine"). This demonstrated coherent understanding of spatial relations, autonomous sequential planning, and adaptive execution across multiple distinct regions, highlighting its embodied reasoning competence and real-world robustness.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Pelican-VL 1.0 represents a significant leap forward in the field of embodied artificial intelligence, carrying profound implications and potential impact:

- **Democratization of Embodied AI:** By open-sourcing a large-scale, SOTA embodied brain model (Pelican-VL 1.0) along with its complete training toolchain (DPPO), X-Humanoid is democratizing access to cutting-edge research. This will significantly lower the barrier for other researchers and developers, accelerating collaborative innovation and fostering a vibrant community around embodied AI.
- **Novel and Robust Training Paradigm:** The DPPO framework offers a groundbreaking approach to training embodied agents. Its metacognitive inspiration, unifying RL and SFT in a continuous, self-improving loop, addresses the critical fragmentation in post-training algorithms. This paradigm ensures both rapid skill discovery (via RL) and stable knowledge consolidation (via SFT), leading to more robust, generalizable, and continuously evolving embodied intelligence.
- **Bridging the Digital-Physical Divide:** The work makes substantial progress in translating the digital perception capabilities of VLMs into robust, real-world physical interaction. By explicitly tackling the deficits in spatial, temporal-causal, and physical reasoning, Pelican-VL 1.0 moves embodied AI closer to systems that can truly understand and operate within the complexities of the physical world.
- **Diagnostic and Holistic Evaluation:** The introduction of a fine-grained, 9-dimension capability taxonomy and its application in benchmarking provide a far more diagnostic and holistic evaluation framework than previously available. This "why it failed" insight is crucial for guiding targeted model improvements and accelerating the development of balanced and comprehensive embodied intelligence.
- **Catalyst for Real-World Robotics Applications:** The demonstrated success in diverse and challenging real-world tasks—from delicate, contact-rich manipulation and adaptive grasping to multi-robot collaboration and complex long-horizon planning—underscores the immediate practical applicability of Pelican-VL 1.0. This can accelerate the deployment of more versatile and autonomous robots in various industries.
- **Enabling Future Self-Evolving Systems:** Pelican-VL 1.0 and its DPPO framework lay the foundation for truly autonomous, self-evolving embodied agents. The vision of integrating this "brain" with high-fidelity simulation and real-world hardware in a closed loop, where the embodiment generates novel data to continuously trigger and refine the metaloop, points towards a future of continuous learning and adaptation for robots.
- **Paving the Way for a "ChatGPT Moment" in Robotics:** The authors' ultimate goal of demonstrating a fully autonomous, self-evolving agent solving a meaningful societal problem (e.g., in manufacturing, logistics, or healthcare) envisions a "ChatGPT moment" for robotics. Such a breakthrough would unlock the full potential of robotics to revolutionize productivity and address grand challenges, marking a pivotal shift in how intelligent agents interact with and contribute to the world. The advanced infrastructure developed for large-scale multi-modal RL also sets a precedent for training even more capable foundation models in the future.
