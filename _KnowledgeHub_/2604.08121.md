# Uni-ViGU: Towards Unified Video Generation and Understanding via A Diffusion-Based Video Generator

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Academy of AI for Science and Fudan University developed Uni-ViGU, a diffusion-based framework that unifies video generation and understanding by extending a pretrained video generator as its core. This framework utilizes a unified flow method and a modality-driven Mixture-of-Experts architecture to simultaneously produce high-quality videos and richly detailed, visually faithful textual captions.

## Problem

- The significant computational cost of diffusion-based video generation makes it challenging to scale understanding-centric Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) for high-quality video generation.
- Existing MLLM-diffusion integrations often feature decoupled training, which limits the direct benefit of generation objectives for visual understanding.
- There is an architectural mismatch between continuous video latents, suitable for diffusion models, and discrete text tokens, which traditionally hinders their unified processing.

## Method

- **Uni-ViGU** extends a pretrained video generator (==WAN2.1==) by integrating a ==unified flow method== that handles both continuous video and discrete text within a single generative process.
- It implements a ==modality-driven Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)== architecture, sharing ==attention layers== for cross-modal interaction and employing separate ==Feed-Forward Networks (FFNs)== for video and text processing.
- A ==two-stage bidirectional training== mechanism is used: =='Knowledge Recall'== to learn video-to-prompt mapping and =='Capability Refinement'== to develop fine-grained video understanding from detailed captions.

## Results

- The framework demonstrates the simultaneous generation of both high-quality videos and corresponding detailed textual captions from noise.
- Generated captions are significantly more descriptive and faithful to the visual content than the initial conditioning prompts used for video generation alone.
- The process enables mutual refinement, where progressively denoised text guides video generation and emerging video latents steer text generation, leading to coherent multimodal outputs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Generation-centric models, specifically powerful video generators, offer a scalable and principled foundation for unified multimodal intelligence due to their inherently rich visual priors.
> - The knowledge acquired by a text-to-video generator can be effectively repurposed and inverted for robust video understanding, minimizing the need for extensive new training.
> - Decoupling modality-specific knowledge into separate FFNs while sharing attention layers allows for efficient cross-modal interaction and disentanglement of domain-specific generation patterns.
