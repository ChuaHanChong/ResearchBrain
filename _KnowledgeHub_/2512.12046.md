# Goal Reaching with Eikonal-Constrained Hierarchical Quasimetric Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Eikonal-Constrained Quasimetric RL (Eik-QRL) and its hierarchical extension, Eikonal-Hierarchical QRL (Eik-HiQRL), propose a new approach to Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning by incorporating continuous-time PDE constraints for trajectory-free value function learning. Eik-HiQRL demonstrated state-of-the-art performance in offline humanoidmaze navigation, with notable improvements in long-horizon tasks and a reduction in collision rates in simpler environments.

## Problem

- Reward engineering in Reinforcement Learning (RL) is complex and time-consuming, often leading to artificial structures and limited generalization.
- Existing Quasimetric RL (QRL) relies on discrete, trajectory-based local consistency constraints, which limit applicability in data-scarce settings and hinder out-of-distribution generalization.
- Complex, high-dimensional environments with non-isotropic dynamics challenge the learning of accurate value functions and maintaining a high signal-to-noise ratio in long-horizon tasks.

## Method

- ==Eikonal==-Constrained Quasimetric RL (**Eik-QRL**) reformulates local consistency using a continuous-time Eikonal PDE constraint, derived from the HJB equation under isotropic dynamics, enabling trajectory-free learning from i.i.d. state-goal samples.
- Eikonal-Hierarchical QRL (Eik-HiQRL) extends Eik-QRL by applying it at a high level within a lower-dimensional abstract space, combined with Implicit V-Learning for low-level control.
- The approach leverages advancements in Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) to embed PDE constraints directly into neural network training objectives for practical optimization.

## Results

- Eik-QRL and Eik-HiQRL achieved significantly lower collision rates in Pointmaze environments compared to the original QRL, indicating improved value function accuracy under isotropic dynamics.
- Eik-HiQRL established state-of-the-art performance in offline Humanoidmaze navigation tasks, outperforming all baselines with substantial gains, especially in long-horizon `stitch` datasets.
- In complex manipulation tasks involving hybrid and contact-rich dynamics (Antsoccer, robotic cubes/scenes), Eik-HiQRL performed comparably to baselines, highlighting current limitations of PDE constraints in non-smooth environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Continuous-time PDE constraints can replace discrete trajectory-based constraints in QRL, enabling trajectory-free learning and acting as an implicit regularizer for value functions.
> - While Eik-QRL benefits from idealized isotropic dynamics, a hierarchical architecture (Eik-HiQRL) is essential for addressing the method's limitations in complex, high-dimensional, and non-isotropic environments.
> - PDE-based constraints lead to improved value function accuracy, resulting in reduced collision rates and enhanced generalization, particularly in tasks requiring trajectory stitching.
