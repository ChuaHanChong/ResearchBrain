# Towards Visuospatial Cognition via Hierarchical Fusion of Visual Experts

> [!summary] Summary
> Kyoto University introduces ViCA2, a multimodal large language model that significantly advances visuospatial cognition by integrating a dual vision encoder and specialized training on the ViCA-322K dataset. The model achieves state-of-the-art average performance of 56.8 on the VSI-Bench, outperforming larger open-source and proprietary MLLMs.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs struggle with robust visuospatial cognition, including fine-grained understanding of object layouts, spatial relations, and geometric attributes, despite strong performance on general vision-language tasks.
- Current MLLM architectures primarily rely on single semantic vision encoders, which often lack the high-resolution spatial details necessary for precise spatial reasoning.
- There is a scarcity of specialized, large-scale training datasets specifically designed for fine-grained spatial reasoning tasks in MLLMs.

## Method

- ViCA2 integrates a novel ==dual vision encoder architecture==, combining SigLIP for global semantics and Hiera for fine-grained spatial features, with an efficient token ratio control mechanism.
- A new large-scale, spatially grounded dataset, ==ViCA-322K==, was developed, comprising over **322,000** question-answer pairs covering diverse spatial reasoning tasks.
- A ==three-stage training strategy== is employed, including Hiera projector calibration, full multimodal capability restoration, and targeted spatial reasoning fine-tuning using ViCA-322K.

## Results

- ViCA2-7B achieves an average score of **56.8** on the VSI-Bench benchmark, outperforming leading open-source models (e.g., LLaVA-NeXT-Video-72B at **40.9**) and proprietary models (e.g., Gemini-1.5 Pro at **45.4**).
- The model demonstrates consistent performance gains across most spatial tasks, with notable improvements of **+20.1** in 'Absolute Distance' and **+17.0** in 'Room Size' compared to the next best models.
- Experiments show ViCA2-7B's accuracy continuously improves with increasing training data size from ViCA-322K, indicating its architecture's capacity is not yet saturated and can benefit from even larger datasets.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Hierarchical fusion of specialized visual experts (semantic and spatial) effectively addresses the limitations of single encoders, enabling comprehensive visuospatial understanding.
> - Targeted instruction tuning with a curated, large-scale dataset like ViCA-322K is crucial for instilling and enhancing specific cognitive abilities like spatial reasoning in MLLMs.
> - Even compact models (7B parameters) can achieve state-of-the-art performance by combining intelligent architectural design with specialized data, potentially outperforming much larger and proprietary systems.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) has witnessed explosive growth, with models like Flamingo, LLaVA, Gemini, and GPT-4V demonstrating impressive capabilities across a wide array of general vision-language tasks, including image captioning, visual question answering (VQA), and video understanding. These advancements primarily stem from aligning vast visual and linguistic datasets through large-scale pretraining, leading to robust generalization. Recent iterations, such as LLaVA-OneVision and LLaVA-NeXT-Video, have further pushed boundaries by integrating multi-image and video processing, often leveraging stronger visual backbones like SigLIP.

Despite these breakthroughs, a critical gap persists: **visuospatial cognition**. This refers to the complex ability to construct, maintain, and reason about spatially coherent internal representations of environments, encompassing fine-grained understanding of object layouts, spatial relations (e.g., "above," "left of"), temporal order, and geometric attributes. While current MLLMs can describe scenes semantically, their ability to perform precise spatial reasoning—such as estimating distances, determining relative directions, or planning routes within a dynamic environment—remains limited.

This paper directly addresses this crucial limitation. Prior work, notably VSI-Bench (Yang et al., 2024), explicitly highlighted this deficit, revealing that even state-of-the-art MLLMs struggled with spatially grounded tasks, especially when dealing with real-world indoor videos. The authors identify key bottlenecks in existing open-source models:
1. **Architectural limitations:** Reliance on a single semantic vision encoder (like SigLIP) optimized for global semantic alignment rather than high-resolution spatial details, often due to low input resolutions.
2. **Data scarcity:** A pronounced lack of specialized training data specifically targeting fine-grained spatial reasoning.

"Towards Visuospatial Cognition via Hierarchical Fusion of Visual Experts" positions itself at the forefront of efforts to bridge this gap. By proposing ViCA2, a novel MLLM architecture with a dual vision encoder and a specialized training dataset (ViCA-322K), the work aims to enable robust visuospatial understanding in a lightweight, open-source framework. This contribution is particularly significant because it not only demonstrates how to achieve state-of-the-art performance in spatial reasoning but also provides a publicly available model and dataset, democratizing access to capabilities that were previously limited to larger, proprietary systems like Gemini-1.5 Pro. It pushes the MLLM landscape beyond mere semantic understanding towards a more profound, human-like spatial intelligence, which is vital for embodied AI applications.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to significantly **advance visuospatial cognition in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**. Specifically, the authors aim to develop an MLLM capable of robustly reasoning about spatial layouts, relations, and dynamics in complex visual inputs, including both images and videos.

The motivations driving this research are multi-faceted:

- **Addressing a Critical Gap in MLLM Capabilities:** While general vision-language tasks are well-served by current MLLMs, fine-grained visuospatial understanding remains an underdeveloped area. The authors emphasize that "visuospatial cognition—the capacity to construct, retain, and reason over spatially coherent internal representations of complex environments—remains a significant challenge." This highlights the need to move beyond simple object recognition and semantic descriptions to a deeper, relational understanding of space.
- **Enabling Downstream Applications:** Strong visuospatial reasoning is a foundational capability for a host of high-impact AI applications. These include:
 - **Indoor navigation:** For autonomous robots or assistive technologies.
 - **Assistive robotics:** Requiring precise object localization and manipulation.
 - **Video summarization:** Capturing not just events but their spatial context and evolution.
 - **Embodied question answering:** Where an agent needs to physically interact with an environment based on spatial queries.
- **Overcoming Architectural Limitations of Current MLLMs:** Existing MLLMs typically rely on single vision encoders (e.g., SigLIP) optimized for global semantic alignment. This design often sacrifices the high-resolution spatial details crucial for tasks like dense OCR, visual grounding, and precise object localization. The motivation is to rectify this by integrating architectural components explicitly designed for fine-grained spatial feature extraction.
- **Mitigating the Lack of Specialized Training Data:** The absence of large-scale, high-quality datasets specifically curated for visuospatial reasoning has been a significant impediment. The authors are motivated to fill this void by creating ViCA-322K, a dataset tailored for instruction tuning MLLMs on a diverse range of spatial tasks.
- **Developing Efficient and Open-Source Solutions:** Current proprietary models (e.g., Gemini-1.5 Pro) show some spatial abilities but are closed-source and computationally expensive. A key motivation is to develop a lightweight, open-source model (ViCA2-7B) that can match or even surpass these larger proprietary systems, thereby democratizing access to advanced visuospatial AI and fostering further community research.

In essence, the research is motivated by the desire to push MLLMs beyond superficial visual understanding towards a more profound, human-like spatial intelligence, making them truly useful for real-world, interactive applications.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The authors introduce **ViCA2 (Visuospatial Cognitive Assistant 2)**, a novel MLLM that builds upon the LLaVA-OneVision framework to enhance visuospatial understanding through several key innovations:

#### 3.1. Dual Vision Encoder Architecture for Enhanced Spatial Awareness

- **Semantic Encoder (SigLIP):** ViCA2 retains SigLIP as a primary vision encoder, leveraging its strength in extracting high-level semantic representations from visual inputs. SigLIP is contrastively pretrained on large-scale image-text pairs, making it effective for general visual-language tasks.
- **Spatial Encoder (Hiera):** To overcome SigLIP's limitations in fine-grained spatial detail, ViCA2 integrates **Hiera** (specifically, `sam2.1_hiera_b+` from SAM2) as a second vision encoder. Hiera is a hierarchical vision backbone pretrained with Masked Autoencoding (MAE), known for its ability to extract multiscale visual features through progressive downsampling. This makes it highly sensitive to spatially dense tasks, robustly recognizing objects and regions of varying sizes and locations, which is crucial for preserving local details (edges, textures) and global semantic structures (object identities, scene layouts).
- **Feature Projection and Fusion:** Outputs from both SigLIP and Hiera are processed through separate linear projectors to align their feature spaces with the language model's embedding space. The projected features are then concatenated along the sequence dimension, enabling the language model to jointly reason over both global semantic content and fine-grained spatial cues.

#### 3.2. Efficient Token Ratio Control Mechanism

To manage computational cost and memory consumption, particularly with high-resolution video inputs and Hiera's resource-intensive nature, ViCA2 employs a token ratio control mechanism:

- **Frame Sampling:** While SigLIP processes all `N_total = 64` uniformly sampled video frames, Hiera processes a reduced set of `N_hiera = 32` frames.
- **Feature Extraction Depth:** Hiera features are extracted from Stage 4 (the deepest layer), capturing abstract and semantically rich information while balancing resolution.
- **Spatial Pooling:** A 2D spatial pooling module with a stride of `S_pool = 2` (using bilinear interpolation) is applied to Hiera features, reducing token count per frame while preserving spatial continuity.
- **Positional Cues:** A learned row-wise special token is appended at the end of each row in the 2D token grid from both encoders, providing positional awareness to the language model.
- **Token Balance:** This mechanism results in `T_siglip = 13,440` tokens from SigLIP and `T_hiera = 8,704` tokens from Hiera, concatenated and fed into the language decoder, with a `1.54:1` ratio balancing semantics and spatial precision. The triplet `(N_hiera, S_stage, S_pool)` allows for fine-grained control over this balance.

#### 3.3. ViCA-322K: A Large-Scale Spatially Grounded Dataset

- **Creation:** The authors developed and publicly released ViCA-322K, a new dataset comprising over 322,000 spatially grounded question-answer pairs.
- **Content:** Derived from indoor videos, it covers a wide spectrum of spatial reasoning tasks:
 - **Configurational:** Object count, relative distance, relative direction, route planning.
 - **Measurement Estimation:** Absolute distance, object size, room size.
 - **Spatiotemporal Reasoning:** Appearance order.
- **Purpose:** This dataset is crucial for targeted instruction tuning, enabling the model to internalize structured spatial knowledge.

#### 3.4. Three-Stage Training Strategy

ViCA2 utilizes a progressive three-stage training strategy to adapt its components for multimodal understanding:

- **Stage 1: Hiera Projector Calibration:**
 - Dataset: `LLaVA-CC3M-Pretrain-595K` (image-text pairs).
 - Focus: Coarse visual-language alignment for the randomly initialized Hiera projector.
 - Trainable: Only Hiera projector; other components frozen.
- **Stage 2: Full Multimodal Capability Restoration:**
 - Dataset: 10% subset of `LLaVA-OneVision-Data`.
 - Focus: Re-align newly added Hiera features with existing SigLIP and language model representations.
 - Trainable: SigLIP, both vision projectors, and the language model; Hiera encoder frozen (due to memory constraints).
- **Stage 3: Targeted Spatial Reasoning Fine-tuning:**
 - Dataset: `ViCA-322K` (spatially grounded QA pairs).
 - Focus: Enhance spatial perception and reasoning.
 - Trainable: Same as Stage 2 (full model except frozen Hiera backbone).

#### 3.5. Computational Infrastructure

The training leveraged DeepSpeed ZeRO-2 with offloading for Stage 1 and DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 with offloading for Stages 2 and 3, optimizing for hardware constraints. The base language model is Qwen2-7B, and SigLIP and Hiera encoders are initialized from their respective pretrained weights.

This comprehensive approach, combining a novel dual-encoder architecture with a specialized dataset and a multi-stage training strategy, allows ViCA2 to achieve superior visuospatial reasoning capabilities.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research yields compelling results that underscore the effectiveness of ViCA2's design and training methodology.

#### 4.1. State-of-the-Art Performance on VSI-Bench

- **Overall Superiority:** ViCA2-7B achieves a remarkable average score of **56.8** on the challenging VSI-Bench benchmark, which evaluates visuospatial reasoning across eight diverse tasks. This score represents a significant leap over both leading open-source and proprietary MLLMs.
- **Outperforming Larger Models:** Despite utilizing a relatively compact 7B parameter language model, ViCA2-7B substantially outperforms much larger open-source models. For instance, it surpasses LLaVA-NeXT-Video-72B (72B parameters), which scored 40.9, and LLaVA-OneVision-72B (72B parameters), which scored 40.2.
- **Surpassing Proprietary Models:** Crucially, ViCA2-7B also outperforms leading proprietary models like Gemini-1.5 Pro (scoring 45.4) and GPT-4o (scoring 34.0), demonstrating that targeted architectural and data innovations can yield superior performance even with fewer parameters.

#### 4.2. Consistent Gains Across Spatial Tasks

- **Significant Improvements:** ViCA2-7B shows consistent performance gains across most VSI-Bench tasks. Notably, it achieves an improvement of **+20.1** in "Absolute Distance" and **+17.0** in "Room Size" compared to the next best-performing model. Strong performance is also observed in "Object Count," "Object Size," "Relative Distance," and "Appearance Order."
- **Task-Specific Limitations:** The model's performance in "Relative Direction" is comparatively lower, a limitation attributed by the authors to insufficient explicit training examples for this specific spatial concept within the ViCA-322K dataset. However, it still achieves the highest score in "Route Planning" (+2.1), suggesting some cross-task generalization of learned spatial reasoning patterns.

#### 4.3. Impact of Training Data Size

- **Continuous Improvement:** Experiments on the impact of training data size demonstrate that ViCA2-7B's accuracy consistently improves as the percentage of the ViCA-322K dataset used for training increases. The model reaches its peak average score of 56.81 when trained on 100% of the data.
- **Unsaturated Learning Capacity:** Unlike prior work with ViCA-7B (their previous model), where performance plateaued earlier, ViCA2-7B continues to show improvement even in the final stages of data scaling. This suggests that ViCA2's more complex architecture and greater representational capacity can benefit from even larger, more diverse spatial datasets, indicating that its learning potential is not yet saturated.

#### 4.4. Qualitative Insights from Training Stages

- **Training Loss Dynamics:** Training loss curves show sharp decreases and stable convergence in Stage 1 (Hiera projector calibration) and Stage 3 (ViCA-322K fine-tuning), indicating effective learning. Stage 2 (base model restoration) shows only mild loss reduction, which is expected given the base model was already extensively pre-tuned.
- **Text Generation Quality:** Evaluation using GPT-4.1-mini as a judge reveals a progressive and significant improvement in text generation quality (informativeness, fluency, spatial relevance) across the training stages, with the model after ViCA-322K fine-tuning achieving the highest scores. This suggests that specialized spatial instruction tuning not only enhances reasoning but also improves overall language generation capabilities.

#### 4.5. ViCA2-Thinking and Comparison with ViCA-7B (Appendix Findings)

- **Degradation with Explicit Reasoning:** Fine-tuning ViCA2-7B for explicit multi-step reasoning (ViCA2-7B-Thinking, which generates a "Thoughts" segment) leads to a notable *drop* in VSI-Bench numerical performance, converging to baseline 7B/8B models. The authors hypothesize this is due to the demanding nature of high-quality free-form text generation required for "Thoughts," contrasting with the more structured numerical/multiple-choice QA, and potential biases from ViCA-322K's egocentric spatial descriptions.
- **Architectural Complexity and Data Needs:** When compared to their previous model, ViCA-7B, ViCA2-7B shows slightly lower overall performance on VSI-Bench. This is attributed to ViCA2's increased architectural complexity (dual encoders, more trainable parameters) requiring an even larger and more diverse dataset than the existing ViCA-322K, which was originally designed and scaled for ViCA-7B. Data scaling analyses further support that ViCA2-7B's capacity is not yet fully utilized by the current dataset, especially for less-represented tasks.

In summary, ViCA2-7B stands as a groundbreaking open-source model that significantly advances visuospatial reasoning, demonstrating that intelligent architectural design and targeted data curation can outperform larger, more general-purpose MLLMs.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The development of ViCA2 marks a significant advancement in the capabilities of Multimodal Large Language Models, with profound implications for both fundamental AI research and practical applications.

#### 5.1. Breakthrough in Visuospatial Cognition

ViCA2 addresses a critical and long-standing challenge in AI: enabling machines to robustly understand and reason about the spatial world in a manner akin to human cognition. By achieving state-of-the-art performance on the demanding VSI-Bench benchmark, ViCA2 demonstrates that fine-grained visuospatial intelligence is achievable, pushing MLLMs beyond mere semantic recognition to a deeper, relational understanding of visual environments. This is a crucial step towards building truly intelligent agents that can perceive, interpret, and interact with complex physical spaces.

#### 5.2. Innovative Architectural Design

The dual vision encoder architecture, combining SigLIP for global semantics and Hiera for fine-grained spatial structure, represents a potent design choice. This hierarchical fusion effectively leverages the strengths of different visual experts, showing how modularity and specialized components can lead to superior performance in specific cognitive domains. The efficient token ratio control mechanism is equally significant, demonstrating how to balance expressive power with computational feasibility, making advanced spatial reasoning accessible without requiring prohibitively large models or massive compute resources.

#### 5.3. Importance of Specialized Data Curation

The creation and public release of ViCA-322K highlight the indispensable role of high-quality, targeted training data. This large-scale dataset of spatially grounded question-answer pairs serves as a testament to the power of instruction tuning for instilling specific cognitive abilities in MLLMs. Its availability will undoubtedly accelerate future research by providing a standardized resource for training and evaluating models focused on spatial reasoning. The analysis on data scaling further underscores that model capacity, even in compact forms, can continuously benefit from increasingly larger and more diverse datasets for specialized tasks.

#### 5.4. Efficiency and Accessibility

ViCA2-7B's ability to outperform much larger open-source models (e.g., 72B parameter variants) and leading proprietary systems with a compact 7B parameter language model is highly significant. This demonstrates a path towards more resource-efficient and sustainable AI development. By making the model, codebase, and dataset publicly available, the authors democratize access to cutting-edge visuospatial AI, fostering innovation and reducing the barrier to entry for researchers and developers worldwide.

#### 5.5. Enabling Transformative AI Applications

The enhanced visuospatial capabilities of ViCA2 have the potential to revolutionize various downstream applications:

- **Robotics:** Improved spatial understanding is crucial for autonomous navigation, object manipulation, and human-robot interaction in complex environments (e.g., indoor service robots, industrial automation).
- **Augmented/Virtual Reality:** More accurate spatial mapping and object interaction will lead to more immersive and realistic AR/VR experiences.
- **Assistive Technologies:** For individuals with visual impairments, AI systems leveraging ViCA2 could provide richer spatial descriptions and navigation assistance.
- **Video Analysis and Summarization:** Beyond temporal events, AI can now summarize videos with a deeper understanding of spatial changes, object movements, and environmental layouts.
- **Embodied AI:** It provides a strong foundation for agents that learn by interacting with their environment, making decisions based on complex spatial reasoning.

#### 5.6. Guiding Future Research Directions

The paper's discussion of limitations, such as coverage gaps in ViCA-322K (e.g., "Relative Direction"), the frozen Hiera encoder, and the simple feature fusion mechanism, provides clear avenues for future work. This transparency is invaluable for guiding the community toward further improvements in dataset expansion, more sophisticated fusion techniques, fine-tuning of specialized encoders, and exploration of scalability to even larger language models. The observed trade-off between explicit reasoning (ViCA2-Thinking) and numerical accuracy also opens up important research questions about how best to train MLLMs for both robust performance and interpretable reasoning.

In conclusion, ViCA2 is a landmark contribution that not only pushes the boundaries of MLLM capabilities in visuospatial cognition but also provides the foundational tools and insights necessary for the broader AI community to accelerate progress in this critical domain.
