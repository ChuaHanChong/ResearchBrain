# Towards Visuospatial Cognition via Hierarchical Fusion of Visual Experts

> [!summary] Summary
> Kyoto University introduces ViCA2, a multimodal large language model that significantly advances visuospatial cognition by integrating a dual vision encoder and specialized training on the ViCA-322K dataset. The model achieves state-of-the-art average performance of 56.8 on the VSI-Bench, outperforming larger open-source and proprietary MLLMs.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs struggle with robust visuospatial cognition, including fine-grained understanding of object layouts, spatial relations, and geometric attributes, despite strong performance on general vision-language tasks.
- Current MLLM architectures primarily rely on single semantic vision encoders, which often lack the high-resolution spatial details necessary for precise spatial reasoning.
- There is a scarcity of specialized, large-scale training datasets specifically designed for fine-grained spatial reasoning tasks in MLLMs.

## Method

- ViCA2 integrates a novel ==dual vision encoder architecture==, combining SigLIP for global semantics and Hiera for fine-grained spatial features, with an efficient token ratio control mechanism.
- A new large-scale, spatially grounded dataset, ==ViCA-322K==, was developed, comprising over **322,000** question-answer pairs covering diverse spatial reasoning tasks.
- A ==three-stage training strategy== is employed, including Hiera projector calibration, full multimodal capability restoration, and targeted spatial reasoning fine-tuning using ViCA-322K.

## Results

- ViCA2-7B achieves an average score of **56.8** on the VSI-Bench benchmark, outperforming leading open-source models (e.g., LLaVA-NeXT-Video-72B at **40.9**) and proprietary models (e.g., Gemini-1.5 Pro at **45.4**).
- The model demonstrates consistent performance gains across most spatial tasks, with notable improvements of **+20.1** in 'Absolute Distance' and **+17.0** in 'Room Size' compared to the next best models.
- Experiments show ViCA2-7B's accuracy continuously improves with increasing training data size from ViCA-322K, indicating its architecture's capacity is not yet saturated and can benefit from even larger datasets.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Hierarchical fusion of specialized visual experts (semantic and spatial) effectively addresses the limitations of single encoders, enabling comprehensive visuospatial understanding.
> - Targeted instruction tuning with a curated, large-scale dataset like ViCA-322K is crucial for instilling and enhancing specific cognitive abilities like spatial reasoning in MLLMs.
> - Even compact models (7B parameters) can achieve state-of-the-art performance by combining intelligent architectural design with specialized data, potentially outperforming much larger and proprietary systems.
