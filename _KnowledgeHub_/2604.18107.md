# Test-Time Perturbation Learning with Delayed Feedback for Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and Tsinghua University developed Perturbation learning with Delayed Feedback (PDF), a lightweight, verifier-free test-time adaptation framework that enhances Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models' robustness against trajectory overfitting. PDF improved average success rate on the LIBERO robotic manipulation benchmark by 8 percentage points over the OpenVLA baseline and achieved a Human-Normalized Score of 1.07 on Atari, a 0.10 improvement over the Jat base model.

## Problem

- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models often exhibit fragility to minor environmental perturbations due to "trajectory overfitting," where they rely on spurious correlations from training data rather than robust semantic understanding.
- Existing test-time adaptation (TTA) methods for VLAs are either computationally expensive (verifier-based) or unstable and unreliable (verifier-free methods relying on miscalibrated self-supervised confidence metrics).
- VLAs tend to reproduce memorized action patterns, leading to suboptimal or incorrect action sequences when inputs deviate slightly from training data, without dynamic, goal-oriented decision-making.

## Method

- The **PDF** framework introduces an ==Uncertainty-Based Action Voting== mechanism that adaptively applies data augmentation based on the model's decision uncertainty, generating multiple perturbed action logits.
- A lightweight, trainable ==Perturbation head $h_\theta$== adds learnable perturbations to the base VLA's action logits, while the large base model's parameters remain frozen.
- ==Delayed Feedback-Guided Adaptation== optimizes the P head using a ==REINFORCE-style objective== and a ==KL divergence regularizer==, utilizing environmental feedback to refine action logits and correct overconfident incorrect decisions.

## Results

- PDF achieved **0.77** average success rate on LIBERO, **+8pp** over OpenVLA baseline (**0.69**) and nearly **+6pp** over MG-Select.
- On Atari-57, PDF reached HNS **1.07**, surpassing Jat base model's **0.97** by **+0.10**, with positive performance changes in **47/57** games.
- Ablation confirmed both uncertainty-based data augmentation and delayed feedback-guided adaptation contribute synergistically.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Trajectory overfitting in VLAs can be effectively mitigated at test time by adaptively perturbing action logits and refining these perturbations with delayed environmental feedback.
> - Quantifying decision uncertainty allows for efficient allocation of data augmentation resources, balancing improved robustness with computational overhead during inference.
> - A lightweight perturbation module, trained with a specialized loss function leveraging delayed rewards, can enable stable and efficient test-time adaptation without requiring computationally intensive fine-tuning of the entire base VLA model.
