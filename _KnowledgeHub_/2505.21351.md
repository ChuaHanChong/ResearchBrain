# EquAct: An SE(3)-Equivariant Multi-Task Transformer for Open-Loop Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> An SE(3)-equivariant multi-task transformer, EquAct, from Northeastern University, integrates geometric understanding with language conditioning for open-loop robotic manipulation. This architecture demonstrates enhanced robustness and sample efficiency, achieving a 53.3% average success rate on 10-demonstration SE(3) randomized simulation tasks, a 15.4% improvement over a strong baseline.

## Problem

- Standard transformer policies for robotic manipulation lack inherent geometric understanding, leading to poor generalization to varied 3D scene configurations.
- Learning SE(3) equivariances from scratch requires extensive data, making existing methods data-inefficient and prone to unpredictable behavior under rigid-body transformations.
- Current approaches do not explicitly enforce SE(3)-invariance for natural language instructions when conditioning robotic policies.

## Method

- Introduces **EquAct**, an SE(3)-equivariant multi-task transformer that integrates continuous SE(3)-equivariance and invariance into its architecture.
- Employs an SE(3)-==Equivariant Point Transformer== U-Net (EPTU) with spherical Fourier features and novel pooling/upsampling operations for geometrically consistent observation encoding.
- Incorporates Invariant Feature-wise Linear Modulation (iFiLM) layers to enforce SE(3)-invariance for natural language conditioning.

## Results

- Achieved an average success rate of **53.3%** in the 10-demonstration SE(3) randomized simulation tasks, outperforming the best baseline by **15.4%**.
- Demonstrated superior real-world performance, with a **65.0%** average success rate across 4 physical multi-task scenarios, compared to **12.5%** for the baseline.
- Ablation studies confirmed that geometric equivariance is critical, with even minor architectural changes leading to a performance drop from **52.8%** to **12.3%** in relevant tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly embedding SE(3)-equivariance into transformer architectures dramatically enhances geometric consistency and generalization to novel 3D scene configurations.
> - Incorporating geometric priors significantly reduces the data required for learning robust robotic manipulation policies.
> - Formally enforcing SE(3)-invariance for language instructions through iFiLM layers provides a theoretically sound method for conditioning equivariant policies.
