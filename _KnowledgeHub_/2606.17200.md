# ACE-Ego-0: Unifying Egocentric Human and Robotic Data for VLA Pretraining

> [!summary] Summary
> The ACE-Ego-0 framework unifies diverse egocentric human videos and robot demonstrations for Vision-Language-Action pretraining, employing a reliability-aware approach to effectively leverage noisy human pseudo-actions. This method demonstrated improved performance on both simulation benchmarks and real-world bimanual robotic manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- The acquisition of large-scale and diverse robot demonstration data is costly and labor-intensive, limiting the scalability and generalizability of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models.
- Integrating heterogeneous data sources like egocentric human videos and robot demonstrations is challenging due to discrepancies in spatial representations, embodiment structures, and temporal dynamics.
- Actions inferred from human videos ('pseudo-actions') are inherently noisy compared to sensor-logged robot data, potentially degrading policy learning if treated equivalently.

## Method

- A ==Unified Action Representation== projects both robot and human actions into a canonical head-camera coordinate frame, using morphology conditioning and time-aligned action chunking to normalize various data conventions.
- A Reliability-Aware Training Objective employs spatiotemporal weighting for action dimensions and separate primary/auxiliary losses to robustly integrate high-fidelity robot data with noisy human pseudo-actions.
- A multi-stage egocentric video-to-action conversion pipeline processes human videos to generate pseudo-action labels, including 3D hand reconstruction, action parameterization, and extensive quality control filters.

## Results

- ACE-Ego-0 achieved **72.8%** average success rate on the RoboCasa GR1 TableTop benchmark and **91.12%** on RoboTwin **2.0** Easy/Clean setting, outperforming comparative baselines.
- On a real-world ARX bimanual platform, the framework attained an average success rate of **78.3%**, demonstrating a **6.6%** improvement over fine-tuned 
p0.5 and strong transfer capabilities.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the reliability-aware human auxiliary loss was critical, and human data augmentation in data-scarce scenarios yielded a **4x** improvement in success rate for specific tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Effectively leveraging large-scale egocentric human videos, which are cheaper and more diverse, can overcome the robot data bottleneck and enhance VLA model scalability.
> - Explicitly modeling and accounting for data heterogeneity (spatial, structural, temporal) and supervision quality (reliable robot vs. noisy human) is crucial for successful joint training.
> - The reliability-aware training approach allows noisy human data to provide beneficial auxiliary supervision without corrupting the learning of robust control policies from high-fidelity robot data.
