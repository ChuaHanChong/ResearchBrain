# Foresight: Failure Detection for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation with Action-Conditioned World Model Latents

> [!summary] Summary
> A new three-stage framework named Foresight detects failures in long-horizon robotic manipulation by leveraging action-conditioned world model latents and Functional Conformal Prediction. The system achieved calibrated balanced accuracy of up to 0.94 on LIBERO-Long and improved ROC-AUC by 0.04 on BEHAVIOR-1K compared to baselines, while offering policy-agnostic monitoring with statistical guarantees.

## Problem

- Detecting failures in long-horizon robotic tasks is challenging due to ambiguous failure onset, accumulated small deviations, and the impracticality of dense temporal annotations.
- Interpreting robot states requires integrating temporal and action-conditioned context, as visual observations' implications vary with task stage and intended actions.
- Existing failure detection methods are often policy-specific, relying on internal representations, which limits their generalizability across diverse robot policies.

## Method

![[2606.23085-method.png]]
*he Foresight framework operates in three stages: extracting features from an action-conditioned world model, training a causal sequence model to predict failure scores, and calibrating those scores for reliable deployment.*

- The **Foresight** framework extracts ==action-conditioned predicted latents== from a frozen V-JEPA 2-AC world model, which, combined with positional encodings, forms timestep tokens.
- A causal sequence model (e.g., Transformer) is trained on these tokens using only sparse, trajectory-level success/failure labels to predict per-timestep failure scores.
- Functional Conformal Prediction (FCP) is integrated to generate adaptively calibrated, time-varying thresholds, providing statistically guaranteed false positive rates for binary failure alarms.

## Results

- Foresight-Transformer achieved the highest calibrated balanced accuracy (**0.94**) and ROC-AUC (**0.89**) on LIBERO-Long, and improved balanced accuracy to **0.78** on BEHAVIOR-1K, consistently outperforming state-of-the-art baselines in simulation.
- The framework demonstrated strong real-world applicability with ROC-AUCs up to **0.93** on ReactorX and **0.89** on Franka arms, showing robust performance across different robot platforms and policies.
- Ablation studies confirmed the superiority of action-conditioned predicted latents over hidden latents for failure detection, with a **0.04** ROC-AUC improvement on LIBERO-Long, and validated the efficacy of causal sequence models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Action-conditioned predicted latents from world models are crucial for detecting long-horizon failures, as they effectively identify inconsistencies between intended actions and observed state transitions.
> - Causal sequence models, particularly Transformers, excel at processing temporal dependencies in these latents, leading to more robust failure prediction than feed-forward networks.
> - A policy-agnostic framework can be developed using general world model embeddings, reducing reliance on policy-specific internal signals and enhancing scalability across different robot policies.
