# XEmbodied: A Foundation Model with Enhanced Geometric and Physical Cues for Large-Scale Embodied Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> XEmbodied is a foundation model enhancing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with intrinsic 3D geometric awareness and efficient physical cue integration for large-scale embodied environments. This work achieves state-of-the-art performance across 18 benchmarks in 3D understanding, semantic reasoning, and embodied affordance, facilitating robust cloud-side data annotation for autonomous driving and robotics.

## Problem

- Generic Vision-Language Models (VLMs) lack intrinsic 3D geometric awareness and domain-specific knowledge crucial for interpreting complex 3D concepts in embodied environments.
- Current industrial cloud-side data annotation pipelines rely on weak metadata, resulting in insufficient interpretation of complex, safety-critical scenarios for autonomous driving.
- Native embodied modalities like point clouds and 3D bounding boxes are not seamlessly integrated into standard VLM contexts, leading to a disconnect between semantic and physical understanding.

## Method

- A ==3D Adapter (3DA)== integrates ==3D geometric priors== into the 2D semantic token stream of a ==Multi-Modal Large Language Model (MLLM)== backbone via a ==cross-attention mechanism==.
- An ==Efficient Image-Embodied Adapter (EIEA)== distills heterogeneous ==physical cues== (e.g., BEV occupancy, 3D detection) into compact, plug-and-play tokens using a ==Mamba-based interpreter== and a ==hybrid distillation loss==.
- A ==four-stage progressive training pipeline==, combined with an ==automated cloud-based data curation== system using ==spatial richness scoring== and tiered data taxonomy, adapts the **XEmbodied** VLM to embodied tasks while preserving general capabilities.

## Results

- **XEmbodied** (SFT) achieved state-of-the-art or competitive performance across **18 public benchmarks**, including **55.28%** accuracy on Ego3DBench for 3D understanding and **77.01%** on DriveLMM-o1 for semantic reasoning.
- The model demonstrated robust zero-shot generalization on strictly out-of-distribution (OOD) datasets like Cosmos-R1 and DriveBench, indicating strong adaptability to unseen tasks and scenarios.
- Ablation studies confirmed the effectiveness of the 3D Adapter (improving Embodied Affordance performance by **8.44%**), the EIEA (reducing Ego3D-Bench RMSE from **9.44** to **6.85** with efficient inference), and the progressive curriculum learning strategy (achieving a **90.13%** weighted total average score versus **68.97%** for naive mixed training).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The proposed cross-attention mechanism in the 3D Adapter effectively injects 3D geometric priors into the VLM's semantic stream, dynamically aggregating spatial information and improving embodied affordance.
> - Distilling raw physical tool outputs into compact, interpretable context tokens via the EIEA enables efficient and seamless physical-augmented reasoning within the VLM, with low inference overhead.
> - A progressive, multi-stage curriculum learning approach is crucial for successfully adapting a VLM to domain-specific knowledge and complex embodied tasks, preventing catastrophic forgetting and ensuring robust generalization.
