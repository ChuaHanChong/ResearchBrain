# XEmbodied: A Foundation Model with Enhanced Geometric and Physical Cues for Large-Scale Embodied Environments

> [!summary] Summary
> XEmbodied is a foundation model enhancing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with intrinsic 3D geometric awareness and efficient physical cue integration for large-scale embodied environments. This work achieves state-of-the-art performance across 18 benchmarks in 3D understanding, semantic reasoning, and embodied affordance, facilitating robust cloud-side data annotation for autonomous driving and robotics.

## Problem

- Generic Vision-Language Models (VLMs) lack intrinsic 3D geometric awareness and domain-specific knowledge crucial for interpreting complex 3D concepts in embodied environments.
- Current industrial cloud-side data annotation pipelines rely on weak metadata, resulting in insufficient interpretation of complex, safety-critical scenarios for autonomous driving.
- Native embodied modalities like point clouds and 3D bounding boxes are not seamlessly integrated into standard VLM contexts, leading to a disconnect between semantic and physical understanding.

## Method

- A ==3D Adapter (3DA)== integrates ==3D geometric priors== into the 2D semantic token stream of a ==Multi-Modal Large Language Model (MLLM)== backbone via a ==cross-attention mechanism==.
- An ==Efficient Image-Embodied Adapter (EIEA)== distills heterogeneous ==physical cues== (e.g., BEV occupancy, 3D detection) into compact, plug-and-play tokens using a ==Mamba-based interpreter== and a ==hybrid distillation loss==.
- A ==four-stage progressive training pipeline==, combined with an ==automated cloud-based data curation== system using ==spatial richness scoring== and tiered data taxonomy, adapts the **XEmbodied** VLM to embodied tasks while preserving general capabilities.

## Results

- **XEmbodied** (SFT) achieved state-of-the-art or competitive performance across **18 public benchmarks**, including **55.28%** accuracy on Ego3DBench for 3D understanding and **77.01%** on DriveLMM-o1 for semantic reasoning.
- The model demonstrated robust zero-shot generalization on strictly out-of-distribution (OOD) datasets like Cosmos-R1 and DriveBench, indicating strong adaptability to unseen tasks and scenarios.
- Ablation studies confirmed the effectiveness of the 3D Adapter (improving Embodied Affordance performance by **8.44%**), the EIEA (reducing Ego3D-Bench RMSE from **9.44** to **6.85** with efficient inference), and the progressive curriculum learning strategy (achieving a **90.13%** weighted total average score versus **68.97%** for naive mixed training).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The proposed cross-attention mechanism in the 3D Adapter effectively injects 3D geometric priors into the VLM's semantic stream, dynamically aggregating spatial information and improving embodied affordance.
> - Distilling raw physical tool outputs into compact, interpretable context tokens via the EIEA enables efficient and seamless physical-augmented reasoning within the VLM, with low inference overhead.
> - A progressive, multi-stage curriculum learning approach is crucial for successfully adapting a VLM to domain-specific knowledge and complex embodied tasks, preventing catastrophic forgetting and ensuring robust generalization.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models represents a central paradigm in the advancement of autonomous driving and embodied AI systems. These models require substantial amounts of high-quality annotated data from complex real-world environments. Current industrial practices for data annotation, particularly in cloud-side pipelines for autonomous driving, often rely on generic Vision-Language Models (VLMs) that primarily leverage 2D image-text data for pre-training.

This approach presents several limitations:
1. **Lack of Geometric Reasoning:** Generic VLMs often lack intrinsic 3D geometric awareness, making them unsuitable for accurately interpreting 3D concepts such as metric distances, lane topology, and occupancy relations, which are crucial in embodied environments.
2. **Insufficient Domain Semantics:** Pre-training on internet-scale 2D data results in sparse coverage of domain-specific knowledge, such as traffic rules and driving jargon, necessary for complex autonomous driving scenarios.
3. **Inefficient Integration of Embodied Modalities:** Native embodied modalities like point clouds, 3D bounding boxes, trajectories, and occupancy grids are not seamlessly integrated into standard VLM contexts, leading to a disconnect between semantic and physical understanding.
4. **Scalability and Robustness Issues:** Traditional rule-based pipelines for annotation are transparent but lack scalability and understanding of long-tail scenarios. Vanilla MLLMs offer general reasoning but are prone to hallucinations and exhibit weak geometric and physical awareness, while naive fine-tuning often leads to catastrophic forgetting and poor out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization.

XEmbodied addresses these challenges by proposing a cloud-side foundation model designed to enhance VLMs with explicit 3D geometric awareness and efficient interaction with physical cues. This work aims to bridge the gap between general-purpose VLMs and the specific requirements of large-scale embodied environments, particularly for scenario mining and annotation within data closed-loop systems. It positions itself as a solution for scalable, high-quality data annotation in the AD 3.0 era, essential for converting massive driving data into actionable learning signals for iterative refinement of VLA models.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the development of XEmbodied are rooted in addressing critical bottlenecks in training effective VLA models for next-generation autonomous systems:

1. **Overcome Limitations of Current Cloud Annotation Engines:** Industrial pipelines predominantly rely on weak metadata (e.g., time, speed, GPS) and lightweight perception outputs (e.g., 2D/3D detection, map segmentation). These signals are insufficient for interpreting complex, safety-critical scenarios (e.g., Y-shaped intersections, school zones) that demand precise spatial reasoning and domain semantics. This inadequacy leads to underutilization of collected driving data and hinders VLA model iteration. XEmbodied aims to provide a more robust cloud-side annotation engine.

2. **Endow VLMs with Intrinsic 3D Geometric Awareness:** Generic VLMs, pre-trained on 2D image-text data, inherently possess "flat-world representations." They struggle to capture essential 3D concepts like metric distances, lane topology, and occupancy relationships. The project's motivation is to integrate 3D geometric reasoning as an intrinsic capability within the VLM, rather than treating geometry as merely an auxiliary input or requiring expensive encoder alignment that might degrade foundational VLM knowledge. This draws inspiration from human spatial cognition, which relies on a structured spatial sense from multi-source cues.

3. **Integrate Interaction with Physical Cues:** Beyond geometric awareness, embodied environments necessitate understanding of physical signals such as occupancy grids, 3D bounding boxes, and High-Definition (HD) map cues. Previous approaches either explicitly invoke these tools, suffering from inefficiency and poor alignment with language reasoning, or do not integrate them effectively. XEmbodied aims to distill these raw physical tool outputs into compact, interpretable context tokens, allowing for seamless and efficient physical-augmented reasoning within the VLM.

4. **Preserve General Capabilities while Enhancing Domain Specificity:** Naive fine-tuning of general-purpose VLMs on domain-specific data often results in catastrophic forgetting of general knowledge and degradation in out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization. A key objective is to develop a training methodology that progressively adapts the VLM to embodied environments and domain-specific knowledge (e.g., traffic rules) while safeguarding its broad general reasoning abilities.

5. **Achieve Robust Performance and Scalability:** The ultimate goal is to build a foundation model capable of robust performance across a diverse range of embodied tasks (spatial reasoning, traffic semantics, embodied affordance, OOD generalization) and scalable for large-scale scenario mining and annotation in real-world closed-loop systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

XEmbodied is designed as a cloud-side foundation model for embodied VQA, integrating enhanced geometric and physical cues. Its methodology comprises three modular components and a multi-stage training pipeline.

**A. 3D Adapter (3DA)**
The 3DA is a geometric connector that integrates 3D geometric priors into the 2D semantic token stream of a Multi-Modal Large Language Model (MLLM) backbone (Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B). It operates with two complementary streams:
1. **Semantic Stream:** A 2D visual encoder (Qwen3-VL) extracts high-level semantic tokens ($e_{2D} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times d_{2D}}$) representing object categories and scene attributes.
2. **Geometry Stream:** A 3D visual geometry encoder (VGGT) generates 3D geometry tokens ($e_{3D} \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times d_{3D}}$), embedding rich spatial priors without explicit 3D attributes. The VGGT encoder is configured with a 518x518 image size and a patch size of 14, outputting 1369 tokens.
3. **Token Alignment and Fusion:** To resolve resolution mismatch, $e_{3D}$ is first mapped onto the 2D token grid using a bilinear interpolation operator to yield $\tilde{e}_{3D}$. This is followed by a two-layer MLP projection to match the dimension of $d_{2D}$, resulting in $\hat{e}_{3D}$. These geometric cues are then injected into the semantic stream via a cross-attention mechanism, where $e_{2D}$ serves as queries and $\hat{e}_{3D}$ provides keys and values. A residual connection ensures preservation of foundational 2D semantic features, producing a fused representation $e_{fusion}$ that replaces the original 2D tokens as input to the language model.

**B. Efficient Image-Embodied Adapter (EIEA)**
The EIEA is designed to integrate heterogeneous embodied modalities (e.g., BEV occupancy, 3D detection, map segmentation) into VLM reasoning by producing compact, plug-and-play physical cue tokens ($Z_{phy}$).
1. **Modality-Specific Feature Extraction:** Off-the-shelf encoders ($E_m$) and lightweight two-layer MLP projectors ($\phi_m$) are used for each auxiliary modality ($X_m$). For instance, DiffMap for BEV map segmentation, PriorMotion for BEVflow, and SG-Net for 3D bounding boxes. These project features to a shared dimension ($d=1024$).
2. **Mamba-based Multimodal Interpreter:** Projected modality tokens and text query tokens ($X_T$) are concatenated into a single sequence with special symbolic tokens (e.g., `<PHY>`, `<MOD>`) for modality separation and learnable latent reasoning tokens. This sequence is fed into a lightweight Mamba-2 130M interpreter ($g_{Mamba}$), with its main body frozen and only input/output projection layers fine-tuned. The interpreter output ($H_{text}$) is then mapped to the VLM's word embedding space ($d_{VLM}=8192$) via a two-layer MLP latent projection module ($\phi_R$) to generate $Z_{phy}$.
3. **Distillation-based Compression:** To enhance efficiency, the interpreter outputs are distilled into compact physical cue tokens ($Z_{phy}$) of a fixed length (e.g., 64 tokens), summarizing intermediate rationales. This compression is optimized through a hybrid loss function combining MSE loss (between teacher/student outputs) and cross-entropy loss (on downstream reasoning tasks). The final VLM input concatenates visual tokens, text query tokens, and these compact physical cue tokens.

**C. Data Classification and Filtering (Cloud-based Data Curation Pipeline)**
A four-stage cloud-based pipeline automatically curates large-scale VQA data for embodied tasks:
1. **Spatial Richness Score:** A rule-based spatial entropy score $H(x_i)$ is calculated, quantifying scene geometric complexity from depth map variance (weighted $\alpha=0.6$) and 3D object distribution statistics (weighted $1-\alpha=0.4$). This score is normalized to $$.
2. **Tiered Data Taxonomy:** Fusing the entropy score with semantic tags, data is categorized into four tiers of increasing cognitive demand: T1 (scene grounding, $H \le 0.3$), T2 (spatial localization, $0.3 < H \le 0.5$), T3 (multi-view/temporal spatial reasoning, $0.5 < H \le 0.7$), and T4 (spatio-temporal understanding, $H > 0.7$).
3. **Data Quality Assessment:** A dedicated quality agent (Qwen3-VL-235B based) evaluates each sample across four dimensions: correctness, completeness, clarity, and relevance.
4. **Quality Filtering:** Low-quality instances are eliminated based on a minimum spatial entropy threshold ($\tau=0.2$) and a minimum quality score threshold ($\phi=0.85$).

**D. Training Pipeline**
XEmbodied is trained using a four-stage progressive pipeline with a Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct backbone, leveraging the Ms-swift framework on 128 NVIDIA H20 GPUs.
1. **Stage 1: Domain Semantic Alignment:** The backbone VLM is adapted to driving/robotics language using curated instruction QA. Only LLM weights are trained, keeping image encoder and 3D modules frozen.
2. **Stage 2: 3D Geometry Alignment:** The 3D Adapter is activated and trained on T2-dominant data emphasizing spatial relations. The backbone VLM remains frozen; 3DA learns to inject geometry into the fixed language space using cross-entropy loss.
3. **Stage 3: End-to-end Geometric Cognition:** 3DA and LoRA adapters on the LLM backbone are jointly trained. The curriculum progresses from T2 to T3-T4 samples, enabling bidirectional coupling between spatial perception and language generation.
4. **Stage 4: EIEA Training (Plug-and-Play Physical Cue Tokens):** The EIEA is trained via cross-entropy loss to encode physical cues into $Z_{phy}$ for seamless integration. This stage also includes reinforcement co-training with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) using a fidelity-focused reward ($r = \lambda_{format}r_{format} + \lambda_{ans}r_{ans}$) against the Stage-3 model as a reference policy.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of XEmbodied involved comprehensive testing across 18 public benchmarks, organized into three core dimensions: 3D Understanding, Semantic Reasoning, and Embodied Affordance.

**A. SOTA Performance**
XEmbodied (SFT) generally achieved state-of-the-art or highly competitive performance across all evaluated tasks compared to open-source and proprietary models (GPT-4o, Gemini-1.5).
- **3D Understanding:** Achieved an accuracy of 55.28% and an RMSE of 9.25 on Ego3DBench. It also scored 83.83% on SURDS, 68.61% on VLADBench, and 27.76% on STRIDE-QA, demonstrating fine-grained 3D spatial cognition.
- **Semantic Reasoning:** Scored 53.18% on DriveBench, 77.01% on DriveLMM-o1, 78.55% on MapLM-v2, and 25.43% on Omnidrive, surpassing proprietary models in driving-related semantic reasoning tasks.
- **Affordance:** Reached 78.50% on Affordance-2K and 4.35% on RoboAfford, indicating strong affordance understanding for robotic manipulation.

**B. Zero-Shot Generalization Capability**
XEmbodied demonstrated robust zero-shot performance on strictly out-of-distribution (OOD) datasets, including Cosmos-R1, DriveBench, and Ego3DBench, which were excluded from training. This indicates the model's generalization capacity to unseen tasks and scenarios.

**C. Ablation Studies**
Systematic ablation experiments validated the contribution of each proposed component:
- **Impact of Data Filter Strategy:** The four-stage data curation strategy improved the weighted total average score from 71.48 to 75.68, demonstrating the effectiveness of automated data refinement.
- **Architecture of the 3D Adapter:**
 - A simple MLP for feature projection resulted in a weighted average of 69.07, indicating its insufficiency for aligning 3D priors.
 - A QFormer-based fusion achieved a weighted average of 72.52, improving semantic reasoning but underperforming on embodied affordance.
 - The proposed cross-attention mechanism achieved the best overall weighted average of 78.45, with a notable increase in Embodied Affordance performance (70.01%), attributed to its dynamic addressing capability for selectively aggregating 3D geometric information.
- **Impact of Latent Physical Cue Tokens (EIEA):**
 - A 7B AgentThink baseline, using explicit tool-augmented Chain-of-Thought (CoT), showed competitive performance (e.g., 71.35% on DriveLMM-o1) but suffered from low inference efficiency (3.17 s/sample).
 - Injecting physical cues via EIEA (alignment stage) significantly improved performance over the XEmbodied-SFT baseline (DriveLMM-o1 from 64.32% to 75.28%, DriveBench from 53.18% to 56.72%), while reducing Ego3D-Bench RMSE from 9.44 to 6.85.
 - Further GRPO optimization in EIEA stage 2 yielded the best overall results (DriveLMM-o1 77.01%, DriveBench 57.33%, Ego3D-Bench accuracy 59.53% with 6.83 RMSE) at an inference time of 0.46 s/sample, demonstrating efficiency and enhanced reasoning.
- **Curriculum Learning Strategy:** The progressive four-stage curriculum learning strategy consistently improved performance at each stage, leading to a weighted total average of 90.13%, substantially outperforming naive mixed training (68.97%). This validates that gradual learning of complex concepts prevents catastrophic forgetting and stabilizes multimodal alignment.
- **Coordination between 3DA and EIEA (Appendix E.1):** Ablations showed that EIEA's effectiveness is significantly dependent on 3DA. Without 3DA, EIEA's performance degraded (e.g., DriveLMM-o1 down to 69.01%), supporting the hypothesis that geometric representation is a prerequisite for EIEA to properly model physical information. Combining 3DA with dual EIEA stages resulted in balanced and competitive performance across benchmarks.
- **Model Size and Data Source (Appendix E.2):** Small-scale models (3B-8B) exhibited limited and unbalanced multi-task performance. The 30B Qwen3-VL model showed superior capacity, with autonomous driving (Drive) data enhancing AD-related tasks (e.g., DriveBench) and robotic interaction (Robot) data improving embodied affordance. The full Drive+Robot data setup yielded the most balanced cross-task performance.
- **Performance on Planning Task (Appendix E.3):** XEmbodied-FT achieved competitive results in open-loop planning on the nuScenes dataset, particularly in L2 Error (0.25m at 2s, 0.45m at 3s) and Collision Rate (0.09% at 2s), demonstrating its ability to handle downstream planning tasks effectively. Ablation showed ego-vehicle historical information to be critical for this performance.

**D. Qualitative Results (Appendix E.4)**
Qualitative examples across autonomous driving and robotic tasks demonstrated XEmbodied's superiority in spatial perception, scene semantic understanding, logical reasoning, and pixel-level prediction over baseline models. This includes accurate relative positioning, 3D coordinate estimation, traffic rule inference, driving efficiency reasoning, lane attribute description (though with some room for fine-grained type recognition), and precise robotic grasping/placement bounding box prediction.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

XEmbodied represents a step forward in the development of foundation models for embodied AI, addressing key limitations of existing approaches and holding significant implications for autonomous systems.

**A. Enhanced Capabilities for Embodied AI:**
- **Intrinsic Geometric Awareness:** By integrating 3D geometric representations directly into the VLM architecture via the 3DA, XEmbodied moves beyond flat-world understanding, providing a more robust foundation for spatial reasoning in dynamic 3D environments. This is crucial for tasks requiring precise metric distances, depth perception, and understanding of spatial relationships.
- **Efficient Physical Cue Integration:** The EIEA's ability to distill complex physical signals (like occupancy grids and 3D bounding boxes) into compact, actionable tokens enables the model to leverage rich environmental information without incurring high computational costs or compromising reasoning efficiency. This "plug-and-play" capability makes the model more adaptable to diverse sensing modalities.
- **Robust Generalization:** The progressive domain curriculum and reinforcement learning post-training not only enhance domain-specific understanding but also preserve general VLM capabilities and improve out-of-distribution generalization. This is vital for deploying models in real-world scenarios with unforeseen complexities.

**B. Impact on Autonomous Driving and Robotics:**
- **Scalable Data Closed-Loop Systems:** XEmbodied's design directly addresses the bottleneck of scalable, high-quality data annotation in autonomous driving's "AD 3.0" era. By offering superior spatial reasoning and traffic semantic understanding, it can facilitate more accurate scenario mining and automated annotation, converting vast amounts of raw driving data into valuable learning signals for iterative model refinement. This improves the efficiency and effectiveness of the entire development cycle for autonomous vehicles.
- **Improved Safety and Decision-Making:** Enhanced geometric and physical understanding contributes to safer and more informed decision-making in autonomous systems. For example, accurate 3D localization, traffic rule reasoning, and obstacle avoidance are foundational for reliable autonomous navigation and planning.
- **Advanced Robotic Manipulation and Navigation:** The model's strong performance in embodied affordance tasks, including fine-grained bounding box prediction for grasping and placement, suggests its utility in complex robotic manipulation scenarios. This could lead to robots that can better understand and interact with their physical environment based on natural language instructions.

**C. Future Research Directions and Limitations (as avenues for impact):**
The paper also outlines several areas for future work, indicating ongoing development and potential for further impact:
- **Long-Term Spatiotemporal Reasoning:** Currently, the model primarily processes single-frame information. Future integration of video sequence data and temporal attention mechanisms, potentially with reinforcement learning, could enable more sophisticated predictions of dynamic object trajectories and sequential robotic planning.
- **Generalization to Extreme Scenarios:** Expanding training data with synthetic and real-world complex scenarios (e.g., rare events, harsh weather, multi-agent interactions) will be crucial for improving robustness in challenging conditions.
- **Reasoning Efficiency and Cost Optimization:** Further streamlining inference through lightweight fusion modules, knowledge distillation, and implicit visual reasoning can make the model more deployable in resource-constrained environments.
- **Depth of Knowledge Integration and Chain-of-Thought Reasoning:** Incorporating structured logical reasoning, external knowledge bases, and explicit Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting can enhance the model's ability to handle complex, multi-step tasks requiring deep deductive capabilities.

In summary, XEmbodied offers a principled approach to integrating intrinsic 3D geometric awareness and efficient physical cue processing into foundation models. This work has the potential to significantly advance the capabilities of VLA models, particularly in enabling scalable, robust, and intelligent autonomous systems.
