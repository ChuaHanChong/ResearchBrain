# VBGS-SLAM: Variational Bayesian Gaussian Splatting Simultaneous Localization and Mapping

> [!summary] Summary
> VBGS-SLAM, developed by researchers at the University of California, Riverside, introduces a fully probabilistic RGB-D SLAM framework that integrates 3D Gaussian Splatting with Variational Bayesian Inference, jointly inferring camera poses and map uncertainties through closed-form updates. The system achieved state-of-the-art or competitive performance in localization (e.g., 0.33 cm ATE on Replica dataset) and 3D reconstruction quality, demonstrating improved robustness and efficiency across synthetic and real-world datasets.

## Problem

- Traditional SLAM methods relying on sparse point clouds or dense voxels are memory-intensive and can result in incomplete 3D reconstructions.
- Existing 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) based SLAM systems are sensitive to initialization, prone to catastrophic forgetting, and lack principled uncertainty modeling due to deterministic, gradient-based optimization.
- Variational Bayesian Gaussian Splatting (VBGS) offers efficient updates and uncertainty quantification for 3D scene representation but traditionally assumes known camera poses, limiting its application to real-time SLAM.

## Method

- **VBGS-SLAM** formulates a unified generative probabilistic model that tightly couples ==3D Gaussian Splatting== map refinement and camera pose tracking, treating camera pose as a latent variable.
- It derives ==closed-form variational update rules== for both splat map parameters and camera poses by linearizing the ==spatial likelihood== around the current pose estimate, enabling efficient concurrent updates.
- The framework incorporates ==keyframe selection== based on co-visibility and temporal constraints, along with ==adaptive Gaussian management== to maintain a compact, high-resolution map without excessive redundancy.

## Results

- Achieved an average Absolute Trajectory Error (ATE) of **0.33 cm** on the synthetic Replica Dataset, outperforming **SplaTAM** (0.36 cm) by **8.3%** and **Point-SLAM** (0.53 cm) by **37.7%**.
- Demonstrated superior robustness on the long-sequence AR-TABLE dataset, achieving the best average ATE of **5.94 cm** and stable tracking where other leading methods experienced failures.
- Produced high-fidelity 3D reconstructions with competitive PSNR (e.g., **37.94 dB** on Replica) and LPIPS, operating at approximately **5x** faster rendering speed than **SplaTAM** on AR-TABLE (**1.374 FPS** vs. 0.115 FPS).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating Variational Bayesian Inference with 3D Gaussian Splatting enables explicit modeling and propagation of uncertainty in both camera poses and 3D scene parameters, enhancing robustness.
> - Treating camera pose as a latent variable within a generative probabilistic model allows for joint, online inference of pose and map uncertainties, improving the coherence between localization and mapping.
> - Closed-form variational updates derived from conjugate priors and spatial likelihood linearization significantly reduce computational overhead compared to differential rendering and separate gradient-based optimization in other 3DGS pipelines.
