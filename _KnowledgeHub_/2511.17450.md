# Planning with Sketch-Guided Verification for Physics-Aware Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> SketchVerify introduces a training-free framework for physics-aware video generation, iteratively refining motion plans using lightweight video sketches and a multimodal verifier. This approach achieves substantially more physically plausible and temporally consistent video outputs while being significantly more efficient than prior iterative refinement methods.

## Problem

- Traditional image-to-video (I2V) models often struggle to produce videos with physically realistic and temporally consistent object motion, leading to unnatural artifacts.
- Single-shot planning methods for motion guidance are prone to error propagation, while iterative refinement methods that generate full videos are computationally expensive and slow, hindering their practical application.
- Ensuring adherence to fundamental physical laws (e.g., non-penetration, gravity, stable deformation) in generated videos is a critical challenge for real-world applications.

## Method

- The **SketchVerify** framework iteratively refines motion plans at test time by generating multiple candidate object trajectories from a high-level **MLLM** planner.
- Instead of full video generation, lightweight video sketches are rendered from these trajectories by compositing object masks onto a static background, significantly reducing computational cost.
- A multimodal vision-language verifier (Gemini 2.5-Flash) evaluates these sketches for semantic alignment with instructions and adherence to key physical laws, scoring and selecting the optimal plan before final video synthesis.

## Results

- SketchVerify achieves the highest instruction following (2.08) and physical law adherence (e.g., 1.00 for Newton/Gravity/Fluid) on WorldModelBench, with an overall "Sum" score of 8.71.
- It demonstrates a planning time of 4.71 minutes, an approximately 93% speed-up compared to iterative refinement methods that rely on full video generation (e.g., PhyT2V at 61.86 minutes).
- On the PhyWorldBench, SketchVerify attains the highest overall score (19.84), boosting object-event accuracy by 22% and physical accuracy by 18% relative to baseline I2V models like Wan-2.1.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling motion plan verification from full video synthesis via lightweight video sketches allows for efficient test-time iterative refinement, providing a nearly 93% speed-up over full video-based iterative methods.
> - Multimodal verification (visual input + language) is crucial for accurately assessing both semantic instruction following and fine-grained physical plausibility, outperforming language-only verifiers.
> - Iterative sampling and scoring of candidate trajectories by MLLM planners and verifiers enables the selection of dynamically coherent and physically consistent motion plans prior to expensive pixel-level diffusion.
