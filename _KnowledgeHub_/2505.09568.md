# BLIP3-o: A Family of Fully Open Unified Multimodal Models-Architecture, Training and Dataset

> [!summary] Summary
> Salesforce Research and collaborators introduce BLIP3-o, a family of unified multimodal models excelling in both image understanding and generation, by systematically investigating hybrid autoregressive-diffusion architectures. The models achieve superior performance on image understanding benchmarks, such as 83.1 on VQAv2 and 83.5 on MMBench, and demonstrate high visual quality and prompt alignment in human evaluations for image generation.

## Problem

- Historically, image understanding and image generation tasks have often been handled by separate models or pipelines.
- The underlying architectural and training principles for unifying image understanding and generation within a single multimodal model, especially using hybrid autoregressive and diffusion architectures, remained underexplored.
- A systematic understanding was needed to determine optimal design choices for unified models regarding image representations, modeling objectives, and training strategies.

## Method

- Salesforce Research and collaborators developed BLIP3-o, a family of unified multimodal models based on a ==hybrid autoregressive-diffusion architecture==.
- They systematically investigated optimal design choices, including image representations (comparing VAE vs. CLIP), modeling objectives for latent representations (Mean Squared Error vs. ==Flow Matching==), and training strategies (joint vs. sequential).
- BLIP3-o models were trained using a ==two-stage sequential== approach: initial multimodal understanding pretraining with a frozen backbone, followed by dedicated image generation training leveraging CLIP features and Flow Matching for the diffusion component.

## Results

- BLIP3-o 8B achieved superior performance across major image understanding benchmarks, including **83.1** on VQAv2, **83.5** on MMBench, and **77.5** on SEED.
- Human evaluations indicated BLIP3-o 8B's generated images were preferred over Janus Pro 7B in visual quality (**5**0.4%** wins**) and prompt alignment (**5**1.5%** wins**), with high statistical confidence.
- The instruction-tuning dataset BLIP3o-60k, curated with AI-generated prompts, yielded immediate and significant gains in prompt alignment and visual aesthetics, demonstrating rapid adaptation to AI-generated data styles.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - CLIP image features provide more compact and semantically richer representations than VAE features, leading to faster training and higher image generation quality.
> - Flow Matching is more effective than Mean Squared Error (MSE) for modeling continuous image distributions, resulting in greater sample diversity and enhanced visual quality.
> - Sequential training effectively preserves the model's image understanding capabilities while developing strong image generation ability, avoiding negative inter-task interference.
