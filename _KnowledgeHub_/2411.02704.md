# RT-Affordance: Affordances are Versatile Intermediate Representations for Robot Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> RT-Affordance introduces end-effector pose affordances as an intermediate representation to guide robot policies, enhancing manipulation precision and data efficiency for learning new tasks on novel objects and scenarios. The method achieved 68% success on novel object grasping and 70% on complex manipulation tasks, utilizing a hierarchical model trained with robot, web, and efficiently collected annotated image data.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models often struggle with precise, fine-grained manipulation, as language instructions are underspecified and goal images can be over-specified.
- Prior intermediate policy representations like trajectory sketches or keypoints lack crucial details such as end-effector orientation, limiting their effectiveness for complex manipulation tasks.
- Learning new robot tasks is resource-intensive, requiring extensive and costly robot teleoperation to collect sufficient task-specific trajectory data.

## Method

- A hierarchical model composed of an affordance-conditioned policy that executes actions based on language, observations, and an affordance plan (sequence of end-effector poses at critical task stages).
- An affordance prediction model that automatically infers the affordance plan from an initial scene image and language instruction, enabling seamless user interaction.
- A training strategy leveraging existing robot trajectory data, large-scale web datasets for general visual-linguistic knowledge, and 'cheap-to-collect' in-domain images post-hoc annotated with affordance plans to reduce teleoperation costs.

## Results

- RT-Affordance achieved **68%** success in grasping diverse unseen household objects, significantly outperforming language-conditioned RT-2 (**28%**) and goal-conditioned RT-2 (**24%**).
- On complex, unseen manipulation tasks (e.g., placing objects, articulated object manipulation), RT-Affordance demonstrated robust performance with a **70%** success rate, compared to RT-2's **3%**.
- The affordance prediction model showed good robustness to out-of-distribution factors, maintaining **77%** success for novel camera views and only a **3%** average drop for novel backgrounds, with a larger but still contained drop for novel object instances.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Affordances, defined as precise end-effector poses, offer an optimal balance between the abstractness of language and the over-specificity of goal images, providing actionable guidance for fine-grained manipulation.
> - Visually projecting affordance poses directly onto the observation image effectively integrates this spatial and orientational guidance into vision-language models, enhancing their control capabilities.
> - Combining large web datasets with a small set of cheaply-collected, manually annotated in-domain images for affordance prediction drastically improves data efficiency and generalization for learning novel manipulation tasks.
