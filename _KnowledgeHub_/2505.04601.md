# OpenVision: A Fully-Open, Cost-Effective Family of Advanced Vision Encoders for Multimodal Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> OpenVision introduces a family of fully open vision encoders that match or exceed OpenAI's CLIP performance on multimodal benchmarks while providing multiple parameter scales and resolution options, achieved through systematic analysis of training efficiency factors and progressive resolution training using open datasets and frameworks.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal systems heavily depend on proprietary vision encoders like CLIP, limiting transparency and reproducibility
- Current open-source vision encoders lag behind proprietary models in performance and parameter scale flexibility
- Known weaknesses exist in spatial understanding and object counting with current encoders

## Method

- Developed fully open vision encoders by leveraging ==enhanced CLIP training== and ==Recap-DataComp-1B dataset==
- Implemented ==three-stage progressive resolution training== with auxiliary text decoder
- Created multiple encoder variants optimized for different input resolutions and computational budgets

## Results

- OpenVision-B/16 and L/14 outperform CLIP counterparts on most multimodal benchmarks
- OpenVision-H/14 shows substantial gains in high-resolution VQA, OCR and retrieval tasks
- Successfully scales from lightweight to high-capacity models while maintaining performance
- Full transparency with released training data, code and checkpoints enables reproducibility

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality synthetic captions and auxiliary text decoder are crucial for strong multimodal performance
> - Balanced allocation across resolutions during training improves efficiency and capabilities
> - Progressive resolution training enables both computational efficiency and high performance
