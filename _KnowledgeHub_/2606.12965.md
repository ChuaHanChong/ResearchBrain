# EmbodiSteer: Steering Embodiment-Agnostic Visuomotor Policies with Joint-Space Guidance for Zero-Shot Cross-Embodiment Deployment

> [!summary] Summary
> EmbodiSteer introduces a training-free framework that enables policies trained with embodiment-agnostic Cartesian actions to execute safely on diverse physical robots by lifting the diffusion process into joint space and applying whole-body collision-aware guidance. The system improved average task success rates by 28.5% in simulation and 36.7% in real-world scenarios, while reducing collision rates by 46.1% and 90.0% respectively compared to base policies.

## Problem

- Large-scale behavior cloning policies, often using Cartesian end-effector actions for scalability, lack awareness of the robot's physical body during deployment.
- Body unawareness leads to task failures due to whole-body collisions in cluttered environments or violations of kinematic limits specific to the deployed robot.
- Existing cross-embodiment generalization methods primarily focus on end-effector behavior, neglecting whole-body constraints and requiring morphology-specific training for safety.

## Method

- **EmbodiSteer** employs a ==training-free== inference framework that lifts a pretrained Cartesian diffusion policy's denoising process into the target robot's joint space using Jacobian-based updates.
- It integrates a CBF-inspired (Control Barrier Function) whole-body collision-aware guidance mechanism, formulated as an efficient Quadratic Program (QP), into each joint-space denoising step.
- The guidance minimizes end-effector and joint-space deviation from the policy's prediction while ensuring whole-body clearance, with a scheduled constraint strength to preserve learned behavior.

## Results

- In simulation, EmbodiSteer increased average task success rates to **64.2%** (a **28.5%** gain over Cartesian-only policies) and reduced average collision occurrence rates to **11.5%** (a **46.1%** reduction).
- On physical UR5 and Franka Panda robots, it achieved 47 out of 60 successful trials (a **36.7%** increase) and reduced collisions to 4 out of 60 trials (a **90.0%** reduction) in obstacle-filled environments.
- The system operates in real-time on physical hardware at **9.61 Hz**, demonstrating its practical deployability for robot manipulation tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Embodiment-agnostic Cartesian policies can be effectively and safely translated to embodiment-aware joint-space execution at inference time without robot-specific fine-tuning.
> - Applying collision guidance directly within the diffusion denoising loop, rather than as a post-hoc correction, allows for more proactive and integrated whole-body collision avoidance.
> - Balancing task-preserving end-effector behavior with collision avoidance through a constrained optimization approach is crucial for achieving high task success rates in cluttered environments.
