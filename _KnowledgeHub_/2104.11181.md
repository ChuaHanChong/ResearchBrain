# H2O: Two Hands Manipulating Objects for First Person Interaction Recognition

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from ETH Zurich, Microsoft, and Samsung AI Center introduce H2O, the first unified, markerless 3D egocentric dataset featuring two hands manipulating objects, complete with precise 3D hand poses, 6D object poses, and interaction labels. A novel Topology-Aware Graph Convolutional Network (TA-GCN) is proposed, achieving 79.25% accuracy in egocentric interaction recognition by adaptively learning inter- and intra-dependencies between hands and objects.

## Problem

- Existing egocentric action recognition datasets lack rich 3D annotations, especially for complex two-hand object manipulation scenarios.
- Prior 3D interaction datasets primarily focus on single-hand manipulations or rely on intrusive motion capture systems, limiting realism and generalizability to markerless real-world settings.
- A unified framework was needed to simultaneously estimate 3D poses of two hands and 6D poses of manipulated objects from egocentric RGB images for comprehensive interaction understanding.

## Method

- The H2O dataset was created using a semi-automatic multi-view RGB-D annotation pipeline, providing 571,645 frames with markerless 3D ground truth for two hands, 6D object poses, and 36 interaction classes.
- A single-shot network was developed for joint 3D hand and 6D object pose estimation directly from egocentric RGB images.
- A Topology-Aware Graph Convolutional Network (TA-GCN) was introduced, which adaptively learns spatiotemporal inter- and intra-dependencies between hands and objects for robust interaction recognition.

## Results

- The H2O dataset demonstrates high annotation precision with an approximate 1 cm error for both hand and object 3D annotations, enabling sophisticated contact modeling.
- The proposed TA-GCN achieved a state-of-the-art accuracy of 79.25% for egocentric interaction recognition on the H2O dataset, outperforming strong baselines like SlowFast (77.69%) and I3D (75.21%).
- Ablation studies confirmed that combining left hand, right hand, and object poses, and explicitly modeling their interconnections through the learned graph topology, is critical for robust interaction recognition.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-fidelity 3D ground truth, especially for two-hand interactions, is crucial for advancing egocentric interaction recognition beyond the limitations of 2D analyses.
> - Explicitly modeling interdependencies between two hands and manipulated objects via an adaptive graph convolutional network significantly enhances recognition accuracy.
> - A carefully designed semi-automatic annotation pipeline can effectively generate precise 3D ground-truth for complex multi-entity interactions in egocentric views.
