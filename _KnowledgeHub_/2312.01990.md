# SARA-RT: Scaling up Robotics Transformers with Self-Adaptive Robust Attention

> [!summary] Summary
> SARA-RT scales up Robotics Transformers by introducing a self-adaptive robust attention mechanism and an "up-training" fine-tuning process. This method efficiently converts existing quadratic-attention policies into linear-attention counterparts, significantly reducing computational load for real-world deployment on physical robots while maintaining or enhancing performance, as demonstrated on RT-2 and Point Cloud Transformers.

## Problem

- Standard Transformer self-attention exhibits quadratic time and space complexity, making large Robotics Transformers (RTs) computationally prohibitive for real-time, on-robot deployment.
- Models like RT-1 operate at a slow 3Hz, which is insufficient for dynamic robotic tasks, and larger models like RT-2 (billions of parameters) exacerbate these efficiency issues.
- Existing linear attention mechanisms often sacrifice accuracy or introduce high overheads, struggling to reproduce the effective attention patterns of full softmax attention.

## Method

- **SARA-RT** introduces ==Self-Adaptive Robust Attention (SARA)==, a novel ==linear attention== mechanism featuring ==learnable pre-processing matrices== and vectors to replace fixed random features.
- A new ==up-training== process is proposed to fine-tune pre-trained ==quadratic-attention== robotic policies by replacing their standard attention modules with SARA variants.
- Theoretical analysis provides guarantees that SARA mappings can accurately approximate the original Transformer's ==attention matrix== with bounded error, using a logarithmic number of trainable parameters.

## Results

- SARA-PCT achieved a grasping success rate of **0.75**, outperforming regular PCT's **0.64**, while providing constant inference time (approximately **100ms**) regardless of point cloud size up to 5000 points.
- SARA-RT-2 (PaLI-5B backbone) maintained comparable mean accuracy (**65.1%** vs **65.8%**) to regular RT-2, achieving a **14% speedup** in forward pass time (**45.7 ms** vs **53.2 ms** on TPU).
- When combined with a vector action representation and 3-frame history, SARA-RT-2 showed a **12%+** mean accuracy improvement, reaching **76.4%** mean accuracy, and achieved **100%** accuracy on specific pick tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - While fixed random feature pre-processing can improve linear attention, learnable features within SARA more effectively mimic softmax attention patterns and performance, even with smaller feature dimensions.
> - The "up-training" strategy offers a robust and seamless method to convert high-performing, existing quadratic-attention policies into efficient linear attention counterparts.
> - SARA's adaptive learning not only maintains performance but can lead to improved accuracy and generalization, particularly when integrated with other optimizations like vector action representations or historical context.
