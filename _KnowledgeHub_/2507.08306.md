# M2-Reasoning: Empowering MLLMs with Unified General and Spatial Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Inclusion AI, Ant Group developed M2-Reasoning-7B, a Multimodal Large Language Model capable of unified general and spatial reasoning, which achieved state-of-the-art performance across 8 diverse benchmarks, including an average score of 45.0 on general reasoning and 82.3 on image-based spatial reasoning.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with understanding dynamic spatial interactions, including motion, orientation, and physical interactions.
- There is a scarcity of high-quality, logically coherent datasets specifically designed for spatial reasoning, especially dynamic interactions.
- Training MLLMs on diverse tasks like general and specialized spatial reasoning leads to conflicts due to data heterogeneity and varying reward signals.

## Method

- A ==multi-stage data construction pipeline== synthesizes high-quality general reasoning (168K CoT instances) and spatial reasoning data (26.2K RLVR instances, image- and video-based).
- A ==two-stage training framework== starts with cold-start ==Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)== and proceeds to dynamic multi-task ==Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)==.
- The RLVR stage utilizes a GRPO variant with task-specific reward formulations, including ==Exponential Decay Numeric Matching (EDNM)== for continuous spatial answers, and dynamic hyper-parameter adjustments for stable training.

## Results

- M2-Reasoning-7B achieved state-of-the-art performance across **8** diverse benchmarks, demonstrating advancements in both general and spatial reasoning.
- For general reasoning, it secured an average score of **45.0**, outperforming prior base-scale MLLMs like InternVL3-8B (**41.4**) and WeThink-VL-7B (**44.3**), with specific strengths in MathVista (**75.0**) and DynaMath (**26.8**).
- In spatial reasoning, it scored **82.3** on CV-Bench (image-based) and **42.3** on VSI-Bench (video-based), establishing new SOTA records for inferring Room Size (**55.4**) and Relative Direction (**47.3**) in video-based tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The two-stage training paradigm of Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed by Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) effectively enhances reasoning capabilities in MLLMs.
> - Explicitly synthesizing high-quality, logically coherent data, particularly for dynamic spatial interactions, is crucial for improving MLLM's spatial understanding.
> - Adaptive training strategies, including task-specific reward functions like Exponential Decay Numeric Matching (EDNM) and dynamic hyper-parameter adjustments, are necessary to unify and optimize diverse reasoning tasks.
