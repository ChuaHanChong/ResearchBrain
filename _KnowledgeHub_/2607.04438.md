# ResearchStudio-Reel: Automate the Last Mile of Research from Paper to Poster, Video, and Blog

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Microsoft Research, National University of Singapore, and Nanyang Technological University developed ResearchStudio-Reel, a system that automates the generation of native-editable research dissemination artifacts including posters, narrated videos, and bilingual blogs from a single paper PDF. It achieved the highest aesthetic scores among automated systems in poster generation benchmarks and integrates these outputs into a single interactive viewer for unified navigation.

## Problem

- Transforming finished research papers into various public-facing formats (e.g., conference posters, talk videos, blog posts) remains a manual, labor-intensive, and time-consuming process for authors.
- Existing multi-format generation systems often lack a shared content extraction process, leading to inconsistencies and redundant efforts when creating multiple artifacts from a single paper.
- The outputs from previous automated systems are frequently not natively editable in standard software (e.g., PowerPoint, Word), hindering authors' ability to easily revise generated content.

## Method

- The system utilizes a shared ==Paper2Assets== layer for a single, comprehensive extraction of text, figures, and structured summaries from a paper PDF, ensuring consistent grounding across all generated artifacts.
- It generates natively editable source files: print-ready PowerPoint posters using a ==staged-fill loop== for optimal layout, and synchronized narrated talk videos with visual attention cues in editable PPTX and MP4 formats.
- **ResearchStudio-Reel** produces bilingual (English/Chinese) blog posts as editable Word `.docx` files, and integrates all generated artifacts into an interactive HTML viewer (==Paper2Reel==) for synchronized navigation.

## Results

- ResearchStudio-Reel achieved the best aesthetic scores (**3.56** overall) among automated systems on a 100-paper poster benchmark, surpassing the average aesthetics of authors' own posters (**3.56** vs. **3.03**).
- The system demonstrated comprehensive capability coverage for video generation, including visual attention cues, editable PPTX decks, and duration control, and for bilingual blog generation with layout checks and editable DOCX output.
- The full pipeline, encompassing shared asset extraction, poster, video, and blog generation, produces all four artifacts from a single PDF in approximately **89 minutes** of summed stage runtime.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Establishing a single, centralized content extraction process ('shared grounding') is crucial for maintaining factual consistency and efficiency across diverse, multi-format dissemination artifacts.
> - Prioritizing the delivery of native-editable source files (e.g., PowerPoint for posters/videos, Word for blogs) empowers authors to revise automated outputs directly using familiar software, streamlining post-generation workflows.
> - An 'experience-level convergence' layer can effectively bind disparate research artifacts into a unified, interactive, and navigable platform, enhancing the overall review and reuse experience for readers.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The work addresses the "last mile" of research dissemination, which involves transforming a finished research paper into various public-facing formats such as conference posters, talk videos, and blog posts. Despite advancements in research automation, this stage frequently remains manual and labor-intensive for authors. The broader research landscape includes a growing body of automation literature targeting these specific dissemination artifacts:

- **Paper-to-poster systems**: Existing systems like Paper2Poster, P2P, and PosterGen focus on generating posters from scientific papers.
- **Paper-to-video pipelines**: Systems such as VideoAgent, PaperTalker, Preacher, and PresentAgent aim to automate the creation of presentation videos.
- **Paper-to-blog and long-form summarization**: Efforts like HERA, PTSPI, GoSum, and Papers-to-Posts concentrate on generating summaries or blog articles from long documents.

While much of this literature is artifact-specific, a recent trend, exemplified by PaperX and OmniPresent, involves multi-artifact presentation suites. ResearchStudio-Reel builds upon this multi-artifact approach by emphasizing native editability of generated outputs in standard tools (PowerPoint, Word) and providing an interactive convergence layer that binds these artifacts into a single navigable experience for revision and reuse. This positions the work as an advancement in integrated, editable, and experience-level coherent multi-artifact generation, moving beyond mere parallel generation of disparate formats.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation for ResearchStudio-Reel is to automate the labor-intensive process of creating dissemination artifacts from research papers, which typically occurs during a critical and time-constrained period for authors (e.g., immediately after paper acceptance). This automation aims to benefit individual authors, industrial research labs for public communications, graduate courses for briefing packs, and multilingual research organizations for cross-region outreach.

The paper identifies three specific gaps (G1-G3) in existing multi-format generation systems that motivated the design of ResearchStudio-Reel:

- **G1: Shared grounding**: Combining artifact-specific systems often leads to repeated extraction of figures, captions, and metadata. This duplication can result in inconsistencies across artifacts and requires manual reconciliation by the user. ResearchStudio-Reel aims to establish a single, shared extraction process.
- **G2: Native editability**: While multi-format suites can generate various outputs, they do not consistently guarantee that these outputs are provided as native-editable source files (e.g., PowerPoint for presentations, Word for documents) that can be easily revised by authors using their accustomed tools. The objective is to produce artifacts that authors can edit without requiring regeneration or specialized software.
- **G3: Experience-level convergence**: Existing systems may produce parallel or consistently rendered outputs, but they often lack a mechanism to bind these finished artifacts into one interactive surface. Such a surface would allow readers to navigate seamlessly between related sections of a poster, video, and blog post, facilitating comprehensive review and reuse. The goal is to provide an integrated viewing experience.

In response to these gaps, the key objectives for ResearchStudio-Reel are:
- To implement a native-editable dissemination workspace that generates a print-ready conference poster, a narrated talk video, and a bilingual blog from a single paper PDF.
- To introduce Paper2Reel, an experience-level convergence layer that connects poster regions, video segments, and blog passages within a single navigable HTML interface.
- To ensure the delivery contract includes a shared Paper2Assets bundle and native-editable source files (PowerPoint for poster/video deck, Word for blog).
- To achieve competitive quality in generated posters, videos, and blogs, demonstrated through benchmarks and capability audits, including specific features like bilingual blog generation with layout-aware repair and narration-aligned highlight transitions for videos.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

ResearchStudio-Reel is implemented as a suite of five composable skills, executable within agent-driven workflows such as Claude Code and Codex. These skills sequentially process a paper PDF to generate and integrate the various dissemination artifacts. The system leverages deterministic primitives for document and media transformations (e.g., Playwright for HTML→PDF, LibreOffice and ffmpeg for slides→video, python-docx for Word files) and Edge TTS for narration synthesis.

The five skills are:

1. **Paper2Assets (Shared Extraction Layer)**:
 - **Purpose**: Addresses G1 (Shared Grounding) by performing a single, comprehensive extraction of assets from the paper PDF.
 - **Process**: It pulls full body text, figure captions, a per-figure manifest, cleaned figure images, metadata (title, authors, institutions, venue, links), a structured nine-section summary (Problem, Motivation, Contribution, Method, Dataset/Benchmark, Key Result, Ablation, Headline Numbers, Takeaway), best-effort institution logos, and QR codes. A second pass adds an inventory manifest and a reading-order narration clip list.
 - **Figure Cleanup**: A significant stage involving a deterministic prefix to remove residue, a visual-AI step for tight bounding boxes, and an independent sub-agent verifier for re-cropping, ensuring clean and correctly split figures.
 - **Contribution**: By providing a single asset bundle with stable section IDs and figure handles, it ensures consistency and avoids redundant, error-prone re-parsing by downstream generators.

2. **Paper2Poster (Poster Generator)**:
 - **Purpose**: Creates a print-ready conference poster from the shared asset bundle.
 - **Deliverables**: Produces a self-contained web page, a print-resolution PDF, a thumbnail image, and a native-editable PowerPoint file, along with optional per-section narration audio.
 - **Design Requirements & Solutions**:
 - **(A1) Composition without a template explosion**: Achieved by assembling posters at build time from orthogonal axes (column layout, visual style, title-band header, bottom QR block) rather than fixed templates.
 - **(A2) A fill loop that converges**: Utilizes a "staged-fill loop" that starts with a lean draft and iteratively adds or trims content. Each section's fill ratio is measured, quantized into five categorical verdicts (EMPTY, SPARSE, FULL, SPILLAGE, OVERFLOW), and a specific remediation is applied. The loop terminates when all sections are "FULL" (90-98%) and figures are adequately sized. A render-time pass stretches under-filled cards to 98% for visual completeness without affecting the loop's convergence.
 - **(A3) A page too large to re-read**: The refinement loop avoids re-reading the entire poster into context; instead, it edits only the off-target sections returned by the measurement process.
 - **(A4) Figures that fill their cards**: Figures are sized through their height cap, ensuring they occupy sufficient vertical room and are not stranded as small elements by text edits.
 - **(A5) A faithful editable export**: Converts the rendered HTML page into native PowerPoint shapes (text blocks, replaceable pictures, native equation objects) directly from the DOM, allowing authors to revise in PowerPoint without re-running the generator.
 - **Quality Gates**: Mandatory checks for convergence of the fill loop, figure fill, an optional Reader-Reconstruction Preference (RRP) gate for answerability, and a deterministic canvas check for PDF dimensions.

3. **Paper2Video (Video Generator)**:
 - **Purpose**: Generates a synchronized video package from the shared Paper2Assets bundle.
 - **Deliverables**: `video.pptx` (editable deck), `video.mp4` (with burned-in subtitles), and `video_no_subtitles.mp4`. Intermediate assets like narration audio, subtitle sidecars, and timelines are also produced.
 - **Design Requirements & Solutions**:
 - **(B1) Duration must be planned before rendering**: Narration is planned and estimated for target length before Text-to-Speech (TTS), with semantic rewrites for large errors and bounded speech-rate adjustments for small errors.
 - **(B2) The viewer needs attention guidance**: Generates narration-aligned visual highlights (e.g., "spotlight_laser" effect) that emphasize specific regions (figures, equations) named by the narration.
 - **(B3) Captions have two delivery contexts**: Renders two MP4 variants: one with burned-in subtitles for sharing (`video.mp4`) and one subtitle-free (`video_no_subtitles.mp4`) for interactive viewers that provide their own caption toggles.
 - **(B4) The video has to remain addressable after export**: Creates `timeline.json` as a canonical sidecar, mapping paper sections to audio windows, subtitle cues, slide frames, and visual cues for downstream navigation.
 - **(B5) Media failures need deterministic checks**: A mandatory package gate verifies video playability, audio stream presence, duration tolerance, subtitle integrity, highlight consistency, and absence of blank/overflowing frames.

4. **Paper2Blog (Blog Generator)**:
 - **Purpose**: Produces a bilingual editorial package (English and Chinese) for human editors from the shared Paper2Assets bundle.
 - **Deliverables**: A Chinese article formatted for WeChat public accounts and an English article for research blogs, both as native Word `.docx` files.
 - **Design Requirements & Solutions**:
 - **(C1) One evidence base for two languages**: Builds a single "evidence map" from the asset bundle, recording core facts (hook, problem, method, claims, results, links, figure roles) to ensure factual consistency across languages.
 - **(C2) Register must be controlled during writing**: The two articles are drafted separately based on language-specific editorial style guides, ensuring appropriate tone (restrained public-account for Chinese, neutral research blog for English).
 - **(C3) Figures need article-level selection and placement**: Selects a small, shared set of relevant figures for both languages, placing them near explanatory text with article-specific captions.
 - **(C4) The deliverable is a Word article, not plain text**: Assembles `.docx` files with embedded images, stable fonts, and fixed filenames, ready for editing.
 - **(C5) Word layout has to be editor-ready**: Incorporates layout checks (image fit, pagination, orphan words, blank pages) by inspecting the document's internal structure and rendered page images.
 - **Quality Gates**: Verifies file existence and readability, sufficient text content, embedded images, absence of placeholder text, font declarations, factual consistency between languages, and layout integrity.

5. **Paper2Reel (Convergence and Presentation Layer)**:
 - **Purpose**: Addresses G3 (Experience-level Convergence) by creating a self-contained interactive viewer that binds the completed poster, video, and blog deliverables.
 - **Deliverables**: An interactive `reel.html` viewer page and an `alignment.json` record.
 - **Interaction Design**: The viewer is poster-first, showing the generated poster initially. Hovering a section provides feedback, and double-clicking opens a modal with the video on the left and blog content on the right. Video playback uses the subtitle-free source with viewer-controlled captions. Slide thumbnails allow seeking, and the blog pane supports language switching.
 - **Content Alignment**: The `alignment.json` sidecar is critical, mapping canonical section IDs to corresponding poster blocks, slide targets, video start/end times, subtitle tracks, slide thumbnails, and blog blocks. This ensures synchronized navigation across all artifacts.
 - **Quality Gates**: Checks for existence of viewer components, alignment record, and all copied assets. A browser-based gate verifies interactive features like poster-first loading, hover behavior, modal opening, subtitle toggling, and video seeking, also confirming direct disk opening.

The overall approach prioritizes generating native-editable artifacts, ensuring content consistency through a shared asset bundle, and enhancing the user experience through an integrated, navigable viewer, all while applying rigorous quality gates at each stage.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of ResearchStudio-Reel focuses on both quantitative poster quality and qualitative pipeline capability coverage.

#### 4.1. Poster Quality (Paper2Poster Benchmark)

- **Benchmark Setup**: The system was tested on the Paper2Poster benchmark of 100 papers. Posters were generated end-to-end and scored by two VLM judges (Claude-4.8 Opus and GPT-5.5) using a six-criterion rubric (three aesthetic, three information) on a 1-5 scale, and a PaperQuiz reading-comprehension probe (% accuracy).
- **Performance against Automated Systems**:
 - **Aesthetics**: The ResearchStudio-Reel (Claude Code configuration) achieved the best scores among automated systems on all three aesthetic sub-criteria (Elements, Engagement, Layout) and the highest Aesthetic Overall score (3.56).
 - **Information**: It achieved the best or tied-best scores on two of three information sub-criteria (Low-level, Logic) and a competitive Information Overall score (3.81).
- **Performance against Author Ground-Truth**:
 - ResearchStudio-Reel (Claude Code) exceeded the authors’ own posters in average aesthetics (3.56 vs. 3.03).
 - In per-paper comparison, it won on overall quality for 74 of 100 papers under the Claude judge and 95 of 100 papers under the GPT judge.
- **PaperQuiz**: While excelling in aesthetics, ResearchStudio-Reel scored lower on PaperQuiz (56.79%) compared to systems that prioritize content density (e.g., P2P at 75.40%).
- **Analysis of Trade-offs**: The results indicate a tension between PaperQuiz (rewarding content density for AI readability) and aesthetic criteria (favoring selective, legible layouts). Systems with higher raw text coverage tended to score higher on PaperQuiz but lower on aesthetics. ResearchStudio-Reel's measured-fill loop targets a balanced density, leading to strong aesthetic scores at the cost of some raw text coverage compared to dense, verbatim extractions.
- **Controlled Comparisons**: The paper highlights that the composition and measured-fill loop, when applied with Claude Code, substantially improved aesthetic scores (e.g., +0.64 on aesthetics, +1.0 on Layout) compared to a single-shot prompt with the same model. The measured-fill loop also transferred effectively to the Codex configuration, achieving the second-highest aesthetic score (3.27) among automated systems.

- **Paper2Video Capability**: Table 2 indicates that ResearchStudio-Reel provides a comprehensive media contract, including visual attention cues, an editable PPTX deck, duration control, and narration audio. This positions it as a robust solution compared to other video or presentation tools which often lack one or more of these features.
- **Paper2Blog Capability**: Table 3 demonstrates that ResearchStudio-Reel offers layout checks, embedded figures, editable DOCX output, and summarization capabilities. This distinguishes it from other summarization tools that might only provide plain text or lack layout awareness and editable formats.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

ResearchStudio-Reel contributes a native-editable, section-aligned workspace for research dissemination, which holds several potential impacts across different applications:

- **Streamlining Author Workflow**: For individual authors, especially during the time-sensitive camera-ready phase, the system automates the creation of print-ready posters, talk videos, and bilingual blog posts from a single PDF. The generation of native-editable source files (PowerPoint, Word) means authors can revise outputs without regeneration, potentially saving significant time and effort. The Paper2Reel viewer also offers a unified review surface.
- **Enhancing Scientific Communication at Organizational Level**: Research labs and industrial groups can integrate ResearchStudio-Reel into their publication pipelines. This enables automatic generation of draft dissemination artifacts, which editorial or communications teams can then refine. The native editability is crucial for hand-off to non-author reviewers, and the bilingual blog feature facilitates broader, cross-regional outreach. The interactive viewer simplifies the review process.
- **Facilitating Educational and Pedagogical Reuse**: Graduate courses, reading groups, or public science initiatives can use the system to generate "paper-of-the-week" briefing packs. This includes editable slide decks for lectures, talk videos for asynchronous learning, and blog pieces for course materials. The cross-linked Paper2Reel viewer allows students to navigate between different formats of a paper seamlessly, while the editable artifacts enable instructors to customize content.
- **Advancing Multi-Artifact Generation Research**: By addressing the identified gaps of shared grounding, native editability, and experience-level convergence, ResearchStudio-Reel provides a framework that goes beyond simple parallel generation. Its methodology, including the staged-fill loop for posters and comprehensive quality gates for all artifacts, demonstrates a practical approach to achieving high-quality, coherent outputs. The emphasis on testable delivery contracts and auditable intermediates sets a precedent for future development in this domain.

The work shifts the paradigm of research dissemination automation from one-way, static output generation to an interactive, editable, and navigable workflow, providing authors and organizations with greater control and flexibility over their public-facing research narratives.
