# EDGE-GRPO: Entropy-Driven GRPO with Guided Error Correction for Advantage Diversity

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Beihang University developed EDGE-GRPO, an algorithm that combines Guided Error Correction for response diversity and Entropy-Driven Advantage for signal refinement to overcome the "advantage collapse problem" in Group Relative Policy Optimization for LLMs. This approach yields over 20% performance gains on mathematical reasoning benchmarks while requiring only 1K training samples for competitive results.

## Problem

- The "advantage collapse problem" in Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) for LLMs, where sparse, rule-based reward functions lead to uniform rewards and zero advantages within sampled response groups.
- This collapse halts effective policy gradient updates, severely limiting sample efficiency and hindering performance on challenging reasoning problems.
- Existing methods for enhancing response diversity or augmenting training signals are often inconclusive, insufficient for limited capacity models, or miscalibrated with respect to response correctness.

## Method

- ==Guided Error Correction (GEC)== enhances response diversity by strategically introducing external guidance (reflection prompts, direct answer injection, or reference solution replacement) for incorrect responses within a group.
- ==Entropy-Driven Advantage (EDA)== refines advantage signals by scaling GRPO advantages with policy entropy, providing finer-grained differentiation among responses.
- The EDGE-GRPO algorithm integrates GEC and EDA to simultaneously enhance response diversity and ==advantage diversity==, fundamentally alleviating the ==advantage collapse problem==.

## Results

- EDGE-GRPO consistently achieved over **20%** performance improvement compared to baseline Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B and Qwen2.5-Math-7B models on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, with the 7B model reaching **53.21%** average accuracy on DeepScaleR-Hard-1K.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both GEC and EDA contribute to performance gains, and their combination effectively mitigates the advantage collapse problem by maintaining consistently higher intra-group advantage variance.
- The method demonstrated exceptional data efficiency, achieving competitive results using only **1K** training samples for one epoch, significantly less than other state-of-the-art open-source models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Relying solely on model reflection for self-correction is often ineffective for LLMs, especially those with limited capacity, and spontaneous reflection can even correlate with lower accuracy.
> - Policy entropy can be miscalibrated, with models showing overconfidence in incorrect answers and underconfidence in correct ones, making simple entropy-based adjustments insufficient.
> - A dual intervention strategy, addressing both response generation diversity and the granularity of advantage signals, is necessary to effectively overcome the advantage collapse problem in GRPO.
