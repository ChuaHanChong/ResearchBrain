# Towards Self-Refinement of Vision-Language Models with Triangular Consistency

> [!summary] Summary
> This research introduces a self-refinement framework for Vision-Language Models (VLMs) that utilizes a "Triangular Consistency" principle to autonomously generate, filter, and learn high-quality supervision from unlabeled images. The approach achieved consistent performance improvements, averaging 1.22% on VQA tasks and 2.1% on MMBench, across various VLM architectures and benchmarks without external supervision.

## Problem

- Acquiring high-quality supervised visual instruction data (image-question-answer triplets) is costly, time-consuming, and labor-intensive.
- Existing synthetic data generation methods often depend on expensive proprietary "teacher" VLMs or inefficient environmental feedback.
- The potential for VLMs to autonomously learn and refine themselves from readily available unlabeled images remains largely unexplored.

## Method

- A three-stage iterative self-refinement framework is proposed: enhancing instruction generation, applying ==Triangular Consistency filtering==, and then instruction tuning.
- The =="Triangular Consistency" principle== serves as an internal mechanism to validate the quality of self-generated IQA triplets by checking consistency between original and reconstructed elements.
- The VLM is fine-tuned with a ==multi-task objective (I→QA, IQ→A, IA→Q)== to improve its capability to generate diverse and precise IQA pairs from unlabeled images.

## Results

- SRF-LLaVA-1.5 consistently improved performance, achieving an average **1.22%** gain on VQA tasks and **2.1%** on MMBench, **1.7%** on MM-VET, and **3.5%** on LLaVA-Wild benchmarks.
- Triangular Consistency filtering was critical, with ablated studies showing that filtered data led to improvements while unfiltered data (or low-quality data) provided only marginal gains or degradation.
- The framework generalized across different VLM architectures (MobileVLM-1.7B, QWen2.5-VL 3B), demonstrating consistent improvements and producing significantly higher quality (**85.3%** GPT-4o accuracy) and more diverse self-generated QA pairs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision-Language Models possess an inherent capability for self-refinement, enabling them to improve autonomously without relying on external supervision or superior teacher models.
> - The Triangular Consistency principle effectively identifies and filters high-quality synthetic data, demonstrating that model-intrinsic signals can reliably guide self-improvement in multimodal contexts.
> - Causal analysis provides a theoretical justification for VLM self-refinement, suggesting that incorporating additional unlabeled images helps refine the model's empirical estimate of conditional distributions for better inference.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The rapid advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs) have paved the way for Vision-Language Models (VLMs), which integrate visual information with LLMs' analytical power. The success of state-of-the-art VLMs like LLaVA, InstructBLIP, and Qwen-VL is largely attributed to visual instruction tuning, a process that requires meticulously curated image-question-answer (IQA) triplets.

**The prevailing challenge in the VLM landscape is the scarcity and high cost of acquiring high-quality supervised visual instruction data.** Traditional methods rely heavily on human annotation, which is time-consuming, expensive, and prone to copyright issues. More recent approaches attempt to mitigate this by generating synthetic data. These typically fall into a few categories:
- **Leveraging powerful proprietary "teacher" VLMs:** Methods like ShareGPT4V utilize models such as GPT-4V or Gemini to generate captions or instructions for unlabeled images. However, this approach faces significant limitations, including high usage costs, API rate limits, and the inherent difficulty of finding increasingly "superior" teacher models as existing models improve.
- **Using LLMs as expert evaluators:** Some methods employ advanced LLMs like GPT-4 to assess the quality of generated data.
- **Deriving supervision from environmental feedback:** Researchers have also explored using downstream evaluation performance as a signal for data annotation, though this can be inefficient for model training.

**This paper addresses a critical gap by proposing a paradigm of *self-refinement* for VLMs, entirely circumventing the dependence on external supervision, human annotations, or more advanced proprietary models.** It aligns with the growing trend of "intrinsic self-refinement" seen in pure LLM research, where models improve iteratively using their own capabilities without external feedback. However, applying such concepts to VLMs introduces unique multimodal challenges that text-only methods do not face.

The work differentiates itself by:
1. **Introducing a novel `Triangular Consistency` principle:** This provides an internal, model-driven mechanism for validating the quality of self-generated multimodal data, a crucial innovation for self-supervision in VLMs.
2. **Developing a comprehensive self-refinement framework:** This structured approach enables VLMs to autonomously generate, filter, and learn from synthetic IQA triplets using only unlabeled images.
3. **Providing a theoretical foundation:** The causal analysis offers a deeper understanding of *why* VLMs can self-refine effectively, distinguishing it from purely empirical self-improvement methods.

By demonstrating that VLMs can autonomously generate high-quality supervision signals and learn from them, this research pushes the boundaries towards more scalable, cost-effective, and continually improving multimodal AI systems, lessening the reliance on external human or proprietary model expertise.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The central motivation of this research stems from the inherent difficulties and bottlenecks associated with supervised data acquisition for Vision-Language Models (VLMs). The authors clearly articulate their driving question: **"Can we refine VLMs without relying on external supervision, using only the model itself?"**

The key motivations are:
- **High Cost and Difficulty of Supervised Data Collection:** Acquiring high-quality visual instructions, such as image-question-answer (IQA) triplets, is a labor-intensive, costly, and complex process that often requires significant human involvement and can lead to copyright issues.
- **Limitations of Current Synthetic Data Approaches:** Existing methods that generate synthetic data often depend on powerful, expensive, and sometimes proprietary "teacher" VLMs (e.g., GPT-4V, Gemini). This dependence creates scalability issues due to usage limits, high financial costs, and the escalating challenge of finding truly superior teacher models as the student models themselves advance.
- **Inefficiency of Environmental Feedback:** While environmental feedback can provide supervision signals, it is often inefficient for model training compared to direct, high-quality data.
- **Unexplored Potential of Unsupervised Learning:** The potential of VLMs to learn and refine themselves autonomously from vast amounts of readily available unlabeled images remains largely unexplored.

Based on these motivations, the key objectives of this study are:
- **Validate Inherent Self-Refinement Capabilities:** To empirically demonstrate that VLMs possess an intrinsic ability to improve themselves.
- **Propose a Self-Refinement Framework:** To develop a structured, iterative framework that stimulates and harnesses these self-refinement capabilities. This framework is anchored by the novel "Triangular Consistency" principle.
- **Enable Autonomous High-Quality Data Generation:** To empower VLMs to autonomously generate high-quality supervised IQA triplets from unlabeled images, thereby facilitating self-learning without external inputs.
- **Conduct Causal Theoretical Analysis:** To investigate the fundamental mechanisms underlying VLM self-refinement from a causal perspective, offering a principled understanding of the phenomenon.
- **Demonstrate Consistent Performance Improvements:** To experimentally show that the proposed framework, using a widely recognized baseline VLM (LLaVA-1.5), can achieve consistent and deliberate performance improvements across multiple benchmarks purely through self-supervision.

Ultimately, the goal is to pave the way for autonomous, continuously improving multimodal intelligence by leveraging the abundance of unlabeled data, thus reducing reliance on costly human or external AI supervision.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The core of this research is a novel **Self-Refinement Framework** for Vision-Language Models (VLMs), built upon a **Triangular Consistency principle**. The framework operates in three iterative stages, aiming to autonomously generate high-quality supervision data from unlabeled images and use it to refine the VLM.

**Overall Framework (Iterative Process):**
The framework starts with an initial VLM ($M^{(0)}$) and a seed human-labeled dataset ($D^{(0)}$). For each refinement round $k$:
1. **Enhancing Instruction Generation:** The current VLM ($M^{(k-1)}$) is fine-tuned to become proficient at generating diverse and precise image-question-answer (IQA) triplets.
2. **Triangular Consistency Filtering:** Using the instruction-generation-enhanced VLM, a large synthetic dataset of IQA triplets is generated from unlabeled images. These triplets are then rigorously filtered based on a "Triangular Consistency" score to retain only high-quality samples.
3. **Instruction Tuning & Iteration:** The VLM is then retrained ($M^{(k)}$) on a merged dataset consisting of the original human-labeled data and the newly filtered synthetic data. This enhanced model then serves as the base for the next iteration.

**Detailed Breakdown of Each Stage:**

#### 3.1. Enhancing Instruction Generation

- **Problem Addressed:** Initial experiments showed that existing VLMs (like LLaVA-1.5) are not inherently good at generating diverse or precise IQA pairs from scratch for unlabeled images.
- **Approach:** The VLM is fine-tuned on its *existing supervised dataset* ($D^{(0)}$) with a multi-task objective to boost its instruction generation capabilities. This training creates a model ($M_g^{(0)}$) capable of not just answering but also posing questions or generating full instruction pairs.
- **Three Specific Tasks for Multi-Task Fine-tuning:**
 - **I → QA:** Given an image, the model is trained to generate a corresponding question-answer pair. This directly fosters instruction generation.
 - **IQ → A:** Given an image and a question, the model predicts the answer. This is the standard VQA task and helps validate consistency.
 - **IA → Q:** Given an image and an answer, the model predicts the corresponding question. This task also aids in validating consistency later.
- **Loss Function:** A combined cross-entropy loss ($L_{all} = L_{qa} + L_a + L_q$) is used, where each term corresponds to one of the three tasks. The input prompts for these tasks are structured using templates (e.g., "For this image, what can be the instruction and answer pair?").

#### 3.2. Triangular Consistency Filtering

- **Principle:** The core idea is that a truly robust and high-quality IQA triplet (Image $I_i$, Question $Q_i$, Answer $A_i$) should exhibit internal consistency. If any element is masked and then re-predicted by the *same model*, the re-predicted element should closely match the original.
- **Process:**
 1. The $M_g^{(0)}$ model generates initial IQA triplets ($S = \{(I_i, Q_i, A_i)\}$) for a large set of unlabeled images ($U$).
 2. For each generated triplet, two consistency checks are performed:
 - **Answer Reconstruction:** $A'_i$ is predicted from ($I_i, Q_i$).
 - **Question Reconstruction:** $Q'_i$ is predicted from ($I_i, A_i$).
 3. A **consistency score (S)** is calculated as the geometric mean of the similarity between original and reconstructed elements: $S = \sqrt{Sim(Q_i, Q'_i) \times Sim(A_i, A'_i)}$.
- **Similarity Metrics ($\text{Sim}$):** The choice of similarity metric is adapted to the data type to ensure accuracy:
 - **Single-sentence texts:** Sentence Transformer (e.g., UAE-Large-V1).
 - **Longer texts:** BERTScore (e.g., bert-base-uncased).
 - **Multiple-choice/True-False:** Direct exact match (score 1 if identical, 0 otherwise).
 - **Region descriptions/localization:** Intersection over Union (IoU) for bounding box coordinates, combined with Sentence Transformer for text descriptions.
- **Filtering:** Only the top 20% of generated IQA triplets (ranked by their consistency score) for each data type are selected to form the high-quality synthetic dataset ($D_{filtered}$).

#### 3.3. Instruction Tuning & Iteration

- **Training Data:** The $D_{filtered}$ dataset is merged with the original human-labeled seed dataset ($D^{(0)}$) (e.g., LLaVA-1.5's llava_v1_5_mix665k).
- **Model Refinement:** The VLM ($M^{(0)}$) is then fine-tuned on this combined dataset to obtain the self-refined model ($M^{(1)}$). The training strategy mirrors typical visual instruction tuning, focusing on the cross-entropy loss (Equation 2).
- **Iterative Nature:** This $M^{(1)}$ model can then be used as the new base model for subsequent rounds of self-refinement with new unlabeled data, enabling continuous improvement.

**Theoretical Analysis (Causal Perspective):**
The paper provides a theoretical grounding for self-refinement using causal models. It conceptualizes the "semantic concept" (S) as an auxiliary variable. Instead of S being a common cause for both language (X) and image (Y), it posits an *asymmetric relationship*:
- The semantic concept (S) acts as a **selection mechanism** for language (X), meaning language elements are chosen based on the desired semantic concept ($X \rightarrow S$).
- The semantic concept (S) acts as a **(latent) common cause** for the image (Y), affecting its visual characteristics ($S \rightarrow Y$).
Under standard causal assumptions like Additive Noise Models (ANM) and Independence of Mechanism, the authors argue that the causal model $P(Y|X)$ (image generation from text) is stable. By incorporating additional unlabeled images ($Y_{new}$), the model can refine its empirical estimate of $P(Y)$. Through Bayes' rule ($P(X|Y) = \frac{P(Y|X)P(X)}{P(Y)}$), improving the estimate of $P(Y)$ (and also $P(Y|X)$ from paired data) consequently improves the target inference $P(X|Y)$, which is what VLMs primarily aim to do. This causal lens explains *why* unlabeled images provide valuable signal for refining VLMs.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments conducted validate the effectiveness and generalizability of the proposed Self-Refinement Framework (SRF-LLaVA-1.5) across various Vision-Language Model architectures and benchmarks.

- **Consistent Performance Improvements:** SRF-LLaVA-1.5 (using LLaVA-1.5 7B as baseline) consistently achieved modest, yet deliberate, improvements across multiple vision-language benchmarks without any external supervision.
 - On traditional VQA tasks (VQAv2, GQA), an average improvement of **1.22%** was observed.
 - For visual perception and reasoning tasks (MMBench, MM-VET, LLaVA-Wild), improvements of **2.1%, 1.7%, and 3.5%** respectively, were noted.
 - When applied to the LLaVA-1.5 13B model, SRF-LLaVA-1.5 13B exceeded the baseline in **all 8 benchmarks**, demonstrating effectiveness at larger parameter scales.

- **Validation of Triangular Consistency Principle:**
 - A strong baseline, Recap-LLaVA-1.5, which used the same 1 million unlabeled images for self-annotation *without* the Triangular Consistency filtering, showed only marginal gains or even slight declines in some benchmarks. This direct comparison highlights that the performance improvements of SRF-LLaVA-1.5 are primarily due to the **Triangular Consistency filtering mechanism** rather than merely training on more data.
 - Ablation studies directly demonstrated that training on the "bottom 20%" of synthetic data (i.e., data with low consistency scores) led to performance degradation compared to the default "top 20%", further validating the filtering principle.
 - Quantitative and qualitative evaluations using GPT-4o and human studies confirmed that QA pairs retained after Triangular Consistency filtering were significantly higher quality (85.3% GPT-4o accuracy vs. 59.9% for excluded, 89.0% human preference vs. 83.0% for excluded).

- **Generalizability Across VLMs:** The framework successfully generalized to different scales and architectures of VLMs, showing consistent improvements for:
 - MobileVLM-1.7B: improved GQA by 2.0%, SQA-I by 2.5%, and MMB by 2.9%.
 - QWen2.5-VL 3B: improved MMMU by 1.9%, MathVista by 2.4%, and MMStar by 0.7%.
 This indicates that the self-refinement capability is a fundamental aspect that can be leveraged across diverse VLM designs.

- **Quality of Self-Generated Instructions:** The SRF framework produced significantly better QA pairs in terms of both accuracy and diversity compared to standard LLaVA-1.5 (even with in-context learning prompts). Specifically, SRF-LLaVA-1.5 achieved 85.3% GPT-4o accuracy, 0.1144 Type-Token Ratio (TTR), and 0.4790 Distinct-2, outperforming LLaVA-1.5's 73.9% accuracy, 0.0888 TTR, and 0.3216 Distinct-2, respectively.

- **Effect of Multiple Iterations:** A second round of self-refinement led to further improvements, particularly on MM-Vet and LLaVA-Bench. While the gains were smaller than the first round, it confirms the iterative nature of the framework and suggests that additional signal can be extracted, though with diminishing returns after exhausting the available unlabeled image distribution.

- **Optimal Filtering Threshold:** The top 20% consistency score threshold for filtering yielded the best overall performance, demonstrating a balance between data quantity and quality is crucial. Even without any filtering (top 100%), some performance gain was observed, indicating that VLMs can learn from lower-quality synthetic data, but the proposed filtering significantly boosts efficiency.

- **Impact of Different Instruction Types:** Ablation studies on specific synthetic data types revealed that captioning tasks contributed the most to overall performance enhancement, followed closely by visual chat data.

- **Qualitative Improvements:** Examples showcased improved world knowledge (e.g., accurately describing the ending of the movie Titanic) and enhanced generalization ability, leading to more detailed and accurate image descriptions without hallucinations, compared to the LLaVA-1.5 baseline.

- **Computational Cost:** The self-refinement procedure adds approximately 40 GPU-hours to the training process (about 0.7 times the baseline training duration), which is a reasonable trade-off for the observed accuracy gains given the high costs of human annotation or proprietary APIs.

- **Theoretical Justification:** An empirical experiment on synthetic data following the proposed causal generative model supported the theoretical premise that additional unlabeled observations of the effect variable (image) help estimate the conditional distribution ($P(X|Y)$).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The work "Towards Self-Refinement of Vision-Language Models with Triangular Consistency" presents a significant step forward in the development of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with far-reaching implications for the broader AI research landscape and its applications.

**1. Enabling Autonomous and Continual Learning:** The most profound impact is demonstrating the inherent self-refinement capabilities of VLMs. This validates that models can autonomously generate and leverage high-quality supervision from unlabeled data. This paradigm shift moves away from static, supervised training towards a more dynamic, self-improving learning loop, paving the way for truly autonomous and continually evolving multimodal AI systems that can adapt and grow with new, unannotated data.

**2. Reducing Data Dependency and Costs:** The framework drastically reduces the reliance on expensive, labor-intensive human annotations and costly, often proprietary, "teacher" VLMs (like GPT-4V or Gemini). This has immense practical significance, making high-performance VLM development more accessible and sustainable, particularly for researchers and organizations with limited resources. It democratizes access to advanced multimodal AI capabilities.

**3. Enhanced Scalability and Adaptability:** By leveraging the vast and ever-growing pool of unlabeled images available online, VLMs can continuously integrate real-world visual information from diverse distributions. This enables models to adapt to new visual concepts, styles, and contexts without requiring specific retraining or human intervention for new domains, thus improving their robustness and generalization abilities in open-world scenarios.

#### 5.1. Novel Methodological Contributions

 - **Triangular Consistency Principle:** This innovative principle offers a robust, model-intrinsic method for filtering self-generated synthetic data, ensuring the quality and reliability of supervision signals without external labels. It provides a blueprint for generating trustworthy self-supervisory signals in complex multimodal spaces.
 - **Multi-task Instruction Generation:** The explicit fine-tuning of VLMs for instruction generation is a crucial step that enhances the quality and diversity of synthetic data, overcoming a common limitation of simpler self-annotation approaches.

**5. Theoretical Grounding for Self-Improvement:** The causal analysis provides a principled, theoretical explanation for *why* VLMs can self-refine effectively. This deepens our fundamental understanding of VLM learning mechanisms and could inspire further theoretical work on self-supervised and lifelong learning in multimodal AI.

**6. Fostering Open Research:** The commitment to publicly releasing the dataset of 2 million images accompanied by generated instructions is a valuable contribution that will accelerate research in self-supervised VLM learning and facilitate comparative studies.

**Potential Societal Impact and Acknowledged Limitations:**
- **Positive:** The framework holds the promise of democratizing AI by lowering barriers to entry for developing and deploying high-performance VLMs, especially in low-resource settings where human annotation is prohibitive.
- **Negative/Risks:** The authors candidly acknowledge potential risks. Learning solely from model-generated synthetic data risks reinforcing or amplifying existing biases and hallucinations present in the pretrained model. The absence of human-in-the-loop oversight can make it harder to trace, audit, and correct unintended behaviors, posing challenges for ethical AI development.
- **Technical Limitations:** The model's improvement may diminish when newly introduced images closely align with the distribution of its existing training data, suggesting that maximum benefit comes from exposing the model to novel visual distributions.

In conclusion, this paper presents a compelling and well-supported argument for the viability and efficacy of VLM self-refinement. By offering a practical framework, a novel consistency principle, and a theoretical explanation, it significantly advances the field towards more autonomous, efficient, and scalable multimodal AI.
