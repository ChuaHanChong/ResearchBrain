# MultiWorld: Scalable Multi-Agent Multi-View Video World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> MultiWorld presents a framework for scalable multi-agent, multi-view video world modeling, integrating a Multi-Agent Condition Module (MACM) and a Global State Encoder (GSE). The model demonstrates improved action controllability, multi-view consistency, and scalability across varying numbers of agents and camera views, outperforming baselines on multi-player game and multi-robot manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Existing video world models predominantly focus on single-agent environments, limiting their applicability to complex real-world multi-agent scenarios.
- Current models struggle with multi-agent controllability, including distinguishing agent identities, managing diverse action strengths, and maintaining visual and geometric consistency across multiple camera views.
- Prior attempts at multi-agent world modeling often assume a fixed number of agents or predefined camera views, restricting scalability and generalizability.

## Method

- **MultiWorld** introduces a unified framework built on an ==action-conditioned diffusion== backbone, leveraging ==Flow Matching== with a Transformer architecture.
- A ==Multi-Agent Condition Module (MACM)== incorporates ==Agent Identity Embedding (AIE)== using RoPE for identity distinction and ==Adaptive Action Weighting (AAW)== to prioritize influential agent actions.
- A ==Global State Encoder (GSE)== utilizes a pretrained 3D reconstruction model (**VGGT**) to extract a shared, 3D-aware global environmental state, ensuring multi-view consistency and enabling parallel generation of views.

## Results

- MultiWorld consistently outperformed baselines on multi-player video-game and multi-robot manipulation datasets, achieving lower FVD (**179** vs. 207-245) and RPE (**0.67** vs. 0.72-0.75) while maintaining high action-following ability.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the MACM significantly improved action-following ability and FVD, and the GSE further enhanced multi-view consistency (RPE reduced from **0.76** to **0.67**) and overall video quality.
- Qualitative results demonstrated MultiWorld's ability to generate physically consistent videos across viewpoints, accurately simulate complex multi-agent interactions (e.g., pushing a board), and support stable long-horizon autoregressive generation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing multi-agent and multi-view challenges allows for a framework that flexibly scales with variable numbers of agents and camera views without requiring architectural modifications.
> - Explicitly embedding agent identities and adaptively weighting individual actions within a shared action space enhances the model's ability to control specific agents and respond to their relative influence.
> - Extracting a unified 3D-aware global environmental state from multiple observations is crucial for maintaining visual and geometric consistency across different camera perspectives during video generation.
