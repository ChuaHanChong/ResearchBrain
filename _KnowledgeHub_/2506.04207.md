# Advancing Multimodal Reasoning: From Optimized Cold Start to Staged Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Chinese researchers develop ReVisual-R1, a 7B multimodal reasoning model that achieves state-of-the-art performance among open-source MLLMs through a three-stage Staged Reinforcement Optimization (SRO) framework combining text-centric cold start initialization, multimodal reinforcement learning with novel Prioritized Advantage Distillation (PAD) to address gradient stagnation in GRPO, and textual RL refinement to prevent capability decay, achieving 53.1% average across 10 challenging benchmarks (+16.8 points over previous open-source SOTA) and outperforming GPT-4o on several tasks including 89.2% on MATH500 and substantial gains on AIME mathematical reasoning benchmarks, supported by the carefully curated GRAMMAR dataset containing 99K samples designed for complex multimodal reasoning development.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal large language models struggle with complex reasoning despite successful reinforcement learning applications in text-only models
- Standard Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) suffers from gradient stagnation in multimodal settings with sparse rewards
- Conventional multimodal pre-training provides inadequate cold start initialization for sophisticated reasoning tasks
- Intensive multimodal RL training leads to textual capability decay, degrading abstract reasoning abilities

## Method

- Develop ==three-stage Staged Reinforcement Optimization (SRO)== framework starting with high-difficulty text-centric cold start using 40K pure text reasoning samples
- Introduce ==Prioritized Advantage Distillation (PAD)== that filters effective samples based on advantage ranges and prioritizes high-value sequences to prevent gradient stagnation
- Apply textual RL refinement phase with frozen vision tower to restore and enhance linguistic reasoning while preserving multimodal gains
- Create ==GRAMMAR dataset== with 99K diverse samples through multi-stage curation including difficulty classification and topic balancing

## Results

- Achieves **53.1%** average performance across **10** benchmarks, **+16.8 points** improvement over previous open-source **7B** SOTA
- Outperforms GPT-4o on multiple tasks including MATH500 (**89.2%** vs **74.6%**) and shows substantial leads on AIME24/AIME25 mathematical reasoning
- Secures top position among open-source models on **9 out of 10** individual benchmarks including MathVerse (**+5.4%**), MathVision (**+13.9%**), and LogicVista (**+9.6%**)
- Ablation studies confirm effectiveness of three-stage curriculum and PAD's role in improving training stability with higher reward accuracy and faster convergence

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Text-centric cold start outperforms traditional multimodal initialization for complex reasoning, establishing foundational reasoning templates before visual grounding
> - Gradient stagnation in GRPO occurs when uniform rewards across response groups yield near-zero advantages, which PAD addresses through selective sampling
> - Sequential training (textual → multimodal → textual RL) proves more effective than alternative orderings for balancing visual grounding with abstract reasoning
> - Efficient-length reward function prevents uncontrolled response verbosity while maintaining reasoning depth and training stability
