# A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering in Large Language Models: Techniques and Applications

> [!summary] Summary
> A systematic survey organizes 41 prompt engineering techniques across 12 application domains for Large Language Models and Vision-Language Models, providing a structured overview of methodologies, applications, and their respective strengths and limitations. This work offers a consolidated resource for navigating the diverse landscape of prompt engineering, aiming to clarify the state-of-the-art and identify open challenges.

## Problem

- The rapid, unorganized proliferation of prompt engineering techniques created a fragmented understanding of the field.
- Researchers and practitioners found it challenging to navigate the myriad of prompting methods and identify suitable approaches for specific problems.
- Traditional fine-tuning of Large Language Models is often computationally expensive, time-consuming, and requires large amounts of task-specific labeled data.

## Method

- A ==systematic review== approach was employed to meticulously analyze a broad spectrum of ==prompt engineering== techniques.
- Techniques were organized and categorized by their primary application areas, such as ==reasoning and logic==, ==reducing hallucination==, and ==code generation==.
- Detailed summaries for each prompting technique were provided, including methodology, applications, models involved, datasets utilized, and an assessment of strengths and limitations.

## Results

- A comprehensive taxonomy was created, categorizing 41 distinct prompt engineering techniques across 12 major application domains.
- Detailed summaries for each technique were compiled, outlining their specific methodology, applications, involved models, utilized datasets, and critical strengths and limitations.
- A practical reference table was generated, summarizing crucial information for quick comparison of techniques based on application area, prompt acquisition, and other characteristics.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Prompt engineering offers an efficient alternative to traditional fine-tuning for adapting Large Language Models (LLMs) to diverse tasks without altering their core parameters.
> - Different prompt engineering techniques demonstrate varying levels of effectiveness across a range of application areas, including enhancing reasoning, reducing hallucination, and facilitating code generation.
> - A structured understanding of the fragmented landscape of prompt engineering techniques is essential for effective LLM deployment and guiding future research.
