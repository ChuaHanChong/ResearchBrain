# Cybo-Waiter: A Physical Agentic Framework for Humanoid Whole-Body Locomotion-Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Cybo-Waiter introduces a physical agentic framework for humanoid robots to reliably execute complex, natural language-instructed tasks involving whole-body locomotion and manipulation in real-world environments. The system transforms high-level VLM plans into structured, verifiable programs with multi-object 3D geometric supervision and targeted recovery, leading to improved success rates on established multi-step and new long-horizon tasks on a Unitree G1 humanoid.

## Problem

- Robot agents, especially those leveraging VLMs/LLMs, exhibit brittleness in real-world scenarios due to underspecified plans, noisy perception, and implicit success detection.
- Humanoid robots present unique challenges with tightly coupled locomotion and manipulation, where minor errors can cascade into significant failures impacting balance and reachability.
- Existing humanoid systems often lack explicit verification and fine-grained diagnostics for execution, making failure attribution and effective recovery difficult.

## Method

- A training-light, hierarchical agent system that translates open-ended VLM plans into structured JSON subtasks, each with explicit predicate-based preconditions and success conditions.
- Multi-object 3D geometric grounding using RGB-D observations and SAM3 to robustly estimate object states, enhanced by temporal consistency filtering to mitigate perception noise.
- A geometry-grounded supervisor module that continuously monitors task progress, provides condition-level diagnostics, and triggers feedback-driven targeted recovery actions (e.g., re-observation, skill adaptation, re-grounding).

## Results

- Achieved comparable or improved success rates on Being-0-aligned multi-step manipulation tasks, including 10/10 for 'Grasp-bottle' and 9/10 for 'Place-basket', surpassing Being-0's 8/10 and 6/10 respectively.
- Outperformed an ablation (W/O Supervisor) on challenging long-horizon tasks (e.g., 'Tidy-desk' 7/10 vs. 5/10, 'Bring-me-a-drink' 9/10 vs. 7/10), demonstrating the supervisor's critical role.
- The framework demonstrated robust whole-body locomotion and manipulation on a Unitree G1 humanoid robot in cluttered indoor settings, coordinating gait-conditioned RL for locomotion with MPC for manipulation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly defining task preconditions and success conditions as verifiable predicates with temporal stability is crucial for robust robot execution in dynamic and noisy real-world environments.
> - Structured 3D geometric grounding of task-relevant entities, combined with object-centric representations, enables precise and verifiable state monitoring for complex loco-manipulation tasks.
> - Feedback-driven replanning, utilizing condition-level diagnostics, allows for targeted recovery actions that enhance system resilience by avoiding full task restarts and addressing specific failure modes.
