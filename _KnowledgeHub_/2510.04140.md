# Selective Expert Guidance for Effective and Diverse Exploration in Reinforcement Learning of LLMs

> [!summary] Summary
> The MENTOR framework introduces selective expert guidance into Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) for Large Language Models, improving exploration by focusing expert input at critical decision points to balance effectiveness and diversity. It achieves average performance increases of 3.2% to 4.3% across various LLM backbones on reasoning tasks, while mitigating entropy collapse and enhancing reasoning diversity by 9.2% in pass@k metrics.

## Problem

- Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) for Large Language Models (LLMs) often struggles with insufficient high-quality exploration, particularly for models with fewer parameters.
- This limitation involves a lack of effectiveness in discovering correct reasoning trajectories for challenging tasks, leading to stalled policy improvements due to near-zero advantages.
- Existing expert imitation methods, while improving effectiveness, restrict exploration diversity by forcing adherence to full expert trajectories, potentially leading to entropy collapse and suboptimal convergence.

## Method

- The **MENTOR** (==Mixed-policy Expert== Navigation for Token-level Optimization of Reasoning) framework provides selective expert guidance, applying expert knowledge only at high-entropy, critical decision points within a reasoning trajectory.
- Mixed-policy Rollout generates trajectories by dynamically interpolating between the model's own policy and an expert policy, with expert influence weighted by the token-level entropy of the model's distribution.
- Mixed-policy GRPO integrates these selectively guided trajectories into the policy update via a modified GRPO objective, where mixed-policy advantages are positive-only and gradually annealed to encourage self-driven exploration.

## Results

- MENTOR consistently improved average performance over on-policy RL by **3.2%** for LLaMA3.1-**8B**, **4.3%** for Qwen2.5-**3B**, and **3.9%** for Qwen2.5-**7B** across mathematical and general reasoning benchmarks.
- The framework mitigated entropy collapse during training, maintaining greater exploration diversity and allowing the policy to converge to a broader, more robust set of optimal trajectories.
- A significant average gain of **9.2%** in pass@32 performance on AIME and AMC benchmarks demonstrated enhanced reasoning diversity, while models selectively absorbed valuable expert reasoning patterns (e.g., 'verify') rather than superficial imitation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High-quality exploration in RLVR necessitates a balance between finding correct solutions (effectiveness) and maintaining diverse reasoning paths (diversity) to prevent premature convergence and entropy collapse.
> - Expert guidance is most impactful when strategically applied to critical decision points (high-entropy tokens) rather than enforcing full trajectory imitation, which can overconstrain the model's exploration space.
> - Dynamically adjusting expert influence based on the model's uncertainty at each token step allows for effective leveraging of expert knowledge while preserving the model's capacity for autonomous and diverse exploration.
