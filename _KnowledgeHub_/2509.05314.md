# ManipDreamer3D : Synthesizing Plausible Robotic Manipulation Video with Occupancy-aware 3D Trajectory

> [!summary] Summary
> ManipDreamer3D presents a framework for generating realistic robotic manipulation videos with physically plausible actions by integrating 3D scene understanding and optimal 3D trajectory planning. The approach yields synthetic data suitable for training robot policies, exhibiting improved video quality and precise adherence to planned robot and object trajectories.

## Problem

- The scarcity of high-quality, diverse training data for robotic manipulation policies, as real-world data collection is time-consuming and expensive.
- Existing generative video models for robotics often lack 3D spatial awareness, leading to physically implausible trajectories, collisions, or inefficient actions in synthesized videos.
- Many current methods for synthetic data generation require significant manual intervention, such as manual object selection or explicit guidance, limiting their scalability and autonomy.

## Method

- Reconstructs a 64x64x64 3D occupancy map of the static scene from a single input image, leveraging neural surface reconstruction for continuous representation.
- Plans locally optimal and physically plausible 3D manipulation paths using a three-stage process involving the A* algorithm and CHOMP-inspired gradient-descent optimization for collision avoidance and smoothness.
- Employs a parameter-free trajectory-guided video synthesis scheme that projects 3D robot and object trajectories into 2D mask maps, then dynamically edits the latent space of a diffusion model to control video generation.

## Results

- Achieved state-of-the-art performance in video quality, with a Fréchet Video Distance (FVD) of 93.98 (DiT version) and superior PSNR/SSIM metrics compared to existing generative models.
- Demonstrated significantly reduced trajectory errors for both the robot end-effector (15.38) and the manipulated object (16.59) using the DiT version, indicating precise adherence to planned 3D paths.
- Successfully generated videos showcasing fine-grained manipulation control, including adherence to specific object 'affordance areas' and ensuring physically safe, collision-free robot movements after trajectory optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating explicit 3D scene understanding via occupancy maps and optimal 3D trajectory planning is critical for generating physically plausible and collision-free robotic manipulation videos.
> - Optimizing A*-generated trajectories with objectives for collision avoidance, path length, and smoothness significantly enhances the safety and realism of simulated robot movements.
> - A latent-editing strategy that dynamically overlays 3D-projected object and end-effector information onto the initial latent provides precise, fine-grained control over diffusion-based video generation without modifying the model architecture.
