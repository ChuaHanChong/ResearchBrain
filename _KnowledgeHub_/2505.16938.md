# InternAgent: When Agent Becomes the Scientist -- Building Closed-Loop System from Hypothesis to Verification

> [!summary] Summary
> The InternAgent framework establishes a unified closed-loop multi-agent system for autonomous scientific research, automating the entire cycle from hypothesis generation to experimental validation. It achieves substantial performance gains and cost-efficiency across 12 varied scientific and AI tasks, including handling complex repository-level codes.

## Problem

- Autonomous systems struggle to generate effective and novel research proposals that balance creativity with scientific rigor.
- A major hurdle in ASR is the seamless, closed-loop integration of experiment design, execution, result analysis, and iterative hypothesis refinement across diverse domains.
- Prior auto-research systems were largely limited to single-file experiments, unable to handle repository-level code with complex multi-file interdependencies.

## Method

- A ==unified closed-loop multi-agent framework== is employed, featuring specialized agents for self-evolving idea generation with human-interactive feedback (Survey, Code Review, Idea Innovation, Assessment, Orchestration Agents).
- A comprehensive ==idea-to-methodology construction process== systematically bridges abstract research ideas to concrete, implementable methodologies (Methodology Development Agent).
- An ==evolutionary experimental planning and execution capability== utilizes an Exception-Guided Debugging Framework and Adaptive Evolution for converting theoretical concepts into functional, iteratively refined code.

## Results

- InternAgent consistently improved performance over baselines and existing auto-research systems across all **12** diverse scientific and AI tasks, achieving a maximum R² of **35.4%** in Reaction Yield Prediction and **95.5%** Overall Accuracy in 3D Point Cloud Classification.
- It demonstrated cost-efficiency, with idea generation averaging around $0.6 per idea and complex repository-level debugging approximately $1.2 per run, making it significantly more economical than some prior systems (e.g., one-sixth the cost of AI-Researcher for AutoRYP).
- Human evaluators (Ph.D.s or candidates) consistently rated InternAgent-generated ideas higher than those from AI-Scientist-V2 in terms of soundness, contribution, and overall quality, confirming its ability to produce effective and novel research concepts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A multi-agent architecture with specialized, collaborating roles can effectively automate the complex, iterative nature of scientific discovery, from literature review to experimental refinement.
> - Integrating human-interactive feedback and self-evolving idea generation mechanisms is crucial for fostering novelty and scientific validity in AI-generated hypotheses.
> - The ability to support repository-level code and an adaptive experimental evolution process is vital for enabling AI to tackle complex, real-world scientific problems beyond simple, single-file tasks.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work is positioned at the forefront of **Autonomous Scientific Research (ASR)** and **Autonomous Scientific Discovery (ASD)**, a rapidly evolving field that seeks to leverage Large Language Models (LLMs) and advanced AI systems to automate the entire scientific research pipeline. The overarching goal is to accelerate the pace of discovery by empowering AI to perform tasks traditionally requiring extensive human intervention, such as data analysis, hypothesis generation, experiment design, and result interpretation.

Prior to InternAgent, several notable systems have contributed to this landscape:
- **LLM-based Idea and Hypothesis Generation:** Early efforts, as referenced, demonstrated LLMs' capability in generating research ideas and formulating hypotheses from scientific literature or web data (e.g., Li et al., 2024b; Wang et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024; Qi et al., 2023; 2024). However, many of these systems often stopped at the idea generation stage, lacking robust mechanisms for empirical validation.
- **End-to-End Research Automation:** More comprehensive frameworks like **AI Scientist** (Lu et al., 2024) and its successor **AI Scientist-V2** (Yamada et al., 2025) pioneered fully automated pipelines in machine learning, integrating problem definition, experiment execution, and reporting. **AI-Researcher** (HKU Data Intelligence Lab, 2025) and **Dolphin** (Yuan et al., 2025) further proposed closed-loop, LLM-driven frameworks, extending automation to various "simple tasks."
- **Human-AI Collaboration:** Systems such as **Agent Laboratory** (Schmidgall et al., 2025) and **AgentRxiv** (Schmidgall & Moor, 2025), and **AI Co-Scientist** (Gottweis et al., 2025), emphasized integrating human feedback or facilitating collaboration among AI agents, aiming to enhance research quality and collective innovation.

InternAgent distinguishes itself from these predecessors and contributes significantly to the broader landscape in several key ways:
- **Unified Closed-Loop Framework:** It presents a truly unified and end-to-end closed-loop multi-agent framework that covers the entire research cycle from "hypothesis generation to experimental validation," thereby addressing the common limitation of prior systems that often lacked systematic empirical validation.
- **Scalability and Diversity:** Unlike many existing systems often evaluated on relatively simple tasks or narrow domains, InternAgent demonstrates remarkable versatility across an unprecedented **12 diverse scientific research tasks**, spanning AI fields (e.g., image classification, semantic segmentation, autonomous driving, VLM fine-tuning) and core scientific domains (e.g., reaction yield prediction, molecular dynamics, power flow estimation, enhancer activity prediction).
- **Repo-Level Support:** A critical advancement is its ability to handle **repository-level codes**, consisting of multiple files with complex interdependencies. This overcomes a major limitation of systems like Dolphin, which primarily supported single-file experiments, significantly expanding the complexity of tasks InternAgent can automate.
- **Enhanced Interactivity and Self-Evolution:** InternAgent integrates robust human-interactive feedback and a self-evolving idea generation mechanism, allowing for continuous refinement of ideas and methodologies based on expert input and iterative evolution paths.
- **Empirical Validation and Efficiency:** The work provides extensive quantitative results, demonstrating significant performance gains with considerably less time cost compared to human efforts, coupled with detailed cost analyses.

In essence, InternAgent represents a significant step towards more sophisticated and generalized autonomous scientific research, pushing the boundaries of what AI agents can achieve in open-ended scientific discovery by integrating complex research processes into a cohesive, interactive, and scalable framework.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of InternAgent is to introduce a **unified closed-loop multi-agent framework** capable of conducting **Autonomous Scientific Research (ASR)** across various scientific research fields. The core motivation stems from the recognition that while Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly transforming scientific paradigms, ASR still faces significant challenges in fully automating the discovery process.

The paper highlights two key challenges that InternAgent aims to address:

1. **Generating Effective and Novel Proposals:** Autonomous systems struggle to identify genuine research gaps and formulate hypotheses that are not only innovative but also scientifically valid. This demands a delicate balance between creativity and scientific rigor, often constrained by the quality and scope of training data available to AI models. InternAgent seeks to overcome this by fostering "self-evolving idea generation."
2. **Implementing Closed-Loop Feedback for End-to-End Experimental Validation:** A major hurdle is the seamless integration of experiment design, execution, result analysis, and iterative hypothesis refinement. This requires robust coordination across diverse domains, adaptability to real-world uncertainties, and the ability to learn effectively from experimental outcomes. InternAgent aims to build a truly "closed-loop system from hypothesis to verification."

Driven by these challenges, InternAgent's motivation is to enable researchers to tackle complicated problems with unprecedented speed and precision, thereby accelerating the pace of scientific discovery and driving innovation across diverse domains. The framework is designed to empower researchers by reducing reliance on time-consuming manual efforts and cognitive biases inherent in traditional research.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

InternAgent is structured as a unified closed-loop multi-agent framework comprising three primary capabilities, each realized through the collaboration of specialized agents:

#### 3.1. Self-Evolving Idea Generation with Human-interactive Feedback

This capability is central to autonomously generating and refining innovative research ideas.
- **Survey Agent:** This agent adaptively searches for scientific papers based on user requirements. It operates in two modes:
 - **Literature Review Mode:** Deconstructs research tasks into keyword combinations, broadly searching academic databases, and evaluating document relevance based on abstracts.
 - **Deep Research Mode:** Downloads and thoroughly examines full texts of relevant papers to generate new, refined keyword combinations for further exploration.
- **Code Review Agent:** Crucial for understanding baseline codes, it analyzes code structures, dependencies, and functionalities at both repository and file levels. It uses static code analysis (`ast` module) and LLMs to generate human-readable descriptions, enabling comprehension of existing codebases for potential enhancements.
- **Idea Innovation Agent:** This agent automates the generation and evolution of ideas.
 - **Idea Generation:** Utilizes a general LLM with a higher temperature setting to produce diverse and creative novel hypotheses based on task definitions, baseline methods, and current scientific knowledge.
 - **Idea Evolution:** Leverages the LLM to improve existing ideas by incorporating critiques (novelty, feasibility, scientific validity) and insights from related literature, generating refined ideas iteratively.
- **Assessment Agent:** Ensures the quality and viability of generated ideas through a rigorous, multidimensional evaluation process. It scores ideas on coherence, credibility, verifiability, novelty, and alignment (0-10 scale), provides detailed narratives, and promotes diversity among top-ranked ideas.
- **Human-interactive Feedback:** A crucial component for managing complex tasks, allowing human experts to provide direct feedback (insights, critiques) on one or multiple ideas, leading to further refinement. Additionally, automatic feedback generated by agents also contributes to this iterative improvement.
- **Orchestration Agent:** The central coordinator, it designs and manages workflows among all other agents, synchronizes tasks, handles data flow, and oversees the timing of human feedback. It ensures efficiency, coherence, and alignment with research objectives.

#### 3.2. Comprehensive Idea-to-Methodology Construction

This process systematically bridges the gap between concise research ideas and concrete, implementable methodologies.
- **Methodology Development Agent:** This agent has two core capabilities:
 - **Methodology Initialization:** Converts research ideas into detailed methodological frameworks by extracting core objectives, identifying key variables, and integrating task descriptions, baseline implementations, and relevant literature. This results in a theoretically sound and practically executable framework.
 - **Methodology Refinement:** Iteratively improves the initial methodological framework by incorporating structured critiques (automated assessments and human feedback) and synthesizing insights from the latest scientific literature. This ensures rigor and completeness.
Collaboration with Assessment and Orchestration agents ensures comprehensive feedback and domain knowledge integration throughout this phase.

#### 3.3. Evolutionary Experimental Planning and Execution

This capability focuses on converting theoretical concepts into functional code and iteratively refining experiments.
- **Exception-Guided Debugging Framework:** Systematically converts methodological descriptions into executable code.
 - **Coder Module:** Employs a dual-strategy approach: Aider for single-file or limited-scope tasks, and OpenHands for complex repository-level codes requiring comprehensive structural understanding and multi-file modifications.
 - **Debugging Cycle:** An iterative process involving (1) execution attempt, (2) exception capture and traceback analysis, (3) contextual code structure understanding, (4) debugging strategy formulation, and (5) targeted implementation, continuing until successful execution or a threshold is met.
- **Experimental Planning and Adaptive Evolution:**
 - **Implementation Planning:** Identifies core modules for modification, then develops a step-by-step implementation strategy with clear priorities and dependencies, operating at architectural, algorithmic, and optimization levels.
 - **Adaptive Evolution Approach:** Involves structured iterations where each implementation attempt is followed by performance assessment and potential refinement. Records of implementation decisions are maintained to track changes and their effects, enabling gradual refinement based on empirical results.

InternAgent leverages state-of-the-art LLMs such as GPT-4o for idea generation and Claude-3.7-Sonnet for code generation and debugging, showcasing a sophisticated integration of modern AI tools within its multi-agent architecture.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

InternAgent demonstrates significant advancements in autonomous scientific research, evidenced by extensive experimental validation across a diverse range of tasks.

#### 4.1. Quantitative Performance Improvements Across Diverse Tasks

- **Outperforming Existing Systems:** InternAgent consistently improved performance over baselines and significantly outperformed existing auto-research systems like DOLPHIN across all 12 tested tasks (Tables 1 & 2). For instance, in Reaction Yield Prediction (AutoRYP), InternAgent achieved a maximum R² of 35.4%, a substantial increase from the baseline's 27.6% and DOLPHIN's 31.8%. Similarly, in 3D Point Cloud Classification (Auto3DCls), InternAgent reached 95.5% Overall Accuracy (OA), surpassing the baseline's 91.0% and even achieving state-of-the-art performance comparable to human experts (95.3% with pre-training, compared to InternAgent's 95.5% without).
- **High Success Rates:** The framework achieved reasonable execution success rates, even on complex tasks like AutoPCDet (3D Point Cloud Autonomous Driving) with 50% and Auto2DSeg (2D Semantic Segmentation) with 90% (Tables 3 & 4). This is attributed to its detailed idea-to-methodology process, which generates concrete methodologies for the coder to implement.
- **Broad Generalization Capability:** InternAgent's ability to support 12 diverse tasks, spanning AI (e.g., semantic segmentation, VLM fine-tuning) and scientific domains (e.g., molecular dynamics, enhancer activity prediction), highlights its strong generalization. This is facilitated by the Survey Agent's adaptive literature search and review capabilities.
- **Support for Repository-Level Experiments:** A key differentiating factor is InternAgent's support for complex, multi-file, repository-level tasks (e.g., AutoPCDet, AutoVLM, AutoTPPR). This significantly expands the scope of automation compared to single-file limitations of previous systems, demonstrating performance improvements, such as increasing DeepLabV3Plus baseline from 78.8% to 81.0% on Auto2DSeg.

#### 4.2. Cost-Efficiency

- **Reasonable Monetary and Computational Costs:** The monetary cost for idea generation (using GPT-4o) averaged around $0.6 per idea, demonstrating cost-efficiency. Coder-debug costs (using Claude-3.7-Sonnet) varied by complexity, with single-file codes costing below $1 and complex repo-level codes around $1.2 per run (Tables 5 & 6). This analysis indicates InternAgent as a cost-efficient framework for automated research.

#### 4.3. Insightful Analyses and Mechanism Validation

- **Effective Survey Agent:** The Survey Agent's two modes (literature review and deep research) effectively gather foundational knowledge and refine research directions, mimicking human expert processes (Fig. 4).
- **Self-Evolving Idea Innovation:** The iterative evolution of ideas, as exemplified by the reaction yield prediction task (Fig. 3), demonstrates how InternAgent refines initial concepts into more sophisticated, practical, and technically advanced solutions by incorporating external knowledge and critiques.
- **Robust Idea-to-Methodology Conversion:** The detailed methodologies generated from initial ideas (Fig. 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12) facilitate accurate and faithful code implementation, crucial for validating the ideas.
- **Adaptive Evolution (AE) Effectiveness:** Ablation studies confirmed that the adaptive evolution mechanism significantly improves performance and stability (Table 8). For instance, on image classification, AE improved max accuracy by 1.7% and mean accuracy by 0.7%, while also increasing the percentage of performance gains and execution success rates. This highlights the value of iterative planning and performance assessment.
- **Multi-Dimensional Baseline Improvement:** Beyond just performance metrics, InternAgent's proposed methods (e.g., ADAGT in AutoRYP) also enhance the **stability** of results, significantly reducing variance compared to baselines in few-shot settings (Table 7), indicating higher quality and more reliable ideas.
- **Superiority over AI-Researcher:** Compared to AI-Researcher, InternAgent consistently showed better performance and was significantly more economical (approx. one-sixth the cost for AutoRYP), primarily due to its ability to generate novel ideas through extensive search and reflection, and its complete experimental planning and adaptive evolution mechanism (Table 9).

#### 4.4. Human Evaluation

- **Higher Quality Ideas:** Human evaluators, qualified Ph.D.s or candidates with peer-review experience, consistently scored ideas generated by InternAgent higher than those from AI-Scientist-V2 across metrics of soundness, contribution, and overall rating for various tasks (e.g., Reaction Yield Prediction, 2D Semantic Segmentation, 2D Image Classification, Point Cloud Autonomous Driving) (Table 10). This confirms the framework's ability to generate more effective and novel ideas that resonate with expert judgment.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The InternAgent framework represents a significant leap forward in the field of autonomous scientific research, with profound potential implications across scientific and technological domains.

- **Acceleration of Scientific Discovery:** By automating the entire closed-loop research cycle—from hypothesis generation and literature review to methodology construction, experimental planning, execution, and result feedback—InternAgent can drastically reduce the time and effort required for scientific discovery. This acceleration could lead to breakthroughs in areas currently limited by human cognitive capacity and research bandwidth.
- **Enhanced Innovation and Novelty:** The self-evolving idea generation, coupled with human-interactive feedback and a robust assessment mechanism, allows InternAgent to explore a broader spectrum of possibilities, identify overlooked patterns, and generate genuinely novel and scientifically sound hypotheses. This pushes beyond human cognitive biases and limitations, fostering creativity in research.
- **Bridging the Idea-to-Implementation Gap:** The comprehensive "idea-to-methodology" construction phase is critical. It transforms abstract research concepts into detailed, implementable algorithms, significantly increasing the success rate of code implementation and streamlining the experimental validation process. This is a crucial step for practical application of AI-generated ideas.
- **Scalability and Versatility Across Disciplines:** InternAgent's validated performance across 12 diverse scientific and AI tasks, including complex, multi-modal, and cross-disciplinary challenges, demonstrates its broad applicability. This versatility makes it a powerful tool for researchers in chemistry, biology, computer vision, natural language processing, and more, allowing them to leverage advanced AI at a lower cost.
- **Support for Complex, Real-World Projects:** The ability to handle repository-level codes, as opposed to only single-file experiments, is a major differentiator. This enables InternAgent to tackle more realistic and complex research projects, reflecting the intricate nature of modern scientific software development.
- **Human-AI Symbiosis:** The interactive interfaces for human expert feedback facilitate a collaborative research model where AI acts as a powerful assistant, integrating human domain expertise to refine ideas and steer the research process. This ensures that the automated system remains aligned with human intuition and strategic goals.
- **Open Science and Reproducibility:** By open-sourcing the baselines and InternAgent-generated codes, the project promotes transparency, reproducibility, and collaborative development within the research community. This lowers the barrier for others to build upon and extend this work.

Looking forward, while InternAgent establishes a strong foundation, addressing future challenges such as advanced knowledge retrieval and representation, continuous agent capability enhancement (self-modification, adaptive learning from feedback), and the construction of more comprehensive scientific discovery benchmarks will be crucial for realizing its full potential in open-ended scientific discovery. Ultimately, InternAgent paves the way for a new paradigm where AI agents become proactive scientists, fundamentally transforming how research is conducted and accelerating humanity's quest for knowledge.
