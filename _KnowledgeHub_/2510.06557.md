# The Markovian Thinker: Architecture-Agnostic Linear Scaling of Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> The Markovian Thinker introduces an architecture-agnostic paradigm that enables large language models (LLMs) to perform linearly scalable reasoning by redefining the reinforcement learning environment. This approach allows LLMs to process significantly longer thought sequences without incurring quadratic computational costs, matching or exceeding prior state-of-the-art performance while achieving substantial efficiency gains.

## Problem

- Reinforcement Learning for Long Chain-of-Thought (LongCoT) reasoning incurs quadratic computational cost and linear memory usage for attention-based LLMs due to treating the entire reasoning sequence as the state.
- The prohibitive cost limits the practical application and further scaling of LLMs for very long reasoning chains, despite the benefits of extensive thinking.
- Existing solutions often constrain reasoning length, optimize inference post-training, or require architectural modifications, which do not fundamentally resolve the quadratic scaling during training for unconstrained thinking.

## Method

- Introduces "Markovian Thinking" through the novel "==Delethink==" RL environment, which ensures the policy always conditions on a constant-sized state regardless of the total reasoning length.
- Delethink structures reasoning into fixed-size chunks (e.g., 8K tokens), where the model's context and KV cache are reset at each chunk boundary.
- A short textual carryover (e.g., the last 4K tokens) from the previous chunk's output is incorporated into the new prompt, effectively creating a textual Markovian state that the policy learns to utilize.

## Results

- Delethink consistently matches or surpasses LongCoT-RL in accuracy on in-distribution math benchmarks (AIME'24, AIME'25, HMMT'25) and out-of-distribution tasks with equivalent thinking budgets.
- Achieves significant empirical computational efficiency, reducing training costs (e.g., 7 H100-months vs. 27 H100-months for **96K** thinking) and maintaining constant token generation throughput regardless of reasoning length.
- Demonstrates superior test-time scalability, improving performance with up to **128K** thinking tokens even when trained with a **24K** budget, while LongCoT-RL plateaus at its trained limit.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Redefining the RL environment to enforce a Markovian state transition enables architecture-agnostic linear scaling of reasoning, decoupling computational cost from total reasoning length.
> - Off-the-shelf reasoning LLMs demonstrate strong "zero-shot Markovian Thinking," adapting to the chunked reasoning paradigm without explicit training, providing a robust initialization for RL.
> - The forced summarization into a constant-size textual state may encourage LLMs to develop more abstract or compressed forms of reasoning, akin to human cognitive processes.
