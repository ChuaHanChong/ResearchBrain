# Improving the Physics of Video Generation with VJEPA-2 Reward Signal

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from FAIR (Meta Superintelligence Labs) enhanced the physical plausibility of state-of-the-art video generative models by integrating VJEPA-2, a self-supervised video world model, as a reward signal. This approach achieved new state-of-the-art PhysicsIQ scores, improving Video-to-Video generation by 6.33 points and Image-to-Video generation by 6.63 points.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art video generative models often produce physically implausible outputs despite high visual fidelity.
- A critical gap exists between visual realism and true physical understanding in current synthetic video generation, hindering practical applications.
- Benchmarks like PhysicsIQ highlight that visual realism alone does not imply physics comprehension in generated content.

## Method

- An existing state-of-the-art ==autoregressive== video generative model, **MAGI-1**, is augmented with a physics-aware reward signal.
- **VJEPA-2**, a self-supervised video model, is employed as a "physics oracle" to calculate a "surprise score" that quantifies physical plausibility.
- This surprise score guides **MAGI-1**'s generation through a modified score function during diffusion and a Best-of-N sampling step, selecting the most physically consistent videos.

## Results

- The approach achieved new state-of-the-art performance in the PhysicsIQ Challenge, demonstrating superior physical understanding in generated videos.
- PhysicsIQ score for Video-to-Video generation improved from 56.31 (vanilla MAGI-1) to 62.64, an increase of 6.33 points.
- PhysicsIQ score for Image-to-Video generation rose from 30.23 (vanilla MAGI-1) to 36.86, showing a gain of 6.63 points.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Self-supervised learning on natural videos can enable models to acquire an implicit "intuitive physics understanding" suitable for evaluating physical plausibility.
> - Integrating an external, physics-aware reward signal, derived from a world model, can effectively steer generative models towards physically coherent outputs.
> - Leveraging specialized "oracle" models to guide general-purpose generative AI is a powerful paradigm applicable to various complex alignment criteria beyond visual fidelity.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research directly addresses a critical and emerging challenge in the field of generative artificial intelligence: the gap between visual realism and physical plausibility in synthetic video. The broader research landscape is currently dominated by a rapid proliferation of highly capable video generative models, such as Sora, Lumiere, VideoPoet, Stable Video Diffusion, Pika 1.0, Runway Gen 3, CogVideoX, and Wan 2.1. These models have achieved astounding levels of visual fidelity, generating videos that are often indistinguishable from real footage in terms of appearance, texture, and motion dynamics.

However, as the paper correctly points out, "state-of-the-art video generative models exhibit severely limited physical understanding, and often produce implausible videos." This observation aligns with findings from benchmarks like PhysicsIQ, which explicitly demonstrate that "visual realism does not imply physics understanding." This deficiency is a significant hurdle for the practical application of generative video models in domains requiring adherence to real-world physics, such as robotics, engineering simulations, scientific visualization, and even realistic content creation.

The present work fits into this landscape by aiming to bridge this gap. It capitalizes on the emerging trend of leveraging self-supervised learning (SSL) to imbue AI models with a deeper understanding of the world. The paper references VJEPA-2 as a self-supervised video model that has shown "intuitive physics understanding" to emerge from its pretraining on natural videos. This highlights a critical sub-area of research: developing "world models" through SSL that can learn to predict and understand the underlying dynamics of the environment.

By integrating VJEPA-2 as a "reward signal" to guide the generation process of a state-of-the-art video generative model (MAGI-1), this research pioneers a novel approach to steer generative AI towards physically consistent outputs. This strategy resonates with broader trends in AI, such as reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) used to align large language models, but instead applies it to physics alignment using an objective, learned "physics oracle." The work directly contributes to the ongoing effort to make generative AI not just visually compelling, but also fundamentally intelligent and grounded in real-world principles, pushing the boundaries of what constitutes "good" or "realistic" generation beyond mere pixel fidelity. Furthermore, its success as the winning entry in the PhysicsIQ Challenge firmly places it at the forefront of research dedicated to evaluating and improving physical intelligence in generative models.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to **improve the physics plausibility of state-of-the-art video generative models**. This overarching goal is driven by a clear recognition of the limitations inherent in current video generation technologies. While contemporary models excel at producing visually realistic videos, they frequently fall short in adhering to the fundamental laws of physics, leading to outputs that, despite their visual fidelity, appear implausible or nonsensical upon closer inspection.

The paper articulates several key motivations for pursuing this objective:

1. **Addressing a Critical Gap in Generative AI:** The introduction explicitly states that "state-of-the-art video generative models exhibit severely limited physical understanding, and often produce implausible videos." This highlights a significant shortcoming that hinders the adoption of these models for applications requiring reliable representations of the physical world. The motivation is to move beyond superficial realism to a deeper, more physically grounded form of generation.

2. **Challenging the Notion of Visual Realism as Sufficient:** The PhysicsIQ benchmark is cited to underscore that "visual realism does not imply physics understanding." This provides an empirical basis for the motivation: simply making videos look good is not enough; they must also behave in a physically consistent manner to be truly useful and intelligent.

3. **Leveraging Emerging Capabilities of Self-Supervised Learning (SSL):** A crucial motivation stems from the observation that "intuitive physics understanding has shown to emerge from SSL pretraining on natural videos". This insight provides a promising pathway to solve the problem. If SSL models like VJEPA-2 can implicitly learn physics, then they can potentially serve as guiding mechanisms for generative models. The research is motivated by the desire to exploit this emergent capability to enhance generation.

4. **Specific Investigation:** The paper clearly outlines its specific investigative goal: "to investigate whether we can leverage SSL-based video world models to improve the physics plausibility of video generative models." This directly translates into the methodological choice of coupling MAGI-1 with VJEPA-2.

5. **Practical Application and Benchmark Performance:** As a "short technical report describing the winning entry of the PhysicsIQ Challenge," a strong motivation was to develop a demonstrably effective solution that could outperform other entries on a standardized benchmark specifically designed to assess physical intelligence. This competitive aspect reinforces the focus on tangible improvements and measurable success in this domain.

In essence, the research is motivated by the aspiration to endow video generative models with a more profound comprehension of the physical world, moving them from mere content synthesizers to intelligent systems capable of generating physically coherent and believable dynamic scenes.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The core methodology of this work revolves around augmenting an existing state-of-the-art video generative model, MAGI-1, with a physics-aware reward signal derived from a self-supervised video model, VJEPA-2. This integrated approach aims to guide MAGI-1's generation process towards physically more plausible outputs.

#### 3.1. Generative Model: MAGI-1

The base generative model used is MAGI-1, an autoregressive video generative model. MAGI-1 operates by iteratively denoising sequences of video chunks, enabling causal temporal video modeling. It is versatile, capable of generating videos conditioned on text, text and image, or text and video. During inference, MAGI-1 utilizes a score function to guide its diffusion process, which combines signals from unconditional generation, context conditioning (past video chunks), and text conditioning. This score function is represented as:
$$ \nabla_{x_t} \log p_s(x_t|x_{<t}, \text{txt}) = (1 - \omega_{<t})\nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t) + (\omega_{<t} - \omega_{\text{txt}})\nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t|x_{<t}) + \omega_{\text{txt}}\nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t|x_{<t}, \text{txt}) $$
where $x_t$ is the current video chunk, $x_{<t}$ are past chunks, $\text{txt}$ is text conditioning, and $\omega_{<t}$, $\omega_{\text{txt}}$ are guidance weights.

#### 3.2. Physics Oracle / Reward Model: VJEPA-2

The crucial component for introducing physics understanding is VJEPA-2. VJEPA-2 is a self-supervised video model comprising a video encoder ($E_\theta$) and a predictor ($P_\phi$). It is trained on Internet-scale video data using a mask-denoising objective in the representation space. The key hypothesis is that through this self-supervised pretraining, VJEPA-2 implicitly learns an "intuitive physics understanding," allowing it to predict future video states based on past observations in a physically consistent manner.

#### 3.3. Leveraging the Surprise Score

The method leverages a "surprise score" (S) derived from VJEPA-2, which quantifies the discrepancy between VJEPA-2's prediction of a future state and the actually generated state. This score is defined as the cosine similarity between:
1. VJEPA-2's prediction of the *next* video chunk's representation, based on *past* video chunks: $P_\phi(E_\theta(x_{<t}))$.
2. The VJEPA-2 encoding of the *generative model's output* for the current video chunk: $E_\theta(G_\gamma(x_{<t}, \text{txt}))$.
The formula for the surprise score is:
$$ S(x_t|x_{<t}, \text{txt}) = \text{sim}\left( P_\phi(E_\theta(x_{<t})), E_\theta(G_\gamma(x_{<t}, \text{txt})) \right) $$
Here, $G_\gamma$ is the generative model (MAGI-1), and $\text{sim}$ denotes cosine similarity. A *lower* surprise score indicates better alignment between VJEPA-2's prediction and the generated content, implying greater physical plausibility as understood by VJEPA-2.

#### 3.4. Two-Pronged Guidance Strategy

The method integrates VJEPA-2's surprise score into MAGI-1's generation process through two mechanisms:

1. **Modified Score Function for Guided Diffusion:** The first mechanism involves altering MAGI-1's score function during the diffusion inference process. An additional term is introduced to explicitly minimize the VJEPA-2 surprise score:
 $$ \nabla_{x_t} \log p_s(x_t|x_{<t}, \text{txt}) = \dots - \omega_s \nabla_{x_t} S(x_t|x_{<t}, \text{txt}) $$
 The term $- \omega_s \nabla_{x_t} S(x_t|x_{<t}, \text{txt})$ actively steers the diffusion process towards generating video chunks that are *less surprising* (i.e., more predictable and thus physically plausible) according to VJEPA-2. $\omega_s$ is a new guidance weight controlling the strength of this physics-aware signal.

2. **Best-of-N (BoN) Sampling:** The second mechanism is a post-generation filtering step. After generating 16 video samples for each conditioning (e.g., text, initial image/video), the method applies Best-of-N selection. Among these 16 samples, the video with the *lowest average surprise score* (calculated across all its chunks using Eq. 1) is chosen as the final output. This further refines the generation, selecting the most physically consistent option among a batch of candidates.

#### 3.5. Evaluation

The approach is evaluated on the **PhysicsIQ dataset**, a benchmark specifically designed to measure physical understanding in generative models. The performance is reported using the "PhysicsIQ Score (↑)," where a higher score indicates better physics plausibility, for both Video-to-Video (V2V) and Image-to-Video (I2V) generation tasks.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research yielded clear and compelling results, demonstrating a significant improvement in the physics plausibility of video generative models when guided by the VJEPA-2 reward signal. The findings are summarized in Table 1 of the paper and establish new state-of-the-art performance on the PhysicsIQ benchmark.

#### 4.1. Quantitative Improvements

The core finding is the substantial increase in PhysicsIQ scores achieved by integrating VJEPA-2 guidance with the MAGI-1 model.

- **Video-to-Video (V2V) Generation:**
 - Vanilla MAGI-1 achieved a PhysicsIQ score of **56.31**.
 - The proposed method, **MAGI-1 + VJEPA-2 (ours)**, boosted this significantly to **62.64**. This represents an impressive increase of **+6.33** points.

- **Image-to-Video (I2V) Generation:**
 - Vanilla MAGI-1 achieved a PhysicsIQ score of **30.23**.
 - The proposed method, **MAGI-1 + VJEPA-2 (ours)**, improved this to **36.86**. This shows an even larger gain of **+6.63** points.

#### 4.2. State-of-the-Art Performance

These improvements propelled the MAGI-1 + VJEPA-2 system to a new state-of-the-art status on the PhysicsIQ benchmark for both V2V and I2V generation. The method successfully surpassed the scores of many other prominent and highly capable video generative models, including:

- **V2V Generation:** Lumiere (23.00), VideoPoet (29.50)
- **I2V Generation:** Sora (10.00), Pika 1.0 (13.00), SVD (14.80), Lumiere (19.00), VideoPoet (20.30), Wan 2.1 (20.89), Runway Gen 3 (22.80), CogVideoX (26.22)

The consistent and notable margin of improvement over the vanilla MAGI-1 model and other leading generative models underscores the effectiveness of leveraging VJEPA-2 as a physics-aware reward signal. The combination of modifying the score function during diffusion and applying Best-of-N selection based on the surprise score proved highly successful in enhancing the physical plausibility of the generated videos. These results directly validate the central hypothesis that self-supervised video world models can be effectively employed to guide generative processes towards physically consistent outputs.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The significance of this research lies in its successful advancement of video generative AI beyond purely aesthetic considerations to encompass a deeper understanding of physical reality. By demonstrably improving the physics plausibility of generated videos, this work has several profound implications and potential impacts:

1. **Elevating the Quality of Generative Video:** This research addresses a critical limitation of current state-of-the-art video generation models. While they excel at visual realism, their lack of physical understanding often leads to "implausible videos." By integrating a physics-aware reward signal, the generated content becomes more coherent, believable, and consistent with the real world. This moves generative AI closer to producing truly intelligent and robust content, not just visually appealing illusions.

2. **Validating Self-Supervised Learning for World Models:** The success of VJEPA-2 as a guiding signal strongly validates the hypothesis that self-supervised learning (SSL) on large-scale natural video datasets can indeed imbue models with an intuitive, implicit understanding of physical laws. This reinforces the importance of SSL in building foundational "world models" that can predict future states and dynamics, opening new avenues for developing more intelligent AI systems that grasp causal relationships and physical interactions.

3. **Generalizable Guidance Paradigm:** The methodology of using an external, specialized model (VJEPA-2) as a reward signal to steer a general-purpose generative model (MAGI-1) is a powerful paradigm. This approach could be widely generalized beyond physics to other desirable properties. For instance, similar techniques could be used to guide generative models towards specific ethical guidelines, common-sense reasoning, stylistic adherence, or other complex criteria, simply by developing appropriate "oracle" models that evaluate these properties. This represents a significant step towards more controllable and alignable generative AI.

4. **Enhanced Utility for Real-World Applications:** Physically plausible videos are essential for numerous high-impact applications:
 - **Robotics and Autonomous Systems:** Generating realistic simulations for training and testing autonomous agents in safe, controlled environments, especially for scenarios involving complex physical interactions.
 - **Scientific Research and Engineering:** Creating accurate visual representations of physical phenomena, aiding in hypothesis testing, design validation, and educational demonstrations where physical accuracy is paramount.
 - **Content Creation and Media:** Producing more convincing visual effects, animations, and synthetic media for films, games, and marketing, where the uncanny valley of physical implausibility can break immersion.
 - **Synthetic Data Generation:** Creating high-quality synthetic datasets that faithfully replicate physical behaviors, which can be invaluable for training other AI models in data-scarce domains or for augmenting real-world data.

5. **Shaping Future AI Research Directions:** By winning the PhysicsIQ Challenge and setting new benchmarks, this work highlights the growing importance of "physical intelligence" in AI. It encourages further research into how AI models can acquire and apply knowledge of physics, whether through explicit physical engines, enhanced SSL, or novel architectural designs. It also underscores the value of benchmarks like PhysicsIQ in driving progress in these specific, challenging aspects of AI.

In summary, this research is a crucial step towards building generative AI that not only creates stunning visuals but also genuinely understands and adheres to the fundamental principles governing our physical world, thereby unlocking new levels of reliability, utility, and intelligence in synthetic content.
