# Self-Supervised Learning to Fly using Efficient Semantic Segmentation and Metric Depth Estimation for Low-Cost Autonomous UAVs

> [!summary] Summary
> Mocanu, Slusanschi, and Leordeanu developed a vision-only autonomous flight system for low-cost UAVs, integrating real-time semantic segmentation with a novel adaptive scale factor algorithm for metric depth estimation. Their system achieved a mean distance error of 14.4 cm for metric depth and enabled robust obstacle avoidance with 100% mission success rates for exploration and landing in simulated and real-world tests.

## Problem

- High cost and complexity of multi-sensor setups (e.g., LiDAR, GPS) for indoor autonomous UAV navigation.
- Difficulty in obtaining accurate, real-time metric depth from monocular cameras, which is crucial for precise obstacle avoidance.
- Computational constraints of low-cost UAV platforms hindering the deployment of resource-intensive perception and control systems.

## Method

- A multi-task learning framework combining efficient semantic segmentation and an adaptive metric depth estimation algorithm for comprehensive scene understanding.
- A novel adaptive scale factor algorithm that converts non-metric monocular depth predictions into real-time metric distances using camera intrinsics and ground plane detection.
- Utilizing knowledge distillation to train a lightweight U-Net for real-time semantic segmentation and an end-to-end student control network for onboard flight policy execution.

## Results

- The adaptive scale factor algorithm achieved a mean metric depth error of **14.4** cm (median **16.28** cm) and a median inference time of **0.0206** seconds (**48.54** FPS).
- The combined depth and segmentation approach demonstrated **100%** mission success rates for obstacle avoidance, exploration, and helipad landing in real-world and digital-twin environments.
- A lightweight student control network (1.6M parameters), trained via self-supervised knowledge distillation, achieved an **87.5%** autonomous mission success rate suitable for onboard execution.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Real-time, accurate metric depth estimation from monocular vision is achievable for low-cost UAVs by leveraging camera parameters and ground plane detection, significantly improving obstacle avoidance.
> - Knowledge distillation can effectively transfer complex perception and control policies from robust teacher models to lightweight student networks, enabling deployment on resource-constrained embedded systems.
> - Integrating semantic segmentation with metric depth provides a more robust and efficient scene understanding compared to purely semantic-based approaches for autonomous navigation.
