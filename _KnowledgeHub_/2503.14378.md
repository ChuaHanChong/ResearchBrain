# Impossible Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the National University of Singapore introduce IPV-BENCH, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating video models' ability to understand and generate impossible scenarios, including 902 high-quality videos across four domains (Physical, Biological, Geographical, and Social Laws) and 260 text prompts for testing models' reasoning capabilities with counterfactual events.

## Problem

- Existing video benchmarks focus primarily on real-world scenarios, lacking evaluation tools for impossible or counterfactual scenes
- Current models' ability to reason about and generate physically impossible content remains poorly understood and evaluated

## Method

- Development of a structured taxonomy covering 14 categories of impossible scenarios across four domains
- Creation of IPV-**BENCH** with curated impossible videos, text prompts, and three evaluation tasks (Judgment, **MCQA**, **OpenQA**)

## Results

- Proprietary models (Gemini, GPT-4o) outperform open-source alternatives but still struggle with impossible video identification
- Current video generation models achieve relatively low IPV-Scores, indicating difficulties in balancing visual quality with prompt adherence
- Video-LLMs particularly struggle with OpenQA tasks requiring independent identification of impossible phenomena

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Models exhibit unbalanced capabilities between visual quality and faithful representation of impossible scenarios
> - Temporal reasoning and world knowledge are crucial but lacking in current video understanding models
> - Physical Laws domain poses the greatest challenge for both generation and understanding tasks
