# EagleVision: A Dual-Stage Framework with BEV-grounding-based Chain-of-Thought for Spatial Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> EagleVision, a dual-stage framework, enhances the spatial reasoning capabilities of multimodal large language models by integrating macro-level perception with micro-level, verifiable BEV-grounded spatial reasoning. The framework achieved an average score of 63.5 on the VSI-Bench benchmark, positioning it as a leading open-source model.

## Problem

- Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) often struggle with robust 3D spatial reasoning, frequently relying on weak 2D methods or opaque 3D reconstruction modules.
- Extending interleaved-modal Chain-of-Thought (CoT) to spatial reasoning faces challenges due to limited token budgets preventing global spatial coverage and the lack of explicit 3D grounding for hypothesis verification.
- There is a scarcity of large-scale, detailed supervision for training MLLMs on multi-step spatial CoT, making learning complex spatial reasoning difficult.

## Method

- A dual-stage framework is proposed, comprising ==Macro Perception== and ==Micro Verification==, to progressively refine spatial understanding in MLLMs.
- Macro Perception utilizes a ==Semantic-Perspective Fusion Determinantal Point Process (SPF-DPP)== to efficiently select a compact yet diverse and semantically relevant set of keyframes from raw video, balancing geometric diversity and query relevance.
- Micro Verification employs a ==BEV-grounded Chain-of-Thought (CoT) agent== capable of active pose querying and real-frame retrieval for iterative spatial reasoning, trained with a novel reinforcement learning reward that incorporates a ==spatial grounding penalty==.

## Results

- EagleVision achieved a state-of-the-art average score of **63.5** on the VSI-Bench benchmark among open-source Vision-Language Models, surpassing its Qwen3-VL-8B baseline by **4.1 points**.
- The framework demonstrated strong performance across various spatial tasks, including **74.9%** accuracy in numerical answers, **72.3%** in object size, and **84.7%** in route planning, even exceeding human accuracy in fine-grained distance estimation.
- Ablation studies confirmed the individual and synergistic effectiveness of the Multimodal CoT, BEV grounding, SPF-DPP for frame selection, and the novel spatial consistency reward in the reinforcement learning setup.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Robust spatial intelligence in MLLMs can be achieved by combining global scene understanding (macro perception) with active, verifiable, and iteratively refined local geometric reasoning (micro verification).
> - Explicitly grounding spatial hypotheses in a Bird’s-Eye-View (BEV) workspace allows MLLMs to query for specific viewpoints and retrieve concrete visual evidence, creating interpretable and consistent spatial reasoning chains.
> - Reinforcement learning with a custom spatial grounding reward function enables effective training of multi-step spatial CoT policies, reducing reliance on extensive and costly step-by-step human annotations.
