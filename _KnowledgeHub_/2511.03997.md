# PhysCorr: Dual-Reward DPO for Physics-Constrained Text-to-Video Generation with Automated Preference Selection

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework named PhysCorr enables text-to-video models to generate physically plausible content by employing a dual-reward Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) strategy with automated preference selection. The approach achieved up to a 2.83% increase in mechanics compliance metrics for leading T2V models while largely maintaining visual quality and semantic coherence.

## Problem

- State-of-the-art text-to-video (T2V) models often produce videos that violate fundamental physical laws despite high perceptual quality.
- Current human preference alignment techniques, like RLHF and DPO, struggle to address physical inconsistencies due to limitations in reward models and human preference datasets.
- Reward models typically prioritize aesthetics and text alignment, lacking efficient mechanisms to quantify physical plausibility.

## Method

- **PhysCorr**, a unified framework, models, evaluates, and optimizes physical consistency in T2V generation using a specialized Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) pipeline.
- A lightweight, dual-dimensional Physics ==Reward Model== (**PhysicsRM**) quantifies physical plausibility by assessing subject-consistency and mechanical verification.
- Physics-Specialized DPO (**PhyDPO**) leverages physics-aware reweighting to prioritize learning from more significant physical inaccuracies, guiding the T2V model towards coherent outputs.

## Results

- PhysCorr enhanced overall video quality and semantic consistency across 24-25 out of 32 VBench dimensions for leading T2V models (VideoCrafter2 and Wan2.1).
- The framework achieved significant improvements in VBench2 intrinsic faithfulness metrics, particularly a 2.29-2.83% increase in the critical mechanics metric.
- Ablation studies validated the contributions of the dual-dimensional PhysicsRM and the reweighting mechanism in PhyDPO to physical realism, and qualitative results demonstrated corrected physical interactions and stable object appearances.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Injecting explicit physics-aware mechanisms throughout the T2V optimization pipeline is essential for moving beyond superficial visual quality to intrinsic faithfulness.
> - A dual-dimensional reward model that efficiently assesses both geometric stability and mechanical plausibility can effectively quantify physical realism.
> - Prioritizing preference pairs with larger physical plausibility differences through a reweighted DPO loss significantly accelerates and targets learning for physical consistency.
