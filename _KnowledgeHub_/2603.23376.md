# ABot-PhysWorld: Interactive World Foundation Model for Robotic Manipulation with Physics Alignment

> [!summary] Summary
> ABot-PhysWorld, developed by AMAP CV Lab, Alibaba Group, is an interactive world foundation model for robotic manipulation that generates visually realistic and physically plausible videos with action control. It achieved the highest reported average score of 0.8491 on PBench, showing superior physical plausibility (0.9306 Domain Score) while maintaining competitive visual quality, and established state-of-the-art zero-shot generalization on EZSbench with an overall average score of 0.8030.

## Problem

- Existing video-based world models often produce physically implausible manipulations, such as object penetration or anti-gravity motion, which limits their reliability for robotic tasks.
- Current models rely on generic visual training data lacking fine-grained embodied interaction signals and utilize standard maximum likelihood objectives that do not explicitly differentiate between physically valid and invalid predictions.
- Current evaluation benchmarks for world models frequently overemphasize visual quality or in-distribution accuracy, failing to adequately assess physical consistency and zero-shot generalization capabilities.

## Method

- Developed **ABot-PhysWorld**, an interactive world foundation model for robotic manipulation, based on a 14B ==Diffusion Transformer== fine-tuned on a curated 3-million-clip real-world manipulation dataset.
- Introduced a post-training ==physical preference alignment== framework, combining a decoupled VLM discriminator with ==Diffusion-DPO==, to explicitly suppress physical violations during video generation.
- Implemented action-controllable generation using ==spatially structured action map injection== via ==parallel context blocks==, enabling precise action execution and cross-embodiment control.

## Results

- ABot-PhysWorld achieved the highest reported average score of **0.8491** on PBench for text-conditioned generation, with a state-of-the-art Domain Score of **0.9306**, demonstrating superior physical plausibility.
- The model established state-of-the-art zero-shot generalization on the new EZSbench, achieving the highest overall average score of **0.8030**, indicating effective generalization to out-of-distribution scenarios.
- For action-conditioned generation, ABot-PhysWorld outperformed baselines in visual quality (PSNR: **21.09**, SSIM: **0.8126**) and trajectory consistency (nDTW: **0.8522**), indicating improved pixel accuracy and adherence to commanded gripper trajectories.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly guiding a diffusion model towards physical plausibility through a preference alignment framework significantly improves adherence to physical laws without compromising visual fidelity.
> - A rigorously curated dataset, incorporating embodied-specific filtering, hierarchical distribution balancing, and physics-aware video captioning, is fundamental for training generalizable and physically consistent world models.
> - Injecting action information via a zero-initialized parallel context block mechanism allows for effective action control while preserving and leveraging the backbone's pre-trained physical knowledge.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of embodied intelligence and robotics is advancing towards creating generalist policies capable of operating in diverse, dynamic environments. A critical component for such systems is the development of robust "world models," which can accurately predict future states given current observations and actions. Video-based world models have emerged as a promising paradigm for embodied simulation, planning, and policy learning, serving as simulators for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies or directly as World Action Models (WAMs) that predict action-conditioned dynamics.

Despite progress in visual fidelity, state-of-the-art video generation models, exemplified by systems like Veo 3.1 and Sora v2 Pro, frequently produce physically implausible manipulations. These issues manifest as object penetration, anti-gravity motion, contactless interactions, or unnatural deformations. Such physical inconsistencies limit their reliability for downstream robotic applications where adherence to real-world physics is fundamental. The existing limitations are attributed to two primary factors: (i) reliance on generic visual datasets that often lack fine-grained embodied interaction signals necessary for learning physical dynamics (e.g., friction, collision response, mass distribution), and (ii) the use of standard maximum likelihood objectives during training, which do not inherently differentiate between physically valid and invalid state transitions.

ABot-PhysWorld addresses these limitations by focusing on integrating physical plausibility and action controllability into a video-based world model. It aims to bridge the gap between visually realistic generation and physically accurate prediction, thereby enhancing the utility of world models for practical embodied AI applications. Furthermore, the work introduces a new benchmark, EZSbench, to address the challenge of evaluating zero-shot generalization and physical consistency, areas often overlooked by existing benchmarks that emphasize in-distribution accuracy or visual quality.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop a robust, interactive world foundation model, ABot-PhysWorld, capable of generating visually realistic, physically plausible, and action-controllable videos for robotic manipulation tasks. The motivation stems from the observed deficiencies in current video-based world models, which, despite high visual fidelity, often fail to adhere to fundamental physical laws.

The detailed objectives and motivations include:

- **Addressing Physical Implausibility:** The core motivation is to overcome the generation of physically impossible scenarios (e.g., object penetration, anti-gravity movement, unphysical deformations) by existing models. This is crucial because such artifacts render world models unreliable for robotic simulation, planning, and policy learning, where accurate physical reasoning is paramount for successful real-world deployment.
- **Enhancing Embodied Interaction Learning:** To counter the limitation of generic visual data, the project aims to leverage a curated dataset rich in embodied interaction signals. The motivation is that training on data specifically designed for manipulation, with physics-aware annotations, will enable the model to acquire a deeper understanding of fine-grained physical dynamics, such as contact, friction, and object properties.
- **Developing Physics-Aware Supervision:** Recognizing that standard likelihood-based training objectives do not prioritize physical correctness, a key objective is to introduce a novel post-training framework. This framework is designed to explicitly distinguish and suppress physically invalid behaviors while preserving visual quality and dynamic consistency, thereby aligning model outputs with real-world physics.
- **Achieving Action Controllability:** For a world model to be useful in robotic contexts, it must be action-conditioned, meaning it can generate future video frames based on commanded robot actions. The motivation is to enable precise spatial action injection for cross-embodiment control, allowing the model to serve as a high-fidelity interface for robot simulation and planning across diverse robot morphologies.
- **Establishing a Robust Evaluation Framework:** The inadequacy of existing benchmarks to assess physical consistency and zero-shot generalization in embodied scenarios motivates the development of a new evaluation tool. EZSbench is designed to rigorously test models on unseen combinations of robots, tasks, and scenes, providing a more comprehensive and realistic assessment of a model's capabilities beyond simple visual quality or in-distribution accuracy. This includes a decoupled evaluation protocol to independently measure physical realism and action alignment.

In essence, the research seeks to move beyond purely aesthetic video generation towards "physically grounded" video prediction, making world models genuinely useful for the advancement of embodied AI.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The ABot-PhysWorld model is built upon a 14B Diffusion Transformer backbone and incorporates a multi-faceted approach encompassing data curation, physical preference alignment, and action-conditioned generation, complemented by a novel evaluation benchmark.

#### 3.1. Data Curation

A significant aspect of the methodology involves a three-stage data curation pipeline to enhance data scale, diversity, and physical relevance for embodied manipulation tasks:

- **Embodied-Specific Data Filtering:** From an aggregation of approximately three million real-world manipulation clips across five public datasets (AgiBot, RoboCoin, RoboMind, Galaxea, OXE), a multi-stage filtering process is applied:
 - **Video-level quality gate:** Removes clips with abnormal resolutions, moving cameras, or inappropriate frame lengths (retains 80-500 frames).
 - **Optical-flow-based motion filtering:** Computes Farnebäck dense optical flow to derive a global kinematic score, removing clips with minimal or unphysical motion.
 - **CLIP-based temporal coherence:** Assesses continuity using CLIP embeddings of equidistant frames, discarding clips with low cosine similarity between consecutive frames.
 - **Vision-action alignment verification:** Projects calibrated action maps (joint actions, end-effector poses, gripper states) onto video frames. A Qwen3-VL model verifies spatiotemporal alignment, filtering mismatches arising from sensor or synchronization errors.
- **Hierarchical Distribution Balancing:** To prevent overfitting to common tasks and improve generalization, a dynamic sampling strategy is applied across four levels:
 - **Intra-dataset diversity preservation:** Retains small sub-datasets within larger collections.
 - **Cross-robot rebalancing:** Upweights underrepresented robot types to enhance cross-platform generalization.
 - **Task-aware quota allocation:** Tasks are tiered by volume. Head tasks (high volume) are capped (8-15%), body tasks (medium volume) are uniformly sampled (40-50%), and long-tail tasks (rare) are fully preserved.
 - **Macro-dataset scale regulation:** Caps large datasets via subsampling while ensuring minimum coverage for micro-datasets, using a three-round supplementation strategy to balance quotas.
- **Physics-Aware Video Captioning:** A multi-level annotation system is designed to provide physically grounded language supervision:
 - **Multi-level action semantics:** Captions are structured at macroscopic (task intent), mesoscopic (verb-noun segmentation), microscopic (Cartesian trajectories, gripper states), and scene-level (physical relations, task outcomes) granularities.
 - **Grounded spatial precision:** Mechanisms like few-shot in-context learning, dynamic vocabularies, and a visible-fact baseline prevent hallucinated spatial relations and imprecise descriptions.
 - **Causal physical modeling:** Explicitly annotates physical causality (e.g., gravity, deformation) and uses a four-stage narrative structure (scene setup, action flow, final state, camera summary) for temporal coherence.
 - This pipeline employs a Qwen3-VL 32B model for structured perception (extracting physical attributes) and a Qwen3 32B FP8 model for physics-grounded narrative synthesis.

#### 3.2. Embodied Video Generation Backbone

The model's backbone is based on Wan2.1-I2V-14B, a Diffusion Transformer, which is fully fine-tuned on the curated embodied dataset to capture visual diversity and fine-grained spatiotemporal dynamics.

#### 3.3. Physical Preference Alignment

To address physical implausibility after initial training, a post-training preference alignment pipeline is introduced:

- **Decoupled VLM Discriminator:** To prevent self-evaluation bias, two distinct VLMs are used:
 - **Qwen3-VL 32B (Thinking model):** Acts as a "proposer," generating a task-specific physical checklist based on hierarchical evaluation (Tier 1 for fatal violations, Tier 2 for micro-physical fidelity). It constructs a balanced mix of positive and negative questions.
 - **Gemini 3 Pro (Scorer model):** Acts as a "scorer," evaluating N candidate video variants against the checklist using Chain-of-Thought reasoning. A multi-round tournament sampling strategy efficiently identifies optimal (y_w) and worst (y_l) samples.
- **Diffusion-DPO Training:** The discriminative triplets (condition, physics-compliant video, physics-violating video) are used to fine-tune the video diffusion model directly in the latent space. The Diffusion-DPO loss actively reduces prediction error for physically compliant videos (z_w) while increasing it for physically violating videos (z_l). To manage memory for the 14B DiT, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) modules (rank 64) are injected into the self-attention and feed-forward layers, allowing the reference model loss to be computed by temporarily disabling LoRA weights.

#### 3.4. Action-Conditioned Video Generation

To enable controllable generation from action sequences:

- **Action Map Construction:** 7D (or 14D for dual-arm) robotic actions are converted into multi-channel spatial action maps. This involves projecting 3D positions to 2D image coordinates, encoding orientation as projected colored arrows, and mapping gripper state to an opacity-controlled circular mask.
- **Action Injection:** Instead of direct concatenation or AdaLN, which can cause catastrophic forgetting, selective blocks from the main DiT are cloned to form parallel "context blocks" that process the action maps (following the VACE framework). The output of these context blocks is projected via zero-initialized convolution layers and added residually to the corresponding main DiT blocks. This approach preserves pre-trained physical priors while gradually learning action controllability.

#### 3.5. Embodied-ZeroShot Benchmark (EZSbench)

A new benchmark is introduced to evaluate physical fidelity and cross-embodiment generalization under out-of-distribution conditions:

- **Evaluation Set Construction:**
 - **Synthetic images:** Generated via Nano Banana (text-to-image) by varying robot morphology, scene, task, and perspective.
 - **Real-world images:** VLM-guided background editing on mechanical arm images, preserving foreground interactions.
 - **Dense description synthesis:** A physics-heuristic framework (visual anchoring, kinematically compliant action simulation, narrative synthesis) creates text descriptions integrating initial state, action trajectory, and final state.
- **Evaluation Method:** Employs a decoupled dual-model protocol to prevent self-evaluation bias:
 - **Qwen3-VL-32B-Thinking:** Dynamically generates a physical checklist (with 30-50% negative questions) based on initial state and text instructions, using System 2 reasoning.
 - **Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct:** Serves as the answering model, performing VQA predictions against the checklist ground truth to compute a physical score.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of ABot-PhysWorld involved comparisons against several baseline models on both established and newly introduced benchmarks.

#### 4.1. PBench Evaluation (Text-Conditioned Generation)

On the PAI-Bench robot domain subset (PBench), which assesses physical plausibility and visual quality in text-conditioned generation, ABot-PhysWorld demonstrated significant improvements:

- **Overall Performance:** The DPO-augmented ABot-PhysWorld achieved the highest average score of 0.8491, establishing a new state-of-the-art. This surpassed its base model (0.8232) and all other baselines.
- **Domain Score (Physical Fidelity):** The model achieved a Domain Score of 0.9306, the highest among all compared methods, indicating superior adherence to physical laws. This contrasts with models like Veo 3.1 (0.8350) and Sora v2 Pro (0.7626), which, despite high visual quality, exhibited lower physical consistency.
- **Quality Score (Visual Fidelity):** ABot-PhysWorld maintained a competitive Quality Score of 0.7676, demonstrating that improvements in physical alignment were achieved without sacrificing perceptual quality. Existing state-of-the-art models like Veo 3.1 (0.7740) and Sora v2 Pro (0.7679) showed a trade-off where their strong imaging and aesthetic quality did not translate to equally strong physical reasoning.
- **Spatiotemporal Stability:** The base model also exhibited high spatiotemporal stability, as evidenced by I2VB (0.9777) and MS (0.9916) scores.
- **Qualitative Analysis:** Baselines frequently demonstrated physical violations such as gripper or object distortion during contact (Sora v2 Pro, Veo 3.1), grasping penetration (GigaWorld-0, Cosmos), non-contact grasping (WoW), and target misidentification (UnifoLM, Wan 2.5). In contrast, ABot-PhysWorld correctly identified targets, maintained spatiotemporal coherence, and avoided deformation or penetration artifacts.

#### 4.2. EZSbench Evaluation (Zero-Shot Generalization)

On the newly introduced EZSbench, designed to evaluate zero-shot generalization and physical consistency under out-of-distribution conditions, ABot-PhysWorld also achieved leading results:

- **Overall Performance:** The model achieved the highest overall average score of 0.8030, establishing state-of-the-art performance for both Quality Score (0.7694) and Domain Score (0.8366).
- **Generalization:** These results confirm that the physical fidelity improvements generalize effectively to unseen robot-task-scene combinations, beyond the training distribution.
- **Qualitative Analysis:** Baselines struggled with complex, long-horizon manipulation tasks requiring logical reasoning. Examples included failure to map object color attributes to correct containers (Wan-2.5, Veo 3.1, WoW), physically implausible contactless grasping (Sora v2), and "generation collapse" (geometric distortion) during contact-rich interactions (Giga R0, Veo 3.1). ABot-PhysWorld consistently followed compositional instructions and maintained physical plausibility without such artifacts across diverse scenarios.
- **Case Studies:** The model demonstrated capability in tasks like multi-object attribute binding, deformable object manipulation with dual-arm coordination, articulated object interaction (e.g., closing a door), rigid-body placement, contact-intensive wiping, and object relocation, all while maintaining physical plausibility and spatiotemporal coherence.

#### 4.3. Action-Conditioned Generation Results

For action-conditioned video generation (A2V), ABot-PhysWorld outperformed baselines in both visual quality and action fidelity:

- **Quantitative Metrics:** The proposed method achieved higher PSNR (21.09), SSIM (0.8126), and Trajectory Consistency (0.8522) compared to baselines like Enerverse-AC (PSNR 20.42, SSIM 0.7542, Traj. Consis. 0.8157) and Gen-Sim (PSNR 18.05, SSIM 0.7413, Traj. Consis. 0.6195).
- **Qualitative Analysis:** ABot-PhysWorld consistently generated contact-intensive manipulation videos while preserving object geometry and visual integrity. Baselines, such as Genie-Envisioner and Enerverse-AC, frequently produced object deformation, contactless grasping artifacts, and target localization errors, leading to distorted outputs or task failures.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

ABot-PhysWorld represents a significant step towards developing more reliable and effective world models for embodied intelligence, particularly in robotic manipulation. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

- **Enhanced Reliability for Embodied AI:** By explicitly addressing and mitigating physically implausible video generation, ABot-PhysWorld contributes to the development of world models that can serve as more trustworthy simulators for robotic planning, policy learning (Vision-Language-Action policies), and direct execution as World Action Models (WAMs). This improved reliability is crucial for bridging the sim-to-real gap and accelerating the deployment of autonomous robots in complex, real-world scenarios.
- **Advancement in Generative Models:** The work demonstrates that it is possible to enforce physical constraints in large-scale video generative models without compromising visual quality. This challenges the common trade-off observed in previous models and sets a new direction for research in physically grounded generation.
- **Novel Methodological Contributions:**
 - **Principled Data Curation:** The multi-stage data filtering, hierarchical distribution balancing, and physics-aware video captioning pipeline provide a systematic approach to constructing high-quality, diverse, and physically rich datasets for embodied AI, which is a key bottleneck in the field.
 - **Physics Preference Alignment with Decoupled Discriminators:** The Diffusion-DPO-based post-training framework, incorporating a decoupled VLM discriminator, offers a novel and effective method for injecting explicit physical preferences into generative models. This approach could be adapted to other domains requiring fine-grained constraint satisfaction.
 - **Robust Action Injection:** The use of parallel context blocks with zero-initialized convolutions for action-conditioned generation provides a robust mechanism for precise spatial action injection, ensuring action alignment while preserving pre-trained physical priors, which is essential for controllable robotic systems.
- **Standardized Evaluation for Generalization:** The introduction of EZSbench fills a critical gap in the evaluation landscape. By focusing on training-independent, zero-shot generalization and providing a decoupled protocol for assessing both physical consistency and action alignment, EZSbench promotes more rigorous and meaningful progress in embodied video generation. Its public release will facilitate standardized comparisons and encourage research into truly generalizable models.
- **Future Directions for Robotics:** The model's current reliance on fixed-viewpoint data and lack of closed-loop evaluation highlight avenues for future research. Expanding to multi-view generation and closed-loop interaction in real-world environments would further enhance its utility and realism, pushing the boundaries of interactive world models.

In summary, ABot-PhysWorld represents a foundational contribution by making video-based world models not only visually convincing but also physically sound and action-responsive, thereby laying a more solid groundwork for the next generation of embodied intelligent systems.
