# DreamVideo-2: Zero-Shot Subject-Driven Video Customization with Precise Motion Control

> [!summary] Summary
> DreamVideo-2 is a zero-shot framework for customized video generation, enabling precise control over both subject appearance and motion trajectory using a single reference image and bounding box sequence. The framework achieves superior performance over existing methods by effectively balancing subject learning and motion control through masked reference attention and reweighted diffusion loss.

## Problem

- Existing video customization methods require computationally expensive fine-tuning for each new subject, limiting their practical application.
- There is an inherent conflict between preserving subject identity and precisely controlling its motion, often resulting in degraded subject appearance or inaccurate trajectories.
- Many current approaches lack precise user control over the subject's motion trajectory within the generated video.

## Method

- Introduces a zero-shot framework that takes a reference image, text prompt, and a sequence of bounding boxes to define subject motion.
- Employs masked reference attention to effectively extract and infuse subject features from the reference image into the video generation process.
- Utilizes a mask-guided motion module with **ControlNet** and a reweighted diffusion loss to balance subject learning and motion control, preventing motion from dominating subject identity.

## Results

- Achieves superior performance in subject fidelity and precise motion control compared to state-of-the-art methods like DreamVideo and MotionBooth.
- Consistently preferred by users in a comprehensive study across text alignment, subject fidelity, motion alignment, and overall quality.
- Demonstrated an 87% user preference for motion alignment and a 77% preference for text alignment in user studies.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Simple joint training of subject learning and motion control often leads to motion control objectives dominating, causing subject identity to degrade.
> - Subject features can be effectively extracted and infused from a single reference image using a novel masked reference attention mechanism without requiring additional networks.
> - Balancing subject fidelity and motion control is crucial and can be achieved by focusing attention within bounding boxes and reweighting diffusion loss to amplify the importance of subject regions.

## Detailed Report

### 1. Introduction

Recent advances in text-to-video generation have shown impressive results, but giving users precise control over both the subject and motion in generated videos remains challenging. While existing methods require computationally expensive fine-tuning for each new subject, the recently introduced DreamVideo-2 framework offers a zero-shot approach that allows users to customize videos using just a single reference image and a bounding box sequence to control motion.

DreamVideo-2 represents a significant breakthrough in customized video generation by eliminating the need for per-subject fine-tuning while maintaining precise control over both subject appearance and motion trajectory. By intelligently balancing subject learning and motion control, the framework addresses a key challenge in customized video generation that has limited previous approaches.

### 2. Limitations of Current Approaches

Current video customization methods face several critical limitations:

1. **Test-time Fine-tuning Requirement**: Existing methods like DreamVideo and MotionBooth require computationally expensive fine-tuning for each new subject, making them impractical for many real-world applications.

2. **Subject-Motion Control Conflict**: There is an inherent tension between preserving subject identity and controlling motion. Previous approaches often sacrifice one for the other, resulting in either degraded subject appearance or inaccurate motion control.

3. **Motion Control Dominance**: During training, motion control objectives tend to dominate subject learning, leading to poor preservation of subject identity.

4. **Limited Motion Control Precision**: Most existing methods lack precise control over subject trajectory, limiting their real-world usefulness.

These limitations stem from the fundamental challenge of optimizing multiple objectives simultaneously - maintaining subject identity while controlling motion in a complex video generation process.

### 3. DreamVideo-2 Framework

DreamVideo-2 presents a zero-shot video customization framework that addresses these limitations through a carefully designed architecture. The framework takes three inputs:

1. A reference image of the subject
2. A text prompt describing the desired video
3. A sequence of bounding boxes defining the subject's motion trajectory

The framework consists of several key components:

1. **Masked Reference Attention**: Extracts and infuses subject features from the reference image to maintain subject identity.
2. **Spatiotemporal Encoder**: Processes bounding box masks to extract motion information.
3. **ControlNet**: Integrates motion information into the generation process.
4. **Reweighted Diffusion Loss**: Balances learning objectives to prevent motion control from dominating subject learning.

The architecture builds upon a pre-trained video diffusion model, with the original 3D UNet parameters frozen while the newly added components are trained. This approach leverages the pre-trained model's capabilities while adding specialized modules for subject and motion control.

### 4. Subject Learning via Reference Attention

DreamVideo-2 tackles subject learning through a novel reference attention mechanism that leverages the inherent capabilities of the video diffusion model to extract multi-scale features from a single reference image.

The process works as follows:

1. The reference image is treated as a single-frame video and input into the original video diffusion model.
2. The model extracts multi-scale subject features through its attention layers.
3. Reference attention then infuses these subject features into the video generation process through a residual cross-attention mechanism.

Mathematically, reference attention can be expressed as:

$$Z_M'' = Z' + \text{Attention}(Q', K_s, V_s) \odot \hat{M}$$

Where $Z'$ represents video features, $K_s$ and $V_s$ are subject features, and $\hat{M}$ is a blended mask that focuses attention on relevant regions.

This approach enables the model to extract rich subject details without additional parameters or networks, while the residual connection ensures stability during training.

### 5. Motion Control via Mask-Guided Motion Module

For precise motion control, DreamVideo-2 employs a mask-guided motion module that translates user-specified bounding boxes into motion guidance for the video generation process:

1. Users provide a sequence of bounding boxes defining the desired subject trajectory.
2. These boxes are converted into binary box masks.
3. A spatiotemporal encoder processes these masks to extract motion patterns.
4. A spatial ControlNet integrates this motion information into the video generation process.

The motion control signal is represented as the inverse of the mask to align with the background, effectively guiding the subject to follow the specified trajectory while allowing flexibility in how the subject moves within the bounding boxes.

### 6. Balancing Subject and Motion Learning

The core innovation of DreamVideo-2 lies in its approach to balancing subject learning and motion control through two key techniques:

#### 6.1. Masked Reference Attention

Traditional reference attention applies subject features uniformly across the video, which can be overtaken by motion control objectives. DreamVideo-2 introduces masked reference attention, which incorporates a blended latent mask into the reference attention mechanism:

$$\hat{M} = M + \lambda_M(1 - M)$$

Where $M$ is the binary box mask, and $\lambda_M$ is a hyperparameter that controls the blending ratio. This blended mask forces the model to focus on subject identity within the bounding boxes while retaining some background context.

#### 6.2. Reweighted Diffusion Loss

The standard diffusion loss treats all regions equally, but DreamVideo-2 introduces a reweighted diffusion loss that differentiates between regions inside and outside the bounding boxes:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{reweighted}} = \mathbb{E}_{t, \epsilon} \left[ \alpha_t \| \epsilon - \epsilon_\theta(z_t, t, c) \|_2^2 \odot M + \| \epsilon - \epsilon_\theta(z_t, t, c) \|_2^2 \odot (1 - M) \right]$$

Where $\alpha_t > 1$ is a time-dependent weighting factor that amplifies the loss contribution of regions inside the bounding boxes, enhancing subject learning while preserving motion control.

These techniques effectively counteract motion control dominance, enabling the model to maintain subject identity while following the specified motion trajectory.

### 7. Dataset Construction

To train and evaluate DreamVideo-2, the authors curated a new single-subject video dataset with comprehensive annotations, including:

- Video captions
- Subject masks
- Bounding boxes for all frames

The annotation process used a combination of advanced models:

1. First, video captions were generated using a vision-language model (VLM).
2. The subject word was identified either through human evaluation or extracted from the caption.
3. Grounding DINO located the subject in the first frame.
4. SAM generated a precise subject mask.
5. DEVA tracked the subject across all frames, generating consistent masks and bounding boxes.

This carefully constructed dataset provided the necessary training data for DreamVideo-2 to learn the complex relationship between subject appearance and motion control.

### 9. Significance and Applications

DreamVideo-2 represents a significant advancement in customized video generation with several important implications:

#### 9.1. Zero-Shot Capability

By eliminating the need for per-subject fine-tuning, DreamVideo-2 makes customized video generation practical for everyday use. Users can generate videos with any subject without waiting for time-consuming optimization processes.

#### 9.2. Intuitive Control

The use of bounding boxes for motion control provides an intuitive interface for users to specify precise subject trajectories, making the technology accessible to non-experts.

#### 9.3. Balance Between Subject and Motion

The framework's ability to maintain subject identity while precisely controlling motion addresses a fundamental challenge in video generation, opening up new possibilities for creative applications.

#### 9.4. Potential Applications

DreamVideo-2 has the potential to impact numerous domains:

1. **Content Creation**: Enabling easy creation of personalized videos for social media, marketing, and entertainment.
2. **Education**: Creating custom educational videos featuring specific objects or characters.
3. **Virtual Try-on**: Allowing users to see how products would look in different environments and motions.
4. **Animation**: Streamlining the animation process by specifying character appearance and motion paths.
5. **Filmmaking**: Providing pre-visualization tools for directors and cinematographers.

### 10. Conclusion

DreamVideo-2 represents a breakthrough in customized video generation by offering zero-shot subject-driven video customization with precise motion control. By addressing the fundamental challenge of balancing subject learning and motion control through masked reference attention and reweighted diffusion loss, the framework produces videos that maintain subject identity while following specified motion trajectories.

The framework's ability to generate high-quality customized videos without per-subject fine-tuning makes it practical for real-world applications, democratizing access to advanced video generation capabilities. As demonstrated through extensive experiments and user studies, DreamVideo-2 outperforms existing methods across all evaluation aspects, confirming its effectiveness and potential impact.

By providing intuitive control over both subject appearance and motion, DreamVideo-2 opens up new possibilities for creative expression and practical applications in fields ranging from content creation to education and entertainment. The zero-shot nature of the framework makes it particularly valuable for scenarios requiring quick iteration and experimentation with different subjects and motion trajectories.
