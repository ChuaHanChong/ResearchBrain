# CDTrans: Cross-domain Transformer for Unsupervised Domain Adaptation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Alibaba Group and Shandong University developed CDTrans, a pure Transformer-based framework for Unsupervised Domain Adaptation. This framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on four UDA benchmarks and demonstrates robustness to noisy pseudo-labels in category-level alignment by leveraging the cross-attention mechanism.

## Problem

- Deep neural networks experience significant performance degradation when deployed in new domains with differing data distributions, known as domain shift.
- Traditional CNN-based Unsupervised Domain Adaptation (UDA) methods struggle with noisy and inaccurate pseudo-labels for target domain samples, especially in fine-grained category-level alignment.
- Manually annotating large datasets for every new target domain is a prohibitively expensive and time-consuming process.

## Method

- ==CDTrans==, a novel pure Transformer-based solution for UDA, employs a ==triple-branch, weight-sharing architecture==.
- It leverages the ==cross-attention mechanism== of Transformers, which is shown to be inherently robust to noisy input pairs, for robust feature alignment between source and target domains.
- A =="Two-Way Center-Aware Pseudo Labeling"== strategy generates high-quality pseudo-labels for target samples and filters out noisy pairs to mitigate misguidance during training.

## Results

- CDTrans achieved state-of-the-art performance on the VisDA-2017 benchmark and delivered highly competitive results on Office-Home, Office-31, and DomainNet datasets.
- It demonstrated remarkable robustness by boosting accuracy on 'hard' categories (e.g., from **10.3%** to **88.6%** for 'person' on VisDA-2017), validating its ability to handle noisy pseudo-labels.
- The Two-way Center-aware Pseudo Labeling method significantly improved pseudo-label precision to **91.3%** on VisDA-2017, leading to overall performance remarkably close to using ground-truth labels.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The cross-attention mechanism in Transformers is robust to noisy input pairs, effectively assigning low weights to dissimilar patches within false positive source-target pairs, thereby reducing their negative impact.
> - A two-way pseudo-labeling approach combined with center-aware filtering significantly enhances the precision of generated pseudo-labels, which is crucial for effective category-level domain adaptation.
> - A triple-branch Transformer architecture with shared weights, using self-attention for domain-specific learning and cross-attention for explicit domain alignment, along with a distillation loss, effectively learns discriminative and domain-invariant representations.
