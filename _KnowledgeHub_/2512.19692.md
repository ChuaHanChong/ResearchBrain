# Interact2Ar: Full-Body Human-Human Interaction Generation via Autoregressive Diffusion Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Interact2Ar is the first end-to-end text-conditioned autoregressive diffusion model for full-body human-human interaction generation. A cooperative denoiser with body/hand/trajectory-specific heads generates dyadic motion sequentially with a Mixed Memory buffer, achieving state-of-the-art quality on Inter-X and enabling adaptive downstream tasks.

## Problem

- Prior interaction models tend to omit hand motions since high hand dimensionality introduces noise and complicates joint body-hand generation pipelines.
- Existing diffusion approaches denoise entire motion sequences simultaneously, failing to capture the reactive, temporally adaptive nature of real human-human interaction.
- Standard Inter-X evaluators are insensitive to degradation, showing near-identical scores even when trajectories are corrupted or swapped between interactants.

## Method

- ==Cooperative denoiser== with body-specific heads: parallel weight-shared streams per person, ==cross-attention== for inter-personal information flow, and separate heads for trajectory, body, and hand poses.
- ==Autoregressive diffusion== pipeline that generates non-overlapping sub-motions sequentially, conditioning each step on a ==memory buffer== of previously generated frames.
- **Mixed Memory** strategy combining a full-framerate short-term buffer with a temporally downsampled long-term buffer, giving large context windows at low computational cost.

## Results

- Interact2Ar beats prior SOTA InterMask on Inter-X across full, body-only, and hand-only metrics, e.g. full R-Precision Top-3 **0.773** vs **0.722** and FID **0.277** vs **0.671**.
- Ablation shows Mixed Memory (ms=15, ml=45, delta=5) reaches a **60-frame** context using only **24** stored frames, roughly **3x** memory reduction over plain memory at comparable FID.
- User study of **35** participants ranks Interact2Ar above InterGen and InterMask on both text alignment and hand-motion realism, approaching ground-truth ranking.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Body-part-specialized denoising heads let a shared encoder scale to high-dimensional hand articulation without the noise-overhead that causes other models to drop hands entirely.
> - Autoregressive step-wise generation trades whole-sequence coherence for reactive adaptability, a useful shift whenever a task involves responsive multi-agent behavior, not just static synthesis.
> - Rotation-based evaluators can systematically hide quality degradation; position-grounded, body-part-decomposed evaluators are a cautionary generalizable fix for benchmarking motion-generation models.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of human motion generation has advanced significantly in recent years, driven by new datasets and generative architectures. However, synthesizing realistic human-human interactions remains a complex challenge, exceeding the difficulty of single-person motion generation. This complexity arises from the need to model not only high-quality individual motions but also coherent spatio-temporal coordination and reactive dynamics between multiple participants.

Previous limitations in this domain include the scarcity of high-quality motion capture data for multi-person interactions, which often lacked diversity, comprehensive textual annotations, or detailed hand kinematics. The Inter-X dataset, which provides full-body interaction data with hand motions, has been instrumental in addressing some of these data limitations and serves as a foundation for the current work.

Many prior models for human motion generation, particularly diffusion-based approaches, typically generate entire motion sequences simultaneously. While effective for isolated single-person motions, this "holistic" approach struggles to capture the inherently reactive and adaptive nature of human interactions, where each participant's movements are contingent on subtle cues and evolving dynamics from their partners. Furthermore, the high dimensionality of detailed hand kinematics, crucial for expressive non-verbal communication and physical contact, has often led to their omission in generative models due to increased noise and complexity.

Interact2Ar positions itself within this landscape by addressing these core challenges. It proposes an end-to-end text-conditioned autoregressive diffusion model that aims to generate high-fidelity full-body human-human interactions, explicitly including detailed hand motions. By adopting an autoregressive pipeline, the work aims to overcome the temporal rigidity of previous diffusion models, enabling more adaptive and responsive interaction generation. Additionally, the paper contributes to the evaluation paradigm by proposing more robust and specialized metrics to accurately assess the quality of these complex interactions.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop a model capable of generating realistic, text-conditioned, full-body human-human interactions, encompassing detailed hand kinematics. This overarching goal is driven by several specific motivations and objectives:

- **Generating High-Fidelity Full-Body and Hand Motions**: Previous methods often omit detailed hand motions due to their high dimensionality and the complexity they add to the generation process. However, hand gestures and contact are integral to human interaction and non-verbal communication. A key objective is to effectively integrate and generate these detailed hand kinematics alongside body motions, contributing to the realism and expressivity of the generated interactions.
- **Enhancing Adaptability and Reactivity in Interactions**: Traditional diffusion models generate entire motion sequences in a single pass. This "all-at-once" generation limits the model's ability to adapt to dynamic changes or to react to the evolving movements of an interaction partner, which is fundamental to human-human communication. The motivation is to introduce an autoregressive generation paradigm that synthesizes motion sequentially, allowing for continuous adaptation based on the most recent interaction state.
- **Efficient Memory Management for Long-Term Coherence**: Autoregressive models rely on conditioning on past frames (memory) to maintain temporal coherence. For long-duration interactions, a simple full-framerate memory strategy becomes computationally inefficient and memory-intensive. An objective is to develop an efficient memory management technique, specifically a "Mixed Memory" approach, that can provide both short-term, detailed context and long-term, broader temporal information without excessive overhead, thereby preventing repetitive motion artifacts and ensuring sustained realism.
- **Enabling Downstream Adaptive Interaction Applications**: By achieving adaptability through autoregressive generation and efficient memory, the research aims to unlock new capabilities for interaction synthesis. These include seamless temporal composition of different actions, real-time adaptation to external disturbances (e.g., unexpected displacements), and the ability to model sequential multi-person interactions beyond dyadic scenarios.
- **Developing Robust and Granular Evaluation Metrics**: The paper identifies limitations in existing evaluation pipelines, noting their potential biases and lack of sensitivity to specific degradation types, especially concerning global positioning and detailed body parts. A significant objective is to propose a set of more robust, body-part-specific evaluators and metrics (e.g., jerk-based metrics for smoothness) to provide a more reliable and informative assessment of full-body interaction quality.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Interact2Ar's methodology integrates a diffusion model with a novel autoregressive pipeline and specialized architectural components for full-body, human-human interaction generation.

#### 3.1. Dataset and Motion Representation

The model is trained on the **Inter-X dataset**, which offers 11K full-body interactions, 40 distinct actions, and detailed textual descriptions. Crucially, Inter-X includes detailed hand motions. To represent interactions, the model exclusively uses **SMPL-X parameters**, specifically (r, φ, θ_body, θ_hands), where `r` denotes root translation, and `φ`, `θ_body`, `θ_hands` represent root, body, and hand joint rotations using a continuous 6D representation. Body shapes are normalized to a neutral configuration due to dataset limitations. This representation choice aims to minimize redundancy and enhance efficiency compared to some prior approaches.

#### 3.2. Full-Body Interaction Generation (Non-Autoregressive Baseline)

The foundational component is a **diffusion model** designed to incrementally remove Gaussian noise from a noised interaction `x_t` conditioned on a textual description `c`. The model directly predicts the clean motion `ˆx_0` at each denoising step, consistent with recent practices.

- **Architecture**: Interact2Ar builds upon the cooperative denoiser architecture from InterGen. This transformer-encoder architecture processes each individual (`a` and `b`) in parallel streams that share weights, with **cross-attention** mechanisms facilitating information exchange between individuals.
 - **Specialized Denoising Heads**: A key architectural innovation is the introduction of an initial encoding module that maps the noised motion into a latent representation. This latent representation then feeds three **specialized denoising heads** operating in parallel: one for global trajectory, one for body poses, and one for hand poses. This design allows for body-part-specific modeling while ensuring coherent predictions based on full motion context.

- **Training Losses**: A combination of commonly used losses is employed:
 - `L_repr`: An ℓ2 loss on the raw SMPL-X parameters.
 - `L_orient`: Penalizes errors in root orientation.
 - **Kinematic Losses (computed via forward kinematics)**:
 - `L_pos`: ℓ2 loss on global joint positions.
 - `L_vel`: ℓ2 loss on joint velocities, promoting temporal smoothness.
 - `L_foot`: Penalizes foot motion when feet are in ground contact, reducing artifacts.
 - `L_dist`: An ℓ2 loss on pairwise joint distance maps between individuals, specifically activated for joint pairs in close proximity in the ground truth, to accurately capture interaction contacts.
 Loss weights (`λ_i`) are tuned through grid search to balance contributions.

#### 3.3. Autoregressive Interaction Generation

To address the need for adaptability, Interact2Ar implements an autoregressive diffusion pipeline. An interaction of length `N` is divided into contiguous sub-motions of length `n`. At each step `k`, the denoiser predicts the next sub-motion `ˆx_0[kn:(k+1)n]` conditioned on a textual description `c`, the current noised sub-motion, and a memory buffer `M`.

- **Mixed Memory**: To overcome the limitations of short-term memory for long interactions, a novel Mixed Memory approach is proposed.
 - **Short-Term Memory (`M_s`)**: Comprises `m_s` most recent, full-framerate previously generated frames.
 - **Long-Term Memory (`M_l`)**: Consists of a temporally downsampled history, retaining frames at intervals of `δ` over a significantly longer window of `m_l` frames (`m_l ≫ m_s`).
 - The complete memory `M_k` is a concatenation of `M_l` and `M_s`. This dual-memory architecture allows the model to leverage immediate context for smooth transitions and broader temporal context for coherence, while optimizing memory efficiency.

#### 3.4. Adaptive Interactions (Downstream Capabilities)

The autoregressive pipeline with Mixed Memory enables several adaptive applications:
- **Temporal Motion Composition**: Seamlessly composes different actions by switching textual prompts, leveraging the memory buffer for smooth transitions.
- **Real-Time Disturbance Adaptation**: Allows the model to adapt to external perturbations (e.g., sudden position changes) between generation steps, facilitating reactive generation.
- **Sequential Multi-Person Interactions**: Extends beyond dyadic interactions by allowing one individual to interact sequentially with multiple partners, maintaining context from previous interactions through memory.

#### 3.5. Evaluation Metrics and Evaluators

Beyond standard metrics (R-Precision, FID, MM Dist, Diversity, MModality), Interact2Ar introduces:
- **Smoothness Metrics**: Peak Jerk (PJ) and Area Under the Jerk (AUJ) from to assess the smoothness of transitions, particularly relevant for autoregressive generation and adaptive tasks.
- **Robust and Specialized Evaluators**: The original Inter-X evaluator is re-trained using **global joint positions** (instead of rotations) to minimize bias and improve sensitivity to degradation. Furthermore, three distinct evaluators are trained: one for all joints (full), one for body joints only, and one for hand joints only, providing granular insights into motion quality across different body parts.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experimental evaluation of Interact2Ar demonstrates its performance across multiple aspects, including quantitative metrics, qualitative assessments, user studies, and ablation studies.

#### 4.1. Evaluator Robustness (Table 1)

The research introduces improved evaluators designed to be more sensitive to motion quality degradation. Comparisons show that the newly trained evaluators, which use global joint positions, exhibit superior sensitivity to degradations such as adding noise to trajectories or swapping trajectories, detecting significant performance drops where the previous Inter-X evaluator remained largely unaffected. This establishes a more reliable foundation for assessing interaction quality.

#### 4.2. Comparison to State-of-the-art Approaches (Table 2)

- **Quantitative Performance**: Both the non-autoregressive (Interact2Ar*) and the autoregressive (Interact2Ar) versions of the proposed model surpass previous state-of-the-art methods, including T2M, InterGen, and InterMask, across various metrics. Interact2Ar demonstrates improvements in R-Precision, FID, and MM Dist for full-body, body-only, and hands-only evaluations.
- **Autoregressive Advantage**: Notably, the autoregressive version (Interact2Ar) consistently outperforms its non-autoregressive counterpart (Interact2Ar*) across all primary metrics. This indicates that sequential motion synthesis contributes to higher interaction quality.
- **Smoothness**: For the jerk-based metrics (PJ and AUJ), which assess transition smoothness, Interact2Ar shows a substantial improvement compared to all previous methods, indicating more fluid and less abrupt motions, particularly important for adaptive scenarios.

#### 4.3. Qualitative Evaluation (Figure 5, Figure B, Supplementary Video)

Qualitative comparisons visually illustrate that Interact2Ar generates higher-quality interactions with improved alignment to textual descriptions and more realistic, detailed hand motions compared to previous state-of-the-art models like InterMask. The supplementary video further highlights the temporal and spatial coherence and overall realism achieved by the method. Specific close-up visualizations demonstrate Interact2Ar's capability in generating intricate hand and body contacts during interactions.

#### 4.4. User Study (Figure 4)

A user study involving 35 participants evaluated generated interactions based on overall quality, textual alignment, and realism of hand motions. Interact2Ar received significantly higher average rankings (lower is better) than InterGen and InterMask, closely approaching the quality of ground truth. This user perception validates the model's ability to produce more convincing and expressive interactions.

#### 4.5. Ablation Study on Memory Configurations (Table 3, Table B)

- The ablation study on memory configurations for the autoregressive model reveals that models without the Mixed Memory strategy tend to degrade in performance as the memory context window increases, suggesting that a simple increase in context can introduce learning complexity.
- In contrast, models employing the Mixed Memory strategy maintain better performance with larger contexts, demonstrating its efficiency in managing extensive temporal information.
- The chosen hyperparameters (`m_s`=15, `m_l`=45, `δ`=5) provide an effective 60-frame context window while using only 24 actual frames, balancing context coverage and computational efficiency.

#### 4.6. Text Encoder Ablation (Table C)

An ablation study comparing CLIP with Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B as text encoders showed minimal differences in performance. This suggests that the textual diversity in the Inter-X dataset might not be sufficiently complex to fully leverage more advanced text encoders, at least for the current task setup.

#### 4.7. Additional Interaction Metrics (Table D)

Interact2Ar consistently performs best across supplementary dyadic-specific metrics: Contact Frequency (ratio of frames with contact), FID_CD (FID based on pairwise joint distances), Interaction Volume Penetration (measures inter-individual penetration), and Physical Foot Contact (PFC). These results further confirm the model's capability to generate physically plausible and interactively coherent motions.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Interact2Ar represents an advancement in the field of human motion generation, specifically for synthesizing human-human interactions. Its contributions offer implications across several domains:

- **Advancement in Realistic Interaction Synthesis**: As the first text-conditioned autoregressive diffusion model for full-body human-human interactions with detailed hand motions, Interact2Ar addresses a longstanding challenge in computer graphics and animation. Its ability to generate high-fidelity, expressive movements, including intricate hand gestures and contacts, moves closer to truly realistic digital human representation.
- **Enhanced Adaptability and Responsiveness**: The autoregressive pipeline, coupled with the novel Mixed Memory strategy, overcomes a critical limitation of previous holistic generative models. By enabling sequential motion synthesis conditioned on evolving context, Interact2Ar can generate interactions that are more reactive and adaptive to dynamic cues, mirroring real-world human behavior. This adaptability opens avenues for synthesizing complex, long-duration, and non-scripted interactions.
- **Enabling New Downstream Applications**: The adaptive capabilities of Interact2Ar directly facilitate several practical applications. These include seamless temporal motion composition, allowing for dynamic storytelling or character development; real-time adaptation to environmental disturbances, crucial for interactive virtual environments and robotics; and the extension to multi-human scenarios beyond simple dyadic interactions, paving the way for more crowded and socially complex simulations.
- **Improved Evaluation Standards**: The introduction of more robust and specialized evaluators, particularly those using global joint positions and focusing on specific body parts (full, body, hands), offers a more granular and reliable assessment framework for interaction quality. The incorporation of jerk-based metrics further refines the evaluation of temporal smoothness, providing a more comprehensive understanding of generative model performance. These advancements can guide future research in motion generation towards more perceptually accurate and physically plausible outputs.
- **Potential Impact on Virtual Characters and Robotics**: The capacity to generate adaptable and realistic human-human interactions has significant implications for various applications. In virtual reality, gaming, and character animation, Interact2Ar could lead to more immersive experiences with agents exhibiting sophisticated social behaviors. For robotics, particularly in human-robot interaction or collaborative tasks, the model could contribute to generating more natural and context-aware robot movements, improving human trust and collaboration.

Despite its contributions, the paper identifies limitations, such as the current model's inability to account for diverse body shapes due to dataset constraints. Addressing this in future work would further enhance the precision of physical contacts and the realism of interactions across varied human morphologies.
