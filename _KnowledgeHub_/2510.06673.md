# Heptapod: Language Modeling on Visual Signals

> [!summary] Summary
> Heptapod introduces "next 2D distribution prediction," a novel generative learning paradigm that enables causal Transformers to learn holistic visual semantics. It achieves state-of-the-art performance for causal autoregressive image generation on ImageNet-1K without relying on classifier-free guidance or semantic tokenizers, demonstrating intrinsic semantic learning from reconstruction-focused visual tokens.

## Problem

- Existing visual generative models often rely on external semantic crutches like Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) or pre-trained semantic tokenizers, masking intrinsic model limitations and compromising reconstruction fidelity.
- Applying 1D language modeling directly to 2D images is challenging due to the lack of intrinsic sequential order and the "curse of locality," which leads models to learn only local interpolations rather than global semantics.
- The research aimed to develop a visual generative learning paradigm that adheres to LLM "first principles": a tokenizer focused solely on faithful reconstruction and complex semantics emerging implicitly within the Transformer.

## Method

- Heptapod redefines "next token" for visual data by training a causal Transformer to predict the token distribution over the entire remaining ==2D spatial grid== in parallel at each timestep.
- The framework employs reconstruction-focused visual tokenizers (discrete ==VQ-VAE== or continuous ==VAE==) to decouple compression from semantic learning, aligning with LLM tokenizer principles.
- A standard 1D causal Transformer backbone is used in conjunction with specialized ==2D prediction heads== (Global or Chunk-based) to map its output to a full 2D spatial distribution prediction.

## Results

- Heptapod-H (**941M parameters**) achieved an FID of **2.70** and IS of **229.8** on ImageNet 256x256 class-conditional generation, dramatically outperforming prior causal autoregressive models like LlamaGen-3B (**3B parameters**, FID **9.38**) with fewer parameters.
- The model's strong performance is achieved without Classifier-Free Guidance or semantic tokenizers, demonstrating that visual semantics can emerge intrinsically from a well-posed generative objective within a causal Transformer.
- The "next 2D distribution prediction" objective demonstrated a substantial performance gain, achieving FID **3.97** and IS **185.3**, significantly outperforming traditional 1D next-token prediction methods (e.g., FID **13.07** for 1D-random scan order).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Predicting the entire 2D spatial distribution compels the model to learn holistic global structures and semantics, effectively overcoming the "curse of locality" observed in 1D next-token prediction for images.
> - The approach implicitly unifies autoregressive modeling with Masked Autoencoding, combining the strengths of both paradigms by having the causal prefix act as context for predicting all future tokens.
> - Reconstruction-focused tokenizers are viable, with continuous VAE tokenizers ultimately yielding superior generative quality due to their better reconstruction fidelity, even with slower initial convergence than discrete VQ-VAE tokenizers.
