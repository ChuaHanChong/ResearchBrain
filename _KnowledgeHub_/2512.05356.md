# AI & Human Co-Improvement for Safer Co-Superintelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> FAIR at Meta researchers Jason Weston and Jakob Foerster propose a "co-improving AI" paradigm, advocating for human-AI collaboration in research to achieve "co-superintelligence." This approach aims to accelerate AI development while enhancing safety, transparency, and alignment, presenting it as a superior alternative to purely autonomous self-improving AI.

## Problem

- The prevailing goal of creating autonomous self-improving AI systems poses significant risks, including potential misalignment and misuse, making robust safety and steerability challenging.
- Relying solely on autonomous AI for self-improvement may not be the fastest or most efficient path to superintelligence, given current AI limitations and the complexity of paradigm shifts in research.
- Existing human-AI collaboration is often a byproduct of general AI capability rather than a targeted development of collaborative research skills.

## Method

- Develop AI systems specifically designed for deep collaboration with human researchers across all stages of AI development, shifting from autonomous ==self-improvement== to ==co-improvement==.
- Establish a comprehensive conceptual framework outlining **12** specific ==co-improvement goals== for human-AI teamwork, ranging from ==problem identification== and ==method innovation== to ==safety co-development==.
- Propose the creation of new ==benchmarks== and ==training methods== tailored to measure and enhance AI's ==collaborative research skills==, fostering a ==symbiotic relationship==.

## Results

- The paper concludes that co-improvement is a superior and safer strategic path for developing superintelligence compared to a purely autonomous approach.
- A detailed conceptual framework was established, outlining specific collaborative activities (e.g., joint experiment design, safety co-development) to guide future human-AI research efforts.
- The proposed paradigm envisions a future 'co-superintelligence' that can accelerate scientific discovery across all domains and effectively address complex global societal problems through collective human-AI intelligence.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Co-improvement leverages complementary strengths of humans (creativity, values) and AI (computational power, pattern recognition) to accelerate scientific discovery and paradigm shifts more effectively than either working alone.
> - Continuous human involvement throughout the AI development cycle, through co-improvement, offers a proactive and iterative approach to ensuring safety, alignment, and steerability.
> - Targeted development of collaborative AI, supported by specific benchmarks and training, can lead to a human-centric superintelligence that augments and empowers humanity rather than replacing it.
