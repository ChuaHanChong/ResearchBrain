# Phys2Real: Fusing VLM Priors with Interactive Online Adaptation for Uncertainty-Aware Sim-to-Real Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Phys2Real, developed by researchers at Stanford and Princeton, presents a framework for robotic manipulation that fuses vision-language model (VLM) inferred physical priors with online interactive adaptation through an uncertainty-aware mechanism. This approach demonstrates improved success rates and efficiency in challenging sim-to-real tasks requiring adaptation to varying object physical properties.

## Problem

- The "sim-to-real gap" presents a fundamental challenge in transferring robotic policies learned in simulation to real-world environments, particularly for tasks requiring precise dynamics.
- Existing methods like Domain Randomization (DR) often lead to averaged behaviors that struggle with out-of-distribution physical properties, while online adaptation methods can fail in manipulation tasks with intermittent contacts due to uninformative interaction histories.
- Robots need to adapt to diverse and uncertain physical properties of objects (e.g., friction, mass distribution) for successful manipulation, a capability often lacking in current systems.

## Method

- The framework utilizes a three-stage ==real-to-sim-to-real pipeline== that includes high-fidelity 3D geometric reconstruction, physics-conditioned policy learning, and uncertainty-aware sim-to-real transfer.
- Deep reinforcement learning policies are trained in simulation, explicitly conditioned on interpretable physical parameters (e.g., center of mass, friction) and optionally fine-tuned with noisy parameters for enhanced robustness.
- During real-world deployment, physical parameter estimates from VLMs and an ensemble of online adaptation models are combined using an ==inverse-variance weighting== mechanism, which dynamically prioritizes estimates with lower uncertainty.

## Results

- In the T-block pushing task with weight at the bottom, Phys2Real achieved a **100%** success rate, outperforming Domain Randomization (**79.17%**) and RMA-only (**79.17%**) baselines.
- For the more challenging T-block pushing task with weight at the top, Phys2Real achieved a **57.14%** success rate, significantly higher than DR (**23.81%**), VLM-only (**4.76%**), and RMA-only (**14.29%**).
- In the hammer pushing task, Phys2Real completed the task **14.2%** faster on average (77.79 ± 44.08 s) compared to the Domain Randomization baseline (90.65 ± 42.03 s), indicating more efficient manipulation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The uncertainty-aware fusion of VLM-inferred physical priors with interaction-based online adaptation is crucial for robust sim-to-real transfer in dynamic manipulation tasks, effectively mitigating limitations of each source alone.
> - Conditioning RL policies on interpretable physical parameters rather than abstract latents enables direct integration of VLM knowledge and improves the policy's adaptability to varying object properties.
> - The proposed method can achieve performance comparable to policies with privileged ground-truth physical information by dynamically combining broad initial visual estimates with precise, context-specific interaction feedback.
