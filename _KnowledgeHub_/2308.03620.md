# Exploring Visual Pre-training for Robot Manipulation: Datasets, Models and Methods

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at ByteDance Research systematically investigated visual pre-training strategies for robot manipulation, developing the EgoNet dataset from egocentric videos rich in human-object interactions. They introduce Vi-PRoM, a pre-training scheme that combines contrastive learning with supervised fine-tuning for visual semantics and temporal dynamics, achieving improved performance on simulated and real-world robot manipulation tasks with reduced demonstration data.

## Problem

- Learning visual semantics and sequential action patterns for robot manipulation typically requires extensive and costly in-domain observation-action samples.
- Existing visual pre-training methods for robotics vary significantly in their choice of datasets, models, and methods, lacking a systematic comparison for optimal strategies.
- General visual datasets like ImageNet often lack the dynamic human-object interaction content critical for robotic manipulation tasks.

## Method

- Conducted a systematic benchmarking study on visual pre-training components, evaluating different datasets (ImageNet, EgoNet), model architectures (ResNet-34/50/101), and pre-training methods (==Contrastive Learning==, Masked Image Modeling).
- Introduced EgoNet, a new benchmark dataset of approximately 500,000 egocentric video clips, extracted from Ego4D, specifically designed to capture human-object interactions.
- Proposed Vi-PRoM (Visual Pre-training scheme for Robot Manipulation), a two-phase scheme using contrastive self-supervised learning on EgoNet, followed by supervised fine-tuning with auxiliary tasks for visual semantics and temporal dynamics.

## Results

- EgoNet pre-training significantly improved success rates in simulated tasks (**40.5%** in Franka Kitchen, **61.2%** in MetaWorld) compared to ImageNet pre-training.
- Vi-PRoM consistently outperformed baseline methods across simulated environments, achieving **43.8%** success in Franka Kitchen and **63.5%** in MetaWorld using Behavior Cloning, an improvement of **3.3%** and **2.3%** over the best MoCo-v3 baseline.
- Vi-PRoM successfully enabled a real robot to perform four contact-rich kitchen manipulation tasks with only 30 demonstrations per task, demonstrating practical applicability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Pre-training with data rich in human-object interactions (EgoNet) is more effective for robot manipulation than using general image datasets (ImageNet).
> - Convolutional ResNet-50 architectures offer a suitable balance of performance and efficiency for robot manipulation tasks, performing comparably to deeper ResNet models.
> - Contrastive learning objectives are more beneficial for learning effective visual representations for robot manipulation than masked image modeling approaches.
