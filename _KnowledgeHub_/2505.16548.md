# Incremental Sequence Classification with Temporal Consistency

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from UiPath and UCL developed Temporal-Consistency (TC-λ) loss for incremental sequence classification, allowing models to make accurate predictions as sequences unfold. This method, inspired by temporal-difference learning, enhanced predictive accuracy across various text tasks and significantly improved efficiency for large language model generation verification by enabling early assessments.

## Problem

- Current sequence classification methods typically predict only at sequence completion or focus on determining the earliest reliable prediction time, not on maximizing accuracy across all unfolding prefixes.
- Delaying predictions until full sequences are observed incurs significant real-world costs and computational inefficiencies, particularly in large language model (LLM) generation and verification.
- Existing LLM verification techniques using direct cross-entropy often provide noisy training signals for early sequence prefixes, hindering efficient early assessment.

## Method

- A novel loss function, ==Temporal-Consistency== (TC-λ), is introduced, extending temporal-difference learning principles to multiclass classification with cross-entropy loss.
- The TC-λ loss enforces a temporal-consistency condition, requiring predictions at any prefix to align with the expected predictions from subsequent prefixes, thus regularizing predictions across timesteps.
- A hyperparameter "lambda" 	0	1	 controls the trade-off between aligning with immediate future predictions and relying on the final observed class label, paralleling TD(lambda) algorithms.

## Results

- The TC-λ method consistently achieved superior predictive accuracy compared to baseline methods across various text classification datasets, with notable gains for early sequence prefixes.
- It demonstrated significant computational efficiency, with a smaller OPT-**125M** model trained with TC-λ matching the performance of much larger OPT-**1.3B** models trained conventionally.
- For LLM verification on GSM8K, TC-λ verifiers enabled earlier and more accurate correctness predictions, leading to a 23-**33%** reduction in generated tokens for "boosted best-of-N" decoding while maintaining answer accuracy.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Incremental sequence classifiers should naturally exhibit temporal consistency, meaning predictions at a given time step should align with expected predictions from the next step.
> - Enforcing this temporal consistency during training via a TD-inspired loss improves data efficiency and robustness, yielding better predictions, especially for shorter, less informative prefixes.
> - The proposed method can enable smaller models to achieve performance on par with much larger, conventionally trained models, indicating significant computational and data efficiency benefits.
