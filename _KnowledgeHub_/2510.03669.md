# Token Hidden Reward: Steering Exploration-Exploitation in Group Relative Deep Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Token Hidden Reward (THR), developed by researchers at the University of British Columbia, Vector Institute, and Amii, introduces a token-level metric quantifying individual token influence on correct response likelihood within Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). This method enables fine-grained control over the exploration-exploitation trade-off in RL-tuned LLMs, leading to up to 4.0% higher greedy accuracy for exploitation and 7.0% higher Pass@K accuracy for exploration on various math reasoning benchmarks.

## Problem

- Effectively managing the exploration-exploitation trade-off in reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs) remains a persistent challenge.
- Existing methods for controlling this trade-off often rely on coarse-grained, question-level interventions or external verifiers, lacking fine-grained control at the individual token level.
- Prior analyses of RL-tuned LLMs, such as GRPO, primarily focused on negative gradients for exploitation, failing to provide a comprehensive framework for both exploration and exploitation, or account for positive gradients and cross-token interactions.

## Method

- The authors define **Token Hidden Reward** (THR), a novel token-level metric derived from GRPO's likelihood change analysis, to quantify each token's contribution to the probability of generating correct responses.
- A THR-guided reweighting algorithm is proposed to dynamically adjust GRPO's advantage scores based on the magnitude and sign of THR, allowing for explicit steering towards exploitation (by amplifying positive THR) or exploration (by amplifying negative THR).
- The method identifies and prioritizes "dominant tokens" (those with significantly high absolute THR values) to refine the learning signal, effectively masking out less influential tokens.

## Results

- For exploitation (`p > 0`), the THR-guided approach improved greedy decoding accuracy (Pass@1) by up to **4.0%** over baseline GRPO on the Qwen2.5-Math-**7B** model.
- For exploration (`p < 0`), THR consistently boosted Pass@K accuracy, achieving up to **7.0%** improvement over GRPO on Llama3.2-**3B**-Instruct and up to **5.0%** at Pass@256 on Qwen2.5-Math-**1.5B**, outperforming question-level baselines by over **1.1%**.
- The THR framework integrates seamlessly with other RL objectives like GSPO-token, yielding average Pass@K improvements of **0.9**-**1.4%**, and incurs minimal computational overhead during training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A small subset of "dominant tokens" with disproportionately high absolute THR values significantly drives the learning dynamics in RL-tuned LLMs.
> - The sign of Token Hidden Reward (THR) directly correlates with the exploration-exploitation trade-off: positive THR reinforces confidence in correct outputs (exploitation), while negative THR preserves probability mass for alternative outputs (exploration).
> - Token-level interventions, such as THR-guided reweighting, offer more effective and fine-grained control over exploration and exploitation in LLMs compared to coarser question-level reweighting or entropy-based methods that neglect cross-token interactions.
