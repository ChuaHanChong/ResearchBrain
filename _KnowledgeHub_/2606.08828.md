# Video2Sim2Real: Full-Stack Autonomous Dexterous Skill Acquisition from a Single Human Video

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed Video2Sim2Real, a full-stack framework that autonomously acquires dexterous manipulation skills for robots from a single human video. The system achieves an average real-world task success rate of 95.7% by integrating object-centric trajectory refinement with a decoupled sim-to-real transfer strategy for robust policy learning.

## Problem

- Existing methods struggle with perception errors from human hand/object occlusion and the embodiment gap when transferring human video demonstrations to robot dexterous manipulation.
- Learning robust contact-rich robot skills in simulation faces a significant sim-to-real gap, requiring extensive data collection or leading to unsafe behaviors.
- Acquiring dexterous manipulation skills typically demands extensive task-specific supervision, posing a scalability bottleneck for robot learning.

## Method

- An autonomous pipeline reconstructs a simulator-ready ==digital twin== and extracts robot and object motion priors using off-the-shelf foundation models.
- A novel object-centric refinement strategy optimizes robot configurations at key manipulation frames using simulator information, then interpolates to create coherent robot trajectories.
- A decoupled sim-to-real transfer approach employs imitation learning (IL) for keyframe pose distillation (geometry adaptation) and residual reinforcement learning (RL) for local finger adaptation (physics adaptation).

## Results

- The object-centric refinement strategy achieved a **91.4%** average task success rate and **100%** safety in simulation, outperforming RL-based baselines by large margins (e.g., baselines 1.9-**52.9%** success).
- The decoupled sim-to-real policy demonstrated a **95.7%** average real-world task success rate across seven diverse tasks, significantly surpassing a pre-trained pose estimator (**15.7%**) and pure IL/RL methods (3-**45.7%**).
- The framework enabled spatial generalization, successfully planning collision-free robot trajectories for novel object configurations in cluttered environments for 5 out of 7 tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Optimizing robot configurations at object-centric keyframes effectively aligns human task intent with robot capabilities, leading to smoother and safer refined trajectories while mitigating the embodiment gap.
> - Decoupling sim-to-real transfer into distinct IL for global geometry adaptation and residual RL for local physics adaptation significantly enhances robustness and real-world performance for contact-rich tasks.
> - Leveraging off-the-shelf foundation models enables autonomous digital twin reconstruction and motion prior extraction, reducing the need for extensive human supervision in the skill acquisition pipeline.
