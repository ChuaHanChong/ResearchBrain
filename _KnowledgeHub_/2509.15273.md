# Embodied Arena: A Comprehensive, Unified, and Evolving Evaluation Platform for Embodied AI

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from a global consortium, including Tianjin University and Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab, developed Embodied Arena, a comprehensive platform for evaluating Embodied AI agents, featuring a systematic capability taxonomy and an automated, LLM-driven data generation pipeline. This platform integrates over 22 benchmarks and 30 models, revealing that specialized embodied models often outperform general models on targeted tasks and identifying object and spatial perception as key performance bottlenecks.

## Problem

- The Embodied AI community lacks a systematic understanding of core capabilities, leading to fragmented research efforts.
- There is an absence of a unified and standardized evaluation system, making direct cross-benchmark comparison and overall progress assessment challenging.
- Embodied AI suffers from a bottleneck in scalable data acquisition, unlike the web-scale data available for large language models.

## Method

- A systematic Embodied Capability Taxonomy was established, defining 7 core capabilities and 25 fine-grained dimensions, which was used to map 22 diverse existing embodied benchmarks.
- A Unified Embodied Evaluation System was developed, integrating 22+ benchmarks and 30+ advanced models with standardized metrics and leaderboards for consistent cross-benchmark and capability-view comparisons.
- An LLM-driven Automated Data Generation Pipeline was implemented to create diverse, high-quality, and continuously evolving evaluation data, addressing data scarcity and preventing model overfitting.

## Results

- Specialized embodied models consistently achieve stronger performance on targeted, domain-specific benchmarks compared to massive closed-source general models of similar sizes.
- Reinforced Fine-tuning (RFT) consistently yields substantial performance improvements and new state-of-the-art results across various benchmarks for reasoning models.
- Embodied pointing is a critical capability that enhances fundamental embodied abilities and improves downstream task performance, although complex instructions for pointing remain a challenge for most models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Embodied reasoning capabilities are heavily dependent on fundamental embodied capabilities (e.g., object and spatial perception), which were identified as significant bottlenecks with lower average scores.
> - Agentic frameworks dramatically boost general models' success rates in navigation and task planning by leveraging existing foundation model capabilities, outperforming end-to-end approaches for long-horizon tasks.
> - A consistent scaling law for embodied tasks has yet to emerge, unlike LLMs, suggesting a need for more diverse data and consistent architectural designs to prevent overfitting to specific datasets.
