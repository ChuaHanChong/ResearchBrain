# Embodied Arena: A Comprehensive, Unified, and Evolving Evaluation Platform for Embodied AI

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from a global consortium, including Tianjin University and Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab, developed Embodied Arena, a comprehensive platform for evaluating Embodied AI agents, featuring a systematic capability taxonomy and an automated, LLM-driven data generation pipeline. This platform integrates over 22 benchmarks and 30 models, revealing that specialized embodied models often outperform general models on targeted tasks and identifying object and spatial perception as key performance bottlenecks.

## Problem

- The Embodied AI community lacks a systematic understanding of core capabilities, leading to fragmented research efforts.
- There is an absence of a unified and standardized evaluation system, making direct cross-benchmark comparison and overall progress assessment challenging.
- Embodied AI suffers from a bottleneck in scalable data acquisition, unlike the web-scale data available for large language models.

## Method

- A systematic Embodied Capability Taxonomy was established, defining 7 core capabilities and 25 fine-grained dimensions, which was used to map 22 diverse existing embodied benchmarks.
- A Unified Embodied Evaluation System was developed, integrating 22+ benchmarks and 30+ advanced models with standardized metrics and leaderboards for consistent cross-benchmark and capability-view comparisons.
- An LLM-driven Automated Data Generation Pipeline was implemented to create diverse, high-quality, and continuously evolving evaluation data, addressing data scarcity and preventing model overfitting.

## Results

- Specialized embodied models consistently achieve stronger performance on targeted, domain-specific benchmarks compared to massive closed-source general models of similar sizes.
- Reinforced Fine-tuning (RFT) consistently yields substantial performance improvements and new state-of-the-art results across various benchmarks for reasoning models.
- Embodied pointing is a critical capability that enhances fundamental embodied abilities and improves downstream task performance, although complex instructions for pointing remain a challenge for most models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Embodied reasoning capabilities are heavily dependent on fundamental embodied capabilities (e.g., object and spatial perception), which were identified as significant bottlenecks with lower average scores.
> - Agentic frameworks dramatically boost general models' success rates in navigation and task planning by leveraging existing foundation model capabilities, outperforming end-to-end approaches for long-horizon tasks.
> - A consistent scaling law for embodied tasks has yet to emerge, unlike LLMs, suggesting a need for more diverse data and consistent architectural designs to prevent overfitting to specific datasets.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

Embodied AI, or Embodied Intelligence, is positioned as a critical pathway toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), complementing the understanding and reasoning abilities of large foundation models (LFMs) by enabling AI agents to perceive, interact with, and effect change in the physical world. While recent advancements in multimodal foundation models, such as OpenVLA and π0, have demonstrated significant potential in embodied scenarios like generalist robotic manipulation and hierarchical planning, the paper argues that Embodied AI is "largely falling behind" the rapid pace of development seen in large language models (LLMs).

The paper identifies three essential challenges that hinder the progress and widespread deployment of Embodied AI:
1. **Lack of Systematic Understanding of Core Capabilities:** The community lacks a unified conceptual framework for the fundamental capabilities required by Embodied AI models, leading to fragmented research efforts without clear, shared objectives.
2. **Absence of Unified and Standardized Evaluation:** Numerous existing benchmarks for Embodied AI are disparate in terms of data formats, evaluation metrics, and targeted capabilities, making direct cross-benchmark comparison and overall progress assessment infeasible.
3. **Bottleneck in Scalable Data Acquisition:** Unlike LLMs, which thrive on web-scale data, Embodied AI suffers from a lack of automated and scalable methods for acquiring diverse and sufficient embodied data, a critical bottleneck for both model training and comprehensive evaluation.

Embodied Arena directly addresses these challenges by proposing a comprehensive, unified, and evolving evaluation platform. It positions itself as a foundational infrastructure necessary to bring the rigor, standardization, and scalability that have propelled LLM research to the Embodied AI domain. By integrating diverse benchmarks, proposing a capability taxonomy, and introducing an automated data generation pipeline, Embodied Arena aims to fill the critical gaps in evaluation and data scarcity, thereby accelerating research and fostering a more cohesive understanding of Embodied AI capabilities. This initiative seeks to provide the community with the tools to benchmark progress systematically, identify weaknesses, and collectively advance towards more capable embodied agents.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The motivation for developing Embodied Arena stems directly from the three critical challenges identified in the current Embodied AI research landscape. The authors contend that without a systematic understanding of capabilities, a unified evaluation system, and scalable data acquisition methods, Embodied AI will continue to lag behind other rapidly advancing fields of AI.

The key objectives of Embodied Arena are meticulously designed to tackle these motivations:

1. **To Establish a Systematic Embodied Capability Taxonomy:** The primary objective is to define and categorize the core capabilities necessary for Embodied AI. This taxonomy is intended to provide a systematic understanding of what Embodied AI models need to achieve, offering clear objectives for future research and facilitating unified evaluation across diverse tasks. It aims to move beyond isolated task-specific improvements towards a holistic view of embodied intelligence.
2. **To Develop a Unified Embodied Evaluation System:** This objective aims to bridge the gap created by fragmented and non-standardized benchmarks. Embodied Arena seeks to create a single, consistent evaluation infrastructure that can flexibly integrate existing and future benchmarks and models. This system will enable direct, fair, and transparent cross-benchmark comparison, a critical feature currently missing in the field.
3. **To Implement an LLM-driven Automated Data Generation Pipeline:** Recognizing the bottleneck of manual data collection, a core objective is to develop a scalable and automated method for generating diverse, high-quality embodied evaluation data. This approach is intended to ensure the continuous evolution, comprehensiveness, and diversity of the evaluation data, mirroring the data-rich environment that has fueled the success of LLMs.

Collectively, these objectives are motivated by the overarching goal of "breaking the three obstacles" to "pave the way for the advancement of Embodied AI research." By providing a comprehensive platform, leaderboards, and actionable insights, Embodied Arena aims to facilitate the establishment of clear research directions, pinpoint critical problems, and ultimately propel the progress of Embodied AI towards real-world applicability and general intelligence.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Embodied Arena adopts a three-pronged methodological approach, integrated into a unified platform, to address the identified challenges in Embodied AI evaluation and data scarcity.

#### 3.1. Systematic Embodied Capability Taxonomy

The foundation of Embodied Arena is a hierarchical taxonomy of embodied capabilities. This taxonomy is derived from insights in cognitive psychology, human experience, and existing embodied tasks, refined and expanded upon prior works. It spans three incremental levels:
- **Perception:** Encompassing Object Perception (Type, Property, State, Count), Spatial Perception (Relationship, Distance, Localization, Size), and Temporal Perception (Description, Order).
- **Reasoning:** Built upon perception, including Object Reasoning, Spatial Reasoning, Temporal Reasoning, Knowledge Reasoning, and Task Reasoning.
- **Task Execution:** High-level capabilities like Embodied Navigation (Object, Location, Instruction Navigation) and Embodied Task Planning (Basic, Visual Reference, Spatial Reference, Temporal Reference, Knowledge Reference Planning).
In total, the taxonomy comprises seven core capabilities dissected into 25 fine-grained dimensions. A crucial part of this methodology involves mapping 22 diverse existing embodied benchmarks to this systematic taxonomy, demonstrating its comprehensiveness and utility for unified evaluation.

#### 3.2. Unified Embodied Evaluation System

This system is designed to standardize evaluation across disparate benchmarks and models:
- **Tasks, Benchmarks, and Data:** The platform currently covers three core embodied tasks: Embodied Question Answering (2D and 3D), Embodied Navigation, and Embodied Task Planning. It integrates 22+ high-quality benchmarks, selected for their academic influence and coverage of complementary capability dimensions, totaling over 64,000 task instances. A "Embodied Wiki" provides detailed benchmark information.
- **Model Support:** Embodied Arena supports a wide array of models, including 30+ advanced models from 20+ institutes. This includes general multimodal LLMs (e.g., OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Alibaba, ByteDance, Meta AI, Shanghai AI Lab, NVIDIA, Microsoft, ModelBest) and specialized Embodied AI models (categorized into 2D and 3D embodied models). Model integration is flexible, supporting API-based, parameter-based, and custom interfaces for both open-source and commercial models.
- **Infrastructure:** A robust, modular, and extensible infrastructure ensures consistent and reproducible evaluations. It provides a standardized evaluation framework with uniform input/output formats, abstracts benchmark-specific details, and offers professional experiment tracking and management for detailed performance analysis and metadata logging.
- **Evaluation Methods:**
 - **Evaluation Metrics:** Task-specific metrics are employed:
 - **Embodied QA:** Exact Matching Accuracy for precise responses; Fuzzy Matching (CIDEr, BLEU, ROUGE, MRA) and LLM-based evaluation for natural language generation.
 - **Embodied Navigation:** Success Rate and Path Length Weighted Success Rate (SPL) for efficiency.
 - **Embodied Task Planning:** Task Completion Success Rate.
 - **Scoring Rules:** Detailed algorithms are provided for calculating scores for individual capability dimensions, total scores for benchmarks, and aggregate scores for fine-grained and core capabilities across multiple benchmarks, allowing for both benchmark-view and capability-view leaderboards.
- **Leaderboards:** Three types of leaderboards (Embodied Q&A, Navigation, Task Planning) are published, offering two orthogonal views: a benchmark view (model rankings on each benchmark) and a capability view (model rankings against each of the seven core capabilities). These leaderboards are updated monthly, with transparent submission policies to ensure fairness and engagement.

#### 3.3. LLM-driven Automated Data Generation for Embodied AI Evaluation

This novel framework addresses the limitations of static, unscalable, and low-diversity handcrafted benchmarks. It operates through two key modules:
- **Automated Scenario Generation:** Constructs realistic and diverse multi-room indoor environments. This hierarchical process involves:
 - **Floor Planning:** Defining room types and spatial relationships.
 - **Functional Zoning:** Dividing rooms into activity-specific areas.
 - **Layout Planning:** Populating zones with assets and optimizing layouts.
 LLMs and VLMs guide these decisions to ensure common sense and semantic coherence. High-fidelity rendering produces RGB, depth maps, and object graphs, with domain randomization for variability. A "targeted scene mode" allows users to specify desired scene characteristics.
- **Capability-Oriented Data Generation & Evolution:** Procedural pipelines generate datasets for each of the seven core capabilities. These pipelines define task templates, load assets, execute scripted procedures, and leverage simulator access for ground-truth annotations and automated filtering.
 - **Difficulty Ladder:** Data is generated along three dimensions: Scene Complexity, Language Complexity, and Task Complexity, enabling granular diagnosis and progressive fine-tuning.
 - **Data Evolution Mechanism:** By regularly analyzing model performance gaps, the platform autonomously generates new, targeted, and challenging data to keep the evaluation set comprehensive and evolving, preventing overfitting. A sampling-based human inspection method is used to filter ambiguous cases and ensure high data quality.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The comprehensive evaluation conducted on Embodied Arena's leaderboards yielded nine significant findings, shedding light on the current state and future directions of Embodied AI research:

1. **Embodied vs. General Models:** While massive closed-source general models (e.g., GPT-o3, Gemini-2.5-Pro) achieve strong overall performance due to their scale and extensive pre-training, specialized embodied models of similar sizes consistently outperform general models on targeted, domain-specific benchmarks. This highlights the power of high-quality embodied datasets and targeted fine-tuning for achieving competitive performance, even with constrained resources.
2. **Benchmark Limitations & Overfitting:** Models exhibit significant performance variations across different benchmarks, indicating that individual, limited-scope benchmarks are insufficient and biased. This suggests that models tend to overfit to benchmark-specific data rather than developing comprehensive embodied capabilities. Only a few large-scale general models demonstrate relatively balanced performance across diverse tasks.
3. **Dependency on Fundamental Capabilities:** Embodied reasoning capabilities are strongly dependent on fundamental embodied capabilities (object perception, spatial perception, temporal perception, embodied knowledge). Object perception and spatial perception are identified as major bottlenecks, with lower average scores and a strong positive correlation with overall reasoning ability. This hierarchical decline in capabilities is attributed to imbalanced pre-training data, dependencies among capabilities, and underdeveloped methods for enhancing embodied reasoning.
4. **Capabilities vs. Downstream Task Performance:** There's a strong positive correlation between a model's fundamental and advanced embodied capabilities and its performance on downstream tasks (navigation, task planning). Notably, this correlation is only moderate in end-to-end (E2E) approaches but becomes significantly stronger when general models are integrated into task-oriented agentic frameworks. Agentic frameworks dramatically boost general models' success rates in navigation and task planning, suggesting their effectiveness in leveraging foundational capabilities.
5. **Scaling Law Not Fully Emerged:** A consistent scaling law for embodied tasks, analogous to those in LLMs, has yet to be observed. While increasing parameters can improve performance locally for specific models and benchmarks, this trend is inconsistent across different embodied models and capabilities. Similarly, increasing task-specific data improves performance but often leads to overfitting, indicating insufficient diversity and scope in current datasets. This underscores the need for consistent architectural designs and scalable, diverse data generation methods.
6. **Reinforced Fine-tuning (RFT) for Reasoning:** Reasoning models fine-tuned with Reinforced Fine-tuning (RFT) consistently achieve significant performance improvements and often set new state-of-the-art results across various benchmarks (e.g., GPT-o3, Space-R, Embodied-R1). RFT appears to enable models to activate and combine fundamental perceptual abilities into complex reasoning skills. However, whether RFT can yield stronger out-of-distribution generalization compared to Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT) remains an open question.
7. **3D Representations and Language Alignment:** 3D representations are crucial for embodied understanding but face challenges in language modality alignment. Models that strategically integrate 2D visual features with 3D spatial priors (e.g., through multi-view synthesis or coordinate injection) significantly outperform those relying solely on naive 3D data processing. This highlights the current limitation of native 3D foundation models and suggests future research should focus on achieving deeper alignment between native 3D information and language.
8. **Embodied Navigation: E2E vs. Agentic Frameworks:** Embodied Navigation can be approached via end-to-end (E2E) or agentic frameworks. E2E VLN-specialized models, leveraging historical frames and domain-specific training, substantially outperform general models. Agentic frameworks, by contrast, achieve better performance for long-horizon tasks by integrating extensible modular architectures and external knowledge, effectively amplifying existing foundation model capabilities without requiring costly retraining.
9. **Embodied Pointing as a Critical Capability:** Embodied pointing is vital for enhancing fundamental embodied capabilities and improving downstream task performance. Dedicated supervised or reinforced fine-tuning for pointing tasks significantly boosts performance on these tasks and can lead to improved generalization on out-of-distribution benchmarks (e.g., Embodied-R1). However, pointing tasks with complex instructions remain a major challenge for most models, revealing a trade-off between specialization and broader understanding. Pointing serves as a grounding mechanism, anchoring abstract language to precise spatial coordinates, thereby strengthening cognitive integration.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Embodied Arena represents a pivotal contribution to the field of Embodied AI, poised to significantly influence its future trajectory. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized across several dimensions:

1. **Standardization and Unification:** By providing the first comprehensive, unified, and evolving evaluation platform, Embodied Arena directly addresses the critical need for standardized metrics and cross-benchmark comparison. This unification allows researchers to objectively assess and compare models from diverse origins and approaches, overcoming the fragmentation that has previously hampered progress. This is analogous to how standardized benchmarks and leaderboards have driven advancements in fields like computer vision (ImageNet) and natural language processing (GLUE, SuperGLUE).
2. **Guidance for Research and Development:**
 - **Clear Objectives:** The systematic Embodied Capability Taxonomy offers a robust framework, providing clear objectives for researchers and helping them identify specific capability gaps (e.g., in object and spatial perception). This shifts the focus from isolated task performance to holistic capability development.
 - **Actionable Insights:** The leaderboards, with their benchmark and capability views, provide real-time, multifaceted insights into model strengths and weaknesses. The nine summarized findings offer empirical evidence and illuminate critical research problems (e.g., the potential of RFT, the need for better 3D-language alignment, the power of agentic frameworks), thereby guiding future research directions and resource allocation.
 - **Preventing Overfitting:** The evolving nature of the evaluation data, driven by automated generation and model performance analysis, is designed to prevent models from overfitting to static benchmarks. This encourages the development of more generalizable and robust embodied agents.
3. **Acceleration of Progress:** By overcoming the bottlenecks of unscalable data acquisition and non-standardized evaluation, Embodied Arena aims to accelerate the entire Embodied AI research cycle. Researchers can more efficiently train, evaluate, and iterate on models, leading to faster innovation and development of more capable and adaptable embodied agents.
4. **Community Building and Collaboration:** The platform's open evaluation access, support for benchmark contributions, and transparent methodologies foster a collaborative ecosystem. It invites diverse models and benchmarks from academia and industry to join, creating a centralized hub for community engagement and collective advancement. This open science approach encourages reproducibility and broad participation.
5. **Bridging the Gap to Foundation Models:** By drawing parallels with the success of LLMs and adopting similar paradigms for systematic evaluation and scalable data generation, Embodied Arena acts as a crucial bridge, helping Embodied AI leverage lessons learned from the foundation model era. It pushes the field towards developing its own "web-scale" data and rigorous evaluation standards.
6. **Future-Proof Design:** The LLM-driven automated data generation ensures the platform remains relevant and challenging as models improve, continuously pushing the boundaries of what embodied agents can achieve. The explicit mention of future extensions to include "more comprehensive manipulation tasks and closed-loop evaluation capabilities" indicates a forward-looking vision for the platform's long-term evolution and impact.

In essence, Embodied Arena serves as a critical infrastructure project, providing the scaffolding necessary for Embodied AI research to mature, standardize, and scale, ultimately bringing us closer to the vision of truly intelligent agents capable of perceiving, reasoning, and acting effectively in the physical world.
