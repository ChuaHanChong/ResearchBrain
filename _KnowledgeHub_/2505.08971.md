# Prioritizing Image-Related Tokens Enhances Vision-Language Pre-Training

> [!summary] Summary
> A novel training approach called PRIOR enhances vision-language pre-training by intelligently weighting image-related tokens during model optimization, achieving 19% relative improvement for heterogeneous LVLMs and 8% for unified LVLMs across standard benchmarks while reducing hallucination risks through better visual grounding.

## Problem

- Standard vision-language pre-training treats all caption tokens equally, wasting compute on irrelevant tokens
- Models tend to overfit to caption noise and generate hallucinations not grounded in visual content

## Method

- Use a ==text-only LLM== to identify and prioritize image-related tokens during training
- Implement ==reweighted next-token prediction loss== based on token importance scores
- Apply framework to both ==heterogeneous== and ==unified LVLM== architectures

## Results

- **19%** relative improvement for heterogeneous LVLMs across vision-language benchmarks
- **8%** improvement for unified LVLMs with better training stability
- Superior scaling properties with higher scaling coefficients compared to baseline
- Reduced performance fluctuations and prevention of significant drops during training

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Tokens that are difficult for text-only LLMs to predict tend to be more visually relevant
> - Importance sampling provides theoretical foundation for token reweighting approach
> - Method maintains strong performance on both image-related and unrelated tokens
