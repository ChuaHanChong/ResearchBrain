# EvoVid: Temporal-Centric Self-Evolution for Video Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> EvoVid introduces a self-evolving framework for Video Large Language Models, enabling them to improve video understanding and reasoning directly from raw, unannotated videos. The approach uses temporal-centric reward signals for question generation and answer grounding, yielding consistent performance gains across benchmarks, such as a 19.3% improvement on TempCompass for the Qwen2.5-VL-3B model, and outperforming supervised fine-tuning in some comparisons.

## Problem

- Video Large Language Models (Video-LLMs) heavily depend on costly, scalable, and human-expert-limited human-annotated data for training, particularly challenging for complex video content.
- Existing self-evolving frameworks, primarily for static modalities, often generate temporally insensitive questions due to generic reward signals that do not explicitly emphasize temporal dependencies.
- The absence of verifiable temporal-centric learning signals prevents Video-LLMs from autonomously learning sophisticated temporal order, causality, and state transitions from raw video data.

## Method

- A temporal-centric self-evolving framework that employs an iterative Questioner–Solver self-play mechanism, where a single base model learns directly from raw, unannotated videos.
- A temporal-aware Questioner reward (`r_Q_temp`) is introduced, which positively reinforces questions that require an understanding of temporal order by penalizing queries answerable from temporally perturbed video frames.
- A temporal-grounded Solver reward (`r_S_temp`) provides explicit temporal supervision, compelling the Solver to not only answer correctly but also localize the relevant temporal segments using automatically sampled K-frame windows as pseudo ground-truth.

## Results

- EvoVid demonstrated consistent performance gains across all four Qwen-VL base models and six video understanding benchmarks, achieving up to a **19.3%** improvement on TempCompass for the Qwen2.5-VL-3B model.
- The framework, using Qwen2.5-VL-7B, outperformed supervised fine-tuning (SFT) by an average of **1.0%** and achieved the best performance among all baselines on benchmarks like VideoMMMU, MMVU, and TempCompass.
- Ablation studies confirmed the positive contributions of both temporal-aware Questioner reward (+**0.7%** average gain) and temporal-grounded Solver reward (+**0.6%** average gain), with their combined effect leading to a **1.4%** increase over the VANILLA self-evolving baseline.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly integrating temporal sensitivity into the reward functions of a self-evolving framework effectively guides Video-LLMs to acquire complex temporal reasoning skills without human annotation.
> - The co-evolution of a temporal-aware Questioner (generating more challenging and temporally dependent questions) and a temporal-grounded Solver (improving its ability to answer and temporally localize) leads to sustained performance enhancements.
> - The dual approach of using temporal perturbation for question generation and temporal segment grounding for answer verification are complementary strategies that foster autonomous temporal understanding in video models.
