# RA-DP: Rapid Adaptive Diffusion Policy for Training-Free High-frequency Robotics Replanning

> [!summary] Summary
> RA-DP enables high-frequency replanning and training-free adaptation for diffusion-based robotic policies, achieving up to 5 times faster control loops and robustly handling unseen dynamic obstacles in both simulated and real-world environments.

## Problem

- Existing diffusion-based robotic policies operate at low replanning frequencies (e.g., ~37 Hz), insufficient for rapid decision-making in dynamic environments.
- Current diffusion policies struggle to adapt to unforeseen environmental feedback or novel conditions during inference without extensive retraining or specific classifier training.

## Method

- Introduces an action queue mechanism that continuously processes and denoises a sequence of actions, allowing for the execution of one high-quality action per denoising step, enabling high-frequency replanning.
- Implements a modified training paradigm for the U-Net that independently perturbs individual actions with varying noise levels and trains the model to predict clean actions, better preparing it for the unique action queue inference.
- Integrates a training-free, loss-based guidance module that uses a user-defined differentiable energy function to compute gradients for adapting to arbitrary unforeseen conditions or constraints during inference.

## Results

- RA-DP achieves significantly higher replanning frequencies (approx. **130.9** Hz for state-based and **82.3** Hz for point cloud-based policies), **3.5** to 5 times faster than traditional Diffusion Policy baselines.
- It maintains or improves task success rates on standard MetaWorld benchmarks (e.g., **66.1%** success rate for state-based policies compared to DP's **63.2%**).
- RA-DP demonstrates superior adaptation to unseen static and dynamic obstacles in simulation (**63.3%** and **45.0%** success rates respectively), outperforming a guided baseline, and successfully performs training-free obstacle avoidance on a real Franka arm.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling the iterative denoising process from the control loop through an action queue enables high-frequency replanning without compromising the quality of executed actions.
> - Training a diffusion model with independent noise levels for each action and directly predicting clean actions during training better aligns with and improves performance in a rapid, dynamic inference scheme.
> - Utilizing differentiable, user-defined loss functions for guidance allows diffusion models to adapt to unforeseen environmental constraints in real-time without requiring retraining or specific data.
