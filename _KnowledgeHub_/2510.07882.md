# Towards Proprioception-Aware Embodied Planning for Dual-Arm Humanoid Robots

> [!summary] Summary
> The research introduces DualTHOR, a physically realistic simulation platform for dual-arm humanoid robots, and Proprio-MLLM, a multimodal large language model that incorporates proprioceptive information. Proprio-MLLM achieved an average 19.75% improvement in planning performance over baseline MLLMs for long-horizon dual-arm tasks within the new simulator.

## Problem

- A scarcity of high-level planning simulators specifically designed for dual-arm humanoid robots, leading to a lack of physically realistic training data for complex, long-horizon tasks.
- Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) exhibit insufficient embodiment awareness, struggling with dual-arm selection logic, body state adjustments, and identifying physically plausible interaction points.
- Current MLLMs struggle with contingency planning and re-planning in uncertain real-world scenarios due to a lack of rich, contingency-aware training data.

## Method

- ==Developed DualTHOR==, a Unity-based simulation platform for dual-arm humanoid robots (Unitree H1, Agibot X1) featuring physically realistic bimanual manipulation, continuous states, and a stochastic contingency mechanism.
- Proposed Proprio-MLLM, an extension of Qwen 2.5-VL-7B-Ins, which integrates proprioceptive information through a novel motion-based position embedding (MPE) and a cross-spatial encoder (CSE).
- Created a robot motion-image-text alignment dataset by retargeting human motion data to robots and replaying these motions in DualTHOR, used for training Proprio-MLLM across motion, image, and text modalities.

## Results

- Proprio-MLLM achieved the highest overall success rates across all task categories and robot types in DualTHOR, showing an average **19.75%** improvement in planning performance compared to state-of-the-art MLLM baselines like DAG-Plan.
- The model demonstrated a reduction in navigation, body adjustment, and logical failure types due to more effective integration of image perception and proprioceptive states, leading to more coherent dual-arm plans.
- Proprio-MLLM, particularly when augmented with reflection prompts, exhibited improved re-planning abilities in tasks with stochastic outcomes (medium and hard difficulty), outperforming other methods in these scenarios.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly integrating proprioceptive information into MLLMs significantly improves their ability to reason about dual-arm selection logic and adapt body states for coherent, physically grounded plans.
> - High-fidelity simulation environments like DualTHOR, supporting continuous states and contingencies, are crucial for generating diverse, physically realistic data needed to train robust embodied AI for dual-arm humanoid robots.
> - Specific architectural components, such as motion-based position embedding and cross-spatial encoders, are vital for enhancing an MLLM's spatial reasoning and understanding of a robot's interaction range and body state.
