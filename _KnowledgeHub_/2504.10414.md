# HUMOTO: A 4D Dataset of Mocap Human Object Interactions

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the University of Texas at Austin and Adobe Research introduced HUMOTO, a 4D animation dataset featuring high-fidelity human-object interactions captured for purposeful tasks. The dataset demonstrates superior physical accuracy with the lowest foot sliding (0.958 cm) and object penetration (0.0068 cm) among comparable datasets, along with detailed hand articulations, as confirmed by quantitative benchmarks and human perceptual studies.

## Problem

- Existing 4D human-object interaction datasets lacked fidelity, often showing physical inaccuracies like object penetration and unnatural hand poses.
- Prior datasets rarely captured complex, multi-object interactions or full-body movements with detailed hand articulations necessary for fine manipulation.
- Many datasets contained isolated, purposeless movements, limiting their utility for learning coherent, task-oriented human activities.

## Method

- Developed a ==Scene-Driven LLM Scripting== framework to generate multi-level, purposeful interaction narratives for diverse daily activities.
- Utilized a multi-sensor capture system with Rokoko smart-suits for full-body and detailed hand tracking, along with artist-modeled 3D objects.
- Implemented a rigorous data processing and two-stage quality assurance protocol by professional artists to ensure physical accuracy and naturalness, including refined object tracking and manual cleaning.

## Results

- The HUMOTO dataset comprises 735 sequences (7,875 seconds) of diverse, purposeful human-object interactions involving 63 artist-modeled objects with detailed multi-level text annotations.
- Achieved an order of magnitude improvement in physical accuracy, demonstrating the lowest foot sliding (**0.958** cm) and object penetration (**0.0068** cm) compared to existing datasets.
- A human perceptual study confirmed HUMOTO's superior quality, receiving the highest scores across all categories (mean **4.78**/5 overall) and strong preference in pairwise comparisons.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Meticulous artist-driven modeling and multi-stage quality assurance are essential to achieve unprecedented fidelity in human-object interaction datasets.
> - Leveraging LLMs for scene-driven scripting provides a scalable method to generate diverse and purposeful interaction narratives, enhancing semantic richness.
> - The precise capture of detailed hand poses and the elimination of physical inaccuracies are critical for enabling realistic generative AI and robust robotic manipulation.
