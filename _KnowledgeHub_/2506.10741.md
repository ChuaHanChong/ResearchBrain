# PosterCraft: Rethinking High-Quality Aesthetic Poster Generation in a Unified Framework

> [!summary] Summary
> University of Hong Kong and Meituan researchers develop PosterCraft, a unified framework that abandons modular design pipelines for aesthetic poster generation, instead optimizing a single diffusion model (Flux-dev) through a four-stage cascaded workflow that achieves near-commercial quality with 0.787 text recall and 0.774 F-score (approaching Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen's 0.798/0.786), significantly outperforming open-source baselines like SD3.5 (0.565/0.542) through specialized datasets including Text-Render-2M for scalable text rendering, HQ-Poster-100K with region-aware calibration that applies differential weighting (1.0 for backgrounds, 0.6 for large text, 0.2 for small text), Poster-Preference-100K for Direct Preference Optimization reinforcement learning, and Poster-Reflect-120K for vision-language feedback refinement, demonstrating that comprehensive workflow optimization can unlock sophisticated design capabilities in foundation models without architectural modifications while consistently producing visually coherent posters with accurate text integration across diverse prompts.

## Problem

- Existing poster generation methods rely on fragmented modular pipelines with separate layout planning and content generation, leading to aesthetic inconsistency and limited visual quality
- Foundation models struggle with precise text rendering and maintaining stylistic coherence across diverse poster elements
- Lack of large-scale, high-quality datasets specifically designed for aesthetic poster generation has hindered development of robust end-to-end systems
- Previous approaches impose rigid layout constraints that restrict the model's expressive freedom and creative potential

## Method

- Unified framework that optimizes a single diffusion model through four specialized stages rather than using separate modules for different design components
- Automated construction of four targeted datasets: Text-Render-2M for text rendering, **HQ**-Poster-100K for poster fine-tuning, Poster-Preference-100K for reinforcement learning, and Poster-Reflect-120K for feedback refinement
- Region-aware calibration during training that applies different loss weights based on text region importance to balance accuracy with artistic integrity
- Integration of Direct Preference Optimization and vision-language feedback mechanisms to capture higher-order aesthetic trade-offs and enable iterative refinement

## Results

- Achieves **0.787** text recall and **0.774** F-score, significantly outperforming open-source baselines (SD3.5: **0.565**/**0.542**, Flux1.dev: **0.723**/**0.707**) and approaching commercial leader Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen (**0.798**/**0.786**)
- User studies with twenty experienced poster designers consistently show superior performance over all open-source baselines and competitive results against proprietary systems across aesthetic value, prompt alignment, and text accuracy
- Ablation studies confirm each workflow stage contributes meaningfully: text rendering optimization improves OCR accuracy, region-aware calibration enhances stylistic coherence, reinforcement learning boosts human preference, and feedback refinement enables iterative quality improvement
- Demonstrates consistent generation of visually coherent posters with accurate text integration, natural font blending, and strong prompt adherence across diverse design requirements and complexity levels

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Comprehensive workflow optimization can unlock sophisticated design capabilities in existing foundation models without requiring complex architectural modifications
> - Region-aware training that differentially weights text and background regions (1.0/0.6/0.2 weighting scheme) effectively balances text accuracy with overall artistic coherence
> - Combining aesthetic preference learning through reinforcement learning with multimodal feedback refinement addresses both low-level text rendering and high-level design harmony challenges
> - Automated dataset construction pipelines using MLLMs for filtering, captioning, and preference annotation can generate high-quality training data at scale for specialized visual design tasks

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of automated visual design, particularly image generation, has seen rapid advancements, moving from early GAN- and VAE-based models to more sophisticated diffusion-based frameworks. However, the paper identifies a critical, yet underexplored, challenge: **aesthetic poster generation.**

Current limitations in the research landscape are multi-faceted:

- **Specialized Focus of Existing Generative Models:** Many existing approaches for image generation primarily focus on foundational tasks like basic text rendering (e.g., TextDiffuser, DesignDiffusion) or the creation of simpler, domain-specific design images such as greeting cards or product advertisements. These methods often fall short in producing the high-quality, aesthetically compelling outputs required for posters, which demand a complex synthesis of content, form, and communicative intent.
- **Drawbacks of Modular Design Paradigms:** Recent attempts at aesthetic poster generation have largely adopted a modular pipeline. This typically involves a Vision-Language Model (VLM) acting as a layout planner (e.g., LayoutGPT, PosterLlama, PosterLLaVa), which then provides constraints for a separate generative model. While this offers some control, the authors argue it fundamentally leads to:
 - **Lack of Aesthetic Consistency:** Decoupling layout and content generation undermines visual and stylistic coherence.
 - **Limited Visual Quality:** The reliance on accurate and robust VLM outputs, combined with the fragmented design process, imposes an upper bound on visual quality.
- **Inadequacy of General Foundation Models:** While powerful foundation models (like Stable Diffusion 3.5, Flux) excel at generating complex natural images, they struggle with the specific requirements of aesthetic posters, such as precise text rendering, abstract artistic content creation, and maintaining holistic stylistic coherence across diverse elements.
- **Absence of Targeted Datasets:** A significant bottleneck identified is the lack of large-scale, versatile datasets specifically tailored for aesthetic poster generation, which has hindered the development of fully generative solutions.

PosterCraft directly addresses these limitations by proposing a **unified framework** that explicitly abandons prior modular pipelines and rigid layouts. Instead of incremental improvements to individual components, it seeks to explore an end-to-end generation process that allows the model to "freely explore coherent, visually compelling compositions." This positions PosterCraft as a significant departure from prevailing modular approaches, aiming to achieve a new level of integration and aesthetic quality in automated poster design. It leverages the capabilities of existing foundation models but optimizes them through a comprehensive workflow and specialized data, rather than through complex architectural modifications.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of PosterCraft is to **rethink high-quality aesthetic poster generation** by developing a unified framework that overcomes the inherent limitations of current modular and simplistic generative approaches. The authors are motivated by the recognition that aesthetic poster design is a multifaceted challenge demanding not only accurate text rendering but also the seamless integration of abstract artistic content, striking layouts, and overall stylistic harmony.

The key objectives and motivations can be summarized as follows:

- **Achieve Unified, End-to-End Generation:** The primary motivation is to move beyond the fragmented, modular design paradigms prevalent in the field. Instead of separate modules for layout, background, and text, PosterCraft aims for a single, comprehensive system that can directly produce high-quality, fully rendered posters from a textual input, ensuring holistic integration of all visual elements. This unified approach is expected to inherently improve visual consistency and aesthetic appeal.
- **Elevate Aesthetic Quality:** Existing methods are deemed insufficient for generating "aesthetically compelling outputs." The authors aim to produce posters with a strong artistic appeal, striking layouts, and overall stylistic harmony that can rival professional designs. They contend that "incremental, component-level improvements alone are insufficient to achieve significant aesthetic gains."
- **Enhance Text Rendering Accuracy and Integration:** Precise and stylistically coherent text rendering is a persistent bottleneck in generative image models. A key objective is to significantly improve text fidelity, alignment, and accuracy, ensuring text is not only readable but also seamlessly integrated into the poster's overall aesthetic.
- **Overcome Data Scarcity:** A major limiting factor in the development of robust generative models for aesthetic posters has been the absence of large-scale, versatile datasets. A core objective is to construct a suite of specialized, high-quality datasets tailored for each stage of their proposed workflow through fully automated pipelines, thereby enabling robust training and facilitating future research.
- **Develop a Scalable and Flexible Workflow:** The team seeks to optimize the generation process through a carefully designed, cascaded workflow that systematically addresses different challenges (text rendering, poster fine-tuning, aesthetic preferences, and feedback refinement). This workflow is designed to be compatible with existing foundation models without requiring complex architectural modifications, making it flexible and adaptable to future advances in base models.
- **Achieve Competitive Performance:** A clear goal is to significantly outperform existing open-source baselines and achieve competitive results when compared against state-of-the-art commercial systems, thereby demonstrating the practical viability and advanced capabilities of their unified framework.

In essence, PosterCraft is motivated by the desire to push the boundaries of automated visual design, moving towards an era where AI can autonomously create complex, artistic, and visually consistent posters, mimicking the creative process of human designers more closely.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

PosterCraft proposes a novel, unified framework that rethinks aesthetic poster generation by optimizing a high-capacity diffusion model through a cascaded workflow, rather than relying on modular designs or intricate architectural modifications. This approach enables holistic integration of textual content, visual elements, and layout considerations, leveraging vision-language feedback for enhanced coherence and aesthetic appeal. The methodology is structured into four critical stages, each supported by a specifically constructed dataset.

#### 3.1. Unified Workflow Philosophy

The core idea is to employ a standard diffusion backbone (initialized from Flux-dev) and optimize its performance for poster generation through a comprehensive training pipeline. This contrasts sharply with previous methods that impose explicit layout constraints or depend on external VLM-based designers, which inherently restrict a model's expressive freedom. PosterCraft aims to unlock the full potential of the diffusion model via workflow optimization, maintaining compatibility with existing techniques.

#### 3.2. Four Critical Stages and Specialized Datasets

Each stage targets a key challenge in aesthetic poster generation:

- **Stage 1: Scalable Text Rendering Optimization**
 - **Problem Addressed:** Persistent bottleneck of accurate text rendering, hindered by scarce large-scale, high-quality text datasets and plain backgrounds in existing ones.
 - **Dataset:** **Text-Render-2M** (2 million samples).
 - *Construction:* Fully automated pipeline generating diverse text (content, size, count, placement, rotation) rendered crisply onto high-quality, diverse backgrounds. Each text instance is paired with precise captions seamlessly merged with image captions.
 - **Approach:** Fine-tune the foundation model (Flux-dev) on Text-Render-2M using the **flow matching loss** (Equation 1). This encourages the model to match the true data flow, significantly improving text rendering accuracy and alignment across various text types (typography, plain-text scenes, long-text posters).

- **Stage 2: High-quality Poster Fine-tuning**
 - **Problem Addressed:** Developing a high-quality dataset and fine-tuning strategy for overall poster style and harmony between text and background.
 - **Dataset:** **HQ-Poster-100K** (100K meticulously filtered posters with masks and captions).
 - *Construction:* Automated filtering pipeline using:
 - MD5 hash to eliminate exact duplicates.
 - MLLM-based scoring (using InternVL2.5-8B-MPO) to remove posters with extensive credit/billing blocks.
 - Perceptual hashing to remove visually similar posters.
 - Gemini2.5-Flash for captioning.
 - HPS scoring (Human Preference Score v2) to ensure aesthetic quality, filtering scores below 0.25.
 - *Masks:* Precise text region masks (Major/Minor) are generated using Gemini2.5-Flash's OCR capabilities for region-aware calibration.
 - **Approach:** **Region-aware Calibration** during fine-tuning. A per-pixel weight map `w(p)` (Equation 3) is applied to the flow matching loss (Equation 4). This map assigns weights: 1.0 for non-text regions (full emphasis on visual style), 0.6 for large text masks (clarity and integration), and 0.2 for small text masks (downweight to prevent collapse from rendering errors). This balances text accuracy with overall artistic integrity.

- **Stage 3: Aesthetic-Text Reinforcement Learning**
 - **Problem Addressed:** Capturing higher-order aesthetic trade-offs (layout balance, color harmony, typographic cohesion) and alleviating residual text errors that pixel-level fidelity cannot fully address.
 - **Dataset:** **Poster-Preference-100K** (6K high-quality preference pairs from 100K generated samples).
 - *Construction:* 5 images generated per prompt using the model from Stage 2. HPSv2 evaluates human preference (best/worst selection). Gemini2.5-Flash verifies text rendering accuracy and style consistency, filtering inconsistent pairs (HPSv2 score difference > 0.025 and complete text accuracy in preferred posters).
 - **Approach:** Frames poster generation as a reinforcement learning problem. Optimizes the **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)** objective (Equation 6) using best-of-n selection. For each prompt, `n=5` variants are generated, and the "winning" (`x+`) and "losing" (`x-`) pairs are selected based on a combined aesthetic-text reward `R(x)`. This directly injects a unified preference signal into the diffusion training process.

- **Stage 4: Vision-language Feedback Refinement**
 - **Problem Addressed:** Refining content and aesthetic quality of initially generated posters through iterative critique.
 - **Dataset:** **Poster-Reflect-120K** (64K feedback pairs from 120K generated posters).
 - *Construction:* 6 posters generated per prompt using the preference-learned model. Gemini2.5-Flash selects the optimal poster (target image) and then generates structured feedback ("Poster Content Suggestions" and "Aesthetic Style Optimization Suggestions") by comparing a poster requiring optimization against the target.
 - **Approach:** **Joint Vision–Language Conditioning**. VLM fine-tuning (Internvl3-8B) is performed on VQA samples (original caption + generated poster requiring optimization -> Gemini-generated feedback). During diffusion model training, textual reflections (`f_c`, `f_s`) are jointly encoded via a text encoder (`E_t`) and concatenated with the original prompt embedding. Additionally, VAE-encoded image-level feedback (`v_img`) is injected into the conditioning branch (Equation 7). The model is then fine-tuned under the conditional flow matching objective (Equation 8), enabling iterative refinement based on multimodal feedback.

#### 3.3. Inference

During inference, PosterCraft takes a single user prompt, which can be enriched by an MLLM (e.g., Qwen3-based Magic Prompter). The fine-tuned model then generates a complete poster end-to-end, without additional inputs or modular designs. An optional VLM-based critique loop can further refine the output iteratively.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

PosterCraft demonstrates significant advancements in aesthetic poster generation, validated through extensive quantitative and qualitative evaluations against both open-source and commercial state-of-the-art models.

#### 4.1. Quantitative Results and Comparisons

- **Text Quality (Table 1):** PosterCraft achieves substantially higher text recall (0.787) and F-score (0.774), indicating a more complete capture of characters and overall text elements compared to open-source baselines like Flux1.dev (0.723 recall, 0.707 F-score) and SD3.5 (0.565 recall, 0.542 F-score). Its text accuracy (0.735) also surpasses open-source and competitive commercial solutions (Ideogram-v2: 0.680). Notably, PosterCraft's performance closely approaches that of the leading closed-source commercial system, Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen (0.798 recall, 0.786 F-score, 0.746 accuracy), showcasing near production-level competitiveness. This validates the effectiveness of the scalable text rendering optimization stage.

- **Overall Quality Metrics (User Study & Gemini-based Evaluation - Fig. 5 & 6):**
 - **User Study:** Evaluations by twenty experienced poster designers consistently show that PosterCraft outperforms all open-source baselines and several proprietary systems across multiple dimensions (e.g., aesthetic value, prompt alignment, text accuracy, overall preference). Its performance is marginally below that of the leading closed-source model, Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen.
 - **Gemini-based Evaluation:** Using Gemini2.0-Flash as an authoritative evaluator via carefully designed prompts, PosterCraft again demonstrates superior performance over most state-of-the-art generative models in aesthetic coherence, prompt alignment, text rendering, and overall preference. It performs nearly on par with Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen in text rendering, with only a slight gap in other aspects. These results collectively underscore the success of the unified workflow in enhancing overall poster quality.

#### 4.2. Qualitative Results and Comparisons

Visual comparisons (Fig. 7, 8, and 9) provide compelling evidence of PosterCraft's superiority:

- **Improved Text Rendering and Integration:** Competitors like Playground-v2.5 often fail completely in text rendering, while SD3.5 only achieves partial rendering. Even Flux1.dev and BAGEL, while improved, show textual errors. PosterCraft consistently produces cleaner layouts with accurate and legible text, and critically, its rendered fonts blend more naturally with the scene content, minimizing rendering errors.
- **Superior Aesthetic Appeal and Prompt Adherence:** Compared to other methods, PosterCraft generates posters with notably improved prompt alignment, especially under long-prompt conditions (Fig. 8). It excels in incorporating specific details from prompts and maintaining visual appeal, whereas other models might miss key elements or produce visually monotonous or disconnected outputs. For example, in Fig. 7, PosterCraft successfully depicts intricate details and stylistic requirements that other models struggle with.
- **Coherent Layouts and Theme Alignment:** Even with short prompts (Fig. 9), PosterCraft maintains a strong balance between visual appeal and accurate text rendering, seamlessly integrating title and scene elements in stylized compositions. Competing models often display legibility issues, omit key elements, or suffer from text-scene disconnection. PosterCraft's results stand out with vibrant layouts, strong theme adherence, and natural visual-text coherence.

#### 4.3. Ablation Study (Fig. 11)

The ablation study rigorously validates the contribution of each stage in the cascaded workflow:

- **Text Rendering Optimization:** Its removal leads to sustained drops in both OCR accuracy and human preference, confirming that the Text-Render-2M dataset and flow matching optimization are critical for text clarity and fidelity.
- **Region-aware Calibration:** Removing this component weakens stylistic coherence and introduces text bias, indicating its importance in balancing text and background integration by adapting to spatial context.
- **Aesthetic-Text Reinforcement Learning:** This stage strongly impacts human preference, demonstrating its crucial role in learning higher-order aesthetic cues like layout balance, color harmony, and typographic cohesion. It makes outputs more visually compelling and corrects residual text errors.
- **Vision-Language Feedback Refinement:** The absence of this stage leads to declines in both OCR accuracy and human preference, showcasing its efficacy in high-level semantic correction and iterative refinement of stylistic integration through multi-modal feedback.

In summary, the results convincingly demonstrate that PosterCraft significantly advances the state-of-the-art in aesthetic poster generation, particularly by achieving a unified approach that produces high-quality outputs with precise text, strong aesthetic appeal, and faithful prompt alignment, performing comparably to top commercial systems.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

PosterCraft represents a significant leap forward in the field of generative AI for visual design, offering several profound implications and potential impacts:

- **Redefining High-Quality Visual Content Generation:** By abandoning modular pipelines for a unified, cascaded workflow, PosterCraft moves the field closer to truly end-to-end design automation. This paradigm shift can lead to more visually consistent, aesthetically harmonious, and semantically coherent outputs than previously possible, setting a new benchmark for generative models in complex design tasks.
- **Democratization of Professional Design:** The ability to generate high-quality, aesthetic posters from simple text prompts has immense practical value. It can empower individuals and small businesses without professional design expertise or resources to create compelling marketing materials, event promotions, and visual content, significantly lowering the barrier to entry for effective visual communication.
- **Acceleration of Creative Industries:** For larger organizations in marketing, advertising, media, and entertainment, PosterCraft can serve as a powerful tool to accelerate the design process. It can generate initial concepts, multiple design variations, or even near-final designs much faster than traditional methods, allowing human designers to focus on higher-level creative direction and refinement.
- **Novel Data Contributions:** The construction and release of large-scale, specialized datasets (Text-Render-2M, HQ-Poster-100K, Poster-Preference-100K, Poster-Reflect-120K) are critical contributions to the research community. These datasets directly address a major bottleneck—the scarcity of high-quality, task-specific data—and will undoubtedly support and stimulate further research and development in aesthetic poster generation and broader text-to-image synthesis.
- **Validation of Comprehensive Optimization:** The success of PosterCraft, achieved by systematically optimizing a powerful foundation model through a series of cascaded stages, validates the effectiveness of this comprehensive workflow design. It demonstrates that significant performance gains can be unlocked in existing models by carefully crafting training strategies and data pipelines, even without fundamental architectural changes. This provides a blueprint for adapting general-purpose generative models to highly specialized and challenging domains.
- **Bridging Academia and Industry:** The strong collaboration between leading academic institutions (HKUST, Xiamen University, NUS) and a major tech company (Meituan) highlights the growing synergy in AI research. This partnership ensures that theoretical advancements are grounded in practical utility and benefit from real-world data and computational scale, leading to deployable solutions.
- **Foundation for Future Research:** The paper explicitly outlines limitations and future work, such as integrating more advanced backbone models, scaling up training with even larger and more diverse datasets, and extending the framework to support multilingual poster generation. This indicates that PosterCraft is not an endpoint but a robust foundation for continued innovation in automated visual content creation, particularly for complex, text-rich design applications.

In essence, PosterCraft significantly advances the frontier of AI-driven visual design, proving that sophisticated, aesthetically pleasing, and highly functional posters can be generated automatically, opening new avenues for creative expression and efficiency across various industries.
