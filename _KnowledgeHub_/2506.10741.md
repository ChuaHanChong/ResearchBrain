# PosterCraft: Rethinking High-Quality Aesthetic Poster Generation in a Unified Framework

> [!summary] Summary
> University of Hong Kong and Meituan researchers develop PosterCraft, a unified framework that abandons modular design pipelines for aesthetic poster generation, instead optimizing a single diffusion model (Flux-dev) through a four-stage cascaded workflow that achieves near-commercial quality with 0.787 text recall and 0.774 F-score (approaching Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen's 0.798/0.786), significantly outperforming open-source baselines like SD3.5 (0.565/0.542) through specialized datasets including Text-Render-2M for scalable text rendering, HQ-Poster-100K with region-aware calibration that applies differential weighting (1.0 for backgrounds, 0.6 for large text, 0.2 for small text), Poster-Preference-100K for Direct Preference Optimization reinforcement learning, and Poster-Reflect-120K for vision-language feedback refinement, demonstrating that comprehensive workflow optimization can unlock sophisticated design capabilities in foundation models without architectural modifications while consistently producing visually coherent posters with accurate text integration across diverse prompts.

## Problem

- Existing poster generation methods rely on fragmented modular pipelines with separate layout planning and content generation, leading to aesthetic inconsistency and limited visual quality
- Foundation models struggle with precise text rendering and maintaining stylistic coherence across diverse poster elements
- Lack of large-scale, high-quality datasets specifically designed for aesthetic poster generation has hindered development of robust end-to-end systems
- Previous approaches impose rigid layout constraints that restrict the model's expressive freedom and creative potential

## Method

- Unified framework that optimizes a single diffusion model through four specialized stages rather than using separate modules for different design components
- Automated construction of four targeted datasets: Text-Render-2M for text rendering, **HQ**-Poster-100K for poster fine-tuning, Poster-Preference-100K for reinforcement learning, and Poster-Reflect-120K for feedback refinement
- Region-aware calibration during training that applies different loss weights based on text region importance to balance accuracy with artistic integrity
- Integration of Direct Preference Optimization and vision-language feedback mechanisms to capture higher-order aesthetic trade-offs and enable iterative refinement

## Results

- Achieves **0.787** text recall and **0.774** F-score, significantly outperforming open-source baselines (SD3.5: **0.565**/**0.542**, Flux1.dev: **0.723**/**0.707**) and approaching commercial leader Gemini2.0-Flash-Gen (**0.798**/**0.786**)
- User studies with twenty experienced poster designers consistently show superior performance over all open-source baselines and competitive results against proprietary systems across aesthetic value, prompt alignment, and text accuracy
- Ablation studies confirm each workflow stage contributes meaningfully: text rendering optimization improves OCR accuracy, region-aware calibration enhances stylistic coherence, reinforcement learning boosts human preference, and feedback refinement enables iterative quality improvement
- Demonstrates consistent generation of visually coherent posters with accurate text integration, natural font blending, and strong prompt adherence across diverse design requirements and complexity levels

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Comprehensive workflow optimization can unlock sophisticated design capabilities in existing foundation models without requiring complex architectural modifications
> - Region-aware training that differentially weights text and background regions (1.0/0.6/0.2 weighting scheme) effectively balances text accuracy with overall artistic coherence
> - Combining aesthetic preference learning through reinforcement learning with multimodal feedback refinement addresses both low-level text rendering and high-level design harmony challenges
> - Automated dataset construction pipelines using MLLMs for filtering, captioning, and preference annotation can generate high-quality training data at scale for specialized visual design tasks
