# HumanoidBench: Simulated Humanoid Benchmark for Whole-Body Locomotion and Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> HumanoidBench introduces a high-fidelity, high-dimensional simulated benchmark for humanoid robots, featuring realistic models and a diverse suite of whole-body locomotion and manipulation tasks. It evaluates state-of-the-art reinforcement learning algorithms, demonstrating their current limitations and advocating for hierarchical approaches to tackle complex challenges.

## Problem

- Real-world humanoid hardware presents significant cost, fragility, and safety constraints, impeding rapid algorithmic development and testing.
- Existing robot learning benchmarks lack comprehensive integration of realistic whole-body locomotion and dexterous manipulation for humanoids.
- Prior simulated humanoid environments often rely on simplistic models, failing to capture the high-dimensional complexity and intricate physics of full humanoid systems.

## Method

- Researchers developed **HumanoidBench**, a ==MuJoCo-based== simulated environment featuring a realistic ==Unitree H1== humanoid model equipped with ==dexterous Shadow Hands==.
- The benchmark includes **27** diverse tasks, comprising **12** locomotion and **15** whole-body manipulation challenges, designed to test complex coordination.
- It offers high-dimensional observation (e.g., ==151D proprioception== + object states, ==448 taxel tactile sensing==) and a ==61-dimensional position-controlled action space== to reflect real humanoid complexity.

## Results

- State-of-the-art **RL** algorithms demonstrated **limited capability**, often failing to learn complex tasks or requiring **extensive training steps** for basic locomotion.
- The inclusion of dexterous hands and their corresponding **high-dimensional action space** drastically hindered learning across various tasks, even when hands were not directly involved.
- A **hierarchical RL** framework, incorporating pretrained low-level reaching policies, markedly enhanced performance for the **`push`** task and aided in grasping for the **`package`** task.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - State-of-the-art, flat reinforcement learning algorithms generally fail to solve most complex whole-body locomotion and manipulation tasks within HumanoidBench.
> - The high-dimensional action space introduced by dexterous hands, rather than merely observation complexity, poses a major challenge for algorithmic exploration and learning efficiency.
> - Structured approaches like Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning, utilizing robust pretrained low-level skills, can substantially improve performance on certain manipulation tasks.
