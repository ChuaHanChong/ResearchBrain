# MLLM-4D: Towards Visual-based Spatial-Temporal Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> MLLM-4D equips Multimodal Large Language Models with 4D spatial-temporal intelligence, enabling them to perceive and reason about dynamic 3D environments from 2D video inputs. It establishes new state-of-the-art performance on the MLLM4D-Bench benchmark with an average score of 72.7%, substantially surpassing proprietary models like Gemini 2.5 Pro.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) lack robust capabilities for 4D spatial-temporal intelligence, specifically perceiving and reasoning about the evolution of 3D space over time from purely visual inputs.
- A critical bottleneck is the scarcity of high-quality, large-scale 4D instructional data, as manual annotation is costly and unscalable.
- Existing MLLMs, even those with 3D understanding, are constrained to static scenes or rely on auxiliary 3D geometric inputs, struggling with dynamic environments without complex architectural changes.

## Method

- A cost-efficient, automated ==data curation pipeline== converts existing stereoscopic video datasets into large-scale, high-quality 4D instructional data, including ==MLLM4D-2M== (SFT), MLLM4D-R1-30k (RFT), and MLLM4D-Bench (evaluation).
- A ==two-stage post-training framework== is applied to standard MLLM architectures, avoiding architectural modifications.
- Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) builds foundational 4D understanding, followed by Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) that incorporates a ==Spatiotemporal Chain-of-Thought (ST-CoT)== prompting strategy and physics-grounded Spatiotemporal reward functions.

## Results

- MLLM-4D achieves state-of-the-art performance on the MLLM4D-Bench benchmark with an average score of **72.7%**, significantly outperforming proprietary models like Gemini 2.5 Pro (**46.6%**) and other open-source MLLMs.
- The framework demonstrates strong generalizability and robustness on the out-of-distribution VLM4D benchmark, maintaining superior performance over base models and specialized 3D spatial reasoning models.
- Ablation studies confirm that the scalable data curation and the hierarchical post-training strategy, particularly the physics-grounded Spatiotemporal Reward, are crucial for building robust 4D understanding and reasoning, with performance scaling observed with increased data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Leveraging existing stereoscopic video datasets with an automated pipeline is a scalable and cost-effective method to generate large volumes of high-quality 4D instructional data.
> - Standard MLLM architectures can acquire sophisticated 4D spatial-temporal reasoning capabilities through a hierarchical post-training strategy, negating the need for complex architectural modifications.
> - Integrating a Spatiotemporal Chain-of-Thought and physics-grounded rewards effectively grounds the model's reasoning in visual physics, leading to more interpretable and physically plausible 4D understanding.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of artificial intelligence, particularly in computer vision and natural language processing, has seen rapid advancements with the advent of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). MLLMs have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in integrating and processing information from various modalities, such as images, video, and audio, along with text. However, a significant frontier that remains largely unexplored and challenging for MLLMs is "4D spatial-temporal intelligence." This refers to the human-like ability to perceive and reason about the evolution of 3D space over time from purely visual inputs, which is crucial for understanding dynamic environments.

**Current State and Gaps:**
Historically, MLLMs have excelled in tasks within static 2D visual domains (e.g., image captioning, visual question answering). More recently, efforts have extended MLLMs to incorporate 3D spatial understanding. These "3D expertise MLLMs" often achieve this by relying on auxiliary inputs like 3D geometric data (point clouds) or 2.5D depth information, or by leveraging 3D reconstruction priors from monocular videos. However, as the authors point out, these models are largely constrained to static scenes with immobile objects and struggle to comprehend or reason about *evolving relationships* within dynamic 4D space. They lack the intrinsic cognitive dimension of time integration that humans naturally possess.

For dynamic scenarios, existing research faces two major bottlenecks:
1. **Data Scarcity**: Creating large-scale, high-quality 4D instructional data is exceptionally difficult and expensive. Manual annotation of dynamic 4D relationships is labor-intensive and results in small, benchmark-sized datasets (e.g., VLM4D, STi-Bench) that are insufficient for training large MLLMs.
2. **Model Limitations**: Current MLLM architectures, even those augmented for 3D, are not inherently designed or trained for robust 4D spatial-temporal reasoning from purely 2D inputs. Many proposed solutions involve significant architectural modifications or reliance on specialized 3D encoders, which can add complexity and limit scalability.

**MLLM-4D's Contribution to the Landscape:**
"MLLM-4D" directly addresses these critical gaps. It aims to endow standard MLLM architectures with advanced 4D spatial-temporal intelligence *without* requiring architectural modifications. This is a crucial distinction, as it suggests that the bottleneck isn't solely in the model's capacity but also in how it's trained and what data it learns from. By focusing on novel data curation techniques and a sophisticated post-training strategy, MLLM-4D pushes the research frontier from static 3D understanding to comprehensive, visual-based 4D reasoning about dynamic scenes. It positions itself as a foundational step towards interactive AI systems that operate effectively in constantly changing real-world environments.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of the MLLM-4D project is to imbue Multimodal Large Language Models with a human-like, vision-based 4D spatial-temporal intelligence. This means enabling MLLMs to perceive and accurately reason about the evolution of 3D space over time, solely from 2D video inputs. The authors are driven by the recognition that while MLLMs have achieved remarkable general intelligence across various tasks, their capability for dynamic spatial-temporal understanding and reasoning remains a significant limitation, despite its critical importance for advanced AI systems.

The motivation for MLLM-4D stems from two primary bottlenecks in the current state of MLLMs for 4D reasoning:

1. **Scarcity of High-Quality, Large-Scale 4D Instructional Data**: Current approaches to developing 4D reasoning benchmarks largely rely on manual annotations, which are extremely costly, time-consuming, and thus unscalable. The resulting datasets are typically small, limiting the ability to effectively fine-tune MLLMs that thrive on vast amounts of data. Without adequate data, MLLMs cannot learn the intricate dynamics of 3D objects and cameras evolving over time.
2. **Lack of Specialized and Scalable 4D-Aware MLLMs**: Existing MLLMs, even those designed for some form of spatial understanding, are primarily geared towards static scenes or depend on auxiliary 3D geometric inputs. These models struggle to generalize to dynamic environments, where objects and camera perspectives are constantly changing. Moreover, current methods often involve adding complex, specialized spatial encoders, which can complicate model architecture and training. The authors seek a solution that enhances 4D reasoning within standard MLLM architectures.

To address these motivations, MLLM-4D sets forth several key objectives:

- **Develop a Cost-Efficient Data Curation Pipeline**: Create an automated system to repurpose existing stereo video datasets into high-quality, large-scale 4D spatiotemporal instructional data. This includes generating specific datasets: MLLM4D-2M for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), MLLM4D-R1-30k for Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT), and MLLM4D-Bench for comprehensive evaluation.
- **Establish Foundational 4D Understanding**: Implement a Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) stage using the curated data to build a basic understanding of 4D spatial-temporal relationships within MLLMs.
- **Catalyze Advanced 4D Reasoning Capabilities**: Introduce a sophisticated post-training strategy involving Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) to develop higher-level 4D reasoning. This includes:
 - Proposing a specialized **Spatiotemporal Chain of Thought (ST-CoT)** prompting strategy that guides the model to reason like a visual physics engine.
 - Designing **physics-grounded Spatiotemporal reward functions (ST-reward)** to ensure logical consistency and penalize physically implausible reasoning.
 - Employing **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)** for effective reinforcement learning, without altering the core MLLM architecture.

By achieving these objectives, MLLM-4D aims to unlock new capabilities in MLLMs, making them more adept at understanding and navigating the dynamic real world.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The MLLM-4D framework introduces a comprehensive approach to instill 4D spatial-temporal intelligence in MLLMs, comprising two main pillars: a scalable data curation pipeline and a hierarchical post-training strategy.

#### 3.1. Scalable Spatial-Temporal Data Curation

Recognizing the critical bottleneck of data scarcity, MLLM-4D develops an automated, cost-efficient pipeline to generate large-scale, high-quality 4D instructional data. This pipeline repurposes existing stereoscopic video datasets, which inherently contain richer 3D information than monocular videos, thus mitigating depth ambiguity issues.

- **4D Spatial-Temporal Metadata Extraction**: The process begins by extracting precise 4D information from stereoscopic videos. This involves:
 - Obtaining left-rectified video frames `{I_i}`.
 - Processing camera poses `{C_i}` (rotation `R_i` and translation `t_i`) using techniques like Structure-from-Motion (e.g., Schonberger & Frahm, 2016).
 - Deriving per-frame metric 3D points `{P_i}` from metric stereo depth (e.g., Wang et al., 2024).
 - Filtering out low-quality estimations to ensure data fidelity.
- **Instance-Level Semantic Annotation**: To enable object-specific reasoning, the pipeline incorporates advanced MLLMs and vision models:
 - A video MLLM (e.g., Gemini-2.5-flash) identifies moving entities and extracts noun categories from the video.
 - GroundedSAM2 (Ren et al., 2024; Ravi et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024b) performs instance segmentation and tracking, providing temporally consistent 2D masks for objects.
 - Scene-level 4D points are projected onto these 2D masks to isolate per-frame, object-level 4D points `{P_im}`.
 - A region-level MLLM (e.g., PixelRefer) generates fine-grained semantic descriptions `{T_m}` for each object.
 - These camera poses, object descriptions, and instance-level 3D points form the rich metadata.
- **Physical-based Spatial-Temporal Relationship Solver**: Ground-truth question-answer (QA) pairs are generated by applying rigorous physics-based computations to the extracted metadata. This covers six distinct categories of spatial-temporal relationships:
 - **Independent Object Motion**: Object absolute distance.
 - **Camera Ego-Motion**: Camera absolute distance, camera relative direction.
 - **Object-Camera Dynamics**: Object-Camera absolute distance, object-camera relative distance change (closer/farther), object-camera relative direction (left/right).
 - Physics-based formulations convert raw 4D data into precise numerical answers, serving as ground truth.
- **Dataset Generation**:
 - **MLLM4D-2M**: A large-scale dataset (2 million high-quality QA pairs from ~100k videos) for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), formulated using templates and rigorous filtering/balancing protocols.
 - **MLLM4D-Bench**: A comprehensive evaluation benchmark (6,000 questions across six subtasks) for 4D spatial-temporal reasoning, designed with dynamic scene complexity, structured 4D motion categorization, and fine-grained temporal evaluation.
 - **MLLM4D-R1-30k**: A dataset (30,000 QA pairs with 4D motion and ground-truth solutions) augmented with Spatiotemporal Chain of Thought (ST-CoT) data for Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT).

#### 3.2. MLLM-4D Post-Training Framework

MLLM-4D employs a hierarchical two-stage post-training strategy, building upon standard MLLM architectures (e.g., Qwen3-VL-8B) to achieve robust 4D understanding and reasoning without architectural modifications.

- **Stage 1: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) for 4D Understanding**:
 - The model is fine-tuned on the MLLM4D-2M dataset using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) to efficiently adapt the model while preserving pre-trained knowledge.
 - Standard cross-entropy loss `L_ce` is used, teaching the model to identify spatial-temporal anchors and basic 4D comprehension.
- **Stage 2: Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) for 4D Reasoning**: This stage is designed to catalyze advanced reasoning.
 - **Cold-Start Alignment for Spatiotemporal Chain-of-Thought (ST-CoT)**: A brief cold-start phase aligns the model's output to a novel, five-step ST-CoT reasoning paradigm. Unlike traditional CoT, ST-CoT compels the model to operate as a 3D perception engine, grounding its reasoning in visual physics and motion dynamics over time. The five steps are:
 1. **Objective Alignment and Temporal Anchoring**: Define objective and critical temporal boundaries.
 2. **Start Frame 3D State Parsing and Anchoring**: Analyze initial visual and geometric state (camera center, object center, scene descriptions).
 3. **Temporal Progression and Visual Cue Collection**: Infer physical shifts from visual transformations (scale, perspective distortion).
 4. **End Frame 3D State Verification**: Generate terminal state and visual summary for consistency check.
 5. **Evidence-Based Synthesis and Probabilistic Inference**: Synthesize visual evidence into a comprehensive 4D trajectory.
 - **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)**: After cold-start alignment, GRPO (Shao et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024a) is applied to the MLLM4D-R1-30k dataset. GRPO optimizes the policy by maximizing a surrogate loss based on relative rewards within sampled output groups, avoiding the need for a separate value function.
 - **Spatiotemporal Reward (ST-Reward)**: Crucial for grounding reasoning in physically plausible 4D states, this reward mechanism complements standard accuracy (`R_Acc`) and format rewards (`R_Fmt`):
 - `R_Acc` evaluates the correctness of the final answer.
 - `R_Fmt` enforces adherence to the specified output structure, including explicit "Object Center" and "Camera Center" coordinate formats.
 - `R_ST` quantitatively evaluates the model's ability to localize the camera and objects at start/end frames by mapping the Mean Euclidean Error (MEE) between predicted and ground-truth coordinates to a normalized `` score via an exponential decay function.
 - The total reward `R` for GRPO is a weighted sum of `R_Acc`, `R_Fmt`, and `R_ST`.

This systematic methodology ensures that MLLM-4D not only understands basic 4D relationships but also reasons about them in a physically grounded and interpretable manner, leading to advanced spatial-temporal intelligence.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments conducted demonstrate that MLLM-4D significantly advances the state-of-the-art in visual-based 4D spatial-temporal intelligence for MLLMs.

#### 4.1. State-of-the-Art Performance on MLLM4D-Bench

- **Superiority Over Baselines**: MLLM-4D outperforms all proprietary models (e.g., GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash), open-source MLLMs (e.g., Qwen2.5/3-VL, LLaVA-NeXT-Video, InternVideo, InternVL), and specialized 3D spatial reasoning models (e.g., VLM-3R, VG-LLM) on the newly introduced MLLM4D-Bench.
- **Quantitative Edge**: The MLLM-4D (Qwen3-VL-8B) variant achieves an impressive average score of 72.7% on MLLM4D-Bench. This is a substantial improvement over leading proprietary models like Gemini 2.5 Pro, which scores 46.6%, highlighting a significant leap in 4D reasoning capabilities.
- **Interpretable Reasoning**: Qualitative comparisons reveal that MLLM-4D not only provides accurate answers but also offers interpretable 4D reasoning through its Spatiotemporal Chain-of-Thought (ST-CoT). It explicitly models "Object Center" and "Camera Center" coordinates and physical motion trajectories. In contrast, baseline models often resort to guesswork or rely solely on 2D visual cues, leading to incorrect or ungrounded conclusions in complex 4D tasks.

#### 4.2. Generalizability on VLM4D Benchmark

- **Out-of-Distribution Robustness**: MLLM-4D exhibits strong generalizability by maintaining superior performance on the VLM4D benchmark, an out-of-distribution dataset. It significantly outperforms its base models (Qwen3-VL-8B and Qwen2.5-VL-7B) and other specialized 3D spatial reasoning models (VLM-3R, VG-LLM).
- **Qualitative Consistency**: Qualitative results on VLM4D further illustrate that MLLM-4D's ST-CoT reasoning paradigm effectively translates its 4D spatial-temporal intelligence to diverse environments beyond its primary training distribution.

#### 4.3. Ablation Studies

Ablation studies systematically validate the effectiveness of the MLLM-4D framework's components:

- **Scalability of Training Data**:
 - **SFT Stage**: Performance on the VLM4D benchmark (real-world videos) shows a rapid increase with initial scaling of the MLLM4D-2M dataset (e.g., from a 52.1% baseline to 57.7% with 200K samples), and continues to scale consistently, reaching 59.7% at 2M samples. This confirms that MLLM4D-2M effectively captures complex 4D patterns.
 - **RFT Stage**: The MLLM4D-R1-30k dataset also demonstrates robust scaling. After an initial adaptation phase, accuracy improves from 59.8% to a peak of 63.1% at 30K samples, confirming the dataset's efficacy for reinforcement learning.
- **Effectiveness of MLLM-4D Framework**:
 - **Impact of SFT**: The Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) stage with the MLLM4D-2M dataset provides a substantial performance improvement (e.g., from 35.3% to 70.1% on MLLM4D-Bench), establishing a robust foundational 4D understanding.
 - **Superiority of Stereo-based Data Curation**: The primary data curation pipeline, which leverages stereoscopic videos, yields superior results compared to an alternative monocular-based pipeline. This confirms that addressing depth ambiguity and enhancing spatial accuracy through stereo data is crucial for high-quality 4D instruction.
 - **Benefits of RFT**: Applying Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with standard accuracy and format rewards alone provides robust performance gains over the SFT-only model.
 - **Advantage of ST-Reward**: The full post-training framework, which includes the physics-grounded Spatiotemporal Reward (ST-reward), achieves the highest performance (72.7% on MLLM4D-Bench and 63.1% on VLM4D). This definitively validates that explicit grounding of reasoning in 4D physical quantities through ST-reward functions significantly boosts the model’s capacity for complex spatiotemporal reasoning and reduces hallucinations.

In summary, the main findings highlight MLLM-4D's ability to achieve unprecedented 4D spatial-temporal reasoning from purely 2D video input, driven by its innovative data curation and a reinforcement learning strategy that leverages physics-grounded chain-of-thought and reward mechanisms.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The MLLM-4D framework represents a significant step forward in the field of multimodal large language models and computer vision, with profound implications for the development of more intelligent and interactive AI systems.

#### 5.1. Advancing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)

The primary significance of MLLM-4D lies in its ability to endow MLLMs with human-like 4D spatial-temporal intelligence. Previously, MLLMs struggled with understanding and reasoning about the dynamic evolution of 3D space over time from simple 2D video inputs. MLLM-4D effectively bridges this critical gap, pushing MLLMs beyond static scene understanding to comprehend and predict movement, interaction, and changes in relative positions within complex, dynamic environments. This capability is essential for MLLMs to operate robustly in the real world.

#### 5.2. Addressing Data Scarcity through Cost-Efficient Curation

One of the most impactful contributions is the automated data curation pipeline. The scarcity of large-scale, high-quality 4D instructional data has been a major bottleneck for training advanced MLLMs. By repurposing existing stereo video datasets and integrating advanced vision primitives, MLLM-4D offers a scalable and cost-efficient method to generate millions of QA pairs with precise 4D metadata. This approach circumvents the prohibitive costs and limitations of manual annotation, democratizing access to the necessary training data for future research in 4D intelligence. The MLLM4D-2M, MLLM4D-R1-30k, and MLLM4D-Bench datasets will serve as invaluable resources for the research community.

#### 5.3. Architectural Efficiency and Interpretability

MLLM-4D demonstrates that sophisticated 4D reasoning can be achieved within standard MLLM architectures, without requiring complex modifications or additional spatial encoders. This finding is highly significant as it simplifies model deployment, reduces computational overhead, and highlights the power of data-driven and post-training strategies. Furthermore, the introduction of the Spatiotemporal Chain of Thought (ST-CoT) provides an interpretable reasoning paradigm. By forcing the model to explicitly ground its thinking in visual physics, including "Object Center" and "Camera Center" coordinates, MLLM-4D enhances the transparency and trustworthiness of its predictions, moving away from "black-box" decision-making.

#### 5.4. Wide-Ranging Potential Applications

The capabilities unlocked by MLLM-4D have direct and transformative potential across several interactive AI systems:

- **Robotics**: Robots operating in dynamic human environments require a deep understanding of evolving spatial relationships to navigate safely, manipulate objects, and interact naturally. MLLM-4D could enable robots to perceive and predict movements of people and objects, leading to more adaptive and intelligent robotic systems.
- **Autonomous Driving**: For self-driving cars, understanding the complex 4D dynamics of traffic, pedestrians, and environmental changes is paramount for safety and decision-making. MLLM-4D's ability to reason about relative distances, directions, and motion trajectories from visual inputs could significantly enhance the perception and prediction modules of autonomous vehicles.
- **Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR)**: More realistic and immersive VR/AR experiences depend on a precise understanding of the user's environment and the interaction of virtual objects with real-world dynamics. MLLM-4D could facilitate the creation of intelligent agents that can seamlessly integrate into and react to dynamic virtual or augmented spaces.
- **Embodied AI Agents**: The ability for AI agents to reason and act effectively in complex 4D worlds, akin to human cognition, is a foundational step towards truly intelligent embodied AI. MLLM-4D contributes significantly to this goal, opening avenues for agents that can learn, plan, and adapt in constantly changing environments.

#### 5.5. Inspiring Future Research

By providing novel datasets, a robust framework, and demonstrated state-of-the-art results, MLLM-4D lays a strong foundation for future research. The authors specifically highlight the challenge of processing long-duration video sequences due to current architectural input length limitations, suggesting long-context 4D spatiotemporal reasoning as a compelling direction. This work is expected to inspire further exploration into more sophisticated 4D reasoning mechanisms, potentially leading to even more advanced and human-centric AI systems.

In conclusion, MLLM-4D marks a crucial advancement in visual-based spatial-temporal intelligence, addressing fundamental challenges in data and model training to bring MLLMs closer to human-level comprehension of dynamic reality.
