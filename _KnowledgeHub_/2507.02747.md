# DexVLG: Dexterous Vision-Language-Grasp Model at Scale

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework introduces DexGraspNet 3.0, the largest synthetic dataset for dexterous grasping with 170 million semantically-annotated poses, and DexVLG, a large vision-language-grasp model. The model predicts language-aligned dexterous grasp poses from single-view RGBD input, achieving 80% success and 75% part accuracy in real-world experiments.

## Problem

- Applying large Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models to multi-fingered dexterous robot hands is challenging due to the inherent complexity of high degrees of freedom.
- A severe scarcity of large-scale, high-quality, semantically-aware data for dexterous grasping hinders the development of generalizable models.
- Existing dexterous grasp datasets are often "semantic-unaware," preventing robots from performing functional, human-like grasps based on specific object parts or intentions.

## Method

- ==Generated DexGraspNet== 3.0, a synthetic dataset of 170 million dexterous grasp poses on 174,000 objects, featuring part-level semantic annotations and language captions.
- Developed a novel "part-aware energy" framework for grasp optimization, ensuring generated grasps are functionally aligned with specific object parts.
- Trained **DexVLG**, a large Vision-Language-Grasp model, utilizing pre-trained Uni3D and Florence-2 backbones, and a flow-matching denoising module for end-to-end grasp pose prediction.

## Results

- DexVLG achieved an **87.7%** simulation success rate and **62.1%** part grasp accuracy on seen objects, outperforming baseline models by a notable margin.
- The model demonstrated strong zero-shot generalization, achieving **79.1%** simulation success on unseen objects and **76.3%** on objects with different part segmentation methods.
- In real-world experiments using a ShadowHand, DexVLG attained an **80%** success rate and **75%** part accuracy for language-instructed grasps on simple objects.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large-scale, semantically-rich synthetic data is essential for achieving robust zero-shot generalization and instruction-aligned dexterous grasping with multi-fingered hands.
> - The capacity of large vision-language models is crucial for effectively integrating language understanding with complex dexterous manipulation without compromising grasp quality.
> - Incorporating color information in point cloud inputs dramatically enhances object and part understanding, leading to a substantial boost in grasp success and part accuracy.
