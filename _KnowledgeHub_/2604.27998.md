# Latent-GRPO: Group Relative Policy Optimization for Latent Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Latent-GRPO is a Reinforcement Learning framework that optimizes latent reasoning in Large Language Models (LLMs), achieving stable performance gains and significantly shorter reasoning chains. It improves average Pass@1 accuracy by 7.86 points on low-difficulty tasks and 14.77 points on high-difficulty tasks over Latent-SFT baselines, while reducing reasoning length by 3-4x compared to explicit GRPO.

## Problem

- Applying Reinforcement Learning (RL) directly to latent reasoning in LLMs is unstable, often leading to model collapse due to unconstrained exploration in continuous latent space.
- Existing latent RL methods suffer from exploration-optimization misalignment, where gradient updates can inadvertently reduce probabilities for positive-advantage latent components.
- Latent mixture non-closure causes harmful mode averaging when multiple correct latent paths for the same prefix are jointly reinforced, leading to invalid subsequent reasoning steps.

## Method

- ==Invalid Sample Advantage Masking== bounds exploration by zeroing advantages for invalid (non-terminating) trajectories.
- ==One-sided Noise Sampling== ensures a strictly positive perturbation margin for latent components, aligning gradient direction with trajectory-level advantages.
- ==Optimal Correct Path First Token Selection== avoids harmful mode averaging by reinforcing only the initial step of the highest-scoring correct path.

## Results

- **Latent-GRPO** yielded **+7.86 pp** Pass@1 over Latent-SFT on low-difficulty tasks and **+14.77 pp** on high-difficulty tasks.
- Achieves **3-4x shorter reasoning chains** vs. explicit GRPO while maintaining or improving performance.
- Ablations: One-sided Noise Sampling is critical for stability; Optimal Correct Path First Token Selection helps complex tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Stable RL for latent reasoning requires explicitly addressing the challenges of off-manifold exploration, misalignment between exploration and optimization, and mode averaging in continuous latent spaces.
> - A well-defined latent manifold, typically established by supervised fine-tuning, is a prerequisite for successful and stable reinforcement learning in latent reasoning.
> - By carefully managing the stochasticity and optimization targets in the latent space, LLMs can achieve significant reasoning chain compression without sacrificing, and often improving, task performance.
