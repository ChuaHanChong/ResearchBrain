# Dual-Objective Reinforcement Learning with Novel Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman Formulations

> [!summary] Summary
> This work introduces novel Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) formulations to expand reinforcement learning to dual-objective tasks, such as Reach-Always-Avoid and Reach-Reach. The proposed DOHJ-PPO algorithm demonstrates leading performance on continuous control benchmarks, achieving higher total success rates and faster task completion, with notable robustness to environmental stochasticity.

## Problem

- Existing Hamilton-Jacobi Reinforcement Learning (HJ-RL) formulations were limited to single-objective tasks (e.g., simple reach, avoid, or reach-avoid).
- Integrating hard constraints and balancing multiple, often competing, objectives in RL typically requires intricate reward engineering and hyperparameter tuning with methods like Lagrangian-based Constrained Markov Decision Processes (CMDPs).
- Challenges in multi-target achievement where rewards are sparse or reaching one goal negatively impacts the ability to reach another.

## Method

- Derived novel ==Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman== (HJB) formulations and value functions for two dual-objective problems: Reach-Always-Avoid (RAA) and Reach-Reach (RR), using state augmentation to capture trajectory history.
- Developed decomposition theorems to break down complex dual-objective problems into compositions of simpler, established HJ-RL problems, allowing for reuse of solutions.
- Introduced DOHJ-PPO (Dual-Objective Hamilton-Jacobi Proximal Policy Optimization), an algorithm that learns composed and decomposed objectives concurrently, utilizing coupled resets and specialized reward functions adapted for non-standard Bellman updates.

## Results

- DOHJ-PPO achieved top-tier performance on diverse continuous control tasks (Hopper, F16, SafetyGym, HalfCheetah), consistently outperforming baselines in achieving total success for both objectives.
- The algorithm demonstrated superior efficiency, reaching both objectives in fewer steps compared to other methods.
- DOHJ-PPO showed robustness to stochastic dynamics, maintaining significant performance advantages (e.g., 8-**22%** peak gap for RAA, over **30%** for RR in HalfCheetah) over baselines under injected noise.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Dual-objective problems like RAA and RR can be algebraically decomposed into simpler, sequential or parallel HJ-RL subproblems, which simplifies their solution.
> - State augmentation with auxiliary variables tracking extremal values (e.g., best reward seen, worst penalty seen) is sufficient to make these historically-dependent objectives Markovian.
> - Stochastic relaxations of the Bellman equations, combined with a custom PPO algorithm using specialized advantage estimates, effectively enable learning optimal policies in stochastic, continuous control environments.
