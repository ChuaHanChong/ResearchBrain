# ArtLLM: Generating Articulated Assets via 3D LLM

> [!summary] Summary
> ArtLLM, a framework by researchers from ShanghaiTech University, Tencent Hunyuan, and HKUST, generates high-quality articulated 3D assets by reformulating 3D articulation understanding as a language modeling problem. It leverages a 3D multimodal LLM to predict geometric layouts and kinematic structures, then synthesizes high-fidelity geometry and applies physics-based joint limit correction, achieving superior accuracy and significantly faster inference compared to existing methods while demonstrating strong real2sim performance for robotics.

## Problem

- Existing optimization-based methods for articulated object reconstruction are slow, produce low-fidelity geometry, and are limited to simple kinematic structures.
- Retrieval-based methods suffer from geometric repetitiveness and poor generalization due to reliance on predefined part libraries.
- Many 3D generative models lack an understanding of kinematic structures, leading to a disconnect between generated geometry and its intended motion, making objects mechanically incoherent.

## Method

- A 3D Articulation Language Model (ArtLLM) leverages a 3D multimodal LLM to autoregressively predict an object's kinematic structure (parts, joints, hierarchy) as a sequence of discrete tokens from point cloud inputs.
- Predicted part bounding boxes condition a part-aware generative model for high-fidelity geometry synthesis, capable of creating novel and diverse shapes.
- A physics-based joint limit correction module refines predicted joint ranges to prevent inter-part collisions during articulation, enhancing physical plausibility.

## Results

- ArtLLM achieved superior quantitative performance on the PartNet-Mobility dataset, with a Part Layout mIoU of 0.6884, Joint Type Accuracy of 0.9084, and Kinematic Hierarchy Graph Acc of 0.7741, outperforming baselines such as Articulate-Anything and SINGAPO across most metrics.
- The framework demonstrated significantly faster inference times (19 seconds) compared to baselines (84-522 seconds) while generating high-fidelity, novel part geometries with accurate and coherent articulations.
- Real2sim evaluations in robotics showed ArtLLM's ability to reconstruct real-world objects into URDF-format articulated assets that accurately reproduce real articulation behavior in simulation, bridging the sim-to-real gap for robot learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reformulating 3D articulation understanding as a language modeling problem enables the use of Large Language Models for joint reasoning about geometry and kinematics, handling variable numbers of parts and joints.
> - Quantization of continuous geometric and kinematic parameters allows their effective prediction within a discrete token-based LLM framework.
> - A multi-stage and multi-task supervised fine-tuning strategy, beginning with geometric foundation and progressively integrating kinematic reasoning, results in more robust and accurate model learning.
> - Post-processing with physically-constrained joint limit correction ensures that generated assets are free from self-collisions and are simulation-ready.
