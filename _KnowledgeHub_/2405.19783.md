# Instruction-Guided Visual Masking

> [!summary] Summary
> Instruction-guided Visual Masking (IVM) introduces a versatile visual grounding model that automatically masks instruction-irrelevant image regions, allowing large multimodal models (LMMs) to focus on task-relevant visual content. The approach, leveraging a 1-million-sample dataset and a novel training framework, improved GPT4-V's accuracy on V*Bench by 26.2% and enhanced robotic control generalization.

## Problem

- Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) struggle with precise visual grounding, leading to misalignment between textual instructions and relevant image regions.
- LMMs are susceptible to distractions from instruction-irrelevant visual content, resulting in issues like 'hallucinations' and factually incorrect responses.
- Existing visual grounding methods are often limited to predefined categories or simple instructions, lacking the versatility for diverse, complex real-world scenarios.

## Method

- Developed ==Instruction-guided Visual Masking (IVM)==, a plug-and-play visual grounding model that generates masks for instruction-irrelevant image regions, to explicitly guide LMM focus.
- Constructed ==IVM-Mix-1M==, a 1-million-sample dataset of image-instruction pairs with pixel-level mask annotations, using an LLM-empowered Mixture of Expert visual grounding pipeline and a small human-annotated subset.
- Introduced ==Discriminator-Weighted Supervised Learning (DWSL)== to train IVM, effectively leveraging the mixed-quality auto-generated and human-annotated data by prioritizing high-quality samples.

## Results

- IVM boosted GPT4-V's accuracy on the challenging V*Bench benchmark by **26.2%**, from **55.0%** to **81.2%**, establishing a new state-of-the-art.
- Improved LLaVA-7B performance across several benchmarks, including **+3.4%** on EgoThink, **+1.3%** on POPE, and **+58** points on MME.
- Enhanced the generalization and robustness of Language-Conditioned Behavior Cloning (LCBC) robot agents in real-world manipulation tasks, allowing successful performance even under strong visual distractions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly masking instruction-irrelevant visual regions significantly enhances LMMs' ability to align with complex instructions and reduces errors caused by visual noise.
> - A sophisticated data generation pipeline, combining LLMs with an ensemble of visual grounding models, can efficiently create large-scale, high-quality, fine-grained visual grounding datasets.
> - The DWSL framework effectively addresses the challenge of mixed-quality training data by weighting samples based on discriminator-assessed quality, leading to more robust model training.
