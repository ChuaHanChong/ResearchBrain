# Dreaming the Unseen: World Model-regularized Diffusion Policy for Out-of-Distribution Robustness

> [!summary] Summary
> The Dream Diffusion Policy (DDP) integrates a diffusion policy with a predictive diffusion world model to enhance robotic manipulation robustness under severe out-of-distribution (OOD) visual disturbances. This system dynamically shifts from relying on corrupted external vision to its internal predictive "imagination," achieving up to a 73.8% success rate on OOD MetaWorld tasks and an 83.3% average success rate across real-world OOD tasks, significantly outperforming existing baselines.

## Problem

- Visuomotor policies, particularly diffusion models, fail catastrophically when observations deviate from the training distribution due to unexpected disturbances like object displacements or occlusions.
- Existing OOD mitigation strategies often introduce architectural complexity, are computationally expensive, or aggressively alter learned actions, potentially compromising skill fidelity.
- Traditional world model integrations are often weakly coupled, limiting their ability to actively substitute corrupted visual inputs and sustain control under severe OOD shifts.

## Method

- A **Dream Diffusion Policy (DDP)** framework that co-optimizes a ==diffusion policy== and a ==diffusion world model==, both sharing a ==3D visual encoder==, to learn unified geometric and dynamic representations.
- An inference mechanism dynamically shifts between physical reality and internal "imagination," detecting OOD events via a ==real-imagination discrepancy== and bypassing corrupted visual inputs.
- When in OOD mode, the policy generates actions conditioned on ==autoregressively forecasted latent dynamics== from the world model, performing localized physical recoveries upon subtask completion to realign with physical objects.

## Results

- DDP achieved a **73.8%** average success rate on MetaWorld tasks and an **83.3%** average success rate in real-world tasks under OOD conditions, drastically outperforming state-of-the-art baselines like DP3 (**0.8%** and **3.3%** respectively).
- The policy demonstrated robust performance under severe visual occlusion, seamlessly completing tasks like 'Stack Blocks' and 'Pour Tea' by relying entirely on autoregressive imagination.
- Continuous reliance on **100%** imagined latents after initial observation maintained a **61.4%** success rate in MetaWorld and **76.7%** in real-world tasks, validating the stability of the internal world model for control.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Deep, co-optimized integration of a diffusion world model actively regularizes the diffusion policy, enabling it to generate actions from internally imagined latent states when real observations are corrupted.
> - The "real-imagination discrepancy" serves as an effective, intuitive detector for out-of-distribution events, allowing the system to switch from physical perception to internal prediction.
> - Recursive imagination, sustained by the world model's autoregressive forecasting, enables coherent and continuous control trajectories even in the complete absence of reliable external visual input.
