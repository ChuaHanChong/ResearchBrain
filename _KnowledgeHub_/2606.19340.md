# ZeroDex: Zero-Shot Long-Horizon Dexterous Manipulation via Multi-View 3D-Grounded VLM Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> ZeroDex presents a zero-shot framework for long-horizon dexterous manipulation, employing multi-view 3D-grounded Vision-Language Model (VLM) reasoning to execute high-level language instructions in real-world settings. The system achieved substantially higher success rates on various manipulation and tool-use tasks compared to fine-tuned VLA baselines, alongside reducing 3D localization errors for key task points.

## Problem

- Dexterous manipulation tasks demand highly precise 3D grounding, where even small errors in contact points or trajectories can lead to critical failures.
- Existing end-to-end Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models require extensive data collection and task-specific fine-tuning, hindering their scalability and generalization to novel scenarios.
- Many zero-shot methods rely on 2D or sparse intermediate representations from foundation models, which are often insufficient for capturing the precise 3D geometric information vital for dexterous tasks.

## Method

- **ZeroDex** implements a modular pipeline that decouples VLM semantic reasoning for high-level planning from physical execution via motion primitives and controllers.
- A ==multi-view fusion strategy== lifts 2D VLM predictions into robust 3D quantities (e.g., grasp points, interaction targets) using a combination of RANSAC-style triangulation and a reference-view ray voting scheme.
- The framework incorporates a 'Bag of Atomic Actions' library for tool-use, an affordance-guided dexterous grasp and motion generator with collision-aware refinement, and a closed-loop verification/replanning mechanism for long-horizon tasks.

## Results

- ZeroDex demonstrated 10/10 successes on 'Throw Away Trash' and 8/10 on 'Broom Clean' over 10 trials each, significantly outperforming fine-tuned VLA baselines (GR00T, Being-HO) which achieved 0/5 successes on these tasks.
- The multi-view fusion approach reduced object-centric grasp point localization error (L_grasp) to approximately **4.58**-**4.77** cm, a notable improvement over the **16.43** cm error of a single-view RGB-D baseline.
- The system showed capabilities in long-horizon tasks, achieving 4/6 end-to-end successes for 'Organize Objects,' and the closed-loop retry mechanism effectively enabled recovery from intermediate execution failures.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling semantic reasoning from physical execution allows for effective leveraging of pre-trained VLM zero-shot capabilities for diverse manipulation tasks.
> - Robustly lifting view-dependent 2D VLM predictions into consistent 3D quantities via multi-view fusion is critical for overcoming depth ambiguity, occlusion, and achieving the precision required for dexterous manipulation.
> - A library of reusable object-centric atomic action primitives, spatially aligned with 3D grounded targets, enables generalizable tool-use and simplifies task execution for complex interactions.
