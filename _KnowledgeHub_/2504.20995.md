# TesserAct: Learning 4D Embodied World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> TesserAct introduces a 4D embodied world model that predicts sequences of RGB, depth, and normal maps, enabling robust reconstruction of dynamic 3D scenes over time. This approach allows robots to achieve higher success rates in various manipulation tasks by providing enhanced spatial and temporal understanding.

## Problem

- Existing 2D pixel-based world models lack explicit understanding of 3D geometry, limiting their use in robotic tasks requiring precise depth and pose.
- Directly generating or simulating full 4D world dynamics (3D + time) is computationally prohibitive.
- There is a scarcity of large-scale 4D datasets annotated with high-quality RGB, depth, and normal information for training.

## Method

- Curates a large-scale 4D embodied video dataset by annotating synthetic (RLBench) and real-world (RT1 Fractal, Bridge, Something Something V2) videos with RGB, depth, and normal maps.
- Develops a 4D generative model by fine-tuning a pre-trained video diffusion model (CogVideoX) to directly predict future sequences of RGB, depth, and normal maps.
- Introduces a 4D scene reconstruction algorithm that refines generated depth maps using normal integration and ensures temporal consistency via optical flow and custom loss functions.

## Results

- TesserAct generates 4D scenes with superior depth and normal accuracy and lower Chamfer Distance for reconstructed 4D point clouds compared to existing 2D video diffusion and 4D point cloud generation models.
- It achieves higher success rates across challenging robotic manipulation tasks in RLBench, outperforming 2D-based world models and behavior cloning agents.
- The model demonstrates efficient high-quality novel view synthesis, achieving better visual metrics (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) in minutes compared to hours for state-of-the-art 4D reconstruction methods.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Generating sequences of RGB, depth, and normal maps (RGB-DN videos) serves as an efficient and information-rich intermediate representation for dynamic 4D scenes.
> - Fine-tuning large-scale 2D video diffusion models with specialized modules can effectively extend their capabilities to generate coherent 4D (dynamic 3D) representations.
> - Explicitly incorporating and optimizing for depth and normal information during generation and post-processing significantly improves the geometric accuracy and temporal consistency of reconstructed 4D scenes.
