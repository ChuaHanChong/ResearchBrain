# Symbolic State Estimation with Predicates for Contact-Rich Manipulation Tasks

> [!summary] Summary
> A Bayesian framework employing binary predicate classifiers enables data-efficient and robust estimation of high-level symbolic states from multimodal sensor observations in contact-rich manipulation. This approach yielded a 0.92 offline accuracy and 0.72 online success rate in insertion tasks, demonstrating superior generalization to unseen tasks compared to direct classification and differentiable filtering.

## Problem

- High-level robotic manipulation relies on brittle hand-tuned heuristics for symbolic state detection, leading to failures due to sensor noise or unexpected disturbances.
- Data-driven methods for symbolic state estimation often require extensive datasets, yield noisy predictions with limited data, and struggle to generalize or scale to new tasks.
- Integrating high-dimensional sensory input into robust, discrete symbolic state estimation, especially for contact-rich tasks, remains a challenge for existing Bayesian and differentiable filtering approaches.

## Method

- A Bayesian framework estimates symbolic states by leveraging intermediate 'predicates'—atomic, binary properties—which are easier to classify and generalize.
- Binary predicate classifiers, trained on high-dimensional multi-modal sensor inputs (e.g., images, forces), act as 'virtual sensors' whose probabilistic outputs are incorporated into the Bayesian update using Gaussian Mixture Models.
- Sensor fusion is achieved by defining modality-specific predicates and assuming conditional independence, allowing complementary information from different sensors to be combined effectively.

## Results

- The proposed 'Pred' method achieved an offline state estimation accuracy of 0.92 and an F1 score of 0.67, outperforming direct symbolic state classification (0.81 accuracy, 0.60 F1) and a differentiable filter (0.85 accuracy, 0.53 F1).
- It demonstrated superior generalization to unseen tasks, maintaining 0.80 accuracy (compared to 0.38 for direct state classification), with motion and force-based predicates showing particular robustness.
- In online closed-loop policy execution, the framework achieved the highest average success rate of 0.72 across error-prone insertion tasks, consistently surpassing a hand-tuned manual baseline.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing complex symbolic states into simpler, atomic binary predicates significantly enhances data efficiency and generalization capabilities for state estimation.
> - A Bayesian framework, when informed by robust predicate classifiers, effectively fuses multimodal, high-dimensional observations to estimate discrete symbolic states even with limited real-world data.
> - Sensor fusion is critical for reliable symbolic state estimation, as different modalities provide distinct and complementary information (e.g., force for contact nuances, vision for object presence and spatial relationships).
