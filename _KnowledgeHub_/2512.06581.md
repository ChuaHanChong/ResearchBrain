# MedGRPO: Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning for Heterogeneous Medical Video Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> MedGRPO introduces a multi-task reinforcement learning framework and MedVidBench, a large-scale benchmark for medical video understanding. The framework utilizes cross-dataset reward normalization and a specialized medical LLM judge to enable balanced training across diverse medical video datasets and tasks, leading to improved performance in areas such as spatiotemporal grounding and dense video captioning.

## Problem

- Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with medical video understanding due to the scarcity of high-quality, instruction-following training data in a conversational format.
- Standard reinforcement learning algorithms exhibit training instability and collapse when applied to heterogeneous medical datasets, as models overfit to 'easy' datasets due to imbalanced reward scales.
- Conventional semantic similarity metrics are inadequate for evaluating the fine-grained clinical correctness, specificity, and contextual accuracy required for medical video captions.

## Method

- Developed ==MedVidBench==, a unified benchmark comprising 531,850 video-instruction pairs across 8 medical video sources and 8 diverse tasks, formatted for instruction-following via a multi-perspective quality assurance pipeline.
- Proposed **MedGRPO**, a novel reinforcement learning framework that employs cross-dataset reward normalization through a dataset-task-specific logistic function to ensure balanced optimization across tasks with varying difficulty.
- Designed a GPT-4.1-based medical LLM judge to comparatively score generated captions against references across five clinical dimensions (e.g., terminology, instrument identification, action accuracy), integrating these scores for both reward computation and evaluation.

## Results

- Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on MedVidBench using Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** vastly outperformed off-the-shelf VLMs (e.g., GPT-4.1) by achieving a CVS score of **0.894** (from **0.018**) and STG score of **0.177** (from **0.014**).
- MedGRPO consistently improved performance over the SFT baseline, with Qwen2.5VL-**7B** showing gains such as STG accuracy increasing by +**0.025** to **0.202**, and Dense Video Captioning (DVC_llm) score improving by +**0.132** to 3.797.
- Ablation studies confirmed that cross-dataset reward normalization prevented catastrophic training collapse (e.g., CVS dropped from **0.894** to **0.020** without it), and multi-task learning with caption generation tasks improved grounding performance (e.g., STG by **4.7%**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Domain adaptation with large-scale, multi-source training on MedVidBench is crucial for medical VLM performance, significantly outperforming general-domain VLMs.
> - Cross-dataset reward normalization is essential for preventing catastrophic training collapse and ensuring stable, balanced multi-task reinforcement learning on heterogeneous medical video datasets.
> - A specialized medical LLM judge effectively captures fine-grained clinical correctness in captions, demonstrating strong correlation with human expert preferences and enhancing performance in medical caption generation tasks.
