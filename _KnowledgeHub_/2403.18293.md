# Efficient Test-Time Adaptation of Vision-Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> The Training-Free Dynamic Adapter (TDA) enables efficient test-time adaptation for Vision-Language Models (VLMs) by eliminating the need for backpropagation. This method uses a dynamic dual-cache system to adapt to new data distributions, improving CLIP's accuracy on ImageNet from 59.81% to 61.35% while processing data significantly faster than prior adaptation techniques.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) like CLIP experience performance degradation when deployed on data with distribution shifts, differing from their training data.
- Existing Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) methods for VLMs, such as Test-time Prompt Tuning (TPT), are computationally expensive and time-consuming due to requiring backpropagation during inference.
- Current TTA approaches often rely on potentially noisy pseudo labels, which can propagate errors and limit adaptation effectiveness.

## Method

- Introduces the ==Training-Free Dynamic Adapter (TDA)==, a lightweight, non-parametric adaptation mechanism that operates without backpropagation.
- Employs ==dynamic key-value caches==: a ==positive cache== stores high-confidence image features and their pseudo labels, while a ==negative cache== captures what classes an image does not belong to.
- Adapts predictions by combining the original CLIP output with information retrieved from both positive and negative caches, which are updated on-the-fly based on prediction confidence.

## Results

- The Training-Free Dynamic Adapter (TDA) processes the ImageNet validation set in approximately **16 minutes**, a substantial improvement compared to **12+ hours** for Test-time Prompt Tuning (TPT) methods.
- TDA improved CLIP with a ResNet-50 backbone accuracy on ImageNet from a baseline of **59.81%** to **61.35%**, outperforming TPT and DiffTPT while being orders of magnitude faster.
- TDA consistently outperforms or matches state-of-the-art TTA methods across various datasets, including ImageNet variants and cross-domain benchmarks, demonstrating superior efficiency and comparable accuracy.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Test-time adaptation for Vision-Language Models can be achieved efficiently without computationally intensive backpropagation by utilizing dynamic key-value caches.
> - A dual-cache system, comprising both positive and negative caches, enhances robustness against distribution shifts and mitigates the impact of noisy pseudo labels.
> - Negative pseudo-labeling, which identifies classes an image does not belong to, provides valuable complementary information to positive associations, improving overall adaptation performance.
