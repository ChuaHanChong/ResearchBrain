# DenseGRPO: From Sparse to Dense Reward for Flow Matching Model Alignment

> [!summary] Summary
> DenseGRPO enhances the alignment of text-to-image flow matching models with human preferences by introducing a method for estimating step-wise dense rewards and adaptively calibrating the exploration space during reinforcement learning. This approach yielded improved scores, including a PickScore of 24.64 for human preference alignment and an OCR Accuracy of 0.95 for visual text rendering.

## Problem

- Existing GRPO-based methods for text-to-image (T2I) alignment suffer from a "sparse reward problem," applying a single, terminal reward from the final image to all intermediate denoising steps.
- This creates a mismatch between global feedback and fine-grained, step-wise contributions, hindering efficient policy optimization in flow matching models.
- Current SDE samplers for exploration use a uniform noise injection strategy, which is suboptimal for the time-varying denoising process, leading to unbalanced exploration.

## Method

- **DenseGRPO** defines a ==step-wise dense reward== as the reward gain (ΔR_i^t = R_i^{t-1} - R_i^t), quantifying each denoising step's contribution.
- Estimates the reward R_i^t for intermediate latents x_i^t by performing a ==multi-step ODE denoising== process to obtain a clean latent x_i^{t,0} and then applying an existing ==reward model==.
- Introduces a ==reward-aware adaptive noise scheme== that adjusts a timestep-specific noise level ψ(t) in the SDE sampler, calibrating the exploration space for balanced positive/negative dense rewards.

## Results

- Achieved superior performance across T2I tasks, including a **GenEval score of 0.97** for compositional image generation and **0.95 OCR Accuracy** for visual text rendering.
- Demonstrated substantial improvements in human preference alignment, attaining a **PickScore of 24.64**, significantly higher than Flow-GRPO (**23.31**).
- Qualitative evaluations showed higher visual and semantic quality, with better color accuracy, text fidelity, and alignment to complex positional relationships in prompts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Providing step-wise dense rewards, particularly defined as reward gain, leads to more effective and efficient policy optimization for flow matching model alignment.
> - Calibrating the exploration space adaptively based on reward feedback significantly improves alignment performance compared to uniform noise injection.
> - The accuracy of dense reward estimation is crucial; performing more ODE denoising steps to derive intermediate clean images (n=t) results in better performance.
