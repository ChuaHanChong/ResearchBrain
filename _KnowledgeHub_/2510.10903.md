# Towards a Unified Understanding of Robot Manipulation: A Comprehensive Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey synthesizes the extensive and fragmented field of robot manipulation, providing a comprehensive overview that unifies diverse methodologies and challenges under novel classification systems. It structures the landscape by introducing new taxonomies for high-level planning, low-level learning-based control, and key bottlenecks, while outlining future research directions.

## Problem

- Robot manipulation research is fragmented across specialized tasks, hardware, and methodologies, leading to a lack of holistic understanding.
- Existing surveys are often limited in scope or depth, failing to provide a systematic and comprehensive overview of the rapidly evolving field.
- The rapid shift towards data-driven, learning-based, and multimodal AI approaches requires an updated and unified framework for understanding.

## Method

- Developed a comprehensive survey that systematically aggregates and synthesizes a vast body of literature on robot manipulation, spanning foundational knowledge to recent advancements.
- Introduced novel taxonomies for ==high-level planning== (e.g., language, code, motion, 3D representations), ==low-level learning-based control== (based on training paradigms), and ==key bottlenecks== (data, generalization).
- Provided a structured overview of hardware platforms, control paradigms, benchmarks, datasets, and real-world applications to offer a complete landscape.

## Results

- Successfully unified diverse sub-fields and methodologies of robot manipulation into a coherent and systematic framework.
- Presented detailed taxonomies for high-level planning and a novel structure for low-level learning-based control, categorizing approaches by learning strategy, input modeling, latent learning, and policy learning architectures.
- Established the first dedicated taxonomy for identifying and analyzing critical bottlenecks related to data and generalization, and outlined four key prospective future research directions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The field has decisively shifted from classical rule-based methods to data-driven, learning-based approaches, particularly Reinforcement Learning and Imitation Learning, integrated with large language and vision-language models.
> - A unified understanding of robot manipulation necessitates new classification systems that account for the interdisciplinary nature and increasing complexity of modern methods.
> - Key challenges bottlenecking the advancement and deployment of robot manipulation include efficient data collection and utilization, along with robust generalization across environments, tasks, and robot embodiments.
