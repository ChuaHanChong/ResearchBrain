# FysicsWorld: A Unified Full-Modality Benchmark for Any-to-Any Understanding, Generation, and Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Fudan University and Fysics AI developed FysicsWorld, a unified full-modality benchmark for evaluating any-to-any understanding, generation, and reasoning across image, video, audio, and text, utilizing a Cross-Modal Complementarity Screening strategy to necessitate genuine multimodal fusion. Evaluations using this benchmark indicate that current omni-modal models exhibit limitations in fusion-dependent reasoning and robust speech-driven interaction.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal benchmarks offer incomplete modality coverage and lack bidirectional input/output across image, video, audio, and text.
- Many current benchmarks suffer from weak cross-modal dependencies, allowing models to complete tasks using only a single modality without true fusion.
- Evaluation is often restricted to text-centric outputs, overlooking crucial multimodal generation capabilities and speech-driven interaction.

## Method

- Development of **FysicsWorld**, a unified full-modality benchmark enabling any-to-any understanding, generation, and reasoning across image, video, audio, and text.
- Implementation of the ==Cross-Modal Complementarity Screening== (CMCS) strategy to ensure tasks necessitate genuine multimodal fusion by filtering out unimodal shortcuts.
- Inclusion of ==speech-driven image/video understanding== and QA, fusion-dependent cross-modal reasoning, and cross-modal audio generation tasks.

## Results

- **Proprietary MLLMs** consistently achieved superior performance in uni-modal understanding tasks compared to open-source counterparts.
- **Unified understanding-generation models** showed weaker alignment with fine-grained textual constraints in image generation and temporal consistency in video generation.
- All evaluated **OmniLLMs** experienced a **marked performance drop** on fusion-dependent tasks identified by the CMCS strategy, struggling to genuinely integrate heterogeneous information.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Proprietary MLLMs generally outperform open-source models in uni-modal understanding, though advanced open-source OmniLLMs are narrowing this gap.
> - Unified understanding-generation models show competitive image synthesis but struggle with fine-grained control and temporal consistency in video generation compared to specialized models.
> - Current omni-modal models exhibit significant performance degradation on fusion-dependent reasoning tasks and speech-driven interactions, indicating a lack of true multimodal fusion.
