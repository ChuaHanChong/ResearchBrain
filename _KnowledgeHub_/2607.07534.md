# Infinite Worlds with Versatile Interactions

> [!summary] Summary
> LingBot-World-Infinity introduces an interactive world model capable of generating persistent, explorable virtual environments at 720p and 60 frames per second for over an hour without visual degradation. The system integrates a "Director-Pilot" agentic framework to support a versatile array of character actions and environmental interactions, advancing real-time interactive world simulation.

## Problem

- Existing interactive world models suffer from long-horizon instability, with visual degradation and scene incoherence accumulating over minutes of continuous autoregressive generation.
- Achieving high-fidelity (e.g., 720p, 60fps) real-time interactive generation while simultaneously reacting to live user input is computationally challenging, often leading to compromises in visual quality or responsiveness.
- Many current interactive world models offer limited interaction capabilities, typically confined to basic camera movements or coarse navigation rather than diverse character and environmental actions.

## Method

- A two-stage training strategy involves pre-training a ==causal world model== with a ==Mixture of Bidirectional and Autoregressive (MoBA)== attention mask for robustness, followed by few-step distillation using ==Consistency Distillation (CD)== and ==Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD)== for real-time efficiency and drift mitigation.
- A unified data engine processes heterogeneous raw videos into a temporally localized, multi-dimensional training dataset with event-level annotations covering subject motion, interaction state, and environmental dynamics.
- An **Agentic Interaction Harness** is introduced, comprising a VLM-based **Director** for macroscopic semantic reasoning and event proposals, and a **Pilot** video generator for low-level physical dynamics and high-fidelity visual rendering.

## Results

- The distilled model achieves over **one hour** of uninterrupted, drift-free generation at **720p** and **60 frames per second**, setting a new benchmark for long-horizon stability and real-time performance among interactive world models.
- The system demonstrates support for a broad action space including combat, archery, spell-casting, shooting, and dynamic environmental changes, managed effectively by the Director-Pilot agentic harness.
- The causal pretrained backbone exhibits superior stability over longer rollouts compared to other causal world models like MAGI and HY-World 1.5, maintaining sharper textures and scene coherence over extended durations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining bidirectional and autoregressive attention (MoBA) with a conditional flow-matching objective effectively reduces overfitting and visual degradation in causal world models, enhancing long-horizon stability.
> - Few-step distillation using a combination of Consistency Distillation and Distribution Matching Distillation allows multi-step diffusion models to achieve real-time, high-fidelity interactive generation while suppressing long-horizon drift.
> - An agentic co-simulation framework, separating semantic control from visual generation, enables complex, open-ended world progression and supports diverse, logical user interactions beyond simple navigation.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How this Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The work addresses key challenges within the field of interactive world models, which are generative systems designed to synthesize environments frame-by-frame in response to user or agent actions. These models are gaining prominence as a foundation for applications such as game generation and embodied simulation. Previous research in this area, often driven by advancements in causal (autoregressive) video generation, has demonstrated the potential for indefinitely unfolding and reactive explorable worlds.

However, existing systems commonly face two primary limitations:
1. **Long-horizon stability:** Errors accumulate over time in autoregressive generation, leading to visual degradation (e.g., smeared textures, warped geometry, scene drift) typically within seconds to minutes of continuous generation. This limits the "persistence" required for truly immersive and long-lasting virtual worlds.
2. **Interactivity at high fidelity:** Generating detailed video in real time while simultaneously reacting to live input is computationally intensive. Prior approaches often compromised on resolution, frame rate, or control complexity to achieve interactivity, resulting in experiences limited to coarse camera movements in largely static scenes.

This research positions itself by directly confronting these limitations. It aims to develop a system that maintains visual quality over extended durations and offers high-fidelity interactivity without sacrificing resolution or responsiveness. The paper contrasts its approach with existing models, including proprietary systems like HappyOyster and Genie 3, and open-source interactive world models, many of which are limited to minutes of generation or narrower interaction capabilities (as summarized in Table 1). The introduction of an "agentic harness" also aligns with a growing trend in AI, drawing parallels with how language models are augmented with scaffolds to perform complex, goal-directed tasks, extending simple frame prediction into a more world-like, self-sustaining experience. By releasing its models and harness as open-source, the work also contributes to the community's resources for further development.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives of this research are to overcome the inherent limitations of existing interactive world models, specifically concerning long-horizon stability and high-fidelity real-time interactivity, and to introduce novel mechanisms for autonomous world progression and diverse user interaction.

The detailed motivations are as follows:

- **Addressing Long-Horizon Instability:** Current interactive world models suffer from error accumulation in their autoregressive generation process. Each generated frame conditions the subsequent one, leading to progressive degradation of visual quality and scene coherence over time. This limits the practical utility of these models for creating persistent, explorable environments. The motivation is to develop a foundational model that inherently resists this error accumulation, ensuring visual stability and plausibility over extended, potentially unbounded, durations.
- **Enabling High-Fidelity, Real-Time Interactivity:** Achieving detailed video rendering (e.g., 720p at 60 fps) while simultaneously processing and reacting to live user input is computationally demanding. Existing systems often compromise visual quality or responsiveness to meet real-time constraints, leading to less immersive experiences. The motivation is to create a system that can deliver high-resolution, smooth video generation with rapid response times, thereby enabling a genuinely responsive and interactive user experience that extends beyond simple navigation.
- **Expanding the Spectrum of Interactions:** Many existing interactive world models offer limited interaction capabilities, often confined to basic camera movement or navigation. The motivation is to significantly broaden the range of actions and events the simulated world can support. This includes diverse character actions such as combat, archery, spell-casting, and shooting, as well as the ability to dynamically reshape the environment (e.g., summoning weather).
- **Pioneering Agentic Control in World Modeling:** A generative core alone does not automatically create a living, evolving world. Inspired by the success of agentic harnesses in other AI domains (e.g., coding assistants), the motivation is to integrate intelligent agents within the world model. This involves a "pilot agent" for planning and executing controllable character behaviors and a "director agent" for synthesizing novel environmental elements and ensuring the world remains dynamic and engaging without pre-scripted events. This aims to transition world models from passive frame predictors to self-sustaining, goal-directed, and open-ended experiences.
- **Facilitating Shared Experiences and Accessibility:** To enhance the utility and impact of the world model, a practical deployment interface is motivated. This includes support for multiple players to interact simultaneously and the provision of a lightweight model variant (1.3B) alongside the primary model (14B) to enable easier deployment on single GPUs, thus improving accessibility for researchers and developers.

In summary, the paper aims to provide:
1. An open, robust causal world model with leading visual quality and strong resistance to drift.
2. A real-time distilled model capable of unbounded, drift-free interactive generation at 720p, 60 fps.
3. A rich, controllable action space beyond simple navigation.
4. An agentic harness (pilot and director agents) to orchestrate a living, interactive experience.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology employed in LingBot-World-Infinity involves a multi-stage process encompassing data pipeline construction, a two-stage model training strategy, and a comprehensive deployment framework.

#### 3.1. Data Pipeline

A unified data engine processes heterogeneous raw videos into a temporally localized training dataset. This engine consists of three stages:

- **Data Acquisition:** The corpus is collected from three sources:
 - **Self-collected egocentric videos:** Capture real-world first-person interactions.
 - **Synthetic data from games and Unreal Engine:** Provide accurate scene geometry and aligned interaction signals (e.g., jumping, attacking) that are difficult to obtain from wild videos.
 - **Large-scale web videos:** Offer broad open-domain coverage and visual diversity.
 Each video is standardized with metadata before processing.
- **Data Profiling:** This stage filters and organizes raw videos.
 - Initial validation removes invalid samples and detects shot boundaries using tools like TransNet V2, followed by filtering based on basic constraints (duration, resolution, decoding stability).
 - Technical scoring filters (visual quality, luminance, OCR text occupancy, motion statistics) provide an initial quality control.
 - A Vision-Language Model (VLM) then profiles retained clips for quality attributes (e.g., editing artifacts) and semantic content (e.g., viewpoint, activity category), organizing data for balancing and prompt routing.
- **Multi-dimensional Video Annotation:** Unlike traditional single-global-caption datasets, this pipeline creates multi-dimensional annotations to address the real-time interaction requirement.
 - **Global Context Construction:** A stable video-level semantic summary is established.
 - **Multi-track Event-level Annotation:** For each temporal chunk of a video, annotations are adapted based on the clip profile. Multiple independent event tracks cover subject visibility, motion, interaction state, environmental dynamics, and static scene state. This decoupled approach reduces temporal ambiguity. Temporally aligned, semantically normalized action context (when available) provides auxiliary evidence for annotation.
 - **Chunk-wise Optimization:** Active track states are composed into chunk-wise captions, refined for terminology consistency and temporal smoothness, and speculative or redundant expressions are removed to yield visually grounded annotations.

#### 3.2. LingBot-World-Infinity Model Training

The interactive world simulator is formalized as a causal generative process where each state depends on past visual observations and current user input. This involves a two-stage training strategy:

- **Pre-Training: Causal World Model:**
 - **Action Representation:** User inputs consist of camera poses and textual prompts. Camera poses are encoded using Plücker embeddings and integrated via an adaptive layer normalization (AdaLN) mechanism. Textual control uses chunk-wise prompts for time-localized semantic control.
 - **Mixture of Bidirectional and Autoregressive (MoBA) Attention Mask:** This hybrid attention mechanism addresses overfitting and visual degradation observed with pure teacher forcing in autoregressive generation.
 - **Self-Attention MoBA Mask:** Starts with a teacher forcing component (current frame attends to itself and clean historical context). A bidirectional component (full attention block) is appended to promote adaptation to flexible-length video generation and regularize against overfitting.
 - **Cross-Attention MoBA Mask:** For the teacher forcing component, each frame attends to a background prompt and chunk-wise prompts of current and preceding chunks in a lower-triangular pattern (preventing future information leakage). For the bidirectional component, a global prompt describing the entire video is used.
 - **Training Objective:** Optimized with a conditional flow-matching objective, training the network to predict flow velocity conditioned on clean context, camera pose, and prompt.
- **Post-Training: Few-Step Distillation:** This stage compresses the pre-trained multi-step diffusion model into a few-step generator for real-time interaction and mitigates drift.
 - **Consistency Distillation (CD):** Reduces the number of denoising steps by distilling the teacher model into a consistency model. It enforces local consistency between adjacent points on the same teacher probability-flow ODE trajectory, ensuring trajectory-invariant predictions from intermediate noisy latents.
 - **Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD):** Refines the student model to improve visual fidelity and suppress long-horizon drift. It optimizes the generator using the gradient of the KL divergence between noised student and data distributions. DMD is applied over long self-rollout trajectories to enhance robustness under deployment-time dynamics, reducing accumulated drift.

#### 3.3. Deployment

The deployment stack focuses on real-time performance, rich interaction, high visual quality, and user-friendliness.

- **Systematic Optimization:**
 - **Model Inference Efficiency:** Compiler-based optimization and efficient attention kernels accelerate denoising. A hybrid parallel inference strategy distributes computation across multiple GPUs. Latent generation and frame reconstruction are pipelined asynchronously with dedicated VAE decoding workers to improve hardware utilization.
 - **Streaming Responsiveness:** Decoded frames are streamed incrementally as they become available, overlapping decoding, transmission, and subsequent generation. An efficient video streaming pipeline ensures low-latency delivery.
- **Agentic Interaction Harness:** A "Director-Pilot" Co-Simulation Framework bridges passive video generation and interactive world modeling.
 - **Director (VLM):** Governs macroscopic semantic rules and causal reasoning, analyzing visual phenomena, anticipating interaction outcomes, and formulating "event proposals."
 - **Pilot (Video Generator):** Simulates low-level physical dynamics and renders high-fidelity visual transitions based on the Director's semantic instructions.
 - **Agentic World Interaction:**
 - **Mode A (Direct Semantic Interaction):** VLM analyzes the current frame, generating dynamic event cards for the subject, and proposing logical consequences for user interactions without explicit object masks.
 - **Mode B (Tracking-Assisted Object Interaction):** For precise manipulation, a SAM-based (Segment Anything Model) action-proposal loop identifies and tracks specific interactive objects. VLM deduces state changes, passing updated semantic conditions to the Video Model.
 - **Agentic World Intervention:** Users can act as "creators" via textual prompts, proposing events to alter the simulation. VLM integrates these logically.
 - **Global State Shifts:** Altering environmental factors (time of day, weather, global events).
 - **Local Entity Injection:** Spawning specific entities into the scene.
- **Visual Quality Enhancement:**
 - **Spatio-temporal Refiner:** A lightweight refiner is appended after VAE decoding. It performs spatial refinement (upsampling, sharper details) and temporal refinement (intermediate frames for smoother motion, higher frame rate). Both stages use TensorRT engines and multi-GPU parallelism.
 - **Dynamic KV Cache Management:** The KV-cache is dynamically adapted based on control signals and input state. This retains relevant history while discarding less informative entries, reducing effective context for faster inference and improving coherence.
- **User Interface Design:** An interface (Figure 9) provides a live generation viewport, low-level controls (WASD, IJKL), and an agentic control surface listing VLM-proposed "Event Proposals" bound to hotkeys. These include fixed keys, context-aware character actions and environmental changes, and user-registered event slots.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of LingBot-World-Infinity focuses on its real-time causal distilled model and its causal pretrained backbone, assessing visual fidelity, temporal stability, responsiveness, and interaction capabilities.

#### 4.1. Causal Distilled Model

- **Comparison with Prior Work:** The distilled model demonstrates competitive visual quality compared to both closed-source systems (HappyOyster, Genie 3) and leading open-source interactive world models. It is reported as the only system in the comparison that sustains high-resolution (720p) generation at 60 fps in real time without visible degradation over extended periods, and it supports a wider range of interactions (Figure 11, Table 1).
- **Long-Horizon Stability:** A significant finding is the model's ability to maintain stability over prolonged durations. A single uninterrupted session of over one hour was generated, covering 20 distinct scenarios (Figure 10). During this hour-long rollout, visual quality showed no perceptible decay, and the world remained coherent and explorable. This indicates that the model's stability is a structural property rather than a short-lived artifact.
- **Versatile Interactions:** Qualitative results (Figure 7 and Figure 8) illustrate the model's broad action space, including combat, archery, spell-casting, shooting, and environmental changes (e.g., weather, time of day, entity spawning). The model supports flexible prompt switching, allowing scene semantics to evolve smoothly, and controllable navigation of diverse protagonists and objects.
- **Agentic Harness Efficacy:** The Director-Pilot co-simulation framework enables both direct semantic interactions and tracking-assisted object interactions (Figure 6), alongside prompt-driven world interventions, allowing users to influence global states and inject local entities logically.

#### 4.2. Causal Pretrained Model

- **Long-Horizon Stability:** The causal pretrained backbone, which serves as the teacher for distillation, also exhibits stable performance over longer rollouts compared to prior causal world models like MAGI and HY-World 1.5 (Figure 12). While competing models often degrade within seconds to minutes due to error accumulation, LingBot-World-Infinity's backbone retains sharper textures, stable geometry, and scene coherence over substantially longer durations. This improved stability is attributed by the authors to their anti-drift training approach.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The development of LingBot-World-Infinity represents an advancement in the field of interactive world modeling, with several implications and potential impacts:

- **Unbounded Interactive World Generation:** The achievement of hour-long, drift-free generation at high resolution (720p) and frame rate (60 fps) signifies a step towards truly "infinite worlds." This extended stability moves beyond the limitations of prior models, which typically degrade within minutes, opening possibilities for more persistent and immersive virtual experiences.
- **Enhanced Real-Time Interactivity:** The model's capacity for real-time interaction with a broad and versatile action space (including combat, magic, and environmental manipulation) distinguishes it from systems limited to coarse navigation. This could enable more dynamic and engaging applications in game generation, interactive storytelling, and simulation.
- **Novel Agentic Control Framework:** The integration of a "Director-Pilot" agentic harness is a notable contribution, allowing the world to be self-sustaining, goal-directed, and open-ended without requiring explicit scripting for every event. This framework abstracts higher-level semantic reasoning (Director VLM) from low-level physical simulation (Pilot Video Generator), suggesting a scalable approach for managing complex interactive environments.
- **Open-Source Contribution:** The release of the models and agentic harness to the community provides valuable resources for researchers and developers. This promotes further innovation and benchmarks in interactive world modeling, allowing for replication, extension, and new applications.
- **Bridging Research Gaps:** The methodology directly addresses the identified limitations of long-horizon stability and real-time high-fidelity interactivity, contributing to the fundamental challenges in generative AI for dynamic environments. The dual-stage training (pre-training for robustness, post-training for efficiency) and specialized attention mechanisms are architectural contributions.
- **Potential for Embodied AI and Game Development:** The system's ability to simulate complex interactions and maintain coherent worlds over long periods has implications for training embodied AI agents in diverse and dynamic environments. For game development, it could enable procedural generation of game worlds that are not only vast but also interactively responsive and logically consistent over extended playtimes, reducing manual content creation.

Despite these advancements, the authors acknowledge several limitations that represent future research directions:

- **Long-Term Memory:** The model lacks true long-term memory; revisited regions are regenerated rather than recalled, impacting world identity. Addressing this tension between memory cost and relevant history is a significant challenge.
- **Identity and Style Consistency:** Characters and overall art style can subtly drift over very long rollouts, requiring further work on maintaining consistency.
- **Physical Understanding:** The model's understanding of physics is learned purely from pixels, leading to occasional physically implausible artifacts (e.g., intersecting objects). Incorporating a more explicit notion of geometry or collision physics is a direction for improvement.
- **Computational Resources:** While the distilled model runs in real time, it still requires substantial computational resources. Further efficiency gains are needed for deployment on commodity hardware.

Overall, LingBot-World-Infinity demonstrates an advancement in generating durable, interactive, and agent-driven virtual worlds, providing both a functional system and an open-source foundation for future research.
