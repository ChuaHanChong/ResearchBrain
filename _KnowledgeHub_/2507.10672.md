# Vision Language Action Models in Robotic Manipulation: A Systematic Review

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive systematic review synthesizes Vision Language Action (VLA) models for robotic manipulation, categorizing architectural trends, benchmarking datasets, and evaluating simulation platforms. Researchers from Khalifa University and Universitat Politecnica de Catalunya identify dominant architectural components, reveal critical gaps in multimodal, high-complexity datasets, and outline challenges in simulation for robotic manipulation.

## Problem

- The rapidly expanding and fragmented research landscape in Vision Language Action (VLA) models for robotics lacks a unified synthesis.
- A clear, structured understanding of diverse VLA architectural foundations, benchmark datasets, and evaluation protocols is missing.
- Difficulty in contextualizing current advancements and identifying key limitations or promising future directions within the VLA field.

## Method

- Conducted a rigorous ==systematic review== of 102 **VLA** models, 26 datasets, and 12 simulation platforms published between 2022 and 2025, using both traditional database searches and LLM augmentation.
- Developed a structured taxonomy for **VLA** model architectures and introduced a novel ==quantitative benchmarking framework== for datasets based on task complexity and modality richness.
- Provided a critical evaluation of simulation platforms and identified persistent challenges across architectural design, dataset availability, and simulation capabilities, outlining a strategic roadmap for future research.

## Results

- Categorized 102 VLA models into architectural paradigms, distinguishing between large generalist architectures (e.g., RT-2, Octo) and modular, task-specialized systems (e.g., DexVLA, CLIPort).
- Benchmarked 26 foundational datasets using a novel quantitative framework, visually demonstrating the scarcity of datasets providing both high task complexity and rich multimodal information, with Kaiwu being an outlier.
- Reviewed 12 simulation platforms, highlighting their diverse capabilities in data generation and sim-to-real facilitation while pointing out common challenges in physics accuracy, visual fidelity, and API integration.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Dominant architectural choices for VLA models include CLIP/SigLIP-based Vision Transformers, LLaMA family language encoders, and diffusion-based Transformers for action decoding.
> - There is a significant and underexplored gap in VLA datasets that combine very high task complexity with comprehensive multimodal integration.
> - Simulation platforms exhibit trade-offs between visual realism and computational throughput, often lacking native language grounding APIs, which complicates data generation and sim-to-real transfer.
