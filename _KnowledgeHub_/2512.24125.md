# Unified Embodied VLM Reasoning with Robotic Action via Autoregressive Discretized Pre-training

> [!summary] Summary
> AgiBot Research and the Shanghai Innovation Institute developed GenieReasoner, a system that integrates Vision-Language Model reasoning with precise robotic action via autoregressive discretized pre-training. This system achieved an 82.72% score on the new ERIQ embodied reasoning benchmark and demonstrated superior real-world task success rates across diverse robotic platforms by resolving the reasoning-precision trade-off.

## Problem

- Ambiguity in diagnosing robotic task failures, making it unclear whether issues stem from high-level reasoning deficits or low-level execution errors.
- The persistent "reasoning-precision trade-off" in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, where achieving broad semantic generalization often compromises high-precision physical control, and vice-versa.
- Limitations in current action discretization methods (e.g., simple binning, VQ-VAE, FAST) which either lack precision or stability, and continuous control methods which suffer from optimization conflicts when co-trained with VLM backbones.

## Method

- Introduced the ==Embodied Reasoning Intelligence Quotient (ERIQ)==, a large-scale benchmark with **6,052** embodied question-answer pairs, to quantitatively assess VLM embodied reasoning independently of physical action.
- Developed **FACT** (==Flow-matching Action Tokenizer==) to discretize continuous robotic actions into compact tokens while preserving high-fidelity reconstruction through a novel ==flow-matching decoder==.
- Proposed **GenieReasoner**, a unified ==autoregressive framework== that uses FACT-tokenized actions to jointly optimize multimodal reasoning and precise low-level control within a shared representation space, leveraging a ==three-stage training recipe==.

## Results

- **GenieReasoner** achieved an average score of **82.72%** on the ERIQ benchmark, representing an absolute improvement of **41%** over baseline VLMs, particularly excelling in action understanding and human intention comprehension.
- **FACT** demonstrated substantially lower Mean Squared Error in action reconstruction compared to baselines, showing an order of magnitude improvement in fidelity, which is critical for precise motor control.
- In real-world experiments on AgiBot and ARX platforms, **GenieReasoner** consistently outperformed state-of-the-art discrete and continuous VLA baselines across all complexity settings, resolving the reasoning-precision trade-off and exhibiting robust generalization in complex tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - There is a strong positive correlation between VLM embodied reasoning capabilities (as measured by ERIQ scores) and end-to-end VLA generalization performance in robotic manipulation tasks.
> - Flow-matching provides a highly effective and stable method for converting continuous actions into discrete tokens, achieving significantly higher reconstruction fidelity and compactness compared to existing action tokenization methods.
> - A multi-stage training strategy, including joint pre-training with diverse VQA and action data, followed by post-training focused on embodied VQA and action data, is crucial for unifying reasoning and action alignment while preserving general VLM capabilities.
