# Unified Embodied VLM Reasoning with Robotic Action via Autoregressive Discretized Pre-training

> [!summary] Summary
> AgiBot Research and the Shanghai Innovation Institute developed GenieReasoner, a system that integrates Vision-Language Model reasoning with precise robotic action via autoregressive discretized pre-training. This system achieved an 82.72% score on the new ERIQ embodied reasoning benchmark and demonstrated superior real-world task success rates across diverse robotic platforms by resolving the reasoning-precision trade-off.

## Problem

- Ambiguity in diagnosing robotic task failures, making it unclear whether issues stem from high-level reasoning deficits or low-level execution errors.
- The persistent "reasoning-precision trade-off" in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, where achieving broad semantic generalization often compromises high-precision physical control, and vice-versa.
- Limitations in current action discretization methods (e.g., simple binning, VQ-VAE, FAST) which either lack precision or stability, and continuous control methods which suffer from optimization conflicts when co-trained with VLM backbones.

## Method

- Introduced the ==Embodied Reasoning Intelligence Quotient (ERIQ)==, a large-scale benchmark with **6,052** embodied question-answer pairs, to quantitatively assess VLM embodied reasoning independently of physical action.
- Developed **FACT** (==Flow-matching Action Tokenizer==) to discretize continuous robotic actions into compact tokens while preserving high-fidelity reconstruction through a novel ==flow-matching decoder==.
- Proposed **GenieReasoner**, a unified ==autoregressive framework== that uses FACT-tokenized actions to jointly optimize multimodal reasoning and precise low-level control within a shared representation space, leveraging a ==three-stage training recipe==.

## Results

- **GenieReasoner** achieved an average score of **82.72%** on the ERIQ benchmark, representing an absolute improvement of **41%** over baseline VLMs, particularly excelling in action understanding and human intention comprehension.
- **FACT** demonstrated substantially lower Mean Squared Error in action reconstruction compared to baselines, showing an order of magnitude improvement in fidelity, which is critical for precise motor control.
- In real-world experiments on AgiBot and ARX platforms, **GenieReasoner** consistently outperformed state-of-the-art discrete and continuous VLA baselines across all complexity settings, resolving the reasoning-precision trade-off and exhibiting robust generalization in complex tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - There is a strong positive correlation between VLM embodied reasoning capabilities (as measured by ERIQ scores) and end-to-end VLA generalization performance in robotic manipulation tasks.
> - Flow-matching provides a highly effective and stable method for converting continuous actions into discrete tokens, achieving significantly higher reconstruction fidelity and compactness compared to existing action tokenization methods.
> - A multi-stage training strategy, including joint pre-training with diverse VQA and action data, followed by post-training focused on embodied VQA and action data, is crucial for unifying reasoning and action alignment while preserving general VLM capabilities.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research is situated at the forefront of developing **general-purpose robotic systems** capable of operating autonomously in open-world, unstructured environments. This ambitious goal necessitates a delicate balance between **broad generalization** across diverse, unseen scenarios and **high-precision action execution** for reliable task completion.

Historically, robotic control often relied on meticulously engineered, task-specific policies. However, the advent of **Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**, pre-trained on vast internet-scale datasets, has revolutionized the field by introducing unprecedented semantic generalization capabilities. These VLMs, when extended to **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models**, attempt to bridge abstract semantic reasoning with concrete low-level physical execution.

The current landscape of VLA models faces a fundamental dilemma:
- **Semantic Generalization vs. Embodied Reasoning:** While VLMs excel at semantic understanding, their application to embodied tasks often reveals insufficient "embodied reasoning" – the ability to synthesize spatial awareness, temporal dynamics, and causal logic for planning and adapting physical actions. Without this, policies can rely on superficial visual-action correlations, leading to brittle performance under novel conditions.
- **Reasoning vs. Action Precision:** Even with strong embodied reasoning, real-world task completion demands high-precision action execution. Current VLA architectures grapple with this "reasoning-precision trade-off."
 - **Discrete Action Quantization:** One class of methods discretizes continuous robotic actions into tokens, allowing VLA models to leverage powerful autoregressive Transformers. However, this approach faces a precision-efficiency trade-off: simple binning requires too many tokens for fine-grained control, learned quantization (e.g., VQ-VAE) lacks fidelity, and rule-based adaptive schemes (e.g., FAST) can lead to decoding instability.
 - **Continuous Control via Generative Heads:** Another approach uses diffusion or flow-matching objectives for continuous action prediction, offering superior precision. Yet, these generative heads often suffer from optimization conflicts when co-trained with discrete VLM backbones. High-frequency gradients from action denoising can degrade the VLM's pre-trained semantic reasoning, necessitating complex architectural safeguards (e.g., "Knowledge Insulation," "Phased Alignment").

The paper directly addresses this persistent tension. It argues that strong embodied reasoning is a *necessary prerequisite* for generalization, but *insufficient* without high-precision control. It positions itself by introducing both a diagnostic tool (ERIQ) to quantify embodied reasoning independently and a novel methodological solution (FACT and GenieReasoner) to unify high-level reasoning with precise low-level control, thereby overcoming the limitations of previous VLA architectures. This work seeks to advance beyond merely leveraging VLMs for robotics to truly integrating their reasoning prowess with the demanding physical requirements of real-world manipulation.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching goal of this research is to advance the development of **general-purpose robotic systems** that can operate robustly in open-world environments by simultaneously achieving **broad generalization** and **high-precision action execution**.

The authors identify two critical motivations stemming from limitations in current Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models:

1. **Addressing the Ambiguity in Robotic Task Failure:** When a robot fails a task, it is often unclear whether the root cause is a deficiency in high-level semantic understanding (reasoning) or an error in low-level motor execution (precision). This ambiguity hinders systematic diagnosis and improvement of VLA models.
 - **Motivation:** To provide a **decoupled and quantitative assessment** of embodied reasoning capabilities, separate from action execution. This diagnostic clarity is essential for identifying and addressing specific bottlenecks.
 - **Objective:** Introduce the **Embodied Reasoning Intelligence Quotient (ERIQ)**, a large-scale benchmark designed to evaluate VLM embodied reasoning independently of physical execution, thereby enabling a clearer understanding of the reasoning-generalization relationship.

2. **Bridging the Reasoning-Precision Trade-off:** Current VLA models struggle to simultaneously maintain strong reasoning capabilities (inherent to large VLMs) and achieve high-fidelity, precise robotic control. Models optimized for reasoning often lack precision, while those excelling in precision may have limited generalization due to weak semantic grounding.
 - **Motivation:** To develop a unified framework that can effectively integrate the discrete semantic representations from VLM reasoning with the continuous control signals required for dexterous physical execution, without compromising either.
 - **Objective:** Propose **FACT (Flow-matching Action Tokenizer)** to convert continuous control into discrete sequences while preserving high-fidelity trajectory reconstruction. This enables **GenieReasoner** to jointly optimize both high-level reasoning and precise low-level action within a unified autoregressive framework, thereby resolving the long-standing trade-off.

In essence, the paper aims to provide both a **principled diagnostic framework** for understanding embodied intelligence and a **novel architectural solution** for achieving robust, general-purpose robotic manipulation by synergistically combining advanced reasoning with precise physical control.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology centers around two core contributions: the **ERIQ benchmark** for evaluating embodied reasoning and the **GenieReasoner system** (powered by FACT) for unified reasoning and action generation.

#### 3.1. Embodied Reasoning Intelligence Quotient (ERIQ) Benchmark

To provide a clear assessment of embodied reasoning, ERIQ decouples cognitive understanding from motor control.
- **Structure:** ERIQ is a large-scale benchmark comprising 6,052 embodied question-answer (QA) pairs. It uses a Visual Question Answering (VQA) protocol, ensuring evaluation is independent of a control policy.
- **Reasoning Dimensions:** It assesses four critical pillars of embodied reasoning, broken down into 15 fine-grained sub-tasks:
 1. **Spatial Perception & Grounding:** Scene Understanding, Task Grounding, Relative Position Grounding, Dual-view Matching. (e.g., locating objects, understanding spatial relationships).
 2. **Planning & Monitoring:** Sub-task Planning, Fine-grained Planning, Trajectory Understanding, Action Understanding, Task Progress, Success Detection. (e.g., sequencing actions, predicting outcomes, evaluating progress).
 3. **Error Detection & Recovery:** Mistake Existence, Mistake Classification, Mistake Recovery. (e.g., identifying, categorizing, and suggesting fixes for operational errors).
 4. **Human Intent Understanding:** Human Intention Comprehension, Human-Robot Interaction. (e.g., inferring human goals to facilitate collaboration).
- **Evaluation Protocol:** All questions are in a standardized multiple-choice or binary (Yes/No) format, ensuring deterministic and reproducible evaluation, avoiding the subjectivity of open-ended generation metrics.
- **Data Source:** All QA pairs are derived from authentic, real-world robotic trials, providing a faithful first-person "Robo View" perspective. The dataset covers 100+ distinct task scenarios across five domains (household, restaurant, supermarket, industrial, office) and integrates three modalities: single image, sequential images, and interleaved image-text sequences.
- **Novelty:** ERIQ is noted as being the most comprehensive benchmark to date, offering exhaustive coverage across all four reasoning dimensions, particularly excelling in areas like error recovery and human intent inference, which are largely absent or partially addressed in existing benchmarks.

#### 3.2. GenieReasoner: Unified Discrete Low-Level Policy Framework

The GenieReasoner system aims to reconcile the discrete nature of VLM autoregressive backbones with the continuous nature of robotic actions.

#### 3.2.1. A. FACT: Flow-matching Action Tokenizer

FACT is designed to discretize continuous actions into compact tokens while preserving high-fidelity reconstruction. It consists of:
- **VQ-Encoder (E_θ):** Takes a continuous action chunk (a_0:H) and maps it to a continuous latent representation (e). A bit-wise quantizer (Q) then converts this into a discrete code (c ∈ {-1, +1}^L×D) based on the sign of its elements. The encoder uses a Multimodal Diffusion Transformer (MM-DiT) architecture.
- **Flow-Matching Decoder (D_θ):** This is the core innovation for high-fidelity reconstruction. It is trained using the Rectified Flow objective to learn a velocity field.
 - **Principle:** It models a straight-line trajectory that transports samples from a standard Gaussian distribution (z) to the target action data distribution (a). The target constant velocity along this path is simply (a - z).
 - **Training:** The decoder D_θ predicts this velocity given a noisy state (a^(t)), the flow time step (t), and the quantized discrete action code (c) from the encoder.
 - **Loss Function:** The training objective combines a flow-matching loss (Mean Squared Error between predicted and target velocity) with quantization regularization losses (entropy loss for codebook usage and commitment loss for embedding fidelity).
- **Inference:** During robot operation, the VLM policy predicts a sequence of discrete action codes (ˆc). The pre-trained FACT decoder then solves an Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE) to integrate the learned velocity field, starting from Gaussian noise (ˆa^(t=0)) and using ˆc as a condition, to reconstruct a smooth, continuous action trajectory (ˆa).

#### 3.2.2. B. GenieReasoner System Architecture

- **Training:** A unified pipeline jointly trains the VLM backbone. FACT tokenizes continuous action sequences. The VLM learns to map multimodal observations (images, language instructions) to compact discrete action tokens, effectively co-optimizing multimodal reasoning and robotic control within a shared representation space.
- **Inference:** The VLM backbone generates discrete action codes. The FACT decoder then converts these codes into continuous control signals, ensuring precise manipulation grounded in the VLM's semantic understanding.

#### 3.3. Training Data and Recipe

- **Training Data:** A diverse mix of general multimodal datasets (e.g., Cambrian-10M, LLaVA-OneVision) to preserve general VLM capabilities, and specialized embodied-intelligence datasets (e.g., NVIDIA Cosmos-Reason, ShareRobot, Robo2VLM) for embodied understanding. Critically, data curated from "AgiBot World" (2D trajectory, grounding annotations, sub-task planning, scene understanding) is used to align reasoning with the specific egocentric perspectives of real-world robots. Large-scale embodied demonstrations from various robotic platforms (AgiBot World, ARX, AgileX) are used for action pre-training.
- **Three-Stage Training Recipe:**
 1. **Stage 1 (FACT Tokenizer Training):** Train the FACT tokenizer independently using its specific loss function.
 2. **Stage 2 (Joint Pre-training):** Perform end-to-end joint training on a mixture of general VQA data, embodied VQA data, and tokenized action data. This stage establishes spatio-temporal grounding and motor control.
 3. **Stage 3 (Task-Specific Post-training):** Conduct post-training on a mixture of embodied VQA and action data. This stabilizes alignment and prevents catastrophic forgetting of reasoning capabilities while fine-tuning for specific tasks.

This comprehensive approach combines robust diagnostic tools with an innovative architectural design and a multi-stage training strategy to address the complex challenges of embodied AI.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments systematically evaluate GenieReasoner's embodied reasoning, tokenizer architecture, training strategy, and real-world performance.

#### 4.1. Embodied Reasoning Capabilities

- **ERIQ Benchmark Performance:** GenieReasoner (Ours-3B) significantly outperforms other VLMs and even larger models like GPT-4o-mini and Gemini-2.5-pro on the ERIQ benchmark. It achieves an average reasoning score of **82.72%**, an absolute improvement of **41%** over the base Qwen2.5-VL-3B (58.64%).
 - Notable gains were observed in high-level semantic tasks such as **Action Understanding (96.67%)** and **Human Intention Comprehension (96.44%)**.
 - Significant improvements also occurred in multi-perspective spatial awareness tasks like **Dualview Matching (+31.01%)** and **Relative Position Grounding (+24.9%)** over the base model, indicating effective strengthening of spatial cognition through embodied co-training.
- **Correlation with End-to-End Generalization:** Empirical analysis on ERIQ revealed a strong positive correlation between VLM reasoning capabilities (ERIQ scores) and end-to-end VLA generalization performance (task success rates). This validates ERIQ as a reliable predictor for downstream manipulation success.
- **General VLM Performance Preservation:** Despite specialized embodied training, GenieReasoner maintained or even enhanced performance on standard open-source VLM benchmarks (e.g., CV-Bench, EmbSpa), demonstrating that the training pipeline effectively avoids "catastrophic forgetting" of general visual-language knowledge.
- **Qualitative Generalization:** GenieReasoner exhibited robust zero-shot generalization and spatial reasoning across diverse robotic platforms (AgiBot G01, AgileX, ARX) and simulation environments (AgiBot GenieSim) for tasks like task-oriented grounding, trajectory prediction, and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) inference, confirming cross-embodiment transferability.

#### 4.2. FACT Tokenizer Ablation

- **Reconstruction Fidelity:** FACT demonstrates substantially lower **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** in action reconstruction compared to the FAST+ baseline across various code lengths (Figure 7). It often outperforms FAST+ by an entire order of magnitude at equivalent compression levels.
- **Precision and Compactness:** This result confirms that FACT's flow-matching decoder provides a more expressive and stable mapping from discrete tokens back to continuous actions, enabling both more compact action representations and higher reconstruction accuracy, which is crucial for fine motor control. The chosen configuration of a 2^12-entry vocabulary and code length of 20 was found to offer an optimal balance.

#### 4.3. Ablation of Different Training Recipes

The ablation studies using the GenieSim simulator evaluated the impact of incorporating Embodied VQA and Action Alignment at different training stages (Table III):
- **Embodied VQA as Foundation:** Comparing Exp #0 (vanilla VLM) and Exp #1 (with Embodied VQA pre-training but no action alignment) showed that Embodied VQA significantly boosts ERIQ scores and language-following metrics (semantic grounding) even without enabling physical execution.
- **Action Alignment for Execution:** Introducing Action Alignment (Exp #2 and #3) led to a substantial leap in execution success rates, confirming it as a prerequisite for end-to-end physical execution.
- **Optimal Synthesis (GenieReasoner Training Recipe):** Exp #4, which utilized the proposed three-stage recipe (joint pre-training with general VQA, embodied VQA, and action data, followed by post-training with embodied VQA and action data), achieved the highest overall performance in both language following and task success rates. This highlights the importance of maintaining Embodied VQA during post-training to preserve reasoning capabilities while aligning with action generation.

#### 4.4. Real-World Evaluation

Extensive real-world experiments on AgiBot and ARX platforms against state-of-the-art baselines provided compelling evidence:
- **Resolution of Reasoning-Precision Trade-off:**
 - **Discrete Baselines (e.g., π_0-FAST):** Showed good language following (semantic reasoning) but poor task success due to high quantization artifacts preventing precise physical contact.
 - **Continuous Baselines (e.g., π_0.5, GR00T):** Exhibited robust manipulation once the target was correctly identified but were highly prone to semantic errors (approaching wrong objects) due to weak connections between their action heads and VLM reasoning.
 - **GenieReasoner:** Consistently outperformed both types of baselines across all five complexity settings (Seen/Unseen Object, Color/Spatial/Pose Variation, Semantic Understanding). It achieved high success rates *and* high instruction-following accuracy (Figures 8-10), demonstrating successful synergy.
- **Overall Performance:** GenieReasoner achieved the highest aggregate score, signifying its ability to guide precise motor control through effective VLM reasoning and flow-matching decoding.
- **Qualitative Robustness:** Demonstrated cross-embodiment robustness and operational generalization on complex real-world tasks, including fine-grained Out-of-Distribution (OOD) manipulation, long-horizon semantic tasks (e.g., shelf restoration), and deformable object manipulation (garment folding) (Figure 11).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

This research makes several significant contributions with far-reaching potential impacts on the field of robotics and artificial intelligence:

1. **Principled Framework for Embodied Intelligence Diagnosis:** The introduction of the **Embodied Reasoning Intelligence Quotient (ERIQ)** benchmark is a crucial advancement. By systematically decoupling embodied reasoning capabilities from motor execution errors, ERIQ provides a much-needed diagnostic tool for VLA models. This clarity enables researchers to pinpoint whether performance failures stem from cognitive deficits or control inaccuracies, facilitating targeted improvements. The established strong correlation between ERIQ scores and real-world task success validates its predictive power, making it a valuable resource for future model development and evaluation.

2. **Resolution of the Reasoning-Precision Trade-off:** The **GenieReasoner system**, powered by the **Flow-matching Action Tokenizer (FACT)**, offers a robust solution to a long-standing challenge in VLA models: balancing high-level semantic reasoning with precise low-level physical control. FACT's ability to convert continuous actions into discrete tokens while maintaining high-fidelity reconstruction through flow-matching is a methodological breakthrough. This allows VLA backbones to leverage their discrete autoregressive nature for powerful reasoning without sacrificing the millimeter-level precision required for dexterous manipulation. This unified approach moves beyond brittle compromises, enabling synergistic co-optimization.

3. **Enhanced Generalization and Robustness in Robotics:** GenieReasoner's superior performance in real-world, open-set tasks and its demonstrated generalization across diverse robotic embodiments (humanoid and arm platforms) is highly impactful. The ability to handle unseen objects, color variations, challenging poses, and abstract semantic instructions without task-specific adaptation signifies a critical step towards truly general-purpose robotic systems. This increased robustness will accelerate the deployment of intelligent robots in unstructured, dynamic environments, from homes and restaurants to industrial settings.

4. **Inspiration for Future VLA Architectures:** FACT's flow-matching approach offers a novel paradigm for action tokenization. It showcases that high-fidelity continuous control can be achieved through discrete sequence modeling combined with a generative decoder, potentially inspiring new hybrid architectures that bridge symbolic and continuous representations more effectively. This could lead to more efficient and stable training of VLA models, overcoming the gradient interference issues faced by previous continuous-action methods.

5. **Advancement in Human-Robot Interaction:** ERIQ's inclusion of "Human Intent Understanding" dimensions and GenieReasoner's proficiency in these areas signify progress towards more intuitive and collaborative human-robot interaction. Robots that can infer human goals and adapt their actions accordingly will be essential for seamless integration into human-centric environments.

In conclusion, this research provides both a critical diagnostic tool and an innovative architectural solution that collectively push the boundaries of general-purpose robotics. By offering a principled framework to diagnose and overcome the reasoning-precision trade-off, "Unified Embodied VLM Reasoning with Robotic Action via Autoregressive Discretized Pre-training" makes a substantial contribution towards realizing robust, intelligent, and versatile robotic systems capable of operating effectively in the complexities of the real world.
