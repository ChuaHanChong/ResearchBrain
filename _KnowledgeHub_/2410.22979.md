# LumiSculpt: Enabling Consistent Portrait Lighting in Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> LumiSculpt, a new framework, addresses consistent portrait lighting in video generation by integrating a unique synthetic dataset, LumiHuman, and a novel dual-branch training framework with a disentanglement loss. This approach enables precise control over light source position, intensity, and trajectory, demonstrating superior inter-frame consistency and lighting accuracy over existing text-to-video and image relighting methods.

## Problem

- Scarcity of high-quality, lighting-specific datasets, especially for videos, that provide explicit lighting information for generative models.
- Complexity in accurately representing and integrating lighting effects into end-to-end video generation models without extensive annotated data.
- Challenge of decoupling specific lighting control from other visual attributes like appearance and semantics to prevent overfitting and ensure diverse content generation.

## Method

- Constructed LumiHuman, a large-scale portrait lighting dataset with over 220,000 videos, created using Unreal Engine for precise control over lighting parameters and diverse human subjects.
- Introduced a novel lighting representation that projects lighting information onto a blank canvas in pixel space, facilitating effective integration into latent diffusion models.
- Developed **LumiSculpt** as a plug-and-play module within latent diffusion models, employing a dual-branch training framework with a disentanglement loss (L_dis) to isolate lighting control from appearance.

## Results

- LumiSculpt achieved superior quantitative performance across five metrics, including a Lighting Direction RMSE of **0.3500** and Frame-wise CLIP Image Similarity of **0.9951**, outperforming Open-Sora and other baselines.
- Qualitative evaluations confirmed LumiSculpt's ability to maintain video coherence and visual quality while achieving precise control over lighting trajectory and intensity, addressing flickering and static lighting issues seen in comparative methods.
- Ablation studies demonstrated the critical roles of caption augmentation for diverse content generation and the dual-branch framework with disentanglement loss for robust appearance decoupling and accurate lighting control.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Synthetic datasets, generated with precise parameter control (e.g., LumiHuman), can effectively overcome data scarcity challenges for fine-grained attribute control in generative tasks.
> - Representing complex lighting conditions as a simple pixel-space projection onto a blank canvas offers an effective and model-agnostic way to integrate lighting information into latent diffusion models.
> - A dual-branch training framework combined with a disentanglement loss provides an effective mechanism to learn specific attribute control (like lighting) while preserving the diverse generative capabilities and preventing appearance leakage from the foundational model.
