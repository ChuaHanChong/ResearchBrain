# One-Step Diffusion Policy: Fast Visuomotor Policies via Diffusion Distillation

> [!summary] Summary
> One-Step Diffusion Policy (OneDP) from NVIDIA and UT Austin introduces a method to accelerate visuomotor policies by distilling multi-step diffusion models into a single-step action generator. This approach enables real-time robotic control, achieving an action prediction frequency of 62.5 Hz in real-world settings, a 42x improvement over previous diffusion policies, while maintaining or improving success rates.

## Problem

- Traditional diffusion policies for robotics, like Diffusion Policy, suffer from slow inference speed due to iterative denoising steps (e.g., 1.49 Hz), limiting their real-time application.
- This slow inference is inadequate for dynamic tasks, human interaction, or rapidly changing environments that demand quick robotic responses.
- Previous attempts to accelerate diffusion policies for robotics still required multiple sampling iterations and faced performance degradation or slow convergence.

## Method

- **One-Step Diffusion Policy** (OneDP) distills knowledge from pre-trained, multi-step diffusion policies into a single-step implicit action generator (Gθ).
- The distillation minimizes a ==Kullback-Leibler (KL)== divergence-based loss across diffused action samples, approximating the score of the generator and the pre-trained policy.
- OneDP offers two variants: OneDP-S (Stochastic) which uses an auxiliary generator score network, and OneDP-D (Deterministic) which directly solves the score.

## Results

- OneDP achieved an action prediction frequency of **62.5** Hz in real-world settings (7 ms per prediction), a **42x** speedup compared to Diffusion Policy's **1.49** Hz (66 ms).
- In simulation, OneDP-S achieved an average success rate of **0.843**, slightly outperforming the pre-trained Diffusion Policy (**0.829** at 100 steps).
- OneDP demonstrated enhanced robustness in dynamic real-world tasks, achieving **100%** success in a task with human interference compared to **80%** for Diffusion Policy.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Distilling diffusion policies through KL divergence over diffused samples can achieve single-step generation without sacrificing performance, and can even improve it by avoiding cumulative errors.
> - Warm-starting the distilled generator and its auxiliary network from the pre-trained teacher significantly accelerates convergence and preserves performance.
> - Stochastic policies (OneDP-S) generally demonstrate greater versatility and often higher success rates compared to their deterministic counterparts (OneDP-D) in complex robotic tasks.
