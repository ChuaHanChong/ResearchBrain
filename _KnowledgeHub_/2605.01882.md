# Chart-FR1: Visual Focus-Driven Fine-Grained Reasoning on Dense Charts

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework, Chart-FR1, enhances Multimodal Large Language Models' (MLLMs) ability to understand and reason over high information density (HID) charts through focused reasoning mechanisms. The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming GPT-4o on the new HID-Chart benchmark and other existing MLLMs across various chart understanding tasks.

## Problem

- Existing MLLMs struggle with fine-grained perception on High Information Density (HID) charts, often omitting critical visual cues.
- Current MLLMs are susceptible to redundant or noisy visual information when reasoning on dense charts, degrading accuracy and efficiency.
- Reinforcement learning algorithms used for MLLM reasoning lack adaptive mechanisms, with fixed KL penalties over-restricting exploration for complex reasoning chains.

## Method

- Introduced ==Focus-CoT== (Focusing Chain-of-Thought), a mechanism that explicitly links reasoning steps to fine-grained visual evidence (OCR text, bounding boxes) through supervised fine-tuning.
- Developed Focus-GRPO (Focus-Driven Reinforcement Learning Algorithm), which refines visual focusing efficiency via an information-efficiency reward and an adaptive KL penalty for flexible reasoning depth control.
- Constructed HID-Chart, a novel benchmark with an information-density metric and human annotation, specifically designed to rigorously evaluate MLLMs on high information density charts.

## Results

- Chart-FR1 achieved a mean accuracy of **67.1%** across five popular chart benchmarks, surpassing the base Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** by **6.1%** and closed-source GPT-4o (**64.0%**).
- On the newly constructed HID-Chart benchmark, Chart-FR1-**7B** achieved **53.0%** accuracy, outperforming Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** by **10.0%**, Qwen2.5-VL-**72B** by **1.5%**, and GPT-4o (**51.2%**) by **1.8%**.
- Ablation studies demonstrated that the adaptive KL penalty and information-efficiency reward in Focus-GRPO each contributed **1.3%** to the average accuracy, confirming their individual effectiveness in improving reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly associating reasoning steps with localized visual cues (OCR text, image regions) significantly enhances fine-grained perception and overall reasoning accuracy.
> - Optimizing visual focusing efficiency by penalizing redundant information and adaptively controlling reasoning depth using a variable KL penalty is crucial for robust performance on complex, dense charts.
> - A two-stage training approach combining supervised fine-tuning for initial visual focus activation and reinforcement learning for refined, adaptive reasoning is effective for developing advanced chart understanding capabilities.
