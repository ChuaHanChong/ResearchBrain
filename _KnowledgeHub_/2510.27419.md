# DeepCompress: A Dual Reward Strategy for Dynamically Exploring and Compressing Reasoning Chains

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Tencent AI Lab developed DeepCompress, a framework that dynamically adjusts Large Reasoning Model (LRM) chain-of-thought length based on problem difficulty to simultaneously enhance both accuracy and inference efficiency. The method achieved an average absolute accuracy improvement of +2.7 points on mathematical benchmarks for the 7B model while compressing response length by 16.6%.

## Problem

- Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) exhibit cognitive inefficiencies, such as 'overthinking' simple problems with excessive steps and 'underthinking' complex problems, leading to unstable reasoning.
- Existing methods for improving LRM efficiency (e.g., SFT on concise CoT or RL with token-length penalties) often reduce reasoning length at the cost of a slight degradation in accuracy.
- A fundamental challenge remains in achieving both superior reasoning accuracy and computational efficiency in LRMs, as current approaches often present a trade-off.

## Method

- **DeepCompress** extends the Zero RL training paradigm by introducing a ==Dual Length Reward== that can either penalize brevity or encourage exploration, dynamically adapting its direction.
- A Model-Aware Difficulty mechanism assesses question difficulty in real-time by comparing a question's group pass ratio (correct responses in a batch) to the overall batch pass ratio, classifying problems as 'Simple' or 'Hard'.
- Robustness is enhanced by applying the length reward only if the generated solution is correct, and by smoothing the batch pass ratio using an Exponential Moving Average to ensure stable training and gradual adaptation.

## Results

- DeepCompress-Zero-**7B** achieved an average absolute improvement of +2.7 points in accuracy over DeepMath-Zero-**7B** on mathematical benchmarks, with notable gains of +6.5 points on AIME 25.
- The framework significantly compressed response lengths, reducing token usage by an average of **57.9%** for the **3B** model and **16.6%** for the **7B** model across benchmarks, demonstrating improved inference efficiency.
- DeepCompress fostered more effective learning dynamics, exhibiting increased policy entropy for exploration and promoting more frequent, yet concise, reflection behaviors on challenging problems, leading to 'smarter thinking'.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Consistently favoring shorter reasoning paths is not optimal; while short responses benefit single-attempt accuracy, longer responses can encompass more correct solutions for challenging problems, improving group accuracy.
> - Dynamically tailoring reasoning length to problem difficulty allows LRMs to compress simple problem solutions for efficiency while exploring longer chains for hard problems to maintain or enhance accuracy.
> - A combined reward structure, integrating outcome correctness with an adaptively scaled length reward, and conditioned on correct solutions, is crucial for balanced learning without reward hacking.
