# Towards High-Consistency Embodied World Model with Multi-View Trajectory Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Midea Group, Tongji University, and East China Normal University introduce MTV-World, an embodied world model that enhances visuomotor prediction and physical interaction consistency by utilizing multi-view trajectory videos as explicit control signals and object-aware priors. The model outperforms baselines on robot manipulation tasks, achieving a Jaccard Index of 54.9 for interaction accuracy on a dual-arm dataset and demonstrating robust generalization.

## Problem

- Existing embodied world models struggle to accurately translate low-level robot actions into precise movements within predicted visual frames, resulting in unrealistic arm trajectories.
- Inaccurate robot arm motion leads to inconsistent simulations of object-arm interactions, undermining the reliability of world models for physical dynamics.
- A lack of objective evaluation metrics that quantitatively assess robotic motion precision and object interaction accuracy beyond perceptual quality (e.g., FID, FVD).

## Method

- Introduces ==trajectory videos== as explicit, visually grounded control signals, synthesized by projecting 3D robot end-effector poses onto 2D image planes across multiple camera views.
- Incorporates an ==object mask== from the initial frame as a ==foreground prior== to explicitly model the interaction between robotic arms and manipulated objects.
- Develops **MTV-World**, a ==Diffusion Transformer==-based ==multi-view architecture== that integrates reference images, text prompts, multi-view video sequences, and the synthesized trajectory videos for robust prediction.

## Results

- MTV-World achieved superior performance across both perceptual and interaction metrics, with a Jaccard Index (J) of **54.9** (View 1) and **45.0** (View 2) on a self-collected dual-arm dataset, significantly outperforming baselines (e.g., Ctrl-World's J of **35.3** for View 1).
- The model consistently outperformed baselines on the RealSource-World dataset, demonstrating generalization capability by maintaining high consistency and accurate object-arm interactions (e.g., J of **41.8** vs. Ctrl-World's **26.4** on the Magazines task).
- Ablation studies confirmed that the trajectory representation, multi-view architecture, and object mask prior individually contribute to improved performance, and MTV-World demonstrated robust zero-shot generalization to novel camera views, manipulation tasks, and objects.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Using explicit multi-view trajectory videos as control signals provides clearer guidance for the world model, significantly improving the precision of predicted robot arm movements and the physical consistency of object interactions.
> - A multi-view architectural design effectively mitigates spatial information loss that occurs when projecting 3D actions to 2D, enhancing overall geometric and physical consistency.
> - Integrating an object mask as a foreground prior facilitates the modeling of contact-aware motion dynamics, leading to more accurate and reliable robot-object interaction predictions.
