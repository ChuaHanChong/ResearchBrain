# Spatio-Temporal LLM: Reasoning about Environments and Actions

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign developed the Reasoning about Environments and Actions (REA) dataset and two Spatio-Temporal LLM (STLLM) baselines to enable large language models to jointly understand 3D environments and egocentric video actions. The STLLM models achieved up to 46.50% overall accuracy on REA, outperforming existing multimodal LLMs on complex spatio-temporal reasoning tasks.

## Problem

- Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) struggle with jointly understanding 3D environments (point clouds) and dynamic egocentric video actions.
- Existing MLLMs often simplify videos as sequences of independent frames or neglect deeper temporal dynamics, failing to integrate global spatial context with local actions.
- A comprehensive benchmark dataset specifically designed for evaluating this joint spatio-temporal reasoning capability was lacking.

## Method

- A novel dataset, ==REA (Reasoning about Environments and Actions)==, was created with 24,371 question-answer pairs requiring joint understanding of 3D point clouds and egocentric video clips, derived from EPIC-KITCHENS, VISOR, and EPIC-FIELDS.
- Two baseline Spatio-Temporal LLM (STLLM) architectures were proposed: ==STLLM-3D==, which directly concatenates 3D features with image and text embeddings, and ==STLLM-Aligner==, which uses a cross-modal alignment module to create compact spatial representations.
- The REA dataset includes five distinct tasks: Relative Direction, Relative Distance, Find My Item, Furniture Affordance Prediction, and Action Planning, to probe various aspects of spatio-temporal reasoning.

## Results

- Existing MLLMs achieved low overall LLM-Judge accuracies on the REA dataset, ranging from **23.85%** to **31.46%** (ChatGPT-4o judge) and **35.74%** to **39.50%** (Gemini 2.0 Flash judge).
- The proposed STLLM baselines significantly outperformed finetuned existing models on REA, with STLLM-Aligner achieving the highest overall accuracy of **41.89%** (ChatGPT-4o) and **46.50%** (Gemini 2.0 Flash).
- In zero-shot cross-dataset evaluation on SQA3D, STLLM baselines and models finetuned on REA showed consistent improvements over vanilla counterparts, demonstrating generalization capabilities for 3D reasoning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Off-the-shelf multimodal LLMs demonstrate limited capabilities in spatio-temporal reasoning, confirming that current models struggle to effectively integrate 3D environmental context with egocentric actions.
> - Specialized architectural designs that explicitly integrate 3D information are crucial for improving spatio-temporal understanding in MLLMs, as evidenced by the superior performance of STLLM baselines.
> - The REA dataset effectively serves as a benchmark for training and evaluating models on complex spatio-temporal prompts, with finetuning on REA showing transferability to other 3D reasoning tasks.
