# Next Token Prediction Towards Multimodal Intelligence: A Comprehensive Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey systematically charts the rapidly evolving field of multimodal artificial intelligence by unifying understanding and generation tasks through the lens of Next Token Prediction (NTP). It provides a detailed taxonomy for Multimodal NTP, categorizing advancements in tokenization, model architectures, training strategies, and evaluation, while also identifying critical open challenges.

## Problem

- The rapidly expanding field of multimodal AI, particularly with Large Multimodal Models (LMMs), lacks a unified conceptual framework that integrates both understanding and generation capabilities.
- Previous surveys on LMMs often focus on specific aspects like understanding, architecture, or applications, missing a comprehensive review from the unifying perspective of Next Token Prediction (NTP).
- There is a need to consolidate current knowledge, trends, and challenges within multimodal learning to effectively guide researchers toward developing more general and human-like AI systems.

## Method

- The survey proposes a comprehensive taxonomy for ==Multimodal Next Token Prediction (MMNTP)==, structuring the field into five core components: multimodal tokenization, MMNTP model architectures, unified task representation, datasets & evaluation, and open challenges.
- It systematically reviews and categorizes existing research on techniques for transforming raw multimodal data into tokens, various model architectures (==compositional vs. unified==), training objectives (discrete vs. continuous token prediction), and strategies for pretraining and finetuning.
- The work details current datasets and evaluation benchmarks while also outlining critical unsolved problems and providing an associated GitHub repository with relevant resources for the research community.

## Results

- The survey establishes a comprehensive taxonomy that categorizes advancements in multimodal tokenization, MMNTP model architectures (compositional vs. unified), training objectives, pretraining/finetuning strategies, and both holistic and emerging evaluation benchmarks.
- It highlights the historical development and current state of NTP-based LMMs, demonstrating their increasing capabilities in unifying understanding and generation tasks across modalities.
- The research articulates four critical open challenges for future work: scaling MMNTP with unlabeled data, overcoming modality interference, increasing efficiency in training and inference, and expanding MMNTP as universal interfaces for new tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Next Token Prediction (NTP) has emerged as a versatile and universal training objective, effectively unifying both understanding and generation tasks across diverse modalities (text, image, audio, video).
> - The field of Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) is experiencing rapid evolution, moving towards increasingly sophisticated architectures that integrate multiple modalities and tasks.
> - Effective multimodal tokenization (both discrete and continuous), strategic multi-stage training (pretraining for alignment, finetuning for instruction/preference), and comprehensive evaluation benchmarks are foundational for advancing MMNTP.
