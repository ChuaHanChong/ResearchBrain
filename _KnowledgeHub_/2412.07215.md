# RoboTron-Mani: All-in-One Multimodal Large Model for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Meituan developed RoboTron-Mani, a multimodal large model that integrates 3D spatial perception and flexible outputs for robotic manipulation, along with RoboData, a unified and aligned dataset. The system achieves strong performance across diverse robotic manipulation tasks in both simulation and real-world environments, often matching or exceeding specialized expert models.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal models primarily focus on 2D image understanding, lacking robust 3D spatial perception capabilities essential for robots interacting in physical environments.
- The high cost and inefficiency of creating large-scale, consistent robotic datasets hinder the development of generalist policies, as existing public datasets often lack 3D information and have misaligned data spaces.
- Current approaches struggle to effectively integrate and leverage heterogeneous multi-platform, multi-robot datasets due to inconsistencies in world coordinate systems, action representations, and missing critical 3D data.

## Method

- Introduced **RoboTron-Mani**, a multimodal large model with a 3D Perception Adapter (UVFormer) for unified 3D view representation and occupancy supervision to enhance spatial understanding.
- Designed a Feature Fusion Decoder with a Modality-Isolation-Mask (MIM) and multimodal decoders, enabling flexible fusion of visual and textual inputs and generation of actions, images, and 3D occupancy maps.
- Curated **RoboData**, a comprehensive dataset by unifying nine public datasets, meticulously aligning 3D space, standardizing action representations, and imputing missing camera parameters and depth maps.

## Results

- RoboTron-Mani demonstrated competitive or superior success rates on various benchmarks, achieving 91.7% SR on LIBERO (vs. QueST 89.8%), 93.8% SR on CALVIN (vs. MDT 93.7%), and 60% SR on RT-1, comparable to the significantly larger RT-2-X model.
- The model outperformed the general model RoboFlamingo* by 19.6% SR on LIBERO and 14.8% SR on Meta-World across four simulation datasets, indicating enhanced generalization.
- Real-world experiments on a UR3 arm showed superior performance across varying task difficulties, achieving 82.5% SR for easy, 70.0% for medium, and 46.6% for hard tasks, outperforming existing baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating advanced 3D perception, specifically through a unified 3D view representation and occupancy supervision, significantly enhances a multimodal model's ability to perform robotic manipulation.
> - Meticulous alignment of heterogeneous robotic datasets, including 3D spatial coordinates and action representations, is crucial for successful joint training and achieving strong generalization across diverse tasks and platforms.
> - Employing multimodal outputs (actions, next-frame images, occupancy maps) with flexible supervision mechanisms (Modality-Isolation-Mask) improves overall action performance by providing richer contextual information during training.
