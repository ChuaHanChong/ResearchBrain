# AlphaSAGE: Structure-Aware Alpha Mining via GFlowNets for Robust Exploration

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Peking University developed AlphaSAGE, a framework leveraging GFlowNets and Relational Graph Convolutional Networks (RGCNs) with a multi-faceted reward to discover diverse and robust alpha factors. The framework achieved superior predictive power and risk-adjusted returns across Chinese and U.S. stock markets compared to existing automated alpha mining methods.

## Problem

- Existing automated alpha mining methods suffer from reward sparsity, where meaningful feedback is only available upon the completion of a full alpha formula, leading to inefficient and unstable exploration.
- Most approaches represent alpha expressions as linear sequences, failing to capture the inherent logical and hierarchical structure of mathematical expressions (Abstract Syntax Trees), which limits semantic understanding.
- Standard Reinforcement Learning objectives often converge to a narrow set of optimal solutions, lacking the diversity required for a robust portfolio of non-correlated alphas in quantitative finance.

## Method

- **AlphaSAGE** utilizes ==Generative Flow Networks== (GFlowNets) to learn a generative policy that samples a diverse distribution of high-quality alpha formulas, modeled as Abstract Syntax Trees (ASTs).
- It employs a Relational Graph Convolutional Network (RGCN) as a structure-aware encoder to process ASTs, explicitly capturing the hierarchical and heterogeneous relationships within alpha expressions.
- A multi-faceted reward function is designed, combining terminal predictive performance (Information Coefficient), structural alignment (Structure-Aware reward), and novelty, further augmented by an entropy regularizer to guide diverse exploration.

## Results

- AlphaSAGE consistently achieved higher Information Coefficient (IC), IC Information Ratio (ICIR), Annualized Return (AR), and Sharpe Ratio (SR) across CSI300, CSI500, CSI1000 (China) and S&P500 (U.S.) markets compared to all baselines.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the GNN encoder provided the largest single improvement, followed by the structure-aware reward, novelty reward, and entropy regularizer, highlighting the contribution of each component.
- Sensitivity analyses indicated robustness to hyperparameter choices and demonstrated that an optimal maximum AST length (around **20**) balances expressiveness and noise, preventing performance degradation from overly complex formulas.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Representing alpha formulas as ASTs and encoding them with RGCNs significantly improves the semantic understanding and generation quality compared to traditional sequential encoders.
> - GFlowNets are effective in learning a generative policy for complex structured objects like alpha formulas, enabling the discovery of a diverse set of high-reward solutions rather than converging to a single mode.
> - A dense, multi-faceted reward system that combines performance, structural consistency, and novelty, along with an entropy bonus, is crucial for efficient learning and robust exploration in sparse-reward environments.
