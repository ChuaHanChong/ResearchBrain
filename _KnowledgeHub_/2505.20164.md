# Thinking with Visual Abstract: Enhancing Multimodal Reasoning via Visual Abstraction

> [!summary] Summary
> The research introduces Visual Abstract Thinking (VAT), a novel paradigm that transforms complex visual inputs into simplified visual abstracts (e.g., sketches) to enhance Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) reasoning. This approach achieves an average performance gain of 16.72% over the GPT-4o baseline and significantly reduces computational costs, requiring only 27.53% of the runtime and 10.00% of the tokens compared to tool-augmented methods.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle to effectively reason with the inherent richness and often redundant nature of visual inputs.
- Existing reasoning paradigms, such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT) and tool-augmented methods, add more information, increasing computational complexity, potential for hallucination, and overall costs.
- There is a lack of cognitively inspired paradigms that actively reduce redundant visual information, unlike how humans use abstract thinking to simplify complex inputs.

## Method

- The ==Visual Abstract Thinking (VAT) paradigm== converts raw images into simplified ==visual abstracts== (e.g., sketches, edge maps) using various methods, including pixel-based and deep learning-based techniques.
- MLLMs are prompted with both the original image and its visual abstract, encouraging a more concentrated visual reasoning mechanism by directing models to focus on essential visual elements.
- The methodology integrates the ==visual abstract== into a simple prompt template alongside the original query, explicitly instructing the MLLM to leverage both inputs for optimal performance.

## Results

- VAT achieved an average performance gain of **16.72%** over the GPT-4o baseline, outperforming Chain-of-Thought (CoT) by **3.56 percentage points** on diverse multimodal reasoning benchmarks.
- The approach significantly improved efficiency, requiring only **27.53%** of the runtime and **10.00%** of the token cost compared to tool-augmented methods like Visual Sketchpad.
- OpenSketch style visual abstracts consistently yielded the highest overall improvements, effectively preserving essential geometric and semantic information while filtering noise for object-centric perception and relation reasoning tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Abstract visual cues, derived from complex images, effectively enhance MLLM visual reasoning capabilities by filtering redundant information and highlighting salient features.
> - VAT offers substantial efficiency gains over existing methods, significantly reducing both runtime and token consumption by utilizing low-cost input tokens rather than expensive generated outputs.
> - The paradigm demonstrates generality across various MLLM architectures and scales, with larger models like GPT-4o showing more pronounced performance improvements, indicating an emergent capacity for abstract reasoning.
