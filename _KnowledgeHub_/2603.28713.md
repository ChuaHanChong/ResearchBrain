# DreamLite: A Lightweight On-Device Unified Model for Image Generation and Editing

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at ByteDance developed DreamLite, a 0.39B parameter diffusion model that unifies text-to-image generation and text-guided image editing on mobile devices. The model achieves 1024x1024 image processing in under one second on a flagship smartphone, demonstrating competitive performance against larger server-side models while operating entirely on-device.

## Problem

- Large-scale diffusion models are too computationally intensive and memory-heavy for practical deployment on consumer-grade edge devices.
- Existing on-device generative models primarily specialize in text-to-image generation, requiring separate models or additional components for instruction-based image editing.
- Achieving real-time, high-resolution (e.g., 1024x1024) image generation and editing performance on mobile hardware presents significant challenges due to latency and resource constraints.

## Method

- A compact architecture featuring a systematically compressed **0.39B** parameter UNet, a lightweight **TinyVAE**, and a **Qwen3-VL-2B** text encoder is developed.
- An ==in-context conditioning== mechanism spatially concatenates target and conditioning image latents (blank for generation, source for editing) with explicit task tokens, enabling a single network for both tasks.
- A ==task-progressive joint pretraining== strategy (T2I > Edit with ==foreground-emphasis mask== > Unified) is combined with post-training (SFT and ==Reward Feedback Learning==) and ==Distribution Matching Distillation== for 4-step inference.

## Results

- **DreamLite** achieved an overall score of **0.72** on the GenEval benchmark for image generation and **4.11** on the ImgEdit benchmark for image editing, demonstrating competitive performance with, and often outperforming, existing lightweight and some larger unified models.
- The model completed 1024x1024 image generation or editing tasks in less than **1 second** on a Snapdragon 8 Gen3 smartphone, with the UNet backbone contributing approximately **0.42 seconds** for 4 denoising steps.
- The UNet backbone occupied **389.00 MB**, enabling efficient on-device deployment and providing a unified solution for both creative functionalities on mobile devices.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The in-context conditioning paradigm, guided by task tokens, successfully unifies distinct generative (T2I) and editing tasks within a single compact diffusion model.
> - A task-progressive pretraining scheme, particularly with foreground-emphasis masking during editing pretraining, is crucial for stable convergence and optimal performance in compact, multi-task diffusion models.
> - Combining a compact architecture with supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and step distillation enables real-time on-device performance while maintaining high visual quality and instruction following.
