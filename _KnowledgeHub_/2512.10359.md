# Tool-Augmented Spatiotemporal Reasoning for Streamlining Video Question Answering Task

> [!summary] Summary
> Tsinghua University researchers developed the "STAR" framework, which augments Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) with a Video Toolkit and a spatiotemporal reasoning strategy to enhance video question answering. This approach yielded an 8.2% accuracy increase over GPT-4o on the VideoMME dataset and achieved substantial efficiency gains by reducing runtime and processed frames across various benchmarks.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle with simultaneously discerning spatial relationships within frames and understanding complex temporal dynamics across video frames.
- Existing tool-augmented LLMs for VideoQA often use unidimensional tools, lack balanced tool management, and have insufficient scheduling strategies, leading to inefficiencies and reasoning shortcuts.
- The inability to progressively localize a 3D Region of Interest (RoI) in video content hinders deep understanding and robust, step-by-step reasoning.

## Method

- A comprehensive and extensible ==Video Toolkit== was constructed, comprising approximately **22** plug-and-play tools categorized into temporal, spatial, and general-purpose functionalities, using lightweight specialized CV models and natural language interfaces.
- The ==Spatiotemporal Reasoning Framework (STAR)== was proposed, an agentic and iterative framework built on ReAct principles, which strategically controls tool invocation using GPT-4o as an LLM Planner.
- STAR employs an ==alternating spatiotemporal tool invocation pattern== to progressively narrow down the 3D Region of Interest in the video, guiding the MLLM through deliberate, step-by-step visual reasoning to overcome 'toolchain shortcuts'.

## Results

- STAR achieved a **70.0%** accuracy on VideoMME, an **8.2%** gain over GPT-4o (**61.8%**), with comparable input frames and reducing runtime from several minutes to **15.8** seconds per video.
- On challenging LongVideoBench, STAR improved accuracy by **4.6%** over GPT-4o (**57.2%** vs. **52.6%**) while significantly reducing the number of processed frames and overall runtime for long-form videos.
- Ablation studies confirmed the spatiotemporal-interleaved strategy outperformed 'No Constraints' (**61.2%** accuracy) and 'Spatiotemporal Disentanglement' (**68.6%** accuracy), achieving the highest accuracy (**70.0%**) and frame efficiency (**30.2** frames processed) on VideoMME.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Interleaving spatial and temporal tool invocation is crucial for effective video understanding, facilitating progressive 3D Region of Interest (RoI) localization and preventing reasoning shortcuts by forcing comprehensive analysis.
> - Augmenting MLLMs with specialized perception tools leads to more accurate and robust outputs for specific tasks (e.g., object detection, action localization) compared to relying solely on direct MLLM inference.
> - A training-free, agentic framework that strategically manages a diverse toolkit can significantly boost MLLM performance and efficiency on complex video understanding tasks without requiring core model retraining.
