# Reinforcement Learning Meets Masked Generative Models: Mask-GRPO for Text-to-Image Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Mask-GRPO integrates Group Relative Policy Optimization into masked generative models for text-to-image generation, introducing specific transition probability definitions and training enhancements to achieve a GenEval score of 0.73 with a 1.3B parameter model and an FID of 8.32 on MSCOCO-30K.

## Problem

- Reinforcement learning (RL) had been applied to diffusion and autoregressive text-to-image models, but masked generative models (MGMs) remained largely unexplored in this context.
- Adapting RL to MGMs was challenged by the lack of a suitable definition for transition probability within their parallel unmasking mechanism.
- RL training for large generative models suffered from instability, high computational costs, and issues like "vanishing samples" (low reward variance).

## Method

- The iterative ==unmasking== process of MGMs was reframed as a multi-step Markov Decision Process to define states, actions, and rewards for RL.
- New definitions for transition probability (p_θ1 and p_θ2) were proposed, specifically accounting for newly unmasked and remasked tokens in MGMs.
- Training stability and efficiency were enhanced through strategies including removing the Kullback-Leibler (KL) constraint, applying computational and unmasking reduction, and implementing dynamic sample filtering.

## Results

- Mask-GRPO improved the base Show-o model's GenEval score from **0.53** to **0.73**, surpassing larger state-of-the-art models in zero-shot settings.
- The Frechet Inception Distance (FID) on the MSCOCO-**30K** dataset improved from 9.24 to 8.32, indicating enhanced image quality and diversity.
- Ablation studies confirmed that removing the KL constraint and implementing sample filtering were key drivers for performance gains, with sample filtering improving GenEval from **0.60** to **0.73**.
- The framework demonstrated robustness across different reward models, successfully improving scores when fine-tuned with either CLIP or ImageReward.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A principled definition of transition probability, rather than naive adaptations, is essential for successfully integrating GRPO-based RL into masked generative models.
> - Removing the KL divergence constraint in GRPO can significantly improve performance for smaller generative models by allowing broader policy exploration.
> - Dynamic sample filtering, which resamples groups with low reward variance, is crucial for mitigating "vanishing samples" and ensuring stable, effective RL training.
