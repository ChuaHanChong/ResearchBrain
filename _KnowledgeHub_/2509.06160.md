# Reverse-Engineered Reasoning for Open-Ended Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> REverse-Engineered Reasoning (REER) is introduced as a paradigm for instilling deep reasoning in Large Language Models for open-ended, non-verifiable creative tasks. An 8-billion parameter model, DeepWriter-8B, trained using REER, achieved performance comparable to or exceeding GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 on various creative and professional writing benchmarks.

## Problem

- Existing deep reasoning methods for LLMs, such as Reinforcement Learning (RL) and instruction distillation, struggle with open-ended, non-verifiable creative generation tasks due to the lack of clear reward signals or objective ground truth.
- RL is sample-inefficient and computationally intensive for subjective domains, while instruction distillation is prohibitively expensive for large-scale application and limited by the teacher model's inherent capabilities.
- There is a significant scarcity of high-quality, deep reasoning trajectories specifically tailored for complex open-ended generation tasks in public datasets.

## Method

- The REverse-Engineered Reasoning (**REER**) paradigm frames reasoning as a search for the ==latent thinking== process that best explains a known high-quality human-written output for a given query.
- A gradient-free local search algorithm iteratively refines an initial reasoning trajectory by evaluating candidate segments based on the perplexity of the reference solution conditioned on the evolving trajectory.
- The DeepWriting-20K dataset, comprising 20,000 high-quality deep reasoning trajectories, was synthesized using REER with context engineering and the injection of human-like thinking patterns, then used to fine-tune an open-source LLM.

## Results

- DeepWriter-**8B** (**8B** parameters) consistently outperformed open-source baselines (e.g., LongWriter-**8B**) across multiple benchmarks, achieving an average uplift of over 18 points on WritingBench.
- DeepWriter-**8B**'s performance on creative tasks (HelloBench HB-B) was statistically on par with GPT-4o and Claude 3.5, and it surpassed Claude 3.5 while remaining competitive with GPT-4o on professional writing tasks.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the REER-synthesized data and the iterative refinement process were the most critical components for performance, and training on creative tasks imparted generalizable abilities to handle nuance across domains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Working 'backwards' from high-quality solutions by reverse-engineering their underlying reasoning processes offers a scalable and effective strategy for instilling deep reasoning in LLMs for subjective, non-verifiable domains.
> - Perplexity of a reference output conditioned on a reasoning trajectory serves as a robust and automatable proxy for evaluating and iteratively refining the quality of synthesized reasoning in open-ended contexts.
> - The explicit injection of human-like reflective and self-correcting thinking patterns during reasoning synthesis leads to more flexible, diverse, and creative reasoning capabilities in LLMs, improving their overall generative performance.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Large Language Models (LLMs) has seen remarkable progress through the "deep reasoning" paradigm, enabling sophisticated capabilities like multi-step planning and complex problem-solving in verifiable domains such as mathematics and programming. This success has largely been driven by methods like Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, Tree-of-Thought (ToT), and various reinforcement learning (RL) strategies, where clear reward signals for correct outcomes effectively guide the model.

However, the existing dominant methods for instilling reasoning—Reinforcement Learning (RL) and instruction distillation—encounter significant limitations when applied to open-ended, creative generation tasks, such as creative writing, which lack a singular, objective ground truth.
- **Reinforcement Learning (RL):** RL struggles with the absence of clear reward signals in subjective domains. Training a reward model to approximate human preferences for qualities like originality or emotional resonance is immensely challenging and prone to biases. Furthermore, the RL process itself is notoriously sample-inefficient and computationally burdensome, making it impractical for large-scale application in these complex, non-verifiable contexts.
- **Instruction Distillation:** This approach, where smaller models learn from powerful "teacher" models (e.g., GPT-4), is often prohibitively expensive due to the high cost of querying proprietary APIs at scale. More fundamentally, distillation is capped by the teacher model’s capabilities; a student cannot acquire capacities the teacher does not possess. This is exacerbated by the scarcity of high-quality queries and deep reasoning trajectories specifically tailored for complex open-ended generation tasks in open-source datasets.

This paper addresses a critical and largely unexplored research question: "How can we instill deep reasoning for open-ended generation in the absence of task verifiability?" It charts a new, "third path" beyond RL and distillation by introducing REverse-Engineered Reasoning (REER). While previous synthetic data generation methods typically build solutions "forwards" for a given query, REER fundamentally shifts the approach by working "backwards." Instead of trying to construct a reasoning process from scratch or imitating one, REER computationally discovers the latent, step-by-step deep reasoning process that *could have produced* known good solutions. This novel perspective aims to bypass the bottlenecks of sample inefficiency and costly model dependencies, offering a scalable and automatable pathway to cultivate sophisticated reasoning in LLMs for creative and subjective domains.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The central objective of this research is to enable Large Language Models to perform deep, structured reasoning for open-ended, creative generation tasks, where traditional methods of verifiability and clear reward signals are absent. The authors aim to overcome the critical limitations of existing paradigms—Reinforcement Learning (RL) and instruction distillation—which prove ineffective or impractical in such subjective domains.

Specifically, the key motivations and objectives are:
1. **Introduce REverse-Engineered Reasoning (REER):** To propose and operationalize a novel paradigm that works "backwards" from known high-quality outputs to discover the underlying reasoning process. This fundamentally shifts the problem from "how to generate a good output" to "what thinking process would lead to this good output."
2. **Overcome Limitations of RL and Distillation:** To provide a scalable, cost-effective, and automatable method that sidesteps the challenges of reward model training (for RL in non-verifiable tasks) and the prohibitive expense and teacher-model limitations (for instruction distillation).
3. **Synthesize High-Quality Reasoning Data at Scale:** To create a large-scale dataset of deep reasoning trajectories for open-ended generation, which is currently scarce. This data, generated via the REER paradigm, should be comprehensive, diverse, and human-like.
4. **Demonstrate Effectiveness with a Capable Model:** To fine-tune an open-source base LLM (DeepWriter-8B) on this synthetically generated data and rigorously evaluate its performance against state-of-the-art proprietary and open-source models, thereby validating REER as a powerful new pathway.
5. **Open-Source Contributions:** To release the curated DeepWriting-20K dataset to mitigate data scarcity in the field and catalyze future research into planning and structured thought for open-ended generation.

In essence, the paper seeks to democratize advanced reasoning capabilities for creative AI by providing an efficient, open-source-compatible methodology for training models to think deeply before generating.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The core of the methodology revolves around REverse-Engineered Reasoning (REER), a novel paradigm designed to synthesize deep reasoning trajectories by working "backwards" from known high-quality solutions. The approach is structured in three key stages: sourcing data, reverse-engineering reasoning trajectories, and using this data to fine-tune an LLM.

#### 3.1. REverse-Engineered Reasoning as a Search Problem

REER redefines the problem of instilling reasoning by framing it as a search for the latent thinking process (z) that best explains a high-quality, human-written output (y) given an input query (x). Since direct verification of reasoning quality is difficult in open-ended domains, REER employs a proxy: the perplexity (PPL) of the reference solution *y* conditioned on *x* and the proposed reasoning trajectory *z*. A lower perplexity score indicates that the trajectory *z* makes *y* seem more probable and logically coherent to the generator LLM, thus serving as a measure of *z*'s quality. This is formalized as `z* = arg min z∈Z PPL(y|x, z)`, where `z*` is the optimal trajectory.

#### 3.2. Iterative Refinement via Local Search

To find this optimal `z*` within the vast search space, the authors propose a gradient-free local search algorithm that iteratively refines an initial thinking process:
1. **Initialization:** An initial, imperfect deep reasoning trajectory, `z(0)`, is generated by prompting an LLM with instructions designed to elicit a plausible plan for a given `(x, y)` pair (see Appendix Listing 1).
2. **Node Expansion (Segment-wise Refinement):** The algorithm iteratively selects a segment `zi` of the current trajectory for improvement. The LLM is then prompted to generate multiple candidate refinements for `zi`, incorporating more details, elaborations, and reflections. Crucially, the prompt provides the full context, including `x`, `y`, and the surrounding refined/initial trajectory segments, and is carefully engineered to prevent simple copying of the reference solution (Appendix Listing 2).
3. **Node Evaluation and Selection:** Each candidate `c` is evaluated by constructing a temporary trajectory `z'cand` (replacing `zi` with `c`) and computing its quality score: `S(c) = PPL(y|x, z'cand)`. The candidate with the lowest perplexity is chosen as the updated segment `z*i`. The original segment `zi` is also included in the candidate set to ensure monotonic perplexity improvement.
4. **Termination:** The process repeats until the perplexity of the solution reaches a predefined threshold or a maximum number of iterations is completed, yielding a refined `z*`.

This iterative local search is distinct from methods like Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) because it uses a global perplexity score on the *complete* reference solution, avoiding expensive roll-outs. It also operates on a "global-to-local" principle, refining an entire initial plan rather than sequentially building partial solutions.

#### 3.3. Training Data Curation (DeepWriting-20K)

The success of REER hinges on a large, high-quality dataset of `(x, z*, y)` triples. The curation process involves:
1. **Sourcing (Query, Solution) Pairs:** Diverse initial `(x, y)` pairs were collected from:
 - Public Writing Platforms (e.g., r/WritingPrompts), using community upvotes as a quality indicator.
 - Public Domain Literature (Project Gutenberg), where GPT-4o was used to reverse-engineer plausible queries (`x`) from opening paragraphs of classic texts (`y`).
 - Public Datasets (e.g., WildChat, LongWriter6K).
2. **Trajectory Synthesis and Filtering:**
 - 20,000 high-quality query-solution pairs were selected across 25 categories.
 - The iterative local search algorithm generated an optimal `z*` for each pair.
 - **Context Engineering:** Two key designs were implemented in the prompts for the generator LLM:
 - **Enforcing Segment-wise Edits via a Meta-Structure:** A meta-structure within the prompt guided the model to localize edits to specific segments, preventing out-of-scope modifications.
 - **Injecting Human-like Thinking Patterns:** Prompts explicitly encouraged the use of phrases signifying cognitive exploration and self-reflection (e.g., "Hmm...maybe," "Wait, that's a bit...") to synthesize more natural, flexible reasoning.
 - **Filtering:** To prune low-quality trajectories, heuristic strategies were applied: "End-of-Thinking Filtering" (discarding samples where thinking patterns persisted in the final 10% of the sequence) and "Repetition Filtering" (removing trajectories with high n-gram repetition, indicating degenerative looping).
 - Analysis showed that this process significantly reduced perplexity and increased trajectory token length, confirming the addition of more detailed reasoning.
3. **Final Dataset Assembly for Fine-Tuning:** The 20,000 synthetically generated trajectories (DeepWriting-20K) were combined with distilled deep reasoning trajectories from public datasets (OpenThoughts) covering domains like mathematics and coding. This mixed-data strategy (37,000 examples total) prevented catastrophic forgetting of general reasoning abilities. The final triples were formatted to explicitly teach the base LLM to perform deep reasoning before generating the output (Appendix Listing 4).

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The empirical evaluation rigorously validated REER and DeepWriter-8B across diverse open-ended generation tasks, comparing its performance against strong open-source baselines and leading proprietary models.

#### 4.1. Main Performance Comparison

DeepWriter-8B, an 8-billion parameter model fine-tuned on a Qwen3-8B base, was benchmarked on LongBench-Write (LB), HelloBench (HB-A, HB-B), and WritingBench (WB-A to WB-F).
- **Outperforming Open-Source Baselines:** DeepWriter-8B consistently and substantially outperformed LongWriter-8B (Llama3.1-8b based) across all benchmarks. For instance, on WritingBench, DeepWriter-8B achieved an average uplift of over 18 points, underscoring the significant advantage of REER over standard instruction tuning for advanced generative skills.
- **Competitive with Proprietary Models:** DeepWriter-8B closed a substantial performance gap with elite proprietary models:
 - On creative tasks (HelloBench HB-B), DeepWriter-8B's score (87.48) was statistically on par with GPT-4o (87.6) and Claude 3.5 (88.3).
 - For professional writing tasks (WritingBench), DeepWriter-8B not only surpassed Claude 3.5 across all six categories but also remained highly competitive with the larger GPT-4o and Claude 3.7 models.
 - Notably, on LongBench-Write, DeepWriter-8B scored 91.28, exceeding both GPT-4o (83.1) and Claude 3.5 (89.3). This suggests that explicit training on structured thinking trajectories provides a powerful inductive bias for maintaining long-range coherence, a critical challenge in ultra-long text generation.

#### 4.2. Ablation Studies

A series of ablation studies meticulously dissected the contribution of each methodological component:
- **Importance of Synthesized Data:** Removing the 20K synthesized trajectories ("- Remove Synthesis Data") led to the most significant performance degradation across all benchmarks (e.g., HB-B: 87.48 → 73.73), confirming that the *quality and relevance* of REER-synthesized data are paramount.
- **Impact of Iterative Refinement:** Training with initial, unrefined trajectories ("- Remove Iterative Search") resulted in a clear performance drop, especially on nuanced WritingBench tasks (e.g., WB-A: 72.20 → 66.72). This validated the effectiveness of the perplexity-guided local search in discovering superior reasoning paths.
- **Effect of Reflection Tokens:** Removing explicit human-like reflection tokens (e.g., "Hmm...", "Wait, that's...") from synthesis prompts ("- Remove Reflection Tokens") caused a noticeable dip, particularly in the Literature & Arts domain (WB-D: 70.57 → 62.04). This suggests these patterns are crucial for instilling flexibility and creativity.
- **Role of Trajectory Length:** Downsampling longer traces ("- Downsample Long Traces") harmed complex, domain-specific tasks, while downsampling shorter traces ("- Downsample Short Traces") had a larger negative impact on creative tasks. This highlights the importance of detailed plans for structured writing and nimbler reasoning for creative ideation.
- **Role of Literature & Arts Data:** Removing data from "Literature & Arts" and "Ordinary Life" domains ("- Remove Literature & Arts data") degraded performance across *all* benchmarks, not just the corresponding WB-D category. This finding indicated that training on creative and narrative tasks imparts a generalizable ability to handle nuance and open-endedness, benefiting even more technical domains.

#### 4.3. Qualitative Analysis

- **Generation Quality of Deep Thinking:** A qualitative analysis, scoring models on Problem Deconstruction, Logical Consistency, Depth of Analysis, Presentation Clarity, and Factual Grounding, revealed DeepWriter-8B's strong and well-rounded reasoning profile. It significantly surpassed LongWriter-8B and was competitive with GPT-4o, notably exceeding Claude 3.5 in Depth of Analysis and Factual Grounding. This confirmed that REER genuinely enhances underlying reasoning rather than just surface fluency.
- **Qualitative Comparison of Thinking Patterns:** Analysis of generated thinking patterns showed that the model trained with explicit "thinking pattern injection" exhibited a more diverse and balanced use of reflective and self-correcting phrases (e.g., "let me think," "maybe," "hmm," "wait"), indicative of a flexible, human-like reasoning process. In contrast, models without this injection relied on a few formulaic phrases, suggesting a more rigid reasoning style.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The introduction of REverse-Engineered Reasoning (REER) marks a significant advancement in the field of large language models, particularly for open-ended, non-verifiable generation tasks. By proposing a novel "third path" that moves beyond the inherent limitations of reinforcement learning and instruction distillation, REER offers a scalable and cost-effective solution for instilling deep reasoning capabilities.

The primary significance of this work lies in several key areas:
1. **Breaking the Verifiability Barrier:** REER successfully addresses the long-standing challenge of applying deep reasoning to subjective, non-verifiable creative domains. By "reverse-engineering" reasoning from high-quality outputs using perplexity as a proxy, it circumvents the need for explicit reward signals or expensive teacher models, which have traditionally faltered in these areas.
2. **Scalable Data Generation:** The gradient-free local search algorithm within REER enables the automated synthesis of high-quality, human-like deep reasoning trajectories at an unprecedented scale. This is a crucial innovation, as data scarcity for structured thought processes in open-ended generation has been a major bottleneck for research and development.
3. **Open-Source Contribution:** The release of DeepWriting-20K, a comprehensive dataset of 20,000 deep reasoning trajectories across diverse categories, is a substantial open-source contribution. This resource will mitigate data scarcity for the wider research community, fostering further innovation in planning and structured thought for creative AI.
4. **Democratizing Advanced Capabilities:** The impressive performance of DeepWriter-8B, an 8-billion parameter model trained entirely on this synthesized data, demonstrates that sophisticated, human-like deep reasoning can be cultivated in smaller, open-source models. This achievement is critical for democratizing access to capabilities previously confined to large, proprietary systems like GPT-4o and Claude 3.7. It implies that researchers and developers with more modest computational resources can now build models capable of complex, nuanced, and creative generation.
5. **New Research Avenues:** REER opens up new avenues for exploring the mechanisms of deep thinking in LLMs. The qualitative analyses, particularly regarding the injection of human-like thinking patterns, provide valuable insights into how models internalize and express complex cognitive processes. This can inspire future work on more nuanced and controllable reasoning capabilities.

In conclusion, this research not only provides a robust and empirically validated methodology for enhancing LLM reasoning in open-ended contexts but also significantly contributes to the open-source community, pushing the boundaries of what is possible with smaller, more accessible models. The REER paradigm is poised to have a lasting impact on how we approach the development of truly intelligent and creative AI systems.
