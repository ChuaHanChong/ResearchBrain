# Continual Policy Distillation of Reinforcement Learning-based Controllers for Soft Robotic In-Hand Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> This research from the University of Pisa and Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies introduces a Continual Policy Distillation framework that enables a single soft robotic controller to learn dexterous in-hand manipulation of various objects sequentially. The framework effectively consolidates knowledge from multiple object-specific expert policies, mitigating catastrophic forgetting and achieving high manipulation performance on diverse objects, with replay-based strategies like Reward Prioritized Experience Replay showing performance comparable to training on all data simultaneously.

## Problem

- Existing reinforcement learning (RL) controllers for dexterous manipulation are often object-specific, limiting their generalizability and adaptability to diverse objects without extensive retraining.
- Controlling soft robots for precise in-hand manipulation is complex due to their high degrees of freedom, deformability, and the challenge of maintaining stable contact and preventing slippage.
- Robotic systems face computational and memory constraints when continuously learning new tasks, making traditional batch learning impractical and leading to catastrophic forgetting of previously acquired skills.

## Method

- A Continual Policy Distillation (CPD) framework is proposed, leveraging offline demonstrations from object-specific expert RL policies.
- Knowledge from multiple expert policies is consolidated into a single student policy via supervised learning using Policy Distillation, primarily with KL Divergence loss.
- Catastrophic forgetting is mitigated through exemplar-based rehearsal strategies (e.g., ReplayBR, ReplayEX, ReplayRP, ReplayRPR) that store and replay a limited subset of past expert demonstrations.

## Results

- Object-specific expert controllers were successfully trained, with an average rotation of 242 degrees for the cube and 126 degrees for the teddy bear, showcasing diverse manipulation gaits.
- KL Divergence loss achieved the highest performance in offline policy distillation (average 142 degrees rotation), outperforming MSE (140 degrees) and NLL (105 degrees).
- Continual Policy Distillation with Reward Prioritized Experience Replay (ReplayRP) achieved performance comparable to cumulative training (e.g., 174 degrees vs. 170 degrees with 10^3 buffer size), demonstrating effective mitigation of catastrophic forgetting compared to naive incremental learning (average performance dropped to 21 degrees).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - KL Divergence proved superior among tested loss functions for policy distillation, effectively capturing the expert's policy distribution.
> - Exemplar-based rehearsal strategies, particularly Reward Prioritized Experience Replay (ReplayRP) and Ex-Model Experience Replay (ReplayEX), effectively mitigate catastrophic forgetting in continual learning for robotic control.
> - The effectiveness of continual learning strategies is significantly influenced by the size of the replay buffer, with larger buffers generally yielding better knowledge retention.
