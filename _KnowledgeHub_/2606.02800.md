# Cosmos 3: Omnimodal World Models for Physical AI

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA introduces Cosmos 3, a family of omnimodal world models that jointly process and generate language, image, video, audio, and action sequences within a unified Mixture-of-Transformers architecture for Physical AI. This framework achieves competitive to state-of-the-art performance across 48 understanding benchmarks and leads open-source models in various image, video, and robot policy generation tasks, including a 39.7% success rate on RoboLab.

## Problem

- Traditional Physical AI approaches rely on compartmentalized models for perception, simulation, and action, leading to fragmented and inefficient pipelines.
- Training Physical AI agents directly in the real world is slow, expensive, and potentially hazardous, hindering scalability.
- Scaling training data and environments for Physical AI agents remains a persistent bottleneck.

## Method

- **Cosmos 3** employs a unified ==Mixture-of-Transformers== (MoT) architecture to jointly process and generate language, image, video, audio, and action sequences.
- The model integrates modality-specific encoders and decoders with distinct Reasoner (autoregressive for understanding) and Generator (==diffusion== for generation) pathways, co-initialized from pre-trained VLMs.
- A multi-stage training curriculum with extensive curated real and synthetic data is used, alongside a comprehensive infrastructure stack for data, training, and serving.

## Results

- Cosmos 3 Reasoner models demonstrated strong performance across 48 benchmarks, outperforming several open-source and closed-source models in domain-specific Physical AI tasks (robotics, autonomous driving, smart infrastructure).
- Cosmos3-Super achieved state-of-the-art results among open-source models in video generation (Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, physics adherence, human motion) and ranked highly in image generation leaderboards.
- Cosmos3-Nano-Policy-DROID achieved state-of-the-art robot policy results, with a **39.7%** success rate on RoboLab and ranking #1 on the RoboArena real-world benchmark.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Unifying multimodal understanding and generation within a single, scalable framework is crucial for developing general-purpose Physical AI agents.
> - A Mixture-of-Transformers architecture with specialized Reasoner and Generator pathways can effectively integrate diverse modalities (vision, language, audio, action) while maintaining coherence.
> - High-fidelity synthetic data generation and scalable training environments are essential for overcoming real-world data limitations and fostering robust Physical AI development.
