# Scaling Offline Model-Based RL via Jointly-Optimized World-Action Model Pretraining

> [!summary] Summary
> JOWA, an offline model-based reinforcement learning agent, scales to diverse visual multi-task environments by jointly optimizing a world model and a Q-value critic via a shared transformer backbone. It achieved an IQM human-normalized score of 78.9% on 15 Atari games and demonstrated sample-efficient transfer to new tasks with minimal data.

## Problem

- Scaling trends observed in large-scale models for NLP and multi-modality have not consistently transferred to reinforcement learning, particularly for TD-based offline RL.
- Existing multi-task offline RL methods often depend on extensive expert trajectories and struggle to generalize effectively to diverse, unseen tasks.
- Scaling temporal difference (TD) learning to larger models can lead to instability or performance degradation, hindering the development of generalist RL agents.

## Method

- Introduces **JOWA** (Jointly-Optimized World-Action model), an offline model-based RL agent utilizing a ==shared transformer backbone== for both ==world dynamics modeling== and ==Q-value estimation==.
- Jointly optimizes the world-part (next observation, reward, termination prediction) and action-part (==Q-value estimation== via ==distributional CQL==) modules using a combined loss, leveraging ==world modeling== as a regularizer for ==TD-learning== stability.
- Incorporates an efficient, ==parallelizable planning algorithm== at inference time to mitigate Q-value estimation errors and identify more optimal policies.

## Results

- JOWA-150M achieved an Inter-Quartile Mean (IQM) human-normalized score of **78.9%** on 15 Atari games, outperforming leading offline RL baselines by an average of **71.4%**.
- Performance consistently scaled with increased model capacity, with JOWA demonstrating the steepest scaling curve among evaluated algorithms from **40M** to **150M** parameters.
- Achieved an IQM DQN-normalized score of **64.7%** on 5 novel Atari games with only **5k** fine-tuning transitions, significantly outperforming baselines and models trained from scratch by **69.9%** on average.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Joint optimization of a world model and Q-value critic with a shared transformer backbone effectively scales TD-based offline RL, with the world modeling loss stabilizing training for larger models.
> - A provably efficient, parallelizable planning algorithm applied during inference can compensate for Q-value estimation errors, leading to improved policy quality and decision-making.
> - Multi-task pre-training in a low-data regime, combined with few-shot fine-tuning, enables strong generalization and sample-efficient adaptation to novel, unseen tasks.
