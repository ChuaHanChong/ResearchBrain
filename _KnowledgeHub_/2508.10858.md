# Hierarchical Fine-grained Preference Optimization for Physically Plausible Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework called PhysHPO enables video generation models to produce content that strictly adheres to physical laws by employing an automated data selection pipeline and a hierarchical, cross-modal preference optimization strategy. This approach consistently outperforms baseline methods on physics-focused benchmarks, improving physical law consistency on VideoPhy and increasing PCA scores on PhyGenBench, while also demonstrating data efficiency and maintained general generation quality.

## Problem

- Current video generation models often lack physical plausibility, producing visually compelling but physically inconsistent content.
- Existing methods for physics-aware video generation are either computationally expensive, require laborious manual dataset construction, or offer only coarse-grained optimization.
- There is an inefficiency in leveraging the inherent physical richness of existing large-scale real-world video datasets for training.

## Method

- An automated data selection pipeline is introduced to curate physically plausible and diverse video samples from large datasets using VLM-based reality checks and LLM-as-a-Judge with evolving prompts for physical fidelity.
- **PhysHPO** extends Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to a hierarchical framework, optimizing preferences at instance, state, motion, and semantic levels.
- The framework generates distinct types of non-preferred samples to explicitly model various generation failure modes at different hierarchical levels.

## Results

- PhysHPO consistently improved physical law consistency, achieving substantial gains on VideoPhy benchmarks (e.g., 45.9% overall physical law consistency with CogVideoX-5B) and higher PCA scores on PhyGenBench (0.61 overall for CogVideoX-5B).
- General video generation quality, as measured by VBench, was maintained or slightly improved (e.g., overall score for CogVideoX-5B from 82.4 to 82.8), indicating no compromise in visual or semantic fidelity.
- The automated data selection process and each hierarchical level of preference optimization were shown to be effective, outperforming traditional supervised fine-tuning in data efficiency and demonstrating enhanced creative generalization on "impossible prompts."

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Achieving physical plausibility in video generation requires fine-grained, hierarchical preference optimization rather than only coarse-grained approaches.
> - Automated selection of physically rich and diverse data from existing real-world datasets offers a more efficient and effective training strategy than extensive new data collection.
> - Explicitly optimizing for physical consistency at multiple granularities improves a model's capacity for generalization, even enabling more coherent generation for semantically "impossible" scenarios.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of video generation has witnessed rapid advancements, enabling the creation of increasingly high-quality and visually compelling videos. However, a significant frontier and persistent challenge remain: generating videos that accurately adhere to the laws of physics, often referred to as "physical plausibility." This capability is crucial for applications demanding high fidelity, such as virtual reality, scientific simulations, and advanced content creation.

Current efforts to achieve physics-aware video generation can be broadly categorized:
- **Test-time reflection-based optimization:** Methods like PhyT2V employ large language models (LLMs) to iteratively refine initial text-to-video (T2V) prompts. While capable, these approaches typically incur high computational costs during inference and are inherently constrained by the LLM's self-correction limits.
- **Training-time tuning-based optimization:** Approaches such as WISA and SynVideo utilize traditional supervised fine-tuning (SFT) paradigms. While effective for learning specific behaviors, SFT heavily relies on meticulously constructed, often resource-intensive, datasets and fixed supervisory signals, which can be suboptimal for targeting nuanced capabilities like physical plausibility. The "One-Model-One-Dataset" paradigm can be particularly inefficient for physics, as real-world videos inherently encapsulate rich physical dynamics.

More recently, **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)** has emerged as a powerful post-training technique. DPO offers an efficient pairwise optimization framework to discern between human-preferred and non-preferred samples, proving effective in enhancing specific aspects of visual generation models (e.g., safety, customization, super-resolution). While DPO has begun to be applied to video generation (e.g., VideoDPO, HuViDPO), existing works predominantly focus on *coarse-grained alignment* at the overall video instance level. The authors identify a critical gap here: achieving true physical plausibility necessitates *fine-grained preference alignment* that goes beyond general visual appeal to incorporate detailed modeling of physical phenomena.

Furthermore, the existing landscape often overlooks efficient data utilization for physics-aware video generation. Rather than endlessly constructing new, specialized datasets (a costly and time-consuming endeavor), the authors argue that leveraging the inherent physical richness of *real-world videos* from existing large-scale text-video datasets could be far more efficient. The concept of automated data selection, while explored in Large Language Models (LLMs), is largely underexplored in the domain of video generation.

PhysHPO directly addresses these gaps by pioneering the integration of *fine-grained preference alignment* within a DPO framework and introducing the novel concept of *automated data selection* for video generation, specifically tailored for physical plausibility. It aims to overcome the limitations of prior methods by providing a more efficient, targeted, and nuanced approach to creating physically accurate and human-preferred videos.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The central objective of this research is to significantly advance the capabilities of video generation models to produce videos that not only are high-quality and visually compelling but also **strictly adhere to the laws of physics**.

The primary motivations driving this work are multi-faceted:

- **Critical Need for Realism:** Generating physically plausible videos is paramount for a wide array of high-stakes applications. Industries such as virtual reality, augmented reality, scientific simulations, robotics, and advanced visual effects demand content that accurately reflects real-world physics. Errors in physical behavior can severely undermine the realism, utility, and credibility of generated content in these domains.
- **Limitations of Current Methods:** As discussed in the research landscape, existing approaches face significant hurdles:
 - Test-time prompt refinement methods are computationally expensive and have inherent limits in correcting deep physical inaccuracies.
 - Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) relies on costly and laborious manual dataset construction, and its fixed supervisory signals are often insufficient for the subtle and dynamic nature of physical laws.
 - Prior Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) applications in video generation have primarily focused on coarse-grained, instance-level alignment, which is insufficient for capturing the intricate details of physical phenomena.
- **Inefficient Data Utilization:** The prevalent "One-Model-One-Dataset" paradigm for specialized video generation tasks often leads to resource-intensive data collection. The authors argue that real-world videos already contain a wealth of physical dynamics, making the construction of entirely new datasets for physical plausibility inefficient and unnecessary. There is a need for a mechanism to intelligently select and leverage this existing "good data."
- **Enhancing Human Preference Alignment:** Beyond mere visual quality, humans intrinsically prefer videos that behave logically and consistently with physical reality. By explicitly optimizing for physical plausibility at a fine-grained level, the goal is to generate videos that are more human-preferred and trustworthy.
- **Bridging the Gap in DPO:** The paper is motivated by the observation that DPO's potential for fine-grained control has been underexplored in video generation. Extending DPO to address hierarchical levels of video content – from overall scenes to specific motions and semantic coherence – is seen as a powerful way to instill physical understanding.

In essence, the paper aims to develop a novel, efficient, and highly effective framework that enables video generation models to learn and apply physical laws, thereby producing more realistic, coherent, and useful content for demanding applications, while optimizing data utilization.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The PhysHPO framework proposes a novel two-pronged approach to address the challenge of physically plausible video generation: an automated data selection pipeline and a Hierarchical Cross-Modal Direct Preference Optimization strategy.

#### 3.1. Automated Data Selection Pipeline

Recognizing the inefficiency of constructing new datasets and the inherent physical richness of real-world videos, PhysHPO introduces an automated pipeline to select "good data" from existing large-scale text-video datasets (e.g., OpenVidHD-0.4M). This pipeline involves three sequential steps:

1. **Reality Selection:**
 - The initial pool of data is filtered to retain only real-world videos, discarding virtual or synthetic content.
 - This is achieved by employing state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models (VLMs), specifically Qwen2.5-VL and DeepSeek-VL2, to classify whether a video is real-world. A double-check mechanism ensures high accuracy in this critical initial filtering step.

2. **Physical Fidelity Selection:**
 - The goal is to identify videos that prominently reflect physical laws. The paper focuses on 17 physical phenomena across three categories: Dynamic (rigid body, collision, liquid/gas/elastic motion, deformation), Thermodynamic (melting, solidification, vaporization, liquefaction, combustion, explosion), and Optic (reflection, refraction, scattering, interference/diffraction, unnatural light source).
 - Instead of traditional classification-based methods, the authors adopt an "LLM-as-a-Judge" paradigm using Qwen2.5. To overcome the LLM's tendency to assign similar scores without reference, an *in-depth evolving prompting* strategy is introduced. This involves augmenting video captions to emphasize specific physical phenomena within each category. The LLM then ranks and scores these augmented samples, yielding fine-grained scores (s_d, s_t, s_o) for Dynamic, Thermodynamic, and Optic categories. A total score `s = s_d × s_t × s_o` is calculated, and videos are sorted based on this score. This method offers a more nuanced distinction of physical phenomena compared to direct scoring.

3. **Diversity Selection:**
 - To ensure the final dataset promotes a robust generation model capable of handling varied user prompts, redundancy is minimized.
 - An iterative method, similar to Deita, is employed to select samples. Captions are encoded into embeddings using LLaMA-1 13B. A sample is added to the selected dataset only if its cosine distance `d` to its nearest neighbor in the already selected set is below a threshold `τ` (set to 0.9), thus contributing to diversity.
 - This step ensures that the selected "good data" is not only physically rich but also diverse, maximizing its utility for model training.

#### 3.2. Hierarchical Cross-Modal Direct Preference Optimization (PhysHPO)

PhysHPO extends the DPO framework by implementing preference optimization across four hierarchical granularities, integrating cross-modal information (visuals, structural features, text) at each level:

1. **Instance-level Overall Preference Optimization (L_Instance):**
 - This level ensures comprehensive alignment between the overall video content and the input prompt.
 - Unlike prior DPO methods that might rely solely on the base model for preference pairs, PhysHPO leverages the *physically-rich, diverse data selected by the pipeline* as the preferred video (y_w) and its text prompt (x).
 - For the non-preferred video (y_l), PhysHPO introduces two distinct types to model different failure modes:
 - `y_err_l`: An error-prone video, semantically aligned with `y_w` but containing physical imperfections, generated by the base model and selected for visual similarity to `y_w`.
 - `y_gap_l`: A semantically divergent video, generated by the base model with random prompt masking, to create semantic differences from `y_w`.
 - The loss function `L_Instance` incorporates a weighted sum of the DPO scores for these two non-preferred types (`β_err` for `y_err_l` and `β_gap` for `y_gap_l`).

2. **State-level Boundary Preference Optimization (L_State):**
 - This level focuses on ensuring realism and coherence by maintaining consistent physical states at the beginning and end of a video.
 - The non-preferred sample `y_state_l` is constructed by replacing the first and last `N` frames of the preferred video `y_w`. This specifically targets the model's ability to anchor plausible physical states.

3. **Motion-level Dynamic Preference Optimization (L_Motion):**
 - To improve the model's understanding of dynamic information and motion, this level extracts *structural information* (e.g., optical flow) from both `y_w` and `y_l` (specifically `y_err_l`).
 - These structural features (`y_motion_w`, `y_motion_l`) provide a more explicit representation of motion differences, allowing the model to align with physical dynamics beyond mere pixel appearance.

4. **Semantic-level Consistency Preference Optimization (L_Semantic):**
 - Moving beyond visual alignment, this level leverages language to describe and optimize for precise semantic differences, inspired by DPO in LLMs.
 - For a `(y_w, y_l)` pair, the prompt `x` is treated as the preferred caption `c_w`. A VLM (Qwen2.5-VL) is then tasked to modify `c_w` based on `y_l`, creating `c_l` (a non-preferred caption) that reflects inconsistencies between `y_l` and `y_w`.
 - This ensures that `c_w` and `c_l` differ only where the videos are semantically inconsistent, facilitating targeted optimization for narrative-visual coherence.

The overall loss function for PhysHPO is a weighted sum of these four levels: `L_PhysHPO = L_Instance + λL_State + ρL_Motion + μL_Semantic`, where `λ`, `ρ`, and `μ` are learnable loss weights. The base models for fine-tuning are advanced T2V models like CogVideoX-2B and 5B, and HunyuanVideo. Experiments are conducted on 8 NVIDIA H100 GPUs.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The extensive experiments conducted by the authors provide compelling evidence for the effectiveness of PhysHPO across both physics-focused and general video generation benchmarks.

#### 4.1. Impact of Automated Data Selection

- The automated data selection pipeline, focusing on reality, physical fidelity (using in-depth evolving prompting), and diversity, proves highly effective. A relatively smaller, curated dataset (21K samples) selected by PhysHPO significantly outperforms much larger raw datasets (433K) and even manually constructed datasets (WISA-32K) when used with DPO.
- The *diversity* aspect of data selection is crucial: the diverse 21K dataset outperforms a larger, less diverse 59K dataset, highlighting that quality and diversity are more important than sheer quantity for targeted optimization.
- This demonstrates that efficient data utilization, rather than extensive new data collection, is a viable and superior strategy for physically plausible video generation.

#### 4.2. Enhanced Physical Plausibility (RQ1)

- PhysHPO consistently and significantly outperforms all baseline methods, including PhyT2V and vanilla DPO, across various physics-focused benchmarks.
- On **VideoPhy**, PhysHPO achieves substantial improvements in overall physical law consistency (e.g., from 41.3% for vanilla DPO to 45.9% for PhysHPO with CogVideoX-5B), especially in complex interactions like Fluid-Fluid (e.g., 51.8% to 58.9%).
- On **PhyGenBench**, PhysHPO demonstrates superior performance across all physical categories (Mechanics, Optics, Thermal, Materials), with an overall PCA score improvement (e.g., from 0.54 to 0.61 for CogVideoX-5B), indicating better physical commonsense alignment.
- Qualitative results (Figure 1, 4) visually confirm PhysHPO's ability to generate videos that more accurately depict physical phenomena compared to base models and other baselines.

#### 4.3. Maintained General Generation Quality (RQ2)

- Despite focusing on physical plausibility, PhysHPO does not compromise general video generation quality.
- On the **VBench** benchmark, PhysHPO shows slight improvements or maintains comparable performance in overall quality, visual quality, and semantic adherence metrics (e.g., overall score for CogVideoX-5B increased from 82.4 to 82.8). This indicates that enhancing physical fidelity also contributes positively to or at least preserves general visual and semantic quality.

#### 4.4. Effectiveness of Hierarchical Preference Optimization (RQ3)

- Ablation studies on VideoPhy confirm that each level of the hierarchical optimization framework contributes to the overall performance.
- Progressive removal of `L_Semantic`, `L_Motion`, and `L_State` losses leads to a steady decline in physical plausibility scores, underscoring the necessity of fine-grained alignment.
- The distinction between error-prone and semantically-different non-preferred samples at the instance level also proves critical, highlighting the importance of explicitly modeling different types of generation failures.

#### 4.5. Efficiency, Effectiveness, and Generalizability vs. SFT (RQ4)

- **Data Efficiency:** PhysHPO consistently outperforms traditional Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) across varying data volumes on both physics-focused and general quality benchmarks (Figure 5, Middle). This suggests that PhysHPO leverages training data more efficiently through its fine-grained preference alignment.
- **Creative Generalization:** Perhaps one of the most intriguing findings is PhysHPO's performance on "impossible prompts" (IPV-TXT). As shown in Figure 5 (Right) and Figure 6, PhysHPO generates videos that align more closely with the semantic intent of impossible scenarios (e.g., paper turning into steam) while maintaining internal consistency. This implies that PhysHPO learns a deeper, more generalized understanding of semantic and dynamic relationships, going beyond mere adherence to fixed physical laws. This ability to "creatively generalize" suggests a stronger model capacity rather than just rote learning of physics.

#### 4.6. Hyperparameter and Non-preferred Sample Analysis

- Detailed analysis reveals optimal weight settings for the hierarchical loss components (`λ=0.4`, `ρ=0.3`, `μ=0.2`).
- For instance-level negative sampling, a `β_err/β_gap` ratio of `0.7/0.3` is most effective.
- Optimal non-preferred sample generation strategies include using 3 generated samples for error videos, swapping a moderate number of boundary frames (`N=2`) for state-level, and utilizing optical flow as the structural representation for motion-level optimization.

In summary, PhysHPO presents a robust and innovative framework that significantly elevates the physical plausibility and overall quality of video generation, demonstrating both data efficiency and an impressive ability to generalize beyond strict physical adherence.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The PhysHPO framework represents a significant advancement in the field of video generation, with profound implications and potential impact across several domains:

- **Elevating Realism and Accuracy:** The most direct impact of PhysHPO is its ability to generate videos that are significantly more physically plausible. This addresses a critical bottleneck in current video generation technology, moving beyond visually appealing but physically illogical outputs. Enhanced realism is vital for applications where fidelity to physical laws is non-negotiable.

- **Enabling High-Fidelity Simulations and Virtual Environments:** For sectors like scientific research, engineering, and product design, physically accurate video simulations are invaluable. PhysHPO could enable more realistic virtual reality experiences, highly reliable training simulations (e.g., for robotics, autonomous vehicles), and more accurate scientific visualizations, directly impacting research and development cycles.

- **Improved Content Creation and Entertainment:** In the entertainment industry, from film and game development to advertising, the ability to generate physically coherent visual effects and animations without extensive manual intervention could dramatically reduce production costs and time, while enhancing visual quality. This framework allows creators to focus on narrative and artistic vision, with the underlying AI handling complex physical dynamics.

- **Resource Efficiency through Data Selection:** The introduction of an automated data selection pipeline for video generation is a groundbreaking contribution. By efficiently identifying and leveraging "good data" from existing large-scale datasets, PhysHPO circumvents the costly and time-consuming process of manual dataset construction. This makes the development of specialized video generation models more accessible and sustainable for research groups and companies with limited resources, democratizing advancements in the field.

- **Advancing Direct Preference Optimization (DPO):** PhysHPO pushes the boundaries of DPO by demonstrating the power of *fine-grained, hierarchical* preference alignment. This approach provides a blueprint for future DPO applications to target subtle and complex aspects of generative models, moving beyond coarse-grained optimization to achieve a deeper understanding and control over generated content. The cross-modal integration further highlights the versatility of DPO.

- **Enhanced Generalization and Creative Capabilities:** The surprising finding that PhysHPO performs well on "impossible prompts" suggests that instilling physical understanding actually equips models with a stronger capacity for *generalization* and *creative adaptation*. By learning the underlying semantic and dynamic structures that govern real-world physics, the model gains a more robust internal representation of the world, allowing it to interpret and generate even contradictory instructions with greater consistency and imagination. This opens new avenues for generative AI in artistic and imaginative content creation.

- **Catalyst for Future Research:** PhysHPO establishes new research directions in several areas:
 - **Data-centric AI for Video:** Further optimization of data selection strategies for various video generation tasks.
 - **Fine-grained Control Mechanisms:** Exploring other hierarchical structures or granularities for preference optimization beyond physical plausibility.
 - **Lightweight Architectures:** Developing more computationally efficient models that can still leverage fine-grained DPO, making it more practical for broader adoption.
 - **Multi-modal Understanding:** The cross-modal nature of PhysHPO will spur further research into how textual and visual cues can jointly contribute to sophisticated generative tasks.

- **Ethical Considerations:** The authors acknowledge potential ethical implications, specifically the misuse of high-quality video generation for creating misleading or harmful content. Their recommendation for safeguards like watermarking and emphasizing responsible use highlights a commitment to ethical AI development, signaling a proactive approach to managing the societal impact of powerful generative technologies.

In conclusion, PhysHPO not only offers a concrete solution to a long-standing challenge in video generation but also introduces innovative methodological paradigms that could significantly influence the future trajectory of generative AI research and its applications across scientific, industrial, and creative domains.
