# UniRecGen: Unifying Multi-View 3D Reconstruction and Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> The UniRecGen framework unifies multi-view 3D reconstruction and generation to create complete, consistent 3D object models from sparse, unposed multi-view images. It establishes new state-of-the-art performance on Toys4K and GSO datasets for 3D reconstruction and significantly improves camera pose and depth estimation.

## Problem

- Deterministic 3D reconstruction methods often produce geometrically incomplete models and lack global structural priors.
- Stochastic 3D generation models struggle to maintain strict fidelity to input-specific details and multi-view consistency, often leading to geometric 'hallucinations'.
- Previous 2D-to-3D unification approaches, which rely on lifting 2D information, suffer from 'representation gaps' and consistency artifacts.

## Method

- A two-stage modular pipeline of **UniRecGen** first trains a ==feed-forward reconstruction module==, then a ==reconstruction-guided controllable generation module==.
- Utilizes ==branch repurposing== in the reconstruction stage to canonicalize camera-centric geometry predictions into an object-centric space, making them compatible with generative priors.
- Employs ==latent-augmented view conditioning== in the generation stage, enriching multi-view conditioning signals with view-dependent geometric context from reconstruction outputs and refined camera tokens.

## Results

- Achieved state-of-the-art 3D reconstruction performance on Toys4K (**Chamfer-L2 0.0175, F-Score 0.7695**) and GSO (**Chamfer-L2 0.0163, F-Score 0.7818**) datasets, outperforming prior methods like ReconViaGen.
- Demonstrated superior camera pose estimation (**ATE 0.0151** on GSO) and improved depth prediction accuracy (**Abs Rel 0.0033** on GSO) compared to baselines.
- Generated 3D meshes with higher structural fidelity and superior multi-view consistency from sparse inputs, showing robustness and effective generalization to real-world environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Native 3D unification of reconstruction and generation, by addressing disparate learning dynamics and representation incompatibility, effectively combines input fidelity with generative completeness.
> - Canonicalizing reconstruction outputs via a targeted 'branch repurposing' strategy enables seamless integration with object-centric generative models while preserving pre-trained priors.
> - Enhancing multi-view generative conditioning with latent-augmented geometric context from the reconstruction module significantly improves 3D shape modeling performance and consistency.
