# MultiCrafter: High-Fidelity Multi-Subject Generation via Disentangled Attention and Identity-Aware Preference Alignment

> [!summary] Summary
> MultiCrafter, developed by researchers from Zhejiang University and Huawei Technologies, introduces a decoupled, two-stage training framework for high-fidelity multi-subject image generation. This approach resolves attention entanglement and aligns with human preferences, achieving a 28.3% relative improvement in Face-Sim score for multi-human generation and outperforming state-of-the-art methods across benchmarks.

## Problem

- Existing multi-subject text-to-image generation methods struggle with subject fidelity, exhibiting "attribute leakage" or "attention bleeding" between subjects.
- Current approaches use a "highly coupled training paradigm" with a single, indirect reconstruction loss, forcing a suboptimal compromise between subject fidelity and alignment with human aesthetic preferences.
- Online reinforcement learning has largely been unexplored for the specialized challenge of multi-subject driven generation, particularly in addressing multi-dimensional human preferences.

## Method

- A decoupled, two-stage training framework built on a ==Diffusion Transformer== (DiT) architecture, separating fidelity learning from preference alignment.
- Stage 1 (Fidelity-Focused Pre-training) introduces Identity-Disentangled Attention Regularization to explicitly guide attention to correct spatial regions, combined with Mixture-of-Experts LoRA (MoE-LoRA) to enhance capacity for diverse spatial layouts.
- Stage 2 (Identity-Preserving Preference Optimization - IPPO) employs an online reinforcement learning framework (GSPO) with a composite reward model that includes novel Multi-ID Alignment Reward (using Hungarian matching for human faces and DINOv2 for objects) to align with aesthetics and text-image fidelity while preserving identity.

## Results

- MultiCrafter achieved the highest overall score in comprehensive benchmarks, demonstrating state-of-the-art performance in multi-human and multi-object customized generation.
- For multi-human generation, it showed a **28.3%** relative improvement in Face-Sim score (**0.5284**) over the next-best method (XVerse at **0.4117**), alongside high DINO-I (**0.8294**) and CLIP-I (**0.8524**) scores.
- Ablation studies confirmed the effectiveness of Identity-Disentangled Attention Regularization in enhancing subject fidelity and MoE-LoRA in handling diverse spatial layouts, while IPPO improved aesthetics and text alignment without sacrificing fidelity.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decoupling the learning objectives of subject fidelity and human preference alignment into distinct training stages effectively mitigates the "proxy-objective mismatch" of coupled training.
> - Explicit spatial attention regularization is crucial for preventing attribute leakage and maintaining high subject fidelity in multi-subject generation.
> - Online reinforcement learning, with a carefully designed composite reward that includes a novel multi-ID alignment component, can stably optimize for complex, multi-dimensional human preferences while robustly preserving subject identities.
