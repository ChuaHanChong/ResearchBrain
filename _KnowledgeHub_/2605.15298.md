# PhysBrain 1.0 Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> PhysBrain 1.0 integrates physical commonsense understanding derived from human egocentric video into embodied AI, enhancing physically grounded reasoning and achieving higher success rates across VLM benchmarks, VLA simulations, and real-world robot tasks. The system demonstrated notable improvements, including a 16.2 percentage point gain in real-world single-object grasping.

## Problem

- Current Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models primarily rely on expensive, platform-dependent robot trajectories, limiting the diversity and coverage of physical interactions learned.
- Models trained solely through action imitation often lack a deep understanding of physical regularities, which is crucial for robust performance under variations in viewpoint, scene layout, or object state.
- Effectively transforming raw human first-person video into structured, actionable physical supervision for robots and transferring these priors without catastrophic forgetting has been challenging.

## Method

- **PhysBrain**'s data engine systematically converts human egocentric interaction videos into structured scene meta-information and physically grounded Question-Answer (QA) pairs, emphasizing ==physical explicitness== and ==depth-aware spatial augmentation==.
- A physically informed base Vision-Language Model (==Qwen3-VL==) was trained with this large-scale, human-derived physical commonsense supervision.
- A ==dual-pathway architecture== for VLA adaptation was introduced, utilizing a frozen general pathway from the base VLM and a trainable embodied pathway for continuous action generation, alongside an ==action-conditioned language alignment== objective to preserve capabilities.

## Results

- PhysBrain 8B achieved top scores on VLM benchmarks, including ERQA (**45.5**) and PhysBench (**50.2**), demonstrating enhanced physically grounded reasoning and general multimodal capability.
- In VLA simulation, PhysBrain 1.0 attained the best average success rates across benchmarks: **80.2%** on SimplerEnv-WidowX, **91.33%** on SimplerEnv-GoogleRobot, and **64.5%** on RoboCasa-GR1, showing improved out-of-domain generalization.
- Real-world experiments on a Franka robot showed a **16.2 percentage point** increase in single-object grasping success (**63.3%** vs. **47.1%**) and a **14.0 percentage point** gain in long-horizon tasks (**45.0%** vs. **31.0%**) compared to strong baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Acquiring general physical commonsense from large-scale human egocentric video *before* robot adaptation can significantly enhance embodied AI's robustness and generalization beyond robot-specific data.
> - Structured scene meta-information and depth-aware spatial augmentation derived from human videos can be effectively transformed into explicit, robot-relevant physical supervision, improving a VLM's perception, state inference, and planning.
> - A dual-pathway adaptation architecture with asymmetric layer-wise fusion successfully transfers human-derived physical priors to robot control while preventing catastrophic forgetting of general multimodal capabilities and maintaining instruction sensitivity.
