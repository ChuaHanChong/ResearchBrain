# MorphSeek: Fine-grained Latent Representation-Level Policy Optimization for Deformable Image Registration

> [!summary] Summary
> MorphSeek introduces a reinforcement learning framework that optimizes deformable image registration by performing fine-grained, step-wise refinements within a low-dimensional latent space. This approach achieved up to 4% Dice score gains and significantly reduced deformation irregularities across 3D medical image datasets while demonstrating high label efficiency.

## Problem

- Medical imaging often lacks sufficient voxel-level annotations, making it challenging for deep learning models to constrain local boundaries and subtle structures effectively.
- Existing deep learning-based deformable image registration models struggle with large, non-rigid deformations in a single pass, and current step-wise methods often rely on fixed, non-adaptive structures.
- Direct application of reinforcement learning to dense deformable image registration is difficult due to the prohibitively high-dimensional continuous state and action spaces, leading to unstable training and high computational costs.

## Method

- The framework reformulates ==registration networks== by decoupling the encoder and decoder and introducing a Gaussian Policy Head to parameterize a stochastic, sampleable latent representation as the policy action.
- A three-stage training paradigm is employed: an unsupervised warm-up phase pre-conditions the latent space, followed by weakly supervised fine-tuning using multi-trajectory Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO).
- Latent-Dimension Variance Normalization (LDVN) is introduced to stabilize GRPO updates in high-dimensional latent spaces, ensuring robust training while preserving policy-gradient direction.

## Results

- MorphSeek consistently improved Dice scores (e.g., over **4%** gain on Abdomen MR←CT for TransMorph) and reduced Negative Jacobian Determinants across OASIS, LiTS, and Abdomen MR←CT benchmarks using VoxelMorph-L, TransMorph, and NICE-Trans backbones.
- The method demonstrated high label efficiency, achieving strong performance with approximately 16 labeled pairs and reaching **98.5%** of its full-label performance using only **60%** of the training data on OASIS.
- MorphSeek introduced less than **3%** additional parameters, maintained comparable inference latency, and the unsupervised warm-up increased stable GRPO training success from **33%** to **79%** while reducing convergence epochs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reframing dense deformable image registration as a latent-space policy optimization problem makes reinforcement learning practical for fine-grained 3D deformations, overcoming the limitations of high-dimensional action spaces.
> - An unsupervised warm-up phase is critical for shaping a stable latent space, preventing posterior collapse, and making subsequent high-dimensional policy optimization significantly more robust and efficient.
> - Multi-trajectory, multi-step GRPO effectively reuses scarce segmentation labels, allowing for strong performance gains with significantly fewer annotated samples and enabling adaptive coarse-to-fine refinement.
