# WorldScore: A Unified Evaluation Benchmark for World Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Stanford University introduced WorldScore, the first unified benchmark for evaluating diverse world generation models, including those for 3D, 4D, text-to-video, and image-to-video. This benchmark standardizes evaluation through next-scene generation tasks and a multi-faceted metric system that assesses controllability, quality, and dynamics, revealing 3D models excel in static consistency while video models struggle with camera control and long-sequence coherence.

## Problem

- The absence of a unified and comprehensive evaluation framework for "world generation" that goes beyond single-scene assessments to evaluate complex, multi-scene environments.
- Existing benchmarks are fragmented, focusing on isolated visual generation aspects like single-scene video quality, without explicit spatial layout control or multi-scene coherence evaluation.
- Incompatibility among current evaluation methodologies, preventing fair comparison across diverse generative models (e.g., 3D, 4D, T2V, I2V) due to varied input and output requirements.

## Method

- Introduces **WorldScore**, the first unified benchmark for world generation, which decomposes the complex task into standardized "next-scene generation tasks" guided by image, text, and camera trajectory inputs.
- Curates a comprehensive dataset of 3,000 test examples covering static and dynamic worlds, indoor and outdoor environments, and both photorealistic and stylized visual domains.
- Develops a multi-faceted evaluation metric system, "**WorldScore**," aggregating 10 individual metrics across three critical aspects: controllability, quality, and dynamics.

## Results

- 3D scene generation models like WonderWorld (72.69) and LucidDreamer (70.40) achieved the highest WorldScore-Static, significantly outperforming video models in generating geometrically consistent static worlds.
- Video generation models exhibited low camera controllability scores (e.g., CogVideoX-T2V at 40.22), substantially lower than any 3D/4D model, indicating difficulty in following explicit camera trajectories.
- Some open-source video models, such as CogVideoX-I2V, achieved overall WorldScore-Static (62.15) and WorldScore-Dynamic (59.12) performances comparable to leading commercial closed-source models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - 3D scene generation models demonstrate superior performance in static world consistency, camera controllability, and geometric fidelity compared to video models, but generally lack dynamic generation capabilities.
> - Video generation models face substantial challenges in camera controllability and maintaining coherence over long sequences, especially in outdoor scenes, limiting their spatial guidance adherence.
> - Dynamic world generation presents a trade-off between motion smoothness and magnitude, where models capable of larger motions often exhibit lower temporal stability, and struggle with accurate motion placement.
