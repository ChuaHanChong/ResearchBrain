# RoboTracer: Mastering Spatial Trace with Reasoning in Vision-Language Models for Robotics

> [!summary] Summary
> RoboTracer enhances Vision-Language Models with 3D spatial intelligence, enabling the generation of precise 3D positional sequences for robotic control. It introduces the TraceSpatial dataset and benchmark, demonstrating superior performance in 3D spatial reasoning and achieving state-of-the-art results in real-world robotic manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Embodied robots struggle to interpret and execute complex, spatially constrained natural language instructions in 3D environments due to limited metric-grounded understanding.
- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) lack precise 3D spatial reasoning capabilities and often operate in 2D, failing to capture absolute metric quantities crucial for robotics.
- There is a scarcity of large-scale, high-quality datasets and benchmarks specifically designed for multi-step, metric-grounded 3D spatial tracing in robotics.

## Method

- Introduces RoboTracer, a VLM architecture extended with a ==universal spatial encoder== to integrate geometric inputs and a ==scale decoder== for explicit metric scale prediction.
- Employs a two-stage training paradigm: ==Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT)== for metric alignment and enhancement, followed by ==Reinforcement Fine-tuning (RFT)== using novel ==metric-sensitive process rewards== for multi-step reasoning.
- Develops the ==TraceSpatial dataset== (4.5M samples) for 3D spatial reasoning, and TraceSpatial-Bench, a challenging real-world benchmark for evaluating 3D spatial tracing.

## Results

- RoboTracer-8B-SFT achieved an **8.58%** absolute improvement over Gemini-2.5-Pro and **20.3%** over NVILA-8B in 3D spatial understanding and metric measuring benchmarks.
- On the challenging real-world TraceSpatial-Bench, RoboTracer-RFT achieved an overall success rate **36%** higher than Gemini-2.5-Pro, demonstrating robust 3D spatial tracing with collision-free path validation.
- RoboTracer enabled UR5 and G1 humanoid robots to execute long-horizon, dynamic tasks in cluttered real-world scenes, generating collision-free spatial traces and adapting to changes at **1.5 Hz**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicit integration of 3D geometric inputs via a universal spatial encoder and direct supervision for metric scale prediction are crucial for enhancing a VLM's 3D spatial and metric awareness.
> - Metric-sensitive process rewards, which supervise intermediate perceptual cues in an order-invariant manner, significantly improve the model's ability to perform complex, multi-step compositional reasoning during RFT.
> - A diverse data recipe combining 2D, 3D, and video data within TraceSpatial is essential for learning robust 3D spatial referring, measuring, and physically plausible trace generation.
