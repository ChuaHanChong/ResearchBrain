# SpeechJudge: Towards Human-Level Judgment for Speech Naturalness

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, ByteDance Seed, and DataBaker Technology developed SpeechJudge, a comprehensive suite for evaluating and aligning speech naturalness. This includes a 99K human-annotated dataset, an evaluation benchmark, and SpeechJudge-GRM, an AudioLLM-based reward model which achieved 77.2% accuracy in judging speech naturalness, surpassing generic AudioLLMs and demonstrating efficacy in post-training text-to-speech models.

## Problem

- A lack of large-scale human preference datasets for speech naturalness hinders the effective alignment of generative speech models with human perception.
- Existing automated metrics and generic AudioLLMs struggle to accurately and robustly assess the holistic naturalness of synthesized speech.
- The absence of a reliable, human-aligned reward signal impedes the application of advanced post-training techniques like RLHF or DPO for speech synthesis.

## Method

- Constructed **SpeechJudge**-Data, a large-scale dataset comprising 99K human-annotated ==pairwise naturalness== preferences for speech synthesized by diverse TTS models across multiple languages and styles.
- Established SpeechJudge-Eval, a high-quality benchmark extracted from the dataset, to standardize the evaluation of various models and metrics in naturalness judgment.
- Developed SpeechJudge-GRM, a generative reward model built upon an AudioLLM (Qwen2.5-Omni-7B) using a two-stage supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning approach to predict human naturalness preferences and provide Chain-of-Thought rationales.

## Results

- SpeechJudge-GRM (SFT+RL) achieved **77.2%** accuracy on the SpeechJudge-Eval benchmark (**79.4%** with voting), outperforming the best generic AudioLLM (Gemini-2.5-Flash at **69.1%**) and a Bradley-Terry reward model (**72.7%**).
- The model demonstrated generalization capabilities in an out-of-distribution evaluation, achieving **62.4%** accuracy (**63.7%** with voting) in distinguishing real human speech from high-quality commercial TTS clones.
- SpeechJudge-GRM successfully functioned as a reward function in an offline Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) setup, objectively improving the perceived naturalness of an unseen zero-shot TTS model in subjective evaluations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current automated speech naturalness evaluators, including objective metrics, MOS predictors, and generic AudioLLMs, perform poorly (often below 70% accuracy) compared to human judgments.
> - A two-stage SFT+RL approach effectively enhances an AudioLLM's ability to capture nuanced human naturalness preferences, enabling it to generalize even to out-of-distribution, high-quality speech samples.
> - Specialized, large-scale human preference data is indispensable for training robust reward models that can guide the alignment of generative speech models towards human-level naturalness.
