# Learning to Reason under Off-Policy Guidance

> [!summary] Summary
> LUFFY introduces a framework that enhances Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) by integrating off-policy guidance into Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR). This approach enables LRMs to acquire new reasoning capabilities from stronger external policies, achieving state-of-the-art performance on math benchmarks, superior generalization on out-of-distribution tasks, and successfully training weaker foundation models where on-policy methods fail.

## Problem

- Existing Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) for Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) operates in an "on-policy" manner, limiting models to learning from their own self-generated outputs.
- This on-policy constraint restricts LRMs from acquiring genuinely new cognitive capacities or reasoning strategies that lie beyond their initial exploration capabilities.
- Models trained with on-policy RLVR often reach performance plateaus, unable to progress further due to a lack of foundational behaviors for advancement.

## Method

- ==LUFFY== extends Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) by integrating off-policy reasoning traces generated by stronger external models into the training process.
- It employs =="Mixed-Policy GRPO"== which incorporates both on-policy and off-policy rollouts for advantage computation, adaptively weighting the influence of external demonstrations based on the trained model's performance.
- =="Policy Shaping via Regularized Importance Sampling"== re-weights gradients for off-policy distributions, encouraging the model to learn from low-probability tokens and maintaining exploration to prevent =="entropy collapse"==.

## Results

- LUFFY achieved an average gain of **+6.4** points across six challenging math reasoning benchmarks and showed a **+6.2** points advantage on average over baselines for out-of-distribution tasks.
- It successfully trained weaker foundation models, such as LLaMA-3.1-8B, in scenarios where on-policy RLVR methods completely failed to make progress.
- Training dynamics revealed LUFFY maintains higher generation entropy throughout training, indicating continuous exploration, and dynamically balances imitation of off-policy traces with self-driven exploration.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Off-policy guidance allows Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) to transcend their initial cognitive boundaries by internalizing complex reasoning patterns from more capable external models.
> - Dynamically balancing imitation of external guidance with self-exploration is crucial for acquiring new skills and preventing models from becoming overly deterministic.
> - Off-policy learning enables the successful training of weaker foundation models that on-policy Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) methods cannot effectively optimize.
