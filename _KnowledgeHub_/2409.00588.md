# Diffusion Policy Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> DPPO introduces a policy optimization framework for fine-tuning pre-trained diffusion-based robot policies using policy gradient methods, demonstrating robust training, improved performance, and a substantial reduction in the sim-to-real gap on various manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Pre-trained robot policies, particularly those acquired via behavior cloning from expert demonstrations, often yield suboptimal performance due to limitations in the demonstration data.
- Fine-tuning diffusion-based robot policies with reinforcement learning (RL) has been challenging, especially with policy gradient (PG) methods, which were widely considered inefficient or unstable for this policy class.
- Existing RL approaches for improving diffusion policies often relied on off-policy Q-learning or weighted regression, which could suffer from instability in complex, continuous control tasks.

## Method

- Proposes **Diffusion Policy Policy Optimization** (DPPO), a novel framework that conceptualizes the multi-step diffusion denoising process as an embedded ==Markov Decision Process (MDP)==.
- Applies Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) within this two-layer MDP structure, allowing task rewards to effectively influence the entire iterative denoising process to optimize action selection.
- Incorporates practical design choices such as fine-tuning only a subset of denoising steps, leveraging DDIM sampling with exploration noise, and modifying noise scheduling for improved training stability and efficiency.

## Results

- DPPO consistently outperformed or matched alternative diffusion-based and demo-augmented RL methods across various OpenAI Gym, ROBOMIMIC, and Franka Kitchen tasks, demonstrating superior training stability and final performance.
- It was the first RL algorithm to solve the challenging ROBOMIMIC `Transport` task from pixel input to high success rates (>**50%**) and significantly outperformed unimodal Gaussian and Gaussian Mixture Model policy parameterizations fine-tuned with PPO.
- Policies trained with DPPO demonstrated robust zero-shot sim-to-real transfer, achieving an **80%** success rate (16/20 trials) on a real robot for a furniture assembly task, where other policy types failed entirely.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Diffusion models enable structured, on-manifold exploration during fine-tuning, allowing policies to efficiently search within relevant success modes near the pre-training data manifold.
> - The multi-step denoising process of diffusion policies contributes to exceptional training stability for policy gradient updates, making them more robust against noise and enabling the learning of long action chunks.
> - Policies fine-tuned with DPPO exhibit superior robustness to dynamic perturbations and initial state changes, leading to a much smaller sim-to-real gap and successful zero-shot deployment on physical robots.
