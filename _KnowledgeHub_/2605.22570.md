# VGenST-Bench: A Benchmark for Spatio-Temporal Reasoning via Active Video Synthesis

> [!summary] Summary
> VGenST-Bench introduces a novel video benchmark for spatio-temporal reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), constructed by actively synthesizing photorealistic videos with generative models. Evaluations reveal top MLLMs are over 13 percentage points below human performance on complex reasoning tasks, showing significant degradation from basic perception to higher-order inference.

## Problem

- Existing MLLM benchmarks for spatio-temporal reasoning are susceptible to data contamination and allow models to exploit shortcuts, leading to inflated performance metrics.
- Passively curated video datasets for evaluation offer limited scalability and diversity, and often fail to truly isolate dynamic spatio-temporal relationships.
- Synthetic data benchmarks often suffer from a 'visual realism gap,' limiting their applicability for evaluating modern MLLMs trained on photorealistic data.

## Method

- **VGenST-Bench** utilizes an active synthesis paradigm, employing video generative models to create a wide array of precisely controlled, photorealistic evaluation scenarios.
- The benchmark is structured around a 3x2x2 taxonomy (spatial scale, perspective, scene dynamics) to systematically cover diverse spatio-temporal contexts, with a dedicated task for each category.
- A hierarchical Question-Answering (QA) design (L1: Visual Perception, L2: Scene Understanding, L3: Spatio-Temporal Reasoning) coupled with question reformulation variants (e.g., 'None-of-these' options, open-ended) is used to enable fine-grained diagnosis and mitigate shortcut exploitation.

## Results

- Human annotators achieved near-ceiling performance at **99.0%** accuracy, confirming the clarity and unambiguous nature of the benchmark tasks.
- The highest-performing MLLM, Gemini 3 Flash, reached **85.9%** accuracy, demonstrating a gap of over **13** percentage points below human performance, with most open-source models performing below **70%**.
- A consistent performance degradation was observed across MLLMs from L1 (Visual Perception) to L3 (Spatio-Temporal Reasoning), with some models dropping from over **90%** on L1 to below **40%** on L3.
- MLLMs showed dramatic performance drops when faced with 'None-of-these' correct answers or open-ended questions, revealing a vulnerability to question reformulation and a reliance on reasoning shortcuts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The active synthesis approach effectively mitigates data contamination and shortcut exploitation, providing a more reliable and diagnostic testbed for MLLM spatio-temporal reasoning.
> - MLLMs exhibit a consistent and substantial performance degradation as cognitive tasks move from basic visual perception (L1) to higher-order spatio-temporal reasoning (L3), highlighting a systematic bottleneck in complex inference.
> - MLLMs struggle significantly with 'None-of-these' answer options and open-ended questions, indicating a reliance on choice priors and positional biases rather than robust reasoning in standard multiple-choice formats.
