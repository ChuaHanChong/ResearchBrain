# RECAST: Expanding the Boundaries of LLMs' Complex Instruction Following with Multi-Constraint Data

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Fudan University and Xiaohongshu Inc. introduce RECAST, a scalable framework for synthesizing complex instruction-following datasets, and RECAST-30K, a 30,000-instance dataset with an average of 13.4 constraints per instruction. The work also presents RLVC, a reinforcement learning method, which collectively enabled models to achieve up to 32.33% average constraint satisfaction on complex tasks, surpassing larger, general instruction-tuned models while preserving general capabilities.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) struggle to accurately follow complex instructions containing a high number of explicit constraints (e.g., more than 10), limiting their reliability in real-world applications.
- Existing instruction-following datasets typically feature only 3-5 constraints, are not scalable for large-scale training, and often rely on narrowly applicable code-verifiable constraints.
- A lack of diverse, large-scale, high-quality training and evaluation data hinders the systematic assessment and enhancement of LLMs' multi-constraint instruction-following abilities.

## Method

- Developed **RECAST** (Realistic Extraction of ==Constraints== for Augmented inStruction synThesis), an efficient and scalable framework for synthesizing datasets with a significantly higher number of diverse, verifiable constraints per instance.
- Constructed RECAST-30K, a large-scale dataset of 29,939 instances, where each instruction is augmented with an average of 13.4 constraints (4.8 rule-based, 8.6 model-based) across 19 distinct types.
- Introduced RLVC (Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Constraints), a method leveraging the dataset's explicit constraint verification mechanisms to provide fine-grained reward signals for simultaneous multi-objective policy optimization.

## Results

- Models fine-tuned on RECAST-**30K**-SFT achieved a **31.25%** average constraint satisfaction rate on the RECAST-Test benchmark, outperforming instruction-tuned variants and models fine-tuned on other datasets.
- The RECAST-**30K**-RLVC approach further enhanced performance, with Qwen-2.5-**7B** reaching a **32.33%** average satisfaction rate on RECAST-Test, demonstrating more pronounced gains at higher difficulty levels.
- RECAST-trained models consistently outperformed all baseline methods on external instruction-following benchmarks (IFEVAL and FollowBench) and maintained competitive performance on general capability benchmarks (GPQA for knowledge, MUSR for reasoning).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - LLM performance consistently degrades as the number of constraints in an instruction increases, with rule-based constraints generally proving more challenging for models to satisfy than model-based ones.
> - Fine-tuning models on RECAST-30K significantly improves their ability to follow complex multi-constraint instructions, enabling smaller models to outperform larger, general instruction-tuned models.
> - Utilizing verifiable, constraint-specific rewards within a reinforcement learning framework (RLVC) further boosts LLM performance in complex multi-constraint scenarios without compromising general capabilities or transferability to other instruction-following tasks.
