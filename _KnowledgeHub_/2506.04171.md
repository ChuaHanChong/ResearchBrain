# Physics-Constrained Flow Matching: Sampling Generative Models with Hard Constraints

> [!summary] Summary
> Physics-Constrained Flow Matching (PCFM) introduces a zero-shot inference framework for pretrained flow-based generative models, enabling the enforcement of arbitrary nonlinear equality constraints up to machine precision for physical systems governed by partial differential equations. The method produces physically consistent solutions, even for systems with shocks and discontinuities, while maintaining generative fidelity.

## Problem

- Deep generative models applied to physical systems often fail to rigorously adhere to underlying physical laws, which are typically "hard constraints" requiring exact satisfaction.
- Existing methods for integrating physics, such as soft penalties or architectural priors, do not guarantee precise fulfillment of physical laws, leading to implausible generated solutions.
- Enforcing complex, nonlinear equality constraints in pretrained generative models, especially flow-based ones, presents challenges in terms of computational efficiency and maintaining generative fidelity without requiring costly retraining.

## Method

- **PCFM** introduces a ==zero-shot inference framework== that continuously guides the generative sampling process of pretrained ==flow-based models==.
- The method interleaves intermediate ==forward shooting projections== onto ==linearized constraint manifolds== with ==reverse updates== using an ==Optimal Transport displacement interpolant==.
- A ==relaxed constraint correction== and a final ==rigorous projection== ensure the satisfaction of arbitrary nonlinear equality constraints up to machine precision.

## Results

- PCFM consistently achieves **machine-level precision** (Constraint Error of **0**) for all enforced hard constraints (initial conditions, boundary conditions, global mass conservation) across diverse linear and nonlinear PDE problems.
- The method demonstrates superior generative fidelity (lower **MMSE**, **SMSE**, **FPD**) compared to baselines like **ECI** and **DiffusionPDE**, particularly in capturing complex phenomena such as shocks in Burgers' equation.
- Integrating more complementary physical constraints within PCFM, such as Total Variation Diminishing properties, enhances generation quality and structural realism, while maintaining exact satisfaction of other constraints.
- PCFM successfully **generalizes to out-of-distribution** initial conditions, maintaining low errors and exact constraint satisfaction **without requiring retraining** of the generative model.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Applying constraint projections at intermediate flow steps is crucial for numerical stability, preventing generative trajectories from drifting significantly from the constraint manifold.
> - Utilizing an Optimal Transport displacement interpolant for reverse updates offers a numerically stable and solver-invariant way to propagate constraint corrections through the flow.
> - The framework can enforce multiple complementary hard constraints, leading to improved generative fidelity without the degradation often seen in soft-penalty methods due to competing objectives.
