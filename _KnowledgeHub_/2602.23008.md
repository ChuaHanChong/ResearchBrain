# Exploratory Memory-Augmented LLM Agent via Hybrid On- and Off-Policy Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Microsoft Research and KAIST developed EMPO², a hybrid reinforcement learning framework that enhances exploration in LLM agents by combining self-generated memory with on- and off-policy optimization. This approach led to a 128.6% performance gain over GRPO on ScienceWorld and improved adaptability to out-of-distribution tasks by an average of 136%, internalizing memory-derived insights into the model's parameters for robust inference without external memory.

## Problem

- Large Language Model (LLM) agents tend to exploit existing prior knowledge, leading to erroneous behaviors and poor performance in environments requiring systematic exploration of novel states.
- Current memory-augmented methods for LLM agents often exhibit performance saturation as they fail to capture the diversity needed for continuous improvement and generalization.
- Online reinforcement learning algorithms used with LLMs, such as GRPO, suffer from insufficient exploration, frequently converging to suboptimal solutions.

## Method

- Developed EMPO², a ==hybrid reinforcement learning== framework that integrates self-generated external memory with a dual-mode policy optimization strategy.
- The agent continuously generates and stores natural-language "self-guidance tips" based on past experiences, used to augment decision-making during rollouts.
- Implemented a two-phase update mechanism (on-policy and off-policy), where off-policy updates distill the benefits of memory-augmented exploration into the LLM's core parameters without requiring memory at inference.

## Results

- EMPO² significantly outperformed baselines, achieving a **128.6%** improvement over GRPO on ScienceWorld with an average return of 75.9, and a **76.9%** success rate on WebShop.
- The framework exhibited enhanced generalizability, adapting to out-of-distribution tasks with an average improvement of **136%** over GRPO in early adaptation steps.
- Ablation studies demonstrated that the integration of all three learning modes and the use of intrinsic rewards were critical for stable and superior learning performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A hybrid approach unifying parametric policy updates with non-parametric memory-augmented exploration leads to superior performance and adaptability for LLM agents.
> - Reward-guided knowledge distillation, performed via off-policy updates, effectively internalizes insights gained from memory-augmented exploration into the LLM's parameters.
> - Stochastic modes for both rollouts and updates, coupled with intrinsic rewards for state novelty, are essential for driving continuous exploration and robust learning.
