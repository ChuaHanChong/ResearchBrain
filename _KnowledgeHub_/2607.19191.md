# ABot-World-0: Infinite Interactive World Rollout on a Single Desktop GPU

> [!summary] Summary
> The ABot-World Team at Alibaba Group's AMAP CV Lab developed ABot-World-0, an action-conditioned video world model capable of generating interactive, persistent virtual worlds. It achieves real-time rollout at up to 16 frames per second (FPS) at 720P resolution with 1.2 seconds of action-to-first-frame latency, running on a single desktop GPU with less than 19.3 GiB VRAM.

## Problem

- Existing video generation models struggle with the joint optimization of visual fidelity, controllability, state persistence, rollout stability, latency, throughput, and memory footprint for interactive world simulation.
- Long-horizon autoregressive video generation suffers from accumulated distribution shift and error, leading to quality degradation and inconsistency over extended rollouts.
- Deploying full-stack generative models at interactive speeds requires significant computational resources, often exceeding consumer-grade hardware capabilities.

## Method

- Developed a ==multi-source data infrastructure== (AAA games, simulation engines, internet videos) with a ==training-feedback-driven closed loop== to continuously refine training data.
- Implemented a ==progressive training pipeline==, including **"LongForcing"** to distill a bidirectional teacher into a causal student, mitigating long-horizon drift by aligning student self-rollouts with an extended-horizon teacher.
- Designed a ==full-stack inference co-design== featuring ==chunk-wise streaming==, a ==lightweight VAE decoder==, ==memory-aware scheduling==, ==low-precision compute (e.g., FP8 quantization)==, and ==efficient attention kernels== for real-time performance on a single GPU.

## Results

- Achieved real-time performance on a single NVIDIA RTX 5090, with up to **16 FPS** (720P), **1.2 seconds** action-to-first-frame latency, and peak VRAM usage under **19.3 GiB**.
- Demonstrated sustained controllability and scene coherence over rollouts extending up to **24 hours**, maintaining recognizable structure and active motion without quality degradation.
- Attained competitive scores on the WorldRoamBench benchmark, with Strict Accuracy of **0.5266**, Aesthetic score of **0.5039**, and a Mechanics score of **0.5223**, indicating plausible physical responses.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Jointly optimizing data acquisition, model training for long-horizon stability, and inference stack for efficiency is crucial for practical interactive world modeling on consumer hardware.
> - LongForcing, by leveraging an extended-horizon teacher for distribution matching on student self-rollouts, effectively reduces visual error accumulation and improves coherence over prolonged autoregressive generation.
> - Strategic co-design of the entire inference pipeline, from VAE decoding to attention kernels and memory management, can enable complex generative models to operate in real-time within strict hardware constraints.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The work addresses the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence, which is transitioning from static content generation to the creation of interactive, persistent, and controllable generative worlds. This domain, broadly termed "world models," aims to develop general-purpose simulators capable of representing environmental states, predicting evolution under interventions, and serving as a foundation for planning, learning, and embodied intelligence.

Prior research has established various components of this agenda. Systems like Genie, GameNGen, Oasis, WHAM, Cosmos, and Waymo World Model have explored playable environments, open worlds, physical AI, and driving simulations. Advances in large-scale video generation, particularly with diffusion models and transformers (e.g., Make-A-Video, VideoPoet, HunyuanVideo, Wan), have significantly improved visual fidelity and temporal coherence.

However, the authors identify four coupled bottlenecks preventing these advancements from becoming responsive, accessible, local, and persistent world simulators:
1. **Data Acquisition:** Obtaining broad, temporally coherent data with reliable action supervision.
2. **User Intent Representation:** Representing control across both camera navigation and embodied character control.
3. **Rollout Stability:** Preventing generated history from drifting and accumulating errors over long sequences.
4. **Deployment Efficiency:** Deploying the full generation and decoding stack at interactive speeds on practical, consumer-grade hardware.

This research positions itself as a comprehensive effort to jointly optimize these factors. It aims to integrate a multi-source data infrastructure, a unified control interface, a progressive training pipeline addressing long-horizon drift (LongForcing), and a full-stack inference co-design to achieve real-time, interactive world rollout on a single desktop GPU. This work extends existing causal and autoregressive video generation methods that focus on mitigating temporal drift (e.g., Diffusion Forcing, Self-Forcing, Causal Forcing, Context Forcing) by explicitly integrating action conditioning and optimizing for real-time deployment constraints. It also differentiates from methods relying on calibrated camera trajectories by using discrete keyboard actions for local, incremental control.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop ABot-World-0, an action-conditioned video world model capable of real-time, long-horizon closed-loop interaction and deployment on a single desktop GPU. This overarching goal is motivated by the need for generative environments that are not merely sequences of visually plausible clips, but persistent, coherent worlds where user actions dynamically influence the state.

The key motivations and specific objectives can be detailed as follows:

1. **Addressing Coupled Bottlenecks for Interactive World Modeling:** The paper highlights that current video generation models, while visually advanced, struggle with the joint optimization of visual fidelity, controllability, state persistence, rollout stability, latency, throughput, and memory footprint. The motivation is to overcome these integrated challenges to create a usable local, persistent world simulator.

2. **Developing a Robust Multi-Source Data Infrastructure:** Recognizing the fundamental "data problem," a core motivation is to move beyond passive data collection. The objective is to build an active, training-feedback-driven system that combines AAA game data, simulation engine data, and internet videos. This infrastructure aims to provide broad visual diversity, precise action synchronization, and geometric accuracy, with a continuous feedback loop to target model weaknesses.

3. **Establishing a Unified and Intuitive Control Interface:** The research aims to enable fluid user interaction by using raw keyboard actions as a unified control interface. This includes supporting both observer-style scene roaming and actor-style character motion. A further objective is to ensure identity consistency for controllable characters during long third-person rollouts through reference-character memory. The motivation is to make the control channel an intrinsic part of the dynamics model, improving responsiveness and usability.

4. **Mitigating Autoregressive Drift and Ensuring Long-Horizon Coherence:** Autoregressive models typically suffer from accumulated distribution shift and error, leading to quality degradation, identity inconsistency, and motion stagnation over long rollouts. A key objective is to develop a training pipeline that progressively distills a high-quality bidirectional teacher into a causal student. This includes introducing "LongForcing" to align long student self-rollouts with an extended-horizon teacher, thereby providing corrective signals for long-horizon contexts and improving stability beyond short-horizon training.

5. **Achieving Real-Time Performance on Consumer Hardware:** A significant motivation is to demonstrate the practical deployment of an interactive world model on a single desktop GPU, specifically an NVIDIA RTX 5090. The objective is to co-design a streaming inference stack that jointly optimizes action-to-first-frame latency, sustained frame rate (throughput), and peak VRAM usage. This involves techniques like lightweight VAE decoding, efficient attention, memory-aware scheduling, and low-bit DiT inference.

In summary, the motivation is to transform video generation from a sequential content creation task into a full-stack capability for interactive, persistent, and accessible world simulation, addressing the critical integration challenges from data to deployment.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology of ABot-World-0 is organized around three pillars: a multi-source data infrastructure, a progressive training pipeline, and a full-stack inference co-design.

### 4. Data Infrastructure

The data infrastructure treats data production as an integral, closed-loop part of model development.
- **Data Sources:** The training corpus integrates data from three complementary sources:
 - **AAA Game Data:** Provides high-fidelity 3D environments with precisely synchronized ground-truth action and observation signals, covering both first-person and third-person viewpoints.
 - **Simulation Engine Data:** Generated from Unreal Engine and ABot-3DGS (reconstructing real-world scenes from proprietary aerial and street-scanning data), offering geometrically precise synthetic data with deterministic action labels.
 - **Real-world Internet Data:** Introduces natural camera dynamics and diverse visual conditions, with action labels derived from pose estimation (pseudo-labels).
- **Collection (WorldExplorer):** An agent-driven system for game and simulation environments:
 - **Navigation Agent:** Autonomously traverses virtual environments using a multi-phase goal selection strategy to maximize scene coverage.
 - **Parallel Capture Pipeline:** Synchronized acquisition of RGB video, camera parameters, control inputs, environment state, and metadata at millisecond precision.
 - **Task Template System:** Organizes collection into structured categories (e.g., navigation, exploration, landmark observation, long-tail scenarios) with parameterized scene configurations (geography, weather, time of day, viewpoint).
 - **Training-Feedback-Driven Closed-Loop:** A module tracks per-category model performance, diagnoses weaknesses, and adaptively updates collection ratios and scene parameters to target underperforming areas.
- **Quality Filtering:** A multi-stage pipeline applies 14 distinct checks across six dimensions (file integrity, visual validity, geometric consistency, game state correctness, action-label alignment, metadata quality), augmented by Vision-Language Model (VLM)-based semantic assessment. Checks range from file existence and resolution compliance to user interface overlay detection, geometric anomaly detection, and game state (e.g., death sequence) removal.
- **Annotation:** Clips are enriched with:
 - **Action Labels:** Source-native controls (games), deterministic derivations (simulation), or pose-estimated pseudo-labels (internet) are converted into a unified canonical 8-dimensional multi-hot keyboard action format.
 - **Scene Descriptions:** VLM-generated natural language captions describing scene composition and environmental characteristics, prefixed with game identifiers for game data, and intentionally omitting camera motion information.
 - **Semantic Tagging:** VLM-extracted tags (e.g., indoor/outdoor, weather, vehicle type) for stratified sampling.
 - **Person Identity:** For third-person data, cropped character thumbnails from four cardinal directions and synthesized canonical frontal portraits provide identity references.

### 5. ABot-World-0 Model Architecture and Training

The model formulates interactive world modeling as predicting the next video chunk `v_t:t+L-1` conditioned on past visual history `v_0:t-1`, future action sequence `a_t:t+L-1`, and multimodal conditions `c` (text prompt, reference images).
- **Bidirectional Teacher Training:**
 - A pretrained video generative model (Wan2.2 backbone) is fine-tuned on the multi-source action-video corpus to learn action-conditioned dynamics.
 - **Action Control Injection:** Raw 8-dimensional keyboard actions are packed (4 frames per 32-dimensional token) and fed into an Action Control Adapter (PixelUnshuffle, convolution, residual blocks), whose output is additively injected into the DiT backbone's patch embeddings.
 - **Identity-Preserving Conditioning:** Reference character images, encoded by the VAE, are converted into identity-memory tokens. These tokens are prepended to the video-token sequence, assigned negative temporal RoPE indices, and interact with video tokens via asymmetric attention (video attends to memory, memory isolated from video).
- **Causal Progressive Student Distillation:** The bidirectional teacher, being non-causal and multi-step, is distilled into an efficient causal autoregressive generator through three stages:
 - **Stage 1: Teacher Forcing:** The bidirectional teacher initializes the student, which is then trained with a causal objective and attention mask. Ground-truth history frames `v_0:t-1` are provided as clean visual conditioning for predicting the noisy target chunk `v_t:t+L-1`.
 - **Stage 2: ODE Distillation:** The Stage 1 causal diffusion model is frozen. A few-step student model is trained to directly predict the clean endpoint `z_c0` from intermediate noisy latents `z_cs` along the probability-flow ODE trajectory of the causal model, conditioned on the same causal context `C_t` (clean visual history, target action sequence, multimodal conditions).
 - **Stage 3: LongForcing:** This stage addresses long-horizon drift. It performs distribution matching against an extended-horizon bidirectional teacher. The student is trained on its own long self-rollouts, and the extended-horizon teacher provides distribution-level corrective supervision to mitigate accumulated errors in contexts rarely seen during short-horizon training.

### 6. Full-Stack Co-Design for Real-Time World Rollout

To achieve real-time performance on a single GPU, the inference stack is co-designed to optimize throughput, latency, and VRAM:
- **Chunk-Wise Streaming:** Generates and decodes chunks of latent frames to amortize overhead.
- **Lightweight VAE Decoder (LightVAE):** A simplified and pruned VAE decoder reduces decoding time and peak memory, directly lowering action-to-first-frame latency.
- **Memory-Aware Scheduling:** Model modules are staged according to execution order and memory requirements, swapping them on/off the GPU to reduce peak VRAM.
- **Fast-RoPE:** Temporal RoPE is re-anchored within the local attention window, and a Triton-based kernel accelerates the rotation operation, reducing positional encoding overhead.
- **Low-Precision Compute:** DiT backbone's linear layers are quantized (FP8 as default, with MXFP6 and MXFP4 variants explored), while numerically sensitive components (VAE, text encoder) remain in higher precision.
- **Efficient Attention Kernels:** SageAttention2 is used as an efficient backend for attention computation without model retraining.
- **Bounded KV Caching:** A local-context KV cache with rolling eviction prevents unbounded growth of context memory. KV-cache quantization is explored to further reduce its footprint and bandwidth pressure.

### 7. Main Findings and Results

ABot-World-0 demonstrates performance in interactive world modeling, achieving real-time deployment metrics and competitive scores on benchmarks, supported by qualitative evidence of long-horizon coherence and physical plausibility.

### 8. Real-Time Deployment and Efficiency (Single NVIDIA RTX 5090)

The system's full-stack co-design enables deployment with the following characteristics at 720P resolution and batch size 1:
- **Throughput:** Up to 16 Frames Per Second (FPS) across optimized low-bit configurations. The FP8 configuration, which balances quality and performance, achieves 12.405 FPS.
- **Latency:** An action-to-first-frame latency of 1.2 seconds. This metric accounts for the complete generation-and-decoding path.
- **Peak VRAM:** Approximately 19 GiB (specifically, <= 19.3 GiB) across optimized low-bit operating points, with the FP8 configuration using 15.925 GiB.
A system-level breakdown (Table 2) illustrates that a base configuration or one with only an efficient attention kernel (SageAttention2) results in Out-Of-Memory (OOM) errors. The introduction of LightVAE makes the pipeline feasible (9.117 FPS, 20.491 GiB VRAM), and subsequent optimizations like FP8 quantization (DiT time reduced from 1191.1 ms to 845.2 ms/chunk) and Fast-RoPE contribute to improved performance and VRAM efficiency.

### 9. WorldRoamBench Evaluation

On the WorldRoamBench benchmark, which assesses action controllability, visual plausibility, physical consistency, and temporal memory, ABot-World-0 (5B parameters) demonstrates competitive performance against other models:
- **Action Fidelity:** Achieves competitive Strict Accuracy (0.5266), Partial Accuracy (0.7290), and Trajectory Score (0.6752). For instance, its Strict Accuracy is higher than Genie 3 (0.4700), LingBot-World (0.3235), and HY-World 1.5 (0.1640).
- **Visual Quality:** Records Aesthetic (0.5039) and Imaging (0.4651) scores, which are competitive with or surpass some other models.
- **Physical Mechanics:** Achieves a Mechanics score of 0.5223, indicating plausible physical responses.
- **Temporal Memory:** Shows a Memory score of 0.5041.

### 10. Qualitative Analysis and Long-Horizon Coherence

Qualitative stress tests, including hour- and day-scale rollouts, illustrate several capabilities:
- **Sustained Controllability and Scene Coherence:** Over rollouts extending up to 24 hours (day-scale), the model maintains recognizable scene structure, viewpoint consistency, and active motion at sampled checkpoints, continuously responding to input actions without collapsing into texture noise, repetitive content, or static frames (Figures 5, 6, 7).
- **Unified Control Generalization:** The unified keyboard action interface generalizes to out-of-domain (OOD) environments and controllable characters (e.g., diverse human characters, industrial warehouses, snowy temples) not seen during training. Generated motion consistently follows actions while remaining coherent with the surrounding scene (Figure 8).
- **Plausible Physical Interaction:** The model exhibits emergent physical consequences, such as objects being pushed, footsteps creating ripples in water, persistent snow tracks, and character collisions with walls or railings without interpenetration. These effects are learned implicitly from interactive video experience (Figure 9).

### 11. Effect of LongForcing

An ablation study comparing LongForcing with a Causal-Forcing-style baseline over 60-second rollouts (Figure 10) demonstrates its effectiveness:
- **Reduced Visual Error Accumulation:** LongForcing maintains a higher HPSv3 aesthetic score and lower values for artifact-related metrics (high-saturation pixel ratio, perceptual blur score, patch repeat ratio) in the later portions of the rollout. This indicates that extended-horizon teacher supervision during distribution matching helps mitigate progressive visual degradation and accumulated drift during autoregressive generation.

### 12. Significance and Potential Impact

ABot-World-0 presents a significant step towards practical, real-time, and interactive world modeling. Its primary significance lies in demonstrating that a unified approach encompassing data acquisition, training methodology, and inference optimization can overcome the traditional bottlenecks associated with generating dynamic, controllable, and coherent virtual environments on consumer-grade hardware.

**Key areas of significance and potential impact include:**

1. **Enabling Accessible Interactive World Simulation:** By achieving real-time performance (up to 16 FPS at 720P) with low latency (1.2 s) and a reasonable VRAM footprint (under 19.3 GiB) on a single desktop GPU, ABot-World-0 makes advanced interactive world models more accessible to individual users and smaller research labs without requiring large-scale data centers. This democratizes the capability of running complex generative simulations locally.

2. **Advancing Long-Horizon Generative Coherence:** The introduction of LongForcing addresses a critical challenge in autoregressive generation: the accumulation of errors and distribution shift over extended rollouts. By providing distribution-level supervision from an extended-horizon teacher over student self-rollouts, the method contributes to the development of models that can maintain visual quality, identity consistency, and plausible dynamics for prolonged interaction, as evidenced by day-scale qualitative rollouts.

3. **Establishing a Comprehensive Data-to-Deployment Pipeline:** The work emphasizes a "full-stack capability" rather than focusing on isolated generative objectives. The integrated data infrastructure with WorldExplorer's feedback loop ensures a continuous refinement of training data based on model performance. This systematic approach, combined with progressive distillation and an optimized inference stack, serves as a blueprint for developing robust interactive AI systems.

4. **Unified and Intuitive Control:** The use of raw keyboard inputs as a direct, unified control interface for both camera navigation and character interaction simplifies user engagement and aligns naturally with existing human-computer interaction paradigms. This removes the need for complex, learned latent action spaces, making the models more immediately usable. The reference-character memory also directly tackles the problem of identity consistency, which is crucial for character-driven interactive experiences.

**Potential impacts of this research include:**

- **Interactive Content Creation:** Artists, designers, and creators could use such models to rapidly prototype and explore dynamic virtual worlds, significantly reducing the manual effort involved in environment design and animation.
- **Game Simulation and Development:** ABot-World-0 could serve as a foundation for generating dynamic game environments, allowing for more diverse, unpredictable, and continuously evolving gameplay experiences. It could also accelerate game prototyping and level design.
- **Agent Learning and Embodied AI Research:** The ability to generate persistent, controllable environments locally provides a scalable and cost-effective platform for training and evaluating embodied AI agents. Researchers can experiment with complex agent behaviors and learning paradigms in rich, interactive settings.
- **Virtual Prototyping and Digital Twins:** In domains requiring dynamic simulations of physical spaces, such as urban planning or architectural walkthroughs, ABot-World-0 could enable interactive exploration and testing of designs in realistic, AI-generated environments.

In conclusion, ABot-World-0's integration of a sophisticated data pipeline, novel training techniques for long-horizon stability, and a highly optimized inference stack represents a concerted effort to make interactive world models a practical reality, potentially opening new avenues for creative, scientific, and industrial applications.
