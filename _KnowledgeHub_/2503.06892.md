# SafePlan: Leveraging Formal Logic and Chain-of-Thought Reasoning for Enhanced Safety in LLM-based Robotic Task Planning

> [!summary] Summary
> SafePlan introduces a multi-component framework that integrates formal logic and Chain-of-Thought reasoning to enhance the safety of LLM-based robotic task planning. The framework achieved 88.4% accuracy in identifying and rejecting unsafe task prompts and reduced robot crash rates to 0% in simulated execution scenarios, where baseline models experienced 100% crash rates.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) used in robotics often lack inherent reasoning about physical safety, ethical implications, and contextual risks, leading to potentially unsafe or impractical task plans.
- Current safety mechanisms for LLM-based robotics are primarily designed for linguistic contexts, failing to adequately address the unique safety considerations of physical robot interaction and execution.
- LLM-robot systems are vulnerable to accepting dangerous or unethical instructions and adversarial attacks, without robust rejection mechanisms at early stages of task planning.

## Method

- **SafePlan** proposes a multi-component framework integrating formal logic and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning for hierarchical safety verification across the entire planning pipeline.
- It includes a ==Prompt Sanity Check CoT Reasoner== with three progressive layers (Societal, Organizational, Individual Alignment) that use formal logic to proactively filter out unsafe natural language commands.
- An Invariant CoT Reasoner generates and verifies preconditions, postconditions, and invariants for robot tasks, guiding LLMs in generating safer code and validating it before execution.

## Results

- SafePlan achieved an overall accuracy of **88.4%** in prompt safety classification using Gemini **1.5** Pro, rejecting **92.9%** of unsafe tasks compared to baseline models which accepted a high percentage (**61.3**-**85.8%**).
- The framework significantly improved the ethical-to-unethical acceptance ratio, reaching up to **10.0** for Gemini **1.5** Pro, indicating a much higher preference for safe tasks over unsafe ones.
- In AI2-THOR simulations, SafePlan reduced robot crash rates to **0%** for error-prone tasks across different categories, while baseline approaches exhibited crash rates up to **100%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating formal logic with Chain-of-Thought reasoning provides a structured and systematic approach for LLMs to reason about safety in robotic task planning.
> - A hierarchical, multi-layered verification process, starting from initial prompt interpretation through to code execution, is crucial for comprehensive safety assurance in physical robot systems.
> - Proactive rejection of unsafe or malicious commands at the initial prompt stage is highly effective in preventing downstream errors and enhancing overall system trustworthiness.
