# Reinforcement Pre-Training

> [!summary] Summary
> Reinforcement Pre-Training (RPT) introduces a novel paradigm for pre-training large language models by reframing next-token prediction as a reasoning task, utilizing on-policy reinforcement learning with verifiable, rule-based rewards. This approach enhances next-token prediction accuracy and improves zero-shot performance on diverse downstream tasks by fostering deeper reasoning capabilities from the foundational training stage.

## Problem

- Standard self-supervised next-token prediction in LLM pre-training primarily optimizes for statistical likelihood, which may not foster deeper reasoning or robust understanding.
- Current reinforcement learning methods for LLMs (like RLHF and RLVR) are mostly confined to post-training fine-tuning due to their reliance on costly human preference data or limited domain-specific annotations.
- A significant gap exists in applying scalable and general reinforcement learning techniques to the foundational, general-purpose pre-training phase of LLMs using vast amounts of unannotated text.

## Method

- Reinforcement Pre-Training (RPT) reframes the ubiquitous next-token prediction task as a =='next-token reasoning process'==, requiring the model to generate a chain-of-thought before predicting the final token.
- The approach employs an ==on-policy reinforcement learning framework==, where the language model performs multiple 'thinking trajectories' for each context.
- A ==deterministic prefix matching reward== (1 for exact prefix match, 0 otherwise) is used to verify the correctness of the predicted next token, leveraging unannotated text data for verifiable intrinsic rewards.

## Results

- Reinforcement Pre-Training (RPT)-14B achieved higher next-token prediction accuracy across all difficulty levels on OmniMATH, reaching **23.75%** accuracy on 'Hard' tokens, outperforming a 14B baseline and matching a 32B baseline.
- Models pre-trained with RPT provided a stronger foundation for subsequent reinforcement fine-tuning, reaching **58.3%** accuracy after RLVR on Skywork-OR1, compared to **52.7%** for the baseline.
- RPT-14B showed enhanced zero-shot performance on general downstream tasks, with approximately **7 points** gain on SuperGPQA and **22 points** gain on MMLU-Pro over a 32B standard next-token prediction baseline.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reframing pre-training as an active reasoning task, where the model explicitly generates a chain-of-thought to predict the next token, enables LLMs to develop deeper understanding and reasoning capabilities directly.
> - Leveraging objective, rule-based rewards derived from the correctness of next-token predictions on abundant unannotated text provides a scalable and general approach for applying reinforcement learning during pre-training, bypassing data scarcity and reward hacking issues.
> - Incentivizing reasoning during pre-training not only fosters complex abilities and generalizable intelligence but also inherently improves fundamental next-token prediction accuracy, suggesting a deeper understanding enhances core language modeling.
