# SSL4RL: Revisiting Self-supervised Learning as Intrinsic Reward for Visual-Language Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> The SSL4RL framework reinterprets self-supervised learning tasks as intrinsic, verifiable reward signals for reinforcement learning, providing a scalable method for post-training vision-language models. This approach consistently improves visual grounding and reasoning capabilities across diverse multimodal benchmarks and also generalizes effectively to graph learning.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) often over-rely on linguistic priors, underutilizing visual evidence and resorting to 'textual shortcuts' in reasoning.
- Applying Reinforcement Learning (RL) to VLMs is limited by the scarcity of scalable, reliable, and verifiable reward mechanisms, with human preferences or explicit verifiers being costly or unavailable.
- The traditional use of Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) is primarily for pre-training, leaving its potential as a source of intrinsic, verifiable targets for fine-tuning behavioral alignment largely unexplored.

## Method

- Reformulates established ==SSL tasks== (e.g., Rotation, Jigsaw, Contrastive, Patch Position) to provide dense, automatic, and ==intrinsically verifiable reward signals== for RL-based post-training.
- Utilizes ==Grouped Reinforcement Policy Optimization (GRPO)== to fine-tune multimodal models, maximizing the SSL-derived intrinsic rewards.
- Trains models to generate natural language reasoning paths that accurately predict the ground truth labels of the self-supervised tasks, thereby reinforcing visual grounding and reasoning.

## Results

- SSL4RL models achieve substantial performance gains on vision-language reasoning benchmarks, with average improvements of **7.39%** on MMBench and **8.94%** on SEED-Bench for the Qwen2.5-VL-3B model.
- The framework demonstrates enhanced robustness against various image perturbations and consistently improves performance on open-ended image captioning tasks.
- SSL4RL successfully generalizes to graph-structured data, showing improvements in node classification and link prediction tasks, confirming its domain-agnostic applicability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Self-supervised learning tasks can effectively serve as intrinsic, verifiable reward signals for reinforcement learning, addressing the reward scarcity bottleneck in VLM alignment.
> - The effectiveness of SSL tasks as RL rewards adheres to a 'Goldilocks principle,' where task difficulty must be appropriately matched to model capacity for optimal performance.
> - SSL4RL fosters stronger visual grounding by compelling models to derive answers directly from image content, leading to reduced reliance on linguistic priors and more robust reasoning.
