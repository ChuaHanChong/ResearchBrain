# DesignX: Human-Competitive Algorithm Designer for Black-Box Optimization

> [!summary] Summary
> DesignX is an automated deep reinforcement learning framework that jointly generates black-box optimization algorithm workflows and dynamically controls their hyperparameters. It consistently designs optimizers that surpass human-crafted algorithms and other automated methods on diverse synthetic and real-world problems, including protein-docking and UAV path planning.

## Problem

- Manually designing and tuning effective black-box optimization algorithms is a time-consuming and labor-intensive process requiring extensive expert knowledge.
- Human experts often lack complete insight into the intricate properties of a novel black-box optimization problem, making it difficult to tailor an optimal algorithm.
- Existing automated algorithm design methods typically address only one sub-task (either workflow generation or dynamic hyperparameter control), limiting their overall effectiveness.

## Method

- **DesignX** introduces ==Modular-EC==, a comprehensive library of 116 algorithm components with hierarchical structure and topology rules, enabling the generation of millions of legal optimizer workflows.
- It employs a dual-agent Transformer-based reinforcement learning system, where Agent-1 generates the algorithm workflow based on problem features and Agent-2 dynamically controls hyperparameters based on real-time optimization progress.
- The system is trained using a cooperative large-scale meta-reinforcement learning paradigm on 12,800 diverse synthetic problem instances to ensure strong generalizability.

## Results

- DesignX consistently outperformed a wide range of human-crafted optimizers and other up-to-date MetaBBO baselines on 3,**200** unseen synthetic test instances, ranking first on almost all problems.
- It demonstrated superior out-of-distribution generalization across challenging realistic benchmarks, including Protein-Docking (**280** instances), HPO-B (**86** instances), and UAV Path Planning (**56** instances).
- DesignX significantly surpassed state-of-the-art general Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 Turbo in algorithm design, which often generated generic and less effective optimizers.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Generating a correct optimizer workflow appears more critical than dynamically controlling hyperparameters for overall performance, as shown by ablation studies.
> - DesignX learns to prioritize specific module types (e.g., DE mutation strategies, population reduction mechanisms) based on problem characteristics like modality and search range.
> - The choice of initialization strategy has a limited impact on final performance compared to later, more critical algorithmic modules, a non-trivial finding validated by experiments.
