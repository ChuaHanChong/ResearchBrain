# Perceptual Flow Network for Visually Grounded Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> PFlowNet introduces a framework for visually grounded reasoning in Large Vision-Language Models by optimizing a structured latent "Perceptual Flow" to guide reasoning. This method mitigates hallucination and language bias by exploring reasoning-oriented perceptual behaviors, achieving state-of-the-art results with a 10.4% average improvement on TreeBench and 18.4% on MME-RealWorld-Lite compared to the base Qwen3-VL-8B.

## Problem

- Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) frequently suffer from hallucinations and poor interpretability, especially in fine-grained visual reasoning tasks.
- Existing methods that distill geometric priors from visual experts (like GroundingDINO) for LVLMs often lead to a "tunnel vision effect," where overly precise geometric evidence excludes necessary broader contextual information.
- Geometric priors optimized for object detection are not inherently optimal for complex visual reasoning, creating a mismatch between expert utility and reasoning needs.

## Method

- **PFlowNet** defines "==Perceptual Flow==" as a structured latent trajectory of visual thoughts, comprising a planning state (query decomposition) and perceptual states (grounded observations with RoIs and captions), decoupling perception from reasoning.
- The framework employs a progressive training paradigm, starting with data curation and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) to teach the model to generate beneficial perceptual flows.
- A ==Variational Reinforcement Fine-Tuning== (RFT) strategy with a multi-dimensional reward function (balancing quality and reasoning efficacy) and a ==vicinal geometric shaping== scheme is used to foster reasoning-oriented perceptual behaviors while maintaining visual reliability.

## Results

- PFlowNet achieved an overall **10.4%** improvement on TreeBench and **18.4%** on MME-RealWorld-Lite compared to the Qwen3-VL-8B base model, setting new state-of-the-art records on 17 out of 19 sub-tasks across general-purpose benchmarks.
- The framework delivered state-of-the-art results on fine-grained benchmarks, including a **13%** gain on V* Bench, 8-**8.8%** on HR-Bench, and 2.5-**7%** on ScreenSpot over the Qwen3-VL series, matching the performance of the larger Qwen3-VL-32B model with the 8B variant.
- PFlowNet demonstrates robust test-time scaling capabilities and an improved performance-efficiency trade-off, achieving comparable or superior performance to agentic frameworks with significantly shorter context lengths and reduced inference latency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Strict geometric precision, while beneficial for localization, can induce a "tunnel vision effect" that hinders comprehensive visual reasoning by limiting contextual understanding.
> - Decoupling optimizable perceptual behaviors from the reasoning process allows for more effective visually grounded reasoning through self-conditioned autoregressive generation.
> - A multi-dimensional reward function combined with vicinal geometric shaping enables LVLMs to explore a broader range of effective perceptual behaviors that enhance reasoning utility beyond just geometric precision, preserving visual reliability without rigid alignment.
