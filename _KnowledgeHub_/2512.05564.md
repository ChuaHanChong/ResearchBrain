# ProPhy: Progressive Physical Alignment for Dynamic World Simulation

> [!summary] Summary
> The ProPhy framework enhances latent video diffusion models by integrating a progressive physical alignment strategy, leveraging a two-stage Mixture-of-Physics-Experts and VLM-guided supervision. This approach enables the generation of physically consistent videos, improving the Joint metric on the VideoPhy2 benchmark by 19.7% with the CogVideoX backbone.

## Problem

- Current video generation models often synthesize visually realistic but physically inconsistent content, violating fundamental physical laws.
- Existing methods struggle to provide fine-grained physical guidance, leading to isotropic responses to prompts and insufficient capture of localized physical interactions.
- Implicit physical prior learning or global-level explicit guidance often fails to enforce physical plausibility in complex, dynamic scenarios during inference.

## Method

- A Progressive Physical Alignment (**ProPhy**) framework modifies latent video diffusion models by adding a "Physical Branch" that complements the ==denoising== backbone.
- This branch incorporates a two-stage Mixture-of-Physics-Experts (**MoPE**): a Semantic Expert Block (SEB) for global physical principles and a Refinement Expert Block (REB) for token-level physical dynamics.
- The framework utilizes specific loss functions, including VLM-guided fine-grained alignment and load-balancing, to progressively inject and refine physics-aware priors.

## Results

- ProPhy, when applied to the CogVideoX backbone, achieved a 19.7% improvement in the Joint metric (from 22.3 to 26.7) on the VideoPhy2 benchmark, demonstrating enhanced physical plausibility and semantic consistency.
- Qualitative evaluations showed ProPhy generated videos with fewer physical inconsistencies in complex scenarios, such as accurate dust emissions during discus throws and correct momentum conservation in object collisions.
- Ablation studies confirmed the necessity of both the Semantic Expert Block and Refinement Expert Block, along with the effectiveness of the proposed loss functions, in achieving superior physical consistency and overall video quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A hierarchical MoPE structure, with global semantic and token-level refinement, effectively integrates explicit and anisotropic physical guidance into generative models.
> - Vision-Language Models can effectively provide fine-grained, spatially localized supervision signals for token-level physical alignment within video generation.
> - Explicitly conditioning generative models on specialized physical experts can lead to more discriminative representations and fine-grained control over specific physical attributes in generated videos.
