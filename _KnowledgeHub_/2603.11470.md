# NFPO: Stabilized Policy Optimization of Normalizing Flow for Robotic Policy Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Zhejiang University and Westlake University developed NFPO, a method that integrates Normalizing Flows into the on-policy PPO framework for robotic policy learning. NFPO achieves stable multi-modal policy learning by normalizing internal scale network outputs, demonstrates competitive or superior performance against Gaussian PPO in high-dimensional tasks, and successfully transfers to real-world humanoid robots for stable control.

## Problem

- Deep Reinforcement Learning policies for robotic control predominantly use restricted multivariate Gaussian distributions, which limit their ability to model complex, multi-modal action distributions.
- Integrating modern multi-modal generative networks into on-policy Policy Optimization frameworks for learning control policies from scratch presents significant training and numerical instability challenges.
- Existing multi-modal DRL approaches often focus on off-policy methods, whereas on-policy methods are often preferred in robotics for their computational efficiency and robustness in simulation-rich environments.

## Method

- **NFPO** replaces the standard ==multivariate Gaussian policy parameterization== in **Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)** with ==Normalizing Flows (NFs)==, specifically using the ==RealNVP== architecture.
- Training instability, common with naive NF integration, is addressed by ==normalizing the scale-network output== `s(x)` in RealNVP coupling layers using a ==scaled hyperbolic tangent== (`l · tanh(s_θ(x))`) to bound values.
- Ablation studies determined that the final NFPO configuration ==omits the entropy loss term== and ==does not add action noise== during training, as these were found to be unhelpful or detrimental to performance and stability.

## Results

- NFPO achieved competitive or superior performance compared to state-of-the-art Gaussian PPO baselines across various high-dimensional locomotion and manipulation tasks, particularly in challenging environments like `G1JoyStickRoughTerrain`.
- The method successfully demonstrated multi-modal behaviors in sparse-reward environments (e.g., Gridworld, UR10 reaching) by generating diverse action distributions and distinct trajectories to targets, unlike unimodal PPO policies.
- Policies trained with NFPO were successfully transferred to a real-world Unitree G1 humanoid robot, enabling stable and agile **29-DoF** whole-body control for joystick-based locomotion and motion tracking.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Naive integration of Normalizing Flows into online Reinforcement Learning causes significant training instability due to issues like overfitting to high-advantage samples, exponentially growing internal values, and unbounded network outputs.
> - Constraining the output of the scale network within RealNVP via normalization (e.g., `tanh` or `clip`) is crucial for achieving stable and robust Normalizing Flow-based policy optimization.
> - Unlike standard PPO, an explicit entropy regularization term and adding action noise during training were found to be unnecessary or even detrimental to NFPO's performance and stability across various robotic tasks.
