# Safe Continual Domain Adaptation after Sim2Real Transfer of Reinforcement Learning Policies in Robotics

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework, Safe Continual Domain Adaptation (SCDA), enables reinforcement learning policies to safely and continuously adapt to evolving real-world conditions after sim2real transfer. It achieves this by integrating safe reinforcement learning and continual learning, demonstrating improved performance and safety on robotic manipulation tasks.

## Problem

- Reinforcement learning policies trained in simulation often struggle with the "reality gap," leading to suboptimal or unsafe performance when deployed on physical robots.
- Existing sim2real approaches typically result in fixed policies that cannot adapt to the inevitable changes in real-world system parameters or environmental conditions over time.
- Direct online adaptation of RL policies in real-world robotics is challenging due to sample inefficiency, instability, and high safety risks associated with exploratory actions.

## Method

- The Safe Continual Domain Adaptation (SCDA) framework combines domain randomization for initial policy robustness, Policy Constrained Reward and Cost Policy Optimization (PCRPO) for safety, and Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) for continual adaptation.
- A pretraining phase in randomized simulation uses PCRPO to learn a general, safe policy, from which the Fisher Information Matrix (FIM) is computed to identify important parameters.
- During real-world adaptation, the pre-trained policy is fine-tuned using PCRPO with an EWC-based regularization term, penalizing changes to important parameters, and operates under stricter safety limits.

## Results

- SCDA consistently minimized safety violations during real-world adaptation, often achieving zero costs, unlike reward-only adaptation strategies that led to unsafe behaviors.
- The framework was the only strategy that improved overall performance across an entire domain of multiple targets, effectively preventing catastrophic forgetting of the general policy.
- On a real robotic grasping task, SCDA increased the grasp success rate from 20% (zero-shot transfer) to 60% while maintaining zero safety violations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating safe reinforcement learning (PCRPO) with continual learning (EWC) enables policies to adapt robustly to dynamic real-world conditions while preventing catastrophic forgetting of previously acquired generalized knowledge.
> - Applying stricter safety limits during real-world adaptation is crucial for ensuring the safe deployment of reinforcement learning policies on physical robotic systems.
> - Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) effectively leverages Fisher information to selectively preserve critical policy parameters from pretraining, balancing specialized adaptation with maintaining generalized capabilities.
