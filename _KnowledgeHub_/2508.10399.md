# Large Model Empowered Embodied AI: A Survey on Decision-Making and Embodied Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> An extensive survey consolidates recent advancements in using large models to enhance embodied artificial intelligence, specifically detailing their roles in hierarchical and end-to-end decision-making, as well as various embodied learning paradigms. The work outlines the current state, identifies challenges such as data scarcity and the sim-to-real gap, and proposes future research avenues.

## Problem

- Embodied AI systems face challenges in achieving human-level intelligence and generalizing to diverse, open-ended real-world scenarios.
- The rapidly evolving intersection of large models and embodied AI lacks a systematic, comprehensive review focusing on how large models empower decision-making and learning.
- Fragmented research efforts and outdated existing surveys make it difficult for researchers to navigate advancements in this interdisciplinary field.

## Method

- Conducts a comprehensive and structured literature review on how large models empower embodied AI, specifically in decision-making and embodied learning.
- Employs a dual analytical approach, horizontally comparing diverse methods and vertically tracing the evolution of core models.
- Integrates a novel discussion of world models, detailing their design and crucial functions within embodied decision-making and learning.

## Results

- Large models demonstrably enhance planning through structured, natural, and programming language outputs, and improve execution via dynamic invocation of pretrained models and closed-loop feedback.
- VLA models achieve improved precision and adaptability in end-to-end control, with enhancements in perception, trajectory action optimization using diffusion models, and reduced training costs through efficient architectures.
- The field still faces critical challenges such as embodied data scarcity, the need for robust continual learning strategies, high computational demands, and effective bridging of the sim-to-real gap.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large models fundamentally reshape embodied AI by providing advanced cognitive and generative capabilities that address long-standing challenges in decision-making and learning.
> - Hierarchical decision-making leverages LLMs for high-level planning and diverse models for low-level execution, while end-to-end VLA models offer streamlined perception-action loops.
> - Large models significantly empower embodied learning through improved policy network construction (e.g., diffusion, Transformers) and automated reward function design, with world models playing a crucial role in simulation and data generation.
