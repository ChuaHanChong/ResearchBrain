# SpatialBoost: Enhancing Visual Representation through Language-Guided Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework developed at KAIST, RLWRLD, and NAVER Cloud enhances existing vision encoders with 3D spatial understanding by leveraging linguistic descriptions generated from visual data through a Large Language Model and a dual-channel attention mechanism. The approach improved DINOv3's semantic segmentation mIoU on ADE20K by 3.8% and its average robot task score on CortexBench by 8.0 points.

## Problem

- Vision encoders, primarily trained on 2D data, often lack comprehensive understanding of 3D spatial relationships between objects and environments.
- This deficiency limits their effectiveness in applications requiring 3D spatial awareness, such as 3D scene understanding, monocular depth prediction, and robotic control.
- Existing methods for 3D spatial learning, like multi-view data collection or full multimodal model re-training, are often resource-intensive and lack scalability for generalizable pre-training.

## Method

- A three-stage training framework (==Feature Alignment==, ==Visual Instruction Tuning==, ==Vision Encoder Fine-tuning==) adapts existing pre-trained vision encoders.
- Dense 3D spatial information from 2D images is converted into structured ==linguistic expressions== using specialized vision models and a ==Large Language Model== (LLM).
- A ==dual-channel attention mechanism== is integrated during vision encoder fine-tuning to acquire new spatial knowledge while preserving pre-trained general visual capabilities.

## Results

- Improved DINOv3's semantic segmentation mIoU on ADE20K by **3.8%** (from **55.9%** to **59.7%** with linear probing) and its monocular depth estimation RMSE on NYUd from **0.31** to **0.25**.
- Enhanced vision-based robot learning, with DINOv3's average score on CortexBench increasing by **8.0** points (from **72.8** to **80.8**).
- Achieved significant gains in complex 3D-centric tasks, including a **3.5%**p improvement in DINOv3's SQA3D BLEU-1 score and an increase in Registration Recall at **0.05**m from **86.9%** to **97.5%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Language can effectively convey dense and hierarchical 3D spatial knowledge, serving as a scalable supervision signal for enhancing vision encoders.
> - A multi-turn Chain-of-Thought reasoning process, ranging from pixel-level to object-level and scene-level spatial queries, facilitates comprehensive 3D understanding.
> - The dual-channel attention mechanism provides an efficient method to inject new knowledge into pre-trained models without catastrophic forgetting of existing representations.
