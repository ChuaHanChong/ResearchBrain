# ReAG: Reasoning-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-based Visual Question Answering

> [!summary] Summary
> ReAG, a multimodal framework, improves Knowledge-based Visual Question Answering by combining a multi-level retrieval pipeline, a critic model to filter noisy passages, and a generator trained with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to produce explicit reasoning traces. It demonstrates accuracy gains of up to 7.8 points on E-VQA and 5.7 points on InfoSeek, while reducing the average number of passages fed to the generator by up to 18%.

## Problem

- Existing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) methods in multimodal contexts often retrieve noisy or irrelevant passages, leading to low precision in Knowledge-based Visual Question Answering (KB-VQA).
- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle to effectively understand, synthesize, and explicitly reason over retrieved external information for knowledge-intensive queries.
- Current KB-VQA approaches often lack transparent or robust reasoning traces, limiting the interpretability of MLLM predictions.

## Method

- A ==multi-level retrieval== pipeline combines coarse-grained and fine-grained approaches (using GroundingDINO and EVA-CLIP) to enhance recall for relevant knowledge base passages.
- A critic model (fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-3B) filters irrelevant and noisy passages from the retrieved set, providing a cleaner and more precise context to the generator.
- A multi-stage generator training strategy begins with Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) for initial reasoning capabilities, followed by Reinforcement Learning (RL) using a modified GRPO framework with rule-based rewards to refine reasoning and answer generation.

## Results

- ReAG achieved state-of-the-art results on E-VQA and InfoSeek benchmarks, outperforming previous methods by up to +7.8 points on E-VQA (Qwen2.5-VL-**7B**) and +5.7 points on InfoSeek (Qwen2.5-VL-**7B**).
- The critic model effectively reduced the average number of passages fed to the generator by **15.9%** to **18.0%** compared to baselines, while maintaining high recall and specificity of relevant passages.
- Ablation studies confirmed that each component of ReAG (critic, fine-grained retrieval, SFT, and RL training) contributed positively to the overall performance, validating the integrated approach.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly filtering noisy retrieved information with a critic model is crucial for improving MLLM performance in KB-VQA, significantly reducing irrelevant context fed to the model.
> - A multi-stage training approach, combining SFT for initial instruction following and RL for refining reasoning capabilities, leads to more robust and accurate knowledge-based generation.
> - Generating explicit natural-language reasoning traces before the final answer enhances both the accuracy and interpretability of MLLM predictions in complex, knowledge-intensive visual question answering tasks.
