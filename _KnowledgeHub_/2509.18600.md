# OraPO: Oracle-educated Reinforcement Learning for Data-efficient and Factual Radiology Report Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> OraPO, an Oracle-educated Reinforcement Learning framework, combined with a FactScore-based reward, enables data-efficient and factual radiology report generation from chest X-rays. Using only 1,000 training samples, the framework achieved state-of-the-art recall (0.832 on CheXpert Plus) and F1 scores, outperforming methods trained on orders of magnitude more data with smaller models.

## Problem

- Mainstream radiology report generation methods are data and compute intensive, relying on vast datasets (hundreds of thousands to millions) and large Vision-Language Models (VLMs), limiting their accessibility and practical scalability.
- The direct application of Group Relative Policy Optimisation (GRPO) to radiology report generation (RRG) suffers from 'exploration failure,' where initial VLM outputs yield uniformly low or zero rewards, leading to vanishing gradients and inefficient training.
- Designing verifiable scalar rewards for long-form, multi-fact radiology reports is challenging, as traditional metrics often fail to penalize sentence-level factual errors or contradictions, potentially yielding fluent but clinically misleading reports.

## Method

- **OraPO** integrates Direct Preference Optimisation (DPO) as an '==oracle step==' to provide preference supervision when GRPO exploration yields all-zero rewards, dynamically mixing DPO and GRPO objectives based on a Zero-Reward Rate.
- A FactScore-based (FactS) reward function assesses factual correctness by extracting atomic clinical facts from generated reports using an external LLM (GPT-4.1) and testing their entailment against ground-truth diagnostic labels.
- The framework operates in a single-stage, RL-only training setting, fine-tuning a small 3B parameter VLM on a highly limited dataset (e.g., 1,000 samples) to achieve data and compute efficiency.

## Results

- OraPO+FactS established new state-of-the-art performance on the CheXpert Plus dataset, achieving an F1 score of **0.341** and a Recall of **0.832** using only 1,000 training samples, outperforming baselines trained on 2-3 orders of magnitude more data.
- The framework demonstrated superior recall and F1 scores compared to commercial APIs like GPT-4.1 and GPT-5 Thinking on human gold label sets, while also offering real-time inference (3.3 seconds/image vs. 25.2 seconds/image for GPT-5 Thinking).
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical role of the FactS reward (+**227%** F1, +**274%** Recall over naive reward) and OraPO's contribution (+**17.2%** F1 over FactS+GRPO), demonstrating their individual impact on data efficiency and overall performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Dynamically switching between GRPO and DPO based on exploration success effectively salvages failed explorations and stabilizes reinforcement learning in domain-specific tasks where the initial policy lacks specialized knowledge.
> - Supervising factual correctness at an atomic-fact level, rather than relying on superficial text similarity metrics, is crucial for generating clinically reliable long-form reports and significantly boosts performance in medical contexts.
> - High-performing and clinically faithful radiology report generation is achievable with significantly reduced data and computational resources by combining efficient reinforcement learning with precise, fact-centric supervision.
