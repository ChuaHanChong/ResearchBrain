# CoTracker3: Simpler and Better Point Tracking by Pseudo-Labelling Real Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> CoTracker3 is a simplified point-tracking transformer that removes global matching and uses lean 4D correlation plus iterative track/confidence/visibility updates, paired with a semi-supervised pipeline where multiple off-the-shelf trackers pseudo-label real videos to train a student. It beats BootsTAPIR on TAP-Vid using 1,000x less real training data.

## Problem

- Point trackers rely on synthetic Kubric data because annotating real videos with pixel-accurate tracks is impractical, creating a persistent synthetic-to-real distribution gap.
- Prior real-data scaling approaches like BootsTAPIR need millions of unlabelled videos plus complex self-training with augmentations, loss masks, and EMA, making the recipe costly and opaque.
- Competing tracker architectures (PIPs, TAPIR, CoTracker, LocoTrack) each add distinct components without clarity on which are essential, leaving design bloat unexamined.

## Method

- **CoTracker3** architecture strips ==global matching== and replaces LocoTrack's ad-hoc correlation module with a simple ==MLP== over ==4D correlation== features, updated iteratively by a transformer with ==cross-track attention==.
- A ==multi-teacher pseudo-labelling== pipeline randomly samples among **CoTracker3**, CoTracker, and TAPIR (all Kubric-trained) each batch to label around 100,000 unlabelled Internet videos for student fine-tuning.
- ==SIFT-based query point sampling== selects trackable keypoints for pseudo-label generation, and a frozen separate confidence/visibility head prevents ==catastrophic forgetting== during real-video fine-tuning.

## Results

- Fine-tuned on only **15k** real videos, CoTracker3 online outperforms BootsTAPIR (trained on **15 million** videos) across TAP-Vid Kinetics, DAVIS, RGB-Stacking, and most RoboTAP metrics.
- On Dynamic Replica occlusion tracking, cross-track attention lifts occluded-point accuracy from **35.9** to **41.0**, and CoTracker3 is **27%** faster than LocoTrack with half CoTracker's parameters.
- Ablations show freezing the confidence/visibility head raises OA from **86.3** to **90.2**, self-training alone adds **1.2** average points, and removing any of four teachers degrades student accuracy.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A diverse ensemble of even weaker teacher models, randomly sampled per batch, yields a student that surpasses every individual teacher, an ensembling and distribution-shift-mitigation effect.
> - Architectural simplification (dropping global matching, using plain MLPs) can match or beat more complex designs, suggesting many prior components were not strictly necessary.
> - Real-video fine-tuning data scaling saturates quickly (around 30k videos) once the student surpasses its teachers, implying data quantity matters less than teacher diversity and quality.

## Detailed Report

### 1. Introduction

Point tracking - the task of consistently following arbitrary points across video frames - is a fundamental computer vision problem with applications ranging from 3D reconstruction to video editing and robotics. Despite significant advances in deep learning-based trackers, two key challenges persist: the increasing complexity of state-of-the-art architectures and the "sim-to-real gap" where models trained on synthetic data struggle in real-world scenarios.

CoTracker3, developed by researchers from Meta AI and Oxford's Visual Geometry Group, addresses both challenges simultaneously. The paper introduces a simplified architecture that removes components previously considered essential while achieving state-of-the-art performance using a novel pseudo-labelling approach that requires dramatically less real-world training data.

### 2. Background and Motivation

The evolution of point tracking has seen a progression from classical methods to modern transformer-based approaches. Recent state-of-the-art models like TAPIR and BootsTAPIR have achieved impressive results but at the cost of architectural complexity and massive data requirements. BootsTAPIR, for instance, required training on 15 million unlabelled real videos to achieve its performance.

The core challenge lies in the "sim-to-real gap." While synthetic datasets like Kubric provide perfect ground-truth annotations at scale, models trained solely on synthetic data often underperform in real-world scenarios due to domain mismatch. Manual annotation of point tracks in real videos is extremely expensive and time-consuming, creating a bottleneck for improving real-world performance.

CoTracker3 is motivated by three key observations:
1. Current architectures may be unnecessarily complex
2. Massive amounts of real-world data might not be essential if used more intelligently
3. Multiple imperfect teacher models can collectively provide better supervision than any single model

### 3. Methodology

#### 3.1. Model Architecture

CoTracker3 employs an iterative transformer-based architecture that processes video frames through several key components:

**Feature Extraction**: A convolutional neural network extracts multi-scale feature maps from video frames, downsampling by a factor of 4 for efficiency.

**4D Correlation Computation**: The model computes correlations between feature vectors around query points and estimated track positions across frames. Unlike previous approaches, CoTracker3 uses a simplified MLP to process these correlations.

**Iterative Refinement**: The transformer core iteratively updates three quantities:
- **P**: Track coordinates
- **C**: Confidence scores
- **V**: Visibility flags

The update process can be expressed as:
```
P^(m+1) = P^(m) + ΔP^(m+1)
C^(m+1) = C^(m) + ΔC^(m+1)
V^(m+1) = V^(m) + ΔV^(m+1)
```

**Key Simplifications**: CoTracker3 eliminates the global matching module present in TAPIR, BootsTAPIR, and LocoTrack, demonstrating that this component is redundant for achieving state-of-the-art performance.

#### 3.2. Pseudo-Labelling Training Protocol

The core innovation lies in CoTracker3's training approach:

**Multi-Teacher Approach**: Multiple existing trackers serve as "teachers," each contributing different perspectives and compensating for individual weaknesses. Teachers include CoTracker3 variants, CoTracker, and TAPIR.

**Query Point Sampling**: SIFT features are used to identify "good to track" points in randomly selected keyframes, filtering out ambiguous cases that could destabilize training.

**Training Strategy**:
1. **Synthetic Pre-training**: Initial training on Kubric dataset using Huber loss for coordinates and BCE for confidence/visibility
2. **Pseudo-label Fine-tuning**: Training on unlabelled real videos using only coordinate supervision while freezing confidence/visibility heads to prevent catastrophic forgetting

### 4. Key Results

#### 4.1. Performance Benchmarks

CoTracker3 achieves state-of-the-art results across multiple benchmarks:

- **TAP-Vid**: Superior performance on Kinetics, DAVIS, and RGB-Stacking datasets
- **Dynamic Replica**: Best performance for tracking occluded points
- **RoboTAP**: Competitive results on robotic manipulation scenarios

#### 4.2. Data Scaling Analysis

The most striking finding is CoTracker3's data efficiency. When trained with just 15,000 unlabelled real videos, it outperforms BootsTAPIR trained on 15 million videos - a **1,000x reduction** in data requirements.

Performance scaling experiments reveal:
- Consistent improvement with more real data up to ~30,000 videos
- Performance saturation beyond this point for most models
- Even 100 videos provide meaningful improvements over synthetic-only training

### 5. Architectural Innovations

#### 5.1. Simplification Benefits

CoTracker3's architectural simplifications yield multiple advantages:

**Efficiency Gains**:
- 2x fewer parameters than CoTracker
- 27% faster than LocoTrack (the previous fastest tracker)
- Linear time complexity with number of tracked points

**Key Architectural Decisions**:
- Removal of global matching module (contrary to recent trends)
- Simplified MLP for 4D correlation processing
- Direct confidence estimation for all tracked points
- Iterative visibility updates integrated into main pipeline

#### 5.2. Cross-Track Attention Importance

Ablation studies confirm that cross-track attention is crucial for handling occlusions (+5.1 improvement on occluded points), enabling the model to infer positions of hidden points from visible ones.

### 6. Data Efficiency Breakthrough

#### 6.1. Multi-Teacher Pseudo-Labelling

The pseudo-labelling approach leverages several key insights:

**Teacher Diversity**: Using multiple imperfect teachers provides better supervision than any single model. Even removing weaker teachers degrades performance, indicating valuable complementary knowledge.

**Frozen Components**: Keeping confidence and visibility prediction heads frozen during pseudo-labelling prevents catastrophic forgetting of these difficult-to-supervise quantities.

**SIFT Sampling**: Using SIFT for query point selection provides natural filtering for trackable features while remaining robust across different sampling strategies.

#### 6.2. Scaling Behavior Analysis

Systematic experiments reveal that:
- Performance improvements plateau around 30,000 real videos
- Self-training (using model's own predictions) still provides +1.2 points improvement
- Iterative scaling shows diminishing returns, suggesting effective knowledge distillation

### 7. Significance and Impact

#### 7.1. Paradigm Shift in Architecture Design

CoTracker3 challenges the trend toward increasingly complex models in computer vision. By demonstrating that simpler architectures can outperform more elaborate designs, it encourages researchers to prioritize essential components and seek more elegant solutions.

#### 7.2. Data Efficiency Revolution

The 1,000x reduction in real-world data requirements fundamentally changes the economics of training robust point trackers. This approach makes high-performance tracking accessible to researchers and practitioners without access to massive datasets.

#### 7.3. Broader Applications

Improved point tracking directly benefits numerous downstream applications:
- **3D Reconstruction**: More accurate structure-from-motion and SLAM systems
- **Video Editing**: Enhanced object manipulation and effect insertion
- **Robotics**: Better visual servoing and manipulation capabilities
- **Content Creation**: More sophisticated video analysis and generation tools

#### 7.4. Future Research Directions

CoTracker3 opens several promising research avenues:
- Optimal teacher selection strategies for pseudo-labelling
- Extension of simplification principles to other computer vision tasks
- Development of better unsupervised metrics for real-world training
- Investigation of continual learning in pseudo-labelling frameworks

The work demonstrates that thoughtful architectural design combined with intelligent data utilization can achieve superior results while reducing complexity and data requirements - a valuable lesson for the broader computer vision community.
