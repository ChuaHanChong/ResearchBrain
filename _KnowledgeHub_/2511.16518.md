# MiMo-Embodied: X-Embodied Foundation Model Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Xiaomi's MiMo-Embodied introduces the first open-source cross-embodied foundation model, unifying autonomous driving and embodied AI tasks. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across 29 benchmarks in both domains, demonstrating positive knowledge transfer between diverse physical environments.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for embodied AI and autonomous driving are highly specialized and fragmented, leading to a significant 'domain gap' between indoor robotics and outdoor driving tasks.
- This fragmentation prevents cross-domain generalization, limiting a single model's ability to understand and interact across diverse physical environments.
- A comprehensive evaluation framework was lacking to assess the overall performance and generalizability of embodied VLMs across both autonomous driving and embodied AI tasks.

## Method

- Developed **MiMo-Embodied**, a unified ==VLM architecture== comprising a ==Vision Transformer==, ==MLP Projector==, and Large Language Model, building on the **MiMo-VL** foundation.
- Constructed a diverse multimodal dataset spanning general visual-language understanding, embodied AI tasks (==affordance==, planning, spatial), and autonomous driving scenarios (perception, prediction, planning).
- Implemented a ==progressive four-stage training strategy==, including ==supervised fine-tuning== for embodied AI and autonomous driving, ==Chain-of-Thought reasoning==, and ==Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning==, to mitigate interference and foster synergistic learning.

## Results

- Achieved state-of-the-art performance across **17** embodied AI benchmarks, demonstrating strong capabilities in affordance prediction, task planning, and spatial understanding.
- Set new performance records across **12** autonomous driving benchmarks, excelling in environmental perception, status prediction, and driving planning tasks.
- Qualitative evaluations showed superior performance in real-world embodied navigation, manipulation, and autonomous driving trajectory planning compared to other advanced VLMs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Autonomous driving and embodied AI tasks exhibit strong positive transfer and mutually reinforce one another when integrated within a unified model using a progressive training strategy.
> - A multi-stage training approach, incorporating specialized fine-tuning, Chain-of-Thought reasoning, and Reinforcement Learning, is crucial for mitigating cross-domain interference and achieving synergistic performance improvements.
> - The specialized cross-embodiment training not only improves task-specific performance but also enhances general visual understanding capabilities without compromise.
