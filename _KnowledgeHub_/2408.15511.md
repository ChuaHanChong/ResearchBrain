# AeroVerse: UAV-Agent Benchmark Suite for Simulating, Pre-training, Finetuning, and Evaluating Aerospace Embodied World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> AeroVerse, a comprehensive benchmark suite, is introduced to advance UAV-agent research by providing tools for simulating, pre-training, fine-tuning, and evaluating aerospace embodied world models. The developed SkyAgentX model, integrating specialized architectures and training, achieved an average 8.52% performance improvement over baseline visual-language models across four core aerospace embodied tasks.

## Problem

- Absence of a standardized benchmark suite, including specific tasks and datasets, for developing autonomous UAV intelligence in complex outdoor 3D urban environments.
- Existing embodied world models primarily focus on ground-level agents, limiting their direct applicability and performance for UAVs in aerospace-specific tasks.
- Significant challenges and high costs associated with acquiring and annotating large-scale 3D data from UAVs in real-world, dynamic outdoor settings.

## Method

- Developed **AeroVerse**, a comprehensive benchmark suite featuring ==AeroSimulator== for high-fidelity UAV flight simulation in realistic urban scenes.
- Constructed extensive datasets, including ==AerialAgent-Ego15k== (real-world) and ==CyberAgent-Ego500k== (virtual image-text-pose), along with five expert-annotated downstream task datasets.
- Introduced **SkyAgentX**, a UAV-agent large model that integrates aerospace embodied ==Chain-of-Thought reasoning== and ==Multitask Curriculum Learning== for perception, reasoning, navigation, and planning.

## Results

- SkyAgentX consistently outperformed existing 2D and 3D visual-language models, achieving an average **8.52%** improvement across scene awareness, spatial reasoning, navigational exploration, and task planning.
- Baseline models, including the GPT-4 series, showed marked performance degradation on more complex aerospace tasks and frequently exhibited hallucinations, especially 3D-LLM when generalizing from indoor to outdoor environments.
- Qualitative analysis and human evaluations confirmed SkyAgent and SkyAgentX produced more accurate, detailed, and coherent outputs for complex tasks like spatial reasoning and planning, closely aligning with ground truth compared to baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Existing 2D and 3D visual-language models show significant limitations and often hallucinate when applied to complex aerospace embodied tasks, highlighting the need for domain-specific solutions.
> - Domain-specific data, specialized architectures like Chain-of-Thought, and progressive training strategies (multitask curriculum learning) are crucial for developing robust and adaptable UAV autonomy.
> - Automated, GPT-4-based evaluation methods can effectively align with human preferences for complex, open-ended tasks in embodied intelligence, providing flexible and objective assessment.
