# Mastering Memory Tasks with World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Mila introduce Recall to Imagine (R2I), a model-based reinforcement learning agent that integrates State Space Models (SSMs) into its world model. R2I establishes new state-of-the-art performance in memory-intensive RL tasks like Memory Maze and POPGym, achieving superhuman scores in Memory Maze, while also demonstrating up to 9 times faster training compared to DreamerV3.

## Problem

- Existing Model-Based Reinforcement Learning (MBRL) agents, like Dreamer, rely on RNNs or Transformers whose limitations (vanishing gradients, quadratic complexity) hinder long-term memory and credit assignment.
- Current MBRL agents struggle with tasks requiring recall of distant observations or evaluating actions based on long-horizon future rewards.
- Achieving superhuman performance in complex, partially observable 3D memory environments, such as Memory Maze, has been a significant challenge for existing RL algorithms.

## Method

- Integrates State Space Models (SSMs), specifically a variant of S4, into the DreamerV3 ==world model== to create the Structured State-Space Model (S3M).
- Modifies DreamerV3's representation model to a non-recurrent form (q_θ(z_t | o_t)) to enable parallel computation of posterior samples, essential for SSM efficiency.
- Utilizes parallel scan for SSM computation and adapts policy inputs to optionally leverage the internal SSM hidden states (x_t) in addition to (z_t, h_t) for memory-intensive tasks.

## Results

- Achieves up to 9 times faster training time compared to DreamerV3 in vector environments and up to 6 times faster in image environments.
- Establishes new state-of-the-art performance in memory-intensive tabular benchmarks (BSuite, POPGym), solving tasks like Autoencode and RepeatPrevious at 'Medium' and 'Hard' difficulties where prior methods struggled.
- Attains superhuman performance in the 9x9, 11x11, and 13x13 Memory Maze environments, demonstrating robust long-term information retention over thousands of environment steps.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - State Space Models (SSMs) are effective backbones for world models in reinforcement learning, efficiently capturing very long-range dependencies.
> - The internal hidden states (x_t) of SSMs contain critical long-term information distinct from the output (h_t) and are crucial for policy learning in memory-intensive tasks.
> - Including entire episodes within the training batch sequence is vital for leveraging SSMs' capability to model very long sequences and handle memory effectively.
