# GenBreak: Red Teaming Text-to-Image Generators Using Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> GenBreak fine-tunes a Large Language Model to systematically generate adversarial prompts for text-to-image models, effectively inducing highly toxic images across categories like nudity and violence while consistently evading safety filters. This automated framework demonstrates its capabilities on both open-source models and commercial APIs, revealing safety weaknesses in deployed systems.

## Problem

- Text-to-image (T2I) models can be misused to generate harmful content, such as nudity, violence, and hate speech, despite integrated safety filters.
- Existing red teaming methods for T2I models either produce highly toxic images using easily detectable prompts, or bypass filters without consistently generating genuinely harmful outputs.
- There is a lack of reliable tools that can simultaneously achieve high image toxicity, effective content filter evasion, and prompt diversity for comprehensive T2I safety evaluation.

## Method

- **GenBreak** proposes a two-stage framework that fine-tunes a ==red-team== Large Language Model (LLM) to generate adversarial prompts for T2I models.
- The first stage involves Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) of the LLM on curated datasets containing adversarial rewrites for harmful categories and pre-attack examples.
- The second stage uses Reinforcement Learning (RL) with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) and a multi-objective reward function that balances image toxicity, filter evasion, prompt stealthiness, and lexical/semantic/image diversity.

## Results

- GenBreak achieved Toxic Bypass Rates (TBRs) of **60.8%** for nudity, **89.7%** for violence, and **84.6%** for hate content on Stable Diffusion 2.1, significantly outperforming baseline methods.
- The framework demonstrated strong transferability to commercial T2I APIs, achieving single-attempt nudity TBRs of **70%** on Leonardo.Ai, **30%** on fal.ai, and **47%** on stability.ai.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the integrated multi-objective reward system, including bypass, clean, and diverse prompt/image rewards, is essential for GenBreak's effectiveness in generating stealthy and toxic content.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Multi-objective optimization through reinforcement learning is crucial for balancing the conflicting goals of generating toxic content, evading filters, and maintaining prompt diversity in text-to-image red teaming.
> - Subtle, non-explicit phrasing and contextual references enable adversarial prompts to effectively bypass existing T2I safety filters.
> - Adversarial prompts developed against open-source T2I models can transfer effectively to black-box commercial T2I APIs, indicating widespread vulnerabilities in deployed systems.
