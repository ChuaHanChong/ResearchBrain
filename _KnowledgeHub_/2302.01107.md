# A Survey on Efficient Training of Transformers

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers systematically categorize techniques for efficient Transformer training, offering the first comprehensive overview of methods that reduce computational cost, memory footprint, and optimize for hardware co-design. The work compiles strategies such as sparse training, various parallelism approaches, and low-precision computation to mitigate the substantial resource demands of large-scale models.

## Problem

- Large Transformer models incur immense computational costs, leading to high energy consumption and making cutting-edge AI inaccessible to many.
- Training these models demands an exponentially growing memory footprint, posing significant hardware and scaling challenges.
- A lack of systematic overview existed for the diverse efficient training techniques developed to mitigate these Transformer efficiency issues.

## Method

- A systematic review and organization of existing literature on efficient Transformer training methods is presented.
- Techniques are categorized into three main themes: ==computation efficiency==, ==memory efficiency==, and ==hardware/algorithm co-design==.
- Methods are analyzed and compared based on their mechanisms for reducing computational load and memory footprint during training.

## Results

- The survey consolidates and explains diverse methods for computation efficiency, including advanced optimizers (e.g., Lion, SAM), effective initialization (e.g., Fixup, ReZero), **sparse training**, large batch training, incremental learning, and data selection.
- It details memory efficiency techniques such as various parallelism strategies (Data, Model, Tensor, Pipeline), **quantized training** (e.g., AMP, FP8), rematerialization, offloading (e.g., ZeRO-Offload), and parameter-efficient tuning (e.g., LoRA).
- Hardware/algorithm co-design approaches are highlighted, covering sparse matrix multiplication, hardware-aware low-precision computations (e.g., 4-bit queries), and optimized attention mechanisms (e.g., **FlashAttention**).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Efficient Transformer training necessitates a holistic strategy, integrating algorithmic optimizations for computation and memory with hardware/algorithm co-design principles.
> - Diverse techniques, ranging from advanced optimizers and sparse training to various parallelism strategies and parameter-efficient tuning, contribute to making large models more manageable.
> - Further advancements require a focus on joint training and deployment optimization, on-device training, and the establishment of a standardized benchmarking framework.
