# Discovered Policy Optimisation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at FLAIR Oxford, UC Berkeley, and Google Brain discovered a new reinforcement learning algorithm, Discovered Policy Optimisation (DPO), by meta-learning a key component within the theoretically-sound Mirror Learning framework. DPO consistently outperforms PPO across various continuous control tasks, generalizes to unseen environments, and is a simple, analytically derived algorithm with provable guarantees.

## Problem

- Traditional reinforcement learning algorithms are hand-designed, leading to methods that can be brittle to hyperparameters, lack robustness, and struggle to generalize.
- Existing algorithm discovery methods often produce black-box solutions without theoretical guarantees, frequently fail to consistently outperform handcrafted algorithms, and struggle with generalization beyond training distributions.
- A lack of interpretability in meta-learned algorithms hinders understanding and systematic improvement of policy optimization principles.

## Method

- Utilize the ==Mirror Learning framework==, which guarantees monotonic policy improvement and convergence, to define a space of theoretically sound algorithms.
- Parameterize the ==drift function== within Mirror Learning using a neural network, imposing architectural constraints to ensure theoretical validity.
- Meta-train the drift function's parameters using ==Evolution Strategies (ES)== to optimize the final return of the inner-loop RL agent, allowing for learning from scratch (LPO-Zero) or fine-tuning from PPO (LPO).
- Analyze the behavior of the learned LPO drift function to derive a novel, interpretable, ==closed-form analytical expression== for Discovered Policy Optimisation (DPO).

## Results

- The meta-learned LPO algorithm consistently outperformed PPO across most Brax continuous control environments and demonstrated strong generalization to unseen environments and varied hyperparameter settings.
- Analysis of the learned LPO drift function led to the formulation of Discovered Policy Optimisation (DPO), a novel, closed-form algorithm that is theoretically sound and easily implementable.
- DPO matched LPO's state-of-the-art performance, consistently outperforming PPO on both **Brax** and **Minatar** tasks, showcasing its transferability and practical utility.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Effective policy optimization benefits from a 'rollback' mechanism for actions with negative advantages and decreased probabilities, and 'cautious optimism' for actions with positive advantages and increased probabilities.
> - The learned drift function implicitly encourages higher policy entropy throughout training without an explicit entropy bonus, promoting robust exploration.
> - Sophisticated, asymmetric update rules, learned by the meta-algorithm, can lead to superior performance and generalization compared to symmetric clipping methods like PPO.
> - Meta-learning within a principled, theoretically-sound framework enables the discovery of high-performing, interpretable algorithms with provable guarantees.
