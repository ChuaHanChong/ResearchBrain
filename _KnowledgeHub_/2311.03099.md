# Language Models are Super Mario: Absorbing Abilities from Homologous Models as a Free Lunch

> [!summary] Summary
> This paper unveils extreme redundancy in Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) delta parameters of large language models, proposing DARE (Drop And REscale) to eliminate up to 99% of these parameters without retraining. Applying DARE significantly enhances model merging performance, enabling the creation of multi-competent models that can even surpass individual specialized source models.

## Problem

- High computational costs and resource demands for developing and deploying diverse capabilities in large language models (LLMs).
- The implicit redundancy within parameters modified by Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT).
- Parameter interference issues encountered when attempting to merge multiple task-specific LLMs into a single, versatile model.

## Method

- Defining ==SFT delta parameters== as the difference between fine-tuned and pre-trained weights, focusing on the specific changes introduced by fine-tuning.
- Introducing ==DARE (Drop And REscale)==, a two-step technique to randomly drop a proportion of delta parameters and then rescale the remaining ones, operating solely on CPUs without requiring retraining.
- Integrating DARE as a ==pre-processing step for model merging== frameworks to mitigate parameter interference and enhance the performance of composite models.

## Results

- DARE effectively removes up to **99%** of SFT delta parameters from large language models with negligible performance degradation, demonstrating extreme redundancy.
- Application of DARE consistently improves various model merging methods, with merged models showing potential to surpass the performance of individual source models (e.g., up to **19.57%** improvement on MBPP over the code-specific model).
- A **7B** parameter merged model, 'supermario v2', created using DARE, achieved the top ranking on the Open LLM Leaderboard for its size class as of January 2024.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) delta parameters exhibit extreme redundancy, allowing for 90-99% removal with minimal performance impact.
> - The rescale operation in DARE is critical for preserving model performance by maintaining original embedding approximations after parameters are dropped.
> - SFT primarily unlocks pre-existing capabilities within LLMs rather than introducing entirely new knowledge, as evidenced by catastrophic performance loss when fine-tuned parameters are removed.
