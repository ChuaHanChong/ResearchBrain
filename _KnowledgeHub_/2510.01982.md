# Fine-Grained GRPO for Precise Preference Alignment in Flow Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai AI Laboratory developed Granular-GRPO (G²RPO), a framework for aligning flow-based generative models with human preferences by introducing Singular Stochastic Sampling and Multi-Granularity Advantage Integration. This approach improves image quality and prompt adherence, achieving up to 6.52% relative improvement on HPS-v2.1 scores over DanceGRPO and superior performance across multiple reward models like Unified Reward.

## Problem

- Existing flow-based GRPO methods suffer from a sparse reward problem, where the final reward is uniformly attributed to all denoising steps, making precise credit assignment difficult.
- Current evaluation of sampling directions is often incomplete, relying on a fixed number of subsequent denoising steps, which provides only a single-granularity assessment.
- These limitations hinder the stability, efficiency, and overall performance ceiling of preference alignment in flow models, potentially leading to policy collapse or overfitting to specific reward biases.

## Method

- Developed ==Singular Stochastic Sampling== (SSS) to localize stochasticity to a single critical denoising step, making the variance in rewards directly attributable to that specific perturbation for precise credit assignment.
- Introduced Multi-Granularity Advantage Integration (MGAI) to evaluate sampling directions across multiple subsequent denoising granularities, generating images at various levels of detail to provide a comprehensive assessment.
- Integrated SSS and MGAI into an enhanced GRPO objective function, where advantages are computed based on the precisely attributed, multi-granularity rewards, leading to more robust model updates.

## Results

- G²RPO without MGAI (using SSS alone) achieved a **6.52%** relative improvement in HPS-v2.1 score over DanceGRPO, demonstrating the efficacy of precise reward signals.
- The full G²RPO framework outperformed baselines (DanceGRPO, MixGRPO) across various reward models, including a higher Unified Reward score (3.783 vs. MixGRPO's 3.661), indicating improved robustness and alignment with human preferences.
- G²RPO demonstrated stronger robustness to varying inference steps, maintaining significant performance improvements even when inference steps were reduced to 10 or 20, and showed superior detail fidelity and adherence to complex text prompts qualitatively.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Localizing stochasticity to a single step dramatically improves the precision of reward attribution, enabling more effective credit assignment in online reinforcement learning for generative models.
> - Evaluating denoising directions across multiple granularities (e.g., coarse to fine) provides a more robust and comprehensive assessment of quality, preventing overfitting to specific reward model biases and enhancing generalization.
> - A unified advantage derived from multi-granularity evaluations mitigates domain collapse and leads to more stable and holistic improvements in generated content across various quality dimensions.
