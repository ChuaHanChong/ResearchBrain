# Video Generation Models in Robotics -- Applications, Research Challenges, Future Directions

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey analyzes video generation models as embodied world models in robotics, detailing their applications in areas like imitation learning and visual planning, while also identifying critical research challenges such as physical consistency and uncertainty quantification. It concludes by proposing future directions for their trustworthy integration into safety-critical robotic systems.

## Problem

- Physics-based simulators for robotics are often limited by simplifying assumptions, expensive asset curation, and a 'sim-to-real gap' for complex phenomena.
- Learned Markovian state-based world models struggle to capture intricate physical interaction processes and fine-grained spatial-temporal dependencies in the real world.
- Language-only abstractions (LLMs/VLMs) lack the expressiveness needed to accurately model the spatial and temporal dynamics essential for physical robot-environment interactions.

## Method

- The paper provides a systematic review of ==generative video models==, particularly ==diffusion== and ==flow-matching== models, positioning them as ==embodied world models== for robotics.
- It categorizes their applications across ==imitation learning==, ==reinforcement learning==, ==policy evaluation==, and ==visual planning==.
- The survey identifies key research challenges hindering their adoption and proposes future directions to address these limitations for trustworthy integration into robotics.

## Results

- The survey identified **4** key application areas for video models in robotics: data generation/action prediction in imitation learning, dynamics/rewards modeling in reinforcement learning, scalable policy evaluation, and visual planning.
- **10** major research challenges were systematically enumerated, encompassing issues like hallucinations, physics violations, uncertainty quantification, long video generation, and prohibitive computational costs.
- Specific future research directions were proposed to address these challenges, including integrating physics priors, improving instruction following, and developing robotics-centric evaluation benchmarks for more trustworthy and efficient robotic systems.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Video generation models offer superior high-fidelity, physically consistent spatiotemporal predictions compared to traditional simulators and language models for embodied world modeling.
> - These models provide broad utility in robotics, acting as cost-effective data generators, dynamics/reward models, scalable policy evaluators, and visual planners.
> - Significant challenges persist, including hallucinations, violations of physics, poor instruction following, lack of uncertainty quantification, and high computational costs, which impede reliable real-world deployment.
