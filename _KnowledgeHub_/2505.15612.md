# Learn to Reason Efficiently with Adaptive Length-based Reward Shaping

> [!summary] Summary
> This paper presents LASER methods (LASER, LASER-D, LASER-DE), which utilize adaptive length-based reward shaping within a reinforcement learning framework to enhance the efficiency of Large Reasoning Models. These methods improve the balance between reasoning accuracy and token usage, achieving substantial reductions in token consumption while maintaining or increasing accuracy on challenging mathematical benchmarks.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) employing Chain-of-Thought (CoT) often 'overthink', generating verbose and redundant reasoning steps.
- This overthinking leads to inefficiency, increasing computational costs and hindering the practical deployment of these Large Reasoning Models (LRMs).
- Existing methods for improving reasoning efficiency often face a steep trade-off, where token reduction comes at the cost of reasoning accuracy.

## Method

- Introduces a unified reinforcement learning framework that formulates efficient reasoning as an optimization problem incorporating correctness and ==length-based rewards==.
- Proposes ==LASER (Length-bAsed StEp Reward)==, a method that applies a step function reward to penalize reasoning chains exceeding a target length.
- Develops ==LASER-D (Dynamic and Difficulty-aware)== which adaptively adjusts the target reasoning length throughout training based on problem difficulty and current model accuracy.
- Introduces ==LASER-DE (with additional Exploration)==, a variant of LASER-D that encourages further exploration on incorrect responses to discover better reasoning paths.

## Results

- The LASER methods achieve an improved Pareto-optimal balance between reasoning accuracy and token efficiency compared to prior approaches.
- On the AIME2024 benchmark, LASER-D and LASER-DE improved accuracy by **6.1 percentage points** while reducing token usage by **63%**.
- The proposed methods demonstrate robust generalization across out-of-domain benchmarks, including MATH500, AMC2023, and OlympiadBench.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A unified framework for length-based reward shaping provides a systematic approach to mitigating overthinking in large reasoning models.
> - Reinforcement learning with length-based reward shaping leads to more concise reasoning chains, eliminating redundant steps and reducing unnecessary self-reflection.
> - Dynamic adjustment of target reasoning length based on problem difficulty allows for adaptive 'fast' and 'slow' thinking, optimizing efficiency without sacrificing accuracy.
