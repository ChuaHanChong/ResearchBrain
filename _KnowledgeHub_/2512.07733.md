# SpatialDreamer: Incentivizing Spatial Reasoning via Active Mental Imagery

> [!summary] Summary
> SpatialDreamer presents a reinforcement learning framework that endows multimodal large language models with active spatial mental simulation capabilities via iterative exploration and visual imagination. This approach, incorporating a Geometric Policy Optimization algorithm, established new top performances on various spatial reasoning benchmarks.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) exhibit fragility in complex spatial reasoning tasks demanding mental simulation, such as object counting with occlusions or perspective-taking.
- Current methods either rely on passive observation of spatial data or employ external, manually configured test-time scaling approaches, failing to internalize active spatial mental imagery within the MLLM.
- Training MLLMs for multi-step, long-horizontal reasoning via standard Reinforcement Learning (RL) is hindered by sparse trajectory-level reward signals, making efficient learning of exploration policies difficult.

## Method

- The SpatialDreamer framework implements an agentic reinforcement learning approach that enables MLLMs to actively explore and reason through an iterative ==`think-imagine-answer` loop==, leveraging an external world model (Stable Virtual Camera) to generate novel egocentric views.
- ==Geometric Policy Optimization (GeoPO)== introduces a tree-structured sampling scheme and a ==hybrid reward design==, incorporating dense step-level rewards and geometric penalties, to stabilize training and encourage geometrically plausible action sequences.
- A Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) dataset, ==SpatialDreamer-SFT==, is constructed using single-pass and error-injection-based reflective reasoning samples to bootstrap the MLLM with preliminary agentic imagination behaviors.

## Results

- SpatialDreamer achieved state-of-the-art performance on the SAT benchmark, with **93.9%** average accuracy on SAT-Real and **92.5%** on SAT-Synthesized, surpassing generalist and specialized MLLMs.
- The model established a new state-of-the-art overall score of **84.9%** on MindCube-Tiny, representing a **55.6%** absolute improvement over the Qwen2.5-VL-7B baseline, demonstrating strong active mental imagery capabilities.
- Ablation studies confirmed that GeoPO significantly outperforms standard GRPO (e.g., **+5.5%** on MindCube-Tiny) and that both the geometric penalty and the comprehensive SFT dataset are critical for achieving optimal performance and efficient training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Equipping MLLMs with active mental imagery, where they iteratively imagine and integrate novel views generated by a world model, allows them to overcome the limitations of static single-view perception for complex spatial reasoning.
> - Geometric Policy Optimization (GeoPO)'s hybrid reward system, which provides dense step-level feedback and penalizes geometrically sub-optimal actions, effectively addresses reward sparsity and ensures efficient, stable learning for multi-step reasoning.
> - A carefully curated Supervised Fine-Tuning dataset, including reflective reasoning samples, is essential for pre-training MLLMs to adopt the iterative `think-imagine-answer` agentic reasoning paradigm and develop self-correction capabilities.
