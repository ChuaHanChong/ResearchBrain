# X-Reasoner: Towards Generalizable Reasoning Across Modalities and Domains

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework combining supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning enables vision-language models to develop generalizable reasoning capabilities through text-only training, achieving state-of-the-art performance on multimodal benchmarks like MMMU and MathVista while demonstrating successful transfer to specialized domains like medicine.

## Problem

- Existing multimodal models require expensive task-specific datasets and training
- Limited understanding of whether reasoning skills can generalize across modalities and domains

## Method

- ==Two-stage post-training approach== using text-only data on vision-language models
- Combines ==supervised fine-tuning using distilled long chain-of-thoughts== with ==reinforcement learning using verifiable rewards==

## Results

- Outperformed existing state-of-the-art models on multiple multimodal benchmarks (MMMU, MMMU-Pro, MathVista)
- Achieved new state-of-the-art results on medical benchmarks after domain-specific fine-tuning
- Ablation studies confirmed genuine multimodal reasoning rather than relying on text-based shortcuts

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Mathematical reasoning trained on text serves as an effective "anchor domain" for developing generalizable reasoning capabilities
> - Text-only training can improve multimodal reasoning without requiring multimodal training data
> - Domain-specific fine-tuning further enhances performance in specialized areas like medicine
