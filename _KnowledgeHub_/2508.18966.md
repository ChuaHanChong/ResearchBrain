# USO: Unified Style and Subject-Driven Generation via Disentangled and Reward Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from ByteDance's Intelligent Creation Lab developed USO, a unified framework that seamlessly integrates style-driven and subject-driven image generation by treating them as complementary tasks. The model, leveraging a novel cross-task co-disentanglement paradigm and Style Reward Learning, achieves state-of-the-art performance on a newly introduced USO-Bench benchmark across subject consistency, style fidelity, and text controllability.

## Problem

- Existing generative methods treat style-driven and subject-driven image generation as separate, disjoint tasks, leading to sub-optimal feature disentanglement.
- This isolated approach results in challenges such as content leakage from style references and difficulty in maintaining subject identity across varying styles or poses.
- A lack of unified frameworks capable of simultaneously handling diverse conditioning, including text, style references, and subject references, within a single model.

## Method

- A ==Cross-Task Triplet Curation== framework was developed to generate a large, high-quality dataset comprising content, style, and stylized content images, crucially including both layout-preserved and layout-shifted variations.
- The Unified Style-Subject Optimized (USO) model employs a two-stage training process: ==Style Alignment Training (SAT)== using a SigLIP semantic encoder for style, followed by ==Content-Style Disentanglement Training (CSDT)== with a separate VAE encoder for content.
- ==Style Reward Learning (SRL)== is integrated during fine-tuning, using a VLM-based reward to explicitly disentangle style from content and optimize for style similarity.

## Results

- USO consistently surpasses competing open-source models, achieving the highest DINO (**0.793**) and CLIP-I (**0.623**) scores for subject consistency and a leading CSD (**0.557**) score for style similarity on the new USO-Bench.
- Qualitative evaluations and a user study confirm USO's superior performance in maintaining subject identity, preserving intricate style details, and aligning with complex textual prompts across individual and combined tasks.
- Ablation studies validate the critical contributions of Style Reward Learning, Style Alignment Training, and the disentangled encoder design, demonstrating their necessity for USO's enhanced performance and artifact reduction.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Style-driven and subject-driven generation are complementary tasks that benefit from a shared "cross-task co-disentanglement paradigm," where improving one task mutually reinforces the model's ability to perform the other.
> - Explicitly disentangling content and style features through separate encoding pathways (SigLIP for style, VAE for content) and a hierarchical style projector is crucial for achieving high-fidelity control and mitigating content leakage.
> - Style Reward Learning not only enhances style fidelity but also has a positive, unexpected impact on subject consistency, reducing common generative artifacts like a "plastic appearance."
