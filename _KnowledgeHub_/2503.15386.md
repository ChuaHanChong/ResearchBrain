# CCDP: Composition of Conditional Diffusion Policies with Guided Sampling

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework, Composition of Conditional Diffusion Policies (CCDP), enhances robot recovery from failed actions by dynamically guiding action sampling away from failure regions. The approach, which utilizes only successful demonstration data, showed improved task success rates and maintained adherence to implicit objectives across diverse robotic manipulation scenarios.

## Problem

- Robots using imitation learning policies inefficiently re-sample actions after a failure, often repeating unsuccessful attempts.
- Existing imitation learning methods lack inherent mechanisms for failure recovery, typically requiring additional failure data, complex high-level planners, or risky exploration.
- Incorporating long-term failure history into policies to enable effective recovery often leads to increased dimensionality and complexity.

## Method

- **CCDP** integrates a ==guided sampling== mechanism into ==Diffusion Policies== by decomposing the denoising process into components conditioned on current state, recent history, and multiple distinct failure features.
- It synthesizes a ==recovery dataset== from successful demonstrations to train specific ==failure models== that actively steer actions away from regions associated with observed failures.
- The framework uses a ==product of experts== inspired composition to dynamically combine these guidance cues, adapting the policy during runtime without re-training.

## Results

- CCDP demonstrated significantly higher task success rates, achieving **99%** in Door Opening and **96%** in Button Pressing, compared to **70%** and **10%** for standard Diffusion Policies, respectively.
- The framework showed improved adherence to implicit task objectives from demonstrations, such as optimal manipulation strategies (**66%** adherence in Object Manipulation) and prioritizing nearest available resources (**73%** in Object Packing, **97%** in Bartender), often outperforming an enhanced baseline.
- CCDP achieved comparable or superior performance to baselines **without requiring explicit labeling** of solution space partitions or prior classification of failure types.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Leveraging past failures as "negative guidance" can effectively steer policy sampling away from undesirable action spaces.
> - Decomposing the policy into a composition of simpler, conditionally-guided diffusion models allows for flexible handling of a variable number of failure conditions.
> - It is possible to infer effective recovery strategies solely from successful demonstration data by carefully synthesizing an auxiliary dataset that defines appropriate recovery actions.
