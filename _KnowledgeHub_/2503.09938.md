# PanoGen++: Domain-Adapted Text-Guided Panoramic Environment Generation for Vision-and-Language Navigation

> [!summary] Summary
> PanoGen++ generates realistic, diverse panoramic environments for Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) tasks by integrating domain-specific knowledge into pre-trained diffusion models. This approach leads to improved performance of VLN agents, including a 2.44% increase in success rate on the R2R benchmark and enhanced metrics on R4R and CVDN.

## Problem

- Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) agents suffer from limited generalization capabilities due to the scarcity of diverse training environments, relying on finite datasets like Matterport3D.
- Existing data augmentation techniques and basic generative models (e.g., PanoGen) lack the domain-specific adaptations required to produce environments that accurately reflect visual characteristics and spatial relationships necessary for effective VLN training.

## Method

- Introduces **PanoGen++**, a framework that integrates pre-trained text-to-image diffusion models with domain-specific fine-tuning for generating panoramic environments.
- ==Applies Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)== to efficiently adapt the Stable Diffusion model to the VLN domain, focusing on attention layers using a VLN-specific image-text dataset.
- Employs two distinct environment generation methods: masked image inpainting and recursive image outpainting, which are then used to augment VLN agent training.

## Results

- Improved success rate on the Room-to-Room (R2R) test leaderboard by **2.44%** compared to state-of-the-art approaches.
- Achieved a **0.63%** improvement on the Room-for-Room (R4R) validation unseen set and a **0.75**-meter enhancement in goal progress on the Cooperative Vision-and-Dialog Navigation (CVDN) validation unseen set.
- Generated panoramic environments exhibit high visual fidelity and semantic coherence, enhancing the training of VLN agents and demonstrating versatility for both indoor and outdoor scenes.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Incorporating domain-specific knowledge into pre-trained diffusion models significantly improves the realism and utility of generated environments for Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) tasks.
> - Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods, specifically LoRA, effectively adapt large generative models like Stable Diffusion to specialized domains (VLN environments) with reduced computational cost.
> - A combination of masked image inpainting and recursive image outpainting provides flexible strategies for generating diverse panoramic environments, balancing consistency with original scenes and creating entirely novel ones.
