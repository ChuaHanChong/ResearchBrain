# WM-DAgger: Enabling Efficient Data Aggregation for Imitation Learning with World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> WM-DAgger introduces a framework that automates data aggregation for imitation learning by leveraging a world model to synthesize physically consistent out-of-distribution recovery trajectories. This approach significantly enhances robotic policy robustness and generalization, achieving up to 96.7% success in soft bag pushing and improving pick-and-place success on unseen objects by over 50 percentage points compared to standard behavioral cloning.

## Problem

- Imitation learning suffers from compounding errors, where small policy inaccuracies lead to out-of-distribution (OOD) states and task failure, limiting reliability.
- The DAgger framework addresses OOD states but requires continuous, resource-intensive human intervention for corrective actions, making it unscalable for complex applications.
- Existing generative methods for OOD data synthesis often produce single-frame or physically inconsistent data, which is inadequate for capturing continuous, dynamic recovery trajectories.

## Method

- Developed an ==Eye-in-Hand Action-Conditioned World Model (EAC-WM)== (EAC-WM) using an Action2Image conditioning module and a specialized training strategy to predict high-fidelity, action-conditioned visual dynamics.
- Implemented a ==Corrective Action Synthesis== Module to generate task-oriented recovery actions by intentionally deviating from expert paths and then synthesizing trajectories back to the expert manifold.
- Integrated a ==Consistency-Guided Filtering== Module that uses DINOv2 embeddings and an adaptive threshold to discard physically implausible or hallucinated synthesized trajectories, ensuring data fidelity.

## Results

- WM-DAgger achieved a **96.7**% success rate in soft bag pushing with **20** demonstrations, outperforming DMD (**56.7**%) and standard BC (**30.0**%), and attained **73.3**% with only 1-shot demonstration.
- On pick-and-place with unseen objects, WM-DAgger maintained **63.3****-76.7**% success rates, demonstrating superior generalization compared to standard BC (**0.0****-10.0**%).
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical role of the corrective action synthesis module (**0.0**% success without directional constraint) and the consistency-guided filtering module (degraded to **46.7**% without filtering) for effective policy learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - World models can effectively synthesize physically consistent OOD recovery data, thereby automating the DAgger process and overcoming its human-in-the-loop scalability bottleneck.
> - Generating task-oriented corrective actions with directional constraints and filtering out world model hallucinations are crucial for creating high-quality synthetic supervision that prevents policy divergence.
> - Including goal-agnostic "play data" during world model training significantly improves the model's ability to internalize diverse environmental dynamics, leading to higher quality synthesized recovery data and more robust policies.
