# Towards Reasoning Era: A Survey of Long Chain-of-Thought for Reasoning Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Harbin Institute of Technology and collaborating institutions provide a systematic survey of Long Chain-of-Thought (Long CoT) in Large Language Models, establishing a formal distinction from Short CoT. The survey proposes a novel taxonomy based on deep reasoning, extensive exploration, and feasible reflection, and analyzes key phenomena observed in advanced reasoning models.

## Problem

- A lack of a comprehensive and systematic survey specifically dedicated to Long Chain-of-Thought (Long CoT) in Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Difficulty in unifying understanding of Long CoT's distinctions from Short CoT and addressing fragmented research efforts.
- Unresolved debates regarding phenomena such as "overthinking" (where longer reasoning chains can degrade performance) and "inference-time scaling."

## Method

- The authors formally define ==Long CoT== by relaxing constraints on ==reasoning depth==, ==extensive exploration==, and ==feasible reflection== compared to Short CoT.
- A novel taxonomy is developed to categorize existing Long CoT methods based on their enabling characteristics: deep reasoning, extensive exploration, and feasible reflection.
- Key phenomena associated with Long CoT are systematically analyzed to provide insights into its behavior and limitations.

## Results

- A formal distinction and unified framework for Long CoT, defining it through its core capabilities: deep reasoning, extensive exploration, and feasible reflection.
- Presentation of a comprehensive taxonomy classifying Long CoT methodologies across various aspects, including reasoning formats, learning approaches, reflection mechanisms (feedback and refinement), and exploration strategies.
- Detailed analysis and explanations of key phenomena observed in Long CoT, such as its emergence, reasoning boundaries, "overthinking," and the "Aha Moment."

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Long CoT is defined by three essential and integrated capabilities: deep reasoning, extensive exploration, and feasible reflection.
> - A novel, multi-faceted taxonomy categorizes Long CoT methods, detailing formats, learning, feedback types, refinement techniques, and exploration strategies.
> - Phenomena like "overthinking" (performance decline from excessive reasoning) and the "Aha Moment" (sudden reasoning breakthroughs via RL) are inherent to Long CoT.
