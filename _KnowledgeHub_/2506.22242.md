# 4D-VLA: Spatiotemporal Vision-Language-Action Pretraining with Cross-Scene Calibration

> [!summary] Summary
> The 4D-VLA framework enhances vision-language-action (VLA) pretraining by integrating 3D coordinate embeddings and multi-frame historical context to resolve issues of ambiguous robot positioning and latent state. This approach achieves a 12.1% higher average success rate compared to OpenVLA on LIBERO benchmarks and demonstrates robust generalization across diverse simulated and real-world robotic tasks.

## Problem

- Existing VLA pretraining paradigms often suffer from "coordinate system chaos," where single-image inputs lack sufficient spatial context to infer a robot's precise position and orientation, leading to dispersed action distributions.
- Models experience "state chaos" when single frames provide insufficient temporal or contextual cues, resulting in ambiguity for actions, such as symmetric trajectories or visually similar observations requiring different movements.
- The limitations of incomplete inputs hinder the efficient pretraining and broad generalization of VLA models across diverse robotic tasks and viewpoints.

## Method

- Integrates ==3D coordinate information== derived from depth data and camera poses into "==spatial vision tokens==," explicitly grounding visual features in a 3D space to resolve coordinate system ambiguity.
- Incorporates temporal dynamics by using an adaptive "==memory bank sampling (MBS)==" strategy to select informative historical frames, augmented with ==learnable temporal positional encodings==, to mitigate state chaos.
- Utilizes a large pretrained vision-language model (==InternVL-4B==) as a backbone, fine-tuned with a ==composite loss function== including translation, rotation, grip, and directional terms for robust action prediction.

## Results

- **4D-VLA** achieves an average **12.1%** higher success rate than OpenVLA on LIBERO simulation benchmarks, including a **25.4%** improvement on challenging long-horizon tasks (LIBERO-LONG).
- On the novel MV-Bench, 4D-VLA demonstrates superior spatial understanding, achieving an **81.0%** success rate in in-view settings (**28.8%** higher than OpenVLA) and strong generalization with **73.8%** in cross-view settings.
- Real-world evaluations using a Franka arm show the full 4D-VLA model achieving an **85.63%** average success rate, significantly outperforming OpenVLA (**27.70%**) in tasks requiring precise placement, instruction following, and robustness to distractors.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly fusing 3D spatial coordinates with visual features, rather than relying solely on 2D images, is critical for achieving precise spatial grounding and mitigating coordinate system chaos in robot control.
> - Adaptive sampling of historical frames provides a more efficient and effective way to capture temporal context and disambiguate actions compared to uniform sampling or single-frame inputs.
> - Combining both 3D spatial information and multi-frame temporal context (4D representation) in a VLA pretraining framework leads to significantly improved generalization and robustness in complex robotic manipulation tasks.
