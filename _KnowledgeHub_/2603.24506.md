# Toward Physically Consistent Driving Video World Models under Challenging Trajectories

> [!summary] Summary
> PhyGenesis introduces a driving world model that generates high-fidelity, multi-view driving videos with strong physical consistency, even when conditioned on challenging or counterfactual trajectories. The system rectifies physically inconsistent input trajectories into plausible 6-DoF motions and leverages a heterogeneous dataset, outperforming prior methods in visual quality and physical plausibility across various driving scenarios.

## Problem

- Existing driving world models lack physical awareness of trajectory feasibility, often producing rendering artifacts and structural failures when given physically impossible or inconsistent input trajectories.
- Current models struggle with physics-consistent generation for rare or extreme (but feasible) scenarios, as they are trained primarily on real-world datasets biased towards nominal, safe driving behaviors.

## Method

- A ==Physical Condition Generator== rectifies potentially physics-violating 2D trajectories into physically plausible 6-DoF vehicle motions, using an attention-based architecture with a Temporal Convolutional Network output head.
- A ==Physics-Enhanced Multi-view Video Generator== (**PE-MVGen**), built on a ==diffusion transformer==, is co-trained on a heterogeneous dataset to synthesize high-fidelity and physically consistent multi-view video sequences.
- A novel ==heterogeneous multi-view dataset== combines real-world data (==nuScenes==) with carefully curated, physically challenging simulated scenarios (CARLA) to imbue the model with robust physical understanding.

## Results

- **PhyGenesis** consistently achieved superior visual quality (**lower FID/FVD**), higher physical consistency (**PHY scores**), and better human preference across nuScenes and challenging CARLA datasets compared to baseline methods.
- The Physical Condition Generator effectively reduced **6-DoF L2 distance errors**, demonstrating its ability to rectify physics-violating trajectories and prevent artifacts like vehicle penetration, particularly in simulated challenging scenarios.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both the Physical Condition Generator and the heterogeneous co-training strategy were critical for improving overall performance, visual quality, and physical consistency under challenging driving conditions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly addressing trajectory feasibility with a dedicated condition generator significantly improves the physical consistency and visual fidelity of generated driving videos, especially for challenging inputs.
> - Co-training a video world model on a balanced heterogeneous dataset, encompassing both nominal real-world data and simulated extreme physical interactions, is crucial for learning robust physical priors.
> - The use of 6-DoF trajectories and a TCN-based condition generator enables the capture and prediction of abrupt physical changes and finer motion details during safety-critical events.
