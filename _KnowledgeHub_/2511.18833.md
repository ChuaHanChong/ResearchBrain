# PrismAudio: Decomposed Chain-of-Thoughts and Multi-dimensional Rewards for Video-to-Audio Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> PrismAudio introduces a video-to-audio generation framework utilizing decomposed Chain-of-Thought reasoning and multi-dimensional reinforcement learning to produce high-quality, perceptually consistent audio from video. The system achieves state-of-the-art performance across all perceptual dimensions and features the computationally efficient Fast-GRPO algorithm for RL training.

## Problem

- Existing video-to-audio generation models struggle with "objective entanglement," where a single loss function attempts to optimize multiple competing perceptual goals like semantic consistency, temporal synchrony, and aesthetic quality.
- Prior approaches often lack alignment with subjective human preferences, leading to generated audio that is technically correct but perceptually unsatisfying.
- Monolithic Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning in V2A conflates distinct analytical tasks (semantic, temporal, spatial, aesthetic), resulting in suboptimal treatment of individual dimensions and potential multimodal hallucinations.

## Method

- A CoT-aware audio foundation model integrates ==VideoPrism== for enhanced video understanding and T5-Gemma for effective processing of structured CoT descriptions.
- Multi-dimensional Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning is decomposed into specialized modules for semantic, temporal, aesthetic, and spatial analysis, generated by fine-tuned VideoLLaMA2.
- A Fast-GRPO multi-dimensional reinforcement learning framework uses distinct reward functions aligned with each CoT dimension and employs a hybrid ODE-SDE sampling strategy for efficient optimization of diffusion models.

## Results

- PrismAudio achieves state-of-the-art performance on the VGGSound test set, with gains in semantic consistency (CLAP: **0.47** vs. **0.43** for ThinkSound), temporal synchrony (DeSync: **0.41** vs. **0.55**), and spatial accuracy (CRW error reduced from 13.47 to 7.72).
- The framework demonstrates robustness and generalizability on the challenging AudioCanvas benchmark, maintaining performance stability and achieving superior subjective (MOS) scores across perceptual dimensions.
- Fast-GRPO converges significantly faster than prior RL methods (reaching comparable performance in less than a third of the steps) and improves inference speed to **0.63** seconds for 9-second audio, validating its computational efficiency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing Chain-of-Thought reasoning into specialized modules for semantic, temporal, aesthetic, and spatial aspects significantly enhances V2A reasoning quality and interpretability, outperforming monolithic approaches.
> - Multi-dimensional reward functions, aligned with distinct perceptual goals, effectively resolve objective entanglement in V2A generation, enabling holistic improvements across all critical audio dimensions simultaneously.
> - The Fast-GRPO algorithm's hybrid ODE-SDE sampling with random-window exploration provides a computationally efficient method for applying reinforcement learning to diffusion models, making multi-dimensional optimization practical.
