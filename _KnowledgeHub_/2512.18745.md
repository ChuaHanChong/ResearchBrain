# InSight-o3: Empowering Multimodal Foundation Models with Generalized Visual Search

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and Huawei developed INSIGHT-O3, a multi-agent framework, and O3-BENCH, a challenging benchmark, to enhance multimodal foundation models' ability to perform complex visual reasoning. The system, featuring a specialized visual search agent, boosted GPT-5-mini's performance on O3-BENCH from 39.0% to 61.5%.

## Problem

- Open multimodal models largely fall short on complex, multi-step reasoning capabilities when interacting with high-information-density images, struggling to integrate advanced reasoning and fine-grained visual perception.
- Existing multimodal benchmarks are insufficient, often focusing on coarse image-level understanding or specific perception/cognition tasks, failing to jointly evaluate high-resolution perception and multi-hop visual reasoning.
- Frontier proprietary multimodal systems (e.g., OpenAI o3) demonstrate superior reasoning-driven image understanding compared to open-source models, highlighting a significant capability gap.

## Method

- A multi-agent framework, INSIGHT-O3, was developed, splitting the workload between a `==vReasoner==` for high-level reasoning and a `vSearcher` specialized in locating detailed visual information through generalized visual search.
- A new benchmark, O3-BENCH, was created with high-resolution, information-dense images (charts, maps) and multi-hop questions requiring interleaved attention to visual details and complex reasoning.
- The `vSearcher` (**InSight-o3**-vS) was trained using a hybrid Reinforcement Learning algorithm, combining out-of-loop direct supervision (IoU-based) and in-loop dynamic tasks with `vReasoner`'s feedback and task-success rewards.

## Results

- The O3-BENCH benchmark proved exceptionally challenging, with OpenAI o3 achieving only **40.8%** accuracy and GPT-5-mini scoring **39.0%**, confirming the need for advanced visual reasoning and perception.
- INSIGHT-O3 significantly improved the performance of various frontier models, with GPT-5-mini's accuracy on O3-BENCH increasing from **39.0%** to **61.5%** (a 22.5 percentage point absolute gain).
- InSight-o3-vS demonstrated strong generalization, enhancing other models like GPT-5-nano and Gemini-2.5-Flash across benchmarks and proving the crucial role of its specialized, RL-trained visual search capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing complex visual-linguistic tasks into specialized agents (reasoning and visual search) simplifies the problem and improves overall system performance, especially for detailed visual understanding.
> - Generalized visual search, capable of identifying relational, fuzzy, or conceptual regions described in free-form language across arbitrary image types, is crucial for empowering multimodal models beyond simple object localization.
> - A hybrid RL training methodology, integrating both direct bounding box supervision and dynamic task-oriented feedback from a reasoning agent, is highly effective for developing robust and adaptable visual search capabilities.
