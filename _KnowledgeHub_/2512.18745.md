# InSight-o3: Empowering Multimodal Foundation Models with Generalized Visual Search

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and Huawei developed INSIGHT-O3, a multi-agent framework, and O3-BENCH, a challenging benchmark, to enhance multimodal foundation models' ability to perform complex visual reasoning. The system, featuring a specialized visual search agent, boosted GPT-5-mini's performance on O3-BENCH from 39.0% to 61.5%.

## Problem

- Open multimodal models largely fall short on complex, multi-step reasoning capabilities when interacting with high-information-density images, struggling to integrate advanced reasoning and fine-grained visual perception.
- Existing multimodal benchmarks are insufficient, often focusing on coarse image-level understanding or specific perception/cognition tasks, failing to jointly evaluate high-resolution perception and multi-hop visual reasoning.
- Frontier proprietary multimodal systems (e.g., OpenAI o3) demonstrate superior reasoning-driven image understanding compared to open-source models, highlighting a significant capability gap.

## Method

- A multi-agent framework, INSIGHT-O3, was developed, splitting the workload between a `==vReasoner==` for high-level reasoning and a `vSearcher` specialized in locating detailed visual information through generalized visual search.
- A new benchmark, O3-BENCH, was created with high-resolution, information-dense images (charts, maps) and multi-hop questions requiring interleaved attention to visual details and complex reasoning.
- The `vSearcher` (**InSight-o3**-vS) was trained using a hybrid Reinforcement Learning algorithm, combining out-of-loop direct supervision (IoU-based) and in-loop dynamic tasks with `vReasoner`'s feedback and task-success rewards.

## Results

- The O3-BENCH benchmark proved exceptionally challenging, with OpenAI o3 achieving only **40.8%** accuracy and GPT-5-mini scoring **39.0%**, confirming the need for advanced visual reasoning and perception.
- INSIGHT-O3 significantly improved the performance of various frontier models, with GPT-5-mini's accuracy on O3-BENCH increasing from **39.0%** to **61.5%** (a 22.5 percentage point absolute gain).
- InSight-o3-vS demonstrated strong generalization, enhancing other models like GPT-5-nano and Gemini-2.5-Flash across benchmarks and proving the crucial role of its specialized, RL-trained visual search capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Decomposing complex visual-linguistic tasks into specialized agents (reasoning and visual search) simplifies the problem and improves overall system performance, especially for detailed visual understanding.
> - Generalized visual search, capable of identifying relational, fuzzy, or conceptual regions described in free-form language across arbitrary image types, is crucial for empowering multimodal models beyond simple object localization.
> - A hybrid RL training methodology, integrating both direct bounding box supervision and dynamic task-oriented feedback from a reasoning agent, is highly effective for developing robust and adaptable visual search capabilities.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research directly addresses a critical and evolving challenge in the field of Artificial Intelligence: enabling multimodal AI agents to perform sophisticated "thinking with images" that integrates both advanced reasoning and fine-grained visual perception. The paper positions itself within a landscape where open multimodal models, despite significant progress in perception (e.g., object recognition or simple visual queries on natural images), still largely fall short on the complex, multi-step reasoning capabilities crucial for real-world tasks. Such tasks include analyzing dense charts and diagrams or navigating intricate maps, which require piecing together subtle visual information from distinct image areas. This gap is particularly evident when comparing open-source models with frontier proprietary systems like OpenAI o3, which exhibit superior reasoning-driven image understanding.

The paper argues that existing multimodal benchmarks are often insufficient. Many classical benchmarks focus on coarse image-level understanding, while newer ones tend to be either "cognition-centric" (emphasizing multi-step reasoning with visually simple images, like STEM benchmarks) or "perception-centric" (requiring fine-grained recognition in high-resolution, text-rich scenes, but often limited to single-region lookups). This work introduces **O3-BENCH** as a novel benchmark specifically designed to jointly evaluate *both* high-resolution perception and multi-hop visual reasoning on high-information-density images, thereby filling a crucial research gap. It explicitly aims to be more challenging than benchmarks like MME-RealWorld by demanding interleaved reasoning across multiple distinct image areas.

Furthermore, the paper situates its proposed **INSIGHT-O3** multi-agent framework within the growing trend of hierarchical agent systems and tool-using LLMs. Prior works like Socratic Models, HuggingGPT, and MetaGPT have demonstrated the benefits of orchestrating specialized AI components. INSIGHT-O3 extends this paradigm by introducing a dedicated visual search agent (`vSearcher`) that works in tandem with a visual reasoning agent (`vReasoner`). This specialization is a key departure from many existing multimodal models that attempt to handle both reasoning and perception within a single monolithic agent, which often struggles with complex, intertwined workloads.

Regarding visual search specifically, current models (e.g., DeepEyes, Pixel-Reasoner, Mini-o3) predominantly focus on locating single, discrete objects in natural images. This work significantly broadens the scope of "generalized visual search" to include identifying relational, fuzzy, or conceptual regions described in free-form language within arbitrary images (e.g., maps, posters, screenshots), and supporting multi-region searches crucial for complex reasoning.

Finally, the training methodology, which heavily relies on Reinforcement Learning (RL), aligns with contemporary advancements in aligning LLMs and MLLMs with human preferences and eliciting complex reasoning behaviors. Techniques like Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), pioneered by models such as DeepSeek-R1, have shown effectiveness in fostering planning and chain-of-thought reasoning. INSIGHT-O3's hybrid RL approach, combining in-loop and out-of-loop training, reflects the sophisticated strategies being employed to optimize specialized AI agents for intricate tasks.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of this research is to advance the capability of AI agents, particularly open multimodal foundation models, to **"think with images"**—a skill that requires a sophisticated integration of reasoning and perception. The authors are motivated by the significant gap between the capabilities of current open multimodal agents and frontier proprietary systems (such as OpenAI o3) in handling real-world tasks that demand deep and abstract visual reasoning.

The core motivations and specific objectives can be summarized as follows:

- **Addressing the Reasoning Gap in Open Multimodal Models:** Current open models often excel at simple visual perception tasks (e.g., object search in natural images) but demonstrably fall short in complex reasoning tasks. Real-world applications like analyzing dense charts, diagrams, or navigating maps require models to synthesize subtle visual information from disparate regions through multi-step, interleaved reasoning, which existing open models struggle with. The primary motivation is to empower these models with such capabilities.
- **Developing a Rigorous Evaluation Benchmark (O3-BENCH):** Recognizing the limitations of existing benchmarks, a key objective is to introduce O3-BENCH. This new benchmark is meticulously designed to evaluate multimodal reasoning with interleaved attention to visual details. It features challenging problems on high-information-density images (composite charts and high-resolution digital maps) that necessitate collecting data from multiple distinct image areas and performing complex, multi-hop reasoning. This benchmark is crucial for driving progress in this challenging domain.
- **Proposing a Multi-Agent Framework (INSIGHT-O3):** To tackle the complexity of "thinking with images," the paper aims to introduce INSIGHT-O3, a multi-agent framework. This framework decomposes the workload between a `vReasoner` (responsible for high-level reasoning and general image understanding) and a `vSearcher` (specialized in locating and extracting specific visual details). The motivation behind this divide-and-conquer approach is to simplify the complex interleaved reasoning burden on a single agent, making it more feasible to build o3-like systems with open models.
- **Specializing in Generalized Visual Search (InSight-o3-vS):** A significant objective is to develop a specialized visual search agent, named InSight-o3-vS. This agent is designed to perform "generalized visual search," which goes beyond simply locating discrete objects. It aims to identify relational, fuzzy, or conceptual regions described in free-form language across arbitrary image types. The motivation is to create a robust and flexible component that can accurately pinpoint relevant visual information, akin to how humans direct their attention to areas of interest.
- **Enabling Plug-and-Play Empowerment for Foundation Models:** The ultimate practical objective is for InSight-o3-vS to function as a "plug-and-play" component that can significantly enhance the performance of existing multimodal foundation models (acting as `vReasoners`). The authors aim to demonstrate that this specialized agent can empower frontier models across a wide range of benchmarks, marking a concrete step toward powerful, open "o3-like" systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The core methodology of this work revolves around a novel **multi-agent framework called INSIGHT-O3**, which partitions the complex task of "thinking with images" into specialized roles. It also introduces a new benchmark, O3-BENCH, designed to effectively evaluate this capability.

#### 3.1. O3-BENCH Construction

The benchmark is built on two core principles: high resolution and high information density, and multi-hop solution paths.
- **Data Sources:** Images are collected from the "Diagram and Table" subset of MME-RealWorld and the Internet. Chart images are filtered to ensure high information density (at least 8 detected layouts). Map images are manually collected, focusing on venue-level maps requiring legend/index reading and visual entity localization, excluding world-knowledge-answerable maps.
- **Annotation Pipeline:**
 - **Machine Pre-annotation:** High-resolution images undergo layout detection using PP-DocLayout plus-L. For each detected layout, Qwen2.5-VL-32B generates a detailed caption and extracts OCR text. GPT-5 then synthesizes 3-5 multi-hop questions (with answers and step-by-step explanations) by composing evidence from these region-level details, without seeing the full image to enforce multi-hop reasoning.
 - **Human Annotation:** Annotators filter, validate, and refine machine-generated QAs for factual consistency and clarity. They also author new questions for information-dense images, ensuring fine-grained detail retrieval and multi-hop reasoning.
- **Difficulty Filtering:** Items solvable by three strong proprietary MLLMs (GPT-5-mini, Gemini-2.5-Flash, Doubao-Seed-1.6) are discarded to ensure high difficulty. A secondary review ensures factual correctness and consistency.

#### 3.2. INSIGHT-O3 Framework

INSIGHT-O3 is a two-agent system:
- **vReasoner (Visual Reasoning Agent):** Responsible for high-level, abstract reasoning, general image understanding, decomposing the user query, and issuing visual search requests.
- **vSearcher (Visual Search Agent):** Specialized in locating detailed visual information requested by `vReasoner`, potentially using tools like image cropping, and presenting the cropped regions back to `vReasoner`.

The key innovation lies in the definition of **generalized visual search**, which `vSearcher` is trained to perform. Unlike conventional visual search limited to discrete objects in natural images, generalized visual search handles arbitrary images (maps, posters, screenshots) and refers to relational, fuzzy, or conceptual regions (e.g., "the area to the left of the wooden chair," "the chart showing revenue").

#### 3.3. Training Algorithm: Hybrid Sub-Agent Reinforcement Learning (RL)

The `vSearcher` (implemented as InSight-o3-vS, based on Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct) is trained using a hybrid RL algorithm with two components to ensure efficiency and practical alignment:
- **Out-of-Loop RL Component:**
 - **Data:** Uses InfographicVQA images. Layout components are detected by PP-DocLayout plus-L. GPT-5-nano generates concise, high-level region descriptions for these ground-truth bounding boxes.
 - **Reward:** `r = I[n_tool > 0] * (λ_format * r_format + λ_IoU * r_IoU)`. `r_format` rewards correct tool call format, and `r_IoU` (Intersection over Union) directly measures the accuracy of the predicted bounding box against the ground truth.
- **In-Loop RL Component:**
 - **Data:** Synthesized collages from Visual CoT and V* training data. Collages stitch multiple low-to-medium-resolution images around a primary "target" image with a QA pair, along with distractor images, to create challenging visual search scenarios. Items solvable by `vReasoner` alone are filtered out.
 - **vReasoner's Role:** During training, `vReasoner` (a strong external model like GPT-5-mini) dynamically generates visual search tasks (`region_description` prompts) and provides feedback on `vSearcher`'s predictions.
 - **Reward:** Replaces `r_IoU` with a pseudo-IoU reward `ˆr_IoU = I[s = c = 1]`. Here, `s` is `vReasoner`'s binary helpfulness rating (relevant to task, helps answer query), and `c` is the correctness of `vReasoner`'s final answer. This links `vSearcher`'s performance directly to the overall task success.
- **Advantage Estimation and Objective:** The training utilizes a modified GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization) algorithm, with global normalization for advantage estimation in the in-loop component, to handle the dynamically generated tasks. The objective function optimizes `vSearcher` to maximize these rewards.
- **Implementation Details:** Max image resolution for `vSearcher` is ~3.2M pixels during training, and ~12.8M pixels during evaluation. `vReasoner` and `vSearcher` are allowed at most 6 sub-agent/tool calls. Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct's vision tower and adapter are frozen during training.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research paper presents several key findings and results that underscore the effectiveness of INSIGHT-O3 and the challenges posed by the new O3-BENCH benchmark.

- **O3-BENCH Difficulty:** The newly introduced O3-BENCH proves to be exceptionally challenging for frontier multimodal models. OpenAI o3, a leading proprietary system, achieves only 40.8% accuracy, while GPT-5-mini scores 39.0%. This confirms the benchmark's design principles requiring complex, interleaved reasoning and fine-grained perception on high-information-density images.
- **Significant Performance Improvement with INSIGHT-O3:** The INSIGHT-O3 framework, utilizing InSight-o3-vS as the specialized visual search agent, demonstrably and significantly improves the performance of various frontier models (acting as `vReasoners`).
 - For **GPT-5-mini**, accuracy on O3-BENCH jumps from 39.0% to 61.5% (an absolute improvement of 22.5 percentage points). On average across a range of benchmarks (V*-Bench, HR-Bench, Tree-Bench, VisualProbe-Hard, MME-RealWorld-Lite, O3-BENCH), GPT-5-mini's performance sees a relative improvement of 20.9% with InSight-o3-vS.
 - This enhanced performance also significantly surpasses that of models paired with an *untrained* Qwen2.5-VL-7B as `vSearcher`, highlighting the critical role of the reinforcement learning-based specialization.
- **Generalization Across vReasoners:** InSight-o3-vS, which was primarily trained with GPT-5-mini as its `vReasoner`, exhibits strong generalization capabilities when paired with other models from different families. For instance, it improves GPT-5-nano's performance on VisualProbe-Hard from 21.7% to 31.4% and on O3-BENCH from 26.5% to 34.6%. When integrated with Gemini-2.5-Flash (a different model family), InSight-o3-vS still provides a substantial 7-10% lead over baselines on benchmarks like V*-Bench and O3-BENCH. This indicates that the learned generalized visual search skills are broadly applicable.
- **O3-BENCH Uniqueness:** The results reveal that O3-BENCH is distinct from other benchmarks. Gemini-2.5-Flash, for example, shows a considerable lead over GPT-5-mini on O3-BENCH when `vSearcher` is not used, a disparity not consistently observed on other benchmarks. With InSight-o3-vS, GPT-5-mini substantially narrows this gap (from 21.4% to 8.2%), underscoring the benchmark's focus on visual detail and the effectiveness of the proposed approach.
- **Impact of Image Resolution:** While higher input image resolution for the `vReasoner` generally leads to better performance, the benefits provided by `vSearcher` become less pronounced in such cases. Conversely, `vSearcher` itself demonstrates robustness to varying input image resolutions, maintaining decent performance even when operating at relatively low resolutions (0.8M pixels). Lower resolutions for `vSearcher` also correlate with a higher average number of `vSearcher` calls, as expected when fine details are obscured.
- **Ablation Study on Hybrid RL Training:** The ablation studies confirm that the **hybrid RL training algorithm** is crucial for optimal performance. Both the in-loop (dynamically generated tasks with `vReasoner` feedback) and out-of-loop (pre-generated tasks with direct IoU supervision) components contribute to the final efficacy. Combining both yields the best results, as they offer complementary supervision sources (high-level `vReasoner` feedback and low-level ground-truth boxes) and data diversity.
- **Reward Design and Training Dynamics:** The proposed reward design, incorporating tool condition, `vReasoner` feedback, outcome supervision, and global normalization, slightly outperforms ablated variants. Training dynamics show that as `vSearcher` improves its localization (indicated by IoU), `vReasoner`'s overall accuracy on the task increases. The average number of `vReasoner` calls initially fluctuates but eventually stabilizes or decreases, suggesting that `vSearcher` becomes more efficient in providing necessary information.
- **Failure Analysis:** Appendix D.4 indicates that most failures of INSIGHT-O3 on O3-BENCH are attributed to the `vReasoner`'s errors (e.g., visual misperception, hallucination, improper search delegation) rather than the `vSearcher`'s inability to locate specified regions. This highlights that even with precise visual grounding, the reasoning capabilities of frontier models in complex scenarios still have room for improvement.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The INSIGHT-O3 research makes several significant contributions with far-reaching potential impacts on the field of multimodal AI:

- **Pioneering "Thinking with Images" for Open Models:** This work represents a crucial step towards equipping open multimodal foundation models with advanced "thinking with images" capabilities, effectively narrowing the performance gap with highly capable proprietary systems like OpenAI o3. By focusing on both reasoning and precise perception, it pushes the boundaries of what open models can achieve in complex visual domains.
- **Establishing a Gold-Standard Benchmark (O3-BENCH):** O3-BENCH is a critical contribution to the research community. Its design, emphasizing high-information-density images (charts and maps) and multi-hop interleaved reasoning, addresses limitations in existing benchmarks. It provides a challenging and realistic evaluation platform that will undoubtedly drive future research and development in visually-grounded reasoning, becoming a benchmark against which new multimodal models will be measured. The detailed explanations and target layouts also provide a transparent basis for verification and analysis.
- **Advancing Modular AI Architectures:** The INSIGHT-O3 multi-agent framework (`vReasoner` and `vSearcher`) offers a powerful architectural paradigm. By decomposing complex tasks into specialized, cooperative agents, it simplifies development, training, and debugging. This modularity could inspire more robust and scalable AI systems, moving beyond monolithic models towards more interpretable and manageable collaborative intelligence.
- **Revolutionizing Visual Search Capabilities:** The introduction of "generalized visual search" fundamentally expands the utility of visual search agents. By enabling `vSearcher` to locate relational, fuzzy, or conceptual regions described in free-form language across diverse image types (not just natural scenes and discrete objects), the research unlocks new possibilities for how AI can interact with and understand visual information. This capability is vital for interacting with complex digital documents, maps, and user interfaces.
- **Practical Plug-and-Play Empowerment:** The demonstrated ability of InSight-o3-vS to act as a "plug-and-play" component that significantly enhances the performance of various existing multimodal foundation models is a testament to its practical value. This allows researchers and developers to immediately integrate advanced visual search capabilities into their preferred `vReasoners` without needing to retrain entire foundation models, accelerating the deployment of more capable multimodal AI.
- **Foundation for Future Research:** The hybrid RL training algorithm and the insights gained into `vReasoner`-`vSearcher` interaction (including failure modes) lay a strong foundation for future research. This could lead to more sophisticated training methodologies, improved reward designs, and ultimately, the development of even more unified, end-to-end multimodal models that seamlessly integrate reasoning and perception at a deeper level. The approach to synthesizing training data for multi-turn SFT is also a valuable contribution for data-scarce complex tasks.
- **Direct Real-World Applications:** The focus on high-information-density images like charts, diagrams, and maps directly translates to critical real-world applications in finance, scientific research, urban planning, logistics, and education. By improving AI's ability to extract and reason with information from these sources, INSIGHT-O3 can lead to more efficient data analysis, better decision-making support, and more intuitive human-AI interfaces in complex professional domains.
