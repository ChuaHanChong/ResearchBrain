# Unified Multimodal Models as Auto-Encoders

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Peking University and Baidu developed Unified Multimodal Models as Auto-Encoders (UAE), a framework that unifies image-to-text understanding and text-to-image generation through a reconstructive reinforcement learning approach where text serves as the latent representation. This method enhanced fine-grained visual perception by up to 60.0% and achieved state-of-the-art text-to-image generation performance, alongside improved overall unification scores.

## Problem

- Existing Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) often optimize image-to-text (I2T) understanding and text-to-image (T2I) generation in isolation, missing opportunities for mutual enhancement.
- Directly integrating I2T and T2I tasks can lead to sub-optimal performance, where optimizing one objective (e.g., diffusion-based generation) degrades the other (e.g., understanding capabilities).
- This often results in brittle joint training processes or necessitates decoupling modules, preventing synergistic benefits from their intrinsic connection.

## Method

- The paper proposes unifying I2T understanding and T2I generation under an ==Auto-Encoder== (AE) perspective, where text acts as the intermediate latent representation for image reconstruction.
- A reconstructive reinforcement learning (RL) method, Unified-GRPO, is introduced to maximize the semantic similarity between an original input image and its text-reconstructed version using CLIP embeddings as the reward function.
- Unified-GRPO is applied as a post-training fine-tuning step to both hybrid autoregressive-diffusion and fully autoregressive UMM architectures, updating only the language model component while keeping visual encoders/decoders frozen.

## Results

- The Unified Auto-Encoder (UAE) achieved the highest overall unified score (86.09) on the new Unified-Bench benchmark, outperforming GPT-4o-Image (85.95) and showing consistent improvements across various vision backbones.
- UAE demonstrated state-of-the-art text-to-image generation on GenEval with an overall score of **0.89** and led on GenEval++ for compositional understanding, indicating improved complex instruction following.
- The model's understanding capabilities showed significant gains in fine-grained visual recognition, with a +**24.4%** increase in the fine-grained overall MMT-Bench score, including a +**40.0%** boost in Small Object Detection and +**60.0%** in Person Re-ID.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Treating text as an intermediate latent representation enables a principled auto-encoding framework for cross-modal understanding and generation, linking I2T and T2I conceptually.
> - Optimizing for high-fidelity image reconstruction from generated text fosters a self-evolving, mutually reinforcing feedback loop that enhances both fine-grained visual understanding and faithful image generation simultaneously.
> - Reinforcement learning, guided by semantic similarity rewards, can effectively bridge the modality gap by encouraging the language model to extract comprehensive visual semantics for robust reconstruction.
