# SpatialFusion: Endowing Unified Image Generation with Intrinsic 3D Geometric Awareness

> [!summary] Summary
> SpatialFusion introduces a framework that integrates a Spatial Transformer with Multimodal Large Language Models and Diffusion Transformers to imbue unified image generation models with intrinsic 3D geometric awareness. This approach achieved over 70% accuracy in object and camera pose tasks on the GenSpace benchmark and showed substantial improvements in spatial relation reasoning compared to prior methods.

## Problem

- Unified image generation models lack intrinsic 3D geometric awareness, leading to difficulties in accurately representing object poses, spatial relationships, and metric measurements.
- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggle to form robust 3D geometric representations during image understanding, providing only high-level semantic embeddings.
- Current diffusion backbones operate without explicit 3D structural priors, resulting in spatially incoherent images despite semantic correctness.

## Method

- **SpatialFusion** integrates a parallel ==Spatial Transformer== with an MLLM via a ==Mixture-of-Transformers== architecture to derive 3D geometric representations from semantic context.
- ==Semantic-guided attention== lets the Spatial Transformer learn intrinsic 3D geometric states, decoded into dense ==metric-depth maps==.
- A ==Depth Adapter== fuses depth maps with the diffusion model's latent space, providing explicit 3D structural priors for geometry-constrained synthesis.

## Results

- **SpatialFusion** achieved highest overall average scores on GenSpace for spatially-aware T2I generation and instruction-based editing.
- Over **70% accuracy** in Camera and Object Pose tasks; **23%** relative gain for allocentric spatial relations vs. GPT-4o.
- Modest **18.5%** parameter increase and **<3%** inference latency increase; competitive or improved performance on standard benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - While MLLMs excel at semantic understanding, their internal representations weakly encode 3D geometry; explicit integration of a specialized Spatial Transformer is necessary to derive robust 3D structural scaffolds.
> - Incorporating derived 3D geometric cues directly into the diffusion model's latent space, before patchification, effectively guides the denoising process to achieve spatially coherent and accurate image synthesis.
> - A progressive two-stage training strategy, including geometric-aware pre-training and geometry-guided joint training, enables the model to effectively learn and leverage intrinsic 3D geometric awareness without sacrificing generative quality.
