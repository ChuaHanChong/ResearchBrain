# Aligning Cyber Space with Physical World: A Comprehensive Survey on Embodied AI

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper meticulously reviews the current state and future trajectories of Embodied Artificial Intelligence, specifically focusing on the integration of Multi-modal Large Models and World Models. It systematically categorizes advancements across robots, simulators, perception, interaction, agent architectures, and sim-to-real adaptation, while introducing the ARIO (All Robots In One) dataset standard to foster the development of general-purpose embodied agents.

## Problem

- Existing surveys on Embodied AI were largely outdated, predating the significant advancements brought by Multi-modal Large Models (MLMs) and World Models (WMs) around 2023.
- A lack of comprehensive, up-to-date resources made it challenging to understand the rapidly evolving landscape of Embodied AI, particularly regarding the alignment of cyberspace and the physical world.
- The heterogeneity and scarcity of comprehensive, multi-modal robotic datasets hindered the development and generalization capabilities of robust embodied agents.

## Method

- The authors conduct an extensive, structured literature review, systematically categorizing recent works on embodied robots, simulators, perception, interaction, agent architectures, and ==sim-to-real adaptation==.
- The survey integrates the transformative impact of ==Multi-modal Large Models (MLMs)== and ==World Models (WMs)== into its analysis, offering a contemporary perspective on agent capabilities and future directions.
- A novel ==ARIO (All Robots In One)== dataset standard and a unified large-scale ARIO dataset are proposed to address data heterogeneity, volume, and cross-platform incompatibility.

## Results

- The survey provides a detailed taxonomy of embodied robots (e.g., humanoid, quadruped) and simulators (general-purpose, real-scene based), outlining their characteristics and applications.
- It synthesizes state-of-the-art methods and datasets across key research targets, including active visual perception, visual language navigation (VLN), embodied question answering (EQA), and language-guided grasping.
- The authors propose the ARIO dataset standard and a unified large-scale ARIO dataset (**3 million episodes**, **321,064 tasks**) designed to facilitate training of versatile and general-purpose embodied agents across diverse robot types and environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Embodied AI is deemed crucial for achieving Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), requiring agents to actively perceive, interact with, and reason within the physical world.
> - Multi-modal Large Models (MLMs) and World Models (WMs) are identified as foundational for enhancing embodied agents' perception, interaction, and hierarchical planning capabilities.
> - Bridging the 'sim-to-real gap' remains a central challenge, necessitating robust adaptation strategies and unified, high-quality datasets to transfer learned behaviors from simulation to physical robots effectively.
