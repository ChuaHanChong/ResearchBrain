# LLMs Meet Multimodal Generation and Editing: A Survey

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey systematically reviews how Large Language Models (LLMs) are integrated into multimodal generation and editing across images, videos, 3D content, and audio. It categorizes existing approaches, details the diverse roles LLMs play in these processes, and outlines critical safety considerations and future research directions.

## Problem

- Prior surveys on Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) largely concentrated on multimodal understanding, neglecting the rapidly evolving areas of generation and editing.
- The explosive growth of LLMs created a need for a systematic framework to understand their application in generating and modifying diverse non-textual content.
- Researchers lacked a unified overview of how LLMs enhance multimodal content creation across modalities like images, videos, 3D, and audio.

## Method

- The authors conducted a systematic review, categorizing methods into pre-LLM (==CLIP/T5==-based) and ==LLM==-based approaches for multimodal generation and editing across various modalities.
- They detailed the diverse roles LLMs assume in these generative pipelines, such as layout planners, prompt synthesizers, backbones, or ==tool-augmented orchestrators== for external tools.
- The survey also examined key technical components, relevant datasets, and dedicated sections to ==tool-augmented multimodal agents== and generative AI safety.

## Results

- The survey documented a clear paradigm shift from CLIP/T5-based methods to LLM-dominant approaches, which enable more controllable and interactive multimodal content creation.
- It elucidated how LLMs fulfill diverse, specialized roles (e.g., planners, synthesizers, backbones) that enhance generation and editing across images, videos, 3D, and audio.
- The research highlighted the critical emergence of LLM-orchestrated multimodal agents and underscored the increasing importance of generative AI safety concerns, including bias, copyright, and fabricated content.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large Language Models (LLMs) are fundamentally shifting multimodal generation and editing from basic conditioning to advanced prompt understanding, reasoning, and interactive capabilities.
> - LLMs adopt diverse and specialized roles across modalities, acting as layout planners for images, temporal prompt generators for videos, semantic guides for 3D content, and coordinated backbones for audio.
> - The rise of LLMs as orchestrators for tool-augmented multimodal agents represents a new paradigm for complex, interactive content creation, further extending their utility beyond text.
