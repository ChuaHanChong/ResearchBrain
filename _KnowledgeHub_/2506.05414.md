# SAVVY: Spatial Awareness via Audio-Visual LLMs through Seeing and Hearing

> [!summary] Summary
> A new benchmark, SAVVY-Bench, is introduced for evaluating 3D spatial reasoning in dynamic audio-visual environments, alongside SAVVY, a training-free pipeline that enhances existing AV-LLMs by integrating spatial audio and visual information within a global coordinate framework, improving Gemini-2.5-pro's overall accuracy by 7.1%.

## Problem

- Current Audio-Visual Large Language Models (AV-LLMs) struggle with dynamic 3D spatial reasoning, especially when objects move outside the visual field or require precise localization from spatial audio.
- Most existing models discard valuable spatial audio cues by downmixing multi-channel audio to monaural, failing to capture rich directional and distance information.
- Prior spatial reasoning benchmarks are limited to static scenes or lack the temporal dynamics of moving objects and the integration of spatial audio cues.

## Method

- **SAVVY** introduces ==SAVVY-Bench==, the first comprehensive benchmark for 3D spatial reasoning in dynamic audio-visual environments with synchronized spatial audio.
- The SAVVY pipeline is a training-free, two-stage approach that augments existing AV-LLMs with structured spatial reasoning capabilities.
- It constructs egocentric spatial tracks using visual and spatial audio cues (Direction-of-Arrival, Coherent-to-Diffuse Ratio), then transforms these into a unified global 2D coordinate system for accurate spatial relationship reasoning.

## Results

- Human annotators achieve **78.7%** overall accuracy on SAVVY-Bench, while current AV-LLMs show significant limitations, with a strong baseline like Gemini-2.5-pro reaching **50.9%** overall accuracy.
- The SAVVY pipeline, augmenting Gemini-2.5-pro, improves overall accuracy by **7.1%** to **58.0%**, demonstrating substantial gains in allocentric direction (+**12.3%**) and consistent improvements in distance estimation.
- Ablation studies confirm that multi-modal integration (Snapshot Descriptor, Text-guided Segmentation, Spatial Audio Cues) and global coordinate transformation are critical, with spatial audio particularly valuable for out-of-view objects.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating spatial audio and visual information within a global coordinate framework is essential for human-like 3D spatial understanding in dynamic scenes.
> - Current AV-LLMs face significant limitations in temporal grounding and object referral, hindering their 3D spatial reasoning abilities.
> - A training-free, modular pipeline can substantially enhance the spatial reasoning capabilities of existing foundation models by explicitly modeling spatial relationships.
