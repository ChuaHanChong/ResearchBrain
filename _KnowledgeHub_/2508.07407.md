# A Comprehensive Survey of Self-Evolving AI Agents: A New Paradigm Bridging Foundation Models and Lifelong Agentic Systems

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper defines and systematically reviews the emerging paradigm of self-evolving AI agents, which bridge static foundation models with dynamic lifelong adaptability. It introduces a unified conceptual framework and a comprehensive taxonomy of evolution techniques, mapping the progression towards continuous self-improvement in AI systems.

## Problem

- Most existing AI agent systems rely on manually configured settings that remain static post-deployment, limiting their adaptability to dynamic, continuously evolving real-world environments.
- Current surveys on AI agents have not sufficiently covered the crucial aspects of agent self-evolution and continual adaptation, leaving a gap in the literature for lifelong, autonomous AI systems.
- Manual reconfiguration of agents in ever-changing scenarios is impractical and does not scale, necessitating autonomous adaptation mechanisms.

## Method

- The paper formally defines "Self-Evolving AI Agents" and proposes a ==unified conceptual framework== (System Inputs, Agent System, Environment, Optimisers) to abstract their iterative feedback loop.
- It introduces =="Three Laws of Self-Evolving AI Agents"== (Endure, Excel, Evolve) as guiding principles for safe, performance-preserving, and autonomous optimization.
- The survey systematically categorizes and reviews existing evolution and optimization techniques for ==single-agent and multi-agent systems==, as well as domain-specific applications.

## Results

- The survey establishes a unified conceptual framework for self-evolving agents, comprising System Inputs, Agent System, Environment, and Optimisers, providing a consistent lens for analysis.
- It yields a detailed taxonomy of agent evolution and optimization techniques, categorizing methods for single-agent and multi-agent systems and by specific components targeted for improvement.
- The paper outlines a fundamental paradigm shift in AI development towards continuous, autonomous adaptation, emphasizing the critical role of rigorous, multi-dimensional evaluation and continuous safety monitoring.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Self-evolving AI agents represent a new paradigm, bridging the gap between static foundation models and lifelong agentic systems capable of continuous adaptation.
> - The progression of LLM-based systems moves from Model Offline Pretraining (MOP) through online adaptation (MOA) and multi-agent orchestration (MAO) to the ultimate vision of Multi-Agent Self-Evolving (MASE).
> - Effective self-evolution requires a structured feedback loop, where optimizers continuously refine agent components (LLM behavior, prompts, memory, tools, workflows, communication) based on environmental feedback.
