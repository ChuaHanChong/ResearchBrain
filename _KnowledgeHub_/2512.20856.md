# NVIDIA Nemotron 3: Efficient and Open Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA introduces Nemotron 3, a family of hybrid Mamba-Transformer Mixture-of-Experts LLMs, achieving up to 3.3x higher inference throughput and 1 million token context length with state-of-the-art accuracy for agentic AI. The project openly releases model weights, training software, recipes, and over 10 trillion tokens of training data.

## Problem

- There is a growing demand for AI models capable of complex reasoning, planning, and multi-step tool use (agentic AI) that are both efficient and robust.
- Traditional Transformer LLMs face significant challenges regarding high inference costs, latency, and memory consumption, particularly with increasing context lengths.
- Many advanced AI applications require processing extremely long input sequences, often beyond what current models can efficiently handle.

## Method

- Developed **Nemotron 3**, a hybrid ==Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Mamba-Transformer== architecture, predominantly interleaving MoE layers with efficient ==Mamba-2 layers== to optimize inference throughput.
- Introduced ==LatentMoE==, a hardware-aware expert design for larger models to improve accuracy per byte, and utilized ==NVFP4== for stable 4-bit floating-point pretraining.
- Implemented ==multi-environment reinforcement learning post-training== on diverse tasks simultaneously to imbue strong agentic capabilities, and integrated ==Multi-Token Prediction== for enhanced reasoning and speculative decoding.

## Results

- Achieved **3.3x** higher inference throughput for common reasoning workloads compared to a similarly sized Transformer MoE model (Qwen3-30B-A3B).
- Demonstrated state-of-the-art accuracy across leading reasoning benchmarks (e.g., Arena-Hard-v2-Avg, AIME25, SWE-Bench) while effectively leveraging up to **1 million token context length**.
- **LatentMoE** showed consistent accuracy improvements across downstream tasks, and **NVFP4** pretraining maintained a train/validation loss difference of **<1%** compared to BF16, confirming stability and accuracy.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining Mamba, Transformer, and MoE architectures, with Mamba layers for efficiency, effectively pushes the accuracy-to-inference-throughput frontier for LLMs.
> - Hardware-aware architectural designs like LatentMoE and ultra-low precision training with NVFP4 can yield substantial efficiency gains without compromising model quality or stability.
> - Simultaneous multi-environment reinforcement learning and specialized long-context training can robustly develop agentic capabilities and support unprecedented context lengths, fostering open AI innovation.
