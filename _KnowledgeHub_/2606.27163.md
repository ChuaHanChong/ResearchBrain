# Learning to Fold: prizewinning solution at LeHome Challenge 2026 (1st place online, 2nd offline)

> [!summary] Summary
> An independent researcher developed a robust robotic system for bimanual garment folding, securing 1st place in the online round and 2nd place in the real-world final of the LeHome Challenge 2026. The solution combines Vision-Language-Action models with reinforcement learning techniques and extensive engineering for sim-to-real transfer, achieving a 79.63% success rate in simulation.

## Problem

- Effectively manipulating highly deformable objects like garments with bimanual robots due to their high degrees of freedom and complex state estimation.
- Overcoming sparse, binary success rewards in robotic tasks, which hinder effective reinforcement learning and policy robustness.
- Ensuring robotic policies generalize to unseen deformable objects and across different physical robot setups without direct access to target hardware.

## Method

- An asynchronous, distributed "flywheel" architecture combines Advantage-Weighted Regression (AWR), RECAP-style advantage conditioning, and human-in-the-loop (DAgger) learning.
- A dense reward signal is constructed from sparse binary outcomes using intermediate checkpoints, success probability predictions, and completion shaping.
- Extensive simulation-centric data collection, heavy domain randomization, and aggressive data augmentation enhance generalization and robustness, complemented by inference-time optimization using a Thompson sampling bandit and best-of-N candidate selection.

## Results

- Secured 1st place in the online (simulation) round of LeHome Challenge 2026 with a **79.63%** overall success rate, surpassing the second-placed team by **6.1** percentage points.
- Achieved 2nd place in the real-world final at ICRA 2026, scoring 865 out of a possible 1080 points.
- The system demonstrated high performance across diverse garment types in simulation, including **93.5%** success on short pants, while maintaining identical retrieval quality.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Vision-Language-Action policies can be significantly enhanced by integrating reinforcement learning techniques, especially when augmented with auxiliary heads that act as a value function for success prediction.
> - Robustness and generalization in deformable object manipulation are critically achieved through meticulous engineering practices, including distributed training pipelines, diverse data collection, and extensive domain randomization.
> - Effective sim-to-real transfer, even without direct access to the target robot, is feasible by focusing on environment alignment, targeted model fine-tuning, and robust motion intensity matching across data sources.
