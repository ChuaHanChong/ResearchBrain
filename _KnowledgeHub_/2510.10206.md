# It Takes Two: Learning Interactive Whole-Body Control Between Humanoid Robots

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Shanghai Jiao Tong University developed Harmanoid, a framework enabling two humanoid robots to perform physically grounded, interactive whole-body motions. It achieves a 0.92 success rate in complex interactive tasks, significantly outperforming single-robot imitation methods.

## Problem

- Existing humanoid motion imitation methods primarily focus on single-robot scenarios, neglecting multi-agent interactions.
- Applying single-humanoid control to multiple robots leads to an 'isolation issue,' causing collisions, interpenetrations, and uncoordinated movements.
- There was a lack of robust frameworks to transfer and control complex, physically grounded human-to-human interactions onto two humanoid robots.

## Method

- ==Introduce Harmanoid==, a two-stage framework for dual-humanoid motion imitation, combining contact-aware motion retargeting with an interaction-driven reinforcement learning controller.
- Implement contact-aware motion retargeting to adapt human interaction data to robot morphology, preserving inter-body coordination and minimizing penetrations.
- Develop an interaction-driven motion controller using augmented observations, shared interaction-aware rewards, and specific contact rewards to enable physically consistent multi-robot behaviors.

## Results

- Harmanoid achieved a **0.92** success rate in dual-humanoid interactive tasks, significantly outperforming single-humanoid baselines that failed to execute these motions (**0.00**-**0.20** success rates).
- The contact-aware motion retargeting component produced physically more feasible reference motions, evidenced by a **0.74** tracking success rate and lower errors (E_g-MPJPE: **157.6** mm) compared to other retargeting methods.
- Ablation studies confirmed that all components of the interaction-driven controller, including interactive rewards, contact rewards, and curriculum learning, collectively contributed to the highest success rate (**0.92**) and contact fidelity (**0.18** Contact F1).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Treating humanoid robots independently for interactive tasks leads to catastrophic failures, highlighting the necessity of explicitly modeling inter-agent dynamics.
> - A systematic approach that first generates contact-aware, interaction-preserving reference motions via retargeting and then refines them with an interaction-driven controller is more effective.
> - Integrating interaction-aware rewards and real-time contact feedback within a reinforcement learning framework enables robots to learn physically plausible and coordinated interactive behaviors.
