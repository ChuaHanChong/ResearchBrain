# Hierarchical Process Reward Models are Symbolic Vision Learners

> [!summary] Summary
> This research from Adelaide AIML and Ohio State University presents a self-supervised symbolic auto-encoder that translates diagrams into interpretable, hierarchical logic forms of geometric primitives. The system enhances diagram reconstruction, perception, and mathematical reasoning by generating fine-grained, rule-based rewards for multi-level parsing and stabilizing reinforcement learning with novel techniques.

## Problem

- Existing pixel-based vision models struggle to capture the precise geometric primitives and hierarchical relationships inherent in diagrams, leading to limited interpretable understanding.
- Fine-grained supervision methods like Process Reward Models often rely on costly human annotations or unreliable LLM-as-a-judge approaches, hindering scalable development.
- Reinforcement learning for diagram reconstruction suffers from poor exploration and training collapse due to sparse visual rewards and low reward variance in semantically sparse diagrams.

## Method

- A self-supervised ==symbolic auto-encoder== (**SymVAE**) was developed to encode diagrams into a structured latent space of logic forms, which are then reconstructed by a deterministic rendering engine.
- A Hierarchical Process Reward Model (HPRM) was introduced, featuring cost-free, rule-based reward functions (SymHPR) that supervise learning across multiple abstraction levels with explicit geometric dependencies.
- The training pipeline incorporates cold-start pre-training, reinforcement learning with HPRM, and self-supervised learning with visual rewards, stabilized by hard negative contrastive learning and power normalization annealing.

## Results

- SymVAE-**7B** achieved a **98.2%** reduction in MSE for geometric diagram reconstruction and surpassed GPT-4o on chart reconstruction with a **6.5%** improvement in layout accuracy.
- On the MathGlance perception benchmark, SymVAE-**7B** obtained **72.6%** average accuracy, representing a **13.4%** absolute improvement over Qwen2.5-VL-**7B**, with **100%** accuracy in relation identification.
- For mathematical reasoning, SymVAE+CoTs-**7B** achieved **51.8%** overall accuracy on MathVerse and **79.4%** on GeoQA, outperforming Qwen2.5-VL-**7B** and other baselines, indicating improved visual grounding through symbolic representations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Representing diagrams as hierarchical symbolic logic forms, rather than continuous pixel-level features, enables more interpretable, structured, and accurate understanding for diagrammatic content.
> - Hierarchical Process Rewards, which condition higher-level rewards on the correctness of lower-level primitives, are essential for enforcing step-level parsing consistencies and improving understanding of complex relations.
> - Stabilization techniques, such as hard negative contrastive learning and power normalization annealing, effectively mitigate the training instability of reinforcement learning in domains with sparse rewards like diagram reconstruction.
