# Self-Evolving Spatial Reasoning in Vision Language Models via Geometric Logic Consistency

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai AI Lab and collaborators developed SAGE, a self-evolving framework that instills robust spatial reasoning in Vision-Language Models by enforcing geometric and linguistic duality consistency. The method improved performance across multiple benchmarks, achieving gains such as +15.0 on MindCube, while using less training data than prior methods.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) exhibit fragile spatial reasoning abilities, often struggling with accurate representations of spatial relationships and object positions, leading to 'spatial hallucinations.'
- Existing VLMs may provide correct answers for specific instances but fail when presented with geometrically or linguistically transformed versions of the same input, indicating a lack of genuine logical understanding ('pseudo-understanding').
- Current reinforcement learning objectives for VLM reasoning do not directly enforce logical consistency across semantically equivalent, paired formulations of spatial relations.

## Method

- Introduced Spatial Alignment via Geometric Evolution (**SAGE**), a model-agnostic and data-efficient post-training framework for VLMs.
- Formalized 'duality consistency' as a training signal, enforcing logical coherence across original-dual input pairs generated through visual (e.g., horizontal reflection, rotation) and linguistic (e.g., logical negation, option permutation) transformations.
- Developed a self-evolving operation pool that dynamically probes the model for inconsistencies, identifies challenging duality operations, and adaptively prioritizes them for active training via a consistency-augmented Group Relative Policy Optimization (==GRPO==).

## Results

- SAGE consistently improved performance across six video understanding benchmarks (e.g., +8.4 on MVBench, +7.6 on VideoMME) and seven spatial reasoning benchmarks (e.g., +15.0 on MindCube, +14.6 on OmniSpatial) using Qwen2.5-VL-7B.
- The method achieved superior or competitive performance against larger state-of-the-art models like InternVL-2.5-8B and SpatialLadder-3B, while utilizing less than **37%** of the training data compared to some prior GRPO approaches.
- Ablation studies confirmed that both visual and linguistic duality components contribute to performance gains, with visual duality showing a stronger impact on spatially grounded tasks, and the self-evolving pool efficiently prioritized challenging operations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Enforcing logical consistency through geometric and linguistic duality operations can mitigate 'pseudo-understanding' in VLMs, fostering a more robust and generalizable spatial reasoning capability.
> - A self-evolving mechanism for managing duality operations is effective, allowing the training process to dynamically adapt and target specific reasoning vulnerabilities of the model.
> - Integrating duality consistency rewards into a reinforcement learning framework like GRPO provides a powerful inductive bias that improves spatial reasoning and generalization across diverse tasks and transformations.
