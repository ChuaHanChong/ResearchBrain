# RePIC: Reinforced Post-Training for Personalizing Multi-Modal Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> RePIC introduces a reinforcement learning framework for post-training Multi-Modal Large Language Models (MLLMs), enhancing their ability to generate personalized image captions, particularly in complex multi-concept scenarios. This approach significantly outperforms existing supervised fine-tuning methods in robustness and generalizability while being more data-efficient.

## Problem

- Multi-Modal Large Language Models (MLLMs) lack robust personalization capabilities for incorporating user-specific concepts from reference images into captions.
- Existing Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) based personalization methods are data-intensive, costly, and struggle with faithfulness, especially in complex multi-concept images and out-of-distribution (OOD) scenarios.
- SFT approaches tend to exhibit poor visual recognition, often failing to differentiate objects or merely replicating provided text, even when visually inaccurate.

## Method

- A reinforcement learning (RL)-based post-training framework, **RePIC**, is introduced, employing ==Group Relative== Policy Optimization (GRPO) to enhance MLLM personalization.
- Three verifiable rewards are designed: Object Consistency Tuning (OCT) for robust visual recognition, Visual Localization Tuning (VLT) for improved object localization, and Identity Consistency Tuning (ICT) for consistent personalized generation.
- GRPO utilizes these rewards to guide MLLM learning and updates, incorporating KL-divergence regularization to maintain base model capabilities while reducing the need for large-scale supervised data.

## Results

- RePIC demonstrated superior performance over supervised fine-tuning baselines in multi-concept personalized grounding, particularly excelling in 2-concept and 4-concept out-of-domain scenarios.
- Human preference evaluations indicated higher quality, faithfulness, and detail in RePIC's generated personalized captions compared to other models.
- Ablation studies confirmed the necessity of all verifiable reward components (OCT, VLT, ICT) for robust personalization, showing increased resistance to incorrect visual demonstrations and reduced "copy-and-paste" behavior from reference texts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Reinforcement learning with carefully designed verifiable rewards offers a data-efficient and robust approach to MLLM personalization, outperforming supervised fine-tuning.
> - Targeted reinforcement of visual recognition and identity consistency is essential for MLLMs to generate faithful personalized captions, particularly in complex, multi-concept scenarios.
> - KL-divergence regularization during RL post-training allows for enhanced personalization capabilities without degrading the base MLLM's existing general image captioning abilities.
