# DeepEyes: Incentivizing "Thinking with Images" via Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> DeepEyes enables Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to perform 'thinking with images' by dynamically integrating visual re-perception into their reasoning process through end-to-end reinforcement learning. This approach, developed by researchers from Xiaohongshu Inc. and Xi'an Jiaotong University, achieves notable performance improvements, including an 18.9% accuracy gain on V* Bench and a 1.8% reduction in hallucinations on POPE compared to baselines.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) primarily rely on text-based Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, treating visual input as static and limiting dynamic visual re-evaluation.
- Prior methods for integrating visual tools often use rigid, pre-defined workflows or require separate, specialized models, leading to sub-optimal performance and poor generalization.
- Lack of a native mechanism for iterative visual reasoning within VLMs, unlike human cognition which seamlessly integrates visual perception and re-evaluation during thought.

## Method

- Introduces ==Interleaved Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (iMCoT)==, allowing the VLM to autonomously invoke an image zoom-in tool to crop and re-incorporate specific visual regions during reasoning.
- Utilizes an agentic ==Reinforcement Learning framework== with a ==composite reward design==, including a conditional tool usage bonus, to incentivize effective visual tool utilization without cold-start supervised fine-tuning.
- Employs a ==tool-use-oriented data selection== mechanism that prioritizes samples where visual tool use provides significant information gain, drawing from diverse fine-grained, chart, and reasoning datasets.

## Results

- DeepEyes achieved **90.1%** accuracy on V* Bench, an **18.9%** gain over the baseline Qwen2.5-VL 7B, and improved HR-Bench 4K by **6.3%** and HR-Bench 8K by **7.3%**.
- It demonstrated improved visual grounding and reduced hallucinations, achieving **87.7%** accuracy on POPE (a **1.8%** increase) by enabling the model to zoom in and verify object presence.
- Qualitative analysis revealed emergent human-like reasoning patterns, including visual search, comparison, and confirmation, and distinct stages of tool-use learning during RL.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The ability for Vision-Language Models to perform dynamic visual re-perception (thinking with images) can emerge natively through end-to-end reinforcement learning.
> - A conditional tool usage reward, given only when a tool is used and the final answer is correct, effectively incentivizes efficient and purposeful tool exploitation during RL training.
> - The model's tool-calling behavior evolves through distinct stages during RL, from exploration to high-frequency usage, culminating in efficient and selective tool utilization.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) has witnessed rapid advancements, enabling multimodal understanding through architectures that combine vision encoders with large language models (LLMs). Contemporary VLMs, often leveraging techniques like Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, can tackle increasingly complex tasks. However, a significant limitation persists: these models primarily conduct their reasoning in the language modality, treating visual information as an input to be processed, rather than an integral part of an interleaved thought process. This contrasts sharply with human cognition, where "thinking with images" involves dynamic, sequential visual fixations that support accurate perceptual decision-making.

Previous attempts to integrate visual information into VLM reasoning fall into several categories:
- **Pre-defined Workflows and Auxiliary Models:** Some approaches involve hard-coding pipelines or utilizing specialized external models (e.g., for region-of-interest localization, latent feature regeneration, or external knowledge integration). While providing some multimodal capabilities, these modular designs often suffer from sub-optimal performance due to the lack of global optimization and limited generalization across diverse tasks. They require substantial supervised fine-tuning (SFT) data for intermediate reasoning steps, which is difficult to acquire.
- **Extension of Text-only CoT:** Many RL-based reasoning approaches in MLLMs primarily extend text-only reasoning capabilities to multimodal tasks, rather than fundamentally altering how visual information is processed within the reasoning chain.

A notable recent milestone is the **OpenAI o3 model**, which successfully demonstrated a more dynamic integration of visual information and image manipulation tools (like zoom-in) within its reasoning process, transcending the language-modality confinement. This approach represented a significant step towards true multimodal reasoning, combining textual CoT and image manipulation in a naturally interleaved fashion. However, the internal mechanisms of o3 remain proprietary and undisclosed to the open-source community, creating a gap for public research to explore and replicate such capabilities.

"DeepEyes" precisely addresses this gap. It positions itself as an **open-source solution** that learns to "think with images" natively, without relying on pre-collected reasoning data for cold-start SFT or external specialized models/APIs. Instead, DeepEyes leverages the VLM's *own grounding capability* as an intrinsic function, enabling an "active perception" mechanism that strategically grounds reasoning in visual information. By doing so, DeepEyes contributes to the broader landscape by:
1. **Democratizing advanced multimodal reasoning:** Offering an open-source alternative to proprietary models like o3.
2. **Advancing end-to-end learning:** Demonstrating that "thinking with images" can emerge natively through reinforcement learning, without the need for complex, manually designed workflows or extensive supervised data for intermediate steps.
3. **Enhancing VLM autonomy:** Empowering the model to *decide* when and how to use visual input, leading to a more flexible and adaptable reasoning process, which is a crucial step towards more agentic AI systems.
4. **Improving efficiency and generalizability:** Overcoming the limitations of modular designs by allowing global optimization and dynamic adaptation across tasks.

This work marks a significant step towards developing more human-like, versatile, and transparent multimodal AI systems, pushing the boundaries beyond text-dominant reasoning in VLMs.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The central objective of the "DeepEyes" research is to enable Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to genuinely "think with images," deeply integrating visual information into their reasoning processes in a manner that mirrors human cognitive patterns. This core aim is driven by several motivations and broken down into specific, measurable objectives.

**Key Motivations:**

1. **Bridging the Human-AI Reasoning Gap:** Human reasoning intrinsically combines visual and cognitive processes, utilizing sequential visual fixations to extract information and inform decision-making. Current VLMs, despite their multimodal understanding, largely confine their thought processes to the language modality. The researchers are motivated to close this gap, enabling AI to process and reason with visual data more naturally and effectively.
2. **Overcoming Limitations of Existing VLM Approaches:**
 - **Text-centric Bias:** Many advanced VLMs, even those employing Chain-of-Thought (CoT), primarily rely on textual reasoning, leading to suboptimal performance in tasks requiring fine-grained visual details.
 - **Suboptimal Modular Designs:** Prior attempts to incorporate visual information often involve pre-defined workflows or auxiliary external models. These modular solutions are criticized for their lack of end-to-end optimization, resulting in sub-optimal performance, limited generalization, and a dependence on complex, often manually crafted pipelines.
 - **Lack of Transparency in Advanced Models:** While proprietary models like OpenAI's o3 have demonstrated advanced "thinking with images" capabilities, their internal mechanisms remain undisclosed, hindering open scientific progress. The authors are motivated to provide an open-source, transparent alternative.
3. **Reducing Data and Training Complexity:** Existing methods often require substantial supervised fine-tuning (SFT) data for intermediate reasoning steps, which is challenging and costly to acquire. A key motivation is to develop a system that can learn complex visual reasoning *without* such pre-collected reasoning data for a "cold-start" SFT.

**Key Objectives:**

1. **Native Emergence of "Thinking with Images":** To develop a model (DeepEyes) where the ability to "think with images" emerges natively through end-to-end reinforcement learning, rather than being explicitly programmed or pre-trained on specific reasoning data.
2. **Intrinsic Grounding Capability:** To leverage the model's own visual grounding ability as an internal, intrinsic function for active perception, eliminating reliance on external specialized models or APIs.
3. **Strategic Active Perception:** To enable the model to learn *when* and *how* to strategically ground its reasoning in visual information, dynamically adapting its visual exploration based on the task and context. This involves generating image grounding coordinates and cropping relevant regions adaptively.
4. **Tailored Training Mechanisms:** To design and implement specific data selection and reward strategies that effectively incentivize and guide the model's active perception behavior during reinforcement learning, particularly without a cold-start SFT.
5. **Seamless Interleaved Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (iMCoT):** To create a framework where visual and textual reasoning are seamlessly integrated and interleaved throughout the thought process, allowing for more accurate perceptual decision-making.
6. **Demonstrate Performance and Generalizability:** To achieve significant performance gains on a wide array of benchmarks, including high-resolution perception, general perception and reasoning, grounding, hallucination mitigation, and challenging mathematical reasoning tasks, thereby validating the effectiveness and robustness of the iMCoT approach.
7. **Analyze Emergent Behaviors:** To observe and analyze the evolution of the active perception strategy during RL training and identify diverse, human-like reasoning patterns (e.g., visual search, comparison, confirmation, hallucination mitigation).

By pursuing these objectives, DeepEyes aims to push the frontier of multimodal AI, creating more intelligent, adaptable, and transparent systems that can reason more effectively with the rich visual information present in the world.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

DeepEyes proposes a novel methodology centered on an "interleaved Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (iMCoT)" paradigm, trained end-to-end via reinforcement learning (RL). This approach is designed to natively cultivate the ability to "think with images" without relying on pre-collected reasoning data for supervised fine-tuning (SFT) or external specialized models.

#### 3.1. DeepEyes Model Architecture and iMCoT

DeepEyes is built upon a unified multimodal large language model, specifically initialized from Qwen2.5-VL-7B (and also scaled to 32B in experiments). Given a user question and an initial image ($I_0$), the model operates in an agentic fashion:
- **Autonomous Decision-Making:** After each step of textual Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, DeepEyes autonomously decides whether to generate a final answer directly or to perform an "image zoom-in" for further visual inspection.
- **Active Perception (Zoom-in Operation):** If a zoom-in is chosen, the model generates a list of bounding box coordinates (e.g., `[x1, y1, x2, y2]`) and optionally an object label. These coordinates define regions of interest within the original image.
- **Image Cropping and Context Integration:** The specified regions are then cropped from the original image. The resulting cropped images (e.g., $I_{t1}$, $I_{t2}$) are appended to the ongoing reasoning trajectory (context). This allows the model to reason over all previously observed text and visual information.
- **Iterative Interaction:** DeepEyes can perform this active perception process multiple times, iteratively refining its visual understanding until it is confident enough to conclude with a final answer. This iterative capability is crucial for fine-grained perception, especially when dealing with small, blurry, or difficult-to-recognize objects.

**Advantages of iMCoT:**
- **Simplicity in Training:** Requires only question-answer pairs, reducing data collection complexity compared to workflow-based methods that need extensive SFT data for intermediate steps.
- **Enhanced Generalizability:** Learns to dynamically select optimal reasoning processes through RL, making it more robust across diverse tasks.
- **Global Optimization:** End-to-end training allows for joint optimization of textual CoTs and visual action decisions, potentially leading to a global optimum.
- **Multimodal Integration:** Seamlessly interleaves visual and textual information, leading to more accurate perceptual decision-making.
- **Native Tool Calling:** Encapsulates the model’s native grounding capability as an internal tool, enabling implicit optimization that external-tool paradigms cannot achieve.

#### 3.2. Agentic Reinforcement Learning

DeepEyes' "thinking with images" capability is incentivized and enhanced via end-to-end RL training using outcome reward signals.
- **Rollout Formulation (Extended MDP):** The traditional Markov Decision Process (MDP) for text-only CoT (state = input prompt + generated tokens, action = next token) is extended. In agentic RL, the state $s_t$ at each step $t$ includes both accumulated text tokens ($X_{\le t}$) and image observation tokens ($I_{\le t}$) from external function calls (zoom-ins). The action $a_t$ is still the next token sampled from the VLM policy $\pi_\theta(a|s_t)$, but this policy now guides both text generation and tool invocation.
- **Reward Design (Sparse, Outcome-Driven):** Since intermediate visual actions lack step-level supervision, the entire reasoning trajectory is evaluated based on the final outcome and the strategic use of active perception. The total reward $R(\tau)$ for a trajectory $\tau$ comprises three parts:
 - **Accuracy Reward ($R_{acc}$):** A binary reward for whether the final answer is correct.
 - **Format Reward ($R_{format}$):** A penalty for poorly structured outputs.
 - **Conditional Bonus ($R_{tool}$):** A crucial component that grants a bonus *only if* the final answer is correct ($R_{acc}(\tau) > 0$) AND at least one active perception step was triggered within the trajectory. This conditional design incentivizes *effective* and *perception-aware* reasoning, discouraging unnecessary actions.
- **Optimization:** Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), a method proven effective for diverse tasks, is used. A token-wise loss mask is applied during training to ignore loss on observation tokens (which are not generated by the model itself).

#### 3.3. Training Data Curation

To address the challenge of initial sampling efficiency without an SFT cold start, a tailored data curation strategy is employed:
- **Data Collection:** A diverse corpus is constructed from three sources:
 - **V* training set (Wu & Xie, 2024):** 22k samples (47%) for fine-grained perception and visual grounding, derived from COCO2017.
 - **ArxivQA (Li et al., 2024b):** 14k samples (30%) for task and image diversity, featuring scientific plots, diagrams, and charts.
 - **ThinkLite-VL (Wang et al., 2025b):** 11k samples (23%) for strengthening challenging reasoning, including arithmetic, commonsense, and problem-solving.
- **Multi-Stage Data Selection:** A pipeline ensures the dataset effectively bootstraps active perception:
 - **Difficulty Curation:** Samples deemed too trivial (100% accuracy) or overly challenging (0% accuracy) by a baseline VLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) are removed.
 - **Standardization and Verification:** Questions are standardized to an open-ended format, and data is verified to eliminate incorrect labels.
 - **Perception-Utility Filter:** Applied specifically to the fine-grained perception data, this filter retains only samples solvable via active perception with ground-truth regions. This step maximizes informational gain and significantly boosts initial RL sampling efficiency. Chart and general reasoning data are preserved in their original, rigorously processed form.

This comprehensive methodology, combining an agentic iMCoT architecture, a carefully designed conditional reward mechanism, and a targeted data curation strategy, enables DeepEyes to learn sophisticated visual reasoning capabilities effectively from scratch.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

DeepEyes demonstrates significant advancements across various multimodal benchmarks, revealing compelling insights into the emergence of visual reasoning capabilities through reinforcement learning.

#### 4.1. Quantitative Performance Gains

DeepEyes, particularly with a 7B parameter model, consistently outperforms strong baselines, including other open-source models and even some larger parameter models, showcasing its effectiveness:

- **High-Resolution Benchmarks:** Tasks like V* and HR-Bench (4K, 8K) demand fine-grained visual understanding of small objects in large images. DeepEyes achieves remarkable improvements:
 - **V***: 90.1% accuracy, an **18.9% gain** over the Qwen2.5-VL 7B baseline.
 - **HR-Bench-4K**: **6.3% improvement**.
 - **HR-Bench-8K**: **7.3% improvement**.
 These results underscore that simple RL can effectively unlock high-resolution visual reasoning without elaborate, predefined pipelines. In some cases, DeepEyes-7B even surpasses the performance of models like ZoomEye (a workflow-based method) and the 32B version of Qwen2.5-VL.

- **General Perception and Reasoning (MME-RealWorld-Lite):** DeepEyes 7B achieves top performance, surpassing both 7B and 32B versions of Qwen2.5-VL, demonstrating superior real-world perception and reasoning capabilities across various subtasks (OCR, Scene Recognition, Document Understanding, etc.).

- **Grounding and Hallucination Benchmarks:** The iMCoT mechanism significantly enhances general visual capabilities:
 - **Grounding (refCOCO, ReasonSeg):** Improved accuracy across various grounding tasks.
 - **Hallucination (POPE):** Substantially reduces object hallucination. This is attributed to the model's ability to actively focus on regions of interest and analyze cropped areas in detail, leading to more confident verification of object presence and mitigating language bias.

- **Challenging Reasoning Benchmarks:** DeepEyes shows consistent performance improvements on complex multimodal reasoning tasks, including mathematical problem-solving benchmarks (MathVista, MathVerse, WeMath, DynaMath, LogicVista), benefiting from its integrated chain-of-thought mechanism.

#### 4.2. Key Findings: Emergence of Active Perception

A crucial finding is the distinct evolution of the model’s active perception strategy during end-to-end RL training, observed through analysis of tool call count, response length, and grounding Intersection-over-Union (IoU) on the V* dataset:

- **Stage 1: Initial Exploration (Steps 0–20):** The model begins to invoke zoom-ins but lacks a coherent strategy. Active perception count and response length initially rise (exploratory behavior) but grounding IoU remains low, indicating ineffective use. A subsequent drop in response length suggests the model is learning to streamline descriptions while acquiring basic perception skills.
- **Stage 2: High-Frequency Engagement (Steps 20–45):** The model enters an intensive phase of active perception, frequently using visual information to boost rewards. Grounding IoU improves, and longer responses with frequent visual interactions suggest a "broad sweep" strategy—over-querying the environment to externalize reasoning. This shows recognition of active perception's value, though not yet efficient.
- **Stage 3: Efficient Utilization (Steps 45–80):** DeepEyes adopts a more selective and precise approach, reducing query frequency and response length while maintaining high grounding and task accuracy. This signifies a "compact visual-linguistic policy," where active perception is invoked only when truly needed, complementing internal reasoning with targeted visual confirmation. High IoU with fewer queries reflects implicit planning.

This progression demonstrates that the model learns to integrate active perception strategically, evolving from broad exploration to targeted exploitation, and highlighting the potential for perception-augmented VLMs to achieve scalable and interpretable multimodal reasoning.

#### 4.3. Diverse Thinking Patterns

During RL training, DeepEyes developed diverse iMCoT reasoning patterns that closely mirror human visual cognition:

- **Visual Search:** Actively scanning different image regions and gathering visual clues for complex problems (e.g., identifying small or hard-to-recognize objects).
- **Visual Comparison:** Iteratively zooming in on multiple objects or regions to facilitate close examination and comparison before drawing conclusions (e.g., comparing data variability across chart sections).
- **Visual Confirmation:** Starting with uncertainty but gradually building confidence by zooming in on image details to gather evidence and resolve doubts (e.g., confirming the shape of an object).
- **Hallucination Mitigation:** Focusing on visual details through active perception to counter linguistic biases and re-ground reasoning, effectively fact-checking its linguistic assumptions against visual reality (e.g., correcting a misidentified object color).

#### 4.4. Ablation Studies and Scaling

- **Impact of Tool Reward:** The conditional bonus in the reward function is crucial. Without it, active perception diminishes or remains static. Conditioning the bonus on *correct* answers leads to sustained, effective, and informative active perceptions, achieving the highest accuracy.
- **Scaling Model Size:** DeepEyes shows strong scalability. From 7B to 32B parameters, the performance gap over the baseline widens, and the larger model exhibits more sophisticated emergent behaviors with longer reasoning chains and higher grounding precision.
- **Scaling Challenging Reasoning Data:** Increasing the proportion of challenging reasoning data in the training set mutually reinforces both perception (V*) and mathematical reasoning abilities, suggesting that stronger abstract reasoning enables more sophisticated visual-grounded thinking.
- **Zero-Shot Tool Generalization:** DeepEyes demonstrates its extensibility by integrating a new "rotate" tool simply through a system prompt, without retraining or architectural changes. This yielded a 3.5% performance gain on a rotated OCR benchmark, proving the framework's flexibility for tool integration.
- **Ablation on iMCoT:** Comparing with an RL baseline trained with text-only CoT, iMCoT consistently achieves superior performance across all benchmarks, particularly pronounced on ultra-high-resolution tasks like HR-8K. This decisively proves that interleaving visual perception with textual reasoning is essential for robust performance on tasks requiring fine-grained visual detail.

These findings collectively affirm the efficacy of DeepEyes' approach in enabling deep, adaptive, and human-like multimodal reasoning in VLMs.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

DeepEyes represents a substantial contribution to the field of Vision-Language Models, offering both immediate and long-term significance and impact:

1. **Paradigm Shift in Multimodal Reasoning:** By demonstrating that "thinking with images" can emerge natively through end-to-end reinforcement learning, DeepEyes challenges the conventional reliance on predominantly text-based reasoning or complex, modular workflows in VLMs. This paves the way for a more integrated, dynamic, and human-like approach to multimodal AI.

2. **Open-Source Advancement:** The release of DeepEyes as an open-source solution is highly impactful. It democratizes access to advanced multimodal reasoning capabilities previously showcased by proprietary models like OpenAI o3, whose internal mechanisms remained undisclosed. This will undoubtedly foster further innovation, research, and development within the open-source community, accelerating progress in the field.

3. **Reduced Data and Training Complexity:** The ability to learn "thinking with images" without requiring pre-collected reasoning data for cold-start supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is a major breakthrough. This significantly reduces the data acquisition burden and complexity associated with training sophisticated VLMs, making advanced multimodal AI more accessible and scalable.

4. **Enhanced Model Reliability and Trustworthiness:** The demonstrated improvements in visual grounding accuracy and hallucination mitigation are critical for building reliable AI systems. By forcing the model to re-engage with visual evidence and fact-check its linguistic assumptions, DeepEyes contributes to more grounded and trustworthy VLM outputs, which is crucial for real-world deployment in sensitive applications.

5. **Interpretability and Human-AI Alignment:** The observation of distinct evolutionary stages in active perception and the emergence of diverse, human-like thinking patterns (visual search, comparison, confirmation, hallucination mitigation) offer valuable insights into how AI models develop complex cognitive strategies. This contributes to the broader goal of building more transparent and interpretable AI systems that reason in ways understandable and relatable to humans.

6. **Broad Applicability Across Domains:** The significant performance gains across a wide range of benchmarks—from high-resolution perception to general reasoning, mathematical problem-solving, grounding, and hallucination mitigation—highlight DeepEyes' versatility. This suggests broad applicability in various real-world scenarios, including:
 - **E-commerce and Content Platforms:** (relevant to Xiaohongshu) Enhancing visual search, product recommendations, content moderation, and user interaction by accurately understanding images and user queries.
 - **Scientific and Technical Analysis:** Assisting in interpreting complex charts, diagrams, and scientific images.
 - **Assisted Living and Robotics:** Enabling more sophisticated scene understanding and interaction in visually rich environments.
 - **Medical Imaging:** Potentially aiding in more precise analysis by integrating fine-grained visual details with textual reasoning.

7. **Foundation for Agentic AI:** DeepEyes’ agentic framework, where the model autonomously decides when and how to interact with its visual environment, is a crucial step towards developing more intelligent and adaptive AI agents. The demonstrated zero-shot generalization to new tools further underscores the framework's potential for building extensible and versatile multimodal agents.

8. **Future Research Directions:** The limitations noted by the authors, such as insufficient richness in reasoning and inaccurate target localization due to the foundational model's size, provide clear pathways for future research. Exploring a wider range of visual tools (e.g., search, drawing auxiliary lines) and scaling to larger foundational models will further unlock the potential of this paradigm.

In conclusion, DeepEyes offers a foundational advancement in how VLMs process and reason with visual information. Its end-to-end RL approach to cultivating native "thinking with images" capabilities, coupled with its open-source nature and robust empirical results, positions it as a significant milestone that will likely inspire and shape the next generation of multimodal AI systems.
