# AI+HW 2035: Shaping the Next Decade

> [!summary] Summary
> An extensive collaborative effort from 30 leading experts across academia and industry presents a 10-year vision and roadmap for AI and hardware co-design, aiming for a 1000x improvement in AI training and inference efficiency. The work redefines progress by prioritizing 'intelligence per joule' and proposes a multi-layered co-evolution framework to overcome current sustainability and technological fragmentation challenges.

## Problem

- The unsustainable exponential growth of AI models leads to escalating energy consumption and environmental footprint, making current scaling approaches economically and ecologically unfeasible.
- A critical disconnect exists between AI algorithm development and hardware design, resulting in fragmented innovation where systems and workloads are misaligned.
- Persistent hardware bottlenecks, particularly the 'memory wall' where data movement costs dominate energy and latency, severely limit further AI performance gains.

## Method

- A multi-layered vision for AI+HW ==co-design== and co-evolution is proposed, fostering deep co-innovation across hardware technologies, algorithms, and application layers.
- The concept of 'intelligence per joule' is championed, redefining AI scaling to prioritize energy efficiency and meaningful capability over brute-force computational power.
- Future computing paradigms are advocated, including memory-centric architectures, 3D integration, and leveraging AI to accelerate and optimize hardware design itself.

## Results

- A strategic roadmap outlines a path to achieve a **1000x** improvement in AI training and inference efficiency within 10 years by optimizing across algorithms, silicon, and system levels.
- A multi-layered framework for AI+HW co-evolution is established, emphasizing continuous cross-layer co-design for future breakthroughs.
- Recommendations include shifting to memory-centric architectures, leveraging AI for hardware design, fostering adaptive self-optimizing systems, and a call to action for public-private partnerships to address the impending power crisis and democratize AI infrastructure.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Achieving a 1000x improvement in AI efficiency within the next decade is an urgent necessity and is possible through combined gains across the algorithmic, silicon, and system levels.
> - Data movement, not computation, now constitutes the primary energy and latency bottleneck in AI systems, necessitating a fundamental shift to memory-centric architectures.
> - AI itself is poised to revolutionize hardware design, with 'AI-in-the-loop' methodologies and generative AI for EDA becoming critical for faster, more optimized system development.
