# PanoEnv: Exploring 3D Spatial Intelligence in Panoramic Environments with Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers introduce PanoEnv, a framework featuring a synthetic, geometry-grounded benchmark and a reinforcement learning method, which substantially improves Vision-Language Models' 3D spatial reasoning in panoramic images. The PanoEnv-RL model achieves a 132% relative gain in open-ended 3D reasoning, outperforming larger baselines and showing strong sim-to-real transferability.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with 3D spatial reasoning in 360° panoramic (ERP) images due to severe geometric distortions and misaligned 2D learning priors.
- Existing VQA datasets for panoramic images lack precise, objective 3D geometric ground truth, hindering the development and evaluation of true 3D spatial intelligence.
- Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) alone is insufficient for fostering complex, generative reasoning and robust 3D understanding in VLMs.

## Method

- Development of ==PanoEnv-QA==, a large-scale VQA benchmark with over **14.8K** geometry-grounded QA pairs programmatically generated from synthetic 3D environments (TartanAir), providing objective ground truth for depth, segmentation, and 3D bounding boxes.
- Introduction of ==PanoEnv-RL==, a 3D-aware reinforcement learning post-training framework using ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== on a VLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B), equipped with a multi-faceted, geometry-aware reward system leveraging PanoEnv-QA's ground truth.
- Implementation of a ==two-stage curriculum strategy== for stable RL training, starting with structured questions and progressing to a balanced mixture of structured and open-ended questions to mitigate catastrophic forgetting and enhance generative spatial reasoning.

## Results

- Benchmarking reveals that state-of-the-art VLMs achieve low performance on PanoEnv-QA, with the best baseline (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) reaching only **49.34%** overall accuracy and a mere **6.39%** on open-ended questions.
- PanoEnv-RL (GRPO-Balanced) on Qwen2.5-VL-7B improves overall accuracy to **52.93%** and dramatically boosts open-ended question accuracy to **14.83%**, representing a **+132%** relative gain over the baseline.
- The PanoEnv-RL model demonstrates robust zero-shot transferability to real-world datasets like OSR-Bench, outperforming larger baselines (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-72B) on tasks such as Object Counting and Relative Distance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current state-of-the-art VLMs are largely incapable of robust 3D spatial reasoning in panoramic environments, particularly for open-ended questions requiring precise metric or compositional relations.
> - Leveraging precise 3D geometric ground truth from synthetic environments for reinforcement learning reward signals, combined with a progressive curriculum, enables VLMs to acquire complex 3D spatial intelligence stably and effectively.
> - Training on high-fidelity synthetic data with physically-grounded rewards can instill transferable geometric logic, allowing models to generalize zero-shot to real-world panoramic reasoning tasks.
