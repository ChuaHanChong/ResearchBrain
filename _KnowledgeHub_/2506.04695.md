# Reshaping Reasoning in LLMs: A Theoretical Analysis of RL Training Dynamics through Pattern Selection

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at The University of Hong Kong conducted a theoretical and empirical analysis of reinforcement learning (RL) training dynamics in large language models (LLMs), revealing that RL primarily reshapes reasoning by optimizing the selection of existing reasoning patterns rather than altering their intrinsic success rates. The study characterizes two distinct convergence regimes for verifiable rewards (RLVR) and explains why internal feedback (RLIF) can lead to initial performance gains followed by degradation.

## Problem

- Despite empirical success, the precise mechanisms and training dynamics through which reinforcement learning enhances LLM reasoning remain largely opaque.
- There was a lack of comprehensive understanding of how granular token-level changes translate to higher-level reasoning pattern shifts during RL training.
- The distinct influences of different reward structures, such as verifiable rewards versus internal feedback, on LLM reasoning dynamics were not fully characterized.

## Method

- Conducted systematic empirical analysis of RL training in LLMs at both reasoning-pattern and ==token levels==, observing dynamic shifts across various models and datasets.
- Developed a formal mathematical framework that models the reasoning process as a two-stage operation: pattern selection followed by answer generation, assuming stable intrinsic pattern success rates.
- Theoretically analyzed the training dynamics and derived optimal policies for two common reward types: Verifiable Reward (RLVR) and Reinforcement Learning with Internal Feedback (RLIF).

## Results

- RLVR consistently leads to stable and continuous performance improvement, with models shifting towards adopting reasoning patterns that possess intrinsically higher success rates.
- RLIF exhibits unstable training dynamics, showing initial performance improvement followed by potential degradation, failing to specialize in more effective reasoning patterns.
- Token-level analysis revealed that RL training sparsely optimizes less than **10%** of critical tokens, indicating a selective modification of probabilities that reshapes reasoning pattern distributions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - RL's primary impact on LLM reasoning is to reweight the *selection* of existing reasoning patterns, as the intrinsic success rates of individual patterns remain stable during training.
> - RLVR training exhibits two distinct convergence regimes: rapid convergence when the base model is already high-quality, and a slow 'entanglement stage' when suboptimal patterns initially perform better than the overall accuracy.
> - RLIF optimizes for output confidence rather than correctness, leading to initial accuracy improvements but a 50% theoretical probability of converging to the least accurate reasoning path long-term.
