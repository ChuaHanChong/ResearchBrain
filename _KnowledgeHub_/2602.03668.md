# MVP-LAM: Learning Action-Centric Latent Action via Cross-Viewpoint Reconstruction

> [!summary] Summary
> MVP-LAM, a latent action model from Seoul National University and Microsoft Research Asia, learns action-centric latent actions by leveraging multi-view videos with a novel cross-viewpoint reconstruction objective. This approach yields latent actions with higher mutual information to ground-truth actions and improves robot manipulation success rates by up to 20.8% on the SIMPLER benchmark.

## Problem

- The high cost and difficulty of acquiring large-scale, action-labeled robot demonstration data for training generalist policies.
- Existing latent action models (LAMs) are susceptible to exogenous noise, particularly viewpoint variation, leading to learned latent actions that are not truly informative about the underlying actions.
- Viewpoint changes, common in human videos, entangle camera motion with action-driven transitions, causing LAMs to overfit to viewpoint-specific visual cues.

## Method

- **MVP-LAM** employs a shared latent action encoder across time-synchronized multi-view videos, extracting visual features using a frozen DINOv2 encoder.
- It introduces a novel cross-viewpoint reconstruction objective, where the decoder reconstructs a future observation from one viewpoint using a latent action derived from a *different* synchronized viewpoint.
- The total objective combines this cross-viewpoint loss with a standard self-viewpoint reconstruction loss and VQ-VAE specific quantization losses, incentivizing viewpoint-independent action features.

## Results

- MVP-LAM achieved the highest estimated mutual information I(Z;A)I(Z; A)I(Z;A) (**1.96** bits vs. UniVLA's **1.42** bits) between latent actions and ground-truth actions on the Bridge V2 dataset, indicating improved action-centricity.
- VLA models pretrained with MVP-LAM latent actions improved average success rates on the SIMPLER benchmark from **39.6%** (UniVLA) to **60.4%** and achieved a **94.1%** average success rate on LIBERO suites, outperforming several state-of-the-art methods.
- The model demonstrated robustness to viewpoint perturbations and synchronization errors, maintaining higher action information and accurate future observation prediction even under challenging conditions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Maximizing the mutual information between latent actions and ground-truth actions requires reducing capacity spent on viewpoint-specific factors beyond actual state changes.
> - Cross-viewpoint reconstruction explicitly discourages latent actions from encoding viewpoint-specific noise, forcing them to capture only the shared, intrinsic dynamics of the action.
> - Including diverse multi-view human manipulation videos during training further enhances action-centricity by prioritizing motion as the dominant source of visual transition.
