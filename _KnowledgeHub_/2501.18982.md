# OmniPhysGS: 3D Constitutive Gaussians for General Physics-Based Dynamics Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> OMNIPHYSGS extends 3D Gaussian Splatting with learnable constitutive models, automatically generating physically accurate dynamics for a wide range of materials and complex object interactions. This framework synthesizes dynamic scenes for elastic, viscoelastic, plastic, and fluid substances, demonstrating improved text-alignment and physical consistency in multi-object scenarios.

## Problem

- Current 3D dynamic scene generation methods, particularly those leveraging diffusion models, often lack strict adherence to physical laws, leading to visual artifacts and inconsistencies.
- Existing physics-integrated methods either require time-consuming manual tuning of physical properties by experts or are limited to specific material categories (e.g., pure elastic), restricting their applicability to diverse real-world scenes.
- No general and automatic solution existed for synthesizing dynamic scenes across a broad spectrum of material behaviors and compositions within a unified system.

## Method

- **OmniPhysGS** introduces ==Constitutive Gaussians== where each 3D Gaussian particle is endowed with a learnable neural network-based ==constitutive model== for predicting material properties (hyperelastic energy, plasticity, physical parameters).
- Employs a ==physics-guided constitutive network== that uses a 3D feature encoder to process initial Gaussian attributes and a physical-aware decoder to assign expert-designed constitutive models (from an ensemble of 12 combinations) to guide material property learning.
- Utilizes a custom, memory-optimized differentiable ==Material Point Method== (==MPM==) re-implemented in PyTorch for simulating dynamics, combined with ==Score Distillation Sampling== (==SDS==) from a pre-trained text-to-==video diffusion model== for high-level textual supervision.

## Results

- Achieved an average improvement of **3%** to **16%** in CLIPSIM (text-alignment) for single objects exhibiting diverse material properties (elastic, viscoelastic, plastic, fluid) compared to state-of-the-art baselines.
- Demonstrated superior performance in synthesizing multi-object scenes with heterogeneous materials, surpassing baselines by approximately **4%** in text-alignment metrics, and accurately modeling distinct interactions.
- The custom PyTorch MPM solver reduced training memory consumption by approximately **75%** compared to existing Warp-based solvers, facilitating efficient end-to-end training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating neural networks with expert-designed physical constitutive models allows for flexible learning of material properties while ensuring physical plausibility and stability, addressing the highly nonlinear nature of material behaviors.
> - A memory-efficient PyTorch re-implementation of the differentiable MPM solver enables end-to-end training on a single GPU, significantly reducing computational demands compared to previous solvers.
> - A specialized training strategy involving mini-batch grouping and multiple iterative optimizations within each simulation stage effectively mitigates error accumulation and stabilizes the learning process for long dynamic sequences supervised by high-level diffusion guidance.
