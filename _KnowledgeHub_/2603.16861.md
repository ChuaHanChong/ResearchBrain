# MolmoB0T: Large-Scale Simulation Enables Zero-Shot Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> This research from the Allen Institute for AI introduces MolmoBot, a system that achieves robust zero-shot real-world robotic manipulation using policies trained exclusively on large-scale, procedurally generated simulation data. The MolmoBot (F=2) policy attained a 79.2% success rate on real-world tabletop pick-and-place tasks with a Franka FR3, outperforming prior state-of-the-art systems trained on extensive real-world data.

## Problem

- Existing 'robotics foundation models' from industrial labs often rely on proprietary real-world data and undisclosed methods, hindering open research and reproducibility.
- The prevailing assumption in robotics is that simulation alone is insufficient for robust manipulation policies, necessitating real-world data or fine-tuning to bridge the 'sim-to-real gap'.
- There is a need for generalist manipulation policies that can perform diverse tasks across various robotic platforms without task-specific real-world training.

## Method

- Developed **MolmoBot-Engine**, an open-source, scalable ==procedural data generation== pipeline leveraging 232,000 indoor environments and 48,000 manipulable objects in ==MuJoCo==, generating 1.8 million expert trajectories.
- Trained multiple policy architectures, including a novel ==multi-frame vision-language model== (**MolmoBot**) and an architectural replication of a state-of-the-art model (**MolmoBot-Pi0**), exclusively on the synthetic MolmoBot-Data.
- Employed extensive ==domain randomization== for visual and physical parameters, diverse object sampling, and language instructions with ==referring expressions== to enhance ==sim-to-real transfer== without real-world fine-tuning or photorealistic rendering.

## Results

- MolmoBot (F=2) achieved a **79.2%** success rate on real-world tabletop pick-and-place tasks (Franka FR3), significantly outperforming baselines like π0.5-DROID (**39.2%**) trained on real-world data.
- MolmoBot-Pi0, trained from scratch on MolmoBot-Data using the π0 architecture, achieved **46.7%** real-world success with the Franka FR3, demonstrating the substantial impact of the synthetic data itself.
- The MolmoBot Door Specialist policy achieved **77.7%** success in simulation on door opening, and showed partial real-world success with a Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1, grasping the handle in **4 out of 9** trials and fully opening the door in **2 out of 9** trials.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large-scale, procedurally generated simulation data, when sufficiently diverse, can enable robust zero-shot transfer to real-world manipulation tasks, challenging the traditional view of the 'sim-to-real gap'.
> - Open-sourcing the MolmoBot-Engine and MolmoBot-Data democratizes access to tools and knowledge for building robotics foundation models, fostering community-wide study.
> - For real-world transfer, an absolute joint position action representation substantially outperformed delta action representations, despite similar simulation performance.
