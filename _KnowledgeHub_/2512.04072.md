# SkillFactory: Self-Distillation For Learning Cognitive Behaviors

> [!summary] Summary
> The SkillFactory framework introduces a three-stage self-distillation method to teach cognitive behaviors like verification and retrying to large language models. This approach generates structured "silver traces" from a model's own outputs, leading to superior generalization on harder tasks and robust performance on out-of-domain tasks after reinforcement learning.

## Problem

- Large language models (LLMs) often lack consistent cognitive behaviors such as systematic exploration, output verification, and strategic retrying, which are essential for complex reasoning.
- Current methods to instill these reasoning capabilities frequently depend on access to stronger, often proprietary, teacher models for distillation or require resource-intensive data curation.
- Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) methods that prioritize immediate task performance may not optimally prime models for subsequent reinforcement learning (RL) to develop robust skill utilization.

## Method

- **SkillFactory** employs a three-stage framework: ==data curation==, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement learning (RL).
- Data curation involves using a base model to generate diverse solution attempts and reflections (critiques and verdicts), then structuring these into "silver traces" that explicitly demonstrate self-correction and retrying.
- SFT trains the model on these structured silver traces to internalize the *structure* of cognitive behaviors, acting as a warm-start, followed by RL (using GRPO) with sparse correctness rewards to refine skill application.

## Results

- SkillFactory consistently achieved superior performance on harder variants of Countdown tasks, outperforming distillation from stronger models (e.g., **25.1%** overall accuracy for Qwen2.5-**1.5B** vs. **21.2%** for R1 Distill on harder Countdown tasks).
- The method demonstrated competitive robustness on out-of-domain tasks, matching or exceeding distillation baselines (e.g., **35.7%** accuracy for Qwen2.5-**1.5B** on OOD tasks) and showing greater resilience to performance regression.
- SkillFactory-initialized models showed enhanced reasoning on challenging mathematical and scientific benchmarks (e.g., **42.1%** at 1k SFT rows for Qwen2.5-**7B**-Instruct, competitive with distillation methods and outperforming them on specific benchmarks like AMC and Math500).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training models on the explicit *structure* of cognitive behaviors through self-generated traces during SFT is more effective for subsequent RL-driven skill acquisition than solely optimizing for SFT task accuracy.
> - Robust cognitive behaviors like reflection and retrying can be instilled in LLMs through a self-contained, self-distillation process, reducing the reliance on external, more capable teacher models.
> - The learned structural biases from SkillFactory's SFT significantly enhance a model's ability to generalize reasoning skills to harder variants and maintain robustness on out-of-domain tasks, indicating effective skill transfer.
