# VLAW: Iterative Co-Improvement of Vision-Language-Action Policy and World Model

> [!summary] Summary
> The VLAW framework iteratively co-improves vision-language-action (VLA) policies and action-conditioned world models for robot manipulation. It leverages limited real-world interaction to ground the world model, which then generates abundant synthetic data to boost policy performance, achieving a 39.2% absolute success rate increase on contact-rich tasks.

## Problem

- Collecting sufficient real-world interaction data for post-training VLA models is prohibitively expensive and time-consuming, limiting their scalability.
- Existing learned world models often lack physical fidelity, exhibiting over-optimism (trained on successful demonstrations) and struggling to accurately model diverse physical interactions, including failure cases.
- Continuously improving VLA policies in the real world is challenging due to the data scarcity bottleneck and the difficulty of optimizing expressive policies without explicit action likelihoods.

## Method

- **VLAW** introduces an ==iterative co-improvement== framework that alternately refines a VLA policy and an ==action-conditioned world model==.
- A pre-trained world model is fine-tuned using a small, fixed budget of real-world online policy rollouts (==D_real==), crucially including failure cases, to improve its physical fidelity and reduce ==over-optimism==.
- The improved world model then generates a large amount of ==synthetic trajectories==, which are automatically filtered for success by a ==vision-language reward model==, providing high-quality data to post-train the VLA policy.

## Results

- The world model, post-trained with online rollouts, demonstrated substantial improvements in video prediction quality (e.g., FVD reduced from **225.13** to **64.12**) and accurately modeled interaction outcomes, effectively mitigating over-optimism.
- VLAW achieved a **39.2%** absolute success rate improvement, increasing the mean success rate of the VLA policy from **0.46** to **0.868** on challenging contact-rich manipulation tasks, outperforming baselines that do not use world models.
- Training with world model-generated synthetic data contributed **11.6%** absolute success rate improvement, highlighting the significant value of scalable, high-quality synthetic data for policy enhancement.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Limited real-world interaction, when strategically used to include diverse outcomes like failures, can effectively ground a pre-trained world model, leading to significantly higher physical fidelity.
> - High-fidelity synthetic data, generated by a grounded world model and filtered by an accurate reward model, provides a scalable and effective alternative to expensive real-world data for improving VLA policies.
> - An iterative feedback loop between policy exploration (generating new real-world data) and world model refinement (generating better synthetic data) enables continuous and scalable performance improvement for robot learning.
