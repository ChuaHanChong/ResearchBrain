# Tuna-2: Pixel Embeddings Beat Vision Encoders for Multimodal Understanding and Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Meta AI researchers introduced Tuna-2, a unified multimodal model that performs visual understanding and generation directly from pixel embeddings, eliminating the need for pretrained vision encoders. This encoder-free architecture achieved competitive performance across nine VQA benchmarks and state-of-the-art results among native UMMs for image generation and editing tasks.

## Problem

- Unified multimodal models (UMMs) traditionally rely on complex, modular designs with pretrained vision encoders (e.g., CLIP, VAE), leading to architectural complexity and potential representation mismatches between understanding and generation tasks.
- Reliance on compact latent spaces from VAEs or high-level semantic features from representation encoders can limit the model's ability to process and reason about fine-grained visual details.
- The use of pretrained, static encoders constrains the degree to which the entire multimodal system can be optimized end-to-end directly from raw pixel inputs.

## Method

- Developed **Tuna-2**, an ==encoder-free, monolithic architecture== that replaces pretrained vision encoders with simple ==patch embedding layers== to directly convert raw images into visual tokens.
- Employed an ==x-prediction and v-loss paradigm== based on ==rectified flow== for direct pixel-space image generation, removing the dependency on VAEs.
- Introduced a ==masking-based visual feature learning scheme== during training, where random image patches are masked, compelling the model to learn more robust unified representations for both understanding and generation.

## Results

- Tuna-2 achieved competitive results across **nine diverse VQA benchmarks**, outperforming earlier native UMMs and demonstrating stronger performance on pixel-centric tasks (V*, CountBench, VisuLogic).
- Attained **state-of-the-art performance among native UMMs** on **GenEval** and **DPG-Bench** for image generation, with LLM-judge evaluations indicating comparable quality to Tuna-R and superior diversity.
- Tuna-2 demonstrated more accurate vision-language alignment in attention map visualizations and greater robustness to misleading linguistic contexts.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large-scale end-to-end training of an encoder-free, pixel-space architecture can effectively learn visual representations that are competitive with, and often surpass, those derived from pretrained vision encoders for understanding tasks.
> - Direct pixel-space generation, when coupled with a robust flow matching paradigm and extensive training, can achieve state-of-the-art performance in image generation and editing without requiring latent-space VAEs.
> - Masking-based visual feature learning significantly enhances both multimodal understanding and generation, particularly for encoder-free models, by encouraging the model to develop robust representations and cross-modal alignment even with partial visual input.
