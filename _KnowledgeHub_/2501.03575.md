# Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform for Physical AI

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA's Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform provides an open-source, open-weight ecosystem for building high-fidelity digital twins of the physical world, addressing data scarcity and safety challenges in Physical AI development. The platform integrates a scalable video data curation pipeline, advanced visual tokenizers, and pre-trained diffusion and autoregressive world models, demonstrating their applicability in generating controllable, realistic simulations for robotics, autonomous driving, and virtual environment navigation.

## Problem

- Training Physical AI systems (e.g., robots, autonomous vehicles) requires vast amounts of interaction data, which is expensive, time-consuming, and risky to collect in the real world.
- Traditional, system-specific models in Physical AI often lack generalizability across different tasks or environments, limiting their scalability.
- Early-stage AI learning in the physical world can lead to safety concerns, including potential damage to systems or environments from exploratory actions.

## Method

![[2501.03575-method.png]]
*Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform overview, with its major components: video curator, video tokenizer, pre-trained world foundation model, post-training samples, and guardrail.*

- NVIDIA introduces the **Cosmos** World Foundation Model Platform, an open-source and open-weight ecosystem for creating high-fidelity digital twins for Physical AI.
- The platform includes a scalable ==video data curation pipeline== that processes **20 million** hours of raw video, producing **100 million** high-quality clips for training.
- It provides state-of-the-art ==visual tokenizers== and pre-trained ==World Foundation Models (WFMs)== based on ==diffusion== and ==autoregressive== architectures, which are then fine-tuned for applications like camera control, robotic manipulation, and autonomous driving.

## Results

- The Cosmos Tokenizer suite achieves **+4 dB** PSNR improvement and **2x-12x** faster inference compared to existing tokenizers, while maintaining smaller model sizes.
- Pre-trained diffusion-based and autoregressive WFMs generate high-quality, photorealistic videos, with autoregressive models achieving **10 FPS** real-time generation at **320x512** resolution using Medusa speculative decoding.
- Fine-tuned WFMs significantly outperform baselines in camera-controlled navigation (lower FID/FVD, higher 3D consistency), robotic manipulation (stronger instruction following), and autonomous driving (lower FID/FVD, **<7cm** trajectory following error, accurate multi-view consistency).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - World Foundation Models (WFMs) are posited as a critical new category of foundation models, analogous to LLMs, capable of generating physically consistent and controllable digital twins of the real world.
> - A multi-stage, GPU-accelerated video data curation pipeline is essential for building the massive, high-quality, and diverse datasets required to pre-train large-scale WFMs.
> - Both diffusion-based and autoregressive architectures, when properly scaled and optimized with techniques like speculative decoding, can achieve high-fidelity video generation and be fine-tuned for specific Physical AI applications.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This research directly addresses a critical bottleneck in the advancement of Physical AI: the challenge of acquiring sufficient and safe training data. Physical AI systems, such as robots and autonomous vehicles, learn through interaction with the physical world, which is inherently time-consuming, expensive, and often risky. The paper posits that a "World Foundation Model (WFM)," serving as a high-fidelity digital twin of the physical world, is a "long-sought remedy" to this data scarcity problem.

The work builds upon the established concept of "world models," which originated from early efforts to learn latent representations of environments for predicting future states (e.g., Ha and Schmidhuber, Dreamer). However, it significantly advances this field by framing world models not just as abstract state predictors, but as *generative models operating directly in the visual observation space* (i.e., conditional video generative models). This approach aligns with the recent explosion of highly capable video generative models (e.g., OpenAI's Sora, Luma's Dream Machine), but crucially re-orients their development towards the specific demands of Physical AI. Unlike general-purpose video generation, WFMs for Physical AI require:
- **3D Consistency:** The generated worlds must respect geometric plausibility.
- **Physics Alignment:** The simulated dynamics must adhere to physical laws.
- **Controllability:** The models must respond accurately to various control signals (e.g., camera poses, robotic actions, vehicle trajectories).

By developing a "World Foundation Model Platform," NVIDIA aims to establish a new category of foundation models, analogous to large language models (LLMs) for text or vision-language models (VLMs) for image-text data. This paradigm shift emphasizes generalizability through pre-training on vast, diverse datasets, followed by specialization through fine-tuning for specific downstream applications. The open-source and open-weight release of the Cosmos platform and its models is a significant contribution to the open science movement, enabling researchers and developers globally to build upon this foundational work, accelerating the progress of Physical AI.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of this research is to **develop and release the "Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform"** to empower developers in building customized world models for their Physical AI setups.

The primary motivation stems from the inherent difficulties in scaling Physical AI systems:
1. **Data Scarcity:** Training Physical AI often requires sequences of interleaved observations and actions. Collecting such data in the real world is highly challenging, laborious, and costly.
2. **Safety Concerns:** Exploratory actions, crucial for early-stage AI learning, can lead to severe damage to the physical system or its environment.
3. **Lack of Generalizability:** Traditional, system-specific models in robotics and automation often lack the ability to generalize across different tasks or environments.

To address these challenges, the paper proposes World Foundation Models (WFMs) as a solution. The core idea is that a WFM, acting as a high-fidelity digital twin of the physical world, allows Physical AI systems to train and interact safely and efficiently in a simulated environment, thereby mitigating real-world risks and accelerating data collection.

The specific goals of the Cosmos Platform are to:
- Provide a robust **video data curation pipeline** to build high-quality, diverse datasets for WFM training.
- Offer **pre-trained world foundation models** that serve as "generalists" capturing broad knowledge of real-world physics and natural behaviors.
- Demonstrate a **pre-training-and-then-post-training paradigm**, allowing these generalist models to be fine-tuned into specialized WFMs for targeted Physical AI applications.
- Include **video tokenizers** as fundamental building blocks for efficient visual data representation.
- Support various **Physical AI applications**, such as policy evaluation, policy initialization, policy training, planning/model-predictive control, and synthetic data generation.
- Ensure **safe and responsible deployment** through a comprehensive guardrail system.
- **Accelerate societal impact** by making the platform and models open-source and open-weight, enabling a broader community of Physical AI builders to tackle critical real-world problems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform is built upon a multi-component architecture, encompassing data, models, and safety mechanisms.

#### 3.1. Data Curation

A scalable video data curation pipeline processes 20 million hours of raw video, yielding approximately 100 million high-quality clips for pre-training and 10 million for fine-tuning. The pipeline includes:
- **Splitting:** Raw videos are segmented into coherent shots (max 60s) using TransNetV2 for accurate shot boundary detection and re-encoded into a consistent MP4 format using GPU-accelerated transcoding (PyNvideoCodec for H.264).
- **Filtering:** Clips are filtered for quality and relevance using lightweight classifiers for motion, visual quality (DOVER-based), text overlay (InternVideo2-based), and video type (InternVideo2-based VLM-annotated taxonomy).
- **Annotation:** A proprietary VLM (VILA 13B) generates detailed, consistent captions for each clip, enhancing textual conditioning for models.
- **Deduplication:** Semantic deduplication using InternVideo2 embeddings and k-means clustering (leveraging RAPIDS for GPU acceleration) removes redundancy, reducing the dataset by about 30%.
- **Sharding:** Processed clips are packaged into webdatasets, sharded by resolution, aspect ratio, and length, for efficient model consumption.
- **Infrastructure:** AnyScale Ray orchestrates the geographically distributed, multi-resource pipeline, optimizing throughput across GPUs.

#### 3.2. Tokenizer

"Cosmos Tokenizer" is a suite of visual tokenizers designed for high compression and reconstruction quality, supporting both continuous and discrete representations for images and videos.
- **Architecture:** A causal encoder-decoder structure operating in the wavelet space, employing spatio-temporal factorized 3D convolutions and causal self-attention. It uses Layer Normalization and supports various aspect ratios and temporal lengths.
- **Quantization:** Continuous tokenizers use a vanilla autoencoder (AE). Discrete tokenizers use Finite-Scalar-Quantization (FSQ) to map latents to discrete codes (e.g., 64,000 vocabulary size).
- **Training Strategy:** A two-stage joint image and video training scheme. The first stage uses L1 and perceptual losses, and the second adds optical flow and Gram-matrix losses, with optional adversarial loss.

#### 3.3. World Foundation Model Pre-training

Two scalable deep learning paradigms are explored: diffusion models and autoregressive models, trained on 10,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs.
- **Diffusion-based WFMs (Cosmos-Predict1-7B/14B-Text2World/Video2World):**
 - **Formulation:** Latent diffusion models (EDM-based) operating on continuous tokens from `Cosmos-Tokenize1-CV8x8x8-720p`.
 - **Architecture:** Adapted from DiT, featuring 3D patchification, hybrid positional embedding (FPS-aware 3D RoPE and learnable embeddings), cross-attention for text conditioning (T5-XXL), Query-Key Normalization, and AdaLN-LoRA for parameter efficiency.
 - **Training Strategy:** Joint image and video training with domain-specific normalization; progressive training (from 512p/57 frames to 720p/121 frames); multi-aspect training; mixed-precision training. Video2World models are fine-tuned from Text2World models, incorporating previous frames as conditioning.
 - **Scaling:** Leverages Fully Sharded Data Parallelism (FSDP) and Context Parallelism (CP) to distribute memory and computation.
 - **Prompt Upsampler:** `Cosmos-UpsamplePrompt1-12B-Text2World` (fine-tuned Mistral-NeMo-12B-Instruct) for Text2World, and Pixtral-12B for Video2World, to bridge the gap between user and training prompts.
- **Autoregressive-based WFMs (Cosmos-Predict1-4B/5B/12B/13B):**
 - **Formulation:** Transformer decoder (Llama3-style GPT) for next-token prediction, using discrete tokens from `Cosmos-Tokenize1-DV8x16x16-720p`.
 - **Architecture:** Incorporates 3D-aware positional embeddings (3D RoPE with YaRN for context extension, 3D sinusoidal APE), cross-attention for text conditioning (T5-XXL embeddings), Query-Key Normalization, and Z-loss for training stability.
 - **Training Strategy:** Multi-stage training (pure video prediction, increased context length, then text-conditioned Video2World) with a "cooling-down" phase on high-quality data.
 - **Inference Optimization:** Implements key-value caching, Tensor Parallelism (TP), Sequence Parallelism (SP), `torch.compile`, and speculative decoding (Medusa framework) for real-time generation. Adapts to lower resolutions (320x512) for 10 FPS real-time performance.
 - **Diffusion Decoder (`Cosmos-Predict1-7B-Decoder`):** A fine-tuned diffusion model that enhances the visual quality of autoregressive WFM outputs by decoding discrete tokens into higher-fidelity continuous tokens, then to RGB.

#### 3.4. Post-trained World Foundation Model (Applications)

The pre-trained WFMs are fine-tuned for specific Physical AI applications:
- **Camera Control:** Fine-tuning `Cosmos-Predict1-7B-Video2World` on DL3DV-10K with Plücker embeddings for camera pose conditioning, enabling 3D navigable virtual world generation from a single image.
- **Robotic Manipulation:** Fine-tuning `Cosmos-Predict1-7B/5B-Video2World` for:
 - Instruction-based video prediction (on Cosmos-1X dataset) with text instructions.
 - Action-based next-frame generation (on Bridge dataset) with 7D action vectors, incorporating new action-embedding modules.
- **Autonomous Driving:** Fine-tuning `Cosmos-Predict1-7B-Text2World` to generate multi-view surround-view videos (6 cameras) from the RDS dataset. This includes view-independent positional embedding, view embedding, view-dependent cross-attention, and optional trajectory control (64-point 3D path).

#### 3.5. Guardrails

A multi-stage safety system for responsible deployment:
- **Pre-Guard:** Blocks harmful text inputs using keyword lists and `Aegis` (LLM-based, fine-tuned Llama-Guard).
- **Post-Guard:** Filters harmful visual outputs using a video content safety classifier (SigLIP embeddings) and a face blur filter (RetinaFace for pixelation of detected faces).
- **Red Team:** A dedicated team actively probes the system with adversarial examples to identify and mitigate safety weaknesses.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research presents compelling evidence for the efficacy and potential of the Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform:

- **High-Quality Data Curation:** The developed pipeline effectively processed vast amounts of raw video data, yielding 100 million high-quality, diverse clips suitable for complex model training, crucial for the scale of WFM development.
- **Superior Tokenization:** The Cosmos Tokenizer suite demonstrates state-of-the-art performance, achieving significantly better reconstruction quality (e.g., +4 dB PSNR on DAVIS dataset) and higher inference efficiency (2x-12x faster) compared to existing tokenizers, while maintaining smaller model sizes. This highlights its effectiveness in compressing high-dimensional visual data.
- **Robust Pre-trained World Foundation Models:**
 - **Diffusion-based WFMs (Cosmos-Predict1-7B/14B):** Generate high-quality, photorealistic videos with strong text alignment and realistic motion dynamics. The 14B model consistently produces richer visual details and more intricate motion patterns than its 7B counterpart. Video2World variants successfully extend generation based on input images/videos.
 - **Autoregressive-based WFMs (Cosmos-Predict1-4B/5B/12B/13B):** Show improved sharpness and motion fidelity with increasing model size. A significant finding is the successful integration of the Medusa speculative decoding framework, which achieves substantial inference acceleration (up to 3.2x token throughput and 6.1x fewer forward passes for the 5B model) without compromising output quality. Adaptation to lower resolutions (320x512) enables real-time video generation at 10 FPS, making them suitable for interactive applications.
 - **Diffusion Decoder:** Successfully mitigates the blurriness and artifacts often associated with aggressive discrete tokenization in autoregressive models, significantly enhancing visual sharpness while preserving content.
- **Demonstrated Applicability in Physical AI Domains:**
 - **Camera Control:** The fine-tuned `Cosmos-Predict1-7B-Video2World-Sample-CameraCond` significantly outperforms the baseline (CamCo) in both video generation quality (lower FID/FVD) and 3D consistency (higher camera pose estimation success rate, lower errors). It shows capability for joystick-like navigation and generating diverse futures from a single input image.
 - **Robotic Manipulation:** Human evaluation of instruction-based models (`Cosmos-Predict1-7B/5B-Video2World-Sample-Instruction`) on the Cosmos-1X dataset shows a strong preference over the VideoLDM baseline across multiple dimensions, including instruction following and object permanence. Action-based models (`Cosmos-Predict1-7B/5B-Video2World-Sample-ActionCond`) outperform IRASim-Action on the Bridge dataset, producing predicted frames closely matching ground truth.
 - **Autonomous Driving:** The multi-view WFM (`Cosmos-Predict1-7B-Text2World/Video2World-Sample-MultiView`) significantly surpasses the VideoLDM-MultiView baseline in generation quality (FID/FVD) and multi-view consistency (Sampson Errors). The trajectory-conditioned model demonstrates remarkable accuracy in following specified paths, with a trajectory following error of less than 7cm, crucial for realistic AD simulation. Object tracking consistency also confirms physical plausibility in generated driving videos. The model can even generalize to rare/out-of-domain scenes.
- **Robust Guardrail System:** The multi-stage guardrail system (pre-Guard with keyword blocking and Aegis, post-Guard with content safety and face blurring), supported by continuous red team efforts, demonstrates a commitment to safe and responsible AI deployment.

**Observed Limitations:**
Despite these advancements, the paper candidly acknowledges several limitations:
- **Physics Adherence:** Current WFMs, including Cosmos, still struggle to reliably simulate complex physical laws, exhibiting issues like lack of object permanence, inaccuracies in contact-rich dynamics, and inconsistent adherence to gravity or fluid dynamics. Larger models do not necessarily correlate with better physics adherence in the conducted evaluations.
- **Evaluation Challenges:** Quantifying "physical fidelity" remains difficult, with human evaluations being subjective and automated metrics potentially misaligning with downstream task performance.
- **Autoregressive WFM (Text-to-Video):** The prompt upsampler did not universally improve autoregressive Text2World models, suggesting that these models, pre-trained mostly on pure video generation, may not sufficiently leverage text inputs.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The "Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform for Physical AI" represents a landmark effort with profound significance and potential impact:

1. **Accelerating Physical AI Development:** By offering a robust platform for creating high-fidelity digital twins of the physical world, Cosmos directly addresses the most critical bottleneck for Physical AI: data scarcity and safe, efficient training. This could drastically reduce the cost and time associated with training and validating robots, autonomous vehicles, and other embodied AI systems, accelerating their deployment and capabilities.
2. **Establishing a New Foundation Model Paradigm:** The work positions "World Foundation Models" as a distinct and crucial category of foundation models. Just as LLMs have revolutionized language tasks, WFMs could become the bedrock for building general-purpose, intelligent agents that understand and interact with the physical world, moving beyond current narrow AI applications.
3. **Democratizing Advanced Simulation:** By making the Cosmos platform, its pre-trained models (`Cosmos-Predict1-7B/14B`), and the associated tokenizers open-source and open-weight, NVIDIA is democratizing access to cutting-edge world model technology. This move will enable a much wider community of researchers, startups, and developers to experiment, build upon, and contribute to this field, fostering rapid innovation and reducing barriers to entry.
4. **Enabling Novel Applications and Research:**
 - **Enhanced Policy Training:** WFMs can serve as proxy environments for reinforcement learning, allowing agents to gain proficiency without real-world risks.
 - **Improved Planning and Control:** Accurate world models enable more sophisticated planning algorithms (e.g., model-predictive control) by simulating diverse future scenarios.
 - **Synthetic Data Generation:** The ability to generate high-fidelity, controllable, and diverse synthetic data can further augment real-world datasets, crucial for improving the robustness and generalizability of Physical AI.
 - **Interactive Design and Prototyping:** The camera control capabilities open avenues for designing and exploring virtual environments with intuitive controls.
5. **Setting Standards for Responsible AI:** The proactive development and public discussion of a comprehensive guardrail system reflect a strong commitment to safe and ethical deployment of powerful generative AI models. This sets a valuable precedent for the development of future Physical AI systems that interact directly with the human environment.
6. **Paving the Way for Future Research:** The candid discussion of current limitations (e.g., perfect physics adherence, robust evaluation metrics) provides clear directions for future research, inviting collaboration and further advancements. The comparative analysis of diffusion-based and autoregressive models also highlights important open questions regarding optimal architectures for different Physical AI needs, stimulating further exploration of hybrid approaches.

In essence, the Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform is not just a collection of advanced models; it is a comprehensive ecosystem designed to unlock the full potential of Physical AI by providing the essential tools for creating intelligent agents that can learn, reason, and operate effectively within a digital representation of our complex physical world.
