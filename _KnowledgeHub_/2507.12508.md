# MindJourney: Test-Time Scaling with World Models for Spatial Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> MindJourney enhances Vision-Language Models (VLMs) in spatial reasoning tasks by enabling them to interactively explore imagined 3D environments using controllable video diffusion models. The framework yields an average 7.7% top-1 accuracy gain on the SAT benchmark, pushing models like OpenAI o1 from 74.6% to 84.7% accuracy on SAT-Real.

## Problem

- Current Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with understanding 3D dynamics and anticipating egocentric motion from static 2D images.
- This limitation hinders the development of robust embodied AI agents that require deep spatial reasoning for navigation, manipulation, and planning in physical environments.
- VLMs lack an inherent internal model for simulating interactions and changes within a 3D world, unlike human cognition.

## Method

- ==MindJourney== introduces a ==test-time scaling framework== that couples VLMs with controllable video diffusion models, functioning as egocentric 3D 'world models'.
- A =='Spatial Beam Search' algorithm== guides the VLM to iteratively plan exploratory trajectories, which the world model then uses to generate corresponding imagined multi-view observations.
- The accumulated multi-view evidence, including generated frames and natural language trajectory descriptions, is then provided to a Question-Answering VLM to produce a final answer without requiring VLM fine-tuning.

## Results

- Achieved an average **7.7%** top-1 accuracy improvement across diverse VLMs on the SAT spatial reasoning benchmark, with individual gains exceeding **10%**.
- Established a new state-of-the-art on the SAT-Real dataset, raising the accuracy of the OpenAI o1 model from **74.6%** to **84.7%** when augmented with MindJourney's Search World Model (SWM).
- Demonstrated consistent performance gains across both real-world (SAT-Real) and synthesized (SAT-Synthesized) scenarios, validating its generalizability across different types of spatial reasoning tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Granting VLMs a 'physically consistent simulator' for test-time imagination offers a powerful and orthogonal method for enhancing reasoning capabilities beyond traditional text-centric approaches.
> - The framework is model-agnostic and training-free, demonstrating its plug-and-play compatibility with various VLM backends (e.g., GPT-4o, InternVL) and world models.
> - MindJourney effectively complements existing advanced reasoning techniques, such as RL-based chain-of-thought scaling, further boosting their performance in spatial tasks.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

MindJourney addresses a critical limitation in current state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models (VLMs) within the rapidly evolving landscape of artificial intelligence, particularly in the domains of embodied AI and advanced reasoning.

**Embodied AI and 3D Spatial Understanding:** The foundational motivation for MindJourney stems from the observation that while humans effortlessly navigate and reason about a three-dimensional world, modern AI systems, especially VLMs, frequently struggle with tasks requiring a deep understanding of 3D dynamics. Endowing embodied agents (e.g., robots, virtual assistants) with comparable 3D spatial intelligence for tasks like navigation, manipulation, and planning in cluttered environments remains a central goal of embodied AI research. Existing VLMs, though powerful in visual recognition and comprehension from 2D images, lack an inherent internal model of 3D space and its dynamics, making them prone to errors when asked to anticipate the consequences of egocentric movements or perspective shifts. MindJourney directly tackles this gap by providing VLMs with a mechanism to "imagine" and explore 3D space.

**Advancements in Vision-Language Models (VLMs):** Recent years have seen tremendous progress in VLMs, with models demonstrating human-level performance on many visual recognition and language understanding tasks. However, benchmarks like SpatialRGPT, SAT, COMFORT, and SPAR have consistently revealed their shortcomings in genuine spatial reasoning, especially those requiring dynamic 3D understanding. This work highlights that VLMs often treat images as static 2D entities rather than interactive portals into a 3D world. MindJourney seeks to augment these powerful but spatially naive VLMs.

**Emergence of World Models:** A parallel development in AI is the rise of "world models," particularly those based on controllable video diffusion. Models like CamCtrl3D, CameraCtrl, and Stable-Virtual-Camera have shown the ability to synthesize coherent egocentric videos from a single RGB frame and a specified camera pose trajectory. These models effectively act as simulators, predicting "what the camera will see" given "where the agent will go." MindJourney ingeniously leverages these video diffusion models as "world models" to provide the missing 3D dynamics for VLMs.

**Test-Time Scaling (TTS) for Enhanced Reasoning:** MindJourney also aligns with the growing research direction of "test-time scaling" (TTS), which aims to boost the reasoning capabilities of large models by allocating additional computational resources *after* initial training, without requiring further fine-tuning. Traditional TTS methods typically involve techniques like best-of-n reranking of multiple chains of thought (CoTs), guided decoding with learned value functions, or MCTS-style tree search for exploring reasoning spaces, often focusing on text-centric reasoning. MindJourney introduces a novel, *physically consistent* approach to TTS by integrating a world model. Instead of relying solely on linguistic or abstract reasoning steps, it provides geometry-aware evidence gathered through imagined exploration of a latent 3D scene, making it a distinct contribution to the TTS paradigm. This approach complements existing TTS methods, as demonstrated by its ability to further enhance RL-trained VLMs like o1.

In summary, MindJourney positions itself at the intersection of embodied AI, VLM enhancement, and advanced world modeling, proposing a unique test-time scaling framework that addresses the fundamental challenge of 3D spatial reasoning in AI by providing VLMs with an interactive, imagined 3D workspace.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The core motivation behind MindJourney stems from a fundamental disconnect between how humans and current state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models (VLMs) perceive and reason about the world.

**The Central Problem:** Humans inhabit and effortlessly reason about a three-dimensional world. Our innate 3D spatial understanding is crucial for everyday tasks like planning routes, manipulating objects, and making decisions in complex environments. This spatial intelligence develops progressively throughout life and underpins almost every physical task. In contrast, while modern VLMs excel at visual recognition and language comprehension from 2D images, they frequently "struggle with tasks as simple as anticipating how a scene will look after an egocentric motion." This struggle is not due to a lack of visual processing power but rather a lack of an *internal model of 3D dynamics*. VLMs perceive static 2D images but do not inherently understand the interactive, dynamic 3D world that these images represent. They cannot mentally simulate a short walk or visualize a next view, which humans do effortlessly.

**The Driving Motivation:** This gap represents a critical bottleneck for embodied AI. To build agents that can truly operate intelligently in 3D physical spaces, they must possess robust 3D spatial reasoning capabilities. The ability to imagine the consequences of movements is indispensable for planning and decision-making in embodied tasks. The motivation is to bridge this gap by granting VLMs this missing capability, allowing them to reason about the actual 3D world that lies behind a 2D image.

#### 2.1. Key Objectives of MindJourney

1. **Grant VLMs 3D Dynamics Capability:** The primary objective is to equip VLMs with the ability to understand and reason about 3D dynamics and egocentric motion.
2. **Utilize Controllable World Models:** To achieve the first objective, MindJourney aims to couple VLMs with controllable video diffusion models, using them as "world models" that can synthesize egocentric views corresponding to specified camera movements.
3. **Develop a Test-Time Scaling Framework:** The solution should be a "test-time scaling framework," meaning it enhances VLM performance *without requiring any fine-tuning* of the base VLM. This makes it a plug-and-play solution.
4. **Enable Interactive Exploration of Imagined Space:** The framework should allow the VLM to iteratively plan and execute exploratory trajectories within an imagined 3D space, gathering multi-view evidence for reasoning.
5. **Achieve Robust 3D Reasoning:** The ultimate goal is to significantly boost the VLM's spatial reasoning performance on representative benchmarks, demonstrating robustness across different VLMs, world models, and various spatial reasoning tasks (egocentric motion, object motion, action consequence, perspective shifts).
6. **Demonstrate Model Agnosticism:** The approach should be effective across diverse VLM back-ends and world models, showcasing its general applicability.
7. **Complement Existing Reasoning Methods:** To demonstrate that this novel approach of "physically consistent" test-time scaling is not mutually exclusive with other reasoning techniques (e.g., RL-based chain-of-thought scaling), but can rather complement and further enhance them.

In essence, MindJourney aims to provide VLMs with an "imagination engine" that allows them to interactively explore and gather information from a simulated 3D world, thereby unlocking their potential for sophisticated spatial intelligence.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

MindJourney is presented as a test-time scaling framework that ingeniously combines the high-level reasoning capabilities of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with the detailed 3D scene understanding and dynamic simulation provided by world models. This is achieved through an iterative, interactive search process.

**Overall Framework: Coupling VLMs with World Models**
The core idea is to enable a VLM to reason by "interactively searching and exploring the imagined 3D space through a world model." Instead of an immediate answer, MindJourney allows the VLM to perform an exploratory process. The framework consists of two tightly coupled components:
1. **Video-Diffusion Models as World Models:** These models serve as egocentric simulators. Given a single initial RGB frame and an egocentric action sequence (camera pose trajectory), the world model synthesizes a coherent video that faithfully follows the specified motion. This effectively transforms a static image into an "explorable 3D world."
2. **Spatial Beam Search:** This is the interactive search algorithm that guides the VLM and world model. Guided by a spatial reasoning question, the VLM iteratively plans short exploratory trajectories, and the world model synthesizes the corresponding views at each step. The VLM then reasons over this multi-view evidence gathered during the exploration.

**Pipeline Overview (Fig. 2 & Algorithm 1)**
The MindJourney pipeline operates as follows:
1. **Initialization:** Given an input image ($x_0$) and a spatial reasoning query ($q$), an empty beam is initialized, and an empty evidence set ($E$) is created.
2. **Iterative Spatial Beam Search (for $n$ steps):**
 - **Trajectory Expansion:** For each trajectory ($\tau$) in the current beam, candidate new trajectories ($C(\tau)$) are generated by applying a limited set of primitive actions (move-forward, turn-left, turn-right) up to $k$ consecutive times. Actions that immediately reverse or exceed a budget are pruned.
 - **Action-Driven Video Generation (World Model $W$):** For each candidate trajectory $\tau' \in C(\tau)$, the world model $W$ (e.g., SWM or SVC) takes the initial image $x_0$ and the pose sequence corresponding to $\tau'$ to generate the imagined egocentric frame $x_{\tau'}$. This produces a set of local observations $O(\tau) = \{(\tau', x_{\tau'}) \}$.
 - **VLM-Based Heuristics (Search VLM $V_{search}$):** For every generated observation $(\tau', x_{\tau'})$, a natural-language description $desc(\tau')$ (e.g., "move forward 0.2 m, then turn right 30 degrees") is created. The Search VLM then evaluates this tuple $\langle q, desc(\tau'), x_{\tau'} \rangle$ based on two criteria:
 - **Exploration Score ($s_{exp}(\tau')$):** How useful is it to continue exploring this trajectory?
 - **Helpfulness Score ($s_{help}(\tau')$):** How useful is this particular view for answering the question *now*?
 - **Pruning and Beam Update:** Candidates with scores below fixed thresholds ($\gamma_{exp}$ and $\gamma_{help}$) are discarded.
 - The top $B$ candidates (by $s_{exp}$) form the beam for the next search step.
 - The top $H$ candidates (by $s_{help}$) are added to a `Helpful Observation Buffer` (evidence set $E$) for final answering.
3. **Answer Generation (Question-Answering VLM $V_{QA}$):** After $n$ search steps, or if the beam becomes empty, all cached helpful trajectories and their associated frames and natural-language descriptions from the evidence buffer $E$ are collected. The Question-Answering VLM ($V_{QA}$) receives the original question $q$ and this multi-view evidence (initial image plus buffered observations) in a single pass to produce the final answer. Note that $V_{search}$ and $V_{QA}$ can be the same VLM.

**Search World Model (SWM) Details**
MindJourney introduces its own world model, SWM, specifically trained for the defined action space:
- **Architecture:** SWM builds upon Wan2.2-TI2V-5B, following ReCamMaster. It conditions video generation on target camera poses by embedding extrinsic matrices directly into the video latent representation within diffusion-transformer blocks.
- **Training Dataset:** To ensure both geometric fidelity and visual diversity, SWM's training corpus is a blend:
 - **Habitat 2.0 (HM3D):** Synthesized 50K navigation clips in AI2-THOR indoor scenes provide metrically accurate movement distances and precise camera control.
 - **RealEstate-10K and DL3DV-10K:** Large-scale, view-consistent real indoor and outdoor video datasets bridge the appearance gap between synthetic data and real imagery, allowing SWM to generalize better.

This intricate dance between VLM-driven search and world-model-powered imagination allows MindJourney to dynamically gather relevant 3D information, which significantly boosts the VLM's ability to tackle complex spatial reasoning tasks without requiring internal 3D modeling within the VLM itself.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

MindJourney's evaluation demonstrates consistent and significant improvements in spatial reasoning across various Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and environments. The main findings are:

**Overall Performance Boost:**
- MindJourney achieves an average 7.7% top-1 accuracy performance boost on the Spatial Aptitude Training (SAT) benchmark. This gain is observed uniformly across multiple spatial reasoning tasks and different VLM backends.
- The largest single gain reported is over 10%, highlighting the substantial impact of the framework.

**Model Agnosticism and Plug-and-Play Nature:**
- The method is shown to be highly model-agnostic, improving the performance of four diverse VLMs:
 - Closed-source: GPT-4o, GPT-4.1
 - Open-source: InternVL3-14B
 - Reinforcement Learning (RL)-tuned with test-time scaling: OpenAI o1
- MindJourney also works effectively with two distinct video world models: the authors' own Search World Model (SWM) and the publicly available Stable-Virtual-Camera (SVC). This demonstrates its robustness and versatility as a plug-and-play solution.

**Performance on SAT Benchmark (SAT-Real and SAT-Synthesized):**
- **SAT-Real:** On the 150 real-image queries of SAT-Real, MindJourney provides uniform and sizable gains. For instance, GPT-4o's accuracy increased significantly (from 60.3% to 70.6% with SWM). GPT-4.1 augmented with MindJourney surpassed the performance of vanilla o1. Crucially, when o1 itself was paired with MindJourney, it achieved a new state-of-the-art on SAT-Real (from 74.6% to 84.7% with SWM), demonstrating that MindJourney complements existing advanced reasoning techniques. These results confirm the method's generalization to real-world, outdoor scenarios.
- **SAT-Synthesized:** On a 500-question subset of the 4000 synthetic questions, MindJourney reproduced the pattern seen on real images, with mean accuracy improving by an average of 8.0%. Again, MindJourney-augmented models (e.g., GPT-4.1 + SWM) outperformed vanilla o1, and o1 further improved when integrated with MindJourney (from 72.4% to 78.6% with SVC). Across all five SAT question types (EgoM, ObjM, EgoAct, GoalAim, Pers), the highest score was consistently achieved by a MindJourney-augmented model, underscoring its consistent reasoning improvement.

**Ablation Studies:**
- **Search Depth (`n`):** The optimal search depth varied depending on the world model and dataset. On SAT-Real, performance with SWM peaked at 2 search steps and slightly dropped at 3, suggesting that SWM, trained mostly on indoor synthetic data, struggled to maintain fidelity for longer roll-outs in complex outdoor real scenes. On SAT-Synthesized, performance continued to rise through step 3, indicating deeper exploration is beneficial when the world model can faithfully render longer sequences. This highlights a current bottleneck in world model capability.
- **Pruning Threshold (`γ`):** The VLM score threshold for pruning candidates was crucial. A lenient threshold allowed low-quality, potentially noisy views into the evidence buffer, diluting the signal and lowering accuracy. This effect was more pronounced on the Real split, where generation quality is inherently more challenging.
- **Trajectory Description:** Ablation studies showed that providing natural language trajectory descriptions (e.g., "move forward 0.2 m, then turn right 30 degrees") alongside the generated images is necessary for optimal performance, indicating that the VLM benefits from explicit context about the imagined movement.
- **Time Consumption:** There's a trade-off between inference time and accuracy, influenced by search steps and world model iterations. Reducing world model iterations (e.g., from 50 to 20) significantly sped up inference but degraded video quality and thus accuracy. However, a setting with 2 search steps and 20 iterations was identified as a cost-effective alternative with reasonable performance.

**Key Analysis Points:**
- **Synergy with RL-based Test-Time Scaling:** MindJourney's world-model-driven search provides information largely orthogonal to the inductive bias learned through RL chain-of-thought, implying that a physically consistent imaginary workspace can *enhance* rather than replace other forms of test-time self-improvement.
- **World Model Capability as a Bottleneck:** The primary limitation identified is the world model's ability to maintain geometric and photometric consistency over very long roll-outs, especially in out-of-domain (real-world) scenarios. Improvements in world model fidelity are crucial for unlocking further gains from deeper exploration.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

MindJourney represents a significant advance in equipping Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with robust 3D spatial reasoning capabilities, moving them closer to human-like understanding of the physical world. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

**Conceptual Breakthrough:**
- **Test-Time Imagination with Physical Consistency:** MindJourney introduces the pioneering concept of granting a reasoning engine a *physically consistent simulator* at test time. This departs from traditional test-time scaling methods that are largely text-centric or rely on abstract reasoning, providing a concrete, geometry-aware evidence gathering mechanism.
- **Complementary to Existing Reasoning Paradigms:** The finding that world-model-driven search *complements* and *enhances* even sophisticated RL-based self-reflection pipelines (like OpenAI o1) is a profound conceptual advance. It suggests that embodying reasoning agents with a "mental workspace" for physical simulation is a powerful, orthogonal dimension to improving AI intelligence.

**Practical Significance and Broad Applicability:**
- **Simple, Training-Free, Plug-and-Play:** MindJourney is a simple, training-free, and plug-and-play framework. This ease of integration means it can immediately benefit a wide range of existing VLMs without costly fine-tuning, accelerating their deployment in spatial reasoning tasks.
- **Model-Agnostic Nature:** Its demonstrated effectiveness across diverse proprietary and open-source VLMs, and with different world models, highlights its general applicability and robustness. This broad compatibility makes it a valuable tool for the AI community.
- **State-of-the-Art Performance:** By achieving new state-of-the-art accuracy on the challenging SAT benchmark, MindJourney proves its practical efficacy in improving real-world spatial understanding.

**Potential Impact and Applications:**
- **Embodied AI and Robotics:** MindJourney could accelerate progress in various embodied AI applications. Robots equipped with better 3D spatial reasoning could:
 - **Navigate complex, cluttered homes** more safely and efficiently for elder care or domestic assistance.
 - **Perform remote inspection of hazardous industrial sites** or disaster zones without human entry, interpreting visual cues with greater depth.
 - **Enhance manipulation tasks** by anticipating object movements and environmental changes.
- **Immersive Technologies (AR/VR):** For education, therapy, or entertainment, richer augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) experiences could be delivered by systems that better understand and simulate user movements and environmental interactions.
- **Safer Decision-Making:** By enabling agents to perform "imagined roll-outs" and evaluate consequences in a simulated environment, MindJourney can lead to safer decision-making, reducing costly and risky real-world trial-and-error in domains like autonomous driving or industrial automation.
- **General AI Development:** By providing a fundamental missing piece of human cognition—3D spatial imagination—MindJourney contributes to the broader goal of building more generally intelligent and robust AI systems.

**Limitations and Future Directions:**
The paper acknowledges several limitations that point to fertile ground for future research:
- **Single Reference View:** Current MindJourney assumes a single input image. Extending it to a multi-source setting, where multiple images serve as "portals" for exploration and evidence fusion, is a natural next step for richer scene understanding.
- **Question-Agnostic World Models:** Current controllable video world models are generally unaware of the downstream query, potentially hallucinating irrelevant or contradictory views. Future work should focus on developing query-conditioned world models or integrating lightweight constraint mechanisms to ensure imagined roll-outs are consistent with the task.
- **World Model Fidelity:** The primary bottleneck identified is the world model's ability to maintain geometric and photometric consistency over much longer and more complex trajectories. Advances in video diffusion models will directly translate to greater benefits for MindJourney.

**Ethical Considerations (Broader Impacts):**
The authors responsibly discuss the broader societal impacts. While the technology offers immense benefits, more capable spatial reasoning could also enhance autonomous surveillance or military platforms. Greater autonomy could lead to job displacement in manual labor sectors. Furthermore, training large video world models consumes considerable energy and can inherit biases from training data (e.g., under-representation of certain environments). The paper advocates for researchers to pair technical advances with robust provenance tracking for generated content, scenario-specific safety constraints, data-diversity audits, and transparent reporting of compute footprints, ensuring responsible development and deployment of this powerful technology.

In conclusion, MindJourney presents a compelling new direction for improving AI's spatial reasoning, offering both immediate practical benefits and a profound conceptual framework for future advancements in embodied intelligence.
