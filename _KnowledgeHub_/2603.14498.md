# R3DP: Real-Time 3D-Aware Policy for Embodied Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and collaborators developed R3DP (Real-Time 3D-Aware Policy), a framework that integrates powerful 3D priors from large vision foundation models into real-time embodied manipulation policies. R3DP achieved an average success rate of 69.0% on 10 RoboTwin benchmark tasks, outperforming multi-view Diffusion Policy by 51.4%, and demonstrated efficient real-world performance with a 62.18 ms observation encoding latency on an RTX 4090 GPU.

## Problem

- Existing visuomotor imitation learning policies often rely on 2D visual inputs, lacking explicit 3D scene understanding crucial for contact-rich manipulation and precise spatial reasoning.
- Large-scale 3D vision foundation models offer strong 3D priors but their high computational cost prevents real-time integration into robotic control loops, which require low latency.
- Current multi-view policy designs frequently fuse camera inputs heuristically without leveraging explicit camera geometry, leading to inconsistent 3D representations and underutilization of geometric cues.

## Method

- R3DP introduces an Asynchronous Fast–Slow Collaboration (AFSC) module, using a 'slow' large 3D foundation model for sparse key frames and a 'fast' Temporal Feature Prediction Network (TFPNet) for real-time feature propagation.
- A lightweight Temporal Feature Prediction Network (TFPNet) is pre-trained to distill 3D-aware features from the large foundation model, enabling consistent and efficient temporal context modeling for intermediate frames.
- The Multi-View Feature Fuser (MVFF) incorporates Projective Positional Encoding (PRoPE) to explicitly leverage camera intrinsics and extrinsics, achieving geometry-aware and consistent multi-view feature aggregation.

## Results

- R3DP achieved an average success rate of 69.0% on the 10 RoboTwin simulation tasks, showing significant improvements over baselines, including a 51.4% gain over DP-multi and 11.4% over DP3.
- In real-world experiments on a bimanual robotic system, R3DP outperformed DP and DP3 by 40.9% and 23.3% average success rates respectively, demonstrating robustness to real-world sensor noise.
- The framework maintained real-time efficiency with an observation encoding latency of 40.3 ms in simulation (with τ=8τ=8) and 62.18 ms on an NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU, while offering configurable efficiency via the key frame interval τ with minimal performance impact.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Powerful 3D priors can be efficiently incorporated into real-time policies by asynchronously updating high-fidelity 3D features from a slow foundation model and propagating them with a fast prediction network.
> - Distilling knowledge from a large 3D vision foundation model into a smaller temporal prediction network enables effective real-time feature estimation and consistent temporal context understanding.
> - Explicitly integrating camera geometric parameters (intrinsics and extrinsics) into multi-view feature fusion significantly enhances spatial consistency and robustness in complex 3D manipulation tasks.
