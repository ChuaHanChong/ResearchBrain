# EgoExo-WM: Unlocking Exo Video for Ego World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> The EgoExo-WM framework demonstrates a method to enable egocentric world models to learn from abundant exocentric video by synthesizing action-aligned egocentric views. This approach reduces L2 embedding error by over 50% on certain datasets and improves goal-directed planning for 3D human motion compared to models trained solely on egocentric data.

## Problem

- Egocentric video data is difficult and expensive to collect at scale, limiting the diversity of activities for world model training.
- Egocentric views often suffer from partial observability, obscuring critical body parts like hands, which hinders precise 3D human motion extraction for defining an action space.
- Abundant exocentric (third-person) video data lacks direct alignment with an agent's first-person perception and action space, making it unsuitable for direct egocentric world model training.

## Method

- The **EgoExo-WM** framework trains egocentric world models using a ==human motion-conditioned architecture== that learns jointly from real egocentric data and synthetic egocentric data derived from exocentric videos.
- ==EgoX-Body== is developed as an extension to synthesize visually plausible and action-aligned egocentric videos from exocentric sources by explicitly incorporating ==3D human kinematic priors== and egocentric hand overlays.
- A ==69-parameter 3D human motion vector==, encompassing root translation and SMPL joint rotations, is used as an explicit and interpretable action representation, complemented by an auxiliary ==wrist-position consistency loss== for hand interaction fidelity.

## Results

- EgoExo-WM consistently outperformed baselines in open-loop future visual predictions, reducing average L2 embedding error by over **50%** on HOMAGE and LEMMA datasets and achieving higher wrist PCK@20 for action consistency.
- The framework enhanced goal-directed planning, yielding the lowest Mean Per Joint Position Error (MPJPE) for both whole-body and wrist motion across all evaluated datasets in model-predictive control tasks.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the performance gains are directly attributable to the action-aligned egocentric conversion from exocentric video, as models trained with raw exocentric data or only egocentric data showed inferior results.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Abundant exocentric video can be effectively utilized to scale egocentric world model training, provided the data is appropriately transformed into an action-aligned egocentric format.
> - Explicitly incorporating 3D human kinematic priors during exocentric-to-egocentric video conversion is crucial for synthesizing high-quality, action-consistent egocentric data, overcoming limitations of prior view synthesis methods.
> - Training a world model with this expanded, action-aligned data, leveraging 3D human motion, substantially improves both future visual prediction and goal-directed planning for embodied agents.
