# Reasoning with Language Model is Planning with World Model

> [!summary] Summary
> The Reasoning via Planning (RAP) framework from UC San Diego and University of Florida integrates Large Language Models (LLMs) with an internal world model and Monte Carlo Tree Search to enable deliberate, multi-step reasoning. This approach allows LLaMA-33B to outperform GPT-4 on plan generation and achieves higher accuracy on math and logical reasoning tasks compared to existing LLM reasoning methods.

## Problem

- Current Large Language Models (LLMs) often lack an explicit internal 'world model' to simulate the consequences of actions or anticipate future states in complex reasoning tasks.
- Existing LLM reasoning paradigms, such as Chain-of-Thought, generate steps autoregressively without explicit feedback mechanisms to evaluate intermediate steps or balance exploration versus exploitation in reasoning paths.
- LLMs struggle with problems requiring deliberate planning and foresight, leading to high error rates in tasks like multi-step plan generation or complex logical deduction.

## Method

- The ==Reasoning via Planning (RAP)== framework repurposes LLMs to serve dual roles: as a reasoning agent to generate actions and as a ==world model== to predict subsequent states.
- ==Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)== is employed as the core planning algorithm to strategically explore the reasoning space, enabling the LLM to balance exploring novel paths and exploiting promising options.
- A flexible ==reward mechanism== is introduced, incorporating action likelihood, state confidence, LLM self-evaluation, and task-specific heuristics, to quantitatively assess and guide the quality of each reasoning step.

## Results

- On Blocksworld plan generation, LLaMA-33B with RAP achieved an average success rate of **64%**, surpassing GPT-4 with Chain-of-Thought by **33%** relative improvement.
- For GSM8K math reasoning, RAP achieved **48.6%** accuracy, with RAP-Aggregation further boosting it to **51.6%**, outperforming strong baselines like Chain-of-Thought with Self-Consistency.
- In PrOntoQA logical reasoning, RAP achieved **94.2%** prediction accuracy and **78.8%** proof accuracy, demonstrating improved performance over Chain-of-Thought and its self-consistency variants.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - LLMs can be effectively repurposed to function as internal 'world models,' simulating environmental dynamics for planning and predicting state transitions.
> - Integrating principled search algorithms like Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) with LLMs enables more deliberate and robust multi-step reasoning beyond autoregressive generation.
> - Explicitly modeling world states and incorporating diverse reward signals are crucial for enhancing LLM performance on complex planning tasks, allowing for guided exploration and evaluation.
