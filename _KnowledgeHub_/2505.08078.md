# What Matters for Batch Online Reinforcement Learning in Robotics?

> [!summary] Summary
> Stanford University researchers systematically investigate batch online reinforcement learning for robotics across six simulated manipulation tasks and one real-world task, discovering that value-based RL combined with implicit policy extraction and expressive diffusion policies achieves up to 2× performance improvement over imitation learning approaches, while proposing a practical three-step recipe (train expressive IL policy, train Q-function, perform implicit policy extraction) that enables robots to autonomously improve from self-collected data with 30% higher success rates in real-world manipulation.

## Problem

- Traditional robot learning relies heavily on expensive human-collected demonstrations, limiting scalability
- Existing batch online RL methods often fail to effectively improve from autonomously collected robot data
- Imitation learning and filtered-IL approaches frequently converge to suboptimal policies when learning from autonomous rollouts

## Method

- Conduct systematic empirical study across three key dimensions: algorithm class, ==policy extraction== methods, and policy expressivity
- Develop practical recipe combining value-based RL with implicit policy extraction and expressive diffusion-based policies
- Add temporally-correlated noise during autonomous data collection to improve exploration diversity

## Results

- Achieves up to 2× performance improvement over previous methods across six simulated manipulation tasks
- Demonstrates **30%** improvement in success rate on real-world tape hanging task compared to baseline approaches
- Shows consistent benefits across diverse robotic manipulation scenarios from Robomimic, Adroit, and MimicGen environments

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Value-based RL significantly outperforms imitation learning approaches for autonomous improvement in robotics
> - Implicit policy extraction (selecting best actions from policy distribution) proves crucial over explicit methods from offline RL
> - Expressive policy classes like diffusion models are necessary for generating and utilizing diverse autonomous data effectively
