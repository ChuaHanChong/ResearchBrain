# Scaling Vision Pre-Training to 4K Resolution

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from UC Berkeley and NVIDIA developed PS3 (Pre-training with Scale-Selective Scaling), a framework enabling efficient CLIP-style vision pre-training at 4K resolution through localized contrastive learning and intelligent patch selection. This approach reduces pre-training compute by 79x and powers VILA-HD, an MLLM that achieved state-of-the-art results on newly introduced 4K-specific benchmarks, outperforming models like GPT-4o by 16.1%.

## Problem

- Most modern vision models are pre-trained at low resolutions (e.g., 378x378 pixels), leading to a fundamental inability to perceive fine visual details required by many real-world applications.
- Scaling vision pre-training to high resolutions incurs a prohibitive computational cost, increasing quadratically for CNNs and quartically for Vision Transformers (ViTs).
- Existing methods for high-resolution MLLMs are suboptimal, either adapting low-resolution models post-training or incurring significant latency and memory overhead by processing entire high-resolution images.

## Method

- Developed ==PS3==, a ==scale-selective pre-training framework== that learns detailed visual representations by focusing on local regions rather than global processing.
- Created a new pre-training dataset of **75M** high-resolution images paired with automated local captions and bounding boxes for salient regions and documents.
- Designed a ==three-stage vision encoder== for PS3 that extracts low-resolution global features, performs text-aware or saliency-based ==multi-scale patch selection==, and processes high-resolution patches using a low-resolution KV cache for global context.
- Integrated PS3 into MLLMs (==VILA-HD==) with specific fine-tuning strategies to align high-resolution features and enable prompt-aware patch selection.

## Results

- Achieved a **79x** reduction in pre-training compute compared to global contrastive learning approaches like SigLIP when scaling to 4K resolution.
- VILA-HD-4K, powered by PS3, significantly surpassed state-of-the-art MLLMs on the new 4KPro benchmark, showing a **16.1%** improvement over GPT-4o and **7.5%** over Qwen2.5-VL with **1.67x** speedup.
- PS3 demonstrated superior efficiency and performance over token pruning methods, consistently providing lower ViT latency and better accuracy while being the only method capable of efficiently handling 4K resolution without out-of-memory errors.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Efficient 4K vision pre-training is achievable with a near-constant computational cost by combining localized contrastive learning with intelligent, selective patch processing.
> - Leveraging high-resolution pre-training data fundamentally improves visual feature quality for MLLMs, leading to superior perception compared to training-free adaptations or post-encoding token pruning.
> - Existing benchmarks often do not genuinely require ultra-high-resolution perception, necessitating dedicated datasets like 4KPro to evaluate models on tasks demanding true 4K understanding.
