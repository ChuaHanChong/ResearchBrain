# Scaling Vision Pre-Training to 4K Resolution

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from UC Berkeley and NVIDIA developed PS3 (Pre-training with Scale-Selective Scaling), a framework enabling efficient CLIP-style vision pre-training at 4K resolution through localized contrastive learning and intelligent patch selection. This approach reduces pre-training compute by 79x and powers VILA-HD, an MLLM that achieved state-of-the-art results on newly introduced 4K-specific benchmarks, outperforming models like GPT-4o by 16.1%.

## Problem

- Most modern vision models are pre-trained at low resolutions (e.g., 378x378 pixels), leading to a fundamental inability to perceive fine visual details required by many real-world applications.
- Scaling vision pre-training to high resolutions incurs a prohibitive computational cost, increasing quadratically for CNNs and quartically for Vision Transformers (ViTs).
- Existing methods for high-resolution MLLMs are suboptimal, either adapting low-resolution models post-training or incurring significant latency and memory overhead by processing entire high-resolution images.

## Method

- Developed ==PS3==, a ==scale-selective pre-training framework== that learns detailed visual representations by focusing on local regions, trained on a new dataset of **75M** high-resolution images with automated local captions and bounding boxes.
- Designed a ==three-stage vision encoder== for PS3 that extracts low-resolution global features, performs text-aware or saliency-based ==multi-scale patch selection==, and processes high-resolution patches using a low-resolution KV cache for global context.
- Integrated PS3 into MLLMs (==VILA-HD==) with specific fine-tuning strategies to align high-resolution features and enable prompt-aware patch selection.

## Results

- Achieved a **79x** reduction in pre-training compute compared to global contrastive learning approaches like SigLIP when scaling to 4K resolution.
- VILA-HD-4K, powered by PS3, significantly surpassed state-of-the-art MLLMs on the new 4KPro benchmark, showing a **16.1%** improvement over GPT-4o and **7.5%** over Qwen2.5-VL with **1.67x** speedup.
- PS3 demonstrated superior efficiency and performance over token pruning methods, consistently providing lower ViT latency and better accuracy while being the only method capable of efficiently handling 4K resolution without out-of-memory errors.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Efficient 4K vision pre-training is achievable with a near-constant computational cost by combining localized contrastive learning with intelligent, selective patch processing.
> - Leveraging high-resolution pre-training data fundamentally improves visual feature quality for MLLMs, leading to superior perception compared to training-free adaptations or post-encoding token pruning.
> - Existing benchmarks often do not genuinely require ultra-high-resolution perception, necessitating dedicated datasets like 4KPro to evaluate models on tasks demanding true 4K understanding.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of computer vision has witnessed tremendous progress, largely driven by large-scale pre-training paradigms such as **CLIP-style vision pre-training** (e.g., CLIP, SigLIP) and **self-supervised learning** (e.g., DINOv2). These models have become foundational for a wide array of vision tasks and, more recently, for **Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**, providing general-purpose, language-aligned visual understanding.

However, a fundamental limitation persists: most modern vision models are pre-trained at relatively **low resolutions** (e.g., 378x378 pixels). This limitation stems from the **quadratic increase in computational cost** for Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and an even steeper **quartic increase for Vision Transformers (ViTs)** as image resolution scales. This computational barrier makes pre-training beyond 1K resolution practically infeasible with traditional approaches, despite the undeniable need for high-resolution perception in many real-world applications like autonomous driving (e.g., spotting a small stop sign) or detailed document analysis.

Previous attempts to address high-resolution visual perception in MLLMs typically fall into a few categories:
1. **Training-free methods:** Approaches like AnyRes and S$^2$ adapt pre-trained low-resolution vision models to higher resolutions by splitting large images into smaller tiles or using multi-scale processing at inference time. While these methods offer some improvements, they are **suboptimal** because the underlying vision encoder was never trained to learn high-quality, high-resolution features from large-scale data.
2. **Increased token processing:** Some MLLMs (e.g., Qwen2-VL) process full high-resolution images, but this comes at a significant computational cost, leading to high inference latency and often memory limitations (Out-Of-Memory, OOM) for ultra-high resolutions like 4K.
3. **Token pruning/selection for MLLMs:** Methods like ToMe, FastV, and VisionZip aim to improve efficiency by reducing redundant visual tokens *after* the initial vision encoding. However, these methods still require the vision encoder to process the entire high-resolution image first, incurring substantial latency in the vision backbone itself, making them inefficient for 4K.

PS3 (Pre-training with Scale-Selective Scaling) positions itself as a **paradigm shift** by tackling the root cause: enabling **vision pre-training itself at ultra-high (4K) resolution with near-constant cost**. It departs from the global contrastive learning of previous models and introduces **localized contrastive learning** coupled with intelligent **patch selection**. This approach directly addresses the computational bottleneck during pre-training, allowing the model to learn fine-grained details from vast quantities of high-resolution data. By integrating PS3 as the vision encoder into MLLMs (resulting in VILA-HD), the work directly advances the state-of-the-art in high-resolution multimodal understanding, pushing beyond the limitations of prior training-free adaptations and post-encoding token pruning strategies. It also highlights a critical gap in existing benchmarks, leading to the creation of 4KPro to properly evaluate true 4K perception.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to **scale CLIP-style vision pre-training to 4K resolution (e.g., 3780x3780 pixels) with a near-constant computational cost**. This overarching goal is driven by several key motivations:

- **Necessity of High-Resolution Perception:** Many daily and professional tasks demand precise perception of fine visual details. Examples include discerning small text on a distant sign for autonomous vehicles, reading detailed charts or documents, understanding complex user interfaces, or identifying minute objects in household or gaming scenarios. Current low-resolution vision models are fundamentally incapable of accurately performing these tasks.
- **Limitations of Existing Approaches:** Current vision pre-training is bottlenecked by the quadratic (or quartic for ViTs) increase in computational cost with resolution. Existing solutions, such as running low-resolution pre-trained models at higher resolutions via tiling (AnyRes, S$^2$) or token pruning *after* full image encoding, are suboptimal. They either fail to leverage large-scale high-resolution pre-training data or incur prohibitive computational overhead for the vision encoder.
- **Unlocking New Capabilities for MLLMs:** MLLMs are increasingly powerful, but their visual understanding is often constrained by the quality of their vision encoders. Enabling truly high-resolution perception at the pre-training stage can significantly enhance MLLMs' ability to reason about detailed visual information, leading to more accurate and reliable real-world applications.
- **Computational Efficiency:** To make high-resolution perception practical, it must be computationally efficient. The project aims to reduce the pre-training compute cost significantly (e.g., 79x reduction compared to SigLIP's global contrastive learning at 4K resolution) by avoiding exhaustive processing of every pixel and instead focusing on informative regions.

In essence, the paper aims to break the resolution barrier in vision pre-training to empower MLLMs with accurate, efficient, and truly fine-grained visual understanding, addressing a critical need for next-generation AI systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The core innovation of PS3 lies in its **scale-selective pre-training**, which efficiently learns detailed visual representations by focusing on local regions rather than processing entire high-resolution images globally. This methodology addresses the challenges through a tripartite approach: redesigned data, a novel model architecture, and a specialized pre-training algorithm.

#### 3.1. Pre-Training Data of PS3

To facilitate localized contrastive learning, PS3 requires a new type of pre-training data: high-resolution images paired with bounding boxes and detailed captions of salient local regions.
- **High-Resolution Images:** A massive dataset of 75M images, ranging from 1K to 4K resolution, was collected. This includes 38M natural images from DataComp and SA-1B, and 37M document images from IDL and PDFA.
- **Local Captions and Bounding Boxes (Natural Images):** An automated pipeline was developed:
 1. **Saliency Detection:** EfficientViT-SAM is used to generate segmentation masks for the entire image. Salient regions are identified as those containing small or dense masks, implying rich details.
 2. **Local Caption Generation:** An off-the-shelf MLLM (e.g., Qwen2-VL) acts as a captioner. It's provided with both the cropped local region and the global image context to generate detailed captions about the local area. This yields 134M pairs of local captions and bounding boxes.
- **Local Captions and Bounding Boxes (Documents):** For document images, existing bounding boxes and OCR results (sentences/words) from IDL and PDFA are directly sampled and used as local captions, generating 148M pairs.
- **Global Captions:** Global captions are also generated for natural images using the same MLLM captioner, complementing the local training.

#### 3.2. Model Design of PS3

The PS3 vision encoder is designed in three stages to enable low-resolution global encoding, selective patch processing, and high-resolution local feature extraction.
- **Stage 1: Low-Res Feature Extraction:** A standard Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture, initialized from SigLIP-SO400M, processes the image resized to a low resolution (e.g., 378x378 pixels) to extract global contextual features.
- **Stage 2: Top-Down or Bottom-Up Patch Selection:** This stage determines which high-resolution regions are most important.
 - **Bottom-Up Selection:** Based on inherent image saliency, using a constant learnable vector as a "prompt" and cosine similarity with low-res visual features.
 - **Top-Down Selection:** Based on relevance to a text prompt, using the text embedding for cosine similarity.
 - **High-Res Refinement:** To locate fine-grained details, an auxiliary light-weight ConvNeXt encoder extracts high-resolution features (e.g., 1512 resolution), whose selection scores are interpolated and averaged with low-res scores.
 - **Multi-Scale Patch Selection:** Top-k patches are selected across multiple predefined scales (e.g., 756x756, 1512x1512, 3780x3780), with k proportional to the total patches at each scale during pre-training, but flexible for downstream tasks.
- **Stage 3: High-Res Multi-Scale Feature Extraction:** The selected high-resolution patches are then processed:
 1. **Patch Embedding:** Selected patches are embedded using the same patch embedding module as Stage 1.
 2. **Scale-Aware Positional Embedding:** Positional embeddings are added, interpolated from low-res embeddings, plus a scale-specific embedding to differentiate tokens from different scales at the same spatial position.
 3. **High-Res Feature Extraction with Low-Res KV Cache:** The gathered multi-scale tokens are processed by the *same ViT as Stage 1*. Critically, the keys and values from the low-resolution feature extraction (Stage 1) are augmented to the self-attention layers, providing global context to the local high-resolution features.

#### 3.3. Pre-Training Algorithm of PS3

PS3 is pre-trained to jointly learn detailed visual representation and effective patch selection.
- **Localized Contrastive Loss:** The model contrasts high-resolution features from selected local regions with their corresponding detailed local caption embeddings using a sigmoid contrastive loss, similar to SigLIP.
 - **Ground-Truth Selection Score:** During pre-training, to guide the learning, patches are selected based on ground-truth bounding boxes (teacher forcing), rather than the model's predicted selection score.
 - **Pooling Only Tokens in Ground-Truth Boxes:** Attention pooling for contrastive loss is constrained to only tokens within the ground-truth box, preventing alignment of irrelevant features.
 - **Mixing Global and Local Contrast:** Each batch includes both low-resolution global image-caption pairs and high-resolution local region-caption pairs to maintain global understanding quality.
 - **Avoiding Intra-Image Contrast:** Batches are curated to ensure each image appears only once, preventing problematic contrasts between visually similar regions within the same image.
- **Box Supervision for Patch Selection:**
 - **Top-Down Selection:** The predicted selection score map (for a local caption) is supervised against a ground-truth map (1 inside the corresponding bounding box, 0 outside) using cross-entropy and DICE loss.
 - **Bottom-Up Selection:** The predicted saliency score (without text prompt) is supervised against a ground-truth map generated from all salient region bounding boxes in the image.

#### 3.4. VILA-HD: Enabling High-Resolution MLLM with PS3

PS3 is integrated into MLLMs, specifically building upon NVILA.
- **Model Architecture:** VILA-HD uses PS3 as its vision encoder and Qwen2 as its LLM, connected via a 2-layer MLP projector.
- **Inference Process:** Low-res global features from PS3 Stage 1 are sent to the LLM first. For top-down selection, the LLM's last-layer latent embedding of the user's text prompt is used by PS3 Stage 2 to select high-res patches. These patches are then encoded by PS3 Stage 3 (with additional positional embedding) and appended to the LLM's input sequence. PS3 can run Stage 2 and 3 multiple times to select an arbitrary number of high-res patches.
- **Fine-Tuning Data for VILA-HD:**
 - **Patch Selection Data:** PS3 pre-training data is repurposed. Local captions are re-targeted into questions using LLaMA-3.1, creating (high-res image, question, bounding box) triplets to fine-tune VILA-HD's top-down patch selection.
 - **High-Res Feature Alignment Data:** Existing low-res VQA data is transformed by pasting images onto large solid-color backgrounds (e.g., 4096x4096). This forces the MLLM to utilize PS3's high-res features for answering questions about the now smaller, informative regions within the large image.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research presents several compelling findings demonstrating the effectiveness of PS3 and VILA-HD:

- **1. Breakthrough in 4K Vision Pre-Training Efficiency:** PS3 successfully scales CLIP-style pre-training to 4K resolution, achieving a **near-constant computational cost** and reducing pre-training compute by **79x** compared to global contrastive learning approaches like SigLIP at 4K.

- **2. Superior Scaling Properties of PS3/VILA-HD:**
 - **Whole-Image Resolution Scaling:** When all high-resolution patches are selected, PS3 significantly improves performance (e.g., 14.2% over SigLIP at 1512 resolution), consistently outperforming training-free baselines (AnyRes, S$^2$) due to superior high-res feature quality from pre-training.
 - **Constant-Cost Scaling:** PS3 improves performance by scaling to higher resolutions (e.g., 1512 to 4K) even when maintaining a *constant number of selected high-res patches* (and thus constant MLLM inference cost). This is because higher resolution allows selection of more *relevant* detailed patches. PS3 uniquely enables 4K processing where baselines OOM.
 - **Constant-Resolution Scaling & Test-Time Scaling:** PS3 allows for flexible trade-offs between compute and performance by adjusting the number of selected patches. Increasing selected patches at a fixed resolution (or at test time after training with fewer) consistently boosts performance, especially for dense information tasks like DocVQA.

- **3. Introduction and Domination of 4KPro Benchmark:**
 - The paper identifies that most existing benchmarks do not genuinely require 4K resolution perception (their Minimum Recognizable Resolution, MRR, is typically lower).
 - **4KPro** is introduced—a new 4K-resolution image QA benchmark across autonomous vehicle, household, gaming, and UI understanding, where questions are *only* answerable at 4K.
 - On 4KPro, **VILA-HD-4K significantly outperforms state-of-the-art MLLMs**, including GPT-4o (16.1% improvement) and Qwen2.5-VL (7.5% improvement with 1.67x speedup). This underscores the critical impact of 4K pre-training for tasks requiring true ultra-high-resolution understanding.

- **4. State-of-the-Art Visual Representation Quality:**
 - **Vision Encoder Comparison:** PS3, particularly its LLM-aligned version (PS3$_{\text{Lang}}$), achieves state-of-the-art results compared to other vision encoders (e.g., SigLIP2, Perception Encoder) across a wide range of benchmarks, especially on high-resolution VQA tasks (e.g., 8.0% and 10.9% improvements on 4KPro and HRBench$_{4K}$ over PE$_{\text{Lang}}$).
 - **MLLM Comparison:** VILA-HD-8B-4K achieves top-2 performance on high-resolution benchmarks (4KPro, HRBench) and competitive SOTA performance on general VQA tasks, outperforming models like NVILA despite using less data and the same recipe, highlighting the benefit of PS3 pre-training.

- **5. Superior Efficiency over Token Pruning Methods:** PS3's learning-based top-down patch selection provides **consistently lower ViT latency** and **better performance** compared to heuristic-based token pruning methods (ToMe, FastV, VisionZip) at similar token counts. Crucially, PS3 is the *only* method capable of efficiently handling 4K resolution where others OOM due to the initial full ViT processing.

- **6. Generalizability of PS3 Pre-Training:** The PS3 pre-training pipeline is shown to be generalizable, improving performance when applied on top of other state-of-the-art vision encoders like SigLIP-SO400M and C-RADIOv2-L.

- **7. Ablation Studies Confirm Key Design Choices:**
 - **Pre-training Algorithm:** Critical elements include using ground-truth selection scores (+5.1% avg. acc), pooling only tokens within ground-truth boxes (+3.7%), and avoiding intra-image contrast (+5.3%).
 - **Model Architecture:** The low-res KV cache, providing global context to high-res features, yields a significant +8.8% average accuracy improvement. Multi-scale feature extraction also proves beneficial.
 - **Patch Selection:** Both bottom-up and top-down selection significantly improve recall and accuracy over random selection, aiding both training dynamics and inference performance. Top-down selection is superior.
 - **Scale Trade-off:** The optimal balance of patch selection ratios across different image scales (e.g., 756 vs. 1512 resolution) varies by task, allowing flexible optimization for diverse downstream requirements.
 - **High-Res SFT Data:** The naive high-resolution SFT data generally improves high-resolution perception, particularly for natural images (+3.1% on V*Bench), helping align PS3's high-res features with the LLM's text space.

- **8. Visual Feature Quality:** PCA visualizations confirm that PS3 produces sharp, fine-grained, and coherent visual features at 4K resolution, unlike the blurry or noisy features observed in baselines (S$^2$, AnyRes) that lack high-resolution pre-training.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The introduction of PS3 represents a significant leap forward in scaling vision models, with profound implications across both fundamental research and practical applications:

- **Breaking the Resolution Barrier in Vision Pre-training:** PS3 is the first work to demonstrate efficient CLIP-style vision pre-training at 4K resolution. This is a foundational breakthrough that fundamentally changes how vision models can learn from high-fidelity visual data, overcoming a long-standing computational bottleneck. The **near-constant cost** for pre-training is a critical enabler for future scaling.

- **Empowering Next-Generation MLLMs:** By providing MLLMs with a vision encoder (PS3) pre-trained on 4K data, VILA-HD achieves unprecedented levels of detail perception. This allows MLLMs to move beyond superficial understanding to truly interpret fine-grained visual information, making them more robust and capable for complex real-world scenarios.

- **Enabling New High-Resolution Applications:** The enhanced perception capabilities of VILA-HD directly translate to improved performance in critical application domains:
 - **Autonomous Driving:** Better recognition of small, distant road signs, traffic signals, and intricate environmental details.
 - **Household/Robotics:** More precise object recognition and manipulation, understanding complex device interfaces.
 - **Gaming/UI Understanding:** Accurate interpretation of on-screen text, complex game UIs, and small visual cues.
 - **Document/Chart Analysis:** Highly accurate OCR and reasoning over dense textual and graphical information.

- **Efficient High-Resolution Inference:** PS3's learning-based, prompt-aware patch selection mechanism makes high-resolution processing not just possible but *efficient* during inference. This offers a superior alternative to prior methods that either exhaustively process images or rely on less effective heuristic pruning. The ability to "scale up resolution for free" and "trade compute for performance" at test time provides unprecedented flexibility for deployment.

- **Catalyst for Future Research:**
 - **Beyond CLIP-style pre-training:** The selective processing idea can be extended to other vision pre-training paradigms (e.g., DINOv2 for self-supervised learning), potentially unlocking new forms of visual understanding.
 - **Scaling to Videos:** The core concept of intelligently selecting informative patches is even more relevant for high-resolution, high-frame-rate, and long-form videos, which contain immense spatiotemporal redundancy. This could pave the way for scalable video pre-training.
 - **Improved Data Curation:** The findings highlight the importance of high-quality, detail-rich images and accurate local annotations, motivating future work in advanced data filtering and synthetic data generation.
 - **Advanced Patch Selection:** Future research could explore more sophisticated, potentially reinforcement learning-based, patch selection strategies that adaptively choose not just *where* but also *at what scale* to extract details, optimizing for downstream task performance.

- **New Benchmark for True 4K Perception:** The introduction of **4KPro** is a crucial contribution. It addresses the lack of benchmarks that truly necessitate 4K visual input, pushing the community to develop and evaluate models on tasks that reflect the real-world demands for ultra-high-resolution perception.

In conclusion, PS3 represents a pivotal advancement in vision pre-training, effectively resolving the long-standing challenge of scaling to ultra-high resolutions. Its impact will be felt across the entire multimodal AI ecosystem, enabling more capable, efficient, and reliable AI systems for a myriad of detail-sensitive applications.
