# Micro-Macro Retrieval: Reducing Long-Form Hallucination in Large Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework, Micro–Macro Retrieval (M2^22R), enhances factual consistency in large language models by integrating external evidence retrieval with dynamic, fine-grained internal reuse of key facts from a model-managed repository. This method significantly improves accuracy and reduces hallucination in long-form generation by ensuring critical evidence is positioned near the generated output.

## Problem

- Large Language Models frequently generate factually incorrect content, a problem intensified in complex, long-form generation tasks.
- LLMs often fail to effectively utilize crucial evidence embedded within lengthy retrieved contexts or forget intermediate reasoning steps over long generation chains, a phenomenon termed "Lost in Lengthy Contexts."
- Current Retrieval-Augmented Language Models (RALMs) do not explicitly ensure key factual evidence is positioned in close proximity to the generated output, diminishing factual accuracy in extended responses.

## Method

- A ==retrieve-while-generate== framework, Micro–Macro Retrieval (M2^22R), integrates coarse-grained external retrieval (macro retrieval) with fine-grained internal retrieval (micro retrieval) from a model-constructed key information repository.
- During reasoning, the LLM identifies and saves "answer-aligned" facts into an internal repository; during generation, it queries this repository on-demand to insert key information directly before corresponding output tokens.
- Reinforcement learning, guided by curriculum learning and rule-based reward functions, trains the LLM to stably acquire skills for effective information retrieval, key information saving, and precise grounding of generated text.

## Results

- M2^22R demonstrated superior answer correctness on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, and Bamboogle, for instance, achieving **44.11%** Exact Match and **65.98%** LLM-as-a-Judge on HotpotQA with Qwen-**7B**-Instruct.
- The framework effectively reduced hallucination and maintained stable accuracy in long-context, multi-question scenarios (e.g., HotpotQA-2Q, 3Q), where other baselines showed rapid decline.
- An ablation study confirmed the critical role of the retrieve-while-generate mechanism of micro retrieval, showing that one-shot grounding of all key information led to weaker factual consistency.
- M2^22R incurs a low inference overhead, increasing latency by less than **10%** on average compared to baselines, while the key-information repository storage is negligible (50-150 tokens).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Ensuring explicit proximity between critical factual evidence and the specific output tokens it grounds is vital for mitigating hallucination in long-form generation.
> - A retrieve-while-generate paradigm with two distinct retrieval levels—coarse-grained external and fine-grained internal from a model-managed repository—effectively addresses the "Lost in Lengthy Contexts" issue.
> - Curriculum learning is necessary for stable and effective training of the LLM to acquire complex skills for multi-level retrieval, key information storage, and precise factual grounding.
