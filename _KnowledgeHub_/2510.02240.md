# RewardMap: Tackling Sparse Rewards in Fine-grained Visual Reasoning via Multi-Stage Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Westlake University and collaborators developed REWARDMAP, a multi-stage reinforcement learning framework that enhances Multi-modal Large Language Models' performance on fine-grained visual reasoning tasks by tackling sparse rewards. The framework consistently outperformed all open-source baselines on the REASONMAP and REASONMAP-PLUS benchmarks, achieving an average 3.47% improvement across six external generalization benchmarks.

## Problem

- Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs) exhibit limitations in precise spatial and logical reasoning over high-resolution, information-dense images like transit maps.
- Applying standard reinforcement learning (RL) to fine-grained visual reasoning tasks is hindered by sparse rewards, leading to unstable optimization and ineffective exploration.
- Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) often proves insufficient for long-chain, multi-step decision-making required in complex visual reasoning, potentially leading to overfitting.

## Method

- The authors constructed REASONMAP-**PLUS**, an extended dataset with five new question categories and dense reward signals to provide a natural difficulty continuum for effective cold-start training.
- They developed REWARDMAP, a multi-stage RL framework built upon Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), incorporating a difficulty-aware reward design.
- The reward design includes format, correctness, and a novel 'detail reward' that provides partial credit for correctly identified components, all scaled by weights based on map and question difficulty.
- A multi-stage GRPO-based curriculum progressively schedules training data, moving from basic visual understanding (REASONMAP-PLUS) to complex visual reasoning (REASONMAP) tasks.

## Results

- REWARDMAP consistently outperformed all open-source baselines and reference models on the REASONMAP benchmark for route planning (e.g., **31.51%** S./**31.77%** L. Weighted Acc. vs. Qwen2.5-VL-**72B**-Instruct's **26.65%** S./**24.22%** L.) and REASONMAP-PLUS for visual understanding (**74.25%** Weighted Acc.).
- Models trained with REWARDMAP demonstrated improved generalization, achieving an average accuracy increase of **3.47%** across six diverse external benchmarks, with a notable **13.51%** gain on SpatialEval.
- Qualitative comparisons showed REWARDMAP successfully reduced visual confusions and hallucinations, consistently producing correct target routes on complex maps.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A multi-stage curriculum combined with a detailed, difficulty-aware reward function effectively mitigates the sparse reward problem in reinforcement learning for fine-grained visual reasoning tasks.
> - Providing intermediate 'detail rewards' for partially correct answers is crucial for stable optimization and learning in tasks requiring long reasoning chains.
> - Training MLLMs through a structured progression from fundamental perceptual skills to complex reasoning tasks enhances both domain-specific performance and general visual understanding and reasoning capabilities across diverse benchmarks.
