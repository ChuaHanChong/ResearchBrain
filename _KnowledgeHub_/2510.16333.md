# RL makes MLLMs see better than SFT

> [!summary] Summary
> Research from KAIST and NAVER AI Lab demonstrates that Reinforcement Learning (RL) with Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) fundamentally enhances vision encoders within Multimodal Language Models (MLLMs) more effectively than Supervised Finetuning (SFT). This approach yields stronger visual representations and improves MLLM performance on vision-centric tasks, leading to the development of PIVOT, an efficient method for evolving existing vision models.

## Problem

- A prevailing assumption that MLLM performance primarily inherits from the Large Language Model (LLM) backbone, leading to an oversight of the vision encoder's independent role.
- A lack of systematic understanding of how different MLLM post-training strategies (SFT vs. RL) specifically impact the underlying visual representations of the vision encoder.
- The absence of an efficient framework to specifically optimize existing vision encoders for optimal performance within MLLMs, beyond traditional vision pre-training methods.

## Method

- Conducted a systematic comparison of Supervised Finetuning (SFT) and ==Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)== as post-training strategies on MLLMs across varying vision encoder and LLM scales.
- Analyzed the impact of these strategies on vision encoder capabilities through vision-only benchmarks (ImageNet, semantic segmentation) and gradient visualizations (==Grad-CAM==).
- Developed ==PIVOT (Preference-Instructed Vision OpTimization)==, an RL-driven auxiliary training process to efficiently enhance existing vision encoders for MLLM applications.

## Results

- DPO consistently outperformed SFT on MLLM performance, particularly on vision-centric tasks like OCR & Chart VQA (**+**4.2%**p**) and Vision-Centric VQA (**+**2.4%**p**), while showing marginal gains on weakly vision-related tasks.
- DPO-trained vision encoders demonstrated superior performance on vision-only benchmarks, including a **+**1.83%**p** improvement in ImageNet Top-1 accuracy and **+**1.08%**p** increase in patch-level recall for semantic segmentation, over SFT-trained encoders.
- PIVOT-enhanced vision encoders achieved significant performance boosts within MLLMs, with a PIVOT-enhanced SigLIP1-So/14 surpassing a more recent SigLIP2-So/16 (**5**3.2%**p** vs. **5**2.4%**p** average VQA score) at less than **1%** of standard vision pre-training computational cost.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - MLLM post-training, particularly with DPO, actively reshapes and significantly enhances the intrinsic visual representations within the vision encoder, rather than merely aligning an LLM with a frozen vision component.
> - DPO's contrastive nature generates more precise, fine-grained gradient signals that focus on semantically relevant regions, leading to improved object localization and stronger vision-language alignment compared to SFT.
> - Larger LLM backbones provide more informative optimization signals during MLLM training, resulting in vision encoders that achieve higher performance on vision-only tasks after post-training.
