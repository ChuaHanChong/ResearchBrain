# TAROT: Targeted Data Selection via Optimal Transport

> [!summary] Summary
> TAROT introduces a targeted data selection framework that leverages Whitened Feature Distance (WFD) and Optimal Transport (OT) to identify data subsets that optimally enhance model performance on specific target tasks. The method consistently achieved superior results in semantic segmentation, motion prediction, and LLM instruction tuning, often using significantly less data than full datasets or prior methods.

## Problem

- Existing targeted data selection methods, often relying on influence estimation and greedy heuristics, struggle with complex, multimodal data distributions.
- Influence estimation in high-dimensional data is susceptible to bias from dominant feature components, leading to inaccurate data selection.
- Prior greedy selection strategies often assume a restrictive linear additive impact of training samples, which fails to capture non-linear interactions within data.

## Method

- TAROT frames targeted data selection as an ==Optimal Transport (OT)== problem, aiming to select a subset of candidate data that minimizes the OT distance to the target distribution.
- It introduces ==Whitened Feature Distance (WFD)== as a novel pairwise cost metric, based on whitened and normalized model loss gradients, to robustly measure data similarity and influence.
- A greedy algorithm, utilizing the sparsity of ==gradient features== and optionally incorporating data weighting via OT potentials, iteratively selects the most relevant data points to minimize the OT distance.

## Results

- TAROT's Whitened Feature Distance (WFD) consistently outperformed state-of-the-art data attribution methods (e.g., TRAK) in terms of Linear Data Modeling Score (LDS) across various tasks like image classification and motion prediction.
- On semantic segmentation (GTA5 to Cityscapes), TAROT's Optimal Transport Minimization (OTM) scheme automatically selected **~24%** of the data, achieving higher mIoU than models trained on the full dataset or other baselines.
- For motion prediction (multi-source to nuScenes) and LLM instruction tuning, TAROT achieved state-of-the-art performance using significantly reduced data subsets (e.g., **<0.5%** for LLMs), demonstrating high data efficiency and transferability across different model architectures.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Optimal Transport offers a principled framework for targeted data selection by directly minimizing the distributional discrepancy between selected candidate data and a target domain.
> - Whitened Feature Distance (WFD), derived from whitened and normalized model gradients, provides more accurate and robust influence estimates compared to prior methods, especially in high-dimensional and correlated feature spaces.
> - Selecting data based on its distributional alignment with the target task, rather than just individual influence scores, enables models to achieve higher performance with significantly smaller, carefully curated datasets.
