# Improving Dynamic Object Interactions in Text-to-Video Generation with AI Feedback

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Google DeepMind, The University of Tokyo, and Stanford University advanced text-to-video generation by leveraging AI feedback from Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to enhance dynamic object interactions. This approach led to a 14.7% increase in human preference for video quality and physical realism in challenging interaction scenarios.

## Problem

- Text-to-video (T2V) models frequently generate unrealistic, physically inaccurate, or static object movements, hindering practical utility.
- Existing metric-based feedback for T2V models does not specifically target the nuanced and complex aspects of dynamic object interactions.
- Scaling T2V models is challenging due to the high cost and labor intensity of curating video datasets with detailed labels for dynamic interactions.

## Method

- Developed a probabilistic framework to unify Reward-Weighted Regression (RWR) and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) for offline ==reinforcement learning== (==RL==) ==fine-tuning== of video diffusion models.
- Introduced AI feedback from large Vision-Language Models (VLMs), such as Gemini-1.5-Pro, to provide perceptual feedback specifically tailored to object dynamics, physical accuracy, and task completion.
- Applied ==VLM==-guided RL fine-tuning to a 3D-UNet ==video diffusion model==, leveraging a custom dataset of prompts designed for challenging dynamic object movements.

## Results

- DPO fine-tuned with AI feedback (DPO-AIF) achieved absolute gains of +14.7% in human evaluation and +3.5% in Gemini-**1.5**-Pro evaluation for the quality of dynamic interaction scenes.
- The VLM-based AI feedback demonstrated the strongest positive correlation with human preference (Pearson R = **0.746**) among all evaluated feedback types.
- The method substantially improved video generation quality in challenging categories such as 'multiple objects' and 'falling down,' which are difficult for pre-trained models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - AI feedback from Vision-Language Models can effectively serve as a scalable proxy for human supervision, demonstrating a strong correlation with human preference in evaluating video quality.
> - Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) consistently outperforms Reward-Weighted Regression (RWR) for fine-tuning, showing better generalization and aligning outputs without signs of overfitting due to its explicit negative gradient.
> - Reinforcement learning fine-tuning, particularly when guided by VLM-based AI feedback, substantially enhances the realism and accuracy of dynamic object interactions in generated videos.
