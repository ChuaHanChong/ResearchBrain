# Nemotron-Cascade: Scaling Cascaded Reinforcement Learning for General-Purpose Reasoning Models

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA's Nemotron-Cascade framework enables the development of general-purpose Large Language Models with enhanced reasoning capabilities by employing a cascaded reinforcement learning approach. This method sequentially fine-tunes models across diverse domains, achieving state-of-the-art performance in areas such as competitive programming, math, and software engineering, while supporting both "thinking" and "instruct" modes within a single unified model.

## Problem

- Integrating diverse reasoning tasks into LLMs with traditional reinforcement learning is complex due to varied response lengths, verification latencies, and reward computation methods.
- Developing a single, unified LLM capable of seamlessly operating in both 'instruct' (direct answer) and 'deep thinking' (step-by-step reasoning) modes without performance degradation remains a challenge.
- Efficiently combining different reinforcement learning objectives, like RLHF for alignment and RLVR for verifiable reasoning, within an LLM training pipeline.

## Method

- Introduced Nemotron-Cascade, a two-stage post-training pipeline consisting of multi-stage Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed by ==Cascaded Reinforcement Learning (Cascade RL)==.
- Implemented ==sequential, domain-wise RL framework==, progressing from general alignment (RLHF) to specialized domains like instruction following, math, code, and software engineering.
- Utilized the ==Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)== algorithm with strict on-policy training and tailored data curation, reward models, and verification methods for each cascaded RL stage.

## Results

- Nemotron-Cascade models achieved state-of-the-art performance across a wide range of benchmarks, including MMLU, GPQA-Diamond, ArenaHard, AIME, LiveCodeBench, and SWE-bench Verified.
- The 14B-Thinking model attained silver medal performance in the 2025 International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI) with **343.37 points** and recorded a **43.1%** pass@1 on SWE-bench Verified.
- Progressive analysis across RL stages confirmed that RLHF improves token efficiency and accuracy, while subsequent domain-specific RL stages further enhance reasoning and problem-solving without degrading prior domain performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Cascade RL effectively addresses the heterogeneity of multi-domain LLM training by enabling sequential specialization, minimizing catastrophic forgetting, and reducing engineering complexity.
> - Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), when applied as an initial step, unexpectedly boosts reasoning performance in subsequent math and code tasks, improving the efficiency of later RL stages.
> - A unified model, trained with a specific 'Half-Half' RLHF strategy, can successfully operate in both 'instruct' and 'deep thinking' modes, challenging the necessity for separate specialized reasoning models.
