# Kimi-VL Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Kimi-VL is an efficient open-source Mixture-of-Experts vision-language model that achieves competitive performance across diverse multimodal tasks, often matching or exceeding larger proprietary and open-source models with only 2.8 billion activated parameters. Its "Thinking" variants enhance reasoning, while the architecture supports 128K extended context and native-resolution visual inputs.

## Problem

- Open-source multimodal models trail proprietary ones in scalability, computational efficiency, and advanced reasoning capabilities.
- Existing open-source vision-language models often rely on computationally intensive dense architectures.
- Current open-source solutions lack comprehensive support for long-context understanding and sophisticated multi-step chain-of-thought reasoning.

## Method

- Employ an efficient ==Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)== architecture for the language model, ==Moonlight==, with only **2.8B** activated parameters.
- Integrate a native-resolution vision encoder, ==MoonViT==, capable of processing diverse image resolutions up to **3.2 million** pixels.
- Utilize a multi-stage pre-training and post-training pipeline, including specialized long-context activation and reinforcement learning for enhanced reasoning.

## Results

- Kimi-VL-A3B achieves **83.1%** on MMBench-EN-v1.1 and **68.7%** on MathVista, often matching or surpassing larger proprietary models like GPT-4o with only **2.8 billion** activated parameters.
- The model demonstrates strong performance in specific domains, scoring **86.7%** on OCRBench, **92.0%** on ScreenSpot-V2 for agent grounding, and competitive results on long-context benchmarks like MMLongBench-Doc.
- Kimi-VL-Thinking variants exhibit significant reasoning improvements, with gains such as **+15.4%** on MathVision, and achieve a **65.2%** score on VideoMMMU, setting a new open-source state-of-the-art.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High performance in vision-language models is achievable with an efficient Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture, significantly reducing activated parameters.
> - Combining native-resolution vision encoding with an extended context window (128K) is crucial for comprehensive understanding of high-detail and long-form multimodal content.
> - Long Chain-of-Thought (CoT) supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning effectively imbue models with robust multi-step reasoning and agent capabilities in multimodal settings.
