# WHOLE: World-Grounded Hand-Object Lifted from Egocentric Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> WHOLE reconstructs 3D articulated hand motion and 6D object trajectories from egocentric videos in a consistent world coordinate frame. The method uses a learned generative diffusion model for plausible hand-object interactions, guided by robust visual observations and Vision-Language Model-derived contact cues, demonstrating improved accuracy and coherence compared to prior approaches, especially under occlusions and challenging views.

## Problem

- Robustly reconstructing 3D articulated hand motion and 6D object trajectories from egocentric videos is challenging due to severe occlusions, egomotion, and objects entering/exiting the camera view.
- Existing methods often estimate hand and object poses in isolation, failing to capture their inherent interdependence and produce globally consistent 4D motion.
- Prior approaches typically operate in local or ephemeral camera-centric coordinate frames, hindering long-term and world-grounded spatial understanding of interactions.

## Method

- A generative diffusion model learns a prior over realistic hand-object interactions, producing refined hand motions, object 6D trajectories, and binary contact labels conditioned on approximate hand trajectories.
- A guided generation framework reconstructs motion by steering the diffusion model with test-time classifier guidance, using reprojection terms from 2D observations and explicit interaction terms.
- ==Vision-Language Models (VLMs)==, enhanced with spatial prompting and in-context learning, provide robust, spatially grounded contact detection cues for the guidance process.

## Results

- WHOLE achieves state-of-the-art performance in world-grounded hand and object motion reconstruction, significantly improving local hand pose quality and object pose accuracy, particularly for truncated and out-of-view objects.
- The method produces realistic, temporally smooth, and spatially coherent reconstructions of hand-object interactions, outperforming baselines in interaction quality.
- Ablation studies confirm that VLM-annotated contact is highly effective, and integrating guidance during the iterative generation process is critical for superior performance over post-hoc optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Formulating 4D hand-object reconstruction as a guided generative process allows for inferring plausible motion even under severe occlusions and missing visual data by leveraging a learned prior over interactions.
> - Jointly modeling hand articulation and object 6D pose with explicit interaction and consistency losses is crucial for achieving coherent, physically plausible, and world-grounded 4D reconstructions.
> - Leveraging VLMs with spatial prompting and in-context learning provides highly effective contact detection cues, which significantly enhances the reconstruction quality and is comparable to ground truth.
