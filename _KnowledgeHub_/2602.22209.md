# WHOLE: World-Grounded Hand-Object Lifted from Egocentric Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> WHOLE reconstructs 3D articulated hand motion and 6D object trajectories from egocentric videos in a consistent world coordinate frame. The method uses a learned generative diffusion model for plausible hand-object interactions, guided by robust visual observations and Vision-Language Model-derived contact cues, demonstrating improved accuracy and coherence compared to prior approaches, especially under occlusions and challenging views.

## Problem

- Robustly reconstructing 3D articulated hand motion and 6D object trajectories from egocentric videos is challenging due to severe occlusions, egomotion, and objects entering/exiting the camera view.
- Existing methods often estimate hand and object poses in isolation, failing to capture their inherent interdependence and produce globally consistent 4D motion.
- Prior approaches typically operate in local or ephemeral camera-centric coordinate frames, hindering long-term and world-grounded spatial understanding of interactions.

## Method

- A generative diffusion model learns a prior over realistic hand-object interactions, producing refined hand motions, object 6D trajectories, and binary contact labels conditioned on approximate hand trajectories.
- A guided generation framework reconstructs motion by steering the diffusion model with test-time classifier guidance, using reprojection terms from 2D observations and explicit interaction terms.
- ==Vision-Language Models (VLMs)==, enhanced with spatial prompting and in-context learning, provide robust, spatially grounded contact detection cues for the guidance process.

## Results

- WHOLE achieves state-of-the-art performance in world-grounded hand and object motion reconstruction, significantly improving local hand pose quality and object pose accuracy, particularly for truncated and out-of-view objects.
- The method produces realistic, temporally smooth, and spatially coherent reconstructions of hand-object interactions, outperforming baselines in interaction quality.
- Ablation studies confirm that VLM-annotated contact is highly effective, and integrating guidance during the iterative generation process is critical for superior performance over post-hoc optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Formulating 4D hand-object reconstruction as a guided generative process allows for inferring plausible motion even under severe occlusions and missing visual data by leveraging a learned prior over interactions.
> - Jointly modeling hand articulation and object 6D pose with explicit interaction and consistency losses is crucial for achieving coherent, physically plausible, and world-grounded 4D reconstructions.
> - Leveraging VLMs with spatial prompting and in-context learning provides highly effective contact detection cues, which significantly enhances the reconstruction quality and is comparable to ground truth.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The paper "WHOLE" addresses a crucial and highly challenging problem in computer vision and robotics: the 3D reconstruction of human hand and object interactions from egocentric (first-person) videos. This field has seen significant growth with the increasing availability of wearable cameras like GoPros and Project Aria glasses, generating vast amounts of egocentric data.

**Current Landscape and Limitations:**
Traditionally, research in this domain has tackled related problems in isolation:
- **Egocentric Video Understanding:** While progress has been made in high-level semantic understanding (action recognition, language grounding) and 2D perception (detection, segmentation, tracking), 3D understanding from egocentric inputs remains complex.
- **3D Hand Pose Estimation:** Methods have advanced significantly, leveraging large-scale datasets and transformer architectures to estimate articulated hand motion. However, many operate in canonical or camera-centric coordinates, failing to provide global 3D trajectories in a persistent world frame. Those that do aim for global hand motion often focus on the hand in isolation, neglecting its interaction with objects.
- **6D Object Pose Estimation:** Advances like FoundationPose can estimate object poses, but they often struggle with occlusions, objects leaving the field of view, or require additional modalities (like depth maps, which themselves can be noisy when estimated from RGB).
- **Hand-Object Interaction (HOI) Reconstruction:** A dedicated line of research focuses on HOI, often emphasizing detailed object geometry or contact patterns over short temporal windows. These approaches typically operate in hand- or object-centered local reference frames, rather than providing globally consistent 4D motion of both entities through space and time.
- **4D Reconstruction in World Coordinate Frame:** While methods exist for global human body motion or general scene dynamics, they typically do not explicitly model the detailed structure and coupled dynamics of hand-object interactions.

**WHOLE's Distinct Contribution:**
WHOLE uniquely bridges these fragmented areas by providing a **unified framework for holistically reconstructing both hand articulation and object 6D trajectories in world space from metric-SLAMed egocentric videos.** Its key differentiators that set it apart in the research landscape are:
1. **Joint Reasoning:** Unlike approaches that process hands and objects separately, WHOLE explicitly models their inherent interdependence, leading to more coherent and physically plausible interactions.
2. **World-Grounded 4D Motion:** It focuses on reconstructing global 4D trajectories (motion through space and time) for both hands and manipulated objects within a consistent world coordinate frame, moving beyond local or short-term reconstructions.
3. **Robustness to Egocentric Challenges:** It specifically tackles severe occlusions, egomotion, and objects entering/exiting the camera view, which are pervasive in egocentric videos and major stumbling blocks for isolated methods.
4. **Generative Prior:** By learning a generative prior over hand-object motion, WHOLE can infer plausible movement even under missing or ambiguous visual observations, a significant advantage over purely discriminative or optimization-based methods.
5. **VLM-Guided Reconstruction:** It innovatively integrates Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for robust, spatially grounded contact detection, enhancing the guidance signal for its generative process.

In essence, WHOLE represents a significant step towards enabling computers to possess a "core cognitive ability" that humans naturally exhibit: connecting what they see egocentrically to a persistent 3D world for purposeful interaction.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching goal of the WHOLE project is to **endow computers with the ability to reconstruct the motion of active objects and both hands within a consistent world coordinate frame from metric-SLAMed egocentric videos** that depict human hand manipulation. This goal is deeply motivated by the desire to enable machines to reason about human interactions in a persistent 3D world, similar to how humans integrate their first-person observations into a coherent understanding of their surroundings.

The specific objectives driving this research are:
- **Holistic Reconstruction:** To jointly and consistently reconstruct the articulated motion of both hands and the 6D (position and orientation) trajectories of manipulated objects. This is a crucial departure from prior work that often treats hands and objects as separate entities.
- **World-Grounding:** To ensure that the reconstructed motions are expressed within a stable, global world coordinate frame, rather than ephemeral camera-centric or local object-centric frames. This enables long-term, spatially consistent understanding.
- **Robustness to Egocentric Challenges:** To develop a method that can effectively handle the inherent difficulties of egocentric video, including large egomotion, frequent and severe occlusions during interaction, and objects moving in and out of the camera's field of view.
- **Coherent Interaction Modeling:** To capture the inherent interdependence and mutual dynamics between hands and objects, ensuring that their reconstructed movements and relationships are physically plausible and consistent over time, especially during contact events.

The motivation for achieving these objectives stems from their critical role in enabling advanced downstream applications:
- **Robot Learning from Human Demonstrations:** Accurate and world-grounded reconstruction of human manipulation provides invaluable data for training robots to perform complex tasks, allowing them to learn from natural human examples.
- **Immersive AR/VR Environments:** A deep understanding of how users interact with real-world objects can inform the design of more realistic and responsive augmented and virtual reality experiences, where virtual objects need to interact seamlessly with real hands and environments.
- **Advanced AI for Human-Computer Interaction:** Enabling AI systems to precisely "see" and understand human manipulation opens doors for more intelligent assistance, context-aware interfaces, and proactive robotic collaboration in everyday environments.
- **Scaling Egocentric Vision:** With the proliferation of wearable cameras, there is a growing need for robust computational methods to interpret this rich data source, transforming raw video into structured 3D information.

By addressing these challenges, WHOLE aims to unlock a deeper level of spatial reasoning about human interactions, crucial for the next generation of intelligent systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

WHOLE's methodology is built upon a novel **guided generation framework** that leverages a learned generative motion prior. This approach departs from traditional discriminative methods by formulating reconstruction as a process of generating plausible hand-object trajectories that are subsequently steered by visual observations. The methodology is divided into two main components: learning a generative motion prior and using this prior for guided reconstruction.

#### 3.1. Generative Hand-Object Motion Prior

The core of WHOLE is a **diffusion model** that learns a generative prior over realistic hand-object interactions. This model is designed to produce refined hand motions, object 6D trajectories, and binary contact labels, conditioned on approximated hand trajectories and an object template.

- **Interaction Motion Representation:**
 - **Hands:** Represented using MANO parameters (global orientation, translation, articulation, shape) augmented with joint positions and velocities, capturing the intricate articulation of human hands. Left and right hand features are concatenated.
 - **Objects:** Represented as SE(3) transforms (position and orientation) using a 9D formulation. The object's 3D geometry is encoded via a Basis Point Set (BPS) descriptor in its canonical frame.
 - **Spatial Relationship/Contact:** To facilitate fine-grained contact reasoning, "Ambient Sensor" features are computed. These measure the displacement from each hand joint to the nearest point on the object surface, effectively a BPS of the posed object using hand joints as basis vectors.
- **Gravity-Aware Local Coordinate Frame:** All hand and object motions are expressed in a gravity-aligned camera coordinate frame at the start of each sequence. This canonicalization anchors sequences to a consistent physical "up" direction, allowing the model to focus on relative hand-object dynamics rather than arbitrary global rotations, and enabling seamless stitching of long sequences.
- **Robustness to Inaccurate Hand Estimation:** To prevent overfitting to specific off-the-shelf hand estimators and enhance robustness, the diffusion model is trained with synthesized imperfect conditioning hand tracks. This involves perturbing ground-truth MANO parameters with trajectory-level and per-frame noise, and simulating occlusions/truncations.
- **Training Objective:** The model is trained using a conditional DDPM loss, which iteratively denoises Gaussian noise to generate clean interaction trajectories. This is augmented with several auxiliary losses:
 - **Interaction Loss ($L_{inter}$):** Encourages realistic hand-object contact by penalizing distances when contact is predicted and enforcing near-rigid transport of contact points across consecutive contact frames.
 - **Consistency Loss ($L_{const}$):** Promotes agreement between predicted hand features and their corresponding MANO forward kinematics, ensuring biomechanical plausibility.
 - **Temporal Smoothness ($L_{smooth}$):** Penalizes large accelerations to ensure smooth and natural trajectories.

#### 3.2. Reconstruction as Guided Generation

At test time, the pretrained generative motion prior is used to reconstruct hand and object motion from egocentric videos through a **classifier-guidance** mechanism. This process steers the diffusion model's generation towards trajectories consistent with visual observations, offering speed advantages over score distillation sampling.

- **Test-Time Guidance:** The diffusion process is guided by modifying the score (gradient of the log-probability) using the gradient of task-specific objectives. These objectives ensure the generated output conforms to visual evidence from the input video:
 - **Reprojection Term ($g_{reproj}$):** Aligns the generated 3D trajectories with 2D observations. This includes a one-way Chamfer loss between the reprojected object mesh and the segmented 2D object mask (to handle occlusion/truncation), and alignment with 2D hand joints.
 - **Interaction Term ($g_{inter}$):** Enforces realistic hand-object dynamics by minimizing distances, encouraging rigid transport under contact, and penalizing motion when contact is absent in consecutive frames (similar to $L_{inter}$ during training).
 - **Temporal Smoothness Term ($g_{temp}$):** Regularizes temporal consistency to produce smooth trajectories.
- **VLM Contact Assignment:** To obtain crucial contact information, WHOLE employs a Vision-Language Model (VLM), specifically enhanced for this task. It leverages spatial prompting by overlaying segmented hand and object masks on the image, improving localization in cluttered scenes. In-context learning (providing annotated examples) further refines the VLM's ability to distinguish true physical touch from mere proximity, significantly boosting F1 scores for contact detection. For efficiency, contact cues are sampled at 3 fps.
- **Blending Long Videos:** Since the diffusion model operates on fixed-length temporal windows (e.g., 120 frames), long videos are processed by dividing them into overlapping sliding windows. The model denoises all windows in parallel, and overlapping regions are blended to ensure smooth temporal transitions and consistency across the entire sequence. The blended full-length sequence is then refined under the guidance, and the process continues.

By combining a robust generative prior with intelligent visual and contact guidance, WHOLE effectively tackles the challenges of egocentric video, producing coherent and plausible 4D reconstructions of hand-object interactions in a global coordinate frame.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

WHOLE's evaluation demonstrates its state-of-the-art performance in world-grounded hand-object interaction reconstruction from egocentric videos, particularly under challenging conditions. The findings validate its core hypothesis that joint generative modeling of hands and objects, guided by robust visual cues, significantly outperforms independent estimation approaches.

#### 4.1. Visualizing Hand-Object Motion Generation

- **Realistic and Coherent Samples:** The diffusion model, conditioned on approximate hand motions, generates diverse, realistic, and physically coherent hand-object motions. This is evident in the natural spatial relationships between hands and objects, plausible object dynamics (e.g., objects staying still without contact and moving with hands during interaction).
- **Diversity in Interaction:** For the same approximate hand motion, the model can generate varied contact timings and relative hand-object poses, reflecting different grasping or release moments. This highlights the generative prior's ability to model the range of possible interactions, not just a single deterministic outcome.

#### 4.2. Guided Reconstruction Performance

WHOLE was evaluated against state-of-the-art baselines like HaWoR (for hands) and FoundationPose (for objects), as well as combined baselines (FP+HaWoR-simple/contact) that attempt to integrate them with post-optimization. The evaluation covered overall performance and challenging subsets (contact, truncated, out-of-view frames).

- **Hand Motion Reconstruction:**
 - WHOLE achieves state-of-the-art or competitive performance across all hand metrics (WA-MPJPE, W-MPJPE, ACC-NORM, PA-MPJPE).
 - Crucially, it shows a clear improvement in local hand pose quality (lower PA-MPJPE) compared to HaWoR, demonstrating the benefit of jointly reconstructing hands and objects.
 - Baselines, particularly those enforcing strong hand-object consistency without a generative prior, sometimes exhibit a trade-off where hand accuracy is sacrificed for object performance. WHOLE mitigates this.
- **Object Motion Reconstruction:**
 - WHOLE achieves consistently strong results across all metrics (AUC of ADD/ADD-S) and, importantly, across challenging subsets like truncated and out-of-view frames.
 - It significantly outperforms FoundationPose and the combined baselines in these difficult conditions. FoundationPose struggles with truncation and out-of-view scenarios, and simple optimization with reprojection loss can degrade accuracy due to severe hand-object occlusion.
 - The model's ability to infer plausible movement even when objects are partially or fully out of view, or heavily occluded, demonstrates the power of its learned generative prior to "fill in the gaps."
- **Interaction Quality:**
 - WHOLE significantly surpasses all baselines in accurately capturing the relative motion and spatial alignment between hands and objects (AUC of ADD/ADD-S for interaction error).
 - This highlights its strong capability to produce coherent and physically consistent hand-object interactions, which is the primary objective of the work.
- **Qualitative Visualization:** Visual comparisons (Figure 5) corroborate the quantitative results. Baseline methods often produce "floating" objects or unrealistic hand-object relations, especially for simple combinations of off-the-shelf tools. In contrast, WHOLE yields temporally smooth and spatially coherent reconstructions that appear natural and physically plausible.

#### 4.3. Ablation Studies

- **VLM-Annotated Contact Labels:** Replacing VLM-annotated contact with ground-truth contact shows only a marginal improvement. This confirms that the VLM, enhanced with spatial prompting and in-context learning, provides highly effective and robust contact cues comparable to ground truth.
- **Intertwined Generation and Optimization:** A variant that first performs unguided generation and then applies post-hoc optimization ("Gen+Opt") performs significantly worse. This demonstrates that alternating diffusion steps with task-specific guidance throughout the generation process is essential for keeping the model's samples within the data manifold while progressively refining the motion under task constraints.
- **Interaction Loss:** Excluding the interaction objectives ($L_{inter}$) during training and guidance degrades object motion and interaction quality, underscoring the importance of explicitly modeling hand-object coupling for faithful reconstruction.

#### 4.4. Zero-Shot Generalization

- WHOLE exhibits a moderate performance drop when evaluated zero-shot on the unseen H2O dataset, which is expected for out-of-distribution data. However, it shows remarkable robustness compared to RGB-conditioned baselines (like H2OTR) that catastrophically fail. This suggests that WHOLE's motion-space prior has a smaller domain gap across datasets than appearance-based representations, indicating a promising step towards generalizable reconstruction.

#### 4.5. Application: Hand-Guided HOI Planner

- The framework's flexibility allows for direct synthesis of diverse hand-object interaction motions given only a coarse hand trajectory, contact timings, and an object template, without video input. This demonstrates its potential as a "Hand-Guided HOI Planner," which could be valuable for robotic task planning.

In summary, WHOLE's findings convincingly show that its generative, world-grounded, and interaction-aware approach significantly advances the state of the art in reconstructing complex hand-object manipulations from challenging egocentric videos, making it a robust and versatile tool for various applications.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The WHOLE framework represents a significant advancement in the field of 3D vision, robotics, and human-computer interaction, with a broad potential impact across several domains.

### 6. Breakthrough in Egocentric 4D Reconstruction

WHOLE addresses a long-standing, critical challenge in egocentric vision: the unified and coherent reconstruction of both hands and manipulated objects in a persistent 3D world. By moving beyond isolated perception to holistic interaction understanding, it significantly enhances the utility of egocentric videos for analyzing complex human activities. The ability to robustly handle severe occlusions, egomotion, and out-of-view objects is crucial for real-world applicability.

### 7. Novel Generative Modeling Paradigm for Interaction

The core contribution of learning a generative motion prior for interdependent hand-object dynamics is a methodological breakthrough. This allows for inferring plausible motion even with sparse or ambiguous visual cues, which is common in egocentric data. The concept of "guided generation" offers an efficient and effective way to constrain a powerful generative model with real-world observations, combining the best of generative and discriminative approaches.

### 8. Enhanced Realism and Consistency

By enforcing physical plausibility through interaction losses and temporal smoothness, WHOLE produces reconstructed trajectories that are not only accurate but also visually realistic and temporally coherent. This level of fidelity is critical for applications that require detailed and believable simulations or analyses of human behavior.

### 9. Empowering Robotics and AI Systems

- **Robot Learning from Demonstration:** WHOLE's ability to precisely "lift" human manipulation skills into a 3D world-grounded format provides an invaluable data source for robotic learning. Robots can directly learn intricate tasks by observing human demonstrations, potentially accelerating the development of dexterous manipulation in unstructured environments. The "Hand-Guided HOI Planner" application directly showcases this potential for robot planning.
- **Human-Robot Collaboration:** A deeper understanding of human intentions and actions, derived from accurate HOI reconstruction, can lead to more intuitive and effective human-robot collaboration, where robots can anticipate and respond appropriately to human manipulation.
- **AR/VR and Telepresence:** For immersive experiences, accurate reconstruction of real-world hand-object interactions is paramount. WHOLE can enable more realistic integration of virtual objects with real hands and scenes, enhancing immersion and interaction fidelity in AR/VR applications and advanced telepresence systems.

### 10. Advancements in VLM-Powered Perception

The innovative use of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for spatially grounded contact detection, enhanced with visual prompting and in-context learning, demonstrates a powerful direction for leveraging large foundation models in specific perception tasks. This approach shows how high-level semantic reasoning can effectively inform low-level 3D reconstruction.

### 11. Future Research Avenues

WHOLE opens up several exciting directions for future research:
- **Scene-Level Multi-Object Interaction:** Extending the framework to jointly reconstruct interactions with multiple objects within a complex scene.
- **Template-Free Reconstruction:** Relaxing the assumption of known object templates by integrating object shape inference or LLM-based retrieval.
- **Scalability and Generalization:** Training on larger, more diverse hand-object interaction datasets (like those emerging recently) to further improve generalization across a wider range of activities and environments.

In conclusion, WHOLE represents a pivotal step towards building AI systems that can robustly understand human interaction with the physical world from a human's own perspective. Its principled approach of combining generative modeling with intelligent guidance lays a strong foundation for future advancements in embodied AI, robotics, and immersive technologies.
