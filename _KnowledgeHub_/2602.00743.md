# SA-VLA: Spatially-Aware Flow-Matching for Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> SA-VLA introduces a framework for robust reinforcement learning adaptation of robotic manipulation policies, integrating spatial awareness into state representations, reward design, and exploration. This approach improves zero-shot spatial generalization by 2.25% and enhances fine-tuning stability under spatial distribution shifts.

## Problem

- Pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies often lose robustness during reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tuning, particularly under spatial distribution shifts.
- The erosion of spatial inductive bias and ineffective spatially agnostic exploration during RL lead to brittle behaviors, failing to generalize across varying viewpoints or object layouts.
- Current RL approaches for continuous-time, noise-driven flow-matching VLA policies struggle with precise geometric reasoning essential for real-world robotic interaction.

## Method

- **SA-VLA** employs ==spatial token fusion==, augmenting 2D visual tokens with implicit spatial representations derived from multi-view features.
- Implements ==spatially-aware step-level dense rewards== by decomposing tasks into generic phases (Reach, Place, Leave) and providing feedback based on signed changes in normalized geometric distances.
- ==Spatially-Conditioned Annealed Noise (SCAN)== guides exploration with a hybrid noise model, combining learned, geometry-aware stochasticity with an annealed minimum floor to ensure persistent and targeted exploration during ==flow-matching RL==.

## Results

- Spatial token fusion alone improved zero-shot success rates on spatial perturbation tasks by **+2.25%** compared to the baseline, reaching **83.25%**.
- Spatially-aware step-level dense rewards consistently led to faster convergence and smoother training dynamics during RL fine-tuning.
- The full SA-VLA framework achieved an **83.75%** success rate, demonstrating complementary benefits from all components and superior performance compared to baselines and ablated versions.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly preserving and reinforcing spatial inductive bias throughout the RL pipeline is critical for stable and effective fine-tuning of VLA policies.
> - A holistic approach, integrating spatial awareness into state representation, reward design, and exploration strategy, synergistically improves robustness and generalization.
> - Step-level dense rewards derived from task geometry significantly stabilize optimization by providing consistent feedback, while spatially-conditioned exploration enhances the discovery of meaningful states.
