# Emerging Properties in Unified Multimodal Pretraining

> [!summary] Summary
> ByteDance Seed introduced BAGEL, an open-source unified multimodal foundation model trained on trillions of interleaved text, image, and video tokens. This model demonstrates emergent reasoning abilities and achieves state-of-the-art performance among open-source alternatives, narrowing the capability gap with leading proprietary systems.

## Problem

- A significant performance gap existed between cutting-edge proprietary multimodal systems and publicly available academic/open-source models, often due to undisclosed techniques and data scaling strategies.
- Previous open-source efforts were often limited by data diversity, relying predominantly on image-text paired data, and architectural bottlenecks that hindered seamless multimodal understanding and generation.
- The research community lacked an open-source foundation model capable of unified multimodal understanding and generation with advanced complex reasoning capabilities across various modalities.

## Method

- BAGEL was developed with a unified, decoder-only ==Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts (MoT)== architecture, featuring shared self-attention operations for bottleneck-free interaction between understanding and generation modules.
- The model was pretrained on 'trillions of tokens' sourced from a diverse, meticulously curated, and interleaved dataset including text, image-text pairs, and especially video and web data for rich context.
- A ==multi-stage training strategy== was implemented, dynamically adjusting data mixtures and resolutions, and incorporating ==reasoning-augmented Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data== for enhanced multimodal reasoning.
- Specialized visual encoders (ViT for understanding and VAE for generation) were integrated, alongside a generalized causal attention mechanism and rectified flow for visual token prediction.

## Results

- BAGEL achieved state-of-the-art performance among open-source unified models on various image understanding benchmarks (e.g., MME, MMBench, MMMU, MM-Vet, MathVista, MMVP) and text-to-image generation benchmarks (**88%** on GenEval and **0.52** WISE score).
- On the novel IntelligentBench for complex reasoning, BAGEL scored **44.9**, significantly outperforming specialist models like Step1X-Edit (**14.9**), and further increased to **55.3** with Chain-of-Thought.
- Incorporating Chain-of-Thought reasoning improved BAGEL's WISE score from **0.52** to **0.70** and its IntelligentBench score from **44.9** to **55.3**, demonstrating enhanced world knowledge integration and instruction following.
- The model showcased robust world modeling capabilities through fine-tuning, including free-form visual manipulation, future frame prediction, 3D manipulation, and dynamic world navigation, generalizing across diverse domains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Complex compositional reasoning and 'intelligent editing' capabilities emerge non-linearly and significantly later during large-scale pretraining (beyond 2.6T tokens) compared to basic understanding and generation.
> - The unified Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts architecture with shared self-attention effectively supports both multimodal understanding and generation without introducing heuristic bottlenecks, outperforming dense and standard MoE designs.
> - The inclusion of visual-semantic reasoning provided by ViT tokens is critical for complex, intelligence-demanding tasks like 'Intelligent Editing,' while having less impact on basic editing.
> - Explicit Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, integrated into both generative and understanding tasks, substantially boosts the model's ability to leverage world knowledge and follow complex instructions, improving scores by up to 0.18 points on WISE.
