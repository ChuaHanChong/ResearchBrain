# Emerging Properties in Unified Multimodal Pretraining

> [!summary] Summary
> ByteDance Seed introduced BAGEL, an open-source unified multimodal foundation model trained on trillions of interleaved text, image, and video tokens. This model demonstrates emergent reasoning abilities and achieves state-of-the-art performance among open-source alternatives, narrowing the capability gap with leading proprietary systems.

## Problem

- A significant performance gap existed between cutting-edge proprietary multimodal systems and publicly available academic/open-source models, often due to undisclosed techniques and data scaling strategies.
- Previous open-source efforts were often limited by data diversity, relying predominantly on image-text paired data, and architectural bottlenecks that hindered seamless multimodal understanding and generation.
- The research community lacked an open-source foundation model capable of unified multimodal understanding and generation with advanced complex reasoning capabilities across various modalities.

## Method

- BAGEL was developed with a unified, decoder-only ==Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts (MoT)== architecture with shared self-attention for bottleneck-free interaction, specialized visual encoders (ViT for understanding, VAE for generation), and rectified flow for visual token prediction.
- The model was pretrained on 'trillions of tokens' sourced from a diverse, meticulously curated, and interleaved dataset including text, image-text pairs, and especially video and web data for rich context.
- A ==multi-stage training strategy== was implemented, dynamically adjusting data mixtures and resolutions, and incorporating ==reasoning-augmented Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data== for enhanced multimodal reasoning.

## Results

- BAGEL achieved state-of-the-art performance among open-source unified models on various image understanding benchmarks (e.g., MME, MMBench, MMMU, MM-Vet, MathVista, MMVP) and text-to-image generation benchmarks (**88%** on GenEval and **0.52** WISE score).
- On the novel IntelligentBench for complex reasoning, BAGEL scored **44.9** (outperforming Step1X-Edit's **14.9**) and, with Chain-of-Thought, improved to **55.3** on IntelligentBench and its WISE score from **0.52** to **0.70**.
- The model showcased robust world modeling capabilities through fine-tuning, including free-form visual manipulation, future frame prediction, 3D manipulation, and dynamic world navigation, generalizing across diverse domains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Complex compositional reasoning and 'intelligent editing' capabilities emerge non-linearly and significantly later during large-scale pretraining (beyond 2.6T tokens), driven critically by visual-semantic reasoning from ViT tokens, while having less impact on basic understanding and editing.
> - The unified Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts architecture with shared self-attention effectively supports both multimodal understanding and generation without introducing heuristic bottlenecks, outperforming dense and standard MoE designs.
> - Explicit Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, integrated into both generative and understanding tasks, substantially boosts the model's ability to leverage world knowledge and follow complex instructions, improving scores by up to 0.18 points on WISE.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of unified multimodal understanding and generation has seen rapid advancements, with proprietary systems like GPT-4o and Gemini 2.0 demonstrating impressive capabilities by integrating various modalities. However, the underlying techniques of these cutting-edge models often remain undisclosed, creating a significant gap between academic and open-source models and their proprietary counterparts. This paper directly addresses this challenge by introducing BAGEL (Scalable Generative Cognitive Model), an open-source foundational model explicitly designed to bridge this gap.

Previous open-source efforts, while showing promise, have often been constrained by data limitations—predominantly relying on image-text paired data—or by architectural bottlenecks that hinder the seamless interaction between understanding and generation modules. BAGEL distinguishes itself by leveraging "trillions of tokens curated from large-scale interleaved text, image, video, and web data," a data scale and diversity previously characteristic of proprietary systems. This extensive and carefully structured multimodal pretraining data is presented as the key to unlocking "emerging capabilities in complex multimodal reasoning."

By releasing BAGEL as an open-source model with its key findings, pretraining details, and data creation protocols, this work makes a substantial contribution to democratizing access to advanced multimodal AI research. It directly challenges the trend of closed-source development in foundational models, aiming to catalyze further opportunities for the broader research community to explore and build upon state-of-the-art multimodal understanding and generation. The paper positions BAGEL not just as another multimodal model, but as a foundational open-source alternative capable of demonstrating properties akin to leading proprietary systems, thereby significantly advancing the open-science frontier in this domain.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop and release BAGEL, an open-source foundational model that natively supports unified multimodal understanding and generation. The authors are motivated by the observed disparity between the capabilities of cutting-edge proprietary multimodal systems (e.g., GPT-4o, Gemini 2.0) and publicly available academic models. They posit that this gap primarily stems from the scale and careful structuring of multimodal interleaved data used in proprietary systems, a factor they aim to replicate and even surpass in an open-source context.

Specific key objectives include:
- **Unification:** To create a single, decoder-only model capable of performing both multimodal understanding (e.g., visual question answering) and multimodal generation (e.g., text-to-image synthesis, image editing) tasks within a unified framework, avoiding heuristic bottlenecks.
- **Scalability and Data Diversity:** To demonstrate that scaling up pretraining with vast quantities of carefully curated, diverse, and interleaved multimodal data (text, image, video, web) can unlock complex reasoning abilities and "emerging properties" not present in models trained on less diverse or smaller datasets.
- **Performance Benchmarking:** To achieve state-of-the-art performance among open-source unified models across standard multimodal understanding and generation benchmarks, and to demonstrate advanced reasoning abilities in novel tasks such as free-form image manipulation, future frame prediction, 3D manipulation, and world navigation.
- **Open-Source Contribution:** To facilitate broader research in multimodal AI by openly sharing the model's architecture, pretraining details, data creation protocols, code, and checkpoints with the community. This aims to foster innovation and accelerate progress in the field.

The motivation is to empirically prove that a combination of a bottleneck-free architecture and massive, high-quality interleaved data is critical for developing truly capable multimodal foundation models, thereby empowering the open-source community to catch up with and potentially advance beyond the current state of proprietary systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

BAGEL adopts a sophisticated methodology encompassing architectural design, extensive data curation, and a multi-stage training strategy.

#### 3.1. Architecture Design

BAGEL is built upon a **unified, decoder-only Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts (MoT) architecture**. This design choice is critical, as it aims to maximize model capacity without introducing heuristic bottlenecks common in prior approaches. It comprises two transformer experts: one for multimodal understanding and another for multimodal generation. These experts operate on the same token sequence through **shared self-attention operations** at every layer, enabling long-context interaction between understanding and generation modules.
- **Backbone:** Inherited from Qwen2.5 LLM, a decoder-only transformer, for its strong performance and public availability. It includes RMSNorm, SwiGLU, RoPE, and GQA, with QK-Norm added for training stability.
- **Visual Encoders:**
 - **Understanding-oriented encoder:** A ViT encoder (initialized from SigLIP2-so400m/14, adapted with NaViT for native aspect ratios) converts raw pixels into tokens, connected via a two-layer MLP.
 - **Generation-oriented encoder:** A frozen VAE model (from FLUX) transforms images to and from latent space. Latent representations are processed by a patch embedding layer.
- **Token Prediction:** Text tokens follow the Next-Token-Prediction paradigm (autoregressive). Visual tokens for generation adopt the Rectified Flow method, known for superior visual quality compared to purely autoregressive visual token generation.
- **Generalized Causal Attention:** A specialized attention mechanism (implemented with PyTorch FlexAttention for efficiency) allows subsequent tokens to attend to clean VAE and ViT tokens of preceding images, but not noised VAE tokens, facilitating interleaved multimodal generation. Diffusion forcing is used for multi-image/video generation. Classifier-free guidance is enabled by randomly dropping tokens during inference.
- **MoT Justification:** Ablation studies demonstrated that the MoT variant significantly outperforms dense and MoE designs, especially for multimodal generation, by decoupling parameters for understanding and generation objectives, thus mitigating optimization challenges.

#### 3.2. Data Curation and Construction

Recognizing data as the "knowledge boundaries," BAGEL is pretrained on "trillions of tokens" from a diverse range of meticulously curated datasets:
- **Text Only Data:** High-quality linguistic data to maintain core LLM capabilities.
- **Vision-Text Paired Data:**
 - **VLM Image-Text Pairs:** Large-scale, high-quality image-text pairs from web alt-text and captions, filtered for quality, diversity, and concept coverage (including OCR documents, charts).
 - **T2I Image-Text Pairs:** High-quality image-text pairs and minimal synthetic data (from T2I models like SD3, FLUX), encompassing diverse caption styles for visual quality and stylistic variety.
- **Vision-Text Interleaved Data (Core Innovation):** To support complex in-context reasoning, this category is crucial:
 - **Video Data:** Sourced from public online resources, Koala36M (instructional content), and MVImgNet2.0 (multi-view objects). Preprocessed into high-quality clips with temporal splitting, spatial cropping, and quality filtering. Inter-frame captions, generated by a distilled VLM, capture temporal dynamics.
 - **Web Data:** Built upon OmniCorpus (from Common Crawl) and open-source image editing datasets. A two-stage LLM-based topic selection and rule-based filtering pipeline ensures high-quality interleaved documents. A "caption-first" strategy, generating concise descriptions before each image, improves image-text alignment.
- **Reasoning-Augmented Data:** Inspired by models like DeepSeek-R1, Chain-of-Thoughts (CoT) data is constructed for both understanding and generation tasks (text-to-image, free-form image manipulation, conceptual edits) to enhance multimodal reasoning. This involves using LLMs (e.g., Qwen2.5-72B) to generate reasoning traces.

#### 3.3. Training Protocol

A **multi-stage training strategy** is employed, dynamically adjusting data mixtures and resolutions:
- **Alignment Stage:** Initializes the ViT connector by training only the MLP, keeping ViT and LLM frozen, using image-text pairs at a fixed resolution.
- **Pre-training (PT) Stage:** All parameters (except VAE) are trainable. 2.5T tokens, dynamic mixture of text, image-text pairs, and interleaved data. Native-resolution strategy with resolution constraints.
- **Continued Training (CT) Stage:** Increased visual input resolution and higher sampling ratio for interleaved data (2.6T tokens) to emphasize cross-modal reasoning as core capabilities stabilize.
- **Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT) Stage:** High-quality subsets from generation and understanding datasets (72.7B tokens) for instruction-tuning.
Hyperparameters like learning rate and data sampling ratios were carefully ablated and tuned, with generation examples sampled more often due to their greater impact on MSE loss, and separate weighting factors for CE and MSE losses.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

BAGEL's evaluation demonstrates significant advancements, revealing "emerging properties" during its large-scale pretraining.

#### 4.1. Emerging Properties through Scaling

A core finding is the observation of distinct learning dynamics and saturation behaviors across different capabilities as the model scales with training tokens.
- **Early Emergence:** Conventional multimodal understanding and high-fidelity generation capabilities emerge relatively early, saturating around 0.18T and 0.68T tokens, respectively (reaching 85% of peak performance).
- **Later Emergence for Complexity:** More complex tasks requiring compositional reasoning show later emergence:
 - Classical image editing tasks (GEdit-Bench) reach 85% performance after approximately 2.64T tokens.
 - "Intelligent Editing" tasks (requiring complex multimodal reasoning, evaluated on the new IntelligentBench) emerge latest, requiring 3.61T tokens to reach 85% performance. This non-linear improvement after 3T tokens, with performance tripling, is highlighted as akin to "phase transitions" observed in other large models.
- **Role of Visual Input:** Removing ViT tokens had minimal impact on basic editing (GEdit-Bench) but caused a 16% drop in Intelligent Edit, underscoring the critical role of visual-semantic reasoning (provided by ViT) in complex, intelligence-demanding tasks.
- **Qualitative Validation:** Qualitative comparisons of generation outputs at different training checkpoints (e.g., text rendering, image editing) consistently support these emerging trends, with abilities like correct text spelling appearing much later (1.5T to 4.5T tokens).

#### 4.2. Quantitative Performance

BAGEL establishes itself as a leading open-source unified multimodal model across various benchmarks:
- **Image Understanding:** BAGEL (7B MoT) outperforms existing unified models (e.g., Janus-Pro, MetaQuery-XL) and even specialized understanding models (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL2.5) on a diverse set of benchmarks including MME, MMBench, MMMU, MM-Vet, MathVista, and MMVP, demonstrating strong visual understanding capabilities.
- **Text-to-Image Generation (GenEval & WISE):** BAGEL achieves an 88% overall score on GenEval (with LLM rewriter), surpassing both specialized generation models (FLUX.1-dev: 82%, SD3-Medium: 74%) and previous SOTA unified models (Janus-Pro: 80%). Even without an LLM rewriter, it achieves 82%. On the WISE benchmark, which assesses world-knowledge integration, BAGEL (0.52 score) exceeds all prior open-source models, trailing only GPT-4o.
- **Image Editing (GEdit-Bench & IntelligentBench):** BAGEL delivers competitive results on GEdit-Bench, matching or exceeding leading specialist models like Step1X-Edit and outperforming Gemini 2.0. On the novel IntelligentBench, designed for complex reasoning, BAGEL achieves a score of 44.9, significantly outperforming Step1X-Edit (14.9).

#### 4.3. Impact of "Thinking" Mode (Reasoning-Augmented Generation/Editing)

Incorporating explicit Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning significantly boosts BAGEL's performance:
- **T2I Generation:** With CoT, BAGEL's WISE score jumps from 0.52 to 0.70, a substantial 0.18 point increase, outperforming all existing open-source models.
- **Image Editing:** CoT improves IntelligentBench score from 44.9 to 55.3, and also shows consistent gains on RISEBench and KRIS-Bench, demonstrating enhanced ability to leverage world knowledge and follow complex instructions.

#### 4.4. World Modeling Capabilities

Through fine-tuning with increased video and navigation data, BAGEL demonstrates robust world understanding and simulation, including:
- **World Navigation:** Following instructions to dynamically generate images for navigation (e.g., moving forward, looking left/right).
- **Rotation:** Rotating an input image.
- **Multi-frame Generation:** Producing multiple images based on a single prompt.
- **Generalization:** Extending world understanding to diverse domains like ink paintings, cartoons, and video games, even when trained on real-world street navigation.

#### 4.5. Qualitative Results

Qualitative comparisons against models like Janus-Pro, SD3-medium, Gemini 2.0, GPT-4o, Step1X-Edit, and IC-Edit highlight BAGEL's superior generation quality, prompt adherence, and advanced editing capabilities across various complex scenarios, often outperforming or matching proprietary systems while avoiding unintended modifications sometimes seen in GPT-4o. The effect of model scaling (BAGEL-1.5B vs. BAGEL-7B) further illustrates the benefits of increased capacity.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The development and open-sourcing of BAGEL represent a significant milestone in multimodal AI research, with profound potential impacts:

- **Democratizing Advanced Multimodal AI:** By openly releasing a model with "emerging capabilities" trained on trillions of tokens and demonstrating performance competitive with proprietary systems like GPT-4o and Gemini 2.0, BAGEL significantly lowers the barrier to entry for researchers. This fosters a more inclusive and accelerated research environment, allowing a wider community to explore, understand, and build upon state-of-the-art multimodal techniques.
- **Benchmarking and Progress in Complex Reasoning:** The introduction of IntelligentBench provides a crucial tool for evaluating sophisticated multimodal reasoning abilities that go beyond traditional benchmarks. This pushes the field to develop models capable of genuine "world modeling" and complex compositional tasks, rather than just basic understanding or generation. The observed emergent properties provide empirical evidence and a roadmap for future scaling efforts.
- **Architectural Validation:** The successful deployment of the Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts (MoT) architecture, coupled with shared self-attention and distinct visual encoders, offers a validated, bottleneck-free design for unifying understanding and generation. This architectural insight can guide future foundation model designs, especially for systems aiming for seamless cross-modal interaction.
- **Data Strategy Insights:** The detailed data curation protocols, particularly for scalable sourcing, filtering, and construction of diverse interleaved video and web data, provide invaluable blueprints for researchers. The emphasis on reasoning-augmented data highlights the importance of not just data quantity, but also its quality and structure, for cultivating advanced cognitive abilities in models.
- **Advancing AGI Research:** Capabilities such as free-form visual manipulation, future frame prediction, 3D manipulation, and world navigation, particularly when enabled by "thinking" processes (Chain-of-Thought), hint at a deeper understanding of the world. These "world-modeling" tasks are foundational steps toward more general artificial intelligence, suggesting BAGEL could serve as a powerful base for developing more cognitive and interactive AI agents.
- **Practical Applications:** BAGEL's high-fidelity image generation, advanced editing, and multimodal reasoning capabilities have immediate practical implications across various domains, including creative content generation, personalized virtual assistants, enhanced human-computer interaction, and robotics.

In conclusion, BAGEL not only pushes the technical boundaries of open-source multimodal foundation models but also provides critical insights into the scaling laws and architectural choices necessary to achieve complex reasoning and world-modeling abilities. Its release is a valuable contribution that will undoubtedly stimulate further innovation and collaboration in the multimodal AI community.
