# GaussianProperty: Integrating Physical Properties to 3D Gaussians with LMMs

> [!summary] Summary
> GaussianProperty is a training-free framework that assigns physical material properties to 3D Gaussians by combining SAM for part segmentation and Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) for property reasoning from multi-view 2D images. This approach achieves higher material segmentation accuracy, enables realistic dynamic simulations, and facilitates adaptive robotic grasping with a 100% success rate.

## Problem

- Estimating intrinsic physical properties from visual data is ambiguous and difficult due to their unobservability and the scarcity of labeled ground-truth 3D data.
- Existing methods for integrating physical properties into 3D models often require manual assignment, are scene-specific, or lack generalization across diverse materials.
- Automating physically plausible dynamic simulations and material-sensitive robotic manipulation is hindered by the absence of a generalized and accurate way to attribute properties to 3D representations.

## Method

- A training-free framework, **GaussianProperty**, leverages ==SAM== for precise 2D part segmentation and Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) for physical property reasoning.
- LMMs are guided by a global-to-local knowledge framework, providing contextualized inputs (global image, part mask, cropped part) and structured prompts to infer properties from a curated material library.
- Estimated 2D properties are projected onto ==3D Gaussian== representations using a frequency-based voting strategy across multiple camera views to ensure consistent and robust 3D property attribution.

## Results

- GaussianProperty achieved an mIoU of 55.83% on the ABO dataset for material segmentation, significantly outperforming the Nerf2Physics baseline (25.59%).
- The framework enabled realistic physics-based dynamic simulations by directly assigning properties to 3D Gaussians, streamlining the workflow by eliminating manual property annotation.
- In robotic grasping, it achieved a 100% success rate (100% Picked-up Rate, 100% No-damage Rate) on 16 real-world objects, demonstrating superior adaptive grasping performance over fixed-force baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) can effectively infer diverse physical properties from visual cues by leveraging their extensive pre-trained knowledge, especially when provided with structured prompts and contextual information.
> - Integrating precise 2D segmentation (e.g., SAM) with LMM-based reasoning enables accurate, part-level physical property estimation from multi-view images.
> - A multi-view frequency-based voting mechanism effectively lifts 2D property predictions to consistent 3D Gaussian representations, enhancing robustness against single-view ambiguities and errors.
