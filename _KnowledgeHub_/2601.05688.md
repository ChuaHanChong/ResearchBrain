# SketchVL: Policy Optimization via Fine-Grained Credit Assignment for Chart Understanding and More

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Xi'an Jiaotong University and Lenovo Research developed SketchVL, a multimodal large language model, and a novel reinforcement learning algorithm, FinePO, to address coarse credit assignment in multi-step visual reasoning. The approach yielded substantial performance improvements, with SketchVL-3B outperforming its base model by 15.32 percentage points on ChartQA and 3.76 points on ChartBench, enhancing chart understanding while preserving general multimodal capabilities.

## Problem

- Automated chart understanding demands precise, multi-step visual reasoning, making it challenging for AI models.
- Existing reinforcement learning (RL) methods for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) use coarse, outcome-only feedback, which struggles with multi-step reasoning by failing to distinguish between correct and incorrect intermediate steps.
- This coarse credit assignment introduces noise into the learning signal, punishing sound intermediate logic or rewarding flawed steps, thereby hindering effective policy optimization for compositional reasoning.

## Method

- The authors introduce **SketchVL**, an MLLM that performs ==iterative reasoning== by explicitly rendering visual markers onto an image within a "Reasoning on Image" (RoI) paradigm, forming a visible reasoning trajectory.
- They propose FinePO, a novel RL algorithm that performs fine-grained credit assignment by redistributing coarse trajectory-level advantages to individual steps using a Fine-grained Process Reward Model (FinePRM).
- A FinePRM is trained using a cross-modal distillation pipeline to provide scalar scores for each reasoning step, evaluating its intent and action based on visual changes before and after the action.

## Results

- SketchVL-**7B** consistently outperformed its base model, Qwen2.5VL-**7B**, on chart-expert datasets, while SketchVL-**3B** demonstrated gains of 15.32 percentage points on ChartQA and 3.76 points on ChartBench over Qwen2.5VL-**3B**.
- Ablation studies confirmed the substantial performance boost from FinePO's fine-grained credit assignment and the critical role of KL action regularization in fostering diverse and robust reasoning strategies.
- SketchVL achieved competitive or superior results on general-purpose multimodal benchmarks such as MathVista and MMStar, indicating that the approach enhances specialized task performance while preserving generalization capabilities.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Fine-grained credit assignment, by leveraging explicit step-by-step visual reasoning, provides a more precise and stable learning signal for MLLMs on complex, compositional tasks like chart understanding.
> - The FinePRM effectively assesses the quality of individual reasoning actions, enabling a granular feedback mechanism that significantly improves policy optimization over traditional coarse reward methods.
> - KL divergence-based action regularization is crucial for preventing "action bias" (over-reliance on easy-to-score actions) and encouraging diverse, robust reasoning strategies, leading to more generalizable models.
