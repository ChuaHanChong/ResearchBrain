# TTT-Parkour: Rapid Test-Time Training for Perceptive Robot Parkour

> [!summary] Summary
> TTT-Parkour introduces a framework that enables humanoid robots to rapidly adapt locomotion policies to complex, unseen real-world terrains within minutes. This system combines a pre-trained reinforcement learning policy with an efficient RGB-D based 3D reconstruction pipeline, achieving near-perfect simulation success rates (99-100%) and robust zero-shot transfer to a Unitree G1 robot (e.g., 60-100% success on challenging obstacles).

## Problem

- Humanoid robot locomotion policies trained in simulation struggle to generalize to unseen, geometrically complex real-world terrains, leading to brittle performance.
- Existing high-fidelity 3D scene reconstruction methods are too slow for rapid adaptation, while faster methods lack the metric accuracy required for physics-based robot learning.
- Robots lack the ability to quickly adapt their behaviors to novel, difficult obstacles encountered in dynamic, unpredictable environments without extensive re-training or manual intervention.

## Method

- A two-stage deep reinforcement learning approach employs a general policy pre-trained on diverse procedurally generated terrains, followed by rapid test-time training (TTT).
- An efficient RGB-D based geometry reconstruction pipeline rapidly generates collision-accurate, simulation-ready meshes from real-world captures within minutes, incorporating automatic scale recovery and frame alignment.
- The pre-trained policy undergoes rapid fine-tuning on the specific reconstructed real-world terrain in simulation, with full fine-tuning of all policy parameters proving most effective.

## Results

- TTT-Parkour achieved 99-**100%** simulation success rates across 13 challenging real-world terrains after rapid fine-tuning, converging to **97%** success within approximately 10 minutes (including capture, reconstruction, and TTT).
- Adapted policies demonstrated robust zero-shot sim-to-real transfer, improving real-world success on a Unitree G1 robot from **0%** (pre-trained) to 60-**100%** on complex obstacles like wedges and stakes.
- The RGB-D reconstruction pipeline accurately produced simulation-ready meshes in 2 minutes 10 seconds, outperforming other methods in speed and fidelity for physics simulation, and proved crucial for real-world deployment.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Rapid test-time training (TTT) on high-fidelity, reconstructed real-world terrains is essential for enabling humanoid robots to master complex obstacles that general pre-trained policies fail on.
> - Accurate and fast real-world scene reconstruction, including metric scale recovery and consistent frame alignment, is critical for bridging the sim-to-real gap for physics-based robot learning.
> - Full fine-tuning of all policy parameters during TTT is more effective than parameter-efficient methods when facing significant domain shifts between pre-training and novel real-world terrains.
