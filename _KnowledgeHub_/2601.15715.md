# RebuttalAgent: Strategic Persuasion in Academic Rebuttal via Theory of Mind

> [!summary] Summary
> RebuttalAgent, developed by researchers at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, integrates Theory of Mind (ToM) into Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate strategic and persuasive academic rebuttals. The framework achieved an average score of 9.42 on an automated evaluation and 9.57 in human evaluations, demonstrating a 7.36% improvement in persuasiveness over GPT-4.1.

## Problem

- Academic rebuttal is a complex, strategic game of incomplete information, requiring authors to persuade reviewers without full knowledge of their biases or knowledge bases.
- Existing LLM approaches for rebuttal often imitate surface-level linguistic patterns, lacking strategic depth and the essential perspective-taking (Theory of Mind) needed for effective persuasion.
- Successful rebuttals demand sophisticated strategic analysis, including when to concede, defend, or reframe arguments, which current AI models struggle to achieve.

## Method

- A novel ToM-Strategy-Response (TSR) framework was developed to systematically model reviewer mental states, formulate tailored persuasion strategies, and generate evidence-based responses.
- RebuttalBench, a large-scale synthetic dataset containing complete ToM-Strategy-Response chains, was constructed, and **RebuttalAgent** was trained using a two-stage process combining supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning via a self-reward mechanism.
- Rebuttal-RM, a specialized automated evaluator, was created and trained on a multi-source dataset to achieve high scoring consistency with human preferences, surpassing general-purpose LLM judges.

## Results

- RebuttalAgent achieved the highest overall average score of 9.42 on automated evaluation (R2-test) and 9.57 in human evaluations, significantly outperforming all baselines including GPT-4.1.
- The model demonstrated particularly strong gains in persuasiveness, showing a **7.36%** improvement over the GPT-4.1 baseline in human evaluations and up to **34.6%** over its Qwen3-**8B** base model in automated evaluation.
- Rebuttal-RM, the specialized evaluator, showed superior alignment with human judgments, achieving an average consistency score of **0.812** and surpassing proprietary models like GPT-4.1 by **9.0%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly grounding academic rebuttal in Theory of Mind (ToM) principles transforms the task for LLMs from linguistic imitation into strategic reasoning and perspective-taking.
> - A structured, multi-stage reasoning process (TSR framework) enables LLMs to systematically analyze reviewer profiles, plan specific strategies, and generate context-aware, evidence-based responses.
> - Combining supervised fine-tuning with a novel self-reward mechanism during reinforcement learning allows for scalable self-improvement in strategic persuasion without relying on external, expert-annotated reward models.
