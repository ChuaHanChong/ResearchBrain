# Open-Vocabulary Multi-Label Classification via Multi-Modal Knowledge Transfer

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shenzhen University, Tsinghua University, and Tencent's YouTu Lab developed the Multi-modal Knowledge Transfer (MKT) framework for open-vocabulary multi-label classification. This framework leverages pre-trained vision-language models to achieve state-of-the-art performance, demonstrated by an 11.7% absolute mAP gain over prior methods on NUS-WIDE ZSL, and accurately classifies unseen and complex multi-word labels.

## Problem

- Traditional multi-label classification systems are limited to predefined labels, failing to recognize new, unseen categories in dynamic environments.
- Existing Multi-Label Zero-Shot Learning methods rely on single-modal textual knowledge, neglecting rich visual semantic information and struggling with complex multi-word 'text labels'.
- Adapting powerful vision-language pre-training (VLP) models for open-vocabulary *multi-label* classification, especially involving unseen text labels, had remained largely unexplored.

## Method

- The ==Multi-modal Knowledge Transfer (MKT)== framework is introduced, utilizing a Vision Transformer as an image encoder and leveraging a pre-trained CLIP model for multi-modal knowledge.
- A ==two-stream module== within the vision transformer extracts both global image features and discriminative local patch features for comprehensive multi-object recognition.
- ==Knowledge distillation== aligns the student model's global image features with robust multi-modal representations from the CLIP image encoder, while ==prompt tuning== refines label embeddings generated by the CLIP text encoder for the multi-label task.

## Results

- The MKT framework achieved new state-of-the-art performance, consistently outperforming prior Multi-Label Zero-Shot Learning methods and a fine-tuned CLIP baseline on NUS-WIDE and Open Images datasets across ZSL and GZSL tasks.
- On NUS-WIDE ZSL, MKT demonstrated an **1**1.7%** absolute mAP gain** over the BiAM method (**37.6%** vs. **25.9%**), and a **6.**5%** mAP gain** over ZS-SDL on GZSL (**18.3%** vs. **12.1%**).
- The framework successfully classifies complex multi-word 'text labels' and demonstrates enhanced localization of objects through Grad-CAM visualizations, all while exhibiting competitive efficiency in terms of parameters and FLOPs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Multi-modal knowledge from Vision and Language Pre-training (VLP) models provides superior semantic and visual consistency for label embeddings compared to single-modal language models.
> - Knowledge distillation effectively transfers powerful VLP capabilities, aligning image embeddings and enhancing generalization to unseen labels while preventing overfitting.
> - Prompt tuning offers an efficient method to adapt VLP text embeddings, making them more discriminative for specific downstream multi-label tasks without extensive fine-tuning.
> - Combining global and local image feature streams within a two-stream module leads to more robust and discriminative multi-label predictions.
