# Agent AI: Surveying the Horizons of Multimodal Interaction

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper defines "Agent AI" as interactive systems capable of multimodal perception and embodied action, proposing a unified architecture for generalist agents by integrating Large Language Models and Visual Language Models. It introduces new benchmarks, "CuisineWorld" and "VideoAnalytica," to facilitate research in multi-agent collaboration and analytical video understanding.

## Problem

- Traditional AI research has been fragmented across subfields (vision, planning, manipulation), lacking a unified approach for interactive, context-aware systems.
- Existing foundation models (LLMs, VLMs) are primarily designed for passive, structured tasks and suffer from limitations like hallucinations, biases, and a lack of environmental grounding.
- The rapid growth of multimodal AI lacks a cohesive framework and standardized benchmarks for developing and evaluating generalist agents capable of real-world interaction.

## Method

- Formally defines ==Agent AI== as interactive systems perceiving multimodal inputs and producing embodied actions, emphasizing a holistic approach to integrate diverse AI capabilities.
- Proposes a new paradigm for training Agent AI based on a ==Unified Agent Multi-modal Transformer== that incorporates specialized ==agent tokens== for domain-specific behaviors and supports end-to-end learning.
- Outlines comprehensive learning strategies (==Reinforcement Learning==, ==Imitation Learning==, ==In-context Learning==) and addresses challenges like hallucinations and biases through environmental grounding and ethical considerations.

## Results

- Demonstrates **GPT-4V**'s ability to understand game context and predict high-level actions in complex gaming environments and its potential for medical image/video understanding while highlighting safety constraints.
- Shows LLM-empowered task planning in robotics, generating coherent plans and adapting to feedback in simulations, with previous work achieving state-of-the-art in vision-language navigation.
- Introduces **2** new datasets/benchmarks: **CuisineWorld** for multi-agent gaming collaboration and **VideoAnalytica** for analytical video demonstration comprehension, focusing on video-to-text retrieval and question-answering.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating LLMs and VLMs into interactive, embodied agents can mitigate issues like hallucinations and improve reasoning by grounding models in environmental feedback.
> - A unified agent architecture with specific "agent tokens" can lead to more customizable, interpretable, and data-efficient systems than modular approaches using frozen foundation models.
> - Agent AI holds transformative potential across gaming, robotics, and healthcare, but requires robust ethical frameworks and dedicated benchmarks to address risks and ensure responsible development.
