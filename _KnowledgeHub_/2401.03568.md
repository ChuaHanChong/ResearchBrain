# Agent AI: Surveying the Horizons of Multimodal Interaction

> [!summary] Summary
> This survey paper defines "Agent AI" as interactive systems capable of multimodal perception and embodied action, proposing a unified architecture for generalist agents by integrating Large Language Models and Visual Language Models. It introduces new benchmarks, "CuisineWorld" and "VideoAnalytica," to facilitate research in multi-agent collaboration and analytical video understanding.

## Problem

- Traditional AI research has been fragmented across subfields (vision, planning, manipulation), lacking a unified approach for interactive, context-aware systems.
- Existing foundation models (LLMs, VLMs) are primarily designed for passive, structured tasks and suffer from limitations like hallucinations, biases, and a lack of environmental grounding.
- The rapid growth of multimodal AI lacks a cohesive framework and standardized benchmarks for developing and evaluating generalist agents capable of real-world interaction.

## Method

- Formally defines ==Agent AI== as interactive systems perceiving multimodal inputs and producing embodied actions, emphasizing a holistic approach to integrate diverse AI capabilities.
- Proposes a new paradigm for training Agent AI based on a ==Unified Agent Multi-modal Transformer== that incorporates specialized ==agent tokens== for domain-specific behaviors and supports end-to-end learning.
- Outlines comprehensive learning strategies (==Reinforcement Learning==, ==Imitation Learning==, ==In-context Learning==) and addresses challenges like hallucinations and biases through environmental grounding and ethical considerations.

## Results

- Demonstrates **GPT-4V**'s ability to understand game context and predict high-level actions in complex gaming environments and its potential for medical image/video understanding while highlighting safety constraints.
- Shows LLM-empowered task planning in robotics, generating coherent plans and adapting to feedback in simulations, with previous work achieving state-of-the-art in vision-language navigation.
- Introduces **2** new datasets/benchmarks: **CuisineWorld** for multi-agent gaming collaboration and **VideoAnalytica** for analytical video demonstration comprehension, focusing on video-to-text retrieval and question-answering.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating LLMs and VLMs into interactive, embodied agents can mitigate issues like hallucinations and improve reasoning by grounding models in environmental feedback.
> - A unified agent architecture with specific "agent tokens" can lead to more customizable, interpretable, and data-efficient systems than modular approaches using frozen foundation models.
> - Agent AI holds transformative potential across gaming, robotics, and healthcare, but requires robust ethical frameworks and dedicated benchmarks to address risks and ensure responsible development.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This paper positions "Agent AI" as a crucial and emerging frontier in the pursuit of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), addressing a historical fragmentation within AI research. Traditionally, AI subfields have made independent progress in areas like vision, planning, and manipulation. However, the authors argue for a return to "Aristotelian Holism," aiming to synthesize these disparate advancements into truly interactive and context-aware systems.

The recent revolution in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Visual Language Models (VLMs) is identified as the catalyst for this paradigm shift. While LLMs and VLMs have demonstrated impressive capabilities in passive, structured tasks (e.g., text generation, image classification), the research landscape is now moving towards empowering these models to act as dynamic agents within physical and virtual environments. This work formally defines "Agent AI" as a class of interactive systems capable of perceiving multimodal inputs (visual, language, environmental data) and producing meaningful embodied actions.

The paper critically examines how Agent AI integrates with and extends existing foundation models, addressing their inherent limitations such as hallucinations, biases, and the need for improved generalization. By grounding LLMs and VLMs in interactive environments, Agent AI seeks to mitigate these issues and enhance the models' capacity for complex reasoning, multi-step planning, and adaptability. It emphasizes "reality-agnostic training" by leveraging generative AI alongside diverse data sources, enabling cross-reality application (physical and virtual worlds).

This survey therefore serves as a foundational synthesis, interconnecting various sub-fields like embodied AI, multimodal interaction, generative models, robotics, gaming, and healthcare. It aims to unify the understanding of these interconnected domains, provide a common vocabulary, and chart future research directions towards creating generalist agents that can naturally interact with users and generalize across a broad range of contexts and modalities. It acts as a comprehensive map for researchers navigating the complex and rapidly evolving landscape of interactive, agent-based intelligence.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching motivation for this work stems from a desire to return to the foundational goals of Artificial Intelligence as envisioned at the 1956 Dartmouth Conference – creating artificial entities that can perceive, collect information, and interact meaningfully with their environment. The authors explicitly draw inspiration from Minsky’s early robotics work, recognizing the need for a holistic approach that integrates various AI capabilities.

The recent advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Visual Language Models (VLMs) are identified as the enabling factor for this return to holism, making it feasible to create novel AI agents that integrate language proficiency, visual cognition, contextual memory, intuitive reasoning, and adaptability. The paper emphasizes moving beyond AI models designed for passive, structured tasks to those capable of taking on dynamic, agentic roles in diverse and complex environments.

The key objectives of this survey paper are multifaceted:
1. **Define Agent AI:** To formally introduce "Agent AI" as a distinct class of interactive systems that can perceive multimodal stimuli and produce embodied actions, thereby accelerating research in agent-based multimodal intelligence.
2. **Explore Integration of Foundation Models:** To investigate how LLMs and VLMs can be leveraged as agents, focusing on their blend of linguistic proficiency, visual cognition, contextual memory, intuitive reasoning, and adaptability.
3. **Address Limitations of Foundation Models:** To demonstrate how developing agentic AI systems in grounded environments can mitigate common issues like hallucinations, biases, and the generation of environmentally incorrect outputs from large foundation models.
4. **Provide Comprehensive Knowledge:** To offer a deep dive into the principles and roles of Multimodal Agent AI (MAA) in contemporary applications, covering data collection, benchmarking, and ethical considerations.
5. **Detail Methodologies:** To illustrate how LLMs and VLMs enhance MAAs through case studies in gaming, robotics, and healthcare.
6. **Guide Performance Evaluation:** To provide guidance on assessing MAAs using relevant datasets, focusing on effectiveness and generalization.
7. **Discuss Ethical Implications:** To highlight the societal impacts and ethical challenges of deploying Agent AI, promoting responsible development practices.
8. **Identify Emerging Trends and Future Challenges:** To categorize the latest developments in various domains and discuss future directions for research in MAA.
9. **Cultivate a Research Ecosystem:** To foster a dynamic Agent AI community by engaging diverse researchers and creating a shared sense of identity and purpose.
10. **Introduce New Benchmarks:** To release new datasets ("CuisineWorld" and "VideoAnalytica") and benchmarks to accelerate research and provide objective evaluation platforms for multi-agent gaming and agentic visual language tasks.

Ultimately, the motivation is to chart a clear path towards developing intelligent agents that can robustly interact with and understand the physical and virtual worlds, potentially serving as a significant route towards achieving AGI.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

This paper is a comprehensive survey, and its "methodology" is primarily a structured synthesis and proposal for future research, rather than presenting novel empirical results from a single experiment. The authors outline a conceptual framework, learning strategies, and application areas to define and advance "Agent AI."

**Defining Agent AI:**
The paper defines "Agent AI" as interactive systems capable of perceiving visual stimuli, language inputs, and other environmentally-grounded data, and then producing meaningful embodied actions. This definition emphasizes next-embodied action prediction, incorporating external knowledge, multi-sensory inputs, and human feedback.

**Proposed Agent AI Paradigm (Section 3):**
A core contribution is the proposal of a new paradigm and framework for training Agent AI, designed to overcome limitations of current systems that often rely on frozen, independently trained foundation models. This paradigm involves five main modules:
1. **Environment and Perception:** Handles task-planning and skill observation.
2. **Agent Learning:** The core learning mechanism.
3. **Memory:** Stores learned knowledge for retrieval.
4. **Agent Action:** Translates decisions into embodied actions.
5. **Cognition:** Facilitates reasoning and problem-solving.

Central to this paradigm is the concept of a **Unified Agent Multi-modal Transformer** (Figure 7). Unlike existing models that connect frozen LLMs and Visual Language Models (VLMs) with adaptation layers, this proposed model aims for end-to-end training. It incorporates "agent tokens" – specialized input/output tokens reserved for domain-specific agentic behaviors (e.g., robotic control inputs, game actions, API calls). This design allows for customization, better interpretability of actions, and suitability for data-sensitive domains.

**Agent Transformer Creation:**
The process of creating such an agent transformer involves:
- **Defining Objectives:** Clearly delineating the agent's tasks, action-space, and specific goals within its environment, including the assignment of unique agent tokens.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Leveraging automatic rules or foundation-model generated/human-annotated data for training, followed by continuous monitoring, feedback collection, and fine-tuning to refine performance and address biases.

**Agent AI Learning Strategies (Section 4):**
The paper surveys various learning mechanisms that can be integrated into the Agent AI paradigm:
- **Reinforcement Learning (RL):** Discusses how LLMs/VLMs can mitigate RL challenges by assisting in reward function design, enhancing data collection efficiency (e.g., through data generation), and tackling long-horizon tasks via hierarchical planning (e.g., LLMs for high-level task decomposition, RL for low-level control).
- **Imitation Learning (IL):** Explores learning policies from expert demonstrations (Behavioral Cloning), advocating for in-context learning with prompts or implicit reward functions to improve generalization beyond direct mimicry.
- **Traditional RGB:** Addresses the "curse of dimensionality" in visual input by using larger datasets (real and synthetic) and incorporating inductive biases like 3D structures or maps.
- **In-context Learning:** Highlights the effectiveness of few-shot prompts with LLMs/VLMs for various tasks, further enhanced by environment-specific feedback.
- **Optimization:** Covers spatial optimization (e.g., inter-robot coordination) and temporal optimization (e.g., task scheduling) within multi-agent systems, including self-play RL and mechanisms for diverse conventions.

**Addressing Key Challenges:**
The survey systematically addresses critical challenges in AI development:
- **Hallucinations:** Proposes grounding language outputs with external knowledge retrieval (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and leveraging environmental grounding to prevent nonsensical or unfaithful outputs.
- **Biases and Inclusivity:** Acknowledges biases from training data (e.g., WEIRD societies) and advocates for diverse datasets, bias detection, ethical guidelines, user feedback mechanisms, and inclusive design.
- **Data Privacy and Usage:** Emphasizes transparency, user consent, secure storage, data deletion rights, and compliance with regulations (GDPR, CCPA).
- **Interpretability and Explainability:** Suggests decoupling learning processes and using agent tokens for better understanding of model decisions, and promoting generalization for emergent behaviors.
- **Inference Augmentation:** Describes enhancing AI's natural inference with external data, improved algorithms, Human-in-the-Loop processes, real-time feedback, and cross-domain knowledge transfer.
- **Regulation:** Highlights the importance of prompt engineering, pre-execution verification, and human guidance, particularly for high-stakes applications like robotics.

By outlining these integrated approaches, the paper provides a cohesive methodology for building robust, generalist, and ethical Agent AI systems.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

As a survey paper, "Agent AI: Surveying the Horizons of Multimodal Interaction" primarily synthesizes existing research and demonstrates the capabilities of current state-of-the-art models, particularly large foundation models like GPT-4V, across various domains. While it doesn't present a single, overarching experimental result, it offers qualitative observations and highlights previous quantitative achievements from the authors' related works, alongside introducing new benchmarks.

**Key Findings and Demonstrations across Application Domains:**

1. **Gaming Agents (Section 6.1):**
 - **GPT-4V's High-Level Action Prediction:** Qualitative examples demonstrate GPT-4V's ability to understand game context and predict high-level future actions in complex games like Minecraft, Bleeding Edge, Microsoft Flight Simulator, Assassin's Creed Odyssey, Gears of War 4, and Starfield. It can incorporate perceived visual information (e.g., holding wooden logs in Minecraft) into its plans. The paper notes its outputs are not inherently suitable for raw low-level action prediction but suggest it can inform supplemental modules.
 - **Small Agent Pretraining Model:** Experiments with a custom agent vision-language architecture pretrained on Minecraft data show reasonable outputs for unseen scenes and potential for low-level action understanding.
 - **MindAgent Infrastructure:** The proposed infrastructure, leveraging GPT-4 as a central planner, facilitates multi-agent collaboration in the "CuisineWorld" text-based game. This highlights capabilities in multi-agent planning and human-AI collaboration.

2. **Robotics Agents (Section 6.2):**
 - **LLM-Empowered Task Planning:** Using ChatGPT for task planning, enriched with affordance information, is shown to generate coherent task plans and allow for flexible adjustment based on auto-generated feedback in simulated environments (e.g., VirtualHome).
 - **GPT-4V for Multimodal Task Planning:** A pipeline integrating GPT-4V for video and scene analysis from human demonstrations successfully outputs sequences of robot actions. GPT-4V accurately selects relevant objects and understands their spatial relationships based on human actions.
 - **Embodied Agents for Robotics Navigation:** Previous work by the authors (Wang et al., 2019) on Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) introduces the Reinforced Cross-Modal Matching (RCM) and Self-Supervised Imitation Learning (SIL) methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance and improving generalization in unseen environments.

3. **Healthcare Agents (Section 6.3):**
 - **GPT-4V's Medical Understanding:** Demonstrations show GPT-4V possessing significant internal knowledge of hospital equipment and procedures for image and video understanding. However, due to safety training, it refrains from making prescriptive or diagnostic queries, highlighting a critical ethical and safety boundary.
 - The paper identifies the potential for diagnostic and knowledge retrieval agents, while strongly emphasizing the severe risks of hallucinations in medical contexts.

4. **Multimodal Agents (Section 6.4):**
 - **Knowledge-Intensive Agents (INK, KAT):** Previous works (Park et al., 2022; Gui et al., 2022a) demonstrate models that combine implicit knowledge from LLMs (GPT-3) with explicit knowledge from websites for Visual Question Answering (VQA), outperforming baselines and enhancing interpretability.
 - **Vision-Language Transformer Agents (VLC):** The VLC model (Gui et al., 2022b), pretrained solely on image-caption pairs, achieves competitive results across various vision-language tasks, showing effective visual representations and scalability.
 - **Video-Language Experiments:** A fine-tuned InstructBLIP variant for video captioning shows abilities in summarizing visible actions but also instances of hallucinated details, emphasizing the need for robust multimodal integration.
 - **Audio-Video-Language Agents with GPT-4V:** Experiments reveal that integrating audio transcriptions with frame captions significantly enhances the accuracy and detail of video summaries, correcting visual misrepresentations and demonstrating a more holistic understanding than vision-only approaches.
 - **VideoAnalytica Pipeline:** A system leveraging GPT-4V to generate challenging "hard negative" queries for video-to-text retrieval and question-answering tasks, using a combination of video, audio, and web-retrieved external knowledge.

5. **NLP Agents (Section 6.6):**
 - **Logic Agents:** "Logical Transformers" (Wang et al., 2023e) are introduced, which embed logical structures into language models to improve abstractive summarization and reduce factual errors.
 - **Knowledge Selector Agent:** The "Retrieve What You Need" framework (Wang et al., 2023g) employs a reinforcement learning-trained knowledge selector to optimize the retrieval of informative passages for open-domain question answering.
 - The paper generally highlights the importance of tool use, improved reasoning and planning (e.g., ReAct, Tree of Thoughts), and incorporating system/human feedback (AutoGen) for language-grounded agents.

**New Datasets and Benchmarks (Section 9):**
- **"CuisineWorld":** A new text-based, multi-agent gaming dataset designed to benchmark collaboration efficiency (using a "Collaboration Score" metric) of LLMs and other systems in dynamic, cooperative scenarios.
- **"VideoAnalytica":** A new benchmark for analytical video demonstration comprehension, focusing on video text retrieval (with hard negatives) and video-assisted informative question answering, requiring integration of audio, video, and language, and domain-specific knowledge.

These findings collectively illustrate the vast potential of Agent AI, particularly when grounded in multimodal data and interactive environments, while also candidly addressing the remaining challenges and limitations that require further research.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

This survey paper on "Agent AI: Surveying the Horizons of Multimodal Interaction" holds significant implications for the future direction of Artificial Intelligence research and its real-world applications. Its significance stems from several key contributions:

1. **Unifying a Rapidly Evolving Field:** The paper provides a much-needed comprehensive and structured overview of the burgeoning field of Agent AI. By formally defining "Agent AI" and proposing a unified paradigm, it helps to organize disparate research efforts across various modalities and domains, fostering a shared understanding and accelerating collaborative progress towards generalist agents. This synthesis is crucial for researchers to navigate the complexities arising from the rapid advancements in LLMs and VLMs.

2. **Charting a Path Towards AGI:** By advocating for a return to "Aristotelian Holism" and emphasizing the integration of perception, memory, learning, action, and cognition within embodied systems, the paper positions Agent AI as a promising avenue for achieving Artificial General Intelligence. The focus on "reality-agnostic training" and cross-reality application highlights a vision for AI that can operate seamlessly across virtual and physical environments.

3. **Novel Paradigms and Methodologies:** The proposal of a "Unified Agent Multi-modal Transformer" and the concept of "agent tokens" offers a fresh architectural perspective that could lead to more efficient, customizable, and interpretable agent systems compared to current approaches that rely heavily on frozen, modular components. The detailed discussion of learning strategies (RL, IL, in-context learning) and optimization techniques provides a robust framework for developing these advanced agents.

4. **Addressing Core AI Challenges:** The paper directly tackles critical limitations of current foundation models, such as hallucinations and biases. By emphasizing environmental grounding, retrieval-augmented generation, human-in-the-loop mechanisms, and diverse training data, it lays out a roadmap for building more reliable, fair, and trustworthy AI agents. This focus on ethical considerations is paramount, especially as AI systems become more autonomous and integrated into high-stakes domains.

5. **Transformative Impact Across Diverse Domains:** The detailed exploration of Agent AI applications in gaming, robotics, and healthcare underscores its potential for widespread societal and economic transformation:
 - **Gaming:** Agent AI can revolutionize gaming by enabling more dynamic, intelligent, and human-like NPC behaviors, fostering richer human-NPC interactions, and greatly accelerating content creation (e.g., scene synthesis, AR/VR environments). This could lead to vastly more immersive and personalized player experiences.
 - **Robotics:** For robotics, Agent AI promises more intuitive human-robot collaboration through natural language conditioned manipulation, robust task planning, and continuous skill optimization. Advancements in sim-to-real transfer are critical for deploying these agents safely and effectively in real-world settings.
 - **Healthcare:** The potential for diagnostic agents, knowledge retrieval assistants, and enhanced telemedicine services could significantly improve patient care, increase access to medical expertise, and streamline healthcare operations. However, the paper rightly stresses the severe ethical and safety implications (e.g., dangerous hallucinations in diagnosis) that necessitate rigorous development and deployment protocols.

6. **Catalyst for Future Research and Benchmarking:** The introduction of new benchmarks, "CuisineWorld" and "VideoAnalytica," provides concrete platforms for the research community to evaluate and compare new models and systems. These benchmarks, especially with their focus on multi-agent collaboration and analytical video understanding (including hard negatives), will stimulate innovation and establish clear performance metrics for advanced agent capabilities.

In conclusion, this paper is a landmark survey that not only synthesizes the current state of Agent AI but also proactively outlines its future directions, technical challenges, and societal implications. Its interdisciplinary nature, strong emphasis on ethical development, and practical applications across diverse sectors make it a vital resource for researchers, developers, and policymakers aiming to responsibly advance the field towards a future of truly intelligent, interactive, and impactful AI agents.
