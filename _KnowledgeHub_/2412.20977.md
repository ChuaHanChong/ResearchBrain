# UnrealZoo: Enriching Photo-realistic Virtual Worlds for Embodied AI

> [!summary] Summary
> UnrealZoo introduces a photo-realistic virtual world platform for Embodied AI, featuring over 100 diverse 3D environments and a rich set of playable entities. The platform optimizes simulation efficiency and reveals key limitations of current AI agents in complex, open-world scenarios, demonstrating improved policy transferability to other simulators and real-world settings.

## Problem

- Existing embodied AI simulators often provide limited environmental diversity and narrow focus, hindering the development of generalizable agents for real-world scenarios.
- A lack of diverse playable entities and robust multi-agent interaction capabilities restricts research into cross-embodiment and complex social intelligence.
- Current simulation tools require optimization for high-throughput, real-time, and multi-agent interactions crucial for effective reinforcement learning and large-scale training.

## Method

- **UnrealZoo** provides a collection of over 100 photo-realistic 3D virtual worlds, spanning various scales, styles, and dynamic elements built on ==Unreal Engine==.
- The platform incorporates seven types of playable entities, totaling 67 unique embodiments, each equipped with ego-centric sensing and customizable locomotion/navigation systems.
- An optimized programming interface, UnrealCV+, enhances rendering and communication efficiency, offering high-level Python APIs and an OpenAI Gym interface for ease of use and distributed training.

## Results

- UnrealCV+ significantly improved simulation efficiency, achieving FPS increases (e.g., **120%** for object masks) and multi-agent interaction gains (up to **100%** for 10 agents).
- Online RL agents attained moderate success in visual navigation (SR up to **0.52**), while GPT-4o performed poorly (SR **0.00**-**0.20**), both lagging far behind human performance (SR **1.00**).
- Agents trained on diverse environments showed higher generalization success rates in active visual tracking, with RL methods outperforming baselines and demonstrating more stable performance under active distractions.
- Policies trained in UnrealZoo, especially with diverse data, exhibited improved transferability to the ThreeDWorld simulator and real-world environments.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current embodied AI methods, including large vision-language models, struggle with complex 3D scene reasoning, fine-grained control, and dynamic navigation in unstructured environments, showing a substantial gap compared to human performance.
> - Training with data from a diverse set of environments is crucial for achieving robust generalization in tasks like active visual tracking across various unseen scenarios.
> - Policies do not seamlessly generalize across different agent morphologies, and low-latency perception-control loops are essential for effective execution in dynamic, high-frequency control tasks.
