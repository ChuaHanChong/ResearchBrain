# FailSafe: Reasoning and Recovery from Failures in Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> An automatic pipeline, FailSafe, generates failure-action data to train Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models in autonomous recovery from errors. A VLM fine-tuned with this data achieves a 90.94% binary success rate in failure detection and boosts the average success rate of state-of-the-art VLA models by up to 22.6% in robot manipulation tasks, demonstrating generalization across novel objects and robot types.

## Problem

- Current Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, primarily trained on success-only datasets, cannot detect, reason about, or autonomously recover from unexpected failures in real-world settings.
- Existing approaches for robot failure address issues are limited by reliance on impractical human supervision or generating high-level textual explanations that lack directly executable low-level robot actions.
- There is a critical lack of scalable, diverse datasets containing both failure scenarios and corresponding executable recovery actions, which is essential for training robust embodied AI systems.

## Method

- Developed **FailSafe**, an automatic pipeline that systematically generates diverse failure scenarios and corresponding ==7-DoF delta recovery actions== by perturbing successful trajectories in simulation.
- Implemented a rigorous ==systematic verification mechanism== to ensure that all collected recovery actions effectively resolve the failure and lead to successful task completion.
- Fine-tuned a state-of-the-art Vision-Language Model (==LLaVA-OneVision-7B==) into **FailSafe**-VLM using the generated ==failure-action dataset==, enabling it to output precise executable corrections.

## Results

- **FailSafe**-VLM achieved a **0.9094** binary success rate in failure detection and a **0.6522** cosine similarity for recovery actions, dramatically outperforming general-purpose VLMs (e.g., GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5-flash) which scored near zero for executable actions.
- When integrated with state-of-the-art VLA models, **FailSafe**-VLM improved average task success rates by up to **+22.6%** for OpenVLA, **+8.0%** for OpenVLA-OFT, and **+4.0%** for πo-FAST.
- The system demonstrated strong generalization, boosting OpenVLA-OFT's performance on tasks with novel object categories by **+17.4%** and improving the 'Stack Cube' task on an unseen xArm 6 robot from **56%** to **76%**.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly training VLMs on systematically generated failure-action pairs, rather than just success-only data, significantly enhances their capability for accurate failure diagnosis and generation of directly executable recovery actions.
> - Integrating a specialized failure-recovery VLM (FailSafe-VLM) as an external assistant provides a scalable and effective method to improve the robustness and success rates of existing VLA models during execution.
> - The learned failure reasoning and recovery principles demonstrate strong generalization capabilities across novel spatial configurations, camera viewpoints, object categories, and even different robotic embodiments.
