# Training Agents Inside of Scalable World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Google DeepMind's Dreamer 4 introduces a scalable and efficient world model that enables learning complex control tasks by reinforcement learning entirely within imagination. It achieves the first-ever offline acquisition of diamonds in Minecraft, reaching a 0.7% success rate while performing real-time interactive inference at 21 frames per second on a single GPU.

## Problem

- Prior world models struggled to accurately predict complex object interactions and game mechanics in highly dynamic and open-ended environments like Minecraft.
- Large generative models, while powerful, were often computationally too expensive for real-time, iterative imagination training needed for efficient reinforcement learning.
- Learning sophisticated, long-horizon behaviors from fixed, offline datasets remained a major challenge due to the lack of interactive exploration and feedback.
- Agents needed to efficiently leverage vast amounts of unlabeled video data to learn general world knowledge and action conditioning with minimal labeled examples.

## Method

- **Dreamer 4** utilizes a three-phase training approach: world model pretraining, agent finetuning with task-conditioned policy/reward heads, and imagination training using reinforcement learning.
- A novel architecture combines a ==causal tokenizer== for efficient video compression with an interactive dynamics model (world model) built on an ==efficient transformer architecture==.
- Key innovations in the dynamics model include ==shortcut forcing== with ==x-prediction== and ==x-space loss== for stable, high-quality, and fast long-duration rollouts, requiring only K=4 sampling steps per frame.

## Results

- Dreamer 4 is the first agent to obtain diamonds in Minecraft purely from offline data, achieving a **0.7%** success rate in 60-minute episodes, significantly outperforming prior state-of-the-art offline agents.
- The world model accurately predicts complex Minecraft interactions and operates at **21 FPS** on a single H100 GPU, surpassing Minecraft's tick rate and prior world models in fidelity and context length (9.6 seconds).
- It learns accurate action conditioning from just **100 hours** of labeled data (out of **2541 hours** total) and generalizes this knowledge to visually distinct Minecraft dimensions (Nether/End) with high fidelity.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Imagination-based reinforcement learning with a scalable world model can effectively learn complex, long-horizon behaviors from purely offline datasets, even in challenging environments like Minecraft.
> - High-fidelity world models capable of simulating precise object interactions can achieve real-time interactive inference, making them practical for iterative agent training.
> - Action conditioning can be learned with high data efficiency, predominantly from unlabeled videos, demonstrating a powerful form of action generalization to visually novel environments.
> - Specific architectural choices like x-prediction, x-space loss, ramp loss weighting, and efficient transformer components (e.g., factorized attention, GQA) are critical for robust and efficient generation in world models.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of Dreamer 4 sits at a crucial intersection of several cutting-edge AI research areas: **world models, reinforcement learning (RL), generative AI, and offline learning**.

Traditionally, intelligent agents aim to understand their environment and choose optimal actions. World models offer a promising paradigm by learning to predict future outcomes of actions, allowing agents to plan or learn behaviors entirely in "imagination." Earlier Dreamer models (e.g., Dreamer 3) demonstrated state-of-the-art performance and robustness in specific, often narrower, environments like Atari games or simplified robotics tasks. However, these models faced limitations in scaling their architectures to handle the complexity and diversity of real-world distributions or highly complex embodied environments.

Concurrently, advances in **generative AI**, particularly with large-scale transformer architectures and diffusion models (e.g., Diffusion Transformers, Genie 3), have enabled the creation of highly diverse and visually rich video content. While these models excel at generating plausible scenes and simple interactions from diverse datasets, they often struggle with the precise physics and complex object interactions required for training successful, actionable agents. Furthermore, their computational cost for real-time simulation, especially for long sequences, often limits their practical utility for imagination-based RL.

Dreamer 4 aims to bridge this gap. It integrates the core philosophy of imagination-based RL from the Dreamer line with the scalability and generative power of modern transformer and diffusion-based architectures. This addresses the critical challenge of developing world models that are simultaneously high-fidelity enough to accurately simulate complex environments (like Minecraft) and efficient enough to enable real-time imagination training on practical hardware (a single GPU).

The work also strongly contributes to the field of **offline reinforcement learning**. In many practical applications, such as robotics, continuous online interaction with a partially trained agent is unsafe, slow, or expensive. Learning purely from fixed, offline datasets is therefore highly desirable. Dreamer 4’s success in solving the "Minecraft diamond challenge" (a notoriously difficult, long-horizon task) purely from offline data represents a significant advancement in this domain, outperforming prior offline RL agents by a large margin and using substantially less data.

Finally, the paper explores the vital concept of **action generalization** and learning from **unlabeled videos**. The ability of a world model to absorb general knowledge from diverse unlabeled data (where actions are not available) and then ground that knowledge with only a small amount of action-paired video is a key step towards more general-purpose AI systems that can leverage the vast amount of unstructured data available online. This positions Dreamer 4 as a crucial step towards building more data-efficient and broadly applicable intelligent agents.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching objective of this research is to develop a **scalable agent (Dreamer 4) capable of learning complex control tasks by reinforcement learning entirely within a fast and accurate world model.** This ambition is driven by several key motivations:

1. **Enhance World Model Fidelity for Complex Environments:** Previous world models, while effective in narrow settings, have struggled to accurately predict complex object interactions and game mechanics in highly dynamic and open-ended environments. A core objective was to build a world model that could genuinely capture the nuances of a game like Minecraft.
2. **Enable Efficient Imagination Training:** For world models to be practical tools for agent training, they must allow for rapid and real-time simulation of imagined experiences. A critical goal was to achieve real-time interactive inference of the world model on a single GPU, overcoming the computational bottlenecks of previous scalable generative models.
3. **Achieve Complex Behaviors Purely from Offline Data:** Online interaction in real-world scenarios (e.g., robotics) is often unsafe, slow, or costly. A significant objective was to demonstrate that Dreamer 4 could learn sophisticated, long-horizon behaviors (specifically, obtaining diamonds in Minecraft) solely from a fixed, offline dataset, without any further environment interaction during learning. This aligns with practical applications where offline learning is essential.
4. **Maximize Knowledge Extraction from Unlabeled Data:** To move towards more general intelligence, agents should be able to learn broad world knowledge from diverse, readily available (often unlabeled) video data. The authors aimed to show that Dreamer 4 could learn general action conditioning from a minimal amount of action-labeled data, predominantly extracting its understanding from unlabeled videos.
5. **Advance the Dreamer Research Line:** As the fourth iteration, Dreamer 4 seeks to integrate lessons from prior Dreamer models with modern advancements in scalable generative architectures, creating a more capable and efficient recipe for imagination training.

In essence, the motivation is to create intelligent agents that not only deeply understand their world through scalable internal simulations but can also learn effectively and safely from existing data, paving the way for more adaptable and general AI systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

Dreamer 4 employs a sophisticated three-phase training approach and a novel architecture that combines elements of diffusion models, transformers, and reinforcement learning.

**Overall Training Phases:**
1. **World Model Pretraining:** The tokenizer and dynamics model are initially trained on a large dataset of videos (and optionally actions).
2. **Agent Finetuning:** The pretrained world model is adapted to predict actions, rewards, and values by adding task-conditioned policy and reward heads.
3. **Imagination Training:** The policy is then further optimized through reinforcement learning on trajectories generated by the world model itself.

**Architectural Components:**
1. **Causal Tokenizer:**
 - **Purpose:** Compresses raw video frames (e.g., 360x640 Minecraft pixels) into a sequence of continuous, lower-dimensional representations that the dynamics model can process efficiently.
 - **Architecture:** Consists of an encoder and a decoder with a bottleneck, designed to be *causal in time*. This means the tokenizer can encode and decode frames sequentially, critical for interactive inference. It uses an "efficient transformer architecture" (detailed below).
 - **Training:** Trained using a **masked autoencoding** objective, which involves reconstructing original video frames from corrupted (partially masked) inputs. This uses a combination of Mean Squared Error (MSE) and LPIPS perceptual loss to ensure high-quality, spatially consistent reconstructions. Masking input patches forces the encoder to learn robust and generalizable representations.
2. **Interactive Dynamics Model (World Model):**
 - **Purpose:** The core of Dreamer 4, responsible for predicting future representations (and thus future frames) conditioned on agent actions. It operates on the tokenized representations from the frozen tokenizer.
 - **Architecture:** Also built on the "efficient transformer architecture." It takes an interleaved sequence of actions, discrete signal levels, step sizes, and corrupted representations as input and predicts the clean representations. Actions are encoded separately (e.g., mouse and keyboard components) into spatial tokens.
 - **Training Objective (Shortcut Forcing):** This is a key innovation, building on diffusion forcing and shortcut models for efficient and accurate generation:
 - **x-prediction:** Unlike many diffusion models that predict noise or velocity (v-prediction), Dreamer 4's dynamics model is parameterized to predict *clean representations* (x-prediction). This is crucial for long-duration, iterative rollouts as it prevents the accumulation of subtle, high-frequency errors common with v-prediction, leading to more stable and higher-quality generated sequences.
 - **x-space loss:** The loss function is computed in x-space (the space of clean representations) for both the standard flow matching term and the bootstrap loss term (used by shortcut models for multi-step prediction).
 - **Ramp loss weight:** A novel linear weighting (0.9𝜏 + 0.1) is applied to the loss, increasing with the signal level (𝜏). This focuses the model's capacity on higher signal levels, which provide more meaningful learning signals, leading to better overall performance.
 - **Inference:** During imagination training, the dynamics model generates future states autoregressively. It uses **K=4 sampling steps** per frame with corresponding step size d=1/4, enabled by the shortcut forcing objective, making it significantly faster than typical diffusion models (which often require 64+ steps). It also slightly corrupts past inputs (𝜏_ctx=0.1) to enhance robustness against small errors.

**Imagination Training (Reinforcement Learning):**
1. **Behavior Cloning and Reward Model:** The pretrained world model is extended with task-specific MLP heads. "Agent tokens" are introduced into the transformer, receiving task embeddings. These tokens are used to predict actions (policy head) and scalar rewards (reward head) over a multi-token prediction (MTP) horizon (L=8). The reward head uses a symexp twohot output for robust stochastic reward learning.
2. **Reinforcement Learning with PMPO:** To improve beyond behaviors seen in the dataset, the policy and value heads are finetuned via RL in imagination, while the main world model transformer remains frozen.
 - **Imagined Rollouts:** Trajectories are generated by unrolling the world model, sampling representations from its flow head and actions from the policy head.
 - **Value Head:** Trained with temporal difference learning (TD-learning) to predict 𝜆-returns, using a symexp twohot output for robustness across value scales.
 - **Policy Optimization (PMPO):** Dreamer 4 utilizes **Preference Model Policy Optimization (PMPO)**. This robust RL objective focuses on the *sign* of advantages (𝑅_𝜆_𝑡 − 𝑣_𝑡), ignoring their magnitude. This ensures balanced learning from both positive and negative feedback without needing return normalization. It uses a reverse KL divergence to a behavioral prior (initialized from behavior cloning) to keep the learned policy within a reasonable action space.

**Efficient Transformer Architecture (shared by Tokenizer and Dynamics):**
- **Foundation:** A 2D transformer with time and space dimensions, employing causal attention in time. It includes standard components like pre-layer RMSNorm, RoPE (Rotary Position Embeddings), and SwiGLU activations.
- **Stability & Efficiency Enhancements:** QKNorm and attention logit soft capping are used for training stability. To speed up inference and training, the architecture incorporates:
 - **Factorized Attention:** Separate space-only and time-only attention layers, with temporal attention only applied every 4 layers to break down computational cost.
 - **Grouped Query Attention (GQA):** Reduces KV cache size, speeding up attention.
 - **Alternating Batch Lengths:** Speeds up initial training and helps with length generalization.
 - **Register Tokens:** Qualitatively improves temporal consistency.

By combining these innovations, Dreamer 4 builds a scalable, efficient, and accurate world model capable of supporting advanced reinforcement learning from offline data in complex visual environments.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Dreamer 4 presents several groundbreaking findings across challenging benchmarks:

1. **Offline Diamond Challenge in Minecraft:**
 - **First to obtain diamonds offline:** Dreamer 4 is the first agent to successfully acquire diamonds in the complex video game Minecraft purely from offline data, without any environment interaction, achieving a 0.7% success rate in 60-minute episodes. This is a significant milestone for offline reinforcement learning in complex, open-world environments.
 - **Superior performance:** It substantially outperforms previous state-of-the-art offline agents, including OpenAI's VPT (finetuned), which only achieved sticks in 53% of episodes, and VLA (Gemma 3), nearly tripling its success rate for crafting iron pickaxes.
 - **Imagination training efficacy:** Imagination-based RL consistently improved both the success rates and the efficiency (faster task completion) of the policy compared to behavioral cloning baselines, particularly for more challenging, long-horizon milestones.
 - **Implicit world understanding:** The world model's representations, even before imagination training, enabled a behavioral cloning policy (WM+BC) to outperform an agent using general representations from Gemma 3, indicating that video prediction implicitly learns valuable world knowledge for decision-making.

2. **Human Interaction and World Model Accuracy:**
 - **Accurate complex interactions:** The Dreamer 4 world model accurately predicts a wide range of object interactions and game mechanics in Minecraft, such as breaking/placing blocks, fighting monsters, using tools, and interacting with crafting interfaces. It successfully completes 14 out of 16 challenging human-designed tasks within its simulation.
 - **Real-time interactive inference:** Despite its complexity, the world model achieves real-time interactive inference (21 FPS, exceeding Minecraft's 20 FPS tick rate) on a single H100 GPU, a critical requirement for efficient imagination training.
 - **Extended context length:** Dreamer 4 offers a significantly longer context length (9.6 seconds) compared to previous Minecraft world models (0.8-1.6 seconds), leading to greater temporal consistency in its simulations.
 - **Outperforms prior models:** It demonstrably surpasses the prediction fidelity and task completion capabilities of previous Minecraft world models like Oasis and Lucid-v1, which either hallucinated or failed to simulate precise mechanics.
 - **Real-world applicability:** Qualitative results on a robotics dataset show accurate physics and counterfactual interactions, suggesting its potential for real-world robotic applications.

3. **Action Generalization:**
 - **Data efficiency in action conditioning:** Dreamer 4 learns accurate action conditioning from a surprisingly small amount of paired action data. With only 100 hours of labeled actions (out of 2541 hours of video), it achieves 85% PSNR and 100% SSIM compared to training with all actions, demonstrating its ability to absorb most of its knowledge from unlabeled videos.
 - **Out-of-distribution generalization:** Crucially, the action conditioning learned from the Minecraft Overworld (natural landscapes) generalizes effectively to the visually distinct Nether and End dimensions (e.g., lava-filled underworlds), achieving 76% PSNR and 80% SSIM relative to full action data. This is a novel demonstration of action grounding generalizing to entirely new visual scenarios known only from unlabeled videos.

4. **Model Design Ablations:**
 - **Shortcut forcing efficacy:** Shortcut forcing with just K=4 sampling steps achieved generation quality comparable to diffusion forcing with K=64 steps, resulting in a 16x speedup.
 - **x-prediction and x-space loss benefits:** Parameterizing the model to predict clean representations (x-prediction) and computing the loss in x-space, along with the ramp loss weight, significantly improved generation quality, especially for long sequences, by preventing accumulating high-frequency errors.
 - **Architectural contributions:** A cascade of architectural improvements (factorized attention, GQA, alternating batch lengths, register tokens, increased spatial tokens) collectively led to substantial gains in both inference speed and generation quality (reducing Frechét Video Distance from 306 to 57).

These findings collectively demonstrate Dreamer 4's unprecedented capability in learning complex behaviors from offline data using a scalable, efficient, and highly accurate world model.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

Dreamer 4 marks a significant advancement in the pursuit of intelligent agents and has far-reaching implications across multiple fields of AI:

1. **Breakthrough in Offline Reinforcement Learning:** By being the first to obtain diamonds in Minecraft purely from offline data, Dreamer 4 establishes a new benchmark and validates the power of imagination training for complex, long-horizon tasks without the need for costly and potentially unsafe online interaction. This is crucial for real-world applications such as robotics, autonomous driving, and industrial automation, where data collection is expensive and online learning poses safety risks.

2. **Scalable and Actionable World Models:** The development of a world model that is both highly accurate in simulating complex object interactions (Minecraft mechanics) and efficient enough for real-time inference on a single GPU is a major technical achievement. It addresses a long-standing challenge in bringing the fidelity of modern generative models to the practical demands of agent training, opening doors for simulating increasingly realistic and diverse environments.

3. **Efficient Knowledge Acquisition from Unlabeled Data:** The demonstration that Dreamer 4 can learn effective action conditioning from a minimal amount of labeled data, primarily leveraging vast quantities of unlabeled video, is transformative. This paves the way for agents to "learn by watching" from diverse web videos, drastically reducing the dependency on meticulously curated, action-annotated datasets. This paradigm shift could accelerate research in general-purpose AI and make intelligent agents more accessible to domains with limited labeled data.

4. **Foundation for Future AI Research:** Dreamer 4 provides a robust and performant starting point for exploring several critical future directions. These include integrating long-term memory for even more temporally consistent simulations, incorporating language understanding for more intuitive goal specification, leveraging small amounts of corrective online data for fine-tuning, and developing methods for automatic goal discovery in open-ended environments. The model's modular design and demonstrated capabilities make it an ideal testbed for these advanced investigations.

5. **Towards General Intelligent Agents:** This work represents a tangible step towards building intelligent agents that possess a deep, learned understanding of their environment, enabling them to generalize, plan, and execute complex behaviors effectively. By showcasing the power of imagination and data efficiency, Dreamer 4 contributes significantly to the long-term vision of creating versatile and adaptable AI systems capable of interacting intelligently with the world.

In summary, Dreamer 4 not only solves a formidable AI challenge in Minecraft but also provides a scalable, efficient, and data-efficient recipe for imagination training, with profound implications for how future intelligent agents will learn and operate in both simulated and real-world environments.
