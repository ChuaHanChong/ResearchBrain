# RoboDesign1M: A Large-scale Dataset for Robot Design Understanding

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from FPT Software AI Center and the University of Liverpool developed RoboDesign1M, a large-scale multimodal dataset comprising 1 million image-text pairs extracted from scientific literature, aimed at training and enabling foundation models for robot design. Experiments demonstrated that models finetuned on RoboDesign1M showed up to 62% improvement in cross-dataset VQA generalization and reduced the FID score for text-to-design image generation from 45.83 to 39.42.

## Problem

- A significant lack of large-scale, specialized datasets for robot design hinders the application and development of foundation models in this domain.
- Existing engineering datasets are often generic, small-scale, or primarily focused on 3D CAD models without rich textual descriptions or multimodal instruction-following data.
- General-purpose foundation models struggle to understand and contribute to the complex, interdisciplinary process of robot design due to the scarcity of relevant training data.

## Method

- The authors created **RoboDesign1M**, a dataset of approximately 1 million multimodal samples (2D images/drawings and associated text) specifically for robot design, sourced from scientific literature.
- A semi-automated pipeline was developed for data acquisition from scientific documents, utilizing robotics-specific keywords and PDF content extraction tools.
- A robust filtering process including an ensemble image classifier for relevance and a text-verified k-==Nearest-Neighbor== method for deduplication was implemented, alongside LLM-driven construction of 1.3 million visual instruction-following (Q&A) pairs.

## Results

- Models finetuned on RoboDesign1M achieved 15-**62%** higher cross-dataset generalization in Visual Question Answering (VQA) when evaluated on Text2CAD and Ghezelbash datasets.
- In text-to-design image generation, finetuning Stable Diffusion XL on RoboDesign1M reduced the Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) from **45.83** to **39.42**, indicating improved generation quality.
- Models finetuned on RoboDesign1M demonstrated superior cross-dataset performance in text-image retrieval and received higher ratings in a user study for text-image alignment in generated designs.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Sourcing data from scientific literature, combined with careful filtering, ensures high reliability and domain-specificity, addressing technical accuracy for robot design.
> - Automatically generating visual instruction-following data using LLMs significantly enhances the dataset's utility for training modern multi-modal models.
> - A large, diverse, and domain-specific multimodal dataset can significantly improve the generalization capabilities of AI models across various robot design understanding tasks.
