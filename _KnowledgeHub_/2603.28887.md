# OccSim: Multi-kilometer Simulation with Long-horizon Occupancy World Models

> [!summary] Summary
> OccSim introduces a data-driven generative simulator for autonomous driving, utilizing a long-horizon occupancy world model with a novel W-DiT block to stably generate multi-kilometer 3D occupancy maps. This approach overcomes previous generation length limitations, creating data that improves downstream perception model training by up to 22.1% relative in mIoU compared to asset-based simulators.

## Problem

- Existing autonomous driving simulators face fundamental trade-offs among realism, content diversity, and interactivity, often relying on pre-recorded driving logs or HD maps which limit scalability.
- Current state-of-the-art occupancy world models are limited to short stable generation horizons (typically fewer than 50 frames), making them insufficient for constructing multi-kilometer maps or entire city blocks.
- Achieving 3D-consistent static environment generation and populating it with reactive, diverse dynamic agents remains a challenge, as many generative models suffer from 'memoryless visual hallucination'.

## Method

- **OccSim** employs a ==decoupled strategy==, modeling simulation as two generative tasks: multi-kilometer rollout for static scenes and conditional generation of dynamic traffic flow.
- A novel ==W-DiT (Warp-DiT)== block is used for long-horizon static ==occupancy generation==, incorporating explicit ==3D rigid transformations== and a ==mask-injected conditioning== mechanism to bound geometric error accumulation.
- A ==two-pass keyframe-based fusion== heuristic and a robust ==topology extraction== algorithm construct consistent, multi-kilometer road maps, while a compact ==Diffusion Transformer (DiT-S)== generates plausible initial agent layouts.

## Results

- **OccSim** demonstrated stable generation of over **3,000** continuous frames, constructing large-scale 3D maps spanning more than **4 kilometers**, representing an **80-fold** improvement in stable generation length over previous state-of-the-art models.
- The simulator exhibited higher generation diversity (measured by Vendi Scores) and maintained superior structural realism and stability over long-term rollouts compared to prior generative baselines.
- Data collected from **OccSim** enabled 4D semantic occupancy forecasting models to achieve up to **67%** zero-shot performance on unseen data, outperforming models trained on an asset-based simulator (CARLA) by **22.1%** relative in average mIoU when scaled fivefold.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly leveraging 3D geometric priors and rigid transformations within a world model is crucial for achieving ultra-long-horizon stable generation of static environments, preventing rapid error accumulation seen in prior models.
> - A decoupled approach that separately models static environment dynamics and dynamic agent behaviors allows for efficient construction of large-scale, consistent worlds and flexible traffic flow generation.
> - A novel SNR-scaled perception loss, combined with dual-head injection in the W-DiT architecture, effectively refines fine-grained geometric details and enhances robustness for long autoregressive rollouts.
