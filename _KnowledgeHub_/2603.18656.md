# Balanced Thinking: Improving Chain of Thought Training in Vision Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> IBM Research introduces SCALe-SFT, a supervised fine-tuning framework for Vision-Language Models that enhances multimodal reasoning by addressing token imbalance in reasoning-oriented data. This method employs a scheduled adaptive loss, leading to improved answer accuracy and more structured reasoning outputs, with gains of up to 3% over vanilla SFT on various benchmarks.

## Problem

- Standard Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) for Vision-Language Models (VLMs) uses a uniform cross-entropy loss, which creates an optimization imbalance in reasoning-oriented tasks.
- Reasoning segments (``) are typically much longer than critical answer segments (``), causing models to prioritize verbose reasoning over factual precision.
- This imbalance can lead to models generating stylistically fluent but factually inaccurate reasoning traces, potentially undermining the effectiveness of subsequent reinforcement learning (RL) phases.

## Method

- **SCALe-SFT** (Scheduled Curriculum Adaptive Loss Supervised Fine-Tuning) replaces the standard ==uniform cross-entropy loss== with a dynamic, ==segment-aware weighting strategy==.
- A ==segment-aware loss== computes the mean ==cross-entropy== independently for reasoning and answer spans, ensuring each segment contributes equally to the loss regardless of its token length.
- A ==cosine annealing schedule== dynamically adjusts the weight assigned to reasoning tokens (decaying from 1.0 to 0.5) while keeping answer token weight constant (1.0), forming a ==curriculum== that shifts focus from reasoning structure to answer accuracy.

## Results

- SCALe-SFT consistently achieved higher answer accuracy than vanilla SFT across models and datasets, with improvements up to **3%** (e.g., LLaVA-v1.6-Mistral-7B on ScienceQA at **82.6%** vs **80.17%**).
- SCALe-SFT alone often outperformed or matched the full SFT+GRPO pipeline, while requiring approximately **one-seventh** of the training time, demonstrating its computational efficiency as a lightweight alternative.
- When used as initialization for GRPO (SCALe-GRPO), it consistently yielded the highest overall performance, with gains ranging from **0.87%** to **5.4%** over vanilla GRPO (e.g., Gemma-3-4B on IconQA: **67.8%** vs **64.33%**).
- SCALe-SFT substantially reduced invalid generations (missing `` tags) by **8.62%** to **87.40%** compared to vanilla SFT, with SCALe-GRPO nearly eliminating such structural inconsistencies.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly rebalancing the loss between verbose reasoning segments and concise answer segments during SFT significantly improves a VLM's ability to generate accurate and coherent multimodal reasoning.
> - A dynamic curriculum schedule that gradually shifts training focus from reasoning structure to answer precision proves more effective than fixed weighting for establishing robust reasoning capabilities.
> - Encouraging explicit termination of reasoning (``) and adherence to answer tags through segment grouping in the loss calculation helps produce structurally consistent and well-formed model outputs.
