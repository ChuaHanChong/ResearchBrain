# ProVision: Programmatically Scaling Vision-centric Instruction Data for Multimodal Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from the University of Washington and Salesforce Research developed PROVISION, a programmatic system for generating over 10 million high-quality, vision-centric instruction data points for multimodal language models. This data significantly improved MLM performance on various vision-centric benchmarks, showing up to an 8% increase in specific tasks.

## Problem

- Multimodal Language Models (MLMs) lack sufficient high-quality, vision-centric instruction data for effective training.
- Existing data generation methods using Large Language Models (LLMs) or MLMs are prone to hallucinations, lack interpretability, are expensive, and may have licensing issues.
- MLMs often struggle with fine-grained visual reasoning tasks like object counting, spatial relations, and depth estimation.

## Method

- PROVISION introduces a programmatic pipeline that leverages ==augmented scene graphs==—structured symbolic representations of images including objects, attributes, relations, segmentation, and depth.
- Human-written ==instruction generators==, comprising 24 single-image and 14 multi-image types with hundreds of templates, systematically synthesize diverse question-answer pairs from these scene graphs.
- An automated pipeline integrates state-of-the-art models (e.g., YOLO-world, SAM-2, LLaVA-1.5-13B, Osprey, Depth Anything V2) to generate ==augmented scene graphs== for images lacking manual annotations, enabling large-scale data creation.

## Results

- Instruction tuning LLaVA-1.5-7B with single-image PROVISION data showed up to a **7%** improvement on CVBench's 2D split and **8%** on its 3D split, along with a **3%** increase on QBench2, RealWorldQA, and MMMU.
- Multi-image PROVISION data led to an **8%** improvement in LLaVA-1.5-7B performance on the Mantis-Eval benchmark.
- Incorporating PROVISION data into both pre-training and instruction tuning stages of xGen-MM-4B models resulted in the best overall performance, averaging a **1.6%** improvement across 11 benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Programmatic data generation offers enhanced interpretability, factual accuracy, scalability, and cost-effectiveness compared to purely generative model-based approaches.
> - While data derived from human-annotated scene graphs generally performs better, large quantities of model-generated scene graph data can achieve comparable performance for single-image tasks.
> - Optimal MLM performance is achieved through a synergistic approach, incorporating PROVISION data in both pre-training and instruction tuning stages, and utilizing a mix of multiple-choice and short-answer formats.
