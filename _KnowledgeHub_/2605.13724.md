# AnyFlow: Any-Step Video Diffusion Model with On-Policy Flow Map Distillation

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA and National University of Singapore researchers developed AnyFlow, a two-stage distillation framework that enables video diffusion models to generate high-quality content across a flexible range of inference steps. This approach, which leverages flow maps and on-policy distillation, continuously improves video quality as sampling steps increase, achieving performance comparable to non-distilled models at significantly fewer NFEs and surpassing previous consistency-based methods.

## Problem

- Existing video diffusion models often have fixed inference step budgets, limiting user flexibility in balancing latency and output quality.
- Prior few-step distillation methods, such as consistency models, suffer from performance degradation when more sampling steps are used, failing to provide reliable "any-step" generation.
- Consistency distillation introduces cumulative bias by repeatedly re-noising intermediate states, causing sampling trajectories to diverge from the ideal Probability-Flow ODE path.

## Method

- A two-stage training pipeline converts a pretrained video diffusion model into an "any-step" flow map model, capable of learning transitions between arbitrary time points (z_t "to" z_r).
- The first stage, Forward Flow Map Training, adapts MeanFlow with interpolated timestep conditioning, a reweighted time sampler, guidance-fused training, and adaptive loss reweighting.
- The second stage, On-Policy Flow Map Distillation, refines the model using a novel Flow Map Backward Simulation strategy that efficiently decomposes rollout trajectories via shortcut transitions, mitigating test-time errors and exposure bias.

## Results

- AnyFlow demonstrates robust "any-step" scaling, with performance that matches or surpasses consistency models in the few-step regime and consistently improves with increased NFEs, unlike previous methods that show degradation.
- Achieved VBench scores of 84.04 (bidirectional T2V) and 84.05 (causal T2V) at 4 NFEs for 14B models, with quality further improving to 84.10 and 84.41 respectively at 32 NFEs.
- Qualitative and quantitative evaluations showed AnyFlow outperforming competing methods (e.g., rCM, Krea-Realtime, FastVideo) in T2V and I2V tasks, producing videos with higher visual quality, more realistic motion, and improved temporal stability.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Shifting the distillation target from endpoint-focused consistency mapping (z_t 
"to" z_0) to generalized flow-map transition learning (z_t 
"to" z_r) inherently supports variable step sizes and adaptable inference budgets.
> - Flow Map Backward Simulation enables efficient distribution matching distillation by leveraging the composition property of flow maps, allowing the simulation of different inference step budgets with consistent computational cost during training.
> - AnyFlow preserves the instantaneous flow field of the base model, enabling continued fine-tuning on downstream datasets, a capability often lost in highly distilled models.
