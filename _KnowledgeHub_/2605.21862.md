# EvoScene-VLA: Evolving Scene Beliefs Inside the Action Decoder for Chunked Robot Control

> [!summary] Summary
> EvoScene-VLA enhances chunked robot control by integrating a mechanism within the action decoder to evolve scene beliefs based on the robot's own actions and correct them with new observations. This approach yielded improved success rates of 88.5% in randomized simulations and 42.0% in real-robot cleaning tasks, showing gains of +2.4% and +4.7% respectively over baselines.

## Problem

- Current chunked Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies often base multi-step action predictions solely on initial visual observations, leading to "stale targets" when robot actions change the scene mid-chunk.
- Robot policies typically lack a persistent, action-informed record of how their actions transform the environment, forcing continuous re-inference from potentially partial or noisy visual evidence.
- This limitation reduces robustness and can cause execution failures in dynamic manipulation environments where the robot's interactions modify the scene.

## Method

- **EvoScene-VLA** extends a VLA framework by introducing a recurrent latent scene interface, using "observation slots" for current image information and "prior slots" to carry an action-updated scene state from previous chunks.
- The action decoder performs joint denoising, simultaneously predicting the next action chunk and a corresponding "scene chunk," which then becomes the prior for the subsequent control call.
- During training, a two-level Geometric Anchor (local depth reconstruction and global 3D Foundation Model distillation) grounds scene slots in 3D structure, and a Scene Predictor generates future scene-token targets, both of which are discarded at inference.

## Results

- In RoboTwin simulations, EvoScene-VLA achieved an average success rate of **89.1%** (+**1.9%** over baseline) in "Clean" settings and **88.5%** (+**2.4%** over baseline) in "Rand" (randomized initial conditions) settings.
- On real-robot cleaning tasks using the Galaxea R1-Lite platform, EvoScene-VLA improved the average success rate to **42.0%**, representing a +**4.7%** gain over the LingBot-VLA* baseline.
- Ablation studies confirmed that all proposed components, including the local depth anchor, global 3D foundation model anchor, Scene Predictor, and the recurrent prior, cumulatively contributed to the observed performance improvements.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Maintaining an action-updated and persistent scene representation across control chunks is crucial for robust robot control in dynamic environments where actions induce scene changes.
> - The action expert itself can be trained through co-denoising to produce the action-updated scene prior at inference time, integrating scene evolution directly into the control loop without needing separate online world models.
> - Grounding abstract scene latent tokens in concrete 3D structure through a two-level geometric anchoring mechanism ensures the recurrent scene state encodes meaningful geometric information relevant to manipulation.
