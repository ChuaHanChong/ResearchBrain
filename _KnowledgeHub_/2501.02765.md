# Visual Large Language Models for Generalized and Specialized Applications

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey organizes the rapidly expanding field of Visual Large Language Models (VLLMs), categorizing their diverse applications into vision-to-text, vision-to-action, and text-to-vision. It also details ethical considerations, inherent challenges, and future research directions for responsible development.

## Problem

- The lack of a comprehensive, application-centric overview for the rapidly evolving field of Visual Large Language Models (VLLMs).
- A need to systematically identify and address the ethical implications and technical challenges inherent in VLLM development and deployment.
- The absence of a structured roadmap to guide future research and foster innovation in VLLM capabilities and applications.

## Method

- Developed a ==hierarchical taxonomy== to categorize VLLM applications across ==vision-to-text, vision-to-action, and text-to-vision== modalities.
- Conducted a systematic literature review discussing representative models, techniques, and datasets within each application category and subcategory.
- Included dedicated sections analyzing ==ethical considerations== and technical challenges, and proposing concrete future research directions for VLLMs.

## Results

- The survey extensively mapped VLLM applications, classifying them into vision-to-text (e.g., VQA, video conversation), vision-to-action (e.g., autonomous driving, embodied AI), and text-to-vision (e.g., image, **3D**, video generation).
- Identified key ethical concerns such as labor market displacement and bias amplification from training data, alongside technical hurdles including efficiency limitations, poor interpretability and explainability, hallucination, and insufficient spatial understanding.
- Proposed future research avenues focusing on security and privacy, efficiency enhancements, improved interpretability, complex reasoning, and the exploration of new application domains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - VLLMs represent a paradigm shift from previous VLMs by leveraging the generative and reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models through instruction-tuning, enabling broader applications.
> - VLLM applications are highly diverse, spanning general-purpose tasks like image captioning to specialized domains such as autonomous driving, medical diagnostics, and embodied AI.
> - Critical challenges, including high computational costs, lack of interpretability, frequent hallucinations, and limitations in spatial understanding, must be addressed for VLLM maturation.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The development of multimodal AI has seen a rapid evolution, moving from specialized models to more general-purpose systems. This survey paper meticulously chronicles this evolution and positions Visual Large Language Models (VLLMs) at the forefront of the current research wave.

Historically, "Conventional VLMs" (prior to 2018) were typically encoder-decoder architectures based on CNNs and RNNs, designed for limited tasks like image captioning and retrieval. These models were constrained by small datasets and architectural simplicity. The period from 2018 to 2022 saw the emergence of "Emerging VLMs," driven by the Transformer architecture and large-scale pre-training on vision-language datasets (e.g., BERT, CLIP). These models introduced stronger zero-shot and transfer learning capabilities but remained primarily discriminative, lacking the multi-task and strong reasoning abilities of generative language models.

The current era, starting around 2022, is defined by "VLLMs," which leverage the powerful generative and reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) through instruction-tuning. VLLMs typically consist of a vision encoder, a projector to align visual tokens with language space, and an LLM acting as a decoder to generate instruction-following responses. This paradigm shift has enabled VLLMs to tackle a much broader array of generalized and specialized applications.

This survey paper distinguishes itself from prior reviews by offering a comprehensive application-centric perspective. While previous surveys have focused on general VLMs, specific methodologies, reasoning, interpretability, or evaluation benchmarks, this work specifically "provides a holistic perspective on VLLM applications, categorizing them into three main aspects: vision-to-text, vision-to-action, and text-to-vision." It goes beyond mere technical categorization to include an in-depth analysis of using scenarios, ethical considerations, challenges, and future directions. This broad scope, particularly its emphasis on the diverse *applications* across various vision modalities (image, video, depth) and action, sets it apart as a timely and crucial resource for researchers navigating this fast-paced field.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind this comprehensive survey are clearly articulated in the abstract and introduction:

- **Provide a Comprehensive Application Perspective:** The authors note that despite significant progress, the literature on VLLMs remains limited, especially concerning their diverse applications. The core motivation is to synthesize existing knowledge on VLLM applications, covering generalized and specialized use cases across various modalities (image, video, depth, action, and language).
- **Examine Using Scenarios:** To offer practical insights, the survey aims to detail how VLLMs are being deployed and utilized in different contexts.
- **Identify Ethical Considerations and Challenges:** Recognizing the rapid deployment and societal impact of VLLMs, the authors are motivated to explore the ethical implications (e.g., bias, labor market impact) and inherent technical challenges (e.g., efficiency, interpretability, hallucination, spatial understanding) that must be addressed for responsible development.
- **Discuss Future Directions:** By analyzing current limitations and emerging trends, the survey seeks to propose promising avenues for future research and development, guiding the community toward impactful innovations.
- **Serve as a Comprehensive Guide:** Ultimately, the authors intend for this survey to be a valuable resource for researchers, providing a structured overview that "will pave the way for future innovations and broader applications of VLLMs."

In essence, the motivation stems from the need to organize and illuminate the rapidly expanding, yet fragmented, landscape of VLLM applications, providing both a current snapshot and a roadmap for future progress.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The authors employ a structured and systematic approach to survey the vast landscape of Visual Large Language Models (VLLMs). Their methodology is characterized by:

- **Taxonomy-Driven Organization:** The core of their approach is a hierarchical taxonomy that categorizes VLLM applications into three primary types:
 - **Vision-to-text:** Models that process visual input (image, video) and generate textual output.
 - **Vision-to-action:** Models that interpret visual input and generate actions or control signals (e.g., for autonomous driving, embodied AI, tool management).
 - **Text-to-vision:** Models that take textual input and generate visual content (e.g., images, 3D models, videos).
 Each of these primary categories is further broken down into detailed subtasks and specific domain applications, allowing for a fine-grained analysis of VLLM capabilities.

- **Systematic Literature Review:** Within each category and subcategory, the authors discuss representative models, techniques, and datasets. They trace the architectural evolution and highlight key advancements, often comparing how VLLMs surpass conventional or emerging VLMs in specific tasks (e.g., improved reasoning, multi-task capabilities, handling complex instructions).

- **Emphasis on Instruction-Tuning:** A recurring theme across the survey is the significance of instruction-tuning and the "Pre-training, Fine-tuning, and Prediction" paradigm, particularly how it enables LLMs to process versatile instructions and generate aligned responses for multimodal tasks.

- **Inclusion of Ethical, Challenge, and Future Work Analysis:** Beyond technical aspects, the survey dedicates significant sections to broader implications. It systematically identifies ethical concerns (bias, labor market impact), articulates key challenges (efficiency, interpretability, hallucination, spatial understanding), and proposes concrete future research directions (security & privacy, efficiency, interpretability, complex reasoning, new application domains). This holistic perspective ensures that the survey addresses not only *what* VLLMs can do but also *how* they should be developed and deployed responsibly.

- **Provision of Supplementary Resources:** The authors provide a public repository (https://github.com/JackYFL/awesome-VLLMs) listing the papers discussed, indicating an commitment to maintain an updated and accessible resource for the community.

This methodical organization, coupled with a critical analysis of current state-of-the-art models and a forward-looking perspective on challenges and ethics, forms the robust methodological backbone of the survey.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The survey provides an extensive overview of VLLM applications, categorized by their input and output modalities, alongside a critical examination of their societal implications and technical hurdles.

#### 4.1. VLLM Application Categories

- **Vision-to-text:** This is the most explored area.
 - **Image-to-text:**
 - **General Domain:** Covers fundamental tasks like image captioning, Visual Question Answering (VQA), and visual dialogue, emphasizing multi-stage training (pre-training for feature alignment, supervised fine-tuning for instruction following). Also includes Referring Expression Segmentation (RES), Referring Expression Comprehension (REC), Optical Character Recognition (OCR) (with a focus on high-resolution data handling), and Retrieval tasks (single-stage vs. multi-stage).
 - **Specific Domain:** Highlights the need for specialized training and datasets for domains like Remote Sensing (e.g., RS-LLaVA, EarthGPT), Medical (Med-VQA, Medical Report Generation, e.g., LLaVA-Med, RaDialog), Science and Mathematics (Science VQA, visual mathematics, e.g., T-SciQ, G-LLaVA), Graphic Design and UI Understanding (e.g., CogAgent, Ferret-UI), and Financial Analysis (e.g., FinVis-GPT, FinTral).
 - **Video-to-text:** Addresses more complex challenges due to temporal dynamics.
 - **General-purpose Video Understanding:** Focuses on models leveraging large-scale pre-training for tasks like video captioning, activity recognition, and event retrieval (e.g., VideoBERT, VideoLLaMA). Highlights the challenge of long-form video encoding and solutions like temporal frame selection (LongVU).
 - **Video Conversation:** Explores conversational agents that perform multi-round reasoning on video content (e.g., Video-LLaVA, VideoChat).
 - **Egocentric Understanding:** Focuses on first-person view understanding crucial for assisted living and robotics, using LLMs for action recognition, anticipation, and program generation (e.g., GPT4Ego, LEAP).

- **Vision-to-action:** Deals with VLLMs generating actions based on visual and linguistic inputs.
 - **Autonomous Driving (AD):** VLLMs enhance AD systems' perception, planning, and prediction capabilities.
 - **Perception:** Scene understanding, object localization, and visual reasoning (e.g., NuScenes-QA, DriveLM, LiDAR-LLM).
 - **Planning:** Generating driving maneuvers, control signals, and hierarchical plans (e.g., ADriver-I, DriveVLM, DriveGPT4).
 - **Prediction:** Forecasting trajectories and actions of other entities (e.g., PromptTrack, BEV-InMLLM).
 - **Embodied AI:** VLLMs for controlling robots in physical environments.
 - **Perception:** Extracting semantic knowledge from RGB/LiDAR data (e.g., OpenEQA, 3D-LLM).
 - **Manipulation:** Object handling based on task requirements (e.g., PaLM-E, RT-X, VoxPoser).
 - **Planning:** Decomposing high-level objectives into subtasks (e.g., NLMap, ELLM, ConceptGraphs).
 - **Navigation:** Interpreting instructions and navigating complex environments (e.g., LM-Nav, MultiPLY, EMMA).
 - **Automated Tool Management:** Leveraging VLLMs to autonomously use external tools (APIs) to perform complex tasks. Tools can include pure vision models, VLMs, web search APIs, and software libraries. Architectures range from integrated to modular and self-instructing systems, also noting trajectory synthesis and self-exploration approaches.

- **Text-to-vision:** Focuses on VLLMs' creative generation of visual content from text.
 - **Text-to-image:** Covers image generation (crafting visuals from prompts, e.g., GILL, SEED), image editing (modifying images based on descriptions, e.g., Kosmos-G, CoDi-2), image synthesis (blending elements from multiple inputs), and visual story generation.
 - **Text-to-3D:** Converts text into 3D models and environments. Includes 3D scene generation and editing (e.g., LI3D, 3D-GPT) and mesh generation (e.g., MeshGPT).
 - **Text-to-video:** Transforms narratives into video sequences. Covers video generation and editing (e.g., LVD, VideoDrafter, Mora, SVD), emphasizing visual quality and consistency.

#### 4.2. Ethics Considerations

- **Impact on Labor Markets:** VLLMs automate tasks, potentially displacing jobs in creative design, customer service, and medical diagnostics, but also creating new roles in AI oversight and collaboration.
- **Bias:** VLLMs inherit and amplify biases from training data (sampling, gender/racial, lingual biases), leading to unfair or discriminatory outputs, particularly in high-stakes applications.

#### 4.3. Challenges

- **Efficiency:** High computational costs for both training (e.g., DeepSeek-V3 costing millions of dollars and significant energy) and inference, limiting real-time deployment and edge device compatibility.
- **Interpretability and Explainability:** The complexity of VLLMs makes it difficult to understand their internal decision-making processes, hindering trust and transparency.
- **Hallucination:** Generating semantically plausible but factually incorrect or irrelevant outputs, posing risks in critical applications like medical diagnostics.
- **Spatial Understanding:** Struggles with relational reasoning, egocentric visual data, and view transformations, which are critical for embodied AI and autonomous driving.

#### 4.4. Future Work

- **Security & Privacy:** Focus on data anonymization, homomorphic encryption, adversarial attack robustness, regulatory frameworks, and secure model deployment.
- **Efficiency:** Research into token reduction, data distillation, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning for training, and dynamic token pruning and KV cache optimization for inference.
- **Interpretability & Explainability:** Analyzing VLLMs from data (visualization, causal inference), model (tokens, embeddings, neurons, layers), and training & inference (pre-training, alignment, CoT, ICL) perspectives.
- **Complex Reasoning:** Enhancing abilities through techniques like Chain-of-Thought (CoT), Tree-of-Thought (ToT), visual prompting, and tree search.
- **Other Applications:** Exploring new domains like facial affective behavior analysis, anomaly detection, and gaming, requiring specialized data and domain knowledge.

It clarifies the state-of-the-art across diverse modalities and tasks, helping new entrants quickly grasp the field and guiding experienced researchers toward unexplored or challenging areas.
- **Highlighting Interdisciplinary Connections:** The survey effectively demonstrates how VLLMs are bridging traditional divides between computer vision, natural language processing, robotics, and other specialized domains like medical imaging or remote sensing. This can foster interdisciplinary collaboration and lead to novel solutions that leverage the strengths of various AI subfields.
- **Emphasizing Responsible AI Development:** The dedicated sections on ethical considerations and challenges are particularly impactful. By explicitly addressing issues like bias, labor market disruption, efficiency, and interpretability, the authors contribute to a crucial conversation about the responsible development and deployment of powerful VLLM technologies. This proactive approach is vital for building trust and ensuring that VLLMs serve societal good.
- **Identifying Critical Research Gaps:** The detailed discussion of challenges (e.g., hallucination, spatial understanding, efficiency) and future directions offers clear targets for future research. This can stimulate innovation by pointing out the most pressing technical and theoretical problems that need to be solved to unlock the full potential of VLLMs.
- **Accelerating Innovation:** By synthesizing existing knowledge and providing an accessible overview, the survey reduces the entry barrier for new researchers and developers. The accompanying online repository further enhances this by offering a curated list of relevant papers, acting as a living resource for the community. This accelerates the pace of innovation by allowing researchers to build upon a well-structured foundation.
- **Informing Policy and Industry:** The insights into VLLM capabilities, limitations, and ethical implications can inform policymakers about the societal shifts brought by AI.
