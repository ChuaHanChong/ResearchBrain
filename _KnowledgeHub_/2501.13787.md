# Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning for Foundation Models

> [!summary] Summary
> Tsinghua University researchers present the first comprehensive analysis of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods across different foundation model types, providing crucial insights into technique selection and effectiveness while establishing a unified framework for understanding and advancing this rapidly evolving field.

## Problem

- Lack of systematic understanding of PEFT methods across different foundation model types
- Need for efficient adaptation of large foundation models without extensive computational resources

## Method

- Comprehensive categorization of ==PEFT techniques into five main categories==
- Systematic analysis of applications across ==language, vision, and multimodal domains==
- Development of ==evaluation framework== for PEFT method selection

## Results

- **First** comprehensive survey covering PEFT applications across all major foundation model types
- Identification of key trends and opportunities in the field
- Development of structured framework for evaluating and selecting PEFT techniques
- Clear roadmap for future research directions in parameter-efficient model adaptation

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Different PEFT methods show varying effectiveness across model types and tasks
> - LLMs and VFMs dominate current research while MFMs remain underexplored
> - Hybrid approaches combining multiple PEFT techniques show promising results

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

- This work addresses a critical challenge in the field of AI: how to efficiently adapt large foundation models for specific tasks without extensive computational resources
- The research builds on the growing trend of parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods
- The paper provides a comprehensive survey of PEFT techniques across different types of foundation models (FMs)

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

Primary objectives:
- Provide a comprehensive overview of PEFT techniques applied to diverse foundation models
- Address critical gaps in understanding techniques, trends, and applications
- Systematically review key categories and core mechanisms of PEFT
- Explore recent applications across various FMs
- Identify potential research and development directions

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The paper takes a systematic review approach by:
- Analyzing various PEFT methods across five categories:
 - Selective PEFT
 - Additive PEFT
 - Prompt PEFT
 - Reparameterization PEFT
 - Hybrid PEFT
- Examining applications across different model types:
 - Large Language Models (LLMs)
 - Vision Foundation Models (VFMs)
 - Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
 - Multi-Modal Foundation Models (MFMs)

### 4. Main Findings and Results

Key findings include:
- The field shows remarkable growth across language, vision, and multimodal domains
- LLMs and VFMs dominate current research with rapid growth
- MFMs remain relatively underexplored, presenting opportunities for future research
- Different PEFT methods show varying effectiveness across different model types and tasks

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The research provides:
- A valuable resource for both newcomers and experts in the field
- Comprehensive understanding of PEFT methods and their applications
- Framework for evaluating and selecting appropriate PEFT techniques
- Guidance for future research directions in the field

This survey represents a significant contribution to the field by providing a structured understanding of PEFT methods and their applications across different types of foundation models, while also identifying promising areas for future research.
