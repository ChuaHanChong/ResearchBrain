# HY-World 2.0: A Multi-Modal World Model for Reconstructing, Generating, and Simulating 3D Worlds

> [!summary] Summary
> Tencent Hunyuan's HY-World 2.0 introduces an open-source, multi-modal framework that unifies 3D world generation and reconstruction. This system creates high-fidelity, navigable 3D Gaussian Splatting worlds from text or images and accurately recovers 3D structures from multi-view inputs, offering competitive performance against state-of-the-art models and facilitating interactive exploration.

## Problem

- Existing 3D world modeling solutions typically specialized in either synthesizing new scenes or accurately reconstructing existing structures.
- Generative approaches often lacked geometric precision, while reconstruction methods could not synthesize novel content or unseen regions.
- The open-source community lacked a comprehensive, multi-modal framework capable of unifying both 3D world generation and precise physical reconstruction.

## Method

- **HY-World 2.0** introduces a systematic ==four-stage pipeline== (==Panorama Generation==, ==Trajectory Planning==, ==World Expansion==, ==World Composition==) that integrates diverse multi-modal inputs.
- For sparse inputs like text or single images, the framework synthesizes high-fidelity, navigable ==3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)== worlds.
- For richer visual observations such as multi-view images or videos, the system performs ==geometrically consistent== and accurate 3D structure recovery using **WorldStereo 2.0** and **WorldMirror 2.0**.

## Results

- HY-World 2.0 generates comprehensive multi-modal 3D assets, including high-fidelity 3DGS worlds, within approximately **10 minutes**, demonstrating higher fidelity and sharper textures compared to closed-source models like Marble.
- Individual components achieve leading performance; **HY-Pano 2.0** secured top scores in panorama generation benchmarks, and **WorldStereo 2.0** achieved the highest point cloud **F1** and **AUC** scores in single-view scene reconstruction.
- **WorldMirror 2.0** improved 3D reconstruction quality across various metrics and resolutions, surpassing prior methods in surface normal estimation and exhibiting enhanced inference efficiency through parallelism and mixed-precision techniques.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A systematic multi-modal framework can successfully bridge the gap between imaginative 3D world generation and accurate physical reconstruction.
> - Component-level innovations, such as Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer for panorama generation and keyframe-based video generation with global and spatial memory, significantly enhance fidelity and consistency.
> - Tailored 3D Gaussian Splatting optimization, including adaptive densification and a probabilistic MaskGaussian model, effectively balances detail preservation with rendering efficiency and artifact suppression.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of world models has evolved to enable artificial intelligence agents to simulate, understand, and interact with three-dimensional (3D) environments. This capability is applied across various domains such as virtual reality, embodied robotics, and video games. Prior work in generative world models, including HY-World 1.0, focused on offline 3D-based world generation, producing explorable 3D worlds compatible with computer graphics pipelines. HY-World 1.5 advanced online video-based world generation, enabling real-time, interactive modeling driven by user actions.

Despite these advancements, the existing landscape of 3D world modeling has largely remained specialized. Solutions typically focus on either generation or reconstruction. Generative approaches, while capable of synthesizing scenes from sparse inputs like text or single images, often do not maintain strict reconstruction accuracy. Conversely, reconstruction methods aim to recover precise 3D structures from dense multi-view images or videos but lack the generative priors to synthesize unseen regions. While some closed-source commercial models, such as Marble, have demonstrated abilities to unify these tasks, a comprehensive, multi-modal foundational world model that bridges the gap between imaginative generation and accurate physical reconstruction has been absent in the open-source community. HY-World 2.0 addresses this by presenting an open-source, systematic multi-modal framework that unifies both generation and reconstruction within an offline 3D world model paradigm.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation for HY-World 2.0 stems from the recognized transformative potential of world models in AI and the existing limitations within the 3D world modeling research landscape. World models, by capturing physical and spatial dynamics, are crucial for applications requiring agents to simulate, understand, and interact with complex 3D environments. While previous HY-World projects laid foundations for 3D-based and video-based world generation, the research community faced a fundamental dichotomy: solutions typically specialized in either synthesizing novel 3D scenes (generation) or accurately recovering existing 3D structures (reconstruction). Generative models often lacked geometric precision, while reconstruction models could not hallucinate new content. The emergence of proprietary, unified models underscored this gap within the open-source domain.

Against this backdrop, the key objectives of HY-World 2.0 are detailed as follows:
- **Unified Paradigm:** To introduce the first open-source, systematic multi-modal world model that seamlessly unifies both "generation" and "reconstruction" capabilities within an offline 3D world model paradigm.
- **Multi-Modal Input Accommodation:** To design a framework capable of accommodating diverse input modalities, including text prompts, single-view images, multi-view images, and videos, adapting its behavior based on the available information.
- **High-Fidelity World Generation:** For sparse inputs (text or single-view images), the objective is to synthesize high-fidelity, navigable 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) worlds. This involves developing a novel four-stage pipeline: panorama generation, trajectory planning, world expansion, and world composition. A key aim is to leverage powerful generative priors from video diffusion models to expand exploratory spaces and enhance visual quality beyond previous models like HY-World 1.0.
- **Accurate World Reconstruction:** For richer visual observations (multi-view images or videos), the goal is to perform geometrically consistent and accurate 3D structure recovery. This reconstruction capability is also intended to serve as a foundational component within the overall world generation pipeline.
- **Component Enhancement and Innovation:** To systematically advance each core component of the framework:
 - **Panorama Generation (HY-Pano 2.0):** Scale up data and model capacity to enable adaptive perspective-to-equirectangular (ERP) transformations from arbitrary viewpoints.
 - **Trajectory Planning (WorldNav):** Introduce a scene-parsing enhanced algorithm to produce camera trajectories that maximize information and avoid obstacles.
 - **World Expansion (WorldStereo 2.0):** Upgrade the controllable video model by generating within a keyframe space, incorporating a more consistent and robust memory mechanism, and refining its acceleration.
 - **World Composition (WorldMirror 2.0):** Enhance the underlying 3D reconstruction model through architectural refinements (e.g., generalized position encoding), improved learning strategies, and tailored 3DGS training to bridge reconstruction with generative modeling.
- **Interactive Exploration Platform (WorldLens):** To introduce a high-performance 3DGS rendering platform featuring a flexible engine-agnostic architecture, automatic image-based lighting (IBL), efficient collision detection, and training-rendering co-design to enable interactive exploration of generated 3D worlds with character support.
- **Open-Source Release:** To release all model weights, code, and technical details to foster reproducibility, democratize spatial intelligence, and support further open research in 3D world models.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

HY-World 2.0 operates as a four-stage pipeline, designed to integrate diverse multi-modal inputs into navigable 3D worlds.

#### 3.1. Panorama Generation (HY-Pano 2.0)

This initial stage synthesizes a high-fidelity 360° panorama from text prompts or single-view images.
- **Data Curation:** The pipeline utilizes a large-scale dataset combining high-resolution real-world panoramas and synthetic assets rendered in Unreal Engine. A rigorous filtering process removes low-quality samples and artifacts.
- **Model:** Instead of explicit geometric warping, an implicit, adaptive mapping strategy is employed, powered by a Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer (MMDiT). This model processes conditional input and panoramic targets in a unified latent space, learning perspective-to-ERP transformations without requiring camera intrinsic parameters.
- **Seamlessness:** Circular padding is applied to latent features, and linear pixel blending is used at equirectangular edges to eliminate boundary artifacts and ensure a seamless 360° output.

#### 3.2. Trajectory Planning (WorldNav)

Following panorama generation, WorldNav derives camera trajectories to maximize coverage of navigable space and provide guidance for world expansion.
- **Geometric and Semantic Scene Parsing:** The panorama undergoes parsing to obtain a panoramic point cloud (via MoGe2 with enhanced sampling), panoramic mesh, semantic masks (Qwen3-VL and SAM3), and a NavMesh (Recast Navigation). Geometric refinements, such as ground snapping, boundary erosion, and bridge polygon synthesis, ensure physically plausible navigation.
- **Trajectory Modes:** Five heuristic trajectory modes are designed:
 - **Regular Trajectories:** Expand visual coverage beyond the origin, covering broader viewpoints with ray-casting for collision avoidance.
 - **Surrounding Trajectories:** Orbit significant objects, with adaptive radii, to enhance foreground detail, utilizing Dijkstra for smooth, collision-free paths.
 - **Reconstruct-Aware Trajectories:** Target under-observed regions (e.g., stretched mesh faces) to mitigate reconstruction gaps, using Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) and iterative orbiting.
 - **Wandering Trajectories:** Explore environmental boundaries to maximize scene coverage, particularly in narrow spaces, using Dijkstra distance fields.
 - **Aerial Trajectories:** Incorporate elevated viewpoints to eliminate blind spots, dynamically adjusting pitch to prevent collisions.

#### 3.3. World Expansion (WorldStereo 2.0)

This stage generates extensive novel views using camera-guided video generation, built upon WorldStereo 1.0, to expand the initialized world.
- **Keyframe-based Spatial Variational Autoencoder (Keyframe-VAE):** Unlike standard Video-VAEs that spatio-temporally compress, Keyframe-VAE performs spatial-only compression on sparse keyframes. This preserves high-frequency details, improving generation and reconstruction fidelity.
- **Explicit Camera Control:** WorldStereo 2.0 builds on a pre-trained video DiT, integrating a lightweight transformer-based camera adapter. It uses camera Plücker rays and point clouds as complementary guidance for precise camera control, with selective fine-tuning of the DiT backbone.
- **Memory Mechanism (Middle-Training):**
 - **Global-Geometric Memory (GGM):** Fine-tunes the model using videos rendered by extended global point clouds (beyond reference points) to internalize 360° environmental structures and ensure global consistency. Robust augmentation strategies (e.g., depth map degradation) are applied to improve robustness against imperfect point clouds.
 - **Improved Spatial-Stereo Memory (SSM++):** Directly incorporates retrieved keyframes into the main DiT branch by spatially stitching them with target views. It uses a modified Rotary Positional Embedding (RoPE) and implicit camera embeddings. Retrieval is selective and based on 3D FoV similarity. Augmentations like motion blur and color jitter are applied to retrieved frames.
- **Model Distillation (Post-Training):** A modified Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) is applied to accelerate inference, distilling WorldStereo 2.0 into a 4-step DiT, with full fine-tuning during distillation.

#### 3.4. World Composition (WorldMirror 2.0 & 3DGS Optimization)

This final stage integrates all inputs into a unified, navigable 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) representation.
- **World Reconstruction via WorldMirror 2.0:** WorldMirror 2.0, an upgraded feed-forward 3D reconstruction model, reconstructs globally aligned point clouds and depth maps from generated keyframes.
 - **Model Improvements:** Normalized Position Encoding (RoPE) replaces absolute coordinates with normalized ones for flexible multi-resolution inference. Explicit normal supervision for depth estimation (depth-to-normal loss) couples depth and normals geometrically, using ground-truth or pseudo-normals. A dedicated depth mask prediction head handles invalid pixels.
 - **Data and Efficiency:** Training data includes synthetic Unreal Engine renderings and normal-only pseudo-label enhancement for real-world datasets. Inference efficiency is improved via token/frame sequence parallelism, BF16 mixed-precision, and Fully Sharded Data Parallelism (FSDP).
 - **Training Strategy:** Employs a token-budget-first dynamic batch sizing and a three-stage curriculum learning pipeline, progressively training geometry heads, introducing depth-to-normal loss, and finally training the 3DGS head.
- **Point Cloud Expansion and Depth Alignment:** WorldMirror 2.0 outputs depth maps, which are then aligned with the initial panoramic point cloud (P_pan) using a RANSAC-based linear alignment to correct scale and shift ambiguities. This process involves rendering P_pan as guidance depth, defining a reliability mask based on multiple empirical masks, and implementing outlier detection for alignment coefficients. The aligned depths form an extended point cloud (P_ex), which is combined with P_pan and voxel-downsampled to form ˜P.
- **3D Gaussian Splatting Optimization:**
 - **Initialization:** The 3DGS model is initialized using the expanded point cloud ˜P, parameterized by opacity, position, and covariance. View-independent RGB colors are used instead of Spherical Harmonics for efficiency.
 - **Growth Strategy and MaskGaussian:** The initial point cloud is segmented into sky and scene subsets. Adaptive densification is applied exclusively to non-sky regions (˜P_scene) to prevent floaters. MaskGaussian probabilistically models Gaussian existence to prune redundant Gaussians in low-frequency areas and suppress artifacts, thereby accelerating rendering and improving detail reconstruction.
 - **Optimization and Losses:** The model is optimized using a photometric loss (L1, SSIM, LPIPS), a geometric loss (L1 on depth, cosine similarity on normal), a scale regularization term, and a mask regularization term. Depth supervision is applied sparsely, while normal maps provide dense geometric constraints.
 - **Mesh Extraction:** RGB and depth maps are rendered from training views and integrated into a Truncated Signed Distance Function (TSDF) volume. A mesh is extracted using the marching cube algorithm, followed by simplification and removal of disconnected components.

**WorldLens:** The framework also introduces WorldLens, a high-performance 3DGS rendering platform. It features an engine-agnostic architecture, automatic IBL lighting, efficient collision detection, and a training-rendering co-design to facilitate interactive exploration of 3D worlds with character support.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of HY-World 2.0 encompasses both its individual components and the integrated system across generation and reconstruction tasks, including comparisons with state-of-the-art open-source and closed-source models.

**World Generation from Text or Single Image:**
- **HY-Pano 2.0:** Quantitative evaluations (CLIP-T/I, Q-Align quality/aesthetics) demonstrated HY-Pano 2.0 achieved leading scores on text-to-panorama (T2P) and image-to-panorama (I2P) benchmarks compared to DiT360, Matrix3D, CubeDiff, GenEx, and HY-World 1.0. Qualitatively, it produced panoramas with superior structural coherence, plausible spatial arrangements, consistent geometry, color harmony, and finer details.
- **WorldNav:** Qualitative ablations showed that the sequential integration of different trajectory types (regular, surrounding, reconstruction-aware, wandering, aerial) progressively improved scene completeness, eliminated blind spots, refined complex object structures, and enhanced distant details, moving beyond the limitations of relying solely on panoramic views.
- **WorldStereo 2.0:**
 - **Single-View Scene Reconstruction:** Achieved the highest point cloud F1 and AUC scores on Tanks-and-Temples and MipNeRF360 datasets, surpassing video-based and 3D-based competitors (e.g., SEVA, Gen3C, Lyra, FlashWorld). This indicated successful synthesis of geometrically consistent 3D structures.
 - **Camera Control:** Demonstrated superior camera control capabilities with lower errors across rotation, translation, and absolute trajectory error metrics, along with higher visual quality and semantic alignment metrics compared to competing video-based models.
 - **Ablation Studies:** Confirmed that the Keyframe-VAE significantly improved visual quality and camera control. Freezing specific layers (cross-attention and FFN) in the DiT backbone achieved the optimal balance between precision and visual quality. The Global-Geometric Memory (GGM) and Improved Spatial-Stereo Memory (SSM++) mechanisms substantially enhanced photometric quality and multi-trajectory consistency. Post-distillation via DMD retained comparable camera control while improving photometric and consistency metrics.
- **World Composition:**
 - **Reconstruction and Alignment:** WorldMirror 2.0 achieved reconstruction quality comparable to video2world while being significantly faster (less than 2 minutes vs. 5 hours), attributing this to its lightweight linear alignment with camera pose priors. The 3DGS reconstructions exhibited superior geometric and textural details due to tailored optimization, favoring direct RGB optimization over Spherical Harmonics.
 - **Gaussian Splattings:** MaskGaussian effectively reduced the Gaussian count by 73.7% with minimal PSNR degradation, enhancing rendering efficiency without compromising detail. Restricting densification to non-sky regions effectively suppressed floating artifacts.
- **Full System Performance:** HY-World 2.0 generates comprehensive multi-modal 3D assets, including panoramas, aligned point clouds, high-fidelity 3DGS renderings, and extracted geometric meshes. These assets support interactive exploration with character support, collision detection, and physical feedback. Comparisons with the closed-source model Marble indicated that HY-World 2.0 achieved higher fidelity to input conditions, sharper textures, and superior geometric consistency in novel views, especially under large viewpoint changes where Marble exhibited blurring and geometric inconsistencies. The overall end-to-end generation of a complete 3D world takes approximately 10 minutes on NVIDIA H20 GPUs, benefiting from integrated efficiency optimizations.

**World Reconstruction from Multi-View Images or Video (WorldMirror 2.0 standalone):**
- **Resolution Generalization:** WorldMirror 2.0 consistently maintained or improved performance across low, medium, and high inference resolutions for point map reconstruction, camera pose estimation, depth estimation, novel view synthesis, and surface normal estimation. This contrasted with WorldMirror 1.0, which showed significant degradation at higher resolutions, thus validating the effectiveness of normalized position encoding.
- **Metric Performance:** WorldMirror 2.0 reduced point map reconstruction errors (e.g., 7-Scenes accuracy from 0.043 to 0.033 at medium resolution) and improved camera pose estimation (e.g., AUC@30 by over 20 points at high resolution). For depth estimation, it reduced AbsRel and achieved competitive δ<1.25 accuracy. In novel view synthesis, it maintained stable quality and achieved the best SSIM at high resolution. For surface normal estimation, it achieved leading results across ScanNet, NYUv2, and iBims-1 datasets, often surpassing dedicated single-task methods, consistent with its explicit depth-to-normal supervision.
- **Qualitative Observations:** WorldMirror 2.0 produced sharper, more geometrically coherent surface normals and tighter multi-view consistent point clouds, reflecting improved geometric coupling and robust invalid-pixel handling. It demonstrated stable and coherent reconstructions across various resolutions and input view densities.
- **Inference Efficiency and Prior Injection:** WorldMirror 2.0 consistently outperformed prior-guided methods like Pow3R and MapAnything, particularly when leveraging camera-conditioned and all-priors settings, highlighting its ability to effectively utilize geometric cues. Efficiency benchmarks showed that combining Sequence Parallelism, BF16 mixed-precision, and FSDP on 4 GPUs significantly reduced memory consumption and inference time, enabling scaling to substantially larger input configurations (e.g., 256 views).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

HY-World 2.0 represents a significant advancement in the field of 3D world modeling by introducing an open-source, systematic, and multi-modal framework that unifies 3D world generation and reconstruction. This work addresses a critical gap in the existing research landscape, which has largely been characterized by specialized solutions for either task, and provides an open-source alternative to proprietary unified models.

The significance of HY-World 2.0 lies in several aspects:
- **Unified Capabilities:** It successfully bridges the inherent trade-off between generative flexibility and reconstruction accuracy, enabling the creation of both imaginative and geometrically precise 3D worlds from diverse inputs.
- **State-of-the-Art Performance:** The framework achieves competitive performance against leading closed-source commercial models like Marble and surpasses existing open-source approaches across various benchmarks, demonstrating robust visual quality, geometric consistency, and exploratory capabilities.
- **Component-Level Innovations:** Each stage of the pipeline incorporates novel techniques (e.g., MMDiT for panorama generation, multi-mode trajectory planning in WorldNav, keyframe-based generation with consistent memory in WorldStereo 2.0, and a geometrically-enhanced WorldMirror 2.0) that collectively contribute to the system's overall performance and robustness.
- **Open-Source Contribution:** The explicit commitment to releasing all model weights, code, and technical details is crucial for democratizing spatial intelligence. This fosters transparency, reproducibility, and provides a robust foundation for future research and development in 3D world models and generative AI.

The potential impact of HY-World 2.0 is broad and extends across multiple domains:
- **AI Research and Development:** It provides a powerful, modular, and open-source platform for researchers to explore new avenues in multi-modal learning, 3D scene understanding, generative AI, and embodied intelligence.
- **Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR):** The ability to generate high-fidelity, explorable, and interactive 3D worlds from simple inputs can accelerate the creation of immersive virtual environments and dynamic AR experiences.
- **Robotics and Embodied AI:** The framework can be utilized to generate realistic simulation environments for training robotic agents, allowing them to learn complex tasks, navigate, and interact within diverse and geometrically consistent 3D worlds.
- **Game Development and Content Creation:** Game designers and content creators can leverage HY-World 2.0 for rapid prototyping of game levels, populating virtual scenes, and generating intricate 3D assets, significantly reducing development time and costs.
- **Digital Twins and Environment Mapping:** Enhanced 3D reconstruction capabilities from real-world data can support the creation of accurate digital twins for various industrial and urban planning applications, as well as detailed environment mapping.

By unifying generation and reconstruction and making its extensive codebase accessible, HY-World 2.0 is positioned to facilitate further advancements in creating and interacting with digital 3D worlds.
