# X-Loco: Towards Generalist Humanoid Locomotion Control via Synergetic Policy Distillation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed X-Loco, a framework enabling a single vision-based humanoid robot policy to perform generalist locomotion, encompassing upright movement, whole-body coordination, and fall recovery. The system, deployed on a Unitree G1 robot, demonstrated versatile and resilient behaviors across hybrid terrains, including recovery from disturbances.

## Problem

- Existing humanoid locomotion controllers are functionally fragmented, excelling at isolated skills like terrain traversal or fall recovery but lacking seamless integration for complex scenarios.
- Achieving autonomous whole-body coordination and diverse behaviors without reliance on pre-defined reference motions or continuous human intervention remains challenging.
- Directly training a generalist policy via reinforcement learning is difficult due to the complexity of multi-skill reward engineering and inefficient exploration in high-dimensional state-action spaces.

## Method

- A synergetic policy distillation framework consolidates expertise from three specialized policies (upright locomotion, fall recovery, whole-body coordination) into a single generalist policy.
- Specialist selection is dynamically managed by Case-Adaptive Specialist Selection (CASS), guiding the generalist policy based on the robot's current state and local terrain.
- Specialist Annealing Rollout (SAR) and Stochastic Fall Injection (SFI) stabilize training and enhance robustness by guiding early exploration and exposing the policy to recovery scenarios.

## Results

- The X-Loco generalist policy achieved strong performance across diverse tasks, including an average **0.939** success rate in upright locomotion, **0.871** in whole-body coordination, and a perfect **1.000** in fall recovery.
- Successful real-world deployment on a Unitree G1 humanoid robot demonstrated seamless transitions between fall recovery, terrain traversal, and whole-body coordination on challenging hybrid terrains.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical roles of Case-Adaptive Specialist Selection (CASS) for skill synthesis and Specialist Annealing Rollout (SAR) and Stochastic Fall Injection (SFI) for robust learning.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Synergetic policy distillation with dynamic specialist selection enables effective integration of disparate locomotion skills into a unified, vision-based generalist policy.
> - Hybrid training strategies, like expert-guided rollouts and stochastic disturbance injection, are critical for achieving robust whole-body coordination and seamless fall recovery transitions.
> - Addressing the sim-to-real gap in depth perception through advanced rendering and data processing is crucial for successful real-world deployment of vision-based control.
