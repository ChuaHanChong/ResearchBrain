# 3DreamBooth: High-Fidelity 3D Subject-Driven Video Generation Model

> [!summary] Summary
> 3DreamBooth introduces a framework for generating high-fidelity, 3D-consistent videos of customized subjects by integrating a 1-frame training paradigm with a multi-view conditioning module, 3Dapter. The model achieves superior 3D geometric fidelity, reducing Chamfer Distance to 0.0177, and detailed texture preservation compared to baselines on the newly established 3D-CustomBench benchmark.

## Problem

- Existing subject-driven video generation methods primarily treat subjects as 2D entities, lacking the spatial priors necessary for consistent 3D geometry across novel viewpoints.
- The scarcity of multi-view video datasets makes it challenging to fine-tune models for robust 3D-aware representations without encountering temporal overfitting.
- Text-driven customization methods often struggle to capture high-frequency details due to the information bottleneck inherent in text embeddings, leading to inefficient optimization and detail loss.

## Method

- **3DreamBooth** employs a ==1-frame training paradigm== on multi-view static images, leveraging ==video Diffusion Transformers'== architecture to decouple spatial identity learning from temporal dynamics and inject an implicit 3D prior.
- A multi-view conditioning module, **3Dapter**, is pre-trained on single-view reference images and then jointly optimized with 3DreamBooth to explicitly inject fine-grained spatial features, accelerating convergence and detail preservation.
- The framework utilizes ==Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)== for parameter-efficient adaptation and achieves rapid test-time optimization, completing subject customization in approximately 13 minutes per subject on a single GPU.

## Results

- The full 3Dapter+3DreamBooth model achieved a **Chamfer Distance of 0.0177** on 3D-CustomBench, approximately halving the error of competitive single-view methods and demonstrating superior 3D geometric consistency.
- Qualitative evaluations confirmed high-fidelity, 3D-aware generation, accurately preserving intricate textures and subject identity across full 360° rotations and diverse dynamic environments.
- The framework exhibited accelerated convergence, achieving high-fidelity 3D customization within **400 iterations (13 minutes)** and maintained high intrinsic video quality and competitive text alignment scores.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training a video Diffusion Transformer on 1-frame multi-view static images, by leveraging its temporal attention bypass for single-frame inputs, effectively implants a robust 3D prior by isolating spatial identity learning.
> - Explicit multi-view visual conditioning through an adapter module can overcome text-embedding limitations, significantly enhancing fine-grained detail preservation and accelerating the subject adaptation process.
> - Decoupling 3D spatial identity from temporal motion enables efficient subject customization using static multi-view images, avoiding the need for extensive multi-view video datasets and preventing temporal overfitting.
