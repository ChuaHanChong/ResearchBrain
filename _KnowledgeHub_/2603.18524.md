# 3DreamBooth: High-Fidelity 3D Subject-Driven Video Generation Model

> [!summary] Summary
> 3DreamBooth introduces a framework for generating high-fidelity, 3D-consistent videos of customized subjects by integrating a 1-frame training paradigm with a multi-view conditioning module, 3Dapter. The model achieves superior 3D geometric fidelity, reducing Chamfer Distance to 0.0177, and detailed texture preservation compared to baselines on the newly established 3D-CustomBench benchmark.

## Problem

- Existing subject-driven video generation methods primarily treat subjects as 2D entities, lacking the spatial priors necessary for consistent 3D geometry across novel viewpoints.
- The scarcity of multi-view video datasets makes it challenging to fine-tune models for robust 3D-aware representations without encountering temporal overfitting.
- Text-driven customization methods often struggle to capture high-frequency details due to the information bottleneck inherent in text embeddings, leading to inefficient optimization and detail loss.

## Method

- **3DreamBooth** employs a ==1-frame training paradigm== on multi-view static images, leveraging ==video Diffusion Transformers'== architecture to decouple spatial identity learning from temporal dynamics and inject an implicit 3D prior.
- A multi-view conditioning module, **3Dapter**, is pre-trained on single-view reference images and then jointly optimized with 3DreamBooth to explicitly inject fine-grained spatial features, accelerating convergence and detail preservation.
- The framework utilizes ==Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)== for parameter-efficient adaptation and achieves rapid test-time optimization, completing subject customization in approximately 13 minutes per subject on a single GPU.

## Results

- The full 3Dapter+3DreamBooth model achieved a **Chamfer Distance of 0.0177** on 3D-CustomBench, approximately halving the error of competitive single-view methods and demonstrating superior 3D geometric consistency.
- Qualitative evaluations confirmed high-fidelity, 3D-aware generation, accurately preserving intricate textures and subject identity across full 360° rotations and diverse dynamic environments.
- The framework exhibited accelerated convergence, achieving high-fidelity 3D customization within **400 iterations (13 minutes)** and maintained high intrinsic video quality and competitive text alignment scores.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training a video Diffusion Transformer on 1-frame multi-view static images, by leveraging its temporal attention bypass for single-frame inputs, effectively implants a robust 3D prior by isolating spatial identity learning.
> - Explicit multi-view visual conditioning through an adapter module can overcome text-embedding limitations, significantly enhancing fine-grained detail preservation and accelerating the subject adaptation process.
> - Decoupling 3D spatial identity from temporal motion enables efficient subject customization using static multi-view images, avoiding the need for extensive multi-view video datasets and preventing temporal overfitting.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of generative AI has seen rapid advancements, particularly in subject-driven customization within image and video synthesis. This work addresses a specific challenge within this domain: generating dynamic, view-consistent videos of customized 3D subjects.

Prior research in subject-driven image generation has explored optimization-based methods like DreamBooth and Textual Inversion, which bind a subject's identity to a unique identifier, and adapter-based approaches that inject reference images directly into the diffusion process to preserve details. These techniques have subsequently been extended to Text-to-Video (T2V) generation, with studies attempting to personalize video models for specific subjects or motions using single-image references or textual prompts.

However, a fundamental limitation of many existing video customization methods is their predominant treatment of subjects as 2D entities. These 2D-centric approaches lack the spatial priors necessary to reconstruct accurate 3D geometry. Consequently, when synthesizing novel views, they often generate plausible but arbitrary details for unseen regions rather than preserving the true 3D identity consistently across viewpoints. This issue is compounded by the scarcity of multi-view video datasets, making it difficult to fine-tune models to capture robust 3D-aware representations without encountering temporal overfitting.

While 3D-aware spatial generation has been explored in the image domain (e.g., Zero-1-to-3, MVDream) and efforts have been made towards 3D-conditioned image generation (RefAny3D) or multi-view conditioned T2V frameworks (MV-S2V), the explicit injection of multi-view images of a 3D subject directly into video diffusion models to achieve robust 3D-consistent customization remains an area with open challenges. Existing 3D-conditioned video generation approaches often rely on large-scale synthetic video datasets, leading to high computational costs.

This work positions itself to bridge this gap by proposing a framework that unifies optimization-based personalization and adapter-based conditioning to achieve high-fidelity, 3D-aware video customization. It specifically aims to mitigate the entanglement of spatial identity and temporal dynamics, leveraging multi-view static images to infer 3D structure, rather than relying on extensive multi-view video data or purely 2D conditioning. The research aims to provide a computationally efficient solution for generating subjects with consistent 3D geometry in dynamic video environments.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation for this research stems from the increasing demand for high-fidelity, view-consistent dynamic videos of customized subjects in emerging applications such as immersive Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR/AR), virtual production, and next-generation e-commerce. Current methodologies for creating such content, like traditional filming, are often costly, time-consuming, and inflexible, requiring repeated sessions for different viewpoints, lighting, or motions. Similarly, game developers require efficient ways to animate custom characters consistently across diverse scenes. The objective is to enable a generative system to understand a subject's appearance and its underlying 3D structure, allowing for faithful rendering across unseen viewpoints and novel scenarios from a minimal input set.

The research identifies several limitations in existing subject-driven video generation approaches that hinder the realization of these applications:

1. **Lack of 3D Understanding in 2D-Centric Methods:** Most current methods treat subjects as 2D entities, focusing on transferring identity through single-view visual features or textual prompts. This fundamental limitation means they lack the comprehensive spatial priors needed to reconstruct accurate 3D geometry. As a result, when generating novel views, these methods often produce plausible but arbitrary details for unseen regions, failing to preserve the true 3D identity and leading to visual inconsistencies.
2. **Scarcity of Multi-View Video Datasets:** Achieving genuine 3D-aware customization is challenging due to the limited availability of datasets containing multi-view video sequences of subjects. Attempting to fine-tune models on sparse video data often results in temporal overfitting, where the model memorizes specific motion patterns rather than learning generalizable 3D identity.
3. **Information Bottleneck in Text-Driven Customization:** While text-driven methods like DreamBooth can bind a subject's identity to a unique identifier, they often struggle to capture high-frequency details (e.g., intricate textures, specific typography). This limitation arises because text embeddings inherently capture coarse semantic concepts, leading to an information bottleneck that makes optimization inefficient and detail preservation challenging.

To address these limitations, the key objectives of this work are:

- **Develop a novel framework for 3D-aware video customization:** This framework aims to generate high-fidelity, view-consistent videos that seamlessly integrate customized 3D subjects into dynamic environments.
- **Decouple spatial geometry from temporal motion:** The goal is to implant a robust 3D prior into the model without requiring extensive video-based training, preventing temporal overfitting and efficiently learning subject identity.
- **Enhance fine-grained texture preservation and accelerate convergence:** This involves incorporating a visual conditioning mechanism to overcome the limitations of text-only customization, providing explicit visual hints for detailed generation.
- **Achieve computational efficiency:** The proposed methods aim to be parameter-efficient and require reduced training time compared to training large models from scratch on extensive datasets.
- **Establish a standardized evaluation protocol:** Introduce a benchmark, 3D-CustomBench, to objectively assess 3D-consistent video customization, facilitating future research.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The proposed framework, named 3DreamBooth, integrates a novel optimization paradigm with a multi-view conditioning module called 3Dapter to achieve high-fidelity, 3D-aware video customization. It unifies optimization-based personalization and adapter-based conditioning, building upon a pre-trained video Diffusion Transformer (DiT) model.

#### 3.1. 3DreamBooth: Isolating Identity from Temporal Dynamics

The core of 3DreamBooth is an optimization paradigm designed to inject a subject's 3D identity into video diffusion models without entangling spatial identity with temporal dynamics.

- **1-Frame Training Paradigm:** Instead of using full video sequences, 3DreamBooth processes multi-view static images of a subject, treating each as a single-frame video (T=1). This design leverages an architectural property of modern video DiTs: when the input is a single frame, the temporal attention mechanism is naturally bypassed. This restricts gradient updates exclusively to spatial representations, preserving the model's pre-trained temporal priors while learning subject identity.
- **Eliciting Implicit 3D Prior:** By training on diverse static views of the target subject, using a consistent text prompt containing a unique identifier token (V) and a class noun (C), the model is encouraged to internalize multi-view spatial variations into the token V. This process transforms V into a consolidated 3D prior of the specific subject, encompassing its geometric structures and view-dependent appearances. During inference, this enriched token, combined with the untouched pre-trained temporal mechanics, is intended to yield temporally consistent and view-consistent videos.
- **Optimization:** The pre-trained video DiT is optimized using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) weights (ϕ_3DB) injected into transformer blocks. The training objective minimizes a velocity prediction loss based on the noisy latent of sampled views and the text prompt.

#### 3.2. 3Dapter: Multi-View Conditioning for Detail Preservation

To address the limitations of text-driven customization (slow optimization, loss of fine-grained details due to the information bottleneck of text embeddings), the framework introduces 3Dapter, a multi-view conditioning module.

- **Single-View Pre-training:** 3Dapter is first pre-trained using a dual-branch architecture, inspired by controllable DiTs. It processes a clean reference image (x^(i)) depicting a single object on a white background and a target image (y^(i)) of the same object in diverse contexts, along with a text prompt. LoRA weights (ϕ_3Dapter) are optimized using a standard diffusion objective to extract robust features from single-view conditions. This stage employs a large-scale dataset of reference-target image pairs (Subjects200K) to learn robust feature extraction. The interaction between references, targets, and text is modeled by concatenating their tensors and applying spatio-temporal attention.
- **Multi-View Joint Optimization:** After single-view pre-training, 3DreamBooth and 3Dapter are jointly fine-tuned for a specific subject adaptation.
 - From the subject's multi-view images (S), a subset of conditioning views (X) is selected for 3Dapter. These views are preprocessed by masking out backgrounds and chosen to maximize angular coverage.
 - The shared 3Dapter processes all conditioning images. The main generation branch utilizes the 3DreamBooth LoRA weights (ϕ_3DB). Both sets of LoRA weights (ϕ_3DB, ϕ_3Dapter) are simultaneously optimized with the velocity prediction loss.
 - For joint attention, Query, Key, and Value tensors for the subject, multiple conditioning views (X), and the text prompt are produced and concatenated. Distinct, sequential temporal indices are assigned to each conditioning view using 3D Rotary Positional Encoding (RoPE) to prevent feature entanglement.
 - This design enables the joint attention mechanism to act as a dynamic, selective router, querying and extracting only relevant, view-specific geometric hints from the multi-view references to reconstruct the current target view. This synergy aims to accelerate convergence and preserve high-fidelity details.

#### 3.3. Implementation Details

The framework is built upon HunyuanVideo-1.5, an 8.3B parameter model. LoRA rank is set to 16 with an alpha scaling factor of 32 for both modules. 3Dapter is pre-trained for 100K iterations on Subjects200K. The joint optimization of 3DreamBooth and 3Dapter is conducted for 400 iterations (13 minutes per subject on a single GPU). Reference images are preprocessed with background masking using birefnet-massive.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The research presents both qualitative and quantitative evaluations to support the efficacy of the proposed 3DreamBooth and 3Dapter framework.

#### 4.1. Qualitative Results

- **High-Fidelity 3D-Aware Generation:** The framework demonstrates the ability to synthesize full 360° geometry of customized subjects, maintaining identity across all viewpoints during dynamic rotations. This contrasts with existing baselines (VACE, Phantom), which often struggle with complex structures and fine-grained details from unseen viewpoints, yielding inconsistent textures and geometries.
- **Detail Preservation:** The combined 3Dapter+3DreamBooth model accurately preserves intricate textures, product tags, logos, and small accessories, which single-view baselines or 3DreamBooth alone struggle to reconstruct or render with clarity.
- **Robustness Across Diverse Scenarios:** The framework consistently preserves subject identity without structural degradation, even when integrated into highly dynamic environments or driven by multiple, distinct text prompts featuring diverse backgrounds and human-object interactions.
- **Convergence Analysis:** Visual comparisons show that 3Dapter significantly accelerates convergence compared to 3DreamBooth alone. While 3DreamBooth alone requires prolonged optimization (e.g., 2000 iterations) to refine coarse shapes, it still struggles with fine-grained details. The pre-trained 3Dapter provides a robust structural foundation, enabling accurate multi-view alignment and high-fidelity 3D customization within a short optimization period (e.g., 400 iterations).

#### 4.2. Quantitative Results

The evaluation was conducted on a newly introduced benchmark, 3D-CustomBench, comprising 30 objects with complex 3D structures, high texture resolution, and full 360° orbital coverage.

- **Multi-View Subject Fidelity (Table 1):**
 - The full model (3Dapter+3DB) achieved superior performance across most metrics, particularly in GPT-4o-based human-centric evaluations (Shape: 4.80, Color: 4.53, Detail: 4.04, Overall Identity: 4.57 on a 1-5 scale), indicating strong identity preservation across views.
 - In feature-based metrics, 3Dapter+3DB scored DINO-I of 0.7420, outperforming baselines. Although VACE showed a slightly higher CLIP-I score (0.8964 vs 0.8871), the authors attribute this to CLIP's emphasis on high-level semantics over precise geometric accuracy, which DINO-I and GPT-4o metrics are designed to capture more accurately.

- **3D Geometric Fidelity (Table 2):**
 - The framework demonstrated superior 3D geometric consistency. 3Dapter+3DB achieved a Chamfer Distance (CD) of 0.0177, approximately half the error of the most competitive single-view method (Phantom, 0.0338).
 - A significant lead in Completeness (0.0172) indicates that the multi-view conditioning effectively recovers the full 360° geometry, unlike single-view methods that fail to infer complete 3D structure from unseen perspectives.

- **Video Quality and Text Alignment (Table 3):**
 - The framework maintained high intrinsic video quality and text alignment. It outperformed existing methods in Imaging Quality (74.33) and showed competitive ViCLIP scores (0.2388), suggesting that robust 3D conditioning does not compromise foundational generation capabilities.

#### 4.3. Ablation Studies

- **Necessity of 3Dapter Pre-training:** Without pre-training, a randomly initialized 3Dapter module failed to converge, even after 400 iterations of joint optimization, producing meaningless noise or structural artifacts. This highlights the critical role of pre-training in establishing a robust structural foundation for visual conditioning.
- **Individual Component Contribution:**
 - 3Dapter alone (single-view) yielded strong aesthetic quality but lacked 3D consistency.
 - 3DreamBooth alone (multi-view) provided better geometry but struggled with fine-grained texture details and required longer optimization to converge.
 - The joint optimization of 3Dapter + 3DreamBooth achieved the best balance, combining robust 3D structure with high-frequency feature injection.

#### 4.4. Extensibility

- The 1-frame training paradigm of 3DreamBooth was successfully applied to other state-of-the-art Diffusion Transformer architectures, such as the Wan 2.1 text-to-video model, demonstrating its generalizability in binding complex multi-view identity to a unique identifier even without explicit spatial conditioning from 3Dapter.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

This research presents a framework for 3D-aware subject-driven video generation, addressing limitations in existing 2D-centric approaches and the scarcity of multi-view video datasets. The work introduces several technical contributions with potential implications across various domains.

**Technical Advancements:**
- **Decoupling Spatial and Temporal Dynamics:** The 3DreamBooth component, through its 1-frame optimization paradigm, efficiently decouples spatial identity learning from temporal motion. This allows for the effective baking of a robust 3D prior into the model using static multi-view images, avoiding the computational redundancy and temporal overfitting associated with video-based training.
- **Enhanced Detail and Convergence:** The 3Dapter module, with its two-stage training and asymmetrical conditioning strategy, explicitly injects multi-view spatial features. This mechanism serves as a "dynamic selective router," querying relevant geometric hints and significantly accelerating the optimization process while preserving fine-grained textures and intricate details.
- **Computational Efficiency:** By utilizing LoRA for parameter-efficient adaptation and a short test-time optimization phase (approximately 13 minutes per subject on a single GPU), the framework offers a more sustainable and efficient solution compared to approaches requiring large-scale retraining of entire backbones or extensive data.

**Practical Implications:**
- **Next-Generation Content Creation:** The ability to generate high-fidelity, view-consistent videos of customized 3D subjects has direct applications in immersive VR/AR experiences, virtual production workflows (e.g., placing custom assets into virtual sets), and next-generation e-commerce (e.g., dynamic product showcases).
- **Design and Marketing:** Product designers can showcase new items in dynamic advertisements from any viewpoint and motion context without costly traditional filming. Game developers can animate custom characters consistently across diverse scenes.
- **Reduced Production Costs and Time:** The framework reduces the need for extensive real-world photography or video shoots for each specific subject, offering a more flexible and cost-effective content generation pipeline.

**Contribution to Research:**
- The introduction of **3D-CustomBench** provides a standardized evaluation suite for 3D-consistent video customization, which can facilitate objective comparison and accelerate future research in this emerging area.
- The framework's extensibility to other Diffusion Transformer (DiT) architectures demonstrates a generalizable approach for injecting 3D priors into state-of-the-art generative models.

While the current work primarily focuses on rigid or static objects, the findings pave the way for future research into adapting this paradigm to more dynamic subjects with complex articulations or state changes. Additionally, extending this optimization framework to video editing models could enable robust, 3D-aware subject insertion into real-world footage, further broadening its impact.
