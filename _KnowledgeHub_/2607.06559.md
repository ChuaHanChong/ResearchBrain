# RynnWorld-4D: 4D Embodied World Models for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> RynnWorld-4D introduces a projective 4D representation for embodied world models, synchronously predicting RGB, depth, and optical flow to capture future scene dynamics. This framework improves robotic manipulation capabilities, demonstrating enhanced success rates on complex bimanual tasks by providing explicit geometric and motion cues for policy learning.

## Problem

- Existing visual world models for robotic manipulation often lack explicit 3D geometric grounding, leading to imprecise 6-DoF pose estimation and physically inconsistent future state predictions from 2D pixel-based representations.
- Current 4D scene modeling approaches are computationally intensive, scene-specific, or lack generative capabilities, making them difficult to scale or integrate with large-scale video diffusion models for robust policy learning.
- There is a scarcity of large-scale, high-quality 4D embodied video datasets with dense annotations (depth, optical flow) necessary for training generative 4D world models.

## Method

- Introduced **RynnWorld-4D**, a generative model that predicts future synchronized ==RGB, depth, and optical flow (RGB-DF)== sequences from an initial RGB-D image and language instruction.
- Designed a ==tri-branch diffusion transformer== architecture with ==Joint Cross-Modal Attention== and ==3D Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE)==, trained with a ==flow matching objective== to ensure cross-modal consistency in appearance, geometry, and motion.
- Curated **Rynn4DDataset 1.0**, a large-scale hybrid video dataset (over 254.4 million frames) with pseudo-annotations for depth and optical flow, to address data scarcity for 4D generative modeling.

## Results

- RynnWorld-4D achieved competitive visual quality (**SSIM 0.754**) and superior geometric accuracy (**δ1 of 0.610**) compared to other 4D world models, alongside precise temporal motion consistency (**AEPE of 0.170** for optical flow).
- The RynnWorld-4D-Policy consistently outperformed baseline methods across six real-world dexterous bimanual manipulation tasks, for example, achieving **65.71%** success in Lid Placement and Bowl Stacking, an **8.57 percentage point** improvement over Diffusion Policy.
- Ablation studies confirmed the importance of joint cross-modal attention, modality adaptation, and large-scale 4D pre-training for maintaining geometric accuracy and motion precision.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Synchronized co-generation of RGB, depth, and optical flow (RGB-DF) acts as a physical regularizer, significantly enhancing geometric accuracy and temporal consistency in world model predictions.
> - A tri-branch diffusion architecture with Joint Cross-Modal Attention and 3D Rotary Positional Embeddings effectively fuses multi-modal features while preserving consistency across appearance, geometry, and motion.
> - Directly leveraging internal 4D predictive representations from the world model as a frozen encoder enables efficient, high-frequency robot control without requiring iterative denoising steps.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How this Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This work is positioned within the field of visual world models for robotic manipulation, specifically addressing the limitations of existing approaches by integrating 4D scene dynamics. Current generative video models, while capable of policy synthesis and planning in 2D pixel space, often lack explicit 3D geometric grounding. This limitation can lead to difficulties in precise 6-DoF pose estimation, depth-aware interaction, and can result in temporal inconsistencies such as fluctuating object scales or unphysical shape morphing, thereby hindering their utility for robust policy learning in robotics. The paper frames the transition from 2D pixel-based models to geometry-integrated 4D scene evolution as an essential step for embodied intelligence.

Existing 4D scene modeling methodologies are broadly categorized into two groups: those based on Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) or 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), and dynamic Structure-from-Motion (SfM) approaches. NeRF/3DGS-based methods can be optimization-intensive and scene-specific or focus on object-centric generation, often requiring multi-view inputs or struggling with complex scene-level environments. SfM approaches reconstruct time-varying point clouds but typically lack generative capabilities for predicting future states from a single image. The paper states that neither category readily provides a compact, scalable representation compatible with the generative priors of large-scale video diffusion models.

RynnWorld-4D aims to bridge this gap by introducing a projective 4D representation that synchronously predicts RGB, depth, and optical flow (RGB-DF). This multi-modal representation is designed to provide explicit geometry and motion cues, allowing for back-projection into 3D scene flow under standard camera assumptions. By remaining in a 2D-aligned format, it intends to leverage the scalability and generative priors of established video diffusion models, while overcoming the geometric and temporal limitations of purely 2D systems and the computational or input-constrained limitations of explicit 3D/4D representations. The work distinguishes itself from other 4D world models, such as those modeling RGB-DN (RGB, Depth, and Normal) videos, by explicitly including optical flow for dynamic cues and direct back-projection into 3D scene flow, which is identified as critical for dexterous manipulation.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary motivation for this research stems from the requirements of robotic manipulation in open-world environments. Such environments necessitate not only visual recognition of a scene but also the ability to anticipate its 3D structural changes under robotic interaction. The paper argues that current 2D pixel-based world models are inherently limited due to their projective nature, which leads to a loss of critical spatial relationships and hinders the generation of physically consistent future states. This deficiency creates a gap between the predictions of world models and the low-level, 3D-oriented end-effector actions required by robotic systems.

To address this, the key objectives of this work are:

1. **Develop a Physically Grounded 4D Representation:** To introduce and validate synchronized RGB, depth, and optical flow (RGB-DF) as a physically grounded representation. This representation aims to capture the underlying 4D dynamics of a scene, aligning visual appearance with geometric structure and temporal motion. It is designed to be directly interpretable into 3D scene flow.
2. **Propose a Generative 4D World Model:** To design and implement RynnWorld-4D, a generative model capable of co-producing future RGB frames, depth maps, and optical flow from a single RGB-D image and a language instruction. The model utilizes a unified diffusion process within a tri-branch architecture that integrates cross-modal attention with frame-wise 3D Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) to ensure consistent evolution of appearance, geometry, and motion.
3. **Curate a Large-Scale 4D Embodied Video Dataset:** To address the scarcity of training data with dense 4D annotations, the researchers aimed to curate Rynn4DDataset 1.0. This dataset comprises over 254.4 million frames from egocentric human and robotic manipulation videos, enriched with high-quality pseudo-labels for depth and optical flow.
4. **Enable High-Frequency Robotic Control:** To propose RynnWorld-4D-Policy, an inverse dynamics head that consumes the internal 4D representations generated by RynnWorld-4D. This policy aims to bypass computationally expensive multi-step denoising processes, enabling the direct output of robot actions in a single forward pass for closed-loop control. The motivation here is to provide a mechanism that translates the model's 4D predictions into precise, real-time robotic actions, narrowing the gap between world prediction and policy learning.

In summary, the motivation is to advance embodied intelligence by enabling robots to understand and predict the physical evolution of their 3D environments, leading to more robust and precise manipulation capabilities than what is achievable with 2D-centric or computationally burdensome 3D/4D world models.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology outlined in the paper consists of three main components: the curation of a large-scale 4D dataset, the development of the RynnWorld-4D generative model, and the introduction of RynnWorld-4D-Policy for robotic control.

#### 3.1. Rynn4DDataset 1.0

To mitigate data scarcity for 4D generative modeling, Rynn4DDataset 1.0 was curated. This dataset is a hybrid collection exceeding 254.4 million video frames, drawn from both human-centric (Epic-Kitchens, EgoVid) and robotic manipulation (RoboMIND, RDT-1B, Galaxea, RoboCoin, AgiBot) sources. Each frame is augmented with high-quality pseudo-annotations for:
- **Video Captioning:** Qwen3-VL (Bai et al., 2025) was used to generate detailed, structured natural language descriptions for video clips, which were sampled at 1 FPS and segmented into 5-second intervals.
- **Optical Flow Annotation:** DPFlow (Morimitsu et al., 2025), an optical flow estimation model, processed frame pairs at native resolution, with flow fields saved as MP4 videos.
- **Depth Annotation:** Depth Anything 3 (DA3NESTED-GIANT-LARGE-1.1 checkpoint) (Lin et al., 2025) provided dense per-frame depth predictions and camera pose estimation. Videos were sampled at 30 FPS, processed at 392 pixels (short side), and depth maps were upsampled, clipped to [0.0, 5.0] meters, and quantized to 8-bit grayscale.

#### 3.2. 3D Scene Reconstruction from Multi-Modal Videos

A core aspect of the RGB-DF representation is its geometric interpretability. The co-generated depth and optical flow allow for the reconstruction of a temporally consistent 3D scene and derivation of metric scene flow.
- **Geometric Unprojection:** Given a depth map $D_t$ at frame $t$, each pixel $p_t = [u, v, 1]^T$ is unprojected into 3D camera space using the camera intrinsic matrix $K$ as $P_t = D_t(u,v) \cdot K^{-1} p_t$, yielding a 3D point cloud $C_t$.
- **Metric Scene Flow Derivation:** Dense optical flow $f_{opt} = [\Delta u, \Delta v]^T$ establishes temporal correspondences. A 3D point $P_t$ is tracked to $P_{t+1}$ using $P_{t+1} = D_{t+1}(u+\Delta u, v+\Delta v) \cdot K^{-1}(p_t + [\Delta u, \Delta v, 0]^T)$. The 3D scene flow is defined as $f_{3D} = P_{t+1} - P_t$, representing per-point metric displacement. This mechanism aims to ensure physically plausible 3D movements.
- **Refinement and Visualization:** A depth-gradient-based edge filter suppresses artifacts, and refined 3D trajectories are projected into a bird’s-eye view.

#### 3.3. RynnWorld-4D

RynnWorld-4D is a 4D embodied world model designed for synchronized generation of RGB-DF sequences. It extends a pretrained video generative model (Wan, Wang et al., 2025a) into a tri-branch architecture.
- **Tri-branch Architecture:** The model consists of three independent branches for RGB, depth, and optical flow latents ($z_t^m \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times C \times H \times W}$), built upon the Wan 2.2-TI2V-5B diffusion transformer (30-layer DiT, hidden dim $d=3072$, FFN dim 14,336). Each branch models modality-specific feature distributions. The depth and flow branches are initialized by duplicating pretrained RGB components.
- **Joint Cross-Modal Attention (JA):** To enforce cross-modal consistency, JA modules are inserted every three transformer blocks (10 modules in total, at layers 0, 3..., 27). Each module follows intra-modal self-attention. Queries for a branch attend to concatenated keys/values from the other two branches. Cross-modal attention is restricted to tokens of the same temporal frame. 3D Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) are applied to queries and keys to inject spatial position information across branches. A learnable gate $g_m^l$ and zero-initialized output projection facilitate smooth training. Text cross-attention Key/Value projections are shared across branches.
- **Phased Training Strategy:** A three-stage curriculum addresses modality distribution gaps:
 1. **Modality Adaptation:** Independent training of branches (JA disabled) to adapt depth and flow to their respective distributions.
 2. **Joint Attention Training:** JA modules are inserted, and only their projections, LayerNorms, gates, and modality embeddings are trainable, with the backbone frozen. Branch Dropout (p=0.2) is applied to depth/flow latents (frames [1:]) by replacing them with Gaussian noise, forcing reconstruction from visible modalities.
 3. **Full-Parameter Joint SFT:** The entire model is unfrozen and fine-tuned on Rynn4DDataset 1.0, with reduced Branch Dropout (p=0.1).
- **Training Objective:** All stages use a flow matching objective (Lipman et al., 2022). The model learns a velocity field $v_\theta^m$ for each modality $m$ to transport Gaussian noise $\epsilon^m$ to data $z_0^m$. The first frame is excluded from supervision. A single Gaussian noise sample ($\epsilon^{rgb} = \epsilon^{depth} = \epsilon^{flow}$) is shared across modalities for temporal alignment. Modality weights $\lambda_{rgb} = \lambda_{depth} = 1$, $\lambda_{flow}$ starts at 0.5 (Stage 1) then 1.0 (Stages 2 & 3).

#### 3.4. RynnWorld-4D-Policy

RynnWorld-4D is utilized as a frozen 4D vision encoder for robot policy learning.
- **Feature Extraction:** A forward pass through the frozen RynnWorld-4D yields a latent trajectory encoding future dynamics. Intermediate hidden states from block 15 of the transformer across all branches are concatenated to form a comprehensive 4D representation $F_p \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times T \times 3C \times H \times W}$.
- **Flow Former:** A Flow Former with learnable queries compresses these high-dimensional 4D features via frame-wise spatial cross-attention followed by temporal self-attention.
- **Action Generation:** A flow matching policy (Lipman et al., 2022) generates actions, conditioned on the predictive 4D tokens from Flow Former, text embeddings, and proprioception. During inference, actions are derived via a 4-step ODE solver.
- **Inference Latency and Real-time Control:** The 4D features are extracted in a single forward pass. Action generation occurs in the lightweight policy head. With action chunking (K=10 actions predicted per pass) and parallel computation, an effective control frequency of approximately 9 Hz is achieved, despite a planning frequency of ≈0.9 Hz. The RynnWorld-4D forward pass is the primary bottleneck.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The paper presents quantitative and qualitative results for both 4D world modeling and robotic manipulation tasks, supported by ablation studies.

#### 4.1. 4D World Modeling

RynnWorld-4D was evaluated against state-of-the-art 2D video generation models (CogVideoX, Wan-2.2-TI2V-5B, Wan-2.1-I2V-14B) and 4D world models (Free4D, TesserAct, 4DNeX) on a held-out test set.
- **Visual Synthesis Quality:** RynnWorld-4D achieved competitive visual quality (IQ) and high reconstruction fidelity (SSIM 0.754, PSNR 17.85, LPIPS 0.269), outperforming 2D video models in fidelity. This suggests a balance between creative synthesis and structural integrity preservation.
- **Geometric Accuracy:** The model demonstrated a δ1 of 0.610 and AbsRel of 0.310, indicating superior geometric accuracy compared to other 4D world models like 4DNeX (δ1 0.327) and TesserAct (δ1 0.279).
- **Temporal Motion Consistency:** RynnWorld-4D provided synchronized optical flow with an Average End-Point Error (AEPE) of 0.170, a capability often absent in baseline 4D models. This indicates precise motion dynamics capture.
- **Qualitative Results:** Visualizations showed that generated depth and flow maps were internally consistent and precisely aligned with RGB texture changes, supporting the hypothesis that joint RGB-DF modeling acts as a physical regularizer.

#### 4.2. Policy Learning

RynnWorld-4D-Policy was evaluated on six real-world dexterous bimanual manipulation tasks (Dual Picking, Block Pushing, Hand-over, Bimanual Lifting, Lid Placement, Bowl Stacking), comparing its success rate against Diffusion Policy (DP), π0, and π0.5.
- **Overall Performance:** RynnWorld-4D-Policy consistently outperformed baseline policies across tasks. For example, in Lid Placement and Bowl Stacking, it achieved 65.71% success, surpassing DP by 8.57 percentage points.
- **Precision and Coordination Tasks:** The model excelled in tasks requiring high spatial precision (Lid Placement, Bowl Stacking) and complex temporal coordination (Bimanual Lifting: 97.14%).
- **Dynamic Object Transfer:** In the Hand-over task, RynnWorld-4D-Policy achieved 28.57% success, significantly higher than π0 (2.86%) and π0.5 (0.00%). This performance is attributed to the model's explicit kinetic and geometric cues from 4D representations, which address limitations of 2D models struggling with relative 3D distances and self-occlusion in such scenarios.

#### 4.3. Ablation Study

Extensive ablation studies validated architectural and training choices:
- **Effectiveness of Tri-branch Fusion:** Training branches independently (Independent Branches) led to significant degradation in depth (AbsRel 0.737 vs. 0.310) and flow (AEPE 0.247 vs. 0.170), confirming the importance of mutual feature interaction for cross-modal consistency.
- **Necessity of Modality Adaptation:** Skipping initial modality-specific adaptation (w/o MA) resulted in substantial drops in geometric accuracy (δ1 0.479 vs. 0.610) and motion precision, indicating its importance for bridging distribution gaps.
- **Significance of Large-scale 4D Pre-training:** Training solely on task-specific robotic data (w/o 4D Pre-training) caused a severe performance collapse, particularly in flow (AEPE 0.729 vs. 0.170), demonstrating the necessity of diverse large-scale data.
- **Effectiveness of Predictive Latents:** Replacing the RynnWorld-4D predictive encoder with a standard ResNet-18 (w/o RynnWorld-4D) significantly reduced success rates across tasks (e.g., Dual Picking 71.43% vs. 94.29%), confirming the crucial role of spatio-temporal dynamics captured by predictive 4D representations.
- **Impact of 4D Modalities:** Ablations showed that individual modalities (RGB, RGB+Depth, RGB+Optical Flow) contributed distinctly, with the full RGB-DF combination achieving the best performance, emphasizing the synergy of visual context, spatial geometry, and kinetic dynamics.
- **Role of 3D RoPE in Joint Attention:** Disabling 3D RoPE in JA (w/o RoPE in JA) caused a decay in reconstruction fidelity (δ1 0.450 vs. 0.610; AEPE 0.210 vs. 0.170), indicating its role in spatially-aware feature fusion.
- **Per-branch FFN vs. Shared FFN:** Using a single FFN shared across all modalities (Shared FFN) led to systemic performance collapse across metrics, suggesting that modality-specific FFNs are essential to mitigate cross-modal distribution shifts.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

RynnWorld-4D introduces a framework that advances generative world modeling from 2D pixel sequences to consistent 4D scene evolution. By proposing a lightweight projective 4D representation—synchronized RGB, depth, and optical flow (RGB-DF)—the work aims to integrate the scalability of video diffusion models with the geometric rigor necessary for robotic manipulation.

The significance of this research can be summarized by its key contributions and their potential impact:
- **Novel 4D Representation:** The introduction of RGB-DF as a projective 4D representation provides explicit geometry and motion cues that are directly interpretable as 3D scene flow. This representation is compatible with large-scale video diffusion priors, potentially offering a scalable alternative to computationally intensive or input-constrained explicit 3D/4D models. This could enable more geometrically accurate and temporally consistent world models for embodied agents.
- **RynnWorld-4D Model:** The development of a tri-branch architecture with Joint Cross-Modal Attention and 3D RoPE demonstrates a method for co-generating physically coherent RGB-DF sequences. This approach offers a way to leverage powerful pre-trained video backbones while specializing for multi-modal dynamics, leading to improved predictive capabilities for future scene states.
- **Rynn4DDataset 1.0:** The curation of a large-scale hybrid dataset with pseudo-annotated depth and optical flow addresses a critical data scarcity issue in 4D generative modeling. This dataset is a valuable resource for training future 4D embodied world models and could accelerate research in this area.
- **RynnWorld-4D-Policy:** The proposed inverse dynamics head directly leverages the internal 4D representations of RynnWorld-4D, bypassing iterative denoising for action generation. This enables high-frequency, closed-loop robotic control, which demonstrated state-of-the-art performance in real-world dexterous bimanual manipulation tasks, particularly those requiring spatial precision and temporal coordination. This suggests a pathway to more robust and precise robot control by integrating predictive 4D understanding directly into policy learning.

The potential impact of RynnWorld-4D lies in its capacity to provide a more robust foundation for general-purpose embodied intelligence. By enabling robots to understand and anticipate the complex 3D evolution of their environments, the framework could lead to advancements in areas such as dexterous manipulation, long-horizon planning, and human-robot interaction in dynamic, open-world settings.

Despite its advancements, the paper acknowledges limitations, including the computational overhead of diffusion denoising, which currently restricts the effective control frequency to approximately 9 Hz on specified hardware, and the model's primary optimization for egocentric perspectives. Extending 4D spatio-temporal consistency to multi-view or multi-robot systems remains an open challenge.
