# HD-EPIC: A Highly-Detailed Egocentric Video Dataset

> [!summary] Summary
> The HD-EPIC dataset provides 41 hours of multi-day egocentric video from home kitchens, capturing unscripted activities with dense, multi-modal annotations including 3D scene digital twins, nutritional tracking, and explicit 'how' and 'why' action clauses. Benchmarking reveals state-of-the-art video-language models achieve only 37.6% accuracy on its Visual Question Answering tasks, significantly below human performance of 90.3%, highlighting current AI limitations in complex egocentric understanding.

## Problem

- Existing large-scale egocentric datasets often lack fine-grained, 3D-grounded, and multi-modal annotations for complex real-world scenarios.
- Richly annotated datasets are typically collected in controlled laboratory settings, limiting their realism and generalizability to diverse, unscripted environments.
- Prior egocentric datasets like EPIC-KITCHENS lacked comprehensive 3D spatial grounding, precise nutritional information, and explicit 'how' or 'why' contextual details for actions.

## Method

- Collected 41 hours of unscripted daily kitchen activities from 9 participants over multiple consecutive days using Project Aria glasses, ensuring high realism and diverse content.
- Developed a multi-video SLAM pipeline to create detailed multi-day 3D digital twins for each kitchen, including 413 labeled fixtures and precise 6DoF camera trajectories.
- Implemented a multi-tiered annotation pipeline, generating an average of 263 annotations per minute, encompassing fine-grained actions with 'how'/'why' clauses, nutritional tracking, 3D object tracking, and gaze priming for interactions.

## Results

- The HD-EPIC dataset contains 41.3 hours of video, 59,454 fine-grained actions, 50,968 audio events, 20,000 object movements, and 37,000 3D-lifted object masks.
- On the 26,650-question VQA benchmark, Gemini Pro achieved an average accuracy of 37.6%, in stark contrast to the human baseline of 90.3%, revealing a significant performance gap.
- Action recognition models, fine-tuned on EPIC-KITCHENS-100, achieved only 51.3% for verbs and 23.4% for actions on HD-EPIC, indicating the dataset's increased difficulty compared to previous benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current state-of-the-art video-language models, including Gemini Pro, perform substantially below human levels on comprehensive egocentric VQA tasks, particularly in 3D perception, temporal reasoning, and fine-grained semantic understanding.
> - The combination of unscripted 'in-the-wild' data with dense, interconnected, multi-modal annotations creates a challenging benchmark that effectively diagnosticates specific weaknesses in modern AI's ability to interpret complex human activities.
> - Egocentric gaze plays a crucial role in interaction anticipation, with 94.8% of object pick-ups and 88.5% of object put-downs being primed by preceding gaze fixation on the object's location.
