# HD-EPIC: A Highly-Detailed Egocentric Video Dataset

> [!summary] Summary
> The HD-EPIC dataset provides 41 hours of multi-day egocentric video from home kitchens, capturing unscripted activities with dense, multi-modal annotations including 3D scene digital twins, nutritional tracking, and explicit 'how' and 'why' action clauses. Benchmarking reveals state-of-the-art video-language models achieve only 37.6% accuracy on its Visual Question Answering tasks, significantly below human performance of 90.3%, highlighting current AI limitations in complex egocentric understanding.

## Problem

- Existing large-scale egocentric datasets often lack fine-grained, 3D-grounded, and multi-modal annotations for complex real-world scenarios.
- Richly annotated datasets are typically collected in controlled laboratory settings, limiting their realism and generalizability to diverse, unscripted environments.
- Prior egocentric datasets like EPIC-KITCHENS lacked comprehensive 3D spatial grounding, precise nutritional information, and explicit 'how' or 'why' contextual details for actions.

## Method

- Collected 41 hours of unscripted daily kitchen activities from 9 participants over multiple consecutive days using Project Aria glasses, ensuring high realism and diverse content.
- Developed a multi-video SLAM pipeline to create detailed multi-day 3D digital twins for each kitchen, including 413 labeled fixtures and precise 6DoF camera trajectories.
- Implemented a multi-tiered annotation pipeline, generating an average of 263 annotations per minute, encompassing fine-grained actions with 'how'/'why' clauses, nutritional tracking, 3D object tracking, and gaze priming for interactions.

## Results

- The HD-EPIC dataset contains 41.3 hours of video, 59,454 fine-grained actions, 50,968 audio events, 20,000 object movements, and 37,000 3D-lifted object masks.
- On the 26,650-question VQA benchmark, Gemini Pro achieved an average accuracy of 37.6%, in stark contrast to the human baseline of 90.3%, revealing a significant performance gap.
- Action recognition models, fine-tuned on EPIC-KITCHENS-100, achieved only 51.3% for verbs and 23.4% for actions on HD-EPIC, indicating the dataset's increased difficulty compared to previous benchmarks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current state-of-the-art video-language models, including Gemini Pro, perform substantially below human levels on comprehensive egocentric VQA tasks, particularly in 3D perception, temporal reasoning, and fine-grained semantic understanding.
> - The combination of unscripted 'in-the-wild' data with dense, interconnected, multi-modal annotations creates a challenging benchmark that effectively diagnosticates specific weaknesses in modern AI's ability to interpret complex human activities.
> - Egocentric gaze plays a crucial role in interaction anticipation, with 94.8% of object pick-ups and 88.5% of object put-downs being primed by preceding gaze fixation on the object's location.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of video understanding, particularly egocentric (first-person) vision, has seen explosive growth, fueled by the development of large-scale datasets and powerful foundation models. Egocentric videos, captured from a wearer's perspective, offer unique insights into human activities, interactions with objects, and environmental context, which are distinct from third-person perspectives. However, they also present specific challenges such such as severe camera motion, frequent occlusions, and subtle hand-object interactions.

Prior research has generated several notable egocentric datasets, each with its strengths and limitations:
- **Large-scale datasets** like **Ego4D** have provided thousands of hours of egocentric footage, making them ideal for training large models. However, these datasets often feature sparse annotations, especially for tasks requiring fine-grained detail or 3D grounding.
- In contrast, **richly annotated datasets** like **Assembly101** or **Aria Digital Twin** tend to be either synthetic or collected in highly controlled laboratory settings. While offering detailed labels, their controlled nature limits their realism and generalizability to diverse, unscripted real-world scenarios.
- **EPIC-KITCHENS**, a predecessor to HD-EPIC, successfully captured unscripted kitchen activities on a large scale. While it provided dense action narrations and object annotations, it lacked comprehensive 3D grounding, precise nutritional information, explicit 'how' and 'why' for actions, and extensive gaze data.

**HD-EPIC strategically positions itself to bridge this critical gap.** It aims to deliver the "most densely annotated dataset of unscripted recordings," combining the realism of in-the-wild data (like Ego4D and EPIC-KITCHENS) with the highly detailed, multi-modal annotations previously found only in controlled environments (like Aria Digital Twin). By collecting new, unscripted footage from diverse home kitchens, HD-EPIC ensures novel content that hasn't been used to pre-train existing models, providing a true zero-shot validation benchmark.

The work directly contributes to the development and evaluation of **multimodal Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **Video-Language Models (VLMs)**. As foundation models become increasingly capable, there is a growing need for benchmarks that can rigorously test their holistic understanding across complex, long-form videos involving human-object-environment interactions, temporal reasoning, and 3D perception. HD-EPIC steps into this role by providing an unprecedented level of interconnected ground-truth labels that demand advanced reasoning from AI models, pushing the boundaries of what current state-of-the-art systems can achieve in embodied AI.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objectives and motivations behind the creation of the HD-EPIC dataset are multifaceted, stemming from identified limitations in existing egocentric vision datasets and the evolving needs of multimodal AI research:

1. **Bridge the Realism-Detail Gap**: The central motivation is to create a dataset that combines the best aspects of current egocentric datasets: the realism and diversity of **unscripted, in-the-wild recordings** (typical of large-scale datasets like Ego4D and EPIC-KITCHENS) with the **highly detailed, interconnected, and multi-modal ground-truth annotations** traditionally found only in controlled or synthetic environments (e.g., precise 3D object tracking, scene digital twins, explicit action semantics). Existing datasets often sacrifice one for the other, leaving a significant void for comprehensive model evaluation.

2. **Enable Comprehensive Validation of Video-Language Models (VLMs)**: With the rapid advancements in foundation models, particularly VLMs capable of processing long video contexts, there is a pressing need for benchmarks that can rigorously test their understanding beyond simple action recognition. HD-EPIC is designed to serve as a challenging **validation dataset** for these models, assessing their capabilities across a wide array of complex tasks, including:
 - **Long-term temporal reasoning**: Understanding multi-day activities, multi-step recipes, and object itineraries.
 - **Fine-grained semantic understanding**: Discerning "what," "how," and "why" actions are performed, and tracking nutritional values.
 - **Embodied AI and 3D perception**: Reasoning about object locations, movements, and interactions within a 3D environment, informed by gaze.
 - **Multimodal fusion**: Integrating visual, audio, and gaze cues for holistic scene understanding.

3. **Expose Limitations of Current AI Models**: By creating an exceptionally dense and interconnected annotation scheme and a challenging VQA benchmark, the authors aim to highlight the shortcomings of even the most powerful current VLMs (like Gemini Pro). The low benchmark scores achieved by these models serve as a clear motivation to drive future research towards more robust, nuanced, and human-like understanding of egocentric experiences.

4. **Provide Novel and Diverse Annotation Types**: HD-EPIC introduces several novel annotation types, such as ingredient nutrition tracking, full 3D digital twins of diverse home kitchens, precise gaze-priming for object interactions, and explicit 'how' and 'why' clauses for actions. These novel labels are motivated by the desire to enable research into previously unexplored aspects of human behavior and embodied perception.

In essence, HD-EPIC is motivated by the aspiration to push the boundaries of egocentric AI, moving beyond superficial recognition to a truly holistic, context-aware, and embodied understanding of human activities in real-world settings, thereby serving as a critical tool for developing the next generation of intelligent systems.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The creation of HD-EPIC involved a meticulous multi-stage methodology encompassing data collection, multi-video SLAM processing, and a highly detailed multi-tiered annotation pipeline.

#### 3.1. Data Collection Protocol

The dataset was collected from **9 participants** who engaged in a long-term commitment (around 50 hours each), recording their **unscripted daily kitchen activities for at least three consecutive days**. This "in-the-wild" approach contrasts with controlled lab settings, ensuring high realism and diversity.
- **Equipment**: Participants were equipped with **Project Aria glasses**, a multi-sensor platform providing 30 FPS RGB video (1408x1408), 60 FPS eye tracking, and 30 FPS SLAM data. In addition, participants received digital weighing scales and a smartphone with the MyFitnessPal app for nutritional tracking.
- **Recording Instructions**: Participants were instructed to wear the glasses and record all activities each time they entered their kitchen, stopping when they left. This resulted in **156 videos, totaling 41.3 hours (4.46 million frames)**, with an average video length of 15.9 minutes.
- **Recipe Documentation**: Following recording, participants provided detailed information on the **69 recipes** they prepared, including source and modifications. They also manually logged ingredient weights and nutritional values via MyFitnessPal, allowing for precise nutritional tracking of dishes.
- **Narrations**: Participants watched their recordings and provided dense, descriptive narrations of their actions using a web-based tool. Crucially, they were asked to describe "what, how, and why" they performed actions, leading to significantly richer and denser narrations (3.8x more words/min than Ego4D) with 81% unique sentences.
- **Post-Processing**: Meta's Aria Machine Perception Services (MPS) were used to process the raw VRS files. This included generating singular **multi-day point clouds for each kitchen**, 1kHz 6DoF camera trajectories, and eye gaze direction. Faces and identifiable information were anonymized to ensure privacy.

#### 3.2. Multi-Tiered Annotation Pipeline

The core of HD-EPIC's contribution lies in its extensive and interconnected annotations:

1. **Recipe Steps and Ingredients**:
 - **Prep-Step Pairs**: The pipeline introduced "prep" annotations alongside "step" annotations for recipes. Prep includes all essential actions to get ready for a step (e.g., gathering ingredients for "chop tomato").
 - **Nutritional Tracking**: Temporal segments for **weighing and adding ingredients** were annotated. OCR readings of scales (manually verified) provided exact ingredient weights. This enables tracking the nutrition of a dish as ingredients are incorporated.

2. **Fine-Grained Actions**:
 - **Transcription**: Participant audio narrations were automatically transcribed using an ensemble of models (Whisper, Qwen-Audio, Nemo) and manually corrected.
 - **Action Boundaries**: Precise start and end times were manually labeled for **59,454 actions**, with a mean duration of 2.0 seconds. A robust inter-annotator agreement process ensured quality.
 - **Parsing and Clustering**: Open-vocabulary narrations were parsed into verbs, nouns, and hands (left/right/both) using spaCy. Crucially, "how" and "why" clauses were extracted from 16,004 and 11,540 narrations, respectively. Nouns and verbs were clustered hierarchically, aligning with and expanding upon EPIC-KITCHENS categories.
 - **Sound Annotations**: Following EPIC-SOUNDS methodology, 50,968 audio events across 44 classes were temporally annotated, focusing on binaural audio to reduce noise.

3. **Digital Twins: Scene & Object Movements**:
 - **Scene Digital Twin**: For each kitchen, a 3D digital twin was created in Blender based on MPS point clouds. This included manually curating and labeling **413 kitchen fixtures** (e.g., cupboards, drawers, appliances).
 - **Hand Masks**: A small set of hand masks were manually annotated per video, used to automatically segment 7.7M hand masks (3.9M right, 3.8M left) using SAM2, with subsequent manual correction for noisy masks.
 - **Moving Objects in 2D**: Temporal segments of object movement were annotated, with 2D bounding boxes at the onset and end of motion. This yielded **19,900 object movement tracks** and 36,900 bounding boxes.
 - **Object Masks to 3D**: Pixel-level segmentations for objects were obtained by initializing with iterative SAM2 and then manually correcting. These 2D masks were then lifted to **3D bounding boxes** using dense depth estimates and 2D-to-3D sparse correspondences from MPS.
 - **Object-Scene Interactions**: 3D object locations were associated with the closest fixture, with manual verification.
 - **Priming Object Movement**: Gaze and 3D object locations were combined to identify "gaze priming"—when an object's location is attended to before pick-up or put-down. 94.8% of feasible pick-ups were primed (avg. 4.0s before), and 88.5% of feasible put-downs (avg. 2.6s before).
 - **Long-Term Object Tracking (Itineraries)**: Object movements were linked across long videos to form complete trajectories, leveraging the lifted 3D locations for efficient annotation.

This comprehensive annotation strategy, leading to an average of **263 annotations per minute**, fundamentally distinguishes HD-EPIC as the most densely and richly annotated unscripted egocentric video dataset to date.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

HD-EPIC contributes a substantial and uniquely annotated dataset, and its benchmarks reveal significant challenges for current state-of-the-art multimodal AI models.

#### 4.1. Dataset Statistics

The dataset comprises **41 hours of multi-day unscripted egocentric recordings** across **9 diverse home kitchens**. This footage covers **69 unique recipes**, yielding:
- **59,454 fine-grained actions** (mean duration 2.0s)
- **50,968 audio events** across 44 classes
- **20,000 object movements** with 36,900 bounding boxes
- **37,000 object masks lifted to 3D**
- **413 kitchen fixtures** with digital twins
- Over 16,000 "how" and 11,000 "why" action clauses
- An average of **263 annotations per minute** of video, excluding generated hand masks.

#### 4.2. The HD-EPIC VQA Benchmark

The core validation effort is a challenging **Visual Question Answering (VQA) benchmark** consisting of **26,650 multiple-choice questions** derived directly from the dense annotations. These questions span **30 prototypes** across **7 distinct categories**:
1. **Recipe**: e.g., recipe recognition, step localization, prep localization.
2. **Ingredient**: e.g., ingredient retrieval, weight identification, adding order.
3. **Nutrition**: e.g., image/video nutrition estimation, nutrition change during cooking.
4. **Fine-grained Action**: e.g., action recognition, how/why recognition, action localization.
5. **3D Perception**: e.g., fixture location, object location, fixture interaction counting.
6. **Object Motion**: e.g., object movement itinerary, object movement counting, stationary object localization.
7. **Gaze**: e.g., gaze estimation, interaction anticipation.

**Performance of State-of-the-Art VLMs**:
The benchmark reveals a stark gap between human performance and leading AI models:
- A **sample human baseline** achieved **90.3% accuracy** on a subset of the VQA tasks.
- **Gemini Pro**, a closed-source, state-of-the-art VLM with the longest context window, achieved only **37.6% average accuracy**. While performing best, it struggled significantly, particularly with object motion and gaze categories.
- Other VLMs like **LLaVA-Video (32.4%)**, **LongVA (29.3%)**, and **VideoLlama 2 (27.4%)** performed even lower.
- Blind (language-only) models like **Llama 3.2 (26.5%)** performed barely above random chance (20%).
- Models struggled with **all video lengths**, performing worst on inputs exceeding one minute.

**Analysis of VLM Failures**: Qualitative analysis highlighted common failure modes:
- **Recipe**: Models confuse steps with common objects or actions.
- **Ingredient/Nutrition**: Poorly estimate ingredient weights (easily readable by humans from scales), leading to nutritional errors.
- **Fine-grained Action**: Struggle when answers share similar nouns or verbs.
- **3D/Object Motion**: Confusion with relative directions (left/right) and fixture types (counters/drawers).
- **Gaze**: Often default to recently moved objects rather than actual fixation points.

#### 4.3. Action and Sound Recognition Benchmarks

Beyond VQA, HD-EPIC provides benchmarks for fundamental recognition tasks:
- **Action Recognition**: State-of-the-art models (SlowFast, Omnivore, MotionFormer-HR, VideoMAE-L, TIM), fine-tuned on EPIC-KITCHENS-100, achieved **only 51.3% for verbs, 36.1% for nouns, and 23.4% for actions** on HD-EPIC. This is significantly lower than their performance on EPIC-KITCHENS-100, indicating that HD-EPIC presents a much harder and more diverse challenge.
- **Sound Recognition**: Audio models (SSAST, ASF, TIM), trained on EPIC-Sounds, also showed a **large performance drop (25-28% absolute accuracy)** when evaluated on HD-EPIC. This suggests current audio recognition models are not robust to new scenes or recording devices.

#### 4.4. Long-Term Video Object Segmentation (VOS)

HD-EPIC introduces a novel long-term VOS benchmark with 1000 sequences, featuring an average of 6.3 objects per sequence and durations significantly longer than existing benchmarks (avg. 561 seconds).
- Models like **Cutie (48.6% J&F)** and **SAM2 (47.4% J&F)** showed limited performance on object segmentation. While SAM2 excelled with hands (89.1% J&F), it performed worse than Cutie on general objects (27.0% vs. 34.6%), highlighting the difficulty in segmenting diverse, often occluded, objects in real egocentric video.

Overall, the findings consistently demonstrate that HD-EPIC serves as a profoundly challenging benchmark, exposing the current limitations of leading AI models across various granularities of egocentric video understanding.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

The HD-EPIC dataset represents a significant leap forward in egocentric vision research, with profound implications for the development and evaluation of artificial intelligence systems. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

1. **Bridging the Realism-Detail Divide**: HD-EPIC uniquely resolves a long-standing trade-off in dataset creation: the choice between real-world, unscripted complexity and meticulously detailed annotations. By achieving both, it offers a gold standard for evaluating AI models in conditions that closely mimic real-life interactions, pushing the field beyond synthetic or sparsely annotated scenarios. This realism is crucial for developing AI that can genuinely assist humans in daily tasks.

2. **Unlocking Holistic Egocentric Understanding**: The multi-tiered, interconnected nature of HD-EPIC's annotations enables a holistic understanding of egocentric activities, far beyond simple action recognition. The inclusion of recipe steps, ingredient nutrition, explicit "how" and "why" actions, 3D digital twins, object movements in 3D, and gaze-priming allows researchers to investigate complex cognitive processes, such as:
 - **Goal-oriented reasoning**: Understanding long-term procedural tasks like cooking.
 - **Embodied spatial cognition**: Reasoning about objects and their interactions within a dynamic 3D environment from a first-person perspective.
 - **Anticipatory intelligence**: Predicting future actions or object interactions based on visual and gaze cues.
 - **Multimodal fusion**: Developing models that can seamlessly integrate visual, auditory, linguistic, and geometric information.

3. **A Critical Benchmark for Foundation Models**: The VQA benchmark, with its diverse question types and demonstrably low performance of even cutting-edge VLMs like Gemini Pro (37.6% vs. 90.3% human baseline), clearly highlights the current limitations of these powerful models. HD-EPIC serves as a crucial diagnostic tool, pinpointing specific weaknesses in areas like fine-grained temporal understanding, 3D spatial reasoning, numerical inference (e.g., nutrition), and complex causal relationships. This will directly guide future research in improving the robustness, accuracy, and generalizability of multimodal AI.

4. **Driving Research in Novel AI Capabilities**: The unique annotations introduce new research directions:
 - **Nutritional AI**: Enabling AI to track and understand nutritional aspects of food preparation from video.
 - **Explanatory AI**: Training models to provide not just *what* happened, but *how* and *why*, mirroring human-level explanation.
 - **Proactive Assistance**: Leveraging gaze priming to develop AI assistants that can anticipate user needs and provide timely support.

5. **Accessibility and Community Resource**: By making the dataset (videos, audio, gaze, Blender digital twin, camera poses) and all annotations publicly available, HD-EPIC provides an invaluable resource for the global AI research community. This will foster widespread experimentation, benchmarking, and innovation in egocentric vision and embodied AI.

In conclusion, HD-EPIC is not merely another dataset; it is a meticulously crafted scientific instrument designed to push the boundaries of AI's ability to perceive, understand, and reason about the complex, unscripted world from a human perspective. Its challenges will undoubtedly stimulate significant advancements in multimodal learning, 3D perception, and embodied intelligence, ultimately bringing us closer to developing truly intelligent and helpful AI assistants for daily life.
