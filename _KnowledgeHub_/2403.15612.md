# InterFusion: Text-Driven Generation of 3D Human-Object Interaction

> [!summary] Summary
> InterFusion generates 3D human-object interaction scenes from text with no 3D interaction supervision. A codebook built from 55K pseudo-SMPL poses estimated from synthesized interaction images retrieves an anchor pose from the text; that pose then constrains separate human and object NeRFs optimized by local-global score distillation. It reaches 0.3308 CLIP score and 65.57% GPT-4V preference, beating DreamFusion, Magic3D and TextMesh.

## Problem

- Mocap and scanned interaction datasets are costly, lab-bound, and cover few scenarios, so data-supervised methods stay confined to their captured distributions.
- Text-to-3D distillation methods target single subjects; on multi-concept prompts they blur textures, drop either the human or the object, and produce wrong contacts.
- Compositional scene generators need user-supplied layouts or bounding boxes, which cannot express the tight spatial relations of a body wrapped around an interacting object.

## Method

- ==Syn-HOI== pose dataset plus codebook: 235 ChatGPT interaction prompts drive Stable Diffusion images, PIXIE estimates 55K pseudo-SMPL poses, K-Means builds a 2,048-entry CLIP-indexed codebook, GPT-4V picks the anchor.
- Separate **H-NeRF** and **O-NeRF** with anchor-pose ==occupancy losses==: COAP occupancy forces human density inside the anchor while penalizing object density there, preventing interpenetration.
- ==Local-global Score Distillation Sampling== combining human, object and alpha-composited interaction score distillation, with gradient truncation toward the human, annealed loss weights, head-focused prompts, and a camera-tracing module.

## Results

- CLIP score **0.3308** and **65.57%** GPT-4V selection, versus **0.3179** and **11.48%** for Magic3D, **0.3027** and **8.20%** for DreamFusion, **0.2761** and **1.64%** for TextMesh.
- CLIP R-Precision reaches **83.6%** and FID in CLIP space drops to **63.7**, ahead of MVDream at **77.0%** and ProlificDreamer at **67.2%**.
- Ablations: dropping interaction SDS collapses GPT-4V preference to **1.64%**, dropping object SDS to **4.92%**, dropping the object geometry constraint to **16.39%**, against **77.05%** full.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Synthesized images plus off-the-shelf pose estimation can substitute for scarce ground-truth 3D interaction capture, widening pose diversity beyond lab mocap archives.
> - An explicit geometric anchor decomposes an entangled multi-concept prompt, letting diffusion priors optimize each part without one concept starving the other.
> - CLIP score barely separates methods here; VLM-based judging exposed gaps the metric missed, a caution when evaluating compositional 3D generation.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How this work fits into the broader research landscape

The generation of realistic 3D content, particularly scenes involving human-object interactions (HOI), is a significant challenge within computer vision and computer graphics. This field has implications for applications such as virtual reality, augmented reality, animation, and embodied artificial intelligence.

Historically, approaches to 3D HOI generation have largely depended on motion capture (mocap) datasets or physics-based simulations. Mocap datasets, while providing high fidelity, are constrained by the specific scenarios they capture and are labor-intensive and costly to produce. These limitations have resulted in a deficiency in generating diverse and contextually rich HOI scenes, especially for novel or complex interactions.

Recent advancements in text-to-3D methods, which leverage large pre-trained text-to-image diffusion models, have marked a shift in 3D content creation. These methods enable the generation of 3D objects from textual descriptions without direct 3D supervision. However, the direct application of these text-to-3D techniques to the more complex task of HOI generation often results in suboptimal outcomes, such as blurry textures or incorrect interactions. This is attributed to the scarcity of paired text-interaction data for training and the inherent difficulties diffusion-based methods face when simultaneously generating multiple concepts with intricate spatial relationships.

InterFusion positions itself within this evolving landscape by addressing these specific challenges. It proposes a zero-shot, text-driven framework for 3D HOI generation that attempts to overcome the limitations of data scarcity and multi-concept generation by introducing 3D human pose estimations as geometric priors. This approach distinguishes it from previous methods that primarily relied on ground truth 3D interaction data as supervision, aiming to enable the generation of a wider array of realistic and detailed 3D HOI scenes across diverse environments. The work draws inspiration from and builds upon advancements in human-object interaction synthesis, text-to-3D content synthesis, and compositional scene generation, seeking to integrate these fields for a more robust HOI solution.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of this research is to develop a method for generating detailed 3D human-object interaction (HOI) scenes from textual descriptions in a zero-shot, text-to-3D manner. This entails creating 3D scenes comprising a human model and an object model that adhere to specified appearance styles and exhibit a tailored spatial relationship accurately reflecting the described interaction.

The motivation for this work stems from two principal challenges identified in the existing research landscape:
1. **Limitations of direct text-to-3D methods for HOI:** Current text-to-3D techniques, when directly applied to the HOI task, frequently produce unsatisfactory results. These include blurry textures and physically implausible or incorrect interactions. This deficiency is primarily attributed to two factors:
 - **Lack of paired text-interaction data:** There is a relative scarcity of large-scale datasets containing paired textual descriptions and corresponding 3D interaction data, which hinders the training of robust generative models for this specific task.
 - **Difficulty in multi-concept generation:** Diffusion-based models encounter inherent difficulties in simultaneously generating multiple distinct concepts (i.e., a human and an object) that must maintain complex and semantically coherent spatial relationships.
2. **Constraints of traditional HOI generation approaches:** Earlier methods for 3D HOI synthesis, such as those relying on motion capture (mocap) datasets or physics-based simulations, have significant limitations. Mocap datasets are expensive and labor-intensive to produce, and they are inherently restricted to the specific scenarios captured. This results in a notable gap in the ability to generate diverse, novel, and contextually rich HOI scenes.

To address these motivations, the authors formulated two key observations that guide their proposed solution:
1. While extensive 3D interaction data is challenging to acquire, estimating 3D human poses based on textual interaction descriptions is more feasible. These pose estimations can serve as valuable geometric priors.
2. Integrating pose estimation into the generation process can simplify the text-to-3D conversion, particularly during the geometry optimization for human bodies. It also provides additional constraints for object generation, ensuring accurate placement and alignment with the textual semantics. Furthermore, separating the generation of the human body and object, followed by joint refinement with the estimated pose, can lead to a more coherent and detailed synthesis of the interaction scene.

Based on these objectives and motivations, the InterFusion framework was designed as a two-stage approach to overcome the aforementioned challenges and leverage the identified opportunities.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

InterFusion is presented as a two-stage framework for zero-shot, text-driven 3D human-object interaction (HOI) generation. The input to the system is a triplet of text descriptions, T = {T_H, T_O, T_I}, specifying the desired human style, object style, and interaction type, respectively. The output is a detailed 3D scene, ψ = {ψ_H, ψ_O}, comprising a human model and an object model.

#### 3.1. Preliminaries

The method relies on **Score Distillation Sampling (SDS)**, a technique introduced by DreamFusion. SDS optimizes 3D model parameters (ψ) by minimizing the difference between predicted noise and added noise, guided by a pre-trained 2D text-to-image diffusion model (ϕ) and text embeddings (y). This process leverages the diffusion model's ability to evaluate the likelihood of rendered 2D images (x) matching a given text prompt.

#### 3.2. Anchor Pose Generation

The first stage focuses on generating a 3D anchor pose from the input interaction text (T_I), which serves as a geometric prior. This stage addresses the scarcity of diverse 3D interaction pose data:

- **Synthetic HOI Image Dataset:** Text-to-image diffusion models (e.g., Stable Diffusion) are used to create a large-scale dataset of synthesized HOI images. ChatGPT generates diverse prompts following "verb-ing a/an/the object" patterns (235 prompts), covering various daily interactions. Images without humans are filtered.
- **3D Human Pose Estimation:** The pre-trained PIXIE model extracts 3D human poses (pseudo-SMPL poses) from these synthetic images, resulting in a Syn-HOI pose dataset of 55K poses.
- **Pose Feature Embeddings:** To link poses with text, images are rendered from multiple perspectives for each SMPL pose. CLIP's image encoder then derives an averaged pose feature embedding for each pose.
- **Pose Codebook Construction:** K-Means clustering is applied to these pose feature embeddings to identify 2,048 key pose centroids, forming a codebook where each cluster represents similar interactions.
- **Anchor Pose Retrieval:** For a given input text T_I, CLIP's text encoder extracts its feature embedding. This embedding queries the codebook to retrieve the top-k (k=7) most similar poses based on cosine similarity.
- **Final Pose Selection:** GPT-4V is employed to select the most precise pose from the top-k candidates as the final anchor pose. Alternatively, sampling from the corresponding cluster allows for diverse results.
- **Neural Occupancy Representation:** The COAP model, a neural occupancy representation of the articulated human body based on SMPL parameters, is integrated. This provides an occupancy prediction network f(x; β, θ) that indicates whether a 3D query point resides within the human body for a given pose (θ) and shape (β). This allows for restricting geometry optimization based on the acquired pose.

#### 3.3. Pose-Guided HOI Generation

The second stage utilizes the anchor pose and input text to guide the generation of a detailed 3D HOI scene. This stage operates in a local-global manner:

- **Decomposed Generation:** The human (H-NeRF) and object (O-NeRF) models are optimized separately but refined simultaneously within a global context to ensure cohesive alignment.
- **Textual Guidance:** The input text triplet (T_H, T_O, T_I) undergoes processing to provide distinct semantic guidance for each component via SDS, utilizing the pre-trained DeepFloyd model.
- **Camera Tracing Module:** A dynamic camera tracing module adaptively adjusts the camera pose during optimization. It focuses on the target's center (either the entire interaction scene or just the object) by aligning with the average position of voxels with high occupancy probability, ensuring optimal rendering detail.

##### 3.3.1. H-NeRF (Human Model Generation)

- **Representation:** Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF).
- **Semantic Guidance:** Guided by text T_H (human style) for pose-specific human avatar generation via SDS.
- **Head Region Optimization:** A specialized optimization process focuses on the head region. The head's location is determined by COAP, and the text prompt is augmented (e.g., "the head of *") to ensure detailed attention.
- **Loss Function (L_H_SDS):** Combines SDS terms for the whole human and the head region.
- **Geometric Constraint (L_H_geo):** The human model evolves directly from the anchor pose. Points within the anchor are encouraged to be occupied (via cross-entropy loss against COAP's occupancy prediction). Points outside the anchor can also be occupied, but with probabilities decreasing with distance from the anchor's surface, allowing geometric details to form while maintaining the overall pose structure.

##### 3.3.2. O-NeRF (Object Model Generation)

- **Representation:** NeRF.
- **Semantic Guidance:** Guided by text T_I (interaction type) and T_O (object style) via SDS.
- **Loss Function (L_O_SDS):** Combines SDS terms for the interaction scene (x_I) and the object itself (x_O).
- **Interaction Scene Rendering:** The interaction scene (x_I) is generated using an alpha-composited rendering that integrates H-NeRF and O-NeRF. Alpha values derived from density determine each component's contribution to the scene's color. Gradient truncation is applied towards the human model to ensure balanced optimization and prevent the object from dominating the generation.
- **Geometric Constraint (L_O_geo):** To prevent object penetration into the human body, a cross-entropy loss term prevents occupancy of points within the human anchor pose by the object model, ensuring physical plausibility.

##### 3.3.3. Optimization

- **Total Loss Function:** The overall optimization minimizes L = L_H_SDS + λ_1 L_O_SDS + L_H_geo + λ_2 L_O_geo + λ_3 L_reg.
- **Regularizations (L_reg):** Includes orientation loss to encourage forward-facing normals and opacity regularization to separate from the background.
- **Weight Annealing:** Loss weights (λ_1, λ_2, λ_3) are annealed during the 10,000 optimization iterations. λ_1 (object SDS weight) linearly increases from 0 to 1. λ_2 (object geometric constraint weight) varies (0.001, 0.01, 0.1) across iterations to control initial object generation, human information elimination, and final contact. λ_3 (regularization weight) remains constant.
- **Training Details:** Images are rendered at 64x64 resolution under randomly sampled camera views. DeepFloyd is used with specific time steps and a classifier-free guidance strength of 20. The Adam optimizer with a learning rate of 0.01 is employed. Each 3D scene generation requires approximately 1.5 hours on a single Tesla V100 GPU.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The evaluation of InterFusion involved both qualitative and quantitative comparisons against baseline methods, as well as ablation studies to assess the contribution of individual components.

#### 4.1. Qualitative Evaluations

- **Comparison with Baselines:** InterFusion was compared against DreamFusion, Magic3D, and TextMesh. The results indicated that InterFusion generated 3D HOI scenes that were more stable, of higher quality, and more consistent with input text descriptions compared to the baselines.
 - Baseline models frequently exhibited issues such as blurry textures, incomplete human body parts, incorrect physical interactions, the mixing of human legs, or failure to generate entire objects (e.g., only a red baseball cap generated instead of a person, or an incomplete policewoman). They also struggled to maintain focus when generating multiple concepts or less common targets.
 - InterFusion, by employing an explicit decomposed optimization and spatially and semantically guided attention, mitigated these issues, leading to more coherent and plausible results.
- **Diversity and Control:** The method demonstrated support for diverse interaction poses within a single interaction type and could generate numerous human and object styles while keeping the interaction type fixed.

#### 4.2. Quantitative Evaluations

- **Main Quantitative Metrics:** The primary metrics used were CLIP score and GPT-4V select (%), which evaluated the semantic similarity between text prompts and generated images, and the completeness/correctness of physical interactions, respectively.
 - InterFusion (Ours-HC) achieved a CLIP score of 0.3308 and a GPT-4V select score of 65.57%.
 - Among baselines, Magic3D was the next highest with a CLIP score of 0.3179 and GPT-4V select of 11.48%. DreamFusion and TextMesh scored lower.
 - An object-centric baseline (Ours-OC) achieved a CLIP score of 0.3203 and GPT-4V select of 13.11%, performing better than other baselines but still significantly below InterFusion (Ours-HC). This highlights the importance of human pose priors.
- **Additional Metrics:** Comparisons with MVDream and ProlificDreamer further showed InterFusion's robustness.
 - InterFusion achieved an R-Precision of 83.6%, outperforming all compared baselines (DreamFusion 68.8%, Magic3D 73.8%, TextMesh 47.5%, MVDream 77.0%, ProlificDreamer 67.2%).
 - InterFusion also exhibited the lowest FID_CLIP score of 63.7%, indicating better visual fidelity compared to all baselines (ranging from 64.8% to 70.0%).
- **GPT-4V Assessment:** GPT-4V was used to select the most 3D justifiable result from multiple generated outputs, considering full human bodies, complete objects, and correct physical interactions. This evaluation method, leveraging the advanced image understanding capabilities of GPT-4V, further supported the superior performance of InterFusion.

#### 4.3. Ablation Studies

Ablation studies were conducted to assess the effectiveness of the proposed components and loss terms:

- **Syn-HOI-pose (Anchor Pose Generation):** The synthesized anchor poses from the pseudo-pose dataset provided more accurately fitted anchor poses for various interaction types compared to poses retrieved using AvatarCLIP from mocap datasets. This indicated the pseudo-poses' potential for greater diversity.
- **Semantic Guidance:**
 - The absence of object-specific semantic guidance (L_O_SDS) compromised the semantic consistency of the object, leading to noisy outcomes influenced by the human body and incorrect contact regions.
 - The absence of interaction-specific semantic guidance (L_I_SDS) resulted in generated objects being confined to the remaining space without exhibiting interaction with the human. Interaction SDS was identified as the most crucial element for semantically and visually plausible results.
- **Geometric Constraint (L_O_geo):** Relying solely on semantic guidance proved insufficient. Without the geometric constraint that prevents object occupancy within the human anchor pose, generations were unstable, leading to object degeneration, spatial conflicts (e.g., objects penetrating the human body), and flawed interactions. This highlights the importance of collision prevention.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

InterFusion represents a contribution to the field of 3D content generation, particularly concerning human-object interactions (HOI). The significance and potential impact of this work can be summarized as follows:

- **Addressing Fundamental Challenges:** The method directly confronts two primary challenges in text-to-3D HOI generation: the scarcity of large-scale, paired text-interaction 3D data and the inherent difficulty for generative models to synthesize multiple concepts with complex, coherent spatial relationships. By introducing a two-stage framework that leverages pose estimation as a geometric prior, InterFusion mitigates the dependency on extensive 3D interaction datasets.
- **Novel Framework for Zero-Shot HOI:** InterFusion is among the initial frameworks to enable zero-shot 3D human-object interaction generation from textual descriptions. This capability allows for the creation of diverse and contextually rich HOI scenes without prior exposure to specific 3D interaction data, breaking through a significant data limitation that constrained previous approaches.
- **Enhanced Generation Quality and Realism:** The local-global optimization process, guided by anchor poses and tailored semantic prompts, facilitates the production of 3D HOI scenes with high realism, detailed appearance, and consistent geometry. The qualitative and quantitative results demonstrate significant improvements over existing state-of-the-art methods, yielding more stable and plausible interactions.
- **Controllable 3D Content Creation:** The decomposed representation of human and object models within InterFusion enables controllable, text-conditioned editing. Users can selectively refine human-only or object-only components (e.g., changing human attire or object type/color) while preserving the intricate interaction relationships and spatial coherence. This feature offers increased flexibility for creators.
- **Broad Application Potential:** The capability to generate diverse and realistic 3D HOI scenes from text has wide-ranging implications across several domains:
 - **Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR/AR):** Enables the rapid creation of dynamic and interactive virtual environments populated with believable avatars and objects.
 - **Animation and Game Development:** Streamlines the process of generating complex character animations and scene compositions, reducing manual labor and production costs.
 - **Embodied AI and Robotics:** Provides a rich source of synthetic data for training and evaluating AI agents in scenarios involving human-robot interaction, object manipulation, and understanding of human actions.
 - **3D Content Creation:** Offers artists and designers a powerful, intuitive tool to generate 3D assets and scenes based on natural language descriptions, expanding creative possibilities.
- **Foundation for Future Research:** The framework establishes a foundation for future work in several areas, including improving local interaction accuracy (e.g., precise hand-object contacts), incorporating more advanced visual language models (VLMs) and large language models (LLMs) for enhanced understanding and generation, and extending the framework to generate dynamic HOI motions over time.

In conclusion, InterFusion contributes a robust and flexible framework for text-driven 3D HOI generation, addressing critical limitations in data availability and multi-concept synthesis. Its capacity for high-quality, controllable, and diverse scene generation holds the potential to significantly impact various fields requiring advanced 3D content creation.
