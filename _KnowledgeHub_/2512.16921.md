# Differences That Matter: Auditing Models for Capability Gap Discovery and Rectification

> [!summary] Summary
> An automated framework named AuditDM helps diagnose and improve multimodal large language models (MLLMs) by training an MLLM to act as an auditor, generating question-image pairs that reveal specific capability gaps. This method identifies over 20 failure categories and facilitates annotation-free rectification, allowing smaller models to outperform larger ones on various benchmarks without manual labeling.

## Problem

- Conventional MLLM evaluation methods provide sparse, aggregate scores that mask heterogeneous performance shifts and fail to offer interpretable insights into model failures.
- Understanding and addressing complex MLLM behaviors for real-world deployment is hindered by the prohibitive cost and lack of scalability of human-intensive evaluation.
- The absence of a systematic, interpretable framework for diagnosing specific capability gaps makes targeted rectification and continuous improvement of MLLMs challenging.

## Method

- Introduces **AuditDM**, an RL-based framework that trains an MLLM as an "==auditor==" to actively generate challenging question-image pairs.
- The auditor aims to maximize disagreement between a target MLLM and a reference ensemble, using generated or edited images and complex questions.
- Identified failure modes are converted into annotation-free synthetic data, enabling targeted fine-tuning and rectification of the target MLLM.

## Results

- AuditDM demonstrated a **91.1%** search success rate in identifying valid errors in PaliGemma2-**3B**, significantly outperforming prompt-engineered baselines.
- The framework automatically revealed over 20 distinct failure categories, including unexpected weaknesses like increased hallucination and struggles with emotion understanding in the larger PaliGemma2-**28B** model.
- Fine-tuning with AuditDM's annotation-free synthetic data consistently improved model performance, allowing PaliGemma2-**3B** to surpass its **28B** counterpart on benchmarks like AI2D, and Gemma3-**4B** to exceed Gemma3-**12B** on Seed-Bench-IMG, MMMU, and RealWorldQA.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Larger MLLMs do not universally outperform smaller variants; the PaliGemma2-28B model, for instance, showed a higher propensity for hallucination and struggled more with emotion and counting than its 3B counterpart.
> - Model auditing with generated challenging inputs can effectively pinpoint and interpret specific failure categories and brittle decision-making patterns in MLLMs.
> - Weakness-targeted synthetic data generated by an auditor provides an annotation-free and highly efficient method for fine-tuning, enabling smaller models to significantly close performance gaps with or even surpass larger models.
