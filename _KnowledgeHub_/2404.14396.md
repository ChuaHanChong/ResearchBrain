# SEED-X: Multimodal Models with Unified Multi-granularity Comprehension and Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Tencent AI Lab researchers introduce SEED-X, a multimodal large language model that unifies multi-granularity comprehension and generation by processing arbitrary-sized images and enabling precise image manipulation. It achieved an overall 51% accuracy on compositional image generation tasks like GenEval and competitive performance across various multimodal comprehension benchmarks, often surpassing or matching specialized models.

## Problem

- Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) struggled to effectively interpret and respond to diverse real-world user instructions.
- Prior MLLMs typically required fixed-resolution inputs, leading to information loss when processing images of arbitrary sizes and aspect ratios.
- A gap existed in unifying high-level instructional image generation with precise, low-level image manipulation and editing within a single model.

## Method

- Developed ==SEED-X==, a unified foundation model built on Llama2-chat-13B, enhanced with a pre-trained Vision Transformer for ==visual tokenization== and a ==two-stage de-tokenizer== for image generation.
- Implemented a ==dynamic resolution image encoding strategy== that divides images into sub-images processed by the visual encoder, augmented with extrapolatable 2D positional embeddings for spatial awareness.
- Employed a comprehensive multimodal pre-training regimen (including image-caption, grounded, interleaved data) followed by ==instruction tuning (LoRA)== on diverse tasks to enable multi-granularity comprehension and generation.

## Results

- The instruction-tuned SEED-X-I achieved competitive performance on established multimodal comprehension benchmarks (e.g., POPE, MME-P, VQAv2), often matching or exceeding specialized models.
- On the GenEval benchmark, SEED-X demonstrated an overall accuracy of **51%** for compositional image generation, showcasing strong instruction-following capabilities.
- SEED-X-Edit exhibited superior high-precision image manipulation, accurately performing specific edits while meticulously preserving low-level details of input images, outperforming existing models like Emu2-Gen and MGIE.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - A unified ViT-based visual tokenizer and a specialized two-stage de-tokenizer training process, which optimizes specific parts of an SD-XL U-Net, can effectively achieve both semantic consistency and fine-grained detail retention in image generation.
> - Processing arbitrary-sized images by dividing them into a grid of sub-images and incorporating dynamic 2D positional embeddings allows MLLMs to comprehend diverse visual inputs without fixed-resolution constraints.
> - Extensive multimodal pre-training and instruction tuning enable a single MLLM to function as a versatile AI assistant, proficient in tasks ranging from fine-grained object detection and reasoning to creative text-to-image generation and high-precision image editing.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) has seen rapid evolution, moving beyond text-only paradigms to integrate diverse data types like images, audio, and video. Pioneering works have enabled MLLMs to comprehend multimodal data by leveraging the powerful generalization capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs). More recently, research has extended MLLMs to not only understand but also generate multimodal content, with examples like Emu, CM3Leon, and the authors' own SEED-LLaMA. These models represent a crucial step towards creating more interactive and versatile AI systems.

However, despite these advancements, a significant gap persists between academic benchmarks and real-world applicability. Many existing MLLMs, while capable of basic multimodal comprehension and generation, often fall short in handling the complexities of real-world user instructions and diverse visual inputs. Specifically, the ability to process images of arbitrary sizes and aspect ratios without information loss, alongside performing multi-granularity image generation (ranging from high-level instructional generation to precise, low-level image manipulation), has remained a challenge. As highlighted in Table 1 of the paper, previous works typically support only a subset of these critical features, with none fully integrating all. For instance, some models might generate images from text but lack fine-grained editing capabilities, or they might struggle with diverse image resolutions.

SEED-X emerges within this landscape as a direct response to these limitations. It aims to unify these disparate capabilities into a single, versatile foundation model. By explicitly integrating arbitrary-size image comprehension, object detection, and multi-granularity image generation (including high-precision editing), SEED-X seeks to create a more robust and adaptable MLLM that can function as a truly capable AI assistant in open-world contexts. This work thus pushes the frontier by addressing the practical demands of real-world applications, moving beyond specialized capabilities towards a more generalized and comprehensive multimodal intelligence.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The primary objective of SEED-X is to **bridge the gap between the existing capabilities of multimodal foundation models and their applicability in real-world scenarios**. The authors observe that while MLLMs have made significant progress in vision-language understanding and generation, their effectiveness in practical, diverse user interactions remains constrained.

This overarching objective is driven by several key motivations:

1. **Limited Responsiveness to Diverse User Instructions:** Current MLLMs often struggle to effectively interpret and respond to a wide variety of user instructions, especially those involving complex visual interactions or requiring nuanced understanding of visual contexts. The goal is to create a model that can serve as a truly versatile AI assistant, capable of understanding multifaceted requests.
2. **Ineffective Interaction with Diverse Visual Data:** Existing models typically demand images of predefined, often square, resolutions, leading to information loss when processing real-world images of arbitrary sizes and aspect ratios. A key motivation is to overcome this limitation, enabling the model to comprehend visual data irrespective of its dimensions.
3. **Need for Multi-granularity Image Generation:** Real-world applications require not just the generation of new images from text prompts (high-level instructional generation) but also precise manipulation and editing of existing images (low-level image manipulation). Previous models often excel in one but not the other, creating a need for a unified approach that can handle both creative generation and fine-grained visual editing.
4. **Creation of a Unified and Versatile Foundation Model:** The authors are motivated to develop a single, cohesive foundation model that seamlessly integrates these advanced comprehension and generation capabilities. This contrasts with approaches that might involve chaining multiple specialized models, aiming for greater efficiency and coherence in multimodal interactions.
5. **Inspiring Future Research:** By demonstrating the potential of a truly versatile MLLM, the authors hope to inspire further research into developing foundation models that are robust, adaptable, and highly applicable across various real-world domains.

In essence, SEED-X is motivated by the desire to move MLLMs from impressive research prototypes to practical, everyday AI tools that can intelligently perceive, interpret, and modify the visual world according to human instruction.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

SEED-X introduces a unified and versatile foundation model designed to overcome the limitations of prior MLLMs by integrating two enhanced features: comprehending images of arbitrary sizes and ratios, and enabling multi-granularity image generation. The methodology is built around a powerful Large Language Model (LLM) backbone, specifically initialized from Llama2-chat-13B, augmented with sophisticated visual processing components.

The core of SEED-X's approach involves three main technical innovations:

1. **Visual Tokenization and De-tokenization:**
 - **Unified Mechanism:** A pre-trained Vision Transformer (ViT) acts as the visual tokenizer, converting images into numerical features for the LLM. This same tokenizer is then leveraged for image generation.
 - **Two-Stage De-tokenizer Training:**
 - **Stage 1 (Semantic Consistency):** A visual de-tokenizer is trained to decode realistic images from the ViT features (average-pooled to 64 visual embeddings). This de-tokenizer utilizes a learnable module connecting these embeddings to the U-Net of a pre-trained SD-XL model, optimizing only specific parts (cross-attention layers, keys/values in U-Net) to generate semantically consistent images.
 - **Stage 2 (Fine-grained Detail Retention):** The de-tokenizer is further fine-tuned to accept an additional "condition image" in its latent space. By concatenating the VAE-encoded latent of the condition image with the noisy latent input of the U-Net, the model learns to retain fine-grained details from the input image, crucial for high-precision image manipulation tasks. This decouples the training of the visual (de-)tokenizer from the MLLM, allowing each component to specialize.

2. **Dynamic Resolution Image Encoding:**
 - **Arbitrary Size Handling:** To overcome the fixed-resolution input limitation of standard MLLMs, SEED-X employs a dynamic resolution encoding strategy. It divides an input image into a grid of sub-images, each processed by the visual encoder at its native training resolution ($H_t \times W_t$). A globally resized version of the image also provides overall context.
 - **Positional Awareness:** To ensure the LLM understands the spatial arrangement of these sub-images, extrapolatable 2D positional embeddings are added to the visual features of each sub-image. These embeddings are computed based on the normalized center location of each sub-image within the grid, allowing the model to generalize to resolutions not encountered during training.

3. **Multimodal Pre-training and Instruction Tuning:**
 - **Pre-training (Stage I):** SEED-X is pre-trained on a massive, diverse dataset including image-caption pairs (some re-captioned for richer descriptions), grounded image-text data, interleaved image-text data, OCR data, and pure text. The training objectives are:
 - **Next-word prediction:** For text tokens and special bounding box tokens (`<box_start>`, `<loc-x_center>`, etc.) to enable detection and referencing abilities.
 - **Image feature regression:** A fixed number of learnable queries (64, matching the de-tokenizer) are fed into the LLM, and their output hidden states are trained to reconstruct the ViT features of the target images using an MSE loss. Special tokens (`<IMG>`, `</IMG>`) delineate the image generation context.
 - **Instruction Tuning (Stage II):** The pre-trained SEED-X undergoes multimodal instruction tuning using LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) on a combination of public and in-house datasets. This stage covers a broad spectrum of tasks, including VQA, conversational AI, image generation, image editing (using large-scale collected data), slide generation, storytelling, and virtual try-on. This fine-tuning leads to general models (e.g., SEED-X-I for comprehension and general generation) and specialized instruction-tuned models (e.g., SEED-X-Edit, SEED-X-PPT) tailored for specific real-world applications.

By integrating these components, SEED-X aims to robustly process and generate visual content at multiple levels of granularity, making it adaptable to a wide array of user instructions and visual inputs.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

SEED-X demonstrates significant advancements in both multimodal comprehension and generation, showcasing its effectiveness in bridging the gap towards real-world applicability.

**Quantitative Evaluation:**

- **Multimodal Comprehension:** Evaluated on a suite of established benchmarks (POPE, MME-P, MMB, SEED (img), VQAv2, GQA, MMMU, MM-Vet), the instruction-tuned model SEED-X-I achieves competitive performance. Notably, it records strong scores across various tasks, often surpassing or performing on par with models specifically designed for comprehension (e.g., LLaVA-v1.5, Qwen-VL-Chat) and significantly outperforming many other unified comprehension-and-generation MLLMs (Table 2). This indicates its robust understanding capabilities across different question types, object detection, and visual reasoning.
- **Image Generation:** On the challenging GenEval benchmark, which assesses compositional image properties (object co-occurrence, position, count, color, attributes), SEED-X achieves an overall accuracy of 51%. This performance is competitive with, and in some aspects surpasses, several dedicated image generation models like SDv2.1 and LDM, and demonstrates superior instruction-following capabilities compared to other unified MLLMs (Table 3). This indicates SEED-X's ability to generate images that accurately reflect complex textual instructions.

**Qualitative Evaluation:**

- **Real-world Applications (Versatility):** SEED-X, after specific instruction tuning, effectively functions as various multimodal AI assistants.
 - **Interactive Designer:** It can generate concept images without descriptive captions while explaining creative intent (e.g., AGI concept, two-story cabin). It can also suggest modifications to existing images and visualize them (e.g., adding decorations to a room).
 - **Knowledgeable Personal Assistant:** It demonstrates the ability to comprehend images of arbitrary sizes (e.g., analyzing refrigerator contents for diet-suitable foods, interpreting floor plans for room sizes) and provide relevant suggestions, even in multi-turn conversations. It can also generate structured outputs like slide layouts based on user input.
- **Image Generation and Manipulation:**
 - **Text-to-Image Generation:** Qualitative comparisons (Figure 9) show SEED-X-I generating images that are more closely aligned with complex descriptive captions and possess higher artistic quality compared to other MLLMs like Next-GPT, SEED-LLaMA-I, Emu2-Gen, and Gemini. This highlights the effectiveness of its pre-trained ViT tokenizer and de-tokenizer for high-quality generation.
 - **High-precision Image Manipulation:** SEED-X-Edit demonstrates superior capabilities in adhering to editing instructions while meticulously preserving low-level details of the input images. For example, it accurately adds sunglasses to only one specific dog, or completely removes an object (a dog next to a baby) without distorting the background or other subjects (Figure 6). This outperforms Emu2-Gen, MGIE, and Mini-Gemini, which often fail to follow instructions precisely or struggle to maintain visual fidelity. Gemini is noted for its inability to perform image editing, often resorting to image retrieval or displaying blank outputs for human-related edits.
- **Multimodal Comprehension:** SEED-X-I shows strong capabilities in fine-grained object detection, text-rich image comprehension (e.g., reading order details, understanding memes), fundamental mathematical computation, world-knowledge and commonsense reasoning (e.g., identifying historical events), and diagram understanding (e.g., explaining CNN architecture) (Figures 10, 11).

**Ablation Studies:**
The ablation studies provide insights into the design choices. For visual de-tokenization, it was found that more visual tokens (e.g., 256 vs. 64) lead to better image reconstruction, but 64 tokens were chosen for efficiency while still providing good performance. Optimizing only keys and values within the SD-XL U-Net (instead of full fine-tuning) prevented distortions. For MLLM image generation, 64 learnable queries to reconstruct 64 ViT embeddings yielded better results than 256 queries, as regressing more features proved challenging. The study also validated the effectiveness of using the ViT tokenizer as a bridge between the MLLM and the de-tokenizer for generating high-quality images.

Overall, the findings robustly support SEED-X as a versatile and powerful multimodal foundation model, capable of unified multi-granularity comprehension and generation across diverse real-world tasks.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

SEED-X represents a significant leap forward in the development of Multimodal Large Language Models, primarily due to its successful unification of capabilities that were previously fragmented across different models. Its significance and potential impact can be summarized as follows:

1. **Enhanced Real-world Applicability:** By explicitly addressing the limitations of fixed image resolutions and the need for multi-granularity generation (from creative conceptualization to precise pixel-level editing), SEED-X makes MLLMs far more practical for everyday use. This moves the technology closer to truly intelligent and versatile AI assistants that can seamlessly handle diverse user requests involving complex visual data.
2. **Unified Multi-granularity Processing:** The core innovation of SEED-X is its ability to handle visual information at various levels of detail – from comprehending images of arbitrary sizes and detecting objects (fine-grained understanding) to generating entirely new images or precisely manipulating existing ones. This holistic approach simplifies the architecture and improves consistency, laying the groundwork for more coherent multimodal reasoning and interaction.
3. **Foundation for Advanced AI Assistants:** The demonstrated capabilities of SEED-X as an interactive designer, knowledgeable personal assistant, and specialized tool for tasks like slide generation or virtual try-on, highlight its potential to power a new generation of AI applications. Such a model could revolutionize creative industries, personal productivity, and educational tools by offering integrated visual and textual intelligence.
4. **Inspiration for Future Research:** By open-sourcing its models, code, and datasets, Tencent AI Lab is actively contributing to the broader research community. SEED-X serves as a strong baseline and an inspiration for future work on building increasingly versatile and capable MLLMs. It encourages researchers to focus on the challenges of unifying diverse multimodal capabilities within a single framework, moving towards more general artificial intelligence.
5. **Robustness and Generalization:** The methodology, particularly dynamic resolution image encoding and the two-stage de-tokenizer training, imbues SEED-X with greater robustness to varied input conditions, allowing it to generalize better to unseen image formats and editing scenarios. This is crucial for deployment in unpredictable real-world environments.
6. **Competitive Benchmarking:** Achieving competitive results on a wide array of multimodal understanding benchmarks while also demonstrating strong performance in compositional image generation underscores the efficacy of SEED-X's design choices. It shows that a unified model can be performant across different metrics, rather than sacrificing one capability for another.

In conclusion, SEED-X's primary impact lies in its comprehensive approach to multimodal AI, proving that a single foundation model can effectively unify complex comprehension and generation tasks. This positions it as a vital stepping stone towards the realization of more capable, adaptable, and user-friendly AI systems that can interact with the world in a human-like, multi-faceted manner.
