# Distributions as Actions: A Unified Framework for Diverse Action Spaces

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at the University of Alberta and Amii developed the "Distributions-as-Actions" framework, which redefines an agent's output as continuous parameters of an action distribution, effectively unifying discrete, continuous, and hybrid action spaces into a continuous control problem. This approach, implemented as DA-AC with Interpolated Critic Learning (ICL), demonstrates improved training stability due to lower-variance policy gradients and achieves competitive or superior performance across a wide range of continuous, discrete, and hybrid control benchmarks.

## Problem

- Reinforcement learning algorithms are often specialized for either discrete or continuous action spaces, requiring different gradient estimators and architectures.
- Policy gradient methods, especially likelihood-ratio (LR) estimators, frequently suffer from high variance, hindering stable and efficient learning.
- A unified, low-variance policy gradient estimator akin to reparameterization tricks for discrete actions and stochastic policies has been lacking.

## Method

- The ==Distributions-as-Actions== MDP (**DA-MDP**) framework redefines the agent's action as the parameters of a probability distribution over primitive actions, treating action sampling as environmental stochasticity.
- The Distributions-as-Actions Policy Gradient (DA-PG) estimator, derived from applying DPG to the DA-MDP, offers lower variance than LR and RP estimators by operating in the continuous distribution parameter space.
- Interpolated Critic Learning (ICL) enhances critic training by updating at interpolated points between the policy's output and deterministic actions, improving gradient curvature and critic accuracy.

## Results

- DA-AC achieved competitive or superior performance against strong baselines (e.g., TD3, SAC, DQN) across continuous (MuJoCo, DMC), discrete (Gym, MinAtar), and hybrid (PAMDPs) control tasks.
- In high-dimensional discrete settings (discretized MuJoCo & DMC), DA-AC significantly outperformed baselines, with performance comparable to its original continuous action performance.
- Ablation studies confirmed that Interpolated Critic Learning (ICL) consistently improves DA-AC's performance across almost all environments, leading to more accurate critic value estimates and better policy optimization.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Redefining the agent's output as continuous distribution parameters enables the unification of discrete, continuous, and hybrid action spaces into a single continuous control problem.
> - The DA-PG estimator rigorously generalizes DPG and theoretically achieves lower variance than both likelihood-ratio and reparameterization estimators, particularly for discrete and stochastic continuous policies.
> - Learning a critic over the continuous distribution parameter space with ICL provides more informative gradients, guiding the policy effectively towards optimal distributions.
