# A Survey of Interactive Generative Video

> [!summary] Summary
> A comprehensive survey establishes the framework and taxonomy for Interactive Generative Video (IGV) systems across gaming, embodied AI, and autonomous driving applications, decomposing the technology into five essential modules (Generation, Control, Memory, Dynamics, and Intelligence) while analyzing technical challenges and opportunities in each component.

## Problem

- Lack of unified framework for understanding and developing interactive generative video systems
- Growing need to make AI-generated videos interactive and controllable for practical applications
- Limited understanding of technical challenges across different IGV application domains

## Method

- Proposed comprehensive five-module framework for IGV systems
- Systematic analysis of IGV applications across gaming, embodied AI, and autonomous driving
- Detailed examination of technical challenges and future directions for each module

## Results

- Established formal definition and taxonomy for Interactive Generative Video
- Identified key technical challenges including real-time generation, open-domain control, and causal reasoning
- Provided structured roadmap for future IGV research and development
- Demonstrated potential applications across gaming, robotics and autonomous vehicle testing

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - IGV systems require integration of generation, control, memory, dynamics and intelligence capabilities
> - Different application domains have unique requirements but share common technical challenges
> - Real-time generation and open-domain control remain significant bottlenecks
> - Long-term coherence and physics simulation accuracy are critical for practical applications

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

This survey paper addresses the emerging field of Interactive Generative Video (IGV). It arrives at a time when video generation has seen rapid advancements, particularly with diffusion models and autoregressive approaches. The paper recognizes that the next step is to make these generated videos interactive and controllable.

The paper fits into the research landscape by:

- **Defining and consolidating the field of IGV:** It provides a clear definition of IGV, distinguishing it from traditional video generation by emphasizing interactivity and user engagement.
- **Identifying key application areas:** The survey focuses on three major application areas: gaming, embodied AI, and autonomous driving. This helps to focus research efforts and highlights the potential impact of IGV in different domains.
- **Proposing a comprehensive framework:** The paper's five-module framework (Generation, Control, Memory, Dynamics, and Intelligence) provides a structured way to understand, analyze, and develop IGV systems. This framework can guide future research by identifying key challenges and opportunities.
- **Analyzing technical challenges and future directions:** The paper systematically analyzes the challenges in each module, providing clear directions for future research. This helps researchers focus their efforts on the most pressing issues.
- **Building on prior work:** The paper acknowledges and expands upon the authors' previous research in this area, demonstrating their ongoing commitment to the field.

The paper situates IGV as the next logical step in the evolution of generative video. It builds upon existing research in video generation, control, and interactive systems. By providing a comprehensive overview and framework, the paper aims to accelerate research and development in IGV.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The main findings and results of the paper are:

- IGV is a crucial technology that combines generative capabilities with interactive features.
- IGV has promising applications in gaming, embodied AI, and autonomous driving.
- An ideal IGV system can be decomposed into five essential modules: Generation, Control, Memory, Dynamics, and Intelligence.
- Each module faces significant technical challenges, including achieving real-time generation, enabling open-domain control, maintaining long-term coherence, simulating accurate physics, and integrating causal reasoning.
- Future research should focus on addressing these challenges to advance the technology toward more sophisticated and practical applications.
