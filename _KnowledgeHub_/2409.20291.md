# RL-GSBridge: 3D Gaussian Splatting Based Real2Sim2Real Method for Robotic Manipulation Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and collaborators introduce RL-GSBridge, a real-to-sim-to-real framework utilizing 3D Gaussian Splatting (3D GS) with a novel soft mesh binding mechanism to create highly realistic simulation environments. This enables zero-shot transfer of deep reinforcement learning policies for robotic manipulation tasks to real robots, achieving an average success rate drop of only 6.6% during sim-to-real transfer across various grasping scenarios and demonstrating superior rendering quality over existing methods.

## Problem

- The 'sim-to-real gap' poses a major challenge for transferring policies learned in simulated environments to real-world robotic systems, limiting practical applications of reinforcement learning.
- Traditional sim-to-real methods often require extensive augmented data or large, complex models, leading to inefficiency and high computational demands for specific robotic tasks.
- Visual and geometric inconsistencies between simulators and the real world can lead to significant policy transfer failures in vision-based robotic manipulation tasks.

## Method

- RL-GSBridge leverages 3D Gaussian Splatting to reconstruct real-world scenes into high-fidelity virtual environments, effectively bridging the visual perception gap.
- A novel 'soft mesh binding' method is introduced for 3D GS, allowing Gaussian units to 'float' along mesh normals for increased optimization flexibility and improved rendering quality, while maintaining geometric accuracy.
- The framework integrates physics dynamics-based GS editing, synchronizing real-time object pose changes from a physics simulator (Pybullet) with the rendered 3D GS scene for dynamic visual consistency during RL training.

## Results

- Achieved robust zero-shot sim-to-real transfer for grasping tasks, demonstrating an average success rate drop of only 6.6% across diverse objects and backgrounds, significantly outperforming an RL-sim baseline (which saw an 80% drop).
- Successfully enabled a pick-and-place task with a 68.75% success rate in simulation and 71.87% in the real world, indicating consistent policy performance.
- The soft mesh binding method consistently outperformed hard mesh binding (GaMeS) in rendering quality across metrics, e.g., improving SSIM from 0.894 to 0.989 and PSNR from 26.53 dB to 35.46 dB for a 'Banana' object.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - High visual fidelity in simulation, achieved through 3D Gaussian Splatting, is critical for enabling reliable zero-shot sim-to-real transfer in vision-based robotic manipulation.
> - The proposed soft mesh binding for 3D GS enhances rendering quality and provides greater flexibility for Gaussian optimization compared to rigid mesh-bound methods, evidenced by superior SSIM, PSNR, and LPIPS scores.
> - Integrating the editable 3D GS representation with a physics simulator allows for real-time dynamic updates of the visual scene, creating a consistent and effective training environment for deep reinforcement learning.
