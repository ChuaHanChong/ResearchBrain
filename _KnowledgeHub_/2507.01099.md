# Geometry-aware 4D Video Generation for Robot Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> A 4D video generation model is introduced that predicts future RGB frames and 3D pointmaps, achieving both temporal coherence and cross-view geometric consistency for robot manipulation. This system enables the extraction of accurate 6DoF robot end-effector trajectories, leading to an average task success rate of 0.64 across simulation tasks, surpassing prior methods.

## Problem

- Existing video generation models struggle to simultaneously maintain temporal coherence and 3D geometric consistency, particularly in dynamic, multi-object robot manipulation scenarios.
- Traditional pixel-based models often lack 3D structure understanding, leading to visual artifacts, while current 3D-aware models are limited to simpler, static scenes.
- Robotic applications require spatio-temporally aligned predictions that generalize to novel viewpoints and facilitate precise 6DoF robot pose extraction for effective manipulation tasks.

## Method

- A 4D video generation model extends the Stable Video Diffusion (SVD) framework to predict future multi-view RGB frames and corresponding 3D pointmaps.
- Geometry-consistent supervision is integrated by training a dedicated ==VAE== for pointmaps and applying a cross-view pointmap diffusion loss, ensuring one view's pointmaps are predicted in the reference view's coordinate frame.
- Multi-view ==cross-attention== is incorporated into the ==U-Net== ==diffusion model=='s decoders, enabling information transfer between views to enhance 3D alignment and accuracy during pointmap prediction without explicit camera pose inputs at inference.

## Results

- The proposed method achieved superior 4D video generation quality, demonstrating lower FVD for RGB temporal coherence, better depth prediction (lower AbsRel, higher 21), and stronger cross-view 3D consistency (higher mIoU) across simulation and real-world tasks.
- The model yielded a significantly higher average robot task success rate of 0.64 across three simulation tasks, substantially outperforming baselines such as Dreamitate (0.12) and Diffusion Policy (0.12).
- The framework effectively generalizes to novel viewpoints and can accommodate additional input views at inference time without retraining, maintaining good visual quality and cross-view consistency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly enforcing 3D geometric consistency through cross-view pointmap supervision is crucial for generating spatially aligned and realistic 4D videos essential for robot interaction.
> - The multi-view cross-attention mechanism within the diffusion model is vital for learning accurate 3D correspondences and enabling robust pointmap projection between different camera viewpoints.
> - Generating geometry-aware multi-view RGB-D videos allows for direct and accurate 6DoF robot end-effector pose extraction using off-the-shelf trackers, improving the reliability and performance of robot policies.
