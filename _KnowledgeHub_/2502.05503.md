# A Physical Coherence Benchmark for Evaluating Video Generation Models via Optical Flow-guided Frame Prediction

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Zhejiang University and Alibaba Group introduce PhyCoBench, a new benchmark and an automated evaluation tool called PhyCoPredictor, designed to assess the physical coherence of videos generated by text-to-video models. This system quantifies adherence to physical laws using an optical flow-guided frame prediction approach, demonstrating strong correlation with human judgment.

## Problem

- Despite advancements in visual quality, current text-to-video (T2V) models frequently generate content that defies fundamental physical laws.
- Existing T2V benchmarks primarily focus on visual fidelity, textual alignment, and temporal consistency, largely overlooking the crucial aspect of physical coherence.
- Manual evaluation of physical realism in generated videos is subjective, labor-intensive, and lacks quantifiable metrics for objective comparison.

## Method

- The authors developed **PhyCoBench**, a benchmark consisting of 120 meticulously designed text prompts that target seven fundamental physical interactions (e.g., gravity, collision, fluid dynamics).
- They propose **PhyCoPredictor**, an automated evaluation model built on two cascaded ==Latent Diffusion== Modules that uses ==optical flow==-guided frame prediction to assess physical coherence.
- **PhyCoPredictor** is trained on real-world dynamic video data to predict future optical flow and video frames, then compares these predictions against a generated video's actual flow and frames to derive a coherence score.

## Results

- Manual evaluation showed that Keling1.5 consistently achieved the highest physical coherence among leading T2V models, significantly outperforming others like OpenSora-STDiT-v3.
- PhyCoPredictor's automated rankings demonstrated positive correlation with human judgment, achieving Kendall’s Tau-b of 0.3367 and Spearman’s Rank Correlation of 0.3751.
- Qualitative comparisons illustrated PhyCoPredictor's superior ability to predict physically natural motion trajectories for complex events (e.g., falling, drifting, rolling) compared to baseline models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Optical flow guidance in video frame prediction is essential for accurately learning and evaluating physically coherent motion, enabling automated assessments to align with human perception.
> - Current state-of-the-art text-to-video models exhibit substantial performance disparities in their ability to generate physically plausible content, indicating a significant area for future improvement.
> - A dedicated benchmark for physical coherence, with an objective automated tool, can provide critical diagnostic feedback to guide the development of more physics-aware generative AI models.
