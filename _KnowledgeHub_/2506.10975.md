# GenWorld: Towards Detecting AI-generated Real-world Simulation Videos

> [!summary] Summary
> This paper introduces GenWorld, a comprehensive dataset of over 100,000 real-world simulated videos, and SpannDetector, a new method for identifying AI-generated content by detecting violations of physical consistency. SpannDetector achieved 72.44% accuracy on challenging world model outputs like Cosmos videos, significantly outperforming existing detection methods.

## Problem

- Existing AI video detection methods primarily rely on pixel-level artifacts, which are becoming increasingly rare and difficult to detect in high-quality generative models.
- Current detection datasets contain too much non-realistic or low-quality content, making them ineffective for training detectors to identify sophisticated, real-world simulating AI-generated videos.
- State-of-the-art AI video detectors struggle significantly with content from advanced world models that produce visually indistinguishable but physically inconsistent real-world simulations.

## Method

- Introducing **GenWorld**, a large-scale dataset of over 100,000 videos focused on real-world simulation scenarios (e.g., autonomous driving, robot operations) using diverse state-of-the-art generative models.
- Developing **SpannDetector**, a novel detection framework that identifies AI-generated videos by analyzing violations of physical consistency and 3D geometric integrity across video frames.
- **SpannDetector** integrates a frozen **Dust3R** model for 3D feature extraction and a temporal memory module to track and analyze multi-view consistency over time, rather than relying on pixel-level artifacts.

## Results

- SpannDetector achieved 72.44% accuracy in detecting videos generated by Cosmos, an advanced world model, outperforming the best competing method (VideoMAE) which reached only 30.38% accuracy on the same content.
- The GenWorld dataset challenges human perception, with participants achieving only 89.4% accuracy in distinguishing real from AI-generated videos, compared to 100% on some existing datasets.
- Traditional detection methods like DeMamba showed severe overfitting, with accuracy dropping from 98.41% on seen generation types to as low as 0.61% on unseen types, while SpannDetector demonstrated better generalization with less severe performance degradation.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - AI-generated videos, even those visually convincing, often contain subtle but detectable violations of physical consistency and 3D geometry across viewpoints or over time.
> - Detection methods focusing on physical consistency are more robust and generalizable against advanced generative models like world models, which are becoming increasingly challenging for pixel-level artifact detectors.
> - High-quality, real-world focused datasets are crucial for benchmarking and advancing AI-generated video detection, as existing datasets often contain non-realistic or easily detectable content.
