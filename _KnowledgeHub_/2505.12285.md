# CALM: Co-evolution of Algorithms and Language Model for Automatic Heuristic Design

> [!summary] Summary
> The CALM framework introduces a co-evolutionary approach for automatic heuristic design, enabling both the heuristics and the underlying language model to adapt and improve. It achieves superior performance across various optimization problems using a compact 7B LLM on a single GPU, outperforming state-of-the-art methods through a combination of verbal and numerical guidance.

## Problem

- Manual heuristic design for optimization problems is labor-intensive and demands extensive expert knowledge.
- Existing automatic heuristic design (AHD) methods, including early LLM-based approaches, typically rely on static language models, neglecting to adapt the LLM's generative capabilities based on performance feedback.
- Traditional AHD approaches often constrain the search space by depending on human-defined, problem-specific components.

## Method

- CALM (==Co-evolution== of Algorithms and the Language Model) employs a hybrid approach integrating verbal guidance (prompt engineering with evolutionary operators) and numerical guidance (reinforcement learning for LLM fine-tuning).
- The framework iteratively generates, evaluates, and assigns rewards to heuristics, using this feedback to update the LLM's parameters via GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization) to enable continuous adaptation.
- It incorporates advanced prompt generation operators (injection, replacement, diversity-aware crossover, simplification) and a collapse mechanism to manage search space, ensure diversity, and escape local optima.

## Results

- CALM consistently outperformed all baseline methods, achieving the lowest average optimality gap (**0.71%**) on Online Bin Packing, a **38.69%** average improvement over FunSearch, and superior results on CVRP and OP.
- The framework demonstrated state-of-the-art performance using a compact Qwen2.5-**7B**-Instruct-INT4 model, running locally on a single **24GB** GPU, showcasing significant resource efficiency.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the reinforcement learning module (GRPO) was responsible for the largest performance gains, while CALM's verbal guidance alone, when paired with GPT-4o-mini, matched or exceeded existing LLM-based AHD baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Co-evolution of the language model and heuristic designs, driven by both verbal and numerical gradients, significantly enhances the LLM's ability to discover high-quality heuristics.
> - Reinforcement learning, coupled with a fine-grained reward function, allows smaller, locally fine-tuned language models to achieve and surpass the performance of larger, static, API-based commercial models in specialized AHD tasks.
> - Carefully designed verbal guidance, including sophisticated evolutionary operators and a collapse mechanism, is critical for effective exploration, exploitation, and preventing premature convergence in LLM-driven evolutionary search.
