# Advancing Multimodal Reasoning via Reinforcement Learning with Cold Start

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University developed a two-stage Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) pipeline to enhance multimodal reasoning in Large Vision-Language Models (MLLMs). Their 7B parameter model achieved an average score of 55.66 on multimodal mathematical reasoning benchmarks, outperforming existing open-source MLLMs and some larger closed-source models.

## Problem

- The prevailing assumption that "aha moment" patterns in MLLMs are solely emergent properties of reinforcement learning (RL) and reliably correlate with reasoning improvement required re-evaluation.
- A lack of robust and systematically validated two-stage training methodologies (SFT + RL) for effectively enhancing complex multimodal reasoning in MLLMs.
- The need for improved state-of-the-art performance in open-source MLLMs on challenging multimodal reasoning tasks, particularly at more accessible parameter scales.

## Method

- A two-stage post-training process combining ==Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) as a "cold start"== to establish foundational reasoning, followed by Reinforcement Learning (RL) to refine capabilities.
- Exploration of various ==multimodal Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data construction strategies== during SFT, including distillation from larger models, reflection, caption-enhancement, and self-critic CoT, to equip models with structured reasoning patterns.
- Utilization of the ==GRPO (Group-Normalized Policy Optimization) algorithm== in the RL stage, employing an accuracy-based reward function to activate and optimize multimodal reasoning.

## Results

- The SFT+RL pipeline consistently outperformed both SFT-only and RL-only approaches across challenging multimodal mathematical reasoning benchmarks.
- The **7B** parameter model achieved a state-of-the-art average score of **55.66**, representing a **+6.19 point** improvement over the base model, and surpassed all other open-source 7B MLLMs, along with some larger closed-source models like GPT-4o (**51.82**) and Skywork R1V-38B (**51.95**).
- The **3B** parameter model achieved an average score of **50.84**, a **+10.84 point** improvement over its base, demonstrating performance competitive with or superior to several existing 7B models.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - "Aha moment" patterns, often associated with self-correction, exist in MLLMs prior to RL training and do not consistently correlate with genuine reasoning improvement, sometimes leading to ineffective or hallucinatory reflections.
> - A strong Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) cold start, particularly with high-quality CoT data distilled from more capable teacher models, is foundational and directly correlates with better final reasoning performance after RL.
> - MLLMs can learn structural reasoning *formats* even from unjudged or incorrect CoT data, suggesting the importance of reasoning structure beyond the correctness of individual steps for initial training.
