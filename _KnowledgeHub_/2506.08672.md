# RuleReasoner: Reinforced Rule-based Reasoning via Domain-aware Dynamic Sampling

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at the State Key Laboratory of General Artificial Intelligence, BIGAI, developed RULEREASONER, a framework that enhances rule-based reasoning in language models through a novel domain-aware dynamic sampling strategy. It achieved an 84.0% pass@1 on in-distribution tasks and a 10.4% improvement over frontier Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) on out-of-distribution benchmarks while reducing training steps by 1.4x.

## Problem

- Current Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) struggle with diverse rule formats, scalability in long contexts, and precise instruction following for rule application.
- A significant generalization gap exists, where models overfit to seen tasks and fail to adapt to unseen reasoning tasks, forms, or difficulties.
- Conventional Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) approaches suffer from sample inefficiency and rely on static, manually engineered data mixing strategies.
- Existing methods tend to overfit to seen tasks and struggle to generalize to unseen reasoning tasks, forms, or difficulties, hindering real-world deployment.

## Method

- Introduces ==RULECOLLECTION==-32K, a novel, diverse dataset spanning eight rule-based reasoning tasks with varying depths (0-7 hops), formats, and inference rules to support curriculum learning.
- Leverages an on-policy Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) framework, adapting the reward function for rule-based exact matching and strategically discarding the KL loss term for better exploration and generalization.
- Develops Domain-aware Dynamic Sampling (DADS), an online scheduler that dynamically adjusts sampling probabilities for different domains within each training batch based on historical performance, prioritizing under-optimized tasks.

## Results

- RULEREASONER-**8B** achieved an average pass@1 of **84.0%** across eight in-distribution tasks, outperforming frontier LRMs (e.g., **4.1%** over OpenAI-o1, **4.8%** over DAPO).
- Demonstrated strong out-of-distribution generalization, with RULEREASONER-**8B** surpassing frontier LRMs by **10.4%** on challenging OOD benchmarks and achieving a **56.0%** gain from the baseline Qwen3-**8B** model.
- Achieved comparable OOD performance with approximately 72 fewer training steps (**1.4x** acceleration) compared to existing RLVR methods like DAPO, indicating improved computational and sample efficiency.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Dynamically adjusting data sampling via DADS effectively balances training across diverse domains, improving sample efficiency and preventing over-optimization on easy tasks while under-fitting on complex ones.
> - Discarding the KL divergence term from policy optimization and the entropy bonus encourages broader exploration of valid reasoning steps, leading to better generalization to unseen rules and tasks.
> - Improved out-of-distribution performance is attributed to the emergence of "meta introspection" in the model, enabling self-simulation, self-verification, and correction of logical flaws during reasoning.
