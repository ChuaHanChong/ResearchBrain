# Self-Aligned Reward: Towards Effective and Efficient Reasoners

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed the Self-Aligned Reward (SAR), an intrinsic signal, to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of large language models in reasoning tasks. This novel approach combines verifiable rewards with a self-guided perplexity-based metric, yielding models that achieve higher correctness while generating 30% shorter, more concise responses.

## Problem

- Existing reinforcement learning for LLMs relies on coarse, binary verifiable rewards, which fail to provide fine-grained feedback on reasoning quality and efficiency.
- This coarse feedback leads to LLMs generating verbose, redundant, or "overthinking" responses, increasing computational costs and inference latency.
- Current methods to reduce verbosity (e.g., heuristic length penalties) often compromise reasoning accuracy by inadvertently penalizing essential intermediate steps.

## Method

- Introduces the **Self-Aligned Reward** (SAR), an unsupervised ==intrinsic signal== derived from the model's policy, defined by the relative perplexity difference between an answer conditioned on the query and the standalone answer.
- SAR quantifies how much an answer's likelihood is reduced without the query context, thereby rewarding conciseness and query-specificity by favoring answers highly dependent on the query.
- Integrates SAR with traditional binary verifiable rewards using a weighting factor within Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) algorithms.

## Results

- SA-GRPO consistently achieved an average accuracy improvement of at least **4%** and reduced answer length by **30%** across four LLMs and seven benchmarks compared to vanilla GRPO.
- The method established a Pareto-optimal frontier in the accuracy-efficiency trade-off, outperforming length-based baselines by improving both metrics simultaneously.
- SAR-enhanced models demonstrated strong generalization to out-of-domain logical reasoning tasks and preserved the frequency of advanced reasoning behaviors (e.g., backtracking, verification) despite generating fewer tokens.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - SAR provides a fine-grained, nuanced reward signal that discriminates between concise and redundant correct answers, and offers partial credit for partially correct reasoning paths.
> - The mechanism of SAR promotes efficient exploitation of query information by assigning high scores to tokens that introduce new query-dependent information and low scores to repetitive content.
> - SAR enables simultaneous improvements in both reasoning accuracy and generation efficiency, demonstrating a content-aware approach that prunes verbosity without sacrificing critical reasoning steps.
