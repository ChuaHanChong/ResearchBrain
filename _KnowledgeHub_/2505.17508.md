# On the Design of KL-Regularized Policy Gradient Algorithms for LLM Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> A new theoretical framework, the Regularized Policy Gradient (RPG) view, unifies KL-regularized policy gradient algorithms for off-policy LLM training, providing exact gradient derivations for various KL divergences. This framework leads to RPG-REINFORCE with RPG-Style Clip, an algorithm that improves LLM reasoning accuracy by up to 6 absolute percentage points over DAPO on AIME24/25, achieving 52% on AIME25 for Qwen models at 8k context.

## Problem

- The design space for KL regularization in reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs) lacks a unified theoretical understanding, especially concerning importance weighting in off-policy settings.
- Existing KL-regularized policy gradient methods often suffer from high variance and instability when applied to LLM reasoning tasks, particularly with off-policy data collection.
- Some current algorithms, such as GRPO, implement KL penalties with importance-weighting mismatches in off-policy settings, leading to biased gradients.

## Method

- The paper introduces the ==Regularized Policy Gradient (RPG) view==, a unified theoretical framework that systematically derives exact policy gradients for various KL divergence types (forward/reverse, normalized/unnormalized) under off-policy sampling.
- It proposes ==RPG-REINFORCE with RPG-Style Clip==, a novel clipping mechanism for importance weights, combined with an ==iterative reference policy update== to stabilize off-policy policy gradient training.
- The methodology includes constructing gradient-equivalent surrogate loss functions and ensuring computational efficiency by allowing pre-computation of log probabilities for the old policy.

## Results

- RPG-REINFORCE, particularly with unnormalized reverse KL (URKL), enhances LLM reasoning accuracy by up to **6 absolute percentage points** over DAPO on AIME24/25 benchmarks.
- The method achieves **52%** accuracy on AIME25 for Qwen3-4B at 8k context length, surpassing the official Qwen3-4B-Instruct model (**47%**) and outperforming other strong baselines.
- RPG algorithms consistently demonstrate more stable training progressions, controlled policy entropy, and appropriate response lengths across various context lengths (2k, 4k, 8k) in mathematical reasoning tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The widely used k3 estimator for regularization is formally equivalent to the unnormalized KL divergence (UKL), providing theoretical clarity to its empirical success.
> - Under off-policy sampling, precise importance weighting is critical for the surrogate objective to yield the exact gradient of the intended KL-regularized objective, correcting mismatches found in prior methods like GRPO.
> - RPG-Style Clip and the iterative reference policy update are crucial for maintaining stable training dynamics and achieving superior performance in off-policy LLM fine-tuning by managing variance and adapting the KL trust region.
