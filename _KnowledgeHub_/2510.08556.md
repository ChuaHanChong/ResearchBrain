# DexNDM: Closing the Reality Gap for Dexterous In-Hand Rotation via Joint-Wise Neural Dynamics Model

> [!summary] Summary
> DexNDM introduces a sim-to-real framework that leverages a joint-wise neural dynamics model and autonomous data collection to achieve generalized in-hand object rotation. This approach enables robust manipulation of diverse objects, including those with challenging geometries, under various wrist orientations and rotation axes in real-world scenarios.

## Problem

- The significant "sim-to-real gap" hinders the transfer of dexterous manipulation policies from simulation to real robots, especially for contact-rich tasks.
- Current in-hand rotation approaches are often constrained by object geometry, manipulation conditions (e.g., wrist orientation), and reliance on specialized hardware.
- Collecting sufficient, diverse, and relevant real-world data for training robust dynamics models for complex dexterous tasks is practically challenging due to safety concerns and data scarcity.

## Method

- Trains category-specific ==oracle policies== in simulation, then aggregates successful trajectories to train a single ==generalist policy== via Behavior Cloning.
- Introduces a ==joint-wise neural dynamics model==, factorizing system dynamics to learn each joint's behavior independently, which theoretically enhances sample efficiency and generalization.
- Employs an autonomous ==Chaos Box data collection== method, using robot interaction with soft balls to generate diverse, scalable, and safe real-world dynamics data.
- Leverages the learned dynamics model to train a ==residual policy== that corrects the generalist policy's actions for real-world deployment.

## Results

- DexNDM demonstrates unprecedented real-world dexterity, successfully rotating complex objects (e.g., animal models, high aspect ratio up to **5.33**, small **2-3 cm** objects) under diverse wrist orientations and rotation axes.
- The framework substantially outperforms existing baselines like AnyRotate, achieving superior rotation performance and handling a significantly broader and more challenging object distribution.
- Ablation studies confirm the joint-wise dynamics model's superior sample efficiency and generalization to out-of-distribution real-world data compared to whole-hand models.
- The autonomous "Chaos Box" data collection is verified as efficient and scalable, providing broad coverage of interaction dynamics and enabling practical teleoperation applications for complex tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Joint-wise factorization of neural dynamics models acts as an information bottleneck, improving sample efficiency and generalization by discarding spurious correlations in high-dimensional contact interactions.
> - Autonomous and safe data collection methods, such as the "Chaos Box," are essential for acquiring the vast and diverse real-world interaction data needed to learn complex dexterous dynamics.
> - Training a generalist policy from multiple oracle policies via behavior cloning, then adapting it with a residual policy informed by real-world dynamics, is an effective strategy for robust sim-to-real transfer.
