# Pose-RFT: Enhancing MLLMs for 3D Pose Generation via Hybrid Action Reinforcement Fine-Tuning

> [!summary] Summary
> Pose-RFT introduces a reinforcement fine-tuning framework for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), optimizing 3D human pose generation from images and text using a novel hybrid action reinforcement learning algorithm, HyGRPO. The framework achieved improved Mean Per Joint Position Error (MPJPE) on 3DPW and Human3.6M datasets and higher Recall@K scores on PoseScript-H2, outperforming previous MLLM-based methods.

## Problem

- Existing pose-specific MLLMs, relying on supervised training, struggle to model the inherent ambiguity in 3D pose generation from single 2D images or subjective natural language descriptions.
- Standard supervised losses (e.g., MSE) fail to adequately capture task-specific spatial and semantic alignment, often generating semantically distinct poses with similar reconstruction errors.
- Traditional reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT) algorithms are designed for discrete token spaces, making them unsuitable for directly optimizing continuous 3D pose parameters.

## Method

- **Pose-RFT** frames 3D pose generation as a ==hybrid action== reinforcement learning problem, concurrently optimizing discrete textual responses and continuous 3D pose parameters.
- A novel hybrid reinforcement learning algorithm, HyGRPO, is introduced to perform group-wise reward normalization and jointly optimize both discrete and continuous actions through a PPO-style objective.
- Task-specific reward functions are employed, including joint location reward for image-to-pose accuracy, semantic alignment reward for text-to-pose consistency, and additional rewards for format adherence and text quality.

## Results

- Pose-RFT reduced MPJPE to **85.9** and PA-MPJPE to **51.6** on the 3DPW dataset for image-to-pose generation, outperforming UniPose (**94.7** MPJPE, **59.1** PA-MPJPE).
- The framework achieved superior text-to-pose retrieval performance on the PoseScript-H2 dataset, with RT2P_{T2P}T2P​ of **42.2**@5, **53.0**@**10**, **65.5**@**20** and RP2T_{P2T}P2T​ of **45.3**@5, **57.2**@**10**, **70.4**@**20**, surpassing previous MLLM methods.
- Ablation studies confirmed that the pose-aware encoder enhances image-to-pose performance and that HyGRPO consistently improves both image-to-pose (joint location reward) and text-to-pose (semantic alignment reward) quality during training.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Jointly optimizing discrete (text) and continuous (3D pose) outputs using a hybrid reinforcement learning approach effectively enhances MLLMs' ability to generate robust and semantically aligned 3D human poses.
> - Verifiable, task-specific reward functions are critical for guiding policy optimization, particularly the semantic alignment reward which significantly improves text-to-pose generation beyond reconstruction-focused metrics.
> - Incorporating a pose-aware encoder, specifically a pretrained Vision Transformer, provides crucial fine-grained pose-relevant features, leading to notable improvements in image-to-pose spatial accuracy.
