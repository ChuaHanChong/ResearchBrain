# QuadGPT: Native Quadrilateral Mesh Generation with Autoregressive Models

> [!summary] Summary
> QuadGPT introduces the first end-to-end autoregressive framework for natively generating quadrilateral-dominant meshes directly from point clouds, addressing a key limitation of existing generative models. The system achieves superior geometric fidelity and topological quality compared to baselines, with user study experts preferring its outputs up to 2.6 times more than conversion-based methods.

## Problem

- The 3D content industry relies on quadrilateral-dominant meshes for their advantages in animation, subdivision, and UV unwrapping, yet state-of-the-art generative models primarily produce triangular meshes.
- Converting generated triangular meshes to quadrilaterals through post-processing often introduces topological artifacts, disrupts edge flow, and degrades overall mesh quality, rendering assets unsuitable for professional production.
- Traditional quad meshing techniques are computationally intensive, multi-stage, and not integrated into end-to-end generative pipelines, while existing learning-based methods still operate on decoupled geometry and topology stages.

## Method

- The research proposes a two-stage autoregressive framework: pre-training with a hierarchical ==Hourglass Transformer==, followed by topological refinement using a novel truncated Direct Preference Optimization (tDPO).
- A unified 12-token block structure is designed for canonical mesh serialization, enabling the model to natively generate sequences containing both quadrilateral and triangular faces directly from input point clouds.
- Reinforcement learning with tDPO uses a specialized reward function to optimize global topological integrity, specifically encouraging the formation of continuous edge loops ('Quad Rings' and 'Quad Lines') and penalizing topological fractures.

## Results

- QuadGPT consistently achieves superior geometric fidelity (lower Chamfer and Hausdorff Distances) and topological quality (higher Quad Ratio of 78-**80%**) across diverse object categories compared to existing generative baselines.
- User studies show a strong preference for QuadGPT's outputs, with expert ratings of 4.8-4.9 out of 5, indicating its perceived quality for production-ready assets and significantly outperforming the next best methods.
- Ablation studies validate the importance of curriculum-based pre-training for stable convergence and the effectiveness of tDPO in enhancing both geometric and structural quality, confirming the benefits of the native generation approach over conversion pipelines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Native, end-to-end generation of quadrilateral meshes significantly outperforms post-hoc triangular-to-quad conversion in producing industry-standard, topologically coherent 3D assets.
> - A curriculum learning strategy, starting with pre-training on triangular meshes and gradually introducing quad-dominant data, is essential for stable and effective learning of complex quadrilateral topologies in autoregressive models.
> - Optimizing for global topological properties through reinforcement learning, rather than relying solely on local cross-entropy loss, is crucial for achieving high-quality edge flow and structural integrity required for professional 3D models.
