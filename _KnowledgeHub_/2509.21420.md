# QuadGPT: Native Quadrilateral Mesh Generation with Autoregressive Models

> [!summary] Summary
> QuadGPT introduces the first end-to-end autoregressive framework for natively generating quadrilateral-dominant meshes directly from point clouds, addressing a key limitation of existing generative models. The system achieves superior geometric fidelity and topological quality compared to baselines, with user study experts preferring its outputs up to 2.6 times more than conversion-based methods.

## Problem

- The 3D content industry relies on quadrilateral-dominant meshes for their advantages in animation, subdivision, and UV unwrapping, yet state-of-the-art generative models primarily produce triangular meshes.
- Converting generated triangular meshes to quadrilaterals through post-processing often introduces topological artifacts, disrupts edge flow, and degrades overall mesh quality, rendering assets unsuitable for professional production.
- Traditional quad meshing techniques are computationally intensive, multi-stage, and not integrated into end-to-end generative pipelines, while existing learning-based methods still operate on decoupled geometry and topology stages.

## Method

- The research proposes a two-stage autoregressive framework: pre-training with a hierarchical ==Hourglass Transformer==, followed by topological refinement using a novel truncated Direct Preference Optimization (tDPO).
- A unified 12-token block structure is designed for canonical mesh serialization, enabling the model to natively generate sequences containing both quadrilateral and triangular faces directly from input point clouds.
- Reinforcement learning with tDPO uses a specialized reward function to optimize global topological integrity, specifically encouraging the formation of continuous edge loops ('Quad Rings' and 'Quad Lines') and penalizing topological fractures.

## Results

- QuadGPT consistently achieves superior geometric fidelity (lower Chamfer and Hausdorff Distances) and topological quality (higher Quad Ratio of 78-**80%**) across diverse object categories compared to existing generative baselines.
- User studies show a strong preference for QuadGPT's outputs, with expert ratings of 4.8-4.9 out of 5, indicating its perceived quality for production-ready assets and significantly outperforming the next best methods.
- Ablation studies validate the importance of curriculum-based pre-training for stable convergence and the effectiveness of tDPO in enhancing both geometric and structural quality, confirming the benefits of the native generation approach over conversion pipelines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Native, end-to-end generation of quadrilateral meshes significantly outperforms post-hoc triangular-to-quad conversion in producing industry-standard, topologically coherent 3D assets.
> - A curriculum learning strategy, starting with pre-training on triangular meshes and gradually introducing quad-dominant data, is essential for stable and effective learning of complex quadrilateral topologies in autoregressive models.
> - Optimizing for global topological properties through reinforcement learning, rather than relying solely on local cross-entropy loss, is crucial for achieving high-quality edge flow and structural integrity required for professional 3D models.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The generation of 3D content is a significant area within computer graphics and artificial intelligence, with applications in industries such as game development, animation, and virtual reality. Within this domain, quadrilateral-dominant meshes are recognized as an industry standard for creating high-fidelity 3D assets due to their advantages in modeling efficiency, deformation stability, and animation readiness. These mesh structures facilitate smoother subdivision surfaces, more natural articulation, and simpler UV unwrapping, which are critical in professional production pipelines.

Existing approaches to 3D asset generation often decouple the problem into geometry generation and topology generation. Methods relying on implicit neural representations (e.g., SDFs, vecsets) combined with iso-surfacing algorithms (e.g., Marching Cubes) typically produce dense, unstructured *triangular* meshes. While these methods excel at capturing geometry, they do not inherently generate structured topology. Concurrently, traditional quadrilateral meshing techniques are often optimization-based or field-guided, which can be computationally intensive, require specific input mesh quality, and are not integrated into end-to-end generative frameworks. More recent learning-based methods have accelerated field prediction, but they still operate within multi-stage pipelines.

A parallel development in 3D generation is the rise of autoregressive models, exemplified by works like MeshGPT, MeshAnything, and Mesh-RFT. These models employ Transformer architectures to directly generate mesh sequences, demonstrating an ability to capture artist-like topology. However, a consistent limitation across these autoregressive methods is their confinement to generating *triangular* meshes. Converting these triangular outputs into quadrilateral meshes typically involves post-processing algorithms that merge triangles. This conversion process frequently introduces topological artifacts, disrupts natural edge flow, and can compromise the overall quality of the mesh, preventing it from being production-ready.

QuadGPT addresses this specific gap by introducing an end-to-end autoregressive framework designed for the *direct and native generation of quadrilateral-dominant meshes*. By moving beyond the triangle-centric generation paradigm and post-hoc conversion, this work positions itself as an advancement in creating 3D assets that meet professional topological standards directly from input data. It aims to bridge the discrepancy between the capabilities of current generative models and the practical requirements of industrial 3D content creation.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The central objective of this research is to develop an autoregressive framework capable of directly generating quadrilateral-dominant meshes in an end-to-end manner, starting from an input point cloud. This initiative is motivated by several considerations:

1. **Addressing Industry Standards**: Quadrilateral meshes are the established industry standard for high-fidelity 3D content creation in professional domains such as game development and animation. This preference stems from the inherent advantages of quad layouts, including improved deformation for animation, more stable subdivision surfaces, and facilitated UV unwrapping. Current generative models predominantly produce triangular meshes, which necessitates a conversion step that often degrades quality.
2. **Overcoming Limitations of Existing Approaches**:
 - **Indirect Generation**: Current methods that first generate 3D geometry via implicit representations and then extract meshes (e.g., using Marching Cubes) inherently yield dense, unstructured triangular meshes. These outputs lack the structured topology required for professional use.
 - **Triangle-to-Quad Conversion**: Even high-quality triangular meshes generated by autoregressive models require post-processing to convert them into quadrilaterals. This conversion often involves heuristic rules, leading to compromised topological quality, broken edge flow, and artifacts that render the meshes unsuitable for production. The process is lossy and irreversible, as the original topological intent from artist-created quads is lost during triangulation and difficult to perfectly recover.
 - **Field-Guided Methods**: While traditional and learning-based field-guided methods aim for structured quad meshing, they are typically optimization-based, multi-stage, and not end-to-end generative, often requiring pristine input meshes and lacking robustness on complex geometries.
3. **Achieving End-to-End Native Generation**: The motivation is to move beyond decoupled pipelines and post-processing heuristics. By formulating quadrilateral mesh generation as a native sequence prediction problem, the framework aims to directly learn and output meshes with artist-preferred topological structures, including coherent edge loops.
4. **Enhancing Topological Quality**: A specific motivation is to enable models to optimize for global topological properties directly, which is not effectively achieved by local cross-entropy loss functions used in pre-training. This necessitates a mechanism to reward the formation of desirable structures characteristic of professionally designed assets, such as continuous edge loops and reduced fractures.
5. **Scalability and Robustness**: The goal includes developing a scalable solution that can handle high-polygon meshes and perform robustly across diverse object categories, from organic characters to hard-surface props, even when presented with potentially noisy input point clouds derived from other AI models.

In summary, QuadGPT seeks to bridge the gap between advanced 3D generative modeling and the practical demands of professional 3D asset creation by directly generating high-quality, topologically sound quadrilateral meshes.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

QuadGPT operates as a two-stage framework: an autoregressive pre-training phase followed by a reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tuning stage.

#### 3.1. Unified Serialization for Mixed-Element Meshes

The method formulates mesh generation as a sequence prediction problem, converting a mesh into a linear sequence of discrete integer tokens.
- **Canonical Representation**: To ensure deterministic serialization, vertex coordinates are normalized to a `[-0.95, 0.95]^3` cube and quantized to 1024 levels (10-bit integers). Unique vertices are then lexicographically sorted by `(z, x, y)` coordinates, and faces are sorted accordingly, establishing a unique mesh-to-sequence mapping.
- **Unified Token Block Structure**: A fixed-length 12-token block represents each face.
 - **Quadrilateral Faces**: Tokenized by directly flattening their 4 vertices, each with 3 coordinates (4 * 3 = 12 tokens).
 - **Triangular Faces**: Prepended with three special padding tokens (`τ_pad`, integer value 1024), followed by their 3 vertices' 3 coordinates (3 + 3 * 3 = 12 tokens). This design allows the model to implicitly learn the face type from the padding.
- This unified structure simplifies tokenization, model architecture, and parallel processing, while enabling the model to manage mixed-element topologies.

#### 3.2. Autoregressive Pre-training

This stage aims to learn the fundamental distribution of mesh geometry and connectivity by predicting the next token in a sequence.
- **Objective**: Standard cross-entropy loss (`L_ce`).
- **Hierarchical Model Architecture**: An Hourglass Transformer is employed, processing the input sequence at multiple levels of abstraction. It uses causality-preserving shortening layers (factors of 3 and 4) to create computational bottlenecks, allowing the model to capture global context at higher levels and fine-grained details at lower levels, before upsampling for prediction.
- **Shape and Topological Conditioning**:
 - **Shape Conditioning**: An input point cloud with normals `P` is encoded into a global shape embedding `E_shape` using a pre-trained Michelangelo encoder. This embedding guides the decoder via cross-attention.
 - **Topological Conditioning**: A learnable embedding conditioned on a quad-dominance parameter `r ∈` is introduced. This parameter controls the target ratio of face types (purely triangular to mixed quadrilateral), supporting curriculum learning.
- **Training Strategy**:
 - **Truncated Sequence Training**: To handle long sequences of high-resolution meshes, training uses fixed-length segments (e.g., 36,864 tokens) for efficient batch processing.
 - **Curriculum Learning**: QuadGPT is initialized with weights from a model pre-trained exclusively on triangular meshes. It is then progressively fine-tuned by annealing the `r` parameter, gradually shifting the training data distribution from purely triangular (`r = 0`) to quad-dominant (`r → 1`). This strategy aims to stabilize learning by first mastering basic geometric syntax before more complex quadrilateral topology.
- **Data Strategy**: A dataset of 1.3 million quad-dominant models was curated from diverse 3D sources (ShapeNetV2, 3D-FUTURE, Objaverse, Objaverse-XL, proprietary assets). This involved an automated triangle-to-quad conversion operator (formulated as an Integer Linear Programming problem to dissolve optimal internal edges while validating geometry to prevent unstable quadrilaterals) and a multi-stage quality filtering pipeline, including a rule-based filter and a vision-based aesthetic quality assessment model.

#### 3.3. Topological Refinement with Reinforcement Learning (tDPO)

This post-training stage addresses the limitation of local cross-entropy loss by optimizing global topological quality using a modified Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) framework.
- **Topological Scoring Standard**: A specialized reward function evaluates mesh subsequences for topological integrity.
 - **Edge Loops**: Quantifies the formation of long, continuous "Quad Rings" (closed loops of faces) and "Quad Lines" (open strips) through an edge-based traversal algorithm (Algorithm 3 in Appendix B.2). The reward maximizes the ratio of faces participating in closed rings and the average length of open lines.
 - **Fracture Detection**: Penalizes generation fractures (`R_frac`) by identifying boundary faces at the current generation frontier (Algorithm 2 in Appendix B.1), signaling topological breaks.
- **Truncated DPO (tDPO)-based Post-Training**:
 - The pre-trained policy (`π_θ`) is fine-tuned using DPO. Preference pairs (`y_w`, `y_l`) are constructed by generating multiple candidates for an input `x` and ranking them based on the topological rewards (higher `L_avg` and lower `R_frac` for `y_w`).
 - To enable tractability for long sequences, tDPO optimizes the DPO loss on *random prefixes* of length `m` within a truncation window `τ` (e.g., 36,864 tokens). This allows for local decisions within the sequence to be optimized towards globally superior topology.
- **Implementation**: Pre-training uses 64 NVIDIA A100 GPUs for 7 days, 1.1B parameter Hourglass Transformer, AdamW optimizer, learning rate 1e-4. RL fine-tuning is performed for 4 hours on the same setup with a reduced learning rate of 1e-7. Inference uses top-k (10) and nucleus (0.95) sampling with T=0.5, achieving approximately 230 tokens/second with KV caching and CUDA graphs.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The experiments demonstrate that QuadGPT consistently outperforms existing baselines in both geometric accuracy and topological quality.

#### 4.1. Qualitative Results

- **Comparison with Indirect Autoregressive Pipelines**: Figure 4 illustrates that methods generating triangular meshes (e.g., BPT, DeepMesh, MeshAnything, FastMesh) followed by triangle-to-quad conversion often produce meshes with significant topological artifacts, loss of geometric detail, or oversimplified structures. QuadGPT, in contrast, generates meshes with greater coherence and artistically plausible edge flow on both AI-generated dense meshes and artist-designed meshes.
- **Comparison with Field-Guided Methods**: Figure 5 shows QuadGPT's robustness compared to QuadriFlow, a field-guided method. QuadriFlow exhibits instability on complex topologies or sharp features, leading to geometric degradation or failures, while QuadGPT maintains high-quality, structured topology.

#### 4.2. Quantitative Results

- **Overall Performance (Table 1)**: QuadGPT achieves superior performance across all evaluation metrics on both "Dense Meshes" (AI-generated) and "Artist Meshes."
 - **Geometric Fidelity**: Lower Chamfer Distance (CD ↓) and Hausdorff Distance (HD ↓) indicate higher geometric accuracy compared to all baselines. For artist meshes, QuadGPT achieves CD of 0.043 and HD of 0.095, for example.
 - **Topological Quality**: A higher Quad Ratio (QR ↑) is observed, with QuadGPT achieving 80% on dense meshes and 78% on artist meshes, indicating a higher proportion of quadrilateral faces.
 - **Perceptual Quality**: A user study (US ↑), where experts ranked outputs from 0 (worst) to 5 (best), shows a decisive preference for QuadGPT, with scores of 4.9 for dense meshes and 4.8 for artist meshes, significantly outperforming the next best (DeepMesh at 3.3 for dense, BPT at 3.1 for artist). This indicates its perceived quality for production-ready assets.

#### 4.3. Ablation Studies

- **Effectiveness of tDPO (Table 2, Figure 6)**:
 - Standard DPO (fine-tuning on full, low-face-count meshes) failed to generalize to complex meshes.
 - Truncated DPO (tDPO, with basic fracture penalty) significantly improved performance.
 - The full tDPO-Pro model (truncated training with comprehensive topological reward) achieved the best results across all metrics, validating its effectiveness in enhancing both geometric quality and structural integrity.
- **Effectiveness of Curriculum-Based Pre-training (Table 2)**:
 - Training directly on quad-dominant meshes from scratch ("From Scratch") resulted in poor geometric fidelity (CD 0.081, HD 0.203) and struggled to converge.
 - Initializing with weights from a triangle-generation model ("Finetune") yielded substantially better results (CD 0.065, HD 0.167), confirming that the curriculum strategy is crucial for stable learning of complex quadrilateral topology.
- **Native Generation vs. Conversion Pipeline (Table 3, Figure 7)**:
 - A strong baseline, TriGPT (identical architecture and RL strategy as QuadGPT, but generating triangles converted to quads post-hoc), was used for comparison.
 - While TriGPT+RL(Q) achieved slightly better geometric scores (CD 0.051, HD 0.138) than QuadGPT (CD 0.057, HD 0.147), QuadGPT demonstrated a substantially higher Quad Ratio (80% vs. 72%) and a user preference score 2.6 times higher (1.3 vs. 0.5).
 - This indicates that although post-hoc conversion can approximate geometry, it struggles to create coherent global structures and artist-preferred topologies, validating the superiority of the native, end-to-end approach for practical utility.

#### 4.4. Additional Experimental Analysis (Appendix)

- **Impact of Curriculum Learning**: Fine-tuning from triangle-pretrained weights showed faster convergence and lower loss compared to training from scratch (Figure 13a).
- **Topology-aware Rewards**: The tDPO-Pro approach with truncated edge loop optimization consistently achieved the highest DPO reward (Figure 13b), validating the design of the topological reward.
- **Limitations of Conversion**: Even with high-quality artist-created quad meshes that are triangulated and then converted back, no method (including an optimized ILP-based operator or standard tools like MeshLab/Blender) could perfectly recover the original edge flow, leaving topological artifacts (Figure 14). This highlights the irreversible information loss and fundamental limitations of post-hoc conversion.
- **Tokenization Strategy**: At the 1B-parameter scale, a simple, direct coordinate representation (used in TriGPT) achieved superior geometric fidelity and topological quality compared to a compressed BPT-style tokenizer (TriGPT+BPT), as corroborated by user studies (Table 4). This suggests that overly complex tokenizers might introduce unintended biases.
- **Data Ablation**: QuadGPT-OS (trained only on filtered public datasets) significantly outperformed all prior autoregressive baselines, confirming the robustness of the methodology. The full QuadGPT model (trained on the complete 1.3 million mesh dataset) showed further substantial quality improvement, demonstrating the synergistic impact of advanced models and large-scale, high-quality data curation (Figure 15).

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

QuadGPT represents a significant advancement in the field of 3D content generation, particularly for professional workflows. Its primary significance lies in being the *first autoregressive framework to directly generate native quadrilateral-dominant meshes* in an end-to-end manner. This capability addresses a critical gap between the output of state-of-the-art generative models and the practical requirements of the 3D industry.

The potential impacts are substantial:
- **Establishing a New Benchmark**: By consistently outperforming existing triangle-based generative models and dedicated quad-meshing techniques in both geometric fidelity and topological quality, QuadGPT sets a new standard for high-quality, structured 3D mesh generation.
- **Bridging the Generative AI-to-Industry Gap**: The ability to directly produce "artist-ready" quad meshes with clean edge flow and coherent topological structures enables a more seamless integration of AI-generated assets into production pipelines for game development, animation, and visual effects. This reduces the need for extensive manual cleanup or problematic post-processing.
- **Automated Production-Ready Asset Creation**: QuadGPT facilitates the automated creation of complex 3D assets that meet professional standards for deformation, subdivision, and UV unwrapping. This can significantly accelerate content creation workflows and reduce human labor.
- **Advancement in Autoregressive Models**: The work showcases the power of combining large-scale autoregressive architectures (Hourglass Transformer) with sophisticated, topology-aware reinforcement learning refinement (tDPO). The innovations in unified tokenization and curriculum learning for mixed-element meshes further push the boundaries of what autoregressive models can achieve in structured data generation.
- **Foundation for Future Research**: By demonstrating the viability of native quad generation, QuadGPT opens new avenues for research into more sophisticated topology control, integration with part-based generation, and the development of reward models that more closely align with nuanced artistic preferences. The identified limitations, such as the current inability to explicitly control polygon count, also highlight specific directions for future development.
- **Practical Accessibility**: The stated intention to provide a public API and online interface suggests a commitment to making this technology accessible, which could foster broader adoption and further community innovation in high-quality 3D asset generation.

In essence, QuadGPT contributes to making AI-driven 3D content creation more practical and aligned with professional production demands, potentially reshaping workflows in the digital media industries.
