# A Survey of Slow Thinking-based Reasoning LLMs using Reinforced Learning and Inference-time Scaling Law

> [!summary] Summary
> A survey details recent progress in Large Language Models designed for "slow thinking" (System 2 cognition) by analyzing over 160 studies across test-time scaling, reinforced learning, and dedicated slow thinking frameworks. The work synthesizes how these methods enhance deliberative reasoning, consistently showing improved performance on benchmarks like MATH, MMLU, and GPQA.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) often lack robust, deliberate reasoning capabilities for complex problems, failing to fully emulate human "System 2" slow thinking.
- The current research landscape for reasoning LLMs is fragmented, lacking a comprehensive survey that integrates cognitive inspiration (slow thinking) with key technical solutions (RL and inference-time scaling).
- LLMs require enhanced deliberative processes to reliably tackle challenging tasks in fields like mathematics, multi-agent planning, and medical diagnosis.

## Method

- The survey systematically reviews over **160** studies, classifying slow-thinking LLM methodologies into three core categories: ==Test-Time Scaling==, ==Reinforced Learning==, and ==Slow Thinking Frameworks==.
- It details solutions involving dynamic inference-time scaling, such as advanced search, sampling, and verification mechanisms, to adjust computational resources based on task complexity.
- It analyzes methods leveraging Reinforcement Learning for iterative reasoning refinement, including policy network optimization, diverse reward design, and self-evolution strategies.

## Results

- LLMs incorporating slow-thinking principles, RL, and inference-time scaling demonstrate consistent performance improvements across diverse reasoning benchmarks.
- Specific models like DeepSeek-R1 achieved **79.8** on MATH and **90.8** on MMLU, while Kimi k1.5 scored **77.5** on MATH and **87.4** on MMLU.
- Test-time scaling methods, including advanced search algorithms and dynamic verification, enhance output quality and reasoning efficiency without altering core model parameters.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - "Slow thinking" from cognitive science offers a potent conceptual framework for advancing LLMs beyond heuristic responses to deliberate reasoning.
> - Reinforcement Learning (RL) and Inference-time Scaling are identified as crucial, synergistic technical drivers for equipping LLMs with System 2-like reasoning capabilities.
> - Future LLM development should focus on hybrid architectures that balance fast and slow thinking, robust RL reward mechanisms, and self-improving learning paradigms.
