# Native and Compact Structured Latents for 3D Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers at Tsinghua University, Microsoft Research, and the University of Science and Technology of China developed a novel "field-free" sparse voxel representation, O-Voxel, that natively encodes both arbitrary 3D geometry and physically-based rendering (PBR) materials. This system, combined with a Sparse Compression VAE and flow-matching generative models, produces high-fidelity, PBR-textured 3D assets from single images with 16x spatial compression and generates 1024³ resolution assets in approximately 17 seconds.

## Problem

- Existing 3D representations struggle to capture assets with complex topologies (e.g., open, non-manifold surfaces) and often neglect physically-based material properties.
- Generating high-resolution 3D assets is computationally expensive, and prior structured latent approaches lack sufficient compactness, hindering efficient scaling of generative models.
- Current image-to-3D pipelines frequently rely on multi-stage processes (e.g., 2D image backbones, multi-view synthesis) that introduce inconsistencies and complexity.

## Method

- Introduced **O-Voxel**, a "field-free" ==sparse voxel== structure that natively encodes arbitrary geometry and comprehensive physically-based rendering (PBR) material parameters into its representation.
- Developed a Sparse Compression VAE (SC-VAE) that learns a highly compact latent space from O-Voxel data, achieving an unprecedented 16x spatial downsampling rate with minimal perceptual degradation.
- Trained large-scale flow-matching generative models (DiT-based) within this compact latent space for end-to-end 3D asset generation, producing geometry, occupancy, and PBR materials directly from images.

## Results

- Reconstruction consistently achieves substantially higher fidelity than state-of-the-art baselines (e.g., TRELLIS, Direct3D-S2) using fewer tokens (**9.6K** for 1024³ resolution) and reduced runtime.
- Generated 3D assets show superior quality with intricate geometric details and physically plausible materials, achieving the highest alignment scores with image prompts and favored by **66.5%** of users for overall quality.
- Inference is highly efficient, generating a 1024³ fully-textured asset in approximately 17 seconds on an NVIDIA H100 GPU, and shape-conditioned texture generation avoids inconsistencies and artifacts common in other approaches.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Directly integrating geometry and PBR materials into a "field-free" sparse voxel structure allows for robust modeling of arbitrary 3D topologies (open, non-manifold, enclosed) and integrated appearance, surpassing limitations of implicit fields.
> - Achieving high spatial compression (16x) of 3D data into a compact latent space via a specialized sparse autoencoder is critical for scaling generative models to high resolutions and accelerating inference.
> - A unified, three-stage generative pipeline operating on these compact 3D latents enables high-fidelity, photorealistic 3D asset generation directly from images, bypassing complex multi-view 2D processing.

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

The field of 3D generative modeling has witnessed rapid progress, driven by innovations in neural representations and the integration of large latent learning and generative models. However, the creation of high-fidelity, topologically complex 3D assets with realistic materials remains a significant challenge. This paper directly addresses several critical limitations of existing approaches, positioning itself at the forefront of this evolving landscape.

Historically, 3D representations for neural networks have largely fallen into a few categories:
- **Implicit Fields (e.g., Occupancy Fields, Signed Distance Functions (SDFs), NeRFs):** These methods excel at producing realistic rendering (NeRFs) or detailed geometry (SDFs). However, they intrinsically struggle with non-watertight, non-manifold, or open surfaces, often requiring closed, manifold geometries. They can also be computationally intensive for high-resolution sampling or lack explicit geometry.
- **Unstructured Representations (e.g., Meshes, Point Clouds, Gaussian Splatting):** While offering explicit 3D data, their lack of inherent structural regularity makes them challenging for direct processing by neural networks and difficult to compress efficiently into compact latent spaces.
- **Structured Latent Representations:** Recent efforts, including prior work like TRELLIS, Direct3D-S2, and SparseFlex, have moved towards structured representations, often combining field-based iso-surface modeling with sparse voxels. These have achieved high geometric accuracy but often rely on 2D image feature inputs, rendering-based supervision, or still inherit limitations regarding complex topologies from their field-based primitives. Crucially, many focus primarily on shape, neglecting the detailed appearance and material properties inherent in real-world 3D assets, or require complex multi-view rendering and baking for texture synthesis, leading to inconsistencies.

This work specifically aims to overcome these shortcomings by proposing a "field-free" sparse voxel structure called **O-Voxel** that fundamentally integrates geometry and appearance. Unlike field-based methods, O-Voxel is designed to natively handle arbitrary topologies, including open, non-manifold, and fully-enclosed surfaces. Furthermore, it explicitly incorporates physically-based rendering (PBR) parameters directly into its representation, addressing the common neglect of material information or its problematic integration via multi-view 2D methods.

The paper also pushes the boundaries of **latent space compactness and efficiency**. While structured latents (like TRELLIS) offer geometric accuracy, they often require a large number of latent tokens, hindering compression. This work introduces a Sparse Compression VAE (SC-VAE) that achieves a significantly higher spatial compression rate (16x) with fewer latent tokens, yet with superior fidelity. This compactness is crucial for scaling generative models to high resolutions and accelerating inference.

By developing a framework for **native, end-to-end 3D asset generation** directly from images, producing high-fidelity, PBR-textured models without complex view-dependent post-processing, this research offers a streamlined and more robust pipeline compared to existing two-stage or multi-view synthesis approaches. It represents a significant step towards enabling efficient and realistic 3D content creation for demanding applications.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

The overarching goal of this research is to significantly advance the state-of-the-art in 3D generative modeling, moving beyond current limitations to enable the creation of high-resolution, complex 3D assets that are both geometrically intricate and visually realistic. The key objectives and underlying motivations are:

1. **Develop a Robust and Comprehensive 3D Representation:**
 - **Motivation:** Existing representations struggle to capture assets with complex topologies (e.g., open surfaces, non-manifold geometry, fully-enclosed structures) and often neglect comprehensive appearance information beyond basic color. Field-based methods are inherently limited to watertight, manifold shapes.
 - **Objective:** Introduce a novel "field-free" sparse voxel structure, O-Voxel, capable of robustly modeling arbitrary topologies and encoding a full spectrum of surface attributes, specifically physically-based rendering (PBR) parameters like base color, metallic, roughness, and opacity, directly aligned with geometry.

2. **Achieve High Spatial Compression and a Compact Latent Space:**
 - **Motivation:** High-resolution 3D assets generate vast amounts of data. Efficient storage and processing require highly compact latent representations, which is a major bottleneck for scaling generative models and achieving efficient inference. Previous structured latent approaches often require a large number of tokens.
 - **Objective:** Design a Sparse Compression VAE (SC-VAE) that can learn a highly compact latent space from O-Voxel data, achieving an unprecedented 16x spatial downsampling rate with minimal perceptual degradation, thereby facilitating efficient and high-resolution 3D generation.

3. **Enable High-Fidelity, Photorealistic 3D Asset Generation:**
 - **Motivation:** While 3D generation realism has improved, models often fall short in capturing intricate geometric details and producing physically consistent materials, leading to less convincing results or requiring extensive post-processing.
 - **Objective:** Train large-scale flow-matching generative models (DiT-based) within the learned compact latent space. These models should produce generated assets with geometry and material quality that significantly exceeds existing models, demonstrating intricate detail and realistic PBR textures from diverse inputs.

4. **Ensure Computational Efficiency for Large-Scale Generation:**
 - **Motivation:** Scaling generative models to handle high-resolution 3D data typically incurs substantial computational costs during inference, limiting practical deployment.
 - **Objective:** Design the entire framework—from the O-Voxel representation to the SC-VAE and the generative models—to be highly efficient, enabling fast inference times for generating high-resolution, fully-textured 3D assets, even with billions of parameters.

5. **Facilitate Native, End-to-End 3D Content Creation:**
 - **Motivation:** Many existing large-scale 3D generation pipelines rely on multi-stage processes involving 2D image backbones, multi-view texture synthesis, baking, and alignment, which can introduce inconsistencies and complexity.
 - **Objective:** Develop a unified framework that performs native, end-to-end 3D asset generation, directly producing high-fidelity, fully-textured 3D assets without any view-dependent post-processing or reliance on 2D image feature inputs for the core representation.

By tackling these challenges, the authors aim to provide a significant advancement in 3D generative modeling, making 3D content creation more accessible, efficient, and realistic for a wider array of applications.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

The methodology centers around three interconnected components: a novel 3D representation called O-Voxel, a Sparse Compression Variational Autoencoder (SC-VAE) for learning a compact latent space, and large-scale flow-matching generative models for asset generation.

#### 3.1. O-Voxel: A Native 3D Representation

O-Voxel (Omni-Voxel) is introduced as a "field-free" sparse voxel structure that simultaneously encodes geometry and appearance. It represents a 3D asset as a collection of feature tuples associated with active sparse voxels on a regular 3D grid. Its key innovations are:

- **Flexible Dual Grid for Shape:**
 - **Concept:** Inspired by Dual Contouring (DC) but critically, it's "field-free." Instead of relying on signed distance fields (SDFs) or other implicit fields, O-Voxel directly derives geometric information from the input mesh.
 - **Encoding:** For each active voxel, geometric features `f_shape` comprise a dual vertex `v_i` (position), edge intersection flags `δ_i` (determining quad connections), and splitting weights `γ_i` (for adaptive triangle subdivision).
 - **Conversion (Mesh → O-Voxel):** Identifies voxel edges intersecting the mesh, computes intersection points and normals (Hermite data). Dual vertices are calculated by solving a Quadratic Error Function (QEF) that includes terms for plane distance, boundary alignment (for open surfaces), and regularization. This process is instant and optimization-free.
 - **Conversion (O-Voxel → Mesh):** Connects dual vertices across intersected edges to form quads, which are then subdivided into triangles based on splitting weights. This process is also instant and rendering-free.
 - **Advantages:** Handles arbitrary topology (open, non-manifold, enclosed), preserves sharp features, and allows neural network adjustments for precision.

- **Volumetric Attributes for Material:**
 - **Encoding:** Material features `f_mat` for each active voxel consist of physically-based rendering (PBR) parameters: base color (`c_i`), metallic ratio (`m_i`), roughness (`r_i`), and opacity (`α_i`).
 - **Conversion (Texture → O-Voxel):** Projects voxel centers onto intersected mesh triangles and samples material attributes from texture maps using UV coordinates and mipmaps. Weighted averaging is used for multiple intersections.
 - **Conversion (O-Voxel → Texture):** Material attributes for any query point on the reconstructed mesh surface (vertex or texel) are obtained via trilinear interpolation of neighboring voxel attributes. This enables instant material reconstruction ready for rendering.

#### 3.2. Sparse Compression VAE (SC-VAE)

The SC-VAE learns a compact latent space from the O-Voxel data, specifically designed for high compression and high-resolution output.

- **Network Architecture:** A fully sparse-convolutional network with a U-shaped VAE design.
 - **Sparse Residual Autoencoding Layer:** Adapts the Residual Autoencoding principle to sparse voxel data. It uses non-parametric shortcuts within downsampling/upsampling blocks, aggregating/unstacking voxel children into/from channel dimensions. This is critical for robust information flow under high spatial compression (16x downsampling).
 - **Early-pruning Upsampler:** Before each upsampling step, a binary mask predicts active child voxels, pruning inactive nodes to reduce computation and memory.
 - **Optimized Residual Block:** Redesigns standard residual blocks by using a single sparse convolution layer followed by a wide point-wise MLP (similar to ConvNeXt) to enhance feature transformation and nonlinearity while maintaining efficiency.

- **VAE Training:** Two-stage approach:
 1. **Stage 1 (Low-Resolution):** Direct O-Voxel reconstruction loss using MSE for dual vertices, Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) for flags and pruning mask, and L1 loss for material attributes, combined with a KL divergence loss for the latent space.
 2. **Stage 2 (High-Resolution with Perceptual Loss):** Adds rendering-based perceptual supervision at higher resolutions. L1 loss, SSIM, and LPIPS terms are applied to rendered mask, depth, normal maps, and material attributes. This encourages high geometric and material fidelity. Decoupled VAEs are trained for shape and material, allowing independent material generation conditioned on shape.

#### 3.3. Generative Modeling

The generative framework uses large DiT-based architectures trained with the flow matching paradigm, adapted from previous work to leverage the new O-Voxel latents.

- **Generation Pipeline (Three Stages):**
 1. **Sparse Structure Generation:** Predicts the occupancy layout of the sparse voxel grid.
 2. **Geometry Generation:** Produces geometry latents within the active voxels.
 3. **Material Generation:** Synthesizes PBR material latents, conditioned on the input image and the generated geometry latents. This stage unifies geometric and material generation in the native 3D latent domain.

- **Architectural and Training Details:**
 - All DiT modules use AdaLN-single modulation for timestep conditioning and Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) for resolution generalization.
 - Image conditioning features are extracted from DINOv3-L.
 - Material generation is conditioned on shape by concatenating shape information channel-wise.
 - High spatial compression from SC-VAE allows for vanilla DiT structures, simplifying complexity.
 - Progressive training strategy: starts with 512³ outputs (32³ latent) and scales to 1024³ outputs (64³ latent), with corresponding image resolution increases.
 - Utilizes a custom high-performance sparse convolution backend, **FlexGEMM** (implemented in Triton), which fuses feature gathering and GEMM, employs Gray code ordering for active voxels, and uses Split-K techniques to achieve up to 2x speedup over existing sparse convolution libraries.

This comprehensive methodology allows for the efficient and high-quality generation of complex 3D assets directly from images, overcoming many limitations of prior representations and generative pipelines.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

The paper presents compelling findings that demonstrate the superiority of their proposed approach across 3D asset reconstruction and generation tasks.

#### 4.1. 3D Asset Reconstruction

The method significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines in reconstructing complex 3D shapes and materials.

- **Shape Reconstruction:** Compared against Dora, TRELLIS, Direct3D-S2, and SparseFlex, the proposed method consistently achieves substantially higher fidelity across all metrics (Mesh Distance, Chamfer Distance, Normal PSNR, LPIPS). Notably, it does so using a remarkably modest number of tokens (e.g., 9.6K tokens for 1024³ resolution, representing a 16x spatial downsampling) and requiring significantly less runtime. The method excels at preserving fine geometric details, sharp features, and accurately modeling internal and enclosed structures, which are challenging for other techniques.
- **Material Reconstruction:** As no direct baseline exists for material-only reconstruction given a shape, the paper reports its own performance. The method achieves high fidelity, with 38.89 dB / 0.033 on PBR attributes (base color, metallic, roughness, alpha) and 38.69 dB / 0.026 on shaded images. This demonstrates faithful material reproduction and consistent geometry-appearance alignment.

#### 4.2. Image-to-3D Generation

The generative capabilities are highlighted by the production of high-quality, PBR-textured 3D assets from input images.

- **Qualitative Superiority:** Visual comparisons against TRELLIS, Hi3DGen, Direct3D-S2, Step1X-3D, and Hunyuan3D 2.1 show that the proposed method generates assets with superior quality. These assets feature intricate geometric details, physically plausible materials, and high visual fidelity, including thin structures, open surfaces, and translucent regions. The materials are vivid, realistic PBR textures that exhibit consistent shading under novel lighting.
- **Quantitative Performance:** On AI-generated image prompts, the method achieves the highest alignment scores across all metrics. This includes visual alignment (CLIP score) and geometric similarity assessed by multimodal models (ULIP-2 and Uni3D), demonstrating clear superiority in both visual and geometric consistency with the input prompts.
- **User Study Validation:** A user study involving 40 participants confirmed the perceptual superiority of the method. It was favored by a significant margin (66.5% preference for overall quality and 69.0% for shape quality), indicating high visual realism, richness of geometric detail, and strong alignment with input prompts.
- **Inference Efficiency:** Despite training large models (4 billion parameters), inference remains highly efficient. Generating a 512³ fully-textured asset takes approximately 3 seconds, a 1024³ asset takes ~17 seconds, and a 1536³ asset takes ~60 seconds on an NVIDIA H100 GPU. This is significantly faster than existing large 3D generation models.

#### 4.3. Shape-Conditioned Texture Generation

The third stage of the generative pipeline can function as an independent 3D PBR texture synthesis model.
- The method outperforms multi-view PBR generation and fusion methods (Hunyuan3D-Paint) and UV-based approaches (TEXGen). It avoids inconsistencies, ghosting, blurred textures, and seam artifacts often seen in baselines. By reasoning about appearance natively in 3D, it generates sharper textures, consistent shape-material alignment, and can synthesize textures for internal surfaces.

#### 4.4. Ablation Studies and Test-time Scaling

- **SC-VAE Design:** Ablations confirm the importance of the **Sparse Residual Autoencoding layer**, which prevents severe quality degradation under high compression (e.g., 69% increase in MD and 0.5dB drop in PSNR without it). The **Optimized Residual Block** also contributes to improved reconstruction quality (16% MD increase, 0.6dB PSNR drop without it) without runtime penalty.
- **Flexible Scaling:** The compact latent space and efficient framework enable flexible test-time scaling of both compute and resolution. It supports cascaded inference, allowing generation at resolutions exceeding the training scale (e.g., 1536³) or improving quality within the trained resolution by refining sparse structure layouts.

In summary, the main findings consistently establish the proposed approach as a new frontier in 3D generative modeling, delivering unprecedented quality, compactness, and efficiency.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

This research presents a significant advancement in the field of 3D generative modeling, addressing fundamental challenges that have long hindered the creation of realistic and complex 3D content. Its potential impact spans several domains:

1. **Enabling High-Fidelity 3D Content Creation:** By introducing O-Voxel, a "field-free" representation capable of robustly modeling arbitrary topologies (open, non-manifold, enclosed surfaces) and comprehensive PBR materials, the paper breaks new ground. This allows for the generation of far more diverse and realistic 3D assets that faithfully capture intricate geometric details and physically accurate material properties, previously difficult or impossible with existing methods. This directly benefits industries like gaming, film, virtual/augmented reality, and industrial design, where demand for high-quality 3D assets is constantly growing.

2. **Advancing Efficiency and Scalability in 3D Generative AI:** The development of the Sparse Compression VAE (SC-VAE) is a crucial step towards making large-scale 3D generation practical. Its ability to achieve a 16x spatial compression rate while maintaining high fidelity drastically reduces the computational burden of training and inference. This efficiency, combined with the fast flow-matching generative models and the custom FlexGEMM backend, makes the generation of gigapixel-equivalent 3D assets feasible in minutes rather than hours, democratizing access to powerful 3D content creation tools. This impacts cloud-based content generation services and real-time applications.

3. **Streamlining the 3D Asset Pipeline:** The native, end-to-end 3D generation pipeline, which directly produces PBR-textured assets from images without relying on complex multi-view 2D rendering, baking, and alignment steps, significantly simplifies the workflow. This reduction in complexity minimizes inconsistencies, improves scalability, and accelerates the entire content creation process. The ability to perform shape-conditioned texture generation natively in 3D further enhances realism and consistency compared to UV-based or multi-view fusion techniques.

4. **Foundation for Future Research:** The O-Voxel representation, being field-free and topology-agnostic, provides a robust foundation for future research in 3D understanding and generation. Its modular design (geometry and material components) allows for independent advancements. The open-source release of code, models, and data will undoubtedly spur further innovation, enabling other researchers to build upon this work, explore new applications, and integrate O-Voxel into diverse 3D tasks.

5. **Democratizing 3D Design:** By making high-quality 3D asset generation faster, more accessible, and requiring less specialized expertise in complex 3D software, this technology has the potential to empower a broader range of creators, from professional designers to hobbyists, to realize their visions in 3D.

While the paper acknowledges limitations, such as potential aliasing for features smaller than voxel size and occasional small holes, these are common challenges in voxel-based representations and decoding processes. The proposed future work of incorporating higher-level structural and semantic information (e.g., part-level segmentation) highlights a clear path for expanding the impact of this representation into more intelligent and controllable 3D generation systems. Overall, this work represents a significant leap forward, setting a new benchmark for quality and efficiency in 3D generative modeling.
