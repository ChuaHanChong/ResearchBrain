# DriveDreamer-2: LLM-Enhanced World Models for Diverse Driving Video Generation

> [!summary] Summary
> DriveDreamer-2 presents an approach to generate diverse, user-customized multi-view driving videos by integrating a Large Language Model for scenario control via text prompts. It achieves improved spatio-temporal consistency across views and demonstrates that its synthetic data enhances 3D object detection and tracking performance for autonomous driving.

## Problem

- Existing world models for autonomous driving heavily rely on structured inputs (e.g., 3D bounding boxes, HDMaps) for video generation, limiting user interactivity and the diversity of generated scenarios.
- Collecting sufficient real-world data for 'long-tail scenarios' (rare, challenging events) for autonomous driving system training is prohibitively expensive and time-consuming.
- Maintaining spatio-temporal consistency across multiple camera views is a significant technical challenge in multi-view video generation, often leading to inconsistencies or inefficiencies in prior methods.

## Method

- The system employs a three-stage pipeline: a finetuned LLM generates precise ==BEV== agent trajectories from natural language queries.
- A diffusion model-based HDMap Generator, conditioned on the BEV trajectory map, produces high-definition maps that align with traffic regulations.
- A Unified Multi-View Video Model (**UniMVM**), built upon ==Stable Video Diffusion==, synthesizes multi-view driving videos by unifying views into a single patch and incorporating conditional inputs like HDMaps and 3D bounding boxes.

## Results

- DriveDreamer-2 achieves superior video generation quality, obtaining an **FID of 25.0** and **FVD of 105.1** without image conditioning, and an **FID of 11.2** and **FVD of 55.7** with first-frame multi-view image conditioning, outperforming prior state-of-the-art methods.
- Synthetic videos generated by DriveDreamer-2 enhance downstream 3D object detection, improving mAP by **3.8%** and NDS by **4.4%** when used to augment training data for StreamPETR without image condition.
- The method also improves multi-object tracking, boosting AMOTA by **8.3%** and AMOTP by **2.3%** without image condition, demonstrating the practical utility of its diverse synthetic data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Large Language Models (LLMs) can be finetuned to translate natural language prompts into precise agent trajectories, enabling user-friendly customization of complex driving scenarios.
> - A Unified Multi-View Model (UniMVM) significantly improves spatio-temporal consistency across multiple camera views by treating them as a single concatenated image, enhancing realism and coherence.
> - Synthetically generated diverse driving videos, particularly those created without direct image conditioning, effectively augment real-world datasets, leading to improved performance in downstream 3D object detection and tracking tasks.
