# GeoVLA: Empowering 3D Representations in Vision-Language-Action Models

> [!summary] Summary
> GeoVLA introduces an end-to-end framework that integrates 3D point cloud information into Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, allowing robots to better perceive their physical environment for manipulation tasks. This architecture consistently achieves state-of-the-art performance on manipulation benchmarks and exhibits robust generalization across various real-world spatial, scale, and viewpoint changes.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models primarily rely on 2D visual inputs, leading to limited spatial awareness and poor depth perception in robotic manipulation.
- This reliance on 2D data hinders VLA models' robustness and adaptability to real-world variations in object height, scale, and camera viewpoints.
- Integrating 3D information often either disrupts the pre-trained alignment of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) or limits the action expert's adaptation to new 3D data, requiring extensive re-training.

## Method

- **GeoVLA** employs a ==dual-path architecture== that processes 2D vision-language information via a pre-trained VLM and 3D point cloud data via a dedicated ==Point Embedding Network (PEN)== in parallel.
- A ==3D-enhanced Action Expert (3DAE)==, based on a ==Diffusion Transformer== with a ==Mixture-of-Experts== architecture, fuses these multi-modal embeddings for precise action generation.
- The PEN utilizes an ==end-effector token== as a spatial anchor for focused 3D feature extraction, and the 3DAE incorporates a ==static routing strategy== to balance contributions from different modalities.

## Results

- **GeoVLA** achieved **state-of-the-art** success rates on the LIBERO benchmark (**97.7%** average) and ManiSkill2 (**77%** overall), surpassing previous methods like OpenVLA-OFT and CogACT.
- In real-world experiments, GeoVLA demonstrated enhanced robustness and generalization, maintaining performance under significant variations in object height, scale, and camera viewpoints.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical contributions of the Point Embedding Network, the end-effector token, Rotary Positional Embedding, and the static routing Mixture-of-Experts design in the 3D-enhanced Action Expert.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Processing 2D and 3D information in parallel preserves VLM pre-trained knowledge while enabling effective integration of fine-grained geometric cues for robotic manipulation.
> - The "end-effector token" in the Point Embedding Network provides a task-relevant spatial anchor, enhancing the action expert's ability to model precise contact and geometric relationships.
> - A static routing strategy within the 3D-enhanced Action Expert's Mixture-of-Experts module mitigates bias towards the stronger pre-trained VLM branch, ensuring balanced and effective utilization of both 2D and 3D modalities.
