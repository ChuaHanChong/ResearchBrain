# ViLL-E: Video LLM Embeddings for Retrieval

> [!summary] Summary
> ViLL-E introduces a unified VideoLLM that integrates text generation with embedding-based retrieval capabilities within a single model. This model significantly surpasses previous VideoLLMs in retrieval tasks, showing over 8% average improvement on moment retrieval, while maintaining competitive performance on video question answering, and enables zero-shot composed video retrieval and retrieval using extensive text descriptions.

## Problem

- Existing VideoLLMs excel at generative tasks (e.g., captioning) but significantly underperform specialized embedding models in retrieval tasks (e.g., text-to-video retrieval).
- Specialized embedding models, while strong in retrieval, often lack generative capabilities and require per-dataset fine-tuning, leading to a need for separate model stacks for different video understanding tasks.
- Current VideoLLMs typically lack effective mechanisms to generate dense, aligned embeddings for efficient similarity-based search, hindering their application in retrieval scenarios.

## Method

- Develops **ViLL-E**, a unified VideoLLM built on a pretrained ==PaliGemma-3B== foundation, featuring a novel ==attentive-pooling "embedding head"== for dense video embedding generation.
- Implements an adaptive ==end-of-sequence triggered mechanism== for video embedding generation, allowing dynamic adjustment of computational steps based on video complexity.
- Employs a ==three-stage training strategy== combining ==generative== (captioning, QA), ==contrastive== (text-video retrieval), and ==matching== (text-video pair classification, temporal localization) objectives across large-scale and high-quality datasets.

## Results

- Achieved an average improvement of over **8%** in moment retrieval benchmarks (ActivityNet, Charades-STA) over specialized VideoLLMs and surpassed fine-tuned dual-encoder models by up to **4%** on text-to-video retrieval (MSR-VTT).
- Maintained competitive performance on video question answering tasks, achieving **top scores** in several categories (e.g., MSR-VTT QA, VCG) among temporally-specialized VideoLLMs.
- Enabled novel zero-shot capabilities, including composed video retrieval with over **5%** higher R@1 accuracy compared to baselines and improved retrieval using long, detailed captions (**11.4%** R@1 T2V improvement).

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Integrating generative and contrastive learning objectives within a single LLM architecture effectively bridges the performance gap between generative VideoLLMs and specialized retrieval models.
> - An adaptive embedding generation mechanism, which adjusts processing based on video complexity, enhances representational quality and generalization without introducing substantial inference latency.
> - Pre-training with large-scale, detailed human-like captions significantly improves the model's capacity to encode fine-grained video information, benefiting both generative and retrieval tasks.
