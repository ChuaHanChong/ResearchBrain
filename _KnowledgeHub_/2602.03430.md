# ProAct: A Benchmark and Multimodal Framework for Structure-Aware Proactive Response

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from HKUST and Tencent introduced ProAct-75, a vision-based benchmark featuring explicit task graphs for proactive robot response, and ProAct-Helper, a multimodal framework. The framework integrates visual perception with graph-constrained planning to enable structure-aware action selection, demonstrating improved task and step detection (e.g., 17.09% higher task F1) and enhanced collaborative efficiency with more parallel actions.

## Problem

- Existing robotic systems typically operate as passive agents, requiring explicit instructions and limiting autonomous proactive behavior in dynamic environments.
- A scarcity of specialized benchmarks hindered the development of proactive agents, particularly those requiring vision-based input and explicit task graphs for structured decision-making.
- Current video understanding datasets lacked consistent temporal granularity, unified annotation schemes, and crucial task graphs necessary for complex planning and workflow optimization in human-robot collaboration.

## Method

- Developed **ProAct**-75, a new vision-based benchmark comprising 75 tasks with detailed annotations and explicit Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) defining step dependencies and parallel execution possibilities.
- Introduced ProAct-Helper, a multimodal framework built on Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), designed to integrate visual perception with structured task knowledge for proactive decision-making.
- ProAct-Helper incorporates a Hierarchical Binding Module (HBM) to enforce cross-level semantic consistency across trigger, task, and step detections, and an entropy-driven heuristic search algorithm to select optimal proactive actions by prioritizing parallelizable threads.

## Results

- ProAct-Helper (7B) achieved a **17.09%** gain in task F1 and an **11.72%** gain in step F1 over the best closed-source MLLM baseline (Gemini-**2.5**-Pro) on the ProAct-75 benchmark.
- The framework demonstrated superior collaborative efficiency with **0.361** Saved Steps (SS) and a **19.41%** Parallel Actions (PA) rate, significantly outperforming baselines.
- ProAct-Helper exhibited strong robustness to cross-view shifts, retaining approximately **73%** of its Task F1 performance on 'Other View' videos, compared to significantly lower retention rates for baselines.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicit task graphs with AND/OR dependencies are essential for sophisticated planning, enabling agents to identify parallel execution opportunities and optimize workflows beyond sequential actions.
> - Enforcing hierarchical semantic consistency across trigger, task, and step detection using a binding module significantly improves the accuracy and robustness of perception, especially for fine-grained and long-tail categories.
> - An entropy-driven heuristic search, leveraging task graph structures, effectively guides proactive action selection by prioritizing parallelizable threads, leading to more stable and efficient human-robot collaboration.
