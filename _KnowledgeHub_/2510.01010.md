# ImageDoctor: Diagnosing Text-to-Image Generation via Grounded Image Reasoning

> [!summary] Summary
> The ImageDoctor framework introduces a unified, multi-aspect evaluation for text-to-image (T2I) generation, providing both scalar quality scores and pixel-level flaw heatmaps via grounded image reasoning. It leverages this dense, spatially aware feedback to enable a novel DenseFlow-GRPO method, which significantly enhances T2I model performance and human preference alignment in reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).

## Problem

- Existing text-to-image (T2I) evaluation metrics rely on single scalar scores, lacking interpretability and specific diagnostic feedback on *why* an image is good or bad.
- Current reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) methods for T2I models use sparse, image-level rewards, which are insufficient for guiding fine-grained improvements or addressing localized visual artifacts.
- A need for an evaluation model that can provide comprehensive, interpretable, and actionable feedback, including spatially grounded flaw indicators, to effectively diagnose and improve T2I models.

## Method

- **ImageDoctor** employs a multi-modal large language model (MLLM) backbone (Qwen2.5-VL-3B) with a lightweight heatmap decoder to generate both multi-aspect quality scores and pixel-level artifact/misalignment heatmaps.
- It utilizes a "look-think-predict" reasoning paradigm, first localizing flaws with bounding boxes ("look"), then generating structured textual reasoning ("think"), and finally producing quantitative scores and heatmap signals ("predict").
- A two-phase training pipeline involves cold start supervised fine-tuning (score prediction, Chain-of-Thought reasoning) followed by reinforcement fine-tuning with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) guided by grounding, score, and heatmap rewards.

## Results

- ImageDoctor achieved state-of-the-art alignment with human judgments on the RichHF-**18K** dataset, with an average Pearson Linear Correlation Coefficient (PLCC) of **0.741** for score prediction and superior heatmap prediction performance.
- It demonstrated robust generalization across GenAI-Bench and TIFA datasets without specific fine-tuning, consistently outperforming previous human preference models.
- When used as a reward function within the novel DenseFlow-GRPO framework, ImageDoctor's combined scalar scores and heatmaps significantly improved T2I model generation quality, achieving the highest gains across various evaluation metrics for finetuning Stable Diffusion 3.5-medium.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Combining multi-aspect scalar scores with pixel-level flaw heatmaps provides a comprehensive and interpretable diagnostic tool for T2I models, surpassing the limitations of single-score metrics.
> - Dense, spatially grounded rewards derived from heatmaps are more effective for fine-tuning T2I models via RLHF than traditional sparse, image-level rewards, enabling targeted local refinements.
> - A "look-think-predict" reasoning paradigm, inspired by human diagnostic processes and supported by MLLMs, can significantly improve the accuracy and interpretability of automated image evaluation.
