# Seed1.5-VL Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> An advanced vision-language model from ByteDance Seed achieves state-of-the-art performance on 38 out of 60 public benchmarks through a three-stage pre-training pipeline and novel data synthesis approaches, demonstrating particularly strong capabilities in GUI control, document understanding, and video comprehension while maintaining a relatively compact architecture.

## Problem

- Vision-language models suffer from data scarcity compared to text-only models
- Existing VLMs struggle with 3D spatial understanding, object counting, and interactive gameplay
- Balancing model capabilities across diverse multimodal tasks while maintaining efficiency

## Method

- ==Three-stage pre-training pipeline== combining ==masked image modeling==, ==contrastive learning==, and omni-modal training
- Custom ==data synthesis pipelines== for generating high-quality training data across multiple domains
- ==Hybrid parallelism== and workload balancing techniques for efficient large-scale training

## Results

- Achieves state-of-the-art performance on **38/60** public benchmarks, demonstrating broad competitiveness across a wide range of vision-language evaluation suites and task types
- Superior performance in GUI agent tasks compared to existing foundation VLMs, plus strong capabilities in document understanding, grounding, counting, and video comprehension
- Successfully deployed in real-world applications through the Doubao chatbot and the Volcano Engine API, validating performance in dynamic, interactive production environments

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training loss on specific data categories predicts performance on related downstream tasks
> - Rejection sampling combined with online reinforcement learning enables progressive model enhancement
> - Balance between model size and computational efficiency is crucial for practical deployment

## Detailed Report

### 1. How This Work Fits into the Broader Research Landscape

- **Vision-Language Models (VLMs) as a Foundational Paradigm:** The introduction positions VLMs as crucial for building general-purpose AI capable of interacting with both virtual and physical environments. This aligns with a major trend in AI research toward multimodal models that can process and understand information from multiple sources (text, images, video, audio, etc.).
- **Addressing Limitations of Current VLMs:** The paper acknowledges that existing VLMs still fall short of human-level performance in areas such as 3D spatial understanding, object counting, and interactive game play. This highlights the group's aim to push the boundaries of VLM capabilities and address known weaknesses.
- **Data Scarcity in VLMs:** The authors correctly point out that VLMs suffer from a lack of high-quality, diverse training data compared to LLMs. A significant part of the research focuses on developing data synthesis pipelines to address this limitation.
- **Competition with Existing Models:** The report benchmarks Seed1.5-VL against leading models such as OpenAI's GPT-4o and Claude 3.7, indicating its ambition to compete with state-of-the-art commercial systems.
- **Agent-Centric Tasks:** The focus on GUI control and gameplay aligns with the growing interest in using VLMs to create AI agents that can interact with and manipulate computer interfaces, automate tasks, and play games.
- **Emphasis on Efficiency:** The relatively compact architecture (compared to some other VLMs) and the streamlined design suggest a focus on reducing inference costs and computational demands, which is essential for real-world deployment.
- **Contribution to the Research Community:** The decision to publish a detailed technical report, including insights into data curation, training methodologies, and infrastructure optimizations, demonstrates a desire to contribute back to the wider AI research community and foster further innovation.
- **Building on Transformer Architectures:** The choice of a ViT (Vision Transformer) backbone and a Mixture-of-Experts LLM shows the reliance on established and successful architectures, allowing the research to focus on more novel aspects like data curation and training techniques.

### 2. Key Objectives and Motivation

- **Advance General-Purpose Multimodal Understanding and Reasoning:** This is the overarching objective, emphasizing the desire to create AI models that can seamlessly process and understand both visual and textual information.
- **Overcome Data Scarcity:** The primary motivation is to address the lack of high-quality vision-language data by developing diverse data synthesis pipelines.
- **Improve Performance in Challenging Tasks:** Specific objectives include improving performance in tasks requiring 3D spatial understanding, object counting, imaginative visual inference, and interactive game play.
- **Enhance Efficiency and Accessibility:** A key goal is to develop a relatively compact and efficient model, reducing inference costs and making the model more accessible for broader use.
- **Develop Novel Training Techniques:** The report details innovations in hybrid parallelism and workload balancing to efficiently train large-scale multimodal models.
- **Comprehensive Evaluation and Benchmarking:** A strong emphasis is placed on rigorously evaluating the model's capabilities on a wide range of public and internal benchmarks.
- **Real-World Deployment:** A major motivation is to deploy and test the model in a real-world chatbot system to monitor its performance in dynamic, interactive environments.
- **Contribute to Open Research:** The authors aim to inspire further research by providing a comprehensive review of their experiences building Seed1.5-VL, covering model design, data construction, and training.

### 3. Methodology and Approach

- **Model Architecture:** The Seed1.5-VL architecture consists of a vision encoder (Seed-ViT) and a Mixture-of-Experts LLM.
- **Vision Encoder Pre-training:** A three-stage pre-training pipeline is used for the vision encoder: (i) Masked Image Modeling (MIM) with 2D RoPE, (ii) Native-Resolution Contrastive Learning, and (iii) Omni-modal Pre-training.
- **Pre-training Data Curation:** A large and diverse pre-training corpus (3 trillion tokens) is created, including generic image-text pairs, OCR data, visual grounding data, 3D spatial understanding data, video data, STEM data, and GUI data. Specific data synthesis pipelines are developed for each category.
- **VLM Pre-training:** A three-stage VLM pre-training methodology is used, including aligning the vision encoder with the language model by training the MLP adapter, training all model parameters on a multimodal corpus, and creating a more balanced data mixture across different tasks.
- **Supervised Fine-tuning (SFT):** SFT is used to equip the model with instruction-following and reasoning abilities. An SFT dataset is created, comprising general instruction data and Long Chain-of-Thought (LongCoT) data.
- **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF):** Preference data is collected through human annotation and heuristic synthesis to train a reward model. PPO is used for optimization.
- **Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR):** RLVR is used to enhance performance in tasks with verifiable answers, such as STEM problems, grounding, visual puzzles, and perception-related games.
- **Training Infrastructure:** A hybrid parallelism scheme, workload balancing, parallelism-aware data loading, and fault tolerance are used for efficient large-scale training.
- **Iterative Update by Rejection Sampling Fine-tuning:** Rejection sampling is combined with online reinforcement learning. The SFT model is progressively enhanced by distilling the RL model’s learnings on diverse prompts.
- **Scaling Laws Analysis:** The scaling behavior of the model is analyzed to understand the relationship between training loss, training tokens, and downstream performance.

### 4. Main Findings and Results

- **State-of-the-Art Performance:** The model achieves state-of-the-art performance on 38 out of 60 public benchmarks.
- **Strong Multimodal Reasoning:** Seed1.5-VL demonstrates strong capabilities in multimodal reasoning tasks.
- **Document and Chart Understanding:** The model excels in document and chart understanding tasks.
- **Grounding and Counting:** A significant strength of the model is in grounding and counting tasks.
- **Video Understanding:** The model demonstrates strong proficiency in video understanding across various dimensions, including short video, long video, streaming video, video reasoning, and video grounding.
- **GUI Agent Capabilities:** The model achieves significantly better performance in GUI agent tasks compared to other foundation VLMs.
- **Out-of-Distribution Generalization:** The model demonstrates strong out-of-distribution generalization capabilities.
- **Efficient Architecture:** The model maintains a compact and efficient architecture.
- **Scaling Laws:** The training loss for most data sub-categories exhibits a clear adherence to established scaling relationships. The training loss achieved on specific data sub-categories can serve as a predictor for performance on related downstream tasks.

### 5. Significance and Potential Impact

- **Advancement of General-Purpose AI:** The work contributes to the development of more capable and versatile AI models that can understand and interact with the world in a more human-like way.
- **Improved VLM Performance:** The model's state-of-the-art results on a wide range of benchmarks demonstrate significant progress in VLM capabilities.
- **Practical Applications:** The model's strong performance in agent-centric tasks and its efficient architecture make it well-suited for real-world applications in areas such as education, healthcare, chatbots, and wearable devices.
- **Enhanced Human-Computer Interaction:** The GUI agent capabilities could lead to more intuitive and seamless ways for people to interact with computers and other devices.
- **Contribution to the Research Community:** The detailed technical report and the sharing of training methodologies and infrastructure optimizations can benefit other researchers in the field and accelerate progress in VLM development.
- **Economic Impact:** The availability of Seed1.5-VL on the Volcano Engine API platform could enable businesses and developers to leverage its capabilities to build innovative applications and services.
- **Future Research Directions:** The identified limitations of the model, such as those related to robust 3D spatial reasoning, hallucination mitigation, and complex combinatorial search, provide valuable directions for future research efforts. The emphasis on unifying existing model capabilities with image generation and incorporating robust tool-use mechanisms also highlights promising avenues for future work.
