# ConsisVLA-4D: Advancing Spatiotemporal Consistency in Efficient 3D-Perception and 4D-Reasoning for Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> A framework from Harbin Institute of Technology, ConsisVLA-4D, enhances spatiotemporal consistency for robotic manipulation by integrating efficient 3D perception and 4D reasoning from 2D observations. It attains a 98.1% average success rate on LIBERO benchmarks and reduces inference latency by 2.31x while decreasing training costs by 1.36x compared to prior VLA models.

## Problem

- Existing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models struggle with inefficient and inconsistent 3D spatial understanding when relying primarily on 2D visual observations, leading to issues like projection bias and high computational overhead for explicit 3D methods.
- Current VLA models exhibit underdeveloped 4D spatiotemporal reasoning in dynamic scenes, often failing to consistently correlate current spatial states with predicted future scenes or understand evolving object identities and geometric relationships.

## Method

- The ==Cross-View Aligner== (**CV-Aligner**) efficiently constructs semantically consistent 3D representations by selecting instruction-relevant object tokens from multi-view 2D inputs and fusing them with 3D features.
- The ==Cross-Object Fuser== (**CO-Fuser**) resolves geometric ambiguity by implicitly aggregating spatial geometric relationships across multiple viewpoints into compact latent representations.
- The ==Cross-Scene Thinker== (**CS-Thinker**) employs ==Spatiotemporal Consistency Attention== to learn implicit local object dynamics and global depth knowledge, enabling efficient 4D visual reasoning for stable action generation without explicit intermediate predictions during inference.

## Results

- ConsisVLA-4D achieved an average success rate of **98.1%** across LIBERO simulation benchmarks, outperforming SpatialVLA by **20%** and CoT-VLA by **14.2%**, and demonstrated robust performance in ManiSkill2 and RoboTwin 2.0.
- In real-world tasks, the framework attained an average success rate of **70.0%** on long-horizon bimanual tasks, showing a substantial improvement over OpenVLA (**28.5%**) and OpenVLA-OFT (**51.8%**) with strong sim-to-real transfer.
- The model demonstrated significant efficiency gains, including a **2.31x** speedup in inference latency (0.110 seconds) and a **1.36x** reduction in training cost compared to OpenVLA, alongside a FLOPs reduction from 8.48 T to 4.59 T.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Instruction-guided semantic object selection and cross-view fusion significantly reduce visual redundancy (e.g., 1/8 of original visual inputs) while maintaining robust 3D semantic consistency.
> - Implicit aggregation of geometric relations across views using learnable tokens provides an efficient method to resolve 3D spatial ambiguities and capture complex object layouts without redundant information.
> - Learning implicit knowledge of dynamic objects and global depth within a Spatiotemporal Consistency Attention mechanism enables effective 4D reasoning, leading to stable action prediction without requiring explicit future scene generation at inference.
