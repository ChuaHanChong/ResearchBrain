# WebThinker: Empowering Large Reasoning Models with Deep Research Capability

> [!summary] Summary
> The WebThinker framework, developed by researchers from Renmin University of China and collaborators, equips Large Reasoning Models with autonomous deep web exploration and an interleaved think-search-and-draft strategy for comprehensive report generation. It achieved state-of-the-art performance among 32B models on challenging reasoning benchmarks like GAIA and Humanity's Last Exam, and produced more complete and thorough scientific reports than existing methods.

## Problem

- Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) are limited by reliance on static, internal knowledge, making them suboptimal for complex, knowledge-intensive tasks requiring up-to-date external information.
- Existing open-source Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) agents often use predefined workflows, restricting LRMs' ability to deeply explore the web and dynamically interact with search results.
- There is a need for systems that can conduct in-depth web information retrieval, mining, and integration beyond shallow search to generate comprehensive and accurate reports.

## Method

- WebThinker introduces a =='Deep Web Explorer' module==, allowing LRMs to autonomously search, navigate web pages (via link-clicking), and extract information to address knowledge gaps.
- An =='Autonomous Think-Search-and-Draft' strategy== enables LRMs to seamlessly interleave reasoning, information gathering, and report writing, using specialized tools for drafting, checking, and editing content.
- The framework employs ==iterative online Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)== to refine the LRM's ability to perceive information needs, reason, and effectively utilize research tools.

## Results

- WebThinker-32B-RL achieved State-of-the-Art (SOTA) performance among **32B** models on complex reasoning benchmarks, including a **+21.5%** gain over its base version on Humanity's Last Exam (HLE) and surpassing stronger proprietary models like o3-mini (High) on HLE.
- On scientific report generation tasks from Glaive, WebThinker-32B (RL version) achieved the highest overall average score (**8.1**), outperforming RAG baselines and advanced non-proprietary systems like Grok3 DeeperSearch.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical contributions of the Deep Web Explorer, especially its link-clicking capability, and the 'Autonomous Report Draft' component, with RL training being vital for complex problem-solving performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Interleaving reasoning with dynamic information gathering and iterative report refinement is crucial for tackling complex, knowledge-intensive tasks, surpassing static RAG workflows.
> - Empowering LRMs with hierarchical web exploration capabilities, including deep navigation beyond initial search results, is vital for comprehensive information acquisition.
> - Reinforcement learning, specifically iterative online DPO, effectively optimizes an LRM's strategic use of research tools, leading to significant performance gains in complex problem-solving.
