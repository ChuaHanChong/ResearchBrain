# Talk2Move: Reinforcement Learning for Text-Instructed Object-Level Geometric Transformation in Scenes

> [!summary] Summary
> TALK2MOVE, developed by researchers at AWS Agentic AI, introduces a reinforcement learning framework for precise, text-instructed geometric transformations of objects within scenes. This method leverages flow-based Group Relative Policy Optimization and spatially grounded reward models, outperforming state-of-the-art models in translation, rotation, and resizing accuracy while maintaining scene coherence.

## Problem

- Existing graphics-based editing tools require significant human intervention and specialized expertise for object manipulation.
- General text-based image editing models struggle with precise geometric commands (e.g., 'rotate 90 degrees') and disentangling specific objects from the background.
- Supervised learning for fine-grained geometric transformations is hampered by the scarcity of high-quality paired 'before-and-after' training data.
- Reinforcement learning approaches for diffusion models are often computationally expensive due to dense rollout generation.

## Method

- The work formulates text-guided geometric object transformation as a reinforcement learning problem within a diffusion framework, utilizing ==flow-based Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)==.
- It introduces ==spatially grounded reward models== that use specialist off-the-shelf tools (e.g., object localization, depth estimation, orientation estimation) to provide fine-grained feedback on object displacement, rotation, and scaling.
- The method incorporates =='off-policy step evaluation'== and =='active step sampling'== mechanisms to significantly improve GRPO training efficiency by focusing on the most informative denoising steps.
- A two-stage training pipeline starts with a small SFT 'cold start' using synthesized paired data, followed by extensive GRPO training with input-only data.

## Results

- TALK2MOVE consistently achieved superior performance across translation, rotation, and resizing tasks, outperforming state-of-the-art models like GPT-Image-1, with a **76.67%** translation accuracy and **29.55%** rotation accuracy on synthetic data.
- Human evaluation confirmed the method's superiority, with TALK2MOVE achieving the highest winning rates across all three tasks (**57.50%** translation, **68.75%** rotation, **63.89%** resizing) due to better editing accuracy and scene coherence.
- Ablation studies demonstrated that the active step sampling mechanism reduced training iteration time by **49%** compared to full sampling and improved editing correctness, indicating both efficiency and performance gains.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Formulating geometric object transformation as an RL problem, particularly with GRPO, allows effective learning without extensive paired 'before-and-after' data, addressing a major data scarcity challenge.
> - Spatially grounded reward models, which directly evaluate object-level geometric metrics, are crucial for achieving precise and semantically faithful transformations.
> - Computational efficiency of RL for diffusion models can be significantly improved by identifying and actively sampling the most informative denoising steps, accelerating convergence and performance.
> - A small amount of supervised pre-training (SFT cold start) provides beneficial initial priors, improving the stability and quality of subsequent reinforcement learning.
