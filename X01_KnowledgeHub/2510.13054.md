# VLA-0: Building State-of-the-Art VLAs with Zero Modification

> [!summary] Summary
> NVIDIA researchers introduce VLA-0, a Vision-Language-Action model that achieves state-of-the-art robotic manipulation by directly representing robot actions as numerical text strings and fine-tuning an unmodified Vision-Language Model. This minimalist design outperforms more complex or extensively pretrained alternatives in both simulation and real-world tasks.

## Problem

- Existing VLA models introduce complexity through architectural modifications, specialized action heads, or vocabulary alterations, leading to trade-offs like restricted action resolution or compromised language understanding.
- The purest form of representing robot actions directly as text using a VLM's native capabilities for low-level control has largely been unexplored, despite its potential for simplicity and arbitrary action resolution.
- Achieving high-fidelity action generation for embodied agents while preserving the foundational knowledge of powerful pre-trained Vision-Language Models remains a challenge.

## Method

- **VLA-0** utilizes an unmodified, off-the-shelf ==Qwen-VL-2.5-3B== Vision-Language Model, avoiding new tokens, vocabulary changes, or additional neural network layers.
- Robot actions are represented as continuous values normalized to an integer range and generated directly as ==space-separated numerical text strings== by the VLM's native ==text generation== capability.
- The approach incorporates a novel ==masked action augmentation== during training and an ==action ensembling== technique during inference to enhance robustness and performance.

## Results

- VLA-0 achieves an average success rate of **94.7%** on the LIBERO benchmark, outperforming all existing non-pretrained VLA methods and securing an average rank of **1.0**.
- It demonstrates highly competitive performance against models pretrained on vast robotic datasets, achieving the second-best average rank (**2.8**) among all evaluated models.
- In real-world evaluations on an SO-100 robot, VLA-0 surpasses a strong pretrained baseline (SmolVLA) by an average of **12.5 points** in success rate across **four** manipulation tasks.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Directly representing continuous robot actions as numerical text strings and leveraging a VLM's native text generation, without any architectural modifications, can achieve state-of-the-art performance.
> - Preserving the integrity of the underlying Vision-Language Model by avoiding architectural changes can unlock latent capabilities for effective action generation and potentially enhance generalization.
> - Simple training and inference strategies, such as masked action augmentation and action ensembling, are crucial for extracting high performance from this minimalist VLA design.
