# OTFusion: Bridging Vision-only and Vision-Language Models via Optimal Transport for Transductive Zero-Shot Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> OTFusion unifies Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Vision-only Foundation Models (VFMs) using Optimal Transport to enhance Transductive Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL). The training-free framework achieves an average accuracy improvement of nearly 10% over the CLIP baseline across 11 datasets, often surpassing training-based methods.

## Problem

- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) excel at semantic alignment but struggle with fine-grained visual distinctions and domain shifts in Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL).
- Vision-only Foundation Models (VFMs) provide rich, discriminative visual features but inherently lack semantic grounding for direct ZSL.
- Existing methods to combine these models are often computationally expensive, require extensive training, or lack broad generalizability across different foundation models.

## Method

- OTFusion proposes a ==training-free framework== that models both VLM and VFM outputs as probability distributions over class labels.
- A ==Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM)== is fitted to VFM features to generate a visual prior, while VLM predictions provide a semantic prior.
- ==Optimal Transport== is employed to fuse these visual and semantic priors, creating a unified probabilistic representation through an ==alternating optimization scheme== with mutual reinforcement.

## Results

- The framework achieves an average accuracy improvement of nearly **10%** over the CLIP baseline across **11** diverse benchmark datasets.
- OTFusion frequently outperforms state-of-the-art training-based ZSL methods by over **10%** on average, demonstrating high efficiency without additional training.
- The method exhibits fast convergence (typically within **5 iterations**) and robust performance across a range of hyperparameters, particularly the entropy regularization strength.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - VLMs and VFMs offer complementary strengths: VLMs provide semantic structure, while VFMs offer fine-grained visual discrimination.
> - Optimal Transport effectively bridges these diverse foundation models by operating in the probability space, allowing for training-free and model-agnostic integration.
> - A joint optimization that mutually reinforces GMM parameter updates with the evolving Optimal Transport plan ensures that visual clusters are not only coherent but also semantically aligned.
