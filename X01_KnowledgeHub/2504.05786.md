# How to Enable LLM with 3D Capacity? A Survey of Spatial Reasoning in LLM

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Tsinghua University and HKUST-GZ present a comprehensive survey of methods for enabling spatial reasoning in Large Language Models (LLMs), organizing approaches into image-based, point cloud-based, and hybrid modalities while analyzing current limitations and future directions for integrating 3D understanding capabilities.

## Problem

- LLMs lack inherent ability to understand and reason about 3D spatial information
- Fundamental mismatch between sequential text processing and continuous 3D spatial structures
- Scarcity of high-quality 3D datasets compared to text corpora

## Method

- Systematic categorization of approaches based on input modality (==image==, ==point cloud==, ==hybrid==)
- Analysis of different ==alignment strategies== for connecting language and 3D data
- Review of architectural modifications and training strategies for spatial reasoning

## Results

- Comprehensive taxonomy for understanding diverse approaches to **3D-capable LLMs**
- Identification of key technical challenges across different modalities
- Analysis of current limitations in dataset quality, computation, and cross-scene generalization
- Roadmap of future research directions in perception, fusion, and real-world applications

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Image-based methods offer data availability advantages but struggle with depth perception
> - Point cloud approaches provide better geometric fidelity but face computational challenges
> - Hybrid methods can leverage complementary strengths through careful architectural design
> - Three main alignment strategies emerge: direct, step-by-step, and task-specific
