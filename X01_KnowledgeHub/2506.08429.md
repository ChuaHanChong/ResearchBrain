# Better Reasoning with Less Data: Enhancing VLMs Through Unified Modality Scoring

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers developed SCALE, an automated pipeline that curates high-quality multimodal instruction tuning datasets by integrating unimodal and cross-modal quality assessments. This method allows Visual Language Models trained on less than 10% of the original data to achieve superior performance compared to models trained on full, uncurated datasets across various benchmarks.

## Problem

- Visual Language Model instruction tuning datasets suffer from noisy image-text correspondences.
- Existing datasets contain ambiguous or misleading textual descriptions, degrading overall data quality.
- Manually curating large-scale, high-quality multimodal datasets is prohibitively expensive and time-consuming.

## Method

- Introduced SCALE, a ==five-stage automated pipeline== for comprehensive data quality assessment, integrating ==unimodal (image and text) and multimodal alignment evaluations==.
- Utilized large language models (Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct) for predicting data tasks and rating text and multimodal quality.
- Employed a lightweight VLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) to generate ==general and task-specific image captions==, unifying visual and linguistic representations into a text-only format for streamlined multimodal comparison.

## Results

- Models fine-tuned on less than **10%** of SCALE-curated data consistently outperformed models trained on the full **500K** instruction tuning dataset.
- SCALE achieved an average score of **84.23** across various benchmarks, surpassing the full data baseline (**83.94**) and all other data selection baselines.
- The internal task prediction component using Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct achieved over **80%** accuracy for all defined sub-tasks, demonstrating its robustness in categorizing data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training VLMs on a significantly smaller, quality-curated subset of data can lead to superior performance compared to using the entire, uncurated dataset.
> - Unimodal data quality assessments (image-only or text-only) are insufficient and can bias selection by overlooking crucial, task-specific aspects or discarding valuable lower-fidelity samples.
> - Generated image captions effectively transform complex image-text multimodal alignment into a unified text modality, simplifying the evaluation process and improving efficiency.
