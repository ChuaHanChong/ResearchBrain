# MViTv2: Improved Multiscale Vision Transformers for Classification and Detection

> [!summary] Summary
> Facebook AI Research introduces MViTv2, an enhanced Multiscale Vision Transformer architecture that refines the pooling attention mechanism with decomposed relative positional embeddings and residual pooling connections. This approach achieves new state-of-the-art results across image classification on ImageNet-1K, object detection on COCO, and video recognition on Kinetics and Something-Something-v2 benchmarks.

## Problem

- Standard Vision Transformers (ViTs) face quadratic computational and memory complexity challenges with high-resolution inputs, limiting their application in tasks like object detection and video understanding.
- Developing a unified architecture that can efficiently achieve high performance across diverse visual recognition tasks (image classification, object detection, video recognition) remains a challenge.
- The initial MViTv1's pooling attention, while efficient, had opportunities for improvement in terms of shift-invariance and optimizing information flow within its attention blocks.

## Method

- Develops a refined ==Multiscale Vision Transformer (MViTv2)== with a hierarchical design that progressively reduces spatial/temporal resolution while increasing channel depth, similar to CNNs.
- Introduces ==decomposed relative positional embeddings==, separating positional encoding into height, width, and temporal components, to improve shift-invariance and reduce memory complexity for high-resolution inputs.
- Incorporates a ==residual pooling connection== that adds the pooled query tensor directly to the attention block's output, improving information flow and facilitating the training of deep pooling attention networks.

## Results

- Achieved **88.8%** top-1 accuracy on ImageNet-1K with ImageNet-21K pre-training and **512x512** resolution, setting a new state-of-the-art.
- Attained **55.8** APbox on COCO object detection using Cascade Mask R-CNN, outperforming Swin-B by **+2.5** APbox with less computation.
- Established new state-of-the-art benchmarks for video recognition, including **86.1%** on Kinetics-400 and **73.3%** on Something-Something-v2, demonstrating strong temporal modeling capabilities and up to **3.9x** faster training on COCO with improved components.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Refinements to the pooling attention mechanism, specifically decomposed relative positional embeddings and residual pooling connections, significantly boost performance and efficiency in hierarchical Vision Transformers.
> - A single, unified multiscale Transformer architecture can effectively serve as a state-of-the-art backbone across diverse visual tasks, from image classification to dense video understanding, minimizing task-specific architectural changes.
> - Pooling attention offers a superior accuracy-to-computation trade-off compared to global or windowed attention, proving crucial for efficient processing of high-resolution inputs in complex vision tasks.
