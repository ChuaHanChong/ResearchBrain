# MLLM-4D: Towards Visual-based Spatial-Temporal Intelligence

> [!summary] Summary
> MLLM-4D equips Multimodal Large Language Models with 4D spatial-temporal intelligence, enabling them to perceive and reason about dynamic 3D environments from 2D video inputs. It establishes new state-of-the-art performance on the MLLM4D-Bench benchmark with an average score of 72.7%, substantially surpassing proprietary models like Gemini 2.5 Pro.

## Problem

- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) lack robust capabilities for 4D spatial-temporal intelligence, specifically perceiving and reasoning about the evolution of 3D space over time from purely visual inputs.
- A critical bottleneck is the scarcity of high-quality, large-scale 4D instructional data, as manual annotation is costly and unscalable.
- Existing MLLMs, even those with 3D understanding, are constrained to static scenes or rely on auxiliary 3D geometric inputs, struggling with dynamic environments without complex architectural changes.

## Method

- A cost-efficient, automated ==data curation pipeline== converts existing stereoscopic video datasets into large-scale, high-quality 4D instructional data, including ==MLLM4D-2M== (SFT), MLLM4D-R1-30k (RFT), and MLLM4D-Bench (evaluation).
- A ==two-stage post-training framework== is applied to standard MLLM architectures, avoiding architectural modifications.
- Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) builds foundational 4D understanding, followed by Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) that incorporates a ==Spatiotemporal Chain-of-Thought (ST-CoT)== prompting strategy and physics-grounded Spatiotemporal reward functions.

## Results

- MLLM-4D achieves state-of-the-art performance on the MLLM4D-Bench benchmark with an average score of **72.7%**, significantly outperforming proprietary models like Gemini 2.5 Pro (**46.6%**) and other open-source MLLMs.
- The framework demonstrates strong generalizability and robustness on the out-of-distribution VLM4D benchmark, maintaining superior performance over base models and specialized 3D spatial reasoning models.
- Ablation studies confirm that the scalable data curation and the hierarchical post-training strategy, particularly the physics-grounded Spatiotemporal Reward, are crucial for building robust 4D understanding and reasoning, with performance scaling observed with increased data.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Leveraging existing stereoscopic video datasets with an automated pipeline is a scalable and cost-effective method to generate large volumes of high-quality 4D instructional data.
> - Standard MLLM architectures can acquire sophisticated 4D spatial-temporal reasoning capabilities through a hierarchical post-training strategy, negating the need for complex architectural modifications.
> - Integrating a Spatiotemporal Chain-of-Thought and physics-grounded rewards effectively grounds the model's reasoning in visual physics, leading to more interpretable and physically plausible 4D understanding.
