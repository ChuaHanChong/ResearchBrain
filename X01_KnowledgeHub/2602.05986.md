# RISE-Video: Can Video Generators Decode Implicit World Rules?

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Tencent Youtu Lab developed RISE-Video, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating the implicit world reasoning capabilities of Text-Image-to-Video (TI2V) models. This framework, which leverages an LMM-based automated judging pipeline, revealed that even leading TI2V models like Hailuo 2.3 achieved only 22.5% accuracy, consistently struggling with higher-level reasoning despite their visual fidelity.

## Problem

- Existing video generation benchmarks predominantly emphasize surface-level qualities such as perceptual realism and temporal consistency, lacking diagnostic granularity for higher-level cognitive reasoning.
- The capacity of current Text-Image-to-Video (TI2V) models to internalize and reason over implicit world rules (e.g., physical laws, social norms, logical inferences) remains largely unquantified.
- Without a dedicated framework, models might generate visually convincing but physically implausible, logically inconsistent, or experientially nonsensical videos, hindering the development of true 'world simulators'.

## Method

- Introduced ==RISE-Video==, a reasoning-oriented benchmark featuring ==467 human-annotated samples== across eight distinct reasoning categories, from perceptual to logical knowledge.
- Developed a ==multi-dimensional evaluation protocol== using four complementary metrics: Reasoning Alignment, Temporal Consistency, Physical Rationality, and Visual Quality, with specialized handling for abstract tasks.
- Proposed an ==automated LMM-based judging pipeline==, primarily utilizing GPT-5, to efficiently assess videos while maintaining high alignment with human judgments.

## Results

- The top-performing TI2V model, Hailuo 2.3, achieved only **22.5%** accuracy on the RISE-Video benchmark, indicating pervasive deficiencies in implicit reasoning across all **11** evaluated models.
- Models performed best on low-level Perceptual Knowledge tasks but showed consistently low performance in Logical Capability, highlighting a fundamental limitation in abstract, rule-based reasoning.
- Sora 2 showed notable strength in Temporal Consistency, while Hailuo 2.3 demonstrated a clear advantage in Reasoning Alignment, outperforming the second-ranked model by **6.6%** in this metric.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Current state-of-the-art TI2V models, despite their impressive visual fidelity, systematically struggle with implicit world reasoning, particularly in logical and complex experiential scenarios.
> - There is a significant performance gap between closed-source and open-source models, with open-source models exhibiting more visual artifacts and lower reasoning capabilities.
> - The LMM-based judging pipeline, validated through ablation studies, demonstrates robust alignment with human preferences, enabling scalable and reliable evaluation for reasoning in video generation.
