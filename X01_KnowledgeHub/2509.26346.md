# EditReward: A Human-Aligned Reward Model for Instruction-Guided Image Editing

> [!summary] Summary
> A human-aligned reward model, EDITREWARD, accurately assesses instruction-guided image edits, leveraging a meticulously annotated 200K preference pair dataset. This model achieves state-of-the-art evaluation performance and enables the curation of high-quality training data, significantly advancing open-source image editing capabilities.

## Problem

- Open-source instruction-guided image editing models lag behind proprietary solutions due to a lack of reliable, human-aligned reward mechanisms.
- Existing evaluation methods like perceptual scores, feature scores, and general Vision-Language Models (VLMs) fail to capture precise editing semantics or align with nuanced human preferences.
- Fine-tuning general VLMs for this task is hindered by noisy, low inter-annotator agreement in crowd-sourced annotations or biased pseudo-labels from proprietary models.

## Method

- Constructed ==EDITREWARD-DATA==, a large-scale (over 200K preference pairs) and high-fidelity human preference dataset annotated by experts across two dimensions: Instruction Following and Visual Quality.
- Developed ==EDITREWARD==, a specialized reward model using VLM backbones, a ==multi-dimensional uncertainty-aware ranking framework==, and a novel strategy to disentangle ties in human preferences.
- Introduced ==EDITREWARD-BENCH==, a robust benchmark with challenging multi-way preference comparison tasks to more accurately evaluate and differentiate reward models.

## Results

- EDITREWARD achieved new state-of-the-art results on GenAI-Bench (**65.72%** accuracy) and AURORA-Bench (**63.62%** accuracy), outperforming proprietary models like GPT-5 and OpenAI-GPT-4o.
- On the challenging EDITREWARD-BENCH, EDITREWARD scored **38.42%**, surpassing leading proprietary models like Gemini-2.5-Flash (**38.02%**) and GPT-5 (**37.81%**).
- Utilizing EDITREWARD for data curation, fine-tuning the Step1X-Edit model on a 20K high-quality subset improved its GEdit-Bench score from **6.444** to **7.086**, making it competitive with top-tier editors like Doubao.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Multi-dimensional human annotation and uncertainty-aware ranking are critical for accurately capturing the nuanced quality of instruction-guided image edits.
> - Decomposing tied preference pairs into distinct dimensional preferences provides richer supervisory signals, leading to more stable and performant model training.
> - Data quality, as assessed by a highly human-aligned reward model, is more impactful than mere data quantity for training superior downstream image editing models.
