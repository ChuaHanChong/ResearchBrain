# ViSurf: Visual Supervised-and-Reinforcement Fine-Tuning for Large Vision-and-Language Models

> [!summary] Summary
> ViSurf introduces a unified, single-stage post-training framework that integrates supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) for large vision-and-language models (LVLMs). The method consistently outperforms existing paradigms across diverse visual perception and reasoning tasks, achieving an average relative gain of 38.6% and mitigating catastrophic forgetting.

## Problem

- Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) often leads to sub-optimal performance and catastrophic forgetting in Large Vision-and-Language Models (LVLMs).
- Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) struggles with tasks requiring knowledge beyond the model's pre-existing base, particularly when self-generated rollouts are unreliable.
- Sequential SFT RLVR pipelines are computationally expensive, complex, and still prone to catastrophic forgetting during the initial SFT phase.

## Method

- ==ViSurf== proposes a unified, single-stage post-training paradigm that integrates ==SFT and RLVR objectives==, leveraging a theoretical analysis of their gradient similarity.
- The method constructs an ==augmented rollout set== that includes both model-generated rollouts and ground-truth labels, with rewards computed for both to influence advantage calculations.
- Three novel ==reward control strategies== are introduced: aligning ground-truth labels with model preference, eliminating thinking reward for ground-truth, and adaptively smoothing ground-truth rewards to balance external supervision with internal reinforcement.

## Results

- ViSurf achieved superior performance across diverse visual-language tasks, demonstrating an average relative gain of **38.6%** over the baseline and outperforming SFT, RLVR, and sequential SFT RLVR methods.
- The framework effectively mitigated catastrophic forgetting, maintaining or improving performance on VQA tasks while fine-tuning for new perception tasks, unlike SFT and sequential methods.
- Ablation studies confirmed the critical contribution of each reward control strategy to improved performance, training stability, and entropy behavior, additionally showing reduced dependence on meticulous prompt design.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - The gradients of SFT and RLVR objectives share a similar structural form, which enables their effective integration into a single, unified objective function.
> - Adaptive reward control strategies, particularly smoothing the ground-truth reward, allow ViSurf to dynamically adjust the influence of external supervision based on the model's internal generative capabilities.
> - Injecting ground-truth labels directly into the RL framework within a single training loop provides robust guidance, mitigating catastrophic forgetting and enhancing performance on out-of-distribution tasks more effectively than multi-stage approaches.
