# A Pragmatic VLA Foundation Model

> [!summary] Summary
> The LingBot-VLA foundation model empirically demonstrates that real-world VLA performance consistently scales with increasing pre-training data up to 20,000 hours without saturation. It achieves improved performance on 100 real-world manipulation tasks across multiple robotic platforms and exhibits enhanced training efficiency and data-efficient adaptation.

## Problem

- There was a lack of comprehensive empirical studies on how real-robot performance of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models scales with increasingly massive pre-training datasets.
- The VLA community lacked a highly optimized training codebase capable of efficiently conducting large-scale scaling evaluations on multi-node clusters due to I/O bottlenecks and communication overheads.
- Existing VLA evaluation methodologies often struggle to provide robust, large-scale real-world assessments across diverse robotic platforms and numerous tasks.

## Method

- Developed **LingBot-VLA**, a ==Mixture-of-Transformers== architecture integrating a pre-trained VLM (Qwen2.5-VL) as its semantic backbone and using ==Flow Matching== for continuous action modeling.
- Assembled a large-scale pre-training dataset of approximately **20,000 hours** of real-world teleoperated manipulation data from **9 distinct dual-arm robot embodiments**.
- Implemented systemic training optimizations including ==Hybrid Sharded Data Parallel==, ==FlexAttention==, and ==operator fusion via `torch.compile`== to enhance throughput and scalability.

## Results

- **LingBot-VLA** with depth achieved an average Success Rate (SR) improvement of **4.28%** and a Progress Score (PS) increase of **7.76%** over the π_0.5 baseline on the GM-100 real-world benchmark.
- The optimized codebase achieved a throughput of **261 samples per second** with an 8-GPU setup, representing a **1.5x to 2.8x speedup** over existing VLA-oriented codebases and exhibiting near-linear scaling up to **256 GPUs**.
- Demonstrated superior data efficiency in post-training, outperforming π_0.5 even with **80 demonstrations** per task compared to π_0.5's full **130 demonstrations**, with the performance gap widening as data increased.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Real-world VLA performance consistently improves as pre-training data volume scales from 3,000 to 20,000 hours, showing no saturation and indicating continued benefits from larger datasets.
> - Integrating spatial awareness through a vision distillation approach with depth information enhances VLA models' success rates and progress scores in both real-world and simulated environments.
> - An optimized training codebase significantly improves efficiency, delivering substantial throughput speedups and near-linear scaling for large-scale VLA model development.
