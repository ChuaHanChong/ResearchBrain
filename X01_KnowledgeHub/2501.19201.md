# Efficient Reasoning with Hidden Thinking

> [!summary] Summary
> A new framework called Heima allows Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to perform complex reasoning efficiently by compressing verbose Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps into single "thinking tokens." This approach reduces token generation to as low as 6% of original counts while maintaining or improving zero-shot accuracy on diverse benchmarks and enabling interpretation of the hidden reasoning process through a dedicated decoder.

## Problem

- Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) using Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting generate verbose textual reasoning steps.
- This verbosity leads to high computational costs, increased inference latency, and significant memory footprint, hindering practical deployment.
- Existing CoT compression or efficiency methods often result in performance degradation or lack generalizability to large-scale multimodal models.

## Method

- Introducing ==Heima==, a novel framework comprising a ==Heima Encoder and Heima Decoder==, for efficient multimodal reasoning.
- The Heima Encoder compresses verbose Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps into single, compact =="thinking tokens"== through a progressive encoding fine-tuning strategy.
- The Heima Decoder, a separate LLM, reconstructs textual CoTs from the ==hidden states of thinking tokens==, validating the encapsulation of reasoning.

## Results

- Heima achieved substantial token reduction, generating as little as **6%** of the original token count on various multimodal reasoning benchmarks.
- The framework consistently outperformed baseline models like Llama3.2-11B-Vision-Instruct and LLaVA-CoT in both efficiency and zero-shot accuracy across 6 diverse benchmarks, notably improving MMBench accuracy from **70.7%** to **72.8%**.
- The Heima Decoder successfully reconstructed textual Chain-of-Thought steps from the hidden thinking tokens, confirming that complex multimodal reasoning information is effectively encapsulated in the compressed latent representations.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Complex Chain-of-Thought reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) can be effectively compressed into compact hidden representations (single "thinking tokens") without sacrificing accuracy.
> - A progressive fine-tuning strategy is essential for MLLMs to internalize verbose textual reasoning into these hidden states while maintaining or improving performance.
> - Separately trained decoders can successfully reconstruct high-quality textual explanations from these hidden thinking tokens, validating that genuine, interpretable reasoning is encapsulated in the latent space.
