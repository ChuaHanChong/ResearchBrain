# A Multi-Power Law for Loss Curve Prediction Across Learning Rate Schedules

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Tsinghua University and partners derive a Multi-Power Law (MPL) that accurately predicts the training loss curves of large language models under different learning rate schedules, enabling optimization of training efficiency while reducing the computational cost of hyperparameter tuning through theoretical analysis and empirical validation across multiple model architectures.

## Problem

- Existing scaling laws don't account for learning rate schedules, limiting their practical utility
- Hyperparameter tuning for large language models is extremely resource-intensive
- Understanding the relationship between learning rate schedules and training loss evolution

## Method

- Developed ==Multi-Power Law (MPL)== combining ==learning rate sum matching== with ==decay-induced loss reduction==
- Created a theoretical framework explaining power-law behavior in optimization landscapes
- Implemented schedule optimization based on loss predictions

## Results

- MPL accurately predicts loss curves for unseen schedules and longer training horizons
- Discovered learning rate schedules that outperform standard cosine decay
- Provided theoretical justification through analysis of quadratic loss functions
- Demonstrated applicability across different model sizes and architectures

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training runs with same cumulative learning rate tend to have similar losses
> - Loss reduction exhibits power-law relationship with learning rate decay
> - Optimized schedules naturally resemble Warmup-Stable-Decay pattern
