# ManiGaussian: Dynamic Gaussian Splatting for Multi-task Robotic Manipulation

> [!summary] Summary
> A novel framework, ManiGaussian, extends Gaussian Splatting to predict future scene states conditioned on robot actions, improving language-conditioned robotic manipulation. This approach enables agents to learn and leverage scene-level spatiotemporal dynamics for more robust task completion.

## Problem

- Perceptive robotic manipulation methods often struggle with comprehensive 3D scene understanding, especially in unstructured environments with occlusions or limited camera views.
- Generative methods, like NeRFs, reconstruct static 3D scenes but largely ignore spatiotemporal dynamics, failing to model how objects interact or move during manipulation.
- Robots lack an explicit understanding of scene-level dynamics, which is crucial for accurately predicting action outcomes and successfully completing complex human-specified manipulation tasks.

## Method

- Introduces a ==Dynamic Gaussian Splatting== (==DGS==) framework that models the propagation of geometric (position, rotation) and semantic features of objects in an embedding space over time.
- Develops a ==Gaussian World Model== with a ==Deformation Predictor== to infer changes in Gaussian parameters (positions, rotations) for each primitive based on current scene state and robot actions, effectively predicting future scene states. This is the core of **ManiGaussian**.
- Utilizes a comprehensive set of learning objectives including ==future scene consistency loss==, which forces the model to learn and embed scene-level spatiotemporal dynamics by comparing predicted and actual future scenes.

## Results

- Achieved an average success rate of **44.8%** across **10** challenging RLBench tasks, outperforming the previous state-of-the-art generative method (GNFactor) by **13.1%** absolute (**41.3%** relative).
- Ablation studies confirmed that the dynamic modeling component (deformation predictor and future scene consistency loss) significantly boosted performance by **4.4%**.
- Demonstrated superior training efficiency, being **2.29x** faster than GNFactor, while also showcasing improved novel view synthesis and accurate future state prediction qualitatively.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Explicitly learning scene-level spatiotemporal dynamics through action-conditioned future scene reconstruction in a Gaussian embedding space is crucial for robust robotic manipulation.
> - Gaussian Splatting, known for its efficiency in 3D representation, can be effectively extended to model dynamic, interactive scenes for predictive control in robotics.
> - Distilling high-level semantic features from pre-trained foundation models into the Gaussian representation enhances contextual understanding for complex manipulation tasks.
