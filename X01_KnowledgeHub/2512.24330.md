# SenseNova-MARS: Empowering Multimodal Agentic Reasoning and Search via Reinforcement Learning

> [!summary] Summary
> Developed by SenseTime Research, Tsinghua University, and USTC, SenseNova-MARS is a multimodal agentic reasoning and search framework enabling Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to dynamically integrate image search, text search, and image crop tools via a novel Reinforcement Learning algorithm, BN-GSPO. This framework achieves state-of-the-art performance, including a score of 41.64 on the new HR-MMSearch, matching Gemini-3-Flash, and excels across various search-oriented and fine-grained visual understanding benchmarks.

## Problem

- Traditional Vision-Language Models (VLMs) primarily rely on text-based reasoning, struggling to seamlessly integrate visual analysis directly into the reasoning process.
- Existing VLM agents often invoke external tools in an isolated or sequential manner, hindering their ability to handle complex, multi-step problems requiring adaptive tool use.
- Human-like proficiency in real-world tasks demands coordinating external tools with iterative reasoning for both extensive knowledge retrieval and precise visual perception, particularly with high-resolution images.

## Method

- Introduces SenseNova-MARS, a multimodal agentic framework that dynamically integrates image search, text search, and image crop tools into a ==continuous, multi-turn reasoning process==.
- Employs a two-stage training pipeline, beginning with supervised fine-tuning (SFT) for basic tool usage, followed by Reinforcement Learning (RL) to refine multi-tool collaboration and adaptive reasoning.
- Develops ==BN-GSPO (Batch-Normalized Group Sequence Policy Optimization)==, a novel RL algorithm that uses a ==two-stage normalization method== to stabilize training and ensure consistent learning signals across diverse multi-tool trajectories.

## Results

- SenseNova-MARS-8B achieved leading performance on search-oriented benchmarks, scoring **67.84** on MMSearch and **41.64** on the new HR-MMSearch, matching Gemini-3-Flash.
- The model demonstrated superior fine-grained visual understanding with state-of-the-art scores of **92.2** on V* Bench, **83.1** on HR-Bench 4K, and **78.4** on HR-Bench 8K.
- The proposed BN-GSPO algorithm significantly improved the stability and effectiveness of multi-tool RL training, outperforming GRPO and GSPO in ablation studies and leading to more streamlined, efficient tool usage.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Dynamically interleaving tool invocation (search and image manipulation) with continuous reasoning enables VLMs to address complex visual and knowledge-intensive tasks more effectively than isolated tool use.
> - Reinforcement Learning, specifically with robust algorithms like BN-GSPO, is crucial for teaching VLM agents adaptive and efficient multi-tool collaboration strategies in dynamic environments.
> - A hybrid training data distribution, combining both search-oriented and fine-grained perception tasks, is essential for developing a unified multi-tool policy that balances external knowledge retrieval with precise visual analysis.
