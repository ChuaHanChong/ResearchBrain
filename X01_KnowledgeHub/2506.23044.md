# Ovis-U1 Technical Report

> [!summary] Summary
> Ovis-U1, a 3-billion-parameter unified model from Alibaba Group, integrates multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and image editing capabilities, achieving competitive performance across these tasks through a six-stage unified training approach that simultaneously optimizes all components.

## Problem

- Developing AI models capable of seamlessly integrating and operating across multiple modalities (text, image) for both understanding and generation tasks.
- Endowing multimodal understanding models with image generation capabilities without relying on frozen or decoupled components.
- Designing an effective unified training approach that allows for collaborative improvement across understanding and generation tasks, rather than separate or sequential optimization.

## Method

- Introduced ==Ovis-U1==, a 3-billion-parameter unified model architecture built on Qwen3-1.7B, incorporating a visual encoder, a ==diffusion transformer (MMDiT)== as a visual decoder, and a novel ==bidirectional token refiner==.
- Implemented a ==six-stage progressive unified training procedure==, updating the LLM and other components using a diverse dataset that includes multimodal understanding, text-to-image generation, and various image editing data.
- Utilized architectural elements like an ==adaptive visual encoder== for arbitrary image resolutions and a learnable CLS token in the refiner for improved cross-modal interaction.

## Results

- Ovis-U1 achieved an average score of **69.6** on OpenCompass Multi-modal Academic Benchmarks for understanding, outperforming other open-source models in its parameter range.
- Demonstrated strong generative capabilities, scoring **0.89** on GenEval and **83.72** on DPG-Bench for text-to-image generation.
- Secured competitive results in image editing, with a score of **4.00** on ImgEdit-Bench and **6.42** on GEdit-Bench-EN, comparable to larger proprietary models in some aspects.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Training a unified model from a language model, with collaborative optimization across understanding and generation tasks, improves performance in both domains.
> - A bidirectional token refiner enhances interaction between visual and textual embeddings, contributing to better conditional guidance in generation and reducing reliance on external models like CLIP.
> - Progressive training stages, particularly incorporating image editing data late in the pipeline, can synergistically boost overall multimodal capabilities.
