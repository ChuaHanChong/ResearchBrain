# Extracting Multimodal Learngene in CLIP: Unveiling the Multimodal Generalizable Knowledge

> [!summary] Summary
> Researchers from Southeast University developed Multimodal Learngene (MM-LG), a framework that extracts generalizable knowledge from CLIP, enabling efficient initialization of diverse multimodal and unimodal models. The method reduces pre-training costs by 2.8 times and parameter storage by 75% compared to full pre-training, while achieving comparable or superior performance across various tasks, notably improving image captioning.

## Problem

- Large multimodal foundation models like CLIP require significant computational resources for pre-training and necessitate repetitive large-scale pre-training when different model scales or architectures are needed for downstream applications.
- Existing 'Learngene' paradigms, which aim to extract generalizable knowledge for efficient initialization, primarily focused on unimodal scenarios and lacked mechanisms to handle the unique characteristics of multimodal knowledge within models like CLIP.
- There was a need for a method to efficiently extract and transfer the powerful cross-modal understanding capabilities of CLIP into smaller, task-specific models without redundant training.

## Method

- Introduces ==Multimodal Learngene (MM-LG)==, a framework that extracts multimodal generalizable knowledge from a large CLIP model, comprising a 'Block Part' (vision, language, multimodal blocks) and a 'Coefficient Part' (learnable coefficients).
- An auxiliary model is constructed whose layer parameters are formed as a weighted sum of these extracted ==learngene blocks== and trained using knowledge distillation from the ancestry CLIP model, combining CLIP's contrastive loss with a logit distillation loss.
- The extracted learngene is used to numerically initialize descendant models of various scales and modalities, followed by a brief =='parameter activation' training phase== on a small fraction of the pre-training dataset.

## Results

- MM-LG consistently outperforms existing unimodal Learngene approaches (He-LG, TLEG) across cross-modal retrieval, image classification, and image captioning, with up to **+4.13%** I2T Recall@1 on Flickr30k over TLEG.
- The framework achieves performance comparable to or superior to the traditional Pre-training and Fine-tuning (PT-FT) paradigm, notably outperforming PT-FT in image captioning by over **5 CIDEr points**.
- MM-LG significantly reduces parameter storage by **75%** and pre-training costs by approximately **2.8 times** compared to PT-FT, while maintaining or improving task performance.

## Takeaways

> [!tip] Key Insights
> - Generalizable knowledge within large multimodal models like CLIP can be effectively decomposed and extracted into distinct unimodal (vision, language) and multimodal interaction components.
> - A knowledge distillation approach can be used to train an auxiliary model that learns to compose these fundamental 'learngene' components, capturing the behavior of the large ancestry model efficiently.
> - A brief parameter activation step using a small subset of data is crucial after numerical initialization from the extracted learngene to effectively prepare descendant models for downstream tasks.
